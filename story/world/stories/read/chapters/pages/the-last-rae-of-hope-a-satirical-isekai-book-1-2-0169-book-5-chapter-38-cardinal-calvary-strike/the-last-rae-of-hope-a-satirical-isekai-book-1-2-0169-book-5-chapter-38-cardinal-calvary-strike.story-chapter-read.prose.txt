General Ragnerus was faster than the simulations.

Galenus's version had been structurally and motivationally accurate. Still, the real deal was in full animus overload and had just blasted out of a sealed canyon, finding the exact person he intended to seek revenge on.

The gap between us closed so fast that my exhaustive training suddenly felt very theoretical.

Except Aurum didn't waver, so neither would I.

My pink aura spread as we closed the distance. Aurum's coat shimmered with it, as Will and Faith caught the rose light at the edges. None of it was the customary gold of the Chosen One.

If Ragnerus was curious about the color change, however, it didn't slow him down one bit.

Ba-thuumm!

Our first pass shoved his crackling greatsword back and away, the impact ringing up Faith's surface and into my elbow. Will came across in the same breath, with Aurum's momentum doing most of the work. I just had to place him.

Thud!

Something caught the slash. To what extent, I couldn't tell, because our pass was already over, with a dark portal hurling us back and over so we were now charging at Ragnerus's left flank.

A group of winged Angels descended from the sky. Bodhil and Beigaldi rose and flickered to intercept, electric-blue sparks shimmering from their weapons.

Second pass. A diagonal strike as Will sliced across the right flank. Ragnerus's downward strike was half a second too late.

Screams of frustration suddenly cut off, then resumed as another portal dumped us out behind him.

More Angels were descending now, with Olethros calling up black vines to tie them to the ground.

"General Ragnerus!" Beigaldi cried from the side. "Stand down! We're here to talk!"

His head turned in response. Then the air around him turned electric. The hairs on my arms stood straight up half a second before the lightning came down.

Three bolts in tight succession. Two struck something above us.

Angels!

They screamed as they dropped.

The last bolt hit about three feet to my left, with the shockwave rolling over Aurum and me.

He flinched, but he was still running, muscles bunching and springing.

My ears rang with a high-pitched whine.

Third pass.

Will slid across the top of Ragnerus's arm—

And he dissolved into mist.

Another portal turn.

Aurum ran straight for his coalescing form this time, rather than angling for yet another pass. I felt the hiss of his blessed horseshoes churning through the vapor, the resulting paralysis snapping Ragnerus into a semi-solid state even as we bore its more-than-just static shock. Something pulled at Aurum as we left the painful area of effect, but he successfully kicked away with a loud snort, launching toward yet another portal.

Bodhil was on Ragnerus's left, pointing at the Demon King and shouting. The General, better formed now, roared, striking out with a free hand. He instinctively plucked her core from her body, then swallowed it even before her blue fans hit the ground, flaring a deeper crimson.

Our fourth pass completed the first-ever Cardinal Cavalry Strike—patent pending.

We had completed a full asterisk of attacks against him, each angle landing a clean hit. The final slash cracked loud enough to cut through the whine in my ears.

He staggered, his greatsword swinging wildly. Then he cracked open, literally. Pink light fractured across his surface. He dropped to his knees, howling with pain and rage.

Olethros was on him before I was.

"You look terrible," he said as he hovered above him, perfectly calm. "Was it something you ate?"

Ragnerus roared even as I charged to intercept, the General's greatsword shooting blasts at the taunting demon king.

A point of clarification now.

No matter what anyone says, I didn't fall off my horse. It was a choice. I pushed off the saddle as momentum carried me forward. I hit Ragnerus mid-chest, sword deflecting sword, my free hand grasping at the demonic core my attacks had exposed. We ended rolling together, but even with his form surging with crimson corrosion, I kept my grip locked.

He screamed as I tightened, both of us bouncing along the dirt.

His core was extremely hot, hotter than it should be, so intense that it could burn through my gauntleted hand.

I held on anyway.

"Yield," I commanded from atop him, directly into his shattered face.

He howled and bucked, squealing in the demon tongue. His core continued to fight me, with black animus crackling up my arm.

"I know what Relias did. He did it to me, too."

He paused, but his rage was still too loud.

Time to show him.

I pushed at him with full amity. I wasn't fighting his overload so much as nudging it aside a bit so it could see what I had to say.

The cold, wet floor of Paradise, covered in my blood.

The image of Olethros's scythe in my back was more of a reconstruction of my imagination, but I played back Relias's voice. Calm, measured, explaining what he had done across the eras and why my death was necessary for him to continue.

It hurt to think about it, but I needed Ragnerus to feel that hurt too, so I reopened the wound that hadn't quite scabbed over.

Ragnerus went very still.

"Irrelevant," he snarled with the slightest quiver. His emotions, however, cried out for his father. His rage didn't disappear, but it was starting to flow in a different direction.

I opened my eyes, still gripping his core.

Somewhere on my periphery, I saw Nora and Tetora throwing gemstones like shurikens, their spiked ends striking and biting the angels' armor with loud clangs. Blue waves of paralysis pulsed through them, halting their advance.

"Yield," I commanded again, pressing Will's tip against his core. "And join us. Or die."

"It's a trick," he rasped, shuddering as the cracks in his flesh spread.

"To what Purpose?' I demanded.

He didn't answer.

"Yield, and help us take Chairo away from him!"

That got his attention.

"I will join you, but I refuse to yield."

"Haaa..." I replied, pushing his core back into him with a bit of a shove before standing. "Fine, semantics then. But no killing any of us. And cough up Bodhil. Now."

Otherwise, I'd never hear the end of it.

There was a long pause, and the crimson drained from his eyes. "I... I ate her...?"

He sat up, heaving, reached into his own stomach through one of his cracks, then pulled and tossed Bodhil's core aside. Beigaldi apparated and took it.

"General...!" he cried gratefully.

The oversized, charcoal-blackened demon lord held up a hand, blasting one of the approaching angels with lightning. "I will... need a report... as soon as—"

Then he dissolved entirely into mist, leaving just his core on the ground.

I picked it up.

It was still hot, but no longer searing to touch. Above us, more Angels regrouped, flinging black Ereborite stones at us, most likely hoping to catch us off guard.

I shielded his core and begrudgingly thanked my sapphire tiara for deflecting an errant Ereborite implantation.

"Galenus," I called.

Multiple portals opened, all at once, forming a ring of dark openings around the party. A few Angels tried to join us, but something in their composition literally melted their metallic forms. The last thing I saw before my portal closed was a clawed Angel hand, desperately clutching at my face before it liquified into nothing.
