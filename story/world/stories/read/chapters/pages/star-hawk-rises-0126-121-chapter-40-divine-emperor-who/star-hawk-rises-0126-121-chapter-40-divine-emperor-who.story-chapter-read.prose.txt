Our group will be starting at one of the engineering stations. ”What the hell is this thing?” I whispered to myself before simply mentally asking the table, ‘what is the problem?’ I wasn’t surprised when it didn’t respond.

It took me an hour just to figure out how to find the problem. I used the table to pull apart the engine and remove a part that looked like it had melted slightly. It then took me another two hours to figure out I needed to print the part and how to do that. Once the part was declared printed, I had to put it back into the engine and reassemble it. I had just managed to finish that when a loud noise blared from every direction.

”Time's up!” The robed woman who had led us here yelled. “You may continue designing your ship for extra points, or forgo that completely and return to the cabin for some relaxation before tomorrow's formations exam begins.
