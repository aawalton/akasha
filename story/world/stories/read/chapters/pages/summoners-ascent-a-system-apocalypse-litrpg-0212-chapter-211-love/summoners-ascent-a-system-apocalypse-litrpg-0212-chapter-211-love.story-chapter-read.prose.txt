"What is this nonsense?" the Summoner asked, standing in front of the obelisk. They looked around the room. "These ruins..."

The moment he heard the Summoner's voice, Ashton fell to his knees.

'They already woke up?' he thought to himself. 'I thought I still had more time...'

"Human, how did you find this place?" the Summoner asked, as the creature twitched in their hands. Ashton's mind was racing, as so many different things came to his mind.

Should he congratulate them on waking up first? Should he explain that he completed the tasks that they gave him? Or should he just–

"Answer me, human."

As the Summoner's command coursed through Ashton's body, he felt fully compelled to do as he was told. It wasn't just the fear. It was physically impossible for him to not do as he was told.

Ashton fell forward and pushed his forehead against the ground.

"As I was travelling the continent, fulfilling your commands, I sought out rumours related to other matters that I know you are seeking out. One of them led me here," Ashton explained.

"And here I thought you were planning on cutting yourself off from me," the Summoner replied. "But you have done well. I thought I had already collected all fragments from this world."

Following their matter-of-fact statement, the Summoner held the creature in their hands above their face. The mask that was covering them at the moment cracked open, revealing the deep, toothy abyss that acted as the Summoner's mouth.

Bite by bite, the Summoner tore the creature's body apart, until not a single speck remained. The Summoner's body briefly shook and shifted in a way that was unfamiliar to Ashton, before settling back down.

"Now let us go. You–"

"Master," Ashton said, interrupting the Summoner. The very fact that he did that made him panic, but he just had to. After all, for whatever reason, it seemed as though the Summoner wasn't able to see whatever that thing in the obelisk was. "That... 'fragment' is not why I came here. I was seeking out the object beside you, hidden in that black cube."

The Summoner turned their attention toward the obelisk and placed their hand onto it. Just like the other creature, it was blistering and melting, but they didn't seem to care much. "Ah. Now I see. They were hiding it from me."

Slowly, the Summoner's body started shaking. The were pulsating, in a way that could only be compared to the convulsing of a dying caterpillar.

The Summoner was laughing.

They pushed their appendage into the obelisk, and slowly lifted the object from within. It was enclosed in their hand, so Ashton couldn't see what it was. There were just some beams of light that were escaping the rapidly decaying limb. Ones that Ashton quickly shielded Oka from.

He turned around, and felt his heart drop into his stomach. Oka had collapsed onto their knees and was shaking at the sight of the Summoner. Ashton quickly shielded them, putting his torso in front of their face. The rays of light escaping from the Summoner burnt his back to some degree, but as long as Oka was fine, he didn't care.

"Th-That thing is... that thing is your summoner?" Oka's voice shook. It carried a fear with it that Ashton had never seen from them before, but one that he recognised just perfectly well. It was the same fear that he felt when he first saw the Summoner.

Even though Oka already knew about them, there was little that could prepare you for that maddening sight.

"Thing?" the Summoner repeated. "Who dares to call me a 'thing'?!"

Ashton snapped his head around in fear. The light from that object was already gone, as if the Summoner had swallowed it whole. They came and moved forward, looming over the pair."This is the toy that you had collected while I let you off your leash?"

Ashton's jaw shook. "Th-This is Oka... my, my spouse..."

"Your spouse? Had you not said that they were naught but a tool to help you achieve your goal?" the Summoner asked, and Ashton slowly averted his gaze.

"I... things changed..." he whispered, but the Summoner wouldn't have it. They grabbed Ashton, enclosing most of his body in one of their appendages. It was the same one they used to grab that powerful object.

But Ashton could feel something pull on his leg.

"No! No, let him be! Leave him alone!" Oka had grabbed onto him, trying to stop the Summoner from simply taking him away.

"Pesky little bug," the Summoner scoffed, swatting one of their hands at Oka. They were thrown away backward against the ruin's wall, and Ashton could hear their bones cracking and their muscles tearing as they fell, vomiting on the ground in pain.

This was the first time in more than twenty years that Ashton let the urge to kill the Summoner bubble up within him. Sure, he had felt hatred for the Summoner; with every part of his being, he despised them. But he could never even hope to dream of hurting the Summoner.

But now, they went too far.

"Don't touch them!" Ashton screamed out, biting into the Summoner's hand. It was the only thing he could think of. He let his mana flow out through his mouth, interfering with the creature's essence. Their hand was already hurt, and as Ashton shifted and twisted their pattern, essentially enchanting the Summoner's flesh, their hand opened up, for just a brief moment.

As Ashton fell to the ground, he let all of his internal energy flow into his legs, and instantly jumped over to Oka's side. He supported their weak body, and as they looked up at him, they tried to speak, but only their cerulean blood sputtered out.

"It's okay, my love. It's okay, you will be okay," Ashton repeated, brushing the hair out of their face. He pushed his hand into his bag, and grabbed one of the potions that he created, and slowly placed it against Oka's lip. The moment it crossed the threshold, they visibly calmed down, and a blue mist rose from their body.

A less painful version of a rapid healing potion that Ashton had created specifically for Oka's constitution. It was toxic to almost every other being in this world, even Ashton himself.

He took off his bag and placed it below Oka's head, before standing up and turning toward the Summoner again.

He bowed.

"Please. I have done everything you have ever asked of me and more, and now, I have found pieces that you have long sought after. Forgive Oka and myself. I beg of you."

The Summoner stared at Ashton, with their numerous beady eyes. "You bite the hand that feeds you, and yet you still ask for forgiveness? How dare you?"

"All I ask for is that you will let Oka live. You will never have to see them again, I promise. If you let them live, then I swear that I will never question you ever again," Ashton said, shaking all over. He could hear Oka pleading behind him, begging him not to leave them.

It seemed that for a few moments, the Summoner seemed to actually consider it. And then, they spoke. "Fine. They shall live for the crime of insulting me, and I will let go your act of physical resistance against me as well. After all, you did present me with two wonderful gifts today."

Ashton let out a sigh of relief. Sure, the way things were going, they wouldn't allow Oka to exist around them, meaning that Ashton might never be able to see them again. But with how things went, it was better than them killing him outright.

"Thank you, Master. I promise, I will never–"

"However," the Summoner started, and Ashton's blood ran cold. "There are three more crimes that you have committed."

"Three... crimes?"

"One. You were not standing ready by my side when I awoke. Two, you interrupted me as I spoke earlier," The Summoner leaned forward. As they did, it was like they were starting to cover this entire chamber. "And three, why are you standing before me, instead of kneeling as you should?"

Ashton looked back at the Summoner in a panic. "P-Please, please don't do this! Please, I love them, just let this go! This one time, please! I–"

"Don't worry. I won't kill them." The Summoner's voices tore into Ashton's ears, as the command that followed compelled him. "You will. Kill them, human."

Ashton felt his body tense up as the Summoner let out that deadly cackle.

He turned around, and looked down at Oka, who was barely able to stay conscious. They were mumbling something, reaching out their hand toward him, as Ashton slowly climbed onto their body.

Every part of his being was protesting against this. Ashton's internal energy was going wild inside of him, tearing apart his muscles, hoping that he wouldn't be able to do this.

As Ashton sat on Oka's torso, he placed his hands around their throat. The Summoner's gaze felt sharper, and Ashton tightened his grip.

Oka's body tensed up, and he could see the fear in their eyes. He couldn't avert his gaze from them.

But even so, with every part of himself, Ashton tried to rip himself from this, but the Summoner's command was absolute.

Ashton's tears dropped down onto Oka's face, as the fear slowly faded from them. They reached their hands up to Ashton, and placed them around his neck. Not to strangle him; to pull him in.

With the last bit of strength left in their body, they pulled Ashton closer and placed a kiss on his lips.

For that moment; for that brief instant, Ashton was able to stop himself. He was freed from the command, and as Oka's weakened body all but went limp, he took his hands away from her, and placed them on the base of his head and broke his own neck.

Death was terrifying. Even though Ashton's body had met its end many times before at this point, it didn't change the fear that Ashton felt. Not for death itself; but for the violation that came after it.

Ashton felt the chains on his soul tighten, as he was ripped away from the calmness of a world beyond life, just to be forced into a newly created body by the Summoner.

"How dare you?! Human, kill them!" The Summoner screamed out, piercing his ears with that command. Ashton, fully nude in this new form, walked over to Oka. He placed a hand onto their cheek, kissed them once again, and then let his internal energy flow into his heart so that he collapsed on the ground immediately.

And again, he was brought back.

The Summoner repeated the command over and over again, but Ashton didn't lay his hands on Oka in violence for even a single moment anymore. The very idea of hurting them was gone from his mind.

It took dozens of times for the Summoner to give up.

"Why?! Why do despise me so, but love that to the point you risk death over and over again?!" The Summoner yelled, grasping the new body that had just been formed. "I have bound your soul, I have grasped your mind, I am the master over every part of your existence! I have set you free and allowed you to roam, I have given you strength that no being from your world could ever even dream of! So how can you defy me?!"

Ashton heard the Summoner's voices, but he could feel nothing but pity in this moment as they threw a child-like tantrum.

And then, the Summoner said something that Ashton had not expected.

"Do you not see that I adore you so much? Why?! Why do you not love me?!"

Ashton stared at the Summoner, and couldn't help but scoff. "I could never love something like you."

The Summoner's grasp on Ashton loosened and he fell to the ground.

"I see... I see," they replied, as they waved their hand and dug deep spikes of stone into all parts of his body. He was locked down and couldn't even hope to move.

The Summoner grabbed Oka.

"Ash...ton..." Oka whispered. Most of their injuries were healed, but they were still too weak to do anything. "Now... and until... the stars turn to ash..."

He tried to protest. He tried to move and break out from this stone, but he couldn't.

"... you have my heart... until the end of time itself..."

Those were the last words that Oka spoke before the Summoner crushed their body.

Blue blood flowed onto the ground, as numerous of the Summoner's limbs grabbed various parts of Oka's body, and tore them apart. They threw small chunks of flesh and bone to the ground, creating a pile before them.

The ring that Oka had not taken off their finger even once slowly rolled over to Ashton's feet.

Once the Summoner was done, they removed the spikes from Ashton and healed him immediately. He fell to his knees, and stared at the ring, as a puddle of blue blood inched closer and closer toward him.

Ashton knelt there, slowly grabbing the ring.

"Oh, and by the way," the Summoner started. Ashton felt them place the wedding crown that Oka had made for him onto his head. "Congratulations on the marriage, human."

Ashton played with the Arcane Heart in his hands. He faced away the entire time that he was telling the story. He didn't want his friends to see his expression as he spoke. Ashton also stopped channelling the wisp, because he couldn't dare see theirs, either.

"So that's it," Ashton said, smiling nervously. "That's the story of Oka and I. Pretty depressing, I know."

Ashton felt two arms close around him, as Clementine pulled him into a hug, and Sam enclosed both of them in an embrace of his own.

Nobody said anything anymore. Of course, they were lost for words. But words weren't needed in this moment anyway.
