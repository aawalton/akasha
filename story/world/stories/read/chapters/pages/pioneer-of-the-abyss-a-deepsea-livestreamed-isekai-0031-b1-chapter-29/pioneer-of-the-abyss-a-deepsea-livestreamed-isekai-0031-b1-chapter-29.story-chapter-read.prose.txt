Making several large tentacles appear before moving into the electricity to swat the jellyfish. Literally bursting them the moment they touch them, showcasing just how strong I’ve become underwater.

All to work together and boost each other as a sort of debut for my career.

Speaking of that music video though, I’ll be going to that the day after tomorrow. Where they’re going to listen to me singing for the first time and get me a trainer depending on how well I do. Or how poorly I do.

It still feels so surreal doing all of this. But that’s a problem for tomorrow me to deal with.

Today me is hunting jellyfish.

After the tentacles finish killing all the jellyfish in the range of their movements, they begin to pick up boulders and other large objects to throw at other bloodzap jellyfish. Killing them as well.

Okay then. Not a single level out of all that.

Very disappointing.

Time for round two.

Instead of summoning the tentacles, this time I go ahead and use the pressurized water. Sending out blades of it one after another that slice straight into the jellyfish. Making a thick red liquid spill out of them mixed with electricity that flows through the water.

Then I proceed to kill the jellyfish one after another using this one skill. Slaughtering the lot of them without pause.

Although I’m rather curious about what the trait will be.

Physique of the Abyssal Kraken obviously comes from the Abyssal Orca. With what looks like a slightly higher strength output than what the Abyssal Orca has and with a different name. Meanwhile Apex Predator of the Depths is from the Kraken side since it’s regeneration.

Oh, and I’ll get another skill at level 25 as well. But the trait is more important.

Traits only appear at specific levels after all, unlike the skills which are a lot more common. Especially at the lower levels like my current level.

After a few seconds of pondering over what my next trait will be though, I go back to slaughtering bloodzap jellies. All the way until I finish clearing out the cavern of them. Then I finally begin to head back for the day.

Because it’s starting to get late, and I still need to make it all the way back to my little home if I want to get enough rest tonight to handle the work tomorrow.

So I swim. And swim. And continue swimming for quite a while before I finally make it back to my little cavern, where I eat, take care of my usual routine, and finally go ahead and strip out of my armor to sleep.

Because there’s no reason to wear my armor while sleeping anymore.

It’s just uncomfortable. And since I’m safe here I don’t need to worry about being attacked in my sleep.

Then I go ahead and lie down before falling asleep rather quickly thanks to being tired from spending all day swimming and hunting.
