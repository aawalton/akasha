Unlike other dragons, she could really go anywhere, and unlike other dragons, she rushed home alone.

She felt it in her bones as she traveled. Something had happened to her home while she was away.

Bubbles burbled up and monsters flung themselves out of her way in fear of her might.

Had all her solitude been for nothing?

She could fly far. She could travel across the lands, over the forests, and claim any place as her home. Any clutch of dragons would have her. She was powerful as any dragon, and all knew her special abilities.

The other dragons tried their best to convince her of their ways when she did attend Meeting. It happened once a year, hosted by the massive clutch of Grand Canyon, and any dragon in North America was welcome. She went, as she was lonely.

But she never gave into their arguments. She knew where her home was.

They tried to appeal to her ego first. How gorgeous her brown scales were. How awe-inspiring her green eyes shined. How none were shaped like her, and it only spoke to her abilities. They were fattened and plump, where she kept herself lean. It helped her move in all the various ways only she could.

She regretted going to Meeting. She should have just stayed in her nest behind the Falls like always. She had no clue what had happened, only that something had.

There’s no such thing as a solitary dragon, they’d argued, besides you. What good is a lonesome dragon? Some would laugh. Some would judge. Dragons are not bound by simple regions, they’d explain. Dragons can fly anywhere, fight anything in a clutch, and any damage is healed by absorbing treasures. Think of all our treasures we hold! We offer it up freely, for you! We gain more power when we share our hordes, can’t you see that? We are all supposed to be part of a clutch.

They called her greedy.

Come and stay in Yellowstone, there’s dozens of dragons available to socialize and mate with. Join the eastern seaboard of Canada clutch. The eating and relics are fantastic, and the salt is good for your scales.

She knew what they wanted. Behind the invitations and judgement, their draconic desire was easily spotted. Due to her dragon type, nothing could hide from her. While they said they could fly anywhere, she could get anywhere. Be anywhere.

She was much like a cat in that way. It was a joke she told herself. She’d laugh alone in her cave of simple treasures. A dragon that liked water and mud.

She wasn’t laughing now.

Had all her patience been for nothing? Had all the fighting against her urges of luxury and laziness been wasted?

Or was what she waited for finally here? Had the flow of water stopped?

Think of the hatchlings you could produce, they said. You’re the only one of your kind, and you owe it to us to spread your abilities. Think of the children! Their own greed shined through their arguments.

She was greedy too, of course. All dragons were. She was just greedy for bigger things than simple treasure and lounging and getting fat. Since she’d been born, she knew where she was meant to be.

Alone at the bottom of the Falls she’d awoken, small and with dull brown scales, wedged in the muddy shoreline. She’s pulled herself out of the muck, and the Title she’d been born with let her know exactly where she should be.

[Princess in Waiting]

[By gushing water of your birth you shall call home. Stay with certainty of a waterfall until water falls uncertainly. The treasure of you is promised all and arrives after deepest uncertainty.]

She had waited from the beginning of her life and made the Falls her home. Alone and for the promise of a treasure from a Title.

Now, she was certain in her uncertainness. She found that funny, despite the feeling inside her, and bubbles trickled up as she propelled herself through the muck faster.

She thought herself special in many ways. Other dragons blathered constantly. They ate and ate, thickening their waists, opting for simple treasures, gluttony, and the comfort of draconic monoculture. They could only fly one way. She was quiet and could fly any which.

She was special. It was there in her Title, [Princess in Waiting], in her attitude, and her abilities. She was certainly greedy; they were correct about that. In fact, she was the greediest of all.

She was a treasure, as the Title said, and promised all. So, she’d guarded herself like one, and her solitary, lonely home was her chest.

Boanne, the [Mud Dragon], who was supposed to be at Niagara Falls guarding her home and waiting, flew.

She traveled as no other dragon could to see if the water had stopped flowing. To see if her waiting was over.

Boanne soared along the bottom of the Lake Ontario, coasting through sand, mud, plants, rock, and water as if it were wind. She flapped her wings, catching a current that sent her shooting out of a cresting wave. She coasted, spotted three beings in the sky travelling the same way she was, and blasted back into the waters to maintain her speed.

Were they going to the Falls too?

Giddiness bubbled within as she delighted in her travel.

Unlike other dragons, everything was air to her wings.

Alex’s belly felt fine. It didn’t tumble or clench. That surprised him, given his current situation.

Acorn’s attack on the chasing birds had blown apart half the Falls. Before, there was one massive fall. Now the top half had been blown fifty feet backwards, making one massive waterfall into two big, step shaped ones. The force had practically burst his ear drums and caused him to lose control of The Expediter.

“GO FORTH!” Dandelion screamed manically from the roof as they flew over the newly formed Falls. “LEAVE THE SHADOW AND ENTER THE FRAY! TOGETHER WE WILL GET THE TAX GUILD STONE!”

They shot out from the shadow of the mushroom cloud. Alex clenched his legs tighter and aimed for the battle on the field next to the Falls. There must have been hundreds of people crammed in, all digging grass while simultaneously attacking their neighbours.

“How do they…know it’s here?” Brody grunted as loud as he could. “Why…so many people?”

Alex didn’t have a clue. He turned his face so Brody and Tank could hear him over the wind and fighting while keeping his eyes forward. Tank took the opportunity to lick Alex’s cheek…again.

“No idea!” Alex yelled. “But Tank’s sure it’s here? You’re sure you understand him? Back left corner is where it is? Fucking hell, we’re risking our lives on your new dog?! It’s chaos down there!”

Tank licked his cheek once more and barked.

The ground was quick quickly, and the crowd that wasn’t too busy brutally fighting turned toward them, pointing and yelling.

“I’m sure. It is…there. And I feel…” Brody grumbled out, not finishing his thought.

“You feel WHAT?” Alex yelled as the ground was suddenly on them. “Fuckkkkkkkkkkk!”

Alex yanked up on the handlebars and backward, intuitively slowing The Expediter. It didn’t work perfectly, and the vehicle landed with a grinding thump as its metal bottom met grass and the fields surrounding cobblestone walkway.

The Expediter hadn’t even stopped before Tank leapt over Alex’s shoulder.

WOOF

The massive pitbull jumped through the missing front window and spun toward the nearby crowd. Confronted with a dog of Tank’s size that was clearly half made of metal, many of the onlookers paled and backed away, opting to dig not near an angry pitbull.

But not all of the Hunters were scared of Tank.

“A feckin’ dog?” A burly, hairy man holding a gigantic club called to other men of a similar size. “We feckin’ hate dogs. Fithly animals.”

“Get it before it sniffs out the Stone!” One of the man’s friends yelled.

The big man swung his club over his shoulder, catching a digging woman nearby in the hip as he did so. The woman screamed as her femur shattered and she was flung twenty feet away. Hawking, the giant club wielding man spat a loogie and began lumbering towards Tank.

Tank barked again, snarling and growling while bearing his sharp teeth.

“We’re finding the Stone! Yer not finding shit! It’s ours! Get over ‘ere and lemme put ye’ down, dog!”

Alex heard Brody’s grunt as The Expediter finally came to a stop, lifting itself slightly off the ground. Then too many things happened at once for Alex to follow as he scrambled to get out of the vehicle and help.

“Oho,” Dandelion giggled from the roof. “Big man! And with such good earth to shoot with!”

A blur that was Brody shot over Alex’s shoulder through the missing front window.

Brody tucked into his dive, summersaulting to right himself, and activated [Elegy’s Exit] right away. He caught the timing right and filtered through the wedges in space and time to close the gap in a jittery dash, moving far faster than just a sprint.

Alex pulled himself through the front just as Brody had, and by the time he looked up from the vibrating ground and blinked, his clone was flying toward the big man in double-legged drop kick.

“I want in on the fun too!” Dandelion called. “Climb faster Acorn! Pull yourself up the rope and get in here!”

In and out of reality Brody glitched, his face a grimace as one frame his legs were tucked, and the next frame…

CRUNCH

Both of Brody’s feet connected with the big man’s knee, folding it backwards in a sickening crunch. The man screamed as he tumbled, swinging his club wildly.

“Not. My. Dog.” Alex heard Brody grumble as the clone landed and righted himself, quickly leaping over the man’s body.

“NOT MY DOG!” Brody barked and slammed his booted heel into the man’s face, stopping his screams as he shattered the man’s teeth into a mess.

The big man’s head smashed against the dirt and then lolled to the side, his giant snores confirming Brody had just knocked him out in two hits.

“De fuck!” Another big man wielding a massive sword shouted. “’E broke Jared’s knee and knocked ‘is teef out! KILL HIM AND DE DOG! If he’s dis strong, he probably already knows ‘zactly where the Stone is!”

Four massive men lumbered toward Brody with murder in their eyes.

Like his pitbull, Brody was practically snarling at the men. His fists were clenched, and Tank came right to his side to bark and froth at them.

“Fuck! Fuck!” Alex shouted, feeling the urge to protect Brody. He knew that Brody was a better fighter than him, but these were massive men wielding vicious weapons.

Alex reached for his teleport to Brody through [Illusory Copy]. It would send him to the unspace, and then Alex could [Phantom Step] them away to a safer position so Tank could show them exactly where—

“Don’t.” Brody held up his hand toward Alex, not meeting his eye. “Don’t you…dare send me away.”

Alex stopped himself as Tank barked in the face of the charging men. They were almost upon them.

He can tell exactly when I’m going to use it? Alex thought, That…that must be horri—

“Aye! Alex! Watch your right!” Dandelion shouted. “And let me help them so Tank can show us!”

Alex glanced right and spotted a similarly sized man holding a hammer the size of a wheelbarrow charging him.

“Where the hell are the drones!?” Alex said, facing the man, ready to dodge. “They would be a lot of help right about now!”

“Oh, don’t you worry,” Dandelion laughed as pebbled shook and lifted off the ground around The Expediter. “I’ll hold everyone off for a bit,”

Then Dandelion laughed, and over the din of battle, it sounded eerie.

Why does she sound so fricken excited? How is she louder than everyone else?

Focussed on the charging man and with worry about Brody, Alex didn’t see Dandelion charge up her GnUzi 45. The little violent Garden Gnome asked Gaia for some ammo, and it was delivered.

From the Earth, pebbles and dirt, silicon and the smallest aspects of life, all that could be considered natural flowed into her GnUzi.

Alright, let’s do what I do best, Alex thought, Be an uncatchable distraction.

Just before the man struck Alex, and the other men were about to run into Brody and Tank, Alex pulled himself upright, gave the man the finger guns, winked, and used [Phantom Step].

The massive hammer struck nothing but earth, and the man spun to search where the little bastard had gone, but all he heard was annoying laughter.

Then he heard the pop pop popping of something, a massive sound of teeth chomping, and his friends screaming in horror.

He turned again, searching, and the last thing that he heard was that annoying giggle before everything went black. He didn’t even feel the pebble that went right through his brain.

Climbing up a rope was difficult with one arm. Acorn had to grip the plant rope with his feet, reach, pull, and do it all again.

It was slow going.

“Right through his head, Acorn! I got him! I’m leading the battle!” Dandelion whispered from far away.

He heard her. He could always hear her. Acorn laughed to himself silently and climbed faster.

“The dog just swallowed some dude’s head in one bite! Brody is like a ghost, he’s broken three arms! Alex…is a surprisingly good distraction! People hate when he winks! Get up here and join the fun! We need you to hold people back so Tank can show us!”

Acorn was tired, but excited. So, he climbed the plant rope up the cliff where he hung to get to his love.

If she said they needed his help, he would do everything in his power to get to her.

“I want to see these drones too! Petal said they’re AMAZING fighters! What do you think they will be like? I bet they’ll have the best battle ballads.”

Acorn was curious himself. Other strange forms of life interested him. Apparently, they were similarly sized to Garden Gnomes.

“Think they’re songs will be all about computers ruling the world? Techno beats? Perfect haikus?”

Acorn laughed internally again, then stopped as he heard something strange from behind him.

He turned and found the source of the sound flying through the sky.

“Oh, come on, Beepy!” A tiny body in the shape of a person yelled as she hung with one arm from a flying drone. “Just let me try to make three of you! Think how cool it would be! Zippy? You up for it?! Caman!”

It appeared that the drones had arrived, and they weren’t at all what Acorn had suspected.

He couldn’t wait to hear Dandelion’s reaction.
