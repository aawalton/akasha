“Do you have any further advice for Samantha, Ozzy?” Azure asked. “How hard should she push, and how long will she be gone?”

Ozzy squinted her eyes and tapped a finger to her lips thoughtfully. “She should go all-out, as she has been, I think. Go not just for gold, but diamond, because she’s that sharp and on-point already. Her ascension is almost complete, her path leading up to the end struggling not to be a cakewalk. She should thus seek out challenges where she can find them before she faces the ultimate one. The final test here, on Calrenazzod, will be no victory lap, mind you. It’ll take every shred of her power to pass it. Every cheat should be harnessed beforehand.”

“I see,” Samantha said, feeling little but excitement at the prospect of the coming simulation. Certainly a far cry from the way she used to be. Being able to face something hard without worrying about the morality every damned step of the way? Priceless. “And the timeframe?”

“That seems hazy! I think the answer is: just right. It’ll take the time you have left, no more, no less.”

Samantha took a deep breath. “In that case, I suppose I'd better get everything that needs doing nice and wrapped up before leaving.”

“At least I’ll be here this time,” Azure said. “I can manage things.”

“You need to take it easy. Doctor Ozzy’s orders.”

Azure grimaced. “We’ll see.”

“Three days, at an absolute minimum. You will agree.”

Azure sighed. “Fine. Yes. I’ll smell the roses and such…” She plucked the cabbit off her head and gave it a pet over its head and ears, which caused it to smile, close its eyes, and purr. “Pet the cabbits, no less.”

Samantha nodded approvingly. “Perfect.”

“There will be farewell sex, though.”

It was Samantha’s turn to sigh. “Granted.” Because Ozzy was grinning impishly and waggling her eyebrows, Samantha waved her hand at her. “And you are dismissed!”

“But I had a final quip to-” Quite satisfyingly, the gremlin vanished mid-sentence.

Glancing at Azure, finding her making cute noises as the delighted cabbit, Samantha decided to just quietly slip away, bound for the ‘realm’ and the copious preparations she’d have to make before leaving. She could feel the hundred questions Jeeves already had lined up for her, not to mention Dax…

✦•············•✦•···········•✦

Preparations, preparations, preparations! They never end.

She had to instruct literally dozens of her top agents on delegated authority usually punted off to her, coach scores of people, and even calm them down in some cases. Poor Estara was already feeling overwhelmed with the duties saddled on her shoulders with Orswyth’s isolation, and now the Goddess herself was leaving, not to mention that Azure would be on vacation or light duty. It would be an organizational nightmare.

Samantha mostly patted her on the back and encouraged her that she would do well, being an ace at putting out fires. Samantha explained that this was what she did all day, anyway. It wasn’t much comfort, but in the days ahead, Estara would see she could handle it — besides, she had Jeeves!

With the coming challenge of Ozra to conduct, Samantha largely put all spell considerations on pause, even regarding the D.I.V.A. crown and the tower — at least when it came to her. The shit Ozra gave her was often deeply transformative, and at the very least, she’d surely gain levels, possibly many, making efforts spent on spellcraft questionable. Inside the challenges, her capabilities wouldn’t matter much, either.

The D.I.V.A. crown, though much of its power was hers alone, was quite useful so long as she was wearing it, if she had secondary control stones distributed. The only question was whether where she’d go would actually allow access…

Azure soon threw these considerations to the wind when she showed up amidst the ongoing debate and declared smugly, “I can take the authority of the crown myself, and use it in all ways.”

At the plentiful expressions of confusion and doubt to this, Azure offered — still smug — to prove it.

A cluster of leaders, including Orswyth, Tashome, Zephyr, the three Daughters, Estara, Ash, and Ana, and a smattering of officials, were present at the top of the tower as Samantha reluctantly handed the crown to Azure, frowning. When Azure put her hands on it, Samantha didn’t immediately let go, and Azure crooked her eyebrow.

Samantha grimaced and forced herself to let go. “I hate this, you know.”

“Oh, I do,” Azure said. With grand and self-important airs, Azure placed the crown on her head, in which it morphed itself to fit around the ram horns and sit perfectly, like it was meant to be there.

Samantha instantly felt the access shift, confirming her lover’s smug claims. Murmurs and whispers resounded from the others, as they no doubt got confirmations roundabout from Jeeves.

“Well, I’ll be blessed,” Orswyth said, smiling. “I won’t have the toy taken away so shortly after being teased by it.”

Zephyr shook his head, bewildered. “Amazing! I take it this is due to your marriage?”

Azure laughed. “Oh, I believe that’s half of it, at least.”

Samantha gasped. She pointed at Azure suddenly. “Because you were the-!” She stopped herself from saying ‘the queen’, knowing only a handful of people knew the truth, as yet. More quietly, she said, “Holy shit, love! Did you sense this even before and not even say anything?!”

To this, Azure appeared uncomfortable, her reaction conflicted. She moved close, keeping their conversation quiet. “To a degree. I did not delve deeply into a strange feeling the crown gave me. I thought it perhaps a bit of contrary, intrusive impulse related to ambition, as exists from my nature. Now, I understand it is a royal calling. One I turned away from in the past, of course, but I still have a connection, even a right, to this crown. The artifact still recognizes this, and it even echoes into our current marriage. Perhaps it would’ve required both for full access. We’ll never know, at this point.”

“Well, it looks good on you. Super good.” Samantha suppressed an instinctive pang of jealousy she tended to have about her shinies. She deliberately let it go and pushed forward. “Not only is this great for everyone else’s access, but you can make use of it in emergency situations. Just, ah, you know, ah…” Samantha rubbed her neck and looked away. “Take care of it for me.”

Azure, grinning devilishly, moved closer and embraced Samantha, giving her an affectionate squeeze. “You can trust me implicitly with your crown, babe. I’ll be keeping this on without exception, until you finally return to me.”

“Yes, thanks, I-” Samantha cut off at the grin on Azure’s face, and she realized her wife meant that for a certain promised ‘session’ before she left, too. Samantha blushed at the salacious imagery it suddenly put in her head. “Hehe! Oh, right, yeah.”

Orswyth cleared his throat. “I believe the tower wizards as a whole, and particularly Ash and Zephyr, would like some direction, advice, and pointers on designing their own custom spells for the newly accessible tower functions. They may not be as strong as what you’ll be able to create, but they may suffice for use while you’re gone, and even for some versatility even after. We don’t know how much time you’ll have to dedicate to it, after all.”

“Right, right!” Samantha said, glad for the distraction of ‘nerd stuff.’ “I’ll draft some rudimentary ideas and frameworks that can be picked through and built on by anyone. No problem.”

Ash raised her hand insistently. “I’d like some direct coaching, please! I’ve built up a lot of inspiration, and your guidance will amplify it. An hour? Two, perhaps?” Her face was never more daughter-like in its insistence.

Samantha grinned to see it. “Of course, Ash. Two hours.”

“Such a softie,” Azure whispered in her ear.

“Quiet, you.”

“Pardon me,” a tower official ventured hesitantly, “but should we address Lady Azure as ‘Your Majesty,’ going forward?”

“Oh, yes!” Azure cried like a predator with new prey. “I think I’m ready!”

“Nooo!” Samantha cried in horror. “It’s mine!”

✦•············•✦•···········•✦

Samantha somewhat painfully hammered through all the shit she needed to do before leaving. Most of it was grueling. There were a few bright points, such as working with Ash on her custom spell designs, correcting, coaching, and unraveling the puzzle that was her intuitive understanding for others to absorb.

She also bestowed upon Ash a special gift: the Mystic Astrolabe. She’d never particularly needed it, but the ritual bonus was excellent for Ash. Besides that, it was a cool ass curio and keepsake that Ash was touched to receive.

Samantha had needed Tashome’s brief aid to ‘disconnect’ its [Bound] status from her, so it could instead be bound to Ash. While such things were possible without Tashome, he made the process much less laborious and expensive, and it didn’t take much energy from him.

She also taught some others in a group. Though a little more frustrating than the cherished moments with Ash, the work explaining to a whole group of mixed-age, multi-cultural assembled spellcasters what she understood about custom spell work was rather edifying, and she honestly learned a lot herself.

She hoped that when she returned, she’d be learning from them, particularly the details of working with the crown and the tower, a subject she had not spent much time on to date.

After everything else was concluded, there was a final bright point — or perhaps ‘dark point’ was more apt — waiting for her in the bedchambers, wearing her crown and a little something else not very regal at all.

When Samantha was finally headed down that strange road upon the Bridge of Edges once more, seeking out the door to Engineering in the underbelly of creation, she had a big, dumb, very satisfied smile on her face, any covetedness about a crown long-forgotten.
