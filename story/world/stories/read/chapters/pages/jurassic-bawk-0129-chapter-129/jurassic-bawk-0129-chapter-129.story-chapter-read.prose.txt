Charlemagne stared with curiosity at the item that he had just completed, wondering if it was better than his top hat. He was considering stealing it from Phatagin if so. The pangolin’s tiny beady eyes were fixated on the newly-crafted helmet as well, wide with surprise.

“Well, let’s see if you managed to mess up my perfectly serviceable creation,” grumbled Balthazan as he hustled over and picked up the helmet. Then he gasped in surprise.

“What is it?” Phatagin asked.

“It’s…it’s far better than I thought it would be,” the rooman admitted.

Charlemagne used the power of his Monocle of Refinement to identify the item’s properties.

Beak of the Predator. This is a growth item. This item is unbound.

Current Powers.

“Bawk!” Charlemagne exclaimed after taking some time to understand the powers attached to the item. It was about as good as his top hat had originally been, with the caveat that his headwear had been invulnerable while the Beak of the Predator did not even have a self-repair option. But, on the plus side, it was also a growth item, and it had some sort of special bond with him.

“There are only a handful of Soul Smiths below the deity level!” Balthazan shouted. “How are you possibly disappointed?”

Phatagin slowly reached for the item and placed it on his head, purposefully avoiding looking at Charlemagne as he did so. After donning the new piece of equipment, he cast around for something to use as a mirror, finally finding a thin piece of sheet metal that would do.

“Hmmm, the aesthetic does not exactly match my preferred style,” he mused as he took in the burnished metal and spiky horns. “It would have been nice to have something a bit more subdued.”

Balthazan frowned and squinted his eyes.

“Try rolling up into a ball again,” he ordered.

The pangolin jumped into the air and began to curl up into his signature cannonball form. As soon as his tail passed between the helmet’s spikes, the item began to morph. The most noticeable immediate change was the color, which faded from a highly polished silver to a much more subdued green that matched Phatagin’s natural color. But the changes didn’t stop there. The twin spikes moved closer together as their tips merged together, forming a “beak” of sharp metal directly above the pangolin’s curled-up tail.

“How does it look now?” Phatagin asked as he floated in the air.

“It looks very distinguished, Squire Phatagin,” Josephine called out. “The comb is a nice touch.”

Phatagin pulled a claw out and carefully explored the surface of his new helmet, taking care not to cut himself on the sharp conical protrusion. He finally found the final change.

“It seems to have taken on some of Ser Charlemagne’s facial features,” he concluded. “I have…very mixed feelings about this.”

“Bawk!” the rooster objected as his power flared through the room, causing everyone but Phasima to flinch.

“Oh no no no, not like that,” Phatagin was quick to amend. “It’s just that not everyone can pull off such a bold and unique look.”

Charlemagne accepted the explanation and reined his mana back in.

“There’s only one real question, now,” Josephine noted as she regained her composure. “If we put the item into the box, will Phatagin get it back? Or will the System take it?”

“Bawk!” Charlemagne squawked angrily.

“I don’t want that either, but it’s not like you can fight the System, Ser Charlemagne,” the Attended added. “And there’s no need to stab the messenger.”

The rooster demanded that the Squiggles appear to answer the question, and they did.

“Bawk!” the rooster explained with joy.

“But we have sufficient time to forge additional items,” the pangolin objected. Balthazan said nothing but nodded vigorously in agreement, clearly impressed by the magnitude of what they had accomplished together.

“Bawk?” Charlemagne asked.

“We’re out of scones, sorry, Ser Charlemagne.”

“Bawk!”

Without waiting for further argument, the rooster looked at the pangolin and nodded his head toward the box. Understanding the nonverbal order all too well, Phatagin removed his new helmet with a sigh and placed it in the receptacle to be scored. For a moment, Charlemagne wondered if there was a step that they had missed. Then the Squiggles arrived to give him his score for the room.

Charlemagne puffed himself up with pride as he realized that he had achieved a score even better than an ‘A’. He announced the score with pride to the group as the door to the next room opened. The rooster ambled over, wondering just how many more rooms the first floor of the Delve contained. With any luck, there would be a way to replenish his non-existent food stocks after the floor was complete. Or, if he was really lucky, he would get a chance to eat whatever Boss monster marked the end of the floor. He couldn’t remember whether or not the Squiggles had told him there would be a Boss, but he felt sure that there would be one. This was a Dungeon, after all…and Dungeons had Bosses. With that thought firmly in his mind, the rooster stepped through the door.

Expecting to appear directly in the next room, Charlemagne was surprised to find him yet again floating in an eternal void. The sensation stirred dim memories from deep within his subconscious, but trying to focus on them merely made them slip away. While the rooster was thus engaged, the Squiggles arrived with a new message.

Charlemagne didn’t even have to consider his choice. If he could get more from the fight by going solo, then that’s what he would do.

“Bawk!” he yelled.

The rooster found himself standing on well-manicured grass in the center of a large field, a roaring crowd made up of strange and diverse creatures seated in bleachers all around him. A rich, deep voice boomed out over the din.

“Welcome one and all! We’ve got an exciting match that you’ve been waiting for! Our challenger is Charlemagne the Prodigy, the Manipulator of Mana, the Savage Savant, the Sword and Shield of Earth! This First Gen level 49 is the Champion of the local deity Grimfalk and possesses both a max level ‘Ember Core’ and the incredibly rare ‘Inferno’s Embrace’ physique. Recognizing his incredible talent, the System moved him directly from the Systemic Games to the Elite Systemic Games after the very first event. Who can stand before this unstoppable juggernaut? Who I ask?”

The crowd, apparently knowing exactly what was expected of them, began to call out, “Drago! Drago! Drago! Drago!”

A trap door at the other end of the field began to slowly slide back, revealing a recessed tunnel. Heavy footfalls rang off the metal floor of the tunnel as Charlemagne’s opponent began to march out, its features obscured by the dark tunnel. Once its head and shoulders cleared the tunnel, the rooster finally got a good look at his opponent.

It was some sort of lizard man, dressed in black armor and wielding an enormous sword with one hand. Its arms, which were bare for some reason, were swollen with thick, corded muscles that ballooned out to ridiculous proportions. The strange creature snorted as it continued to slowly swagger to the center of the field, causing sparks and smoke to puff out from its nostrils.

“Go get him, Ser Charlemagne!” Phatagin’s voice squeaked from somewhere high above. It was barely audible over the roar of the crowd.

Drago took his place opposite from the rooster and nodded at Charlemagne, lowering his head briefly in a gesture of respect. Charlemagne, confused, almost leapt straight into combat before realizing that he wasn’t supposed to start yet. Normally, this would not bother the rooster, but he really wanted a good score. The announcer’s booming voice confirmed his suspicions, the other party needed to be introduced before they could fight to the death.

“Here is a dragon-man who needs no introduction! But, just in case you’ve been living under a rock, allow me to tell you about Ser Drago! This level 49 version of Ser Drago is equipped with a full set of enchanted Cinderite armor and possesses both a fully leveled up Fire Core and a maxed out Physique Skill that, for legal reasons, I am unable to mention. Forged in the breath of his Patron, Belenuthstrom, the Eldest of Dragons, the Bane of the Wealthy, the Unsated, this weapon unleashes devastating heat with every strike while igniting the wielder’s latent power.

Now, Charlemagne, Drago, this will be a fight to the death. There are no special rules that you must follow, but note that chivalrous behavior will be rewarded. For the safety of our audience, an unbreakable force field is now in place, surrounding you on all sides. This means that you cannot leave the pitch, nor will you be able to fly too high or burrow too low. Do you have any questions?

“What he said!” Drago yelled, his serpent-like mouth adding extra emphasis to the “s” sound.

“Then without further ado…begin!”

“I will break you!” Drago yelled as he lifted his sword high into the air and grasped it with both hands. “You may have breezed through your earlier battles, but your winning streak ends here!”

Charlemagne released the full power of his Ember Core, allowing Inferno’s Embrace to drink deeply of the explosive power stored in his body. As his body heat skyrocketed, the rooster smirked at his opponent and charged. With his heart literally on fire, Charlemagne would test whether his opponent’s sword or his own body was truly unquenchable.
