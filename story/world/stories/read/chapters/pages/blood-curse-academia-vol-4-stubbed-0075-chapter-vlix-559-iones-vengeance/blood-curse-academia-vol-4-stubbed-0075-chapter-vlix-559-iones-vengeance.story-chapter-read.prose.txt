Chapter V.LIX (5.59)- Ione’s Vengeance

Only as Kizu sat in the stands, spectating after his most recent victory, did he realize he never actually checked his girlfriend’s next opponent. Ione had mentioned she’d be fighting only two matches after him, but never said her foe’s name. That was odd, because for the last several matches he and her had gone over her opponent and discussed strategy. Somehow, it had never come up in conversation these last few days.

While it was possible that it had simply slipped her mind, though Kizu doubted it. Despite what she led others to believe, Ione was selectively lazy. If she didn’t do something, there was usually a reason behind it.

It only took a second of consideration before Kizu narrowed the options down to two possibilities. After a brief moment of consideration, he amended that to three possibilities if he included himself. But since that was an impossibility, it left only the original two if the faculty wanted to maximize the drama of the competition. She would either face her sister or Kizu’s ex.

Thankfully, it turned out to be the latter of those two options. Kizu felt a smile slip onto his face as Ione stood across from Emilia.

However, he had to give the Tainted girl credit, she remained unflappably calm as she met Ione’s eye. Actually… no, Kizu didn’t have to give her credit at all. In fact, he felt gleeful at the prospect of Ione completely wiping the floor with the overly confident trader girl.

And yet, there always was that small nugget of doubt buried in his stomach. If Emilia had been the one to supply Ulric with those potions that had been used in his first match, it was possible the Tainted girl had something else equally as nasty planned for Ione. Even with her family’s wealth depleted, Emilia’s connections were nothing to sneeze at.

From what Kizu knew of Emilia from dating her, she lacked the raw magical strength required to win contests. Her highest ranks in the academy were in the academic studies. Thankfully, politics and numerology held very little sway over the results of fights like these. But she was a planner, not to be underestimated. Her ranks in the more combat oriented classes might not be the best in the academy, but they remained passably decent. Decent enough to create plans and strategize with.

Kizu would have said as much to Ione, should his girlfriend have chosen to consult him on the upcoming battle.

Instead, the summoner yawned and stretched. She had what looked like a blanket tucked under her arm. Kizu dearly hoped that the blanket signified some sort of mind game to get Emilia to lower her guard, and not an actual intention to take a nap. It galled him that he couldn’t actually be certain. Ione didn’t take academy tests seriously and she might very well view it as enough of a slap in the face if she didn’t deem Emilia worth the effort.

The stadium transformed under their feet, flowers sprouted out of the wooden planks. Moments later, they stood in a field of multi-colored wildflowers. However, unlike the other fields showcased in previous fights, this one had four large yellow mounds in each of the corners. They were oddly shaped, with what looked like jagged edges and patterns etched into their surfaces. It took Kizu a few seconds to realize what they were. Beehives. Massive beehives. And, along with the hives, of course came the bees themselves.

As they buzzed around the two contestants, Emilia let her confident smile slip for a moment, replaced by wariness as she eyed the closest insect. With the girls standing beside them as reference, Kizu could tell these bees were the size of his big toe.

He glanced up at the faculty stands, wondering if the creatures were conjured by Professor Oasaji, but the turkey professor remained in his seat, clucking away in the direction of Professor Grove who floated in the seat adjacent to him. If he was involved in the bees’ creation, then he showed no obvious sign of it.

Down at the stadium, Arclight announced the start of the match.

Kizu assumed that Ione would immediately examine the bees and use the creatures to her advantage. Instead, it was Emilia who darted around one of the massive hives, putting it between herself and Ione. Despite her earlier display of confidence, Emilia didn’t seem eager to underestimate her opponent. Perhaps if it had been one of the tournament’s earliest matches, but by now everyone at the academy had spectated dozens of matches that ended in defeat due to a challenger underestimating their opponent. Kizu knew Emilia was a lot of things, but she certainly wasn’t an idiot who’d let that lesson escape her notice.

Once again, Ione yawned. Then she pulled the blanket from under her arm and unfurled it. It flapped in the wind for a second. Then she laid it flat on the ground, crushing half a hundred flowers. From behind the beehive, Emilia tensed at the movement. She now held a wand in her hand. Rather than point it at Ione, Emilia tapped the tip of the magical conduit on the hive.

Even from Kizu’s seat in the stands, he heard a buzzing erupt from within as the wand touched the hive. Color drained from Emilia’s face as hundreds of bees crawled out and zipped into the air. They swarmed her, obscuring her from view.

For a moment, Kizu thought that Emilia was gone now, that the match had concluded with absolutely no effort on Ione’s part.

Then the buzzing insects split apart, revealing the unharmed Tainted girl within. Somehow, that wand held a means in which to control the creatures.

Kizu leaned forward and squinted, though neither gesture did much to help his spellsense as he examined the wand. It seemed to cast a mental spell. Since nobody in the academy was officially taught that branch of magic, Kizu assumed the only way that anything like it was likely to be cast was through a magical tool, like a wand, doing the heavy lifting.

From what Kizu knew of mental spells, the bugs weren’t likely to put up too much of a mental battle with the spell’s control, especially if she requested they do something they were already naturally inclined to do. It could be a spell specifically designed to strike at the minds of insects, as Kizu had read in his textbooks of exterminators using similar tools through an official capacity back in Hon. However, unless Emilia had insider information on the battle’s terrain ahead of time, that seemed unlikely. More likely, the wand cast a very low tier basic mental spell. Only the weakest minded people would succumb to an attack from something like that, but in the past month, Emilia had made it clear she thought little of Ione’s mental fortitude. She likely believed this to be enough to seize mental control over the summoner. His girlfriend’s academic rankings certainly enforced that idea. Unfortunately, Emilia had taken a safer option by targeting the spell on the bees rather than Ione herself.

The bees attacked in a wave as Ione finished smoothing out her blanket’s folds. Right as the wall of buzzing insects struck her, a pattern on Ione’s blanket lit up with a magical glow. Kizu’s eyes went wide as he recognized a summoning circle stitched into the fabric. Her fingers on the rim of the circle, Ione channeled into it.

A creature unlike he’d ever seen Ione summon before heaved itself out of the circle. Its flesh dripped with molten lava and the bees in the vicinity burst into flames from near proximity, falling into blackened heaps. The blanket, likely enchanted with fireproofing, remained unscathed as the lava monster raised itself to its full height. Its body constantly rippled and churned with its molten mass. While it looked humanoid in its stature, the facial features were little more than slight impressions carved into the creature’s body, nothing indicated that it had organic organs or muscles.

Smoke rose up from the flowers surrounding the blanket. Even from his place in the stands, Kizu felt the warmth rolling off the beast’s molten body. Ione, however, remained completely unfazed by her summon’s excessive heat. With her boot, she pushed aside some of the soot next to her blanket. Then she sat down and used a finger to doodle in the soot.

From her hiding place behind the beehive, Emilia’s eyes went wide. Then she ducked back behind the hive. From there, she sent out several elemental blasts of water at the lava summon. The water hissed on contact, barely leaving a scratch. Kizu thought most of it evaporated before even touching the creature.

Under the mental control of Emilia, the swarms of bees threw themselves at the monster, dying in droves. It wasn’t a particularly effective strategy.

The beast stepped in Emilia’s direction and the flower field burst into flames with every footfall.

Emilia darted from around the hive and charged in Ione’s direction, trying to get around the lumbering summon. It was a sound strategy. Everyone knew that attacking the physical body was the simplest way to end a fight with a summoner. They were notoriously weak bodied.

Unfortunately for Emilia, Ione also knew of this obvious weakness of hers and took it into account. Kizu watched his ex’s eyes go wide and her body stiff as she stepped on something buried in the flowers. Without seeing the creature, Kizu knew immediately that it had to be one of Ione’s caldrops. The electrical, sea urchin-like creatures took little blood to summon and Ione had been sketching in the soot ever since her big monster’s appearance. She’d used the flashy summon as a distraction as she set up traps across the battleground.

Still, Emilia remained on her feet and she stumbled in Ione’s direction, out of breath, but not out of the competition. The Tainted student raised her wand and pointed it at Ione.

If Emilia cast a spell from the wand, Kizu saw no physical sign of it. Only his spellsense picked up on a slight aura that was quickly dismissed and dispersed around Ione.

Ione yawned and stretched, not bothering to even glance in her opponent’s direction.

Her hulking lava beast moved in Emilia’s direction instead. She stared down at her wand with a look of bewilderment as the summoned creature stepped right up to her. At the last second, she turned on her heel to face the beast. The tip of the wand pressed against the lava monster's chest and burst into flames.

She yelped and dropped the wand in surprise. Only then did Emilia break out of her stunned stupor. She took a step back, with the clear intent to retreat, but stepped on another caldrop. The little creature set a jolt of electricity through her and knocked her to the sooty ground.

“But you’re weak!” Emila cried out, her voice hoarse. “How… how did you resist my spell? You can’t possibly have enough mental fortitude!"

In response, Ione simply offered her opponent the biggest eye roll in history.

Her summon stepped forward and stomped down on the paralyzed form of Emilia. The massive foot seared a hole in the girl’s torso.
