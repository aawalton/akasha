Chapter 122: Another Surprise

It was definitely a great desperation play they’d just set up. In addition to double-teaming Highfive with some prayer of a chance of wounding him significantly, thanks to an obviously practiced misdirection tactic, they now had Defiant on a track to go after Bo. Likely, that was supposed to induce a little panic.

Jack did not panic. He also just about gave up on utilizing his trapdoor trap, sad as that made him. But it just wasn’t meant to be.

Maybe I laid the bullshit on a little too thick?

The lack of instant results to Defiant’s plot suggested the obvious: ignore him and attack Hiccup. Jack did not bother trying to entangle her. Instead, he branched down multiple tendrils from the ceiling ending in blades, and went for a storm of them coming at her.

Hiccup couldn’t ignore these. With Highfive pressing her on one side, and the blades heavily pressing her on every other side, she went heavily defensive. Conventional defense fended off the heavily telegraphed smashes of Highfive, but she thus had to use her displacement powers to avoid Jack’s ‘feint, trap, and punish on the flank’ strategy, which he was quite practiced at for dealing with evasive foes like her. Granted, she was among the best he’d ever faced at it, which was just a little bit sickening considering she wasn’t even an Acrobat.

She couldn’t defend against the full power of their team, however. Bo waited for the properly-timed moment in between her displacements to dodge blades, right when she planted a foot to move away from a looming Highfive. It would absolutely happen, all other things not considered. But Bo’s disruption was just to hold her, to freeze her for that split moment, keeping her right there.

It wasn’t done for Highfive’s sake, though. A small dagger seemed to materialize while flying through the air from directly behind her, flashing with empowered energy right before it plunged into her planted calf — or would have, anyway. It pierced the suit and drew blood, but was blocked by arena processes.

Hiccup let out a strangled groan as the leg was locked, and thus could not keep her balance, so she fell over.

Highfive might’ve been relatively telegraphed with his strikes, but that didn’t mean he was slow. He came smashing down on her with a sheath-thumb that was dead-to-rights.

Defiant had no choice but to zip across space and block Highfive’s attack yet again, with another bullshit maneuver. Meanwhile, Hiccup began to react, twisting and displacing to grab the dagger in her leg and toss-

Jack was waiting for that. He raked the blade backward at the last moment, slicing her fingers similarly to how he’d cut the others, drawing shallow cuts but causing them to forcefully curl inward and lock as the System simulated severing.

This time, she screamed. Much like with Délight’s scream, it was mostly one of frustration.

Jack tried to follow up with the knife, intending to jab it in over the neckguard, but there was Defiant, grabbing it. This time, he held onto it.

Highfive was ready this time. He grabbed Hiccup by the legs with his sheath and just immediately squeezed, crushing them in a giant fist. She cried out in pain and tried to displace before more damage was dealt, but Bo disrupted her powers this time, blocking them. Crunch. It was the sound of a physical field effect being ground against by another field; a rock and a hard place.

Defiant was there, of course, but this time, he was greatly occupied in trying to pry Highfive off of Hiccup. A tall order, and he had a problematic surface area for such a task. His first attempt merely pried back a single giant finger.

“I’m fatigued,” Bo reported. “Not gonna have much to try disrupting Defiant.”

“Meh,” was all Jack had the focus to respond with.

Jack moved his blades in quickly, stabbing and cutting, going for whatever he could as Hiccup waved her hands around in panic.

Finally, Hiccup displaced herself away. But her attempt to be in a standing position resulted in her flopping immediately with a yelp, having no use of her legs. She tried once more to get up and couldn’t, groaning in terrible pain, bleeding from multiple shallow cuts. Finally, she just flopped on her back and stared up at the ceiling, panting and moaning.

Defiant took a couple of steps toward her and paused, finally turning around and glaring at them.

Okay, I gotta try this. Jack focused hard on a spot in the stony floor behind Defiant and did everything he could to not think of Defiant at all. To zoom in on that ‘stupid’ floor; man, how he hated it! He wanted to smash it! Just the floor. It was going to take it hard!

And then he jammed a metal spike into it with all his strength, causing a small explosion of rocky bits right behind Defiant.

Defiant literally jumped as his legs were pelted with stones. He spun around almost as quickly as he moved to defend people, backing away, looking down in alarm.

There was definitely something to it.

Jack made a connection to Defiant via the Mini-Mems and sent a message: <I know your secret, guy. You might as well surrender.>

Defiant apparently received it, as he spun around again and glared at Jack. A response came. <I was going to, anyway, genius.> He then threw his hands up and looked upward. “I concede, damn it!”

The environment pulsed with light, and the Sim Arena voice chimed. “Defiant has surrendered, and Hiccup has been ruled unable to properly continue. Please cease any ongoing, active power use.” The light began to flash red and blue alternatingly. “Team High Life has beaten Team Irresistible Style! Team High Life is the winner!”

“Yes!” Highfive cried, holding his fists up to the heavens and shaking them. “Yeeeeees! How sweet it is, oh, how sweet it is!”

The suppression fields over everyone cleared, and the Sim Arena voice chimed again. “Observation of the arena has ceased. Both teams may proceed to either exit point at their leisure."

As Jack pulled off his helmet and opened the wall for Bo, she immediately came rushing out to jump into Highfive’s arms, nearly screaming in celebration. Hiccup threw herself upward, nearly stumbling in her haste to stomp off without a word. Chime hurried after her.

“Hey!” Jack called. “Good match.”

Hiccup didn’t even slow down. Chime glanced his way but didn’t say anything.

Defiant took off his helmet, popping his neck around with his eyes closed. He took a deep, deep breath, and when he opened his eyes, he wasn’t scowling quite as much, despite some remaining sourness.

He met Jack’s eyes for a long moment and — shockingly — held out his hand for a shake. “Good match. You’re a terror.”

Jack got over his shock to return the shake. “Is this because of what I know?”

Defiant shook his head. His grip got stronger as he pulled in and held Jack’s eyes. “It’s because it’s the right thing to do when the masks are off. Is yours?”

Jack nodded slowly as their hands finally released. “I hope so.”

Defiant might’ve smiled faintly. He turned and went over to Highfive and Bo, who were regarding him warily. Highfive’s face was already apologetic at this point, though.

The big softie. So predictable. Despite his thoughts, Jack was fighting off a smile.

Délight walked up to him, still with a slight limp, smiling faintly, and holding out her hand. “Good match.”

Jack took it and nodded. “Likewise. That’s a great aura. Sorry you didn’t get to show off more of your talents to the brass.”

Délight chuckled and shrugged, eyes sliding away. “You did what you were supposed to: narrowed the exposure time. I’m glad I have the aura now, that’s for sure.” Her eyes strayed to look curiously at the wall that was down, where the thickness of metal plates and brightly-colored insulation filling could be seen. “An insulated room. Unbelievable. How on Earth did you do all this shit in seven minutes? The sims weren’t even a quarter this elaborate. Pretty unfair, you know.”

It was said teasingly, and Jack chortled and grinned in response. “It definitely was.”

As Defiant spoke to Inkblot, Highfive and Bo walked over to Jack, their eyes not quite looking Délight’s way. The awkwardness was thick.

Délight studied them, though. “Good match, you two. A truce is waiting for you — from me, at least — if and when you’re ever ready. Apologies, too. I’ll try with Light, but I-... I’ll try.”

Highfive had his tongue poking his cheek outward from the inside, eyes darting anywhere but at her, but he nodded reluctantly. Bo didn’t meet her gaze, but didn’t have anything barbed to say, either. She just frowned faintly.

Délight nodded to herself. With a final glance and smile for Jack, she walked off to the waiting Defiant. Along the way, she had a brief, cordial exchange with Inkblot, and then Defiant took her arm, and they headed for the room with the exit platform, their heads held high.

Highfive walked up to Jack and started emitting a gleeful, indulgent laugh, face all grins. “You did it, you son-of-a-bitch! You fraggin’ did it! Haha!” Highfive held his hand out for a sideways hand slap, and when Jack relented to offer his hand, got himself a wince-inducing impact. Highfive laughed again and grabbed Jack in a bearhug that lifted him off his feet.

Jack patted Highfive’s back and said in a strangled voice, “We did it… Teamwork, my man, teamwork… also, please stop…”

After being put down, Bo moved in for a hug, murmuring awkwardly, “Thanks for the leadership… and everything.” Her eyes furtive and embarrassed to offer these words, she finally quipped, “Besides locking me in a room by myself. Real dick move, there.”

Jack shook his head as they parted from the hug. “Nah, it was a textbook Jackass move. Get it right.”

Bo just rolled her eyes.

After exchanging almost silent congratulations with the stoic Inkblot, who was still favoring one arm awkwardly from damage, they left the arena, dumped any extra gear that wasn’t personal to them, and made their way to the lobby, where a few people were scattered around and chatting, including Délight and Defiant speaking with Stitcher. He didn’t see that one ominous figure anywhere, though.

Ira and Augur were there, close by, and waiting, apparently. Augur veritably hopped over to them, a big smile on his face and stars in his eyes. “I told you! I told you you’d win, didn’t I?! I said so! Jack! Jack, oh my god, you were so owning them- oh, oh, guys! You’re not going to believe-!” He squeaked at the end.

“Calm down, Augur,” Inkblot admonished. “Your voice is getting too high-pitched to understand.”

Ira was moving in even as they spoke, a big smile painted on her face. From their bond, Jack had been feeling a combination of pride and relief. “Congratulatioooons, all!” She hugged Jack first, murmuring more of the same, before moving on to Bo and then Highfive.

They naturally shifted so they were in something of a circle. Augur was still hopping with excitement, eyes shifting over them as he part collected himself and part was just waiting for Ira to finish her ‘rounds.’

Ira nodded over to Augur after, her countenance amused. “Go ahead, Augur. Before you pop! Like a balloon!”

Augur instantly launched. “So there we were among all the other people watching on the public observation deck before the match started, and a memorial agent walks up to us and whispers, ‘Agent Stitcher has invited you both to the high command observation deck, would you like to come?’ Can you believe it?!” Augur’s face indeed showed he could not. “Of course, we said yes! They take us around to a fancier spot with guards, and there’s Stitcher and General Fletcher, and-and, well, a bunch of high-rankers I don’t actually know very well. But… wow!”

“It was a small cluster, honestly,” Ira replied.

Augur’s eyes narrowed, and his voice got low and conspiratorial. “And General Mask was there; he-”

“That can’t be his name, right?” Jack asked incredulously.

“Nah,” Highfive answered, shaking his head. “No one knows his handle. That’s our unofficial codename, I guess.”

Bo added, “Simple and dumb, that’s what you get when you don’t get out ahead of it. He made his bed, though something tells me he couldn’t care less.”

“Shush!” Augur hopped impatiently. “I’m talking, please?! So, there he was, sitting in a fancy central chair like it was his throne, like he owned the place, slightly but perceptively separated from everyone. Stitcher and General Fletcher were the closest on either side. So, so, Ira sits down next to Stitcher, and I sit behind them, but there’s not much talk before the match begins, and everyone is watching monitors.” He paused for a breath and shook his head. “I tell you, though, General Mask wasn’t even looking at the monitors. I could just tell. Somehow, he was watching even closer with a special interface. But here and there, General Fletcher leaned over to whisper in his ear. I heard him grunt once!”

“Scintillating,” Bo muttered sarcastically.

“If Stitcher was conversing as things went along, she was doing it via the System. But she cackled a few times at stuff you guys did. Especially Jack. Oh! I even overheard General Fletcher whispering your handle, Jack! Ferronaut.”

Highfive barked a laugh and clapped Jack on the back. “Hell yeah! My bro, Big Iron, he’s a superstar in the making!”

Jack took a breath and raised his brow, simply enduring the praise as best he could.

Really puts a cramp in my self-deprecating stylization.

“I saw General Mask sit up just once,” Augur continued, “when Team Irresistible Style burst into the fortress, and Jack closed the wall, splitting the team up. Even though he was observing through an interface, I think it was an unconscious movement signalling him paying more attention.”

“That was the major turning point,” Inkblot said. “You could say they lost when they were unable to counter that move fast enough.”

“Yep! Anyway, once Highfive crushed Hiccup’s legs, General Mask stood, and everyone else did immediately after! He, like, paused, I guess? For just a moment, standing there before walking off. As he did, Stitcher and General Fletcher exchanged a glance, and the general smirked. Then, General Mask walked off, his cane clicking on the floor, and when he was gone, everyone sat back down. I was finding it kind of hard to watch all this and the match at the same time. I’m definitely going to rewatch from multiple angles over a few hours!”

“Interesting,” Jack muttered, not entirely sure what to make of it. “Anyway, let’s get out of here. Ice cream, anyone?”

Agreements resounded to that idea.

On the way out, Stitcher met his eyes and gave him a thumbs-up. He returned it.

Highfive had been Level 6 for quite a while, so it was expected, but Level 7 was fairly rare to achieve in the PACCs on the whole and would definitely be an advantage. On the other hand, it wasn’t a big power upgrade level like Level 6 was.

Regardless of whether Jack’s Level 5 was light if he was getting the rarer big upgrades only on even levels, it was still a level where he could see a big power boost through enhancing Create with his free allocation, as any multiple of five in a level did.

Jack and Highfive were fairly eager to jump in and see what they got, so they both finished their ice cream and headed home. Indeed, Jack hopped himself right into a meditative position and dove into Calibration.

Giant Starry Memoria was smiling before him. “Congratulations on your victory! It was very impressive.”

“Thanks,” Jack replied, returning the grin.

Before he could say anything more, Memoria gestured, causing sparky novas to erupt in front of Jack, bloom, and show the new selection deets.

“Wow,” Jack said, sighing grandly, mostly in theatre. “I don’t feel special anymore!”

Memoria chuckled. “Aww, don’t pout, my son! You’re still very special. Getting those even-level enhanced upgrades is quite a powerful and rare perk. You could hold out hope for Level Seven, still, as ‘emptier’ levels occur for others on even levels, you see? They vary. It’s possible that the ‘spare power,’ if you will, accrues gradually.”

Jack pondered it. “So, what, hit big on Level Six, as everyone does, but there’s leftover from what Five didn’t give? It shows up on what Seven gives, reflecting with more powerful options? And then skips… Eleven? Because Nine is a multiple of three and another big one.”

“Yes, possibly. Or perhaps your pattern is to skip even or odd, going forward. In which case, your next less relevant level would be Level Eight. The point is, you are obtaining extra power, but it might just not be enough to enhance every level. Perhaps it's every other, perhaps it's two-thirds.” She shrugged. “Again, I don’t have a lot of data on this, especially of newer generations.”

“Mmn.” Jack looked back at his options. “I think the enhancer out of these options is poopy compared to taking Control. Just to verify, does-”

“Yes, you can take Control again, next level. Bonuses from the three options never restrict that.”

“Got it… Yeah, I think I’m going with Control. I mean, I’m a Controller. The nearly forever grappling duration isn’t the worst thing I could imagine, but I don’t have a lot of fatigue problems, and I’m continuously improving on it.” He punched his selection in and felt the telltale integration begin.

The options disappeared, replaced by the stat selection interface, and he didn’t think at all about this one — he hit the option for Create and felt pretty good about the level, getting both power and precision in one go. As he did, though, he asked, “Mother, I don’t suppose I’ll get any info asking after that super-powerful guy I saw? General Mask.”

Memoria winced. “Such an awful nickname! He asked for it, though. In any case, who he is doesn’t matter to you yet. One day you might know more than you ever wanted to.” She suddenly grinned widely and gestured with her hands. “The selections are made! Prepare for cementation and a return to meatspace!”
