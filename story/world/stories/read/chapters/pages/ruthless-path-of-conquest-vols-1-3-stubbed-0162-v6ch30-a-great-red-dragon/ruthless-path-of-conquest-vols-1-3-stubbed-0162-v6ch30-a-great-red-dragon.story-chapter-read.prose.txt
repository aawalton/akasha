Roscuro, dagger, please.

“Happy hunting,” James replied, nodding in return.

He felt a slight tension from Han Jianguo. James thought the big man might be looking at him differently after witnessing James put an end to the Ruler who had been lying helpless on the ground. Killing an enemy who couldn’t defend himself would normally have given James complex feelings, but here he knew he was justified.

Maybe he was projecting some of his own point of view onto Han Jianguo, though. He didn’t actually say anything.

The two took off, and James took stock of his situation.

I guess it’ll be a while before someone brings me another Holy King to kill. Feels like I was incredibly lucky to get those five here without any of them dying in transit or killing off my allies. We’re almost halfway there now. Plus, I took out that archer who was probably helping them. Good progress, all around.

He guessed that perhaps half an hour had passed since the melee began. James and the others helping him had been very consciously ignoring and avoiding getting drawn into the surrounding fights, but there was a great deal of bloodshed happening everywhere around, if James looked at any spot more than forty feet away from himself. The other prospective enemies had been giving him and his entourage a bit of space so far.

It’s a good thing the arena is so big. Otherwise, I’d be getting pulled into fights left and right.

As he had that thought, a weight struck the ground just twenty feet away from him. Too close to be unintentional or unrelated to him.

Just when I was about to activate Invisibility again… Well, I should have known this would happen eventually. Not like I could just sit back in stealth mode the whole time.

James turned to look, and he saw a creature that looked like it had sprung to life from a children’s lunchbox or perhaps an anime. A western-style dragon, red-scaled, with six horns on its head, bat-like wings, four legs, and a big, thick tail.

That additional limb thumped against the ground excitedly as the creature stared at James with its big, golden eyes. It was roughly James’s height, but noticeably bulkier and covered in dense muscle.

Was that a look of excitement from the creature?

“Fisher Emperor, I heard that you were strong, back before, when you were just the Fisher King, and I’ve come to learn if it was true! Let’s fight!” The monster’s voice was unexpectedly high-pitched and had an almost childish quality to it.

Huh. Identify.

[Juvenile Red Dragon Zigfried, Lv. 51]

It’s a fucking baby dragon? I guess that makes sense…

“Sorry, kid, I’m really not taking on all comers right now,” James said. “I’m destroying the souls of people who threaten my Empire and my family, not sparring.”

There was another tail thump, this time conveying a clear feeling of indignance. “Hey, who are you calling kid? I’m fifty-six years old! And after I came all this way to fight in the newly integrated universe…”

Wait, is he from another universe?

“You don’t sound like fifty-six, but sorry to be rude.”

“I did spend seven years traveling through the void to get here, but still, I figure I’m older than you, at least!”

“Yeah, like I said, sorry.” He turned invisible again.

Warp Step.

James raised his foot on the ground beside the dragon and then set it down on the side of the mountain he’d started at earlier.

Thank goodness I got that new Skill before the Summit. I’ll have to signal to the group where I’ve moved somehow, once I figure out where to settle down. Maybe the dragon will get bored and move on first, and I can go back to where I was.

Then he heard a wingbeat from close beside him.

No way.

“You know I can still smell you, right? Turning invisible isn’t enough to get away from me.”

James turned his head and saw exactly what he knew would be there. The dragon had appeared alongside him.

Uh oh.

“You’re a quick one,” James said.

“I can use Basic Space Magic too,” the dragon replied proudly. “I said I went through the void. Didn’t say I went through at the normal speed. I would have been too late for this! It’s usually considered kind of rude to warp out in the middle of a conversation, by the way. I know your Earth is new to the System, but that’s typical etiquette for dragons, at least. I asked for a fight politely, too, just like dad said.”

Right. He traveled through the void alone to get here. So, I’m struggling to escape from an unaccompanied minor…

“Yeah… and I said no.”

“Well, I’m starting, whether you like it or not!”

Seemingly disregarding the fact that James was invisible, the monster turned its side toward the Fisher Emperor and then rammed its full body weight into James’s side.

What the fuck?!

The Fisher Emperor allowed his body to fold to the impact, minimizing the force of the blow. It still felt like he had been hit by something much heavier than the Juvenile Red Dragon looked.

Okay, I guess I’ll play along now, he thought. I can’t spend the whole day trying to run away from a baby dragon. Remember you asked for this!

James unleashed a flurry of punches into the monster’s back, and it at least seemed to register them as attacks and take some small amount of damage—or perhaps just mild discomfort.

Zigfried let out a little grunt, the only sign of pain or debility apparent after it had taken a dozen blows that would have easily crippled an average human.

Those scales are tough!

Still, he persisted. With one final blow, James cut his wrist open on one of a dozen sharp spines that stood prominently on the dragon’s back.

He immediately deactivated Invisibility. It wasn’t helping him evade the dragon, and he needed to properly see what he was doing to deliver his most precise attacks. If he had been able to see his own hand, he wouldn’t have punched the spiky part of the monster.

“Finally!” the dragon exclaimed. “You’ve accepted that we’re in a fight!”

Meteor Strike!

James’s fist burst into flame, and he jabbed Zigfried hard in the side of the head with his burning hand. The dragon’s neck snapped to the side slightly, the force seemingly actually affecting it a bit.

But the flames were sucked from off of James’s fist, directly absorbed into the scales. They vanished from view a second after he’d made contact.

“Don’t use fire on a dragon!” Zigfried said. “Ordinary flames just make us stronger!”

“Thanks for the tip.”

Lightning Strike!

James threw a heavy blow at the dragon’s side, and he felt its body compress under his lightning-infused punch. A fist-shaped crater appeared where his hand had been, and the dragon writhed for a moment, its body still under the influence of the electric shock.

This is my chance.

He pulled his hand back and surrounded it with Wind Mana.

Air Strike!

As James was aiming a decapitation strike for the dragon’s neck, the spasming creature managed to open its jaws wide. He saw a ball of Fire Mana gathered there, rapidly expanding from a small, concentrated orb.

James’s instincts told him to get out of the way, and he complied, throwing his body down and to the side in a flash. The blade of wind went astray, flying off in some direction James wasn’t looking at.

A cone of fire burst forth from the dragon’s mouth and engulfed the spot where James had just been. He breathed a sigh of relief and then realized that a corner of his clothing—he wore a cloak over the Scale Armor of the Demi-Hydra—was on fire.

James quickly tore the cloak off and threw it away, then watched as it rapidly burned away to seemingly nothing.

Shit. Can’t let myself get hit by that.

“What was that?” he wondered aloud.

I could really use a Skill like that, honestly.

“Conceptual Fire,” Zigfried replied. “It burns every particle of whatever it touches, down to the last atom.”

What the fuck?! That’s so ridiculous!

“How are you here?” James asked.

“I’ve told you twice already, I came through the void!”

The dragon seemed to be in a talkative mood, so James decided to take advantage of that to pause the fight while he tried to come up with some countermeasure for this Conceptual Fire.

“What void?”

“The void between universes.”

“And that was allowed…? Are dragons allowed to be on our world? Can you all do that? Travel wherever you want? And also burn everything down to nothingness…?”

“Look, most dragons wouldn’t find it worthwhile to visit your Earth anyway. But new universes don’t join the System that often, and I’m… young… and my dad thought it would be a good experience. I was excited about it. I needed to be an Earth resident for a year to qualify to participate in this thing, so I set out as soon as we heard about the new integration.”

“Someone as strong as you… your dad must be a Ruler, right?”

“Yeah!”

A dragon prince. I guess I should be honored he decided to pick me for a fight. Now the massive aura I’ve been sensing from him makes more sense. It felt like a Ruler’s power, and a lot larger than average, for sure—but unstable and a bit diffuse. As if he’s not really accustomed to wielding it like a Ruler yet. Which I guess he’s not. But if my power is like a blowtorch, he’s a bonfire. Once he learns to control it properly…

“Like I said, most dragons wouldn’t find it worthwhile to visit this planet, not in its current state. Give it twenty years, and I’m sure one of us will want to plant a flag somewhere. Maybe even me.”

“That’s awfully confident of you. Well, I guess we can spar until my next friend arrives with someone I need to kill. Then we’ll take a break, okay? And can we stay in this general area?” James gestured broadly from the mountain to the plain where he and Zigfried had initially encountered each other.

“Sure, sure!” the dragon replied eagerly. “I could even conjure us a ring, if you’d like! We’ve got time.”

James’s lips twitched in what he realized might have been the first really straightforwardly happy smile he had made since he arrived at the World Leaders Summit. It was nice to be dealing with someone of such childlike innocence and good will.

It feels like I’m in an anime right now. One where the main characters just really love fighting. No lying, no complicated schemes, no trying to commit murder before the other guy murders you. Even if we kill each other here, it’s just a goofy accident for us to laugh about later. And it’s the first time I can ever remember looking forward to getting my ass handed to me.

It was hard for James to imagine defeating even a Juvenile Red Dragon. This encounter, in which most of his more powerful attacks had done no apparent damage, while a single serious attack from the enemy had seemingly shown the potential to kill him, just reminded him that he was still a relatively small fish in a vast, indifferent universe.

Still, that didn’t mean he wasn’t going to try.
