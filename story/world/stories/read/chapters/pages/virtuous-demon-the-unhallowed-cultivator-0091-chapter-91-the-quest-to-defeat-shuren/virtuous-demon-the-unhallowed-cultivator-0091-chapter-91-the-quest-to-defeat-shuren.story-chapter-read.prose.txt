Mandatory SSS Grade Quest

Core Skill Awakening Mission: Survive for over 10 minutes and endure 100 blows from Liu Shuren.

Rewards: Unlock Core Skill.

Gain Title: Slapping Young Masters. (Bonus 10% stat boost to successors of prominent clans).

Single Use Path Absorb Ticket (Requires Core Skill usage. One time per ascension).

Tez stared at the transparent and opaque screen in front of him when Shuren challenged him to a duel.

Are you kidding me? He went from an S Grade Threat to giving me a SSS Grade Quest? Isn’t the System’s threat gauge relative to my strength? I should’ve bridged the gap by now!

It was the highest difficulty rated quest he’d ever gotten, even worse than when he had to explore the ruins at the corner of Dezuan Forest where he found the Nebulous Treasure Hashin’s Ring (Fractured).

From what he could gauge, the way the threat calculations worked was wholly based on things within his realm of power. The threat level of Grand Elder Wanshang was Unknown Grade because his strength was something Tez couldn’t deduce whatsoever.

There were ranks even above S? And this guy, I’ve already analysed him, his stats are too much for me. He literally has stats comparable to the average Path Foundation cultivators! I wouldn’t even be surprised if he could kill a Rank 2 cultivator!

In the end, Shuren came for him. He could blame it on the fact he taunted the Liu clan by crippling Liu Wei, but the quest he had been given and the things he had done were far too despicable.

Shuren looked him down with a sinister smile. He had already gotten uncomfortably too close to Tez. In the worst-case scenario, Tez could use an escape talisman to instantly teleport a few kilometres away, but he really didn’t want to expose his capabilities to a sect disciple.

His cold blue eyes stared down into Tez. This was a man that definitely felt no remorse for what he or his cousin had done.

No, wait. That wasn’t right. What am I thinking? Even if Liu Wei was trash, Liu Shuren is different. But, even so, it’s only natural for him to come defend his cousin.

“You only need to bring me down to my knees to win.”

“I’ll even seal my cultivation stage to your level.”

Those two lines made Tez reconsider his actions.

As expected of a young master, he has full confidence. With this, the mission actually seems somewhat feasible, even though it’s still ranked SSS.

“Two weeks. You can go break into Path Foundation if you want. That’ll be even better.” Tez said, full of confidence.

Waiting for Shuren to leave, Tez heaved a sigh. That reward, it’s ridiculous. Now that I understand what Paths truly are, how the hell can the System homogenise and distribute it at will? Paths are the convergence of one’s life experiences and their understanding of Qi. Transferring it to someone is akin to giving a newborn baby a loaded rifle. It just doesn’t make sense.

“Everything is falling into place perfectly so that I’m forced to fight him. But Liu Shuren is a man of reason, even if he is a young master. His arrogance is backed with purpose, with an actual brain. There’s a possibility I can find a pacifist solution if I bring enough evidence to him.”

Tez’s neck craned towards the rewards of the quest. Even though he himself didn’t think of that action.

“But… Those rewards… And how hard would it be to convince him anyway?”

He shook his head to ignore it, focusing on the matter at hand. “Finally, I can buy some useful things from the shop. First, I need to get a Perfect Grade medicinal pill for Sarya.”

Tez made his way to an abandoned shed on the outskirts of the dorm, not far off from the outskirts of the Evening Clouds forests. He found a familiar Silvent outside as well.

“Arene?” Tez ran up to her. “How’s the situation in the sect?”

“Rumours have already spread everywhere. I don’t even know how it spread so fast,” Arene vented out with frustration.

“That dog, Luo, it has to be him! He’s been sucking up with Wei and that purple-haired bastard this whole time!” Tez muttered under his breath, his fists enclosed with anger. But that anger soon turned to misery, as he gnashed his teeth. “Damnit! If only I was stronger, more powerful!”

Arene patted Tez’s shoulder to knock him out of his thoughts. “We have to go to the Hall of Punishments! Listen to me, we can still rely on the sect, they wo-”

“I already tried! Those bastards wouldn’t even let me in! I don’t even know why, but whoever Wei knows, they easily made all of them block me from even entering. Almost like they’ve all been ordered like their life depends on it. What the hell do I even do in this case, not even Sarya’s in her right mind!” Tez cut her off out of frustration, before resting a hand on his forehead to calm himself down.

Tez wasn’t convinced, but he’d entered the abandoned shed with a Vulfoan girl. Not only did she have a vulpine orange tail, her fluffy vulpine ears were also fused to the crown of her hair. She had been kicked out of her dorms by her own dorm mates, with nowhere else to stay. He instantly used 100 spirit stones to purchase a spiritual medicinal pill (Perfect Grade) and knelt below Sarya. Her eyes were empty as she gazed out the window.

“Take this Sarya, it’ll help.” Tez quickly placed the pill in her mouth, and she instinctively swallowed.

"Tez…thank you, but you don’t have to do this. As long as I stay put, I’ll be fine,” She refused to look him in the eyes, instead looking down at the ground.

“I crippled Wei.” Tez revealed to her. “He won’t be able to cultivate for the rest of his life.”

Sarya looked at Tez with utter fear. As if the quiet and humble friend she knew was a completely different person. “H-have you gone crazy? You’re going to die when they find out!”

Tez’s golden eyes that now dimmed with cold rage, looked down at her, recognising what she meant. “Who else is backing up Wei? Who is that purple-haired man, and why is everyone so afraid of him? Why can’t you tell me?”

Tez gritted his teeth. He held both of his hands firmly on her shoulders, facing her directly.

“Tell me, Sarya, am I some sort of clown?” Tez didn’t hear a response, so he nudged her a bit. “Look at me!”

“Ever since that dog entered the sect, the only thing he’s done is waste his time bullying others! That scumbag has done nothing but aim for Silvents and Vulfoans, especially women, treating them like trash and even using his surname to beat them up, and worse! Three times, three times I had to face Wei and even fight him in defence of your honour. What kind of trash would I be if I can’t even help my friends in need!”

Tez took in a deep breath, softening his tone. “Just because you came from a different land doesn’t mean you have to be treated like this. Even the Sect Leader himself was once a refugee outcast from the far east. Why hold your head down? Just because someone has a powerful background, or is a stronger cultivator, or has connections, they’re so different from us? Because last I remember, we all have a heart, and a brain and intelligence that separates us from animals. We all bleed the same. Right?”

Sarya stared at Tez for a moment, before chuckling. “You’re so strange… But, you’re right.”

Listen, the person backing Wei is an outsider close to the Liu Clan. And…” She took out a small and intricate gem from her robe, placing it in his hands. “I want you to keep this safe for me.”

It… looks similar to that necklace I saw on Kraus…

Tez began to piece the puzzle together, bit by bit. He took it in his hand, and it disappeared into his System’s storage. One of the advantages he had, without needing to break through to Path Foundation.

He smiled at Sarya with reassurance, even patting her lightly on the head.

“Don’t worry so much, I’ll figure something out, otherwise my name’s not Tez the Thinker.” Right now, he was too weak. With the duel in two weeks…An idea popped into Tez’s mind.

If we’re gonna play rep for rep, then fine, I’ll bring out my trump card, even if there is a price to pay.

Tez brought out a letter laced with a red string, received from an old master he took care of in the archipelagos. “Arene, please give this to Grand Elder Wanshang. Explain to him everything you know. Tell him my requirements are to take care of Sarya. For now, I need to prepare for my duel in two weeks.”

“A duel?” Arene was confused as she grabbed the letter.

Tez left the shed without seeing the expressions on the two, instead stretching his right arm as he looked at the moonless night sky.

“Liu Shuren… He is definitely unlike Liu Wei. But… You would definitely come to support your cousin, no matter what, right?”

Tez thought back to when he went on his own to find that Legacy in the Dezuan Forest.

To that beat down he had from an already injured Shuren.

And he didn’t know why, but the things Shuren said at the end almost resonated with him.

Back to his first experience in this world, in that village, with his second family.

Am I… Going about this the right away? It feels like I’m walking into an obvious cliche that shouldn’t happen.

With those doubts coming into Tez’s mind, a sharp pain came in his head.

And in that final moment of clarity, Tez realised the trap.

“As I thought. You’re tampering with my mind.”

“System.”

And yet those final words had disappeared alongside the headache.

The world was ringing around him, his vision was blurry.

WARNING HEALTH BELOW 10% STAMINA BELOW 10% QI BELOW 10%

Only the ringing of the System blared his mind.

Core Skill Awakening Mission: 3 minutes and 3 hits left.

Tez felt a soft force on his chest, bringing some semblance of clarity back to him.

“I will admit. You are strong, Tez. But your path ends here,” Shuren claimed with cruelty, ready to cripple Tez with the next move.

What a load of self-righteous bullshit… I was… so close…

Tez could only heave and wheeze on the spot, barely standing his ground.

He took one last gaze at the man before him. A young man who, even when sealed to a lower cultivation stage, exuded the aura of a powerful master of the martial arts. Even if Tez was the same age as him, this man was no mere spoiled young master of a powerful clan.

He had honed his strength and went through his fair share of strife.

These people aren’t coddled when they act above others. They worked their hardest to gain strength to such a high level, even with their blessings. And that’s what gives them that inherent arrogance, that ingrained superiority when compared to others. There’s no such thing as a humble billionaire, after all.

Tez couldn’t even deny that he himself began to feel the same. When he saw a mortal on the side, living with ignorance while believing in Immortals like they were some sort of god. A small flame flickered deep in his heart, that he was better than them.

But in the end, I-

“Stellar Strike.”

He heard Shuren say these words with such softness that he didn’t expect to feel anything. For a moment, Tez didn’t even notice anything.

Then he felt it.

His blood churned and boiled, as a sharp pain travelled from the centre of his chest throughout his torso. He felt almost bloated, and even though no foreign object entered his body, a strange sensation of Qi gave him the feelings of sharpness and cold metal. Just a single movement that moved his internal organs out the way as it wished to stab through. He could taste warm metal even in his mouth, and discomfort surging in his airways. Tez couldn’t even breathe. Let alone control his legs, that felt so heavy that his entire body began to stumble back.

Like a sword had been stabbed straight through his torso.

Ding.

Tez coughed out blood as dizziness wrapped around his head, making him fall to the ground. He lay there, spasming out as his internal injuries caused his blood to run wildly like a bull seeing red.

But he couldn’t even concentrate on his injuries. The only thing on his mind was that stupid sound that now began to drive him crazy.

Ancient glyphs of a lost language were all he saw, and his wide golden eyes absorbed every single letter of it.
