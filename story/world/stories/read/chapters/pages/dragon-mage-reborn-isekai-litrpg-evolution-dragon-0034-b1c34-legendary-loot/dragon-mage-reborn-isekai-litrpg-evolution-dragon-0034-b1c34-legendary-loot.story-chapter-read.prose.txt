The queen screeched, a horrible sound that made my ears ring as the toxin spread through the blood vessels in her forehead. Instantly, branched veins enlarged, spreading through her eyes and down her cheeks. Her massive body convulsed violently, her legs curling inward as she thrashed against her web throne.

“You... treacherous... worm!” she gurgled as she foamed from her fanged mouth.

Nolan holstered Scarlet and turned to me. “Sorry about the beating. Had to make it look convincing.” He then reached in his inventory and offered me something small. It looked like a tiny red jawbreaker candy. “Here, eat this,” he said. “It’s a one-time use magic amplifier, enough to scorch that bitch and her throne. You do the honors.”

I was so confused! I continued looking up at him baffled. Here I was, debating how to roast my friend to survive, and then he turned around and offered me a super treat!

“Hey, do you want to score some easy experience points or not?”

I shook my head and snapped out of it. Nolan tossed the candy in my mouth, and I rushed in.

It would be my honor to roast the queen! However, she wouldn’t go down without a fight. While she was sluggish and weakened by the neurotoxin, she still had enough strength to pull herself forward on her throne. Eight massive legs pierced the ground as she hissed, her backside hunched over as she prepared to strike me.

“You think this is over?! I’ve slaughtered hundreds! You are no different!” Her voice gurgled as more foamy spittle dripped from her fangs.

I felt the candy dissolve in my furnace, a strange tingling sensation spreading throughout my body. My scales began to glow with a red-hot intensity I'd never experienced before. Heat coursed through me as I picked up speed, feeling a lot faster as I dodged web shot after web shot from her spinnerets. The queen launched a volley of venomous spikes from her abdomen too, tossing everything in the book to pin me down. But I was beyond her reach now. The magic amplifier had transformed me, making me faster, stronger, and more agile than before. I felt like I was flying as I darted between the attacks, my body a blur of motion.

“Burn in hell!” she cried as fire bubbled in my chest. This wasn’t my usual flame—this was something else entirely. The heat inside me intensified to levels I’d never experienced before, turning me into an actual flame ball.

‘You first!’ I fired back as I leaped. And when I opened my mouth, a torrent of blazing crimson fire erupted. The amplified flames engulfed the queen entirely, her screams turning to gurgles as her exoskeleton blackened and cracked. The web throne melted away like snow in summer, and the heat was so intense that even the stone floor beneath her began to glow red.

The smell of burning chitin filled her lair as her body collapsed in on itself, legs curling inward in that classic dead-spider pose. I kept the flames coming until there was nothing left but ash and a scorched outline on the ground. When I finally stopped, the boost faded. I felt exhausted but exhilarated. I’d never created a big dragon fire like that before. It felt... incredible!

“Damn.” Nolan whistled as he walked up beside me. “There’s nothing to decompose after that attack, huh?”

I looked up at him, still trying to catch my breath. ‘You... you were faking it the whole time?’

He shrugged, brushing some ash off his cloak. “I had activated a mind control shield crystal when she had attacked you,” he explained. “It’s not a dungeon core item; instead, it’s a specialty item crafted for multi-use. It negates mind control from any source, just as long as I activate it in my inventory on time. Another one of those good things that the selfish witch made, even though it wasn’t intended for me. Lasts only for a couple minutes before it becomes useless.” He scoffed. “But I didn’t fake an entire fight with you for the hell of it. My neurotoxin slug shot needed to finish its internal crafting, and as soon as it was ready, well….” He trailed off, looking over to the ash flurries of the dead queen. “Once she showed me she had a force field, I used a skill scroll enhancer to buff my slug shot. With the ability to break through impenetrable walls, drilling a hole in her head was easy.” He slipped his gun away and looked back at me. “I wanted her to think that she was winning for as long as I could, that way, she wouldn’t interfere and try to attack you. As long as I gave her a show, you were safe. The shot is one of those things that lose potency quickly, so as soon as the mixture is complete, it needs to be used.”

‘My god, Nolan! You couldn’t have told me all of that through our private link?!’ I barked at him.

“And risk the entire exchange looking fake? Now, why would I do that? It needed to look genuine.”

‘You nearly gave me a heart attack! I could have killed you when you cornered me!’

He chuckled. “I would have been fine.”

He was daring, more daring than me! And I didn’t like that. ‘You didn’t know that.’ I hissed. ‘It was a gamble. You’re gambling with your life. I seriously thought I would have died there! I could have turned all dragon mode on your ass!’

Nolan shrugged. “There are items in my inventory that you have no idea what they do, short fry. Trust me when I say I would have been fine. Besides, I saw how you were fighting—holding back, aiming for non-lethal spots, trying to immobilize rather than hurt me.”

‘And you?!’ I pouted. ‘Why didn’t you give me the same service?!’

“Oh, I did.”

I deadpanned him.

“Come on, did you really think I was trying to really hurt you?? All of my real attacks would have gone for your head or neck. You need to remember that I needed to make it look real enough that she wouldn’t suspect anything. You’re welcome, by the way.”

‘You’re a rascal, you know that?’

“The queen thought you were finished, didn’t she? So I’d say my plan worked as intended. And, speaking of finished...” he said, just as the dungeon’s treasure chest appeared at the other end of the lair. “We finished this first round, and we have some loot to collect.”

I snorted. ‘You say that like we are going to go through this charade again!’

He tossed me a smirk when he turned his head over his shoulder.

I was strongly reconsidering roasting that guy.

Honestly, I shouldn’t be complaining. It wasn’t like we’d have to face the queen again. Besides all of the bad stuff that happened in this run, I did overcome my fear of bugs, and received a lot of experience points for my troubles. Not to mention, I finally obtained my last evolution stone! ….Which I currently couldn’t use because I was inside of a dungeon! Crap!

“Just wait until we finish the waves,” Nolan insisted. “It’s not like it’s going anywhere.”

After Nolan and I split the rewards, we looted the centipede too. Before we walked through the portal that would bring us back to the start of the dungeon, the gunslinger [Inspect] the legendary item, Myrim’s Magical Base Elixir.

“So it’s legit… a legendary item in a tier three dungeon,” he mumbled to himself as he stroked his chin. “What are the odds?”

‘Wouldn’t be the first abnormal thing about the dungeon,’ I reminded him. ‘I activated it, remember?’

“Right, and we happen to find not only your last evolution stone, specifically for stage 1, but also a legendary item.”

‘Well, I’ve been pretty lucky with collecting the stones I need so far.’

“True, but Myrim is a powerful titan, daughter of the God of Godorion. Folks pray to him when they need wealth, prosperity, and health. She had long ended her crafting days, and by long ended, I mean over a century ago. So running into this gem, especially in a tier three dungeon, is special in ways I shouldn’t expect you to understand.”

‘Are you planning on selling it?’

He looked at me like I was crazy. “Freya, this is a magical base elixir, crafted from a titan. Normal magical bases are already hard to come by. If they are crafted, they tend to have their limitations. And if you happen to get a dungeon core to spit one out, they cannot be replicated. Normal bases are great for potion making, but what we have here is a legendary base. It’s far more potent than your standard—no limitations, replication capable.”

When I realized where Nolan was headed with this, my jaw dropped. ‘Are you saying that—’

“We can craft a cure for King Leech’s parasites.”

‘Aww, I wanted to say it!’

“We will finish up here and head back to The Enchanted Shelf. Esme’s our best bet in breaking down that asshole’s empire.”
