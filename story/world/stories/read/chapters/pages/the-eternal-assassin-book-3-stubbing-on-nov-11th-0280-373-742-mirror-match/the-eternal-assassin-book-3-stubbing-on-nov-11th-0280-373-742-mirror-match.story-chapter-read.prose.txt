Going into this final battle, Asher had known that there was next to no limit to the number of crazy skills they might find themselves going up against. Every one of the liches they’d taken down thus far had displayed powerful abilities. Every battle had resulted in them taking on significant injuries, and even permanent losses. Honestly, it had been something of a miracle that they’d managed to take down as many members of the Council as they had before they’d started suffering casualties. While Groln had lost a precious teammate and Amanda had lost many members of her Order, at least temporarily, Lanra had been the first real loss that Asher personally experienced.

And now he’d suffered his second.

Asher took advantage of his brief break from time to stare at the glowing copy of himself that had just appeared out of nowhere. Mako hadn’t stood a chance. One second, he’d been speaking with nothing around him, and the next, a glowing copy of Asher had popped into existence as if he’d always been there. For the first time, Asher got a taste of exactly what so many of the people who had fallen to his daggers experienced in their final moments, and he realized immediately why he’d been given the moniker of The Reaper.

His appearance was sudden and silent. His attack already halfway completed before he even winked into existence. Unless one had an automatic defensive skill or some other way of countering him…

They’d never even see their death coming.

Asher’s frozen time quickly came to an end, and the pain and anger of witnessing his friend fall at ‘his’ hands slammed into him all at once. And he wasn’t the only one either.

“Mako!” Cora shouted as Jameston fell to his knees in shock, each of their eyes wide and fixed directly on their comrade’s corpse. Before Mako’s body had even hit the ground, the fake-Asher had already vanished, winking out of existence as though he’d never existed. Realizing precisely what he was doing, Asher started before lunging forward and slipping into the astral himself.

The moment he shifted realms, the fake-him appeared once more, making a beeline straight for Cora with his own gleaming Crescent at the ready. Snarling, Asher leapt forward, moving to intercept and drive the real Crescent straight through his clone’s heart. Right before his attack could land, the fake-him returned to the material realm, and Asher blinked when his dagger hit nothing but empty air.

This isn’t like fighting the greater demon that existed in the material and astral realm at the same time… This is like fighting against Mutae again.

Just like when he’d gone up against the realm-traveling-wolf living in Richard’s room, Asher quickly realized what was going on. Though rather than bumble his way through another fight where two different people were constantly flicking in and out of the astral realm, he decided he had a better idea.

Right before his clone could reach through a compressed tunnel of space and dive its dagger into Jameston’s or Cora’s chest, Asher returned to the material as well, throwing up a Spatial Lock around him and his clone and yanked them both into the astral. As his clone’s attack failed to land and the glowing version of him realized it was trapped, the fake-him turned to narrow its eyes at him.

“Is that what I look like doing that?” Asher muttered, shaking his head at the thought. He’d been ready to start screaming after seeing Mako’s decapitation before his very eyes, but his time spent within sped-up time, separated from his emotions, had helped him think clearly. Unlike with Lanra, who had perished from severe soul damage, Mako was another story. Most importantly, they still had his body. And even if the lich had managed to replicate his Sever the Thread skill along with his Spatial ones, the moment the clone was dealt with, the skill would end.

All that to say, Mako wasn’t getting out of life that easily.

Deal with the clone, deal with the lich, resurrect Mako, Asher mentally repeated to himself, lowering his center of gravity slightly before launching himself at the fake him.

Unfortunately for him, while the clones of the Eight Crests’ had been noticeably slower and less battle-hardened than the real things, Asher didn’t have years of fighting experience under his belt. Sure, he’d spent the last couple of months in plenty of life-or-death situations, and he’d had some training here and there thanks to Moxy and Mornamol. But that didn’t equate to nearly as much instinct and muscle memory as a decade or two of training.

That meant as he crossed blades with the fake him within the astral, it became quickly apparent that the two of them were almost equally matched.

Asher gritted his teeth as he clashed with his clone, the two Crescents and two Nails sparking and scraping against one another as he battled with himself. It was like fighting against his own reflection. The fake him seemed to know everything he was going to do before he did it, and without access to Distortion or Astral Dip, Asher was forced to rely on nothing more than genuine martial skill to try and finish the clone off with. He had the slightest edge against the fake-him, which was enough for him to land a few slight cuts here and there that bled glowing, violet light, but try as he might, he couldn’t land that finishing blow.

During all of this, Cora and Jameston wept over their fallen comrade. Jameston had silent tears streaming down his face, and Cora’s fists shook while electricity crackled back and forth between them, as if searching for an appropriate outlet. Asher wanted nothing more than to flicker back into the material realm and scream at them to go after the lich, but the moment he dropped his Spatial Lock, he was all but certain his clone would take the opportunity to kill another one of them.

I can’t keep this up forever, he thought, wincing as the clone took advantage of his lack of focus in order to carve a slight gash across his forearm with the fake Crescent. However, rather than the injury giving him cause for concern, he felt nothing but relief as he witnessed the gash slowly seal itself shut when Eternal Regeneration got to work.

That confirms it. The clone doesn’t have Sever the Thread. In fact, I bet it doesn’t have any of my Eternity skills packed away in there. Ploryn is supposed to be a master of the elements. I bet his clones are created from his own knowledge. He might personally know almost every skill and every element that exists in this world, but there’s no way in hell he’s got any personal knowledge of the Eternity element. That has to be why it went after Jameston instead of Cora. Mako was trying to lead us to the lich to stop the ritual, so he had to die first. But after him, Jameston is the larger threat. The clone doesn’t have Complete Resilience! Jameston can mind-lock it just like he did the other clones!

Unfortunately, this revelation didn’t really help him all that much. At the moment, Jameston was still in the throes of mourning his fallen comrade, and Asher wasn’t exactly going to get the chance to explain all of this to the realm wanderer before the fake-him chopped off Jameston’s head. It was nice to have confirmation that Sever the Thread hadn’t been used on Mako, but Asher was on his own when it came to figuring out how to put the fake-him down.

As the two of them continued clashing blades, Asher racked his brain for a way to put his clone down for good. Even if they weren’t on something of a timer, what with the lich repairing his ritual and preparing to drop an entire mountain on the people living in Dormaul, Asher was slowly but surely running out of tritessence. The drain of moving about within the astral was lessened when he dragged a chunk of the material realm over with him like he had with Spatial Lock, but lessened didn’t mean nonexistent. Of course, that meant the clone had to be suffering as well, but in stark contrast to the flickering, shadow bodies of the Knuckles, it seemed Ploryn’s cloning skill was packed with far more tritessence than the average person. Other than the scattering of light slashes Asher had managed to land during their exchanges, the fake him didn’t seem injured at all. As far as Asher could tell, the clone wasn’t bothered in the slightest by the constant tritessence drain. Once he confirmed he wouldn’t be winning a battle of attrition, Asher doubled down on trying to come up with another win condition.

Ploryn wasn’t able to duplicate my Eternity skills… but I don’t think I can use any of them to win here. Though that begs the question.

Was there anything else he wasn’t able to duplicate?

Almost as soon as he had the thought, Asher felt Secret Seeker kick in. The skill drew his attention down to the clone’s hip, and Asher grinned when he found his answer. While the clone did have an abyssal sheath and dagger sitting within it, his skill was telling him that something was ever so slightly off about the way the dagger sat compared to how his own did.

Secret Seeker - lvl 37

Naturally, whipping out his void dagger and using that to take down his clone had been one of the very first things Asher had considered doing while fighting the fake him. But the thought of what a void-on-void collision might end up doing terrified him to his very core. Forget risking his own life, the description of fifty million people vanishing into thin air from the Void element being used three thousand years ago had run through his head, which had made him decide not to risk pulling out his dagger so long as his clone didn’t. But now that he’d figured out there were things that Ploryn wasn’t able to copy…

I really hope I’m not about to wipe out the entire Noala Kingdom, Asher thought as he took a deep breath and ripped his void dagger from its sheath.

Instantly, his tritessence began flowing down the handle and into the blade, making the tritessence drain from moving about within the astral feel almost nonexistent in comparison. In response, his clone yanked its own void dagger out…

Revealing nothing more than an empty hilt with nothing attached to it.

Even the clone seemed surprised to discover its most powerful weapon hadn’t been recreated properly, and Asher couldn’t help but laugh at the look of shock on ‘his’ face. Still, he had a mission to carry out, and he’d already wasted enough time playing with himself. Before the clone could fully recover, Asher darted forward, swiping his void dagger at the fake-him’s throat. The clone managed to get fake-Crescent up in time to block, but of course, the blade of pure void cared for mithral as much as it did any other substance from the material realm. After eating through the masterwork dagger like it didn’t exist, the blade of void continued forward, slashing straight through the clone’s neck and causing the clone to explode into a burst of violet lights just like the others.

As soon as the clone was gone, Asher shoved the void dagger back into his sheath, grimacing as he realized he was already down to almost a quarter of his energy reserves. They hadn’t even made it to Ploryn yet, let alone all the liches that were to come after. Still, that was a problem for future him, and he dropped his skill as he returned to the material realm. His entire fight against the fake him hadn’t even taken a full minute, and he shouted to snap Jameston and Cora out of their grieving.

“He’s not gone forever, we’ll bring him back!” Darting over to Mako’s corpse, Asher crouched down and yanked the two pieces into his Personal Rift, ignoring the notification that the skill had officially reached level 39. That done, he grabbed the glowing ball of violet shards hovering in the air before turning to fix the last two members of the Eight Crests’ realm wandering team with a hard look. “We’ll bring him back, but we can’t do that until we’re done here! Where is the lich?!”

“Right,” Cora said. The crackling electricity faded from her fists, and the vacant look in her eyes retreated while she shook her head. “Right!” Turning, she slapped Jameston across the face, snapping her teammate out of his stupor as he fell over. “No time for that, Jameston, we have to move! I wasn’t able to make the lich responsible for Lanra’s death pay, but that’s not the case this time! I’m going to rip the very spine out of that bag of bones and then beat him to his second death with it!”
