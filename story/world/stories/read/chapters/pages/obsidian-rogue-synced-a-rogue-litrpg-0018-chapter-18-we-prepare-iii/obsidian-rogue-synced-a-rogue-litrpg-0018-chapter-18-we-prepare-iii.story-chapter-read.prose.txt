No matter how hard he thought about the issue, he just couldn’t figure it out. He’d met only a handful of people since he’d gone on the run: The mage who’d given him the stone, Silas, the guards outside the saferoom, Xara, and the barkeep. The mage, Silas, and Xara were the only three people who’d seen the brand on his forehead. Silas and the mage didn’t seem like people who’d be able to track him down to Pelossi. Silas was unsynced and wouldn't be able to use Cerpitals' method to reach his location in such a short time. The mage was another person that he’d have to disqualify, mainly because he assumed that the mage would’ve already died.

Judging by the last time he’d seen the mage, he highly doubted that the synced could've found a way to escape Ghost’s onslaught. Xara also wasn’t someone he’d suspected, especially since he’d been with her the whole time, so he ruled her out as well. It wasn’t set in stone that it was any of the three, but he was less suspicious of them. However, given how unlikely it was to be any of the three, Levi was in a bit of a mess. Fortunately for him, those three weren’t the only ones on his list.

The guards who’d directed him to the Inn were also worthy suspects. Zen seemed like a cool guy, but Mantos was a bit annoying. It might just be based on interaction bias, but Levi put the guards at the top of his list of suspects. The older guard Mantos, had endured humiliation from Zen because of Levi. Of course, it’d been the old guard's fault, but he highly doubted that the synced would see it as such, which meant that the old guard might've felt the need to retaliate somehow. Everything seemed to point to Mantos. The old guard had a motive, knew his location, and possibly had enough clout to get him into Levi’s room without being questioned.

That may be it.

His eyes drifted to his door. It hadn’t been forced open, which meant that the person who’d broken into his room had done so with a key or a lockpick. If a lock pick had been used, it’d mean that the person who’d broken into his room had at least some semblance of skill, indicating they were competent. A quick once-over of his room made him disregard that notion. His room was in total disarray, a fact that lent more weight to the possibility that whoever had broken into his room had done so with a key and not a lockpick.The person, or persons, who’d broken into his room were uncaring with their movements. There had been a lack of restraint with their movements; they'd been brash. A trait that he’d seen in the old guard. It wasn’t certain that Mantos was the one who’d broken into his room. But the guard was the prime suspect, and just to be safe, he’d include Zen as well. There was no telling how many people had visited him while he was out. One thing that did stand out to him was that the barkeep might’ve been a part of it; heck, he had to place her up top, whether the guards were in on it or not. Someone had broken into his room without kicking the door down.

First off, whoever came to look for him would've needed to know the exact room he was staying in and would've also needed a key; both were things the barkeep had access to. This made the middle-aged woman a prime suspect, but he could be wrong. The person who might've come for him might’ve also been a mage. This behavior also fit with what the robber had displayed; they'd been arrogant, a well-known trait of synced mages.

“We have to leave,” Levi breathed. Pelossi wasn’t safe for them.

“Where to?”

“Plantrith… Do you know the way there?”

“Yeah, it's to the west. It will take us a couple of days to get there.”

“A few days,” he muttered to himself. “What do you know about the black baron?”“I don’t really know much, but most address him as the butcher of Talpha. From the little I know, he’s a big deal in Rinesta. But at the same time, he’s one of those few high-level synced who simply disappeared within the last decade. No one has been able to find him in years. And almost everyone thinks that he’s dead.”

Dead? It can't be. There's no way that the mage used his dying breath to tell me to find someone who's dead.

“I see,” Levi said. “We’ll leave for Plantrith as soon as possible. I assume you know the way?”

“I do,” She nodded. “But before we do that, we should check out the stuff we bought from Sersil’s.”

“Right.”

Levi watched as the mage stretched out a hand towards an unoccupied section of the floorboard of his room. The bagged items that they'd purchased from Sersil's store appeared out of thin air. From the corner of his eyes, he caught the glint of metal from one of her outstretched fingers. There was no doubt in his mind that the mage had made use of the storage ring. His mouth watered at the thought of how much something like that would cost. There was envy in his eyes, but no greed. He might be a thief, but he wasn’t a greedy person. He’d gone into that line of work to feed his family. Nothing more, nothing less.

“These are yours,” Xara said, pointing to a pile of items she’d taken out of the bag.Levi nodded in appreciation before going through the pile. The mage had bought him a new cloak and boots, basically a re-up on the starter kit he’d gotten from cerpital. But those weren't the only things that Xara had bought him. In the pile were also four glass orbs containing a murky darkness. Picking one off the floor, he rolled it around in his palm. The orb had a comfortable heft, neither too heavy nor too light. The orb was also very smooth. It was also transparent, so when he rolled it around, he could easily see the murky darkness rolled inside—moving with the flow of the orb.

Interesting. He thought as he raised the orb to eye level. He activated his [analyze] skill to get information on the orb as well as the other items he’d been given.

[Smoke bomb: To use this item, simply strike it against any sturdy surface to break the orb. The darkness from the void, which is contained within the sphere, will permeate the air and make it difficult for foes to spot you. The darkness within the orb can only spread out within 5m of where it is released.

Note: This is a one-use item.]

Sick.

Time to check the others.

[Night Cloak (uncommon): This equipment is not upgradeable. It is more durable than the common cloak, and it also comes with a special effect, Unknown.

—>Unknown: This ensures that your hood does not fall off your head unless intentional force is applied.

Note: All hooded cloaks above the common level possess this special effect.

Note: This cloak also comes with a +5 point modifier to your dexterity stat.

]

The equipment was certainly a step up for him. The cloak and the boots were nice, but it was the smoke bomb that had his attention. He could think of a few ways that such a thing could come in handy. It’d be of great use to him if he were ever in a position where he needed to escape. He rolled the orb in his palms again, a smile blooming on his face at the thought of him smashing it against the floor and using the distraction to escape. Shaking his head to clear his errant thoughts, he put on his new gear.

As far as he could tell, the new gear fit him like a glove. The items seemed to have been made specifically for him, which was a bit weird since he was sure he hadn’t shared his measurements. It was probably some skill of the non-combat synced who'd made the equipment. He did not doubt that that was the case; after all, it was impossible for both the cloak and boots to fit him perfectly. There had to be another side to the gear that he just didn't know about. With a sigh, Levi pushed his ignorance to the side as he stared at the mage. She seemed to be organizing her own gear, and it seemed he’d still had a few moments to himself. So he pulled up his status screen with a thought.
