Even if Asher was paralyzed, that didn’t mean there was anything wrong with his skills. He slipped into the astral at the last moment, narrowly dodging the giant hammer as it smashed straight through him and into the dead ground, shaking the earth and throwing clods of dirt in every direction. He’d been half afraid that the hammer was big enough that he wouldn’t be able to phase through it, but seeing as he hadn’t been reduced to a pile of paste in the astral realm, it seemed it came in just under the wire.

Asher let his head fall back onto the cold ground, groaning as he reeled from having taken a direct hit from Grunt’s metal-clad fist. His body felt like it was damaged more than ever before, and he was honestly shocked that he’d not only survived the hit, but even remained conscious.

Maybe because she tore her own arm out of its socket and punched backwards? Probably wasn’t able to get all that much force. But that would mean she can hit even harder than that.

His thoughts drifted slowly through his brain as if he were thinking through pea soup, further confirming he had a severe concussion. Thankfully, he wasn’t really moving all that much at the moment, which meant even without False Death active, he had plenty of time within the astral realm.

Alright, I have to do this carefully…

Even if he’d wanted to just lie there for a few hours and let his shattered body heal via Eternal Regeneration, that wasn’t an option. He didn’t understand how, but one thing he’d noticed immediately upon entering the Deadlands was that the Death Aura was somehow present in the astral realm as well as the material. He was deep enough into the Deadlands that he’d die to the Death Aura long before his body finished healing, which meant he needed to speed things up a bit.

Focusing on his Personal Rift, Asher withdrew one of his very few potions of Restoration. They had plenty of Lesser Restoration, but those potions were a bit more limited, and he feared he’d sustained too much damage for one or even two of them to patch him up.

The tricky part was pulling out the potion of Restoration directly in front of his mouth, and angled the correct way. Not being able to move a muscle from the neck down made things difficult, but not impossible. While he pulled the potion from his rift, he distinctly didn’t bring the cork with it. That meant an uncorked, upside-down potion worth more than what most people made in a couple of years appeared a few inches above his face.

Which then promptly fell, bounced off his nose, and spilled its content all over his cheek and dead ground.

God damn it, Asher thought, sighing to himself as he tried and failed to lick at the bit of potion that had managed to land on the side of his face. Being concussed messed with his reaction time and finer motor skills. Something he should have thought of before attempting that. While wasting such a valuable potion was a pain, at the very least, he had one more Restoration potion in his rift.

This time, Asher opened his mouth first and summoned the upside-down potion directly in between his teeth. Once he was confident he had a firm hold on it, he pressed his tongue against the cork and used Distortion, shrinking it for just a moment and allowing the magical liquid to flow out, directly into his waiting mouth. Keeping the cork pressed against his inner cheek as he really didn’t want to accidentally swallow that and end up choking, he eagerly drank the potion of Restoration.

To his surprise, the potion was almost cold, as though he’d taken it straight out of the fridge. Lesser Restoration potions didn’t really taste like much of anything, but he could actually feel the sheer amount of healing magic contained within a full Restoration potion. It was almost like carbonation for his soul, and Asher shuddered as he felt the chilling sensation sweep throughout his body.

Bones popped and shifted as they mended themselves and were yanked back into realignment by muscles reknitting. His ruined eye regrew near instantly, fixing his depth perception and watering as it blinked at the sudden light it had never seen before. It took nearly ten full seconds for the expensive potion to finish coursing through his body, which just went to show what kind of shape he’d actually been in.

Note to self, Asher thought, shaking out his arms and legs and stretching to ensure everything still worked as he got to his feet. Don’t let the giant undead monster hit you with her equally giant fists. Or the hammer.

While he’d been struggling away, Grunt had simply stood there, her massive hammer raised high above her head as if waiting for him to reappear so she could smash him for real. The arm she’d damaged to strike him hung limply at her side, the muscles in her shoulder ripped apart, but she clearly only needed one arm to wield her hammer. Crescent had long since reverted back to its regular dagger size, still jutting out of the back of her neck. From the quantity of blood that had stained Grunt’s armor and the surrounding Deadlands, it looked as though an entire goat had been slaughtered just under her chin, but the undead didn’t seem bothered in the slightest.

“Forget about him, Grunt, hit me instead! That looks like fun!”

Asher ignored Smiles’ distant calls as he tried to come up with a plan. Not only was Grunt a giant with incredible strength, she was remarkably quick to destroy her own body just to land a hit on him. He hadn’t expected her to chop her head halfway off just to look at him, which was a mistake he wouldn’t make again.

“Undead are a real pain to fight,” he grumbled, clutching his living wood dagger as he returned to the material realm.

The moment he reappeared, the giant roared, smashing down with her hammer in an attempt to turn him into paste. Compressing space with Distortion, Asher stepped forward, traveling between her legs and ending up behind her the moment her hammer smashed into the ground. While she was half bent over, he launched himself up with another angled use of Distortion, landing on her back and slamming his now living wood longsword between the blood-soaked portions of her neck armor. With a roar of his own, he grabbed Crescent in his other hand just to hold on, before yanking his living wood blade to the side with as much force as he could muster.

The wooden blade sliced through a solid foot of muscle, releasing a fresh wave of long-rotted blood all over him, but it wasn’t quite enough. Even if three fourths of Grunt’s neck was now severed, her unnatural physique made it so that the last quarter was more than strong enough to keep her head attached to her body. Asher swore as Grunt released her hammer and threw herself backwards, forcing him to slip into the astral and phase right through her to survive being crushed as her back smashed into the ground with earth-quaking force. Landing on her chest, he returned to the material realm and stabbed down with both daggers, aiming for that last chunk of neck that he still needed to cut through. Grunt didn’t even attempt to stop him. Letting him slice deep into her neck and fully sever her head in order to slap him with her good arm, she yet again willingly suffered damage to herself in exchange for landing her own blow, and Asher was sent flying off to the side as his newly healed bones were broken all over again.

Tumbling across the ground a good few dozen feet, Asher eventually came to a painful stop. Groaning, he forced himself back to his feet, staggering and cradling his mangled left arm as he took stock of his current state.

Shattered left arm, busted left knee and ankle, dislocated left shoulder, feels like a fractured hip… Let’s just save time and say the left half of me is currently ‘ow.’

The good news was he hadn’t been hit in the head this time, which meant no concussion or slowed-down thoughts. Slowly, Asher staggered over to the fallen giant, doing his best to ignore those unpleasant sensations of fractured bones shifting around as he limped along. Coming up alongside her head, he peered down, letting out a painful chuckle as he confirmed his suspicions.

Based on the shifting of her eyes under her visor and the low growl he could hear, Grunt was still ‘alive.’ There was only one reason why she’d trade having her head severed in order to land another powerful blow on him, and that was because she hadn’t known just what he was capable of.

“Smiles!” Asher called out, lengthening Crescent as he climbed onto Grunt’s motionless chest. “Can Grunt control her body if her head is severed like you can?”

“Well yeah! That’s a pretty common ability for empowered undead, not just us Servants!”

“So your plan was to jump right back up, completely headless, and finish me off, huh?” Asher asked, staring down at the still-growling undead as he raised his blade with his good arm. “Sorry, but that’s not how my skill works.”

Plunging Crescent into her head, Asher cut once, twice, and then three times before he felt the tritessence keeping her alive finally sever. Letting out a sigh of relief, he sagged, shoving his dagger back into his rift.

He didn’t know if it was the remnants of the potion of Restoration still in his body, but he could actually feel his flesh knitting itself back together in real time for a change. By the time he’d made it all the way back to Smiles, he felt good as new.

Well, good as someone wandering deep within a Death Aura powerful enough to kill most people in an instant. Which meant he actually felt pretty crappy as his body continued to decay practically before his very eyes.

“That was fun to watch!” Smiles grinned, his eyes focused on Grunt’s distant corpse. “You’re really good at healing, you know that?”

“I have a lot of practice,” Asher drawled, wondering if Smiles had officially outlived his usefulness. He’d gone through all of the Servants the undead had expected would be brought after him, and he had a feeling he wasn’t going to get any more useful information out of the severed head.

“You should have let her hit you with her hammer,” Smiles said, a disappointed look flashing across his face. “You could have given me the memory!”

“I think I need to survive in order to share memories with you,” Asher pointed out, looking around. “Where’s Watcher?”

“Dunno, she vanished the moment you killed Grunt, which means she must have been watching her instead of you. Oh, you should go take the giant hammer! Take it and crush me with it!”

“I can’t store something in my rift if I can’t carry it,” Asher said, eyeing up the massive hammer lying on the ground. “That thing must weigh a ton.”

“You should probably start working out,” Smiles suggested. “Then you could store more stuff in there.”

“Look, Smiles… As much fun as this has all been. Now that I’m getting close to the center of the Deadlands, I’m thinking I should probably finish you for good. I’m intending to sneak up on the King of Death and take him out silently if possible, and you’re not exactly… quiet.”

“As long as you smash me with a giant hammer like you promised, that’s fine by me.”

“I don’t have a giant hammer. Not one I can lift!” Asher snapped as Smiles’ eyes went straight to the massive hammer lying behind them. “I can make stuff smaller while touching it, but that doesn’t change their weight. What if I borrowed Rulfar’s axe and used that? Good enough?”

“Hmm…” Smiles hummed, thinking it over carefully. “I really wanted to know what it felt like to be crushed by a giant hammer…”

“Look, I’m trying to be accommodating for God only knows what reason, but I could just stab you with a dagger,” Asher pointed out. “While you’ve been a little helpful here and there, you are still an undead monster that’s killed hundreds of people.”

“Oh, thousands. Definitely thousands by now,” Smiles corrected, grinning with his jagged teeth. “I’ve been around for a while, and it’s pretty fun. I’m fast enough that I can normally find intruders before the other Servants, so I actually have the highest kill count!”

“Not really helping your case here, Smiles.”

“Fine, fine, a big axe is close enough I guess,” Smiles said, actually pouting as he made the decision.

“Thank you,” Asher said, turning away as he did one last sweep for Watcher. He’d been eager to try the Flare plan Samantha had helped him come up with, but the freaky eye-based undead was nowhere to be seen.

“Oh hey! Is this an am—”

Asher spun around just in time to witness the edge of a red robe before it vanished, leaving him standing alone near the center of the Deadlands. Only one undead wore a red robe and had the ability to move Spatially, which meant it wasn’t hard to figure out what had just happened.

Watcher had finally revealed herself. And she’d rescued Smiles.
