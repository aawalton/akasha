“Asher!” Brian shouted, running over and leaping onto him as Asher Recalled to the secret meeting room within the truthseeker program. “I missed you guys!”

“You just saw us last week!” Asher laughed, desperately trying not to fall over with a twelve-year-old clinging to his waist. Even with his newly enhanced strength, getting tackled immediately after using Recall while he was still getting his bearings was a bit jarring. “Come on, let’s Recall back to Samantha. Then you can tell us all about how the program’s been so far.”

With a nod, Brian grabbed his hand and let himself be pulled into his Personal Rift. Before going anywhere, Asher took a look around the empty room containing nothing more than a small chair and table. He still thought it was strange that they always found Brian waiting all by himself in this room whenever it was time to pick him up or drop him off, but he understood why that was the case. The truthseeker academy was about as top-secret as anything got in this kingdom, and Donvath didn’t want Asher laying eyes on a single person working within it. Putting the thought aside, Asher focused on Samantha’s mark, forcing the world to shift as he Recalled over to her position on the edge of the palace grounds.

“Got him?” Samantha asked, looking more like she was prepared for another day of fighting demons than a leisurely stroll through the capital based on her sea serpent leathers and enchanted bow slung over her back. As much as it would be nice to try and let their guard down for the day, the Head themselves operated out of the capital, which meant they needed to be ready for anything. Especially when they had Brian in tow.

“Nah, I thought I’d just leave him in that room for the day. Build some character, you know?” Asher said, grinning as Samantha rolled her eyes. Not wanting to leave Brian in his Personal Rift any longer than needed, Asher pulled him out.

“Sam!” Brian shouted, doing a repeat of his greeting with Asher as he ran over and leapt onto his sister. “I missed you guys!”

“Hey now, I thought I was special!” Asher said, planting his hands on his hips while the two siblings hugged.

“I think you’re plenty special enough already,” Samantha said, smirking at him as her hug lasted even longer than the one Brian had given him. Once Brian finally let go and stepped back, she smiled down at him. “So, how’s the program? I can’t believe it’s already been three weeks!”

“Me neither, it keeps going by so fast!” Brian said, puffing out his chest a bit. “My instructors told me I had the most impressive archery skills they’d ever seen for someone my age. I shouldn’t have any issue getting the Bow element!”

“Wow, that’s some high praise coming from someone who teaches the truthseekers,” Asher said, his brow raised. Truthseekers entered the program from a very young age, meaning they would have been training for years before getting their origin element. Even if Brian was only in the auxiliary program and not going to become an actual truthseeker, it was nice his skills were still being recognized.

“I’m not surprised, seeing as you bolted from the dinner table without finishing practically every single day the last few years in order to get more practice in,” Samantha teased. “Don’t forget you still have a few years before your origin element. You need to keep up the hard work!”

“Maybe I’ll be so good at archery I’ll get my origin element before turning sixteen,” Brian said, continuing to puff out his chest perhaps just a little too far.

“It would certainly make you pretty famous,” Asher laughed, ruffling his hair. “Anyway, any preferences for what we do today? We’ve got the run of the capital, and plenty of shards to burn.”

“I don’t know,” Brian admitted, looking around at the people wandering to and from the palace as he scrunched up his face. “The last two breaks I had we did whatever I wanted to do. How about you guys take a turn? What do either of you want?”

“Nothing really comes to mind, to be honest,” Samantha said, fidgeting with a piece of her hair and frowning as she failed to come up with an answer. “It feels like I just spend all my free time training these days. That or flying around, but that’s not really something three people can do.”

“Why don’t you take Brian and do some laps in the air for a bit?” Asher suggested, trying not to laugh at the excited look on Brian’s face at the idea. “I was planning on hunting for a new artifact anyway, and I don’t want to bore you guys with my shopping. That way the two of you can talk all about me without me hearing it!”

“This might surprise you, but I have plenty of other things I enjoy talking about with people beyond you,” Samantha snorted.

“I want to try flying!” Brian said, running over and all but scrambling up Samantha’s back while she laughed. As soon as he was in position, he thrust a finger into the sky. “Away!”

“Alright. But hold on tight!” It was a testament to how confident Samantha had become in her flying ability that she didn’t look worried in the slightest about dropping her brother as she crouched down and prepared to take off. Turning to give him one last smile, she nodded. “We’ll be back later. Have fun shopping!”

With that, she manifested her emerald wings and activated Rapid Flight, shooting up into the sky as Brian screamed from the sudden acceleration. Rolling his eyes at her showboating, Asher ignored the many fingers pointing as people craned their necks to watch Samantha rise higher and higher into the air, until she was little more than a green speck. They’d largely given up on trying to keep a low profile at this point, seeing as even people in other countries knew about The Reaper, let alone this one.

Of course, the good thing about having a reputation as a known assassin killer and demon hunter was that even if people suspected who he was, few people ever dared to approach and strike up a conversation with him. It was sad to say that even with all their laws and Donvath’s best attempts at keeping things within the kingdom fair, ‘might makes right’ was still a pretty powerful concept that most people fell behind.

Asher watched Samantha and Brian himself for a few seconds, before turning and walking off the palace grounds. Thus far, he’d really only used Opal’s shop when it came to artifact shopping, but he’d heard that there were at least a couple of even larger ones within the capital he wanted to check out. He had a feeling they might not have quite the same backroom selection Opal offered certain clients, but he was still curious about the options available to him.

Last night, he had run Richard’s proposal past his team, asking for their thoughts on what they wanted to do. Thankfully, Rulfar had returned from his Hibernation in the forest by that point, his strength once again returned to him and ready to do battle whenever they were. The bloodhound had been fine with the proposed trade, seeming far more interested in the blanket favor from a wizard than an extra chunk of shards.

Moxy had been found in the sparring arena as per usual, and she’d told him to do whatever he wanted, saying she’d come up with something fun for Richard to take care of down the line.

Samantha had been the most hesitant of the three, which didn’t surprise him seeing as she valued shards more than anyone else he’d ever met. Asher hadn’t tried to convince her one way or the other, but in the end, she agreed to give Richard the corpse. Asher was pretty certain what swayed her most was Richard’s declaration that he’d notify them of any of the greater demons’ additional weaknesses. At this point, with how greater demons naturally weakened enchanted items just by getting close to them, Samantha was the weakest of the four of them when it came to fighting them. She relied primarily on her bow, and the piercing enchantment just didn’t pack enough of a punch when weakened.

All that to say, Asher had been handed another million shards by Richard last night before the wizard had run off, actually giggling in excitement with the greater demon corpse floating behind him. That brought his total up to a bit shy of six million shards, but he needed to conserve as many of those as he could for his future skill evolutions. Ideally, rather than anything crazy powerful that would cost an arm and a leg, he wanted to find another fairly cheap artifact with a rather unique effect most people wouldn’t find useful, such as his Ring of False Death. He didn’t really have any specific effect in mind, however, so he started off by doing some window shopping.

Identify - Lvl 33

As expected, wandering through a few different stores dedicated to showcasing magical artifacts was an excellent way to level up Identify as well. There were a handful of interesting artifacts that stood out to him, such as a brooch that granted the wearer a Fire Shield skill, or an earring that allowed the wearer the use of Gather Noise. But it wasn’t until his third shop that Asher stumbled upon the perfect artifact for him, and he laughed as he read the card set beside the display case.

[Boots of Levitation] - Grants the wearer access to the Levitation skill (Tier 1).

According to the card, Levitation was a utility skill that allowed one to hover anywhere from a few inches to nearly ten feet off the ground. The biggest downside to the skill was that the wearer couldn’t move horizontally while using them, so they couldn’t do anything nearly as fun as walking on water or walking around above everyone’s heads. The ability to go a couple of feet straight up or down probably wasn’t all that impressive to most people wealthy enough to purchase artifacts, but it was perfect for Asher’s purposes.

Finally, he’d have a method of going up from one room to the next while within the astral realm.

One hundred and sixty-five thousand shards poorer, Asher walked out of the artifact shop with a pair of brand-new boots on his feet, excited to give them a test run.

Hilariously, whoever had created the boots appeared to have used sea serpent leather in their construction, which meant they even matched with his current outfit. That wasn’t too surprising though, as the tailor he’d commissioned his clothes from in Poltar had informed him that sea serpent leather was the best choice when it came to high-end footwear. It made the boots completely resistant to water, and was far more durable than most other forms of leather.

It seemed Brian and Samantha were still zipping around in the air over by the forest beyond the city walls, so Asher headed back to the palace instead to wait for them. He was eager to get inside and start playing around with his boots without drawing the eyes of everyone around him, but it appeared messing with his latest artifact was going to have to wait.

There was something of a crowd blocking the entrance to the palace.

Wanting to break in his new boots, Asher had elected to walk rather than simply Recalling back to his room, which meant he quickly found himself standing at the back of a large crowd of people all trying to peer over one another’s heads at something. With a frown, he contemplated Recalling back to his room after all, before realizing this was the perfect time to try out his boots. Chuckling to himself, Asher focused on his new Levitation skill and jerked upward into the air, raising two feet straight up in an instant. That gave him all the height he needed to see over the crowd and figure out what was going on, and his eyes widened as he made out what was causing all the commotion.

Standing just inside the palace grounds, looking as though they were waiting for someone, were three heavily armed warriors. Each one looked like they could personally take down a lesser demon without breaking a sweat, and even without using Judgement, they radiated nothing but confidence in their abilities. Though it wasn’t their experience or looks that made Asher excited, but their class.

Each and every one of them was a realm wanderer.
