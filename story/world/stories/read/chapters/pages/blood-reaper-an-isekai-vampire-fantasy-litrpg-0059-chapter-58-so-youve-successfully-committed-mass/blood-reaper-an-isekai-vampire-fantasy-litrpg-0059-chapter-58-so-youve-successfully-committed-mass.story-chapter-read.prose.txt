Ace could barely see.

Wounded. Battered. Bloody. This fight hadn’t gone to plan at all. In this world, it apparently wasn’t as easy as grinding through low-Level beasts to Level up in a more controlled way.

This world was too full of blood-hungry beasts for that to work.

Here he was, at the precipice of total defeat, and he had precious few options left. Twenty-six targets. Critically low Soul Meter. Armor compromised. Team at risk. No Shadow Realm escape route.

His military training had prepared him for last stands, but not like this—not against gelatinous nightmares controlled by a hive mind that adapted with each passing second. Traditional tactics were exhausted.

It was time for something a little more desperate.

He stumbled, trying to get to his feet as the slugs closed in, when a familiar voice chirped through the air.

"Well, isn't this just delightful?" the System said cheerfully.

Ace glared briefly over his shoulder to find her perched atop a nearby boulder, utterly ignored and untouched by the slugs who surrounded it. They didn’t even glance her way, as if she were invisible to them, and her blonde curls bounced as she clapped her small hands together.

"Such creativity!" The System's childlike face lit up with genuine amusement. "Though your form could use work. All that military training, and yet you're still wasting energy on unnecessary movements."

Ace ignored her, too focused on staying alive as a dozen slugs converged on his position. "I’m a little busy here."

"Oh, don't mind me." She swung her legs playfully, her feet never quite touching the acid-slicked stone beneath her. "I just love watching you work. So much more entertaining than the others who simply gave up and, you know, died."

The alpha slug pulsed as it built up for another attack. Its eyestalks pivoted, directing its remaining minions to form a tightening circle around Ace as they prepared for the kill. One of the slugs got a little too excited and inched ahead of the others. He swung at it, the Crimson Sword feeling heavier with each swing. His muscles burned from exertion and acid damage.

Not enough energy to maintain his current approach.

Not enough time for the team to reach safety.

"What'll it be, Sergeant?" The System tilted her head, those red eyes gleaming with anticipation. "Heroic last stand? Desperate retreat? Or perhaps something... unexpected?"

It was like she had read his mind.

A man like Ace—well, he didn’t give up easily.

He wouldn’t just lay down and die.

Ace took a deep breath and reached inside himself, to the dark well where shadow and blood intertwined. Instead of carefully drawing power for a single attack, he grabbed everything remaining—every drop of his Soul Meter, every ounce of his magic—and channeled it simultaneously through his Duskblade Manifestation ability.

He meant to summon just three in a final, desperate strike at the alpha's central nervous system. But as his concentration to the shadows within him faltered, the magic slipped from his control. It shivered. It fought him. It ached with a life of its own.

His vision blurred, the edges darkening as his depleted SM pulled energy directly from his HP instead. The power surged, wild and untamed, no longer responding to his will but to his raw desperation.

The effect was immediate…

…and horrifying.

Three dozen shadow-forged daggers materialized around him, hovering in a perfect half-sphere that arched above his head. He teetered, on the bring of passing out, but managed to stay on his feet. Each hovering blade trembled with unstable energy, their edges bleeding darkness into the air like ink dissolving in water. The void-tinged metal didn't merely absorb light—it devoured it, creating pockets of absolute darkness that distorted the natural physics of the ravine.

Raw power hammered through Ace's body like lightning strikes—one after another, again and again, slamming against his very soul as though he'd ripped open internal floodgates that were never meant to be breached. Heat flooded his veins, invasive and caustic. Blistering rage scorched through him with merciless intensity.

Reality splintered into overlapping images, each slightly out of sync with the others. The heady tang of copper filled his mouth. His muscles expanded, stretched by the unfiltered power being forced through channels never meant to accommodate such volume.

His vision blurred again, his consciousness fragmenting as a surge of something else, something other, built from within the recesses of his being—a presence that had always lurked at the periphery of his consciousness during Shadow Realm Immersion. Not quite him, but not entirely separate either. A passenger that had been waiting, watching, learning his rhythms and weaknesses.

It was as if the shadow entities he always glimpsed in the Shadow Realm were no longer content to observe.

Mine.

The thought wasn't his own. It reverberated through his skull with cold, alien precision—a voice made of vacuum and the void.

The System's eyes widened with genuine surprise. "Now that's interesting, Sergeant."

He didn’t listen.

Frankly, with his body and mind on the brink of shattering, he didn’t care.

With a shaky hand, Ace reached for the nearest Duskblade. It trembled at his fingertips, just out of reach. Its shuddering became more violent the closer he got to it. He gritted his teeth, determined to rein in his magic before he lost control of it completely, but the damn thing shot out of reach before he could grab the hilt.

These weren't obedient weapons anymore.

The dozens of Duskblades hummed against his mind, vibrating with discordant energy that crawled up his spine like claws digging into his bones, pushing his limits, testing the boundaries of his dominance.

It was as if they had become semi-sentient fragments of the Shadow Realm itself.

"Do what you’re told!” he snarled.

Blood trickled from his nose as he imposed his will on them. The daggers quivered in the air, their edges bleeding shadow and void that twisted and writhed violently. Each blade hovered between multiple states simultaneously—solid yet fluid, present yet elsewhere, tools yet alive. They pulled against his control, aching for even more blood than he was willing to give.

His consciousness stretched thin across all the simultaneous connections, his mind fracturing like glass under pressure. The pain was excruciating—not the familiar burn of overexerted muscles or the sharp sting of a bullet to the arm, but something fundamentally wrong, like his nervous system was being rewired without anesthetic.

“No,” he growled, his voice dangerously low.

Just, no.

No, he would not surrender.

No, he would not give in.

For a heartbeat, the daggers went completely still—a predator pausing before the killing lunge. Then something shifted in the balance of power. The shadows submitted to him. They bent. They cowered.

They obeyed.

In unison, the shadow daggers shot outward in every direction. They streaked through the clearing, leaving inky after-images of absolute darkness that hung in the air like scars in reality. Several slugs reared up in front of the alpha, sacrificing themselves to take the blow instead. Each blade found a target, burying itself to the hilt in the slugs unfortunate enough to be in their way. Where they struck, shadows bloomed—intricate tendrils of darkness that pulsed hungrily with the need to eat away at whatever they touched.

To destroy.

Tendrils of midnight essence connected each slug, creating a web of power with Ace at its center. Each dagger became a conduit for Lifeforce Siphon, but instead of drawing energy individually to Ace, they connected to each other, creating a cascading chain reaction.

And when it finally reached Ace, it hit hard.

It transformed into a torrent of power that slammed into him with physical force. His back arched, his mouth opening in a silent scream as the essence of other lives flooded his system. Pain and euphoria intertwined, his body struggling to process the sudden influx of power.

“Oh, you sweet little twit,” the System said with a sweet little pout. “You weren’t supposed to figure that out so fast.”

He could barely hear her over the rush of magic flooding his body. As much as he wanted to tell her to shut the hell up, his jaw refused to open. His teeth clenched tighter as more and more energy flooded into him, and all he could do was absorb what came his way.

In a flash of light, the transfer ended. Ace fell to his knees, his hands barely strong enough to keep him from collapsing to the ground as steam rolled off his body. His vision blurred again, and he shook his head to fight off the dizzy spell threatening to take him under.

He had to push through.

He had to finish this.

Around him, the slugs that had been attacked all convulsed and collapsed, their goopy bodies dissolving into puddles of blobby slop. Three dozen simultaneous kills, their combined life force now coursing through Ace's veins like liquid fire.

But power came with a price.

Foreign sensations overwhelmed him like a thousand new nerve endings blooming beneath his skin, awareness fracturing into multiple simultaneous perspectives as his body integrated attributes from the dying creatures.

Attributes that his body couldn’t handle.

The world tilted sideways as new sensory input flooded his consciousness. In a rush that made the hair on his arms stand on end, Ace could perceive chemical signatures in the air, tracking the slugs' movements through molecular trails rather than visual confirmation. His perception fragmented, processing multiple angles simultaneously as though viewing the battlefield through compound eyes—front, sides, partial rear awareness combining into a disorienting omnidirectional tableau.

It was too much.

He couldn’t sustain it.

A warning flashed in the back of his mind, and on instinct, he glared up at the alpha slug as it reared to its full height. Its massive body quivered with rage—rage he could feel in his very bones.

Rage he very much reciprocated.

His body trembled with acid burns and exhaustion. The System watched from her perch. Though he had some HP and SM, now, he was exhausted, and he didn’t have much left in him for the attack.

He had one last chance to kill this thing. One last chance to channel all he had at this monster, all at once.

It all came down to this one final, desperate move.

The alpha’s mouth widened. In the second before it could fire more acid at him, Ace launched himself forward with every ounce of his remaining strength. In the same moment, he summoned more Duskblades into the air around him. They hovered again, but this time, they didn’t fight.

They were as bloodthirsty as he was, and they all wanted the same thing—death.

With a yell of exhaustion and pure rage, he lifted the Crimson Sword above his head and unleashed the small cluster of Duskblades hovering around him. The blades shot directly at the alpha as he brought the Crimson Sword down on the bastard’s head. The blood-channeling grooves on his sword burned bright red as it descended in a perfect arc.

Everything hit at once.

Duskblades dug deep into the alpha’s body. His sword plunged into the undulating goop that served as the creature’s neck. The blisteringly sharp blades cut through the monster’s neural clusters, through its limbs, through its eyestalks, through everything that kept it alive.

He cleaved its head clean off.

Even when decapitated, the alpha's death scream vibrated through the forest, rattling Ace’s very bones. Its massive body convulsed around him, its internal chambers rupturing with black blood as its neural network collapsed.

Ace landed hard on his shoulder and rolled. He reached for the Duskblades embedded in the writhing corpse and, with a surge of will, forced them all to evaporate in a rush of smoke so that they couldn’t be absorbed this time.

The alpha’s death throes intensified, its body collapsing inward as it died. The smaller surviving slugs scattered in disorganized retreat, the hive mind shattered beyond recovery.

Something popped in the corpse’s torso, and a geyser of acid billowed into the air. Another pop, and another, and another, until the entire cadaver sprayed concentrated bursts of acid in all directions.

“Shit,” Ace muttered.

He launched himself sideways, but he wasn’t fast enough.

A massive burst of acid caught Ace across his back and shoulders as he tried to twist away. It seared through the Thornhide Armor this time, like molten metal through ice, sending spirals of caustic smoke into the air as the armor’s protective plates disintegrated. Each drop that hit the armor ignited Ace's skin with white-hot agony. His nerve endings screamed. His jaw wired shut. He stifled a raw yell of pain as his skin bubbled and sloughed away. The stench of dissolving leather and burning tissue filled his head—metallic, acrid, with undertones of charred meat that made his stomach heave.

System notifications flashed across his vision, each alert more urgent than the last as his HP gauge hemorrhaged. He stared in horror as the numbers plummeted in sickening free-fall.

Closer and closer to zero.

Closer and closer to death.
