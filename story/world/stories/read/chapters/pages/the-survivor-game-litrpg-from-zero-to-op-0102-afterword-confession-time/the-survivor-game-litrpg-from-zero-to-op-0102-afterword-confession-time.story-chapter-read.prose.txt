Okay, let's be honest.

Without a doubt, this story won't win any awards, nor will it make me rich or anything like that. The ratings say so, and so do the statistics. Probably even those of you reading this story know that. But I can say that I finished it, and I'm proud of that.

RoyalRoad is a place where the vast majority of stories are abandoned. I, on the other hand, managed to write THE END. I put my heart and soul into it. Is it imperfect? Of course. Are there cringe-worthy moments? Lots. Are there chapters I would completely rewrite today? I think the whole book should be rewritten from scratch.

The point is that this book had to be finished somehow. I think finishing this book taught me more than many creative writing courses I could have taken because it taught me:

- Discipline (publishing every week even when I didn't feel like it);

- Humility (reading that the numbers aren't growing or that reviews and comments can be negative hurts, but it makes you grow);

- Perseverance (reaching more than 100k words is difficult).

To the followers who supported me: you are crazy, in the best sense of the word. Thank you for following the adventures of Marshmallow and the Goat Horse to the end. If you are reading this, it means you stayed with us from beginning to end. I have nothing but respect for you.

THE END (really this time). And thank you.
