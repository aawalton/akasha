Brian wandered the perimeter of their now-enclosed space. As the Widow led Alexander and Johnny off on their crusade, the trees had all shifted into a tight perimeter around them, leaving a circular space with a fifty-yard diameter centered on the covered pool.

“What do you think, Cassidy?” Brian asked, surveying the wall of trees surrounding them.

“Three fireballs to one point of the trees could likely break through,” she nodded.

“That may activate a defensive response from the trees. Who knows if the roots wouldn’t immediately try and swallow us up afterward?” Judah noted nervously.

“The real question is, will the crackpot and the kid want to come with us?” Cassidy asked with a raised eyebrow.

Brian looked over at Megan, who was just sitting down looking aimless. Then at Linda, who was in a complete stupor, clawing lightly where the pool had been covered. She was manic with her desire to see the waters again.

The party watched nervously as Brian took out the hair clip Alexander had given him and walked over to Linda. He placed it on her head, and he watched as the slightest awareness returned to her eyes.

“Linda?”

“What!?” she snapped in aggravation.

“Will you be ready to leave if we get the chance to escape?” Brian asked, testing her response.

Linda’s face scrunched up in indignant fury.

“Why would I leave? The pinnacle of all oils lies here just below our feet! The finest, most pure essence the world could ever give us is in our hands! I could reign over the hierarchy of Naturoleum as its Queen! I could...”

Brian removed the hair clip. Linda immediately trailed off from her rant to look longingly at the ground.

“I could likely carry her if she was willing, but if she is fighting me, it would be impossible,” Judah commented.

Brian sighed and walked over to Megan.

Saying a silent prayer, he placed the hairpin in Megan’s hair. She looked up at him, seeming more lucid than Linda.

“Megan?”

“Yes? Do I know you?” she asked before looking at her surroundings with concern.

“No. My name is Brian Ortes. I am leading a group that came to rescue you.”

“Rescue me?” She looked confused.

“Yes. Are you feeling okay?”

“I’m... I’m scared, but I am okay physically. Where are we? What is this place?”

“This is a different world. You fell through a portal during a play. Do you remember?”

“...Yes. I was in the woods. Then I saw these weird screens in front of me, and a child came up and offered to help me, but she wasn’t a child, and... I ended up here.”

“Screens? Like a blue screen from a computer game?”

“It was a green screen that asked me if I wanted to accept a title.”

“Green? Not blue?”

Megan thought for a second.

“It was definitely green.”

Brian waited a second before asking, “What title was it offering you?”

“It said, ‘You have been invited to accept the title Plane Warden.’”

Brian mentally shrugged when he recalled that he was simply informed that he was now a thief.

“Have you accepted yet?”

“No. I was too weirded out. I don’t even know what a Plane Warden is!” Megan responded.

Brian waved Judah over, and he jogged up to them.

“Do you know what a Plane Warden is?” Brian asked him.

“Plane Warden? That sounds more grandiose than a class. More like an office or a title,” Judah suggested.

“She says she was offered the title of Plane Warden by the system.”

“Well, what abilities were you offered?” Judah asked excitedly.

“She hasn’t accepted it yet.”

Judah rolled his eyes and groaned.

“Well, you are wise to take all the consequences of your decision into account first,” he said unconvincingly. “The only thought that comes to mind is the planes of existence. Different dimensions, so to speak. You might be the first human to travel to another dimension, and that may have made the system go, ‘Hey! This arbitrary achievement means we should give you amazing powers relating to dimensional travel!’” Judah said exasperatedly, waving his hands.

“That doesn’t sound so bad, does it?” Brian prompted.

“No, but that seems overly simplistic, doesn’t it?” Megan asked.

A large crack sounded from the surrounding trees, causing everyone to stand up and look around. Another crack from the other direction had them turn to see the trees shifting. The sound increased in regularity as the trees all started to shift inward and the ground beneath them started to move.

“Umm, something tells me that the battle is not going well!” Judah yelled to everyone.

“Be ready to defend yourselves!” Brian yelled. “Megan! I suggest you accept the gift of supernatural abilities and figure them out quickly!”

“Firebolt!” Cassidy yelled.

A burst of flame hit a root that was reaching up to grab at Judah’s legs.

Judah jumped back and pulled the trident around to attack the root. He was able to swing and cut a large gash into the burning root.

Branches started to bow downward toward the party. The branch closest to Brian shot out faster than he expected and cut his arm. It tried to wrap around him, but he flicked his dagger out of his hand and teleported away.

“Cassidy! I hope you can burn a hole for our escape!” Brian yelled.

“Fireball!”

Cassidy’s strongest attack flew, scouring the ground as it traveled. It hit the trees, and they shook and wailed as they burned. As they watched, more trees filled in the small gap that had formed.

“That didn’t look promising!” Judah yelled.

“Okay, I accepted!” Megan shouted to the group.

“Judah! Assist Megan the best you can. I will try and use my shield pins to defend you. Cassidy! Conserve your mana and delay the attack from the roots and branches!”

“On it!” Cassidy said as she used the sign language Alexander had shown her to fly above the roots and watch for branches.

“The pool! The pool is opening!” Linda yelled with glee.

Judah ran up to Megan.

“Do you see any abilities or skills?”

“Um... I see a small list of abilities.”

“Do any of them look like teleportation abilities?”

“Umm, let me read them.”

Brian watched as the glow from the pool of water started to fill the clearing.

“Hurry. Hurry! That pool will wipe our minds out.”

“I’M READING!” Megan yelled, stressed out.

“Calm down. You’ve got this.”

Cassidy threw several firebolts into the branches and at a root that started to move near Megan. Brian jumped in front of a branch aimed at Judah’s head, putting his full trust in Alexander’s shield pin. He felt a surge of adrenaline as the shield burst from the pin on his chest and blocked the sharp nails of the branch.

Megan yelled out but quickly concentrated on her list.

“Would shifting count?”

“I think so... what’s the name?”

“Um, Planar Shift.”

“PERFECT! Take that one!” Judah exclaimed.

He watched as a book fell into her hand.

“Yes! Learn it!”

The book burst into light particles and floated into Megan’s head.

“G... got it.”

“What do you need to do to activate it?”

“Um... everyone needs to get near me... then I activate it.”

“GRAB LINDA AND EVERYONE GET CLOSE!” Judah shouted.

Brian teleported to Linda’s side and began to pick her up, but Linda scratched at him and struggled to look at the opening pool of water. So Brian took the pommel of his dagger and smacked her hard across the head, knocking her out.

He struggled over the moving roots as Judah and Megan ran toward him. Cassidy came swooping in to land beside them.

“Fireball!” Cassidy roared as a tree limb came shooting at them. “That pool is opening wider! Everyone keep your minds sharp!”

“Okay... there is a problem. I think that everyone who teleports with me has to be willing to go!” Megan explained hastily.

“Crap! Well, Linda isn’t going to accept whether or not she’s conscious,” Brian said, dropping the woman at his feet. “Start the process, and we will try to wake her up to agree to the teleportation!”

Megan nodded and reached her hands out to begin a chant.

“Father of the foundations, make us cohorts. The eternal distances are now to be short...”

“Linda! Linda! Wake up!” Brian said, smacking her in the face. Her eyes started to crack open.

“Take these who wish to flee from destiny unknown. Bring us to comfort in the place we call home... Do you willingly travel the planes?” Megan turned from her chanting and asked the group.

“I do,” Cassidy said shortly as she shot off another firebolt.

“I do,” Judah followed.

“Linda! Say ‘I do!’”

“I’ll never marry again! I’m a strong independent woman!” she responded groggily.

“Linda!”

Brian shouted again to get her attention, but the limbs grabbed her up.

“Crap! We have to go! I do!”

A magic circle flared underneath them, and light surrounded them just as they heard a fourth voice growl:

“I do.”

Brian looked up as a wolfish smile appeared out of thin air before they flashed away together.
