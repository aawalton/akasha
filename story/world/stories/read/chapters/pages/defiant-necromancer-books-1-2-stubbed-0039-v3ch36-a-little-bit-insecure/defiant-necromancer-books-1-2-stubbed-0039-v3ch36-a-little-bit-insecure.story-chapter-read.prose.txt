The High Elf King laughed politely.

The violet haze in the air faded a few seconds later, and Colin summoned one of his staff with a snap of his fingers.

These islands must be quite miserable for that to be sought after.

Without ever mentioning it. Not that I can think of many situations where it would have been useful now that she’s joined. I haven’t done much hiding lately. Fort Hadrian could have been made slightly safer with that power, but I deliberately left her protecting Victoria and Vidalia.

Lots of things, Tybalt thought. From the situation with people trying to assassinate the King to this matter of him wanting to tie your families together, to what happened to the last two necromancer high priests that Colin interacted with…
