Phatagin retrieved the Predator’s Beak from the box and put it on before following Charlemagne through the doorway. He emerged to find himself in a small room that overlooked a grassy field. Looking through a glass window set in the “front” of the room, he saw that he was surrounded by spectators seated on long benches that circled the field, with each bench slightly higher and slightly further back than the last. A few boxes that looked exactly like the one that he was in dotted the top of the bleachers. It appeared that the pangolin had emerged into a room that was reserved for those of some importance, since the space was not only large but luxuriously appointed. After a moment, Josephine and Phasima appeared as well, looking around at their new surroundings with interest.

“Well, looks like a good old-fashioned duel,” Phasima said as she finished getting her bearings. Then she hovered over to the front of the box before lowering herself onto a plush, comfortable-looking armchair in the front of the room. “Based on the strength that Charlemagne exhibited in our own, this should be a piece of cake, as long as he somehow doesn’t get a bad matchup.”

“How so,” Phatagin inquired as he used a small bit of stored momentum to leap into the chair next to her. It was comfy indeed.

“Well, as I said earlier, the System didn’t expect your boss to literally speedrun my scenario. You were supposed to perform tasks that weakened me, empowered yourselves, or acquired allies. You didn’t do any of…”

The former Empress broke off as the announcer began introducing the two fighters. Josephine, who had been admiring a painting, crossed over and sat down as well.

“I will break you!” Drago, the Delve Floor’s Boss yelled as the battle commenced. The trio watched as Charlemagne met the challenge head on, charging into combat with an acceleration that turned him into a whirlwind of feathers. His figure was further distorted by a haze of heat that roiled off his body, blackening the grass around him even as his powerful claws kicked up the turf with every stride. Drago dropped into a stance, both hands gripping his sword Phosphor with the ease that came from thousands of hours of practice. Rather than meeting the rooster’s charge with one of his own, he held his ground.

“He’s doing something to the area around him, isn’t he?” Josephine asked Phasima. “Like he’s establishing some sort of field that will give him an advantage…”

Phasima leaned forward, her humanoid appearance slipping as she activated some sort of Skill or Special Ability.

“Ah, yes, I see it now. It’s some sort of shadow domain. Very tricky to pull off when your primary weapon is fire-based, but there are some synergies that can work. Especially since it lets him use his weapon with two hands. I wouldn’t care to get hit by that sword…the sheer amount of power stored inside that thing is absurd.”

Charlemagne, either unaware or unconcerned, continued his charge. His martial arts style, which had never been his strongest Skill set, was nevertheless well-suited for fighting opponents that relied on a weapon. He used his tried and true method of covering his approach with well-placed mana shields, impeding Draco’s ability to swing his enchanted sword. A hint of a smile appeared on the draconic humanoid’s face as the rooster’s beak lashed out, aiming straight for Draco’s armored groin.

It missed.

“I am perplexed!” Phatagin cried. “How could he miss?”

“He used Charlemagne’s shadow to throw off his aim. It’s a pretty neat trick, I’ll give him that. Unless you’ve fought someone with shadow manipulation before, it’s very difficult to figure out what’s going on, much less counter it,” Phasima responded.

The pangolin winced as Draco threw his entire weight into a devastating counter-attack, smashing through Charlemagne’s defensive barrier with ease. Charlemagne was over-extended and unable to fully evade: Phosphor bit deep into his side as he rolled away. Undeterred by his initial failure, the rooster flowed into a stream of attacks, using both beak and claw in any attempt to overwhelm his opponent. But Draco’s blade seemed to have a mind of its own, endlessly moving into just the right place at the right time before blazing off again into position to parry the next blow. Despite the furious pace of the melee, neither champion gave an inch. Charlemagne attacked with increasing fury as he began to slowly take on additional wounds despite blocking the majority of his opponent’s blows with either his beak or deflecting them with a mana shield. Draco, however, remained almost entirely uninjured. He took more than a few close calls, however, and it was only a matter of time before one of the rooster’s attacks got lucky.

“Charlemagne is taking an awful lot of damage,” Josephine said, sounding genuinely concerned for the rooster’s plight as the fight went on. Both Phatagin and Phasima snorted at the same time.

“Getting hurt only makes him stronger. You can see that he’s still speeding up. Oh…he’s going to be so hungry after this!! What am I going to feed him?” the pangolin complained.

“Oh, does he have some sort of rage skill? Those take a lot out of you…or so I’ve heard,” Phasima added.

“Yes, his Class’s signature Skill makes him stronger as he gets hurt. What’s remarkable is that it increases his ability to heal, so he can fight through wounds that would cripple most creatures from my planet,” the pangolin confirmed.

“Yeah, mine too,” muttered the Attendant as Charlemagne deflected a particularly nasty strike at the last moment to avoid being bisected.

“At any rate,” the Vessarian said after a few moments, “the sword doesn’t seem to be doing as much damage as you might think. I think that Charlemagne is actually healing from the extreme heat it’s giving off. The real question I have is whether or not he’ll run out of energy before Draco does. Manipulating shadows constantly has got to be draining him pretty quickly. He’s lucky that the sword doesn’t require charging up as well.”

The trio fell silent after that, too engrossed in the battle to say anything further. The crowd, however, was only getting louder. Everyone was up on their feet, or what passed for feet, shouting, cheering, stomping, and generally having a great time. Cries of support for both fighters rang out almost equally, with a few particularly ardent supporters nearly coming to blows over the issue. The fight had reached a wonderful stalemate, allowing Charlemagne and Draco to showcase their power and skill to the fullest.

It couldn’t last forever. Eventually, something would tip, and one of the fighters would fall.

“He can’t keep that up much longer,” Josephine muttered as Charlemagne took a blow to the shoulder that almost took his wing clean off.

“Yeah,” Phasima agreed. “Soon there won’t be any shadows left for him to manipulate.”

“I was talking about Ser Charlemagne,” the Attendant clarified. “How much longer can his body keep going after taking so many hits?”

“Charlemagne seems to be fine,” Phatagin broke in. “In fact…something is happening…”

The pangolin stared with all his might down at the pitch, watching the two figures locked in combat. Using his Momentum Core in a novel way, he absorbed the vibrations from the air around him, silencing the noise from the crowd. Pushing his nearsighted eyes to their limits, he focused only on what he could see. Something inside him stirred. It was faint, but it was definitely there. He had felt this particular sensation only once before, but it had fled before he could grab hold of it. Phatagin was determined not to let this opportunity pass him by.

System, could I please join Ser Charlemagne down there, he inquired.

The System responded instantly.

Query detected: Am I allowed to attend to my Knight?

Answer available: Your Knight has chosen to keep you safe during this event. You may not place yourself in harm’s way.

Then let me stand just outside the force field, the pangolin begged.

Query detected: May I get closer to the battle if I remain safe?

Answer available: Yes, you may.

Without bothering to explain what he was about to do, Phatagin jumped out of his chair and, using his new helmet for the first time, broke through the window that separated him from the crowd. Sound returned to the world as he gave up on absorbing the slight vibrations all around him. Shouts and screams followed the sound of breaking glass, but the pangolin ignored them. He was close to a breakthrough.

Moving at top speed, the pangolin landed on the edge of the pitch. His momentum carried him a step too far, and his helmet bounced off the impenetrable shield that was protecting the crowd from mishap. Had he not done so, Phatagin might have never guessed that the shield was even in place: it was invisible, odorless, silent, and otherwise invisible to all his senses. However, once he knew exactly where it was located, he could easily see how the momentum of the nearby air molecules was affected by the impermeable barrier. He looked up and breathed a sigh of relief. He was close enough now.

Slowly at first, a haze began to form around the two fighters as they moved with furious grace and frightening speed. The haze above Charlemagne was larger, while the one above Draco seemed to be composed of two different emanations. What was even more confusing was that part of Draco’s shroud seemed to be surrounding Charlemagne, moving ever so slightly every time the rooster attacked.

All of a sudden, Phatagin understood what he was seeing. This was mana! He could finally see it…and it was beautiful. All of a sudden, the System chimed.

Skills Added:

Mana Sense 3

Seeing the notification, Phatagin closed his eyes and found, to his delight, that he could still see the forms of the two combatants. Their Cores glowed with incredible power, two miniature stars that battled for dominance in a great void. Draco’s sword Phospor also shone to the pangolin’s newfound sense, its store of fire-aspected mana blazing almost as bright as Draco himself. Phatagin kept his eyes firmly shut and experienced the world entirely through his other senses. Despite the force field, he could sense every movement Charlemagne and Draco made with his Momentum Core. He could see the tension in their muscles, the rush of limbs, and the shockwaves that followed every blow. His magical senses caught the flow of power thrumming throughout the fighters’ bodies, the fiery mana that roiled off Charlemagne and Phosper, the shields of force that stopped or deflected devastating sword strikes, and the thin wispy ropes of shadow that did their best to impede the rooster.

He could see it all!

“Just die already!” Draco screamed as he brought his sword above his head. Phatagin watched in fascination as mana flowed into the Knight’s beefy arms and stimulated the muscle fibers, allowing them to contract with far greater force than mere biology could muster. Phospor streaked down like a meteor, slicing through the air so quickly that it broke the sound barrier several times over. But fighting meteors was something that Charlemagne had experience with. Instead of dodging or blocking, the rooster braced himself. Mana surged into his head, reinforcing not just his beak but his entire skull. Then Charlemagne leaned in and caught the blow with his top hat.

Why did he…ohhhh! the pangolin realized, having forgotten that Charlemagne’s top hat was, in fact, indestructible.

The sword struck with a resounding clang and bounced back, the shock reverberating up Draco’s arms and causing him to freeze up for the barest moment.

That was all it took.

Charlemagne, having evidently prepared for this moment, made his move. Despite having been driven deep into the ground by the sheer power behind the blow, he immediately leapt up, catching Draco’s sword in his open mouth.

The dragon-man screamed something incomprehensible as a huge burst of mana overwhelmed Phatagin’s mana sense. The world suddenly went completely white, sending a burst of pain straight into the pangolin’s brain. With a cry, he fell to the ground, but the indescribable agony passed almost as quickly as it had arrived.

Although the pain disappeared, Phatagin’s Mana Sense Skill was completely disabled. As soon as he recovered enough sense to open his eyes, the pangolin did so…and gasped.
