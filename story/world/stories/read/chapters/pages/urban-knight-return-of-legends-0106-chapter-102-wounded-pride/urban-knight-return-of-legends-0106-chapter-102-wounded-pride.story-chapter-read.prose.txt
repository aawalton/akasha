The air grew thick with tension, the oppressive aura of the stranger clinging onto their bodies like damp, cold clothes. Ascalon clenched his fists, his demeanor shifting into battle mode as he recognized the person before them as their enemy—one of the wicked villains who dared ‘play’ as the cardinal sins.

While everybody except the knight was kneeling against their will—with Moonstone being slightly protected by Ascalon’s presence—Lyra was, also, trembling in pure fear.

At the mention of Wrath, memories of Starugnia had flooded in, the happy time she had been enjoying being replaced by a dreadful sensation. Not only had they come across another of these vile beings, but it had to be on the very place that Ian’s betrayal had taken place.

Sweat fell from her forehead in huge droplets, both due to her exhaustion ramping up as she struggled against the unknown force, and because of the agitation building up as the dark feelings clouded her mind.

Lilithra, unable to move, cursed her weakness as she side-glanced her friend. Lyra’s distress was more than evident, and it crushed her heart. Once again, the only one standing tall in the face of evil was Ascalon, the pure, radiant knight… yet she couldn’t help but feel it wasn’t fair, jealousy creeping its way into her thoughts.

Selorien, on the other hand, was simply annoyed and alerted. Greed had already been a pain in the butt to fight, and now they were face to face with someone whose presence was enough to render them unable to move—it was extremely worrying, to say the least.

As his friends dwelled on different matters in their minds, Ascalon stepped forward. His frame stood before the three of them, letting them rest their eyes on his broad, reliable back—a beacon of reassurance.

Under the heavy pressure, Ascalon shifted Moonstone to his right hand, while his trusty shield appeared from his inventory on his left. He took a deep breath, then struck with all his might in a swift, sweeping motion. The abrupt, thunderous blow bashed against the air, sending a powerful shockwave in the unknown woman’s direction, causing her to flinch in surprise.

As she raised her arms to block Ascalon’s attacks with a cross-guard, the rest of the group could feel the intense pressure easing up, letting them catch their breath for a moment. Meanwhile, the shockwave hit the woman squarely, her boots dragging across the rocky plateau as she withstood the blow without faltering.

Then, with a sudden, fluid motion, she broke her guard by flinging her arms outward in a wide, sweeping arc. The air around her erupted in a fierce blast, reverberating across the area as it dissipated the remnants of Ascalon’s attack with ease.

“Whew. Not bad, for some nobody.” She spoke in a mocking manner, brushing dust off her cloak with a casual pat. She looked pensive for a moment, her hand grabbing onto her cloak.

“Bah, why should I bother with appearances now, huh?” She added as she seized the collar of her hooded robe. With a quick, violent motion, she tore it away and casted it to the side, finally revealing her form to them.

She looked a bit older than Lilithra, yet had around the same height—but her most distinctive features were the reddish skin, and her toned, well-defined body. She had short, black hair, and deep, yellow eyes partly hidden by an angry frown.

A single, small red horn protruded from her head, adding to her already hostile appearance. Her attire was simple but practical, letting a glimpse into her no-nonsense personality: black shorts, a white top to cover her chest, and a pair of thick, brown boots.

Letting out a relieved sigh, she stretched her arms high and tilted her head from one side to the other. The sound of her bones cracking echoed in the air as she released the tension from her body, a bold, half-smile adorning her lips.

Ascalon immediately recognized the features of a race he had tangled with many times in the past, as most of their kin had joined the demons’ side: Ogres—some of the most fearsome, ruthless warriors he had ever faced. It was surprising to see one so short, yet Ascalon knew that her size had little to do with her strength.

“Ah… ah… R—Ravanna?!” Lyra suddenly spoke up, her eyes opening wide and focusing sharply on the stranger. Her nervous, timid voice hid a hint of admiration, causing the rest to look at her with curiosity. Then, a flash of realization crossed both Selorien and Lilithra’s faces.

“Ah… busted already, huh? No matter.” The reddish woman clicked her tongue, her foot tapping the floor impatiently. Ascalon arched an eyebrow in confusion, his tension easing up a bit as he wondered whether his friends had recognized her.

“W—what?! The real Ravanna?!” Lilithra chimed in with clear surprise in her voice, which only heightened Ascalon’s curiosity. Seemingly sensing his thoughts, Selorien spoke up.

“She’s kinda well-known, a member of Chariot’s Flight. Uh, that’s a Guild, by the way.” The elf revealed, letting Ascalon finally understand. This Ravanna girl was a public figure of sorts, yet she seemed to be in cahoots with the wicked Wrath—but to what end?

“Yeah, yeah. I won’t sign you any autographs.” Ravanna dismissed the conversation with a wave of her hand, her tone full of sarcasm as she rolled her eyes.

“Why… why would someone like you be involved with him?!” Lilithra questioned in an anguished voice, disbelief and doubt clear in her visage, her fists tightly clenched around her halberd.

“Why?” Ravanna began, her frown easing up a little. “For you, of course…” She bit her lips, smiling. “It’s all for you, Lilithra…” Ravanna revealed cryptically, just like Wrath and Greed had done before her. Strangely, her tone was tender, almost lovingly, a stark contrast to her earlier, violent demeanor.

Ascalon gripped his shield tighter, feeling a profound sense of discomfort. Once again, that vile group talked about Lilithra as if she were a crucial part of their evil machinations—and he didn’t like that in the slightest.

“All of you…” Lilithra muttered, her voice almost inaudible. “All of you acting like you know me… it makes me sick!” She exploded in a fit of rage, her entire body trembling, her thunderous voice making even Ascalon flinch in surprise.

“Talking about some weird plan you have for me…! Acting so familiar when I don’t know who the hell any of you are…! Why?! why?!” Lilithra’s voice grew in irritation, demanding answers for things she had no idea about.

“Why…? Because you’re the key, Lilithra!” Ravanna answered enthusiastically, her palms open toward Lilithra. “Just come with me and I’ll explain everything.” She suggested with a proud smile, her hazy gaze causing Lilithra to shiver.

Ascalon stepped forward, shielding Lilithra from Ravanna, his posture unwavering as he locked eyes with her. Ravanna’s whole demeanor shifted dramatically, right back into her hostile, angered self. But before Ravanna could say anything, Lilithra gritted her teeth and pushed Ascalon to the side, confusing everyone.

“I don’t need to be protected every single time, Ascalon!” She reproached, her words coated with uncharacteristic sharpness, urged by an unreasonable sense of pride. Even Lilithra felt it was weird to react like that, as she was usually glad to see the gallant knight care for her. But somehow, something gnawed at her at the back of her mind.

“I see, I am sorry.” Ascalon apologized, nodding respectfully toward her. His response was simply in answer to her wishes, yet Lilithra’s heart ached at the sight. She extended her hand toward him, wanting to apologize too—but Ravanna’s voice cut through the air, startling everyone.

“You… you’re getting in the way in more ways than one… you annoying wanderer…!” Ravanna hissed with clear anger, a vein visibly bulging up in her forehead. The earlier, overpowering pressure returned, albeit more focused onto Ascalon rather than everyone else.

The knight felt his shoulders droop slightly, the force pushing down on him feeling way stronger than before—yet it was not enough to bend him nor his will. Fearing the force would harm the cub, Ascalon bent down and placed Moonstone on the ground, and the pup quickly fled toward his friends.

Relieved, he stood up again, and met Ravanna’s irritated gaze head-on as Exemplaris materialized in his right hand. The silver lance gleamed as he pointed it toward Ravanna, who let out a mocking chuckle at the sight.

“Fine! This is what I came here for, anyway!” Ravanna growled, her fists rising into a fighting stance once more. “Just sit tight and wait for me, Lilithra.” She added, flashing a smile toward Lilithra—though she was met with relentless opposition.

“Stop it!” Lilithra snapped. “Stop using my name so casually! Who the hell do you think you are?!” She shouted with fierce intensity, her friends being completely caught off-guard by her reaction. At the sight, Ravanna let out a deep sigh, her eyes narrowing as she sized up Lilithra.

“Crap, she’s being affected by my pride… although…” She began to ponder, smirking.

Ascalon latched onto those words immediately, his mind racing as he recalled similar events back at Starugnia, where something similar had happened to Selorien.

“How did I not notice…?” Ascalon whispered to himself, realization setting in as he glanced over at Lilithra.

Ascalon had never faced a true sin back in Excadia, for they had already abandoned the world long before he could’ve had a chance to witness their influence. Yet the remnants of their power continued to affect certain parts of the world.

Gluttony, envy, greed, pride… certain people would be influenced by a sin more than others, even large groups of them could be forced to act unreasonably by being exposed to the lingering, subtle energy of the powerful sins.

Could these people, perhaps, be exerting some sort of influence over their surroundings, too? Simply by embodying a cardinal sin? The thought was both worrying and terrifying, as Ascalon had witnessed first-hand how deranged Wrath and his comrades were. It also reinforced the idea that they weren’t merely playing around—somehow, they were tapping into power beyond their knowledge.

Just as Ascalon braced himself for a fight, fueled by righteousness and the urge to vanquish the evil before him, a familiar speaker chimed in from Ravanna’s position—a coarse tone dripping with malice.

“Even though I knew you would act on your own, I’m still disappointed, Pride…” Wrath’s distinctive voice echoed in the plateau, the air growing thicker with tension.

“You even went and revealed your appearance to them… this complicates things, as you’re fully aware, yes?” Wrath asked sarcastically, as Ravanna—or Pride—furrowed her brows in anger, producing from thin air a red mask with the motif of a wolf.

“Shut the fuck up, Wrath!” She lashed out to the mask, her voice brimming with irritation. Everyone else watched the exchange in silence, while Ascalon focused on the accessory on Pride’s hands. It reminded him of Greed’s, which was still in his inventory.

“I warned you, Pride… Please, reconsider.” Wrath pleaded in a firm yet slightly worried tone, his voice echoing faintly through the mask. Ravanna clicked her tongue and looked away, locking eyes with Ascalon, unwilling to utter another word.

A sigh escaped the wolf accessory, and suddenly, a dark, dense fog began to emanate from it.

“Wait, what are you doing?!” Pride demanded as the fog moved with an eerie will of its own, slithering through the air and moving past her. For a moment, Ascalon had thought it was an attack meant for them, yet the direction made its real target clear.

The gryphon, still struggling against the invisible force pinning him down, screeched in pain. The dark cloud enveloped it, the sound of crushing bones and tearing flesh echoing grotesquely across the plateau. After a brief moment, a notification appeared before everyone.

“You…!” Pride muttered in pure anger, her whole frame trembling.

“I know you will not back down, so at the very least, use these two hours to learn your place… just don’t go dying on me, Pride. Your seat is the one that would be hardest to replace.” Wrath spoke with a calm, detached tone, clearly done with the conversation, his presence vanishing from the mask.

“You really think I will lose!?” Ravanna growled, agitated, her pride more than wounded. Yet the mask remained silent, causing her to grab onto it strongly, as if wanting to crush it.

“Fine… I’ll make it quick!” Pride scoffed, her intense aura spreading in all directions, like a wave of unbridled defiance. As her yellow eyes met Ascalon once again, she vanished the mask back into her inventory.

“And I won’t even need to use this.” She declared with utmost confidence.

But would she be able to back her words?
