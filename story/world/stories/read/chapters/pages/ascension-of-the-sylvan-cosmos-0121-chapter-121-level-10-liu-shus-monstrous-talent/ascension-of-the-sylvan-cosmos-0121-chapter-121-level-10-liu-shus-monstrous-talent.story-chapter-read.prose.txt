The first thing Liu Shu did was to destroy the small energy circuit she created as a test. The white lights that she filled disintegrated, and this made her feel the loss of energy affinity she gained.

Using her observation capability, her mind quickly processed the available pathways all across her body, root system, and everything linked to herself. Her focus on the outward world was completely cut off.

After she had completely analyzed everything from the canopy to the tip of her roots, Liu Shu realized how special the three branch accessories she had were. The <Sylvanium Branch>, <Cognium Branch>, and <Celestium Branch> had many empty pathways, a lot of which she was still not precise enough to thread through.

When creating her energy circuits through the <Energy Cycle> process, her accuracy was limited. Even if there were smaller pathways that she could distinguish, when she tried creating a path through them, the circuits were too thick and rough to pass through them, making it impossible.

Liu Shu guessed that these were paths for future Energy Cycles to use.

What was curious is that her ability to perceive energy increased when she completed more of her Energy Cycle. While she already had the ability to see the energy flow because of her nature as a tree, which often didn't rely on optical vision but energy vision, now it felt as if that was enhanced. From seeing rough shapes and clouds of energy, she could now distinguish everything much better.

Liu Shu painted her incredibly complex <Energy Cycle> over everything, and when she finished and connected the last opened circle, she felt an incredible rush of energy and strength.

[<World Tree Wisdom (SSS Rank Level MAX)> activates! <Energy Cycle> Completion rate is 76%. It can be significantly optimized. Do you want to activate the assistance?]

As the skill counted more of the mistakes she committed, parts of the map that were filled with white lines were now filled with red to pinpoint the location of the mistakes.

Liu Shu looked in stunned silence as the skill told her everywhere she made mistakes. While the completion rate was 76%, the red color was all over the energy cycle. It felt that the percentage didn't follow a linear increase but an exponential one. 'W-Where does my 76% completion rate come from!?'

[<World Tree Wisdom>: World Tree Wisdom is too high-ranked of a skill. Tianlian Liu Shu can't bring out the true value of the skill until she gains enough strength.]

'How much of your utility am I using?'

[<World Tree Wisdom>: Unquantifiable. Like taking sips from an ocean.'

'Anyways, let's focus on redoing the <Energy Cycle>.'

On her first try, Liu Shu tried to start from the outside of her body to the center. Now, she would do the opposite. Moreover, with the errors pointed at by the skill, Liu Shu felt much more confident.

Starting from her core, Liu Shu extended the lines of light through half of the paths available to expand outward. Then, cautiously, she continued painting the lines of light across everything.

Once she reached the canopy, she made sure to follow the advice and first went to the top, creating a thick middle energy path and then branching it out. Previously, she had tried to create a circular path for every branch, but that was clearly inefficient.

Connecting her bark and trunk was another tricky point. Her bark was her skin, so she needed to create paths along the meeting points of the trunk and the bark.

Previously, she had created energy paths even inside the bark, and that was clearly not efficient because the bark was a protection organ that needed to focus on, well, protecting. Being damaged was easy, so creating energy paths through it would eventually deform them. Exceptions to this rule naturally existed.

Her next major mistake was how she connected her town and other structures to herself. 'Hm… Should I not close the cycles inside the buildings and instead connect them with my root system as if they were not individual trees but part of a bigger "myself"?'

Working on big and small problems, time flew by, and eventually, she finished everything.

The second she finished, her mind felt as if it was expanding as the energy purity in her body transformed. 'Ah… Such a difference… I can feel it now.'

Her leaves felt as if they could breathe individually, her vision as if it could see through everything, and her thoughts were clearer than ever. The slight immaturity of her thoughts significantly diminished…
