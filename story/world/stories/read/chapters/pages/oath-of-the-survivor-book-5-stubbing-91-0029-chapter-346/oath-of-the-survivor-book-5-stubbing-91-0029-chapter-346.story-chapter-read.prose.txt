Phaegos wasn’t entirely sure when he started to gain a broader understanding of the world around him.  It might have started when Kyle attained the Core of the Parasitic Devourer after defeating the Storm.  Potentially earlier, at the skill selection for Parasitic Resonance; or even during Kyle’s evolution to D Grade, and choice to embrace the way of the Parasite.  Perhaps pieces of his consciousness had come into being the moment that Kyle had first begun to absorb the ambient power of the Forebear into himself, moments after the cataclysm that destroyed the human’s home.

Through their years together, Phaegos was only marginally aware of the world around them.  All emotion was expressed through hunger; everything through Kyle’s eyes viewed as little more than potential prey.  And, for all of his faults, Kyle fed him well.  Every step along the journey forced Kyle to lean on him more and more, with both of them steadily growing in strength.  Each life-or-death encounter led to opportunities as Phaegos became Kyle’s weapon of choice to dispose of his enemies.  All building toward the moment when Phaegos would emerge, taking its place as the Core of the Parasitic Devourer fully awakened.

And then, Kyle’s Adaptive Anatomy developed the Pack Leader’s Instinct.  Phageos understood the moment that it locked into place that something was fundamentally shifting in their relationship, even if he didn’t have the full cognition to articulate it.  Part of being a Parasitic Devourer meant being undetected, slowly whittling away the host from the inside.  That was no longer a possibility.  He was known.  Seen.  Understood.  Worse, he could feel the inherent superiority from Kyle’s role as the Pack Leader.  Just as the weaker shrikes were subservient to the matriarch, Phaegos was subservient to Kyle.  Their bond wouldn’t allow it any other way.

The dynamic changed further when the Heart of Creation awoke, infusing concepts that Phaegos didn’t fully understand even now.  Slowly, methodically, the constant exposure to Kyle, C.H.A.D.D., and the myriad concepts evoked through Heart of Creation shaped him, molded his thinking into something altogether different.  While Phaegos was still an offshoot of the Forebear’s power, he’d also forged a deep connection with both the drone and human.

That bond was exposed when Kyle nearly died, and C.H.A.D.D. spurred him into action to save Kyle’s life; pushing him harder than ever before.  Under ordinary circumstances, what does a parasite care if its host dies?  Even if that means its own destruction, the parasite’s job is not to preserve, but to usurp, to consume.  And yet, at the drone’s instruction, Phaegos stepped into action, forcibly activating skills in a way that he never knew he could.

And, after his full awakening, he’d been largely used to preserve, shape, and create.  He’d only really been allowed to cut loose against the Scourge.  Despite its bountiful mana, it hadn’t really been a terribly exciting meal.  While much of the energy drained seemed to be helpful for the World Tree Seed and the drone, Phaegos hadn’t found the experience to be particularly stimulating.

No, when he thought back on the limited memories and experiences from before he’d awakened, the one that stuck out the most vividly was the moment Kyle had been pierced through by Gregory Valentine.  There, at the border of life and death, Phaegos felt something resonate within himself, though he hadn’t been able to fully lay hold of it.  Now, siphoning away this strange mana, Phaegos was finally beginning to understand.  There was something about the concept of Death that resonated deeply with him, something that made him want to consume more.

The more Phaegos immersed himself in the concept, the more he felt something stir; a missing piece of himself that he hadn’t realized was gone.  Kyle had leveraged the Forebear’s power in many different ways through the years, but he’d struggled to bring out its full potential.  During his fights with the abominations on Er’Mithren, it had been Phaegos’ unconscious reflexes that had finally allowed him to condense and break through their absurd regeneration.  It’s not that Kyle didn’t have the skill; he simply didn’t resonate with the concept.

That wasn’t a problem Phaegos shared.  Echoes of instinctive knowledge began to take shape as Phaegos immersed itself in the Death-attuned mana, finally taking in how it fit into the Forebear’s path.  The Progenitor’s design was one of growth through death; sacrificing the weak that the strong would struggle and rise to the top.  From the deaths of workers defending a colony to the death of a mighty beast becoming fuel for the next generation, conceptual Death was little more than fuel along the road paved by the Forebear.

From Death, comes Life.

Nowhere was that truth more fundamental than in the existence of the Parasitic Devourer.  It consumed, killing so that it could strengthen and grow.  As it did, a portion of each creature consumed was carried along, their deaths sustaining and empowering the Devourer to their next challenge.

The truth was profound to Phaegos; intoxicating in its promise.  Unfortunately, that excitement led to a lapse in control.  The mana which Phaegos had been systematically absorbing and deconstructing built up far more than he’d intended, and a wave of Death-infused energy was unleashed inside Kyle’s Center.

Oops.

Immediately, Phaegos felt the energy attack Kyle from the inside out.  The highly-condensed pocket of energy ravaged Kyle’s organs and bloodstream while the moment of surprise left him vulnerable to the power outside trying to tear into him.  Flesh started to darken as necrotic energy seeped through the mana network, finding stubborn resistance as Kyle continually activated Healing Impetus to reinvigorate the damaged tissue.

A moment later, Phaegos felt golden power flood through the mana network as well, adding to the defense as power was pulled from the recesses of the World Tree Seed to join with Kyle’s own.  The bolstering effects from Genesis Engine further helped to arrest the runaway damage, though by now the worst of the damage had been done.

Phaegos felt C.H.A.D.D.’s fury through Pack Leader’s Instinct, and understood that the decaying power had managed to seep into the drone’s personal garden, causing some sizeable setbacks to C.H.A.D.D.’s various projects.  An unfortunate bit of collateral damage, and one that Phaegos knew he’d not hear the end of for a good long while.  The drone was nothing if not whiny when it came to its plants after all.

He tried not to let too much satisfaction leak through their bond as a result.

By now, Kyle was suppressing the most prominent effects of the death-attuned energy.  Revitalized cells and surges of golden power condensed the foreign energy into small pockets, allowing his body to heal the rampant damage while systematically purging the damaging concepts.  Not wanting to miss an opportunity, Phaegos refocused on the motes of power being pushed through Kyle’s mana network, greedily devouring as many as he could.  After all, there was no better way to understand the concept than through immersion.

As he gobbled up the last morsels, he felt a faint sense of connection to the Forebear, a mix of approval and amusement leaking through.  The feelings left an odd sensation in Phaegos’ mind, one he reflected on as he digested the last bits of the concept he could.  While the drone may have lost something in the exchange, Phaegos certainly hadn’t.  Now, it was up to Kyle to make the right choices to allow these seeds to bear fruit.

~~~

As the pain wracking his body started to ease, Kyle took a deep breath and let his shoulder slump.  The unexpected wave of death from inside his Center had taken a lot more out of him than he’d feared it might, requiring a full activation of Genesis Engine to ward off the worst of the effects.  Four more sets of internal organs were rotted away by the intense energy, and C.H.A.D.D. even lost a portion of its space within the World Tree Seed, which was made vulnerable during one of the organ transfers.

Pockets of rot were being expelled, with Phaegos trying to eat everything it could before the last bits of Death-attuned energies were finally gone from within Kyle’s body.  The whole experience had been a harrowing one, and Kyle felt the telltale surge of power from a couple more levels from surviving the bomb of energy being detonated within his Center.  He mentally probed Phaegos to see how the spirit was doing, but didn’t get a direct response, just a vague sense of frustration.  He was concerned, up until he realized that the spirit was reflecting on the concept of Death, wrestling with some of its aspects.

That didn’t leave Kyle feeling great.  Phaegos was very honest that it was designed to drive Kyle’s evolutions in a different direction, and the idea that it was finding ways to attune itself to an aspect antithetical to his own wasn’t comforting.  Still, he didn’t feel any malice coming from his Center.  In fact, he felt the opposite.  It was almost as though Phaegos was trying to make a gift for him, and was deep in the creative process.  An odd feeling, but not altogether unpleasant.

C.H.A.D.D.’s feelings, by contrast were much easier to understand.

[GLUTTON, YOU HAD ONE JOB.  ONE JOB!  AND YOU DID NOT DO IT.  YOU NEARLY KILLED DR. MAYHEW.  AND MY GARDEN!  DO YOU HAVE ANY IDEA HOW LONG IT WILL TAKE TO RECOVER IT?  DR. MAYHEW WILL NEED TO GIVE ME TWENTY THREE PERCENT MORE MANA FOR DAYS, AT LEAST!  THIS WAS THE MOST IRRESPONSIBLE…]

The drone’s rant continued, largely ignored by the Parasitic Devourer as it continued its work, only sending a halfhearted and altogether-too-cheery apology after a minute or so.

For his part, Kyle turned his attention back to his own recovery.  The activation of Genesis Engine did put him on a bit of a timer, particularly as the mental strain of infusing the concept of Immutability into his skills was growing unsustainable.  He was about ready to call it done and head back into the shuttle, when his nav bracelet confirmed that the core inside the probe was almost fully saturated.

Quickly walking back to the metal probe, Kyle waited until the exact moment it flashed that the task was complete, then sealed the item away.  Only when he was back on the shuttle did he deactivate Genesis Engine, tension leaving his body as he slumped into the nearby chair.  Too tired to even meditate properly, Kyle closed his eyes and allowed himself to drift off to sleep.  Turning in the probe would be a quick process, and then it was time to meet the Collective’s investigator.  But for now, it was time for a nap.
