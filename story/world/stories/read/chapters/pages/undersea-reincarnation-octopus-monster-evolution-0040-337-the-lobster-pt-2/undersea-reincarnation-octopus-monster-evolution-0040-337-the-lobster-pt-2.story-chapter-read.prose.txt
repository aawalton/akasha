The electrical bomb scoured Henry, blinding his senses, shocking his flesh until he lost control of his limbs, and burned into his soul. Henry had experienced the former two plenty of times, but the last one was new. It was like fire ants were eating and burrowing into his soul, causing spasms and flashes of pain enough to have him scream, if he actually had control over his body.

The whole experience was extremely unpleasant, and for a second, Henry knew nothing but pain. Not even his Octominds could be heard from the all-consuming shock, but a moment later, the world came back into his consciousness as his green Octomind pumped him full with Life Surge.

That gave him enough clarity to realize that the electric bomb wasn’t the only thing he had suffered from.

Looking down his arm, Henry immediately saw the issue. Well, it was mixed bag, really. The large, teeth-capped limb he’d whipped down had pierced through the lobster’s back. Henry didn’t recall if he’d aimed for the joint, if it had been luck, or if the teeth had just skidded and got caught by one of the edges, but the result was that the joint between the plats was easier to break through, and it meant that Henry had stabbed pretty deeply into the lobster’s flesh, which was now conducting an incredible amount of current up his arm and into his body.

It still wasn’t as bad as the bomb, though.

Tearing his arm out, Henry blinked away, his free Octominds joining in with the green one to flush out the foreign essence out of his body. His limbs were still violently jerking about, and the sight of his main body was still wracked with alternating bright and dark spots. He could even feel his hearts stuttering and skipping beats, but a regular flow of healing helped calm those down.

He might have taken some damage to his nervous system. But nothing some healing couldn’t fix.

Still, it had been incredibly painful, but Henry had gotten the lobster as well. Blue blood flowed freely out of the lobster’s back, and the sight reminded Henry of clouds and storms. The billowing blood was crackling with electricity, but Henry couldn’t admire the phenomenon for long. Anytime now, the lobster would come back at him, and he’d rather be done flushing out the foreign influence before he had it interfere with his body.

Not willing to stay in place, Henry circled the crustacean, temporarily pulling the attention of the Octominds handling the looting and exploring to help him purge the foreign influence faster. He didn’t disturb the one working on integrating his Skill, but thankfully, it was already nearly done, so the electric bomb hadn’t caused too much damage.

That had been nasty, he thought as his own Lifeweaving essence wrestled and pushed the foreign essence out. Life Surge could clean out foreign essence, but the difference in rank made the process tedious. It was like trying to scrub grease while only using water. But he was making progress. The violent jitters eased, making his movement smoother, and as he watched the lobster furiously click its claws as its sight tracked him, Henry readied himself for what was coming. He’d just dodge away until he felt ready. The lobster’s body suddenly glowed as its lowered itself into the sand, and Henry tensed.

Right then and there, he sensed Toxic Ray settle into his soul, and a pulse spread out as it spread its roots.

The lobster kicked off. Henry saw the sand bloom, and almost instantaneously, his sight was obstructed as the A-rank appeared in front of him, open claw crackling toward his eyes. Having been ready, Henry ducked right in time to save his brains and stomach from being pulped. The two smaller of the crab arm zipped forward, trying to get a grip on him, but they snapped down on nothing.

Henry had already teleported away.

Having detected the flare of magic, the lobster’s body shone again, looking up as its tail tensed to propel it at Henry, only to be blasted by a green sickly ray right in its mouth.

Henry immediately grimaced.

It really wasn’t powerful enough, he realized while the lobster shook its head, irritated. Readying another blast, Henry narrowed his attention on the skill and sensed how it stubbornly refused to channel the venoms he wanted it to carry. He had suspected that it wouldn’t use his own venoms, but now that he felt how the two skills were completely independent, it miffed him. He had more at his disposal than the simple toxin of the ray. He wanted to be able to dictate what kind of venom it used. He wanted to use his own cocktails.

This won’t be resolved right now, he thought. He’d have to do some work to fuse the skills and get them to work in concert. But it wasn’t about to happen now. Which meant that Henry would need to get down there and bite himself, or use some the Octominds’ projection to slip in a bite or two.

The lobster rushed after him once more, but Henry just switched places with another clone and had the one who took his place detonate to give him time. Channeling some of his Predation into the still red Octomind, he sent it forward, aiming to get a Bite in where the shell had been pierced, but he had to keep the monster busy. And Henry didn’t want to waste the projections of his Octominds. He also didn’t want to get down there himself. Sticking to the A-rank while it was still this lively was an awful prospect.

Fortunately for him, he didn’t have to. Because the lobster did not care for his Octominds and it was still focused on him.

“Go invisible, and wait for my signal,” Henry ordered the red Octomind while he dodged another bum-rush and as he did, he pulled from Predation and Thaumaturgy, channeling them to his beak, which now glowed with an ominous silvery blue. Henry angled his beak up, and the empowered ray blasted out, a wide burning pillar of luminescent gray that instantly engulfing the lobster. Just looking at the magic was making Henry’s eyes burn, and that only got worse when he was forced to dodge another charge that put him in the ray’s run off.

But it was worth it. Eyeing the monster, Henry noted how the once pristine shell was discolored by pale blotches. Henry’s own skin and gills burned, but he was pretty resilient to toxins, much less his own. But while the attack had some damage, it still wasn’t enough.

The most damage I’ve done was with the stab. The ray does some… but I’m eating into my mana and I’m not recovering enough from the surrounding water, he calculated. If he could latch on, he could steal much more, but he believed that even if he empowered his defensive Skills with all of his Aspects, he’d still get zapped pretty hard.

Or…

Henry hummed, eying the large limb, then his other free arms as new idea floated in the back of his mind. Could it work? He did posses the ability to mimic matter, which he had gotten from Rocky. And with Matter Mimicry now part of Shapeshift, his idea could work.

Oh what the hell. It’s worth a short, he thought as he activated the Shapeshift, switching places with another poor clone that got pulverized before it could detonate, while his skill began altering his skin. In seconds, a rigid, interlocked raised pattern spread up his large limb, before it bloomed and materialized all over his skin. It traveled around his mantle, and Henry made sure to surround his innards with another layer. It was uncomfortable when he moved, but it was better than let his brain and hearts get fried. Then he made sure all off non-tooth capped arms were now capped with the same substance and in the glowing, crackling light emanating out of the lobster, the pattern looked like a metallic cage.

If this doesn’t work, then I might pull all of my clones and… skedaddle…

Henry trailed off, his attention suddenly pulled by the clones and the two Octominds he had tasked with looting. In the brief moment where he checked in on them, Henry got some tidbits about what that Octomind had recovered, and he was almost tempted to bailing right then and there. To go back to Thalis and tell everyone what he’d found. But as his eyes fell on the lobster, he pushed the thought away.

No, not yet, he told himself as he took one more look down at the metallic sheen between his stabbing limb and his remaining arms. It will most likely burn like hell, and it will still leak into his body. But if this worked as I hoped, then he’d last longer in the lobster’s proximity. Which then he’d capitalize on.

With a beat of its tail and as lightning crackled upon its shell, the lobster shot up at him like a bullet, only to disappear in a cloud of disorienting ink as Henry took a clone’s spot near the seabed.

With a deep breath, and as he ignored the tiny voice in his head that told him this was stupid, Henry drove all but the fused limbs into the sandy seabed. As for the remaining arm, with an activation of Lifeweaving and Dancing Shadows, he turned it fully invisible and looked up as the lobster crackled within the cloud of dissipating ink.

“[Come on. I know you got more in you,]” Henry called up, meeting the lobster’s eyes while his hearts hammered in their cage. The lobster coiled, ready for another charge, supremely confident in its defenses and in the punch it packed. It had every right to. Henry knew that if it hadn’t been for his high Constitution and for the essence he’d been feeding Trickster’s Fortitude, he would have been out of the fight ever since the first exchange.

So Henry braced and channeled even more Lifeweaving into his defensive ability while raising the long, leviathan-teeth capped limb that he filled up with bones, transforming it into a pike. Then the lobster’s for form blurred.

The impact was like a damn truck had been launched into Henry face. But one of the advantages of having no bones in his body was that he could take such impacts better than most. It did hurt like hell, of course, and he was going to be black and blue, relatively speaking, but it wasn’t as effective as it could have been.

And I did get you, Henry thought as he eyed the soft clicking arms of the lobster while they gingerly reached for the gaping hole in its thorax. A moment later, the hole filled up as Henry’s rigid arm appeared, bent as the bones within broke, but Henry couldn’t focus on that. Scrambling, he put his metal-coated arms back in the soil and braced. Anytime now, he was going to get shocked. But he could also give the lobster a little gift before it recovered.

“Now,” Henry told his Octomind, and the red projection appeared above them, plunging toward the stab he’d scored earlier and at the same time, both himself and his Octomind began pumping the weakened shell and the flesh below with venoms. Henry used Voracious Grip through a couple of suckers embedded deep into the lobster’s thorax, and the Octomind just used its beak to spew everything Henry had, included his new special, personal venom.

But the lobster wasn’t an A-rank for nothing.

Holding on to it was like trying to get a hold of a thunderous cloud. A storm of mind-frazzling arcs of electricity flew in every direction, blinding Henry and burning his skin. Its presence was overbearing, and while it was still trying to get the pike out of its chest, any moment now, it’d start swinging, and Henry would need to run. But for now, he was dumping all of his venoms in its flesh, stealing its mana and draining everything he could.

Lightning surged out the creature, making Henry jolt with pain, but unlike the other times, the pain and burning was mostly limited to his skin and arms. The protection wasn’t absolute, and he could feel the tips of his arms burning as the sand around them was turned to molten glass.

But this… this I can do, he grinned.
