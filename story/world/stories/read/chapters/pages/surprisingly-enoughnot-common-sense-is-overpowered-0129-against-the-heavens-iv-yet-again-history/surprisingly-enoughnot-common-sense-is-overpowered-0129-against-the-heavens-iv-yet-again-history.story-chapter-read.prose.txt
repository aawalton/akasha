The readers of Against the Heavens had long since accepted that Xiao Fan possessed an unusual relationship with fortune.

Ancient inheritances, mysterious treasures, forgotten techniques, and conveniently placed opportunities had become such regular occurrences that nobody was particularly surprised anymore.

Still, there were limits.

Or at least, the readers had once believed there were.

The latest chapter found Xiao Fan returning to the Pure Qi Sect after another eventful expedition.

What had originally been a simple mission to investigate a disturbance near Blackthorn City had somehow escalated into a confrontation with a demonic cultivator, the discovery of a ruined underground chamber, and an unfortunate misunderstanding involving the daughter of a local cultivation family.

The last incident had been more troublesome. Xiao Fan had merely helped the young woman escape from a group of pursuing cultivators, only for her fiancé to arrive at precisely the wrong moment and reach an entirely incorrect conclusion about their relationship.

The resulting confrontation ended with another young master swearing that Xiao Fan would regret humiliating him.

The readers were sympathetic.

"Bro went out to complete one mission and came back with another mortal enemy."

"At this point, Xiao Fan should start charging people for becoming his enemies."

"Average Tuesday for a xianxia protagonist."

Naturally, Xiao Fan returned with more resources than the mission originally promised.

Among them was the corpse of a peak Qi Condensation Ironback Boar.

The beast had appeared near the end of his expedition, charging out of the forest while Xiao Fan was recovering from his fight with the demonic cultivator. After a very unpleasant battle, Xiao Fan had killed it and discovered that its flesh contained an unusually dense concentration of spiritual qi.

Unfortunately, that created another problem.

The meat was far too valuable to waste, but Xiao Fan's body could only absorb so much spiritual nourishment at once. Eating too much would simply cause most of the excess energy to dissipate.

And so, for once, Xiao Fan decided to do something peaceful and strategic.

He visited the Scripture Pavilion, hoping to find some way to make better use of the resources he had obtained. Perhaps there was a suitable body-refining method, or a technique that could help him process the spiritual energy more efficiently. He wasn't very optimistic, but there was no harm in looking.

After browsing for nearly an hour, he noticed an old manual wedged between two thicker volumes. Its cover was faded, and its contents appeared to describe an ordinary method of nourishing the body through food.

Xiao Fan was about to return it when he noticed something strange.

The cover was slightly thicker than it should have been.

A little investigation revealed a hidden compartment containing a narrow jade sheet.

Xiao Fan blinked.

Someone hid something in here?

The readers, meanwhile, were far less composed.

"..."

"HE DID IT AGAIN."

"BRO IS JUST BROWSING THE LIBRARY."

"The heavens saw Xiao Fan looking for a technique and immediately restocked the secret inheritance section."

"Imagine being the disciple who borrowed that manual yesterday, only to find nothing."

"Skill issue."

Xiao Fan carefully channeled qi into the jade sheet.

Several lines appeared.

In this world, everything is illusory. Swords are external objects. Spears are external objects. Merits and wealth are also external objects, all of which may disappear one day.

Only what I eat is truly mine.

Thus, I care more about the quality of my food than anything else.

Xiao Fan stared.

What kind of senior had written this?

The technique itself, however, was surprisingly useful. It described a method of improving the body's ability to absorb spiritual nourishment, reducing the amount of energy wasted when consuming spiritual food and medicinal resources.

Xiao Fan's eyes gradually brightened.

With this technique, he could make far better use of the Ironback Boar meat sitting inside his storage pouch.

What fortunate timing!

The readers had already reached a different conclusion.

"ONLY WHAT I EAT IS TRULY MINE. The Dao of Eating."

"WAIT. FATTY LI."

"HOLY SHIT, THIS IS PERFECT FOR FATTY LI."

"Forget Xiao Fan. Give that man the technique immediately."

"You know, being the fat friend of a xianxia protagonist is actually an OP strategy."

"Real. You're guaranteed to survive the early arcs, get dragged into absurd opportunities, and eventually awaken some ridiculous physique."

"Don't forget the free cultivation resources whenever the protagonist returns from robbing another ancient tomb."

"And somehow the fat friend always ends up becoming a legendary merchant, chef, or sect master."

"Fatty Li really won the character archetype lottery."

"Meanwhile, the arrogant young master archetype has a life expectancy of three chapters."

"Can we talk about how Xiao Fan conveniently got an entire peak Qi Condensation boar right before finding a technique that helps him digest it?"

"Protagonist luck, baby."

"He has enough meat to feed a small village, and the heavens immediately hand him better digestion. At this point, if Xiao Fan ever gets thirsty, I expect him to discover a thousand-year spiritual spring."

Xiao Fan continued examining the technique, increasingly pleased with his discovery.

He was so absorbed that he failed to notice someone approaching.

"So, what are you doing?"

"E-eep!"

Xiao Fan spun around and threw a punch on instinct.

"Wait, wait! It's me!"

Ji Ning narrowly avoided the attack.

For several seconds, the two stared at each other.

Then Ning smiled.

"Also, eep, huh? I really didn't expect a manly guy like you to make such a girly sound when startled."

Xiao Fan's expression darkened.

"You will forget that immediately."

The readers, naturally, had no intention of forgetting.

"E-EEP."

"THE HEAVEN-DEFYING PROTAGONIST, EVERYONE."

"Bro can stare down demonic cultivators but gets jumpscared by his own friend."

"Ning is never letting him live this down."

After promising Ning retribution should the incident ever be mentioned again, Xiao Fan finally showed him the jade sheet.

Ning read the opening passage, nodded solemnly at the senior's dedication to eating, and began examining the cultivation method.

To Xiao Fan's surprise, this guy seemed genuinely impressed.

What followed was a discussion about spiritual nourishment, absorption efficiency, and the importance of supplementary cultivation techniques.

Ning even explained his personal method of categorizing techniques into seven aspects: attack, defense, movement, healing, storage, investigation, and utility.

According to him, a cultivator who covered all seven would be far better prepared for unexpected situations.

Xiao Fan considered the explanation.

He had always focused primarily on combat, which was understandable considering how often someone tried to kill him.

But Ning had a point. Having more options could make the difference between surviving an encounter and dying because he lacked one particular skill.

The readers were quite impressed.

"Wait, that's actually useful."

"Why does Ning always have an entire lecture prepared?"

"Attack, defense, movement, healing, storage, investigation, utility. Huh. That's a pretty decent checklist."

"I swear this guy thinks about cultivation like he's building an RPG character."

"Wait a minute. I think I figured out Ning's actual role."

"What?"

"He's the exposition NPC."

"..."

"HOLY SHIT, HE IS."

"Think about it. Every time Xiao Fan encounters something confusing, Ning shows up and explains how it works."

"Bro is literally the tutorial NPC."

"Wasn't he supposed to be the merchant friend?"

"The author keeps giving him more roles lmao."

Eventually, Ning mentioned how difficult it was for ordinary cultivators to obtain high-level spiritual beast meat.

Xiao Fan laughed awkwardly.

He decided against mentioning the enormous quantity currently sitting inside his storage pouch.

Then Ning suddenly tossed him a jade bottle.

"Here, take this."

Shining Crystal Spiritual Liquid.

Xiao Fan's eyes widened. It was a valuable resource compatible with Pure Eyes, and he had been looking for something like it for quite some time.

Ning explained that he was offering it as payment for allowing him to study the technique.

Xiao Fan tried to refuse, but Ning insisted. Eventually, Xiao Fan relented, though he still felt that the payment was very generous.

"Oh, and if you ever happen to come across the next part of the Pure Eyes Technique, let me know," Ning added casually. "No need to go looking for it specifically."

Xiao Fan nodded. Considering this guy's generosity, keeping an eye out for a technique hardly seemed like too much to ask.

The readers, however, had noticed something familiar.

"Wait a minute. DID NING JUST GIVE HIM ANOTHER FETCH QUEST?"

"HE DID LMAO."

"First the spiritual beast, now the Pure Eyes Technique."

"Merchant NPC. Exposition NPC. Quest giver."

"HOW MANY ROLES IS THE AUTHOR GOING TO GIVE THIS GUY?"

"At this rate, Ning's going to become the entire supporting cast."

"I'm telling you people, Ning knows something."

"Oh god, this guy again."

"LISTEN. Normal people don't keep handing the luckiest man alive specific treasure-hunting requests."

"Actually, that's exactly what a normal person should do."

"If your friend keeps stumbling into ancient inheritances, why WOULDN'T you ask him to look for something?"

"The funniest part is that Xiao Fan keeps completing them."

"And Ning pays him actual rewards. Bro has turned protagonist luck into a legitimate business model."

"Best NPC in the novel."

Meanwhile, Xiao Fan carefully stored the spiritual liquid away.

He had come to the Scripture Pavilion hoping to find a useful supplementary technique.

Instead, he had discovered a hidden inheritance, received a valuable resource for Pure Eyes, and gained several useful insights into cultivation.

All without spending a single spirit stone.

It had been a remarkably productive afternoon.

Xiao Fan smiled.

Perhaps he should visit the Scripture Pavilion more often.

The readers had only one thing to say.

"PLEASE DON'T. THE OTHER DISCIPLES NEED SOME TREASURES TOO."

If there was one thing the readers of Against the Heavens could always look forward to, it was a good old-fashioned cultivation competition.

After all, what could be more fitting for a xianxia protagonist than a competition where defeating someone stronger meant taking their ranking and even their cultivation residence?

For Xiao Fan, who had entered the inner sect with a spiritual root that most elders considered barely worth nurturing, it was the perfect opportunity to prove himself.

Unfortunately, it also meant dealing with the sort of people who considered his very existence an insult.

The trouble began with an invitation from the Rising Dragon faction.

Gu Zheng, one of the top ten inner sect disciples, had taken an interest in Xiao Fan's Earth-ranked cultivation technique. His followers offered Xiao Fan their protection and generous cultivation resources in exchange for joining their faction.

The terms sounded reasonable until Xiao Fan realized that their generosity depended entirely on him surrendering the secrets of his inheritance.

Naturally, he refused.

And just as naturally, they took offense.

Within hours, several disciples had begun challenging him in the arena.

The first was a seventh-stage Qi Condensation cultivator who declared that Xiao Fan needed to learn his place.

The second was a seventh-stage female cultivator named Miaomiao, who claimed that Xiao Fan had insulted the dignity of the Rising Dragon faction.

Both lost without much fanfare.

By the time Xiao Fan left the second arena, his robes were torn and his mood had deteriorated a lot.

The readers, meanwhile, were having a wonderful time.

"Ah, yes. The obligatory inner sect bullying arc."

"Low talent, mysterious inheritance, refuses to join the strongest faction. Xiao Fan is collecting death flags for other people."

"Why do these guys always send one disciple at a time?"

"Because if they attacked together, the arc would end in three chapters."

"Gu Zheng is already giving off future stepping-stone energy."

Xiao Fan made his way toward another arena, where he had heard that Ling Yinran was participating in a ranking challenge.

He knew her through Ji Ning, and he was curious to see how a skilled puppet cultivator fought.

By the time he arrived, Ling Yinran was facing an insect cultivator named Shen Wei.

The arena was filled with a buzzing cloud of Ghost-Faced Locusts, while two metal puppets struggled to contain the swarm.

Then Shen Wei suddenly charged forward, his sword covered in writhing insects.

Ling Yinran appeared completely exposed.

Xiao Fan frowned and couldn't help but say, "Your senior sister's situation looks quite dangerous."

"Everything is going exactly how she wants it," Ning replied.

Then, for some inexplicable reason, his expression changed.

"Actually, promise me you won't say anything that sounds like a victory speech. Don't jinx it."

Xiao Fan stared at him.

"What?"

"Just stay quiet for ten seconds. Please."

Before Xiao Fan could ask what nonsense he was talking about, Ling Yinran summoned a third puppet and decisively defeated her opponent.

The readers were delighted.

"LMAO NING KNOWS ABOUT THE VICTORY SPEECH CURSE."

"Never say 'we've won' before the enemy actually falls."

"Xiao Fan was one sentence away from triggering Shen Wei's hidden bloodline awakening."

"The fact that Ning genuinely panicked makes this ten times funnier."

After the match, Ning noticed Xiao Fan's injuries and handed him a healing pill.

Xiao Fan accepted it, although he made it clear that medicine would not make him forget the earlier insult.

Their conversation soon turned toward the challenges Xiao Fan had been receiving.

"Why the hell is everyone picking fights with me?" Xiao Fan complained. "What do they even stand to gain?"

Ning offered some sympathetic words, although the smile on his face suggested he was enjoying the situation far too much.

He explained that ordinary newcomers were rarely targeted because their rankings were too low to be worth challenging.

Xiao Fan was apparently a special exception.

"Besides," Ning added, "since I'm not you, the odds of someone randomly targeting me are practically zero."

Xiao Fan narrowed his eyes.

"You make it sound like I go looking for trouble!"

Ning merely stared at him.

And then, as though the heavens themselves had been waiting for the opportunity, an angry shout echoed across the arena.

"Xiao Fan! Step forward and face me! How dare you humiliate Junior Sister Miaomiao in the arena!"

Xiao Fan closed his eyes.

He had defeated Miaomiao in a perfectly legitimate ranking challenge.

Apparently, that was now a crime.

"HAHAHAHA THE TIMING."

"NING DIDN'T EVEN GET TO FINISH HIS POINT."

"Xiao Fan's enemies have started spawning automatically."

"Imagine being so unlucky socially that beating someone in an official tournament creates another enemy."

"To be fair, this is how 90% of xianxia conflicts begin."

Before Xiao Fan could accept the challenge, another disciple descended into the plaza.

"You! Face me. Don't think you're scurrying away this time!"

Xiao Fan's irritation deepened.

"Can't you see I've already got a guy lined up?"

The newcomer looked confused.

"Who are you? I'm talking to Ji Ning."

For a moment, silence descended.

Then Xiao Fan slowly turned towards Ning, who looked equally surprised.

The readers, however, immediately understood the significance of this development.

"SOMEONE IS CHALLENGING NING?"

"FINALLY. THE NPC HAS HIS OWN YOUNG MASTER."

"Bro spent so long making fun of Xiao Fan's trouble magnetism that the heavens decided to correct the imbalance."

"Character development. The supporting cast is getting its own face-slapping arc."

The challenger was Lao San, a farming disciple who apparently held a grudge against Ning over some disagreement at a previous gathering.

Xiao Fan watched with growing amusement as Ning attempted to talk his way out of the confrontation.

Instead of accepting the challenge, Ning calmly explained that defeating him would provide almost no material benefit.

Then he suggested something even more extraordinary.

Why not sell the challenge opportunity to someone who could make better use of it?

Ning proceeded to explain how the limited number of challenges, ranking restrictions, and potential rewards created an opportunity for an entirely new market.

At first, Xiao Fan thought Ning was merely trying to distract Lao San.

But as Ning continued explaining the potential benefits, something remarkable happened.

Lao San actually began considering it.

The anger on his face gradually gave way to hesitation. A challenge opportunity was valuable, after all, and wasting one on a personal grudge offered very little in return.

For the first time since arriving, Lao San seemed genuinely interested in something other than punching Ning in the face.

Across the comment section, an extraordinary realization was beginning to spread.

"Wait. Hold on."

"IS HE ACTUALLY LISTENING?"

"OH MY GOD."

"GUYS. ARE WE WITNESSING SOMETHING NEVER BEFORE SEEN IN XIANXIA HISTORY? A CONFLICT BEING RESOLVED THROUGH PEACEFUL NEGOTIATION?"

"WITHOUT ANYONE LOSING FACE? WITHOUT SOMEONE SHOUTING COURTING DEATH?"

"HISTORY IS BEING MADE."

"QUICK, SOMEONE RECORD THIS. FUTURE GENERATIONS WILL NEVER BELIEVE US."

"After 100 chapters of people trying to kill each other over minor insults, we're finally getting diplomacy."

"Ji Ning, the first cultivator to discover that you can simply talk to people."

"Don't celebrate yet. The young master hasn't left."

"SHUT UP. DON'T JINX IT."

Xiao Fan, meanwhile, was genuinely impressed.

He had never considered that ranking challenges could be treated as something with monetary value. Selling an opportunity to someone who needed it certainly sounded smarter than wasting it over a personal grudge.

And so, completely unaware of the delicate situation, he decided to contribute to the conversation.

"I mean, if someone challenged me over something from months ago instead of using the opportunity properly, I'd honestly think they were an idi-"

"Wait-" Ning tried to stop him.

It was too late.

Lao San's face turned crimson.

"You!"

And just like that, the negotiations were over.

"..."

"XIAO FAN."

"YOU ABSOLUTE FUCKING IDIOT."

"WE WERE SO CLOSE."

"ONE HUNDRED CHAPTERS. ONE HUNDRED CHAPTERS AND WE ALMOST HAD ONE CONFLICT END PEACEFULLY."

"HE DIDN'T EVEN MEAN TO INSULT HIM LMAOOOO."

"The protagonist's passive ability activated. Diplomacy check: Success. Xiao Fan: I'd like to roll again. Critical failure."

"The heavens simply refuse to allow a xianxia conflict to end without someone getting beaten up."

"Forget it!" Lao San roared. "I'm still challenging you!"

Ning slowly turned toward Xiao Fan.

Xiao Fan looked back at him.

For a moment, neither spoke.

Then Ning sighed. "You just had to do it, didn't you?"

"Well, it's not my fault," Xiao Fan protested. "Don't worry. If you can't beat him, I'll avenge you."

Somehow, that only made Ning look more tired.

"And he's offering revenge now. THE CYCLE CONTINUES."

"Ning tried to invent diplomacy, but unfortunately, he's friends with a xianxia protagonist."

"The Dao of Peace was briefly glimpsed today. May we someday see it again."

A short while later, the match began.

Lao San was a seventh-stage Qi Condensation cultivator, and his command over spiritual plants was impressive. Within moments, the arena was covered in rapidly growing vines, carnivorous flowers, and clouds of corrosive pollen.

Xiao Fan watched with interest.

Farming cultivators were rarely considered formidable combatants, yet Lao San's techniques demonstrated how dangerous a properly prepared spiritual farmer could become.

Unfortunately for him, Ning appeared to have a rather straightforward answer.

Fireball.

The vines burned.

More fireballs.

The flowers burned.

And when Lao San released an enormous cloud of poisonous pollen, Ning simply ignited that as well.

"FIREBALL! OP! PLEASE NERF"

"DID NING JUST SOLVE THE ENTIRE FARMING CULTIVATION SYSTEM WITH FIRE?"

"To be fair, wood loses to fire."

"Bro brought a plant build to a pyromancer fight."

"I feel bad for Lao San. Those seeds probably cost a fortune."

"Imagine spending three years cultivating a rare spiritual vine just to watch someone turn it into charcoal."

Lao San eventually abandoned his ranged techniques and activated Bronze Body, charging directly at Ning.

For a while, the two appeared evenly matched.

Lao San possessed the higher cultivation realm and greater physical strength, while Ning relied on his movement technique and precise spear strikes to avoid direct confrontation.

But Xiao Fan gradually noticed something.

Ning was barely wasting any energy.

Every dodge was measured, and every spear strike forced Lao San to expend more qi maintaining his defense.

By the time Ning finally announced that he was going to attack seriously, Lao San was already exhausted.

A single spear thrust ended the match, its tip stopping just short of his throat.

Lao San surrendered.

Xiao Fan let out a low whistle.

He had expected Ning to win, but watching him systematically dismantle a stronger opponent was still impressive.

The readers agreed.

"Okay, that was clean."

"Ning really spent the entire fight studying Lao San before deciding he'd seen enough."

"The funniest part is that he genuinely didn't want to fight."

"Xiao Fan fights people because they keep offending him. Ning fights people because Xiao Fan accidentally ruins his negotiations."

"I think we've found the real villain of this chapter."

After the match, Ning was offered a choice between taking Lao San's courtyard or improving the spiritual density of his own.

Naturally, he chose the latter.

The referee congratulated him and announced his courtyard's new ranking number, which would replace its previous designation in the sect records.

"Sixty-six."

Ning froze.

Xiao Fan, still waiting for his own challenge, noticed the sudden change in his expression.

"What's wrong? Didn't you just get a better courtyard?"

"I did," Ning replied quietly.

"Then why do you look so miserable?"

Ning glanced at him, sighed, and shook his head. "You wouldn't understand."

Xiao Fan frowned. He genuinely couldn't understand what there was to be upset about.

Little did he know that Ning had just lost something he considered far more precious than a few extra spirit stones.

His previous courtyard number had been sixty-nine.

The readers were speechless.

"..."

"HE'S UPSET ABOUT THE NUMBER?"

"BRO WON THE MATCH AND STILL THINKS HE LOST."

"Xiao Fan is fighting for his dignity and future cultivation resources while Ning is mourning a courtyard number."

"The author really gave this man an entire face-slapping arc just to end it with a 69 joke."

"Peak cultivation literature."
