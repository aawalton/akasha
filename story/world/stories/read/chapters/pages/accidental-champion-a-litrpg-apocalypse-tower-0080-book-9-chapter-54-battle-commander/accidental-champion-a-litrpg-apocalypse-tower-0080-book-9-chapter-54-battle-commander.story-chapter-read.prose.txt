Xavier idly flapped his wings at the S Grade True Dragon.

The beast was huge, though not the largest beast Xavier had ever faced. Xavier had trouble imagining it ever growing to the size of the Interdimensional Dragon, yet he supposed it must be possible.

Though perhaps not for this True Dragon.

The massive dragon moved incredibly sluggishly, stuck outside Xavier’s time dilation field as it was. And Xavier’s time dilation field had grown significantly stronger since those four A Grade dragonkin had performed their transformation.

Still, it seemed as though it had grown faster since then. He couldn’t see how that would be possible. It certainly hadn’t killed enough enemies to gain a level, especially as an S Grade. Which made Xavier wonder how levels even worked for this thing. The levels of the A Grades it had come from hadn’t been S Grade high, and yet here it was…

Now’s not the time to contemplate such things.

The portal was open behind Xavier. A single portal that had thousands of other portals leading to it. Portals all around the massive population centres of this world that had been transformed into one giant city. Every single Denizen on the planet—excepting those who’d recently become System age and hadn’t had the time to learn the necessary techniques—had Territory Defence and Unity Defence active, making their bodies, minds, and souls more powerful.

Xavier, of course, did not have Territory Defence and Unity Defence active. He didn’t rule this world, he didn’t wish to conquer it, and he certainly wasn’t going to pay fealty to Queen Aecia. But that, he hoped, didn’t matter. His body was significantly more powerful than it had been the last time he’d done something like this. Not only had he gained an entire advancement, he’d also been strengthening his body with Refined Celestial Energy as much as possible.

He should be able to handle the influx of power.

Queen Aecia and her entourage, not to mention those on her advisory committee—men and women who had their noses stuck in books far more than they ever trained—had pushed for a more direct involvement in the fight.

They were, each and every one of them, feeling drunk off the power Xavier had fed them. Gaining a Reality Core put every Denizen on the planet down an extra path of advancement. And learning spell patterns and how to create time dilation fields without needing to become a time mage meant they didn’t need to worry about cooldowns anymore—though he hadn’t taught them how to manifest spell patterns, so they still needed to draw them the “old-fashioned” way.

These people would have a huge advantage over others in this universe because of what he’d done. Hopefully, nothing like this will never happen on their planet again.

Assuming they survive this.

Xavier had refused to do the fight differently. If he involved others, they would die in droves. Even if he had them cast spells from the other side of the planet and funnelled those spells through the portal, it wouldn’t be near enough to actually help.

No, what he needed most was their energy.

Roln appeared beside him. The man’s hands were folded behind his back, and he wore a weary expression. “You can still step back from this. You’ve already given these people an escape plan should this all go south. They’ll be safe, even if you do go.”

Xavier looked over at the mental construct. You know me better than anyone, he replied in his mind. Tell me, do you think I’m ready to step back from this?

Roln sighed. “Don’t say I didn’t warn you.” He flashed out of existence once again.

Xavier took a moment to give the dead Wanderer’s words some thought. Before coming here today, he’d retreated deep inside his mind, all the way into that spherical room, and tried to commune with his Ancestral Soul, wishing he could get some insight into whether he should go through with this fight or not.

He’d felt the Ancestral Soul’s presence. Felt its eyes on him. But no matter how many times he tried to talk to it, he got only silence in reply.

The being wanted him to make this decision.

There was a part of Xavier that was reassured by that, but there was also another part that wondered if perhaps he was simply doing what the Ancestral Being wanted him to do anyway. This was the path that felt right to him. Did that mean it was a path he was being nudged toward? And if he was… did he even mind?

Thinking about all that simply made his head hurt.

Besides, he’d come this far. He’d trained up an entire world in order to fight the True Dragon. It would be disappointing if he simply walked away.

A grin slipped onto his face as he stared at the dragon.

Why the hell would I ever want to walk away from a fight like this?

Sheer excitement filled his entire body. He crackled with the energy of the moment. All around the planet, Denizens had drawn up spell patterns and were staring up at the portals positioned in the sky above them, waiting to initiate those spell patterns.

Waiting to offer up all their power to him.

Xavier knew this fight wouldn’t be like the fight he’d had against the World Destroyer. It wasn’t going to be a one-punch affair. This was going to be so, so much more than that.

Xavier manifested several spell patterns inside his body. The first of those spell patterns was, of course, Energy Funnel. He cast the spell. Energy coalesced in the air and formed the funnel, the broad side of it facing the portal behind him. Then, he cast the other spell patterns. Energy Transference was one of them—or rather, it was ten of them. He’d decided he needed to split up the energy that was coming toward him into different pocket spaces so he could use it as he chose.

He was also well aware that he wasn’t pulling on the same amount of energy that he’d been pulling on when he faced the World Destroyer. This wasn’t the power of billions of planets from two different Silver River sectors. This was power from only a single planet.

It’s going to have to be enough.

There was a third type of spell he activated, one that would let him take advantage of the energy in a different way. Energy Transference simply turned the incoming energy into a destructive force that he could wield against his enemy. For this kind of fight, Xavier would need more than mere destructive energy.

He would need all types if he was going to defeat an S Grade.

This third spell was called Energy Buff, and unlike Energy Transference, he cast it twenty times, creating twenty separate pocket spaces within his body to hold the energy that would come his way, making a total of thirty with the Energy Transference pocket spaces.

Queen Aecia: [Are you ready, Xavier Collins? The entire population of Galadsea is standing by awaiting your order.]

Xavier gave a beat, scanning his body and mind, feeling nervousness, fear, and excitement. He released a long breath.

Xavier: [Oh, I’m ready. Relay the order.]

Queen Aecia: [This is an order you will have to give, Xavier. I may be their queen, but for this fight you are my Battle Commander.]

Xavier glanced up at the sky. Thousands of observation crystals hovered in the air, taking a three-hundred-and-sixty-degree recording of what was happening in the area and broadcasting it as a hologram back to everyone watching around the planet.

Xavier had two separate time dilation fields active for this fight. One field was encompassing the planet—except for the area ahead, where the S Grade True Dragon had taken flight—while the second field only encompassed him. That second field was the one he’d be dumping power into.

He stared at the nearest observation crystal. “Galadsea, lend me your strength and I will do everything in my power—in our power—to ensure your planet is saved from this threat.” He bowed his head. “I give the command.”

Billions of Denizens all around the world initiated their spells, transferring their energy through the portals above them, sending all that energy toward him.

The influx of energy flowing through the portal behind him was intoxicating. Unlike the last time he had done something like this, he could now use his Immortal Soul lens to feel that energy. He felt it so much more strongly than he ever did when he merely had his spiritual sense—and even his normal spiritual sense, unenhanced by his Immortal Soul lens, was a great deal more powerful these days.

He breathed it all in and watched as it all flowed through the Energy Funnel. The Energy Funnel, along with the ten Energy Transference and Energy Buff spells he’d cast, were significantly more robust than they’d been when he’d faced the World Destroyer. Roln had helped him improve the spells dramatically, as Xavier had practiced this on a much smaller scale many times over the years he’d been training the Galadsean people toward this end. The amount of little tweaks that had been made gave it a far, far better degree of efficiency. There was almost no energy loss at all.

That energy flowed from the funnel and was pulled into his body by the Energy Transference and Energy Buff spells, flowing into the thirty different pocket spaces within him. Each was basically an artificial core, and each held far more energy than even all his cores combined. No matter how powerful he’d become, he still didn’t hold as much energy as billions of different Denizens, even if he had no doubt that he could single handedly take out everyone on this planet if he needed to.

Xavier drew in a long breath as that power was held inside him. If he’d been crackling with energy before, now he was absolutely electrified with it. He felt like an explosion about to go off. Like a nuclear bomb ready to explode.

The instant after he’d taken in all of that energy, the S Grade True Dragon turned its head, shifting its gaze directly toward Xavier. He’d been concealed a moment before, but there was no way he could conceal all the energy that had just come to him. He’d known all along that the True Dragon would be able to sense the energy when it came.

Xavier held his breath as he watched just how quickly the S Grade was able to move outside the time dilation field. As each fraction of a second passed, it seemed to grow faster and faster, as though it was somehow adapting, pushing more and more of its resources toward physical speed in a way Xavier had never witnessed before, until its entire body was turned toward him.

Damn, that thing is even faster than I thought.

Xavier tilted his head to one side then took that moment to adjust his time dilation field. Up until then, he’d had it synced to the same speed as the first time dilation field that covered this planet, so he could communicate with the queen and her populace with ease.

Now, he sped it up to his natural limit, watching as the S Grade’s wings beat slower and slower the more he amped his field up. But even with his field as powerful as he could make it, the S Grade True Dragon was moving faster than he’d expected it to. Fast enough that he could easily perceive its movements.

Even so, it would still take a long, long time for it to reach him.

As Xavier watched his enemy, a glow began at the beast’s throat, and its maw slowly opened. The S Grade True Dragon was about to breathe fire. A stream of flame that would no doubt be able to reach him where he was.

Xavier grinned. He tapped into one of the Energy Buff pocket spaces and dumped all of that energy into his time dilation field, transferring 1/30th of the power of the people of this world into his second active Time Alteration spell.

The influx of energy was incredibly intense. To use the energy in this way, all that energy had to run through Xavier’s channels. The sheer power was enough to cause him a huge amount of physical pain. It burned his channels to the point where it would take some time for him to recover from the damage.

But as all that energy was funnelled into his time dilation field, the field sped up to the point where the S Grade True Dragon looked like nothing more than a hulking statue floating frozen in mid-air.
