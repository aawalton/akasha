In the morning, they were woken by Aenir's laughter.

"What the hell is this? Renir, why are you underneath the bird?"

Renir instantly scrambled from underneath Liora, nearly throwing her off balance.

"Hey!" Cee protested, still huddling close. Caspian was back to his human form. They had to dig a hole in the tent and fill it with water, where he had to clatter his teeth for three hours, before his Landwalker skill reset.

Thankfully, Cee could now produce water thanks to the divine core she was absorbing. Well, it was generous to call it production. She could spray a light mist of water from her leg, so it took a good while for them to fill the small hole. But hey, at least they had a way to keep their merman wet.

"That is none of your concern!" Renir grumbled angrily.

"You know, you shouldn't encourage brooding behaviour in your familiar. She might just fly off with another bird and settle down to form a family," Aenir chuckled.

"That would never happen!" Renir and Liora blurted out at the same time.

"Wow, yeesh! You two can't take a joke."

"Your jokes are never funny, Aenir!" Renir grumbled.

"Leave them, Mister Mage. Better show us the breakfast you brought us," Cee said. "I can smell something delicious!"

Aenir chuckled. "You truly have an amazing sense of smell, Miss Spider. I figured you might need energy to climb the mountain, so I brought lots," Aenir said, and snapped his fingers.

All sorts of decadent dishes appeared before them alongside tables. There was a whole roast pig, several roast rabbits, a bunch of roast potatoes, a plethora of decadent fruits, and enough nuts to last Liora a month. Not to mention the giant plate of raw meat for Smokey.

The wyvern didn't wait for anyone. He dug in without hesitation, chomping and swallowing large chunks.

"Smokey, chew your food! You'll get a stomachache at this rate," Cee chastised.

Smokey did slow down a little and gave each a few chews before swallowing. Not great, but it was as much self-restraint as the wyvern could afford.

"You are not getting any of this back, you know!" Liora said, grabbing a roast potato and chewing on it.

Caspian also grabbed one, but his face instantly scrunched up as he ate it. "What in the name of ocean depth?! Why is it so spicy?!"

The merman hurriedly grabbed a water jug, which had also appeared with all the food, and downed it in one gulp.

"Really? I don't feel anything. Tastes like a regular potato to me," Liora said, taking another bite.

"Birds can't taste spice, remember?" Cee said.

"That's correct. I'm surprised you know that bit of knowledge," Aenir noted.

"It was quite common knowledge where I am from," Cee shrugged.

Aenir's eyes narrowed at the spider. "It is common knowledge among spiders? I find that rather hard to believe."

Cee deflated, realizing she had said too much. Renir glared at her, while Liora continued eating the potatoes.

"So is everything here spicy? As a merman, I don't really enjoy spicy things," Caspian tried to shift the topic.

"Leave it be, Aenir," Renir said, glaring at his brother.

"So there really is more to this group that meets the eye, isn't there?" Aenir asked.

"Yes, but I'm not telling you. It is quite fascinating. I was astounded when I found out," Renir said, clearly trying to tease his brother.

"What is it? Are they the descendants of some unique beastkin? Beastkin mixed with humans? What is it?" Aenir asked.

"I'm not telling you!" Renir laughed, almost childishly. "You won't guess the right answer even in a thousand years."

"Renir!"

"Nope, not telling!"

"Can you two knock it off?" Liora grumbled. "Some of us are trying to enjoy our food."

"Liora, maybe you can tell me the secret of your nature?" Aenir pleaded.

Liora thought about it. While Aenir wasn't as bad as Renir had made him out to be, she didn't fully trust him yet. One could never really trust a man whose mind was so focused on a single woman. Love could make the most reasonable person act illogically, even stupidly.

"Maybe once all this is over," Liora replied evasively.

"Aww…" Aenir slumped.

Once they had eaten their fill–with Caspian dunking everything in water first to lessen the spiciness–and pulled everything else into their inventory, they prepared to set out to Titanspike Mountains.

Aenir had two camels prepared and would escort them for a while. He couldn't leave the sultanate for long since he was the royal magician, not to mention that as a mage he would be mostly useless in the mountains anyway.

Caspian was very eager to ride on the second camel, glancing at Liora's sharp talons the whole time. How ungrateful! He didn't appreciate her efforts at all. Did he think she enjoyed being a flying mule for them all? She would much rather fly alone, but their slow speeds made that quite impossible.

"So how do I carry you now?" Liora asked, looking at Renir.

"Liora, you are salivating…" Renir noted, taking a step back.

"I am not!" She screeched, but couldn't look away from his shiny body. So much shiny! Was there even a treasure this large anywhere else in the world?

Every part of Renir's body glittered with golden light. Specks of orange shone throughout, and frankly, it was ridiculous that something this large was made out of a precious gemstone.

"You most definitely are…" Renir outstretched his hand to keep her away, but Liora merely admired the colorful, shiny specs in it.

"I think a saddle is required at this point. She could carry you in her talons, but it limits her maneuverability. But riding her without a saddle could damage her feathers and even cause injury."

"Where are we supposed to get a saddle?" Liora exclaimed.

"I have already thought of that," Aenir said, and with a snap of his fingers, a row of saddles appeared in the sand. "I have horse saddles, camel saddles, donkey saddles, and even miniature horse saddles."

"I am not wearing a donkey saddle!" She announced.

"I suspect it won't be a good fit anyway," Aenir said.

Half an hour later, after trying on all the saddles and with a bit of assistance from Cee, Liora was fully equipped and ready to carry Renir. In the end, a small camel saddle turned out to be the right choice because it was inherently more adjustable.

Liora could still flap her wings comfortably, and Renir could sit in the saddle, secured by Cee's thread. However, it wasn't the best setup. It weighed Liora down. She imagined that it would make flying quite difficult once she had to carry Caspian too.

"I need to evolve again. You're getting too heavy. I won't be able to carry you anymore," Liora grumbled.

"If things go well, then once we're in the mountain, you, Cee, and Smokey should reach your evolution thresholds quite quickly," Renir said.

"Is that because it's filled with high-level monsters?" Cee asked.

"Are you sure you'll be able to manage with just them? I suggest you search for a few mercenaries to hire and—" Aenir began.

"No," Renir shook his head firmly. "I want to travel with people I can trust."

"Literally nobody in your party is a person…" Aenir sighed. "Suit yourself, little brother. I know better than to argue with you."

With that said, they were off. Liora had expected that the following camels would be slow, but they could run surprisingly fast. Even if they looked a bit silly while doing it.

They headed northeast, and Liora worried that they might run into some of the antkin since they did cross their territory for a brief moment. But soon it became clear that their way stretched even further on.

After travelling for as long as Caspian could remain in his human form, they stopped at an oasis. They could see the mountains far into the distance, but they didn't look all that grand. Nothing more than a speck on the horizon.

"This is as far as I can take you. Keep heading northeast and find the dry riverbed. Eventually you'll find the dam I've created to stop the corrupted water from leaking into the sultanate. Just follow that, and eventually, in theory, you should reach the source of the corruption."

They all nodded.

"Was blocking the river really the only option?" Renir asked.

"It's not fully blocked. I've created a special purification device to cleanse some of the water and let it flow through. But without proper access to holy magic, there wasn't much I could do. I had to rely on other mages just to create it, and even so, the amount I can purify is not enough. People have no choice but to rely on oases for water."

"I see…" Renir drawled out.

"I hope with all my heart that you all will succeed. I'll try to buy as much time to stall the war, but the sultan's patience is running out. People are dying. Every day is off the essence. Do not get sidetracked."

Everyone nodded again. With that, Aenir bowed to them and left, taking both the camels with them.

"How much longer until we can keep going, Princess?" Cee asked the merman, who was soaking in the water.

"30 minutes, give or take," he said, then looked at Liora. "Do I really have to travel via those talons?"

"You could always walk?" Liora shot back.

That shut him right up. It was Liora doing all the hard work. In fact, when they took off and continued on their way to the mountain, Liora was very tempted to drop the merman. He, combined with Renir's weight, made flying quite a difficult task.

Smokey could fly faster than her now.

"One of you two needs to learn how to fly?" Liora grumbled, looking in the distance. The mountains were still quite a distance away.

However, she did see the dam that Aenir had spoken about. It was quite difficult to miss, even for those who weren't gifted with great eyesight like herself.

A small bit of water flowed from the dam into the river. Well, it couldn't really be called a river anymore. It was nothing more than a small stream.

But that wasn't the shocking part. The shocking part was the water beyond the dam. A giant pool of corrupted purple water. It was emanating a dark, disgusting aura, which seemed to evaporate into the air, creating a sickly, purplish fog, similar to the one that had settled over Abylport before Liora cleansed the island.

"Disgusting," Liora growled. "Let me deal with that real quick."

She put down her two passengers and withdrew the saddle into her inventory. The relief she felt was immense. Those two were too heavy for her to carry. She prayed that Smokey would evolve into an even larger wyvern or a dragon so he could carry Caspian at the very least.

But that would come later. First, the cleansing. Liora flew above the nasty pool of corrupted sludge water and activated Winds of Purification by combining Wind Blast and Purify.

The sparkling winds settled over the pool of corrupted water and cleansed away the fog and the surface level. However, the bottom layers were just as disgusting as always.

I always have to do the dirty work, don't I?

Liora dove into the water and once again activated Winds of Purification. Her Fluid Flight neatly converted the skill to be usable underwater. The purifying lights spread throughout, cleansing the corruption. It took her two tries to cleanse the whole pool, leaving her mana pool halfway empty.

But it was done.

Liora flew out of the water, wanting to admire her work. Unfortunately, she couldn't celebrate.

"That's quite the problem…" Renir called. He and others of their party had come close to the pool.

The water was cleansed; however, freshly corrupted water was already pouring into it, corrupting everything once more.

"It appears it won't do much good to cleanse the water unless we get to the source," Cee said. "Why can't things ever be easy?"

"We already expected as much. We must go up the mountain."

Liora sighed. What other choice did they have?

She was about to withdraw the saddle from her inventory and prepare to fly when she noticed slight tremors by her feet.

"Something is coming! Something big!" Caspian warned, his sensitive ears hearing the danger before them.

Liora hurriedly flew up to see up the mountain. The moment she saw it, Liora wished that they all would have returned to Velithra and left the humans and antkin to their own devices.

A giant rhino-like creature with black crystals covering its back was running down from the mountain, black spittle flying from its mouth. Its eye sockets were hollow.

A corrupted monster.
