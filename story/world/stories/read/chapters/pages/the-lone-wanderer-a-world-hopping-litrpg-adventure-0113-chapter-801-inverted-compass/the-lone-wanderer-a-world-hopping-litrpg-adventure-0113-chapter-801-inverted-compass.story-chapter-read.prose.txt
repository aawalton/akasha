As it turned out, the answer to most of Percy’s questions was either “yes”, “probably”, or “yes with caveats”.

He didn’t have the time or dream mana to invest into another Melodian expedition, nor was registering Advanced Soul Control a priority, so he wasn’t able to confirm that he could take advantage of an Elemental Source from afar. However, he saw no reason why it wouldn’t be possible, because he could lend the entirety of his brainpower to his soul clone to train with.

The same was true of his familiar’s Divine Spark mutation – which made Percy quite excited for the future. The only issue was that his dream mana output still limited him to a couple of powered-up cords, so he had to carefully choose where he allocated them until his next promotion.

Things were more complicated when it came to the Command trait.

Percy could share the instincts it granted him with the clone in Nephthys’ body – who could potentially control her subjects through her Charged Antennae and royal authority. Unfortunately, he couldn’t pass the Command trait directly to the Ruby Princess, because the wisp possessing her didn’t contain the actual fiend. The clone was simply borrowing the effect from Percy’s main body.

He also found that there was no point in strengthening his connection to his familiar. Dream Harmony wasn’t that much better than Dream Resonance, and there was still a thin barrier preventing Percy from fully synchronizing his mind with Nephthys.

It was more efficient to connect himself to the clone, as that gave both Percy and his familiar most of the benefits of the enriched cord while leaving him enough of the pink resource to invest in a different connection.

Modifying Ludwick’s Compass also proved laughably easy. Percy didn’t fully understand the inner workings of the mindset, but applying the required changes was as simple as swapping a few mental structures around.

[Congratulations! Your mindset has evolved: Ludwick’s Compass -> Percival’s Inverted Compass!]

Percy wasn’t a huge fan of the new name because the modified mindset still relied on the deceased demigods’ hard work, though he supposed that Saint Ludwick and his mind user colleague would forgive him for accidentally hogging all the credit.

Opening his Status, he read the new description.

___

Mindsets [28,600/1,600]:

___

The Inverted Compass worked slightly differently from how he had originally envisioned it, but it should allow him to do what he wanted all the same.

Automatically tracking the most recent twenty-four hours of movement was even better than having to manually place a mark at the start of the journey, because Percy might not always know in advance that he was heading towards an interesting destination. Even better, the upgraded mindset could still be used like the original version if necessary, and Percy had been extra careful to preserve his previous marks.

With two powerful tools added to his kit, Percy had to consider his next steps.

Nearly a year had passed since he activated his third core, and he had already extracted most of its value. The easy promotions had been completed, his boosting art had been perfected, his mindsets had been overhauled, and he had even received a couple of nice surprises in the form of his mind clones and Dream Harmony. There was still plenty of room for improvement, but his gains should greatly slow down over the next decade.

‘It’s probably a good idea to keep a few minds in charge of improving Supercharged Cognition, but my next priority should be to start putting my soul clones to better use and investing most of my cognitive power in their adventures,’ he decided.

The clone in his familiar’s body – which he could now control like an extension of his own – had already started preparing the seals inside her beast core. Percy would also need to keep a couple of cords empty so that he could begin granting the Moirais’ Decree to the Starry Knights. Spending the next few years possessing bugs would prevent him from doing much else with his main body, but they’d already delayed Micky’s new core long enough.

The biggest question was what to do with the second enriched cord.

Percy was tempted to send his special clone to Mithril. With the entire army of sentries and a powerful domain at his disposal, he would have an easier time scouting the planet and figuring out how to get his hands on the Focus. In fact, his ability to transfer his spatial seals back and forth might also help him obtain the powerful artifact without having to visit the greater spring in person.

‘Not yet,’ he thought with a sigh.

As he had previously concluded, his personal strength wouldn’t be enough to win this war anyway, so there was no reason to risk exposing himself to such an advanced faction ahead of time. Accelerating his familiar’s development was more urgent, and his new spell had given him an idea on how to accomplish that.

Percy needed to give Nephthys another mutation before her next evolution, to hopefully trigger a chain reaction and cause her existing mutations to merge. Ideally, that would help with both her jelly output and her ability to control her subjects. The only problem had been finding a suitable mutation on short notice, though he now had the means to potentially grab one that had previously been out of reach.

With his mind made up, he recalled the special clone back to Remior while planning out his new trip.

***

A few hours later, Percy found a part of himself inside the half-eaten body of an alien fish, in the process of being chewed alive by a much bigger one. The water surrounding his host sported an ominous red colour, though only a part of that hue was caused by the spilled blood. The rest was evidence of the magical, ruby-coloured powder mixed into the ocean.

Percy had returned to Ruby Heart – the place where his Ruby Bones mutation had originated from.

Unleashing his domain, he pulverized both the predator gnawing at his flesh and the other Yellow- and Green-cored creatures in the vicinity, buying his host a few extra minutes of life.

Flooding his borrowed body with Green healing potions from his seal, he tried to restore it as best as he could. Due to the two-grade gap, knitting his wounds closed and mending his broken bones was easy enough, though the potion wasn’t potent enough to restore damaged organs, of which he had a couple.

Percy was forced to plug the holes and reinforce the mangled tissue with willpower. Between his domain and the potions, he hoped that his host would last another day or two, which might be enough to accomplish his goals.

In theory, this could have all been avoided if he had simply chosen a healthier host.

Percy generally tried to pick bodies in better condition so that he wouldn’t have to deal with injuries like these, but he hadn’t found any Orange creatures dying of old age, starvation, or blood loss on this planet. Anything that died at such a low grade and in such a chaotic environment tended to violently lose its life in the jaws of a much larger creature.

His host’s physical strength was inconsequential, which was precisely why Percy had wanted to possess a smaller beast. It would be easier to protect and move it around if it was no larger than a watermelon rather than one as bulky as an elephant.

‘Care to fill me in on your plan?’ Micky suddenly asked, breaking him out of his thoughts.

Even though Percy had completely taken over his soul clone’s mental faculties, his friend could still accompany him on his adventures by peering through their connection. The best part was that Micky could now opt out of the boring parts of the journey and only rejoin Percy whenever he wanted, sparing both of them the headache.

‘It’s pretty simple,’ Percy replied with a mental shrug. ‘The giant eels clearly have the ability to generate and expel huge quantities of ruby powder from their bodies, which suggests that they possess a more potent version of the mutation. I want to see if we can harvest their glands, feed them to Nephthys and upgrade her own.’

The Huehuan gasped through their connection, treating Percy as if he had lost his mind.

‘Percy… has this new power gotten to your head?’ Micky wondered. ‘I could see us taking a quasi-divine beast on with our main bodies, but there’s no way we can fight against it while possessing a half-dead Orange fish. Even if we can match the eel’s domain – and that’s a big if – willpower alone won’t be enough to kill it.’

Percy chuckled, not bothering to explain himself. His friend would understand the plan soon enough.

‘Just lend me your spiritual blood. We’ll need better visibility if we’re going to bring one of those monsters down.’
