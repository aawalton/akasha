If Luis had been present, he could have warned Jade not to be taken in by the rage demon's bulky figure. Unfortunately, he wasn't, and Jade hadn't been present to witness his showdown with the demon's kin in the second floor corespace. The subsequent misconception that a large, muscular brute of a demon would be relatively slow, and that his disproportionately large axe would be cumbersome and unwieldy in his hands, nearly cost Jade her life in the opening seconds of their clash.

The two met in the center of the camp, moving at speed. Jade sidestepped the demon's powerful, overhand strike, bringing her blades up in a fierce slash--

WHAM

There was a flash of light and a sound like breaking glass as her armor's barrier collapsed under an enormous impact. The world flipped, spinning out of control as the blow launched Jade a solid fifteen feet before she crashed to the ground. Jade rolled to absorb the fall, her body moving on instinct while her mind struggled to process what had just happened, and she jumped to her feet even as a sharp, intense pain blossomed across her side. Just as well, the rage demon was already closing the distance at a sprint, axe drawn back to deliver a finishing blow. This time, Jade was ready. The axe flashed towards her like a bolt of lightning, only for the illusory copy left by Mirage Step to explode in a blinding flash of light right in her attacker's face. He bellowed in rage, blindly sweeping his weapon in wide, cleaving arcs, but not before Jade landed a few strikes of her own on his unguarded back. She darted away before one of his wild swings connected, leaving a couple of less fortunate demons to be rent in two by the powerful attacks.

Click, click.

Jade didn't waste the small window of opportunity, sending the two click "go" signal through her earpiece to her waiting friends. She had their attention, now. The entire camp was already forming a circle, leaving a large space for the impromptu duel. It wasn't the original plan, but it would do.

"Rraggh!"

The rage demon, the system gave his name as "Grahz", unleashed another inarticulate roar as his vision cleared. He glared daggers at Jade, approaching more slowly this time, and she didn't need her succubus born empathy to detect the murderous intent behind his burning gaze.

"Stand and fight!" He growled, coming at her again. "Or is running away all your kind can do!"

Jade didn't even try to block his next attack, not after the devastating first hit had shattered her armor's defensive wards with force to spare. She danced away, staying out of Grahz's reach, flinging Phantom in his direction every chance she had. Hit or miss, the enchanted dagger blinked back to her hand a moment later, and she didn't let up as he continued to chase her around the makeshift arena. Other demons called out to them, cheering them on in harsh, guttural tones that were hard to interpret even with the Labyrinth's assistance. Still, the general emotional ebb and flow from the gathered crowd made it clear that most were not wishing her well.

Really?! She thought, incredulously. You're rooting for the guy who's already killed at least three of you in the past minute?!

Just how terrible a reputation did succubi have?

Wonderful. Even other demons hate us.

The whistling sound of a near miss snapped Jade's full attention back on the fight. This wasn't a foe she could afford to take lightly, one more direct hit with his massive weapon would probably cripple her, defensive abilities or no. Jade continued to fight him at range, using her slight edge in speed and disabling spells to keep out of Grahz's reach. Clinging Darkness was especially useful, clouding his vision and allowing her to score more hits on his unarmored body. Unfortunately, even her enchanted daggers lacked the power to cut deep into his flesh. They scraped off his skin like he was made of marble, leaving only shallow cuts marring his bare chest and back.

Damn it! Jade launched herself skyward as her blinding spell faded again, delivering another pair of slashes to his head before flipping and flaring her wings, leaving herself hovering ten feet off the ground on the other side of the arena. Grahz stomped towards her, fury twisting his face into a truly monstrous visage. Blood dripped from numerous cuts on his arms and torso, but even the bleeding enchantment fused into Phantom was unable to create serious wounds on this tough a foe. If she could just use her aura blades, it would be different. Focused mana had immense penetrating power, but that didn't do Jade any good here. As wonderful a gift the celestial energy infused in her spirit was, it wasn't something that could be hidden when her aura was forcefully projected. Jade wasn't sure how a crowd of demons would react to one of their number manifesting luminous essence, but she was pretty sure it wasn't an experience she'd survive.

That being the case, taking on the rage demon directly wasn't going to work. If Jade wanted to win this fight, she had to get creative.

Regrettably, Grahz was not the type to kindly wait for his opponent to dream up an effective strategy. He charged towards her with another roar, and this time Jade felt a spike of magic pulse within his powerful body. The demon grew in size as he stampeded towards her, his body erupting in searing flames that swirled around him like a hellish cyclone. The demons forming the part of the ring behind Jade screeched in fear, shrinking away from the oncoming monster. Jade flew to the side, dodging his first attacks with a set of nimble aerial maneuvers and dropping rune traps as often as the spell's cooldown would allow. If she could just get him pinned down in one of her traps…

Her train of thought was interrupted by an eruption of flame that lanced towards her like a striking serpent. She dropped to the ground and rolled under it, feeling a flash of heat powerful enough to singe her wings even through her innate resistances as it passed overhead. Jade leapt back to her feet, a feeling of intense irritation growing with each new ability this creature threw at her. She clicked her tongue in annoyance.

Did I get the demon race with awesome fire powers? Of course not.

She dodged another strike, then attempted to charm the rage demon as their gazes briefly met. Unsurprisingly, the spell failed to take hold of her enraged opponent.

Nope, I got stuck with the weird sex powers.

Her armor sparked with flashes of blue energy, its overloaded runes struggling to reform a new barrier around her. Grahz approached, flames continuing to course over his enlarged frame. He leered at her, breathing in short, ragged bursts. The rage demon looked worse for wear as well, but he still had plenty of strength.

She'd received Naomi's message. Her friends were through the portal. Now, all she had to do was follow them… easy, right? She stole a quick glance at the crowd of bloodthirsty demons cheering at the spectacle of their battle. Yeah, right. Like they would just let her thr--
