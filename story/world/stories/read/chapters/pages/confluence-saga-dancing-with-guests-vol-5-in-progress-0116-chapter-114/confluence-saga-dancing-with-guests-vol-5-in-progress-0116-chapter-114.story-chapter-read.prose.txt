Nothing is more terrifying as a Diver than running into a Depthsborn where none should be. They stick to their caves, they seem unaware of the tunnels that we Awakened use to reach them.

They repopulate, creatures appearing between one visit and a next if enough time has passed. They present a new challenge, a new set of Affinities, sometimes even new constructions or preparations for ambushes. You cannot be certain you will face the same threat in the same cave, only that it will be of similar difficulty.

Roamers though, you never know where or when you'll find them. Where they'll find you. Or worse, where they decide to settle down and make themselves at home.

~ * ~

Anya sighed as she picked at her food before forcing a smile back onto her face. They were here for Sandra, tonight was her night, her party. She could feel Seb taking in every moment despite his eyes rarely leaving his plate. She'd identified most of the attendees at his request and Sandra had pointed out most of her friends.

So far the evening had gone exactly as she expected, the ritual greetings, the traditional speeches by the Sovereign and relevant members of the Court, even the selection of dishes could have been the backdrop to any of her birthdays in the past. Little ever changed, at least little of importance. The Courtiers rose and fell in importance, the vintage of the wines had changed, apparently it was possible to run out of a traditional favorite, but the festive air was one of routine.

Sandra was taking it slightly better than she would have, her impatience manifesting in the drumming of her heel and the ever so slightly rushed pace at which she ate. Their mother knew but had said nothing, not yet.

Thankfully she had been sat between her sister and Seb, she had no reason or opportunity to speak to her mother. Seb could devote all his attention to the meal and to her, his position at the rightmost of the head table meant he was safe from politics, at least for the time.

She had been worried she might be seated at the Sovereign's left, bumping Tomas to some other seat. She had wondered if they might try to separate her from Seb, seat him with as much precedence as possible rather than at her side. She wondered who had decided on the seating. She knew Sandra was happy to have her there, to be next to her sister once more after so many years, but this was unlikely to have been her action.

Every year? Seb sent.

Every birthday of anyone important enough to be seated at the high table for the night. So dozens of times a year.

The food is good, as good as any restaurant, but always the same?

Changing it would be an offense to someone, she repeated from memory. If we change the menu for one, then we must for each. If we rotate through menus then what guarantee you get the one you wish for?

So nothing ever changes. No one can be offended by the food, the speeches, anything.

Exactly.

Lys stood, glass in hand once more. The hall quieted within moments.

"I trust you have all enjoyed your meals and offered up your best wishes to my Heir," she said into the silence. "This marks the end of our traditional celebration, earlier than has been typical."

Anya heard a few muttered comments, nothing clear enough to be considered a complaint. Seb had clearly noticed the source of a handful, drawing her attention to them as though they were mid combat.

Seb…

You were wondering. And most of them were her peers. Anxious to get to their party?

"For those of you who are not aware, which I trust is none of you, Princess Lysandra has requested that she be allowed to have a celebration that she and her peers would find more enjoyable. We have consented to her request with the understanding that all festivities be constrained to this hall and end at an acceptable hour."

What time is acceptable do you think? Seb sent.

She wanted 10, Sandra wanted 4, I think they agreed on 1. So probably 2?

When do you want to leave?

Anya found herself sighing again. She wanted to leave now, she wanted to have stayed home rather than come to Holding Lys. She was here, she was supporting her sister on her birthday.

An hour ago. But we're going to be here a while. Unless you can think of a reason we need to leave?

~ * ~

Seb had forgotten how much he disliked parties. Dinner hadn't been horrible, he'd been to a handful of weddings and corporate events so he hadn't been completely lost. Apparently Sandra and her peers enjoyed similar music to what he'd heard when he was their age, not the exact same, the few artist names he heard mentioned implied that this was Awakened music, but the roots were there. Heavy beats, deep, complex rhythms, a focus on energy and repeated movement with minimal vocals.

So much easier when we don't have to yell, he sent.

Huh? Oh, the band? Anya answered before leaning against him as they sat alone at the table.

A small handful of Courtiers had remained, from the looks on their faces they were there as chaperones, or something similar. They certainly weren't enjoying themselves if the looks the gave each other were any indication.

She looks like she's having fun, he sent.

Sandra? She always does.

He felt something over the bond and squeezed her hand. It took Anya a moment to reciprocate, a hesitation that he knew wasn't normal for either of them. He could feel her attention focused on the dance floor, the empty space, not the dancers.

Anya?

..Yes?

Dance?

No. I'm fine. Its her party, we're here for her.

He shook his head and stood, pulling gently on her hand and looking down at her. She came to her feet smoothly, her movements matching his yet still hesitant.

You sure?

"Anya!" Sandra shouted as she rushed over. "You got mother to agree! We can have a party and not just a dinner."

"You're having a party," Seb said. "You should be with your friends."

"Right!" she yelled before spinning away, losing herself in the small crowd among shouts and cheers.

Thank you, Anya said.

He didn't say anything, simply took a half step away from her and tugged. She didn't stumble, she didn't fall, she stepped past him reflexively, nearly touching but avoiding the collision. He felt her hesitation fading, the underlying feelings still there but disrupted.

He moved again, his feet finding the beat as his proximity forced her to move in time. She didn't object, her eyes lifting to his as he pulled her into the music on the edge of the dance floor.

He'd never been one for dancing, he'd never been the one that drew attention or did more than sway to the sound while lost in his own world. He hadn't been a good dancer, nor an enthusiastic one, but he'd managed to enjoy himself all the same. Dancing now that he was Awakened was different. His body could match the tempo without effort, his balance and coordination never left him awkwardly out of place when the beat changed.

He felt Anya relax, felt the depth of her smile as they danced together. It wasn't sparring, it wasn't fighting, yet at the same time it could have been. The music dictated, the music liberated. They found their meld, their awareness swallowing the entire hall, keeping them to the periphery and out of the spotlight.

The song changed, the rhythm growing more complex yet in the moment it could not have been clearer. He caught a glimpse of Sandra as they turned, her eyes on them in that moment as she smiled before someone took her hand and spun her away as well. He felt Anya's pleasure, her movements growing more confident, more aggressive as she pushed him to keep his attention focused.

This- she sent. Thank you.

He felt the moment the music went to change, his Affinity pulling him through the change and dragging Anya along with him as he held her close. The pace slowed, the energy calmed. Anya all but melted into him, neither leading, neither following as they moved as one. The rhythm still allowed for antics, for showing off, while calling for comfort and closeness.

Even the adult couples moved to join in, finding their own islands of movement within the chaos. Sandra flitted by, her steps confident as she let one partner slip away before twirling through the arms of another and into a cluster that giggled as they all seemed to be undecided as to how to pair off.

She all but flew back to them, their coordination letting her slip between them in an impromptu trio before they took turns letting her spin and leap, all but tossing her between them when she seemed insistent on trying.

Seb steadied her as the song came to a close, giving her the moment to regain her center before she ran back to her friends.

"Thank you! You don't have to stay any longer than you want sis!" she shouted over her shoulder before the music swallowed her whole.

Home? Anya sent.

Home, he agreed.

They had nothing to collect, no reason to linger. They could leave without any further ado. Seb felt Anya tug lightly on his hand as she paused at the entrance to the hall, her eyes focused on the dance once more.

I never… before.

She shook herself before turning to lead him out of the Holding.

Any time you want from now on, he answered as the music faded into the distance.
