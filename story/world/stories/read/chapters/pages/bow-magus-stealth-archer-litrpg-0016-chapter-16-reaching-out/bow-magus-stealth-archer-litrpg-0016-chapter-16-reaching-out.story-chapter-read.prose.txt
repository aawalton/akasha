Freyja stepped out from the cover of the brush, her eyes locked on Rex’s. “Found you.”

“Look,” Rex said, raising his hands, ready to activate [ Transference ] if needed. “Whatever they told you, it’s a lie. I didn’t hurt anybody intentionally. They came looking for me. And sure, I might have messed up a little. But I sure as the hells didn’t do anything that deserved getting murdered for.”

“Murdered?” Her face softened, looking innocent. But Rex had seen it. When she had first spotted him, there had been a sharpness to her gaze. “What are you talking about, Rex?”

I’d better be careful with my words.

“The hunters. The guys who work for Mulvaney. They came looking for me. I could sense the killing intent in their auras. I just defended myself.”

“Really? You sensed them? That’s impressive. Not everyone can read auras like that,” She said. “To tell you the truth, I didn’t think you had that in you. You don’t look like a killer to me. What exactly happened?”

Rex took a step back.

That face. It was odd, he thought as he tried to make sense of the weirdness. Something about her is off, but I still have my backup plan, and I came all the way out here. I might as well hear her out.

“I was just out hunting. Nothing out of the ordinary. When I came back, I overheard the other hunters looking for me. And that’s when I felt it. They wanted to kill me,” Rex said slowly and carefully. “And so, naturally, when they found me, I defended myself.”

“Against all of them?”

“Some goblins were involved. But I handled myself fairly well.”

Her eyes widened like a curious child. “Goblins? You must be amazing. Tell me, how did you do it?”

“Um,” Rex tugged on his collar. She was creeping him out a little too much, but it was also the longest he’d spoken to a pretty girl. The sooty fiends of Dorog were not much to look at. “—I… would tell you, but…”

“But?” Her lashes fluttered.

“Tell me, you don’t think there’s anything weird about the farm?” He asked.

“Weird? I mean, yeah. Obviously. We’re out here in the middle of nowhere guarding farmers. That’s not particularly normal.”

“Right. But what about the farmers? Like how they’re treated.”

“What about them?”

It’s now or never.

“Well, the truth is, we think they’re slavers.”

“We?”

“Oops. Slip of the tongue. I mean, I do. I think they’re slavers.”

Shit! I don’t know if I can trust her yet; I can’t have her suspecting Andy of anything.

“Really? Real slavers… that changes everything. The lords will have our heads if they think we’re working them. Assuming they really are slavers. But how can you be so sure?”

I can’t tell if she’s on my side, but she’s clearly curious. I can use this.

“Um, look. I’ll tell you more, but first I need to speak with Andy.”

“Andy?”

“Yeah. The guy I shared a room with. I want to speak with him.”

She bent her head—confused.

I sound like a madman. But if this might be my chance.

If Freyja really was just another naive recruit, she likely had no power to help him with anything. But Andy was the only person he felt he could trust, and he had to at least try to get in contact while he had a little leverage.

“You want me to bring your friend here?” She said, craning forward as if trying to pull the response out.

“Yes! Exactly! Can you?”

She was still for a moment, and then nodded. “Maybe? I mean, I could try. But you would need to stay right here. I can't go searching the entire forest for you again, now, can I? How’s that sound?”

Suspicious as fuck, but if she’s telling the truth.

“Deal,” he said. “You bring Andy, and I’ll stay right here.”

“Okay, I'll go get him,” Freyja said, walking up to him and placing a hand on his shoulder before heading back to the farm. “See you soon.”

He kinda liked her touch and kind of felt like punching himself for letting his imagination get carried away. Now was not the time.

Calm down. You’re an adventurer now, and there will be plenty of girls on the road… Well, hopefully.

Of course, Rex wasn’t just going to put his trust in someone he had just spoken to for the first time. She seemed nice enough, but that look she had given him still sent a shiver down his spine. And why was she out here alone?

But Rex remembered Andy. He genuinely believed that he was a good guy and couldn’t live with himself if he didn’t at least try to do right by him. He needed to tell him everything that had happened. He didn’t trust Credo’s men in the slightest, and he didn’t want Andy to become the next unlucky guy to be put on their hit list.

You’re probably overthinking. She’s just a girl. It’s not like she’s been here long enough to be indoctrinated anyway.

Besides, he still had Slimer, who was a great backup.

I need a trap, but something I can set up fast.

If Freyja were to betray him, he knew that Jango and Mulvaney were the strongest, with the most powerful auras. And he figured he would be able to sense them coming easily enough. If he did, he’d run. Otherwise, he felt like he had a chance, especially if he had a nice trap for backup.

There was a large tree right by where he was standing that was perfect for what he had in mind. Rex used [ Corrosive Touch ] on the trunk, and used [ Harden ] on the same spot a few seconds later.

“Whew. That actually worked.”

He had waited until the acid had just made its way through the trunk and quickly used [ Harden ] before the tree collapsed on him, creating a mana tether. In truth, he hadn’t been entirely sure whether or not it would work.

I am a genius, aren’t I?

The tree looked sturdy enough for now, but he certainly didn’t want to be around when he deactivated [ Harden ].

He also got an arrow ready with [ Corrosive Touch ], stabbing it into the ground at his feet. Then he drank from his canteen and had some jerky as he waited, hoping to recover as much mana as possible.

He really, really hoped that Freyja was as honest as she was cute. And that everything he had just done was for nothing. He genuinely wanted to catch up with Andy, and who knew, maybe a happy life in a big house on Elysia with a beautiful, freckled wife one day.

He had gotten a little distracted with his daydreams. But his senses were as tight as ever, and he looked up the moment he felt something, spotting two silhouettes coming through the trees.

About time.

He quickly spotted Freyja, but Andy had his cowl over his head, and his aura felt a little different.

This doesn’t feel right.

“Hey, Rex,” she waved and winked.

The moment she took another step closer, Rex knew he had fucked up. She was smiling. A bit too widely. She was obviously trying to hold his attention. That wasn’t what caught his attention. It was her aura. There was an obvious mismatch between it and what she was displaying, like she was trying to hold something back.

Despite knowing something was wrong, he tried to remain calm. If they believed he was falling for their trap, or whatever was planned, then he still held an edge over them.

That was when he noticed four others. There were two of them on either flank, carefully creeping through the forest brush. They were doing their best to shroud themselves and their auras as they made their way around to encircle him. And they were pretty good, but not good enough.

He had to make a decision now. He could easily escape with [ Transference ], but doing so might just spell Slimer’s demise.

That little bastard, Rex groaned, knowing he was already too attached to throw the little blob's life away unless he had absolutely no other choice.

But he couldn’t just run, either. If they had Skills like [ Powershot ], they’d easily be able to shoot him down. Besides, he hadn’t hit level 6 yet and hadn’t gained the Attribute boost that came with it. Without it, they would likely outrun him.

He still had the tree, though.

What should I do? Just wait for them and spring the trap?

If he just waited for Freyja to reach him and deactivated [ Harden ], there was a good chance he would just kill her and perhaps whoever was with her. The problem was, he still didn’t really know if she was in cahoots with Jango.

After all, she could have easily been tricked or threatened into betraying him. He wasn’t about to risk his life for her, but he certainly didn’t know enough to target her just yet.

I need to take out Jango’s men somehow. Then I can deal with these two.

“Rex, are you okay?” Freyja said, slowing as she approached him.

“Um, yeah? Why do you ask?” Rex smiled, trying to act casual.

“You’re sweating. And twitching. Calm down. We’re here now. It’s all going to be fine.” She reached out as she kept walking forward.

“Yeah. Right. All is fine. And is that Andy?”

Freyja nudged the man at her side.

“Hi, Rex,” said the cloaked figure. He didn’t speak loudly, probably because he wasn’t Andy.

His mind was moving a million miles a minute. He needed a plan. The four men had already surrounded him, and they were getting closer. He could also feel their auras spike a little, as if planning to attack.

“Thank you!” Rex said suddenly, lunging forward and pulling Freyja into a tight hug, turning as he did, so that her back would face the flanking men.

“Ah-ha-what are you doing, Rex?!”

He could feel her squirm and push back against him. But he didn’t let go, hoping that they weren’t going to just kill both of them, which was a massive gamble since he had a feeling they didn’t care too much about the lives of their recruits. But there was something about Freyja he just couldn’t shake. She was different somehow.

“I know this is an ambush,” he whispered. “I don’t know why you betrayed me, but I know I’m not going down alone.”

Suddenly, Freyja’s entire demeanor changed.

“Let go of me! I’m not on your side,” she hissed. “You have no idea who I am.”

“Huh? What are you talking about?”

“Shut up and let go of me! If you release me this instant, I will make sure they don’t kill you.”

“Wait, you’re not a recruit, are you? Who are you?”

Rex had known something was off about her, but he had not expected such a shift.

“If I told you anything more, I would have to kill you. Let go this instant, and I’ll see to it that you’re only made a slave. Who knows? If you’re as good at sensing auras as you claim, I might even have use for you. Heck, if you do it right now, I might even tell you where the real Andy is.”

Rex didn’t trust her, but he relaxed his grip enough to look into her eyes. Who in the hells was she? She glared back at him, but it was different from the odd feeling she had given him before.

Is she actually telling the truth?

He focused his senses on Jango’s men; they weren’t far away, and he got a pretty good read on them. Then, he released her, ready to activate [ Transference ] at a moment's notice.

It wasn’t that he trusted her, though. Her mention of Andy had compelled him. Where was he, and why hadn’t she brought him?

The moment she was free, Frejya glared back and dusted herself off. Then, she raised her hand.

“It’s okay. He has surrendered. You can come out.”

It took a second, but four heads popped out from the brush, looking across at each other, very confused.

“Jango wants him dead.”

“So?” Freyja barked back. “You are aware that I am here as a direct report to Credo, aren’t you?”

The men held their lips. They obviously were aware, but shared some doubtful glances with one another.

“Good. We’ll take him back to the farm,” Freyja said, then looked at Rex.

“He killed a bunch of Mulvaney’s men.”

“So? A few weak thugs got killed by some kid who just gained their Class, and you expect me to care? There are bigger things going on here than hunting deer in the woods. Besides, killing him will not answer any of my questions. And finally, if you talk back to me one more time, Credo will hear of it.”

The man swallowed and didn’t say a word.

It’s now or never, Rex thought.

He was conflicted. He didn’t trust them in the slightest and still had his tree trap. Was it really wise to let them take him? Then again, with [ Transference ], he could escape at any time, even if his hands were bound. And it didn’t look like they were about to kill him.

Fuck. This sucks. But what if Andy is in trouble?

Could Rex live with the knowledge that his actions had gotten Andy into trouble, and when an opportunity arose, he discarded him, caring only for his own safety?

“Hands,” Freyja snapped her fingers, and Rex did as he was told.

One of Jango’s men came and tied his wrists, then gave the end of the rope to Freyja, who led him back toward the farm.

“So, what about Andy?”

“Oh, don’t worry. You’ll be reunited soon enough. But first, I have a few questions.”
