Twelve hours later, with the purple moon still hanging menacingly in the sky, the encampment had been reinforced with stone walls. It was all thanks to James and his raptor, who had carried carts full of cement and stones from the quarry.

As the timer ticked down to zero, another portal spawned to the south, merging with the one that already stood there. Four chariots, each pulled by a black triceratops-like monster, surged out from the gate. Each chariot carried a cart with three doghead archers inside. James didn’t hesitate or wait—he released his arrow into the mix.

Andrew was a step behind him. His spear glowed golden, and a gleaming meteor chased after James’ arrow. James used his bow skill, Homing Shot, so even though the enemies were still on the horizon, his shot pierced one of the dinosaurs’ heads.

Meanwhile, Andrew’s skills were less sophisticated, brute-forcing their way through the triceratops’s skull. Both beasts slipped mid-charge, flipping the carts behind them and wrecking them completely. The remaining two chariots continued their charge, golden light flashing from the archers inside. Moments later, a rain of yellowish bolts shot toward the encampment.

“Incoming! Take cover!”

The rain of arrows punctured fist-sized holes into the reinforced wall, prompting the mercenaries to scramble for cover—even if it meant risking exposure to the moonlight. Fortunately, the enemies’ bow skills were linear, making them easier to dodge.

James drew another arrow, ready to fire again, while Andrew reached for another spear to throw.

“The north gate also spawned cavalry! They’re heading for us!” one mercenary shouted.

“They’re not coming out together?” Andrew asked, bewildered.

“Infantry approaching from the sides!” another mercenary added.

“James, we’ll handle the south! You take the north!”

“On it.”

James sprinted toward the northern gate, his axe glowing blue. The moment he arrived, he hurled it at one of the charging cavalry, activating his axe skill, Boomerang. The axe struck true—the beast froze solid, and its own momentum sent it crashing and shattering on the ground, crushing the cart behind it.

As the cavalry charged toward the base like a runaway train, James fired his mind magic. When the purple orb hit the beast, it inflicted excruciating pain—far beyond anything it had ever felt. The pain was so overwhelming that it slipped on its legs and collapsed to the ground, incapacitated, throwing its riders from the chariot.

But James couldn’t stop them all. Two of the triceratops slammed their bodies into the defensive emplacements—the abatis that Andrew had constructed.

The abatis were designed by medieval empires specifically to counter cavalry charges, to slow infantry advances, and to break formations. It worked just as intended. The wooden stakes sank deep into the triceratops’ bodies, skewering them. James had to nod at the NPC-like behavior of these monsters, charging headfirst into sharpened logs.

Alas, the poor wooden stakes from the forest couldn’t fully absorb the momentum from the triceratops’ charge and were destroyed. The beasts tumbled, throwing their riders out. The impact broke part of the roof, allowing the menacing moonlight to pour in.

Using his ice magic, James finished off the remnants of the army.

[You have cleared the eighth wave. Casualties: 0. Injuries: 15.Time elapsed: 55 / 120 hours. The next wave is in 30 minutes.]

[Congratulations, you have received 500 coins. A supply of 500 kg of Portland cement and 500 stone blocks will be delivered.]

“What the fuck? Thirty minutes? Hurry up! Get to the wounded! Buy potions if you have to! Civilians, fix the roof and get back to the shelter!” Andrew shouted as he ran around the encampment. There were many wounded—the archers who survived the fallen chariots had given them a hard time. Moreover, the infantry charge from the west had been far more intense than expected.

“Why did those archers come with chariots?” James muttered.

“They’re inspired by medieval Egyptian or Arabic horse archers,” a mercenary who knew his history replied. “But none of them ever charged straight into spikes.”

“Stop talking and start working! We need to fix those abatis ASAP! We don’t know when another huge monster will come. Hurry up, people!” Andrew ran about, casting healing magic and throwing ropes toward the villagers, who even braved the moonlight to repair the broken roof and abatis as quickly as possible.

Alas, there wasn’t enough time before the timer hit zero. They hadn’t even patched the holes in the wall caused by the archers’ bow skills.

As Andrew feared, the large gate spawned two monsters—this time, armored giant rhinoceros beetles. They crept toward the encampment, dragging two chariots filled with soldiers. James fired an arrow at one of them, but the golden arrow bounced harmlessly off its metallic shell.

“Its underside should be soft. We’ll have to wait until they get closer,” James muttered. He could probably deal with the one at the north gate, but what about the south gate? Could Andrew handle it alone?

James considered charging in with his raptor to deal with the threat up close. If those beetles were alone, he could do it—but the soldiers on the chariots surely had weapon skills. It was a risky move.

“Fuck it. Since when have I been such a coward? That guy’s mine! You guys can take the one at the south gate!” James called for his raptor and donned his cloak.

“What are you doing?” Andrew shouted.

“We’re only halfway through this mission. If those beasts reach the encampment, they’ll destroy the barracks and the walls. We won’t survive that. I have to fight them out in the open,” James said.

He then charged forward with his raptor. The ten-foot beetle loomed even larger up close. Ice spears shot up from beneath it, but contrary to James’s expectations, they shattered on impact—its underbelly was tougher than he thought.

Still, the attack was enough to draw its attention. The beast turned toward James as he fired ice bullets at the naked dogmen riding on the chariot.

The dogmen didn’t stay still—they roared and half of them leapt down from the cart. Spears in hand, they charged at James while two archers and a mage behind them unleashed attacks toward him and his raptor.

James’s mind magic worked wonders to incapacitate the enemies. His raptor leapt onto the beetle, aiming to rip the dog’s throat apart with its claws. After dispatching the mage and the archers, James dismounted and faced the three approaching spearmen.

The hard part about this enemy wasn’t the soldiers—it was the beetle, which resisted his mind magic and continued marching toward the encampment. Not even James’s sword skills were enough to injure it. Worse, his cloak had been torn during the fight, and the moonlight began to sap his strength. He had to finish this monster quickly.

“Oi, how the heck am I supposed to deal with this beetle? It’s immune to all attacks!” James complained to the system.

[This beetle can only be defeated in a very specific way. Further information classified.]

“So there is a way to beat it. But how? Should I mount it and peel its armor off piece by piece?” James muttered. When he lodged his sword into the beetle’s shell, it barely left a scratch.

The beetle kept charging toward the abatis, plowing through it as if it were kicking pebbles on the road. The one on Andrew’s side also showed no sign of stopping.

After destroying everything in its path, the two beetles collided with each other. Their horns pierced through each other’s armor as they thrashed about inside the settlement. That’s when James saw it—a gap in their armor where the flesh was exposed.

James lunged forward. A red glow flashed from his sword as he plunged it deep into the exposed flesh. The smell of cooked meat filled the air as the beetle burned from within, collapsing lifelessly. James turned to the other beetle and drove his Sword of Khine into it—the beast met the same fate.

[You have cleared the ninth wave. Casualties: 0. Injuries: 25. Time elapsed: 55 / 120 hours. Congratulations, you have received 500 coins. You have leveled up to Level 60. Your Attacker class has leveled up to Level 44. The next wave is in 20 minutes. A supply of 500 kg of Portland cement and 500 rebars will be delivered.]

The notification made James wince. The assault wasn’t over—and this time, the next wave would arrive even sooner.

“Hurry! Potions! Civilians, get out of the barracks and rebuild it! We need a roof over our heads, right now!” Andrew, being the leader type, quickly rallied the team.

James handed two of his potions to Andrew, drank the last hematonic potion he had bought earlier, and picked up his axe. The potion restored his strength, which had been drained by the moon’s effect. James darted toward the logs, spending a thousand coins to buy fifty meters of steel wire. He and the group patched up the roof with it and the cement provided by the system.

Time passed in the blink of an eye; the next assault came long before Andrew finished repairing the destroyed roof. Hundreds of dogmen and jackals emerged from the portals with the sole intention of killing the survivors.

“I’ll handle the north gate,” James muttered, and Andrew nodded.

The fight was bloody for the mercs. James was doing fine at the north gate—he often delved into dungeons solo, after all. Moreover, his skill set was the bane of living monsters here. His fire breath, ice, and mind magic worked wonders against the crowd. Every time he swung his sword, two or three monsters were cut down.

By spamming his sword skill Hurricane and axe skill Power Strike, James held the tide by himself. The jackals and dogmen bit him like rabid animals; their fangs were long and sharp, even drawing blood from his flaming fur—but James persevered.

An hour later, the base had won against insurmountable odds. The hastily patched roof and structures collapsed, but they had survived. Moreover, they had done so without a single casualty.

[You have cleared the tenth wave. Casualties: 0. Injuries: 40. Time elapsed: 56 / 120 hours. Congratulations, you have received 500 coins. The next wave is in 40 hours. A supply of 500 stones, 500 meters of steel wire, and 500 rebars will be delivered.]

[Warning: An ambush party has been detected. Survivors will be attacked when they try to gather resources in the forest, the oasis, and the quarry.]

The menacing moon soon sank beneath the horizon as the warmth of the sun rose. With bloodied hands and weary bodies, they welcomed the dawn.
