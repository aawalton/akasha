Summary of book 1

We were introduced to Zero-Twelve, a killbot with a composite mind including a Human Resources Module (brain in a box), called Nicolai, back on Cyberpunk Earth in the year 2400ish.

Zero-Twelve is/was the property of GRECKON inc, one of the largest corporations on Earth.

During an attempted hit on Claire Del’Brugnie, an oligarch, Heaven’s Great Game arrived, seizing Earth and forcefully inducting it into the Game.

Those on Earth were given a choice: Join now, or join later (for join later, the Game decides when they join). All human’s on Earth will be brought into the Game within a year from the time the choice was given.

Nicolai accepted. Zero-Twelve was seemingly destroyed but the Modules remained with him, asleep in the back of his mind.

A gigantic Eye told him to Find the Lizard.

Appearing in the Castle, Nicolai worked to understand the world around him, as well as surviving and gaining power. He met Kleos, a talking severed head, who provided him guidance.

After gaining the ability to fly he reached the library, retrieved a book on Rituals, and with it created a Soul Trap.

Descending into the prison with the intent of using that Soul Trap alongside the many Oma crystals down there to rapidly complete his Seed of an Undefined Soul, he met a man called Harold. He also met a strange and still corpse calling itself Forgotten, who asked him to retrieve its Heart. After mentioning the Lizard to this being, its attitude shifted.

Nicolai and Harold found the Heart, but a demonic Centipede killed Harold.

After completing his Seed, Nicolai gave the Heart to Forgotten, who revealed itself to be Paxolnaz, a powerful Demon. In payment Paxolnaz cast a spell that prevented the undead from perceiving Nicolai, who robbed the undead’s store room, taking a number of Imbued items (enchanted weapons and apparel).

Back in his safe place with Kleos, Nicolai had completed a Quest for returning the Heart to Paxolnaz, and was rewarded with a Shimmer Poncho, which uses light bending technology to give the user a form of invisibility. He had also retrieved a Glove of Shielding, which created a magical shield, a Darting Talon Rapier, which can fly through the air, under the control of the user, and a few other things.

However, he was unable to remove the slave band around his neck, requiring the aid of another.

Thus he attempted to rescue Johan, someone he had once encountered, who was being attacked by Chosen—a large and growing group of Earthborn cultists, serving a powerful Cyborg called Vikrum.

His attempt failed. Injured and on the edge of consciousness, he retreated, only to blunder into a room where a group was hiding.

Following this he ended up joining with this group, which included John, Cait, Perro, Azure, Karl, Elena, Old Ben, Katie, and Sara.

With his Seed complete, and his band removed, Nicolai is able to at last become a Cultivator. He gains his first Node, a heart Node, and gains true Soul Sense. It transpires he has quite good talent/aptitude.

He also discovers his newly grown Soul has some unexpected parts—such as a spiritual Mask, which is attempting to be human.

Immediately after this his safe place is attacked by Chosen. Nicolai slays them. His Mask convinces him to stay with the others.

He leads them to a new safe place, sneaking into the home of Jo and Beth, two sisters. He forms a Contract with them, after providing materials to allow Beth to become a Cultivator and thus saving her from a strange illness.

From there, he learns of the location of the Trade Link, and his stock in the castle begins to rapidly rise.

Using the Trade Link he works to gain funds and influence within the castle. He fights the Chosen wherever they are. He goes to the Library and encounters an undead Guardian while collecting Memory Tomes, which contain a great deal of useful information; one on Symbiotes, one on creatures of Nightmare, and one on Natural Treasures.

While retreating from the Guardian and attempting to set an ambush, he and the others are attacked by group of humans.

After killing them he has a bit of a moment and discovers there is a Hole in his Soul, one leading elsewhere.

Thus he begins to realise something is seriously wrong with him and he needs to deal with it.

Later, while attempting to rescue Maxine, the radio woman, he finds an Angel on a towertop, guarded by two Centipedes; Brother and Sister.

After bargaining with them, and aiding them in slaying the Bird, he talks with the Angel.

It tells him he is being pursued by a True Demon—Paxolnaz, the Demon who he gave freedom to. It wishes to turn him into a Skin, possessing his body.

The Angel tells him that he can listen for the calls of the Unwinder, who Paxolnaz is bound to free. When called away to this duty, he will be free to act without it watching.

Paxolnaz, in response to this, uses the Blind Judge, a powerful Heaven-Type Symbiote, to alter the Contract between Nicolai and it. It infects him with Soul Rot, a dangerous malady that will destroy his Soul and lead to his death. The only way to be free of it, is to follow the Demon’s bidding by seizing the Demonic Blade, guarded by an undead in the depths of the castle.

Maxine, rescued from the tower, begins work forming a Coalition against the Chosen. Nicolai aids her in this and acts as a trader and arms dealing, arming the Coalition.

But during this time his primary focus is on securing whatever he needs for the endgame between himself and Paxolnaz.

He slays the Angel from a distance, after claiming an M99 anti-material rifle in a battle with Gilvine, one of Vikrum’s elites, who he leaves alive after realising she has treacherous intentions towards Vikrum. The Angel’s golden blood forms a pool on the towertop.

The endgame arrives. Nicolai sets a bomb above the castle core, which traps the Unwinder, and tricks Paxolnaz into using its second use of the Blind Judge, so the Symbiote is no longer a danger to him. He seizes the Demonic Blade but with the aid of the Modules, a spine-jack, and the Mask, he is able to reach the towertop with the Angel’s blood.

There he enacts an energy flip, but not on himself. He does it to the Demonic Blade, which turns into an Angelic Blade.

With this balancing force, he is able to better control the darkness within him. His major weakness is removed.

At the same time, by destroying the weakened castle core, he frees the Unwinder and Paxolnaz is torn away from the castle, forced to follow along with its masters aims.

He receives numerous rewards, including a recharge of his Artifact Blade, an Assimilator (a machine-factory capable of operating alone and creating many things), and two Mark enhancements; PathFinder and TreasureHunter.

The castle collapses and Nicolai floats away into the jungle, leaving everyone behind. His journey to find the Lizard begins.
