The first of the stone searchers to arrive outside the metal encased Pearson airport clashed. Screams belted, skills fired, lives were taken, and Cores of monster and human variety were ripped from navels or from what passed as navels. With six inches of steel encasing the building, people and monsters tried to break through while fighting everything and everyone, all in hunt of the Tax Guild Stone rumored to be there.

They fought in a pool of sulfuric sludge. Dead and unable to hold the countless Links together, the automatic turrets and stabbing pencils of Keanu Bereaves that had previously guarded the fortress had melted away into component parts.

Adventurers backstabbed their fellow clan members with high grade weapons. Tiny, pea-sized monsters came from the underground like a carpet of gnashing teeth to nip at ankles and inject stacking poison. Pigeons and crows swarmed and charged in aerial battle formations. One man lofting a tattered Canadian flag from an age past called forth a gust of wind just to sweep over himself in hope of an action photo. Larger monsters swallowed limbs and peeled faces, and whenever someone had enough time, a potent skill was cast to break through the airport’s last physical, non-magic protection.

CRASH

BOOM

THUNK, THUNK, THUNK, THUNK, THUNK

A torrent of flame melting steel and cracking apart concrete cut off as the enflamed searcher had their head ripped off.

And inside the airport, a mouthless gnome wielding a tiny, clear shield scrambled to avoid getting eaten.

“Sir Brody!” the female Gnome, Dandelion, attempted to command from atop a priceless, unique vehicle, “Please ensure your Bonded does not eat Acorn! He and I are here to help, not hinder.”

“We’re trying! Tank!” Alex yelled as he chased after the dog. “Stop it! Stop trying to eat Acorn and Dandelion!”

Brody grunted and reached for the pup again, but Tank lowered his large body and weaved around a modified Nitsubushu Lancer that was more engine than car. The dog screeched against the floor, sliding first, then crashed into a blacked-out ice cream truck and crumpled the metal.

Acorn Turtleshell, barely introduced by his partner gnome Dandelion Datura before Tank had gotten excited, with a hole in his porcelain face where his mouth should have been, spun to face Tank and raised his shield. Only a foot high and concaved, barely larger than he, the shield was thick clear plastic strapped to his one remaining muscled arm. With deep brown eyes he stared down the hound through nothing but plastic and braced his legs.

Tank clanked playfully after the male gnome and activated his [Big Chomp]. Like a snapping turtle, and faster than normal eyes could catch, his lower jaw dislocated as his head shot out to swallow the colourful creature whole.

Acorn waited until the last moment. Trained by Marzipan herself, their Chief Battle Warden, he knew this was an opportunity to raise his and Dandelion’s regard amongst the Garden Gnomes of Allen Garden. With physical limitations inflicted upon him prior to his awakening, he would never regrow his limb or mouth, but Marzipan had seen courage in his eyes and Dandelion had seen beauty in his heart. This opportunity to prove himself would not be squandered. He would ensure the safety of Alex and provide aide in finding the Tax Guild Stone. There wasn’t any other option. Emilio himself had ordered it so, and Petal Gravewhistle had let it be known.

At the last possible moment, more so to test himself, Acorn leapt and tumbled in the air toward his love and squad commander, Dandelion Datura.

CRUNCH

CRASH

“Aye, Acorn!” Dandelion called proudly while pointing her miniature gun to the roof. “Defensive posture on me!”

Tank’s skill swallowed a barrel size chunk of the car, and a moment later the dog pounded into remaining ruined metal. The car lurched from the impact, and quick as a hungry hound, Tank was back on his feet searching for the escaped gnome.

“Tank!” Brody grunted. “They…friends! Enough!”

“With your Link!” Alex shouted. “Tell him with your Link!”

WOOF!

“Protection is admirable, Big Hound,” Dandelion chuckled with a high voice as she lovingly watched her man spinning in the air. “I protect Acorn just as he protects me. He is just much better than any realize upon a first glance. You see one arm, no mouth, and a shield. But look at his eyes.”

Acorn landed with a clink on the car’s hood in front of Dandelion with his shield forward.

WOOF! WOOF!

Tank spotted the Gnomes and bounded again, ready to barrel through Brody and Alex.

“…No!” Brody shouted, trying and slightly panicking as he reached for his connection to the dog.

“Got to tell him with your soul, Lord Brody,” Dandelion poked her head from behind Acorn and waved her gun lazily.

Fashioned in military garb before the System arrived, Dandelion sported camo pants, combat boots, and a green tank top over her muscled arms. With a permanent buzz cut and eye black grease under her eyes, she’d ditched the porcelain rifle for something much more apt of her skills, magical or otherwise.

A violent thud against the exterior of the airport shook the ground.

“Oh, they’re getting closer now,” She sniffed. “We best be getting you out of here and to the stone.”

Tank was about to filter his big body through the boys, but Brody reached internally instead of shouting out.

Stop! Stop it! They’re our friends and not toys! They’re here to help us, Tank. Please, stop trying to eat them! For me?

Finally hearing him, Tank put on the breaks.

Sparks flew as he dug metal claws into concrete and tore up the floor before coming to a stop right in front of Brody. Once stopped, he simply lapped his tongue up at his Companion and tilted his head in confusion.

Don’t eat the creatures? But he’d always eaten everyone and everything for his last master. He supposed he didn’t have to, even if they looked like toys from his past.

“Phew,” Dandelion said while leaping from the car. “Now we can get down to business.”

She landed gracefully with a roll and walked toward the boys and the dog. A moment later, Acorn jumped to land beside her, his shield held casually by his side.

“Sorry…about that.” Brody apologized while scratching Tank behind his ears. His tail beat the floor so hard it threw chips into the air.

Dandelion waved with her free hand. “Happens. Now, how may we be of assistance?” She gestured toward the shaking walls and screams of fighting outside. “Those walls won’t hold long, and I don’t believe there are any Gnomish tunnels nearby we can reach.”

“Wait, wait, wait,” Alex stepped forward. “Get the Stone? Look, I’m happy Petal sent you to help us, and sorry about Tank, he just gets excited, but get the Stone?” He jammed his thumb over his head toward the walls.

“Hear that? That’s thousands of people coming to tear this place apart. Thousands to come and tear us apart!”

Dandelion gave her squeaky chuckle again, shaking her head.

“See this?” She said, hoisting her gun up. “GnUzi 45, enchanted silencer, a Relic red sight, and importantly,” she flashed teeth, “One of my Skills chums surrounding matter into whatever bullet I want.”

The gun must have been the size of her whole body, but the Gnome held it up comfortably.

Brody and Alex blinked at her, then at each other, then back towards the Gnome.

Alex shook his head and started, “Infinite bullet gnome, gotcha,” he then nodded toward Acorn. “And him? Does his shield absorb everything?”

Dandelion clapped Acorn on the shoulder and threw her chin in the air.

“Oh, don’t you worry about Acorn. Missing an arm, but I’m still alive a hundred times over because of him.”

Acorn didn’t respond or give a reaction, just stared at them all with unblinking eyes behind his shield. Dandelion’s hand moved from his shoulder to caress the mouthless Gnome’s cheek.

“Ninja shield gnome. Alright, that’s badass. Then how the hell are we even going to get out of here? They’re coming on all sides! We don’t even know where Keanu Bereaves hid the bloody—”

WOOF!

Everyone’s heads snapped toward Tank. The dog, excited by the attention, barked again, and looked at his master.

“Brody?” Alex asked, noticing the sly grin his clone wore.

Arching an eyebrow, Brody picked his head up and looked at them all.

“Tank knows…where Stone,” Brody gurgled. “Tank…take us. Right…boy?”

WOOF!

“Oh, shit on the System,” Alex pinched the bridge of his nose and sighed as the walls shook around them.

The banging was getting louder as the searchers applied more force. They were close to breaking in.

“And how are we going to get there? We’re going to fight through a mosh pit of people and monsters?!” Alex asked, throwing his hands up and turning around. All around him were countless magical vehicles.

“I’m fast, Brody’s fast, Tank is a fucking tank, and the Gnomes can probably handle themselves. But we need to fly out of here to have a chance!”

WOOF!

Tank bounded off with a grinning Brody in tow towards one vehicle that the dog had always wanted to ride. His previous master had never let him come along.

“Brody, where are you going?” Alex called, but Brody just followed his dog.

“Where—where are they going?” He said to the gnomes.

Dandelion snorted and ran after Brody and Tank. Acorn, forever silent, finally looked at Alex and shrugged before chasing after his love.

“Hate not being in the loop.” Alex muttered, last to chase after everyone but quick to catch up.

Skills cracked against the thick metal exterior. The battle raged outside, with people and monsters slopping through blood and sludge to hack, bite, and cast forth hurt. Bodies pressed forward or were trampled underfoot, and those with a semblance of sense worked together in short bursts to break in.

“Unless one of the Gnomes has something they’re not telling us, we should be hiding!” Alex yelled as he caught up. “They’re going to—”

He shut up the moment he realized what Brody and Tank waited beside. Of course, the smarmy bastard crossed his arms and leaned against it.

“[The Expediter],” Alex said breathlessly as he took in the vehicle. “We’re stealing the fucking Expediter. I mean taking it from a dead guy.”

Alex drooled over the ride alongside Brody.

“Damn long leggers.” Dandelion huffed as she and Acorn caught up and noticed the vehicle. She whistled loudly.

Floating a hair above the ground, the vehicle was a mix between a car, a motorcycle, and Tron. Two thick wheels glowing purple sat in the front, and an extra large one, similarly glowing, jutted out from the rear. Handlebars sat above a seat meant for one butt in front of a much smaller seat where a passenger could hug the driver. It was all wrapped in a protective metal cage, and the rest of the body was covered in clear pipes that coursed magic.

“Well? Are we even going to fit? Can this thing even fly?” Dandelion asked while looking over her shoulder. She expected guests any second.

“This thing can fly. Can feel it in my bones. And we’ll fit.” Alex answered. He wasn’t sure how he knew it could soar, but something in his belly told him he was right.

He and Brody looked at each other at the corner of their eyes at exactly the same time.

“I’m driving.” Alex yelped, charging for the seat.

“Me!” Brody squawked and similarly charged.

WOOF!

The pair scuffled, tugging each other’s hair and elbowing ribs. Tank and the Gnomes were forced to watch as they fought out who would drive.

“Ha!” Alex cheered as he planted his rear onto the seat, winning the battle. “I said I’m driving!”

Brody huffed as Tank came to thunk his body into him. Alex searched for the start button. Dandelion cleared her throat as the sounds outside sounded much closer.

“Um, Alex? Brody? Tank?” She said, “I think we need to—”

“Got it!” Alex shouted, pressing the giant red button that read ‘START/STOP’.

A dozen monster cores suspended in the modified racing engine flared, reached for suspended Essence in the tank, and [The Expediter] started. Like a mechanical bike had a child with a laser beam, the conduit wrapping the bike flared, the engine turned, and finally, exhaust tinted through with glitter shot out just above the massive rear tire.

“Oh yeah!” Alex cheered as he revved the engine. Raw Essence popped out in a purple flame. “OOOOOOH YEAH!” He looked at everyone with wild eyes. “Jump on and let’s get—”

A red beam crashed through the wall ahead in the hall, sending an eruption of noise and letting the outside light in.

Everything was quiet for a moment outside as the searchers realized the fortress’s last protection was finally broken.

And then all hell broke loose.

“Get on now!” Alex shouted as the screams of the searchers started again. Only this time, it wasn’t in battle rage. It was in primal desire of the hunt.

Seconds later, their bodies began to dive through the hole.

Tank, urged by Brody, jumped into the smaller rear seat behind Alex. The Gnomes leapt and clasped onto the exterior cage of The Expediter.

“Brody, let’s go! Let’s go let’s go let’s go!” Alex yelled, staring wide-eyed at the manic crowd raging and looking for anything that might lead them to the Tax Guild Stone.

Ushered by Brody’s mental speech, Tank pressed his massive body against Alex’s back. The clone squeezed into the cage behind his dog, his butt hanging off the back of the seat.

“There!” A mean-eyed searcher pointed in their direction. “They must have the Stone!”

Suddenly, everyone searching wasn’t killing each other. Now that someone had pointed at a likely target, a stream of people and monsters charged.

“Time to go,” Dandelion laughed darkly, climbing like a squirrel to stand on top of the cage of the bike. She looked at Acorn, who waited clasped on with one pinky.

“Acorn, you guard the rear, and I’ll take the point,” She turned down her head to look down at Alex.

“You. Drive!”

“And how are we getting out of here!?” He barked back, suddenly realizing how ridiculous the bike idea was.

Dandelion Datura chuckled her infamous high-pitched laugh, somehow cresting above the roars of the incoming killers. She stood unafraid, just as she’d been trained, and stared out at those who sought to harm her, her lover, and those she’d been ordered to aide and protect.

“Drive, Alex. Fucking drive,” She turned and pointed her GnUzi at the roof. “And I’ll get us out of here just fine.”

“Fine!” Alex said and torqued the throttle.

[The Expediter] shot off, the controls coming easily.

Up it went in a tight curve as Alex revved hard. Pull the handlebars in for up, press forward for down, and twist for direction. Alex took to driving it like a natural, which should have surprised him, but he was too panicked to notice.

Skills fired towards the bike to dismember its riders. Glowing blue, The Expediter flew and twisted mid air, too fast to track easily. People hollered and screamed bloody murder, taking more careful aim toward the target.

Acorn positioned himself and his shield just as Dandelion had ordered him so. At the rear for protection. What he was good at. If he could smile, he would, but not for the Skills he would block with his [Indominable Plastique Shell] or return with his [Twice Returned Damage].

No, he would smile because he loved to hear Dandelion’s laugh, and she only laughed like this in very special occasions.

“I said I’d get us out of here. Now I’ll show them.” She said quietly to herself, but Acorn still heard her. He could always hear her.

Her giggle called out as she unleashed a specialized [Datura Slug] from her GnUzi. The skills coming to kill were about to reach them, so Acorn raised his shield, and a moment later, the blast from Dandelion’s skill was felt and heard across the airport.

Above the explosion in the roof, Dandelion’s laugh could be heard.
