My first impression of Ankmapak, many moons ago, had been that I really didn’t want to mess with the place.  Seemingly automated ballistas had locked on and tracked me as I flew closer, and they were doing the same this time.

I’d just been a baby back then, barely six feet from nose to tail, and now I was dozens of metres long, but there was no way I was going to risk flying straight at them.  The death of the dragon in the arena and the rain of gore was still fresh in my mind.

It was a vast, misty place, a splodge of murky grey in an otherwise green world.  The ribbon of the river went from sapphire blue to brown sludge as it passed through the city and urinary systems of its denizens.  It was going to be mine, but there was the small problem of making that goal a reality.

The walls were lined with soldiers; in amongst the shiny armour—which I approved of on general principle—were flowing robes in purple and scarlet.  Mages, bunch of bastards.  Adventurers stood out, even as I soared overhead; their equipment was all unique and a harsh contrast to the uniformity of the noble houses' troops who made up the bulk of the opposition.

In the distance, out to sea across the grim estuary, stood the furries' fleet.  Tall triple-masted ships floated like mother hens as smaller, metallic chicks swam amongst them.  The magitech destroyers all had what looked suspiciously like mana cannons aimed towards the city.  Cannons that moved to track me as I approached.

I ignored them.  I located the largest of the ships and settled onto the foredeck with a light thud that nonetheless made the whole ship sway.  All those mass increases had left me a wee bit chunky.

“Is Alicya about?” I asked a terrified-looking ratkin.

“Not bossman.  No tell.  Shoo please.”

“Which ship is Alicya on?” I growled.  This rat dude was sort of charming for a rodent.  Suspiciously brave, as well. The end had been clipped, ending a foot short in a dirty bandage. “Mistress on Wave Skipper.  That one.”  He pointed to a ship almost as large as this one.

Wave Skipper was crewed by the owlkin, and within moments of me landing, Alicya appeared on deck, smoothing back the fur across her shoulders.
