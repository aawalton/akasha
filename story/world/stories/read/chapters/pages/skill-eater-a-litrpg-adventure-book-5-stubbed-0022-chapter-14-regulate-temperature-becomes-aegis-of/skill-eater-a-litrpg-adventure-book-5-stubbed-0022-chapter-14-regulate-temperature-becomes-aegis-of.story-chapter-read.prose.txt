When Edge activated Extraction, the twin chains of oblivion released their weapons and dove into the beast’s core, invading its inner world as his mind followed a heartbeat behind.

Over the course of his adventures, he had learned that a creature’s central chamber was an idealized version of their favorite place. He found himself standing in the predator’s lair, gazing at the forest outside.

It seemed the beast preferred a different season. Instead of a brisk autumn breeze, winter’s chill filled the air. Beyond the cavern’s threshold, fresh snow was piled up to his waist and icicles hung from the branches. Tracks in the powder revealed prey was plentiful, including dozens of boot prints that confirmed the nanex’s fondness for devouring human flesh.

Edge had been on Ord for less than a year and had never experienced the biome’s coldest season, which reminded him that winter was just around the corner. He was hoping to steal three skills before the beast breathed its last, so he cut his tour short and went to work.

There were several fortuitous surprises when he examined the creature’s skillset. The first was the chains sensed an avatar they wanted to consume. He gave his permission without hesitation. Unlike the other powers in his collection, Manifest Chain was a picky eater. The living links only devoured skills that suited their palate, and their unique avenue of advancement didn’t count as an Extraction.

While the chains hunted the unfortunate avatar, he scanned his surroundings to see what else was on the menu. Edge grinned when he noticed that in addition to some decently-ranked Uncommons, the nanex had a Rare skill too. Stealing it would require Mortium, but he was flush after acquiring so many lifecoins during his trip to the Haunted Vale.

The moment the chains finished their meal, he sent his intentions into the System.

To extract a Rare skill, you must sacrifice 2 Mortium.

Would you like to proceed?

When he confirmed the prompt, the turgid power of death infused the animate loops, granting them the energy they needed to subdue the Rare skill that shielded the nanex from elemental damage, whose avatar looked like a blanket made from its fur.

Non-combat skills rarely put up much of a fight. The chains captured it within a matter of seconds and yanked it out of the core. They flung the avatar between his lips, and he ground it down in short order.

It tasted like enduring the wrath of winter and fury of summer. Of deflecting heat, cold, and electricity without letting the deadly elements penetrate the surface of his skin. Also, like cinnamon and wintergreen for reasons he couldn’t even begin to imagine. Now that he’d sampled the flavor, he knew it would provide protection against acids and bases too, which made him want it even more.

When Edge swallowed the subdued skill, it slid into his core, and he stepped inside his inner world to find out what he’d claimed.

You have extracted Resist Elements (Rare, Rank 5).

It will take 2 days to digest it.

He really wanted to keep it, which meant he had a dilemma on his hands. This was a type of magic he’d been hoping to pick up for some time, but he was saving his only empty slot for the Epic power he had stolen from the lobster.

He could start the digestion process to buy time, but that wouldn’t solve the problem since it would be another week before he was ready to evolve. He could Absorb one of his powers to free up some room, but he liked every skill in his collection, and it took considerable time and energy to develop his bonds.

A solution presented itself when Regulate Temperature floated over and tapped his shoulder to get his attention. The avatar, which took the form of a thick fur coat, was holding Edge’s Skill-Meger Token.

He was astonished that it was communicating its intentions so directly, as he usually needed Sea or Chibime to translate. Every skill in his village had been growing more intelligent and expressive over time, and his Skill Bond ultimate allowed for interactions and mergers that weren’t possible for anyone else on the planet.

“You want to merge with Resist Elements?”

The avatar bobbed its agreement. Edge didn’t have any objections. Since Regulate had suggested the idea, he was sure the skills were compatible. Their functions and appearances had a lot in common, which boosted his confidence that this was a good idea.

That being said, it would be foolish not to consult with an expert before pulling the trigger. He walked over to the black door, tapped on its surface, and said, “Would this be a wise use for my token?”

Skill-Eater didn’t speak, but he felt sleepy agreement flowing through their bond, which was good enough for him. Before the window to make his decision swung shut, he addressed the planetary AI.

“I want to use my token to merge Regulate Temperature and Resist Elements.”

When he voiced his decision, the metal disc rose from his hand and started to spin, shining with iridescent energy as its magic engulfed the avatars he’d named. The magic grew more intense over the next few minutes, painting his core with brilliant rainbows and buzzing like a battalion of bees.

When the lightshow faded away, only one avatar remained. It looked much the same as before, only now, the coat had the same blue tint as the nanex’s fur. When he experienced a brief vision revealing the details, Edge began to laugh.

He welcomed the avatar to the team while the rest of the gang came running over to say hello, then pulled up his Guide to read the description in full.

Aegis of Nullification <passive, aura> (Rare): Rank 2. Paths: Elemental Protection, Aura.

This skill provides potent insulation against temperature change, including elemental attacks. It will dramatically reduce your electrical conductivity and coat the exterior of your body and gear with a non-reactive layer that shields against acids and bases.

The skill can be activated as an aura, extending its benefits to any allies within 100 feet for no additional cost per person. It will count as a passive skill when only being used on yourself.

“Hell yes.” He danced in excitement when he realized how well the merger had gone.

Aegis had gained the full properties of both components, taking Regulate to Rare in the process. Better still, since they were highly compatible, the merged skill had been reset to rank two. Rank one would have been even better, but this outcome would let him pick a new rank-four upgrade, which was amazing opportunity to make Aegis of Nullification even stronger than it already was.

In addition to boosting his thermal insulation, he was thrilled to have picked up some corrosive and electrical resistance in the bargain, filling two holes in his rapidly shrinking list of attacks he didn’t have any direct protection against. It was cause for celebration, but throwing a party for his new friend would have to wait a little longer.

Edge returned to his body, where only a few seconds had passed in the outside world. The nanex was still alive, and he used Extraction to claim another skill.

You have extracted the skill: Enhance Natural Weapon (Uncommon, Rank 5).

This skill is a component of a merged power in your collection.

Its experience will be added to your existing skill.

Rank five. That might be enough to do the trick. Sure enough, when the avatar was Absorbed by Dragon’s Greed, the ball of light began to pulse—packed full of transformative energy and ready to advance.

The skill Dragon’s Greed has advanced to Rank 3.

Its mana efficiency has been enhanced.

Upon reaching Rank 3, you may control the volume of mana the skill consumes, adjusting its output at will.

The luminescent orb representing Greed was bigger and brighter when the process was complete, and the ghostly weapons floating inside looked sharper and more detailed than before. “Way to go, my friend. I’ll be right back.”

With that, Edge popped back into his skin and used the final charge of his ultimate, grabbing another high-rank Uncommon and feeding it to Rejuvenation. When he looked at the cup representing the avatar’s experience, it was nearly full. A few more days should do it. I can’t wait to find out what options are available when I take Rejuvenation to rank four.

After putting the beast out of its misery, he popped into his inner world to commemorate the moment, partying with Rue and the gang until he reached his daily limit. The twin chains emerged from beyond the black door to join in the festivities. The ravenous lord didn’t make an appearance, but he could sense her smiling before drifting back into dreams.

Next, he used Dragon’s Greed to determine which parts he wanted to harvest. The claws shone in his treasure-sensing sight, so he cut them off at the base and put them in his vault. The fur wasn’t as bright, but Edge was planning to add some winter gear to his kit, including a coat to keep him warm when couldn’t use magic or ran into an environmental hazard like the subzero rain in the Sea of Trees.

Using the techniques Talia had taught him, he removed the fur, treated the skin so it wouldn’t rot, and stored it inside his rune. He wasn’t going to eat meat from a creature that had consumed human flesh. But when he felt hunger flowing through his Companion bond, he decided he didn’t have any qualms with turning the nanex into sausage to feed the gluttonous fox, who was growing far faster than a normal beast.

It must be a combination of all the magicytes in these extreme-threat biomes and how much experience I’ve been earning lately.

When he finished butchering the carcass—a job that would have made Izzio proud—he stowed the meat in his vault. Rue devoured a fat slice and Edge asked Elemental Blade to freeze the rest, taking advantage of a strange quirk of his Spatial Storage Rune that let his avatars interact with the contents. Or maybe it was a consequence of Skill-Eater’s unique nature, he wasn’t really sure. The bones were shining faintly, so he grabbed them too before stepping into the cave.

When he surveyed the creature’s lair, he spotted some spears and helmets piled in a corner, which had belonged to an unfortunate crew that had met its end at the nanex’s claws. Lowtown’s residents were in desperate need of weapons and armor, so he grabbed everything that seemed salvageable and tossed it in his rune.

When he was done, Edge rinsed himself off with Repel Water, shifted into shadow form to erase his trail, and resumed his journey to the free city. It took him several hours to leave the sub-biome. The woodlands were a treasure trove of mid-grade crafting materials. He gathered over a thousand pounds of stone and timber along the way, along with some scattered beast parts from old battle sites.

All in all, his solo hunt had gone extremely well. The distraction had helped him calm down and get his head back in the game. He crossed the Lakelands with a spring in his step, ready to make the most of the two weeks at his disposal before he concluded his business with Coalition one way or another.
