I'm pretty damn excited. Not only because I respect Selkie et al., but because I designed QuestWright around the audience that reads those books. I spent time analyzing the stories Mango was publishing, looked through the books and authors they signed, then took my idea for 'Where do Quests come from?', and put a little Mango spin on it. It's not foolproof, but I love the story and characters, so I feel like I did a good job.
