That evening, rather than talk strategy or go do their own thing, Asher ran his team through his time spent in the wild realm. Each person reacted in about the exact way he’d expected them to. Samantha had been rightfully pissed off with Richard and his actions, Rulfar had been intrigued by the evershifting nature of the wild realm, and Moxy had demanded he take them back there so she could try fighting against one of these constantly changing enemies. Once he’d promised Moxy they’d check it out again some time in the future, he managed to pull the conversation back to more important matters.

“At the very least, Richard did come through with his end of the bargain,” Asher said as he revealed the two brand-new artifacts he’d been given. “With these, we should be able to see the ebb and flow of the realm of ages. It won’t do us any good if the greater demons actually find a patch of sped-up time, or however the hell that realm works, but we can at least find areas more suitable for fighting them in.”

He’d already tried wearing one of the rings out of curiosity, and actually seeing the realm of ages overlaid on the material realm was a strange experience. It was almost like seeing constantly moving, greenish shadows all over the place, and he knew these had to be the pockets of time that the greater demons tapped into to move at incredible speeds. As far as he could tell, the green pockets were larger in areas with more people and activity, and less prominent in remote locations. He didn’t know if the green pockets moved about to follow this activity, or which pockets would speed up time versus slow it down, but at the very least, they now knew how to avoid them.

“This is the final thing we needed,” Rulfar said, slipping a ring onto his finger and frowning as he looked around at the shifting green pockets of time. “It will still be difficult to lure the greater demons into areas where they cannot access their trump card, but at least we’ll know when we need to take cover.”

“Just make sure you shout when that time comes,” Moxy chuckled, picking up the other ring and tossing it to Samantha. “I’m not going to know, otherwise.”

“I guess that solves the issue of there only being two rings and three of us,” Rulfar said, handing his ring over to Asher long enough for him to use Permanence on it. Now that his skill had evolved and reached level 24, he was capable of locking three foreign skills onto him. At the moment, those were the tier 2 Hidden Presence skill that helped him hide his elements, the tier 2 Shard Detection skill that helped him sense danger approaching, and now the tier 1 Lesser Realm Sight skill, to help better fight against the greater demons.

He still hadn’t gotten around to testing just what Permanance’s tier 2 ability did here on the material realm, but there’d be time for that later.

“All that’s left is for us to finish picking out artifacts, and then I think we’re ready to start tracking down the lich,” Rulfar said, frowning at Moxy. “If Moxy continues to insist she doesn’t need any, and I already have my four, that just leaves the two of you.”

“Moxy does in fact continue to insist,” Moxy said, winking at the bloodhound as she flashed him a grin.

“I have the Amulet of Perception, but I think you’re forgetting about my bow,” Samantha said, twisting her brand new-Ring of Lesser Realm Sight. “My soul is already filled to bursting with foreign skills.”

“With how much training we’ve been doing and the battles ahead of us, it won’t be long before you join the Stage of Bleed yourself,” Rulfar explained. “The biggest hurdle is the sheer cost of evolving all of one’s skills. Seeing as you don’t have to worry about that, we should prepare your fourth artifact now.”

“Other than my Ring of False Death, I’m completely open,” Asher chimed in, doing his best to take the focus off Samantha for a moment. He could tell she was falling into her old habits and feeling uncomfortable about the thought of someone buying an artifact just for her before she could even use it. “So I could snag two more artifacts. On top of that, as soon as Judgement hits level 20 and evolves, I’ll have another opening as well.”

“So three more artifacts in total,” Ruflar nodded, ignoring Moxy’s attempts at antagonizing him as he placed a booklet for the Grand Auction on the table between them and tapped at the page. “Samantha, I think this one would be good for you, considering it nullifies one of your biggest weaknesses.”

“Boots of Cloud Step?” Samantha asked, reading from the listing he was pointing at for tomorrow’s auction. Her eyes went wide as she scanned over the appraisal’s description. “I mean, the ability to walk on clouds definitely sounds interesting. But I can already fly.”

“It’s not just walking on clouds,” Rulfar explained. “Look, see here? You can also use them to conjure up a small cloud wherever you want. Your biggest weakness is that you can’t effectively shoot while flying. Even after a week of practice, the flapping throws your aim off too much. These would allow you to set up a vantage point and snipe from up above in relative safety.”

“I’m just glad my Air element didn’t give me such a weird skill,” Moxy snorted, peering at the listing. “But yeah, for your fighting style, that would be a good pick up. That or you need to learn to start using those Razor Wings more effectively.”

“I definitely prefer fighting at a distance, where I can watch what’s going on and provide ranged support to whoever needs it,” Samantha said, pointedly not looking in Asher’s direction.

“Then that settles your last artifact,” Rulfar said, turning to Asher and flipping to a different page in the booklet. “Asher, you’re next to impossible to hit, and will heal through just about anything regardless, so you don’t need to worry about defense too much. What you need is some actual stopping power. Frankly, I’m shocked you hadn’t already picked up something like this.”

“Belt of Strength,” Asher read out, nodding along with the description. “I’ll admit, tripling my strength doesn’t sound bad at all. I guess the reason why I haven’t gone for something like this already is that I’m sort of like Moxy, to a lesser extent. It’s similar to why I don’t just start every battle with my mithral dagger. I don’t want to grow reliant on it or a specific artifact and then be helpless if I lose it. Or, you know, when we get close to the greater demons.”

“Wait, won’t that be a problem with the Rings of Lesser Realm Sight?” Samantha asked. “The whole point of the artifact is to help counter them. Will they still work when we get close?”

“They’ll be fine. Opal said that Spatial artifacts are protected against their presence,” Asher explained. “They’re just so rare that few people bother including that distinction.”

“Regardless, grabbing a Strength artifact should be one of your two choices,” Rulfar argued. “They’re expensive, seeing as it’s a constant passive that greatly improves not only combat, but even just everyday life, but you’ve got the shards for it.”

“You know I was hoping to have my vast fortune for more than just a few weeks,” Asher said, his brow raising as he noted the actual starting bids for the two artifacts Rulfar had picked out for them. He knew tier 2 artifacts were more expensive, and combat-applicable ones even more so, but their total cost was still shocking to him.

“Think of it as an investment,” Rulfar said, giving him a small smile. “Your fortune came from a greater demon corpse, right? Well, you’re about to have two more of them.”

The next day was spent running through their list of preparations and making sure everything was ready. First, Asher Recalled back to Dormaul to snag another potion of Feather to replace the one he drank falling through the wild realm, along with a potion of Sensitivity for Moxy, to replace the one he’d used to try and push Permanence to evolve. Despite the warrior not seeming all that interested in the potions in the first place, he still felt bad about drinking something he was technically just holding onto for someone else. After that, he popped back over to the tailor’s in Poltar, handing off his serpent-leather armor that had been bitten through by a few dozen snake-birds.

Once that was done, and the tailor finished cursing him out, Asher returned to the capital and hit up the Grand Auction. According to a random employee he asked, Bellod was actually away on vacation, which made a lot of sense considering recent events. The auction master had earned a small fortune just from appraising Asher’s greater demon corpse, and he’d worked tirelessly for a week appraising hundreds of books from the lich’s personal library. Asher could only hope he’d picked out somewhere nice to visit, like Poltar. When the city wasn’t being attacked by giant sea serpents, it was a beautiful place to enjoy the water.

Utilizing one of the private booths he had access to as a big spender, Asher was able to snag the Boots of Cloud Step and Belt of Strength without issue, even if the price ended up being driven up higher than he would have liked. Those two artifacts alone, each one being a second tier skill and incredibly useful ones at that, had set him back nearly six and a half million shards. He knew from Opal’s shop that more useful artifacts were outrageously expensive, like the Cloak of Invisibility she kept trying to pawn off on him that she was selling for a few million shards, but it still stung to actually have half his fortune wiped out in an instant.

Still, securing the belt around his waist and feeling strength like nothing he’d ever experienced flow through him definitely helped ease the pain a bit.

Strong Body (Tier 2) - Triple your natural strength and endurance

From his reading of the Big Book of Elements: Sixth Edition, Asher knew there were just as many different types of skills for the Strength element as there were for any other. When most people thought of warriors with the Strength element, they thought of people with the ability to punch through solid rock, or send enemies flying with a swing of their hammer. There were certainly skills that could do things like that, but they were active ones. Skills that could be triggered for a quick burst of impressive strength, at the cost of a large chunk of energy.

Instead, Asher decided to go with the other approach.

Strong Body was a purely passive Strength skill. So long as he kept the belt loaded up with shards, he’d simply have three times whatever his base strength was. Not only was it constant, that meant he also had room for improvement, as with every ounce of new muscle he earned from working out or sparring, his gains would functionally be multiplied three-fold. He wouldn’t be suplexing any giant monsters or throwing boulders, but he’d much rather have a flat, constant bonus to rely on anyway.

Not to mention he finally had something to encourage him to actually work out beyond sparring with his team.

With Samantha’s boots purchased and his belt secured snuggly around his waist, that just left one last artifact slot for him to fill. Rulfar had pointed out a few different ideas, but beyond the belt, none of them really jumped out at him as particularly necessary. He briefly considered waiting until something new came in that called to him, but seeing as they were about to go after The Council of Death in earnest for the first time, he wanted to be as prepared as physically possible. In the end, he went with an artifact that Rulfar hadn’t thought was worth it with so many healers on the team. It had cost him almost another two million shards, but in his opinion, it was worth every one.

His last artifact purchase of the day, was a Life amulet with quite the enticing skill.

Improved Recovery (Tier 2) - Your natural healing is greatly increased

Improved Recovery was yet another passive skill that he had high hopes for. While Asher had an incredibly powerful healing skill with Eternal Regeneration, the fact of the matter was that his personal healing simply wasn’t fast enough to be applicable in combat. He could literally come back from the dead in a couple of hours, and he could heal broken bones in only a matter of minutes. But when most battles were decided in what sometimes felt like the blink of an eye, Eternal Regeneration did absolutely nothing for him during that time.

Thankfully, some quick testing proved that this new foreign skill did in fact stack with Eternal Regeneration, and Asher couldn’t help but grin at the sight of a moderately deep gash he’d sliced into his arm actually close up slowly before his very eyes. Finally, his powerful healing abilities would come into play before the end of a fight.

That knowledge was more than a little reassuring, because with that purchase, they were officially full up on artifacts. With potions in hand, artifacts equipped, and their training complete, there was no more delaying the inevitable.

It was time to strike back against The Council of Death.
