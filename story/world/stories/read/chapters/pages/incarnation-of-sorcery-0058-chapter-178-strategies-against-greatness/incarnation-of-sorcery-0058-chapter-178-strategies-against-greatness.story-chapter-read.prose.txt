Viren was surprised he was still conscious with everything else going on. Maybe he should have been surprised he was speaking too, but in truth, that was part of what kept him grounded. Even with all the impossibilities he was forced to witness one after the other.

He had always known that a mage of Ryland’s calibre would undoubtedly be involved with creatures and beings that would break Viren’s brain.

Despite knowing that, the sheer scale of a dragon so monstrously huge enough that it could host an entire civilization on its back was an impossibility that Viren barely managed to wrap his head around. And then the Archdragon, Vorkorax, had revealed that Ryland had been in a fling with a Great God of all people.

Insane.

Who in their right minds courted a Great God? Sure, it had sounded like a one-sided affair on Ryland’s part, but still. Who in their right minds made a Great God fall for them? Viren hadn’t even heard of people directly interacting with Great Gods in such a manner.

But then again, he supposed if there was one person in the universe who could woo a Great God of all beings, then it had to be Marionys Ryland.

That thought clashed with a recent addition, one that was nowhere near as charitable. If Ryland was so all-powerful, then how could he have allowed Acrypha to be stolen? How could he still remain so far away from them even now?

Viren knew those were idiotic thoughts. Everyone had limits. Even Ryland. He knew this. It was normal.

Yet, there was some deep, ingrained part of Viren that had ended up believing Ryland really was infallible. That there was nothing in the universe that could impede him. Well, nothing save for the task that the Archdragon had set them. Because, really? Moving an entire star? Who even came up with such things?

The ability to think of using a star’s gravity as some sort of cosmic catapult made so much sense yet felt so astronomically alien that Viren was just left flabbergasted.

It didn’t help that Viren was practically useless for such tasks. What could a first-year academy student ever be capable of in such situations? It was why the euphoria of having his Soul Glyph spontaneously evolve had simmered away so quickly. All because he had accepted his lot in his new life.

Viren wasn’t going to be of much use.

Especially when he saw to just what extent Ryland was happy to take himself to accomplish his goals. The amount of mana his master had ejected and how his manasoul had burned against the sun had made Viren’s heart feel like he was shattering. Ryland was ready to burn his own soul as fuel to fulfil his task.

Until, of course, he had drawn on the power of the Overrealm and its divine energy to empower his spell. Now that had been something. An awestriking sight. An impossible incident.

A lone mage moving an entire star. Who could ever believe that was possible?

And then they had been taken hostage by the Great God. It felt like What’s His Name had been lying in wait for just such an opportunity. Just that sort of moment where Ryland was once more forced to tap into the Overrealm to draw upon its bountiful divine energy. For that was what had opened the pathway for a Great God to come and kidnap them both.

The whole situation had given an awful feeling of sympathy. The whole time he was trapped in the Overrealm, he couldn’t help but wonder if this was what Acrypha was going through.

And now, they were moments away from being crushed by a megalomaniacal and all-powerful entity.

It was strangely humbling for Viren to find himself in the presence of a being who outstripped even Ryland in terms of sheer prowess. There weren’t even any spells involved. Reality kept being reshaped by the Great God himself, countering any measures and any spells they could come up with.

He didn’t understand how he had found his voice to oppose Jahaevo, as he called himself. Maybe it was Ryland’s unmoving stoic presence next to him that had granted him confidence. Whatever the case, he was glad to have found his voice, because he had no intention of dying silent.

But it turned out death might not have been in store for him in the first place. Ryland came to the rescue again. As always, it was his rifts.

This one created an opening against the wall of the Overrealm pocket, through which that enormous Archdragon was pushing in its gargantuan maw.

“What in the—”

It was honestly extremely relieving to see how the surprise had unmoored Jahaevo, to the point he no longer sounded like a Great God to begin with. These weren’t unfathomable beings that transcended human comprehension. Sure, they were more powerful than anything Viren could have possibly imagined, but they weren’t infallible.

As was proven by the dragon roaring in with a burning attack.

“[Star Scream]!” Vorkorax cast. The scream of its incantation made the entire Overrealm crack, and the barriers that Ryland placed around Viren threatened to shatter entirely.

Viren’s eyes widened. Incantations. That Archdragon was casting magic.

It wasn’t alone either. Ryland’s manasoul exploded, enraging with such fury that it blazed out of the crystalline cage the Great God had placed them both in. Then his spell joined that of the Archdragon.

“[Rift Cage]!” Ryland incanted.

Things were happening so quickly, Viren barely caught the actual effects of the various spells. But he saw enough. Just before the apocalyptic stream erupted from the maw of the huge dragon, Ryland had clad the entirety of the Great God in a coating of his rifts. Jahaevo’s magic had already started suppressing the rifts, but the Archdragon’s flames struck first.

If Viren thought he had been bathed by Vorkorax’s flames before, then he had been grossly mistaken. The blast that erupted out of the gargantuan beast’s maw now dwarfed any stream of energy he had ever seen.

It was like a volcanic eruption combined with the flare of a sun itself. Plasma and liquid energy frothing with light-warping heat and all-consuming power shot out of the Archdragon’s maw faster than lightning. Jahaevo had next to no time to evade, or perhaps he never intended to dodge, out of sheer pride. Whatever the case, he was definitely blown away by that stream.

But that inconceivably powerful blast of light and heated fury had Viren’s eyes feeling like they were witnessing a star’s enraged vomit.

So this was why Ryland was looking to recruit the Archdragon to their side.

As if blasting the god with several tons of dragon fire had distracted him, Ryland managed to break both himself and Viren out of their respective crystalline cages. Shards of brilliant light shattered out and fell away towards the bottom of the Overrealm pocket. It was almost beautiful watching them fall apart.

“Are you alright, Viren?” Ryland asked. “[Lifeward].”

Viren was surprised he could move again without feeling like he was about to dismember himself. He could feel Ryland’s mana brush against him and his manasoul through that [Lifeward] spell. “I’m fine, sir. But that Great God…”

Ryland turned with a burning determination in his eyes that Viren had never seen before. “Don’t worry. This was all part of the plan. And now, it’s time for me to take care of things for good.”

He broke open more rifts nearby him, inserting his hands and pulling out a few different items, all with such a rapid pace that Viren was almost fooled into thinking he had grown several extra limbs. Ryland swallowed down a glimmering potion, put on an amulet, crushed a few crystal orbs, placed a strange fetish at his waist, all in just a few blinks.

Then he rifted away once more, this time heading towards his adversary.

The Archdragon’s flaming fury was being stopped. Right. Much of the initial effectiveness was due to Ryland’s first spell and the element of surprise.

But now that the Great God had gotten some time, he was quickly turning things around. Golden energy burned in from the edges of the Overrealm, striking back at the unending stream of fiery fury from the Archdragon, quickly overcoming and pushing it back.

Until Ryland joined the fray.

Viren’s eyes grew wide as saucers. He wasn’t sure just what kind of enhancements or other similar things Ryland had taken, but they were clearly helping him take on a Great God of all beings.

His Incarnation form blazed brightly enough to outshine the Archdragon’s power. His spells came out so quickly, Viren was barely able to note them all. And power was frothing out of him with such brilliant intensity, he was warping space itself around him. The clash between Ryland and the Great God turned titanic.

Whenever Ryland attacked, the Great God would either use one of his crystals to defend himself or try to turn the very spells into a representation of light within the crystals. He was turning everything attacking him into literal light.

And that was just what Viren was able to make out. The chaos was so bright, the flashes so potent and intense, Viren was honestly surprised he was blinded already.

That they had no trouble seeing through the blinding illumination was just par for the course.

However, what Viren could barely make out through his eyes he made up for with his sense of hearing. To some extent, at least.

When he noted the Great God managing to unleash a devastating array of crackling light beams in every direction, the distinct voice of Ryland cut through the din of the explosion with his counter spell.

“[Eternal Ruin]!”

Rifts became just barely visible. Viren caught a spew of apocalyptic fury from twin rifts tearing through the Overrealm. One blasted out what appeared to be a volcanic eruption, with the other sending in a mirrored, opposite reality with a lightning-wreathed blizzard. The Great God was blasted from both ends by insane power.

And then there was a second set of devastation. It took a moment for Viren to realize they were reflections too. From the Great God.

That was Jahaevo’s thing. Manipulating mirrors and light. Through those abominable crystals of his.

It wasn’t just Ryland fighting against the Great God. The Archdragon had entered and was giving no quarter. Even with its maw closing, even with its gargantuan size, now that he was a part of the battle, he was determined to see the battle through to the end. Though its maw had closed and was no longer spewing [Star Scream], it attacked with gigantic talons and tails.

There was something strange going on with its sheer enormity. Viren had thought the space within the Overrealm pocket was limited. That there was no way a dragon bigger than planets could ever fit in fully within this strange prison he was trapped in.

Yet, the entirety of the dragon pushed into the Overrealm pocket. It almost felt the space was actively expanding to contain the burgeoning size of Vorkorax.

Which was good for the dragon because it could start adding clawing strikes and ramming headbutts to its arsenal against the Great God. Viren saw it all, thanks to how its sheer size overcame even the brilliant destruction that Jahaevo was shattering out.

This was greatly needed because Jahaevo kept trying to regain the momentum of the battle. His unfathomable power gathered at a frightening pace, his energy shot out with no warning and with fatal intent. But every single time he seemed to almost become overwhelming, the Archdragon was there to interrupt him before darting away. An annoying planet-wide gnat never allowing its target to gain a true footing.

And yet, Ryland was stealing the thunder from both the Great God and Archdragon.

It was that more than anything which filled Viren’s heart with actual hope. He had been foolish to ever doubt Ryland. Just because Acrypha was gone didn’t mean Ryland’s power had diminished, and in fact, it was pulling out more from him than Viren had ever thought he’d see.

All those enhancements, all the items and Relics he had adorned himself with to give himself of all people a powerful boost, they all came into play. His form blazed, his eyes glowed like suns, his power was warping reality itself around them. He was almost matching a god.

More spells and more destruction rained everywhere. Viren wasn’t sure if he heard correctly, but he was certain he made out certain impressive-sounding sorceries from Ryland like [Eye of The Archfiend], [Awakened Avatar], and more. The effects of each spell caused little detonations, left warps and twists in the devastating sea of light that the battle was causing.

There was one spell that really blew Viren away, however. He thought he had seen almost everything that Ryland could dish out. Not so.

[Wings of Salvation] from Ryland combined with his earlier spells, turning him into a living comet trailing an eradicating blaze. The speed, the fury, the incredible energy and momentum with which he fired himself after those strange, blistering wings burned off his shoulders made Viren gawk.

Especially when he used another [Star Scream] from the Archdragon to propel himself like a projectile fired from a world-breaking cannon.

Viren could do little but stare.

Jahaevo barely managed to react in time by calling upon more of those crystals and reflections. A mirror wall appeared in front of the Great God, and Ryland’s burning image appeared on its surface. They collided, and the sheer pressure from the blast left Viren’s barriers entirely cracked and his ears ringing, his eyes blinking away the supernova blast.

And then Ryland broke through. The collision lasted a mere instant, a brief blink, after which Ryland was continuing onwards with unbroken momentum

Just to crash into the surprised Great God.

Then he broke away. The impact somehow lasted even less time than the one with Ryland’s own reflection, almost as if even with all the power and boosts Ryland had gathered, he had no intention of lingering long in the presence of a Great God. Especially when he was able to tear away a bit of the divinity’s essence.

What else could that glimmering strand of golden power be? Especially when the Great God’s own form was left jittering and distorted.

“You have not won!” Jahaevo screamed, his words penetrating through the thick pandemonium clouding Viren’s ears. “You will not escape—”

Ryland was next to Viren in a flash, his rifts crackling to life with the speed of lightning.

“[Gods’ Tangle],” he shouted.

Right behind Viren, maybe a handspan or two away, another rift formed. This one literally broke reality to make chunks of its void-like mass simply fall into whatever space Ryland had opened a rift to. And then Ryland was pulling both himself and Viren through the opening, throwing them both back onto Rhel.

Viren was barely able to get his breath back before he was getting back to his feet, the words he had been forced to keep to himself now vomiting out of him. “Sir! The Archdragon!”

“Don’t worry, Viren.” Ryland was already standing, not at all looking like he had just come from a desperate battle against a being who technically eclipsed even him. “Vorkorax is already retreating through the rift I opened for him.”

Viren could only shake his head slowly. The whole thing felt insane to him. They had actually encountered a hostile Great God, and they had come out of the altercation alive and mostly intact. This was impossible. No one would believe them. Viren himself still felt as though he was dreaming.

Ryland stretched, breaking a little bit of Viren’s staggering disbelief. “Now, I think I need a little bit of rest. Before—”

The sky grew dark and Rhel was blanketed in shadow. Everything started trembling. Viren looked up to see the Archdragon appearing in Rhel’s atmosphere. It shouldn’t have been a worrying sight after that battle against Jahaevo, if not for Viren sensing that the dragon was enraged. Oh no.

“Before that happens,” Ryland said with a sigh.
