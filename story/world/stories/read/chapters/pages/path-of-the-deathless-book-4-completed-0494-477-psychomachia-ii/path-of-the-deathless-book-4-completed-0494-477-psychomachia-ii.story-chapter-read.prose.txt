To this day, the mystery still lingers.

Who is Valor Thann? How much of his legend is exaggeration, distortion, a mirage of eons past? How much is true?

Who truly is Valor Thann?

As these pages approach the epilogue, I still linger on this question. I still find myself wondering, seeking answers that I know I will never receive. The Eldest Pathbearers, those who represent each Faith, all know him by name, have fallen at his wrath, or found themselves spared. This is beyond doubt. What is also beyond doubt is his knowledge, bequeathed by him as an act of magnanimity that allows us as Necrotechs to flourish and endure, unlike any other, that allows us to circumvent the destruction of our physical vessels and press forward. Animancy, Necromancy, the Ritual of the Dichotomous Soul. The lores in question have been revolutionized by his hand, and without Valor Thann, the latter would not even exist—yet, even so, that goes no further toward answering my question: Who is Valor Thann?

Who is the founding father of our people? What drives him? What defines him? What is he like in person, in character? How can so many different people encounter the same man and never share a common depiction? And how can the same people, who have tasted his mercy, his generosity, suddenly fall prey to him in the span of years for seemingly no cause?

And though there are claims that he has been wed, why haven't there been any true sightings of his beloved? She is known to us as a passing shadow and nothing more. No one has laid eyes on the illustrious Silence Valeros once named Gehenna. Even the First Blood deny her existence, scrubbing her away down to the last detail, as if there is some great shame or terror they seek to conceal.

And then there is the matter of Udraal Thann. Udraal Thann, who is supposed to be Valor Thann’s son. Udraal Thann, who only became known to us as an adult, and not in any of his childhood years.

Could it truly be that Valor hid his most precious kin away on another world? Or could there be something else at play?

Perhaps I have questioned too long, but still my question ails me…

Who is Valor Thann?
