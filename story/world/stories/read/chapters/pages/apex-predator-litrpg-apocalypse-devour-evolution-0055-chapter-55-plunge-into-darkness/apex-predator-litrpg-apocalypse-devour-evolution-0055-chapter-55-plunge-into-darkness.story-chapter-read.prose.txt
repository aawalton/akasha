There was a storm of tentacles around Don. He created a psychic bubble of telekinesis around him. It wasn’t an insane reality altering cheat like he had seen in the Dominator fight, but it was critical.

It slowed down the tentacles that were coming at him from a direct angle. It prevented attacks at off angles, and misdirected the angles. Don used most of his focus on maintaining the bubble and fending off the tentacles.

Fortunately his fighting skills allowed that to happen. He was a dancing dervish of destruction. The claws cut left and right, up and down. Don’s Energy was surging down, as he used all he had. He was a blur of movement while he maintained the intricate Psychic control.

Most of his time was spent cutting down the tentacles. There were dozens and dozens attacking him. Whenever he cut a few down, more surged out, using the space created.

Every once in a while, he managed to get an attack in directly on the boss, who was glaring at him with murderous intent.

Don slashed down. One claw raked across the mask with a loud crack. A spiderweb fracture split from the point of impact. Don followed with another slash, then a boot straight to the edge of the mouth.

The mask didn’t break, but it bent. A chunk of the cheek collapsed inward into the round mouth like compressed plaster. Beneath the mask was black flesh. Don saw it twitch.

The swamp rumbled. Morshoff was angry. Don had a feeling it had not been challenged like this in ages.

All around him, the tendrils snapped. The ones that had been writhing in the water below recoiled, then launched upwards. All of them. All of them.

It looked like Morshoff was finally taking him seriously. No more reserves. All of the tentacles it had were above water.

Don saw them rising like a forest of whips.

He smiled. This was what he had been waiting for.

He reached into his satchel, pulled a sonic bomb from the case. Small, gray, round. There was a seam, splitting the bomb in the middle. He twisted the halves and the device clicked when it rigged.

Don dropped the bomb at his feet and threw up a psychic vacuum just in time.

The detonation didn’t explode outward like fire or shrapnel. It pulsed.

A single, brutal wave of sound exploded across the shell. The air vibrated. Water sprayed. Don’s bones shook. His vision doubled. But the effect was immediate.

The tentacles froze. Morshoff groaned and shook in the water, which almost made Don slip out of balance.

Dozens of the tentacles hovered mid-air, twitching like spasming muscle. Some collapsed outright, flopping into the water. Some shook violently. Don didn’t wait to see which was which.

He went to work.

His claws flashed in a blur. He cut down four tentacles immediately. Then another three. Don spun and tore and ducked and cut, moving across the shell like it was hot.

Every second, he moved. Every second, a tentacle was severed. No movement was wasted. Every step was an attack, every attack a step. He dug into the mess of them, hacking them apart before they could recover.

[General Skill Increased: Unarmed Fighting!]

[Unarmed Fighting Level 7]

They did recover.

The stun didn’t last long. Ten seconds. Maybe less.

The ones that had collapsed started to twitch. Then they came alive again, faster and angrier than before. Don saw a fresh batch rising from the depths below, longer and thicker.

He hurled another spear, then reached into the satchel and pulled out the second sonic bomb.

He twisted it and threw it long across the shell.

It detonated midair.

Another pulse. Another stop. Another perfect moment.

Don didn’t waste it.

He leapt into the air, came down with claws buried in the base of a cluster of tendrils. He tore straight through. Black sludge coated the shell. Steam hissed from open wounds. The murky water around him had a hundred tendrils floating around.

The creature shuddered beneath him. It was trying to attack with some kind of psychics, but Don’s mind brushed it off.

He kept going.

Claws and telekinesis. Spears flying and shields intercepting stray whips. He kept throwing the spears with his mind while his claws cut anything they could reach.

But once the stun dissipated, the attacks grew more fierce. There seemed to be no end to the tentacles. So Don kept on dancing and weaving on top of Morshoff.

Dozens became hundreds. The air thickened with them. They slithered and coiled and snapped. Some struck from blind angles. One clipped his shoulder.

[Health at 82%]

Another wrapped his ankle. Don hacked it off and kept moving.

[Health at 75%]

[Energy at 32%]

The third sonic bomb was still in the satchel.

He couldn’t waste it.

Not yet.

He needed to make space. To get an opening. He had a plan and he needed to stick to it.That meant keeping pressure up.

But he was getting tired. His Energy was getting dangerously low, and despite killing almost two hundred tentacles, they kept coming. His breathing was winded, and his movement was starting to slow down.

Fighting was fucking hard work.

Then the large tentacle showed up again. It had a hard time reaching on top of the boss, but it still clipped Don and boy did it hurt. Don took a hit to the side and rolled with it.

[Health at 65%]

Don sliced into it with both of his claws and ripped them apart, splitting the thick tentacle like a plastic wrapper.

It exploded in gore.

He got one breath through his nose. Just one. That helped.

But that was all he got. The tendrils, while thinned out were still numerous and they were on Don.

The whole sky seemed to darken with them. Every angle, every direction.

This had to be it. Any longer and he would just get worn down. Morshoff must sense weakness and this is an all-out attack. If there were still reserves of tentacles under the water, Don was cooked. But if there weren’t… He had an angle.

This was the only viable plan.

Don didn’t hesitate.

He pulled the last bomb.

Twisted it. Dropped it behind him.

Then he dove.

Straight into the black water.

The surface exploded behind him as the bomb detonated.

Don didn’t see it. Didn’t hear it. He was already under, already deep, already swimming into darkness. He had a psychic bubble on his head, which prevented the murk from going into his eyes. But it was still dark. His Night Vision was a literal lifesaver.

But even so, the swamp was thick and heavy around him.

The water closed over his head like a fist. Cold. Thick. It wasn’t clean water. It was oily. Viscous. Swimming in it was like swimming through gelatinous soup.

He kicked hard, dragging himself deeper. His cloak dragged behind him like seaweed, his limbs straining against the pull of the bog.

He pushed down further. Darkness swallowed him. The light from the shell above became a glow, then a blur, then nothing.

There were no tentacles. But they would come soon. Don didn’t have a lot of time. He went deeper.

But he could see the murky outline of the thick stalk that connected into Morshoff.

It extended deep into the earth. A single massive cord of black flesh, rooting Morshoff to the bottom of the swamp like an anchor.

Don pushed forward.

He reached the stalk.

Massive. Pulsing. Covered in layers of muscle that flexed and trembled.

Don planted a foot against it and pressed a hand to the surface. It twitched beneath his touch.

He sunk a claw into it, so he wouldn’t be dislodged. Then he reached for his satchels. His mind was going on overdrive. He only had few precious seconds left.

But then his hand clicked with it. The crystal flask. Oil of Everburning Fire. The vengeance of the Grimoids.

If this didn’t work underwater, Don was fucked. But if it did…

Don slammed the flask into the stalk.

Then an inferno swallowed him.
