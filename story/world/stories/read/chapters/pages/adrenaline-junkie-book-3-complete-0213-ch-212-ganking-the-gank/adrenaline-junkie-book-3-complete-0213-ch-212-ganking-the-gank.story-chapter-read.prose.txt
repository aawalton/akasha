The sound of splintering wood barely registered before a flicker of motion caught his eye – he snapped his head back just in time to avoid several elongated pink spikes erupting outward from the Amphislime that was still glued to his right arm. They stabbed at where his face had been a split second prior, missing his skull by a few inches.

“Ah, I should have expected that,” Archie remarked out loud while he thought to himself, Damn, these are a lot more dangerous than the ones I found near the volcano in the Group Tutorial.

Not wanting to waste a single second, he activated Thornscourge Expansion atop his right arm, causing vines and jagged thorns to erupt atop his gauntlet and forearm guard in a spiral that extended outward, transforming his right arm into a spiked mace.

He then twisted his wrist sharply, manipulating the thorns to churn and rotate, spinning them like the teeth of a chainsaw. The serrated barrage screeched against gelatinous flesh, shredding through the gelatinous and green flesh of the Amphislime.

Chunks of its semi–liquid flesh sprayed outwards, only to snap back together like stretched rubber bands. It didn’t matter how much he tore, the gelatinous creature refused to come off his arm. Worse, the slime’s grip constricted harder, stalling the movement of his spinning thorns, eventually causing the chainsaw-like motion of Thornscourge Expansion to lock up midturn.

Archie’s jaw tightened as he felt himself unable to rotate the thorns and vines atop his right arm. “…Fuck.”

The other Amphislimes gave him no time to recover from learning his ability practically dealing zero damage to the Amphislime on his right arm. All five lunged at once, bodies snapping forward with incredible speeds, tree trunks exploding as they tore straight through bark and branches like battering rams. Archie barely kept ahead, darting and vaulting from branch to branch, weaving between their strikes while the first slime dragged against his balance like a ball and chain strapped to his arm.

“Use Spirit Bond on me,” Vul spoke up from within his Soulscape.

Archie didn’t verbally respond to Vul as he was dodging in between the attacks of the other Amphislimes while reaching for the connection between him and Vul before activating Spirit Bond on it. In doing so, molten heat surged through his veins and significantly raised his body temperature while simultaneously unlocking his Magma Affinity.

As a result, the thorns of Thornscourge Expansion were now glowing red–hot as molten mana flooded through them while the vines turned dark red.

“Right, I forgot,” Archie’s lips curled into a savage grin.

The chainsaw–thorns roared back to life, this time searing with a magma sheen. They spun and bit into the Amphislime’s body once more, only now, instead of bouncing back, the gelatinous flesh hissed, bubbled, and outright melted away. The slime shrieked, its body convulsing violently as its form liquefied under the heat, charring at the edges like burning plastic.

“Gel + Fire = Big damage,” Archie muttered, satisfaction dripping off his tone as he felt the grip around his arm loosening.

He didn’t stop there.

With his left gauntlet–clad fist, magma mana poured outward in a concentrated shell of molten force. He drove it forward, piercing straight through the Amphislime’s elevated head where its jittering eyes spasmed wildly. His hand speared through something hard; an orb buried inside its body, thrumming with mana.

Archie’s fingers closed around it and ripped.

The Amphislime let out a guttural, distorted shriek before collapsing, its entire body unraveling and sliding off his arm in twitching chunks. As the gelatinous remains fell, they didn’t fall further than a couple of feet before vanishing into midair as they were sucked into a Spatial Rift. Archie flicked open at his side, then funneled neatly into his Spatial Storage alongside the intact Amphislime Core clutched in his palm.

*[Amphislime Lv 101] has been slain – XP has been given.*

Archie flexed his arm, rolling his shoulder to shake off the phantom sting of constriction. He glanced down at the Core once before pocketing it, eyes snapping back up toward the other five Amphislimes.

Their bodies twitched violently, eyes jittering in erratic rage.

“Yes!” Archie shouted, laughter bleeding into his voice as molten thorns rippled across his body. Thornscourge Expansion spread out like wildfire, vines bursting from his armor to coil and wrap across every inch of him until he looked like an embered knight plated in living bramble.

Each jagged thorn across his body glowed red–hot as magma mana coursed through them, then, with a thought, the thorns roared to life – a thousand spinning blades, chainsaw edges screaming in unison as he hurled himself forward.

The five Amphislimes lunged to meet him, intent on avenging their fallen brethren.

Archie crashed into the first, his shoulder check alone detonating the gelatinous body into a bubbling mess that instantly began to liquefy under the molten grind of his armor.

His fist hammered forward into the second, chainsaw–thorns chewing its body apart as if he were plunging a tire into a metal shredder. It shrieked as he ripped through the core with his other hand, yanking it free in one brutal jerk before the rest of its body slid away in smoking heaps before splattering onto the branches below.

The third tried to spring onto his back in an attempt to catch him unaware and smother his head before crushing and liquifying it, however, Archie caught sight of it out of the corner of his right eye.

Vines flaring outward from his torso like a dozen writhing serpents as they then wrapped around the slime mid–leap, constricting before the spinning magma–thorns shredded it like a blender. Burning chunks sprayed out in all directions, splattering the branches. Another core was snatched up and pocketed before it even touched the ground.

Archie grinned widely, firelight dancing across his armor. “Come on then! Let’s– ”

The canopy above burst apart, and a massive, yet thin, serpentine body dropped down to him like a guillotine, mottled green and brown scales flashing in the late morning light as it whipped downward and struck the branch on which Archie had previously stood.

Archie steadied himself atop the new branch he landed on as his eyes darted up to see not one, but several Clawserpents slithering out of the crowns of the massive trees. Each one as thick as his own arms, while not the largest snake he’d seen, they were at least the length of one and a half 4WD trucks lined up together, and along with their massive size, they had stubby clawed limbs sprouting in uneven rows across their bodies.

[Clawserpent Lv 102]…[Clawserpent Lv 104]

“…Oh, shit.”

Before Archie could even adjust, three of the scaled monsters lashed onto the two remaining Amphislimes, tangling them in coils. Their claws raked through gelatinous flesh while hooked fangs tore chunks away, shredding them in seconds. Slime shrieks were silenced beneath the sound of scales constricting and bones creaking.

Archie barked a laugh at the sight of the creatures that were just about to gank him getting ganked themselves.

His excitement grew even more as four more Clawserpents surged straight for him.

“Yes!” Archie shouted again, voicing a war cry this time as he whipped his arm forward, as the adrenaline within him was picking up. The vines obeyed instantly, exploding outward in a barrage of roots thick as spears. They shot across the clearing, aiming to skewer the lunging serpents midair, just like how he did with the other Amphislime.

One serpent wasn’t fast enough as three of the forty bramble vines that shot out of his arm speared through its midsection with wet crunches, its eyes bulging in surprise of being killed so suddenly by a creature lower leveled than it.

Archie clenched his fist and the roots bloomed inside its body, sprouting barbed thorns and vines that tore outward in every direction. The Clawserpent writhed, body convulsing as its insides were being shredded to pulp seconds before Archie recalled back the vines to deal with the other three Clawserpents that remained unharmed by the bramble vines.

One twisted its body into a corkscrew, spiraling around the root with unnatural grace before snapping its jaws inches from Archie’s throat. To which it was met with Archie grabbing its head just before it reached him with his left Thornscourge Expansion-clad gauntleted hand and easily crushing its skull.

Another serpent scythed through the air with its claws, raking across Archie’s side. While Archie remained unharmed as the claws tore through the bramble vines that covered his armor, the sheer force of its strikes still sent him crashing backward through a branch and hurtling towards another tree.

Archie caught himself midair, boots slamming down onto a quickly conjured mana platform before he kicked off it and hurled straight back at the Clawserpent that had sent him flying.

Activating Savage Charge midway through his lunge, the serpent could barely react to the sudden increase of speed before Archie’s right hand locked around its lower half and heaved it aloft with a single arm.

Without slowing, he swung it like a living flail, smashing its upper body against anything in reach; tree trunks, branches, and even more tree trunks – until he angled it into the path of another Clawserpent that lunged at him. And upon their two heads colliding with one another, it was followed by twin sickening crunches as their skulls exploded.

Chunks of scale, blood, and bone sprayed across the canopy before their bodies fell limp,  and onto the branch he stood on, where their corpses writhed for a few seconds before Archie let the mangled remains slip from his grasp.

While he stood braced on the branch, the distant gurgling shrieks of Amphislimes reached him again. His head snapped to the side just in time to catch sight of the last two being constricted by three Clawserpents. What made his brow furrow, though, was how suddenly the serpents seemingly died to the Amphislimes, before remembered physical attacks barely did anything to them.

“Do you need to be able to use fire in order to properly damage their bodies, or could it be just anything other than physical if you can’t reach their core in time?” Archie muttered, eyes narrowing.

Before he could question any further, the Amphislimes writhed with a pulse of pink light before their bodies started pulling together, merging into one another with a wet suction. The serpents constricting them thrashed harder, as though realizing what was about to happen, but they were too late.

Both slimes fused into one that ballooned outward until it was nearly one and a half meters tall, gelatinous body trembling as it solidified into a new, bloated shape.

Archie made a noise of surprise as he witnessed the slimes merging into one. “So, they can do what slimes do in RPGs… but since it's also in the shape of a lizard, can it also snap off its own tail or part of its body to live and escape?” he muttered as he thought back to a couple of the games he used to play back on Earth. “That would be both annoying… yet cool.”

The Clawserpents, weakened beyond reason, were entirely submerged into its body, fangs and claws gnashing uselessly while in its body. Seconds later, only muffled thrashing remained inside its gelatinous bulk.

The enlarged Amphislime twitched violently as it finished devouring the three Clawserpents that attacked it. Shortly after, its chameleon-like eyes twitched around before locking onto Archie.

As a result, its limbs elongated unnaturally before it launched itself at Archie like a cannonball. The speed was faster than the serpents, faster than the other Amphislimes had been. It closed the distance in an instant, its warped claws stretching outward to engulf him.

But before it could reach him, the entire canopy shuddered as thick tree limbs burst outward from the trunks around Archie, snapping like striking vipers. They coiled around the Amphislime mid-air, intercepting it barely a meter from reaching Archie.

The slime screeched, writhing against the sudden prison of wood that now crushed inward against its body.

Archie’s grin widened, teeth bared as his molten thorns pulsed brighter across his armor. “Earthen Grasp + Botanokinesis is really OP in a forest,” he quipped, voice dripping with satisfaction.

The tree limbs constricting the Amphislime suddenly ignited, magma mana surging down their length. Fire spilled out, heat waves distorting the air as bark turned molten red, flames roaring through the wood and into the slime’s gelatinous flesh.

The Amphislime wailed, its body bubbling and melting where the burning wood pressed into it. It lashed out in desperation, claws and elongated tendrils shooting in every direction, desperately trying to reach Archie. Each time it extended, the molten branches only coiled tighter, shredding and searing through the extensions before they could even graze him.

Archie stood in place, watching with a wolfish grin as the flames roared higher and black smoke began curling through the canopy as the enlarged Amphislime convulsed and began liquefying into sizzling, dripping sludge.
