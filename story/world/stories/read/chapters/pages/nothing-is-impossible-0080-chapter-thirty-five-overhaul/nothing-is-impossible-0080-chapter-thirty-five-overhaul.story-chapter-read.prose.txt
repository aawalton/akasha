Chapter Thirty-Five: Overhaul

Processing complete. Drastic Changes have been made.

Many Titles have been lost because you no longer reach the requirements or require the direct help of the system to maintain.

Titles lost: Shadestrider, Synthesizer, Fate Breaker, Debuffer, Great Credit, Star and Team Assassin have all been lost.

You have lost two skills because of actions you have made and loss of system connection.

Skill lost: Spell Crafting and Dungeon Explorer.

You have lost access to one Candidacy but gained access to another and reinforced your main Candidacy.

You have lost 6 ties to Avatar of Death, but have gained 5 in Skill Sage and 4 in Void Sage.

You have changed a large aspect of yourself and blocked off the system’s direct influence. The system no longer can find you, neither can any of its shards or their underlings.

You have gained the title Fatechanger by changing the direction your fate was headed drastically.

Fatechanger: You have broken Fate’s hold on you and strove to become something more, something better, and from that endless pursuit, you have gained power beyond what your level implies. Every time a skill, spell, title, class, or other ability levels up, it will always gain a Lucky Bonus, which is now called a Fate Bonus. (Please note that Fate Bonus cannot be applied to everything, so it will only work on certain abilities) There is also a 0.1 percent chance of Insane Fate Bonus, and a 0.001 percent chance of Worldshaking Fate Bonus.

Your Title Skill Sage has reached a new level by altering all of your skills.

Skill Sage -> Self Skill Sage

Self Skill Sage: You are a Sage at creating skills, but have decided to focus more on yourself and care less for the skills of others. It is now 100 times easier to create and level said skills. Skill experience gain is no longer increased by 20% at each level and 200% at each tier, and instead becomes 1% and 10% increase respectively. (This effect is not retroactive). You will no longer be given skills by the system. (This does not apply to skills gifted from achievements). You can change the name of your skills, even if they are not 10 words or greater, and you can subtly change the effects of a skill, but it can never be stronger than the maximum its rank and tier suggest. You can view and alter the Nexuses of your skills, but cannot delete or create new skill Nexuses for your skills.

Your skills will all now be tied to the concept of Skills itself.

Your skill [Everflame Core] has become [Skillborne Core].

You have gained the Title Void Master because of your new deep connection with the void.

Void Master: You are a master of the void, and you can now feel its presence at all times, calling upon its unsettling power. Whenever you call upon it, you can choose to either buff yourself, or debuff your enemy. The buff or debuff is always Epic or higher rarity, but is otherwise random. The Void has a lesser hold over you, and you can shake off debilitating effects that originate from it as well as breath in the Void.

You will be ejected from your mindscape in 2 hours.

All of your soulbound items have become broken because of your abrupt change and lose of Avatar of Death Candidacy as well as deleting all connections to Death.

Atlas read through the changes, and he felt joy fill him. He had lost some titles, sure, but he would have made the same choice every time. He was so proud of himself for taking hold of his own fate and steering it in a new direction. Now that the system no longer had as much of a say in his life, he felt free for the first time. He looked at all of his skills and grimaced. While he was here, he might as well fix them all up, he had the time for it.

He first took all his void skills besides Eternal Vigilance and combined them together.

Combining…

Because of your title Skill Sage, and your Skill Sage Candidacy, you are able to choose between 6 options instead of leaving it up to luck.

Skill Upgrade options:

1: Fusion: All skills fuse into a stronger skill. How skills normally combine.

2: Evolution: Skills fuse to each other and a higher concept, effectively tiering them up and Fusing them at the same time.

3: Divergence: Skills combine and then separate, creating two slightly weaker skills with a wider variety of effects.

4: Evolutionary Divergence: Skills go through evolution and then divergence, in that order. Rarest effect, as it requires evolution and divergence to happen at the same time, which is rare in nature.

5: Mutation: The skills are fused and then a mutation concept is chosen. Mutation concept is completely random but must be a concept that you are connected to in some way.

6: Simplification: Skills concepts get simpler, but also more powerful after fusion.
