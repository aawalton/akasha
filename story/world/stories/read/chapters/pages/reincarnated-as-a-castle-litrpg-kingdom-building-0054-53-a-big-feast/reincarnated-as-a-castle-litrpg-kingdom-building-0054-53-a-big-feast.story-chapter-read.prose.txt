Gopugiga lowered the last massive chunk of the bear carcass with a heavy thud.

The moment it touched the ground, I swiftly stored it inside my inventory. At the very least, that should help preserve it a little longer. It had already been left outside for quite some time, after all.

The big fellow dropped onto his butt with a long, exhausted groan. Then he panted hard, chest heaving, breathing heavily through his mouth. The sound was so loud it was almost like a steam engine chugging away.

Nearby, the others were just as spent.

Every goblin was panting, their bodies drenched in sweat and soaked in sticky bear blood. Some leaned forward on their knees, gasping for air, while others simply collapsed where they stood, too tired to take another step.

I would have loved to give them quests to dismantle the carcass right away and start collecting materials as soon as possible.

But first, these guys needed rest and food.

So I let them take a proper break.

I bought a few large jars of water for them to wash and rinse themselves. It was drinking-grade water, yes, but since it was already almost nighttime, I didn’t want to make them trek all the way to the river for a bath. It was okay to be a little bit wasteful this once.

One by one, they picked up their jars and started pouring water over their bodies, scrubbing vigorously with their hands. They even helped wash each other’s backs and hard-to-reach spots, laughing and splashing water around like playful children.

The good thing about the System was that I could easily top up their jars every time they emptied them.

There was no soap at the moment though, so it was a bit hard to wash the stubborn blood off completely. Though… it’s not like goblins had the best sense of hygiene in the first place. Just being somewhat clean was more than enough.

And after that, it was finally time for a nice dinner for everyone.

****

Gopugiga and Gopuro went outside first. A few minutes later, they came back carrying armfuls of logs and branches to build a huge bonfire! I donated some extra wood from my inventory as well.

Using my [Lighting Manipulation] skill, I lit it up.

Fwwoom!

The big fire came to life with a roaring whoosh, bright flames rising and dancing wildly. The goblins sat down in front of the crackling blaze, their long shadows stretching and swaying across the courtyard with every flicker of the fire.

The night had fully settled by then, and the forest around us had grown dark and quiet. The sky was filled with sparkling stars, and I could see the crescent shape of the glowing moon rising higher by the minute.

But here, right in front of my walls, it was warm, bright, and alive.

Soon, the food appeared.

I materialized the bear meat from my inventory. Gopuran, with her [Cooking Lv.1] skill, volunteered to do the cooking. Gopuro’s job was sandwich assembling, while the others watched with drool dripping down their chins, their stomachs rumbling like battle drums.

As the meat started to grill, a wild yet rich and mouthwatering aroma spread throughout the air. Fat sizzled and hissed as it dripped onto the flames. The new citizens all grew restless, some even attempting to snatch the steaks and eat them raw, but I stopped them and promised that it would be worth the wait.

And just a few minutes later, it was ready.

Every piece of meat was perfectly grilled, golden brown with a nice charred crust.

(Damn…)

Even I was entranced by how good those pieces looked.

(So this is bear meat…)

The smell was a bit wild, a bit gamey, yet so rich and inviting. I wondered what bear meat actually tasted like. Would it be like beef? Or more like boar? I had never tasted bear meat before in my life. I wanted to know badly.

(If one day I can eat food again, I will definitely sample every kind of meat!)

Gopuran then distributed the food to everyone.

And they all chomped down.

They ate like feral beasts, moaning in bliss, tearing into the meat with wild enthusiasm!

“Mmmhm!!”

“Giii…!”

“This… this is good!”

Meanwhile, Gopugiga was on an entirely different level. That guy wasn’t eating.

He was inhaling!

Every time he opened his mouth, nearly half a kilo of meat disappeared without a trace in the blink of an eye!

At one point, he grabbed a foot-long sandwich… and finished it in two bites!

TWO!

(Haha, what a monster!)

Even I was a little stunned.

Still, the big guy absolutely deserved it.

If it hadn’t been for him hauling those massive chunks of the bear back here, I would have been forced to leave at least half of the carcass behind. So, if anyone had the right to eat like a bottomless pit, it was him.

Just then, I suddenly remembered something important.

"Oh shit! Puran!" I called out. "Use this too!"

From the Food Shop, I purchased a small white rock the size of a large egg.

"Crush this, and sprinkle it into your food! It will taste much better!"

Gopuran received the rock, tilted her head in confusion. She then licked it, her face scrunching up in distaste.

"Blergh! Big Boss, this edible too?"

"Yup! Trust me! Crush it and use just a little on your meat, but not too much!"

"Hmmmmm…"

Gopuran stared at the white rock, still skeptical. But eventually, she shrugged.

"If Big Boss says so!"

She placed the rock down on a clean stone she had washed earlier. Then, using another rock, smash! She pounded the white rock into smaller chunks!

"Keep going, you need to make it into powder!"

"Okay!"

And soon, the white rock had been crushed into fine powder!

Gopuran took a small pinch and sprinkled it on a piece of meat just like my instructions. She then took a bite. Everyone else had been watching her intently for some time now.

And in an instant…

"MMMMMMHHHH!!"

"DELISHHH!!!"

Gopuran gasped, her face lighting up with pure wonder!

"Big Boss, this is so DELISH!"

"Haha! I told you!"

Because that white rock… it was salt!

S-A-L-T.

SALT!

Back when I upgraded the Food Shop to Level 2, I was overjoyed the moment I saw it. But I could only purchase it now that I had some points to spare.

After all, a tiny 200g chunk of rock salt…

75 points!

OUCH!

But with this, their meal had improved to a whole new level!

If I could somehow figure out how to get pepper, then it would be even better. It would be perfect!!!

And so, everyone started sprinkling salt on their food with eager little pinches.

Some of the sillier ones even put salt into their milk and drank it, faces scrunching up in surprise before breaking into delighted grins at the new taste!

Watching my citizens this happy made me feel so warm inside too. There was a strange, fluttering sensation spreading through my stone walls.

(Perhaps it's just the bonfire. Haha…)
