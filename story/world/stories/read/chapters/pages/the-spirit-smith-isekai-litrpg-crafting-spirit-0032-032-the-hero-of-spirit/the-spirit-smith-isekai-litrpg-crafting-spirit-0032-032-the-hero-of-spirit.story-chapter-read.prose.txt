True enough to Claire’s words, I soon come upon a main road. I slow down to a more reasonable pace, so as not to randomly jump out of the woods and scare people. Only to walk out to a stampede of people running down the street.

It is pure chaos, as thousands of people try to move as fast as possible on the extraordinarily overcrowded street. What are they running away from? Some sort of monster most likely, and probably not one I can fight.

As I step further out from the trees, hundreds of eyes turn towards me. Fuck, I forgot to hide my cloak again. People stop moving, and just stare at me like I am the answer to all of their problems.

A young boy stumbles forward, having been pushed out of the crowd. He kneels down before me, like I am some bloody royal. “O great hero, please. Please save us all!” He cries out, and suddenly those behind him start to kneel and bow as well.

Everyone, from the youngest child capable of walking, to even some elves with gray hair that must be older than Blackharbor itself. The air fills with pleas for help and assistance. Some mention missing family members, buildings that they own, keepsakes.

I stare out at the crowd, jaw hanging open just a bit. They all want me to do something? I guess I am a hero or whatever, but this is just all too much. I don’t even have a clue what has happened.

Eventually, the commotion settles down a bit and people seem like they expect me to say something. Using every shred of my public speaking skills, I manage to get two words out. “With what?”

People look at me like im stupid and start looking back the way they came. Following their gaze, I see it.

About twenty kilometers away, the entire sky just turns bloodred. A few specks catch my vision, and I immediately recognize them as monsters. And behind them, more monsters, and more. As far as my perception allows, there is just a wave of monsters, stretching out and likely consuming all in their path.

Alrighty then, I will just get right to dealing with that. Hey Dovey, are you ready for a bit of combat?

Master, you must be joking. He replies.

Of course I am. I say, and then turn around and walk right back into the forest. See, the goddess did say that I could just ignore all of my responsibilities, right? So honestly, I will just take her up on that.

I continue walking into the forest, ignoring the notifications. Though it is far harder to ignore the thundering sound of feet as a crowd follows behind me. Most did continue running away down the path, but many are following me, as if I am going to do something.

Turning back, I am just about to tell everyone to fuck off when I feel a familar wet nose poke my cheek. “Yip?” My eyes meet Luma’s and I swear this fox is way too damn cute. A strange presence pushes against the edge of my mind, it feels oddly like Luma. “Yip!”

Sighing, I realize she is trying to show me something, and I let her. My world shifts as I let her into my mind. Gone is the forest, and instead is replaced by a vast grassland. A faint breeze wraps around me as I survey the area. Something tells me this is a memory, though I can tell it does not belong to Luma.

A few seconds pass, and then a white furball pops into view. Bounding over the grassland, in pursuit of some four-legged animal. The fox launches into the air, before dashing forward and tacking the beast to the ground.

Just as she is about to take a bite of her new meal, the terrain shifts. All of the grass goes from lush green, to a dark red. I glance upwards and see the once lovely blue sky tinting the same color.

Evil permeates the air, and I can almost feel it choking me.

My body instinctively turns to look up in the sky, a crack appears. It is small at first, but quickly widens, spiderwebbing across the sky. A thunderous boom shakes the land, and then the sky breaks.

A red rift forms, and monsters start to pour out. They drop down by the thousands, scouring the terrain. I watch as the little fox is trampled under the heels of a boar-monster.

Suddenly, my vision shifts out to a wider perspective. In the distance, I see a small village. An actually small one, home to not more than a thousand. Monsters trample it like it never even mattered.

Ah, I see now. This is what malice creates, not just monsters, but destruction incarnate. My perception is flown even further out, until I can see a land so vast that it defies all logic.

I realize that I must be seeing a good portion of the Realm of Humanity. To my horror, thousands of those rifts can be seen, breaking apart the beautiful landscape like a sort of corruption. Worse still, is that they just keep expanding, and expanding. Eventually, I am not able to see a single scrap of life remaining.

Suddenly the vision disappears, and my consciousness is abruptly snapped back to reality. I double over, my vision spinning, and I just barely manage to hold in my last meal.

I feel something fuzzy nuzzle my ear, as if checking to make sure I am okay. “Yip!” I turn and look at Luma, and the fox merely stares back at me. I swear there is a slight grin on her face.

If only she wasn't so damn cute. After giving her a few headscratches, I stand up and look towards the crowd that has been following me. Most of them are young, too young. Yet, each one holds a weapon.

I could tell them all to fuck off, then turn around and go find some random mountain to live on for the rest of my life. I could protect it, hide away from the problems of this world. Of that I am certain.

But, then, billions would die. That vision showed me what would happen. This malice would not stop. It would just keep spreading, until nothing is left.

While the goddess did say we had a choice, I bet she realized that not many would choose to turn away. Like, sure, I could turn away. But I have power, enough to save this world, I suppose.

Perhaps not alone, I will need help. Not just from more spirits, but allies, the other heroes even. A few cool abilities wouldn’t hurt too, I suppose. Plus, when I think about it, such a war would require a lot of very powerful gear… gear that is going to need a smith to make.

Heaving a great sigh, I open up those notifications.
