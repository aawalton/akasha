I was doing important work.

I spent time with the adventurer I love.

I was happy and content.

Time flew by.

A year flew by.

Nothing of note happened.

Maybe some things of note happened.

The new adventurers integrated well into the town. They bickered, squeaked, and made a lot of noise, but other than that, they eventually found jobs in NPC shops. Some joined the Stormcrows.

One day, I sold to five different customers and earned a total of 9 gold and 62 silver.

Another Scorpion Suricata monster joined us. A brother of Kim, named Stalin.

After two months, the one I love could walk without the sticks.

I killed a Huge Zebra-Deer. All by myself.

More adventurers joined the Stormcrows.

The one I love became more busy.He was working hard on his gas magic, and also now had what he called "administrative duties."

He could sit and speak into a magical device with faraway adventurers. He could be at that for hours. It was annoying to me.

We still had time together. But less than I wanted.

Santiago was now very good with the bow. I didn’t need to train him anymore. He could hunt alone, without Father.

I gave bow lessons to multiple adventurers. The one I love included—he was now good with the bow.

I made a horned snake pelt of supreme quality.

Kamatouu got conquered again by Aldioli forces. No one died in the battle. Hina and Pedro and Abel left. The old governor returned.

The one I love gave me a really nice present: a magic mirror.Each time he forgot something, the mirror would show his face at the moment of forgetfulness, for a whole day.

More adventurers join the Stormcrows.The one I love was more busy.

I had new important work:To teach Stormcrow recruits bow and arrow, hunting, and forest survival. I was more busy.

Even when the one I love and I were busy, we always found time to do things together.

I hunted another large Zebra-Deer game.Even if an annoying Stormcrow recruits made too much noise.

This year was peaceful.I was happy.The one I love was happy.

Then another thing of note happened.

The cabin door opened.

Vel’nari stood there.She was holding a little, sleeping baby goblin in her arms.Baby color was a paler shade of green.

The one I love looked surprised. He looked at the baby—And then paled a shade himself.His eyes filled with tears.

“She’s six months old,” said Vel’nari. “She can walk. She likes meat. And doesn’t need tit’s milk from me anymore.

In our tribe… it would be hard for her to grow. Goblins will treat her badly because of her human looks. I love her. But it’s better she grows up with humans. My place is with the tribe.”

Quiet.

“What… what’s her name?” he finally asked, voice shaky.

“We don’t name our young before they can fight,” Vel’nari explained.“You name her. Do it now. I want to know the name.”

The babe opened her eyes.She didn’t cry or make a sound. Just looked—curious.

The one I love looked at the babe.

“She has fierce eyes,” he said.

He smiled faintly, then looked down, his voice barely above a whisper when he spoke again.

“Miriam,” he finally said.

“Miriam,” Vel’nari repeated. “It sounds strong. I like it.”

Her eyes were wet with tears.She gave her babe a kiss and a squeeze.Then she put her in his arms.

“She… she is tough,” Vel’nari said quietly. “She will be a fierce fighter.”Then she left the cabin.

The one I love stood there, lost. Eyes full of tears.Miriam started crying, made a soft “mmm…” sound.Probably wanted her mommy back.

“Shh, shh,” he said, cradling and rattling her gently, trying to calm her.It didn’t work.Miriam gave a full-on crying now.

“Shh, shh,” he kept trying. He looked miserable and lost.He wasn’t good at that.

I took the babe from his arms.

She was half adventurer, half monster.She was different than any other creature I’d ever saw.

Then I sang.A song my mother taught me.Father said I should share it with kind travelers.It was a beautiful song.And I sang it beautifully.

“♪ Hush, hear the whisper, the forest takes breath,Wind through the branches, no shadow of death. ♪

♪ Spirit of greenwood, so gentle, so kind,Guide every traveler, guard every mind. ♪”

The babe loved the song.And after a few moments, she stopped crying entirely.

“♪ River runs silver, the moon’s gentle light,Owls keep their vigil through velvet of night. ♪

♪ Spirit of stillness, O keep us from harm,Cradle our footsteps in leaf and in charm. ♪”

Now the babe was asleep.I put her to bed and laid a small pelt over her to keep her warm.

The one I love came to me and hugged me.

“I don’t know,” he said in a shaky voice.“I don’t know how to be a father.”

“A steady hand and a patient heart can raise a tree tall enough to touch the sky,” I said.

Then I added,“If your heart is steady, and your spirit gentle—you will not fail.”

Then I added again,“Your strength may falter. But mine will be there to steady it.”

He looked at me and smiled.“You’re right,” he said. “With you… there’s nothing I can’t do. Even be a kickass parent.”
