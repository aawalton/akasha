To Asher’s surprise, rather than suddenly finding themselves standing in a stone-and-mortar tunnel or the basement of some spiraling base, walking through the fake rocks led them into what looked like a completely natural cave. While it was certainly a large one, easily a few hundred feet wide, it definitely didn’t scream ‘evil wizard lair’ at him.

“Huh… I don’t see any demons,” Moxy said, her disappointment clear on her face as she looked around, dropping her fighting stance. “So far, not impressed.”

“The cave leads into the mountain,” Rulfar pointed out, still holding his gleaming battleaxe at the ready. “Maybe it goes straight into the actual base. The lich’s scent fills this whole cave, I still can’t tell any more than that.”

“It’s not as if we have any better ideas,” Samantha said, her eyes never pausing as they scanned the cave from wall to wall and back again.

As a team, the four of them stepped gingerly forward, careful to try and keep their footsteps light. Even Moxy, who wasn’t often a fan of skulking around, seemed to understand the severity of the situation they were in. Seeing as Asher had shared with her the memory of him being torn literally in half from the greater demon he fought, she definitely knew what they were up against.

Once they left the entrance, the light level in the cave quickly dropped, until it was nearly pitch black. Rulfar had a skill with his Bear element that allowed him to see in near total-darkness, and Asher had his evolved darkness resistance, but Samantha and Moxy ended up needing to drink their potions of Darkvision. With that taken care of, they continued deeper into the cave, navigating around stalagmites and chunks of rock as they pushed ever deeper.

Now that they were practically creeping through the lich’s basement, the mood had changed completely from when they were flying over here. Not a single one of them made so much as a sound, all of them completely focused at the task at hand.

It was that intense focus that ended up saving all their lives.

As they crept along the cave, there was some sort of… sensation from up ahead, beyond the stalagmites and rocks blocking their vision. It wasn’t quite like anything Asher had ever felt before, but if he had to pick something that it reminded him of…

A far more concentrated wave of Draken’s death aura.

“Move!” Asher screamed, expanding the space in front of them with Distortion as much as he could to buy them an extra second. He’d made the right call, as the four of them barely managed to get out of the way before a thick line of darkness blasted straight through the rocks in front of them, directly through where they’d been walking a moment ago, and continued right on through the rocks at their backs. Asher stared in horror at the wide hole that had been blasted in a completely straight line all the way back to the entrance to the cave, letting in a bit more natural light as he peered outside. Wherever that darkness had touched, the rock simply ceased to exist. It hadn’t been melted, or disintegrated, or anything along those lines.

It was just gone.

Before they could fully recover, there was an ear-splitting roar, and something that simply shouldn’t be rose up from the cave in front of them. Due to all the rocks and natural cave features, they hadn’t been able to see it until now, but that was hardly a problem anymore.

The creature stood nearly two stories tall, and was covered from head to toe in dark purple scales. For a moment, Asher thought they were facing off against a dragon. It had the wings, the scales, and the sweeping tail of a dragon.

But where its head should have been, there was simply a neck that ended in a gaping maw of purple-dripping teeth, each drop of which seemed to erode straight through the stone floor where they fell. Despite the fact that it didn’t have anything even closely resembling eyes, the creature turned and swung its maw in their direction, roaring again.

“What the hell is that thing?!” Asher shouted, his mind drawing a blank as he tried to skim through various bestiaries he’d Incorporated during his time in the library. Because he wasn’t all that interested in monsters, he hadn’t actually taken the time to thoroughly read any of those books until now, but he was pretty certain something like this would have stuck out to him.

“It looks like a dragon!” Rulfar said, already morphing into his battle form. As fur sprouted out of his skin and he rose to his full ten-foot height, he gripped his battleaxe in both hands as he began running at the monster before them. “I’ll keep it distracted! Samantha, cover!”

He needn’t have said anything, as while the three of them had all dodged to the side, Samantha had used her wings to dodge up. Already, the hunter was crouched on a small floating cloud, trying to pick out a weak spot on the monster. But as soon as Rulfar asked for her help, she obliged.

Glowing arrow after glowing arrow shot forth from Samantha’s position, each one being used to test various parts of the monster for weaknesses. Despite her bow’s piercing enchantment, not a single one of the arrows managed to find purchase. Each one bounced harmlessly off the purple scales, and the beast roared again, before turning and letting out another blast of darkness directly at Samantha.

Deactivating her boots, Samantha fell straight through her small cloud, narrowly dodging the deadly attack. Catching herself with her wings, she began flying around the cave, doing her best to keep it distracted. Her aim suffered greatly while flying, but keeping the thing’s focus on her gave the rest of them all the openings they needed.

Rulfar was the first to reach the monster, his speed while charging in battle form nothing to scoff at. With a roar of his own, he cleaved forward with his axe, the metal shimmering violet as he finally activated its ravage enchantment. What should have happened was whatever injury Rulfar managed to inflict upon the creature, nearly three times the blood would be spilled from the wound. It was the perfect enchantment for a Blood element user, and Asher had thought it practically fate after they’d stolen the battleaxe from the lich’s old tower.

What actually happened, however, was the glowing battleaxe bouncing harmlessly off the creature’s scales with a powerful clang just like Samantha’s arrows.

That’s not good, Asher thought as he followed up right behind Rulfar. In his battle form and with his Strength element, Rulfar was definitely their heavy hitter. If he wasn’t able to get past the creature’s scales with his strength, the rest of them weren’t going to fare much better. Still, that didn’t mean they wouldn’t try.

Asher ran to the other side of the creature still trying fruitlessly to blast Samantha out of the sky, and he used Distortion to elongate his mithral dagger before attempting to plunge it in between two of the purple scales on the creature’s flank. To his horror, even Crescent bounced off without so much as a scratch, and he stared at his masterwork mithral blade in shock. Nothing had ever managed to shrug off Crescent so effortlessly, and he was at a complete loss as to what to try next.

Luckily, Moxy didn’t suffer from such issues.

With her feral grin plastered across her face, Moxy went next, laughing as she began shadow boxing, punching a wild barrage of air bullets directly at the creature’s neck. To Asher's continued shock, each of her punches actually seemed to do something, the spread of them hitting with enough force to make the monster stumble sideways and nearly topple over. With a fresh roar, it changed its focus, firing a blast of darkness toward Moxy instead.

Moxy launched herself out of its path with an explosion of air, throwing out a few more punches as she soared through the cave. Asher watched as, yet again, the monster seemed actually annoyed by these punches. They weren’t bypassing its scales or doing any real damage, but where he and Rulfar had been completely ignored, Moxy’s attacks were at least doing something.

But why? He wondered, looking at Rulfar and noting how the bloodhound hadn’t let up in the slightest. Again and again Rulfar used his muscular form to slam his enchanted battleaxe down onto the side of the creature, desperate to so much as lay a scratch on it. Yet despite all his efforts, the shimmering battleaxe continued to bounce off, the ravager enchantment not even able to take effect.

Wait a second…

With a start, Asher cancelled his use of Distortion, returning his mithral dagger back to its normal length. While the monster was busy trying to blast Moxy out of the air as she leapt from stalagmite to stalagmite, laughing all the while, Asher stepped forward.

And rammed his mithral dagger straight into the monster’s nearest leg, the gleaming blue blade punching through the scales without any issue whatsoever.

For the first time, the monster let out a roar that sounded a lot higher pitched than the earlier ones, and black blood began spilling out of its injury. Asher slipped into the astral just in time to dodge the spray of foul liquid, which seemed to have the same weird effect as whatever this thing’s breath attacks were. Everywhere the blood landed that wasn’t part of the monster was simply erased. A quick check of Crescent confirmed that at the very least, mithral seemed strong enough to hold up to whatever that liquid was, and he quickly returned to the material realm, shouting out his findings.

“It’s immune to skills!” he shouted, using Distortion to dart to the creature’s back leg and slam his dagger into that one as well, hopefully cutting through something important as the monster screamed yet again. This time, it attempted to slash at him with its tail, spinning around so fast that Asher barely managed to slip into the astral in time to dodge having his broken body slapped all the way back to the cave entrance.

Astral Dip – Lvl 34

Returning to the material, he continued. “I don’t know how, but skills won’t work! That goes for enchantments as well!”

“Guess my air blasts are somewhat effective since they’re mainly air!” Moxy laughed, taking advantage of Asher drawing the thing’s attention to start laying into the monster’s neck once more.

“Its blood is just like its breath though!” Asher called out, right before Rulfar could try slamming his battleaxe into the monster without the enchantment active. “Don’t waste your axe! I got this!”

“We'll keep it distracted!” Samantha shouted, streaking through the air far closer to the monster’s mouth than Asher was comfortable with as she tried to fire an arrow straight down the creature’s gaping maw. Despite missing the shot, she succeeded in drawing its attention off him once more, and the thing’s entire neck snapped forward, barely missing taking a chunk out of her.

Not wanting to see his girlfriend get either erased or eaten right in front of him, Asher went as fast as he could. Even if he wasn’t able to make Crescent longer, he could still use Distortion to run around below the creature far faster than most people, and he used that to his advantage. Dragging Crescent along the thing’s flesh and stabbing it again and again, Asher focused on its legs first, trying to hamstring it and slow its movements. Whatever the creature was, however, he was beginning to suspect it didn’t have any sort of muscle like a regular creature. No matter where he cut or stabbed, nothing more than that black, deadly bile spilled out.

Sever the Thread – Lvl 25

At the very least, that stuff still seemed to be rather important, as it wasn’t long before the creature began to noticeably slow.

“It’s slowing down!” Samantha shouted, the first one to notice as she zipped past its maw yet again. Sure enough, Asher realized she was right, as the monster attempted to crush Asher with its tail again, only to stumble and spin around at nearly half of its original speed. Seeing his attacks were actually working, Asher went at it again with renewed vigor, stabbing and slashing into it everywhere he could reach. None of his wounds were very deep, considering the monster was multiple times his own size, but thanks to Sever the Thread, not a single one closed up. Dozens upon dozens of wounds continued to trickle that creepy black bile all over the ground and eat up the cave floor, and the monster steadily grew weaker with every strike Asher landed. Finally, after what felt like far too many close brushes with death, it happened.

The monster tried to step and slap Asher with its tail yet again, only for its leg to give out and it to collapse to the ground in a powerful heap. Rulfar, who hadn’t done much besides try and draw its attention until now, leapt forward, landing on its neck and using his impressive strength to keep it pinned down. From how he was jerking about, it seemed the monster was still actively trying to ‘look’ around for something to blast.

“Finish it!” Rulfar shouted, snarling as he slammed the neck down again.

Needing no further encouragement, Asher ran over to the neck that had been largely out of reach all this time. Slamming his dagger into it, he sprinted along the length of the neck, slicing the entire thing open from end to end and causing a truly tremendous amount of black bile to spill out and start eating into the stone floor. It seemed that amount of bile was simply too much for the monster to lose, as it let out one final, weakened roar before going limp.

Panting, Asher stared at his team, raising his dagger into the air and letting out a whooping cry of victory that was quickly joined by the others. Not a single one of them had any idea what they’d just fought, but that didn’t matter.

Their first real battle as a team was finally over, and they’d come out on top.
