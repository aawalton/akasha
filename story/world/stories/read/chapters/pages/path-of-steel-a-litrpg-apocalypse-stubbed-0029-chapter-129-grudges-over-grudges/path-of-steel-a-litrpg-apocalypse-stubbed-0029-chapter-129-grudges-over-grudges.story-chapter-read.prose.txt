“Everyone,” Rollo Frollo said, his voice turning serious. “I have a final fighter to present. The Heaven Reaching clan’s number one junior disciple. A man head and shoulders above his peers. He has a high earth talent, but that wasn’t enough for him. Day after day, he trained hard, until he reached a level that most other competitors would need binoculars to even stare at.”

The projection grew until it covered half the arena. A single man appeared, a long ponytail fluttering to the wind. The women in the audience cheered. He wore cyan robes, and his back could be seen, where the number one was embroidered in golden silk.

This was his last match before the finals. A woman flew forward with her spear extended, the projection switching to an angle which highlighted her enormous power. Feng Shen brushed the spear aside with his fingers. The woman declared her defeat.

“It was a wonderful strike,” Feng Shen said.

“The kind, invincible, absolute senior brother of the Heaven Reaching clan! Will anyone steal his throne? Can they even force him to reveal his true power?” Rollo Frollo yelled, his voice rising an octave. “I present to you, the absolute favorite of this tournament, the child of the spear, the yin yang prodigy… Feeeng Sheeen!”

The audience cheered again, so hard that their voices flooded the stadium, shaking it to the foundation. Feng Shen stood up from the disciple seats and raised a hand. The cheering intensified.

“He truly has the aura of the first-ranked disciple…” Cog muttered. “Look at that hand raise. So professional.”

“Lin Xin and Master Colorful told me we have no chance against him,” Gary said.

“Focus on Sui Duang, Gary. You must defeat him!” Alicia said.

The projection changed, from a person to an empty board. It was the finals brackets, but the names were blurred out. “Without further ado, here are the match-ups!” Rollo announced.

The names turned clear at the same moment that two deacons pulled a veil off the actual board, placed at a wall high in the stadium. Gary’s eyes searched for his name. When he saw his first opponent, he breathed a huge sigh of relief.

“Hey!” Cog said. “That’s awesome!”

“Yeah,” Gary replied.

“That guy’s an asshole! Beat him up, Gary!” Goldie said.

“Thank God we don’t have to jump him,” Alicia said with relief.

A line connected Gary’s name to that of his first opponent: Lord of Pot. The same person who’d injured both Cog and Alicia during the preliminaries. He’d been particularly heavy-handed.

“Time to return the favor,” Goldie said.

Gary looked higher up in the disciple seats, meeting his opponent’s gaze. The Lord snorted and looked away. There had been no fear in his eyes. Gary grinned.

“Before we proceed to the matches of the first day, only the rewards remain,” Rollo Frollo said, sparking curiosity in the audience. “First, though, a special announcement. Something that hasn’t happened in years. Look here!”

A blue arrow appeared, pointing at a certain part of the brackets. It was a semifinals match, both participants still unnamed.

“This will be a special match!” Rollo Frollo announced. “Assuming they both win their first rounds, Gary Steel and Sui Duang are going to clash here…but it won’t be a simple match! Those two are enemies! Gary Steel accuses Sui Duang of harming his friends and attempting to harm Gary himself. Sui Duang accuses Gary Steel of grave disrespect and slander, along with sabotage during the clan’s recent expedition to the third floor. Their differences are mortal and completely beyond reconciliation! Just two weeks ago, Gary Steel publicly challenged Sui Duang to a Duel of Honor, and that duel will be held right here, on the sands of this arena!”

The audience cheered. Gary felt fear rising in his heart, but he remained calm. He knew this time would come. Meeting Sui Duang on the semifinals, on the third day, was good enough timing.

“What if one of them is eliminated?” a loud person in the audience asked.

“If either disciple is eliminated before they meet, they will fight in the afternoon of the same day! Even if it risks making them tired for the next day’s match, they have both agreed that the Duel of Honor takes priority and should not be delayed!”

Gary had never been asked about this, nor had he agreed.

“Gary, speak up!” Goldie said.

“I’m no coward,” he replied calmly. To object this decision would show fear. He didn’t plan to lose to either of his opponents, and Sui Duang wouldn’t either. Feng Shen was in the other bracket.

Wait. He wouldn’t lose on purpose, would he? Gary realized. As he thought better about it, however, there was no way Sui Duang would throw away the tournament and his honor just to fight Gary a day or two earlier.

He looked across the disciple seats, where Sui Duang was seated. Their gazes met. Sparks flew. The tall man smirked. Gary narrowed his eyes.

“Boy, he’s ugly,” Goldie said.

“Will the water drown Gary? Or will Sui Duang lose his hair?” Rollo announced. “Of course, the number two disciple is the clear favorite, but Gary undoubtedly has an ace or two up his sleeve. I’m sure the audience would love to know what the fighters think! I wonder, would someone care to comment? A particular talkative spirit, for example?”

As every eye fell on them, Gary paled.

“They want me to speak, Gary! I’m famous!” Goldie exclaimed.

“Please don’t,” Gary said.

“But he asked me!”

The crowd started heckling.

“Yeah, spirit! Tell us more about the presenter’s mother!” someone shouted.

“The bald snake shall speak!”

“Please tell us, can the baldinator win?”

“Of course he can win,” Goldie said, drawing some aura from Gary to amplify her voice.

“Please don’t reveal anything about the songs or offend unnecessary people,” Gary whispered. She winked back at him.

“Sui Duang is a piece of shit. Gary will slaughter him,” she said. The audience cheered.

“What a bold statement!” Rollo exclaimed. “I wonder, Spirit of Golden Bolt, aren’t you afraid of retribution at all? The Sui family holds tremendous power and prestige in the Heaven Reaching clan.”

Goldie snorted and almost said something, then paused for a moment and thought better about it.

“A little,” she replied.

Bravo, Goldie! Don’t let him bait you! Gary thought.

“I’ve heard that Gary Steel is a duel connoisseur! During the clan selection, he challenged and eliminated the younger cousin of Sui Duang, then killed a Rat family member in another Duel of Honor shortly after joining the clan. It’s almost like he has a bone to pick with those two families!” Rollo said.

“It was a coincidence. Gary fears no one but also hates no one. He only responds when provoked,” Goldie replied.

“So the families provoked him?”

“Those particular disciples did. I don’t know about any families,” Goldie replied.

“Fascinating!” Rollo said. If he was disappointed at not getting the wrong comment out of Goldie, he didn’t show it. “What about this duel? Gary Steel is supposed to be weaker than his opponent. What gives you the confidence he will win?”

“He works hard and does things right. Most importantly, he has me. He will win, one hundred percent.”

“What do you mean he does things right?”

“He isn’t impulsive. He respects his master’s teachings and practices diligently and smartly. Also, he’s very smart and kind.”

“His master!?” Rollo exclaimed, like he’d gotten what he wanted. Gary frowned. Master Colorful being his master was no secret. Many in the clan knew. Was there a problem?

“Now I see,” Rollo said, laughing lightly. “It did seem weird that Gary Steel made so many enemies of the Sui and Rat family, but come to think of it, his master is Vice Head Colorful. It makes more sense now—”

All of a sudden, Rollo choked. The sands around him were pressed to the ground. The projection fizzled out. Silence spread in the stadium.

“Keep my name out of your little mouth,” a calm voice came from the VIP seats. Colorful hadn’t even stood up. He remained sitting, yet his seemingly casual words had reached every ear, and his aura was choking Rollo.

As everyone reeled, the man with the tall hat coughed lightly. The pressure over Rollo disappeared, and he could breathe again. Colorful snorted in disgruntlement. “You were hired to raise the spirits, not to pit senior clan members against each other,” Colorful said.

The presenter directed a venomous glare at the VIP seats but said nothing. Elder Sui, however, did speak.

“He wasn’t starting trouble, merely stating the facts,” he said, not standing up. “If those facts are shameful, Colorful, that’s nobody else’s problem. You just choked a man. Don’t clad your oppression in righteousness.”

“You speak of shame? Truly, the heavens must have turned today,” Colorful said.

“Um, Gary? What’s going on?” Alicia whispered.

“I haven’t the slightest idea,” Gary replied. The audience had fallen quiet, not daring to support or jeer at Elder-level figures.

Elder Sui laughed. “I, too, was wondering how a mere drafted disciple could be so conceited as to challenge the geniuses of my family. It all came together when I learned he was your disciple. The apple falls under the apple tree. Did you really need to place your vendettas on the younger generation?”

“Gary’s enemies are his own, Sui. I respect my word. His enmities were forged before I took him on as a disciple,” Colorful replied.

“But you still trained him. How is that not acting against my family? Only less than a century after you took your oath before the Clan Head, and you already break it in spirit, if not directly. If the main clan’s Enforcement Hall hadn’t objected, I would have apprehended you and passed judgement already. A tool is only useful if it doesn’t bite its master’s hand.”

“If you want to apprehend me, you can try anytime,” Colorful replied, eyes gleaming.

“Oh yeah? How will you resist me without a spear?” Elder Sui said, laughing out loud. The VIP seats exploded. The powerful people sitting in the line between Elder Sui and Colorful were suddenly standing to the side, while chairs rained to the sands below. A baleful aura reigned. Elder Sui stood slowly, pushing through the pressure, his face red by the effort. Elder Rat stood up with him.

“There is a limit to my patience, dog. I am barred from acting against your family, but if you keep pushing me, it will be personal,” Colorful said. He was also standing, and his robes fluttered to an unseen wind. While they normally seemed ridiculous and clown-like, nobody was getting that impression today. Some cautious spectators were already departing the arena.

The man with the tall hat stepped in between them. Many broken chairs cracked. A chair leg flew over the stands. The pressure on Elder Sui visibly lessened.

“Please calm down, everyone. What’s done is done. Why must we feed the public our clan matters?” he asked, more pleading than commanding. The air over him shimmered. The pressure wasn’t really affecting him, but he wasn’t strolling around either.

Elder Sui ignored the Clan Head. “What a temper! Truly, spears never dull, and old men never learn. I meant no personal attack—I was merely stating the facts, and what I considered righteous. Since when can Elders, even toppers, use their aura to injure people that the clan has hired? Since when can the vice head of a branch, banished here for precisely this kind of behavior, throw away our dignity?”

“Elder Sui, that’s enough,” the clan head said, more forcefully this time. Elder Sui smiled bitterly and took a step back, not sitting yet.

The entire arena held its breath. Colorful was silent. His eyes were dark and hidden, his rage a bottled storm. From the fluctuations of his aura, Gary and the entire audience felt emotions brushing against them—anger, shame, frustration, helplessness. Pain. Colorful sat back down and retrieved his aura. He said nothing, acknowledged no one.

The stadium’s previously lively atmosphere had been catapulted out the window. Most people had forgotten about the tournament. Everyone had different thoughts, but as the deacons flew to the VIP seats and swiftly gathered the wreckage, Gary’s mind was awash with realizations.

“That was too much,” a nearby disciple whispered.

“That Colorful is a tyrant…” someone whispered. She probably hadn’t intended for Gary to hear her, but high Vitality increased the range of his senses. He didn’t care, anyway.

My master was humiliated… Gary thought. People blame him, and I guess they’re right. It really was too much. But why? He’s not an idiot. What made him lash out like that? What did Rollo say?

Since when does Master Colorful have enmity with the Sui family? Is that why he picked me at the clan selection? Because I messed with Keng Sui?

He was banished to this floor. Why? What did he do?

Most importantly…

“How will you resist me without a spear?” was the taunt that made Colorful erupt. Like it touched an extremely sensitive subject. Memories flew to Gary’s mind, which he only now understood. Something obvious he’d never noticed.

He remembered Master Colorful using his fingers to demonstrate a technique. Picking a stick off the ground and using it as a spear.

“How will you resist me without a spear?”

The puzzle was completed in Gary’s mind. His eyes widened to the extreme. Master Colorful…has lost his spear!?
