Another small demon lunged at Luke, who was in too much pain to mount any sort of decent defense. Sharp, long teeth sank into the muscle of his arm, but the pain was nothing compared to the surging agony radiating out from his Meta-Heart. Taking infernal mana into himself in the middle of a fight had not been the most thought-through decision.

A thread of mana with Needle of Life affixed to its end moved out from the mana channels in Luke's arm and in through the monster's mouth before slicing upward. These assholes didn't have much in the way of brainpower, but stirring around in there still produced the desired effect. The orange-glowing, dim light in its otherwise empty little eyes died, leaving two dark marbles. It fell to the ground, jaws opening.

Closing the wounds left by the demon's teeth, Luke pushed to his feet and retrieved his metal quarterstaff. By then, the pain had lessened somewhat, but the infernal mana still swirled around in there without integrating with his own.

"You okay?" Ray shouted. "I'm running out of explosion potions! Need sulphur to make more!"

"Fresh out of sulphur!" Luke shouted back, hefting his staff and feeling the strength bonus from wielding it empowering him. The unceasing flow of demons was still, well, unceasing, but he thought they had perhaps slowed down a little. Moving forward, he slammed the staff into the head of a demon, then blocked a blow from another. Its claws scraped against the metal of the staff, throwing up sparks. He ignored them and the slobbering mouth trying to reach him. A thread of mana was all he needed to deal with that particular monster, and he continued on after putting a new infernal core into his inventory.

Luke hadn't been wrong. The flow of demons lessened. It gave him a glimmer of hope that this annoying fight would be over soon, but that hope shattered when the ground shook again in what he now knew was no earthquake.

A monstrous, large hand reached out from the rift, dust and debris falling away. Ray's explosion potions had collapsed the opening in the ground, but the new arrival muscled through, ignoring the obstacles like they were nothing but mere annoyances. The hand and subsequent arm were reddish with thin lines of orange glow running along the skin. What surprised Luke about this particular demon was that its hands looked human. Large, yes, and red, but still human. Unlike the smaller demons, the fingers did not end in sharp claws. Another hand appeared, and then it heaved itself up in one smooth motion.

"What the hell is that?" Ray shouted from atop the bus stop.

"Hell is right," Luke muttered, staring at what amounted to a man as it climbed out of the rift. A tall, red man. Perhaps giant was a better way to describe it, as it stood twice as tall as Luke, but there was something human about it. Perhaps it was the fact that it wore clothes that looked a lot like a black T-shirt and shorts. Or maybe the faux-hawk, it took time adjusting, while Luke stared at it. The glowing-orange eyes did give it more of a demonic appearance, as well as its long but lithe tail.

"Greetings," it said, its voice a low grumble. A smaller demon crawled out of the rift with a roar, but the new arrival sneered and raised a foot, crushing it.

"Hey," Luke said. "You're a demon."

"My name is Arazek, and I'm no demon."

"You look like a demon, though!" Ray shouted.

Arazek turned his head toward Ray. "My father was a demon, but my mother was not."

"Why have you come here?" Luke asked, taking the lull in action as a good way to create some healing mana for his Meta-Heart.

"That should be obvious, I believe," Arazek said, a sword as wide and tall as Luke appearing in his hand. "I have come to conquer!"

Luke: "Lemira, do you know a half-demon called Arazek?"

He saw Ray disappear into stealth as the demon took a step forward. Now that Arazek no longer blocked the rift, more of the smaller demon variety climbed out and joined the fight. Luke steadied his breathing and waited for their main opponent's move. Every second meant more time for mana regeneration, and he would need every speck before this fight was over.

Lemira: "Is he alone?"

Luke: "Bunch of smaller demons with him."

Lemira: "Frienza isn't present?"

Luke: "Not that I can see."

Lemira: "That is well. Arazek is known to me. He is powerful, but not to a ludicrous degree. His mother was a non-demon, so he is considered low-born in the eyes of most. He is associated with Frienza, who was one of my father's main competitors. If you see her, you must flee."

Lemira: "Promise me, Luke. I am nearing the guild headquarters. Be careful."

The communication happened in the blink of an eye, since the text sort of printed itself in his consciousness rather than Luke having to read it, as if off a screen. Even so, during that brief moment of inattention, Arazek made his move. Charging forward, the half-demon raised his sword high. It closed the distance between itself and Luke with impressive speed, chopping down. Metal met stone as it hit the ground where Luke had stood with enough force to bury it.

Luke, having leapt to the side to avoid the obvious attack, retaliated by slamming the staff down on the half-demon's arms, hoping to disarm the bastard. While Arazek did let go of his weapon, it was before Luke had a chance to connect. Rather than hit Arazek's arms, the half-demon intercepted the staff, grabbing the end of it in one massive palm.

"Humans are no better than children," it growled, yanking the staff out of Luke's hands. After giving the weapon a once-over, Arazek threw it over his shoulder with a disdainful snort. The weapon hurtled away at an upward angle and crashed through the window several floors up.

Following that display of strength, Arazek leaned forward to grab its sword. Luke wasn't about to let that happen. Lunging for it, Luke got there first and took it into his inventory.

Demonic Blade of Unparalleled Strength: +25 Strength. Increased sharpness and weight.

The half-demon bellowed in rage, and the reaching hand turned into a fist. Before Luke knew what'd happened, he was on the ground, pain radiating from his left shoulder. It was crushed with bone fragments so small it might as well have been pieces of Lego. Weavestepping to avoid his head being stomped, Luke found himself right next to one of the smaller demons. It bit down on his leg, the corners of its mouth turning up, as if smiling.

Arazek kicked the demon. The force of the blow popped it like a balloon, its orange, glowing innards spraying out in a shower. Having the monster's clamped-down jaw removed by force meant Luke losing a chunk of flesh from his leg. With wounds like those, he had no choice but to use Weaver's Renewal, despite the massive mana usage.

Restored, Luke avoided a kick and closed in on Arazek. A physical fight against this opponent wouldn't end well. That left him with few options other than using Threads of Mana. To Luke's surprise, he found a Meta-Heart in the half-demon's chest, rather than an infernal core. And a welcome surprise it was. Mana, oh delicious mana. Luke drank it in even as he weaved out of the way of a punch by ducking under his opponent's legs.

It was in that moment Ray found the opening he must have been waiting for, since he hadn't joined the fight right away. The Assassin appeared in the air, above Arazek's head, and used the falling momentum to drive two daggers into the half-demon's unprotected neck. Roaring, Arazek reached up and grabbed Ray, squeezing the life out of him. The decoy fell to the ground, and Ray appeared again, this time right in front of Luke, who'd positioned himself just behind Arazek's legs. Ray drove his daggers straight up and through the underside of the half-demon's shorts.

The roar of pain was much louder after the second attack. Ray winked at Luke, then disappeared again. Wanting to end the fight, Luke used The Healer's Moment, and time froze.

Inside the half-demon's mana channels, mana rushed from its Meta-Heart to the injured areas. Or it would have if Luke didn't have the time-stopping ability that had saved him so many times. Not wanting to waste the opportunity, Luke pushed several threads of mana into the meta-heart, drawing mana from it even while cutting into it. Most of the time, he would have attacked the physical heart at the same time, but Arazek didn't have one. While the metaphysical aspect of his physiology matched that of a human, the physical part was all demon, all orange sludge.

Something pushed against Luke. Frowning, he kept up the attacks until the sensation returned. It was like someone from the outside was trying to crack his little bubble of calm before time was up. Was this what had happened when the skill failed in that previous fight? Refusing to let a repeat of that happen, Luke pushed back with his will, forcing the sensation away. It was a struggle, but he managed it without wasting too much time or focus.

The moment Luke returned his attention to Arazek's Meta-Heart, he saw that the mana inside was starting to move. That should be impossible. Time was Luke's during the effect of this skill. The Healer's Moment remained unbroken, but that did nothing to stop what was happening inside the half-demon. As if that wasn't bad enough, orange, burning sludge was seeping into the mana channels and even the meta-heart, the two mixing into some horrible concoction.

It snaked its way into Luke's threads of mana, and before he could cut them off, some of that new mix of mana and sludge made its way into Luke's mana channels.

The moment it took hold inside him, time resumed as The Healer's Moment shattered.
