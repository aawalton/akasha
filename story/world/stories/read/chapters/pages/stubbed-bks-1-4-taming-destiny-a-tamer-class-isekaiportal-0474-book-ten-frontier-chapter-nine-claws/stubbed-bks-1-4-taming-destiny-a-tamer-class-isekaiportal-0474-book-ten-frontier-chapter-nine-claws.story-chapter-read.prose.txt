I briefly consider pushing her back into unconsciousness. It would be easy enough with the control I have over her body through Flesh-Shaping and the Bond.

But I don’t. Because I know that Kalanthia would see it as a betrayal for all that it would be to protect her as much as anyone else. I’ve worked too hard to build up her trust to break it now.

However, I have to prepare for her to wake up in a panic. The wounds littering her skin told an awful tale and I’ve suffered enough trauma in my life to know that it casts a shadow long after it has ended. For all I know, Kalanthia doesn’t even realise she’s out of wherever she was – she may not have been conscious when Bond Teleport activated.

At least there are very few others still around who might get hurt if she turns violent. I urge Tiria and Keshaka to back away, which they do with wary glances at the nunda. For myself, I decide to stay close but activate my shields in both my tattoo and armour – she was unable to hurt me too badly through my armour when we tested it after I first got it so the combination should make me safe enough.

Then, placing my hand on her shoulder, I rub it gently, projecting my voice calmly through our Bond.

Kalanthia, you’re with the pack, you’re safe, you’re home.

I repeat the words again and again, accompanying them with waves of caring reassurance.

I feel it as she comes to full consciousness. She doesn’t move – she freezes, her muscles tensing like they’re made of iron.

Her side of the Bond is unusually open – I feel it as panic flares hot and intense. It’s followed with a wave of fear that crashes over her a moment later. I reply with my own wave of reassurance and calm, projecting my litany on repeat.

Slowly, her initial reaction begins to ebb a little. I feel tiny movements as she shifts very slightly – most probably to feel her limits, to feel the bindings that have torn away, the wounds that no longer pain her.

And then she shifts her head and freezes.

I close my eyes in silent prayer. She’s found the muzzle.

If I am home, if I am safe, why is this still here? she demands with volcanic fury, fueled by her fear and leashed only by the faintest trace of hope. Her mental voice is slightly blurred, almost sounding like it’s coming from underwater. That alarms me as it indicates just how much she has fallen into her more base instincts. But that she’s speaking at all is a massive relief.

Because it’s blood-bound and I haven’t managed to get it off yet, I tell her honestly, shoving my sincerity down the Bond towards her. I swear, the moment I figure out how, it will no longer mar your face. It is no less than an instrument of torture and I will not tolerate it around a single second longer than I must.

She’s not convinced. Not entirely. What has happened to her? I wonder with an ache in my heart.

Trailing my hand down her shoulder so she knows where I am at all times, I round the corner and stare into her golden gaze. It hurts me to see those orbs so filled with remembered pain and fear instead of the predatory confidence that I’m used to.

“Kalanthia,” I murmur, wincing as she flinches at the sound of my voice. Another piece to the puzzle. Kalanthia, I don’t know what you’ve been suffering, or why – only that you clearly have. But you are home, you are free, and the moment I can rid you of this muzzle, I will. If you don’t trust that, then look – I am here. Do as you will.

She cannot bite me – the muzzle prevents that. But her paws are free. I even deactivate my shields – I only activated them just in case she reacted unthinkingly, guided more by trauma than reality. Standing before her as an easy target, I make sure there’s nothing but trust in the Bond and in my eyes.

Her gaze searches me. I wonder for a moment whether her telepathy is as curtailed as her earth-magic has to be – maybe that’s why she’s been so distrustful.

And then there’s the moment that I see, I feel her decide to trust.

Her eyes slide shut, then open again after a moment.

I can do little to you anyway, she admits quietly, sounding more downcast than I’ve ever heard her. She half-lifts her front right paw, then replaces it onto the stone. They filed my claws to stumps.

I have to swallow back the sudden wave of fury that threatens to crack my calm facade. They did what?

Kalanthia gazes at me with more life in her eyes than I’ve seen so far. A flicker of amusement drifts down the Bond and I realise I must not have controlled my reaction as well as I’d thought.

Ironically, though, my lacking control seems to have done more to reassure her than any of my words so far. Realising that, I let a little more of my honest white hot fury at the state I found Kalanthia in leak into the Bond. I didn’t want her to think I was angry at her, but since she clearly realises that it’s directed at whoever did this to her, I can share it.

Well, let’s do something about that, I tell her firmly, placing my hand on the closest of her paws.

Closing my eyes, I sink my mind into her body, again demonstrating my trust in her by being completely vulnerable before her – claws or not, being hit by one of her paws would hurt.

Sure enough, I find that her claws are a stump of what they should be – filed almost all the way back to the bone. I didn’t register them before because it doesn’t count as an injury. I do note that whoever had her decided to file them rather than remove them entirely. A telling choice, especially when taken in context of the muzzle.

Regrowing her claws takes only a few minutes and it isn’t long before I open my eyes having done the same to all four of her paws.

Done, I tell her. She lifts one paw, the look in her eyes fearful rather than excited.

For a moment, she’s frozen with her paw held up before her eyes. I sense that she’s extremely fragile right now – that hope has pulled her out of a shell within which she was sheltering. And if it is dashed now….

And then she flexes her paw and the long, sharp white claws scythe out.

She looks at them for a moment and then almost seems to melt as tension leaves her muscles. Her ears relax on her head; her fur flattens.

The Bond is even more telling. Wonder, amazement, relief, hope all flood through it – almost uncomfortably intense. It appears this little thing is what has finally convinced her that she’s safe.

I tried to sharpen them as much as I could, but you’ll probably want to make sure they’re up to your standards, I say slightly-forcibly cheerfully, trying to raise the mood before I get dragged into the maelstrom of her emotions.

She looks down at me, warm golden orbs sparkling once more. Before I can react, she drags me in close with a paw. I tense briefly from surprise but then lean into her shoulder as she presses me close.

Thank you, Markus Wolfe. Thank you.

I smile and scratch at what I can reach of her chin.

You’re welcome. I wish I’d done it sooner, I admit. If I’d known that she was going through this I would have found a way to get her back long before now.

I tried to reach out to you, but I could not, she mourns.

I let her have a moment of quiet, sensing she needs it. Then, pulling back a little, I tap the muzzle. Shall we be rid of this?

Kalanthia brushes a paw over her face, her golden gaze becoming more guarded again.

Yes. I have worn it far too long, she emphasises.

I give her a long look.

Perhaps you would be willing to share exactly how it came to be that you’re wearing it at all, I request carefully. Because there are so many implications of her state and they’re all concerning.

She flinches a little, and I sense that she wishes to deny me. Then she breathes out heavily and her head lowers a touch.

Later, she promises.

I nod. Later is fine as long as it isn’t too far in the future – I sensed through Bond Teleport just how far she was when I summoned her, so we most likely have time. But it would be foolish of me to dismiss the possibility that they will chase her – and find us.

I shift into a higher state of alertness when Kalanthia tenses, her lips lifting; her claws peeking out from her fur.

Fortunately, when I look around her shoulder, I see it’s just Master Mirena accompanied by Alyna and Hunter – I relax again.

I asked them to come, I tell Kalanthia. They may need to inspect the muzzle – will you be alright with that?

She’s clearly not pleased.

The human is no longer part of the pack, she remarks.

I needed space for extra Bonds, I explain. She looks at me curiously. I’ll tell you more about it later. Is it a problem?

She considers the question for an unusually long moment, and I notice her paws flexing.

It is well if you stay near me, she replies tensely. Clearly her wish to be rid of it surpasses her reluctance to let those not in the pack near her right now.

Then I will. I then gaze at her seriously. Will you be fine if I speak? I noticed you didn’t like it before, but I can’t communicate with Lizil or Alyna otherwise.

I will be fine, she tells me firmly.

I decide to take her at her word, stepping out past Kalanthia’s shoulder and smiling tensely at the three.

“Thanks for coming,” I tell them, keeping half an eye on Kalanthia. She tenses slightly, but doesn’t flinch this time. “We need to get this thing off.” I gesture towards the muzzle and then update them on what my Inspect told me.

Lizil nods at my explanation, her expression serious, though I can detect a hint of excitement in her eyes.

“May I inspect it myself?” she asks me politely after I’ve finished. “I will be able to gain a better understanding of the limitations of the enchantments with my own Skills.”

“Kalanthia has agreed, but please keep your examinations as short as possible,” I reply.

While Lizil inspects the muzzle, I try to deal with the ropes on Kalanthia’s back paws, though hit a wall with those too. Perhaps they’re linked to the muzzle in some way. Giving up after a few minutes, I check how things are going on the Tier four front.

We are succeeding in keeping him distracted with stories of Lathani’s cubhood, Bastet replies to my query, sounding a little tense. But only because the sun is not yet up. When it rises, stories won’t be enough to hold him.

I check the watch Keshaka gave me back on the ship and clench my teeth. Dawn is less than half a mark away now. The ritual might not have taken that much time, relatively speaking, but it’s taken a while to heal Kalanthia and calm her down.

I consider letting the nunda know that Kalanthia is back, but then dismiss the idea as a last-resort – if he knows she’s here, he’ll want to see her. If he sees her with the muzzle on, he’ll get the wrong idea. Sure, she can tell him that we’re not the ones to put the muzzle on her, but he has no guarantee we’re not forcing her to lie. The fact is that I can't be sure how he’ll react, so better not to risk it.

Just keep doing what you can. We’re working hard on our side to get the muzzle off, I tell Bastet.

I check on the muzzle progress. Lizil is still busy and Hunter and Alyna have come closer too. Kalanthia is tense, clearly unhappy, but stable for now. Since I have a moment, I reach out to others.

Receiving quick status updates from the lords and Loran, I’m relieved to hear that things are going back to normal – we’ve been operating at crisis-level for too long and it’s strained our resources and people to the breaking point. We have as many soldiers as are in good condition manning the wall just in case things go south with Tilathis, but if things go as planned, we won’t need them.

Kalanthia is increasingly tense so I step closer to her and begin stroking her shoulder, pressing calm into the Bond between us. The three enchanters are having a serious discussion, but, just like when Tiria and Keshaka get going, I can only understand one word in three.

It’s alright, I’m sure they’re almost done, I reassure Kalanthia soothingly.

I’m fine, she tells me in a slightly prickly tone. Have you verified that you are well? I can sense that you used a great deal of mana – and that it was not all your own. Are your Energy channels undamaged?

I want to say I’m fine, but hesitate, remembering what I saw at the end of the ritual.

Will you be alright if I check?

I’m fine.

Message received. It doesn’t surprise me that the proud nunda doesn’t like what she probably sees as coddling.

For the sake of not irritating her further, I just nod and then close my eyes, slipping easily into my Core space.

There, I feel a sinking sensation, for all that I don’t have a physical presence. I was right that the ritual has had some serious effects – and it’s worse than I remember.

As I’d more than half-expected, the channels that bring in Energy from the outside via my right hand have completely disintegrated. Worse, the mandala-like matrix in that area is clearly damaged – the normal golden colour of the filaments has dimmed to bronze shot through with black. It’s not nearly as bad as the damage the Pure Energy did to me, but it’s going to take some time to fix.

Still, at least I know where to start with that. Not so much the main other issue I see before me.

Even though I had been expecting it, I’m still shocked by the disturbing sight of a completely blackened Skill. It stands out from the luminous blackness of my Core space like a reverse image of what it should be. I might have thought that the Skill had disintegrated, but for the fact that I can see that the channels still exist – they’ve just changed colour drastically.

However, the issue is clearly deeper than just a colour change. When I try to press a little mana through the channels, it refuses to cross the border where gold suddenly transforms into black. It feels almost like the Skill is no longer part of me, of my Class – yet it continues to exist.

Thoughtfully, I pull out of my Core space and check my notifications since I have the nagging feeling that something’s waiting for me there.

As it turns out, it’s not a notification that Bond Teleport is undergoing an evolution as I was wondering might be the case. However, the contents bring a smile to my face.

Nicholas is going to love this.
