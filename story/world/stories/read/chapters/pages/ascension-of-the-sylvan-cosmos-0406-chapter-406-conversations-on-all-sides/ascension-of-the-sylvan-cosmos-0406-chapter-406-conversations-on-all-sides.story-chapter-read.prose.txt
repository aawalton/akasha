The news about <Sprouts Of Chaos> spread far and wide like a forest fire. It even crossed the ocean and reached the ears of top-level people on the continent. In the capital of the Celestial Lotus Empire, a gargantuan city that surrounded a mountain-sized white and pink lotus, a Sylvan with uncommon brown eyes and hair was looking at a document. Usually, Sylvans had colorful hair and eye colors, so having comparatively normal colors and close to human standards was considered unusual.

Still, that didn’t reduce her beauty. The average beauty of Sylvans was, after all, relatively high. The reason was that facial features would be developed toward a female’s perfected standard due to being a race of only female-like bodies.

This woman was Galia's most influential superior. She had sleek and naturally wavy brown hair, her strength having reached the <Fourth Energy Cycle>.

“Ho~.” After a soft exclamation of interest and placing down the scroll, the woman laughed faintly. “I sent little Galia thinking that she would have an easy time over there, but I greatly miscalculated that young World Tree’s potential.”

She tapped the table thoughtfully, her eyes glancing toward the colossal Lotus in the middle of the city. “What do you think, Lady Lotus? Should we send one <Fourth Energy Cycle> to make sure no accidents occur?”

A voice as vast as the sky and profound as the ocean reached her, echoing in the room with incredible strength. “No. There is no need for that.”

The woman blinked twice, confused. “Why not? She has the potential to grow tremendously.”

“To grow beyond myself.” Answered the Celestial Lotus without hesitation. “I’ve not yet become old enough to eagerly feed a World Tree that might surpass me in the future.”

The powerful Sylvan laughed. “But you are old enough to let her grow by herself.”

“... Indeed. Azure, Verdant, and I have already accepted that our strengths are too evenly matched. Whoever takes the first step will be the one to perish. Moreover, the Fifth Energy Cycle feels so far away.”

The Sylvan sighed. “Indeed. The Fifth Energy Cycle is the benchmark that separates all creatures. Even World Trees can be stuck and never be able to evolve.”

Her eyes analyzed the Celestial Lotus, and a mostly hidden information interface appeared.

Even if it ultimately falls into the Nexus’ hands, it won’t affect us much. The level and tier difference between continents is absolute. It’s not something that can be pushed unless several generations pass.”

Naeris leaned back on her chair and muttered. “Well, I guess you are right. Still, Lady Lotus, don’t you think that the Nexus’ insistence is a bit strange? The Wild Continent is located south, somewhat separated from most of the landmass here up North. Still, that separation is both a disadvantage and an advantage. As difficult as it is for them to reach us, reaching them is equally complicated. If the Nexus dominates the Wild Continent, taking back control would need too many resources.”

She added. “We now have a very promising seedling that might take control over it. This would make us the third Global Power dominating more than one continent.”

“You are too hasty.” The Celestial Lotus spoke calmly. “Tianlian Liu Shu’s growth is indeed fast. However, it will soon slow down. She is just a C Rank World Tree. Even if her potential is great, becoming powerful enough to dominate a Continent is not something she can do. Wait a century to see how things develop—”

“A century?” Naeris shook her head. “I’ll wait two decades. Lady Lotus, let me continue paying attention. I feel that there might be a big surprise soon.”

“If something really happens in twenty years, I’ll listen to your words.”

Naeris chuckled. “Lady Lotus, aren’t you worried that the cities and World Trees in the <Wild Continent> will be overrun like the Covenant’s?”

Celestial Lotus commented. “They are important. However, they are not essential. The thing that would sting us if we lost were the World Trees that had grown there. From what I know, there is a B- Rank World Tree, right?”

Naeris nodded. “Indeed…” Then, she smiled. “Well, that’s enough for now. Sorry for disturbing your meditation, Lady Lotus.”

“Hm.” Celestial Lotus' presence retreated after the simple answer, leaving Naeris with her own thoughts.

Evangeline laughed. “I am not needed. Of course. That’s why I was almost threatened to come here and, well, do this.” She snapped her fingers.

A woman started convulsing without any indications, and her limbs began twisting at strange angles. Foam and blood pooled in her throat and mouth, spilling out with gargling noises.

Twenty seconds later, the woman lay dead on the ground. Vaelin’s face changed several times before he shouted at her. “Evangeline! Explain yourself!”
