“They’re all gathered there.” Ajax said as he looked on.”Should we skip the formality of sending in the  regular soldiers first?”

A sloth beastkin that had invested her stats specifically for her odd ritual trapping fighting style and always went delving and to war together with a team yet her rituals were powerful enough to raise her to the level of a champion.
