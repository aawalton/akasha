After Silvia left, Kae made her way back to the capital of Restoria. She reached the church and leisurely walked up the massive steps, leading into the chapel. When she stepped inside, the fox took a much-needed look around.

"I never noticed how much detail was put into this place."

Kae murmured as she looked about the multicolored murals that painted the walls of the church. One that stuck out most to her was Rexia playing that harp of hers.

"Hm, the Harp of Harmony..."

The Foxian recalled the lore fondly. The instrument that always felt gentle and soothing on the ears when she strummed it. Rexia, the Goddess of Harmony, had the power to strengthen her people's souls through her blessings.

So, when a player in the world chose one of the main classes...

Swordsmen

Thief

Archer

Merchant

Mage

Acolyte

It was Rexia who blessed them, pushing them forward to get stronger...

"I wonder if Rexia will bless me when I choose a class?"

The fox pondered. That would go against their death game. But it is, by the rules of Keostar Online, part of the game. It would be like scoring a goal for the other team if the one trying to kill her is blessed by her, too.

"What is your true aim, putting me in Keostar, Rexia?"

"Ah, Foxian child. What might you be doing here?"

A calming voice of a man, aged with wisdom and love, caught Kae's ears. She turned to see a burly man with curly brown hair and azure eyes approach her. He was very tall, with a long black robe and a holy book in his left hand. He was one of the True Heroes of the world, Father Brinkhorn.

"Oh, ah, here."

Kae fumbled around in her pocket before handing him his brother's, Father Gerado's, priest badge. This was like handing an NPC any old quest item to Kae. But, to her surprise, Father Brinkhorn sighed deeply.

"So, you've seen the truth of what happened to my dear brother?"

Mournfully, he asked. But before Kae could speak up, he continued.

"Thank you for bringing me his priest badge. I'm sorry you had to go through the effort and... see the atrocities he committed."

Shamefully, Father Brinkhorn turned away. But Kae still caught a glimpse of a tear falling down his cheek. The fox couldn't believe her eyes, seeing such a human reaction coming from him. The Father turned back her way.

"Ah, sorry for your... brother, Father Brinkhorn."

She murmured as she tried her best to avoid his gaze.

"It's okay, young child. Thank you for your sympathy."

Father Brinkhorn responded, surprisingly.

"Huh?"

Kae tilted her head back at him, but he continued on.

"If you ever wish to become a member of this church, an acolyte of my congregation, take this badge."

Kae obtained [Holy Heroes Amulet] and [Priest Badge].

With that, Father Brinkhorn walked away, towards the podium of the chapel. She watched his tall figure for a while longer before turning away.

"Hm, I thought only [Sentient] Fallen Angels could talk back?"

She pondered, her tail flicking back and forth. Something was happening that extended beyond Rexia and her blessings to the Fallen Angels...

When Kae exited the church, the streets of Restoria were bustling as usual. Well, it was way more crowded than an average day...

"Well, maybe it's time to take a short break..."

The Foxian had been doing nothing but farming and fighting since she fell into Keostar Online. Feeling a small sense of accomplishment, she decided to make something special and started gathering ingredients at the local shops.

"[Apple], [Sugar], [Dough]."

She mumbled, listing what she needed. Fighting through the busy crowd of players, Kae found a semblance of peace when she exited the city. In the calm field, there lay a few apple trees about.

The fox walked over and started picking a few of them. The gentle breeze of the field washed over her cheek. The wind was calm and peaceful. As she gathered up more apples, an idea came to mind.

[Mini Fox]

The little fox popped out in a small puff of smoke. Kae kneeled down and handed him her [Brush], which she brought from the merchant the other day.

"Hey, little guy. Can you brush my tail for me?"

The little fox took her [Brush] and jumped on her back. Without question, the mini fox did as she asked, happily brushing about her tail, a convenient way to avoid doing it herself.

"Huh, so that did work."

She whispered as the corner of her lips raised in a soft smile. The more Kae realized she was a part of the world, the better she could tweak her skills to her advantage. Leisurely, the fox picked more apples as her mini tail groomed her fluffy tail.

It was relaxing, one of the few words Kae would have ever associated with Keostar Online.

"Hm, I'll need to buy [Sugar] and [Dough]. I could make [Dough], but it's cheaper to buy from the bakery."

With her apples stored in her inventory, Kae made her way to the Golden Bread, a famous bakery in Restoria. According to the lore, people from all over Keostar would come to try the pastries. The two bakers were world-famous.

"Come on, you."

She said, lifting the mini fox onto her shoulders. The adorable little pet nuzzled its fur on her cheek.

"Mmmm!"

"Oh, you're being very affectionate today."

"I'll have to name you soon, won't I?~"

She mused.

Naming things wasn't Kae's strong suit. She often relied on the random names the game generated for the characters she made. If Rexia hadn't given her the name, Kae Loredom, naming herself would have been the hardest challenge yet.

Thank goodness Rexia didn't find out about her true weakness!

"Don't worry, I'll give you the perfect name, little guy!"

The mini tail bounced happily on her shoulder, acknowledging her promise. Again, Kae noticed that Restoria was busier than usual, with players crowding in. She and her little fox had to weave their way into the bakery.

"Welcome!"
