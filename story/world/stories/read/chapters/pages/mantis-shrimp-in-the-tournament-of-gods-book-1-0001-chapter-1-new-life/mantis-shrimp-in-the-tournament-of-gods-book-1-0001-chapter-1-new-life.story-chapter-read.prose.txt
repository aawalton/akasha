Truth be told, joining some tournament created by sadistic gods was not on my checklist that day. Or any day, ever.

It began like any other, with me waking up like I always did - pissed off. Looking around, I took in all the colors of the ocean, frowning at its majesty. Don’t get me wrong, it was a sight worth waking up to, it just lacked one simple thing; blood. Specifically, the blood of my enemies. Who was my enemy? Well, in the depths of the ocean, everyone was. None of the cretin here could oppose me, of course - a Peacock Mantis Shrimp had no true enemies. Only victims.

I left the crook in between the rocks that I slept in, using my whole body to wiggle out, and went out to find myself some breakfast. Stray beams of sunlight penetrated deep into the ocean, alighting my path. Not that I needed it - my vision was good enough to not need light- but I still liked the colors it brought to life.

A few minutes of walking on the ocean floor brought me to a perfect place. This was it - I found myself a nice and cozy hunting burrow, hid myself, and prepared for the hunt.

Now came the waiting part. One would think that I’d get bored by sitting in hiding spots for hours on end, but honestly? I kinda liked it. My brain wasn’t even large enough to get bored, and I liked waiting for the unlucky creatures that would just happen to fly a bit too close to the sun. The sand covered my body almost completely - My gorgeous chitin made me a bit too conspicuous, and so I had to hide like a burglar. But I’ve long gotten used to the injustices of being beautiful.

Something moved. I darted out with a punch!

…

It was a damn piece of plastic. Could not eat that, sadly.

I went to burrow myself back, but something moved once again. I shifted my eyesight and barely swam out of the way on time. A king crab, huge and monstrous, just attacked the place I was in moments ago.

How dare he? Attack me? This “king” clearly had no idea who he was messing with! I swam close, my smashers ready to obliterate him. His other claw suddenly swooped up, and grabbed my whole body. Hey! Not cool!

My chitin held on under the press, cracking in several spots but not giving in completely. I knew it would hold - it always did. Pain shot throughout my body, but I held on, waiting for an opportunity that I knew would come. This wasn’t even the first creature that was ten times my size that thought I’d be an easy meal, and this would end just like the others did.

The dumb crab then brought me to its mouth, ready to eat me. He truly had no idea who he was attacking. I wiggled myself in the claw, doing my best to turn toward the mouth.

A small nudge. Another. I continued moving, the moment almost slowed-down in my mind. I was just at his mouth now, the maw opening right in front of me.

Good enough!

I punched. And the crab shook. His mouth, transformed, broke into small parts and swam in all directions. The flesh around it was burned from just the attack itself. That showed him!  He let go a little, and this meant I could cook him properly now. I swam even closer, and punched again. A hole replaced his fleshy, non-chitinous parts, and he hovered in the ocean, dead. Lovely. I adored the fact that my lunch sometimes served itself and walked right to me.

I came closer, fully intending to eat my fill. And then a voice talked in my head.

This one will do, &!/@?(#

What the hell was that? I looked around for the source of the weird sound. Who dared disrupt my meal? Was it another meal that chose to walk up to me? Oh, that would be your last mistake, buddy!

And then, out of nowhere, my body started disappearing into motes of light.

I didn’t get scared, okay? And I didn’t run around because I failed to understand the phenomenon that was happening to me. Definitely not!

The lights were barely frightening. A nuisance, really!

No, I started running around because I was searching for an opponent to fight! Who dared attack my magnificence? I was a mantis shrimp, and I would tolerate this affront no longer!

And what was this, anyway? The lights did not hurt, but it definitely wasn’t natural.
