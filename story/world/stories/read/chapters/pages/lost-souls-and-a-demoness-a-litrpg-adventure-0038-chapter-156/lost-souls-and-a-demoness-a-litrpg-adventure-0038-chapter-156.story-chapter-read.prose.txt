Upon entering the Labyrinth, Jade and her companions were met with a scene of choking smoke and smoldering fire. Most of the nearby L-ReF buildings bore signs of heavy damage, and more than a couple had been reduced to rubble. It was a shocking sight after having passed through a perfectly intact version of the outpost mere hours earlier. The vehicle lurched as Luis ran over a moderately sized piece of debris, making them all lurch in their harnesses. He slowed their advance further, laying off the gas long enough for them to get a better look around. It was only then that Jade saw the bodies.

Soldiers lay dead, their corpses scattered along both sides of the dirt road. Many of their uniforms had been shredded, while others bore large burn marks on their chest or face. They weren't alone, however. Jade spotted at least four demon corpses scattered amidst the humans, mostly the regular demon bruzaks. One part of her mind, the portion detached from the grizzly spectacle, noted that those particular demons seemed to make up a large portion of the invader's forces.

"Luis, stop!" Naomi urged, reaching forward to tug on his sleeve. "Some of them are still alive! We have to help!"

Indeed, a good portion of the bodies they could see were stirring, some only weakly. Many were being attended to by medics, but the outpost hadn't been prepared for an attack coming from inside its defenses, and it looked like resources were stretched thin. They had the ability to help, but…

"No." Jade gritted her teeth, forcing the painful words out. "We have to get to the node before they do!"

"We can't leave these people to die, Jade!"

"Would you rather let everyone die after we let the demons get everything they need to start their invasion?" Jade snapped.

"Enough." Fenrin said firmly, laying a hand on each of their shoulders. "The node isn't far, Naomi. If the demons really are going there, we have to stop them. But, as soon as we do, we're coming back to help."He looked expectantly at Jade, and she nodded.

"Of course. I want to help them, too, Naomi."

"I… I know. I just hate feeling so powerless."

Luis drove them out of the camp as quickly as he could, only slowing to navigate a small handful of craters that had been blasted into the road. As they drew further from the entrance portal the state of the camp buildings improved, showing few to no signs of damage. Officers and soldiers marshaled at the perimeter of the compound, an array of heavy, mounted weapons now fully manned. One of them tried to flag them down, but Luis ignored the woman, swerving to avoid her and continuing towards the exit. It was clear that the attack had been executed hastily, and judging by the number of demon bodies lying around, it had been met with a large amount of resistance.

"There can't be that many of them left." Jade said, more to herself than to anyone else. "A lot of them didn't make it back out."

"Don't get complacent." Fenrin said, thumbing through his quiver and sorting through his remaining arrows. "If you're right about how important this is to their plans, they'll be throwing everything they can at it."

"Good point." Jade acknowledged. The last thing they needed was to tempt fate.

The rough, wild terrain of the steep, wooded hills beyond the L-ReF outpost were rough on the vehicle's occupants, and the constant bouncing and rattling forestalled further conversation. Jade constantly swept her magical perception throughout their surroundings, trying to get a sense of if they were closing in on their targets and how many of them there were. Naomi kept her interface's map open, supplying directions to Luis as he drove them towards the node. Fortunately, the area wasn't so thickly filled with trees so as to be impassable, but there were enough of them to make the experience harrowing. Privately, Jade was glad Luis was behind the wheel. Magically reinforced agility she may have, but she was under no illusions that she would've been as successful maneuvering the large vehicle over terrain this challenging.

One minute passed, and then two. They were drawing closer to the node, and the tension between them grew by the moment. Worse, the temporary reprieve from frantic battle was giving Jade's mind time to consider everything that had just transpired. The loss of Alan's cure was the wound that stung the most, but nearly losing Naomi and the sight of the mangled corpses leftover from the demon incursion were almost as affecting. Jade had naively thought herself inured to her new, violence-filled life, but now realized her will was far from unassailable. Before she had time to dwell on that unpleasant train of thought, her sharp eyes finally picked up a flash of movement from up ahead.

"There!" She shouted, leaning forward and pointing. "Between those two--"

One of the shadowy figures flickered out of sight. It reemerged a moment later, flashing towards them with blinding speed. Luis reacted immediately, throwing the vehicle into a sharp turn that sent it rocking alarming to one side, slamming Jade into the hard frame of the door. She got the sense of something large and fast rushing past them, heard a loud screech, and then the car slammed to a stop. The six of them poured out of the vehicle with Jade in the lead, weapons drawn. Four figures stood before them, one closer than the others. Two of them, the nearest of them and one other, appeared to be the same kind of creature, some sort of living, humanoid shadow. They bore some resemblance to the demonic woman that had nearly killed Naomi only a short time ago, but they didn't have the same feel. Her form had been ghost-like, but still resembled that of a person. Not so with these creatures. They had no features of any kind, appearing more like dark silhouettes made of darkness itself. Jade took advantage of their return to the Labyrinth proper to identify them.

Name: Xxrcha

Level: 38*

Race: Shadow Demon

Class: None

Origin: [Restricted]

*Warning: Labyrinth systems are unable to properly interface with this creature.

The "38" glitched in the interface, flickering in and out of focus. While the Labyrinth might not be able to get a perfect read on demons, the ballpark estimate was better than nothing. A quick scan of the other three creatures showed that they were all around the same power level. The other two individuals were much more visually distinct. One was a large, hulking monster of a demon, with a bulky frame and skin comprised of jagged shards of stone. The system aptly labeled it as an earth demon, and the long, metal container it was carrying drew Jade's attention. She was too distant to read any of the many labels covering its dark surfaces, but the bright colors of warning labels and multiple heavy locks left her with few doubts as to its contents. Before she could share her suspicions the last of the four demons turned to face her, giving Jade a good look at his features for the first time. She stopped in place, a jolt of surprise coursing through her.

"Hello, Jade." Sam said, giving their group an easy smile. He held up a clawed hand, gesturing for the other demons to wait. "I didn't expect to find you here."

"You!" Jade growled, glaring at the incubus. She sensed Naomi and Luis's shock and anger, their reactions mirroring her own. "How long were you with us?!"

"Long enough to get homesick." Sam, or Sa'merkul as the Labyrinth named him, grimaced with distaste. "And believe me, that's quite remarkable."

Jade wasn't buying this affable act for a second. The incubus's claws were stained red, and the monsters beside him radiated killing intent. But still, neither she nor her friends moved to attack. These demons were powerful, and they hadn't fully recovered from their battle with the wraith demon. Jade's magical senses didn't detect any other approaching enemies, and surely L-ReF would be sending backup after them as soon as possible. Maybe they could stall them long enough for help to arrive.

"We have you outnumbered." Luis said, stepping to the front of their group and pointing his silver blade towards them. "Give up the weapon you stole, and we'll let you go."

Like hell they would! Jade wasn't about to let any of the demons walk away after all the death and misery they'd sown. She doubted Luis was serious, but just the thought of letting these monsters get away made her clench her fists.

"I'm afraid we can't do that." The demon said, raising his hands in a placating gesture. "But I'm sure we can come to an agreement."

One of the shadow monsters leaned towards the incubus, and Jade noticed his gaze shifting to Luis's angelic blade. Sa'merkul's smile faltered, although he regained his composure quickly.

"How about we lay down our weapons and have a… civilized discussion?"

Maybe… maybe that wasn't such a bad idea. Some of the tension bled from Jade's body and she relaxed, loosening her grip on her weapons. She'd already nearly lost Naomi today, did she really want to risk--

A wave of warmth washed over Jade, accompanied by a short, lively tune. Her focus snapped back on the demons just in time to see the large earth demon charging straight for her. What?! How?!

"Jade! Move!"

Jade dodged to the side, recovering just in time to evade a charge that likely would have crushed her. She shifted into her succubus form, landing beside Naomi and levelling a murderous glare at the incubus. He'd charmed her. Charmed her! For some reason, getting hit by her own signature spell struck a nerve.

"Thanks." She grumbled.

"Cleanses are my job." Naomi chirped, shifting gears and beginning her fleetfoot melody. "Time for you to do yours!"

Jade rolled her eyes. Was this really the time to be glib?

Did that potion do something to her?

That was a concern for another time. In her brief moment of distraction, the two shadow demons had joined the fray. One faced off against Luis, while Fenrin held the other at bay with lightning infused arrows. Tara was casting rapid fire spells at the earth demon, which still hadn't recovered from its failed charge. With Naomi and Siora supporting the team, that just left the incubus.

Good.

Jade launched herself into the air, arcing towards Sa'merkul with impressive speed. Naomi's agility buff had grown stronger at C-rank, and was a true force multiplier when it came to Jade. She fell upon the incubus like an angel descending from the heavens, surrounded by a halo of radiant light. He opened his mouth to speak again, but Jade didn't give him the chance. A twitch of her wrist was all it took to send a hail blade straight for him. The trio of incandescent daggers slammed into his throat and chest, turning whatever he'd been about to say into a choking gurgle. Phantom blinked back to her hand as she landed beside him and she struck again with blinding speed, hammering him with a barrage of attacks. Jade slashed his chest and then his legs, following that up with a double blink that brought her behind his back. A strike to his exposed wings made him shriek in pain, and his eyes flashed in anger. The incubus fought back with reckless abandon, clawing wildly at any part of her she could reach.

For all the ferocity in his attacks, it was obvious that the incubus wasn't an especially well-trained warrior. Jade ducked and dodged, dancing in and out of range of his attacks while ruthlessly taking him down slice by slice. The radiant essence suffusing her aura proved just as effective against the incubus as it had against the demon assassin, and he was soon staggering away, taking to the sky and trying to flee. Jade was about to follow when her path was blocked by one of the shadow demons. The monster literally emerged from her own shadow, raking her chest with a pair of dark, shadowy claws that were far more solid than their ethereal form suggested. The attack shattered what remained of her armor's wards, penetrating far enough to score bloody grooves into her skin.

"Gyaa!"

The wound wasn’t severe, but the attack caught her by surprise. The flash of pain sharpened her focus, and she snarled, moving to counter. Before she could, the demon screeched in pain, startling her again. The monster shied away, a sizzling hiss emanating from its umbral form. Jade's confusion only lasted until she noticed the way her aura seared at the creature's dark essence, burning it away like it was standing in front of a blow torch.

That's more like it.

Jade lunged after the fleeing monster with a savage grin, tearing it apart with a series of radiant slashes. The monster faded with an echoing wail, dissolving into a pile of grey soot at her feet.

"You're not the only ones with surprises."

The battle didn't last much longer. Both shadow monsters were easily felled by Jade's luminous blades, and Fenrin expertly nailed the fleeing incubus in the back with an overcharged electric arrow. Later, he confided to Jade that he'd actually been trying to capture the demon for interrogation, but it turned out he'd overestimated the creature's resilience. They weren't sure if it was the eruption of lighting through his body or the fifty foot drop onto a pile of boulders, but by the time they reached his charred and broken body, Sa'merkul was very clearly dead.

The only one that gave the group any real trouble was the earth demon. It was strong, faster than it looked, and possessed an incredible fortitude that had it shrugging off almost any attack. Jade was more than a little alarmed when the monster gave up on throwing boulders at them in lieu of smashing them with the giant metal case it was carrying. While she was pretty sure that nuclear weapons weren't liable to detonate when suddenly hit by a large force, it was a belief she had no desire to put to the test. It didn't help that the only attack able to consistently deal large amounts of damage to the demon were Tara's fireballs. Even Jade's radiant aura blades failed to penetrate the creature's stony skin far enough to deal decisive damage. They finally brought it down when Luis and Siora made use of some of their control spells, pulling the demon into one of Jade's rune traps. The magical chains held the monster still long enough for Tara to drop a final, concentrated blast of fire right on top of it, reducing the demon to loose stone and cinders.

"We… did it?" Naomi asked tentatively, letting silence fall over the sparse woodland as her spell faded.

Jade wasn't convinced. She remained tense, expecting demon reinforcements to arrive at any moment. When they didn't, Luis simply shrugged.

"Let's get this thing back through the portal while we can."

Jade quickly looted the bodies while Fenrin and Luis inspected the stolen case. Miraculously, it had survived Tara's repeated fireballs and its brief experience as a particularly unwieldy club. She couldn't help but be impressed with Tara's control over her magic, although Jade still had no intention of being anywhere near the tek'kalla's blasts when they went off. They were just securing the case for transport when, finally, L-ReF reinforcements arrived in force. More than a hundred soldiers and Wayfarers swarmed the scene, all prepared for a fierce fight. This time, Jade remembered to shift into her human form before being spotted by any of them, and no one appeared to connect her with one of the demon marauders who'd attacked the the headquarters.

She just hoped that would last.

They returned to Earth as a group, stopping in the ruins of the camp for Naomi and Siora to provide healing to the worst of the wounded, earning them a lot of gratitude from the rank and file. Not for the first time, Jade wished she had the ability to heal others. Her own powers centered around death and deception, and they were getting frighteningly effective. Their group had just torn through a quartet of powerful demons, monsters that had fought their way through multiple squads of trained soldiers. They had been wounded and run down by the time Jade's group encountered them, but still…

Jade's gaze drifted back to the line of bodies next to the road, now covered by white sheets.

So much death…

Now that the action was over, the weight of it all was returning.

Finally, they were escorted back to the L-ReF compound by a group of officers who were very eager to hear their reports, and who were even more so to retrieve the large weapon case from Luis. Despite successfully putting a stop to the demon plot to secure what was likely one of their most critical ritual components, Jade's mood remained heavy. After losing so much it was hard to feel triumphant, and she couldn't help but wonder what they were missing.

"Is anyone else waiting for the other shoe to drop?" Jade muttered quietly, glancing at her friends.

No one replied, but she could sense similar feelings coming from each of them. The demons had put too much time and effort into their plans for the failure of a single raid to stymie them. One way or another, this wasn't over.

Before she could worry about that, though, Jade had more pressing concerns. As the adrenaline of battle faded she was forced to confront the events of the day in full. Repressed pain resurfaced, squeezing her heart like a vice. Her eyes stung with unshed tears as she contemplated how she was going to face what came next.

She owed her brother a visit. And an apology.
