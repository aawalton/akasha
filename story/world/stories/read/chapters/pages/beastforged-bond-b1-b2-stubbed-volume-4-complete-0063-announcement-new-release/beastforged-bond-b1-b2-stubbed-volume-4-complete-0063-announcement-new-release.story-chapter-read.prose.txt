✨ Savant Barbarian [LitRPG Adventure] ✨

by HideousGrain

Among his people, strength is everything. Weakness means death.

When Hakon finally completes the Rite, he expects at least a modest Ancient Power. Instead, he awakens something his tribe scorns: A Power of the Mind, of Enhanced Comprehension

No brute force. No herculean strength. Just the ability to understand.

But while others dismiss him, Hakon sees what they cannot. Patterns. Weaknesses. Possibilities.

As tribes stir, ancient grudges reignite, and whispers spread, prophesying the rise of a Warlord. A warrior born from the blood of his enemies. A man whose very existence heralds change. And Hakon decides to become that very man.

To unite the tribes. To reclaim lost lands. To rise where everyone expects him to fall.

But first, he has to survive long enough to understand his Power…and to teach those who refuse to accept the strength of the Mind.
