Twenty-nine years. He'd asked to be left alone, to be left to his thoughts for a half century. There were few things that would prompt an early disturbance, especially that were not critical and treated as urgent.

"Yes?"

"The twins your Eminence."

"They are well?"

If they had been so foolish as to ruin their potential he would be furious. Their path forward had been clearly defined from before their birth. The only accommodation that had been made was for there being two, of opposite gender. "Yes your Eminence. Nine and a half years post-Awakening, they qualify."
