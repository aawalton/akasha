Recently, I reread this story from the beginning. It's an interesting story with great potential, but I wrote it very naively. I wondered if it could be salvaged, but I've realized that I now want to write something with a heavier emphasis on comedy. Regrettably, I want to share that I will no longer continue this story. Maybe one day I might pick it back up again, as I really liked the concept when I was writing it.
