Seriously, I saw it all the time back in the day, and I see some of it echoed here and now. Folks want to think that if they work hard and do the right things, they'll prosper. Folks want to think that when random bullshit falls out of the sky, the things even secular people refer to as 'acts of god', that there is in fact a God deciding when and how and who those acts affect. They want there to be some kind of someone in control, even if it's some diabolic, cartoonishly evil conspiracy, because that way at least there's someone to blame for shit. That way someone is in control.

I get it, I really do. Back in the day I think that made my depression and anxiety worse. I watched as some people did all the right things and wound up catching stray bullets. I watched as others did the same and worked themselves to death without anything to show for it. I watched people do the 'smart' thing and turn to dealing, or turning tricks, or even start up an OF page and rake in cash. Some even succeeded.

Most of them just crashed and burned. The higher they soared before Murphy caught up to them, the harder they hit when they crashed. Okay, the more I felt it, because I realized that there seemed to be no real difference between the tiny minority who made it out and the vast majority who didn't. That hits hard as a kid on her own, with nobody to say 'I'll be there for you'. Fuck, I'm not sure how much better it would have been if I'd have had a family around supporting me.

Okay, yeah, it might have been better. Because that's what I've got now. A family who supports me, who I do my best to protect. Even if sometimes I get here a little late, I can still fix a lot of shit even if it has gone wrong. Given what I know now, I might even be able to make it right if somebody like Ria died. That thought that we'll be here for one another, that together we can deal with things that no single one of us, even the biggest and strongest one, can deal with on their own. Okay, given how fear resistant I seem to be, I guess that means it scares me a lot. At least to me, family is life. It's why I'm here. It's a reason for existence. If some random event so big that it destroys my entire family in a way I can't bring them back hits, that can't take away the past. It can't take away the fact that we were here. It can't take away the love given and received.

Yeah, maybe there are things big enough to kill me. Even now. Maybe there are things that can erase even my memory of what has gone before. But that doesn't change the fact that they still existed. That for some brief shining moment or two, we were here, together, in this place. That, for me at least, makes things worth pursuing, even if with all my power, I can't be sure pure random chance won't fuck it all up.

Yeah, I don't know why, but somewhere along the line I realized that the universe itself is uncaring, and so big that there's no way to control all of it. I realized that no matter how you skew the odds, you can still get unlucky. Or lucky, for that matter. So all you can do is the best you can, and enjoy what you have.
