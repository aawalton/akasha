Two things were obvious as solutions. One was drawing from the great excess of the main tower and the nexus to link them and borrow that definition. Embrace it. The other solution was embracing its tender ‘identity’ and role as that lesser form of the greater. A cherished offshoot rather than a competitor. This would cement it as its own thing, but potentially interfere with the link from the main tower.

Quickly, she realized she simply had to do both and bring them into an equilibrium.

Fully invoking [Deific Pneumamancy], Samantha began the willwork, taking hold of a fluid, nascent thing’s soul and rewiring it; contorting and forging its spirit into proper shape. Even those levels couldn’t change Itha Ranon itself, or the main tower — not without months or years of effort — but a new thing she had created herself was very much on the table for quick alteration.

The aura around the auxiliary tower grew into form, and the mere ghost of a building gained definition, light, and contours, coming alive in truth and standing prouder, growing even taller here in the spirit, where perception was reality.

The main tower seemed to ‘accept’ its ‘child’ spontaneously, and the link grew into a bond in an instant. The conduit between them flared with energy as the main tower’s power flowed within the center, giving the auxiliary tower a brighter inner core.

Samantha counterbalanced this new power by restricting the flow and reinforcing the definition of the offshoot tower, primarily through the concept of ‘Spellhold.’ It was literal, and it was perfect, so that became its name.

Once it felt like it was in balance and healthy enough, Samantha stopped, resisting the temptation to keep pouring into it. It was better for some of it to happen naturally with time, and she was certain the function it was meant for was secure.

Within the shadow of the brightness of Itha Ranon, Spellhold interrupted that relative darkness like a gleaming island, its light new compared to the older glow of the restored ancient construct.
