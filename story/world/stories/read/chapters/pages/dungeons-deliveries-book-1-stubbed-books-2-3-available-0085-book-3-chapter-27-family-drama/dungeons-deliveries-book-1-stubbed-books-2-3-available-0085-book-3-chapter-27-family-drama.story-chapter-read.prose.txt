“If it isn’t Emilio and must be me who claims the Stone, Alex,” Petal Gravewhistle said. “Then I swear to wield it with grace. You have my word that the gnomes will inspire the weary and enact order. We will martial in the great gnomish age!”

Alex pinched the bridge of his nose. “Martial in a new age? That’s…no. Petal, I never said it wouldn’t be Emilio. But giving it to my cat? That just seems—”

“Preposterous!” Mr. Mystical interrupted, floating over to hover before Alex. “The cat is powerful, of course, and loves you, but think clearly, Alex. My dear boy, you read the description to us all. It will multiply the holder’s planar acumen.” The ghost spun so that the spout pointed at a lounging Emilio. “Do we really want him to multiply in power? He’s killed a gold grade lich already. And from what Petal explained—which was too annoyingly poetic and much like a ballad, mind you—Emilio and the gnomes are already looking to expand!”

“Passion is the root of my bearing, ghost. Your claims to not shame me.” Petal answered coldly. “We must expand. It is our duty. To Gaia, who emboldens our soil, and to Emilio, who has deemed it so. The Beast of the Blightwood lays claim to the north, building a force to overrun the region of Toronto. We hold the line for now, but the full might is coming. Without claiming new ground, why, our own fertile soil will become barren. Every gnome is dedicated to our cause. There is little rest. You saw me send Acorn and Dandelion on a new mission? You saw them shoulder it with haste? That is what it takes to kill evil. And it is evil that we must weed out!”

Petal finished his speech, turning to gaze at them all.

Alex was impressed. The gnome could deliver a good argument and looked ever more transformed. Shining steel was infused throughout his porcelain body, and he spotted other flecks of what looked like stone.

Most impressed of all were the Paladrones. They’d been hanging onto every word of Petal since the start of the discussion. It sounded like a lot of evil to balance out.

“Look, Petal,” Mary said, stepping in. “I just have a hard time believing it all. Sure, there are hordes of monsters. That’s not new. Toronto’s been protected by all the rewards for adventurers. Keanu did a pretty good job leading the Council. But a Dungeon Boss being a Tax Guild Stone holder? Melding animal and monster? To take over a region? That’s not how Dungeon Bosses act. They’re know for being fucking cra—”

Mary cut herself off and glanced toward Snu and Loopy.

“Are what?” Snu asked while tilting her head. When Mary didn’t answer, she repeated herself more sternly. “Most Dungeon Bosses are what?”

“Nothing,” Mary responded quickly. “Nothing. I didn’t mean anything. I wasn’t say—”

“Murderous loons?” Snu snapped. “Unable to do anything but kill? Little better than brainless monsters?! Good for farming? What, Mary? What the fu—”

“Aye! Statazit!” Nino boomed out, looking back and forth between both women. “Is no way to speak about one another. Or at each other. We say we be calm, so be calm.”

Snu stared Nino’s flaming eyes down with both fists clenched by her side. Then movement behind Nino’s muscled form broke her gaze, and she found herself looking at Nina hovering over his shoulder.

Nina looked terrifying with her raggedy hair, eyes full of ice, and form skeletal under a tattered flower dress. Snu wasn’t one to judge. She knew that when Nina and Nino left their domain, only by carrying their phylactery among their person, they assumed their real forms.

Despite what the old woman looked like, Snu felt her furious and frustrated heart melt some. Nina looked at her with the love of a caring grandmother.

“It is not easy to not give shit when someone insult you.” Nina said to her. “But it’s only hard when you insult you.” She turned to Mary. “And type of people are not all one thing. If anyone should know this, it is you.”

Mary gulped and turned to Snu. “Sorry. I really didn’t mean anything by it.”

Snu turned to Mary, then to Alex, and saw his expression of worry. Not about how she was acting. He gave her a little smile to let her know he was there for her.

“It’s fine,” Snu said to Mary. “I’m sorry for getting so heated. I know you don’t think like that.”

Both of them gave each other nods, and just then, Beepy was pushed up to his feet by Zippy to join in.

“WE UNDERSTAND WHY EMILIO AND PETAL WANT TO HOLD THE STONE,” He said, stepping up to the edge of the hotdog stand to scan them all. “THEIR CLAIM HELPS THEIR OWN GOALS WHILE LOOKING TO VANQUISH EVIL. THE PALADRONES WILL SUPPORT THIS CAUSE IN WHATEVER FORM IT TAKES.”

Beepy stopped for effect, rushing over to the corner to lean and direct his body at Nino and Nino.

“BUT YOU TWO,” He toned, and tilted only his head toward Mr. Mystical. “NO, YOU THREE. WE FIND YOUR REASONING AND EXPLANATION UNSATISFACTORY.”

“Esplanation?” Nino tilted his head, though Beepy saw Nina’s frown at his claim.

“YOU CLAIM YOUR LICHNESS BEGS PERSISTENT GROWTH. A RATIONAL EXPLANATION, AS MANY OF US FEEL THE DRAW TO CONSTANTLY IMPROVE. YOU CLAIM YOU SAW AN OPPORTUNITY TO ENGRATIATE YOURSELF WITH THE FORMER TAX GUILD STONE HOLDER. THAT WAS WHY YOU SENT ALEX TO DELIVER THE PIZZA. AND NOW, YOU CLAIM THAT YOU ARE TRUSTED POWERS. THAT YOU SHOULD CLAIM THE STONE. BUT WE SEE A FLAW.”

Nina’s frown deepened as Beepy let his speech hang. After a moment, he sighed a terribly dramatic noise, clasped his hands behind his back, and lowered his head as he walked back to the center of the hotdog stand roof.

“EMILIO AND THE GNOMES HAVE STATED THEIR SELFISH ENDS. NOW IT IS YOUR TURN. YOU ALWAYS HAVE A LARGER PLAN. WHAT DO YOU GAIN BEYOND POWER? YOUR CURRENT REASON IS NOT ENOUGH. SPEAK TRUTH AS WE HAVE.”

The little robot stared down all three members of the powerful lich. Zippy and Aria got up to stand at each side of Beepy.

Nino sucked his teeth and looked at his wife. Nina herself mulled something other and finally looked back at Nino. He gave her a shrug, and Nina sighed deeply before immediately floating away.

She paced, floating in the air, in a circle, thinking things over and picking at her hands. Everyone watched her and waited for her to speak. It was a lich of three parts, but they all knew it was she that sat at the top.

Nina stopped, seeming to come to a decision and raising her head. Alex was stunned upon seeing her face. He’d never seen that expression from her before.

Is Nina…scared?

“We have our selfish ends, you can all see them. We always want to expand. Always want to gain power. It is the way of lich. That we cannot help.” Nina said, licking her dry lips. “But there is more. Something you have to know about me…about us.”

Nina looked them all over and settled on Alex. She gave him a sad smile, then turned back to address them at large.

“The gnome are right. To the north is evil. We have to kill it…her.”

“See?” Petal interrupted. “The lich agrees with our claim! Beat back the Beast and claim the region north under Emilio’s control!”

“No, no,” Nina said. “Beast of Blightwood is just now. I say we have to kill her.”

Everyone was taken aback, confused as to what she meant.

“Her?” Alex asked, a very unsettling feeling stirring in his gut. “Kill who, Nina?”

Nina clenched her jaw at Alex’s question. Then, she finally did something she swore she’d never do. To talk about family matters out in the open.

“My sister. It is her that is problem.”

“You have a sister?!” Alex stood up straighter. “What? What are you talking about? Nina, please, what are you talking about?”

“Is too long a story, Nina.” Nino snorted, elbowing Alex. “You know sister drama.”

Nina ignored the comment, for once. She was battling with herself.

“So much history, I won’t go from the start,” Nina said. “But she is bad news. Evil. I no use that word ever. For anyone. But her? No problem.”

“Ok…,” Alex said, genuinely confused. “What does the Beast of Blightwood have to do with your sister?”

“I should have known, Alex,” Nina said. “I should have known when it was the cursed bracelet.”

“What? The cursed bracelet?”

“It was her.” Nina said, floating over to him. “She was always so good at hiding her poison. I…I thought she was gone. But she’s not. I can smell her now. She in the north. I did not know then. But it was her, Alex. My sister made that evil thing.”

“Your sister made the cursed bracelet?” Alex reeled. He wasn’t sure what to believe. “Why would she do that? To get to you? Through me? Wait, what?”

Nina could see Alex getting frustrated, but she couldn’t stop now. She’d made the decision. Alex had a right to know.

“She’s…I no know. Involved with the Beast somehow. Controlling maybe? But it is her. And she is evil bitch, but she is also strong.”

“A horror controlled by another horror of ages past,” Petal Gravewhistle said, practically shaking in excitement. “A conquest, nay, a battle across the ages!”

The horrible feeling in Alex’s gut rose.

“Ok, say we believe you,” Alex went on. “Say your sister, who you thought was dead, is doing…something with the Beast of Blightwood. Say we believe you that it was her that created the cursed bracelet. Why now? Why is she doing this all now, Nina?”

Nina seemed to freeze at his question. She knew the answer, of course, but saying it…

“Tell him,” Nino said to his wife.

“Tell me what?” Alex spun, feeling his heart racing and his bile sloshing. “What do you have to tell me?”

Nina shook her head, nodded seriously to herself, and looked up to meet Alex’s eyes.

“Because of you, Alex.” She said. “She do this now because of you.”

His stomach dropped, somehow even further, and his breath hitched. He’d learned to listen gut and his body. And right now it was telling him to get away.

“Because of me? Why me?”

“Because you are her grandson, Alex.”

It felt like a punch from every angle. As Nina said the words, Alex reeled as several things happened at once.

He felt the System send him a notification. It flashed in his vision, and he immediately pushed it away in anger.

[Title: Chosen One]

[Requirements: 1/4 Complete]

[Requirement 3. Become family with the lich. Complete.]

He felt the shame, loneliness, and anger surrounding the loss of his parents that he’d fought so hard to work through rise up as if exploding into his being.

He felt like he was going to vomit.

His heart raced. He felt dizzy, as if he couldn’t breathe right. Emotions rolling through him that he’d sequestered for so long in his mind bubbled against the warnings from his innate ability to feel warnings through his body.

“Got…you.” Brody’s voice suddenly cut through, and Alex felt his clone’s strong hand on his back. “Hey. I got you. It’s…okay.”

Alex stared dumbfounded at Brody, then at everyone else.

They were all looking at him with pity. Like he needed to be helped along and cared for.

A strange but all too common thing happened in his mind. It happens to many who feel alone for so long. To many who feel like they need to do it all on their own. The cry of help of the lonesome heart, his heart, flared to anger.

“You…you…,” Alex muttered, glaring daggers at Nina. “You knew?”

She tried to speak. “We—”

But Alex cut in. “You knew? You fucking knew? And you didn’t fucking tell me?”

“Alex…” Petal tried to help.

“I thought you said I could trust you!” Alex yelled at Nina and Nino. “And you didn’t fucking say anything?”

Nino tried to speak. “We though we keep you safe—”

“Safe!? By lying to me? By using me? How long have you known!? This whole time? THIS WHOLE TIME? Since the beginning?!”

Nina deflated, ashamed. She’d had her reasons, but Alex was right. When both Nino and Nino didn’t answer, Alex looked back on forth between them, fuming.

“Fucking exactly. Hiding things again. I’ve done nothing but do right by both of you, and this is what I get?”

Alex spun away, and Brody grabbed his shoulder. “Alex…wa—”

“No,” Alex shrugged out of Brody’s hand. His eyes were still on Nino and Nina. “I’m done waiting in the dark. Tired of everyone fucking using me. Everyone uses everyone for every damn fucking thing. Show some loyalty and get fucked for it.”

He shook his head, and laughed, the stress trying to escape his body.

“You know what? Fuck this shit. I’m out.”

Snu felt his skill bubble in the air and called out. “Wait, no! Alex, wait!”

But between one word and the next, Alex was already fifty feet away, sprinting away from the group. He burned multiple [Phantom Step]s and pumped his legs as fast as he could, Tax Guild Stone still in hand, unclaimed.

The remaining group looked around at each other, unsure how to proceed.
