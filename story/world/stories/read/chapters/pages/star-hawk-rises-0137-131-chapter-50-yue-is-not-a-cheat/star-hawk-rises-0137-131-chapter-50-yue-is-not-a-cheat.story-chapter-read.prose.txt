Then the scarlet blood-refining pill, the violet soul-calming pill, the Evergreen Longevity pill, the spirit vein opening pill, and the heavenly jade purification pill. I refined the violet soul-calming pill, hoping its difficulty would impress the Divine Emperor's descendant.” I said confidently, before seeing her eyes snap open wide with shock, before turning to look at me.

“Uh… two perfects, one high, and the last one was a minor.” I said carefully.

She winced. Then looked at me with pity. It took a few more heartbeats before she finally spoke, the wait had made me feel anxious about what I was starting to think was going to be a bombshell.

“The good news is you are in the sect, guaranteed. The bad news is you will end up in an alchemy lab somewhere producing those evergreen pills for the rest of your life.” She said with a heavy sigh, like I was being sentenced to death.

”Yue… they’re longevity pills. Even the minor ones, with the high impurities in it, can extend your lifespan by ten years… wait what do you think Divine Emperor Rowan meant by extra funds?”

“A dozen medium grade spirit stones.” I said, suddenly unsure.

Her jaw dropped. “How poor are you?” She suddenly asked.

“Not that poor! My family makes a few medium spirit stones a year, five during a good year.” I replied defensively.

She groaned and buried her face in her hands. “Oh my heavens and earth, your family’s about to drown in spirit stones they won't even know how to spend. Yue, the pills you refined will sell at a treasure hall or pill pavilion for medium spirit stones, yes. However, the Evergreens are different, they are longevity pills.”

“A minor evergreen pill is an extra ten years, a cultivator at the end of their lifespan will pay high amounts of spirit stones for a little more time to break through a realm. I’m talking in the high-grade spirit stone area, and not a small amount, either. The last one I heard about being sold went for one hundred high-grade spirit stones.” She told me as she gave me a meaningful look.

“And the uh, high one? I asked slowly, starting to feel trepidation rushing up my spine.

“No idea, rank 5 alchemists make those, and they rarely appear outside an auction, my clan certainly could not afford one.” She exhaled. “Yue, do you even understand what you did? Perfect Evergreen Longevity Pills are thought to be myths. Maybe refined by Rank nine or ten alchemists, at best. And you ‘someone without a cultivation technique’ made one.”

“So… think it got commander Kenna Mackenzie's attention?” I asked weakly.

“Bitch, I think it got the Sect Master's attention!” She screamed back at me.

I froze. She went on, quieter now, “Divine Emperor Rowan Mackenzie was the Sect’s alchemy Elder centuries ago… Look, the Void Walking Sect is a small sect, we are not one of the big sects, like the Heavenly Dao Sword sect. They have a sister sect in the immortal realm that they are still in contact with. Elder Rowan made Evergreen Longevity Pills for those kinds of sects, trading them for protection and passage. And now you’ve just… recreated his work.”

Silence hung between us while I processed that information and began to feel my pulse quicken before looking into Akemi’s eyes.

“I am in trouble, aren't I?” I whispered.

“You’re about to become either the Sect Master’s Personal disciple, the Sect Alchemy Pavilion Elder’s successor, or a refining slave who never sees daylight again,” she said grimly.

I swallowed hard, staring at the door. “I am so screwed.”
