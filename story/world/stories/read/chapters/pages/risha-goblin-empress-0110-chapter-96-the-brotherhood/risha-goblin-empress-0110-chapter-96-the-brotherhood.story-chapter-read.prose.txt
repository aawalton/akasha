The building was still just as noisy, but Risha could see the curious stares of those who watched their full table.

Klu stood standing behind Risha as she sat. Klaz’zks stared at her chair as if it were an annoying bug, then slowly lowered herself into it.

Siyah only had eyes for the powerful demon across from them, and Risha couldn’t blame her. The brief glance she’d had of his spirit still elicited the smallest bit of fear inside her.

“I’m surprised you came.” Erin looked at Damien with a mix of emotions that Risha couldn’t quite read.

Damien looked over all the gathered creatures, then slowly lowered his hood. Veins of white flowed through his lighter skin, in stark contrast to the red veins and darker skin of the other demons at the table. His eyes were red, and two long horns rose from the top of his head and pointed forward. His hair was medium length, curling around his face artfully. For the briefest moment, he reminded Risha of the marble she’d spotted in Shyanne’s home in the Council of Grand Mothers.

Then he smiled, his eyes meeting Risha, and the illusion disappeared. “Our restrictions have been lifted.”

“O-our?” Erin asked.

He pulled his eyes away from Risha and looked at Erin. “Yes. The entire brotherhood.”

Erin glanced at Risha’s party, and Risha got the sense that she was missing something important. She wracked her memory, trying to remember a brotherhood of demons.

Mother Maya had said something once, about how the Demons might have an Old Brother left. It had been a passing comment, but it sparked something in Risha. She’d heard mention of an Old Brother again, but she couldn’t remember where.

“Why, after so long?” Erin asked.

Damien shrugged, “It’s complicated. Tell me more about this madness.”

Erin frowned, but answered without complaint, “It started a month or so ago. Delvers would come back from the depths, their minds gone, and acting like wild beasts. If they drew blood, it would spread. Healers couldn’t cure it. There was an orc shaman,” She glanced at Klu, who stood behind Risha. “He said that their spirits were being corrupted, but it was beyond his ability.”

Damien leaned back in his chair, tilting it so that it was on two legs. “What have you done?”

“I treat those who come through here as best I can. I don’t understand spirit well enough to fight whatever this is from here, and I can’t leave to fight it at its source.”

Damien looked between Risha and Klu with a small smile. “what a coincidence that two experts in spirit have landed in your lap. Tell me, Risha, what was your encounter with the madness?”

Risha’s eyes narrowed at the flippant tone of the demon, “I treated that demon over there.” She pointed to their guide, who was standing in the back awkwardly, waiting for them to finish their business.

The beggar immediately noticed their gazes, and he shrunk into himself in fear.

Damien frowned, and pulled his gaze away, a flash of guilt disappearing instantly to be hidden behind a thoughtful face.

“Do you know where the source is?”

Erin shook her head, “I know the general area, but the person I sent to investigate was killed.”

“I can lead them.” Nyx spoke to Risha.

“I might know.” Risha said.

Damien turned to her curiously, then smiled. “Good. Then we can set out right away.” Damien stood, and the others at the table started to follow him.

“Ah-” Siyah’s voice paused the table. The young demoness stood slowly, then bowed her head in fear as Damien turned his gaze to her.

Damien looked away from her broken horns, his fists tightening. “Did you have something to say little sister?”

Siyah looked at Risha, and Risha indicated for her to continue.

“I’m looking for something. I don’t know what it is exactly, but I must find it.”

Damien pulled his hood up, hiding his face before looking at her again. “That doesn’t tell me much.”

“I haven’t been told much. But… something tells me that you might know.”

Damien tilted his head, “Is that so?”

Siyah looked around at the many people listening to their conversation. “Never mind.”

Risha gently placed her hand on the Demoness’s back. She looked at Damien for a long moment, considering. “We can talk after we deal with… whatever is causing this.”

“A great idea. Want to come with, Erin?”

Erin gave him a look, “You know I can’t leave.”

“What’s one day away?”

Her eyes narrowed. “Damien…”

He held up his hands, “Right, right. I’ll just take the goblin, her scary looking orc, and spider, to the depths of the ancient cities all on my lonesome.” Damien waved in the general direction of Klaz’zks.

Risha froze at the fact that he’d recognized the Spider Empress in her humanoid form.

Erin glanced at Klaz’zks, then shrugged, “You’ll be fine. You’ll want to leave through the east gate, and head down the Naafiri path”

Damien nodded, “Thank you Erin. We’ll talk more soon.”

“We better.”

Damien laughed, then motioned for Risha’s party to join him.

Risha hesitated for a split-second, then walked up beside him.

He led them through the tavern, then paused at the exit, looking at Siyah, “Sister, it might be best if you don’t join us in this.”

Siyah looked at Risha.

Risha nodded, “Klaz’zks, can you take her back to the others?”

The Spider held Risha’s gaze for a long moment, then bowed. The spider motioned for the Demon Beggar to step out of shadows, and the three of them left towards the inn.

Damien watched them go, then stretched. He walked in the opposite direction, towards the exit of the city. “I wanted to talk to you alone, but I suppose a Mystic can be trusted. Tell me, Risha, how do you treat the demons of your empire?”

Risha looked at the demon’s back as they walked, “You know who I am, don’t you?”

“Of course. A Mother’s spirit is unforgettable, and yours reminds me of a certain bastard all too much.”

Risha paused, suddenly making the connection. She felt stupid that it took her so long, “The Old Shaman Spirit, he’s a Brother too, right?”

“He hasn’t told you much, has he? Always was too secretive.” Damien stopped, and Risha stopped with him as a group of Dwarves walked through the street. “You didn’t answer my question. How do you treat the demons?”

Risha glanced at Klu, whose eyes were watching the passing dwarves. “They are my children, as much as anyone.” Risha followed the orc’s gaze to a large box with doors, held up by four larger demons. Her gaze met piercing blue eyes from inside, before the box passed them by.

“And Siyah? Did you do that to her horns?”

Risha turned to the demon, and bared her teeth, “Risha would never. It was your own kind that did that to her.”

Damien deflated slightly, “I see. I’d hoped that wasn’t the case.” He walked through the street and up to a small gate of the city, leading into the rockface.

Two guards walked up to them. Damien held something up, and they stopped. The two guards backed up and the gate was slowly opened.

They stayed in silence for a while as they walked through the low-lit tunnel and onto the ancient road of a long-buried city. They walked until they came to several branching paths.

Damien stopped, and turned to Risha, “Alright, you’re taking us from here.”

Risha paused, then turned to the dark branching paths. “Nyx. You followed it, right?”

The black cat leapt from her onto the ancient cobblestone of the road.

Damien didn’t react, unsurprised at the sudden appearance of the cat.

She sniffed the air for several seconds, then took a left. Risha followed her into the darkness.

A small flame appeared in the air next to Damien as the stones around them stopped producing light.

“Do you know what we are looking for?” Risha asked the demon after a moment.

“No, but I have my suspicions. Whatever it is, I can take care of it. I’ll just need you two to protect my spirit from the madness while I do what I do.”

Klu grunted in agreement. He’d been strangely quiet since the appearance of the demon, and Risha wondered what was on his mind.

“You can’t protect yourself?” Risha turned down another dark hallway, following where she knew Nyx to be.

“I have some skill in spirit, but my specialties are elsewhere. I’d much rather trust the First’s little sister and a Mystic.”

“The First’s?” Risha had heard the term before, when the Demon introduced himself.

“The First Servant, the First Revolutionary, the First Brother, take your pick.” Damien ducked under a low hanging piece of stone.

Risha ducked under the same stone, a step behind him. “I’ve heard about the First Servant. A Dragon said he was a villain.”

Damien snorted, “Depends on who you ask. When I was younger, he was my hero. But he has done many villainous things. Many are dead because of him. A Dragon would see him as a villain. Unfortunately, he’s just a Goblin. Flawed, as we all are.”

Risha thought about the goblin she knew. He shared many traits with her, but she had seen the brief glimpses of sadness he carried with him. The way he looked at Elle’s cabin in particular always carried a heaviness.

Nyx stopped at a set of large doors, unfamiliar script running across them. The three of them stopped behind her, and the small cat jumped back into Risha.

“The trail continues past here, but something strong waits on the other side.”

Risha slowly unwrapped the chains from the swords at her waist and rewrapped them around her hands.

Klu drew his sword.

Damien just stared at the doors, a small frown on his face.

He glanced back at them, his eyes landing on the swords Risha carried. “You like our weapons? They are nice, aren’t they.” He held out his hand, and white magma poured out over his arm, gathering into a chain ending in a large sword. Then his other hand raised, and dark liquid gathered into another sword with a chain. “They’re one of my favorites too. Stay back, whatever is behind this door has been affected by the madness.”

He stepped up to the doors, then spoke in an ancient language that translated through the power of Risha’s ability. “This child of the Sparks uses his authority. Open, on the command of Damien, General of the First Order.”

The script on the walls shifted, and squirmed like snakes, then pulled back to the walls. The doors slowly opened to darkness.

Flames spread across the floor, lighting up a huge cavern and revealing a four legged creature pacing across the floor. It was massive, Twenty eyes covered the creatures face, and short fur stood on end as it turned towards them. It opened its mouth, revealing sharp teeth that spiraled down its throat.

Before Risha could react, the creature and Damien met in the middle of the cavern.

The earth shook.

Then it was over.

The creature fell, its head rolling across the floor, and its blood soaking the ground.

“Risha!” Damien’s call drew her gaze to the Demon. His veins glowed with power under his skin. “Check me for madness. Hurry.”

Risha ran forward, looking at the Demon’s spirit. She watched for several seconds before she found it. An insidious worm that burrowed its way through his spirit.

She called upon Igni to burn it away, and Nyx to fight the presence behind it.

Klu’s hand rested on her back, adding his spirit to hers as she burned the madness away.

After several heated seconds, she pulled back, letting out a breath.

“Is it gone?”

She nodded.

“Good.” Damien turned to the hall, where the sounds of footsteps, strange howling and crashing echoed through the corridors. “You’ll need to do that again soon.”

The walls burst open as more creatures poured into the cavern.

Damien smiled, raising his swords as the magma white of his veins became blinding.
