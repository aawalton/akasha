[Time]: 10:50 AM[Location]: The Approach to the Great Library

They left the noisy Commercial District behind.

From the outside, the Library looked deceptively modest: a sleek, ivory spear roughly the size of a standard cathedral. It sat politely within the campus skyline, obeying the laws of perspective.

But every senior student knew that the "building" was a lie.It was merely the Doorframe.The interior was a Folded Dimension, forcibly crammed into a physical anchor point.

And the path leading to it?

They stepped off the stone pavement and onto... Void.

The path was a Scar torn from the cosmos. On both sides of the walkway, colorful nebulas swirled in a silent, suffocating vacuum. Distant stars flickered around them and below them, and the ground beneath their feet was a translucent ribbon of solidified starlight.

Hathaway’s breath hitched. She recognized the texture of reality here.

This was the Astral Ruins itself.

She remembered the history of this place. Centuries ago, the Arch-Witches had reached into the shattered cosmos, ripped out a chunk of a galaxy, dragged it back to the academy, and nailed it down here to serve as a driveway. A bridge across the void would have been too mundane.

It was the ultimate flex: We don't navigate the universe; we pave our sidewalks with it.

"Keep walking," Victoria said, her voice echoing strangely in the thin air. "Don't look down."

Hathaway looked down.

Through the translucent floor, she saw a spiral galaxy spinning slowly, millions of light-years beneath the soles of her boots.

The sensation of being a speck of dust suspended over an infinite abyss made her knees weak.

The orange cat on her head seemed to sense the cosmic horror. It dug its claws tighter into her hair, shivering, its purring stopped by the sheer scale of the nothingness.

Around her was the absolute zero of the vacuum, the silent majesty of dead stars.But she was carrying five buckets of spicy fried chicken that were radiating heat like a localized furnace.

This is surreal, Hathaway thought, clutching the greasy cardboard as her only anchor to reality. I am walking on a galaxy, holding fast food, wearing a terrified cat.

Finally, the path ended, short of the Library.

Right in the middle of this cosmic grandeur, suspended by massive, enchanting adamantine chains over a swirling purple vortex, was a structure that defied all logic.

It was a small, cozy Wooden Tea House.

[Do Not Knock if You Are Boring.]

Victoria stopped. She straightened her gloves, took a deep breath, and raised her hand.
