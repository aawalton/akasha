A sudden shrieking whistle appeared behind them as Georg’s shield smashed into the orc’s. The human man grunted as he was driven back a half step, but his shield resting against Amy and Ezra’s kept him from being driven too far away. Ezra grunted in effort as his shield smashed into his aching elbow again, and he wished he’d been able to fill his inner world more. He had no idea what that whistle was, until the Pseudo-Knight they were defending spoke.

“I have to focus on the Dragonkin!” Basil announced from behind the squad. “Hold the line, Chloe’s coming! Focus on giving ground if you need to, just stay alive.”

Ezra scoffed at that, and then one of the bugbears grabbed a goblin in both hands. Though the brown furred goblinoid was much larger than the warty green one, it was only half a meter taller. Even so, it didn’t seem to struggle to pick up its ally and hurl it into the battle lines. The goblin spun wildly through the air, screaming as it flew at about head height towards Ezra. He didn’t think before he braced, lifted his spear above his head, and intercepted its torso with his spear. The body slid almost effortlessly all the way to the top of the spear, and thick purple ichor dripped down the haft of his weapon.

Behind the bugbear approached the Dragonkin, its scales glinting in the light of morning as it roared commands at the goblinoids. Whatever language it was speaking, Ezra couldn’t understand it, but the goblinoids all shifted according to the command. The orc pressed in to Georg again, its club falling and Georg defending against it. The iron bands rang like a bell against the man’s iron shield and the Lieutenant fell to his knee from the attack, though only for a second. He surged back to his feet with a roar of challenge and stabbed with his spear. The monster’s shield got in the way, but Georg pressed the attack.

With a curse, Ezra turned and dumped the corpse onto the ground, facing the bugbear that rushed forward on the orc’s flank. Behind him, Leah shouted, “If it does it again, I’ll take care of it!”

“Yeah!” Ezra replied as the bugbear on the opposite side did the same thing. Boyd shouted something that he couldn’t quite understand, and then the bugbear was in his face. A small part of his mind worried about the Dragonkin, but Jormun shouted for him to focus. He did so, just in time for the bugbear’s hands to snap out towards his shield. It struck faster than anything that he’d fought against before, and he tried to react. Ezra pulled back as the fingers grasped at his shield’s edge, while ensuring he didn’t compromise his position on the line. It wasn’t enough.

Its fingers caught the bottom of his shield and yanked him forward. Leah screamed something behind him as Ezra was pulled out of line and beside the orc. The bugbear’s fingers twisted, and an inhuman strength ripped Ezra from his feet. He was thrown onto his back, and the orc paid him no mind. Instead, he saw as the line tried to fill in for him, but not quickly enough. Two goblins rushed into the gap as Ezra knew this failure of his would lead to everyone’s death.

Before that, though, he faced his own death at the hands of the bugbears. The one that’d thrown him down twisted the shield and his arm with it, leaving Ezra cursing that he’d properly strapped himself into it. His injured elbow screamed in protest as his chest was exposed and the bugbear’s talons shot down towards his chest. Ezra screamed as he desperately swung his spear at the monster. The blade cut through the fur to hit its shoulder, but not anywhere near enough to drive it off.

Then Helen arrived, her spear screaming through the air. The bugbear turned and, with the same strange speed it’d caught his shield with, dodged the attack. She was a blur, though, her spear flashing forward three times and keeping the bugbear on its back foot.

“Get up already,” Artemis commanded as she grabbed Ezra by the scruff of his uniform and threw him to his feet. He didn’t mind the assistance and stabbed at the closest goblinoid as it swung at Artemis’s leg. His spear caught the beast’s shoulder and sent it back stumbling as he fought to keep his feet.

The Dragonkin seemed to take notice of the two Adepts at that point and ran forward. The embers in its mouth flowed down to cover both of its hands, but before it could close in on Helen, five arrows appeared, flying from five different angles at its head and body. With two swipes of its claws, all but one of the arrows were cut out of the air, the last and final one slamming into its chest. Each one of the arrows exploded with a burst of wind, those that hit its hands blowing out most of the fire. The one that hit its chest ripped the thick scales there apart, blood bursting out of the now wounded creature.

“Get back to the line. They need you,” Artemis continued as she turned to support her sister against the bugbears and Dragonkin. Helen continued striking in a blur of movements until one of her strikes burst with flame right in front of the bugbear’s face. The flames caught the fur alight, and the bugbear screamed in fear as its face burned. Ezra wanted to stay and watch the display of aura, but forced himself to retreat back to the line, swiping his spear at the two goblins that attempted to approach. He sprinted forward to the line, just in time to see Georg’s shield bend down and permit the club to smash into his helmet.

Ezra felt the scream tear itself from his throat involuntarily as his lieutenant, the man who’d recruited him, went limp and fell. The line would fall with him, and every one of Ezra‘s friends would die. He would never allow that to happen. The small traces of aura that remained in his inner world, the little embers that he had been using to try to keep his elbow strong, instead started flowing to his right hand and then swirling around the haft of his spear. He didn’t have a conscious thought before he took one step forward with his left foot, twisted with his whole body, and threw the spear at the small of the orc’s back.

Wind exploded all around him, knocking him back as the spear exploded forward. Upon reaching the orc, the head of the spear flew without any resistance into its spine and the monster’s legs went limp. It fell as Leah dragged Georg back and the line stepped back together, Troy serving as the new head of the formation.

“Good fucking show!” Boyd whooped and hollered.

“Now get back to us!” Troy demanded as one of the bugbears turned its attention away from the two Adepts that were ripping its fellow apart. Instead, it stared at Ezra, who swayed on his feet. He recognized the feeling from when he accidentally used an aural attack the first time, the exhaustion of his inner world, and he’d done this this time while probably having about the same reserves, and just being more trained. Fortunately, the greater discipline and practice had resulted in a much more effective attack that nearly knocked him out.

He took two faltering steps, his knees shaking, and then he fell, unable to move. Unlike before, he didn’t lose consciousness, but there was still nothing he could do besides shakily raise his hand. The bugbear jumped, its claws reached out towards him, and again, Artemis was there. Her shield swelled, a dozen centimeters of water appearing on the face of it as the bugbear found itself trapped in her defense. The water swirled around it and crawled up its arm as it yanked desperately, trying to escape.

“I thought people were tellin’ tales about you,” Artemis said as she managed to hold the monster in place with her aura. Helen flashed up behind her and buried her spear in the back of the bugbear‘s neck three times, each strike as accurate as the last as she slew the beast.

“What… did you hear?“ Ezra asked. An answer never came as a half dozen more arrows screamed overhead towards the Dragonkin. Each one burst against its chest, but despite being wounded and having three shafts buried in its chest, it wasn’t dead yet and seemed, despite the blood flooding down its chest, to be in good fighting shape.

It ducked another three arrow shots from Basil and closed in on Helen. She raised her shield and tried to defend herself, the attacks smashing into her defenses and driving her back as she willingly gave ground. Her shield burst out with flame into the massive scaled creature's face, but it didn’t even seem to blink. Instead of recoiling at the flames, it leaned into the fire to deliver a quick blow with its clawed hands held in a knife hand position. With an almost casual display of deadly strength, it plunged its hand straight through the shield and into Helen’s belly.

The wind whooshed out of her lungs with a pained hollow gasp, and she fell back, blood coursing down her torso. Unlike the Dragonkin, she couldn’t shrug off this attack, and she wheezed in pain as she collapsed. Artemis screamed beside him, calling out for her sister.

Ezra wanted to do something, but he couldn’t even raise his hand any more, and was forced to watch as Artemis fought her best to stave off the beast’s attacks. It was just too fast, and seemed to play with her as the rest of Ezra’s squad dealt with another five goblins that came forward and harried at their flanks.

Another three arrows screamed towards the Dragonkin, distracting it enough to keep from dispatching Artemis the way it did her sister, though the beast still defended from the strikes. Artemis took the opportunity to close in and strike at the huge wound in its chest, her spear’s blade covered with water.

The Dragonkin twisted, its hands blocking the shots from Basil while its tail smashed into Artemis’s shield. It cracked and her arm buckled behind it, but she abandoned her own limb to thrust forward and cut into the thick meat of the Dragonkin’s neck. Its scales seemed to latch together to protect itself, but she managed to draw blood as the water on her weapon expanded the wound further.

With a roar, the Dragonkin twisted around to focus on Artemis again, ignoring two more arrows that smashed into its shoulders. Ezra could feel aura gathering towards Basil somewhere in the direction of the soldiers, but he would be too late. Artemis stood her ground and dodged an attack, but with one arm broken and weighed down by her shield, she wouldn’t be able to do anything. She jumped back, no more aura amplifying her movements as she grunted in pain.

Two more attacks blazed forward from the Dragonkin, the first smashing her spear and the second knocking her prone. The monster leapt forward and, with flames licking from the corners of its mouth, leaned down to rip her head off with one bite.

From behind, a spear punched forward and slammed into the beast’s hip. The attack pierced through scales and knocked the attack aside. Helen grinned at the monster, blood trickling from her mouth as she collapsed again. Then, all the combatants close enough to save Ezra, Helen, and Artemis’s lives were downed, and the Dragonkin was wounded but more than capable of killing them.

And a glowing fist appeared and smashed into its face.

“Yeah, that’s not going to fucking happen.”
