I didn’t need him. I wanted nothing to do with him. Not anymore. This time, this time, he had gone too far. I was done.

Each foot forward was a hard stomp. If only I could crack the ground, if only I was stomping on his stupid face. Or perhaps, something more sensitive. I ground my heel into the gravel and gave the road a particularly vicious twist.

A cat yowled in the far distance and knocked something over with a clatter, making me jump, then grin.

Yes, he’d sound just like that with a well-placed kick.

Laughing maniacally, I kept walking. Then started sobbing. Then I stopped sobbing, the tears boiling away before the burning pyre of my rage. Too angry for tears. So angry I couldn’t think. Couldn’t think, couldn’t walk, couldn’t do anything! Stupid, stupid man with his stupid, stupid ‘I totally have it under control’, absolutely not a gambling addiction addiction. ARG.

“Rancid, wretched, idiot, fucking ASSHOLE!”

The thought of starting over – I couldn’t process what I was feeling. Someone was screaming incoherently inside my skull. Angry hornets. Somehow, I carried on walking. I was going to explode out of my skin. So busy, so busy with my hornets, I didn’t see the car.

It didn’t see me either.

I didn’t feel anything when it hit me. Didn’t feel anything but my incandescent anger lighting me up from the inside.

There was physical pain, somewhere, far away, but that was all.

Pain and rage and wild, wild thoughts and hard prismatic diamond shards of glass – or was that before? No, it was now, all of it swirling in patterns of grief and anger. I had left the pain of it behind. That was happening to someone else. Belonged to someone else.

They say, when you die, your life flashes before your eyes. That part was true, sort of. I saw many things, most of which pissed me off.

There was a light at the end of a tunnel, though.

Was I supposed to resist the urge to walk into it?

Ian’s voice echoed in my head.

Fuck that, I was done here. I wanted to see what was next.

Lights flashed overhead.

Not lights. Lightning.

In between me and the gorge were battlements. Heavy, stone, battlements.

How did those get here? Actually never mind that, how had I got here?

Fat raindrops splashed against my exposed skin. Where was my coat? A sharp gust of wind buffeted me, carrying with it the ragged scents of pine, smoke and iron. I inhaled, trying to collect myself. I was standing on a stone balcony atop some sort of tower. Clutching at the stones, I peered out into the gaping dark. Suddenly I was afraid the buffeting wind would topple me headfirst into that ravine. I couldn’t see the bottom. For all I knew there was no bottom, and I was in hell.

Although I hadn’t imagined hell would be quite so… windy.

My fingers dug into the cold hard stone. My fingers... my fingers? Another flash of light showed me - hands, blackened to the wrist, the nails long and delicately pointed, almost clawlike - also black. I didn’t remember having hands like that? Mud? From falling? No. What-

Shreds of memory crept back. Had I been …hit by a car? Or is this some kind of drunken hallucination? No, I did not feel drunk. Not anymore. In fact, I was stone cold sober. I didn’t think I had ever felt more sober in my life.

Suddenly a textbox, a textbox of all things, filled my vision. It strangely overlayed my view of everything, gleaming a soft, diffuse blue.

[Welcome, lost soul! Devourer of hearts, hope and kingdoms! Find your place, doom herald, spirit crusher, night enchantress! All will quake at your coming! The light spurns you, as you spurn it! “Um.”

[Choose your class. Unlock your potential! Unlimited power is at your fingertips! Think of all you can accomplish!

Classes available:

Farm Boy with Magic Sword, Not Like the Other Girls, Lion-Hearted King, Dangerous Damsel, Evil Enchantress, Dark Sorcerer, Swashbuckling Pirate, Shining Paladin, Undead Knight, Lowborn Thief, Golden Cleric, Lonely Ranger, Shadow Assassin, Lowly Court Jester, Sentient Bucket, Merchant Queen, Mysterious Unassuming Pauper, The Hand Behind the Throne]

I must have hit my head and now I was hallucinating in hospital. My damaged brain must be dredging up old gaming memories. Though that didn’t explain why I was hallucinating such strange classes. Even for a wild night out, this was seriously taking the biscuit.

“Why are all the options crossed out except Evil Enchantress?” I said to the glowing box. “And why would anyone want to become a bucket for that matter? How can a bucket be a class?”

I tried to make the box go away, swiping at it with wild gestures. The annoying thing moved with me. Perhaps if I double blinked? No.

Nothing.

“I’m going to need more information about this, especially before picking some that literally says ‘evil’ on the tin,” I said firmly. Just because I was hallucinating a game, that was no reason to be stupid about it. No response.

[The Evil Enchantress is anger, spite and bitterness made manifest. Skill focus is Enchanting, Demon Summoning, Sacrifice. The Evil Enchantress casts spells via imbibing the viscera of her enemies, grows her spell and minion pool by sacrificing the lives of light-aligned creatures, and suffers a growing sanity debuff in exchange for ever-increasing power. What’s a little madness in exchange for global domination, ey?]

“Enchanting sounds nice,” I said. “But not the rest.” Sanity de-buff my arse. Who wanted to rule the world if you spent your free time talking to mirrors or fingerpainting with bodily fluids? Even a filthy casual like me knew picking a character and class was not something one did lightly. ‘Evil Enchantress’ sounded like a monkey’s paw situation if I ever saw one.

I had more pressing questions though, like was I alive?
