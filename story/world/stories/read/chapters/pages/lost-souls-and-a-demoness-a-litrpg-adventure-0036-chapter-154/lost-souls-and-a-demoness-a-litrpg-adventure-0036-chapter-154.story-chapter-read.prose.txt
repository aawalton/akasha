The last time Jade had been shot, her defensive barrier deflected a couple of bullets at most before falling apart. With upgraded armor, and against rifles instead of a minigun, it fared somewhat better. Around ten bullets ricocheted off the sparking ward before it cracked and some began to penetrate. That was about how long it took Jade to realize why the soldiers were shooting at her instead of focusing on the wounded wraith demon.

Oh, right. She was still in her succubus form.

She'd shifted to add speed and power to what she'd hoped would be a killing blow. Instead, whatever magic the wraith demon afflicted her with had fogged up her mind, and she hadn't remembered to shift back before charging into full view of the L-ReF compound. And of course, that just happened to be when reinforcements arrived.

These thoughts, along with a few choice curses, flashed through Jade's mind in nearly an instant. Unfortunately, bullets are speedy things, and several more of them reached her in that "nearly an instant". What felt like a trio of hammer blows slammed into Jade's chest and arm, sending bolts of pain radiating through her. To her amazement, none of them penetrated her skin. She actually felt them bouncing off her like she was a concrete wall. That wasn't to say she didn't take damage, however. More by reflex than intent, Jade sent a surge of mana through her legs and blinked away, leaving an illusory duplicate in her place. She appeared in the shadow of a nearby stack of crates, heart pounding like a jackhammer. Her hands felt for bullet holes even as she gasped out the incantation for invisibility, confirming that she was still in one piece. Satisfied that she was, Jade pushed the pain aside and immediately sought her quarry. She didn't have to look far.

Shouts of alarm mingled with the rapid clak clak clak of automatic weapons' fire as the L-ReF soldiers scrambled to organize themselves against the literal demon wreaking havoc in their midst. Vythis moved like a shadow, visible only as a blur of motion as she tore through their formation leaving sprays of blood and screams of pain in her wake. It had only been a few seconds, and she'd already dispatched a quarter of them. Another surge of anger threatened to overtake Jade as she bore witness to the slaughter. She was up and moving a moment later, heedless of her wounds. Her mind had room for only thought. Stopping this nightmare of a creature before it killed anyone else.

Jade rejoined the fight just as Fenrin, Luis, and Tara tore out of the aisle. Charging into the chaotic crossfire of bullets, arrows, and Tara's energy beams would be suicide, magically boosted fortitude or no. Instead, Jade took to the air, silently homing in on her target from above. Vylith dodged one of Fenrin's lightning arrows, phasing straight through one of the soldiers to do so. He exploded into a shroud of red mist, but not before a mote of light flung by Tara struck the demon on the side. It adhered to her, glowing brightly in the dim light of the warehouse, making it far easier to keep track of her rapid movements. The soldiers rallied, five of them unleashing a concentrated barrage on the now-visible monster. The bullets must have had some magic in them because, to Jade's surprise, some of them crackled with blue energy as they passed through the demon's insubstantial form. The attack slowed her for a moment, but it was enough. Jade appeared in a nova of radiant light, projecting and shaping her aura as she descended like a meteor. Her skin cracked and burned, scorched by the very same energy manifesting from her spirit.

"Hyaaa!"

The roar rose unbidden from her lips as she crashed into Vythis, slamming her blazing blades through her with all the strength she could muster. The wraith demon was hellishly swift, but even she had her limits. One of Jade's strikes missed, but Phantom found its mark. There was a blinding flash of light, a moment of resistance, and then a sickening sizzling sound followed by a hollow thump. Jade landed in a crouch, blinking away stars. She'd just recovered enough to see the blackened, severed arm lying on the on the floor when a clawed foot caught her in the chest. Vythis's screech was utterly inhuman, filled with pain and rage. Jade caught a glimpse of her face, now distorted into something dark and alien, as the blow blasted her away like she'd been shot by a cannon. She crashed into one of the huge stacks of crates with a hollow thunk accompanied by multiple loud cracks in her chest that sent fresh waves of pain lancing through her.

The world was spinning. Jade groaned, blinking rapidly and trying to recover her senses. Everything was still coming back into focus when a loud, ominous creaking sound caused her to look up. A large shipping container teetered precariously on the lip of the stack of crates Jade had impacted. She could only watch as it tipped a little further and then began plummeting straight for her. Reflexes kicked in, but Jade's body failed her. She crashed back to the floor before making it to safety, wracked by fresh waves of pain she could no longer ignore. No good. She wasn't going to--

Luis appeared out of nowhere. His body ignited with rippling waves of energy, the immovable object talent massively reinforcing his strength and endurance. He reached up and braced--

WHAM!

He caught it. The force of the impact drove the large man to his knees, and a thunderous boom rang out, as if he'd just struck the world's largest gong. The concrete floor cracked beneath his knee and the room quaked beneath her. Jade couldn't tear her eyes away, awed by the spectacle. He'd caught it. Holy…

"Graaa!"

Luis let out a strained, groaning yell as he heaved, shifting the enormous container just enough for it to slide off his shoulders. It slammed to the floor with another resonating boom, coming to rest between them and the others.

"Luis! You…" Jade faltered, rendered speechless both by the pain in her chest and the utterly superhuman feat of strength. "You saved me." She finished in a chough and then was a little alarmed when her hand came away splattered in blood. Although, she reflected in a daze, only being a little alarmed by coughing up blood was concerning in and of itself.

"Don't mention it." He groaned, collapsing to his hands and knees. He gave her a strained smile. "It's what friends do.""You're hurt!" Jade finally managed to pull herself together. She lurched to his side, pulling a health potion from her pouch. Ignoring her own pain, she forced it into his hands. He drank it without a word, sighing in relief as the elixir worked its magic. Jade was just basking in the soothing relief of a potion of her own when a chorus of pounding footsteps announced the arrival of the remaining soldiers.

"Shift back!" Luis hissed.

"What? Oh!" In the chaos, Jade had completely forgotten that she was in her succubus form. She morphed back into her human shape just as four black-clad soldiers rounded the corner with weapons raised, blinding them with a pair of rifle mounted flashlights. Luis and Jade rose to meet them as they came to a stop with uncertain postures. Jade wasn't sure how much they'd seen of her. The warehouse was dark and the battle chaotic, but these men were on edge. Fear and suspicion rose from them in a palpable stream, and she tensed in anticipation of violence.

"Nice one, Jade." Luis said in an overly exuberant manner, clapping her on the shoulder. "It's a good thing you have that flight power!"

He held up his Wayfarer mark and Jade quickly did the same. They were joined a moment later by Fenrin and Tara, whose nonhuman appearance startled the other men. Outside, the sounds of battle raged on unabated. The tension was broken when another man, apparently the group's commander, shouted for them to regroup. He quickly conferred with Luis, then left two men to care for the wounded and set off back towards the distant conflict. While that was happening, Tara surprised Jade by ensnaring her in a tight embrace.

"Jade! You're okay!"

"Thanks to Luis…" she groaned. "And Siora. Those potions of hers…"

She trailed off, eyes going wide as she shook off the last of her disorientation."Naomi!"

Jade knelt next to her friend and knew she was dying.

Siora had moved Naomi from the open courtyard into a sheltered alley between two buildings, well removed from the ongoing battle. Jade paid the distant conflict no heed. Her mind felt frozen, unable to process what was happening right in front of her. Naomi had a fist-sized hole in her torso, right where the wraith demon had torn through her. An attack that had clearly been meant for Jade. She held her friend's hand, blinking back tears. She could feel her pain, tasting it as it flowed freely from her mind like the blood dripping from her failing body.

"Why…?" Jade asked, looking down at her friend. "Why now?"

She didn't answer. Naomi's eyes remained shut, her breathing fast and shallow. Only Siora's constant stream of healing magic was keeping her alive, but even her unconcious spasms were growing weaker. Finding the two hadn't been difficult, the bloody trail leading to their refuge literally painted a sanguine carpet leading straight to them. Fenrin and Tara were a short distance away, keeping watch for them. The demon assassin had vanished as quickly as she'd arrived, either dead from her grievous wounds or fleeing to tend to them. Luis knelt opposite her, beside Siora. The elf's reserves were nearly spent. Jade could see the sweat beading on her forehead as she continued to channel her spell. She glanced up, and then averted her gaze."I'm sorry, Jade…" Siora said, her normally cheerful voice stricken with grief and self-recrimination.  "My healing spells aren't strong enough to… to fix a wound like this."

Luis started to say something, but Naomi let out a groan, stirring weakly between them. Jade waited, half-expecting something miraculous to happen. But no. If she wanted a miracle, she had to provide one herself.

And by some cruel twist of fate, she had the means.

Slowly, Jade withdrew the elixir Siora had poured so much of her time into making. The elf's eyes widened, one hand covering her mouth. The stream of life energy flowing from her ebbed, the limit of her power reached. Luis said something to her, but Jade didn't hear him. Everything around her faded until the world was simply her, the vial, and her dying friend.

Not just dying. Dying because she’d taken a blow meant for her.

This wasn't fair. It wasn't FUCKING. FAIR. Jade felt like screaming at the sheer injustice of it all. A hiss escaped her lips, and she clenched her fists so hard, her nails gouged bloody furrows into her palms. Her brother was staying right here, housed with Luis's family. She was minutes away from delivering unto him a panacea that could restore the life he had lost. No more hospitals, no more isolation, no more nights spent alone and fearful of a future he might not live to see. But no, she hadn't been good enough. If she'd noticed the assassin sooner, if she'd reacted in time, if they'd just been a little faster in returning. If, if, if.

Intellectually, Jade knew it wasn't really her fault. None of them had expected demons lying in wait to ambush them the moment they entered the L-ReF compound, supposedly one of the most secure sites on the planet. This was the one place they were supposed to be able to let their guard down. Even then, they'd been alert. Thinking that was one thing, but feeling it was something else entirely. The elixir in Jade's hand could almost certainly heal Naomi, but doing so could condemn her brother. Maybe she could find a way to track down, buy, or steal enough money and legendary ingredients to have a similar potion created. Maybe she could find and convince an A-rank healer to travel to her world to treat him. Jade doubted it would be possible anytime soon, not with the impending invasion looming over everyone. Who knew what condition her brother would be in by the time she figured something else out, or if the new solution would have as potent an effect? It had taken incredible luck, not to mention immense generosity from multiple friends, to acquire this potion, and to count on repeating the feat in a timely manner would be a foolish dream.

So, she had to choose. The friend who saved her, the one who had grown so much over these past months, blossoming from a scared young woman to a stalwart companion who would throw herself onto an assassin's blade to protect her life. Or her little brother, the boy who had taken the shattered pieces of her broken heart and carefully pieced them back together when all her hopes and dreams had come crashing down. Jade's heart ached with a piercing throb that went soul deep, a pain more agonizing than that of any wound of the flesh. That this choice wasn't really a choice at all only made it harder. Willing her trembling hands to still, Jade carefully unstoppered the vial.

"She's fading." Siora whispered, her own eyes welling up with tears. "I'm… I…"

"It's okay."

Jade's voice was strained, but she managed to give the elf a genuine, if sad, smile.

"You were incredible."

She meant it. Jade wouldn't have thought a wound this severe was survivable for half a minute, much less for however long it had taken them to return. She couldn't afford to waste any more time. In the end, there was really only one way forward. Jade knew her brother all too well, and he would never willingly allow someone else to die so that he could live. Especially not a hero like the woman lying before her. He was a kind and generous soul, which only made the life of pain and broken dreams he lived all the more heart wrenching.

Slowly, gently, Jade tipped the vial of liquid radiance into Naomi's mouth while a silent Luis held her steady, his head bowed. The final drops trickled out, mingling with the first of Jade's falling tears.
