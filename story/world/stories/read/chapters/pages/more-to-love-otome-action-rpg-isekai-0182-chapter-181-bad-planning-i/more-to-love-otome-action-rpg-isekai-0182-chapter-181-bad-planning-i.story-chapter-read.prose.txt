Levi’s warning about not flaunting her mana abilities, along with the near miss she had with Ernie—which admittedly ended up being innocuous in the end, but could easily have swerved in another direction—lingered in her mind.

Susie—as her neat name tag on her vest labeled her as—looked at them with a slightly patronizing air, with a look that Aida recognized as someone resigning themselves to being bogged down by customers who thought themselves clever and proceeded to find every possible loophole to justify receiving preferential treatment.

“Yeah, I guess I didn’t think that far ahead,” she said airily. “I was just panicking since we don’t have a way to get there…”

At least this request is more tangible and actionable than the other one.
