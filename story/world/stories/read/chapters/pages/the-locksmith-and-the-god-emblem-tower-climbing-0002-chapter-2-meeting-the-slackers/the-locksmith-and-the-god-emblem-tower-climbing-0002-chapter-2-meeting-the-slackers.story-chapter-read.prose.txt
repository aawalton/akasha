Should I just rip the door off already? Harry thought to himself as he seriously considered his entry. No, it has to be subtle, but not so subtle that it can't be noticed...

Anxiously, Harry took several deep breaths and observed the terrain around the Logos Tower. In the sky, birds that had reached the edge of the dome appeared on the opposite side, repeating the direction, unaware that they were flying in circles.

Furthermore, when he tried to create a door, it simply wouldn't work. Like an anti-Token barrier, the powers emanating from the Door Token wouldn't be of use, at least not outside of the tower. Let's hope this all blows over once I'm inside...

He straightened his tie and academic robes again. Reaching the door, with a determined look, he opened the door to the first floor of the Logos Tower.

Entering it, he stopped himself from taking another step as a huge pit swallowed the ground; an unending darkness. The place was barren. What the... Harry then felt a slight change in pressure inside his body. Instinctively, he tried to create a door, which, to his relief, worked. Creating another one near the pit, he made a passage and saw it worked.

On his left, also stood a stone set of stairs that circled upwards to the floor. Like an ant colony, pockets of corridors extended into the floor. Cautiously, Harry climbed the stairs as he then heard screams from one of the corridors. Not yet daring to explore the pit of darkness he peeked at one of the corridors.

"Help me!" A man yelled far back into the darkness as he seemed to notice Harry's presence. "Someone, please! It doesn't stop, it never stops! I don't know how many cycles have been since..."

Harry, fearing it may be a trap, simply took a lock from his satchel and threw into the darkness. He continued his climb as the screams became more common. What looked like beggars could also be seen peeking from upwards.

What are common people doing in this God forsaken place? As Harry began thinking about resorting to his door for faster movement, a masked man suddenly appeared in front of him. Without wasting a second, he grabbed a lock and readied a knife, frowning at the man.

"Interesting." The man wore a bird like mask and a hood. "So it's you."

Confused, Harry did not back down. "I am myself. You're correct. Now who the fuck are you?"

The Bird Man turned his head slightly to the left as it seemed to focus on his leather satchel. Harry then promptly placed it behind him and asked again. "What do you want?"

"The same as you." He answered, his voice calm and deep. "Freedom."

"...I don't think you and I know each other that well, buddy." Harry narrowed his eyes. "Now listen. Do you know a way out of this place? I need to climb—"

In a swift movement, Harry then created a smaller door in front of him, blocking a projectile. The object in question was a small pebble. It clashed with the sturdy but small door as it fell onto the ground. The Bird Man then twisted his head once again.

"...Nice try?" Harry said, his hands clutching hard. "Now who are you? Answer me."

"I'm the oldest of the prisoners, and with luck, you'll be the last." The masked man replied.

He then snapped his fingers and fell onto the void. Staring deeply into the man as he fell, Harry was left utterly speechless. Who was the man and what was he talking about? The speed at which he threw the pebble could only be the result of a Token. Why did he jump? Harry continued looking onto the abyss, unsure how and what to feel. What was that apology for?

Remaining on the stairs, before he could think more about the subject, he felt a pull from behind him. With his knife ready, he quickly looked back and put the knife on the individual's throat. The person in question was a woman, her blue eyes giving contrast to her brown and curly hair. Not backing down, he stared at her, frowning.

"Hey!" She pushed him back. "Calm down. I'm just saving your ass from..."

The woman narrowed her eyes as she saw something in Harry's attire. As if in awe, the woman looked to her right, where the stairs and the abyss were.

"...What are you—" And before Harry could finish, she once again interrupted his thoughts.

"Get down!" She yelled and promptly fell onto her feet. Harry, instinctively, did the same.

A moment later, a rumbling came from behind them, coming directly from the abyss. It clashed with a bunch of stairs as it made a growling sound. Cracks and bents could be heard as the stones broke into hundreds of pieces. As Harry took a peek, he saw only a red texture as it seemed to devour everything on its path. A force of destruction.

It went away as quickly as it came. Harry then took a deep breath and got up.

"What the hell is happening!?" He pointed to the girl, who now had a grin on her face. "Who the hell are you; why did that guy apologize and what the fuck just passed in there?"

"Okay, Harry. So—"

"How do you know my name?" He asked, taking a step back.

"...You said it earlier." She said, sounding calm. The woman then added. "Also, it's written in your, um... diary."

Not bothering to check if she was telling the truth, he took another step back and pointed a knife at her.

"Just..." Harry asked once again. "How do I get out of here. I need to get to the next floor."

"Oh, don't we all?" She said, getting up. "Look, I know you're confused, I can see that. If you put down the knife, maybe we can chat. We know a way out of here."

We?

"And how am I supposed to trust you?" He said, slightly lowering his weapon.

"Really, guy?" She put a hand on her waist and gave him a look. "You're the one who's pointing the knife at me."

"Didn't know the virtue of prudence was frowned upon..." Harry then sighed deeply and stored his knife in his holster. "Fuck. Okay, but you're going in front of me. What's your name?"

"Call me Prism... Harry."

"So..." Harry asked, unsure where to start. "What was that? The thing that almost ate us, I mean."

"Oh, Billy." Prism replied simply, not looking back as they continued on the now broken stairs.

"Billy?" Harry asked once again. "Who's Billy?"

"Billy." Prism answered.

"God, could you maybe give answers longer than two syllables?" Harry said, stomping his feet, indicating he was no longer following her. "What are we, twelve?"

"He's a Billy, okay?" She turned around, frowning. "I don't know what he does, how he eats or his natural habitat. He's a thing that comes up every couple hours and catches anything in his path. Now could we please?"

"Wow..." He then followed Prism, who grunted as they kept walking. "Didn't realize it was such a touchy subject. Alright, I won't ask again. Billy it is."

A few minutes passed as they climbed in silence, the occasional screams that came from the corridors being the only audible thing in the entire floor. Would it always be like this? Harry was now remembering the chief's words before he got in. You'll need all the help you can get.

"Who's we?" Harry asked. In response, Prism just looked at him, confused. "You said we know a way out. So who's we?"

Sighing deeply, she paused and turned to him.

"In case you haven't realized," She began, her skin almost steaming. "We're being watched. Every second, each information you give here can and will be used against us in some capacity. Even from upside, you can hear the fuckers already creeping in with their bald heads and smelly breaths. Commoners or not, I don't give a shit. We need to keep going until we reach them. Can't believe its still twelve sets of stairs yet..."

Looming downward, a great darkness still echoed the sounds of loneliness as parts of the stairs often fell into it; a void. Harry then looked up and calculated where the place would be based on Prism's complaining. It wasn't totally accurate, but it was enough.

In a swift movement, he plucked her from the stone stairs and held her close. Together, they plunged into the abyss as Prism's screams were muffled by the penumbra. In another moment, however, they appeared flying upwards into the vertical tunnel. Before they could plunge again, Harry created another door and used it as a platform for him and Prism to stay.

Still hugged to him, she had her eyes widened, while Harry simply straightened his tie and grinned at her.

"What?" He said, calmly. "I could feel your exhaustion. And if we're being honest, I'm not much of a walker myself. I much prefer the high-risk high-reward type of movement, you know?"

Harry said that last part whilst he looked down. Long way down, huh?

"...I can see that." Prism opened her mouth as if to say something else, but restrained herself. Harry could note that a grin was forming on her face, but before it became obvious, she turned around.

Stepping out of the door and into one of the corridors, the door then disappeared. Harry followed her into a corridor as she then paused and gently put her hand on his face, raising his chin. "Don't ever do that."

He narrowed his eyes and stared at her, already gripping his dagger. "Or else?"

"Or else you'll know why they call him Billy." And just like that, she turned around and continued onto the corridor.

"...That doesn't make any sense." Dumbfounded, Harry sighed and followed her. Am I a magnet for crazy fucking people?

"Okay then." Prism said near one of the doors. "Don't make me regret this."

Faking a smile, she turned the doorknob and entered the room. The interior, opposed to the cynical architecture of the rest of the floor, was... cozy. Rustic, the place was well-lit with an unusual number of candles everywhere. Several books lay on the floor, some half-open and others cut up. Among pillows and armchairs, a large rounded table stood in the center where a man with dark skin lay. He played with a long candelabra; his fingers even seemed to be fanning the flames of the candles, but perhaps it was only Harry's imagination. In his shoulder, there seemed to be a holster carrying candles of various sizes, as if it were his ammunition.

To the man's right, in one of the chairs, sat an older woman in dense, detailed clothing. Completely absorbed in a book, she didn't even notice Prism opening the door and only realized something when the man who had entered became present.

They look like a group of beggars... Harry thought, forcing a smile. But hey, when I first met Devon, I also thought he was a tramp. He dressed like one, though, so it wasn't totally my fault...

"Hey guys. This is Harry!" Prism announced Harry's title with a praise he never had, "And he's joining the team!"

"According to who?" Harry said, but barely anyone heard it.

With the candelabrum still in his hand, the man got up as his gaze seized Harry. Only a few moments took for him to drop the scrutinizing look and smile at the man.

"Every friend of Prism is a friend of Vela!" He then made a gossip gesture as he glanced at the candles. "Just don't tell the other ones. They get jealous."

What other ones? Is he talking about the candles? Harry already knew they'd be great friends; he just couldn't tell the others, whoever they were.

On the man's left side, the old lady wasn't so welcoming. Her frowning grew deeper as she widened her eyes and gave a dry laugh.

"Really?" Her stern voice soon shattered the woman's expectation, "Another one? Another stray you took from downstairs that so suddenly was filled to the brim with courage and wanted to help? We've talked about this, Prism."

Before the woman could answer, Harry stepped forward and answered for her.

"I'm a stray?" He said with a smile while fixing his tie, "Have you ever looked at your outfit, ma'am? Are you disguising yourself as a monk in the capital?"

If she was shocked, she didn't show it. Vela, on the other hand, escaped a laugh. The woman then remained quiet for a few moments before opening her mouth, "I bet you don't even have an Emblem, do you? You're a newcomer. That means you messed up out there, which in turn means it is in your nature not to be trusted. Now get out of here and pretend you never saw us. Do you understand—"

A door suddenly appeared in front of her and opened on the same instant, hitting the woman. Different from the previous ones, this one was more sturdy and denser, and as such, she fell from her chair.

Prism weirdly suppressed a laugh as she suddenly shifted to a concerned look. Rushing to the woman's side, she jumped across the table, "Gods... Brun, are you okay?"

"Yeah, yeah..." Brun replied with a bloody nose and a smile, "So you do have some fire inside you, hum, Locksmith?"

Shit, did I hit her too hard? The poor woman isn't even making sense... Harry went after Prism while he scratched his head, his face showing concern.

"Fire means good, Mr. Locksmith." The candle man said to him, his smile bringing some comfort to Harry, "It is the only good there is."

"Forgive me, ma'am, but—"

"No, no, don't apologize." With the help of Prism, she got up and said in a warm tone, "It is a bad habit of mine, testing people. I'm happy you took pride in your image, though, it means you actually stand up for things."

"Oh, right..." Harry mumbled a few words.

"This door thing you did, is this your... Emblem?" The lady asked as she got closer to him.

"Yeah, it is." He answered truthfully; no reason to lie if Prism had already seen him in action.

"Flexible and strong..." She seized him up, making him feel uneasy, "Good enough for me."

"...What are we talking about, exactly?" Harry took a step back while confusion painted his face.

"Saving my dad." The old lady chuckled, "What else could it be?"
