In a way, this dungeon tested the group’s cooperation. It was a psychological attack. If anyone slipped up and let more than ten people die, everything they had worked for would be for nothing. The thought of abusing such a system crossed James’ mind, and he queried the system—but as always, it went silent.

“System, what would happen if I intentionally failed the quest?” James asked. So far, he had survived six waves quite easily. That meant he had earned 3,000 coins in this dungeon—a huge sum even for James, and even more so for others. That amount of coins could boost their weapon skills or magic by two or three levels, directly propelling them to mid-tier survivors.
