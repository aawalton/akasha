Gun Gal

Who Needs Plot Armor When You've Got an OP Girlfriend?

Adam Colt and Mei Mori are just your average college couple. Well… Adam is average. Mei? Not so much. She’s a Japanese exchange student obsessed with all things cute, fashionable—and fully automatic. When they're not cramming for soul-crushing finals, they’re either gaming at home or dominating local airsoft matches.

Their day started like any other. Mei carried Adam through yet another game of airsoft—he heroically leapt into enemy fire to save her… and got shot. She mopped the floor with the enemy team, like always. He’s the support; she’s the carry. Balance, right?

Then the world ended.

Welcome, Players. Tutorial initializing. Objective: Eliminate all hostiles. Survive.

Enjoy the Great Cosmic Games!

Adam and Mei were suddenly thrown into a deadly “tutorial zone” filled with deadly mobs that want to rip them apart.

Mei pulled an overpowered Gunslinger class. Adam… accidentally picked Support. Again.

But with Mei’s lethal aim and Adam’s clutch buffs and heals, they might just survive the tutorial—and figure out what the hell happened to Earth.
