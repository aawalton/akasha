Tez’s empty hand slashed diagonally toward Shuren. The blood that exploded from the tip of his thumb to the rest of his right hand was the only thing that came out.

He was willing to debilitate his right hand already, just to land a single hit on Shuren.

Shuren heard sharp whistles cutting through the air, rapidly approaching him. The sharp whistles almost sounded like the cry of an Artec Bird, known for their shill cries in the Kallas Jungle. It was far from the sound a sword slash should make.

Even with his Qi enhanced eyesight, nothing could be seen in his view. But the grim lines had formed, cutting through the bedding of the Dueling Colosseum, like a ravenous, seven clawed monster ravaging the floor.

What is the aspect of that sword? Fire invisible to the eyes, or is it a Spatial artifact? I don’t have time to dodge, I’ll have to block!

The slices of lines intensified and wrapped towards Shuren, daring to cut him into pieces. He held out his Ruyi Staff in a cross stance, and focused himself on defending his vitals. With the confidence of his Iron Talisbody, and the efficient defensive guard and manipulation of his Qi aura to protect himself, Shuren bore the brunt of the attack.

Small and shallow cuts formed on his arms.

Minor wounds, that amounted to nothing.

But the worst had yet to come.

The eerie sensation he had felt before, like a hole had been pricked in his meridians, had expanded. Soon he felt like he went from being submerged in the waters of Qi to being in a heavy thunderstorm instead. In comparison, his shallow cuts were the least of his problems.

His Qi circulation and Spirit Meridians fell into utter discordance.

Even the overseer of the duel, Elder Wanshang, held a strange gaze as he focused on the weapon.

By instinct, Shuren almost took out a spiritual medicinal pill. Healing items weren’t allowed in duels, only damaging offensive reagents if that was your specialty.

That weapon’s siphoning away my Qi to this extent?! Mad runt, is me sealing my cultivation to 2 stages below you not enough?!

As Tez fell, he stabbed his invisible weapon into the ground to balance himself, bearing the repercussions of using his strongest technique.

Shuren glanced at his Ruyi Staff, embedded in the ground. While his robes were barely discoloured from his own blood, Tez was also in a sorry spot. He was almost staggering, holding unto nothingness. His entire right hand had hung down, his thumb’s skin ripped till only muscle could be seen.

Right now, Tez was in the disadvantage with the most wounds and exhaustion, but Shuren had to account for the strange effects of his weapon.

With a step, Shuren dashed forth. The distance between him and Tez sharply decreased, but right before Shuren reached him, he stabbed his Ruyi Staff deep into the ground. Grabbing the top half with both hands, he pushed it down like a lever.

In a single breath, the entire battlefield changed.

Chunks of debris, dirt and dust exploded out from the ground, and Tez’s vision had been obscured. Before he had the chance to back off, one of the larger debris behind him had a large shadow resurface.

Tez held his sword in both hands, looking around to prepare.Shit! He’s going to-

With a large ringing clang, Shuren’s staff clashed with Tez’s blade, now visible with the bits of dirt and dust covering it. Tez clicked his tongue for falling under Shuren’s scheme, and his right arm tried reaching for his sheathe.

Shuren slammed his left leg into Tez’s injured arm, pushing him back only a few steps. Shuren continued the offence, continuing a rush of over a dozen clashes with Tez’s blade.

All for an opening.

Shuren’s eyes continued to observe silently, calculating the trajectory and style of Tez.

I’ll have to disarm him. That sword is a ridiculous artifact.

Shuren strode forth towards the sword with a smirk and began weaving and swaying through each slash. Not a single hit got anywhere near close to hitting him.

Tez grimaced at the realisation. His offence with the sword was far too weak, or was it that Shuren’s defence was far too strong?

Shuren grabbed the wrist of Tez’s sword arm with ease, locking him on the spot.

“I’m going to break it,” Shuren said with nonchalance.

With a crack, the sword finally fell out of Tez’s arm, and he groaned at the pain. Shuren flicked it across the battlefield with the swing of his staff. Now with both arms weakened, Tez was like trash weed to be uprooted.

I won’t aim for the head for an easy victory. First, I’ll break both your legs. It’s over.

But when Shuren noticed Tez’s feet digging deep into the ground, he remembered that same technique he used from a year. His Iron Boar Tackle.

Shuren snorted. What an obviously easy move to dodge.

But before he knew it, Shuren had already lost his balance. Tez’s entire body turned into a blur, transitioning to a low angled grapple.

Catching Shuren completely out of guard.

Shuren lost his balance as Tez dropped him to the ground, the dust of the ground covering his robes. Shuren clenched his jaws with irritation, but dropped his staff to the side to free his hand.

“Just evening the odds. I’ve been beaten enough times with a stick, you blond rag,” Tez taunted as he engaged in a mount, gaining the upper hand.

“You’re not used to playing dirty like this eh, young master?”

Pinned beneath Tez’s weight, Shuren felt the pressure of the mount bearing on him. But Shuren’s expression was relaxed, and he sneered at Tez’s words.

“You think I don’t know something as basic as grappling?”

With a deep breath, he channeled Qi towards his core, stabilising himself. Shuren trapped Tez’s injured sword arm against his chest, and at the same time, he hooked his right foot around Tez’s ankles to anchor it firmly.

In a single burst of explosive motion, Shuren bridged his hips upwards, using the strength of his legs and momentum of his body to disrupt Tez’s balance. Lifting him off of himself, Shuren rolled to his side, still retaining the control and leverage on Tez’s arm and leg.

The world spun around, and Shuren escaped the mount, wasting no time to deal with Tez. He raised both his fists enclosed together, slamming into Tez’s nose, until a burst of blood spurt out of it.

“That bastard! Why the hell does a high born guy like him know how to play dirty?! Tez, use your Turtle Shell technique!” Fatty Chen yelled out when he saw Tez’s only chance of up heaving the situation disappear. He could only hope while watching with wide eyes, taking it all in.

Some of the spectators, especially the Inner Disciples that came from a peaceful, sophisticated life, didn’t expect the awesome clash of weapons to turn into rolling around in the dirt. In fact, some of the girls gasped when they saw grappling for the first time.

It wasn’t until Junyu, with a fan in his hand, commented that they slowly began to understand.

“Why is it so surprising? If we end up in a fight amongst our enemies, we need to use everything we have to kill them. Otherwise, you’ll be the one to die. Although, the thought of rolling around in dirt is… eurgh,” Junyu said, one of the first members who joined with Shuren’s group. The ice radiating fan did a fantastic job swaying away in the heat, but he took out a small perfume bottle to spray on himself.

The fragrance of honey and cinnamon spread amongst the spectators in his vicinity.

“I suppose it makes sense. Only until we become cultivators able to manipulate the Paths to our whims would battles not end up looking like this.” Some of the others agreed with the sentiment, watching the fight with a new light.

Of course, to the Outer Disciples and those who were raised in the streets of Cloud City, they weren’t surprised much. Only at the fact that someone like Shuren was capable of such battle tactics himself.

Tez felt a sharp, intense pain that radiated throughout his face, and his eyes began to water. He gritted his teeth, ignoring the impending dizziness by taking both arms above his face and torso, forming a simple guard.

Shit! I can’t move if I use Turtle Shell Skill! I’ll need to find an opening for the moment!

And as Shuren continued to batter Tez with multiple punches, he felt the bulwark defence of Tez barely get a dent. As if he was perfectly optimising his Qi to protect his body at the moment of impact.

Even when he aimed for the fractured wrist and bruised arm, Shuren couldn’t deal as much damage. So he decided to break the grapple stance. He used Tez’s stomach as a stepping stone to leap out from the mount.

It then dawned on Shuren, the difference between multiple stages in a fight.

His Iron Talisbody, martial prowess and battle instinct may have been far higher, but the bursts of speed and defence one could gain the stronger they were as a cultivator was not to be ignored, bridging the gap as well.

But he refused to let up, kicking Tez in the neck as he tried to stand up, this time making him stagger back. Tez barely rolled away, keeping his right hand on his chest.

His defences dropped when he moved. It must be some sort of technique he has from staying still.

Shuren staggered.

His Qi fell in discordance as his Spirit Meridians slowly began to be tightened. It was like he went from swimming in the ocean to suddenly being surrounded by a dessert.

I’m already being efficient with my Qi. Don’t tell me his sword has another effect?

Ignoring the ugly sensation inside, Shuren kept his balance. He heard Tez mumble a few strange words, but paid no heed, aiming to punch at his liver.

Tez barely stood straight, almost inviting Shuren to land his blow, and Shuren hesitated to continue, for an instance, but he pulled his left fist back to for as much strength as possible.

He slammed his fist right into Tez’s body.

Tez once more barely flinched, taking in all the pain, and swallowed the urge to gasp for air. Even though the urge to vomit spit and saliva almost took over.

“Checkmate,“ Tez said with glee, though Shuren didn’t understand the word.

And as he firmly placed the palm firmly on Shuren’s abdomen, he said something Shuren could never expect.

“Wind Burst.”
