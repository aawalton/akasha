📜 Synopsis:

Three writers. One world. No way out.

When three friends find themselves in the grimdark fantasy world of their novel, they must grind, battle, and barter to survive the deadly landscape. Redacle is the cold, unforgiving setting for Chronicles of Destiny, Bernard, Kehinde, and Cádo's successful grimdark novel. But when the coauthors find themselves its three newest adventurers—trapped in the perilous landscape of their own creation—they realize not all is as they intended.

With their story gone rogue and filling in plot holes they never imagined, the trio must learn how to level up, allocate skill points, and wield their class abilities to survive a world of deadly foes.

Soon they’re raiding troll lairs alongside witchfinders, fighting gangs with makeshift explosives, and working as mercenaries for gold. But as they journey deeper into Redacle, they must decide: save themselves… or the countless others suffering in their world?
