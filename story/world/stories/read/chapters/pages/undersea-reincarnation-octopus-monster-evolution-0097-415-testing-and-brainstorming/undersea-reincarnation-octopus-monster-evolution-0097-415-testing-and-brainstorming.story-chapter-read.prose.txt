With the dead green and blue feathered serpent coiled around them, creating a large space they used as a camp, Henry activated another instance of Blink-Find The Path and this time, he targeted the leviathan tooth. It was one of his own items, one he’d used in multiple fights. It wasn’t as connected to him as his severed arm had been in the last test, but it would do.

The skill activated, gobbling up a pretty decent chunk of mana and once more, Henry was shunted through the tunnel of dark and purple… stuff. There was no fizzling out, and no disorienting movement that left him half-buried in the soil. Instead, he exited the tunnel inches above the tooth, on top of the raised plateau he’d left it on. And far up in the sky, his Avatar watched the scene.

Both this clone and the tooth had been nearly fifty miles away from their improvised camp. Henry had made sure he wasn’t using the avatar’s switching ability by accident, somehow. This was a pure, clean teleport.

Maurice’s theories had been mostly right, and any other time, the confirmation would have been a joyous occasion for them. But instead, Henry just picked up his tooth, then switched places with one of the Avatars still hovering around the serpent.

“Works just fine,” he simply said, glancing toward Maurice who was mid-bite, face painted red from the blood of the serpent as he tried—and failed—to tear a piece out. Maurice raised a thumbs up, then grumbled something. A moment later, a small bubble detonated against the serpent’s ribs, and Maurice caught all of the torn flesh with a mix of Telekinesis and his own Domain.

While the serpent was much less resilient now that it was dead, it had still kept some of its toughness, and as Henry eyed the beast for a moment, he had to admit. The discoveries about the teleportation’s limitation were disappointing. But they were leveling, and they’d also just gotten a real, honest-to-God flight skill. This A-rank had been able to use it impressively, twisting and dashing through the air with its massive bulk and now it was theirs.

Henry had already taken it out for test-drive, and the difference when compared to Telekinesis was day and night. It was like he had gotten used to walking on rickety stilts for a while, and he could finally use his own feet. No Nexus manipulation was needed, and it was much cheaper than Telekinesis. Even as a C-rank skill, it outclassed the latter, in both speed and maneuverability.

Frankly, Henry could gush about the new skill for a while still, but they were working right now, so he turned his attention back to the crab. With plenty of meat in his grasp to chew on, Maurice responded telepathically as he sat back down and continued eating. Teleporting around was tiring, they had quickly discovered, but not as tiring as A-rank fights.

“[I’ll note that down,]” Maurice said, summoning a little notebook out of storage and using Telekinesis to control the pen. “[So… here’s a summary. We can teleport to the target of a Find the Path, whether it’s an inanimate object or a person, so long as we have a visual on it. Just knowing the direction isn’t enough. And just being familiar with a person or a spot isn’t enough. So… it looks like we’re still missing something. Maybe it’s easier for the turtles because of their nature? Maybe their perception skill is really beyond what we’re imagining. Or… maybe it is the same skill that we’ve been testing, but theirs is so advanced that it now has fewer requirements? That, or they have a way of seeing into places they haven’t gotten to yet. It’s hard to tell at this point.]”

Henry hummed, a little bit annoyed. They needed to speak to the turtle that was still around. Because right now, they couldn’t really go forward with the fusion. Without a way to perceive their destination, the skill was broken. Missing something. What was the point of a teleporting skill that always required him to have a clone nearby to see the destination? And sure, maybe it could eventually evolve. But that was not guaranteed. If it didn’t, he’d be left with a half-assed skill he’d have to disassemble because so far, he didn’t have much of a baseline to build from.

Henry sat with that thought for a second, brows furrowed, while Maurice continued to chew on the bloodied rib of the serpent, though Henry could see the crab’s unblinking gaze. They were both turning the problem around in their minds. Did they really need the turtle? He still didn’t want to try tracking it when it could run. So what were the alternatives? What was it that the Rainbow turtles were using to make their teleporting-jumps through worlds?

It must be some sort of divination, he thought. While Find the Path gave the direction toward something, that same link could likely be exploited visually. Or audibly. Maybe he could whip up something through Unfettered Echoes and Find the Path, but how was he to integrate the concept of Sight? Mana Sight? That skill felt a bit too remote from what he wanted, but it could work.

“Sight… Perception… Seeing things remotely. Clairvoyance? Was that a skill? What kind of creature would have that? Something that didn’t want people to come close to it, maybe. Or some sort of haunting, tracking predator?”

Henry was muttering at this point, but Maurice was paying attention.

“Maybe Arisia would have something like that. Or close to that?” suggested Maurice between bites.

Henry pressed his lips together into a line. In his frantic flight to find Maurice, he’d sort of swung a sledgehammer through his network, and the furthest bits of it had taken the brunt of the damage, namely the briefly reconnected Thalis and First Landing. He’d had to cannibalize his network to spread his Avatars closer. Since then, he’d started spreading out his clones again, but with everything that happened, he wanted a stronger network here. It did help that both the rescue and the last couple of A-rank fights had helped him gain yet another level in Unfettered Echoes, but the Reach was massive. Even if he wasn’t keeping them directly by his side, he wanted them available.

“The network’s a bit… out of order right now. But I’ll try to reconnect it. In the meantime, let’s try this angle.”

Henry proceeded to explain what he pictured. With a mix of Unfettered Echoes, Find the Path, Mana Sight, and maybe some of Maurice’s Seer essence, they could try for some sort of scrying fusion. One that, with enough of a connection through Find the Path, would allow them to see through the distance. Mana Sight wasn’t the best candidate for this, but hopefully Unfettered Echoes and the Seer essence would let the skill break through its limitations.

“Got it,” Maurice said, throwing a bit of snake-rib behind him. “I’m ready.”

Henry smiled and nodded. “Good. But it’s gonna have to wait. We’re about to have some company,” he said, turning his gaze in the direction of the approaching black-and-blue feline. It almost looked like a tiger, if its stripes were black and the rest was a pale, translucent blue that seemed to phase in and out of reality. This time, Henry made sure to identify the creature before it made it to them, just so they wouldn’t be forced to run with their tails between their legs, like they had with the level 40 quartz bear.

If they kept this up, they might hit A-rank in a matter of a day or two.
