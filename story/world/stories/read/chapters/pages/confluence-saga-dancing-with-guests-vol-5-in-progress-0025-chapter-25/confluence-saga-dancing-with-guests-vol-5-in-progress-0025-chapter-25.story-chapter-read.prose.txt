Long distance travel for Awakened usually occurs in one of two ways. There's a third option of traveling under your own power, but realistically who wants to spend that kind of time.

First option is via Portal. This requires that you know the Energy Pattern Address for your destination and be allowed to go there. If you aren't authorized you'll be unable to make the trip, or at best you'll be stuck waiting for someone at the other end to let you through. EDN access can help with finding the appropriate address, as can having someone at either end establish the connection for you.

Second option is Hub Transit. This is typically slower and comes with an Energy fee at embarkation. You enter the transit system and follow the pre-defined route then exit at your destination. The greatest benefit of Hub Transit is that it allows for bulk transport of mundane goods that cannot be stored in Vaults and that are too large to be transported through Portals.
