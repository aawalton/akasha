Her blade slipped through the monster’s flesh like water, reducing it to chunks.

Azrael had trekked what felt like thousands of miles, the only thing keeping her going being her undead constitution and inscribed gear.

She'd fought her way through every biome she'd ever heard of, and a few that were completely alien to her as well.

She'd quickly learned that her senses were guiding her towards portals, like the ones Ambrose could make but much more colorful. As she closed in on these holes in reality, her senses would bend around them, and she'd enter almost against her own will.

And then she'd be in another biome.

She'd crossed savannahs, frozen mountain peaks, swamps, plains, and so much more, often stuffed full of monsters that she could hardly describe.

Azrael was certain she'd outpaced Ambrose in level by now, as she was closing in on her tenth, but that just made her wonder how he was doing. She knew time was running out– the deadline he'd been set seemingly a lifetime ago was crawling ever closer. But, had he lost track again? Would he be unprepared for what was coming?

She could do little but pray to the god she'd named herself for and hope that he'd be okay.

As Azrael’s blade sliced through another giant monster in a single cleave– this one a giant cat with scales and wings in the place of its front paws– she felt something shift, and quickly checked her Skills.

Azrael

Absent Wraith 10 (Max, Specialization Blocked)

Specialization blocked? What?

Azrael had never even heard of that before. Had she done something wrong? Or was there some special requirement for reaching her next specialization that she had never heard of?

The undead woman gritted her teeth as the pull echoed through her mind once again, stronger than ever before. Truthfully, it felt like the entire world was pulling her forwards, and the feeling only got stronger the closer to her final destination she grew. The feeling of existential weight grew with every step, and she somehow knew that there was something important here.

The time was coming. She knew there couldn't be much further to go.

The end of the world was calling.
