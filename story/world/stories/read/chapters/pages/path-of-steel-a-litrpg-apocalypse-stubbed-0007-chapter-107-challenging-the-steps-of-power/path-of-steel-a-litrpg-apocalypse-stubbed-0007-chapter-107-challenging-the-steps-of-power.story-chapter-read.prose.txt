Invasion Quest: Clash of Floors

Attention, all Climbers. The de-facto monster leader of the third floor, the Thousand-Face Puppeteer, has secured permission to ascend. It and its army are currently invading the fourth floor’s Gymnasium.

To ensure wide and fair participation in the Invasion Quest, the fourth floor will be time-frozen for one month. In this time, all eligible climbers should finish up their business in other floors and prepare themselves to the best of their ability. All climbers currently on the fourth floor have been teleported back to the Clan Floor.

Once the one-month preparation period is over, the invasion will begin, and you may re-enter the fourth floor through the Stairs of Ascendance or other methods. Once you enter the floor, you may not leave until the event is over. Climbers above Level 50 may not participate in this quest, and they are banned from entering the fourth floor while the invasion is ongoing.

All eligible climbers are encouraged to visit the floor and participate in the invasion. On arrival, you must pick a side to fight for, so make sure to communicate with your allies and clans beforehand. At the end of the quest, all surviving climbers of the winning side will receive bountiful rewards according to their contribution points.

The invasion will end once either floor boss is taken down. Prepare yourselves and join. The Tower favors the bold.

On behalf of the Eightfall Emperor and the Eternal Empire,May you ever climb.

***

The birds chirped. The wind whistled, errant gales rippling the grass, while small animals jumped around in blissful innocence. Chatting echoed in the wind; the spears of cultivators clanked, either in sparring or training. Someone laughed. The air was electric. Alive.

Gary Steel walked across the mountain paths of the Heaven Reaching clan, deep in thought. He’d grown so much stronger in the past week.

Just before the expedition, he’d combined his Ruin and Lightning vibrations into the more advanced Black Lightning. He’d also gotten the Golden Bolt—Goldie! Both were high jumps for his battle power. Then, during the expedition, he’d leveled up from 12 to 21 and acquired the Seed of Eternal Flesh; a soul artifact which even the Thousand-Face Puppeteer was crazy about. If Gary’s assumptions were correct, the puppeteer had hatched from the seed, making him a motherstealer.

It had only been a day since then. Gary had rested and sent some messages. Then, to get familiar with all his new powers, he obviously needed to train by himself for a long period of quiet meditation.

He approached the Steps of Power, which were exactly the opposite.

“Hey there, Gary Steel,” a voice greeted him. It belonged to a man of medium stature, wearing a wide, flat straw hat.

“Deacon Song,” Gary said, smiling. “I didn’t expect to find you here.”

“It is my post. I heard you made a splash during the third-floor expedition.”

“You know me. I can’t take a few steps without someone challenging me to a duel.”

They laughed. The crowd around the Steps was thinner than usual, but there were still a dozen people gathered in groups of one to three, lounging on the rocks as they observed braver disciples get the shit kicked out of them.

Gary didn’t know much about Deacon Song, but the deacon had helped him in the past.

“How come you’re here?” the deacon asked. “Most disciples train in seclusion after returning from expeditions.”

“Yeah, but that’s slow. What better way to acclimate than real combat?”

“If I didn’t agree, Gary, I wouldn’t be working at this post. It’s certainly busier, with the tournament coming up.”

“The one in three months?” Gary asked.

“What three months? It’s in two weeks! They moved it up because of the invasion quest!”

“Really?”

“Absolutely. They’re accelerating the whole thing. The tournament will be over before you enter the fourth floor. They want you all as strong as possible for the invasion.”

“I see,” Gary said.

The runes along his spear’s black shaft lit up, from the bottom to the top, until the golden spearhead also thrummed with power. A zap hit the air, coalescing into the form of a little golden snake with black eyes.

“Hi Gary, hi Deacon Song! Does that mean you need to fight Sui Duang sooner?” Goldie said.

“Yes,” Gary said.

“Why would you fight Sui Duang?” Deacon Song asked, sneaking glances at Goldie.

“I challenged him to a—”

“He didn’t say hi to me!” Goldie protested.

“What?” the deacon said.

“Goldie’s right. She greeted you, but you didn’t greet her,” Gary said.

“Um… Hello, Spirit Golden Bolt.”

“See? That’s how you’re supposed to refer to me, Gary!”

“I thought I should call you Miss Bolt.”

“That, too.”

“I challenged Sui Duang to a Duel of Honor. It will be either in the tournament, if we meet it, or right after it,” Gary said.

“Why would you do that?” the deacon asked in horror.

Gary shrugged. “Our differences were too much. We’d have to kill each other eventually, so why not do it in a packed stadium?”

“You don’t understand! Sui Duang is the number three disciple—number two now, after Li Wei’s passing. He’s extremely powerful! Is that why the expedition returned ahead of schedule?”

“That was the monsters. They suddenly went amok—probably because the invasion quest was starting,” Gary said. Of course, he knew that the real reason for the monsters going berserk and the invasion starting was that he’d taken the Seed of Eternal Flesh from the puppeteer, but he couldn’t possibly reveal that. The only people who knew were himself, Lin Xin, Sui Duang, and Feng Shen.

Thankfully, the #1 disciple had no problem staying silent. Everyone had their secrets, he’d said.

“Let’s get started. Please step into the mist,” Deacon Song said.

The Steps of Power were a spiral staircase of nine steps, each half as large as a football field. Disciples entered the array as projections of their real bodies, letting them fight without concern of injury or death.

Gary appeared on the first step, a flat slab of stone surrounded by brown mist. The Golden Bolt remained in his hand, but Goldie was missing. The Steps could replicate the spear itself but not its spirit. Gary sighed. She usually hid inside the spear during battle, anyway.

“Who’s going to chat with me now?” he wondered aloud.

Aura coalesced into a vaguely humanoid, spear-wielding shadow across from him.

Spear Phantom, Level 7

Hey buddy! I’ve been reinstated! I’m not dead!

“Quirk!” Gary exclaimed, then laughed out loud. “You really had me worried, you son of a bitch! What happened?”

Quirk was his System liaison, responsible for a lot of things, including the monster description. Towards the end of the expedition, Quirk had disobeyed orders from his supervisor to help Gary, and the last Gary knew, he was getting executed.

The shadow charged. Gary casually put his spear through its head. Another shadow formed.

Spear Phantom, Level 8

It was my fault, really. I thought I could get away with everything. Good thing I apparently can.

“I’m sorry, Quirk. That happened because of me.”

The shadow perished, leading to a new one.

Spear Phantom, Level 9

Don’t worry about it. It was a clusterfuck of entity and clan politics. Thank God for those pretty fucking stupid decisions I once had to damage control, am I right?

“I have no idea what that means. I’m just glad you’re alright. Can you tell me what that Seed was, or is it still taboo?”

Spear Phantom, Level 10

Whoops, got to go, I think I left my cat in the oven.

Gary laughed. “Why are you talking through the descriptions, anyway? You can just message me in the chat.”

Two shadows formed after he killed that last one.

Spear Phantom, Level 7

Why are you talking aloud? You can also message me.

Spear Phantom, Level 7

Descriptions are my bitches. Well, it’s more accurate to say that your perception is my bitch, bitch.

“That’s mildly upsetting.”

Spear Phantom, Level 8

By the time people care about using the System to play you, you’ll be strong enough to see through it.

Spear Phantom, Level 8

Anyway, I got to go. Keep up the good work, buddy, and don’t forget about that quest. You’re really making me proud!

“I’m not doing that,” Gary muttered helplessly. On earning advanced System access, he’d received a quest to slap any non-friendly Branch Elder in the face. Given that any of them could finger-flick him into oblivion, he wasn’t keen on completing the quest. He had no idea why it even existed.

The two level 8 shadows charged him, but to the current Gary, they were painfully slow. He recalled how fiercely he’d fought them the first time he entered the Steps of Power. Back then, the level 8 shadows had pushed him to his limit, and the level 9 shadows had left him bleeding on the floor. Now…

A casual flick of the spear eliminated both shadows. When the level 9s appeared, they suffered the exact same fate. They were ants before Gary. He briefly pictured himself on Earth, stopping criminals like Batman. Or was he stronger? He was definitely stronger.

If the average pre-Tower person had 3 Strength, and I have 34, does that mean I can bench press a ton?

And that’s my weakest stat.

He read his status screen while casually murdering the level 10 shadows.

Gary Steel #00301, Level 21

Floor: 3

Clan: Heaven Reaching

Strength: 34

Speed: 47

Vitality: 79

Fortitude: 7

Wisdom: 5

Aura Volume: 19

Aura Control: 18

Vibrations: Black Lightning, Seed of Eternal Flesh (U)

“My vitality’s almost at the triple digits. Wow.”

The last wave of the first step consisted of a single level 11 shadow, but that put it at the Birth of Aura realm. It was a massive spike in difficulty from the two level 10s before.

Of course, to the current Gary, it wasn’t a problem. He let its spear graze him. It barely broke the skin. Emboldened, he let it land a solid strike. He bled—that was all. His flesh was dense like stone.

79 Vitality… he thought with a grin.

He killed the shadow in a single strike and got teleported to the next step. “I wonder if I can skip steps the next time…” he thought aloud.

This step was the same as the previous one—empty, flat, half a football field wide—but the mists surrounding it were tinted green rather than brown. The first wave appeared before him.

Spear Phantom, Level 15

It’s a phantom. It has a spear. You know what to do.

Gary started becoming serious. He killed the phantom instantly. Then came a level 18. It didn’t possess a vibration, as far as Gary could tell, but it had the stats, technique, and aura field of a proper Birth of Aura cultivator. This was where he’d stopped last time—he’d blown up his legs to take the shadow with him.

Reflecting the spike in difficulty, he killed the shadow in two strikes rather than one.

I’ve become so much stronger…

The thought hyped him up. He licked his dry lips, falling into a battle stance. He itched to test himself. To find his new limits.

How strong am I?

Anything from here on out was new territory. On the ranking tablet outside, he’d seen that Sui Duang had reached the fourth wave of the third step, while Feng Shen—the number one disciple—had reached the eighth wave of the third step. Gary didn’t know what exactly they contained, or how difficult they were, but he was keen to find out.

Incidentally, while Sui Duang and Feng Shen held the two best records for Birth of Aura disciples, the next people in line were close behind Sui Duang’s fourth wave record. Two disciples had reached the third wave of the third step, while several had reached the second wave. That Feng Shen was so far ahead, at the eighth wave, spoke volumes of his ability.
