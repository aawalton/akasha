The first thing Percy learned was that his domain was now corrosive towards the dark patches. The moment he grabbed his test subjects with his willpower, the sludge started evaporating and shrinking under his magical grip, albeit somewhat slowly.

A few quick experiments revealed that the same was true of his phantom mana. By channelling it through the clone and into a patient’s soul, he could at least prevent the sinister mass from growing larger and might eventually purge it completely, though it would likely take several days for each person.

‘I guess that possessing a near-dead host greatly accelerates the process,’ he concluded, feeling even more confident that his current body had been one of the infected before its death and subsequent resurrection.

The creature’s injured soul had likely left it more susceptible to change. By oozing willpower inside its body and feeding phantom mana directly into its spectral wounds, Percy could likely affect the dark mass several times more easily.

‘Curing them like this would be a piece of cake, don’t you think?’ Micky asked.

‘If I were to possess everyone on the planet, sure,’ Percy admitted with a shrug.

He had no idea how long it would take. If he tried that, his first patients would end up corrupted again by the time he finished cleansing everyone. Even if he somehow managed to cure every single sapient, the wildlife would infect them afterwards. In fact, that was probably why the local deities hadn’t tried to carry the mortals away from the planet.

Demonic infestations couldn’t follow their victims to outer space, nor could they survive without carriers, but the gods couldn’t possibly relocate all the animals at the same time. Maybe they could try moving the people away, exterminating every species they couldn’t preserve, and trying to restore Mapel’s ecosystem to the best of their ability upon returning, though Percy didn’t blame them for opting to request help from other factions before resorting to that.

In any case, Percy didn’t have the time nor willingness to go to such lengths either, nor would he entertain the possibility of giving everyone the Moirais’ Decree when he hadn’t even done that for his own people.

Besides, even if possessing every creature on Mapel was enough to cure them, it wouldn’t necessarily work on those suffering from stronger infestations, and he would rather develop a more universal solution.

‘This is still encouraging though,’ Micky said. ‘Thanks to our innate resistance, we won’t have to worry about our own safety. I bet that our augmented mana and willpower will also make our formations more effective than they used to be.’

Itching to put that to the test, Percy began constructing a purification formation, sticking to the same design he had experimented with on Spiralis B-3. His phantom mana slabs were of a different affinity and lower grade than Kassorith’s metal mana, but he could at least borrow his familiar’s soul-zapping lightning mana to charge them with. Preparing the formation only took a few minutes because Percy was a lot faster and could easily handle more complex unit cells than those he had used in the tournament.

Feeding a trickle of the greyish-purple substance into the runes, he willed them to activate. He started off carefully, because his patients were far weaker than the elephant-sized creatures he had cleansed before, and the spectral component of his mana far deadlier.

The first critter writhed in agony as the red light of the formation enveloped it, forcing Percy to dial the beam down further. The mana didn’t singe the squirrel’s fur, because it acted directly on its soul, but the silver flames within its body shrank and evaporated almost as fast as the dark sludge.

The beast died a minute later.

‘Orange creatures are clearly far more fragile than Greens…’ Micky lamented. ‘Then again, spectral attacks are also much deadlier than corporeal ones, so maybe we should phase a part of the beam into the physical plane to lower the damage it causes.’

Percy nodded, tweaking his mana so that only half of it acted on the next test subject’s soul, while the rest burnt its flesh. That seemed to improve the situation slightly. The beam incinerated the bird’s feathers along with its soul, but the damage on both sides of its existence accumulated more slowly, while the dark mass shrank at roughly the same rate.

This time, the small beast was still alive when the corruption vanished, though it looked like it would have been better off dead.

‘Progress,’ Percy thought with a sigh as he put the poor thing out of its misery.

So far, his formation had only accomplished similar results to the one from the tournament, though he knew that cleansing the tiny critters without killing them was supposed to be much more difficult than it looked. Even this much would have been impossible if not for Ningirima’s Decree and the purification runes inside his Cradles altering the very nature of his mana.

Still, he needed to do better. He couldn’t subject literal children to such a violent treatment.

For the third animal, he decided to take things more slowly. By cutting the intensity of the beam down to a fifth, he was able to greatly lessen the creature’s suffering. The experience wasn’t exactly pleasant, but the bird’s injuries were mild enough that it could probably endure and even recover from them without external healing.

Unfortunately, tuning the formation down had also lowered its effectiveness dramatically. The beam had a much tougher time permeating the animal’s flesh and soul to reach the underlying corrupted mass, causing the procedure to last much longer than it should have.

Percy had cleansed the previous beast in just five minutes, but lowering the intensity by a factor of five had extended the duration by dozens of times. Four hours after starting, the dark mass had been cut down to half of its original size, yet the process slowed down as the surface of the sludge shrank and receded to the depths of the creature’s soul.

After another eight hours, the corruption stopped shrinking entirely, causing Percy to frown. ‘If the beam is too weak, it won’t finish the job regardless of how long we wait. And even though the current iteration is far gentler, I wouldn’t want to subject anyone to such a nasty experience for twelve hours straight.’

For the fourth test subject, Percy used a combination of the weakened beam, his willpower and phantom mana. The last two were much easier on the patient, and might even be enough to eliminate the corruption by themselves, because they could penetrate deep into the creature’s soul without harming it.

However, raw mana and willpower also acted more slowly, because they lacked the aggressive quality of the superheated beam and the additional layers of purification runes within the formation. Percy hoped to accelerate the early phase of the cleansing session with the beam and finish the corruption off with his domain and mana when the formation stopped being effective.

It worked, though it took around a full Remiorian day.

Such a result would have been useless in the tournament, since Percy and Kassorith had been in a race against time to accumulate as many points as possible. Thankfully, this wasn’t a competition, so Percy was just glad that his hybrid approach had left the first of the two lizards – a Red creature no less – in a far better state than the beasts he had grilled to near-death on Spiralis B-3.

‘It’s not exactly a picnic, but it should be within most people’s ability to endure,’ Micky concluded.

‘It’s still far slower than possessing them though. At least on an individual basis,’ Percy argued. ‘There’s also nothing subtle about it. The natives might agree to let us use it on them, but that goes against our original goal of cleansing them in secret.’

Thinking about the problem some more, he figured that he could probably tweak the formation into a large-scale enchantment that affected hundreds of people at the same time. Curing them one by one via possession would still be faster with his current capabilities, but that would hopefully change as the size and potency of his formations rose with his strength and magiscript skills, so that was a more promising area to focus on if he wanted to cleanse entire countries or even planets one day.

If only he could make the process even more effective and less painful…

While he had been busy experimenting on the animals, the sentries spying on the villagers had kept gathering information about the infestation. The sapients’ arguments had seemingly always started for coherent reasons. They typically only insulted one another while bartering or discussing business matters, which probably ruled out the purer and more mindless dominion of Wrath.

Percy still wasn’t sure whether he was dealing with a manifestation of Pride or Avarice, but it should be fairly easy to figure it out using the captured animals. Reaching into his spatial seal, he pulled out a vial of nectar and uncorked it in front of the final beast’s reptilian snout.

The reaction was immediate.

Upon detecting the potent meal, the darkness within the lizard’s soul expanded severalfold and began flinging chunks of corruption in every direction. One shot toward Percy’s host, only to be melted down to nothing by his innate resistance before it could even touch the squirrel’s soul. Another piece nearly struck the reptile that Percy had previously cleansed, though he blocked it with a wall of willpower which he promptly wrapped around the corrupted creature.

Curious to see how the dark mass would respond to the purification beam during its outburst, Percy reactivated the formation and aimed its crimson light directly at the rampaging lizard.

To his delight, the sludge evaporated faster than before, which should allow him to either purify the creature rapidly or reduce the intensity of the beam further. Evidently, triggering the corruption didn’t truly increase its overall volume by much. By stretching itself thin and exposing a larger surface area near the exterior of a creature’s soul, the sludge simply rendered itself more vulnerable to purification.

Percy’s human lips curled up as an idea took root.
