Zach was grinning fit to burst, and maybe it was a trick of the dungeon's lighting, but his eyes seemed brighter than ever. He looked like someone who just had something awesome happen to him, and Thomas could easily guess what it was.

Though, because they were friends, he wasn't going to make it easy on him.

"Yes," Thomas said slowly, "The System had stuff to say to me."

"I knew it!" Zach practically bounced on his feet. "So? What did it tell you?"

Oh yeah, Zach totally had a class, too, at the very least.

Thomas grinned. "Nah, what did it tell you?"

"Dude, I was the one who asked first--" he started, then immediately broke. "I'm a System Warden, bro! Title and a class a level early and everything! At first, I was like, 'Who, me?' But it really believes in me, and Thomas, you're not going to believe this, but the more I think about it, the more I think it's a bonus class."

Thomas was grinning all through his babble, happy and not at all surprised Zach was offered Warden, too. That last bit took him by surprise. "Wait, what? A bonus class?"

"Yeah, it didn't say anything about negating the level 5 class, and the title gives the option to turn off the Warden class from view, but doesn't say the same for the level 5 class. That's a hint." He airily waved it away as if it were nothing. "So? Did you get the scroll, too?"

"Yes, I did," Thomas admitted.

Zach grabbed both of Thomas's shoulders and shook him once in pure delight. "That's so sick! Oh, hey, wait. You totally said yes, right?"

Thomas hadn't seen Zach so enthusiastic about anything since before failing to save Jo and getting shot up with those mana arrows. Then, of course, there had been his past with his family and Antonin. It had been as if he'd been carrying a darkness with him, and now that had lifted. It was good to see the return of his friend.

"Of course I said yes." He grinned.

"Awesome!" Zach let him go. "So, what did you pick? Oh, combat healer, right?"

Thomas thought about leaving him hanging since Zach hadn't yet come out and said his exact class, but what the hell: the enthusiasm was catching.

"I picked one called Prosperity Warden. Basically, I get a lot of aura powers where my allies and I get enhanced vitality, luck, and loot drops... among other bonuses." He wouldn't hear the last of it if he went into the whole livestock fertility thing.

Zach's face lit up. "That's wicked! So basically you're a lucky charm now."

"I'm really not. Well, I mean, there's some luck, but... okay, what'd you pick?"

He counted the choices off on his fingers. "Okay so, they gave me four choices, but there was one clear winner. The first I got was full-on fire mage, but as much as I love fire, I don't want to go full on in that one element when there's so much else out there."

"That's fair," Thomas said, secretly relieved.

"Yeah, also I had Warden of Spatial Research, which... like, I never thought of myself as a scientist type, but just imagine learning about the theories of folding space and all that. It's like a lifetime of work, bro. Really satisfying stuff."

Zach was a lot smarter than he tried to let on, though Thomas could imagine he'd still keep all the 'dudes' and 'hellas' even with a full-on PhD.

Zach went on, the joy fading a little. "But then it gave me, like, a messed up one called Warden Despoiler. It was all about stealing mana straight out of people. Like, enemies and allies, if I really needed it. Didn't matter, it all would empower me." He glanced at Thomas. "That was a super hard pass."

Ah. This was the reason he was going into the long story. He didn't ask it straight out, but Thomas still understood the question.

"Yeah, it gave me a dark one, too. I would have been able to cleanse people of all impurities, and you can imagine that slippery slope."

Zach nodded and looked a little less tense. "So, the last was the one that sold me." He took a breath. "I'm a Warden Monk. The skills are about, you know, staying in tune. Really choice physical fighting bonuses, too."

"Zach," Thomas said seriously, "That is perfect for you."

"I know, right?!" The grin was back. "It's like the System got me."

Thomas knew exactly what he meant. It had been as if the description of Prosperity Warden had chimed with his soul. "You really think we're going to get a class on top of this, though?"

"Yeah, especially with the System favor," Zach said. "We're supposed to be the ones standing between the Light Elves and," he gestured vaguely around, meaning the whole planet, "until System Integration. The two of us are at level four, and Jo's not far behind. Twitch can get there, too. We'll definitely hit five before the Integration date. I'm thinking that will be the real power up--"

He stopped as they both heard the sound of feet on stone. Thomas turned to see Twitch come out of the dungeon's door, mid-stride.

Twitch didn't pause for a second and walked right up to them. "Okay, did either of you have an entire conversation with the System through a stupid scroll?"

"System Warden?" Thomas asked.

Twitch started to nod but then stopped and looked at both of them. "Shit." He grinned. "Both of you too?"

"Both of us," Zach said cheerfully. "Welcome to the club."

"What about Jo?" Twitch asked, looking around.

"She's still in the dungeon," Thomas said. "My guess is she's being careful about her choice. So, what'd you get?"

"Well, the boon. Duh," Twitch said.

He and Zach exchanged a quick, sheepish glance. In the excitement, they had both forgotten about the initial reward.

"So you insta-heal yourself now?" Turning back to Twitch, Thomas gave a theatrical sigh. "I'm going to be out of a job."

"Nah, I still bleed when I stop. You can fix me up then. Besides, it's not like my Gift's a secret," Twitch said pointedly.

He was right, and Thomas felt a pinprick of jealousy about that.

"That was one reason why I picked the free skill," Thomas admitted. "Having loot fall out everywhere anytime someone dies near me is great and all, but it's a little obvious."

Twitch looked shocked. "You're saying you just turned down all that free money?"

Zach laughed. "You have no idea the class he picked. It's all good."

Twitch turned to him. "What about you? The crazy stuff you pulled with your mana—"

He stopped when Zach shook his head. "No, bro. I mean, it was fun and all, but so intense. I upgraded my teleportation skill." His eyes went a little distant. "It feels a lot more efficient than before. Still sucks for short range, but that's what I got Light Speed for. Anyway, Tom, what skill did the System give you? The reward said it would have been a free skill slot for me. Same for you, right?"

"That's right." Since Jo was taking her time, Thomas decided to show instead of tell. He concentrated on his new skill, picturing a large, fist-size A-Grade Wellspring mana crystal.

The skill activated, and the mental image grew razor sharp in his mind. It was as if he were actually seeing it with perfect twenty-twenty vision, including how the light in the room would hit every facet.

Feeling like a showman, he mimed taking something out of his back pocket, and a moment later, it was as if the huge mana crystal had jumped from his mind to the palm of his hand. It sat there looking perfect, from the bright inner glow of the mana type to how the shadows hit his hand as if the crystal was really there. It was effortless and perfect.

"It's a mana skill?" Twitch asked, confused.

Zach cocked his head. "Storage skill? But you don't have any spatial-type mana."

With a flick of his hand, Thomas dispelled the image. "Illusion skill. It runs off my Light mana." That little trick hadn't used much at all. He wasn't sure if it was the skill or the upgraded mana in his core, but it had been easy.

"Oh, wicked," Zach breathed. "You can pull so many tricks with that."

"Yeah, like not getting stabbed again if the real you is on the other side of the room," Twitch added wryly.

"I would very much like not to get stabbed again," Thomas agreed. "Also, I notice you didn't tell us your class."

Twitch flashed a grin. "Alright, you got me. The System gave me a few choices, mostly combat-heavy, which is dumb because I weigh a buck forty soaking wet. Also, there was a really bad choice." He briefly scowled.

"Dark class?" Zach asked, then after Twitch nodded, "Yeah, bro. We both got one too."

"Don't matter: I didn't pick it. Then I saw Evasion Warden. It's kinda crazy: I got a skill that makes everything in the room want to hit me, but the longer I'm in motion, the harder it is for them to get a hit, so it's supposed to be super frustrating for them."

Zach whistled. "So you're a dodge tank."

"Dodge tank?" He looked briefly surprised. "Yeah, that fits."

Thomas chuckled, and it came out warmer than he expected. "You're going to do great with that."

"Yeah?" For a second, Twitch looked almost shy at the praise. Then he shook it off and straightened. "Yeah, well, obviously I'm a natural at frustrating people. So."

Thomas couldn't agree more.

The door to the dungeon opened again, and Jo stepped out.

Immediately, Thomas registered that something about her had changed. Nothing was outwardly different, except for the way she moved. Her slinky stride was that of a predator.

"Hey," Jo said, pushing a piece of hair back behind her ear, and just like that, the moment was gone. "Sorry it took a bit. The System separated me from you guys, and—"

Twitch broke in. "You got the whole System Warden pitch, didn't you?"

She stilled, looked at the three of them, then threw up her hands. "Oh, for crying out loud. You guys, too?"

"You did say yes, right?" Zach asked with faux innocence. As if there was any doubt.

"Of course I said yes." She crossed her arms. "Let me guess: Everyone picked a class, and I was the last."

"We were waiting for you," Thomas said. "What reward did you get?"

"The boon." Her upper lip twitched upward as if she were repressing a smile. She was only trying to look annoyed, and was quickly breaking.

"Yoooo, me too." Twitch held up his hand for a high five, and she finally broke her fake grumpy look and returned it.

"What about your class?" Zach asked.

Jo blew out a breath. "I had a tough time picking, but now you're looking at a brand new Warden Hemlock."

Thomas's mind briefly blanked. He wasn't a big plant guy, but wasn't hemlock poison?

"Like the poison plant?" Twitch asked, having the same thoughts.

"Yup, poison and knives, basically. There's a skill that makes it easier for me to hit somebody if they don't know I'm there, which I thought would work with my Gift." She took a breath. "It's a really good class for what I want to do."

There was a brief silence. Twitch, who didn't know her history and reluctance with her skills, said the obvious, "Cool. So you're like an assassin."

"Yeah." Her chin firmed, and she looked straight at Thomas. "I am."

"Can I ask why?" Thomas said, carefully. "You said a few times you don't like the whole assassin idea. What's changed?"

Her smile was a little sad. "That was before I got taken. I'm not having that happen again, ever."

It made sense, and people were allowed to change their minds. Plus, considering what they were facing, a sneaky combat class was the smart option. He knew that, and it would be beyond hypocritical of him to frown on her for it while approving of Zach and Twitch's choices, but... he still felt a little sad for her.

He made sure not to show it, though. That would be condescending.

"Okay," Thomas said, forcing a smile. "It sounds like a good class for you."

"It is." Her shoulders loosened a fraction, and he realized she'd been waiting for his approval.

Twitch, of course, shattered the moment. "You guys, we need a team name."

Turning from Thomas, Jo arched an eyebrow at him. "Aren't we already the Wardens?"

"No, hear me out." Twitch grinned a shark-like smile. "We're a bunch of, like, mismatched weirdos thrown together by cosmic forces to protect the planet. So we're obviously the Guardians of the--"

"No," Thomas said loudly, and Zach snorted a laugh.

"No," Jo repeated, backing him up, and therefore proving she hadn't picked the dark class out of her choices. (Who was he kidding. She probably had.) "We're going to workshop this."

"Later," Thomas said, then turned his backpack around to dig around in it. As he did, he continued, "The System doesn't seem to have another quest for us this second, so in the meantime we gotta train as a team, figure out these new classes, and get you two up to level four." He pulled out his copy of the dungeons the National Guard weren't watching. "Let's go over our classes in detail so we know what we can all do. Then, I think we need to go diving."
