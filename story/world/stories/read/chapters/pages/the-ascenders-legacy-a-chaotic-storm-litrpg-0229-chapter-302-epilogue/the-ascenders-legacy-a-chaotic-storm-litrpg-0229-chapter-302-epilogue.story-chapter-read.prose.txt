Gordon was smaller than Lutia, but its essence density was far stronger, its beasts more abundant, and its lands far more perilous.

It was a land of sects and cultivation.

And the first thing Aodhán noticed as they arrived at the base of a colossal mountain range—many of its peaks lost in the heavens—was the thousands of storm cores blazing all around him. Everything, even the air itself, was drenched in storm essence so thick his nostrils burned with every breath.

Thunder boomed above—so loud it felt like the skies would split open at any moment. Lightning arced incessantly. Winds howled, and a torrential downpour immediately soaked him to the bone.

Yet, Aodhán couldn’t have felt more welcome.

The pain in his chest eased a bit, and with a shudder, he looked toward the swirling mass of cloud that blanketed the sky for miles and asked, “Where are we?”

Zarxan smiled beside him. “Welcome to the Storm Sect. Every storm cultivator in Gordon lives here.” He placed a hand on Aodhán’s shoulder. “And as of today, so do you.”

“Wait—” Aodhán’s eyes widened. “Every storm cultivator? In one place?”

He couldn’t believe it. The implications crashed over him like the mountains themselves. Competition. Knowledge. Power. And danger—so much concentrated ambition in one location. It was intoxicating and terrifying in equal measure.

But then, that was exactly what he needed to reach the Mythic class as soon as possible.

Not just competition—but competition among his own kind, rivals who understood the storm as deeply or even better than he did—all wielding and pursuing a thousand different truths of the same affinity.

Aodhán’s gaze rose to the roiling sky, and despite the pouring rain, a small, sad smile tugged at his lips.

Tier 100. He’d reach it here or die trying.

Thunder boomed once more, rumbling like a promise, and suddenly, this new chapter didn’t seem so hollow anymore.
