Following Lanra’s funeral, the coalition didn’t waste any time in preparing for what came next. So it was Asher soon found himself double-checking to ensure his bandoleer of daggers was firmly secure across his chest and his healing potions hanging from his hip. His void dagger had been returned to him by Amanda the moment she’d finished using it to carve through the last lich’s bunker, the paladin wanting nothing more to do with a small sliver of the void than anyone else.

“You’re going to want to keep these rings on at all times while we’re within the quarantined zone,” Groln explained as he marched up and down the row of warriors. Despite Lanra’s passing, Mako, Cora, and Jameston had unanimously agreed to remain with the coalition and help them take down the rest of the Council. As the official captain of the team, Mako was now the liaison for the Eight Crests, but that wouldn’t matter all that much until their next meeting. “We believe that the lich within the Hojon Empire, who goes by the name Eupher, has a Mind element. Mind and Death have been all but confirmed, though we are unsure as to his third one. These rings will prevent his Mind skills from directly harming you, but should still allow simple, harmless effects to work, such as the surface-level telepathy Jameston uses to communicate.”

“Thanks, but we’ll pass on the rings,” Mako said, waving Groln off when the slayer tried to hand them out. “We rely on Jameston and his own invasive skills far too much for that. We’ll just have to rely on his ability to shield us from the lich’s skills as well.”

“That’s a bit of a risk,” Groln grunted, eyeing Mako up before seeming to decide it was their funeral. “Alright. Everyone else, make sure the ring is on tight before we venture inside.”

Seeing as the snake from the Lambit Republic had been the entire reason Asher was wearing the Ring of Honest Ears in the first place, he decided to swap that one out for the ring that would protect him from having his mind turned into scrambled eggs. He could always rely on Complete Resilience’s immunity, but he wanted to keep that option flexible until they figured out what Eupher’s third element was. Along with the Ring of Mind Shielding, he had his Belt of Strength, his Amulet of Improved Healing, and his Boots of Levitation. His Ring of False death sat patiently within his rift, unable to be equipped lest he overload his soul. The Stage of Bleed meant he could only have four active artifacts at a time, and while that seemed like a lot, Asher had been surprised at just how quickly he’d amassed more artifacts than he could actually use.

As amazing as Permanence’s second-tier effect revealed itself to be, it was unfortunate that for whatever reason, continuing to level the skill didn’t seem to increase the number of effects he could keep permanent on himself due to the first-tier ability. Three was still the hard limit, which meant his Hidden Presence, Shard Detection, and Lesser Realm Sight enchantments were all still locked into place for the moment.

“Our mission is to separate and take out the powerful minions that the lich has active under his control,” Groln continued, all the while walking up and down the line of warriors like they were in some sort of hellish boot camp. “As this is within the Hojon Empire, I will be taking point on this attack. I have already spoken with my Queen, and she has agreed to lend us the aid of two of the empire’s greater powers. However, they will not be fighting with us. They will be taking care of maintaining the quarantine, meaning we don’t need to worry about the lich running away once we break through his defenses.

“We will be splitting into three teams, in the hopes of drawing the attention and isolating the three different undead that were once greater powers,” Groln continued. “For the sake of communication, the nine corpses that were once within the Stage of Power will be regarded as revenants, while three corpses that were once greater powers will be called greater revenants. Each team will attempt to take down one of these greater revenants. Should you find yourself not facing one for whatever reason, travel to the center of the quarantine as fast as possible and try to wipe out the lich. Alternatively, should your team find themselves facing two or more of the greater revenants, do your best to fight defensively and buy the other team time to advance. If the lich falls, so do the undead.”

“These greater revenants were once greater powers for the Hojon Empire,” Mako said, standing tall as he looked up at Groln’s demonic features. “What can you tell us about them?”

“Some of you may have heard their names from history books,” Groln grunted. “According to our intelligence, Eupher somehow managed to get his hands on the corpses of Blood Storm, Crescent Moon, and…” he paused, gritting his teeth as if this next admittance caused him physical pain. “…The Giant.”

“The Giant?!” Cora shouted, catching Asher off guard as the usually stoic woman turned and looked at Groln like he was insane. “And you’re only just telling us this now?”

“As I said before, the fact that the Hojon Empire lost the corpses to some of its greatest warriors of old was considered top secret,” Groln said bluntly, ignoring her outburst.

“What’s the big deal about The Giant?” Moxy asked, looking far too curious for Asher’s liking as she licked her lips. “Is he tough?”

“Tough is an understatement,” Cora snorted. “I remember my grandfather telling me stories about him. Legend said that he was over a hundred feet tall, and capable of punching through a mountain in but a single blow. Do you have any idea how difficult it is to make a name for yourself that’s so impressive even people in other countries know about you? Asher sort of cheated in that regard because Spatial element holders are already huge news, and word of them spreads whenever a new one pops up. The Giant did it the old-fashioned way.”

“To clarify, The Giant cannot grow his physical body to such heights,” Groln said. “He possessed the elements of Weight, Strength, and Soul. A trifecta of which made him an absolute monster and feared across the continent.”

“Soul? How does one get an element of Soul?” Samantha asked.

“The same way most of the truly unique elements are discovered,” Groln said. “A team of realm wanderers discovered the element during one of their trips through the wild realm and brought it back for the Queen. Though admittedly, it has been seen before. There are records of the daughter of a great sage unlocking it as her origin element after spending the majority of her childhood deep in reflective meditation.”

“More importantly than how he got it, what does it do?” Rulfar asked.

“The Giant is capable of projecting his soul outside of his own body. And combined with his other skills, as Cora already explained, he can take on the form of a massive giant taller than any castle. It is his ultimate skill, and one that made him utterly unstoppable. We don’t even know if he is capable of using such a skill as a greater revenant, but if he is, the only option we’ll have is to try and buy time. He cannot be killed while using his skill, and he can keep it up for days at a time.”

“Can’t be killed, huh?” Asher repeated. He grinned as he summoned Crescent to his hand and enjoyed the feeling of his skills working again without issue now that his soul had been healed. “I’d like to put that to the test.”

“With our luck, you just might get your chance,” Groln said, shaking his head before moving on. “While not nearly as famous as The Giant, Blood Storm and Crescent Moon are still incredibly dangerous. Blood Storm has the elements of Blood, Water, and Air, and was most well-known for creating a typhoon of boiling, jagged blood that only grew larger and more deadly the more victims it claimed. He once wiped out an entire city with a single skill, before directing the storm to claim the lives of all the fleeing refugees as well. Crescent Moon has the elements of Sword, Steel, and Light, and was once an unparalleled master of the sword. She never lost a single duel, and it was said she was so skilled that she never shed even a drop of blood across her entire life.”

“And let’s not forget these are just the greater revenants,” Amanda reminded them, frowning as she twisted the Ring of Mind Shielding onto her finger. “There are nine other revenants as well, each one of whom was once in the Stage of Power. Even if they didn’t make names for themselves as greater powers, they will still be quite dangerous.”

“From what we’ve managed to observe, it seems each of the greater revenants leads three of the lesser ones with them as they roam about the quarantined zone. Just as Amanda has warned, that means each team will need to be ready to face off against multiple opponents. For that reason, the teams are as follows. Asher and his team will join forces with Amanda. You’ll be the smallest in number, but with you and Amanda combined, you’ll be arguably the deadliest when it comes to direct firepower. The rest of her Order will split into two groups, with one half joining me and my team, and the other half joining the team from the Eight Crests. Mako, you will be in charge. Can you handle it?”

“No need to worry about us,” Mako said, saluting with his spear before driving the butt of the weapon into the ground. “We’ll take out any undead that come our way.”

“Good. And don’t forget, these are just the outer defenses. Once we break past the revenants, we still have to take on the lich himself. For that reason, the teams without Asher will carry equipment with one of his marks on them so that he can join us in an instant should we need his skill to put the lich down for good.”

“What does the terrain look like?” Rulfar asked. “Do we need to worry about innocent people, or have they all been evacuated?”

“Everyone that was once living within the area has either already fallen prey to the lich before we could get them out, or has been relocated,” Groln nodded. “I personally aided in a few of the rescue missions to retrieve villagers from deep within the quarantine zone. The lich seemed rather indifferent to them thankfully, but some of his revenants appeared to have a bit of an evil streak. I don’t know if it is something that naturally happens upon waking up and suddenly finding oneself both an undead monster and bound to a lich, but regardless of why, it was the revenants going around wreaking havoc. The lich himself hasn’t been seen since his first appearance.”

“Wait, how do we even know he’s still in there then?” Moxy demanded. “I’m going to be angry if we fight our way to the center only to discover he skipped town a decade back.”

“He couldn’t have broken through the quarantine without us noticing, and there’s no reason to raise so many powerful undead only to leave them behind,” Groln argued. “He’s in there, even if we haven’t laid eyes on him in some time. With that said. Asher, are we all set to go?”

“The pegasus courier that Donvath sent out arrived earlier this morning,” Asher nodded, stopping himself at the last moment from adding ‘during Lanra’s funeral.’ Everyone was doing their best to put their sorrows behind them and keep moving forward like she would have wanted, and he wasn’t about to be the one to drag them all back to that somber mood. “I’m good to teleport everyone to the Hojon Empire whenever you are.”

“In that case, stick with your groups, and happy hunting,” Groln said, sending shivers down most of their spines as he grinned with his blackened, demonic teeth. “Let’s bring the Council of Death down to their knees.”
