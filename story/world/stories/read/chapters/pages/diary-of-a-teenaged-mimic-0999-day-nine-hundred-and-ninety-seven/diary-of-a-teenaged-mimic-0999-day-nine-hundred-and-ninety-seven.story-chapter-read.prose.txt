On the one hand, I know that random stuff I've forgotten to do, especially if it was stuff I never actually told anybody else about, isn't going to consciously make alliances with other stuff I've forgotten to do. The idea of different random bullshit self-organizing into an assault on my posterior is, fundamentally, bonkers.

On the other hand, ear bud cables.

Yeah, I know. 'WTF, Tabitha' in full effect. But hear me out on this. Ain't nobody got the money to pick up infinite sets of wireless ear buds, but the cheap assed nigh disposable wired ones are like a buck at the dollar store. But the cables are slightly less durable than an equivalent mass of cotton candy, so you gotta practice good cable management with those things. Unfortunately, 'good cable management' is nigh mythical in the first place, and when you add in the levels of ADHD that routinely fuck with my brain, that's just not gonna happen. But because replacing even cheap assed ear buds on the regular gets spendy, I used to wrap those up super careful and shit before putting them away.

Thing is, no matter how neatly I wrapped those things up, overnight they'd spontaneously tie themselves into the kind of knots that Boy Scouts wished they could tie. Shibari ain't got shit on ear bud wire tangles. At first I thought it was lying there in bed next to me, so I put them in my pocket. No joy, so I left them on my dresser. Nope. Even putting them in a fuckin' drawer didn't help. In fact, since I'd usually buy a handful of them, every set of ear buds in the drawer would become one with every other set. Looking like some kind of skinny snake orgy. Wound up having to plug a random plug into my phone, crank the volume up, and try to figure out which buds had noise coming out.

My point here, in case somebody forgot, but I'm returning to it because like I said I'm tryna follow up on things, is that all those random things I've left undone don't need more sentience than a fuckin' set of ear bud cables to become inextricably tangled together, and Murphy being who he is the eventual shape they'll coalesce into will be one that shoves the worst of the ass biting fangs into a set of self-inserting anal beads.

So, short version, I know too late, I'm not just tryna follow up because that's what the person I want to be would do, it's because the perversity of the universe tends toward maximum, and that will wind up with fanged ears ramming their buds up my ass, which is a situation I'm eager to avoid.
