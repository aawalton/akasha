The four smaller wolf-creatures pounced at the same time, signalling the start of the battle. Instantly, Lee Jia, Takeda, and Guan Yi moved to intercept the pouncing beasts. Unfortunately, even with the largest monster staying back, their front line was outnumbered, forcing Eui to fill in and take on a wolf of her own.

While Eui’s close combat skills weren’t the worst in the group, they didn’t quite measure up to the other front liners, and she was in danger of being overwhelmed almost as soon as she’d engaged the monster.

“Rika, cover Eui!”

Jia barked her order without thinking, using Takeda’s given name for the sake of expediency. They had known that being outnumbered was a possibility, and had plans in place to account for it.

The first was Eunae, who was now engaged in what appeared to be a staring contest with the largest of the smoldering wolf creatures. Floating around her were two ghostly green fireballs, one hovering above each of her tails. Her face was a mask of intense focus as beads of perspiration formed on her forehead.

The second was Takeda, who quickly ducked under a vicious claw swipe and made a few arcane gestures. As she completed the gestures, two shimmering copies of her rushed out to assist Eui. The copies were crude facsimiles of Takeda, imitating her appearance but lacking depth and substance. As Ienaga had told her before, she had a long way to go before her illusions were convincing.

Luckily, the mindless beast didn’t seem to notice the difference, lashing out at the copies as aggressively as it would against Takeda herself. Despite the lack of intelligence, the creature did seem to catch on after the first few attacks passed harmlessly through the illusions, causing them to dissipate into mist before quickly reforming. Luckily, Takeda had another trick up her sleeve.

As Eui fell back behind the illusions, the beast tried to follow after her, ignoring the ethereal clones as they struck at its face. The creature recoiled as the first blow landed, then retaliated fruitlessly with a snarl as the second strike followed soon after.

Takeda had learned Guan Yi’s trick of combining her qi projections with ki, allowing her to channel her telekinetic martial art through her clones. The strikes were weak, but undeniably solid. There was a limit to how many times she could attack with each clone, but it lent an extra layer of credibility to her distractions.

Even with the support from Takeda’s clones, Eui struggled to keep her mark occupied as it lashed out mindlessly at anything it could reach. When a giant icicle lanced into it from the side, the wolf immediately turned and pounced in the direction of Kim Yongsun. In the blink of an eye, the burning claws smashed through the icy barrier that Kim had hastily erected. Eui tried to distract it again by throwing her knives, but its ashen fur was as hard as stone!

Kim Yongsun was falling back rapidly, but the beast was fixated on him and the others were all distracted by their own battles. With a growl of frustration, Eui channeled her ki into a knife, ignoring the sharp spike of pain that lanced through her arm. She closed the distance and stabbed the charged knife straight into the creature’s flank. This time, her attack struck true and the wolf immediately turned its attention back on her.

Eui didn’t have time to completely dodge out of the way of the creature’s claws as it retaliated, smashing straight through the mana shield she had cast earlier and sending her sprawling to the ground. Though her protections had managed to save her from the worst of it, the attack left nasty gashes along her side and the sheer impact of it had her coughing up blood.

Even so, Eui came out ahead on the exchange as her knife detonated, annihilating a large chunk of flesh and knocking the wolf to the ground with a whimper. As it limped back up onto its feet, brightly glowing blood dripped onto the ground and caught fire wherever it landed.

“Stage three!”

Eui’s voice was weak as she barely managed to croak out the warning, but everyone heard and understood her. Elemental manifestations in the blood of a magical beast were a sure sign of a stage three creature.

A dark cloud fell over the group as they realized how dire their situation was. They had been prepared to take on a stage three creature as a group, but an entire pack of them was beyond their expectations.

“Xin!”

“I’m on it!”

Lee Jia wasn’t able to do much more than hope that Xin Wei understood her intentions as she focused wholeheartedly on her own fight. The creature was incredibly fast, and its reflexes dwarfed her own. Only the predictable nature of its mindlessly aggressive attacks gave her even the slightest chance of holding her own against it. Even worse, its hide was unnaturally tough and without meridians or an aura, her lightning-based attacks had very little effect.

Lee Jia spared a glance in Xin’s direction and saw him rushing towards Eui. Satisfied that her command had been understood, she returned her attention to her own foe in time to duck out of the way of a bite. She countered with a corrosive qi burst, but suffered a few burns from the creature’s burning saliva for her trouble.

The beast recovered slightly faster than she did, and all Lee Jia could do was brace herself to receive the claw swipe. Before the attack could land, a devastating wave of frost blasted the creature away from her, earning her a reprieve. Looking for the source of the attack, Lee Jia saw Dae, his expression grave as he quickly prepared another spell.

Gone was the goofy, slightly nerdy boy Lee Jia knew. With a glowing scroll held open in front of him, and dozens of talismans floating in an orbit around him, Hyeong Daesung was the very picture of a war mage. As he fired off spell after spell, Lee Jia realized that Dae was engaging every single wolf at the same time.

When Takeda was sent off balance by a missed strike, Dae flicked a talisman towards her and a muddy barrier appeared between her and her opponent. When the wolf struck the barrier with its claws, the mud instantly exploded into solid stone shrapnel that distracted the beast long enough for Takeda to regroup.

When Guan Yi’s weapon failed to cut all the way through the tough hide of one of the beasts, a sudden quagmire of quicksand forming under its feet put the creature off balance long enough for Guan to dislodge his weapon and re-engage. Over and over these scenes repeated themselves as Dae used his impressive arsenal of spells to support the group.

Even so, they were barely managing to hold on, and they had yet to do any significant damage. The injured beast was being harassed by Kim Yongsun’s ice lances, which targeted the wound on its flank. With Takeda’s clones distracting it—and Xin Wei slowing it down with some kind of gravity technique—they were wearing it down, but too slowly. Even if they managed to bring down the injured wolf, they’d have four more to deal with—including the alpha.

“I don’t know how much longer I can hold it!”

Speak of the devil—Eunae was struggling with her own invisible battle against the larger beast. One of her ghostly flames had disappeared, and the other was flickering like a candle in the wind. Her expression was pained as she struggled to hold the creature under her thrall, sweat dripping profusely from her face.

“Eui! I need you to kill the big one before it breaks loose!”

It was a painful decision for Lee Jia to make, knowing how much it would cost her best friend, but the cost of failure was greater still. The equilibrium of the battle had to change soon, or they were all going to die here.

“With all due respect, Miss Lee. Miss An isn’t in any condition to—”

“Shut the fuck up you big sissy! I’ll do it.”

Xin’s protests were cut off by Eui as she rose to the challenge, blood still dripping from her right arm. She cracked her neck as she approached Eunae, a dark grin on her face. From her sleeves, Eui produced a fan of knives in each hand—every single one she had. As she began to charge them all with destructive energy, she whispered to Eunae.

“Just hang on for another minute, Miss Princess. Your subjects are depending on you.”

Eunae grimaced at Eui’s sarcastic encouragement.

“D-don't...call me...that…”

Eui chuckled, hiding a wince of pain as the black veins began to crawl up her arms.

“Whatever you say, Your Majesty.”

Eunae frowned, but couldn’t afford to be distracted by banter anymore as she called on the last shreds of her willpower to hold back the mindless monster’s killing intent. After a few more seconds had passed without Eui making a move, Eunae let out a pained grunt.

“I-I can’t…”

“Just another few seconds!”

Eui’s voice was a snarl due to the excruciating pain of the black veins that had made it all the way past her shoulders and now began to creep up her neck towards her face. She was almost finished, just another—

“Aaaaagh!”

Eunae’s shriek of pain coincided with the disappearance of her flame. The massive wolf creature moved immediately, dashing in a straight line towards Eunae and Eui. With no more time to spare, Eui threw the first handful of blades at the monster, but it dodged to the side, rebounding off the ground to pounce on the two girls.

With a swipe of its massive claws, Eunae was sent flying. She landed on the ground with a sickening crack before rolling several meters and stopping motionless. The creature followed up by snapping its jaws hungrily at Eui, who surprised it by throwing her second handful of blades straight into its open mouth.

It recoiled briefly, but recovered fast and resumed its attack. Eui, too exhausted and injured to avoid the attack could only throw up her hands to shield her face as the creature’s huge teeth closed around her.

Then it’s head exploded. Caught at the edge of the blast, Eui was knocked back violently, her entire body wracked with pain. That was the least of her problems, however, as she was splattered by a torrent of the creature’s flaming blood.

The blood-curdling scream pierced the air and shook Lee Jia to her core. The entire battlefield seemed to stand still for a moment—even the beasts seemed to have been given pause by Eui’s visceral cry of pain.

Without even a moment of hesitation, Jia ran towards Eui, praying to every ancestor, spirit, and god that she was still alive.

“Miss Lee, wait!”

Dae was forced to focus most of his attention on the wolf that Lee Jia had abandoned as the creatures rallied once again, apparently unperturbed by the loss of their alpha. Luckily, by now the injured beast had finally been felled, allowing Kim, Xin, and Takeda’s clones to divert their attention as well.

Jia ignored all of that as she raced to Eui’s side. Her friend was an absolute mess. Covered head to toe in horrible black burns and lying in a disturbingly large pool of blood, Eui’s breathing was extremely weak and came in choked gasps.

“No no no no no! Eui!”

Jia’s eyes welled up with tears as she panicked over her best friend. This was all her fault! She shouldn’t have pushed Eui like that! She should have spotted the ambush sooner! She should have led the team in battle more effectively—or better yet let someone else do it! She had been overconfident and now Eui was going to pay with her life!

“Jia! We need you!”

Takeda’s urgent tone went unnoticed as Jia knelt next to Eui and wept. Her ears drooped against her head as she went over, again and again, all the ways that she had failed. All the things she could have done better. She wanted to do something, anything to save Eui, but she felt completely powerless. She was finally broken from her thoughts by a familiar feeling on her cheek.

Eui had poked Jia with her tail. It was weak, her tail trembling intensely just to barely brush up against Jia’s face before flopping limply to the ground. Jia met Eui’s eyes and saw her friend staring up at her.

Though her body was weak—dying, even—Eui’s spirit was indomitable. Though Eui couldn’t speak through her weak, hitched breathing, after sharing mind and spirit so often over the last months, Jia could practically hear Eui’s thoughts.

“Go! You need to fight! Let someone else take care of me!”

Jia clenched her fists so tightly she could feel her own claws piercing her skin. With a pained expression, she blinked her tears away and stood. She summoned every last herb, pill, and salve within her storage ring and left them next to Eui. With one last steadying breath, she turned and shouted.

“X-Xin! Save Eui! Do whatever it takes even if you have to use your own treasures! I will pay you back, I swear it!”

Xin, who had been busy trying to stall the unoccupied wolf jerked his head toward her with shock written on his face.

“She’s still alive!?”

Lee Jia was already charging towards the beast with her expression of steely resolve only slightly ruined by her puffy red eyes.

“She is, and I want you to keep her that way. We’ll handle the last three!”

She wasn’t certain how, yet, but the beginnings of a plan were starting to form. Her team seemed to lack offense, but at the same time none of the front line fighters had taken any injuries. Kim Yongsun had been the one with the most effective attacks other than Eui, and Lee Jia had been hoping that Eunae would have been able to make similar contributions once she was freed up.

Unfortunately, Eunae was down as well—Lee Jia hoped that she was alright—but why was it that the strongest mage in the group was doing so little damage? As she saw Dae splitting his attention between the three remaining monsters, casting spells as fast as he could from his seemingly unlimited supply of talismans, she realized what the problem was.

“Dae! Switch to offense and focus your attention!”

“Miss Lee? But what if someone else gets-”

“That’s our job Dae! The ones at the front take the hits so that you can focus on eliminating the threats! Now get eliminating!”

Lee Jia was overcome by a sense of self-loathing as Dae and Kim Yongsun both began to focus their offensive ice magic on the wolf she was fighting. Wasn’t this the same as telling Eui to go after the largest one? Was this what leadership was? Deciding who to sacrifice for the sake of victory? She hated it. Hated that deep down she knew that her decision was the right one. Hated that she found herself thinking that Dae was being soft by protecting the team instead of killing their enemies.

Thinking about it became too painful, so she stopped. Her face became a cold mask of focus as she threw herself into the fight. Duck under a claw swipe, sway back from a bite, take advantage of an icicle lance to land a quick flurry of blows to a wounded portion of the hide. The blood burned her hands but she didn’t care—her corrosive qi was more effective when delivered to the bloodstream directly.

Soon the creature was slowing down due to the corruption ravaging it from within. The fight became easy after that, and it fell not long afterwards. She swept her gaze across the battlefield Eunae and Eui were still—no! Not important right now. Guan and Takeda were injured but Takeda had it worse. She’d been forced to recall her illusions and was using them to fight purely defensively—the same tactic she’d used to stall Hayakawa in spars.

Lee Jia shot across the battlefield like a lightning bolt, surprising the wolf with a vicious spear hand to its throat infused with as much lightning ki as she could manage. She broke her fingers on its hide, and it seemed to ignore the lightning entirely, but she didn’t let that slow her down.

“Rika, help Yi.”

Lee Jia was surprised at her own voice. The emotionless, commanding tone sounded completely foreign to her ears, even knowing she’d been the one speaking. As Kim and Dae rained spells down on Lee Jia’s opponent, the injured Takeda moved to assist Guan Yi. She stayed out of range herself, too injured to get close, but used her clones to support to the best of her ability.

She didn’t know how long the fight lasted, but by the time the last beast fell Lee Jia was covered in gashes, burns, and bruises—she had lost count of her broken bones. Takeda and Guan hadn’t fared much better, and only Xin, Dae, and Kim had managed to avoid injury.

Almost as soon as the last monster fell, Kim and Takeda ran to check on Eunae. Lee Jia limped over to where Xin was still fussing over Eui. He glanced up at her with an uncharacteristically worried expression.

“I’m doing the best I can, but she’s barely holding on. I don’t know where you got all this medicine, or where you were keeping it—or rather, I can guess but I don’t want to believe the answer. It’s the only reason she’s still alive right now, but I’m running out of qi and her condition isn’t improving. It’s as if something in her body is rejecting the treatment.”

Jia knelt down next to Eui and took her hand. She saw that the black veins which appeared whenever she used destruction energy hadn’t receded like they usually did. Still in the calm, focused state she had attained during the fight, Lee Jia felt an unnatural sense of clarity. Eui had pushed herself too far, and now her destruction ki was rampaging through her body unchecked.

As she had during the fight, Jia made her decision immediately. She took Eui’s hands in her own, bowed her head, and began to meditate.
