After the man left, we entered the room, and I was stunned by its size. It was much larger than it appeared from the outside, resembling a two-bedroom apartment. The walls were made of black material, with silver specks shining in and out instead of colorful clouds.

Teddy clamped a hand on my shoulder and nudged me forward. "Beautiful, isn't it? We all had the same expression you did the first time Uzu gave us the room. The other two rooms on the floor are equally amazing but not like the Dreamer. Wait till you lay on one of the beds."

The mana spread throughout the room and raced like water down a pipe. After a couple of seconds, he took his hand off the wall and grinned widely as the ceiling came to life. Six different coloured clouds floated down and settled on the floor in a semicircle. The clouds bobbed on the floor until they flattened into a more circular shape.

The 'bed' was only slightly depressed, which meant whatever the material was, it was solid enough to hold up the large oni even with his armour on.
