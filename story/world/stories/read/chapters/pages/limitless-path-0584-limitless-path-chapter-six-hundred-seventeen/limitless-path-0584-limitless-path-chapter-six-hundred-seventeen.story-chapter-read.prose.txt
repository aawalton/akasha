None of them helped, none stopped the urges or the feelings, and even copious amounts of alcohol didn’t dull the desires any longer. To feel the blade slice the flesh, it was a thrill that he couldn’t get in any other way, and a satisfaction his mind craved in a way that nothing else fulfilled.

He had tried everything, every thrill out there, even the simulated ones that were supposed to help with the kinds of…urges he had developed early in his life. None of them worked, and none of them got it or really replicated the feeling. Nothing that he had experienced had filled in that gap, had brought any light or life to his gray, dull world; it wasn’t until the first time he had cut them that he really understood. It wasn’t even his fault, not really, not with the way things had happened. He had been attacked, set upon by those after his possessions, or just those who didn’t like the kind of person he was developing into; it was always hard to say, with these things, exactly what had happened. Maybe depraved people just recognized depraved people on some level.

Whatever the case might be, he had been stronger than the two of them, and the second of them hadn’t died during the fighting. No, in fact, they had lived for quite a while, though much of it was in his ‘care,’ their life slowly ebbing, a few potions or small items keeping them alive just that bit longer, extracting just that little bit more blood and enjoyment from them. It eventually got to be too much for them and, even though they were still alive, the fun was really gone at that point, the person’s body so mangled they were more hunks of broken flesh clinging to crack, dry bones than they were a person. That was part of the thrill, but also part of knowing when to be satisfied. He had learned over the years since, learned when to stop, when to feel that sense of joy, to savor the high and let it slowly fade over time, disposing of his latest ‘toy’ with only a bit of care and time.

That was another difficult thing, when no one understood how he thought or what he wanted, what he needed to do; getting rid of the evidence, as they called it. He had had a few close calls over the years, a few times where someone far too strong, far too powerful, had caught on, or nearly caught on. Those times were the most tense, but maybe the most thrilling of all, especially when he still had a ‘toy’ he was playing with while they hunted him. Even now, thinking of it, he hiccuped a little giggle at the phantom sensation that echoed through him, glad nobody else was around him to hear that bit of unhinged laughter. It had become so much easier as his levels and stats grew, as his skills, in particular, grew and aided him in his…tasks. If he was honest with himself, some part of him very deep down understanding something of what he was and what he was doing and what it meant, then he knew it was just for a sick thrill, but he couldn’t really rationalize it. That voice had been quiet for years, not even a whisper, and he was glad, for hearing someone else in his head, someone that sounded so sad and desperate, had really intruded on his play. Now, there was no one, nothing, just the thrill of the blood and his ideas on better ways to cut to watch them bleed slowly.

He did still have to be careful sometimes; he had to move from time to time, obviously, as the hunting grounds would become quite dangerous after a while. So far, he had managed to avoid anyone truly overwhelming getting close to him, and hit was during those times that he was…not running, but relocating…yes, that was it. Relocating. He giggled again as he became a bit distracted, watching his next prey, a young woman of extraordinary beauty casually walking along the lake shore, before he focused again. He would have to move soon. He would have to…relocate…from this lovely world, and that was always where the biggest problems happened. The two times he had come closest to being caught where when he had stayed on a world too long, moving to a new place on the same planet, no realizing the authorities had already been hunting him. A shiver raced down his spine as he thought about the close call, his grin becoming almost feral in nature as he glance at the beauty walking barefoot through the white sand, the very small tidal motions of the lake causing water to lap over her feet and ankles. He would cut her. He would make her blood run. He would watch as the knife sliced so prettily. He would have to move then, and that was the second time, the bigger problem, the worst part. Going from one planet to another was difficult, expensive, and dangerous, though that last one applied to him far more than to others. It also didn’t help that he spent little time fighting beasts, exploring, doing commissions, crafting, or anything else that might turn a profit. Traveling irked him, annoying him to no end, with all the expense and all the security. Why couldn’t he just stay by the warm, pretty lake and make their warm, pretty blood flow?

This woman would be his last here, he knew it. It wasn’t from a skill, not exactly, but he had a sense, after years of the doing the same thing all across the galaxy, some sixth sense that half a century of kidnapping and killing the prettiest and most handsome ones had honed in him. He had rejected it, categorically, understanding that there was nothing wrong with him. He wasn’t sick or broken like they claimed, he was just different, though he had almost believed them at first. He had nearly lost himself to useless hedonism, trying to mute the urges they had told him were so bad, but that hadn’t worked, and there had come a day where he had seen through it.

Even the ones they proclaimed as the most righteous, the very ones they held up as the standard for what even others of that prattling lot of fake goody two-shoes, weren’t any different from him. They had urges, they had desires, and they acted on them.
