The wind roared loudly alongside the harpies mocking screeches, their arrival being a testament to their strength. After all, they had completely ignored Lilithra’s passive fear skill—their levels were still high enough to be a challenge for them.

Even so, confidence flared amongst the group. They had defeated such creatures before, albeit with Ascalons help, so Lilithra and Selorien already knew what to expect. On the other hand, while Lyra had experience against the harpies, she was a bit ill suited to fight them. Her skills were more effective against ground targets, though she could easily hold her ground nonetheless—aside from finding the beasts grotesque, she was ready to fight alongside her friends.

Ascalon, ever the mentor figure, wanted to see how his companions would fare this time, so he was resolved to not actively engage in combat, but rather, take a more defensive approach. He decided to not use his taunt skill unless his comrades were in danger, and he would refrain from attacking harpies that didnt target him directly. Of course, he had no intention of letting his friends know about his stance, in order to let them focus on the battle.

The Guild leader herself looked calm, exuding a reassuring aura, her grip on the large halberd tightened strongly. She felt completely different this time, barely any nervousness in her mind—instead, she felt eager to test herself against the enemies that had put her to shame in the past!

“It's payback time!” She exclaimed with firmness in her voice, garnering the harpies’ attention due to her loudness—their bird-like, angered eyes focused on her, their shrieking letting Lilithra know she was the center of attention this time around.

While it wasn’t entirely her intention, her words served as a signal to start off the fight, prompting Selorien to vanish into the shadows as per usual, while Lyra rapidly crouched to place a hand on the floor.

“Rising Tide!” The Felinae chanted, her voice reverberating across the rocky walls. As expected, water started to flow freely from her hand, quickly gathering in one point and forming a moving, shallow puddle—she was intent on having the liquid ready to form a barrier when—or if—the need arose.

Meanwhile, a couple of harpies plunged at Lilithra from above, perhaps feeling challenged by her earlier words. Lilithra met their descent head on, drawing her arms to her right side as she prepared to attack. The weight of the halberd was astounding, yet she found it in herself to not only withstand it, but also overcome it.

Drawing the halberd further back, she stepped forward and pressed hard on the ground with her left foot, an echoing stomp emanating from her position. Then, she used the small momentum to pivot slightly on her left leg, twisting her body forward as she raised her halberd in a diagonal, upward slash.

At the sight, the two creatures spread their wings, halting their descent before flapping downward, not only avoiding Lilithra’s slash but also gaining a bit more altitude to prepare for a counterattack—it was clear these beasts were very intelligent and used to battling grounded targets, showing just how much of a difference Ascalon’s skills had made in their last encounter. But not even the cunning harpies would’ve expected what happened next.

The sheer mass of the halberd slicing through the air strained Lilithra’s arms, shoulders, and even part of her torso, yet at the same time it showed its worth. The massive armament was swung with extreme force that, even after being avoided, generated a shockwave so strong that the resulting gale collided with the harpies, causing their wings to painfully bend back under its might.

Both the harpies and Lilithra shared the same shocked expression, as neither of them expected such raw power to emanate from her weapon. The whole thing about taming it made a whole lot more sense now—it was like a beast; a powerful, prideful one!

Even Ascalon was taken aback by the sudden development, an astonished smirk spreading across his face as he watched the two beasts fall to the ground with a resounding thud. Their wings were likely broken, which was even more surprising. Ascalon knew full well that the harpies could navigate even amidst the most tempestuous wings, their feathery limbs capable of resisting even the most unruly winds.

“Just how strong was that single air slice?” Ascalon mused to himself, admiring Lilithra and her weapon with keen interest. She was growing strong, and it filled him with both pride and glee!

While Ascalon rejoiced, the two bird-like monsters screeched in pain as Selorien emerged from stealth, stabbing them both with immaculate ease, a huge grin plastered on his face. Quite opportunistic, that elf!

At the same time, the harpies circling above shrieked in anger, flapping their wings furiously, as if throwing a maddened tantrum. Immediately after, they plummeted downward in unison—all save for one. The remaining creature remained in the sky, gliding around, her chest puffing out as it took in a big, deep breath.

“Here it comes.” Ascalon warned, his instincts causing his attention to shift to what appeared to be the ring-leader of the beasts. Everyone instantly realized what he meant—a song was coming!

But they were prepared this time! Selorien ran toward Lilithra with a smirk on his face. “Get ready to strike, Lil!” He stated as he looked up, his eyes focusing on the harpy that was about to sing. Lilithra was momentarily confused, but her trust toward the elf spurred her to action.

This time, Lilithra raised the halberd high, sweeping it in a wide arc and almost losing her balance as its weight pulled behind her back. Her whole torso arched backward under the strain as she prepared for a powerful slash, her eyes intently focused on her friend closing the distance between them.

“Now!” Selorien exclaimed as his figure shifted into a shadowy blurr, his [Shadow Swap] skill activating for the first time in a while. The elf’s form distorted and changed, before being replaced by that of the harpy that was about to sing, confusion and panic clear in its hideous face.

With utmost decisiveness, Lilithra stepped toward the beast, bending her whole body forward as she swung the halberd overhead, bringing it down in a cleaving, devastating arc. Adding her entire weight into the attack, the armament descended with thunderous momentum, the air furiously tearing apart to make way for the chimera-forged blade.

Needless to say, the blow was both destructive and effective, cutting through the still-shocked harpy with such ease it was almost scary. The blade finally stopped as it hit the ground, the whole area trembling slightly under its might as both dust and feathers flew in all directions.

The harpy parted in two, both of its bleeding halves falling at the sides of the halberd with a sloshing thud, while Lilithra watched with a mix of shock and awe in her eyes. The chimera’s weapon was beyond mighty, and she could feel a surge of excitement rushing through her whole body each time she wielded it. Unbeknownst to her, the ‘taming’ was already underway!

But as she regained her bearings, she snapped her head upward, her eyes trying to locate Selorien. As she feared, the elf was airborne, plummeting down! Perhaps he hadn’t thought things through, as per usual!

“Selly!” Lilithra exclaimed with concern, causing Ascalon and Lyra to follow her gaze. But just as their worry began to swell up, Selorien’s frame blurred as he used his skill again, slowly shifting to a more muscular, bigger frame—Ascalon’s. The knight blinked a few times as he felt his body fall, just as Selorien’s voice reached his ears.

“Sorry, Ascalon!” The elf smirked from below, standing at Ascalon’s previous location, his voice coated with mischievousness. Ascalon sighed in mid-air and shook his head, his short hair ruffled by the wind as gravity pulled him down.

“Oh, well…” Ascalon said in a resigned voice, twisting his body and facing the ground with his lance—though more precisely, aiming for one of the harpies that were still lunging at his friends. With his frame gaining momentum in his descent, he pierced downward, skewering the harpy and driving it into the earth with a resounding boom.

Once more, dust, debris, and feathers rose up, while the other beasts shrieked in a mix of anger and fear. The harpies were smart enough to know things weren’t going their way, so Ascalon knew they had to end the fight quickly, before they had a chance to flee!

Fortunately, Lilithra and Selorien’s combinated attack had put a stop to the singer before it even had a chance to charm them! Ascalon was quite impressed by their resourcefulness and their quick-thinking in the middle of combat, something clearly forged by the bond they shared. Still, Ascalon decided to keep a close eye on the remaining harpies, just in case another one decided to sing.

“Eep!” A cute whimper resounded out of nowhere, drawing everyone’s attention. It was Lyra, swinging her scythe with clear difficulty at one of the harpies above her head. Lyra’s face was lit up with determination, and motivated by Lilithra’s recent feats, she had decided to start using her new weapon.

But it proved more difficult than she had thought, as the weight of the armament was just too much for her fragile arms to bear. Even so, resolve flared inside her, fueling her to push herself to her limits! But it was not enough.

“H—help!” She suddenly cried out, her big, brown eyes shimmering with unshed tears. Even if she was motivated and doing her best, the reality was she still didn’t have enough strength to keep swinging the massive scythe for too long! She needed some more training before properly wielding a weapon like that—especially when she wasn’t accustomed to using any kind of armament to begin with.

Just as her mood worsened, she felt reality shift, her existence blinking across the battlefield. Ascalon and Lilithra watched as Lyra changed places with Selorien, who had, once again, used his [Shadow Swap] skill to switch locations with her. As the elf appeared, he swung both his daggers upward, slashing at the harpy’s wings with precision.

Caught off-guard by his sudden appearance, the beast couldn’t even react, and descended to the ground with a shriek, clearly hurt. Ascalon let out an impressed ‘oh~’ as he stared at the elf, while Lilithra sighed in relief, her gaze shifting from Selorien to Lyra. Lyra was dumbfounded, her cheeks reddened as she realized what had just happened.

“Damn if my skill isn’t amazing!” Selorien blurted out excitedly, his usual carefree demeanor completely overridden by unbridled enthusiasm—a sight that happened rarely, so maybe it was time to make a wish! Ascalon couldn’t help but agree with him though, as the skill was indeed incredible, and it had been quite the helpful move each time it was used—both in this fight and prior ones!

“Thanks!” Lyra exclaimed from Selorien’s previous location, now more composed and surely glad to have been rescued.

Ascalon noticed she was having a bit of difficulty carrying her weapon, as she had placed it in the ground beside her… or not? Paying closer attention, he saw Lyra’s palms on the ground, water emanating from them and quickly joining with the puddle she had made beforehand.

“Ocean’s Shackles!” She chanted with determination, her voice resounding with a mystical reverberation. The water she had summoned rose up and coiled around the harpy that Selorien had brought down, circling its limbs and shackling it to the ground!

“Nice!” Selorien grinned as he swiftly stabbed the now trapped harpy, finishing it up before it regained its strength. As she had shown in Starugnia, despite her timid nature Lyra was quite quick-witted in combat, which was both reassuring and commendable!

Seeing their numbers diminish rather quickly, the remaining beasts flapped their wings to gain altitude, increasing the distance between them and the group. The harpies exchanged wary, confused glances with each other, unsure of what to do after seeing their brethren being slain so easily.

“Not so fast.” Ascalon suddenly declared, his unwavering gaze set in the sky above. “Come down, you filthy wretches.” The knight commanded, his authoritative skill [Heroic Challenge] coming into effect and compelling the beasts to attack him against their will.

While he hadn’t intended to use his skill at first, it was clear the creatures were going to escape anyway, so he decided it was better for them to be vanquished rather than be left alone—mostly because he knew their materials could be useful to his friends!

“Let’s finish this!” Lilithra declared with enthusiasm and a confident smirk as she rushed to Ascalon, halberd held tight. Selorien quickly joined in, a mischievous smirk spreading across his face as he twirled his daggers in hand.

The ensuing scene was almost the same as the last time they had fought harpies, with Ascalon defending against the creatures' frenzied attacks while his friends finished them up. This time, however, both Lilithra and Selorien were swifter and more precise, slaying the beasts with experienced ease.

They even took a moment to let Lyra use her scythe on the beasts, trying to help her get accustomed to handling her weapon, and teaching her a few movements she could use in combat.

“Owie… it—it’s the first time I cut something… living…” Lyra said at some point, her words carrying a hint of regret—though it earned her affectionate stares from her friends. It was to be expected she would feel that way, since she was used to handling fights with magic. A melee weapon felt crude, more ‘up close and personal’, so to speak.

After a few minutes, the whole flock was defeated, and everyone quickly split to gather up some materials. Meanwhile, Lyra took a moment to compose herself, drinking some water. Ascalon watched over his friends with a satisfied grin, proud of their accomplishments. Even before he had interfered, everyone had showcased their growth, and his chest swelled with glee.

Perhaps, this time they could even face off against the boss of the dungeon!
