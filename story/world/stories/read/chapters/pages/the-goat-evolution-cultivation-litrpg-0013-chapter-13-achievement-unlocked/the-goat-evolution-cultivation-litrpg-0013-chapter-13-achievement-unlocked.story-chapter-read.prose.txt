As night falls and emptiness settles in, Lin looks around at the too-quiet darkness, feeling his hooves itching for something to crush. Grinding through daily missions and sleeping alone each night, it’s just too dull. If this were the real world, I’d be gaming. Watching beautiful vlogs with vpn or out for a walk, he mutters, frustrated by the monotony.

The only spark of interest is the mystery of Rokan—the goat who saved him and then disappeared completely, as if he just dropped off the map.

After stewing in that restless boredom for a few days, Lin decides: tomorrow, he’ll track down Bojo. He needs to see where that kid is sneaking off to. After all, he has a mission to finish and wants every scrap of EXP he can get. No point in waiting if he wants to get stronger.

The next day, after knocking out his daily goals, a notification popped up:

───────────────────────────── Achievement unlocked: You’ve performed 50 headbutts for three consecutive days! Reward: 10 EXP

Achievement unlocked: You’ve jumped 500 times for three consecutive days! Reward: 10 EXP ─────────────────────────────

Nathan Lin

These days, he could do about fifty headbutts, a huge improvement from the fifteen on his first day. As for jumps, five hundred was doable. Chewing? Almost fifteen hundred. Steps? Nine to ten thousand. The progress was good, but Lin wasn’t satisfied. Not yet.

After all, he was getting 10 EXP just for doing a little daily mission. What if he managed to complete the full list for several consecutive days? How much reward would he get then? Just thinking about it fired him up. I have to work harder, he thought.

After a while, Lin decided it was time to find Bojo. I can’t just grind daily missions for a week straight all alone, right? That’s way too introverted, even for me. What would people say if they saw a goat with no friends? “Hey, look at that goat—no friends, just endless self-improvement.” Like I’m not weird enough already. He had to laugh at himself and snorted out loud.

Besides, I need to become strong. I can sense it when I reach level one; I'll see some changes, so I need to gather exp faster. I'll go and find Bojo first. If I see the mission is too hard, I'll then decide on what to do. No matter what, a living goat is better than a dead one.

—----------------------------

So, where should I go first? He weighed his options: either head toward the main gate, where the pen was, or look around here first.

He chose to look around the area, eyes scanning for any sign of Bojo’s shadow. He didn’t stop his exercises, though; he kept up the jumping, headbutting, chewing, and pacing as he searched.

Time slipped by. Nearly two days later, Lin stopped near a grove of trees before a goat’s half-decayed body. Maggots already swarmed it. From the look of things, the goat hadn’t been dead long, maybe for a week or more. Judging by the size, it was a yearling.

Bojo? Lin couldn’t help it; a tear slipped down his cheek. Poor kid didn’t even get to enjoy his goat life before he died.

He sighed, raising his chin. “System, is there any reward for sobbing over a friend’s death? I’m not picky—give me some EXP, one per tear. Seems fair. Hell, I’ll cry louder for bonuses.”

The system stayed silent, as if it didn’t care. Lin checked his mission window. The “Make a Friend” quest was still there, unchanged. Does that mean… this isn’t Bojo?

Relief and confusion tangled in his chest. He shook his head and trudged back through the grass, a little adrift. How far have I even gone? He glanced up for the first time in what felt like days—and saw mountains on the horizon. Were those always there? Or am I just noticing because I’m finally looking up instead of at the damn grass? Typical.

For a breath, he let the moment sink in—then shook it off. Focus, Lin. Bojo could be anywhere. No more distractions.

Resolute, he decided to head for the pen. Still, as he prepared to leave, the thick, lush grass begged him to come back. After I finish this Bojo mission, I’m coming back here for a feast. Priorities, goat. First—the pen. If Bojo’s there, great. If not… well, we’ll figure it out.

The next day, Lin finally reached his target: the pen. Could’ve made it here yesterday. Why risk a night run? Wait until morning, live to graze another day. Good call this time.

His caution paid off immediately. In front of the main gate, he saw countless goats milling around, at the center of the throng, a floating, round object pulsing with eerie light. Lin narrowed his eyes, unsettled by the subtle vibration—he could practically feel it rattling his teeth. What the hell is that? Magical artifact? Weird human tech? Doesn’t matter—I’m not going anywhere near it. Like hell I’m letting my fur get singed.

He watched closely and saw two humans examine a goat before leading it away. Instantly, nervous tension twisted his gut. Whatever they’re doing—it's nothing good. That’s for damn sure.

He stayed at the edge of the herd, heart pounding and ears flicking. Bojo’s gotta be somewhere in this mess. Or maybe he’s already… no, don’t think that. Not giving up. That’s not who I am anymore.

Lin weighed his options, tail flicking. For now, it was safest to watch from the sidelines. No heroics today. Stealth goat mode: activated. He also wondered why he wasn’t affected by the strange object. Okay, Lin, think. Why am I not hypnotized like the rest? System protection? Secret goat immunity? Or maybe I’m just too stubborn for mind control. Wouldn’t that be something? He snorted quietly.

There was a third option, too: the device might just be spreading a female scent, and the goats, driven by instinct, were being lulled to sleep by the humans.

What do I do now? I can’t just walk in there; I’ll get captured for sure. Lin decided to wait it out, hoping the humans would eventually leave. He settled into the grass, ears perked for danger, and kept grinding his daily quests. His body was changing; he could feel it. Every jump, every headbutt, every mouthful of grass made him a little stronger, a little faster. Progress. Not exactly glamorous, but hey, I’ll take it. If I keep this up, maybe I’ll evolve into Super Goat. Or at least Outrun-the-Other-Goats Goat.

Just as he was settling into a rhythm, a series of system notifications appeared, each one punctuated by a soft, familiar chime:

─────────────────────────────

Achievement Unlocked! You have completed a new achievement.

Reward: Exp 10

For the rest of the day, Lin continued with his daily tasks and finally completed three more achievements.

Achievement Unlocked! You have completed a new achievement.

Achievement Unlocked! You have completed a new achievement.

Achievement Unlocked! You have completed a new achievement.

Reward: Exp 10 x3

System, oh system, why do you feel like you are becoming more and more likable? He was close to leveling up. He currently has around 90 exp.

The next morning, Lin woke to find the herd finally scattering. Most of the goats had already wandered off, and the few that remained were drifting away. The pen felt emptier, the air lighter.

Oh, fuck. Lin cursed inwardly. I’ve slept for too long. Just be there, Bojo. Just be there.

He started looking for Bojo but didn’t approach the pen. He wasn’t worried about his safety anymore; he figured he could outrun most of the yearlings now. Full-grown goats? That was another story. Not long ago, he’d seen a goat sprinting at what felt like a hundred kilometers an hour.

However, his own speed had nearly doubled over the past few days, thanks to all the daily missions. If nothing else, I’m getting stronger. I’m sure I could squash that goat now. Lin snorted, remembering the yearling who’d beaten him up.

Gradually, the pen cleared out, and the main gate came into view, massive, almost intimidating. Beyond it, he could see the houses outside the farm.
