The teleportation was only slightly unpleasant, feeling like a minor pinch all over the body for a split second. There were no complications, nor did it persist when Trevor and Adabelle realized they were in a completely different part of the world.

They, along with Lora, were standing on a dark stone terrace next to a small hut made from the same material. There were chairs for lounging as well as tables with wide umbrellas, one of which had a pair of sleek, black binoculars on it. Trevor only glanced at them for as long as it took to register what they were because a whole new scene unfolded before his eyes.

“You can see so far!” he laughed as he approached the cliff’s railing. Not taking his eyes off of the sight, he summoned Clay’s stoat husk. The earth elemental to dipped inside, took control, and clambered onto his shoulder. He absently scratched under their chin.

Just as Klar had explained at his bachelor party, the place they were staying was at the top of a three mile tall cliff. If he squinted, he could see that the entirety of the Wildlands was enclosed like this, as if it were settled inside a huge crater. The sun was setting, but it still cast plenty of light into the world below.

Large lizards roamed the plains in the center of the Wildlands. It was hard to tell their actual size from this distance, but there were several smaller ones running around while others were big enough that he could make out their general shape.

They were spread out in an area that had to have been miles across, though most gathered around a huge, crescent-shaped lake. Dozens of rivers flowed into and out of it, disappearing underneath the dense forest that was omnipresent around the cliffs all the way around.

Trevor and Adabelle panned their gaze across the sight, finding an area that looked like the tree’s leaves were made out of crystals. The light of the sun reflected a dazzling array of colors up at them. Small primates seemed to scurry about the area, coming and going with great speed.

The noblewoman quickly grabbed the binoculars from the table. She offered them to Trevor first, but he smiled and pulled his own pair out of his inventory. Adabelle giggled. “You’re always so prepared, my love.”

“I also have a pair for you, but that one’s probably better,” he replied. She bumped her hip against his, her tail wrapping around his leg, and they both turned their attention back to the sight. Trevor grinned as he looked around the Wildlands. “Ah, they are dinosaurs!”

“They are very similar to the ones in the Primordial Wood back home,” Adabelle said as she focused on the central plains. “Remember? I told you about it the first time we went to Eskretet together.”

“I sure do.”

“The only difference is that some of these seem to be feathered, but even then they’re not so different,” the demonkin explained, not pulling the binoculars from her eyes. “Same shape, same walk, same… indulgence for creatures smaller and weaker than they are, it seems.”

“That’s survival of the fittest,” Trevor said, focusing on the monkeys. They were hard to see because of the bright reflection off of the crystal trees, but he managed to get glimpses of them on the outskirts of the area.

They appeared to be some kind of red-furred chimpanzees. They clambered to and from the crystal trees, carrying large blue fruits that could have been melons. The sun fell low enough that the area became obscured by darkness.

A small, polite cough came from behind them, and they turned to look at Lora, who was smiling.

“I’m so very glad that you find this miraculous sight to be as amazing as we did, and I’m sure the boss will agree when I describe Trevor’s surprise face to him later,” she said, rocking back and forth on her heels. “May I say a few words?”

“Yeah, sure,” Trevor answered.

Lora cleared her throat and then approached, standing beside them. She adopted a tour guide persona as she spoke. “The Wildlands have been protected by Todarro and its neighboring countries for over three hundred years, after it became abundantly clear that no one was going to make any headway into the area. The cliffs, at their shortest point, are still well over a mile tall, and building anything that lasts long term draws the ire of the wildlife.

“Todarro, and its neighboring countries, haven’t given up, they just recognize that they need the protection of Summoned if they want to claim the lands and resources. This seems to be a fool’s errand because many of the ones they have brought into this world have gone, ‘Oh cool, dinosaurs. That’s neat, I really want to fluff their feathers and play with them despite the danger.’ So it’s a work in progress.”

Lora turned and gestured towards the stone hut. “These are the accommodations Klar has provided for you, but don’t think that it’s just some dinky little nothing place. That’s the stairwell, which leads to the residence. There are three floors, each of them built into the rock face with windows and balconies, which you can see if you just look down. I was assured neither of you were afraid of heights.”

“I have a few reasonable fears, but heights isn’t one of them,” Trevor said as he put a hand on Clay and leaned over the railing. There were a few balconies jutting out of the cliff, including a very large one where he thought the second floor would be. “This is really cool.”

“It is!” Lora agreed happily. “And, don’t worry, there are no fliers down there and the climbers can’t make it this high up. Everything is perfectly safe and there’s no one for miiiiiiiles! In fact, it’s kind of a pain to get here unless I’m involved. That’s why Klar had this place built, other than the view and the aforementioned really cool dinosaurs who have feathers I’d like to fluff.”

“Did that quote come directly from you, Lora?” Adabelle asked as she smiled at the shorter woman.

“It did!” she confirmed with a series of fast nods. “I was summoned here in Todarro to help with the Wildlands, but decided to work with the boss after he showed up to do business. But, no one wants to hear about that. You’re probably tired, and I was told to leave before I wore out my welcome. If you want more information on the Wildlands, there are plenty of books down below. Navigating the place is pretty easy, and it’s not so big that you’ll get lost. Food’s in the kitchen, and I’ve tucked away snacks here and there for you, as is proper when one hosts important guests. Any questions?”

“No, but I think I’d like to hear that story sometime in the future,” Trevor replied.

“Someday, maybe,” Lora said with a smile. “Anyway, later! See you in a couple of days!”

She raised her hand to wave goodbye and disappeared on the spot without scrunching her face up this time. There was no sign that the woman had been there at all.

“That’s such a cool Power,” Trevor stated, a little jealous that the space Power he had accidentally received wasn’t like that.

“It is,” Adabelle agreed before setting the binoculars back down on the table. She smiled at her husband. “Shall we head inside?”

The courier offered his arm. “I think we shall.”

They smiled as she looped her arm with his. Clay, always curious, jumped down from Trevor’s shoulder. The familiar dipped into the stone a little bit before bouncing back up, neither they nor the terrace changed despite the action. With a questioning glance upwards, their master nodded, and they disappeared into the cliff.

“Clay will be fine,” Trevor said as he led his wife to the door. “New area, new stone, and plenty of places to explore.”

“Quite the little adventurer, that one,” Adabelle remarked. She opened the minotaur-sized door, revealing a spiral staircase leading downwards. The newlyweds descended.

While it certainly wouldn’t have been anything special to Klar, they should have known better than to trust his word when he said it wasn’t too extravagant.

The first basement wasn’t much more than a few small bedrooms and storage spaces, the second opened up into an expansive lounge and kitchen combo. The biggest sitting area recessed into the floor with four staircases and corner couches between them. A room off to the side had a small gym along with a note saying that it was free to use, but they didn’t think they would get the chance.

A quick check of the kitchen showed them that it had everything they would need for at least a week despite not being there for more than two days. Options were nice, though, and they were glad to see that they had them in spades. They were already thinking of breakfast out on the balcony, which stretched from one side of the dwelling to the other.

After spending a few minutes exploring, they finally made it to the lowest floor. There were a few guest bedrooms that were more luxurious than those above, but only one had a sign calling it the “honeymoon suite.”

Walking in, Trevor and Adabelle found a large canopy bed bigger than even a minotaur would need. White lace sheets surrounded the mattress, allowing them to see silhouettes from the outside. Several pillows graced the dark silk sheets, which were softer than anything they had back home, and it took all of their effort not to slink into it right then and there.

Trevor went about emptying his inventory of their clothes into the wardrobe and dresser while Adabelle admired the jacuzzi in the room. It was quite clearly magical, as there was no place for water to come from or go, which was standard so far from civilization. The bathroom was in an adjacent room, and it was almost as large as some of the other bedrooms. Their shower took up half of it with several heads on the ceiling, allowing the water to fall like rain.

Finally, they stepped outside onto the balcony. The one above them provided cover from the sun, and there were a pair of plush chairs along with a chilled plate of chocolate covered strawberries. Trevor took one and offered it to his wife. Instead of taking it, she leaned over, watching him as she stroked her braid, and took a bite.

“Mmm,” she moaned, covering her mouth as she straightened back up. Trevor arched an eyebrow at her, but she took her time chewing and swallowing, savoring it. Once the demonkin was done, she pouted. “Why does Klar always have the best food?”

He hadn’t been expecting that, and laughed. “What do you mean?”

“I mean that he’s always surrounding himself with the tastiest things,” Adabelle complained jealously. “He owns Mateo’s, every time he comes to Tosa his non-Summoned chefs manage to make something that comes as close as natives can to someone with the Power of Cooking, which is saying a lot, and just about everything they serve is top end.”

“You got all that from a chocolate covered strawberry?”

“Taste it for yourself and see,” she said, picking up another and holding it in front of his face.

Smiling, he didn’t hesitate to take a bite the same way she just had. The fruit was juicy and sweet, more so than any strawberry he had tasted back in Tosa or on Earth. Not only that, but the chocolate was creamy and rich. Together, they created an exquisite flavor that delighted his palate.

“Mmm,” he moaned, looking down at what was left of the fruit.

“Right?” she asked, half-laughing. “I’m just a bit jealous, that's all.”

“As well you should be,” Trevor agreed after he swallowed. “Luckily for the both of us, he likes me, so we should continue to mooch off of him when it comes to food.”

“Normally, I’d want to stay self-sufficient or at least pay my own way, but…” Adabelle shrugged. “I suppose I can allow it specifically for this.”

“As nice as this balcony is, shall we eat the rest in bed?” he asked.

“That is a phenomenal idea,” the demonkin answered, agreeing quickly. Setting the piece of strawberry her husband hadn’t eaten down, he was quick to do the same. He took it all into his inventory just as Adabelle’s tail wrapped around his wrist started pulling him along. “Trevor, darling, I have something of a big request for you, if you’re feeling up for it.”

“Anything you need, love,” he replied easily.

Adabelle approached the bed and parted the lace curtains. Turning, she pulled Trevor into a passionate kiss that was fierce enough to make him fumble slightly at first, but he recovered gracefully. Her tail left his wrist to start messing with his buttons. When she pulled away, she sat down onto the plush mattress.

“I’m feeling a little constrained in this wedding dress. Help me out of it, will you?” she requested, looking up at him with a sultry smile and eyes so hungry they seemed to glow more than usual. Trevor nearly tripped over himself to comply.
