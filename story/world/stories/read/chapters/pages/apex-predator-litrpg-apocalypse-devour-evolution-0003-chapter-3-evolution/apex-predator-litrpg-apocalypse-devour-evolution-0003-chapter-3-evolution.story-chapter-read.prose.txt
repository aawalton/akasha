Don was no doctor, but he knew what that meant. He was bleeding out. That was bad. Real bad. Don got up against the boulder and examined the damage on his shoulder. The flesh was ripped and moving his arm caused pain. It was bleeding for sure, but it didn’t seem like an artery had been damaged.

“But even if I tie this wound, I have an infection. I don’t see any pharmacies around. Did I just get fucked just like that?”

That was a bitter pill to swallow. One that Don refused to do. There had to be a way.

“Wasn’t this… supposed to be a tutorial…?” He managed between gritted teeth. The system dinged.

[Tutorial Hint Activated: Use your racial ability [Devour] on the Vulpin.]

Slowly, Don moved to the body of the black fox-monster. He tried managing his status screen and selecting the [Devour] skill. It didn’t work like that. It took Don some trial and error, but finally he managed to turn the skill on. It was like finding the right muscle to use. Like wiggling your nose.

When the skill turned on, Don was immediately assaulted with an overpowering hunger. He did not think or hesitate. He simply threw himself on the Vulpin, ripping into its back with his fingers. His strength surprised him and he managed to break the fur and skin. The scent of warm and fresh meat overpowered him. He bit into the back muscles and felt a powerful surge of vitality. It was more than the blood and muscle he was ingesting. It was some kind of essence.

In fact, it seemed like eating the actual meat was not the main course. But the longer he fed, the longer he sucked in the nourishing pure vitality that had animated the monster. Once it was drained, Don sighed contently and wiped the blood off his face.

He looked at the shoulder the Vulpin had mained, and was not surprised at all to see the skin completely healed. Not even a scar remained.

[Racial Skill increased: Devour!]

[Devour Level 2]

[Evolution trait Vulpine: Consumed]

[8 Evolution Points acquired!]

[Health at 100%]

[Energy at 100%]

[Debuffs: N/A]

He felt great. Amazing, even. He looked at the Vulpin on the ground. Don was glad he hadn’t made a complete mess devouring the monster. Just a single clean flesh wound from where he had bitten into the beast. The Vulpin looked smaller, like it had dried up a little. Whatever that vitality essence was, it was real. And apparently the system had rewarded him with 8 Evolution Points, whatever the hell those were.

Don opened the system and looked at his other racial skill [Evolve]. It opened a menu that didn’t have much on it for the moment.

[Evolution Points: 8]

[Evolutions available to upgrade: N/A]

Don wasn’t sure how he was supposed to get evolutions, but from his limited experience with video games, he was starting to get an idea on how to get stronger.

“Fight stuff to level skills and race. Devour them for points. Get evolutions and upgrade said evolutions.”

He had most of it figured out now, which was nice. What the System Points were supposed to be and how he unlocked evolutions was another question.

It was getting darker by the minute. Don needed to move. He was feeling great and wanted to hunt, so he could use [Devour] again. Perhaps Evolutions worked the same way skills worked. He had acquired Stealth and Unarmed Fighting by using them. What was the difference between level 1 Stealth and level 2 Stealth was still to be determined. Before Don moved further, he allocated his 2 Attribute points.

He tried to ask the tutorial on what each stat meant, but it would not answer. Fortunately the stats were almost self-explanatory.

Power must govern physical strength and speed. Agility could also be speed, but since there are so few attributes, I think not. Agility must mean nimbleness, mobility, dexterity and all that. So Power for sprinting and punching, Agility for jumping and climbing trees, is my initial guess. Constitution is vague. Does it determine my health? Hardiness? Ability to resist poisons and damage? His physical endurance? All of them? I will need to find out. Energy is what it says on the tin. I move around, I get tired. I rest, it recuperates. I can be fairly sure of this because of how my energy got topped off after using [Devour]. It’s like I just had a good night’s sleep. Now what’s Mental then? Does it mean raw intelligence? Processing speed? Perception? Or is it some kind of magic thing, like mana? That’s usually a thing in games. Before I know more, I think I’ll just increase my Power and Agility.

Don put one point in each, doubling his Power and Agility from 1 to 2. The effect was almost immediate. There was a pleasant tingling sensation that was followed by a clear increase in his physical capability. His body felt a little bit lighter and a little bit stronger. Enthused by his new abilities, Don surged into a sprint through the forest.

In the dimming light, Don ran and grinned. He sprinted, jumping over branches and rocks with great speed. He had always figured his physique was normal. Don was not a slouch, but definitely not an athlete.

As he ran, he figured he might just be one soon.

[General Skill Acquired: Sprinting!]

[Sprinting Level 1]

Don stopped and checked his Energy. It had dropped to 80%. His breathing was heavy, but he knew he would be able to sprint again soon, if need be. His breath evened slowly as he watched his energy bar in the top left corner of his vision. Indeed, after twenty seconds or so, his Energy jumped to 81%.

To his amusement, Don realized he was having a great time. Some system had come and completely ruined everyone’s lives on Earth, including his. But Don genuinely did not mind. He welcomed the new reality. The world was clear and equal. Everyone was starting from level one, and there was a clear objective. Get levels to get stronger, and survive.

What happened to Baxter stung, and Don wondered if his buddy had died or survived. Probably died. But he had to push these thoughts away. There would be a time to mourn. For now he needed to focus on the now. He needed to get strong. The Vulpin had almost killed him, and there had to be worse things in this dark forest.

Don slowed down to listen to the forest as his heartbeat lowered. It evened out really fast. Way faster than he had been used to. Because of his heart condition, his heart rate shot up easily and was resistant to cool down. Now, just minutes after a full sprint, it was slowing down to a steady, calm rhythm.

“Did my heart condition just get fixed by system bullshit? I’ll take it.”

Soon enough Don saw some movement in the dark forest. A yellow glint from two eyes. They were bobbing up and down, and not looking at him. Don crept closer, until he saw a dark shape on four legs. It was another Vulpin. This one was slightly bigger than the last one. Don got fairly close, only ten feet away, before he hid behind a thick tree trunk.

The Vulpin was enjoying human intestines for dinner. The body of a young man in jeans and jacket lay on its back. The eyes were wide with shock and his mouth hung open. His head shook idly when the Vulpin ripped a chunk out.

Don found the sight distasteful, but he didn’t dwell on it. Instead he tried to circle around, so he could ambush the Vulpin when it was focused on its meal.

He crept along quietly, but soon he stepped on a twig. It was a small one, but Don cringed at the clear crunch it let out. The Vulpin must have heard it, but it didn’t seem to react, as it was still busying itself with eating the human it had killed.

“That’s odd. Animals should have good enough hearing to pick that up.”

For a moment Don wondered if these monsters were intelligent enough to feign not being aware to bait him into a reckless attack. Don banished the notion. He couldn’t operate under the assumption. His initial experience with a Vulpin had demonstrated that these were just a basic beast. Besides, if the monsters could think strategically, he never had hope to begin with.

After getting as close as eight feet behind the Vulpin’s back, Don was ready.

[General Skill increased: Stealth!]

[Stealth Level 2]

Don rushed at the Vulpin, aiming a savage soccer kick at its head. The Vulpin managed to turn, but not dodge. The kick connected and sent the snarling beast into a nearby bush, where it thudded against the ground.

This time Don would not give the damn monster a chance to wrest the initiative. He immediately dashed to close the distance, so the Vulpin couldn’t lunge at him. It tried to bite his ankle with the massive maw, but Don pulled the leg back and snapped another kick at the monster’s face.

It howled in pain and took a few paces back assessing its foe. The Power and Agility increase was noticeable. It gave Don confidence. He could do this.

Don watched its haunches. He had seen enough dogs fight, both playfully and seriously. The haunches generated the momentum for a charge. The beast snarled and snapped its jaws. The thick tongue rolled off its mouth, ready to entangle him.

Suddenly, the Vulpin jumped. It lunged at Don’s throat, but since Don had expected it, he brought his arm up. The Vulpin sank its teeth on the arm and wrapped the thick tongue around it. Blood spurted and Don was assaulted with eye-watering pain. But this was according to plan.

[Health at 84%]

[Energy at 88%]

[Debuffs: Moderate Bleeding, Mild Infection]

“I got you, bastard,” Don said through gritted teeth.

Immediately Don hugged the beast and slammed himself forward at the ground, leaving the beast under him. It growled, but did not let go. That was fine by Don. It tried to rip the flesh of his arm off, but Don kept the Vulpin’s head in place with his free hand, while he inched his knee between him and the frantically clawing back legs of the Vulpin. One knee slipped on one of the legs. Then Don turned and slipped the other knee on the creature’s belly. After that he pushed his free hand against the Vulpin’s throat and pressed himself towards the ground with all his weight. Slowly, but surely the creature suffocated. It thrashed rabidly, but Don had it pinned down well. His legs and stomach were full of minor scratches, and his arm was absolutely burning with pain.

But Don did not let go. He kept pressing and moment by moment the Vulpin’s struggles got weaker. After a minute or two it went limp. Don still kept squeezing, to make sure the beast wasn’t just unconscious. Soon enough, the notifications came.

[General Skill Increased: Unarmed Fighting!]

[Unarmed Fighting Level 2]

[You have slain [Vulpin – lvl 3] – You have gained experience for defeating an enemy. 3 System Points earned]

Certain of himself this time, Don knew what to do. His Health and Energy were going down and the arm was bleeding profusely. Not as bad as the shoulder, as the system suggested he had around an hour before incapacitation. Regardless, he activated the [Devour] ability, and bit into the Vulpin, draining it of its vitality. There was more of it this time, he was sure. It was just as sweet as the first time, and Don was disappointed when the essence was gone. However, his Energy and Health were topped up again and he gained some points.

[10 Evolution Points acquired!]

[Evolution trait Vulpine: Consumed]

Don sat down in the cool grass and wiped his mouth. He looked at his notifications.

What is this Evolution trait thing? Am I accruing something specific to Vulpins? I don’t feel any different.

Don looked at his System interface and tried to figure out what it was, but there was nothing. He shrugged and decided to move forward.

Energized by the vitality he had drained and the adrenaline and sweet sense of victory, Don looked around after he was done with the Vulpin. The forest was almost pitch black. Some slivers of moonlight punctured through the forest canopy, but it wasn’t much to go on. Don couldn’t see very far ahead of him. But that didn’t cause unease. He felt strong. And the thrill of fighting these beasts excited him. He was fairly sure that if he killed one or two more, he would level up again. That would strengthen him further. Maybe then he could even take on two Vulpins at a time. The possibilities opened up in his mind, but he had to stop fantasizing and focus. He should move soon.

The forest was quiet. There were distant shouts sometimes, clearly by humans. Don checked the tutorial screen from his system.

Designation for Don Damer: Tutorial Area 30011

Tutorial Contestants: 2975/3000

Objective: Survive

Length: 89d, 21h, 29m

Points Collected: 5

It hadn’t even been three hours and already twenty five people had died. Don wasn’t surprised. His first kill had been a scramble, and it had almost killed him. His racial ability was amazing. He could keep himself fighting and looking for enemies, without the need to rest. It also meant he could completely reframe what kinds of risks he could take. He could take almost fatal damage, as long as he won. He wasn’t sure how much the vital essence would heal him if he had his guts spilling out and his health around 50% or less. Don hoped he didn’t have to go through that experiment, but if he did, he had the three Health potions as a backup.

“Oh. The health potions,” Don muttered to himself.

He knelt next to the human the Vulpin had been eating and took his leather satchel. The Vulpin had damaged it, and the belt was slightly frayed, but Don took it anyway and put it on above his own belt.

In the satchel was one Health Potion and Two Energy potions, as well as a ration bar. The knife was gone. Don found the missing potions strange. Someone had already had to resort to using the potions? Well, the guy’s loss was Don’s gain. He made a note of remembering to loot any humans he might see.

Not that he saw much of anything right now. Don considered making a torch. He had two sets of flint and tinder, and he could find a stick and get cloth from the dead guy’s clothes. But a torch would only make him see so much. He would be double as blind after it was snuffed, and most importantly it was a big sign to monsters and hostile people saying “I’m here!”

Don started moving. He would get used to the darkness. As he moved through the foliage, brushing aside branches and searched for movement in his dim vision, he revisited the torch idea. On one hand, it might be a beacon for monsters and hostiles. But it could also invite regular people to band with him. It would be much easier to take out the monsters in a group. That was what humans were good at. Doing shit together.

But there were downsides to that. It was safer, sure, but in no way would it increase the amount of monsters around. The experience would have to be shared. That meant less leveling. Not to mention, the more people in a pack, the slower they moved. If Don hunted alone, he could go at his own pace. And because he didn’t have to worry about his Energy or Health, it would be downright idiotic to band up with people who would need to stop to rest and heal.

“Not to mention, that they probably wouldn’t be exactly thrilled about my [Devour] ability…”

That would most certainly be a problem, if and when he ran into humans. Don would definitely like to find some. He wasn’t sure to what degree Energy governed sleep, but he was sure he would need some eventually. It’d be a much more fun and safe experience to sleep, when someone was keeping watch.

Slower experience or not, he would need to play nice eventually.

But not yet.

Don moved through the brush, slow and deliberate, letting the forest speak. A rustle here, the faint chirp of a night insect there. And then Don sensed something. It was subtle and primal, but it was there, and Don chose to listen.

He crouched by a root-tangled log and waited. The seconds stretched, and the darkness seemed to lean in. Then he saw it.

A quadrupedal silhouette moved from tree to tree. Another Vulpin. This one was leaner than the last, but it was alert. Sniffing around. It looked in Don’s direction and faint yellow light reflected off the eyes in the darkness.

Don’s pulse quickened with anticipation. This was perfect. He wanted another fight. Another chance to improve and grow.

He circled wide, moving opposite the wind, staying low. Every motion calculated. Don took a deep breath and held it as he approached. When he was within striking range, he exploded forward, silent but fast.

The Vulpin reacted instantly, hurling itself to the side. Smart. It hadn’t even seen him until the last second.

Don didn’t hesitate. He pivoted and closed the gap attempting to stomp on the Vulpin’s back. The beast dodged, but Don had expected that. He smirked as his knee came up and caught it clean in the side.

The monster let out a bark and lashed back, claws raking the air. Don caught a shallow slash across his ribs—nothing deep, but enough to sting. He ignored it.

[Health at 92%]

It lunged, maw open, tongue flicking, teeth flashing. This Vulpin was more aggressive. It was going for the throat with its obscene tongue.

With his improved attributes, Don could sense it better now. He was quick enough. Don grabbed the tongue mid-whip, planted his foot, and yanked hard. The Vulpin thudded into the dirt with a surprised yelp. Don was on it instantly. Fists slamming. Elbows. A full-body press. He didn’t give it a second to think, to rally. The monster thrashed, but Don was a boulder, immovable and punishing.

A claw caught his cheek, and he tasted blood.

[Health at 86%]

Fine. Worth it.

With a growl of his own, Don forced his hand against its jaw, clamping it shut. His other hand reached back and grabbed a thick rock—he hadn’t even realized he’d noticed it earlier—and slammed it down against the creature’s skull.

Once. Twice. Again.

The monster finally stilled.

[You have slain [Vulpin – lvl 3] – You have gained experience for defeating an enemy. 4 System Points earned]

Don dropped the rock, panting. His hands were slick with blood—some of it his. The kill had been faster this time. Cleaner.

Still riding the high, he activated [Devour]. The now-familiar hunger came like a wave, and Don didn’t resist. He bit in deep.

The taste, the essence—stronger than before. It filled him with heat and clarity. His wounds began to knit. His energy surged. The system messages confirmed what he already felt.

[Health at 100%][Energy at 100%][Debuffs: N/A][12 Evolution Points acquired!][Evolution trait Vulpine: Evolved!]

Don sat back and wiped his mouth with the back of his hand. He glanced around, but the night no longer pressed in the same way. The forest was still dark—but it wasn’t empty.

He blinked. And blinked again. The shadows peeled back.

Contours sharpened. Contrast deepened. His eyes caught movement in the branches, small creatures watching from the high trees. He could see their outlines now. Clear, distinct.

“What is this?” Don whispered.
