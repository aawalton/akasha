Now we’re really running out of time. I have to focus more on holding the space together than staying in place – I begin sliding backwards. This will be over soon enough, but I want to give Tilathis as much time as possible to consider my words.

Finally, when I’ve already been pushed so close to the end point that I can no longer feel the nunda’s emotions and his mental voice is quiet, he responds.

You are an odd human. I do not believe you entirely but…I am willing to give you the chance to prove yourself.

With satisfaction mixed with relief, I finally release my hold on the space, returning to an awareness of my body – the pounding headache and blood trail from my nose included.

I really need to figure out a better way of doing this, I grouse to myself, even as I pull on Flesh-Shaping to heal the aftereffects. Still, as I look at the still-thoughtful nunda trapped in the pit, and the still-tense nunda crouched between us, I decide that it’s a fair price to pay if it means that this family isn’t ripped apart needlessly.
