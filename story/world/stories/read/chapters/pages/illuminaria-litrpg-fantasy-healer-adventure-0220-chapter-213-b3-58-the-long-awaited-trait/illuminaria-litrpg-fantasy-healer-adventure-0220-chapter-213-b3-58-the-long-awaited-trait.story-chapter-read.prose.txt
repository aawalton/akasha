Chapter 213 / B3-58: The Long-Awaited Trait

‘WHAT IS THIS EGG YOU BROUGHT BEFORE ME, DORIAN?’

You have resisted being {Dazed} by a psychic deluge.

Your skill [Stun Block] has increased to rank 20.

The countless hours of Yuk’s gentle telepathic commentary did nothing to prepare Joe for the mental bullhorn that was the voice of the phoenix. Even so, he was better off than Kenda, who was reeling from the hammering force of the shrill mental query. He tossed a [Purge] her way to stop her from falling over.

You have removed the {Dazed} condition from Kendell Bracey.

‘I CANNOT SEE INSIDE ITS SHELL, YET IT BEARS A SPARK, A HINT OF THE FIRE OF LIFE.’

“You are being discourteous, Pyrrhos,” Dorian snapped in a steely tone. “There is no need to batter us so with your voice. Speak in civil tones, or I will revoke this offer and add to your debt for failure to negotiate in a respectful manner.”

He then softened his voice to add, “He is one of the hidden folk of the fey, shielded from scrying yet adaptable enough to accept gifts of other races. This one, Joe, is particularly well-suited for gifts from your noble heritage.”

The phoenix’s next telepathic words were still uncomfortably loud, but they no longer were quite so overwhelming as they had been.

‘I CAN SENSE REBIRTHS, AND IT WIELDS A HATCHLING’S FLAME. IT DOES NOT YET HAVE ANY TRUE LIFE-FIRE. IT IS NOT UNWORTHY OF OUR HERITAGE. I WOULD SEE THIS EGG BECOME A CHICK.’

“And settle your debt,” the leprechaun drawled.

‘OBVIOUSLY,’ the phoenix replied tersely. Its flaming face turned back to Joe, and its voice, while still commanding, was somewhat more gentle, like a parent speaking to a child. An authoritative parent, for sure, but still, it was the most care the blazing elemental being had shown so far. ‘LOOK UPON ME, MORTAL, AND SEE WHAT COULD BE YOURS.’

A sheet of flames burst into existence. Within the searing plane of fire were words. Most of them were indistinct, charcoal smudges hiding in a vertical writhing inferno. Two lines were clearly readable: the legendary creature’s two racial traits.

TRAITS

[Ashen Rebirth] (Racial) - Upon receiving fatal damage, resurrect in a burst of flame with full health restored. This rebirth creates a new instance of you, instilling minor to moderate alterations to your pattern. Cooldown: Extended

[Heart of Fire] (Racial - Evolution) - You gain the Phoenix Bloodline. This trait will transmute your pattern and unlock avenues for future development

Hawking had warned Joe to be careful with his pattern, and yet here he was being offered two different bounties that would both inject changes to his essential essence.

He was less interested in the ability phoenixes were known for, [Ashen Rebirth]. He already had two different get-out-of-death abilities. While they didn’t raise him back to full health in the process, they also didn’t alter who Joe was each time they saved his bacon.

[Heart of Fire] was the one that really drew his attention. A bloodline? Whatever that meant, it would be significant, since the Mark of Death on his arm was vibrating with eagerness for the evolutionary trait.

But what would it mean to be given a completely new bloodline? Would he lose changeling as a race? Would he no longer even be tentatively a fey being? This could be a trick by the wheeling-and-dealing leprechaun to remove Joe from contention for the crown. Joe thought he had been pretty damn clear that he didn't want the Mantle of Spring for any longer than he had to to break the chimera curses, but maybe Dorian wasn’t taking any chances.

He was frozen by uncertainty for only a second because the one person he trusted to answer these questions was standing right beside him, and they had prepared for this. He brushed his finger over the tattoo Ello had etched between his eyebrows. Kenda already had her eyes closed with her paired ink-mark showing on one of her closed lids. Once activated, the tattoo would allow her to see what Joe saw for the next five minutes, as long as she had the tattooed eye shut.

After barely a second had passed, her eyes snapped open. Joe held up two fingers to her, indicating the second trait, the one he wanted. She agreed. Oh boy, did she agree. The skill savant nodded her head so vigorously that Joe heard her neck crack. If doing this somehow removed changeling from him, then Kenda must have thought the trade-off was worth it.

‘Okey dokey. Here goes,’ Joe thought to himself. He accepted [Heart of Fire].

And that is what he got. Flames poured off Pyrrho’s sheet and dove into his chest. He could feel his body being set alight by the flames. The heat and pressure around his heart doubled and then tripled. Joe fortified his will, expecting to feel the agony of being burned alive, and yet even though every aspect of himself was being doused with fire, there was no pain at all. There was a comfort to this exchange that was completely unexpected. He had the weirdest impression, like he had just pulled his favorite outfit out of the clothes dryer and was putting it on while it was still nice and toasty warm.

Nestled into those flames, Joe found a presence taking shape. It was as fierce as the wildness, but prouder, more regal. He could sense the multiple aspects of himself perking up to greet this newcomer. The Mark of Death was so dense with eagerness it was pulling his arm down, making Joe feel like he was holding a heavy gym weight. The wildness rumbled a fervent greeting as well. The soulbound spirit pack was in awe of the newcomer. Even the shifting [Sign Spring’s Aspirant] seemed to take notice of the bloodline.

Joe fully expected the phoenix to find a spot inside his cohort of identities and companions. So he was completely unprepared for the fiery essence to launch into an attack. The burning bloodline didn’t go after Joe himself. Instead, the flames of the phoenix surged through his veins, searing their way to Joe’s right forearm and enveloping the Mark of Death. It was clear the bloodline was trying to burn away the prophetic mark.

“What the hell!’ Joe hissed, clamping his other hand around the two marks, the Thirteenth Omen under attack and the Spring sigil. The watercolor flower felt like it was trying to shift itself away from the raging heat trying to incinerate the Mark of Death.

Joe healed, but nothing happened. This pain was not physical; it was spiritual, the one category Joe had no resistance to. Joe was already standing within a [Halefire], so his own phoenix fire was clearly not going to help. Joe had tools like [Purge] or [Curse Cleaver] which might stop the assault, but Joe didn't want to lose the bloodline; he wanted it to behave and stop being such a dick.

Joe fell to his knees and curled himself around the scalding agony filling his arm. As sweat poured off him, the Phyrros’s voice thundered in his head once again.

‘WHY IS IT REJECTING THE TEMPERING, DORAIN? DOES IT WISH TO REMAIN FLAWED?’

“How in Blu’s beauty would I know, Phyrros. Why not ask him?” the leprechaun lord replied saltily.

‘NO NEED. EITHER THE CHICK WILL SURVIVE THE TEMPERING OR NOT. IF IT DOES, THEN WE WILL MEET AGAIN. REGARDLESS, MY DEBT IS PAID.’

Joe felt the great bird’s attention shift away from him as the pain in his arm dug even deeper around the glittering tattoo. Phyrros addressed Dorian, once more. ‘FAREWELL, SCHEMER. SHOULD WE CROSS PATHS AGAIN, I WILL KNOW BETTER THAN TO TRY TO BARGAIN WITH THEE. IF YOU WOULD DO ME A COURTESY, SEND ME WORD IF THE EGG HATCHES OR DIES.’

There was a blast of heat that Joe could only imagine was the phoenix launching himself away from the balcony, and the next thing he felt was Kenda’s hands cupping the back of his neck.

“What can I do?” she whispered to him, worry filling her voice.

“Tempering?” Joe hissed through clenched teeth. “What is?”

“It's a way of strengthening your pattern,” Kenda answered quickly. “Like working glass. You heat the material to refine it, work out impurities, then control how it cools so it doesn't crack under its own stresses. Tempering does something similar to your body or spirit. It forces your pattern to endure something that would normally damage it, until it learns to withstand the strain.”

“And if it doesn't?”

“Then it breaks.” Kenda tightened her grip on his neck. “Joe, I’ve read that tempering is rough, but this seems excessive. Tempering usually doesn't happen until our thirties,” she said, which Joe knew meant levels, not years. “I can't think of anyone in their teens who's undergone tempering. Maybe that's what's wrong.”

“No,” Joe groaned. “Bloodline attacking Mark of Death. Spirit damage.”

“Then you need to face it. Just hold on, Bo,” she breathed in his ear before planting a kiss on the crown of his head. As she dashed off, Joe was surprised by the endearment, but it was taking everything he had not to scream. He had found a grip on the blazing flames inside his body. While most of the fire remained focused on the Mark of Death, he could feel lesser flames spreading through the rest of him. That part wasn’t so bad. It felt like the less hostile heat was merging with the low-level healing he was always subconsciously performing on himself. Throughout his body, the heat was finding small flaws it didn't like. Each was seared away with a prick of burning pain, but Joe could handle those little bursts without much trouble.

It was the major bug up the bloodline's ass over the prophetic mark that was the real problem. And Joe wasn't sure how much longer he could hold them apart.

Kenda’s footsteps returned. “Joe. You’ve been to the spirit plane before. We can send you back there now, if you want us to. It should give you a chance to integrate the bloodline into your pattern while your body tempers. There is a spell circle here on the balcony. What do you say? Yes? No?”

Joe barely processed her words, but it sounded like she had a plan. And this was Kenda; he’d trust her plans any day.

“Do it,” he rasped as the heat in his arm redoubled. Apparently, the bloodline understood they were moving to thwart it and wasn't the least bit interested in cooperating.

Joe felt himself sliding across the smooth marble balcony. He heard Dorian begin an incantation, and the world dropped out from under him.
