Ashton parsed through the long mass of information. The spell that he had just cast brought him everything that he needed. However it really was a lot. It was basically a short book's worth of text, mostly because there were a lot of useless things in here. While there was everything that he needed here, there wasn't only what he needed.

Random data that was accidentally dragged along through the veil. That seemed to be the biggest issue with this spell, and why it still required a lot of effort to make contact with and learn about a new potential summon.

You had to isolate exactly the parts that you needed and get rid of the rest. It was probably pretty complicated for most, but for Ashton? It was shockingly easy.

Most summoners probably weren't all too versed in things like enchanting, and definitely not to the degree that they were able to accurately sense a mana pattern the way that Ashton was. So, for him, by simply matching the different mana patterns, he was able to sort all the information.

And it wasn't like it was a completely black and white ordeal, either. A lot of this information was rumours or stories about Alice and her predecessor that held the modifier Daughter of the Scarlet Heart before her.

Things that were fairly important for a Summoner—they had to know about what kind of summon they were going to try and contract after all—but weren't directly useful to create an actual contract circle.

Then there were things about hell and other demons. Blood demons in general were mentioned a lot. It was information that Ashton knew to some degree already, though. Blood demons were essentially the origin of vampires. They weren't vampires directly, but rather, they were a step above them. Extremely powerful demons were sometimes able to travel to other realms. And in the case of Blood demons, when they did, they sometimes created servants in those realms, birthing the first 'Vampires'.

These were things Ashton already knew, but having some more details in general, and learning about the current state of hell, was actually not a bad thing, either. There also seemed to be some information in here that he could use as a branching point to learn more about other demons using that summoner spell. This seemed to be the main way that summoners spread out their reach, and also why most summoners specialised with specific sort of summons.

Most of the time, they simply knew vastly more about type of summon due to the way that this knowledge-acquisition worked.

It would be useful for Ashton as well, and he made sure to keep it all in mind. Maybe he should try and use this search spell on some of his old friends. That might not be the worst idea.

But for now, it was time for him to build the contract circle for his little sister. His thousands-of-years-old, constellation, blood demon little sister.

Ashton had managed to isolate all the useful information. Sigils, specific information on her abilities and feats, the runes that described her species; things like that.

With all the information parsed to its fullest, it was time for Ashton to move on. It wasn't like he could actually draw that contract circle yet, nor did he actually have all the summoning ingredients.

For a blood demon, obviously, a huge amount of blood was needed. Finding the right monsters for that was also important. For someone like Alice, it couldn't just be anyone's blood. Something like elite or boss monsters were the best option here.

From what he found, he would also have to offer a fair amount of his own blood. That was rough, but with the right potions, he could make that happen pretty easily. He would probably refine his blood into the base for the ink in the first place.

However, it wasn't like Ashton was just dealing with a regular old demon. He was dealing with a demonic constellation. And for that, it seemed like he would need something that was similar to the 'Crystalised Veil Fragment'; the piece that was left behind from the mana well that Ashton destroyed.

It didn't say anything specific, so maybe that veil fragment was already enough, but it just didn't feel quite right. The crystalised veil fragment was something that seemed to be created at the core of every mana well; it was the place where the distance between the veil and the material plane was the thinnest.

But when Ashton saw mana wells from this side, what was floating there in the place of that circular 'point' was similar, but not the same at all, to that fragment. It was like it was just one half of it.

So, right now, it was time for Ashton to go on a scouting trip and find out some more about that other 'half'. He had already taken a look at one of them when he was here last time, but his trip ended up being cut short.

He was going to avoid the wyvern this time. He didn't want it to chase him around again, because this time, he wouldn't be able to trap it somewhere again, at least not that easily.

But instead, Ashton made his way to a whole other part of town; the Costco!

Since he had found the crystalised veil fragment there, Ashton figured it might be the best place to look into the rest he was hoping to find.

Luckily, in this space, the trip to the costco was terribly easy. He had gone here last time as well and found the frost elementals, many of which were still very happily playing around for now, not having even reached the 'lesser' stage yet, though their numbers had decreased a good bit as the environment recovered. It seemed like the magic of the veil made the snow stick around a lot longer than it would have otherwise, but it still wasn't permanent.

If Ashton wanted to consider forming a contract with one of these guys, he should do it soon. It was easier to form contracts with elementals that actually existed in the vicinity.

But for now, that wasn't his focus. Unlike last time, Ashton made his way into the costco. He could see the traces of the 'outpost' that had been set up here to properly sort through, take care of, and distribute everything in here. Ashton should visit sometime; it was possible that there were some special items or shop-like things hidden in here.

Ashton made his way to the place where the ritual circle that the draconid had set up was. This was exactly the spot where Ashton had shattered the mana well. And to his surprise... there was a trace.

An imprint of that exact moment. Of Ashton hitting the veil fragment with the incomplete technique in the middle of the battle with the draconid.

Ashton wasn't sure what exactly he did, though. Though it was technically the core-shattering technique, he didn't know how it could possibly interfere with the veil like that. Maybe it had something to do with Ashton's power as a Summoner that boosted the power and let him interfere using a technique, but he would have to investigate it later.

For now, he was more focused on that thing that was floating in the air, right at the tip of Ashton's staff.

He reached out to it. The moment his fingertip touched it, though, the orb fell down into his hand. It was weird. This was the first somehow 'solid' thing here in the veil.

Just like the crystalised veil fragment, this piece was halfway between the veil and the material plane, though this seemed a little more aligned with the material plane. It felt like a step more solid than the elementals that lived within the veil.

Ashton 'tagged' this with the Pen of Insight. He wasn't sure if it would work, but if it did, that was already good. Either way, Ashton would bring this back to Calibur and would try to see if he could interfere with it somehow from the material plane. At the very latest, he would be able to inspect it then.

And then, with this piece picked up, Ashton returned to Calibur. There were a lot of other things he wanted to look at, but somehow, he felt kind of weird. Being in the veil too long was exhausting in a way. Not in any physical way, not even mentally, but it was like his mana was feeling lethargic.

Going back and resting was a good idea. Maybe he should be more careful with astral projection in the future.

And so, Ashton placed the other fragment down beside him in the tent, and then slowly sunk back into his body.

This time, he really drifted off into sleep. But he was still conscious. It was the same process as last time. It seemed like they were waiting for Ashton to enter the dreamscape. Dreams were a special thing, especially now that this world had mana and the veil.

And while the dreamscape wasn't exactly another realm, it was something that could be magically accessed and entered if you knew what to do. And if you were a powerful spirit, it was obviously quite easy.

So, as the brief disorientation of becoming conscious within a dream settled down, Ashton realised that he found himself on the moon.

Really, the moon. He was looking over at the earth right now, standing underneath the black sky. His body felt lighter, too.

"Neat choice," Ashton said. He turned, with a smile on his face. In front of him stood a woman in grand yellow robes. They covered her whole body, and her face was hidden behind a sort of veil with her clan's symbol placed on top of it.

No part of her was exposed in the slightest. But even so, Ashton recognised her instantly anyway, of course. Even though her robes were vastly more extravagant than before, there was really only one clan that dressed like this.

"Lady in Yellow. So you were the next one?" Ashton smiled brightly.

"Of course. Though the Mask Maker got in ahead of me, I couldn't let up the opportunity to be the second to meet you," the Lady in Yellow said. It wasn't the first time that Ashton had heard her voice, but the last time, she spoke far more meekly. She wasn't exactly shy, but the Ladies in Yellow were a small, yet powerful, clan. Even the weakest and youngest were born at middle grade. They were extremely proud, and the Summoner had broken that pride completely.

She was actually one of the summons that came after Ashton, and he had gotten quite close to her, maybe because they could actually communicate properly.

"I saw the message that you left on the staff's case. I was happy that you didn't forget about me."

"Forget about you? Who do you think you're talking to?" the Lady in Yellow smugly replied. "I'm the Elder of my clan now, after all."

"Didn't expect anything less," Ashton replied, standing in front of her. "So... it's been a long time, but–"

Before Ashton could even finish asking for one, the Lady in Yellow exposed her gloved hands and pulled him in closely into a hug.

"I've missed you so much, Ashton," the Lady in Yellow's fingers dug into his clothes. She soon pulled away, placing her hand onto Ashton's cheek. "And I'm so terribly sad that now that you finally returned, you have ended up in such a state."

Ashton nodded. "It's... it's not the best. But it's okay. I'm already formulating a recipe for some new eyes. I've got bigger issues at hand, really."

"I know."

"Of course you do. If there's anyone that would know, it's a Lady in Yellow. As the Elder, is there anything that you don't know?" Ashton scoffed, and the Lady in Yellow just stood there.

"There are few things that stay hidden even to me. However, I am learning. Now that your world has reconnected to the veil, I am parsing the records to learn what happened."

Ashton raised his brows. "Parsing the records... Lady in Yellow, I know we don't have a contract, but–"

The spirit shook her head immediately. "I cannot let you access the records yourself. Without the filter of a summoner's contract, it would be too much for your mind. Especially for a mind like yours that refuses to let go of anything it takes in."

"Of course... right, sorry, I should have known," Ashton apologised. "But well..."

"I can still tell you certain things. I'm sure you have plenty of questions for me. About the other summons, for example. About Alice."

Ashton nodded without hesitation. "Yes! Yes, please! The Mask Maker told me a lot already, but I figure you would have a lot more details."

"That I do," the Lady in Yellow responded. "However, before then, I would like to reveal something else to you."

The Lady in Yellow slowly grabbed Ashton's hand as he listened to her. "Just like the Mask Maker, I would like to bestow my true name onto you."
