The shanty castle turned out to be a dark, smoke and fire filled wooden labyrinth.

The narrow corridors sprawled into all directions, and soon Don realized the castle was much larger inside than it had looked outside. System bullshit being System bullshit as always.

Still, Don was excited, and his Night Vision was good enough to see in the dark corridors. After a few turns and dead-ends, Don found two more Grimoid hobgoblins walking side by side in the corridor, absolutely blocking it. You could barely squeeze a coin through their shoulders. Don crouched in a corner and turned his camouflage on.

He trusted his Stealth. Even if it failed to give him a surprise attack, he could take two head on. Their spears would definitely be the ideal weapon in such a narrow passageway, but Don had versatile options with his Telekinesis.

But the two monsters simply turned the corner, and the latter one exposed his back to Don.

Don lunged.

In his charge he flicked his claws open and went for the neck. He sliced into the thick flesh, but the hobgoblin turned and roared. It was immediately ready for battle, as if it had expected for someone to jump at it. It spun with its shield, trying to slam it into Don.

The shield clipped Don’s ribs, hard, knocking him sideways into the wall. It didn’t matter. His claws were in the thing’s neck and it was bleeding.

The second hobgoblin reacted instantly. Spear thrust, no hesitation. It came low, aiming to skewer Don against the wall like a kebab.

Don ducked under it, twisted, and yanked the first hobgoblin by the neck into its buddy’s attack path. The spear slammed through flesh with a wet crunch. The first hobgoblin screamed, more in rage than pain, as it shoved off the spear and turned, clutching at its new wound.

Don dropped low and slashed the back of the spear-wielder’s knee. Flesh tore. Blood spilled. The hobgoblin staggered.

Now the corridor was his.

He surged forward, raking claws across the wounded leg. The hobgoblin tried to pivot with its spear, but it was off-balance. Don caught the shaft mid-swing, pinned it with Telekinesis, and twisted the thing’s own weapon into its face. It smacked the creature’s forehead and gave Don just enough of an opening to jam both claws into the side of its neck.

It collapsed.

Don turned just in time to catch the first hobgoblin’s shield square in the chest. He flew back, smashed into the wall, cracked a plank behind him.

[Health at 91%]

Not bad. These guys were not completely mindless. The first one had yanked a handaxe off its belt and was charging now, ignoring the blood running down its neck.

Don grabbed one of the many blowpipe darts tucked into his satchel, sent it flying with his mind, but the hobgoblin batted it away with the shield. It raised the axe, readying a full-body swing.

Don waited until the last moment, ducked under the axe, and bodychecked the Grimoid into the wall. It slammed into the wooden support post hard enough to shake the torch hanging above them.

Then Don dropped and swept its legs. The hobgoblin toppled backwards. Don used Telekinesis to push it backwards to seal the deal.

Don pounced on it and drove a claw into its side just under the ribs, then the other into its armpit to keep it from lifting the shield again. The creature screamed, bucked, smashed the back of its head into Don’s chin.

Stars danced in his vision. Didn’t matter.

Don shoved his weight down, used a flare of Telekinesis to pin the creature’s limbs and drove his claws deeper.

The hobgoblin struggled hard for a second. Then something gave.

Don felt the pop in his wrists as the claws slid through.

[You have slain [Grimoid Hobgoblin – lvl 12] – You have gained experience for defeating an enemy. 47 System Points earned]

He pulled free, blood hissing and steaming on his claws. His face was cut, lip swollen, but he was grinning. This was good. These bastards could fight. Not just well, but with some kind of structure. They weren’t like the Grimoid swarm-fodder. These moved with intent. Discipline.

Don took a breath, wiped blood off his chin.

Then Devoured both.

[172 Evolution Points acquired!] [Health at 100%] [Energy at 100%] [Debuffs: N/A] [Evolution trait Grimoid Hobgoblin 2x: Consumed]

“Alright…” he muttered, licking his split lip. “Let’s go find a few more.”

The corridor creaked behind him.

Don turned and saw another pair coming. These two didn’t hesitate. They were already moving in sync. One carried a spear and net. The other had twin short blades. Both had armor strapped to their chests and legs, makeshift but effective. Chain links and leather scavenged from somewhere. They looked like they’d been through a few scraps and walked out grinning.

Don dropped into a crouch.

The spearman hurled the net.

Don caught it with Telekinesis mid-air and flung it backwards. Not just away, but into the shortblade hobgoblin, who stumbled and hissed as the net tangled around his arms.

Don sprinted forward.

The spearman dropped the second weapon and drew a hooked dagger, trying to intercept Don’s charge. But Don had the edge in speed. His claws flashed in an upward arc and tore open the Grimoid’s stomach. Blood hit the walls. The creature didn’t go down, it struck out wildly, blade carving through air. Don leaned back just far enough to miss it.

Then he stepped forward and rammed both claws under the thing’s ribcage and yanked upward.

It crumpled.

[You have slain [Grimoid Hobgoblin – lvl 12] – You have gained experience for defeating an enemy. 43 System Points earned]

He turned.

The other hobgoblin had cut himself loose from the net and came at him fast, blades whirling. Don fell into rhythm. Blocked the first blade with Telekinesis, ducked the second, caught the thing’s elbow with his own arm and jammed a claw into its gut.

But this one didn’t stop.

It bit Don. Right in the collarbone.

It fucking bit him.

[Health at 89%]

[Debuff inflicted: Mild Bleeding]

Don howled in pain and grabbed the thing by the face and slammed it into the wall. His shoulder was bleeding profusely, and he wanted some payback.

Once.

Twice.

Three times. That one had a satisfying crunch.

The monster let go.

Don took a step back and punched it square in the jaw. The creature’s head snapped sideways. It staggered, dazed, and Don didn’t wait. He tackled it, dragged it to the floor, and started slicing into its torso until it stopped moving.

[You have slain [Grimoid Hobgoblin – lvl 12] – You have gained experience for defeating an enemy. 46 System Points earned]

He Devoured both without ceremony.

This was good. These enemies were a challenge. He wanted more. Three at a time.

Don wondered why they didn’t sound the alarm or call for help. What kind of a castle wasn’t alerted when an intruder was inside? He shrugged. It was probably some kind of dungeon-System-nonsense-kind-of-thing. He didn’t mind.

The situation gave him an opportunity to test his new Camouflage ability and maybe get a Stealth level. The Camouflage seemed to work really well. At least in this lighting against these enemies. But Don could definitely feel it was affecting his Stealth through his Meta-awareness. He knew he should look into that concept too at some point, but he would do it when he had forced downtime. Right now he was a fighter, not a philosopher.

Don took another deep breath, feeling the air shake with the steady drumbeat further inside. It was getting hotter the further Don ventured into the shanty castle labyrinth. Soon the drumbeat got louder and he started feeling the deep thuds in his body. He kept turning towards the sounds until he saw the narrow corridor widen. The wooden floor creaked as he stepped forwards into an opening. He immediately activated his Camouflage when he realized he was standing on a balcony.

Don peered down.

The castle hall was cavernously large. There were dozens of Grimoid Hobgoblins sitting at the long tables. Braziers were burning everywhere and the monsters drank from tankards and ate meat, cutting them with brutal cleavers.

On the other side of the room, on a giant throne sat a massive Grimoid. It was still bowlegged and hunched like the rest of them. The knobby skin and the yellow eyes were there. But the sheer mass of this monstrosity was staggering. It was almost as big as Blazeclaw. It was hard to say because it was sitting, but Don estimated it was at least ten feet tall.

On its head was a wooden crown and leaning against the throne was a massive wooden club, reinforced with iron. Don used Identify.

[Homass, King of Grimoids, Level 14 Dungeon Boss]

Don grinned.

Oh, this is going to be fun.
