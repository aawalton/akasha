“I believe that I have an idea that could allow us to bolster the Sun Queen’s assault,” Phatagin explained, once he had everyone’s attention.

“Teem is ready to assist,” Ser Teem promised, nodding in recognition of the Squire’s initiative.

“Oh, well, that is to say, I do not know the extent of your abilities, Ser Teem, but thank you for the offer,” the pangolin responded before turning to Phasima.

“In my opinion, the biggest synergy between your powers and Charlemagne’s involves fire: both of you can summon heat using your Cores.  Working together, you could intensify the solar beam that the Sun Queen is emitting, allowing her to overpower the mana shield that protects the invaders.”

“That ‘solar beam’ she’s using is composed of superheated plasma!” Phasima objected.  “It’s far hotter than anything I can conjure up.”

Charlemagne took another look at the Sun Queen’s retina-scarring attack.  There was a lot of heat, yes, but it seemed like much of the energy was being converted to light, which meant that it was being wasted.  The heat itself, however, was being almost perfectly contained within the beam.  He didn’t know how to covert the light back into heat, but he felt that he could do something to raise the temperature of the channeled energy.  At least a bit.  But something instinctual told him that likely wouldn’t be the best use of his resources.

Turning his attention to the mana shield itself, the rooster couldn’t help but notice that the frequency of the barrier seemed to have been dialed in with the Sun Queen’s mana signature in mind.  That meant that the shield would be particularly effective against Elidi’s mana.

“Bawk?” he asked Phasima.

There was a moment of silence as the Vessarian considered Charlemagne’s proposal before responding.

“That…that might just work.  But how are you going to alter the mana signature of that much power all at once?”

“Bawk,” the rooster admitted.

“I think we should at least hear Phatagin’s idea first, then.  Maybe a bit more oomph will get us through that barrier,” Phasima decided.

“Bawk?” Charlemagne demanded.

“All right, all right, there’s no need to get salty.  Let’s see what Phatagin has thought of,” the former Empress of the Forest suggested.

“Thank you,” the pangolin jumped in before Charlemagne could further object.  “Now, I suggest that Phasima channel as much heat as she is able to and direct her flames at Charlemagne.  Since our…er…fearless leader possesses a fiery physique, the heat should empower him, magnifying his attack power and allowing him to add his own stream of thermal energy to the Sun Queen’s assault.”

“All right, that sounds interesting and all,” Phasima responded after considering the idea, “but wouldn’t it be more efficient if we just combined our attacks with my sister’s directly?  Why go through a complicated process when we don’t need to?”

“It would be kind of neat?” the pangolin offered.

Phasima sighed.

“Listen, my sister is fighting for her life over there.  I appreciate that you’re trying to help, but let’s focus up here.  I need ideas that will actually help!  Charlemagne, are you willing to aid my sister in breaking down that barrier?”

The rooster considered the situation before nodding.

“Good,” the Vessarian continued.  “Teem, do you have anything that would be suitable here?”

“Hmmm,” the strange humanoid said thoughtfully.  “Perhaps if Teem had some high-quality Astral Sand and a smelter…”

“I’m a living smelter, but Astral Sand?  Never heard of it,” Phasima responded with a shake of her head.  “So, it’s just me and the crazy bird, per usual.  Got it.  Come, Charlemagne, let’s show these goons the meaning of heat.  Aim for the spot where Elidi’s beam is hitting the shield.”

“Bawk!” the rooster agreed.

Charlemagne drew a slow breath, feeling his Ignition Core quicken in response to his will.  Mana and heat twinned together in a double helix as they raced through his mana channels, feeding Inferno’s Embrace as his internal body temperature soared.  The air shimmered around him as his companions, save for Phasima, pulled away from the incredible heat that wreathed him like a living shroud.

But Phasima, now that she was no longer shackled by the constraints of the Delve, was a mistress of flames herself.  While her Manifold Core wasn’t specialized towards fire the way that Charlemagne’s Core was, she could channel the fury of any element with a mastery that few could match.  Her translucent body took on a reddish sheen as her Core glowed crimson inside her, and steam rose as her body purged itself of excess water.  That was short-lived phenomenon, however, because the incredible heat dispersed the steam in mere seconds.

“On three!” Phasima shouted to the rooster.  “One…two…three…go!”

Charlemagne released his flames on three, as requested.  With a caw that seemed to emanate from his very soul, he unleashed an inferno in the general direction of the enemy.  A second later, Phasima unleashed her attack, throwing her hands forward as a jet of flame joined Charlemagne’s.  The two attacks merged into a towering pyre that turned the Sun Queen’s throne room into a veritable hellscape.

“I said on three!” the Vessarian yelled.

“Bawk!” Charlemagne objected.

“It’s understood that you say ‘one, two, three, go!’” she retorted.

“I beg your pardon,” Phatagin yelled over the crackling flames, “but didn’t you just tell me to ‘focus up’?”

“Not helping!” Phasima roared, her anger translating into a burst of effort that served to intensify her flames.

Although the flames obscured his vision, Charlemagne had no difficulty tracking the status of the enemy’s barrier through his mana senses.  Although much of the heat was merely raising the ambient temperature rather than attacking the mana shield directly, that was helpful in its own way.  The cube-shaped shield was constantly reinforcing itself by diverting power to the focal point of the Sun Queen’s assault.  With the temperature inside the throne room reaching levels that were hazardous to even high-level combatants, the shield was stressed at all points simultaneously.  In fact, it was so hot that the Elite Guards that were assaulting the shield were beginning to falter.

“Guards, fall back!” the Sun Queen commanded, before turning her attention to the balcony.  “I appreciate the support, but…sister?!  Is that you?”

“It’s me!” Phasima called back.  “We’re helping!”

Sure enough, the shield was beginning to show signs of degradation.  The entire structure flared up as it was forced to expend more and more energy to protect those inside it from the murderous heat.  Even better, the spot where the three attacks converged was glowing deep red.  If the trio could keep up the assault for just a few more minutes, they’d be through, at least if Charlemagne’s mystical senses were accurate.

“Slave of the System, your reinforcements have arrived too late,” a gravelly voice called from inside the inferno.  “But have no fear: you will not suffer once we strip stripped the System from this dimension.  Not for long, I hope.  And I will pray for your souls myself…although it is more than you deserve.”

“Save your prayers!” Elidi spat as she poured more power into the solar beam with a fury that Charlemagne had rarely seen in other creatures.  Her renewed assault chewed through the last layers of the barrier protecting the interlopers from her searing radiance.

“Bawk!” Charlemagne shouted encouragingly.

He and Phasima put their all into the assault as well, forcing the Teems and Phatagin to further retreat into the corridor to escape from the oven-like environment.  But through the crackling of flames and the deafening roar of the Sun Queen’s plasma, the sound of fevered chanting rose in both volume and fervor as the invaders performed a complicated ritual.  Charlemagne felt the mana within the barrier begin to swirl in a vortex that seemed familiar somehow but also very strange.

Then the rooster felt something yank on his very essence.  It was agonizing, but, even worse, it was a pain that he wasn’t very familiar with.  He heard both Phasima and the Sun Queen’s screams over the sound of his own anguished squawk.

Their attacks began to falter, although Charlemagne, for some reason, didn’t seem to be as affected as the two Vessarians.  But the rooster didn’t have time to be curious about that particular turn of events, because the Squiggles arrived with an update just then.

System Quest Updated: The Partial System lockout has progressed, and a Full System lockout is imminent.  Defeat the caster of the ritual immediately to end the threat and restore full System access.  Rewards have been doubled.

Reward: Variable based on performance

The barrier shield that had been protecting the invading force began to shrink in size, restoring itself as it sacrificed volume for greater protection over the remaining area.  Charlemagne squawked in outrage as all of the group’s hard work was undone in mere moments.

The Sun Queen was the first to cease her attack, although whether it was due to despair or simply a change of tactics remained unclear to Charlemagne.

Phasima pulled her flames back moment’s later, allowing the heat to return to her Manifold Core in an effort to recharge her depleted energy stores.

The rooster, for his part, was still in good condition despite the strange weakness that had accompanied the injury to his very essence.  The enemy was attacking the connection point between his being and the System, he realized.  That meant that the pain was coming from his very soul.  For some reason, that made Charlemagne angrier than if they had attempted to harm his flesh.  He glared at the purple-robed being standing alone and unharmed inside the protective field, surrounded by the charred corpses of his comrades.

“Your demise is at hand, slave of the System,” the four-armed giant promised the Sun Queen from within the now vastly shrunken cube.  “And yet I am not totally without mercy.  There is still a possibility for you to survive the loss of your precious System, providing you are willing to exchange the false power you have been handed over to me.”

“I would rather die go back to what I was before the System,” Elidi yelled back, her eyes flashing with barely restrained power.  Sunlight flooded into the throne room from above to recharge the Sun Queen’s spent energy, but the radiance it contained felt anemic.  There was no way that she would recover before the System lockout was completed.

“And I’ll die proudly with my sister,” Phasima added as she flung herself through the air to float by Elidi’s side.  “The System rescued us from the destruction of our universe, allowed us to grow and earn a place among the powerful.  In just a few more centuries, I will be more powerful than you can possibly imagine!”

“I have seen beings that would unmake you and your sister with just a glance,” the giant answered with a laugh.  “And your precious System did not rescue you from the death of your reality…it is far more likely that it caused its destruction in the first place.  If you surrender now and survive the winnowing of the System from your soul, you would be free to explore the truth.”

“Bawk!” Charlemagne yelled, feeling left out of the conversation.  But even as he voiced his objection, he was seeking out any sort of weakness in the mana shield that still protected the strangely-dressed, four-armed giant.

“It would actually be easier for us to purge the System from you, since you were not born to it, my feathered friend.  But I fear that you might lose your sapience in the process.  Are you willing to take that chance?”

“Never!” came the ready response, but not from Charlemagne or the Vessarians.  Phatagin had summoned his courage and joined the other three in the throne room, followed by Teem and Teem.

“The System turned me from a slavering idiot into the sophisticated and intelligent creature that you see before you.  I would rather die than go back to the way that I was before!”
