“I’m very satisfied you managed to stay out of trouble so far.”

“Thank you, Master.”

“Mm.”

Vice Head Colorful nodded repeatedly. Gary kept a straight face. As much as he disliked lying, he couldn’t mention the Muscle Sap operation. Nor was there a reason for Master Colorful to know. What he could probably help with was the Seed of Eternal Flesh, but Gary was hesitant to reveal it. Their relationship was still new and superficial. What if Master Colorful wanted to steal the Seed?

Gary had no idea about the value of Unique-ranked items, but given that the Lower Floors Overseer had personally stepped in and a tower-wide event had started, he wasn’t going to risk it.

“I’m a peaceful person, Master,” Gary said.

“I’m sure you are. So? How did the expedition go?”

“I didn’t gather many resources, but I found a box of Satan Sprinting Shakes that increased my Vitality,” Gary lied.

“That was extremely lucky. You may have some fate on you, after all.”

“Thank you, Master.”

Gary had found no box of Satan Sprinting Shakes. That was just a vitality-enhancing medicine that Asim had told him could be found on the third floor, which Gary used to justify his increased vitality. The Satan Sprinting Shake was actually a fourth-floor resource, but the Gymnasium dumped its waste on the third floor swamp, and real resources sometimes slipped through the cracks.

It was, quite literally, flushing treasure down the drain.

Gary stood in his master’s courtyard, in the mansion that seemed like a medieval castle. Master Colorful stood straight, inspecting Gary with his chin cupped. It had been two days since the expedition ended, but this was the first time Gary visited.

“Hi, Master Colorful!” Goldie said, emerging from Gary’s spear.

“Hello, Golden Bolt. I’m flattered you chose my disciple as your wielder.”

“Gary’s great! Of course I chose him!”

“You must have been bored out of your mind on the third floor, since nothing interesting happened.”

“Oh. Yeah. Super bored,” Goldie replied. Gary sighed inwardly in relief.

“Very well. Show me what you got, the two of you.”

Goldie dove back into the spear. Gary took it out of its sheath and thrust it in the air, unleashing a bolt of black lightning which pierced a bush ten feet away. He turned a smug smile at Master Colorful.

“My garden plants are not targets, Gary,” the old man said.

“Sorry.”

“But, that was a good attack. Good vibration. I see Golden Bolt already taught you some things.”

Gary beamed with pride. “She made me go through a heavenly tribulation. I had to fuse my vibrations or die.”

His master nodded. “I had foreseen this happening, but not so soon. Seeing as you already got a good spear and extra vitality, that’s three birds with one stone. Have you visited the Steps of Power yet?”

“Third step, second wave.”

“Good. Your strength has improved tremendously since the last time I saw you. With this and intense training, as well as a little bit of luck, you might be able to survive your duel against Sui Duang.”

“Thank you?”

“Speak respectfully, Gary.”

“Sorry. Thank you!”

Colorful sighed. “Are you familiar with the tournament rewards?” he asked.

Gary’s eyes brightened. “No.”

“The tournament will be held in the Empyrean Steps, so participating will be a reward in itself. The longer you can remain in the tournament, the more pressure you will get to experience. Anyone who reaches the top sixteen will be awarded ten drops of Muscle Sap and one thousand aura stones, with the rewards increasing the higher you rank. There is a reason it’s the clan’s most popular annual event.”

“You said it will be held at the Empyrean Steps? The tower in the middle of the Shores of Glory?”

“Exactly. The clan rents its arena for a few days every year to host this tournament. It’s meant to showcase our power to the world, as well as force our youth to perform under the gazes of many high-ranking cultivators. Sometimes, even the Shoresmaster makes an appearance during the finals…though I doubt you’ll get that far.”

“The Shoresmaster…” Gary muttered.

“Ranking as high as possible in the tournament is critical, Gary. The upcoming invasion made us move up the tournament, which is a disadvantage for those of you who entered during the recent clan selection, but we’ll do our best to prepare anyway. We have a lot of work to do. Are you ready?”

“Yes!” Gary replied.

“Show me your aura field.”

Gary galvanized the aura inside him. It vibrated at maximum power, spreading ripples outside his body. However, different body parts vibrated at slightly different frequencies, making a majority of the ripples clash against each other and disappear. In the end, Gary’s aura field spread three feet around his body—an area in which he held absolute perception.

Master Colorful watched, neither nodding nor shaking his head.

“You are now stably in the Birth of Aura realm. You wield a great spear and a powerful vibration, but you shouldn’t rely on them. Your cultivation is subpar. Less than subpart—it’s trash.”

“Trash!?” Gary exclaimed.

“Mm. Compared to the top disciples of the previous clan selection, you’re sorely lacking.”

“But they’ve been in Birth of Aura for months, Master. I just broke through two weeks ago.”

“So what? They are the ones you’ll face in the tournament—they should be the ones you compare yourself against. As it stands, your aura control is sloppy, and your skill with the spear is only decent. If not for your attributes, vibration, and spear, you’d be the most average Birth of Aura disciple in the clan.”

“Should I work on my aura control?”

“Precisely. We have less than two weeks until the tournament. In this time, you should prioritize your aura control, and especially focus on widening your aura field.”

“Shouldn’t I focus on my combat techniques?”

“They feed into each other. But also, strengthening your aura field is useful in combat, as you’ll soon realize.”

“I understand.”

“I will teach you some basic exercises to increase your aura control. Besides that, investing in your vitality was the right decision—your vibration would be useless otherwise. Now that you have the capital to use it without blowing yourself up, we can expand your arsenal.”

“My arsenal, Master?”

“Your combat techniques. Thunder Step serves as a good foundation for your footwork. You have, however, two massive gaps. Do you know what they are?”

“Time and experience?”

“Offense and defense, disciple. Your current offensive techniques are limited to thrusting variations: your Nail Gun, Black Thrust, and that ranged attack you showed me before are all different versions of the same thing, and none is particularly powerful. You need a powerful finishing blow. Besides that, however, your greatest—”

“I have a finishing blow,” Gary said.

“Oh? Show me.”

He adopted a thrusting stance and pooled all his aura into the spear until its spearhead hummed with power. “Thrust of Mercy!” he roared, shooting it forth. The tip shattered the wind, blowing back the leaves on some of Colorful’s bushes.

“That’s decent, but it’s not a finishing blow,” Master Colorful said.

“It’s not?”

“No, Gary. That’s just a full-power thrust. The only reason it seems special is because your spear matches your vibration quite excellently.”

“Oh,” Gary said, deflating.

“A proper finishing blow is a powerful technique which can utilize at least twenty percent of your total aura reserves. Your Thrust of Mercy—interesting name, by the way—can handle at most five percent. With proper technique, you can achieve greater destructive power for the same aura volume.”

“When I fought Sui Duang during the expedition, he used a technique called Razor Wave. Was that a finishing blow?”

“Describe it to me.”

“He turned his aura into water which floated around him. More water appeared with every move of his spear. When he attacked, the water followed his movement to attack as well, and then he followed the water to attack again.”

“Ah, yes. That’s a highly technical application of water aura. That Sui fellow is not half bad.”

“He’s my enemy, Master. Please don’t compliment him.”

Master Colorful chuckled. “I’m sorry, disciple. No, that wasn’t a finishing blow, just his standard fighting technique—though I’m sure he has a real finishing blow tucked away in his arsenal.”

“Okay.”

“Your spear style is more orthodox than his. That’s good, because it means we have a wealth of techniques to choose from. I will teach you two, which should form the core of your fighting style from now until at least the middle Core Formation realm. Do you wish to learn them?”

“Of course!”

“The first is an offensive technique: Nine Songs of the Spear. It relies more on comprehension than aura control, which suits you just fine. The second is a defensive technique meant to cover the gross gap in your capabilities. The spear is a weapon unsuited for defense, but you, my boy, took it a step further by learning zero defensive techniques whatsoever.”

Gary realized that was true. The greatest defense he’d used so far was stabbing the enemy attack until it went away. “Whoops,” he said.

“Don’t whoops me, disciple.”

“Sorry, Master. I guess it’s a good thing I got high Vitality.”

“Indeed, but relying on your body to take the hits is as silly as it sounds. You’re not a body cultivator. Thankfully, black lightning is a versatile force. Observe.”

A stick lying on the ground flew to Master Colorful’s wrinkled fingers. He twirled it between them, the stick spinning faster and faster until it resembled a wheel.

“Don’t look away,” Master Colorful said.

Lightning aura seeped into his fingers, then into the stick. It spread around the stick’s length, concentrating more on its edges. The glow bled together.

“Try the ranged thrust you showed me,” Colorful said.

Gary drew his spear back and thrusted. A black bolt of lightning shot straight, at high speed, towards his master’s center of mass. The moment it approached the spinning stick, it was pulled off its trajectory, arcing towards the old man’s shoulder. He easily leaned away, and the attack went flying into the sky, dissipating a few feet later.

“Wow,” Gary said. Looking up at his master’s face, he thought he saw it darkened, but the expression was gone as quickly as it came.

“This technique is called Fan the Spear,” Master Colorful explained, seeming cheerful again. “It’s not particularly powerful among defensive techniques, but it’s decent and easy to learn. It also suits you well because it demands a high familiarity with the weapon to perform the spinning motion, which you already possess, given how much you train.”

“So basically, I spin the spear to deflect incoming attacks?” Gary asked, trying to spin the spear. He failed, but it didn’t feel that hard.

“That’s the basic application. By spinning the spear within your aura field, you create a spinning aura field which naturally pulls projectiles and aura attacks off their course. There are more proficient applications. Strike me again.”

Gary performed the same attack as before. This time, the black bolt didn’t veer off its trajectory, but was instead slowed down tremendously as it approached the spinning stick. It finally lost all its momentum and disappear just a foot away from the stick.

“Try again, but harder,” the old man advised.

Gary repeated the thrust at full power. The black bolt flew at almost double its previous speed, but the same thing happened. Its deceleration was even more pronounced. It stopped half a foot away from the stick, instead of one foot, and dissipated.

“If you compress the spinning aura field in front of the spear in just the right way, you can create a dense area pushing outwards. This blocks all incoming attacks, be they aura or physical.”

Gary whistled. “That was impressive, Master. Why did the faster black bolt decelerate harder?”

“The faster something moves through this aura field, the more resistance it will face in every step of the way. I also employed the properties of lightning to increase the resistance exponentially as your attack approached my spear. This resistance converges to infinity.”

“Ah yes, electromagnetism.”

“Excuse me?”

“Nevermind, Master. Just some science from my planet.”

The old man gave him an odd look.

“All approaches converge, disciple. Magic, science, psionics, the elements. It’s all aura.”

“Yes, Master.”

“Practice the spinning motion for now. When you can keep the rotation going at high speed for over a minute, you can add your aura to the mix to create a spinning aura field. As for the compressed wall application, I don’t think you’ll achieve that before the invasion quest,” Master Colorful said tiredly.

He was dismissing Gary, but Gary stayed put. “Can you also show me the offensive technique, Master?” he asked.

Master Colorful raised a brow. “If I show it to you, you will practice that instead of the defensive technique. I know you now.”

“I promise I won’t.”

He sighed. “Fine then. Here’s the Nine Songs of the Spear—a core technique of our clan, passed down by the ancestor himself.”

Master Colorful’s expression was complex again, but Gary quickly focused on the old man’s movements instead. He’d clenched his hand, extending two wrinkled fingers. They were clad in aura. As he galvanized his energy, the aura around his fingers took a brown hue. “First Song: Mud,” he said, stabbing his fingers forward. A brown thrust shot out, slow and thick, stopping just an inch before the courtyard wall.

“Second Song: Leaves,” he said next. A sideways, green aura slash passed straight through a leaf of the garden bushes.

He did not announce the third attack, but he cleaved his fingers upward to dissipate the remaining energy. A blue aura slash flew to the sky, blending into the blue. Gary assumed it dissipated eventually.

“There are nine songs in this technique, hence its name. Each builds upon the rest to become exponentially more powerful. A Birth of Aura disciple should only be able to perform the first three songs. If you can achieve that, it will be a finishing blow powerful enough to contend with the top disciples of the tournament. Fan the Spear comes first, however. We can’t ignore such a gaping hole in your fighting style. Work on it, as I instructed you, and also on your aura field. Only when you can create a simple version of the spinning aura field will I show you how to practice the Nine Songs of the Spear. You are dismissed, disciple—this old man wants to rest.”

“Yes, Master... Master?”

“Yes, disciple?”

“What if I can master more songs, Master?”

Colorful laughed, the sound deep and complicated. “If you can use the fourth song in Birth of Aura, that marks you as a great talent. If you can use the fifth, then you’d be a genius, disciple… A true, unfettered genius carrying the clan’s lost hopes on your shoulders, and your loss would be… Ai.” He sighed deeply. “Leave me, disciple. Go practice, and if you can use the second song during the tournament, I will be satisfied. Dismissed.”
