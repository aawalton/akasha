The day had finally arrived, and the seer began to walk at dusk.

As Esheth walked through his home of Yalten, he felt a strange twinge in his heart. He had a sense of certainty deep within his Mentum pool– he wouldn't be seeing this place again, at least not as it was.

Yalten had never been perfect, and at times he'd outright scorned it, but it was home. It had been home for a long long time.

But he had a self-assigned prophecy to fulfill. He would obtain the lich’s immortality or die trying.

The walk was long, though, and he would be taking the scenic route. Esheth had plenty of time to stop and smell the roses, even if the quivering of his hands told him he needed to rush.

The Dead Belt was, well, dead, but for the first time he was able to appreciate the beauty of the place. He supposed that if he were to become a lich in order to secure his own continued existence, he’d need to appreciate death, in all of its many aspects.

He could have made the trip in hours– his ability with Annihaen could have allowed him to simply corrode the distance between himself and his destination, but the long, leisurely walk enabled him to come to understand what it was, truly, that he sought.

Then night fell, and a strange feeling came over the prophet. He pulled on his Soothen, and a wave of emotions crashed over him, coming from Yalten.

Fear. Horror. Disgust. Chaos. And… something else, too, a much more subtle feeling of… was that devotion?

How strange. Esheth felt oddly detached from the feelings, though he knew he should be rushing back home.

His Soothen caressed the back of his mind, and he knew with certainty that it had to be this way. There was nothing he could do– nothing he should do.

And an image returned to his mind.

An undead horde rising against his kingdom and devouring it whole, converting his homeland into a place of death.

“Ah, so your prophecies have begun to unfold, Fate. But, why show them to me? What is my role in this?”

The only response he felt was a sensation of utter jubilation– all was right in the world, for the time being.

Now, everyone just had to play their roles, and they would pass through the eye of the needle unharmed.

But one word resonated through the back of Esheth's skull– one word that he knew could cause all to come to ruin.

One word, or perhaps… one name.

Mana.
