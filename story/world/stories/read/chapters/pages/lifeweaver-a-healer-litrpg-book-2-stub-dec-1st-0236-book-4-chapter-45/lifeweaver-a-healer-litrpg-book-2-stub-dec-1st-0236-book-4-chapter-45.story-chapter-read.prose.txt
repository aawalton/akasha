Thirty skills. A lot, to be sure. The knowledge of how to use them was in Luke’s grasp, but the great number of options sometimes made for a difficult time choosing, especially in a life-and-death situation. Weaver’s Perception was a combination of many skills becoming one. In that transformation, Luke’s understanding had grown, and he now wielded the different aspects of that one skill with greater precision than anything he’d been able to accomplish before the change. Since he’d done it before, why shouldn’t he be able to do it again, and grow stronger in the process? With the new skill, Adaptability, shouldn’t it be even better now than before?

First, Luke focused on the skills concerning Threads of Mana and Needle of Life, his instruments of healing and death. Many skills were either dependent on Threads of Mana or changed the skill’s behavior or limitations in one way or another. In that state of complete focus, he saw the skills before his inner eye, not as a set list but as a malleable creature in need of taming. Picking skills was nothing more than the first step, he realized then. True ownership could not be claimed without making them his own. Luke had chosen Lifeweaver as his class and picked skills as they became available, picking them from a prepared list. Someone else’s list. Luke was more than a carbon copy of some long-forgotten template. Those above, those of The Greater System, did everything in their power to keep him in line, to make him play by their rules. No more. It was high time Luke threw off the weight of their preconceived notions and made Lifeweaver his class, and his alone.

The list of skills called to him, and Luke answered. Through will and focus, he found himself able to shape his class into its own thing. There were limits, of course, imposed by the system itself. Those he worked within. What he sought was not more power, that would have been impossible, but rather a clearer focus, a greater simplicity. Through a lens of complete focus, Luke melded skills with Threads of Mana, making the list shorter but the skill a more complete version of itself, a version with purpose and simplicity. Weaver’s Grasp was one of the first skills Luke chose, and it was a core part of the Lifeweaver skill-set. By now it was as natural as breathing, extending threads into the physical realm to reach another person to heal or harm. The ideas behind the two skills trembled before his will, and soon, with an extra push, he turned them into one.

That done, Luke considered what had just happened, what he had done, and found it to his liking. Weaver’s Grasp was gone from the list, but the inherent knowledge of the skill remained within Threads of Mana. Combining the two hadn’t changed Threads of Mana in any meaningful way, but it was one less stop for his thoughts and will each time he would extend threads from that point on. That was worth a lot. Satisfied, Luke continued on to the next skill. Weaver’s Mercy and Lifeweaver’s Sacrifice were both aimed at the same thing: keeping the one he healed alive. Come to think of it, that was true for another skill as well. Threads of Life. In his mind, the three became one. Of course Luke would accept damage in his patient’s stead, and any thread extended into a friend should heal no matter what, given the right intent. Combined, the patient would sustain no new damage for a brief moment, then that damage would be Luke’s instead. Mana requirements would go up, but that was nothing he wasn’t ready to accept. A simple concept that sounded just right to his own mind. The three disappeared from his list of skills, absorbed into Threads of Mana, but he wasn’t done just yet. Four more skills spoke to him in that state, the concepts behind them sharing a common thread with those he had just worked through. Cascading Threads, which extended threads from person to person, or monster to monster, was one that helped by doing something he could do himself. Taking that one and combining it with Threads of Mana would give him even greater control of all threads, but at a slight cost of mana. A good trade, if ever there was one. Strengthen Thread made the threads stronger, something Luke was learning to do by applying his will. In its current form, it was unnecessary. Combined with the others, though? Stronger. Easier to manipulate, and with no downsides. A done deal. Making those he healed stronger while he was healing them was another concept that rang true, which meant bringing Weaver’s Boon into the fold was a natural progression of what he was attempting. That also rang true for Heartthread. So far, he’d used it just once, with Smudge. At that moment, Luke didn’t see himself using it a whole lot more, but if needed, it was better to have it as an option from within the threads themselves. On the opposite end, the threads were also tools to cause harm. Weaver’s Whip had proven itself a formidable option for his arsenal, and it would be an excellent facet of the threads themselves, putting that damaging aspect right at his fingertips.

That left one more skill: Explosive Reach. Here, Luke hesitated. In its current form, he needed to activate it. In doing so, the reach grew to an incredible degree, but at a staggering cost to his mana reserves. Pondering how it would react to being combined with Threads of Mana, Luke found the answer to be simple. Reach farther at will without having to activate a skill, but that reach would cost him. It was a result with far greater benefit than in its current form, but with costly implications if he applied it without forethought. Fair enough.

Now that he knew how to perform the skill transformations, the many skills became few, and from that great change, Threads of Life became something greater than the sum of its parts.

Turned into.

True Threads of Mana: Shape the weave, Lifeweaver.

By the time Luke was done, his head was throbbing, and sweat poured down the small of his back. The concepts at play during the session were simple, but that didn't mean they were easy. Willing the system itself, re-forming the weave around you, was an incredible challenge that left him feeling like a tank had just run over him. None of that mattered, however, because he had done it. He had succeeded in what he set out to do, and he was only getting started.

Not wanting to wait another second before testing if his work had produced the results he hoped for, Luke wielded several threads. One, he used to pick up objects from the ground farther and farther away, feeling how the strain affected his mana. While doing so, he used another thread as a weapon, striking at other pieces of debris to cut them in half. While that was going on, he also used threads to harm and heal his own body from the inside, testing how the healing abilities interacted now that they were all integrated into the one skill. Luke’s control had increased, and it had increased a lot.

“This is what I’ve been looking for,” he murmured, nodding to himself as he dropped the threads to let mana regenerate.

After checking in with Hannah to make sure everything was good on her end, Luke drank a bottle of water from the Integrated Shop and poured another one over his head before digging into a ready-made meal which turned out to be a kebab wrap with garlic sauce. Delicious. Once back to full strength, he closed his eyes and relaxed, feeling the world around him bleed away as he sank deeper into meditative concentration. The list appeared again. It was time for Needle of Life to get the same treatment as Threads of Mana.

For this, Luke brought forward a thread of mana and affixed Needle of Life to it. The two parts worked together as one, but even so, they were separate. Combining Needle of Life with Threads of Mana wasn’t right. That didn’t mean the small, now malleable, needle couldn’t improve by combining it with other skills. A few on the list called out to him. First, Weaver’s Rupture. It hadn’t felt natural to combine this one with Threads of Mana, because it was an extension of that skill in a way that made Luke consider it a mismatch. Now, though, with Needle of Life, it was like one piece of a puzzle finding another. Together, he would be able to form whatever shape he wanted, either inside or outside an opponent’s body, then blow it up. Perfect. Next up was Bleed the Weave. It wasn’t a skill that had seen much use so far, but in prolonged fights, it could be a valuable tool. In its new configuration, Luke knew he’d be able to use Needle of Life and choose whether the one standing against him would bleed blood, mana, or both. One more skill stood out as a good option for this process, and it would turn Destabilize Spellweave from an active skill to a passive one, his gut told him. Cutting through spellweaves with a needle felt more natural, but also far more badass.

Malleable Needle of Life.

Weaver’s Rupture.

Bleed The Weave.

Destabilize Spellweave.

Turned into.

Lifeweaver’s Needle: Your tool of destruction or life. Wield it with caution.

“Aw, yeah,” Luke said. Speaking made his concentration waver and his meditative trance tremble, so he quieted his mind and got right back to work. He wasn’t done with this just yet. First, he folded Draft Insight, the skill allowing him to read spellweaves, into Weaver’s Perception, since the two had belonged together from the start. It didn’t change the skill at all, except for removing its rank. At first, he was horrified, thinking he’d just made it worse, but he detected no change. That also made him realize that both Threads of Mana and Needle of Life had lost their rank as well. Did that mean they could rank up again and improve even more? Wouldn’t that be nice?

Once he’d made sure all was as it should be, he turned to the next set of skills, the ones giving him more control over himself, the world around him, and his place in it. Will of the Weaver, Weave Integration, and Greater Meditation worked side by side as instruments of will, focus, and manipulation. Combined into one, they would become greater than the sum of their parts. To Luke’s surprise, a fourth skill trembled in step with the others. Adaptability. Since he’d just chosen the skill, he considered leaving it out of the transformation. In the end, he chose to include it, trusting in the place of instinct and intuition he was working from within. All this was coming from himself, after all.

Will of the Weaver.

Weave Integration.

Greater Meditation.

Adaptability.

Turned into.

Weaver’s Presence: No one can doubt the Lifeweaver’s mastery.

So much done, but still a little more to complete before Luke would allow himself to return to enchanting. Or maybe rest. Rest didn’t sound like a bad idea. This last combination of skills, he could feel what they would turn into before even working through the full picture. A defense to rival anyone’s, as long as he had the mana for it.

Bountiful Recovery.

Guardian Weave.

Weaveguard.

Loom’s Reminder.

Turned into.

Lifeweaver’s Bulwark: Protect yourself within and without.

That… didn’t sound as cool as he’d hoped. Still, the three protection skills would work together either with his direction or on their own, while Loom’s Reminder added a layer of thorns for those striking him by extending threads between the two points of contact as long as Luke wanted it to happen. The mana cost went up, of course, but it was a fair trade-off in his mind. Weaver’s Renewal could have its place in Lifeweaver’s Bulwark, he knew, but that would mean it would only work for himself going forward. While it would become a much stronger skill, being unable to restore his friends was too much. Even now, he was still a healer.

Other skills sang to his tired mind, as did the vitality, stamina, and mana coursing through him, but the process had spent every ounce of will and focus available to Luke. Rest. He needed rest. Dragging himself back into the present moment, where sat on the ground, robe soaked with sweat, he perused the new list of skills, glowing with a sense of a job well done.
