Climbing out of the crevice, Kyle immediately activated Aura Force, feeling mana leave his body to infuse the area around him.  It drew an alarming amount of power, but he chose not to activate Genesis Engine quite yet, instead focusing on the sensation of the skill being active.  A few moments later, he felt the expansion slow, then stop.  Willing a gentle blast to form at the border, of the skill, he noted that it was about sixty meters in front of him.  The blast went straight into the ground below, and to Kyle’s delight, he felt most of the mana diffuse back into his domain, not having been fully expended.

He tried again, this time with a blast to smash a wave of incoming ice shards, while another tightly packed zone of force moved to intercept a wave of rolling fire.  Soon, he was juggling five different applications of Repel.  Kyle moved himself and the domain around him by using his body as a point of origin, while maintaining planes of force in the air directly around him and blasting away projectiles with swiftly created bursts.

Next, he narrowed the range of Aura Force, drawing it closer to his body.  As he did, a good portion of the energy he’d projected into the space around him returned, though he noticed that a little over ten percent was left behind to dissipate in the air.  While narrowly focused like this, his control was better than ever.  Though spent energy wasn’t reclaimed unless it stayed inside the domain, Kyle was able to create small barriers of force directly around his body as he rocketed through the air, continually propelling himself with controlled streams of energy.

[DR. MAYHEW, THIS IS QUITE AN IMPROVEMENT,] C.H.A.D.D. noted, a hint of jealousy coming through Pack Leader’s Instinct.

“It’s an impressive upgrade,” Kyle agreed, lowering himself down to the ground.  “But not something I can use nonstop.  The energy expenditure is really high for something you can do almost effortlessly.”

[I AM HAPPY TO BE OF SERVICE,] C.H.A.D.D. replied, clearly feeling a bit better that its propulsion was still valuable to Kyle.  [NOW, ARE THERE ANY OTHER TESTS YOU ARE LOOKING TO RUN?  I CAN TELL THAT YOU ARE GROWING MORE FATIGUED.]

“Just a couple,” Kyle promised, taking in a breath before pushing Aura Force out as far as he could.  The normal sixty meter radius quickly expanded past one hundred meters, stabilizing near one hundred and thirty meters in all directions.  However, every meter beyond the baseline caused more and more mana to leak out into the atmosphere, and his attempts to create any effects on the fringes were almost entirely thwarted.  It was just too much for him to try to control.  At least, as he was now.

With a breath, Kyle activated Genesis Engine.  He felt the power coursing through him as he redoubled his efforts, with the range encompassed by Aura Force rapidly expanding outward.  Pushing as hard as he could, the range expanded to nearly two hundred meters before running into the same issue as before.  Now, he willed the entire space to quake, releasing blasts of opposing forces that collided with one another.  Flame and frost alike were forced out by the resounding thunderclap as, for a brief moment, Kyle truly laid claim to the space.  Then, his vision blurred, and he felt himself lose control of the skill.

Staggering to a knee, he let the rejuvenating power of Genesis Engine wash over him as he tried to regain his bearings.

[DR. MAYHEW, WHAT HAPPENED?] C.H.A.D.D. asked, worry moving through their bond.  Even Phaegos seemed concerned, and Kyle took a moment to reorient himself.  He wasn’t physically harmed, and his mana reserves were being constantly topped off by his boosting skill.  No, this fatigue was something altogether different.  It was if he’d strained his mind by trying to manipulate too much energy outside himself; the artificially boosted Willpower not able to cover for his own weakness.

“I just overdid it,” he told the drone, rising on unsteady feet.  “I could definitely use a break though.  Let’s get back to the crevice.”

[I WOULD WARN YOU NOT TO OVERDO IT, BUT I SUPPOSE IT IS BETTER TO TEST YOUR LIMITS HERE, RATHER THAN IN A MORE DANGEROUS AREA.  NEVERTHELESS, I ALSO AGREE WITH TAKING A BREAK.]

“So long as you’re not expecting any big influxes of mana, we’ll be just fine,” Kyle said, feeling a tinge of disappointment from C.H.A.D.D.

Fire and ice continued their endless combat as a large, rolling flame was met with a flurry of frozen shards, leaving nothing but steam and a whisper of intent behind.  By now, that mystery had been largely unraveled, the intent behind the dueling forces being one of conflict and dominance.  No, the only thing that Kyle was still trying to figure out was how the intent was so perfectly mirrored by both the heat and cold.

By now, he was convinced that the state of this planet was manufactured, with the Pillar’s scientists having invested considerable resources in creating such an exact balance.  Even a slight difference in terms of intensity would have resulted in the equilibrium being broken, and yet the warring elements stayed in exacting opposition.  To a certain degree, this was the sort of balance he and C.H.A.D.D. strove for with the Graft skill.  If they could figure out how to maintain perfect balance throughout the process, their possibilities would open up tremendously.

Watching another storm of icicles get swallowed in a blazing fireball gave him some ideas to experiment with, but it would have to wait until after he got some rest.  He was just too tired to work on it right now.  Still, between the levels he’d gained and the skill upgrade, Kyle was thrilled with his progress.  He settled into the vine hammock he’d created, closing his eyes and allowing his mind to wander.  This was a great start.  And, with four more of the probes to deliver, he was hopeful that there would be more gains ahead.

Drifting off to a dreamless sleep, Kyle was awoken by a buzzing sound from both his nav bracelet, and from the probe still stuck in the crevice wall.

Courier,

Sufficient data has been collected.  A teleportation pod has been prepared, and will be landing in your vicinity shortly.  Please secure the probe, and infuse energy into the teleporter for evacuation.  More instructions will follow.

“Say, C.H.A.D.D., was there a per diem on this job?” Kyle asked, thinking about the months he’d spent on the planet.

[THERE WAS, DR. MAYHEW.  A SIZEABLE ONE AT THAT.  ALL TOLD, YOUR TAKE OF THE PAYMENT WILL LIKELY BE IN EXCESS OF TWO MILLION CREDITS.]

“We’ll certainly take it,” Kyle said, thinking about the cost associated with purchasing C.H.A.D.D. some new components to replace the ones used in his recovery.  Marcus still hadn’t sent the invoice, but Kyle knew that they’d likely be even more expensive than the first batch.  After all, Marcus was about his business at the end of the day.

Another ping from his nav bracelet coupled with a crash outside alerted Kyle to the pod’s arrival, and in the span of a few minutes Kyle found himself back on the shuttle that brought him to the space station.

Place the probe in the receptacle, and another will be provided for you.

The voice was neutral and robotic, and Kyle followed the instructions, placing the probe in an open tray near the laboratory door.  It closed and was taken away, with a second appearing a moment later.

This probe is looking to measure the acidity of the headwaters of the Geer’ath River on a nearby gas giant, Nraxelm-863.  As before, storms near the target area make it very difficult to drop close to the source, so you will need to navigate a short distance to it.

The probe will be fitted into a containment device to prevent contaminants.  Your job will be to navigate the river until you find the pool where the acids spring forth, submerge it, and then reseal it after it is withdrawn to limit other outside contamination.

Be warned, poisonous gasses are common across the planet.  Furthermore, their corrosive properties can wear through most D Grade protective equipment, given time.  You don’t want to be exposed to the toxins for long, so bring backup gear.

This probe will just need to be immersed for a few minutes, so it should be a reasonably quick drop off and pick up.  Good fortune.

-Roole

A quick review of the data packet confirmed the presence of toxic gasses, and Kyle even went as far as to prepare a gas mask before going to the planet.  Not that he planned on using it, necessarily, but rather to have backup gear handy.

He needn’t have bothered.  He practiced by keeping Aura Force active as he moved across the planet, simultaneously speeding his journey while repelling the toxic gasses.  As he and C.H.A.D.D. approached the headwaters, he did stop to get a few deep breaths of the poison.  It was a nasty neurotoxin that even had some effect on him, though a quick cycling of Adaptive Regeneration cleared up the effects almost immediately.

Something unexpected was how the neurotoxin also impacted his plant-based organs.  He’d hoped that his integration with the World Tree Seed would have made him almost immune to poisons like this, however his artificial organs’ functions were slightly hampered by the poison.  He dispelled the toxins quickly as a new set of artificial organs were put into place, reminding him of his limitations.  Of course, that also came with unique opportunities.  What kind of upgrades could he make to the organs?  How could they be transformed into something new, something stronger?

Shaking his head, Kyle turned his attention back to the task at hand. There would be plenty of opportunities to keep refining the plant-based constructs once he got a better sense of the Graft skill, but it wasn’t something he could do right now.  His job was to follow the river and deal with the acidic environment trying to melt him, and so he did.

Soon, the second probe was submerged in the river of acid indicated in the data packet, and he once again empowered a teleportation pod to take them back to the space station.  He’d even gotten another level from the experience, which was an impressive achievement given the rapid pace he’d experienced in the last assignment.

By now, Kyle was looking forward to the third probe, but had an unexpected message waiting for him.

Your progress is faster than expected.  Nice job, but there are some things we’ll need to get organized first.  Feel free to stay on the station for a little while, we’ll be back in touch with the third probe in a couple of days.  After that, we’ve been informed that we’ll need to give you a brief leave to return to the Hub and conduct some business.  We’ll provide transport back and forth at cost.

-Roole

Just moments after he finished reading the note, a message came across his nav bracelet from Suierrillax.

Kyle,

The Investigator has been assigned, and they want to meet you.  They want you to wrap up what you’re doing for the Pillar, but it sounds like your employer would rather this get taken care of.  I’ll keep you posted, but we’ll see you back here soon.
