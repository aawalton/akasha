Ning recited the sutra with focus.

Fenghuang was present as well; he ignored the mumbling youth and instead basked in the morning sun, which coated his feathers, making them shine even more.

So, instead, he focused on his own situation.

What was he doing, one may ask?

He was trying to comprehend Buddhist scriptures. After learning about the soul-ferrying scripture, Ning had been reading and comprehending various Buddhist scriptures to further his mind.

To comprehend such absurd things, Ning used these scriptures as a gateway.

As for why Ning called such a thing absurd? It was simply because it was a xianxia world.

After knowing that this technique required something like a state of mind, he had tried to gather as much information about it as possible and soon found the relevant info.

And finally, piecing the information together, let's just say he was not surprised to find that, like any good xianxia, the states of mind were also reflected in different realms.

The mortal heart: ordinary, reactive, full of wants and fears. It was an ordinary state of mind that most people had.

The clear heart: the first real cultivation milestone for the mind. Emotions clarified, resolve strengthened, the cultivator no longer blown around by every passing circumstance.

Ning was firmly here. He had been here for a while, the calmness that had confounded Li Houran's rage-baiting and caused his master to fail in her enticement several times.

The inner demon calamity he faced certainly helped with this, and along with his rich otherworldly experience and the sheer adaptation to this strange world he had undertaken, it was not strange for him to reach this level. After all, he was a chill guy in essence.

Beyond that: the mirror heart, the empty heart, and realms Ning only had secondhand descriptions of.

For an orthodox cultivator, the path moved from mortal to clear to mirror, the heart becoming increasingly still, increasingly reflective, increasingly separate from worldly attachment.

The Buddhist path was different.

Buddhist cultivators, the text explained, did not cultivate toward stillness by removing themselves from the world. They cultivated toward stillness by becoming completely present within it. The difference was subtle but, in practice, enormous.

An orthodox cultivator with a mirror heart was like a still pool, undisturbed because nothing touched it.

A Buddhist cultivator's ferrying mind was like a still pool that everything moved through, completely present with whatever entered, completely unretained when it left. Simply put, the water was the same temperature entering and leaving. Nothing stuck.

So, that was the problem. The Soul Ferrying Scripture was a Buddhist technique, and Buddhist cultivators, having more focus on the state of mind, had a slight variance in the realms of mind compared to the norm.

In addition to having a clear heart, to reach the first solid step and use this scripture, he had to attain what the manual called the ferrying mind.

Compassionate, but not attached to the outcome of compassion. Present, but not consumed by presence. Self-aware enough to remain oneself while holding another's soul.

So, yeah, even the description itself was something to comprehend before even diving in. Coupled with the inherently prose-worthy descriptions in the Buddhist scriptures found here, Ning was having a tough time reaching this ferrying mind.

Moreover, being in the eastern wilderness, far apart from the western desert where Buddhism flourished, it was hard to get his hands on authentic Buddhist teachings with less mumbo-jumbo.

"Fortunately, I still have some memories regarding such things, however vague they may be," Ning mumbled.

As for whether the Buddhist scriptures from his previous world worked in this one, Ning was pretty sure they would.

In fact, Ning couldn't ignore the fact that his having transmigrated here at all meant there must be some connection between his previous world and this one.

After all, with something like xianxia being in his previous world, which perfectly reflected his current world, said volumes about the connection. Alas, at his current stage, he could not understand the connection between the two worlds.
