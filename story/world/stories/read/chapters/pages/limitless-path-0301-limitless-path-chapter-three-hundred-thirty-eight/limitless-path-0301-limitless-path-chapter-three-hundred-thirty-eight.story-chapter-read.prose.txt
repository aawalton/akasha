"So good that you could join us in such a timely manner," Azael commented with a slight edge of sarcasm in his voice.

"Senior Azael, we're sorry," the leading woman said with a bow. "We were just, uh, a little excited."

"I see," Azael said calmly, taking another sip of his tea. He let them fidget for another moment before putting his tea down and saying, "These are valued Emerald customers of ours and, as you can clearly see, they have brought in quite a few items for us to evaluate."

"But, the monthly auction just happened…" the second woman said quietly, glancing at Beth and Blood before looking at the table and then looking to Azael.

"None of these are auction worthy," Beth said with a snort, waving at the table.

"She's correct," Azael said calmly. "Not to impinge upon Emerald customers, but these items don't rise to the quality of a monthly auction. Instead, they would like to sell these items to us at cost to clean out their storage powers and make some money to help prepare for the yearly auction. I called you two up here as I thought this would be some valuable experience in analyzing a large lot of customer acquired goods."

"We do trade in information," Azael said. "But, I'm afraid we don't have exactly what you're looking for. Anything we know about locally of that level of value has already been collected or is being farmed for its products. You can try to join in, but I wouldn't recommend jostling for a position in the rotation at this point."

"Well, that's disappointing, but I'm sure we can find something," Beth said with a nod. "Other than that, we're good."

"Let me escort you out," Azael said, standing and gesturing to the door. The two young women stood a moment later and bowed, caught a bit off guard at the sudden end of the negotiations. Azael took them down to the lobby before seeing them off for the day.

After they left, he moved back into the building with the two woman trailing before sitting down with them to review the results. "There were a number of errors in your estimates on the armor."

"Yes, sir," Angeline said. "I still can't believe they sold so much at once."

"You have to become accustomed to working with the VIP customers," Azael said calmly. "They will often transact in diamonds or multiple mithril coins for general sales and purchasing. Those two young ladies bought a mithril coin of goods from us just a short time ago."

"Unbelieveable," Jennifer breathed quietly.

"I can't imagine spending years fighting to acquire so much and then not even negotiating over the price," Angeline said with a small shake of her head.

"Years? Unlikely. I would guess that table full of materials was six months or less of effort for them," Azael said calmly as he sorted the things from Beth and placed several on shelves in the room.

"That can't be possible," Angeline argued.

"Entirely possible," said Azael calmly. "The two of them are currently collecting the materials to complete their third rebirth as a True Rebirth, and I have no doubt it is not their first True Rebirth."

"Even still," Angeline protested weakly.

"You still have a number of preconceived notions of what it means to be a Wayfarer," Azael lectured calmly. "Dismiss them. Our customers rarely follow even the conventional paths of Wayfarers, which are far broader than you know."

"Yes, sir," they both responded calmly.

Meanwhile, Beth and Blood had returned to their room at the CRA. Once they were on the couch in the room, Beth said, "So, here's the plan."

"Hm?" Blood asked cogently.

"We're gonna farm your essence for the next four weeks," Beth started.

"Not my essence; that'd be exhausting," Blood interjected with a smirk.

"Shut up. I meant the devil essence and you know it," Beth said with a sigh as she rolled her eyes. "We're gonna farm for four weeks, which means around twenty-eight days. If we can get even one essence a day, that will mean we can get another twenty-five to thirty essences by the time four weeks is out. Once we're a week out from the auction we stop, do a rebirth, and then farm levels for five or six days objective time. That should get us back past level fifty, maybe even up to level one hundred by the time the auction rolls around. Thoughts?"

"Tedious," Blood grumbled and Beth just shrugged. "No other thoughts. Head empty."

"Fine, let's rest and then start with the devils," Beth said, going to start cooking some food. They ate first before resting for an hour, after which Beth couldn't waste time any longer and opened the gate, cajoling Blood through to start their hunting.

They did almost exactly as Beth had said, though she was pretty surprised with their luck with the array plates. The grind was quite tedious, but they were not only finishing preparing for a rebirth, Beth was preparing to make quite a bit of money. Blood wanted to stop when they had twenty-five essences and do a rebirth, but Beth shot that down and had them continue to kill devils for quite a while before they stopped. She had wanted to go through all the rest of the plates they had, but that aforementioned luck had seen them get three or four essences out of quite a few of the plates, with only two plates breaking while giving two essences. Beth wasn't sure if the quality of the plates was better than advertised or if they really were just on a crazy hot streak, but she didn't really question it. The plates had yielded them thirty-four essences over the four weeks, which meant they had thirty-eight in total when counting the ones that they had gotten before buying more plates. They had also use eleven plates for that, meaning they still had four of the expensive things stashed in Beth's necklace, which just looked like further future income.

They took a full twelve hours to rest after they had finished their farming of the essence. Beth was eager to get on with the rebirth, but they really needed to make sure they were in a good headspace for the event. They bathed, ate, and slept for a little before relaxing for two hours. They then headed to one of the training rooms, booking the large, heavily shielded room for an hour to get their next rebirth done.

This one wasn't any different than the previous one, and Beth was quite happy to see their stats were growing quite nicely. With what they had now at level one, she didn't foresee any problem in them getting to level one hundred before the auction. In fact, they would likely get some way into the low hundreds and still have a day off before the auction to turn in their items and rest. She checked their stats after the rebirth was finished, eager to see where they were at.
