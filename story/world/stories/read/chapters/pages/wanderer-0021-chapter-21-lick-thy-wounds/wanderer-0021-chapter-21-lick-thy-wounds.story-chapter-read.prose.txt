The berserker's corpse slumps on my chest, and suddenly I can't breathe again. Gone is that fiery rage from moments earlier. Panic grips me, and I frantically try to push him off, but both my hands and his skin are so slick with blood I cannot find any purchase. Instead, I just spread the stain of what I just committed further, until it consumes me. Tendrils of tingling crawl up my arms and face like a foul creature, and my desperation to shove him away becomes manic.

Get...off me.....Get off me...Get him off me! GET HIM OFF ME!!! FRODE!!!

I scream for him without speaking, reach for him without seeing, my terror smashing through the walls of this inn until it strikes true.

As I choke for air, the tendrils now clawing their way over my face completely, I distinctly hear the sound of wood exploding and a door flying off its hinges. The whistling of an axe flying through the air, and a body collapses to the floor. Heavy footsteps rush to my side and in one full swoop, the berserker is thrown off of me like a sack of potatoes. Large, strong hands cradle my face, then gently stroke my blood soaked hair out of my eyes.

"I am here, fiery one."

A sob breaks its way from my throat, but it sounds strangled.

His words and his touch make the tendrils melt away, and my lungs breathe in my first full gulp of air greedily. I reach for his arm and grip it, like he's the lifeline preventing me from being pulled down a raging river. My body starts shaking, so Froðe pulls me into his arms and his lips graze my forehead. I hiss as the back of my head alerts me that I did in fact hit it pretty hard, and my vision swims. Froðe goes deathly still.

Another slash of flesh and the last berserker falls. Arne wipes his brow with one hand while the other grips his left side. He's wounded, but we've finally cleared the inn.

"You let her be harmed."

Froðe's voice is deadly calm, and I know the sinister promise just beneath the surface of those words.

"It wasn't...his fault." Oh my god...my voice. I try to form the words, but my vocal chords are so damaged that they come out in whistling, hoarse whispers.

Froðe's grip on me tightens.

"I trusted her with you, Arne."

Deep sorrow flits across Arne's handsome features, followed by what I would describe as self loathing.

"I know, brother. I am sorry. But she fought well...you did well, Beatrix."

I reach for Froðe's face, pulling him back to me. Raging Ice blue eyes find mine and immediately soften.

"Please...don't...blame him." Fuck I sound terrible. "I...made the choice...to..engage. I couldn't...let any...thing happen...to Sorcha and Iona."

Froðe nods, and brushes his lips against my forehead.

"Try not to speak right now. Let's get you cleaned up." He lifts me up off the bloodstained floor, cradling me in his arms like something precious. As we pass Torsten to head further into the inn, Froðe says over his shoulder, "Clear the rest of the village with Arne, then bring me the Seeress. She will see to Beatrix's wounds, and she must answer for why she did not foresee this."

Torsten nods and promptly exits the tavern, taking Arne with him.

***

I'm in the tub, and Froðe is carefully bathing me, washing the blood caked on my body with a linen washcloth. His hair is unbound, sweeping over his face as he kneels beside the tub to tend to me. He dunks the cloth in the lukewarm water, then slowly draws it down my skin from my neck to my wrist, the backs of his knuckles lingering on my wrist before he dunks the cloth again. The motion is intimate, more of a caress than the act of bathing someone, and his acute attention to it alone tells me he would do this all night if he could. But his face is troubled, brow furrowed, eyes often wandering worriedly to my neck to where I am sure deep bruising is blooming.

Even despite this, even with everything that happened, his touch still sets me on fire. We lock eyes, and Froðe pauses. I look into those ice blue depths, and suddenly I can feel myself teetering over the edge of a very steep cliff.

Fuck, I'm definitely in trouble.

The corners of Froðe's mouth lift in his telltale smirk.

"Arne is right you know, you did fight well today," he says, before dunking the cloth once again. It tints the already pink water into more of a red, but my skin is slowly becoming mine once again.

I drop my eyes to the muddled bathwater. While I appreciate the compliment, especially from him, I feel like I have crossed a bridge that I cannot come back from. I should feel disgust for the act I committed tonight...but...it does not come, and I think that's the most terrifying thing of all.

I killed someone.

Yes, it was in self defense, but, I was never trained to kill anyone in my many years of martial arts. We were trained to deflect, attack simultaneously, immobilize weapons and rely on these ingrained reactions to get out of dangerous situations.

What am I becoming?

Froðe sighs, sensing the turmoil inside of me, and sets the washcloth aside.

"This is the first time you have killed, isn't it?" His voice is soft, compassionate, not what I would expect from a viking Jarl trained to kill at such a young age.

I nod.

"Hmmmm...." Froðe looks inwardly in thought, and I can see him trying to find the words to console me. Clearly he's never had to do something like this before, in a world built upon volatile human kingdoms and the fight for survival, or in a realm in which he is recognized as a god of war.

"It's...not the act...itself that disturbs me..." I whisper hoarsely after a moment, reflecting. "It's...that it came...so easy to me. That...I didn't hesitate...for a second...and how I felt afterwards."

Froðe says nothing, quietly waiting for me to continue.

"Maybe...I am a monster...after all..." I finish, and a tear escapes down my cheek.

"You are not a monster Beatrix," Froðe says, taking my face in his large hand and pulling me back to him. "You are a survivor," he whispers into my hair, and kisses my head. I cling onto those words, and to him, because I think without them I would spiral into the abyss.

After we've both bathed and clothed, Foðe gently lays me into my bed and there's a knock on the door.

"Enter." Froðe doesn't look up, but instead drapes the furs on my bed over me with care. I'm clean, but I feel like a 200 pound man tackled me, tried to choke me to death, and I definitely have a concussion.

The thump of staff on wood tells me it's Skuld. Although I'm still livid at her for what she did at the ritual, I'm grateful that she came. The weariness in Skuld's eyes tell me she's had a rough night as well, but I don't miss the look of relief when she scans the rest of me for other injuries and doesn't find them. Froðe grinds his teeth and shoots her daggers with his eyes, but gets out of the way so she can assess me.

Her eyes go straight to my throat and a flutter of emotions cross her face, but she sits on the edge of the bed and gently brings a hand to my forehead.

"I heard that you hit your head as well."

I nod, and Skuld gently lifts my head to examine the lump I imagine is growing there. She carefully sweeps my copper curls aside, likely looking for cuts, then slowly returns my head to the bed. She then reaches for her hip satchel and pulls out a corked, small water skin. Once she removes the cork, a familiar, putrid smell reaches my nostrils and I immediately know what it is.

"This will not completely heal your injuries," Skuld warns, tilting the bottle to my lips. "You will need rest. At least a couple weeks before any...strenuous activity." She makes a pointed look at Froðe, who arches an eyebrow, arms crossed.

Damn...that sucks. But honestly...probably for the best.

If it weren't for my injuries, I have a feeling Froðe and I might rip this tavern room apart the moment we are alone again. And I don't want to think about how that will complicate things for us further when I was supposed to just be having fun.

Treacherous heart.

The potion goes down a little smoother this time, but I still have to fight the urge to gag towards the end. Froðe must hear the choking sound I make as I finish, as he pours me a cup of water to wash it down shortly after.

"I'll make sure she rests," he says. "Can you tell me why we did not see this coming Skuld?"

Skuld lowers her head in shame. She looks like she's aged 10 years in one night.

"Forgive me Jarl. I was so focused on your overall future, and giving Beatrix her chance to seek her own truth, that I failed to see any incoming threats."

As I feel my body's jump start on healing take place, anger also flares to life inside of me.

"Giving me a chance? You fucking drugged me without my consent!" I snap, my voice mostly returned, and the sharp pain from my head finally ebbing into a dull ache.

Skuld flinches at my words, but bows her head lower.

"I have also wronged you, my little flame, and I am sorry for that, but it had to be done. Did you not get the truth you sought!?" She lifts her head and looks at me in desperation, searching for something within my eyes.

No..she's not going to turn this around and make it sound like she did it for me.

"It doesn't matter! I explicitly told you that I was not okay with taking henbane, and you gave it to me anyways!"

Skuld has the audacity to harrumph at this, and my anger burns even hotter.

"I do what is necessary for the success of my Jarl...and for those who are an important part of his future. Which includes you, little flame." Skuld's voice is also rising, her porcelain skin flushing red.

"I DECIDE MY OWN FUTURE!" I yell, and Skuld growls in response. She's completely disheveled, a strange contrast to the sultry, manipulative witch I know. Energy in the room crackles, and it definitely feels hotter in here, but I'm not done with her yet.

Froðe remains silent during our exchange, arms crossed, but slides an odd look my way. His head is tilted and a brow is lifted, and there's definitely that glimmer in his eye he has when he's noticed something of interest.

"I'm sick of everyone here telling me that I have some sort of epic destiny to fulfill!" I continue, throwing off the furs because I'm now boiling. "I'm not helping you achieve anything until you learn to do things on my terms. If you can't handle that, then find some other fucking pawn."

Skuld sneers, but I can see her considering my words and she begins to collect herself.

"I will let you rest now," she says, lifting her chin and taking her leave. Thump...Thump...as she reaches the door, she hesitates, taking a deep sigh. Then she shoots another narrowed eye look at Froðe over her shoulder. "And a couple weeks means a couple weeks, Jarl....do not forget."

Froðe gives Skuld a curt nod, then his eyes slowly slide to mine with a wicked glimmer.

"I will not."

I can't help but return his gaze with an intensity of my own, fires still blazing within me from my fight with Skuld. Only now, my anger has turned into lust with the snap of a finger. No one else has ever pulled me in so strongly with just a look alone. Like a moth to a flame, and every moment our eyes linger on each other the more the air crackles around us with energy.

Skuld scoffs, shaking her head, but there's an undeniable smirk on her face before she finally leaves us alone and shuts the door.

As soon as the door shuts, Froðe strides over to his bed, crouches, and begins shoving it towards mine. The legs screech across the wooden floorboards obnoxiously, but he brings it so that the edge of his is flush with mine. The corner of my mouth lifts in a crooked grin.

"What are you doing?"

Satisfied with his work, Froðe crosses over to the fat lamp and blows it out.

"Doing a better job of watching over you..."

In the darkness, I hear the sound of a bed creak and furs rustling. Then a strong arm gently reaches for me, pulling me into a muscular, warm body. I wrap myself around him, burying my face in his neck, the smell of mint and thyme on his soft tresses bringing a sense of calm over me. A feeling of home. Froðe pulls me closer, then hooks his right leg over mine, entangling us further. I smile softly into his neck.

"Is this alright?" he asks, stroking my shoulder gently with the backs of his knuckles.

"Yes," I say, snuggling in further.

Why do we fit together so perfectly? As if he were made for me and I for him?

"Tell me more of what you were like in your home world..." Froðe asks, interrupting my thoughts. "You must have been quite a force to be reckoned with."

I snort into Froðe's neck, and he turns into me.

"No?"

"I was quite the opposite," I say, laughing.

"Hmmmm...how so?" Froðe asks, starting to stroke my curls lazily. It sends little shivers through my scalp.

"I let people walk all over me..."

Froðe's fingers freeze.

"You let people walk on top of you?" he asks incredulously, eliciting a giggle from me, and he resumes running his fingers through my hair.

"No...it's an expression," I whisper into his neck. "I mean that...I didn't fight for myself before, and people used that to take advantage of me or manipulate me." Like my asshole boss.

Froðe ponders this for a moment, then brushes his lips against the side of my head.

"Clearly, you have begun to learn how to overcome that. And I will continue to help you, fiery one."

My voice becomes the ghost of a whisper, because maybe I am afraid of the answer, but I also can't live without it.

"Why?"

Froðe sighs gently, wrapping his other arm back around me.

"Because you are important to me, and I want you to be strong so that you can continue to be by my side."

My heart flutters at his answer, and just this once, I allow myself to revel in that feeling. Then I nestle into him even closer as my eyelids become heavy with sleep.

"While you recover...you'll get to see the Jarl-like things I do everyday," Froðe says into my hair, using the term I've thrown at him before. "In a few days time, we will hold The Thing, and you will learn more of our laws and ways."

"Mmmmm..." I say, drifting off in his arms.

"Goodnight, my fire," he whispers, and we fall asleep that way, entangled in one another.
