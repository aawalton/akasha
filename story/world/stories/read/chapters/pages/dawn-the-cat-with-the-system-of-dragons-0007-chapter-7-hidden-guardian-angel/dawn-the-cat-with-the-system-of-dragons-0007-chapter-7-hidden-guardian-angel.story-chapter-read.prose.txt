The black cat mother suddenly found herself flying through the air. The sudden hit to her belly led her baby to be thrown from her mouth. After she fell, she finally understood what had happened.

She immediately looked for her baby. After frantically looking for a few seconds, she saw her baby lying not far from the humans who were lining up to enter the gate.

She instantly ran towards her baby and began inspecting it. Fortunately, her baby wasn't hit. The kitten simply fell on the ground after she lost her grip on the scruff. She was relieved.

She picked up her baby and started running fast toward the forest. After reaching the nearest bordering tree of the forest, she stopped and turned back to look at the immediate threat.

No one was there. She only saw a single man who was walking toward the line. It was clear who had hit her on the belly. She didn't see his face, and she didn't have time to deal with him. Her baby was more important.

She just started thinking about her current situation. She didn't know how to get inside. Her baby was in critical condition.

After a while, in desperation, she decided to climb the huge wall while holding her kitten by the scruff. She was feeling very energized after killing the rat. Tearing the rat’s body flesh by flesh and eating it made her feel energised for some reason.

She held her baby by the scruff and decided to take the risk. She was feeling that she might be able to pull it off for some unknown reason.

She then reached the wooden log wall away from the town’s gate. Then she started climbing the wooden log border with her claws. When she reached the top, she turned her head and looked at the human who had walked toward the line earlier. His clothing and face were etched into her memory deeply. She swore that if she ever got a chance, she'd definitely attack that human.

Then she turned again to look at the other side of the wall. Descending while holding her baby was risky, but she had to do it. Facing down toward the ground was not a good choice, but she braced herself and began descending carefully with her claws. It took her some time to get down the wall. She was being very careful. Fortunately, she was able to descend safely.

After a successful descent, she began walking at a fast pace to reach her previous hideout. The human girl was not there yet. She decided to wait, and she put her baby on the ground.

After half an hour, the human girl came, holding a bag of meat and a water pouch.

When the black cat saw her from afar, she picked up her kitten and ran toward the girl. After reaching her, the black cat put her baby in front of the girl's feet and started mewing while touching the kitten with her paws.

When Cinthia noticed that the black cat was carrying her kitten, she was delighted. She thought maybe the cat wanted to show her its baby. She stopped moving forward and stood there to let the cat do its thing. She didn't want to get hissed at again. But when the black cat reached her, she noticed that the cat was bleeding from her neck.

Seeing this, the girl was shocked. She didn't know what to say or what to do. Then the cat suddenly put her kitten near her feet and started meowing while touching the kitten with her one paw.

The kitten wasn't moving at all. It was just breathing heavily. The girl touched the kitten and noticed that it was sick.

She didn't know what to do. Cinthia thought that the cat might hiss again if she picked up her kitten suddenly. But surprisingly, the cat mewed again and touched the kitten. Now Cinthia understood what the cat wanted.

She sat down, pointed her finger at the unconscious kitten, and said, "Do you want me to heal your kitty...? But I can't heal anyone…. Ohh"

Then she pointed her finger toward the town. "Mama can heal your kitty... Can I pick her up and bring her to Mama?"

The cat kept mewing... she didn't understand anything. She decided to pick the kitty up gently in her hand and looked at the black cat. Strangely, the black cat didn't do anything; it just looked at her.

Cinthia then pointed her finger toward the inner town and said, "Mama is at home, she will heal the kitty... come with me..." Then she started running toward her home. The black cat followed her from behind.

After a while, both reached home. Cinthia began banging on the door. Bang! Bang! Bang! Bang!

"Mama... Mama... open the door!... The kitty came with me... And and..."

"What happened, Cin darling... Stop banging the door... I'm coming..." Elenora said from inside.

Elenora opened the door to see what had happened. As soon as her vision locked on the kitten, her demeanor changed. "Bring the little kitty and put it on the table."

Cinthia ran inside, and the injured black cat followed her. Elenora noticed the injured black and then closed the door and went inside as well. After reaching the table where Cinthia had put the kitten, she looked at the black cat, who had a deep wound on her neck.

The cat was oozing elemental energy. Elenora knew there was only one way to get so much Elemental energy at a lower level.

"So you have defeated an evolved beast, huh..." Elenora said, looking at the black cat. She was impressed. Defeating an evolved beast is nearly impossible for an unevolved beast. It was almost unheard of. But right now, she just saw a living example of nature’s game. Then she chuckled, slightly.

Not only did the black cat defeat a beast, but it also ate its elemental core. This elemental core was providing the black cat with a good amount of elemental energy. It was more than enough to push through its injured body.

Elenora could use elemental energy to sense various things, and she was sensing an elemental core inside the black cat's stomach. It was healing the cat's injured body very, very slowly, and it was not sustainable. For the time being, it was keeping the cat alive.

Since the elemental energy wasn't being utilized properly, it would soon disappear and mix unevenly into the cat's body... There was nothing Elenora could do about it. Saving its kitten was the priority.

Elenora gently touched the small black kitten's body with her palm and started examining it. She found a dislocated hip bone and some cracked rib bones.

It felt as if the kitten was crushed under a rock. There were no significant injuries. The kitten's breathing was very slow. It probably lost consciousness from the immense pain of the injury.

Thankfully, the bones protected the organs, and the bones weren't broken, only cracked a bit. The only problem was the kitten's dislocated hip bone. She didn't know if she could fix it, but Elenora could only try.

Then she used her elemental energy to scan for broken bone particles, but there were none. The cat was mostly okay and only needed to have its hip bone reset and its cracked ribs healed.

She first picked up the kitten by its hind legs, then used her thumbs to push the hip bone back into the right place. As soon as she pushed it, the little kitty mewled slightly. The mother cat, who was sitting on the table, suddenly growled when she heard her baby cry out in pain. But the cat didn't attack; it only glared, ready to pounce if it felt her kitten was in danger.

Elenora glanced at the black cat for a moment and then ignored it. She put the kitten on its side and touched its chest with her fingers. She began to transfer her elemental energy to the cracked ribs.

This allowed her to forcefully activate the natural healing process of the bones. Since this was a baby, she was extra careful not to exceed the elemental energy, as it might cause the rib bone to grow out of proportion and cause even more damage in the future. She also transferred some of her elemental energy to the hip bones just to be on the safer side.

It took several minutes to properly heal the kitten. Then Elenora used elemental magic one last time to scan for anything else. Thankfully, she found nothing. She kept transferring her elemental energy to remove the kitten's exhaustion. After that, the kitten was sound asleep, and its breathing became normal. Elenora picked it up and put it in front of the black mother cat. The black mother cat looked at the sleeping kitten and then started licking it.

After a few seconds, the kitten opened its eyes. The black kitten was delighted. Elenora moved behind the black cat and said, "You're also injured... look at me..."

The cat didn't understand anything, but it understood that Elenora was trying to say something to her. The black cat looked at Elenora, and then Elenora put both her palms on the black cat's sides and started healing her. Feeling the healing process, the cat jumped back.

Elenora was confused... she didn't know why the cat rejected her. The cat now clearly knew about her healing abilities. Even Cinthia was shocked, but she didn't say anything, only letting her mother handle everything.

The black cat jumped down from the table and went near Elenora’s legs. Then the cat bit her long skirt and started pulling. At first, she didn't know what the cat wanted to do. Just then, the cat started pushing with her head instead of pulling her skirt.

Now Elenora understood that the cat wanted to bring her somewhere. To be sure, she also scanned the cat's body and found out that its heart was beating fast. It was scared.

The cat wanted to bring Elenora somewhere. Elenora bent down and patted the cat's head lightly. The cat stopped and stared at Elenora.

Elenora turned to her daughter. "Cin darling, will you stay home for now? Kitty wants to take me somewhere."

"Mama, I want to go, too!"

"No dear, the place might be dangerous, and the black kitty is very scared... How can I protect both you and the kitty?"

Cinthia became sad and was about to cry, but her mother's words were final. "Can't I come? I can also fight!"

"Look, Papa will come back soon... Don't you want to welcome him home? One of us has to stay here for Papa... Do you understand?"

"Hmm..." Cinthia was sad. She couldn't refuse now.

"Tell him everything. Okay? When he comes back, tell him everything that happened now in detail, okay?"

"Hmm..."

Elenora went to another room and picked up her staff, and then went out of her home. The cat, seeing Elenora running out, jumped on the table and picked up her kitten, and started following behind Elenora. After going to the main street, Elenora looked at the cat. The cat understood what she wanted it to do. The black cat started running toward the town gate while holding her baby in her mouth. Elenora started running behind her.

When they reached the town’s gate, Elenora’s face darkened. Now she understood what might be happening. She knew that the black cat’s den was out of town, probably inside the forest. She prepared herself for the worst. Maybe all of the babies were severely injured.

The cat led Elenora to the forest. As she followed the black cat, she became even more tense; it was early night. Night beasts are very dangerous, and it was hard to detect them because most nocturnal beings were skilled at hiding.

While running, she held her magic staff in front, prepared to attack at a moment's notice. The cat led the way very meticulously. Elenora noticed that since they entered the forest, they had not encountered any beasts.

This was very unusual. She figured that the cat must know the way to avoid evolved beasts, even at night when dangerous nocturnal beings are usually active.

After half an hour of constant running, she started having a weird sensation. It felt as if someone was observing her from somewhere, but she couldn't pinpoint the observer's location, even after trying to sense the surroundings with elemental energy.

After a while, they reached a humongous tree. The tree wasn't visible from the town, but seeing it up close, she could sense that space distortion magic was being used to keep it hidden. This was very unusual.

On the ground, she saw blood marks. It must have been from the evolved animal the cat had defeated. By seeing the bloodstains, she concluded that the dead animal must have been dragged from below the tree roots out to the forest undergrowth.

Years of adventure had taught Elenora a lot of things. It was clear that an evolved beast must have attacked the den, been killed by the cat, and then dragged away to avoid attracting predators.

While she was thinking all this, the cat entered below the tree roots. She took her baby inside, then brought another one out to her. This kitten was mewling in pain. When she checked, this kitten also had similar cracked bones, but no dislocated or broken bones and no internal organ damage. So, she started the same healing process she had used before.

After a while, the baby kitten was sound asleep. The black cat noticed this and brought it back to her den, and then brought out another one. After healing two more kittens, the black cat brought a strange baby.

Its body structure was a bit like a cat's, but it was totally white, and its tail was similar to a lizard's. Its closed eyes were unusually big. Though it had a cute, lizard-like face, it was clearly not a lizard. It felt like a cross between a cat and a lizard.

After scanning the baby's body, she wasn't sure what she was observing. The baby had a few unusual organs inside its body, and its skin and muscle structure were strange.

Getting back from her distraction, she started focusing on the injuries on its body. It also had cracked bones, but it was the least affected one. Elenora healed its bones, and it started sleeping soundly.

The cat turned around and was about to go put the creature back in her den, but Elenora suddenly held the black cat from both sides.

Finally, she started healing the black cat. The black cat didn't struggle and just stood there while holding the baby in its mouth. After healing the black cat, she started petting its head. The cat stayed there for a few seconds, then went inside its den.

Then Elenora stepped back a bit and looked at the tree. "I know you’re there. Can you at least be discreet if you're so closely observing me?"

"Hmm… So you’re not oblivious to your surroundings... Anyway… thanks. Kid, you have saved someone I am looking after."

"You're welcome. I'm Elenora Pagonis, a healing expert living in the nearby town. Who might you be, Sir?"

"I'm no one important for the town folk to know about. Just don't say anything about me. I don't like being bothered."

"As you wish. Since my work is done, I'm taking my leave. Have a great day."
