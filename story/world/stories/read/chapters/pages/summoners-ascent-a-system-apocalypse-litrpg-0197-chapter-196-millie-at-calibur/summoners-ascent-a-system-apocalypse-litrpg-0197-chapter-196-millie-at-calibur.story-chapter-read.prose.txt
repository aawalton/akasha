"Want to do the honours?" Ashton asked. The girl beside him nervously nodded her head. She held forward her hand and knocked on the metal door in front of her.

"Hello? We want to get in," Millie said in a low voice. Ashton chuckled and placed a hand onto herr head.

"A bit louder, and introduce yourself. Say who we are," he explained, and Millie slowly nodded her head.

She knocked on the door harder. "Hello! My name is Millicent! I'm here with Ashton! We want to come in!"

"Better." Ashton focused on what was going on at the top of the wall. Instead of having people stand on top, the people of Calibur set up mirror networks so that they could see who was standing in front of the doors and keep an eye on potential monsters.

It didn't take long until the door was opened up.

"Ashton? I didn't even know you woke up," the gatekeeper said with a laugh before glancing at Millie. "But, uhm... who is this?"

"She introduced herself, didn't she? She's Millicent, so she goes by Millie," Ashton explained. "She's my apprentice."

"Hm? Apprentice? Will we have another summoner running around?"

Ashton shook his head. "No, but I'll be teaching her magic."

"That so? Well, fair enough," the man replied as he closed the gate again. Usually, people would be a bit confused why Ashton just showed up with a kid in tow, but he had done weirder things already.

Plus... there was nobody here that wasn't aware of the fact that there were plenty of unprotected children running around who had lost their parents over the past two weeks. Ashton was someone they knew they could trust, so they didn't seem to have any doubts about him.

For now, Ashton just showed Millie around. He showed her where to get food and where the first aid station was in case she got hurt. Of course, he also introduced her to all the people she could turn to in case Ashton wasn't available for one reason or another, though right now, most people were out.

It was just past noon, so a lot of people had gone out and were either hunting or trying to find more supplies and crafting materials.

Ashton also showed Millie the office that he was using as a bedroom so far. She was surprised by all the stuff all over the place, though Ashton was already taking care of that.

A lot of the materials here were things that could be sorted away through the Backpacker's new ability, and the rest could be stashed away in the Living Abode later. It might be a little cramped, but that was fine. The Living Abode should grow fairly fast at the start.

"Alright, for now, let's go up to the roof. The view from there's pretty nice," Ashton explained as he gave a bottle to the Drunken Bear, asking them to start carrying some of the the bulkier items that didn't quite fit into the Backpacker's bag.

"What do you care about the view?" Millie asked with a baffled frown.

"Shut up," Ashton retorted.

The two climbed up the stairs to the very top of the building. The watchman greeted Ashton as usual.

"Any problems with flying monsters recently?" Ashton wondered, and the watchman slowly nodded.

"Somewhat. There aren't any regular attacks, but sometimes we have the rare flying monster wander over here. I'm actually working on getting the Archer class as my second so that I can deal with that as soon as I see them," the watchman proudly explained.

That was actually a pretty good idea. The synergy between those classes was pretty good, especially if the Archer class was developed along the 'Sharpshooter' path.

"Good. Keep that up. Let me know if something approaches while we're up here," Ashton said, stretching his hand out toward the Backpacker. They quickly gave him the folded-up Living Abode that was hanging off the side of their bag, as well as a couple of magic stones.

Ashton channelled the construct as he threw it forward. Still mid-air, the tent began to unfold itself and the tent's pegs quickly embedded themselves on the rooftop. By channelling the tent, Ashton was able to guide those pegs to be in the places where they would cause the least amount of trouble and long-term damage. That didn't mean that the watchman wasn't surprised, though. It was a fairly large tent just at its base, after all.

Luckily the rooftop was generally empty, since most of the freestanding things here were used as materials for other matters. The planters that used to be up here were also brought down so that people could use them to grow food.

The only thing that was left was a single table and the benches that belonged to it, which was kept here by Ashton's request, so that there was a space where he could be in peace when working on important matters.

Ashton directed the Backpacker and the Drunken Bear to put down certain items inside. He had collected some scrap wood from benches and fences on the way, and used it to create a few more storage chests to keep things organised.

Millie pulled out a pillow out of the front of the tent to sit next to Ashton as he was seated right before it as well. Usually you couldn't move linked objects out of the Living Abode, but in this particularly case, anything that was within the confines of the circle created by the pegs still counted as 'within'.

"You're going to call the merchant guy now, right?"

"They're called a 'Salesman'."

"Same thing, right?"

Ashton quickly shook his head. "No, not at all actually. This comes back to the importance of names that I mentioned before. While spirits don't usually reveal their true names to others, they do take a lot of pride in the name of their clan. It does adjust to the language of the person they interact with, but in English, it's 'Salesman'."

"But why?"

"Hm?"

"Why is it Salesman? Can't it be Merchant? They mean the same thing, right?"

"Well... technically, yeah. But it's more complicated than that," Ashton explained. He hadn't actually thought about this all too often, but he figured he might as well explain it to Millie. "You see, spirits have their own language, and it's largely magical in nature. While it's generally not possible to understand that language if you don't have the right type of 'ears', they specifically made it so that the names of clans can be understood by anyone, since they're so important. And when you hear them, they're translated into your language. And in English... the name of the Salesman's clan translates to 'Salesman'."

Millie slightly tilted her head, not sure she quite understood. With a smile, Ashton focused on the spirit sitting beside him. "Do you mind telling Millie the name of your clan?" he asked.

The small figure nodded and stood up, standing in front of Millie. Before long, something did sound out. It was like an escaping whisper, like thin silk flowing through one's fingers. This is what the spirits' language sounded like. Most of it couldn't be understood, but at some point, there was a single specific word that popped into Ashton's head. It wasn't audible in any way, but he just knew that this is what the spirit said.

"Backpacker," Millie said, repeating the word that popped into her head. A broad smile appeared on her face. "That's so cool! Can you tell me about some other spirits?"

The Backpacker quickly shook their head, sitting back down next to Ashton to continue playing their game on their phone.

"A spirit can only communicate the name of their own clan like that."

"Awe..." Millie lowered her head sadly, though the Drunken Bear also quickly came over, telling her the name of their own clan.

And while she was distracted with that, Ashton quickly got out his tome. Flipping to the right page, he quickly dropped some coins onto it and summoned the Salesman.

Millie snapped her head back forward, watching as the carpet fell down from above out of seemingly nowhere and the Salesman came crawling out from underneath it.

They stared at Ashton for a moment as they patted down their cloak, and he laughed apologetically.

"Sorry about that, I'll find a more pleasant place for you to come out next time," he said, though there was something more important right now. "Though for now, I've heard that you have a few things for me."

The Salesman nodded, quickly starting to place the items they bought up in front of them on the carpet.

"Whoa... Is that like the kind of guy that Batman is?" Millie asked, and Ashton turned his head surprised.

"Excuse me?"

"You know, Batman."

"I know Batman, but that's not what they are. Batman is just a guy dressed up like a bat, this one here is a spirit that just so happens to have bat-like traits," Ashton explained, and Millie narrowed her eyes.

"So... they're more like Manbat."

"Sure," Ashton smiled, getting out the Pen of Insight. "Salesman, I figured I should ask first, but do you mind if I inspect these items?"

Of course, the spirit smiled, slowly pushing out one of their hands from underneath their cloak. They waved it over the items in a 'go ahead'-like gesture.

And so, Ashton quickly inspected all the things that were in front of him. Just like he had requested, most of the items were books, so he had the Pen of Insight write a simple list of them. The pen even linked to the Salesman's ability and wrote down the cost of each book. There were some that stuck out the most.

And once Ashton read the description that the Pen of Insight wrote out for it. It was simple, and quite short. But well, the effect was clearly just as simple.
