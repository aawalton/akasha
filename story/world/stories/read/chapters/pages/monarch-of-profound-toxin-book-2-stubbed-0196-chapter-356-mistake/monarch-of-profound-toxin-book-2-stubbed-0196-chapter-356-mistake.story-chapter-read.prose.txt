Chapter 356

Rushing in to flank him, the spawn approached at speed, its filthy nails now morphed into meter-long claws that looked more like the blades of a scythe than anything else.

Rather than continuing his retreat, Eik now did a virtual one eighty and suddenly exploded forward, taking the monster by surprise. Conjuring a katana at double the length of the previous, Eik thrust it into the spawn’s chest with a palm on the butt of the weapon in the same moment as multiple shells along his shoulder, arm, hip, and leg took the brunt of the descending claws.

The Earthling grunted in pain, but more so from the ache in his stomach wound than from the direct attack itself. Taken aback by Eik’s abrupt aggression for only a moment, the spawn immediately changed its approach and ducked in for another claw attack.

Extinction Zone had already had a bit of time to build up some toxicity and combined with the katana’s worth of Profound Toxin that he had applied through the wound in the knuckles earlier, Eik didn’t even bother to dodge.

Accelerant rocked the fiend violently, the powerful internal detonation threatening to tear it apart from the inside out. Around the fleshier parts of its body—not that there was really much flesh to speak of anywhere on the spawn, since it was practically a skeleton from head to toe—the skin bulged grotesquely as explosive power fought to escape.

Glints of bright blue jetted across its skin in rapid bursts as several booming pulse waves cracked through the air, throwing Eik back as well. But he had expected it and had already prepared blocks of crystal under his feet to propel him directly back at speed before the distance between them became anything significant.

Using the momentum of the forward movement, he drove two thick spikes into the spawn’s body, with one in the chest and one just below the rib cage. They melted into its body in the next second.

Finally regaining its bearings, the beast snarled and made to throw itself at him when a massive rod of pure crystal, released by the Apocalypse Canvas above, hammered into it and carried it into direct free fall. Despite frantic mental commands, the projectiles launched by the Apocalypse Canvas hadn’t stood a chance of catching the Lord of the Moon as he retreated. He had simply been too fast.

A deadly sequence of seven titanic, razor-sharp blades followed the rod into a plunge. First to hit the ground, the rod pierced the crust of the world like a screwdriver into a wad of soft butter and melted as it began to lose momentum, further intoxicating the spawn while simultaneously providing space for the first of the blades to land.

Each impact altered the topography of the surrounding areas dramatically, erasing hills and small mountain, and annihilating forests and rivers.

And although the stomach wound was seriously beginning to addle him, as the spreading black discoloration of his skin had reached his clavicle and middle of his thigh, he still found the strength to hurl one crystalline javelin after the other into the mix, reaching a final count of a little over three dozen projectiles.

Taking a page out of his own book, he did what he had done during the spar with Chop the Blade and let thick, blue mist scatter vastly, concealing him completely from sight as well as providing an aural signature identical to his own which would make it virtually impossible to track him.

He then went back down to the ground where he could move more freely, despite his adeptness at explosive, airborne mobility.

Now that the chance was there, he took a moment to redouble his efforts with Panacean Quintessence and circulated it relentlessly, flooding the injury with as concentrated a substance as he could. It should have had an instantaneous effect and done wonders, but the wound remained.

Damn it all to hell…

It didn’t feel like he was about to lose consciousness or anything like that, but for the first time since his Awakening, he was feeling something similar to what he had made his victims feel in nearly all fights he had been in. And damn, if it didn’t suck ass.

The earth was still rumbling and churning under his feet as the aftershocks of the eight major impacts crumbled everything under the surface. But, unfortunately, the creepy bastard wasn’t dead yet. Eik could still feel the presence of his Profound Toxin inside an extremely powerful foreign system, and that could only be the X-ranked spawn.

But it was weak. Weak enough that one, last strike should be enough to do the trick. Hopefully. He couldn’t keep up his usual pace with an injury that refused to get better, so this shit had better end soon.

As a result of the continuous barrage of devastating destruction with the spawn stuck in the center of it all, an extreme dose of Profound Toxin sat ready within. With an echoing snap of his fingers, Eik triggered a ruinous Accelerant.

A resounding boom announced the beginning of a shock wave of such a magnitude that it first whipped the earth up into ripples in the same way as a pebble thrown into a tranquil lake, before everything was sucked back into the depths as if by a vacuum-powered sinkhole kilometers wide. Not even a second later, enough soil to create an entire island in the sea erupted into the air.

It felt as if the world was being ripped to pieces by a natural disaster. He had been X-rank for a little while at this point and even done a bit of fighting, but witnessing something like this with the knowledge that it was genuinely a demonstration of his own power was still almost unbelievable.

He had spent the majority of his adult life cowering away from any signs of danger, well aware that his strength was worthless against the monsters that had been running wild on Earth since their world’s induction into the Unified Mass. For the first many years of the apocalypse, until he had established himself properly as a medicine merchant, the best he had been able to offer his brother Torbjørn and Olivia, who had fought so valiantly for their survival, was a shameful word of gratitude when they came home in pain.

A thank you… That was what he could give back then. That had been the limit of his power.

So how the hell had he ended up here, fighting a world-destroying battle against one of the most powerful beings in the universe?

With a shallow sigh of pain, he felt for a sign of life from the spawn. Nothing.

In the last moment, there had been the clear, familiar sensation of a system expiring. It was unmistakable. Unless it somehow had the ability to come back to life, this fight was over.

Sinking to his knees and then down to his back, propped up on an elbow, he glanced down at his arm as the level notifications ticked in in glowing text. Dust still felt thickly after the explosion whose power had easily rivaled that of an eruption of a super volcano, but a thin sheet of aura across his whole body kept it from actually landing on him, instead rolling off smoothly as minuscule vibrations guided it to the ground.

He read the messages as the toxic mist settled and dispersed.

[Acquired Living Manifestation — Lv. 277]

Huh. That level was probably from his son at home, doing whatever. Everything gave that kid levels, and therefore Eik got levels as well. Pretty sweet deal in general.

[Acquired Monarch’s Will — Lv. 388]

[Acquired Monarch’s Will — Lv. 389]

[Acquired Monarch’s Will — Lv. 390]

Closing in on Level four hundred. Hopefully that would be a serious milestone.

[Acquired Profound Toxin — Lv. 492]

[Acquired Profound Toxin — Lv. 493]

[Acquired Profound Toxin — Lv. 494]

[Acquired Profound Toxin — Lv. 495]

And even closer to a big milestone than Monarch’s Will was Profound Toxin. Five hundred… Halfway to a thousand. That had to mean that there was something extra special in store for that one, no?

There had better damn well be.

[Acquired Apocalypse Canvas — Lv. 198]

[Acquired Apocalypse Canvas — Lv. 199]

[Acquired Apocalypse Canvas — Lv. 200]

[Acquired Apocalypse Canvas — Lv. 201]

[Acquired Apocalypse Canvas — Lv. 202]

It had been a while since he’d really used Apocalypse Canvas in a way that had netted him levels in the skill, so it was nice to see it finally get some progress. And with the rounding of level 200…

[Skill evolution available. Skill available for evolution: Apocalypse Canvas]

[Choose one]

[Apocalypse Canvas — Extinction Canvas]

And? Where was the rest? Oh, for crying out loud, was this another single option evolution? On one hand, whenever it was only the one option, it was always something good, but on the other hand…

“If there is only a single option to pick, at least don’t tell me to ‘choose one’. What is that if not rubbing it in?” Eik muttered under his breath, chuckling to himself despite the wound. As he watched, those exact two golden words slowly faded from his arm, as if some invisible hand had swept in to scratch it out with a common eraser. “Uuh, thanks, I guess…”

It kind of seemed like Toxin had more control over his system messages the more he grew in power, as if it somehow loosened his connection to the System. The first time it had happened, it had taken the Worldbreaker a few tries to get the messages to change properly, but now it it had been smooth and seamless.

“Well, I suppose I don’t have much choice in the matter, then, do I?” Eik mumbled and selected Extinction Canvas. “Now, what does this do? No, actually…”

Maybe now wasn’t such a good time to check out what the new trait did. Considering his pretty terrible condition, he needed all the energy he could spare.

It wasn’t like there was even a single healer or medical specialist in the Nidafjeld Alliance who would be capable of healing a destructive ability originating from the Lord of the Moon. Not if his own Panacean Quintessence couldn’t.

Shit. There had to be something he could do about this. He refused to just lay back and die. And he sure as hell wasn’t going to go back home and let his kids see the hole through his stomach.

With clenched teeth, he raked a finger slowly through the blackened wound.

Fuckin’ hell, it had been a while since he felt pain like that. That pain alone was a testament to the power of whatever was affecting him.

His finger coming away black with a viscous substance, he held it up to his eye to study it more closely.

At first, there was nothing particularly remarkable about it. It was just a disgusting, undulating mass of pitch, but the more he looked at it the more he began to see things that didn’t look right. Here and there, it would suddenly separate into strings so thin that even his X-ranked vision could barely make it out.

They wiggled.

They wiggled a lot.

Perhaps the strongest case of the shivers he had ever experienced ran down his spine as the realization struck, that what covered him both inside and out was, in fact, not sticky tar, but billions of trillions of unbelievably tiny, cosmic parasites.

This was not something that was fixed with medicine.

No, for this he needed fire.
