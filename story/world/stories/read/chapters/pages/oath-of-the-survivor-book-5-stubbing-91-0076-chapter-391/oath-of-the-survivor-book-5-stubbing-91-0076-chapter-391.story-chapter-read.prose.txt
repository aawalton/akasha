“I told you to leave some for me!”  Skippy complained as another group of fungal constructs were pulverized by Aura Force as the group of C Grades blurred toward their target.

“Then be fast enough to get them before I have to clean them up,” Kyle shot back, releasing another destructive wave of force to crush the newcomers.

Kyle and Tillienne flew low, just above the ground, and Skippy easily kept pace with them as his flame-wreathed body skated along just below them.  The fungal constructs were barely an afterthought, with the smaller groups not even warranting the use of the Death concept or Wither.  Instead, he simply squished them where they stood.  A few of the larger tendrils appeared from time to time, attempting to bind or crush them, but otherwise the surface-level defenses were relatively benign.  Kyle expected that to change rapidly as they got closer to the core.

[THE CAVERN WE IDENTIFIED SHOULD BE AHEAD.  HOWEVER, IT LOOKS AS THOUGH THE ENEMY HAS TAKEN PAINS TO ATTEMPT AND CONCEAL IT.  MY SCANS ARE STILL UNABLE TO FULLY PIERCE THE CRUST AND GET BELOW, HOWEVER IT IS SAFE TO SAY THAT THE MYCELIUM IS INVOLVED.]

C.H.A.D.D. projected a view of the upcoming area, highlighting where the cavern ought to have been.  As they looked ahead, all they could see was more of the rocky soil, a testament to the stealth abilities the fungi seemed to have.

“Mine!” Skippy shouted, sprinting ahead of Kyle and Tillienne.  He drew his hand back as his flaming aura intensified for a moment, then released a palm strike toward the space the cavern should have been.  A focused blast of force and flame rushed out, tearing a deep furrow through the rocky ground.  The space around the edges of the attack felt odd to both Auric Perception and Synaptic Barbs, though despite his focused attention, he couldn’t quite pin down what other concepts the attack was using.

All Kyle knew was that it was effective.  The concealment had been entirely blown away, along with much of the original stone cavern.  What remained was a wide cave mouth that descended further into the moon’s subsurface.  A writhing mass of pink and gray fungal flesh was exposed, with much of it still burning in the wake of Skippy’s attack.  The skrell widened his lead, rushing into the cavern and releasing another gout of flame as he disappeared from sight.

Kyle and Tillienne exchanged a look.

A smile pulled at the edges of Tillienne’s mouth.  “That’s what you get for hogging all the action.  Do try to keep up.”

With that, she pulled ahead further, disappearing within the ruined and burning cavern.

Kyle sighed as he pushed his skills further to keep pace.  His party members were fast.  Kyle’s long-distance travel speed might have been better, but when it came to quick bursts of speed and acceleration, these two had him beat.

[SKIPPY’S ATTACK DAMAGED MANY OF THE CONCEALMENT FORMATIONS, DR. MAYHEW.  I CAN SEE THE COMPLEX BENEATH.  THE ENTIRE SUBTERRANEAN SYSTEM IS SHIFTING.]

Kyle swore, picking up speed.  He flew into the open mouth, only to see the tendrils of fungal mass already shifting and twisting.  The light of Skippy’s flaming aura disappeared as thick walls of fungal mass erupted to block Kyle’s path.  He responded by pushing forward with Devourer’s Domain and Wither, draining the tendrils and crumbling them away, but it wasn’t enough.  C.H.A.D.D.’s map highlighted the ever-changing labyrinth, and as Kyle destroyed the fungal masses coming his way, both Skippy and Tillienne had already been pulled in different directions, the entry to the cavern having been split up.

Kyle sent them both a quick message explaining the situation, then swore again as he evaluated the plan.  The relative weakness of the fungal constructs they’d faced thus far left them all feeling confident, and that cavalier attitude was dangerous.  Regardless of the means at DeRosa’s disposal, his best method to taking out the C Grades was to divide them and focus his efforts on each individually.  That’s not to say he’d have an easy time of it, but their lack of caution inadvertently handed DeRosa his optimal scenario.

A message from Tillienne came across his nav bracelet, and he turned from his frustration back toward it.

Looks like a lot of effort is being put into moving these hallways.  I’ve cut through quite a bit of the biomass, and more keeps coming to replace it.  Kyle, any idea how much mana that must be taking?

Before Kyle could respond, Skippy replied.

Who cares?  Race to the target, hit him until he’s dead.  Any of us should be able to manage that much.

Kyle felt a vein throbbing in his forehead.

The mana expenditure depends on whether or not he’s using pre-grown biomass or growing it fresh.  And Skippy, it’s not like he’s going to take you right to his lair.  If C.H.A.D.D. is correct, we’re all being pulled farther away, not closer.

Skippy’s reply was almost instant.

That’s a letdown.  I didn’t think he’d be such a coward.  Hold on.

Auric Perception felt a massive spike in power, just as the tendrils around him started moving.  A moment later, the whole cavern began to shake as Kyle felt Skippy’s power explode outward.

[DR. MAYHEW, I BELIEVE THAT MIGHT HAVE BEEN A BIT EXCESSIVE.]

C.H.A.D.D. reinforced its point by sharing a map of the nearby area, where a massive void had been opened within the cavern.  Kyle focused Devourer’s Domain in the direction the drone indicated, using Aura Force to push open a path through the deadened fungus.

Intentionally or not, Skippy had severed many of the connecting tendrils that supported the network of mycelic tunnels.  That meant that for now, he wasn’t being shepherded away.  However, that wouldn’t last for long.  C.H.A.D.D.’s scanners indicated that more were flooding into the newly-opened chamber to try and rebuild what had been broken, and Kyle’s goal was to get close enough to the newly-opened weak point to put a more permanent stop to the tunnel network.

He was making steady progress, when a deep voice rumbled through the cavern system.  Kyle felt as much as heard the grating words, seeming to come from everywhere and nowhere.  It was as if the walls themselves spoke, the fleshy fungal masses all vibrating in unison.

You have proven to be quite disruptive to my plans.  Now, die.

The vibrations across the walls increased in intensity, and Kyle felt his connection to them fade as they simply withered of their own accord, hardening into thin strands, devoid of any life.  Kyle was confused for a moment, until the rumbling all around him intensified.

Kyle felt a tug on his mana, and he allowed C.H.A.D.D. to utilize their connection to draw from him, even as he activated Cultivate and Infuse.  Thick tree roots exploded out of the World Tree Seed, pushing against the collapsing stone of the tunnels and covering Kyle in a protective cocoon of wood.

C.H.A.D.D. guided the growth as Kyle looked at his nav bracelet and frantically sent a message.

Are you two okay?  Do you need help?

He waited, staring at screen until he finally saw a response come through from Tillienne.

I’m fine, just a little stuck under the rubble.  Should be able to get free pretty soon, but didn’t expect him to drop everything on us.

Skippy responded a moment later, and Kyle could almost see the wide smile on the skrell’s face.  Should have been better prepared.  I’ll be out in a moment, I can come lend a hand.

Satisfied his friends were doing fine, Kyle turned his attention back to his own predicament.  The thick wooden construct prevented Kyle from getting crushed, but that didn’t mean that he was free and clear.  Every minute Kyle spent being buried beneath hundreds of tons of collapsed stone was a minute he gave DeRosa to prepare.  And that just wouldn’t do.  C.H.A.D.D. provided detailed scans of the area above, and Kyle used the roots to apply pressure in the exact places he needed in order to shift the larger pieces.

As he did, he slowly extricated the large cocoon by shifting the wood around him, creating space to shape the wooden construct around the shifting debris.  He looked down at his prosthesis, which was dangling beneath the erupting tree roots.  The control training for all the vines really paid off, he thought.  Managing the mass of roots was neither easy nor mana efficient, but it was possible thanks to the near-constant practice he’d had managing the vines that made up his daily prosthesis.

He wasn’t sure how Skippy and Tillienne were faring, but decided the best thing he could do was get himself out, then try to unbury them after.  Minutes dragged by as he continued the monotonous work, all the while trying to think through the next move.  All chatter on the nav bracelet had ceased, and when Kyle finally broke free from the entrapping stone, he looked up and confirmed his suspicion.

Just as DeRosa had done twice before, the upper atmosphere was covered in a cloud of dark spores.  The signal-jamming technique was within their calculations, though the timing was truly unfortunate.  Kyle hadn’t expected that DeRosa would be able to separate the C Grade strike force in the way he had, and the limited communications would only make their next decisions more challenging.

Should they regroup with the ship and reevaluate, or press onward and try to regain some pressure on DeRosa?  Neither choice was perfect, and –

Kyle’s thought process was rudely interrupted as he felt a pulse of mana from the rubble nearby.  He turned to look as space itself seemed to warp, followed by the fallen stone rippling and shattering, revealing Skippy and Tillienne.

“I told you that Mayhew was going to be fine,” Skippy admonished her.  “And I would have been out first if I didn’t have to pick you up.  So this doesn’t count.”

Tillienne rolled her eyes.  “Yes, yes, you’ve made that clear.  I’m sorry that I don’t have spatial warping abilities to cheat my way out of a spot like this.”

Skippy pointed accusingly at Kyle.  “Neither does he, and he got out just fine.”

The fae woman glared daggers at the skrell.  “And I would have gotten out, too.  It just would have taken me a little longer is all.”

“Now that we’re all here,” Kyle said, interrupting the argument, “how do we feel about pressing forward?  Do you have the resources you need to keep going?”

Skippy gave an offended look.  “Of course I do!  It takes a lot more than collapsing a cave on me to make me stop.”

“Likewise,” Tillienne said.  “We aren’t the same as we were on Kertan Six.  All he managed to do was slow us down.”

A smile tugged at Kyle’s mouth as he thought back to the collapsing settlement that nearly took them out back then.  They’d come a long, long way.  “Then let’s get going.  And make sure to stick together this time.  We got lucky being able to regroup, but I doubt he’ll make the same mistakes again.”

“I know, I know,” Skippy said as both Kyle and Tillienne looked at him.  “Just promise to let me hit some of the constructs if we run into any.”

The look on the skrell’s face was almost comically pleading, and Kyle gave him a slow nod.  “Sure, so long as you stick with the plan.”

The skrell leapt onto a nearby piece of rubble in excitement.  “Then stop wasting time!  We have a mushroom to hunt.”
