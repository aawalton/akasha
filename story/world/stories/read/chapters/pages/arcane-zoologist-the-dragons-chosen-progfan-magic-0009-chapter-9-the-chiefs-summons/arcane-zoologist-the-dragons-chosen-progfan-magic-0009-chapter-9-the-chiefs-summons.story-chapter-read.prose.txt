When Callie got home, she was humming with glee. It had been a wonderful day, even though her hands were covered in painful blisters from using the bow drill, and Aurelia and Gerda confronted her. The fact that she had used magic and, better yet, grown a petal on her mark made her incredibly excited.

She wasn't sure how magic worked or why the petal had appeared. But it most likely had something to do with Wiggles. The mark reacted to arcane beasts, but in what way exactly, Callie wasn't sure. The fact that Wiggles had gone out of its way to save Callie was also a huge discovery.

Mirelings were very much capable of thought. Wiggles had saved her, which in Callie's opinion would make it smarter than Aurelia. She didn't have the smarts required to understand that other people had feelings too. Although the burns Wiggles gave her and Gerda were concerning… Hopefully they would heal quickly.

"Welcome home, Callie!" Callen greeted her, giving her a gentle hug. "You seem to be in a good mood. Did something good happen? Learn something interesting in your survival lessons?"

"You could say that…" She said, unable to suppress a happy grin forming on her face.

Callen raised an eyebrow and smiled. "Do tell your big brother!"

Callie motioned him to lean closer. He obliged, turning his ear towards her.

"I… used magic!" She said in a barely restrained whisper.

Callen reeled back. "You what?!"

"Don't worry! Nobody saw. But when we had to light a fire, I couldn't do it, my hands were so tired and hurting," Callie said, showing the blisters on her hands.

"Your hands!" Callen exclaimed, grabbing them and inspecting them. His expression quickly turned to one of anger. "Did Mister Blackthorn push you to this point?"

"Yup! But it's a good thing he did. I don't think I would have been able to use magic otherwise. When I felt like I would never be able to do it, I felt this… energy swell within me and next thing I knew, I had the fire going."

"Are you sure he didn't see it?" Callen asked nervously.

"No! He was too busy sharpening blades to pay attention to me."

Callen's expression grew even more sour at this.

"But you know, something even more amazing happened afterwards. Look!" Callie said and rolled her sleeve up, revealing the mark with a single petal.

"What?! It's bigger. How did that happen?" Callen asked, inspecting it carefully. "Does it hurt?"

"No, it doesn't. It happened after Wiggles saved me from Aurelia and Gerda. This definitely proves that arcane beasts are capable of thinking. In fact, I'd say that Wiggles is smarter than many of the villagers," Callie said proudly.

A mix of emotions could be seen on her brother's face, and none of them were happy ones. Callie deflated a little. "A-Are you mad at me?"

"What?! No! Why would I be mad at you?" Callen said, pulling Callie into a hug. "You did great. Although please be careful not to use any magic in front of anyone. We don't know what would happen if somebody found out. And about… Wiggles, you called him. You should be more careful around it."

"I promise to be more careful with magic. I didn't mean to use it. I happened because I felt... frustrated," Callie assured her brother.

"Frustrated?" Callen asked in confusion.

"Yeah, the magic just burst out of me before I could do anything about it."

Callen straightened and let out a heavy sigh. He began pacing around the room, rubbing at his temples. Callie watched him nervously.

"B-Brother?"

"I… Sorry, there's just a lot of things happening. I'm just trying to figure out how we best approach this…" Callen muttered.

The door opened, and Dad came inside, his eyes filled with anger as he locked onto Callie. She had no clue why he was so mad, but Callie instantly hid behind her brother, clutching onto his arm. Something gave her the feeling that he was mad at her.

"Callie, could you please explain why the village chief has summoned you and me to his home first thing tomorrow morning?" Dad growled.

Callie clutched onto Callen's hand even tighter. Callen, her trustworthy big brother, shielded her from Dad's view, staring straight back at him.

"Likely because Aurelia has spun another pretty tale where Callie has been turned into a villain," Callen shot back.

"Aurelia and Gerda have horrible burns on their legs! They said that Callie burned them after their survival lessons," Dad roared.

"I…I didn't! It was…" Callie stopped mid-sentence. She couldn't say that it was Wiggles. They would hunt and kill him.

"It was what?" Dad demanded.

Callie then realized that she would be blamed for this. No, she had to take the blame for this. If she didn't, then Wiggles would be in danger. A heavy knot of desperation formed in her stomach, wondering what kind of punishment the village chief would give her. If it were just more chores, she could deal with it. But what if he… hit her?

"Father!" Callen growled, clutching Callie's hand firmly. "Why do you always believe it to be Callie's fault? Have you even stopped to ask her side of the story?"

Dad looked surprised at the firm way Callen was speaking, his expression softening a little. "T-That's what I wish to find out. Callie, tell me what happened."

Callie thought about it for a minute. She couldn't tell Dad the truth. He would kill Wiggles. She had to lie.

"I did it," Callie said quietly after a long pause.

"What?!" Callen and Dad exclaimed in unison.

"I-I'll accept any punishment from the chief!" Callie said and rushed into her room, unable to handle her father's stern expression anymore. She felt that a moment longer and she would burst into tears.

Dad and Callen were arguing about something for a good while, but Callie did her best to ignore it. It wasn't that hard. Her mind was filled with the horrifying punishment the village chief could give her. Forbid her food? Maybe he'd just expel her from the village altogether?

But I can't let them find out about Wiggles. No matter what.

This strong resolve was the only thing that kept Callie grounded. That night, she didn't sleep much.

"No matter what, you can't say that it was your fault. Got it?" Callen said while holding Callie's hands firmly.

Callie nodded numbly as they walked. She was barely able to hold herself back from trembling. They were on their way to meet the village chief.

Dad hadn't spoken all morning; the only reaction he gave was the occasional glare in Callen's direction. Callie wished Mom were here. Mom always came up with creative ways to solve seemingly impossible problems. But then again… What could Mom have done in this situation? Callie felt like it was all hopeless.

The villagers stared at them as they passed by, heading to the center of the village where the largest house of all stood. It was the only house in the village that had two floors. The bottom floor was where the village chief handled all his work, while the top floor was where he and his family lived.

The village chief was standing by the entrance, staring them down as they approached. Callie could feel his piercing gaze on her. She held onto Callen's hand as firmly as she could. It felt like it was the only thing protecting her.

"Thank you for coming first thing in the morning, Rowan," the village chief said.

"Of course, Aemon," Dad inclined his head towards the man. Callie dared to steal a short glance at him while he wasn't looking at her.

The village chief was older than Dad and larger, too. He apparently had been a hunter before taking over as the chief. The many scars on his arms showed that clearly. He had thick, black hair, greying at the temples, and cold, dark eyes. This man has always terrified Callie.

The village chief glanced at Callie, his eyes even colder than usual, and she shrank behind Callen.

"You don't have to be here, Callen," Chief Aemon said.

"Yes, I do," Callen said firmly.

Callie was amazed by her brother's bravery. How he was able to endure the chief's cold gaze, Callie had no clue. But she was thankful he remained with all her heart. She wasn't sure what she would have done without him.

Before long, they were all gathered in a large room with a large table in the center. The chief's wife and Aurelia were sitting there, as well as Gerda and her parents. To Callie's relief, Granny Ethel was there too. At least somebody else who didn't hate her.

She didn't hesitate to take the seat right next to the old woman, who gave her a friendly pat on the shoulder. Callen sat down right next to Callie, while Dad sat next to Callen. Chief Aemon sat down at the very end of the table.

"Alright, everyone, let's not dally around and get straight to the heart of the matter. Yesterday evening, my daughter returned home with a nasty burn on her leg with ulcers on it. Gerda likewise had a similar burn on her leg. Both of them claim that it was Calluna who inflicted these burns on them," Chief Aemon said, looking everyone in the eye in turn.

Callie didn't dare to return his gaze, instead staring at the table in front of her.

"I would like to hear how the events went from Calluna's point of view. Calluna, would you be so kind as to tell us what happened?" he continued.

Callie could feel his and everyone else's intense stares on her. She didn't dare to look up from the table. How was she supposed to say anything with everyone staring at her like that? She opened her mouth in an attempt to speak, but her voice didn't come out. It was like she couldn't breathe.

"I don't think it's fair to force Callie to speak on her own like this. She's the youngest one here and feels incredibly pressured. Allow me to retell the events in her stead," Callen said, reaching out his hand under the table and squeezing Callie's hand reassuringly.

"Callen, you were not there. How are you supposed to give an accurate account of what happened?" Chief Aemon said sternly, making Callen flinch back. "Callie, tell us what happened."

Callie had no clue what to say. If she said it was Wiggles, the whole village would no doubt try to hunt it. Wiggles couldn't get hurt. He was the first arcane beast friend Callie had made. It had protected her.

Callie took a deep breath and managed to find her voice for long enough to say, "I-I'll accept any punishment."

"See, she admits it!" Aurelia said victoriously.

"Silence!" The chief said sternly, making his daughter sink deeper into her chair. "Callie, am I to understand that you admit to burning Aurelia's and Gerda's legs?"

"She didn't do it!" Callen said fiercely. "Do you really expect that Callie would be able to overpower two girls stronger than herself and inflict burns upon them? And who just wanders around carrying fire with them?"

"W-We didn't think it was possible either… But it did happen straight after our survival lesson. When we were learning about how to make fire. Callie came to us carrying fire in her hand," Aurelia said, teary-eyed.

Callen glared at her fiercely, making her actually look guilty. But only for about half a second. Soon enough, she had the same fake-sad expression on her face. Callie couldn't believe that the adults were believing this. But then again, they also believed all arcane beasts to be murderous monsters, so it wasn't surprising that there was something wrong with their perception.

"Callie, just tell us what happened. Nobody will interrupt. I just want to hear your version of this story," Chief Aemon asked again.

Callie remained silent. She had already said she would accept her punishment. Why couldn't they just leave it at that?

"Callie, tell everyone what happened!" Dad demanded. She heard the anger rising in his voice.

The pressure she felt at that moment was intense. There was nothing she could say that would protect both her and Wiggles.

"She already admitted to being guilty. The girl is so ashamed that she can't even speak. Why are we even discussing this any further? Chief, you must enact punishment. Gerda might have a scar on her leg for life! This isn't a joking matter—going around and burning people," Gerda's mother demanded.

"That's right, Chief! Your own daughter might be left with an ugly scar, just because this… this troublemaker had decided that a little fire would be a fun prank. Calluna has always caused nothing but trouble. I think it's way overdue she received just punishment," Gerda's father added.

Callie shrank deeper into her chair. It felt like with each word, her heart was beating faster and faster, her mind reeling with fear.

"Callie is not guilty!" Callen jumped up to his feet, slamming his hands down on the table.

"Sit down, Callen, before I send you out of here! I understand your care for your younger sibling, but it doesn't give you the right to speak out of order!" Chief Aemon growled, then cleared his throat.

"Since it seems like Callie refuses to tell the truth, I'm going to clarify that burns my daughter and Gerda sustained are not burns from a fire. Granny Ethel explained that they were inflicted by acid," the Chief explained, giving his daughter a significant look.

Aurelia and Gerda both went as pale as a sheet, having been caught in their lie. Not that it made Callie feel any better.

"I… I… I didn't see exactly what it was. It happened so fast," Aurelia stammered.

"Y-Yes, she approached us from behind," Gerda added nervously.

"Lies! You two were the ones bullying Callie just as you always do," Callen accused.

Both girls became even paler if that were possible. They both admired Callen, so seeing him angry at them, likely didn't feel nice.

Chief Aemon narrowed his eyes on his daughter. "Is this true?"

"I… Aren't we here to talk about the injury Calluna inflicted on us? Fire or acid, what does it matter how she did it? She should be punished!" Aurelia demanded.

"Where would she even get acid strong enough to burn human skin? It was actually a—" Callen began, but Callie firmly pulled on his arm.

No, brother! You can't tell them about Wiggles!

"It was actually what?" Chief Aemon demanded.

Callie's mind reeled. They were about to find out about Wiggles. She had to say something. Anything to protect the little mireling.
