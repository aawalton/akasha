Alex, free falling…

Air rushed over me as I studied the green light sparkling here and there from below. Black armor covered my body and my wings were ready, but this was the quickest way to get away from the hornets-from-hell.

The cloud surged after me for at least several feet before I left them in the dust. Given how large the cloud was, I was thankful.

The lack of mana in the air reminded me of when we had first arrived on the planet. Whatever was happening above, it wasn’t reaching down here, at least not yet. The green light streamed out of a crack far below me.

My momentum started to slow down without me doing anything.

Suddenly, I jerked to a stop, like a car slamming on its breaks. Then I hung there in the air. Rocky cliffs went in both directions, but I hadn’t hit anything.

I reached out and poked my hand in the direction I wanted to go. Then, I latched onto the cliff. Something pulled at my body, like pulling myself out of water, but it eventually let go and gravity resumed like normal. Yet, I faced the other way. The old down wasn’t down anymore.

I pushed the weirdness out of my mind, not understanding how it was possible.

My focus returned to crawling up the wall. Something touched the farest reaches of my radar. High above me, a dark shape crawled down.

[Bone Horror, Claw Strike, Level 199, Prey, Unknown.]

[Warning: Uncivilized Creature Detected.]

It didn’t even glance in my direction as it traveled down. I let it go, and just watched as it tried to cross the line where I’d floated in the air. One of its skeletal feet crossed the line, then it screamed as the leg turned to dust.

The creature tried again, but it lost its hold on the wall.

Then it was gone.

What the fuck?

Another dot appeared on my map, and I stayed where I was, trying to figure out what had happened.

Again, the Bone Horror approached the divide, but this time it let go and pushed itself off the wall, free falling much like I had.

I triggered second sight, searching for answers as it hit. This time, the creature crossed the divide and grappled on the wall, pulling itself up slowly.

Yet, patches of it flaked off into dust as it touched the tiniest bits of mana in the air, and it gained levels with each hit of damage.

I went back to my climb, slowly moving up the wall and letting my mind ponder. Again, this wall was covered with handholds and footholds, but that made more sense if those creatures literally used this as a pathway.

Another dot appeared on my map, and then another.

None of them paid me any attention yet, and their levels were far below mine. But the steady increase worried me. More and more of the Bone Horrors appeared, all around the same level and following almost a direct line down the cliffside.

Finally, the cliffside changed. A green bubble the size of a car rippled up ahead.

[Bone Nest, Level 300.]

A piece of white flashed, pushing through the green substance and becoming a leg, followed by another. The nest pulsed, then the bone horror broke free.

[Bone Horror, Claw Strike, Level 199, Prey, Unknown.]

[Warning: Uncivilized Creature Detected.]

My stomach roiled at the sight and I shivered, spotting several of these on the cliff side. I continued up, then paused.

Level 300.

Far beyond mine, true, but not so much further than some of the things I’d killed lately. Especially since it didn’t seem to do anything but create those creatures.

Lenna's voice warning me to finish my quest echoed in the back of my mind, but still I found myself moving closer to the nest. A bone horror crept down, not paying me any attention.

Taking a deep breath almost caused me to start gagging. The smell coming from the bubble of green made my eyes water. Still, I swiped out with my claws. Dark lines slashed through the air, extending the last several feet and cutting deep into the nest.

The surface rippled, then popped, spraying green light all over the area and leaving nothing behind.

[You have gained a level.]

Seeing no reactions from the rest of the nests, or the Bone Horrors, I plotted a course up the rest of the cliffside that would take me near the rest of the nests I could see. I used my free stat points to even out my stats, then I got to work.

Each notification of a level gained filled me with joy.

Finally, I took out the last green bubble on the cliffside, and the result was different. Green light rippled in the air longer, and something inside me warned me to flee.

The light started to gather in a large blob in the center of the cliff. I climbed away from the twisting green light. It darkened, then something black broke through.

[Bone Terror, Level 250, Predator, Unknown.]

A scream ripped from the massive creature, and the green light remained, creating three glowing eyes from the skull that was the thing’s body.

I raced up the cliffside, aiming for the top. I pulled myself over the edge as it screamed a second time. A leg pushed out from the underside of the skull. Then a second. Both slammed into the cliffside, gaining purchase. I searched the edge of the cliff for a boulder. They covered the area, blocking my view of anything beyond. I finally spotted one I thought I could move, and darted closer to it.

Carefully, I tried to roll it to the edge as another scream echoed up from below. I glanced over the edge once, then pushed the stone. I darted away without waiting to see if my first rock struck, back to the other boulders to grab another.

A cry of anguish came from below, and I pushed the second over. This time I watched as it hit the skull dead on. One of the three eyes had dimmed. The second hit almost knocked it off the cliffside.

I pushed myself to grab a third and roll it off the edge as quickly as I could.

This one took the massive creature with it, pulling the two legs buried into the cliff edge out.

I watched as it fell.

It hit the floating zone with more pull than I’d anticipated, and dissolved completely.

[You have gained a level.] x 5

Energy surged through me and I shivered. I hadn’t had a reaction to gaining levels like this in a long time. I smiled, then froze as a scream came, not from the cliffside, but from beyond the boulders blocking my view.

I scrambled up a rock, letting my wings shrink back into my armor, glad I hadn’t needed to use them. A sharp rock jutted up into the air, and I pressed into its shadow as I peeked beyond.

The crack in the sky on this side glowed with a deep green color, casting weird colored shadows across the rocky landscape. Nothing grew. No trees or scrubs, or even grasses. Just rocks, followed by more rocks, and then freaking glowing cracks. Massive cracks of green goo flowed across the landscape like rivers.

A much smaller mountain floated in the air on this side, with four chains that anchored it below the crack in the sky. The green energy filled the space between the heavy links. The sky remained a dark, hollow color without any change.

Bright green flashed near the edges of the river nearest me, and something pulled itself onto the bank. Dark shapes flew from the nearby rocks, tearing into it. Then the shapes retreated when it died.

Another massive scream echoed over the rocks, but it came from the sky. A dark shape flew through the air. It had four wings, two tails, and a long neck.

[Dark Drake, Fearful Scream, Level 350, Predator, Unknown.]

Green light flickered around its head, but it only did circles around the floating rock. A long chain connected it to the floating island. It wasn’t long enough to let it land on the lower land. It flew another couple of circles, then vanished onto the floating rock.

The star on my map highlighted the damn thing. Not the Drake, but the floating island.

I pressed my lips together, then took two steps within the shadows of the jagged rock.

Something growled deeply within the darkness, and five red dots suddenly appeared on my map. The jagged rock behind me moved. A long, thin leg stabbed out from the top of it, then stabbed out into the shadows.

The dots scattered.

A whimper cut off the growl, and the leg retreated back to the rock with something speared on the end of it.

[Bone Hound, Level 240, Prey, Unknown.]

The creature struggled to free itself, but couldn’t. The leg lifted it to the top of the rock, which suddenly cracked open like a jagged smile. The hound was tossed inside. Then the rock slammed shut.

I stayed completely silent and unmoving during the entire thing.

Very carefully, I took another couple of steps away from the jagged rock. Immediately I noticed several more, all scattered around the area. I quickly plotted a path that didn’t bring me close to any of them.

Inch by inch I made my way across the rocky landscape, doing my best to stay away from the river and the jagged stones. My sheath of shadows wrapped around me like a blanket, and nothing spotted me. The drake did another fly by after some time, but that was all.

It felt scheduled.

The river gave birth to something, then the creatures would eat it. Like clockwork. One of the jagged things would eat something from the darkness, the drake would circle, and it’d start again. It was like a movie stuck on repeat. A short, rather boring movie.

The pattern amazed me, that something like this could be natural. The more I moved closer to my target, the more I felt it couldn’t possibly be natural. Even though the System meant ‘natural’ had a much wider definition than back on old Earth, this was beyond that. It felt constructed by a mortal mind, not the System.

Energy from the green crackle pulsed. I reached a ridge, then monitored the area below. The river rushed down into a massive hole in the land. The green light poured over the edge, almost like a demonic waterfall. Bits of green light twinkled in the air. Light pulsed up from the hole, and I couldn’t help but wonder if this was where the green light on the other side came from. Pouring from the land itself.

More growls cut through the air, but this time it didn’t come on schedule. An arc of green light rose from the waterfall and splashed out on the bank.

One of the hounds darted to the wet spot and lapped at it. The creature grew bigger. Another arc splashed out, and it lapped at that one, too. Again, it increased in size.

The red dots of the other hounds backed away slowly. The jagged rock behind them cracked open and stabbed out. The now-massive hound launched itself at the jagged rock, pushing it over.

Silence ruled for a moment, then howls broke out from the rest of the pack of hounds. Another lapped at another arc of green light that splashed the bank.

I quickly moved on from the scene, but something dashed right in front of me.

It grazed the side of me and spun, snapping. My claws sliced deep into its snout. It didn’t dodge, but whimpered at the pain.

The light from the waterfall increased behind me, and the sound changed.

The hound attacked, but missed by a mile, obviously not knowing where I stood.

I rolled even farther away as the creature snapped at nothing.

The green glow in the area increased. Everything changed all at once. Suddenly, the hole filled with the green river and overflowed its banks. One of the hounds was caught in the sudden wave and screamed in pain. Light flickered over its body as I quickly backed away.

The river didn’t stop, and there was nowhere else for it to go, as it flowed onto the land, heading in my direction.
