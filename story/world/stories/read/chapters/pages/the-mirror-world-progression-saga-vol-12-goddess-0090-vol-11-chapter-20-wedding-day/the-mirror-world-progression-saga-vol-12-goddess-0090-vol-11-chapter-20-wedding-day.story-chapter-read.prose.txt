The wedding of the Goddess Samantha and Lady Azure was a sudden, scrambled-together, but nonetheless momentous affair, in which they allowed no more than three days of preparation. Fortunately, invitations were delivered immediately thanks to the realm’s vast and efficient communication and travel network. Ensuing requests for ‘special transports’ to attend in time were more than could be handled, but Azure had the shrewd idea to help raise money by charging for it, quickly separating the ‘wheat from the chaff’ in terms of actual motivation to attend.

In order to mitigate the ‘snubs’ and leverage the political gains, Itha Ranon and its islands would continue ‘celebrations’ for a week after the actual wedding, giving extensive free accommodations in the tower and on the island, holding banquets in the cathedral, and at least ostensibly, having the newlyweds pop in and out. Calrenazzod had no conception of a ‘honeymoon,’ and Samantha avoided even mentioning it deliberately. Neither of them had time for that on top of the relative splurge of the wedding itself.

In typical Southlander noble fashion, they’d schmooze with everyone after the ceremony, which was traditionally very brief, as things like the ‘words sharing’ were done during the already completed, private betrothal.

All in all, between Redberry’s guidance and Samantha’s desires, the wedding was a synthesis of Southlander conventions and those of Earth. She wouldn’t really touch Redberry’s words — that would more or less remain hers to speak. But she wanted the ‘walk,’ and the big, ridiculous dress, and the music, and so on. To the people of Calrenazzod, the whole thing would be slightly eccentric, but not offensive by any means. She’d checked about that. Pretty much, as long as they removed the ribbon bindings, exchanged rings, and there was music, dance, feasting, and wine, the rest was flexible.

Naturally, Samantha spared no expense on the tailoring, hiring many tailors at exorbitant prices to custom-design everything and utilize magic for any tricky or time-consuming aspects. This included her dress, Azure’s ensemble, bridesmaids, and Azure’s ‘groomsfolk.’

Interestingly, Azure’s ring-bearer was Terrance Bahiore, who’d recently become the ‘caretaker’ of the Isle of Gold. Amidst all the other wars and moves of power, the assassination and deposing of the scumbag, Andemen — whom Azure had once been a concubine to — was kind of a footnote. They hadn’t even been involved… Andemen had thought himself clever in arranging his aging noblewoman wife’s death, unaware of contingencies the woman’s family had in place should she pass under mysterious circumstances.

Andemen had finally gotten his just due more or less by his own hand.

When the news reached Samantha, she simply ordered they sweep in and claim the Isle for ‘stability.’ As she was basically seen as an unstoppable empress in those parts aside from in Zek Sephel, there were no protests. Aside from that, the technical heir was a very dubious and tenuous business, a mess that would’ve resulted in years of bickering and languishing territory. The Isle of Gold’s technical promise was now dubious itself without Andemen, on top of its waning influence after Samantha and Redberry began dominating the surrounding culture.

In any case, the concubines, practically slaves, were freed entirely. Samantha helped them however they desired. A few wanted little more than passage elsewhere, a few took the opportunity to come to Itha Ranon, and quite a few stayed, wanting roles on the island.

Two former concubines were also part of Azure’s groomsfolk. One was a younger girl Azure had only had some cordial interactions with, but the other… had been a fierce, old rival by the name of Karisa. Now they were laughing and carrying on like the best of friends. A little weird, but whatever — it had to be a good thing to find a little reconciliation from her old life. Somehow, their rivalry must’ve had a special kind of respect.

Azure didn’t otherwise show much interest in affairs on the Isle, content to know it would be put to use. Her feelings were just too mixed, between the facility as well as the majority of the girls. The two she’d reconciled with were the exception, not the rule.

It was good she had Karisa and Terrance, though. They were the closest thing she had to family, perhaps.

She could ask me about her actual creation, now. I could look into what Goldie wrought and tell her what is false. But it's her choice if she feels she needs to let it all go.

The ‘day of’ for the wedding came all too quickly, set for an early, relatively sober ceremony, so that the festivities afterward could become anything but. Speeches were for the banquet to come.

‘Backstage,’ before the walk to come, Samantha had no jitters at all. She was just happy to be gifted with a moment she scarcely felt she deserved, but would take all the same. Her dress was a big, breathtaking, beautiful, elaborate, white wedding dress, completely ridiculous, and you'd better stay out of its way, because it had an AoE aggro to it in a wide radius.

That tailor cast quite the spell. It’s outdone the Goddess of Magic herself!

The only thing it didn’t have was a forward veil. That whole little element was a bit corny to her, personally, and inappropriate with the way the Southlands viewed the prior betrothal.

Once the music began, and the ceremony began, everyone packed into the cathedral, and the procession was ready to start. Samantha made sure to cheat by looking through a mirror at the ceremony.

Redberry was first down the ‘isle,’ a path flanked by bushels of every color of wildflowers. The Goddess of Nature was impressive and graceful as always, without trying — Samantha was somewhat grateful for that, because she could steal the show if she did try. She wore a simple but delightful green leaf gown, with her massive length of hair in a complex updo.

Next was Azure, escorted by Raphael. Samantha gleefully switched to multiple angles to see her love’s dress, done all in red and in sharp, aggressive folds and cuts, with golden jewelry highlights. Her dark hair was done up elaborately, somewhat similarly to Red. Her tail swished visibly behind her, from underneath the dress.

Oh my gaaaawd! No one has ever looked hotter in all of time and reality!

Azure was beaming in pride and joy, wowing the crowd, which murmured and gasped as she sauntered to the front, Raphael almost tugged along in her arm.

That bright smile flashed to a mirror briefly and winked, which made Samantha giggle.

More sharp and beautifully dressed friends walked down, always in pairs — Bast and Majorie (her struggling with tears made Samantha struggle, too), Dax and Canmore (gazing at each other enough that it was a wonder they didn’t trip), Estara and Crow (Estara was goo-goo-eyed at him, but Crow kept focused ahead, clueless), Constance and Dart (Constance ignored Dart completely, goo-goo-eyed for literally everyone and everything else present), Tashome and Gabriel (the former looking annoyed at the latter, large figure crying her eyes out the whole time)... on and on it went, with representatives of every stipe, people, and creed imaginable.

Most of these, they aren’t just friends… they’re much, much more.

Their family, as a whole, was rather big, and this ceremony showed it in a way Samantha had scarcely realized before.

I guess we did okay, Dad. I know you’re here with me in spirit. Your stand-in for this is pretty cool. I think you’d really like him.

Finally, it came time for the bride to walk down the aisle. She approached the waiting, tired-looking, finely-dressed Orswyth, who held out an arm to her and smiled. He asked, “Ready, Your Majesty?”

Samantha smiled back and nodded confidently. “I am so ready, Orsy. I’ve never seen you in a fancy hat. I like it.”

Orswyth chuckled as he touched a hand to the brim. “Why, thank you, dear. Little did I know feathers would ever look good on me.”

Naturally, all the men were required to wear fancy, wide-brimmed hats, whether they liked it or not, though she suspected they did. It was already getting more popular throughout her controlled lands, in emulation of key figures like Dax and Canmore. Naturally, Dax had totally converted Canmore, and with little resistance at that.

Our nefarious plot unfurls, mahaha!

The music changed as Samantha began walking down the aisle in her glittering, silver, diamond-studded, white dress. Her eyes were wide open for this, and she kept a continuous [Conjure] effect going to prevent the appearance of blood tears. A cantrip absorbed the blood, mainly to avoid staining the dress.

All heads were turned to see her, and a satisfying gaggle of gasps, exclamations, and wide-eyed whispers took the crowd as she walked, smiling.

When her eyes met Azure’s, who was caught gasping herself, Samantha beamed uncontrollably. No reaction could be more satisfying than that one.

Suddenly so high that she was in a daze, Samantha soon found herself in front of Redberry, smirking as usual as she looked between them. Once everyone was fully in position, the nature goddess lifted her hands and called, “Friends and loved ones, bear witness to a joyous and sacred deepening of an eternal bond intimately tied between the souls, Samantha and Azure, joined inseparably together under the noble trees and principal witnessing eyes some time ago, and remaining together in happiness since. Groom, bride, bring forth the rings.”

The ringbearer, Terrance, knelt and opened the container for Azure, who pulled out the jewel-encrusted gold ring; simultaneously, Samantha’s maid of honor, Ash, knelt and offered another ring, and this was perhaps one of the rare things Naugite-like in the ceremony. Instead of a typical setting, it was a smooth band with diamonds inset all down the middle, in the diamond pattern, which was in the traditional Naugite fashion.

Here was the part that required some manual dexterity to not result in mild embarrassment. Azure moved to position the ring one-handed, with just a teensy bit of trembling. Gleeful and solemn at once, she slipped the ring on with one hand, while Samantha did the same for Azure, all while maintaining contact. They then helped each other remove the ribbon around their wrists, one-by-one, loosening it to slip it off without untying the knot, preserving the first symbolic joining together.

Redberry approached, inspecting the knots quickly, then lifting Samantha and Azure’s joined hand abruptly, declaring, “The knot remains, and their fortune holds bright indeed — let us cheer it is so forever!”

Cheers rang out, many hats were thrown up, and the applause was deafening.

Redberry nonetheless got their attention to gesture at them insistently, snickering and calling, “You can kiss now! Go on, already!”

So they did, and it was sweeter than ever.
