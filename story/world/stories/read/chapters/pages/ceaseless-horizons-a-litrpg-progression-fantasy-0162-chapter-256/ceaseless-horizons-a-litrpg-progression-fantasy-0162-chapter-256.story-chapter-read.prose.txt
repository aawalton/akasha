The first to reach him wasn’t a monster but a flood of feathers. Their steel edges gleamed under the bright daylight of the dungeon, shattering multiple shieldwalls in a bid to reach him.

Lev stayed stationary. The assault had originated from incredibly far away, and as long as the Steelfeather didn’t come closer, he wasn’t going to waste mana in a ranged battle.

In the meantime, he prepared a zone. Dozens of shieldwalls were arrayed around the boss and the humans, all around his sizable manipulation range.

Each had the best second-threshold cores from his collection slotted inside, powering up both the invisibility and the durability enchantments to the fullest.

Hidden cables were spread out between them, connected to the shield walls and forming a barricaded zone. Lev only made them slightly noticeable to the humans, for they already knew what and where the cables were.

For the monsters, they would seem like nothing more than a clump of mana. They had extremely sharp senses, which was why he made the cables leak mana from a select few points. That would hide the actual structure of the cables. In their usual monster fashion, they would target anything related to the humans, and hopefully, the durability of his constructs would give them a nasty surprise.

A small portion of his willpower was relegated to occasionally causing pulses inside the shields to reveal their location. Through the communication item, he explained that every shieldwall would pulse briefly to let them track their locations accurately. Since they were invisible, it wouldn’t do to hinder his own allies due to that.

That was most of his job done. Recalling the bubble, he stepped inside and powered up all the concealment enchantments unrelated to auras. Right next to it, the disk was now primed to be used, brimming with a large amount of pure mana.

This much without activating any of the offensive enchantments, Lev sighed, downing a healing potion in advance. He could feel dozens of auras converging on their location, which left little choice. Using the disk was inevitable, and he started preparing by directing a portion of his regeneration to the healing mana converter.

A barrier orb stored all of that particular output. It was enchanted with common runes specializing in keeping any stored mana from leaking, though a gradual drain would remain. The amount was negligible, so he continued to fill it with even more.

“Now, it’s up to you guys to keep me safe,” Lev sent to everyone and lowered the bubble to the ground. “I’ll provide support from the back.”

“Don’t be stupid, okay?” Isabella sent back, making the message audible to everyone. “I still don’t fully understand how taxing that disk is going to be on you, and that’s saying something. Have some faith in us.”

“It’s not a matter of faith,” he sighed, his voice soft. “I’ll be careful, though.”

No more time was given to them as the ground rumbled. Earthen Destroyers sought to devour them whole, and the Hunter retaliated in kind. It dashed straight down, the very ground opening up in a blink to accommodate its passing.

Realizing what was about to happen, Lev formed a barrier floor all around the lake, stretching it to the edges of his manipulation range. The stag and his allies all jumped on top of it, all in time for the ground below to rumble violently.

Multiple places would’ve erupted in showers of dirt and debris. His floor prevented them all, cracking at a few points. The damage was fixed in a blink just as the first ground monsters reached them.

Trolls came forth, their physical strength empowered to obscene heights by hidden shamans. Lev identified them, his concern rising.

Melvine, Viktor, and Elias moved ahead to meet the stampede. Melvine’s element spread out, shrouding the other two humans as well.

A large amount of mana circulated through Elias’s powered armor. The power didn’t come from him. Instead, the armor’s reserves were just that vast, and even mostly automated, Lev could see the warrior struggling to use the fuel with precision and efficiency.

Viktor initiated the brawl with an axe throw. It was deflected by something unnoticeable. A massive icebolt tore the air towards the unarmed warrior, something Aryan was already waiting for.

He materialized behind the hidden shaman and, with a quick flick, decapitated it.

Lev returned his attention to the disk. It was still stable. All of the runes worked fluidly in tandem, and he wasn’t expecting them to be problematic either.

The real issue was what came next. All of his remaining mana regeneration was converted into wind and lightning, the efficiency for the former leaving a lot to be desired.

Ignoring the waste, he mentally went over all of the enchantments at the edges of the disk, just in case. When nothing worrying stood out, he started by infusing wind mana.

The disk’s bladed edge was composed of three different layers. Two of the outer layers were designed to be supportive, and wind mana infused them in droves to augment the primary layer.

Wedged between the outer layers, the middle layer was responsible for all the sharpness. Lightning was infused inside it for the maximum possible sharpness, augmenting the damage with its properties further.

Deep breaths… Lev repeated in his head like a mantra, acting on the thought. This is going to suck.

With a thought, the disk sprang into motion. It floated and began to rotate, slowly at first, then fast enough that it cut through the air with a shrill whistle.

Lev took a moment to marvel at the sense of danger he could feel from the construct. The environmental resistances to its motion were negligible, courtesy of the enchantments and infusion. It would glide across the dungeon as if moving through a vacuum.

And this is not even a fourth of its maximum rotation speed…

Gulping, he directed a large chunk of the pure mana inside the disk into the Kinetic Acceleration formations. Several of them were combined to perform a single task, and they did so spectacularly.

In a blink, the disk’s spinning speed tripled. No longer was the air simply cut through. Instead, the sheer power in its movements created a small storm around it, slowly giving birth to something akin to a horizontal tornado.

He didn’t let it get to that point. Now that it was about two-thirds of its maximum, non-overloaded power, Lev commanded the disk to move slightly.

It instead shot off, covering nearly two dozen meters before he got it back under control.

Chuckling, he tried his best to ignore the mental feedback and upped the amount of mana being infused. His own pool was tapped into just as Melvine’s battle neared his furthest shieldwalls.

Four trolls followed her, showing a remarkable degree of coordination as they tried to gang up on her. Melvine, however, was way more agile.

Mists obscured her form as she danced between the flurry of limbs. Punches struck the ground, forming craters even in the frozen terrain. Instead of hindering her, though, she just stepped upon the icy chunks, halting them mid-air to serve as platforms to jump off of.

She finally reached one of his shieldwalls. Invisible, it towered over her and the monsters. Melvine guided the battle to its edge, right where a dense cable was connected between it and another shieldwall.

Lev had made sure to keep the cables at a level where humans could easily move above or below them. The one behind Melvine was half as thick as her head, the same height as her shoulders.

Without reacting to its presence, she crouched, dashing backwards to create distance. The first troll that followed jerked in surprise as its torso thudded into the invisible cable, and that was all the opening Lev needed.

Before it could move back or strike the faintly noticeable construct, a sharp, gleaming blade passed through its head. The disk didn’t even register the flesh and bone as an obstacle, continuing forward unimpeded.

Lev, however, was very much struggling because of that same property. The kinetic energy packed in the disk felt like sandpaper in his head, scrubbing his mind as he forced the construct to come to a stop.

Holy shit. He breathed out, looking at the disk with pained eyes. How are you so useful and terrifying at the same time?

The test was a massive success, at least. He made the disk ascend as the other two trolls gazed at it warily, evaluating if it was going to attack again. That was enough for Melvine to release a large burst of mist and end them in a few seconds.

He was too focused on the disk to observe her anymore. The construct moved at a speed that tracking it by sound became pointless. A few unfortunate trolls were bisected before they could ever hope to react, and then the real threats showed up.

A massive Ebonwalker landed a few hundred meters away from the battlefield, preferring to battle on the ground. Lev gulped, his gaze roaming its wooden wings and the strength of its aura.

This one is nearly level 600, for sure.

The monster crouched, preparing to run. Isabella fired a barrage of blue fireballs at it, resorting to the evolved element from the start.

Each landed with a palpable impact, despite the massive distance. Lev was also about to aid her when Aryan became visible, close to Lisa and Kora, who were hanging in the back.

The rogue dipped one of his daggers into the same black vial he had used against the serpent, his hands a blur even to Lev’s senses.

Urgency spiked. Lev followed the instantaneous throw, just in time to note the Steelfeather diving towards his bubble.

His prepared lances were hastily fired to make it abort the lunge. The projectiles were easily shattered by sharp feathers, and by the time Lev’s disk parted the resulting curtain of flames and lightning, the monster was nowhere to be seen.

Adrenaline forced the discomfort from manipulating the disk to the back of his mind. His wide eyes scanned the skies, unable to locate the avian monster that he knew didn’t use any stealth abilities. It was just that fast.

Before he could prepare for its inevitable return, dozens of stone shards flew at this bubble. They were spread wide, and only a few were going to actually land on the construct, but he still blocked them all with normal shields. The defenses broke by the dozens, easily putting an end to that assault.

Troll shamans were present somewhere below him, biding their time. Lev knew for a fact that they were present atop his barrier floor, somewhere inside his maze of shieldwalls.

A large amount of lightning was infused into every shieldwall near the origin of the stone shards. The monsters weren’t given a chance to run away, for the lightning shot out in waves, each empowering the others to cover a large area.

Three writhing forms were revealed. Lances gored them before they could recover.
