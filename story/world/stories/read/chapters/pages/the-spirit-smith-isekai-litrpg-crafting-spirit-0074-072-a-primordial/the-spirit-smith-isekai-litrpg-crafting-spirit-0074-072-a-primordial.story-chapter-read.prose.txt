I take a step back as the dragon speaks to me. Is dragon even an accurate descriptor of it? This being is level two thousand five hundred, a primordial as the system calls it.

“Yip!” Luma says and jumps on her feet a bit. “Yip, yip. Yip?” I have no clue what she just said, but it would seem the primordial does.

Yes, you, little fox. It was through a combined effort that you made it here. As can be expected, given that was a requirement of the trials.

“Trials?” I say.

Oh my, you were not aware? This has gotten even more interesting, then. To pass the trials without any knowledge or preparation… that is quite literally unheard of.

“Wait, what are you even talking about? Hell, what are you? What is this place? What do you want from me?”

The ground shakes as the primordial laughs. My my, I forgot how the young are full of questions. As for what I am, well I am a primordial. I assume the system has told you at least that much. Furthermore, you have entered my domain, and passed my trials, thus earning yourself my attention. As for what I want? Nothing. It is you that is here seeking something, after all.

Biting back my terror, I step up beside Luma and stand strong. “Let me clarify then, what is a primordial?”

He laughs again. I see, ah right, heroes are not native to this world. Your core seems young as well, by the goddess, it is like you were summoned yesterday!

“It has been six months, I ain’t that new.” I retort.

But you see, six months is practically yesterday. At least to one such as I, who has dwelled in this mountain for dozens of millennia. Ah, right, your prior question. What is a primordial you ask? Well that is quite a difficult thing to answer. What a primordial is depends on the primordial. Though I suppose the unifying idea is that we have all reached the peak of this world-system, the level cap so to say. Some may refer to us as five star beings, but the system seems to recognize us as above the star classifications. Naturally, this comes with some perks, such as true immortality, amongst others that are more specific to the individual. Oh, and of course, there is always more power to be gained, despite the leveling process ending.

So, he is just a really old dragon that got really strong. Okay, that makes sense I suppose. Still fucking terrifying, but way more manageable. Also, this guy seems to like talking a lot. Though this must be the first time he has had a visitor in a while, so that is only natural.

Mustering my newfound courage, I figure I should continue with my questions. Meanwhile Luma seems content to just sit and watch, while soaking up some of the lovely sunlight. “So how many of you are there? Primordials that is.”

Hmm. I can feel around a thousand beings of my own power across the realms. Though most are naturally living in higher realms, I am somewhat of an oddity for deciding to live in this realm. But I have my reasons. Of course, there could be countless more beings above my power or otherwise hiding themselves from my gaze.

“Fair enough, so uh, you said I came here to seek power? Which I guess I did, but I was only following this compass.” I pull out the compass and show it to him.

Sorry to say, but that thing is worthless. You got it from a quest, I presume? Seems the system made it just to lead you here, to me.

I sigh, “Figures. So do I uh, like get some sort of reward for making it here? Or like, how does this work? Wait, what can you even do?”

Ha, more questions. A reward, hmm, lets see. First, it would probably be easier to explain that I am a Spirit Mage, as you might’ve guessed. Though the class has long since evolved into something quite different. To start off, would you like to see my companions? I swear the giant dragon face manages to smile as he asks that last bit.

I don’t have anything better to do, and I bet he has met lots of cool spirits, right? “Sure.”

The tip of Valdir’s tail flicks ever so slightly. Then out of thin air, spirits appear. Hundreds of them. They fill the canyon in front of me, and the sheer variety is insane. Floating in the air are thousands of much smaller spirits, simple ones perhaps? But of course there are more powerful spirits, like a whole pack of wolf spirits. There are also more magical creatures. One of which is a smaller dragon, I think. Heck, there is even what appears to be a sandworm, though it quickly hides beneath the ground. To say Valdir’s companions are vast and varied is the understatement of the millennia.

What’s more is the sheer power I can feel radiating off the spirits. Each one is high level, though I sense some variance. Not all have hit four stars, nor are any of them at five stars, of that I am certain. Notably, I don’t think any of them are great spirits. I have no way of knowing for sure, but it is like none of them feel quite the same as Luma does. Despite that, I am certain that any one of those spirits could look at me the wrong way and end my existence.

“Yip!” Luma says just before she leaps off the cliff. I worry about her getting hurt for a split second, as she lands on the ground lightly, as if she didn’t just fall almost a kilometer. She races off into the horde of spirits and I quickly lose track of her. But she seems fine, and is probably just excited to meet more of her kind.

“So what is a spirit anyways?” I ask. “How do they form? I notice you have quite a few, but I have barely found any of them.”

That is a simple question, but I guess everyone must start somewhere. Spirits are remnants that are left behind when a creature dies. Most are extremely weak, like these simple ones you see all around me. However, some creatures have strong enough wills and enough power to leave a stronger remnant. A spirit, that is. Of course, the technicalities are a lot more complicated. But I heard news about a banquet? I presume you do not want me to ramble on for years about such things.

Now, as for how to find them? You don’t. There are ways, of course, but those are truly cruel. Very simply, you will encounter a spirit if it is meant to be so. Though I am surprised you found two, let alone a great one, in this realm. The Realm of Humanity is quite lacking as far as spirits go. It doesn't get much better until you hit the realm of dragons, my kind tend to like nurturing them and as such they are far more common. There are even cities where spirits and cored beings coexist.

Don’t pay too much worry to it as of yet. Spirit Mages do start off quite weak, it isn't until a few stars in that the true strength of the path is revealed. I sense you have a crafting class as well? Perhaps focus on that, perhaps you could even mix the two some day.

I sigh. I figured it would be something like that. Though I suppose it doesn’t matter that much. After all, I barely have any contract capacities left. I am several hundred stat points in spirit away from the next, too. My guess is that at higher stars I will get a lot more stat points, though, and thus way more capacity.

If Valdir’s display is anything to go by, it must be possible to get thousands of capacities eventually. But I am a long way off from that. The possibility of mixing my smithing and spirit stuff does sound cool, though. “Hmm, that makes sense. I would love to sit around and listen to what you have to say. I do have places to be, eventually. A few days here wouldn’t hurt, though.”

What I can only describe as a surge of joy runs through the dragon. His eyes twinkle a bit too, as if this is the best thing he has heard for centuries. I shall have to make the most of our time then. It would seem that you are quite lacking in the fundamentals of not just spirit magic, but this world. Earlier, you asked about a reward. Well, what better reward could I offer than knowledge? Oh, and I think I have some tea around here somewhere, if you would so indulge me.

Woah, who would’ve guessed the being of supreme power would want to sit down for a cup of tea. I laugh to myself at my own joke. Tea is fine, but I have never been that much of a fan. Perhaps it is a taste one automatically acquires after a few centuries of age. I have no reason to refuse Valdir’s offer. He seems fairly nice, if a bit intimidating. But wait, how can a dragon that large drink tea? He would need oceans of it to be satisfied.

“Sounds great. But uh, how is there even enough tea in this realm for you? You uh, aren't exactly small.”

Valdir’s laugh is like a thunderclap, and the sheer force of it nearly knocks me off my feet. Hero, you are truly hilarious. Much better than the last one that came to see me. He says, and then I feel a shift in the aura around me. My jaw hangs slack as I watch the gigantic dragon shrink, and he just keeps shrinking. Eventually I lose sight of him, until moments later I feel a bit of teleportation wrap around me.

I let him teleport me, and I appear in the middle of a lush cave. It is small, by the standards of this world.

“Shifting forms is quite easy once one gets to be of such power as I.” A voice behind me says. I turn to see a small man, shorter than me even. His hair is totally gray and his face wrinkled beyond any measure of reason. “I was originally human, but humans aren’t that intimidating in the grand scheme of things. Damn does it feel nice to be back in this body though.” Valdir says.

Once again, I sigh. Somehow, I am not even that shocked. Guess this whole magic bullshit is just becoming normal. Which I am not sure is a good thing.

“Please, sit, dearest guest.” The man says with a smile and gestures to a normal wooden table. On it is a teapot and two teacups, oh and it looks like there is some sort of pastry too!

That alone makes my choice of specialization into finding ridiculously powerful beings worth it. I do wonder what Ari would think if she was with me. She would either have run away, or would be nerding out right about now, I bet.

Luma hops up onto my lap just as Valdir launches into some story of one of his conquests. I pay as close of attention as I can. Who knows what sort of tricks I might learn.
