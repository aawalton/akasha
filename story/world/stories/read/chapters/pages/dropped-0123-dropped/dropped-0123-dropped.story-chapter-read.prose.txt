Unfortunately, I’ve come to the conclusion that I can’t really continue this story. For context, I launched the book last November, and initially had a lot of passion for it, but almost immediately after the initial run and coming off of Rising Stars, I got hit with a double whammy of my apartment getting flooded, followed by COVID immediately after. The long hiatus this caused, between repairs and recovering from long COVID, it basically killed my desire to work on this book, and even after multiple attempts to get back into it over the past months, I still face persistent writer’s block, and have been unable to find that passion again.

I’ll leave the character sheet here for reference, but I won’t be working on this any more, unless I somehow find the motivation again in the future. Thank you for your understanding.

Will Swindell
