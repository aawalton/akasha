Void Star

Name: Gateway

Level 1

Contracted Owner: Risha, Mother Of Monsters

Connected Realms: Everglade Forest (unclaimed)

Restrictions: Spirit-Locked

Modifications: None

“What’s this?” Risha stared at the new screen, looking at it from every direction as she tried to understand its meaning.

The Ancient Shaman looked up at the small star Risha had contracted, partially hidden by the brightness of the second star it followed. “The System likes to make certain things easier. Are you ready to see your new realm?”

Risha nodded enthusiastically, but a hand on her shoulder stopped her from stepping into the star.

“Give me a tiny piece of your spirit. It will be my key to follow you.”

Risha gently touched his hand, and sent a piece of her spirit into him, then stepped into the star.

The world changed into a forest of spirits. Tall trees rose into the sky, supporting a canopy that was loud with life. The undergrowth wasn’t too thick, and felt comfortable to Risha, reminding her of the undergrowth of her village.

A second later, the Ancient Shaman appeared beside her. He looked around with a smile. “I knew this would be perfect for you. I don’t sense any void-spirits, do you Nyx?”

The cat stepped across the soft ground, and lifted her nose to sniff the air, “No. There are a few predators, but nothing to worry Risha.”

The Ancient Shaman clapped his hands, and turned to Risha. “Let’s claim this realm then. I’ll help you.”

“Claim the realm?”

He gently took her hand and placed it against the ground. The twigs and dirt were slightly damp, but that just gave Risha a small amount of happiness at the life all around her. “Suffuse it with your spirit, and bind it fully to your star. The star will do the rest, closing off other paths, and allowing this realm to grow with the star’s level.”

His spirit gently wrapped hers, then pushed it into the ground. Risha’s eyes widened as she sensed his spirit. A vast ocean, stronger even than the two Sparks she’d met. It was overwhelming, but kind. A guardian that watched and protected.

She looked up as his spirit became visible to her, a giant of nature, predator and prey wrapped by the vines of life. The spirit itself was humanoid, and wore ancient paints across its face, but as she found its eyes, all else became lost. The power of the sun was held in those eyes, distant, terrifying, and ever present.

Then their combined spirit spread through the land, and she sensed the realm, the tree spirits, the animal spirits, the rock spirits, the wind spirits, all of whom created the life of the spirit realm.

Would you like Void Star Gateway to claim the realm: Everglade Forest?

Yes? / No?

“Say yes, little Risha.”

Her brain came back to her with those words, and she focused on the screen asking for her attention. “Yes.”

He smiled.

She felt as the connection was made between her, the land, and her star.

His spirit left her, and he stood up, looking around with his cheshire grin. “It’s been a while since I’ve done that. Brings back good memories.”

Risha sat on the ground, still reeling from the power of the spirit she witnessed.

“You alright there?”

Risha blinked in surprise as the older goblin’s face blocked her vision.

“Do you want to wait before going to the next step?”

She shook her head, then pushed herself to her feet. “What’s next?”

“Now, we create a spirit enhancing formation.” The Shaman walked towards a clearing, a spring in his step.

Risha hurried to catch up with the shaman, “What’s that?”

“Lots of runes.” He stopped, in the middle of the clearing then pulled out his staff. With a tap of the staff, the undergrowth was pushed away, creating a circle of bare ground. He looked around, then started drawing runes with his spirit, “Watch closely. There’s a few we haven’t covered.”

Risha stepped up beside him, “What do they do?”

“They help your realm and your Star grow. Nyx and Igni might enjoy it as well.” He sends a glance towards the cat who was next to a flower on the ground, “Don’t steal it all.”

Nyx hissed, then went back to sniffing the yellow flower.

Risha followed him for the next hour, learning the runes as he etched them into the ground. Once that was done, he told her how she could teach her new students the beginnings of spirit.

Risha sat at a fire across from the three Scaled, all of whom watched her with curious gazes. Risha didn’t look up as she carved runes into a bracelet with her knife. Around the small group, Risha’s warriors hurried to put the camp together for the night

Risha cleared her throat and she looked up from her project to her Scaled apprentices. “Risha greets her new apprentices. What are your names?”

The three of them shared a look, then the oldest, the white-scaled and scarred woman bowed her head. “I am Zahra of the White.”

The boy with orange-gold scales was next. “I am Kian, of the Orange.”

Risha looked to the last one, the girl with red scales, she bowed her head, then softly said, “Ava, of the Red.”

Risha smiled as she looked the three of them over. “I am Risha. Did Elderoa tell you about our agreement?”

The two younger looked to the oldest of the three, Zahra The White. She bowed her head, “Your secrets are not to be shared with anyone, even Elderoa, without your permission.”

Risha smiled wide, “Good, will you bind yourselves to that?” Her eyes focused on the two younger ones first. Both of them nodded, then her eyes turned to Zahra. Zhara lifted her chin, “The promise of my chief, is one I will follow with my life.”

Risha placed the bracelet she’d been carving into over her wrist, then held out her hands, one towards Zahra The White, the other towards Kian The Orange.

Both Scaled took her hands, then Risha motioned for them to complete the circle with Ava The Red.

The circle completed, Risha sent her spirit through her bracelet.

Reality warped, and they appeared in the void of stars that was the Spirit Realm.

All three Scaled froze as they took in the immenseness of the void.

“Don’t wander off.” Risha said to the shocked scaled, then called softly to Nyx.

The black cat jumped from her, landing on a roadway of shadow. “I still don’t approve, Risha, bringing them here so soon.”

“It will be fine. You are here to guide the path.”

Nyx didn’t respond as she led the way through the void to the familiar star. As they travelled, Risha told them of the three rules the Ancient Shaman had given her.

“Rule one. Don’t travel the void on your own.”

A large purple beast slithered between the stars some distance away.

“Rule two. Don’t call to the void.”

The small star Gateway became visible, no longer hidden in the radiance of the star next to it.

“Rule three. Trust your instincts.”

Risha turned to the three scaled. Ava shifted nervously, trying to peer into the distance. Kian had puffed his chest out, but she could see the slight trembling of his frame. Zahra was the only one who looked unaffected, although her eyes still watched the distance warily.  “I’ll need your hands again.” Her words brought their eyes back to her, and one by one, the recreated the circle.

Risha smiled, then stepped through Gateway, taking them with her.

The three stumbled as their feet stood on solid ground.

“This is my realm. A realm made of spirit. Here, you will learn how to sense Spirit.” Risha sat down crosslegged and showed all of her teeth to the three. “Let’s see how long it takes you.”

***

Risha returned to her body an hour before they had to continue their journey, then called to Klu to help the three Scaled into the cart. Nyx watched over them in the spirit realm as they continued their training, stretching her connection to the Spirit strangely thin.

The carts continued through the darkness, lit only by their torches and the occasional crystal in the wall.

Wild animals attempted to stop their progress, and if they could not be reasoned with, her warriors made quick work of them. Nothing truly powerful tried to stop them, but the darkness kept Risha on alert.

Their first sight of Undercity Freerala were bright torches in the distance, and a large door, big enough for a Spark to walk through.

They stopped there, and a Dark Elf in heavy armor spoke with Klu for a long time before the gates were opened.

Beyond the gates, the city of Freerala was bright with light. The city sat on the edges of a huge ravine, with lanterns, crystals, and magic lighting the world. Merchants called out to them as soon as they saw them, selling items of all kinds. The smell of food instantly hit Risha, making her turn to one of the many stalls that contained meat over a fire. Dark Elves, Dwarves, Shades, Demons, and even the occasional human walked through the city, bringing nearly as much variety to it as Risha’s own home.

They made their way deeper into the city, the sounds of the merchants falling behind them as one of the Demons led them to the inn they would be staying at.

The three Scaled started to stir as they pulled up to the Inn, and Nyx returned to Risha.

Risha jumped off of the wagon before they were fully up, then looked over the city. Far in the distance, a castle of impenetrable dark stone stood high over everything else. Risha wondered at the dwarven king who called that place home. Maybe she would meet him.

But first, she wanted to go to the Delvers guild. Risha quickly found Siyah, the Demoness.

The woman stared over the city, lost in thought, her body frozen.

Risha walked over to her, and hooked her arm through the demon’s.

The Demoness nearly jumped at the contact, then looked at Risha.

Risha smiled, and pulled her towards the crowded streets. Some cloaked warrior fell into step behind them, but they kept their distance as they should.

“Are you ready, Siyah?”

“No. I don’t think I am.”

Risha laughed, “Neither was Risha. But the system doesn’t care if you’re ready.”

“Where are we going?”

“The Delvers guild! I think we’ll find what we are looking for there. Risha has a good feeling. Unless you’ve had guidance elsewhere?”

A soft sigh escaped the Demoness. “No. She’s been quiet.”

Risha nodded, “Then trust Risha.” she pounded her chest for emphasis. “Risha is a smart goblin.”

Risha stopped at a crossroads and looked both ways. Now, all Risha had to do was find this Delver’s Guild. Surely it wouldn’t be too hard.
