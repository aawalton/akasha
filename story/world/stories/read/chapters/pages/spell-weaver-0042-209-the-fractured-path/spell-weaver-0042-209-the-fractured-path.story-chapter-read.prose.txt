Everything in the Rift was coated in a gritty, dripping substance. In most areas, it was bright pink, reminiscent of Pepto-Bismol, while in others it was shaded to an artificial-looking purple.

One felt like power, another like freedom, and the other like death. He’d been cautious of the third path in all of his other experiments. Though as he sat now, thinking through what he’d learned, he realized that the death he felt wasn’t his own.

That’s the Empower Rune. It upgrades the Rank of the Rift.

He shifted his mind’s eye to the second path of flowing power, pulsing in time with a natural rhythm. It felt like open skies and soaring through the air. Some of the most unfiltered feelings of freedom he’d felt in his life.

That must be the Fracture Rune. It is meant to break open the Rift.

Knowing what he knew now, it became more clear that was the intent of the elves. They’d used a slightly different combination of runes to achieve their goals, and if it hadn’t been for his timely intervention, he was sure that the Rift would have opened up to allow the Sun Elves free access to Earth.

The third path was a bit of an anomaly to him, though.

It felt dangerous.

It can’t be the other runes in the ritual. Portal, Storage, and Channeling don’t make sense for this feeling.

He carefully sent his mind down the path, intrigued by the discovery. While he did, he knew that the sense of death and danger wasn’t applied to him. If it followed the same logic as the other paths, then whatever the effect was should apply to the Rift, not to him.

The path turned and twisted, its angles sharp and sometimes narrow. It felt different to travel when compared to the wide, flowing river of power he’d experienced days before when ranking up the first Rift.

Fracture.

It was another aspect of the same rune. A different kind of intent. The more that the realization settled on him, the more certain he was that the rune could have two very different effects based on the intent he pushed into the rune. Similar to how intent mattered when setting a rune during enchantment.

It was easier for him to get a sense of direction.

If the path did what he assumed, then it could change everything.

The day before, when he’d realized that the second path led to breaking open the Rift, he’d very intentionally ended the ritual early, not wanting to get anywhere near that completion.

The excess mana that was produced from interrupting the ritual was redirected into prototype mana batteries as to not be wasted.

At present, though, he wasn’t thinking about ending it prematurely. Mana ran through his veins, and as he came closer to the completion of the ritual, he grit his teeth. He touched the small button of power and grunted from the effort of the last few mental steps.

There was a long moment where everything around him seemed to hold its breath.

His clones, the Rift, even the magical energy itself seemed to freeze.

When the pieces of the still portal hit the ground, they dissipated, no longer visible to his normal vision.

His eyes grew wider by the moment as the Rift ceased to exist.

“What?” The pace of his breathing quickened further. “Oh. Fuck.”
