Chapter 530

Lawrence wanted to complain about their first loss in the matchups, but as his mana pool was rapidly refilled, courtesy of Titan’s Concept, he had to admit the Clans team, On The Last Line, had earned the win. Most of the time they fought people or teams they were able to predict their next moves, as no matter how ‘chaotic and random’ combatants thought they were, when it came down to moment to moment actions and reactions, most fights had little variance.

On The Last Line were what other teams claimed to be. They had so much variance in their actions that neither he nor Esther had been able to predict what their opponents next move would be. It was equally as likely they would continue on with their current plan or change mid way the moment one of them got a ‘good idea’ with no apparent thought put into the idea’s actual viability. Even worse, Brian seemed to have some ability to interact with their discarded, ‘dead’ timelines in a way that shouldn’t have been possible. While he didn’t seem to have any amount of control, he clearly had some degree of awareness, and kept sending Moe to hit them in completely new ways, effectively removing their normal refuge as an option.

In that quagmire of possibility, he and Esther had found some minor opportunities and landed enough wounds that Krodag had needed to shout himself hoarse, but it was also where they’d been beaten. They’d eventually been caught completely off guard one time too many and hadn’t been able to create the time or space to recover.

He was hopeful that they’d have a better showing against the Empire team. Theoretically they had the edge but he knew how close battles at their level could be. If one team didn’t have a direct counter, it often came down to mid-fight execution and that was normally where they shined.

He did notice that Esther used the waiting time to review Maya's fight with the Empire team, but waited for her update rather than join her. That would have necessitated trying to have a conversation split up by possible subjective hours and he hated that, but he really did want to chat with Ellen about the weaknesses she’d revealed in his swordsmanship skills and they didn’t have all that long between fights.

Maya had explained that since they’d all gathered for the fights, especially in Minkalla, that the war would only end after their bouts, just in case one of them truly fell. While none of their fights mattered for war score as their Great Powers weren’t battling each other, the fights gave their opponents without Ascenders a bit of extra breathing room, and as such the Tier 50s would be waiting for them.

Not that any of them wanted to delay.

Maya couldn’t sit still and was teasing On The Last Line about how she’d crush them one final time, while the dwarven team in question kept loudly fantasizing about the accolades they’d get for a clean sweep of the other Gladiators. The Empire team was quieter, they were intently focused, which he interpreted as silent communication, but no less eager for the fight.

Their countdown was the same as it was the first round, with Legion starting them off. After they hit one, none of them moved even as they sensed the explosions of Maya and On The Last Line’s fight. The five of them waited in perfect stillness for the other to make the first move; which Lawrence hated, as it indicated they’d understood how much of their fighting style relied around taking what their opponents sent at them and using it against them. However, Esther forced everyone's hand by starting to raise her own.

In that instant all five of them sprung into motion like hunting dogs that had found the scent.

Titan opened the fight by casting one of his signature skills, [Mana Beam], aiming at Lawrence while also producing a [Hail] for Wraith, who vanished into the ice under a veil of illusions. That wouldn’t help her as much as they were used to, but it wouldn’t be effortless to pierce that veil either. Already the futures he could see were starting to populate with illusions and falsities. While he could identify the true from the false with enough effort, the illusions only grew denser and more real the longer the fight went on and they limited his and Esther’s ability to accurately see into the future past the next move.

While Wraith wasn’t quite on the level of Maya with the reality of her illusions in terms of depth, she seemed far more skilled at wide scale effects and creating a lot of noise in the battlefield. With two strong combatants to back her up it proved a far more effective strategy than they’d hoped and was another reason he needed to isolate her immediately.

A task made doubly difficult, given he had to search while dodging the bar of death Titan tried to chase him down with. His Quickling Dragonfly bloodline helped an inordinate amount as Lawrence was able to tap into his hunter’s instincts to dodge the chest thick attack.

Worse yet was the knowledge that small of a beam size meant Titan was probing him and he could quite literally do that all day. Lawrence’s mana pool, on the other hand, was already half used by his reserve buff spells and while he could recover his mana through reversion, doing so against Gladiator level opponents could be risky, somehow leaving rapid converters as his most reliable option. Normally they’d have the wiggle room to use his Tier 3 to undo some of their more expensive spells and workings, but they couldn’t spare even a single reversion here.

After flitting around to get Titan expectant, Lawrence halted, letting the bar hit him. Or visually that was what Titan would see. In actuality, Lawrence dove into the future while leaving a [Lingering Presence] in his place as a perception trap.

In that brief moment where he was unnoticed, he slipped into a [Time Ripple] as he waited for an opportunity to rush Wraith.

She was the team's weakest piece without her Aspect, while also being Lawrence and Esther’s biggest counter with her Spirit Space. Ultimately, he and his sister were melee combatants and even with their ability to fiddle with time, they needed to get close to deal with someone on their own level. Once her Spirit Space expanded, they’d find it impossible to approach without being detected and frozen solid.

Which was why Lawrence was mildly annoyed at Maya for highlighting the same flaw so recently and putting them doubly on guard, as seen by the change in their formation. If they could take her out of the fight, even temporarily, their odds of victory went up massively. They’d recognized that early in their strategizing, so most of their planning efforts had gone into how to achieve that outcome.

Lawrence had his job and knew how to do it, but doing so wouldn’t be easy.

The support in question had already blanketed the battlefield in chill winds, mirage-like illusions, and debuffs aimed at both of them. If that wasn’t enough, Titan cast [Diffusion Shield], widening the scope of [Mana Beam] from a precision instrument to a cone of destruction that spread out so far, it became almost inevitable that it would strike him.

Triply so, as he seamlessly took over Esther's duty of folding and twisting space, an alternative to stabilizing it that actually managed to prevent Wraith from opening her Spirit Space. They’d simulated and theorized the fight enough times to know that if they let her get even the smallest foothold, she’d leverage that into a victory. They knew it and their opponents knew it too.

The frozen wastelands were something like a singularity of ice - once they were within Wraith’s spirit, their eventual future was being frozen. It made any temporal manipulation a rather delicate affair and one they knew they couldn’t manage for long.

It was why they’d defined taking Wraith out before she opened her Spirit Space as their primary opportunity to win the fight. Legion's pressure on Esther had increased to the point she needed to defend herself with her Aspect, but that was why he was there to pick up any slack before it gave their opponents a chance. Given the two versus three nature of the fight, Wraith would eventually find an opening to slip through.

Still, he didn’t rush to make his move. That would be as bad as not attacking at all. Instead, he waited in the ever-shifting moment of time where no one could easily notice him hidden within his [Time Ripple]. A task that got exceptionally harder when Titan started manipulating the gravity of the battlefield with large sudden swings. They forced Lawrence to stay in his bloodline form to keep up with the rapid temporal blindspot’s movements, which made his strike at Wraith harder but it was that or risk being hit by a stray spell larger than some islands he’d vacationed on.

Worse, he saw Titan’s possible actions were even more detrimental, as he’d either start summoning and activating hundreds of attack talismans, or sweeping the field with [Telekinesis]. That second action combined with all the other obstructions already present on the battlefield would undoubtedly force him out from his hiding place, placing another timer on him.

Lawrence still refused to rush the attack and waste their best opportunity but it became harder and harder to stay hidden as each moment passed. He flitted about the battlefield with most of his attention focused into the possible futures where he frantically searched for one timeline that looked most favorable.

He found it just before he was crushed by two passing waves of [Telekinesis] and pulled both himself and Esther through to that potential future. There they worked together to make their current timeline reality rather than possibility. When they shifted the future it changed the present, which displaced them from their previous locations and battles, though not their opponents who hadn’t been affected at all by the shift. That would have required an inordinate amount of effort for little gain against any Tier 35, let alone ones as strong as the Empire Gladiator team.

While he was still hidden in the [Time Ripple], Esther’s sudden relocation forced her to take a tarnished black and gold curse on her leg. She was able to revert the blood congealing curse, and he provided what assistance he could as she drew on his Tier 3 Talent to keep herself from solidifying from the inside out.

He was worried that Legion was able to overpower his sister’s spiritual defenses so easily. It was another factor that diminished their victorious futures.

Thankfully, she was still able to launch a throwing dart at Titan’s eye from her new position. That forced him to dodge and gave Lawrence the opening he needed to go after Wraith.

Five of him stepped out of the [Time Ripple] as he muddled the present with the possible futures, creating illusions so real he could swap between them if their position was better than his own. Still, he was glad to be able to drop the draining spell, as nearly a tenth of his mana pool had gone to fuel it for so long.

He darted out among himself as all five of him went after Wraith where she was hidden in the ice and snow. Sadly, as secure as her hiding spot was, it wasn’t so perfect that nothing could unveil her. In this case it was a possible future where he’d raced around her field of ice after letting his spatial suppression flicker.

She revealed her Spirit Space in that future, which gave his and her location away when she captured him. Instead of manifesting that timeline into reality and trapping himself, Lawrence used what he saw, which in turn changed the possible futures, and he quickly identified her location before she’d ever known she was caught.

His blade landed right as her latest invisible dash ended. He knew the blow wouldn’t kill her but he hoped to force her into her [Deathly Cold] state, ideally, or any one where she’d be fully ice. Or at least they’d theorized it would make their trap for her all the more effective.

Titan must have felt Lawrence's attack nearly land through their bond, as he felt the Empire Gladiator as he tried to activate the ring he shared with the phoenix Legion hidden on Wraith’s shoulder behind its own illusion. Lawrence had been ready for that, however the swap effect was strong enough he was afraid that blocking it would let Wraith open her Spirit Space and he chose not to fight a losing battle.

Instead, he swapped to one of their contingencies in case Titan got within blade range.

Lawrence was an excellent swordsman, but like most people he didn’t have a counter to someone swinging around a small moon that happened to be sharp enough to still cut anymore than he did if it was dull. Being hit meant death, whether it be through blunt force trauma or very efficiently applied blunt force trauma. It was something two of his possible futures selves could attest to.

The remaining three possible future Lawrences did manage to land their attacks. Compressed by [Temporal Edge], his spatha’s entire force landed in a single instant rather than spread out, which helped them cut through the robust physical armor. He met far more resistance when they hit the magical armor underneath, let alone the subdermal armor, where his weapon halted in the upper layers of flesh and bone instead of slicing through his target as it normally did.

His spathas being his Intent’s Anchor were enchanted to be as sharp as possible without regard to their durability, which meant they had a chance of breaking in any fight but also meant there were few things that could actually stop his blade once it bit flesh. That was doubly true when the weapon was boosted with the mana hungry [Temporal Edge]. At two-hundred thousand mana per strike it was an investment that normally ended most battles, as the spell targeted the possible future damage whatever it struck might develop.

He mitigated the first problem by never letting his Intent’s Anchor experience such a timeline and he solved the second by draining the rest of the rift mana stones he’d preloaded into his rapid mana converter. While normally he could out-spend most mages, that was a fight he knew they’d already lost the moment spells started flying. Between Titan both having and giving practically unlimited mana to the rest of his team, they knew that they couldn’t win a battle of attrition.

Thankfully, all of his possible futures selves opened up just enough space for him to land his strike on Wraith. Though he had to do so while dodging an intricate barrage of spells from Titan and talismans from Wraith. They were random in where they shot but few were actively controlled, which meant it was possible for Lawrence to pilot a course through while striking at the support.

He failed twice, as the first attempt had to be discarded along with a usage of his Tier 3 to revert his body state as a thin line of [Telekinesis] exploded his chest. It was cast so perfectly there wasn’t even a ripple to sense and ruined the attempt. The second was a cleverly disguised ice spell from Wraith hidden inside a double layered illusion that caught him off guard.

Avoiding both allowed him to appear between Wraith and Titan, doing two things at once with each hand. With his off-hand, he pushed a Time modified [Immobilize] at Titan. It was a simple and common full-body restraint used by law enforcement in the Republic but he combined it with a clever mixture of his and Esther’s Talents to lock Titan in a single moment in time rather than a physical restraint.

They knew it wouldn’t last for long in their planning but his ability to see possible future timelines with Esther's Talent told him it wouldn’t last more than a fraction of a heartbeat. Still, it was enough time for his one strike, and that was all he needed.

With the melee threat disabled, Lawrence was able to thrust his spatha into Wraith's chest and twist.

The weapon bit flesh. He felt it and saw it through the timelines but the wound seemed to vanish along with the body as they dissolved into light. Without his ability to see the future, he’d have fallen for the inversion of real and false and he wouldn’t have activated [A Moment of Reminiscence] at the optimal moment to begin the trap.

However, he did and managed to catch the same instant as Wraith was forced to shift her body into an ice and snow form with [Winter Spirit] to avoid him cutting her in half and fatally wounding her. It wasn’t quite as good as if he’d managed to force her to use [Deathly Cold] in conjunction but he accepted it for what it was.

A carefully chosen instant later, Lawrence reached out and twisted space, deflecting attention away through the draperies of his Domain to capitalize on Wraith’s use of illusions and falsity to build a layer of his own on top of hers. He leveraged the connection between the workings into a boundary around Wraith.

Then, with a line drawn, no matter how indecisive and indistinct it was, he was able to isolate the temporal area around her. With her separated from the rest of the battlefield, he shoved that entire relative area off kilter from the normal passage of time as hard as he could.

To his perspective, she froze. Her body, her spirit, the half finished decoy she was creating out of ice and light, even the tracking spell she tried to hit him with in return slowed to a crawl then halted as that entire moment was captured with [A Moment of Reminiscence].

The [Cracked Greater Display Case] Maya had used to lock Wraith down earlier was a better prison, but Lawrence’s Time modified spell had its own advantages. The main one being it didn’t give his opponent the opportunity to escape on their own. The effect would only end when the working ran out of energy to stretch that moment of time like putty.

That didn’t mean it was easy. Lawrence’s spirit burned with the effort of casting the spell and spending so much willpower to shift the relative passage of time, but it worked. Sadly, while its success was untenable, Lawrence wasn’t ent—

Lawrence felt the world jitter as time skipped a few moments ahead for him. The sword that should have exploded his chest traveled forward, along with the blue armored man who hadn’t taken the attack on his team's support well. Stepping forward in time had let Lawrence dodge the blow, but it cost him as he was caught in the follow-up spells Titan launched in his wake to counter that particular ability.

With his primary objective achieved, Lawrence found a timeline where he was still in his position where he’d fled the original onslaught of talismans and spells, swapping places with that version of himself. Thanks to Esther’s own maneuvering during his rush on Wraith, it was close to where she fought Legion.

Or rather, where three of Legion tried to pin Esther down and take her out.

It was perfect coordination and numbers, versus being able to see the future and react to what hadn’t happened yet. Sadly, the battle was closer than he’d have liked and he joined in the conflict with a [Splitting Second] to one of Legion's winged bodies, intending to split her in half.

His attack worked, but it did so a little better than he’d hoped. As the hyper-thin spell neatly sliced through the armor and into the flesh underneath, causing real damage, he saw the moment where she stopped resisting and let the spell pass through her.

Instead of simply reforming, the wound closed as he’d seen her do before, with the two halves of a person bubbling as they reformed into two separate bodies, each facing in an opposite direction.

The Legion facing him created a spear out of her omnipresent feathers and thrust it forward even before she was completely formed.

Rather than engage, he dropped a talisman they’d prepared for her. The [Cleansing Light] contained inside struck out in a blinding bar of purifying energy, similar to Titan’s earlier [Mana Beam] but with far less outward destruction.

When the spell hit Legion it started to dissolve her. It was trying to replicate the tactics they’d pilfered from the Collective’s recent knowledge about her weakness in liquid form. It destroyed her half formed parts, but it had little effect on her more physical body.

Lawrence noted that down before he discarded the future and returned to the moment before he’d withdrawn the talisman.

In his second attempt, he went with a heavily modified [Dust Swarm] talisman. The wave of brown it spat at her didn’t seem dangerous, but the ultra absorbent material threatened to absorb any piece of Legion that shifted.

Seeing his ploy work, as her possible futures were restricted to ones where she didn’t shift as much, he flew upward to dodge the [Mana Beam] that tried to catch him and Esther lined up.

He wasn’t going to let himself get hit and [Far Step]ped twice in rapid succession, relocating to a future where he’d joined Esther in the melee.

His spatha slashed out and cut deep into a Legion that hadn’t expected his arrival. He successfully captured the woman's attention as two spears slammed into him and twisted. Worse, edges of the golden feather constructs twisted and broke off into his body where they sought to run rampant.

Legion added fuel to the fire by barraging him with a plethora of curses that would have taken him out of the fight in short order if he wasn’t able to revert his body back to its previous healthy state with his Talent and avoid the consequences of letting the blood mage land a spell.

He blocked the next volley with a [Negation Field] but he knew that with Titan still in the fight any exchange of mana was one he’d lose eventually.

Thankfully his distraction had given Esther the time she needed to flit through the timelines.

All of Legion's feathers from her wings, spear, and armor dissolved into nothingness as Esther reappeared covered in bloody scratches with a savage grin as she fluctuated some useful half truths to hopefully discourage their opponents.

“Those feathers were more than a little annoying so I went to the future, found the timeline where you three lost and destroyed them all. Then it's just a matter of reverting that. We may not be Maya who can alter reality itself, but we can cut away your futures which amounts to the same thing.”

Privately, she added to Lawrence, explaining why she’d gone with their plan C. “All the plans for Titan didn’t do shit. I couldn’t break either his sword or his armor, let alone propagate such a change back. The feathers were almost as hard but destroying one let me cut the rest of them… but I had to revert without recovering mana, so I’m most of the way out. Also, we need to hurry the fuck up. Even with Wraith out of the fight, the longer this stretches the worse it is for us.”

The several Legion bodies, all covered in the absorbent dust and without her feather weapons and armor, didn't hesitate to continue the offensive, relying on her main body's spear and for the clones, her fists.

Esther saw her start to shift the dirt still caked on her using the elemental manipulation effect of the spear just in time to dodge the returned burst of dust that was sent her way. Once free from the containment, Legion almost instantly started to rebuild her armaments out of crystallized ichor constructs with her feathers taken out of the fight.

Lawrence wanted to continue to help but before he could do so he was forced to revert his body once again as Titan’s sword brushed past him and obliterated his left leg and hip with the casual contact.

He nearly flicked forward in time at the blow but after the first usage of that ability Titan was prepared and the moment he stepped forward in time the other man would bombard the area with a layer of attacks so dense he’d never escape alive let alone uninjured. While everyone understood the logic of his position remaining static when jumping forward in time, the amount of variance Lawrence had when using Esther’s Talent combined with his future sights ability to pick the best moment to jump to, usually ensured he could dodge any follow up strikes.

However he was forced to realize that such an ability only worked against people who needed to conserve their mana. He could see the future and knew that even if he tried to jump forward an hour or two, Titan would not only be willing but capable of saturating the area with attacks that he’d lose the fight anyway as surely as if he’d taken the hit or surrendered.

When his body was recovered, he spun up a new timeline and escaped into it for a brief moment of respite as Titan cast [Meteor Shower], creating hundreds of obstacles around them.

Legion wasn’t bothered by the flames or impacts, but he and Esther weren't so lucky and had to dodge into a [Time Ripple] together.

While he was there, he took the time to see how effective locking Titan down was and was glad to have done so. Contrary to their before-fight estimates there was no ambiguity to see in Titan’s response to being locked in a moment in time. The method they used against Wraith wouldn’t work at all, as Titan’s mass was too high to push that far off the temporal axis to extend time appreciably against his will, and combined with his Domain the strategy never worked.

Going the other way and sending Titan into the future did have the possibility of working, but it would take both him and Esther to have a chance to send him far enough to be useful and they discarded a few possible timelines as they refocused their earlier plans on how to do so.

Sadly they weren’t able to plan forever, as, if left alone, Legion quickly started to rebuild a crystallized arsenal that was almost as irritating as the feathered one.

Moving together, he and Esther activated all of their temporary buffs, extended them to remain for longer than a literal instant, and rushed Titan.

Esther’s whip entangled his right arm enough to disrupt his attack but the unfathomably durability of the Time Carp’s spinal column was only barely strong enough to not tear as Titan resisted the movement. If the struggle had continued for too long it would have torn, but she was able to guide rather than fight his energy. At least enough to let Lawrence get in close, where he landed a thrust to Titan’s chin, aiming to drive up and into his brain.

He’d seen where Titan had survived a similar attack at Tier 27, but he was still flabbergasted at the Empire Gladiator’s complete disregard for what should have been a fatal blow. Instead of being bothered by the blade in his brain, the man used the opportunity to try and squish Lawrence with a [Telekinesis] strike aimed at his own body.

The part of Lawrence that wasn’t fighting for his life couldn’t help but be impressed with how good the armor was. He felt at least two layers of resistance that shouldn’t have been there in Titan’s skull. He couldn’t help but hope the Corporations Tier 50 could make something as useful as Titan’s armor for their commission after the war even as he dodged the wave of invisible force that would have pulverized him if it had landed.

The clocktower they’d designed would have proven itself helpful too. As it was, he and Esther only had their weapons’ connection with Titan to try and toss him into the future. Still, with both their weapons as temporal anchors, he’d hoped their move would have worked better than it did. Sadly, he saw the results almost the moment they successfully sent Titan forward in time. They only managed to send Titan a fraction of a second forward, which was worse than they’d predicted.

Worse yet, he instantly knew it wouldn’t work a second time at all. Every possible future Lawrence saw now showed Titan rapidly increasing his and his sword’s subjective weight to counter the move.

A gravity well that heavy was the same as a temporal anchor set in the past for all that Lawrence and Esther could affect it. Time was elusive and few people had reliable counters, but then again most people could never generate so much pseudo-mass they were able to break out of a temporal toss from that angle alone.

With a limited window of opportunity for a one versus two, he didn’t dally and joined Esther against the four Legions.

Instead of going on the defensive as he might have, the Empire Gladiator went on the offensive and struck out with a reckless ferocity, having seemingly no consideration to the amount of mana she spent with each attack.

It proved effective as Legion's third blow slipped through Esther’s defensive stance and she landed a spear thrust to her flank. The wound in and of itself wasn’t all that serious, but he saw as the blood that left her immediately started to revolt and turn against her.

Mirroring Legion, his sister stopped caring about her own defense and sprung to the attack. She took a savage blow to her neck that nearly decapitated her but managed to entangle one of the Legions with her whip.

Esther’s body shimmered as she used his Talent to recover her body from the earlier damage as she pulled on her weapon. Her move sliced through the body, cutting Legion into four pieces. Against a foe like Legion the physical damage itself was secondary to the spiritual.

He was nearly upon one of the other Legions who was primarily focused on casting spells when he saw that the pieces of that body didn’t immediately reform, giving him some of their first good news during the battle. That proved the method to deal with her worked and spurred him on to redouble the pressure.

Lawrence dumped what felt like every spell in his core and inner spell slots at the body he was fighting and he knew for a fact Esther did as well.

They lost over half their bodily reversions in exchange but they both managed to take down their opponent in record time. However the moment the final Legion died, her blood kindled as her phoenix bloodline activated to revive her.

Together, they moved to suppress the effect and keep her down, but Legion had been ready for her inevitable death and she carried with her enough magical momentum they were unable to smother her flames, even abusing their ability to alter the future.

Lawrence wanted to complain that if they’d been fresh and not multiple people into the fight they might have managed it, but his complaints meant nothing as things almost immediately went from bad to worse. A moment after Legion revived in a wave of fire and lightning, Titan replaced Lawrence’s opponent, signifying he’d returned to the battlefield and evened the fight back out.

Lawrence dodged the first massive crescent of mana by virtue of skipping forward in time, but he was forced to keep the maneuver short as he foresaw Titan would simply carpet bomb the area, which made it a balancing act. In the end, he erred on the side of caution and nearly lost his left arm from the error as the second wave of mana caught him in the side.

Thankfully it was smaller than the first and only shattered his arm and armor on that side, which didn’t necessitate an immediate reversion.

What did cause a full reversion was Titan’s fist that landed squarely in his chest with enough force to pulverize him and his armor to a fine mist.

While Lawrence was able to protect his brain where it was hidden in his thigh, he was still sent flying into and then through Minkalla as the world teleported him to the other side before he could interact with it.

That was where he nearly caught a hammer to the face as Greathammer attacked Maya, assisted by Mastermind. He could tell that she was failing to restrain the reality warper so her stronger teammate could land a solid blow. More than that, he could see where both of that team's supports had been trapped. Lifegiver was contained within a coffin of white light his pounding fists and curses failed to break him free of, while Deadlord’s skeletal frame had been separated into thoroughly disabled piles of bones.

He narrowly slipped through the fight and stopped himself just as Titan came barreling through the planet, his blade already charged and glowing, looking to finish him off.

Lawrence quickly glanced through the possible futures and grabbed one where he’d been hit by the hammer and sent flying back to the other side of the planet.

He had to take the blow and revert himself, but it relocated him back to the other side of Minkalla faster than Titan could react, and he barreled into Esther and the two remaining Legions she was fighting, hoping they could take her down before they ran out of mana.

It didn’t work.

Legion and Titan simply refused to go down and their repertoire of time based skills proved irritatingly ineffective. Titan was too heavy to easily effect and had too much mana to try and imprison while Legion’s multiple bodies made it difficult to properly isolate her. The blasted phoenix both had revivals and no true main body to protect which meant missing even a single drop of her in any time based shift would let her escape.

They gave it their all to try and take back their momentum but as Maya finished off the final two members of the Clans team, Wraith broke out of her prison and that signaled the end. With the fox free, both of them felt their opponents speed up, grow stronger, all while illusions started to populate the future.

Lawrence and Esther tried to refocus but the white of a winter forest appeared around them, first as an illusion and then a moment later as reality, as Wraith used their distraction to expand her Spirit Space.

They tried to flee but being able to see the future showed them that while they’d be able to break out, doing so would force them to empty their willpower reserves. That would mean a guaranteed loss against Maya and they weren't willing to risk one of the few fights they believed they had a good chance at winning for a hopeless continuation of one that was already lost.

The battle had lasted too long, shifting from their advantage of quick surgical strikes to end a battle quickly to the Empire team's primary strength, endurance.

All of that was proven the correct decision as he once more was pulled under Gladiator Titan’s Concept and his mana pool rapidly climbed. Being able to fight without care for rationing one's mana pool was a hard to overcome advantage but not one he thought was insurmountable.

He was already devising plans and counters to what they’d seen today and was confident that their next bout with the Empire team wouldn’t end the same way it had today.

Matt wasn’t exactly happy with their fight with Tick and Tock but he didn’t have too much to complain about, given they were the only team that hadn’t been defeated yet.

With Maya beating On The Last Line, if they were able to secure a win over their older peers they’d get the clean sweep. That would wipe away any negative feelings he had about their previous win.

It wasn’t that he didn't appreciate Esther and Lawrence's fighting style, he just didn’t have all that much fun fighting against opponents who perfectly dodged half of what he threw at them because they’d already seen it happen. While he managed to get some hits in, at no point did he really feel like he had the upper hand in the fight. At most the three of them managed to surprise the Republic Ascenders a few times as they countered their plans, but overall they’d been on the backfoot most of the fight.

The fight had come down to the fact he and Liz could handle damage better than their opponents could. One or two successful counterattacks more than made up for the constant barrage of attacks they’d been under from the time duo.

It took a few minutes for everyone to heal up and prepare for their last fight but Matt didn’t mind all that much.

Aster was distracted, and he felt through their bond as she rapidly made notes about her experience in her own time shenanigans.

Where Matt had simply found himself a few moments forward in time, Aster had experienced a single moment as if it were an eternity and that had apparently been unique enough for her to have ideas.

He couldn’t be sure they would lead to anything, but he did know that he was happy for her.

The Clans quartet must have realized the solemnity of their final fight as Tier 35s, as they approached silently without their usual amount of banter and internal jokes.

That didn’t mean they didn’t take the opportunity to speak.

It just wasn't internal. “Ayy, this is where we always knew we’d end up, didn’t we? Me, passing on the torch to you, the new, weaker replacement they needed to fill in, now that I’m retiring.”

Matt couldn’t help but quip right back to the hammer wielding dwarf, “What, no accent?”

Moe coughed into his hammer before slurring out. “O’ve ‘een ‘orking on me proper speaks for all my next adventures.”

Liz called right back as she shook her head. “No one will ever believe that. Don’t you hate what the movies do with it? At least if you were consistent one way or the other they could settle. Is that how you want your final battle to be shown?”

Moe shrugged out a casual, “I like to see who’s paying attention and who isn’t.”

Matt didn’t believe a word of it and he didn’t think Liz did either but it was all moot as there was only one thing any of them really cared about.

The fight.

And for Matt that desire was narrowed down even further.

Moe.

The feeling in the space sharpened the instant they entered combat, and Matt plowed forwards as fast as [Cracked Air Slide] could take him, nearly outrunning Aster’s own [Tailwind] in his speed to lock Moe down.

If they could do that the rest of the fight became far more manageable and they had a Liz trap prepared exactly for that but it relied on them getting it close. After Moe was entangled he and Aster would focus on taking out Krodag and Brian while Liz occupied Ellen and Moe.

Moe started a moment later, but blasted forwards with a massive jolt, trailing magic of his own as he began to swing his hammer in an arc. Matt closed in, turning his charge into a full power thrust while pulling himself off-axis slightly to avoid Moe’s first strike.

Then the wake of magic surrounding Moe spiked, and Matt recognized Krodag’s work within it, announcing exactly what he was about to be struck with. In the instant before the magic fully coalesced, Matt reinforced his defenses to the absolute max, preemptively shielding against every curse or debuff On The Last Line tried to hit him with.

“[Chain Yeetening]!” Krodag’s voice announced, and Matt’s eyes went wide as Moe and his hammer both jerked suddenly, landing a low impact but highly magical blow directly on Matt’s heart.

Right where Liz had taken residence within him.

Matt tried to brace himself with his Intent but that proved to be a mistake. The skill had already landed and it rampaged through his body, where it grabbed hold of every golden droplet of Liz intertwined through his veins and blasted it out of him. In that split second, Matt felt the empowerment Liz always brought with him torn away as the golden lightning raced off into the distance from where he’d come.

While he stopped, Liz hadn’t. In that one moment almost every soft tissue in his body was shredded as Liz tore her way out of every vein in his body through his back. He quickly healed thanks to [Regeneration], but the damage to his augment took longer to repair as the pieces tried to find their proper places.

Things continued to go poorly as normally when a Liz was forcibly ejected from her maximum range it disconnected in a way that only minorly inconvenienced his wife. A piece of her breaking from the greater whole was rarely more than a small sting.

But Moe never broke something if he didn’t want to, and that worked out terribly for the collective Liz.

Both the two human bodies and the phoenix on Aster’s shoulder were equally knocked away, pulled as if on a rope connected to the original Liz. The four of them vanished from the battlefield at a speed Matt had only ever associated with higher Tiers, where they were sent flying into the emptiness between star systems.

Ellen didn’t waste the opportunity as she blurred past where he’d been with a dozen minions of Brian’s, all of whom rushed Aster spells blazing whilst they cast as if he’d kept them under his Concept.

Matt’s initial reaction was to swap places with one of the Lizzes to get her back into the fight, but she was already well out of range by the time he could react.

He knew they needed to hold until she could return and tried to join Aster so they could support each other. Sadly, On The Last Line knew the same thing and they’d instantly moved to prevent him from doing exactly that.

“[On My Mark]!” Moe kept on Matt, but Krodag was working with Ellen and Brian, his skill announcements reverberating through the battlefield.

Brian wasn’t gradually ramping up from basic skeletons to his best juggernauts, like he usually preferred, he started off with a bang. Two massive Tier 35 wyvern bodies crawled out of his robes and charged after Aster, and once they got close, Ellen climbed out from their rib cages with a glowing rapier, which she used to carve into Aster’s ice and illusions alike.

Matt fired off a quick [Breach] at one of the wyverns, but Moe landed an attack on him before he could do anything more, absolutely annihilating Matt’s left arm and half his chest.

With [Regeneration] too slow to help, Matt made a split-second decision to call upon the half-completed merge between [Cracked Phantom Armor] and [Archmage’s Presence], holding the combination together with [Reinforce Mana] while he melded his Aspect and Intent.

His armor blazed forth a pure mana facsimile of his missing parts with its outer layer. The most eye-catching change was the black hole that formed in his chest from where it spread a wave of mana-limned darkness across his armor as his Intent’s concentration boost made his mana that much stronger.

“[Combo Breaker]!” Krodag called out. However the comment was aimed at Moe, and then the strongman was attacking again, this time going for Matt’s lower body. He lost his leg and hip, but instantly replaced them with black armor equivalents.

Matt saw and felt the excitement radiating off of Moe but that didn’t slow the dwarf down, and he only grew faster as Krodag’s buffs intensified.

He’d have loved to spar with the dwarf but the fight had already turned against them and he had no room to play around with them. As such he activated [Mana Beam] aiming to burrow through the dwarf but Krogad cried out. “[Locked but Not Loaded]”

His [Mana Beam] didn’t shatter in his spirit but there seemed to be an invisible cork that had been applied. The raw overwhelming output of Matt’s mana quickly started to burn through the obstruction but even that small window of opportunity proved too much free time for the two dwarves.

“[Stunlock Lockdown]! [Chain Hit]! [Juggling Strikes]! [Solo Volleyball]!”

Moe’s attacks were hitting with less physical strength the more Krodag layered magic into their duel, but that was more than offset by the complete and total incapability Matt had to break out. Each blow with Moe’s hammer or fists splintered Matt’s body, yes, but it also was locking him into very specific recoil patterns with even more force than Matt’s Intent could manage. He was knocked from place to place, Moe moving incredibly fast to continue swatting Matt around back and forth like a pinball.

However, that hardly meant Matt was defenseless or sat there and took the blows.

He couldn’t break free but with each blow, Matt was able to adjust his oldest spell to weather the technique and the physical impacts better and better. Doing so  midfight was only possible given the merge was only made possible by his [AI] integration and centuries of practice. For all that most people may have thought being actively pummeled by one of the absolute strongest people in the Realm was a bad time to do delicate skill operations, Matt was almost content to get the ultimate form of stress testing and would have been downright happy if it wasn’t his literal only remaining course of action. Still the battering let him refine aspects of the combination in record speed at levels he rarely got to encounter.

Matt broke out of doing his best Gan Le impression when Aster swung by and stripped the debuffs from him and wove them back onto Moe. That gave the dwarf a piece of his own medicine and launched him into space as the force was retransmitted.

Matt’s bond was looking rough. She was held together mostly through [Deathly Cold], aurora-glimmering chunks of ice filling in a number of cracks throughout her body. Her teeth were bared which exposed how extensive the damage truly was as she met an onslaught of spirits from Brian with a wall of aurora. However, that maneuver let Ellen grab the aurora with her bare hands, where she dramatically pulled on it before she tossed it like it was cloth.

The other support took that highly colorful cloak and swept it around like it was a cloak that he was using to unveil…

“[Instant Replay]!”

Moe was the target, because of course it was.

Matt avoided getting stuck in a full pinball loop by denying Krodag’s debuffs with an intense Millennium Willow Lifesap flush. He tried to leverage that advantage but Moe used a literal chain he had somehow rendered unbreakable to keep Matt tied to him while the other three Legends pushed Aster as far from Matt’s duel as was possible.

Matt continued to do his best to fight free, but Moe and Krodag kept Matt from breaking free of the all too literal tether he was locked down by. Matt tried to cut off his own limbs to escape the chain, but Krodag slipped in a “[Relash]” to spontaneously change where he was tied down. That did bring Moe in close enough for Matt to strike back, but Moe literally punched through Matt’s layered attack of [Telekinesis], [Cosmic Pressure], and [Dragonflame].

That counterpunch did leave Moe smoking, parts of the dwarf’s skin melted at the contact, and Matt slammed him with a point-blank [Mana Beam] to try and wear him down further. Moe was forced to dodge that, which allowed Matt to finally nail him with a [Titan’s Wrath] that knocked him back and snapped the chain binding them as he was sent flying away… directly towards the melee where Aster was holding off the other three Legends.

“[Stunning Reveal], [All According to Plan]!” confirmed Matt’s assessment he had done something wrong, and Moe twisted in midair to barrel towards the tussle hammer-first. Matt charged forward at maximum speed, closing the distance even faster than Moe was.

One of Aster’s decoy illusions was forced into visibility, her staff locked in place against Ellen’s sword, which allowed Brian to pile hundreds of monstrous skeletons onto them in a dense miasma of Death that coalesced into a wicked curse.

“[I Can’t Believe It’s Not Wraith]!”

Moe’s attack annihilated the pile of skeletons, Ellen somehow slipping out at the very last moment before the entire area became saturated with necrotic-laden bone shards. Aster’s body, having been swapped with her illusionary decoy just as the attack landed, was utterly annihilated, leaving just her aurora-glimmering [Deathly Cold] [Winter Spirit] in its place.

Her tiara manifested upon a ghostly brow, and Aster’s Spirit Space washed out across the battlefield, usurping the zone of death with her own queendom of cold as bone fragments became snowflakes. The frozen lands spread across the battlefield, and Matt moved to guard Aster’s spirit as she began to reconstitute her physical form.

Then the ice around them turned back to bone, and Aster doubled over as a grand magnifying ritual channeled a spell Matt recognized as [Capture Spirit] directly onto his bond. She jerked unnaturally, and the Spirit Space collapsed around them, closing off the surroundings.

“Well this is embarrassing," Aster fluctuated. “Matt, could I get a focused and Aspect-boosted Concept boost from you?”

Matt complied, and Aster became something approaching real even as the surroundings partially faded away. Unfortunately, the two of them were trapped within a reliquary Brian was keeping on his belt, the necromancer having taken advantage of Aster’s momentarily spiritual state, and Matt’s position within her spirit, to capture both of them as though they were incorporeal undead. They could see beyond that, to the battlefield, but were very much immobilized until then.

Matt’s eyes scanned for weaknesses within the reliquary, and found a few. “Okay. I think we can get out of here with enough time. They were burning through a lot to get us, but I think the long game is still ours. How are you holding up?”

“I need a bit more time to pull myself together. Keep up the buff, but I can probably be battle ready again in time.”

Matt nodded and began pulling out talismans and creating a formation within Aster’s spirit. He was most of the way there when Liz re-entered the battle. Unfortunately, but probably unsurprisingly, the four versus one - particularly given they’d had the opportunity to prepare the battlefield in their favor and partially recover from their blitz against Matt and Aster - didn’t go particularly well for her, and they forced her into a second rebirth in as many hours. Much like with what happened to Aster, Brian took advantage of the moment of death to ensnare Liz’s spirit, sending a message as he did so.

“I can put her in there with you two if you surrender, and then we can all go get front row seats to the Gladiator match.”

The prideful part of Matt wanted to resist for resistance's sake, but he had no real idea beyond throwing an unquestionable limitless amount of mana at the problem and revealing his Talent for a mostly friendly spar would be the height of foolishness.

“Two out of three isn’t bad,” Aster sighed. “Do we accept?”

“It would be stupid not to,” he glanced at his sister to check her reaction before sending the message.

Rather than Liz appearing inside with them, the two of them were dumped next to the still lightning crackling and smoldering Liz.

And at least watching Esther and Lawrence beat Maya was a solid way to wrap up the day, as the time traveling duo slipped their way around and saw through her alterations to reality well enough they were able to secure the win.

Far from upset, the moment the fight ended, Maya and all four members of On The Last Line Tiered up to Tier 36. An instant later a message spread from Minkalla across all the battlefields. The wars were over, peace had been settled.
