From now on, Liu Shu would need to reach to nearby Solar Systems and take them over without creating outrage. Due to the general atmosphere in the galaxy, actions of conquest were marked as disruptive as long as the population was not affected. It was similar to the system Liu Shu was currently implementing in her Golden Sap Empire.

She allowed some cities and smaller countries to fight each other, but the condition was to not bother civilians. The second civilians were maliciously added in the conflict, Liu Shu’s ants and local forces would swarm the place and take down whoever did it.
