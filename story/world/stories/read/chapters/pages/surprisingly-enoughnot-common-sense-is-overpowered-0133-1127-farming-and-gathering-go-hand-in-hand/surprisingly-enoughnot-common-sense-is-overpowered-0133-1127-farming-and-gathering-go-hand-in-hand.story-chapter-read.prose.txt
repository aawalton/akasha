Truly, my precious.

Then, of course, there was Know Thyself.

Hmph. They have eyes but can't see Mt. Tai.

The First Fold of the Sevenfold Pure Origin Art concerned the harmonization of qi.

At first glance, the principle appeared straightforward. A cultivator absorbed spiritual energy, refined it into qi, circulated it through their meridians, and stored it within the dantian. Repeatedly following the circulation prescribed by the scripture would gradually bring the qi into a uniform state.

The difficulty lay in what the scripture meant by uniform.

Two portions of qi could possess identical attributes and purity while still differing in their internal characteristics. Some might be denser from repeated compression, while others could retain subtle variations introduced during refinement.

Ning closed his eyes and directed his attention inward, observing the shallow pool of spiritual liquid that had accumulated at the bottom of his dantian.

When he first entered the Seventh Stage of Qi Condensation, he'd managed to condense only a single drop. Several months later, that drop had grown into a small pool, surrounded by gaseous qi that had yet to undergo liquefaction.

Through Know Thyself, Ning could perceive the tiny disturbances that occurred whenever newly condensed spiritual liquid merged with his existing reserves. Although his qi was exceptionally pure, each portion had undergone slightly different degrees of refinement and compression, leaving subtle differences in its internal structure.

That was precisely where the First Fold came into play.

Ning lit a stick of Spirit Concentration Incense, allowing its faint fragrance to settle his mind before beginning the circulation of the Sevenfold Pure Origin Art.

Drawing a portion of qi from his dantian, he guided it through the prescribed meridians before returning it to his reserves.

He'd spent quite some time studying the different interpretations of the First Fold. The scripture spoke of harmonizing qi, but the commentaries offered various explanations. Some approached it through Yin and Yang Returning to Unity, others through Heaven and Man in Harmony, while a few drew upon the principle of Ten Thousand Things Returning to Their Origin.

Each had its own reasoning, and Ning had found something worth considering in several of them. However, after comparing their explanations with his observations through Know Thyself, he'd gradually developed his own understanding.

Equilibrium.

The concept was familiar from his previous world. When two systems with different internal conditions interacted, they could gradually approach a common state of balance under the right circumstances.

He'd spent months testing whether something similar could be achieved with spiritual qi. Increasing the circulation speed had only made things worse, disturbing the spiritual liquid and introducing new irregularities. A slower, steadier flow worked better, allowing the different portions of qi to influence one another without disrupting the existing balance.

After numerous adjustments, Ning had finally settled on a rhythm that produced consistent results.

He regulated the circulation, drawing both gaseous qi and spiritual liquid through the prescribed meridians. With each circulation, those differences gradually diminished, allowing the spiritual liquid returning to his dantian to merge more smoothly with the existing pool.

[Sevenfold Pure Origin Art (First Fold: 99.998 → 99.999/100)]

Ning maintained the circulation, making small adjustments whenever he detected a disturbance. There was very little left to correct now, and after several more cycles, the remaining differences finally settled into the equilibrium he'd spent months trying to achieve.

His gaseous qi and spiritual liquid remained in their respective states, but the differences between individual portions had been reduced enough for them to circulate and merge with minimal disturbance to his existing reserves.

A familiar panel appeared before him.

[Sevenfold Pure Origin Art (First Fold: Completion)]

Ning opened his eyes and let out a breath of turbid air. "That's one down."

Each Fold of the Sevenfold Pure Origin Art brought its own benefits, depending on the principles comprehended.

For Ning, completing the First Fold meant he could now reliably reproduce the equilibrium he'd established, gradually harmonizing newly refined qi with his existing reserves. The resulting uniformity also granted him finer control and reduced spiritual energy consumption when using techniques.

This would be especially useful during liquefaction, where every newly condensed drop had to be incorporated into his existing reserves.

Maintaining that balance would still require cultivation, but he now knew how to restore it whenever new qi entered his dantian.

With the First Fold finally behind him, there were still six more waiting.

Ning glanced at the next portion of the Sevenfold Pure Origin Art, which he'd already studied several times.

The Second Fold concerned the harmonization of meridians.

While the First Fold brought the qi itself into equilibrium, the Second sought to harmonize the meridians through which it circulated. Every meridian differed in width, resilience, and resistance to qi, and bringing them into harmony would reduce strain and allow for smoother circulation.

Ning understood the general principle, but experimenting with his meridians would require much more caution.

Well, that can wait.

His attention shifted toward his cultivation progress.

[Seventh Stage Qi Condensation (Liquefaction: 864/3400)]

There was still quite a distance before the Eighth Stage, but Ning wasn't dissatisfied. His progress had been steady, and completing the First Fold should make his future cultivation more efficient.

Besides, there was something else he wanted to try.

His gaze settled on another entry.

[Infinite Circle (Accumulating: 3/100)]

Completing the First Fold had given him something he'd been waiting for: a sufficiently stable circulation to continue his experiments with Infinite Circle.

He'd already spent months trying to incorporate the Sevenfold Pure Origin Art into his proposed system of automatic cultivation. The difficulty was getting one circulation to initiate the next without disrupting the refinement and harmonization that the technique was supposed to achieve.

Ning lit a fresh stick of Spirit Concentration Incense and directed a small portion of qi through the Sevenfold Pure Origin Art's circulation route, using a connecting pathway he'd devised for his latest experiments.

The first cycle proceeded smoothly, following the prescribed meridians. However, when he tried redirecting the returning current toward the beginning of another cycle, the two flows interfered almost immediately.

He reduced the amount of qi entering the connecting pathway, only for the returning current to become too weak to initiate another circulation.

"Hmm... too little?"

Increasing it produced another disturbance.

"Too much, then. What about the angle?"

Ning adjusted the pathway and tried again, using his panel to examine each failure.

The problem remained the same. Returning qi settled into his dantian, while the outgoing current required a separate impulse to begin moving. Forcing the two currents together disturbed the circulation, and some arrangements even weakened the harmonizing effect of the First Fold.

By the time the incense had nearly burned out, his experiments had produced little beyond a growing collection of failed approaches.

Ning rubbed his forehead.

There has to be a better way to do this.

He began sketching the arrangement of the currents, recalling the experiments he'd conducted in the Heavenly Origin Tower.

Back then, he'd learned the hard way what happened when he tried forcing qi into a continuous rotation through his meridians. The resulting accumulation had been too much for them to withstand, which was why he'd eventually moved Infinite Circle's rotating qi into the dantian.

The problem was that his current project required successive circulations through those same meridians. He needed each completed cycle to initiate another, but forcing the returning qi back through the entire route would eventually lead to the same problem.

Ning studied his diagram for a moment.

Wait... why does it have to be the same qi?

A waterwheel didn't require the same water to climb back up and flow through it repeatedly. The passing current simply transferred its force to the wheel, allowing fresh water to keep it turning.

What if he applied the same principle here? He could let most of the returning qi settle into his dantian as usual, while diverting a small portion to push fresh qi into the next circulation.

"Yes! That's what I was missing!"

Ning immediately began another experiment. This time, he allowed most of the returning qi to settle into his dantian, diverting only a thin current toward the fresh qi waiting at the entrance of the outgoing meridian.

His first few attempts failed, but eventually he found a promising balance. The returning current transferred just enough momentum to set the fresh qi in motion, sending it through the outgoing meridian.

Ning watched the process closely.

The new current followed the prescribed route through his meridians before eventually returning toward his dantian.

He resisted the urge to intervene.

The returning current once again set the waiting qi in motion, initiating another circulation without Ning's intervention.

Ning resisted the urge to celebrate, afraid that losing concentration would ruin the experiment.

The second cycle completed successfully, followed by a third. During the fourth, however, the flow weakened enough that the returning current could no longer provide the necessary impulse, forcing Ning to intervene before the qi stagnated within the connecting pathway.

Ning couldn't help laughing at the results.

"Hahaha! It actually worked! Now we're getting somewhere!"

He immediately repeated the experiment, managing only two consecutive circulations on the next attempt and three on the one after that.

The results were hardly stable, but Ning had finally demonstrated that the returning current could initiate another cycle without his conscious guidance.

More importantly, the qi had continued following the Sevenfold Pure Origin Art's prescribed route, and Know Thyself hadn't detected any obvious disruption to its refinement and harmonization.

Now he just needed to figure out how to keep the process going.

Excited by the results, Ning hurried toward his desk.

He pulled out several sheets of paper and began recording everything he could remember, from the circulation route and the proportion of qi entering the connecting pathway to the conditions under which another cycle had successfully begun.

"First Fold... equilibrium... yes, that explains why the earlier attempts kept failing. But why does the returning current lose strength after several cycles?"

He drew a rough diagram, marked the incoming and outgoing currents, and added a few notes beside the connecting pathway.

Once he'd recorded the results, Ning pulled another sheet of paper toward himself and wrote a title across the top.

Perpetual Cultivation Project

1. Continuous Circulation

The completion of one cycle would initiate the next without his conscious intervention. He'd already demonstrated that it was possible, although the circulation still weakened after a few repetitions.

2. Self-Maintaining Circulation

Getting the qi to circulate repeatedly was only the beginning. He still needed a way for the flow to maintain its stability without constant supervision, and the Second Fold of the Sevenfold Pure Origin Art might prove useful in that regard.

3. Automatic Spiritual Energy Absorption

Eventually, the circulation would need to replenish his qi reserves by drawing spiritual energy from the surroundings. Refining that energy into usable qi presented another problem, especially since the rate of absorption would vary depending on his surroundings.

There were other things to consider, including regulating qi consumption, compensating for disturbances, and adapting the circulation as his cultivation advanced. He had a few ideas, but most were still little more than guesses.

Final Objective: True Perpetual Cultivation!

Profit.
