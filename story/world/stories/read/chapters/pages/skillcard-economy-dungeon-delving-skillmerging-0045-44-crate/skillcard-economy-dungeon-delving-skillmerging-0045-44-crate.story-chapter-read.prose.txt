I walked back to the unknown naked man. I shook him. “Hey, hey, wake up, wake up.” I shook him harder. His eyes shot open, and the pale gold eyes locked into me. I instinctively stammered back. He sat up straight with the strength of his back. His long blond hair tumbled down covering his back. He was slender, but not gaut. He was weak but not scared. He wasn’t a victim, he was a predator. An instinctive fear settled down on me.

Who is this guy? Why am I feeling scared? Some high ranked walker?

“Where am I?” His voice was authoritative. Aran’s voice was also authoritative but in a different kind. His was off a frantic priest who believed he was bringing salvation to everyone. This guy’s was different. It was of a ruler, and I was his subject.

Like fucking hell I was. I was no one’s fucking property. I stopped myself from shivering and stared him in the eyes. He looked at me curiously, as if he did not expect me to answer his gaze.

“You were kidnapped, unconscious and stuffed in this trunk”

“Not you specifically, but I am glad if I did.”

Whatever it was that was making me fear him vanished.
