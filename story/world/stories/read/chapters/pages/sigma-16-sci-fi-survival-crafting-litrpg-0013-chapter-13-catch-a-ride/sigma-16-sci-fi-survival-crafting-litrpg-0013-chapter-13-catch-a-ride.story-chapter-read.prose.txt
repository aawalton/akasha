It only took a few minutes to haul the fabricator out of the escape pod and set it up. She moved fast despite feeling slightly fatigued. Once again, she used the crate as a makeshift seat while the fabricator provided a workspace and tabletop. The quick pace left her waiting for the sun to come up.

Five minutes of waiting proved too much for her restlessness and she set about retracting the solar panels, removing the antenna, and detaching the module from the pod. Once back on the ground, she cut down the antenna, then welded it to her suit’s shoulder.

It was a bit nerve-wracking to work on her suit with the clumsy multi-tool, but Neo encouraged her to complete the task. It didn’t even cost her any extra material.

She set the fabricator up to stand freely on a rocky spot that she was fairly confident would support it. A few ground bolts would protect it from any light breezes.

The fabricator emitted a soft hum as she reconnected all her wiring. Sitting back down, she eyed the escape pod that had been her refuge so far.

If the Bootstrap Module was damaged, she’d need something for repairs, and the ratios Neo had reported for mining other than the insanely rich bioluminescent jungle stuff was very bad. Something that would need to be automated if she didn’t want to die doing manual labor.

“We’re going to make the Rideable Scout first, then the Quadcopter.” Talia said. “What about the Drone Control Upgrade? How much will it cost?”

[Informative: SDC upgrade would normally require one unit of Durasteel for a suit mounted antenna, however, User has already installed a compatible device, and this requirement is subverted. No further change is required due to the upgrade being primarily a software implementation.]

Talia’s eyes narrowed. That ate at her the wrong way, although she was sure someone had to develop the software. Shouldn’t it have been included with her suit? She wasn’t surprised, though. Cortex Inc. seemed to like to upsell all the extras.

“How much Battpack is going to be needed? I’d like to build the Rideable first.” Talia said.

[Informative: Approximately 56% charge will be required for the Rideable Scout Drone, while 24% should be sufficient for the Quadcopter unit.]

Talia hummed as she took that in. She’d be burning a lot of power that she’d just recharged. She estimated that she’d be somewhere above 50% by the end of the day if she kept the solar panels charging the batteries the entire day.

A night without shelter would be annoying, but not the end of the world. She’d have her new ride, at least.

“Let’s get started.” Talia said.

She went through the Pod looking for anything of value that wasn’t better off being recycled. She didn’t find much. The escape pod was only a single use unit and the batteries in it weren’t even rechargeable. The fusion cells were still valuable, but there was no reactor on board that used them. She wasn’t sure why they were even included.

Each one weighed approximately 50 kg and was the size of a sports ball. She placed them inside the crate for later.

She moved her equipment and the alien shield artifact to a safe distance after a final check inside and then began to deconstruct the Pod. The beams worked their way over it rapidly, slowly disintegrating the material layer by layer. The pod was almost halfway gone, sliced in what looked like half, when her tool reported that it was full with an angry beep.

Unloading the hexagon material cylinders was annoying, and she propped each one up against the fabricator before continuing. The entire process took approximately two hours, and she sighed in relief when it was finally finished. Nothing but a deep gouge in the sand remained of her arrival vehicle.

[Informative: A significant amount of resources have been obtained.]

“Thanks Neo, I know,” Talia said while rolling her eyes. She reviewed the list anyway, just to make sure she had what was promised and needed. She wasn’t disappointed.

She sorted the resources into different stacks, each one forming a small interlocked triangle thanks to the hexagon shape of the cylinders.

She flipped the touchscreen to look for the ATV schematic, but the fabricator only had basic parts listed. “Neo, where is the rideable?”

[Notice: A 150 Cortex credit surcharge is required for RSU schematic.]

A pang of panic filled her. “Are these one-time use like the exo-skeleton?”

[Informative: Unit fabrication schematics are multi-use unless otherwise noted.]

Talia let out a sigh of relief. Not that she needed more than one vehicle at the moment, but if anything happened to it, she didn’t want to have to pay credits each time. She’d already burned them all, and she wasn’t sure when she’d get more chances to scan new things.
