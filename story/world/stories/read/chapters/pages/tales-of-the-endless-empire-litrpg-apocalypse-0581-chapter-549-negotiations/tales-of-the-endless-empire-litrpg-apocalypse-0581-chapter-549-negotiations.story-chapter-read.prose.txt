Already thinking about which forms to use and what to say to the enemy leader, Thalion walked toward his home when an elf with a braun vest and long braun hair interrupted him.

"Sorry, sir. There are some items that were sent to you which formerly belonged to Ankhet." The elf lowered his head in respect.

"Excellent." Thalion grinned and immediately activated his movement skill, flying over to the portal. What he found there were several boxes covered in dust and spider webs. Right now probably wasn't the best time, but Thalion still wanted to take a quick look.

Excited, he hurried back to his room, carrying all of the boxes at once. Dust fell around him like rain, and Thalion had to use two vines to catch one of the boxes after it slipped from the pile as he entered through the door.

The boxes were filled with all sorts of things. There were books, but also ritual daggers, teapots, and a few stone tablets mixed in. Thalion began to skim through the books, which appeared to be records of experiments conducted in the past. Since they were experiments regarding the soul, Thalion was more than just a little excited.

The records themselves, however, were truly evil.

Slowly piercing the left eyeball with a dagger results in light fluctuations in the Thalamus section on the left. Soul weakens ten percent more compared to stabbing the shoulder, leg, finger, or chest.

Ankhet must have conducted countless experiments like these, as there were dozens of scrolls filled with similar observations. Thalion continued to skim through the material, and the more he read, the more he felt his own darker nature stirring. There were even detailed drawings explaining how to perfectly torture the soul—or, as Ankhet had called it, maximum stimulation.

When Thalion reached the stone tablets, he noticed that one of them appeared empty. In the middle of the stone rested a single drop of blood. He could see it clearly with his Blood Vision. The Bloodseed immediately wanted to consume it and tugged greedily at the blood. It seemed that tiny channels had been carved inside the stone, and only for Thalion did the blood form words across the tablet.

The system holds you through pain and spreads from there like a tumor with every evolution. Only with the system's mark gone can you be free... ... Freedom through pain. Only then can your soul be fully restored.

It was as if a lightning bolt had struck Thalion as he stared at the words before him. A hint. He had actually found a hint on how to get rid of the system's mark. Through the countless sketches of the human soul, Thalion knew exactly where the pain center was. The problem was that the tablet was still incomplete, and the whole procedure sounded more than just a little dangerous. But it was a direction.

The best part was that it sounded as though Ankhet had already succeeded in freeing himself from the system.

Countless theories raced through Thalion's mind, and he didn't even notice the hours slipping by. If the system's mark spread throughout the soul, removing it must have been nearly impossible and would likely come with tremendous damage and a very long period of weakness. What if Amun Ra had also destroyed the system's mark, which was the true reason his entire empire had fallen? The other gods might have sensed his weakness and seized the opportunity. There were still several pieces that didn't fit, such as why those gods had also disappeared and why nothing had survived what history called the Apocalypse. Still, this was finally a light on the horizon.

Thalion quickly began sorting out everything that seemed useful, especially the materials related to the system's mark. Just as he finished, someone knocked on the door.

"Thalion, are you still here? The teleportation circle is about to go online!" Jack and Josh shouted from outside, snapping Thalion out of his thoughts.

"Yes, I'm on my way." Thalion almost growled as he pushed the boxes aside and hurried downstairs. Now he felt bad that he hadn't spent more time thinking about how to intimidate the enemy leader. No... after reading all of Ankhet's records, he was ready to go full evil mode.
