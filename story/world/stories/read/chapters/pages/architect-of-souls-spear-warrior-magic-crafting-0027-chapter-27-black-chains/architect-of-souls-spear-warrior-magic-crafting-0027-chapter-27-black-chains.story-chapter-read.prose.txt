The chains draped down the side of the fort’s walls were solid, and a quick tug assured Ferris they would hold his weight.

Looking up, no new demons had appeared, but a single blue frog demon still watched him from atop one of the blazing towers. He tried circling to approach from the opposite direction, but it had moved towers to follow him.

Aiming the crossbow, Ferris fired a shot at the creature. The precious bolt flew true this time, solidly hitting… the spot the frog had just vacated. The demon had waited until he fired, then dodged slightly to the side. It watched him mockingly from the perch, next to his wasted bolt.

Ferris looked at Jade. He would be unable to hold the slime, his spear, and climb the wall at the same time. The slime’s body could grip weakly, climbing up surfaces like Ferris’ leg without consuming them. The grip wasn’t strong, as he’d been able to shake Jade off without much effort. Still, the slime could do it, wrapping slightly around things to hold on.

Jade had no trouble gripping the chains hanging down the fort’s walls, although climbing was very slow. They tested if the slime could move up the sheer wall, but Jade couldn’t get a good hold on the flat surface and just fell.

Ferris began climbing, with Jade leading the way on the chain above him. His spear was not a good weapon to wield one-handed when climbing a wall, but Ferris did his best. He had to go slow, as Jade set their pace. Ferris had climbed many ropes over the years—a personal favorite activity of some of his trainers—so at least he knew what he was doing.

Predictably, about halfway up, the frog demon attacked. Ferris was ready, and swung his spear one-handed to knock the demon out of the air before it reached him. As long as he didn’t let it grab ahold of him, he could force the fight to the ground and overpower it.

Out of nowhere, a second frog demon slammed into his back. He had no idea where it came from. It dug its claws in. Only the crossbow on his back stopped it from tearing into him. Thankfully, Ferris and Jade had discussed a plan beforehand if he missed his swing and the demon landed on him. They hadn’t expected the second one, but the plan would still work.

Jade let go of the chain and fell on top of Ferris, able to move much more quickly downward. Flowing around him, the slime quickly engulfed the demon’s small head. It had no leverage to fight back, and both fell off Ferris’ back.

With the second frog under control, Ferris looked down at the first. He’d gotten a solid swipe, knocking it towards the ground. Bracing against the wall for leverage, Ferris jumped down towards the creature, spear first. The demon saw him coming, and was able to dodge in time, but Ferris pressed his attack. One on one, the beasts were nothing special, and he soon caught it with a jab that pierced deep enough to kill it.

Jade rolled over, having consumed the other frog, and rippled happily at him.

Ferris, on the other hand, was less excited at their victory. He pulled the crossbow off his back and inspected it. The second demon frog had hit him directly in the back, slamming into the crossbow first. It had saved him from receiving anything more than light scratches, but his face fell as he looked at it.

The stock had some claw marks, and there was a light crack down the side of the bow limb, but the worst damage was to the string: the demon had sliced it in half.

Tentatively, Ferris sat down to see if it was salvageable. The stock was bulky, and the claw marks would not matter. The bow limb was more worrisome. He flexed it slightly, and it appeared to still work, but the crack might snap the limb mid-battle.

The string was in two pieces. Ferris knew some knots, but he wasn’t sure if the crossbow would still work if he tied the string. When he’d crafted the weapon, he’d made the string extra-long, not trusting his ability to get the size right, and wrapped the extra around the limb. There wasn’t enough length left on either side, but he was able to let out some slack to tie the knot.

Carefully, Ferris test fired the repaired crossbow. The tension was different, and the bolt veered off to the left slightly. He practiced a couple of shots, attempting to adapt his aim, but he didn’t want to stress the weapon.

Having repaired what he could, Ferris and Jade returned to climbing the wall. He looked around for any more frog demons, but he didn’t spot any. They were able to climb unobstructed.

Reaching the top of the wall, Ferris was able to see inside the fort for the first time. It had a square courtyard, with seven chain demons surrounding the base of the frame holding up the oath statue. Six of them looked like the normal, fat demons he was used to, but one in the center was different.

It was lanky, taller than the others—almost as tall as Ferris. While the shorter demons had an awkward lurching to their movements, this demon moved smoothly. He had never seen such a dramatic variation of a demon’s form before. Ferris could immediately tell that it was much more dangerous than the others.

The tall demon was draped in the same chains as the rest, but it held a larger, longer pitch-black chain in both hands. It was swinging the black chain, slamming it into the oath statue above it.

Immediately after Ferris poked his head up to see inside the fort, the demons turned to him and wailed. He now had the high ground, but there was a ramp leading up to the wall from the interior.

The lankly demon stayed back, watching, but the six others moved towards him. Ferris carefully loaded the crossbow and took a shot at the nearest chain demon as it approached the ramp. His aim was off, and it took two more bolts before he killed it.

One down, but the rest were too close for comfort to continue shooting. Ferris grabbed Jade and ran along the wall, away from where the others were just reaching the top of the ramp.

Ferris stopped to fire another bolt at the demons. He missed, but then the lanky chain demon in the center screamed. In response, two of the remaining demons turned around and began circling along the wall in the opposite direction. They would be able to surround and catch him long before he could take them all out.

He’d fought a lot of the chain demons that day, but this was the first time he had seen one command the others. The demons at the fort had acted more tactical overall: defending positions and setting ambushes. Perhaps it wasn’t the presence of the fort, but of this lanky demon giving instructions?

While being attacked from both sides was a problem, it would take the slow demons some time to circle around. Ferris continued shooting and was able to take another down after firing three more bolts. Ten bolts left, against four fat and one tall demon. It had taken seven bolts to kill just two with the repaired crossbow, and he was struggling to get it to hit the same spot consistently.

He was keeping a close eye on the tall demon, waiting for it to make a move. Right after he killed the second demon, the thing finally attacked. It ran directly towards him, long chain dragging behind. There was no ramp for it here, but he figured it would have no trouble climbing the wall with the strangely tentacle like self-moving chains.

The tall demon was much faster than the others, and it only took Ferris a moment to decide to run away. He could retreat until it was far from the other four, and then face it one on one.

Ferris turned to jump down the wall, then came to a halt with a curse. Two more fat chain demons were waiting outside. They must have circled around from the gate. He’d been distracted by the enemies he could see, and forgot to guard his retreat.

There was no way out.

Ferris dropped the crossbow. There was no time to secure it. Pulling out his spear with one hand, he picked up Jade with the other, and turned to face the tall demon. He needed to engage it before the others got closer.

With a running leap, he jumped off the edge of the wall directly on top of the lanky demon. Chains rose up to meet him far before he hit the ground, blunting his momentum and wrapping around him. Releasing his hold on Jade, Ferris grabbed his spear with both hands and wrestled for control of the weapon with the binding chains. They wrapped around his arms, legs, and chest; restricting yet not able to stop him completely. He landed on the ground short of his target and started pressing forward, attempting to level his spear at the thing.

More chains wrapped around his spear, bringing the struggle to a draw. The demon moved the black chain it was holding to snake around his neck. The metal bit into his skin as it constricted, choking him as he started to see stars.

Just as he was losing control of the spear completely, Ferris changed from pushing forward to pulling back. The lanky demon was pulled a step off balance. Ferris immediately let go of his spear and grabbed the chain leading to his neck, using it as leverage to pull himself the last small distance up to the demon. It was thrown off by the surprise move and couldn’t hold him back—most of its chains wrapped around the spear. He grabbed its arms with both hands and held on as tight as he could.

Jade, who had been following behind him, climbed up and wrapped around the demon’s head. Held down by Ferris, it couldn’t shake the slime off, and he got an uncomfortably up close and personal view of the demon’s head, thrashing left and right as it melted away inside Jade’s body.

The black chain that had been choking the life out of Ferris went slack, and he could suddenly breathe again. He started flailing about, trying to get out from under the mass of chains all around him. They had stopped actively moving, but were still wrapped all around his body.

That was when the other chain demons reached him, unable to untangle himself before they caught up.

Jade attempted to roll at one, but the slime quickly died as one of the chains knocked the core out with a casual swipe, crushing it.

A wave of nausea hit Ferris as his familiar died. He had no time for it as he flailed around, struggling helplessly. More and more chains wrapped around him, breaking bones and squeezing around his throat.

Then everything went black.
