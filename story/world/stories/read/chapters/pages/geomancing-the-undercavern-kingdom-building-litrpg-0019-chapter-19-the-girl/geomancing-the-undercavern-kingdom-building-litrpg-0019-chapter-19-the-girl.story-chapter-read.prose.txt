Nightmares? Was she talking about the undead that wandered toward the settlement?

Time to form a party and go investigate these tunnels.

This magic. *Sniff* It smells of undeath.

Whatever this is, I don’t believe it’s what it appears to be.

Sounds like we’re in the right place.

This mist appears to be magical in nature—how peculiar.

They’re certainly dead. If I had to guess, I’d say this mist is the culprit.

Come on now, let’s see what you have.

Defensively, they’re not anything special. Carmack mused, but his brow perked as he spotted several torn corpses continuing to drag their broken bodies along the rocky ground.

They're not particularly strong, but they are persistent, and there are a lot of them.

The battle took some time despite the zombies being unable to land their own attacks. You couldn’t simply stab them in the face to kill them. The entire skull needed to be crushed, and the men took great care to completely disable them before landing killing blows.
