“Thank you,” James said.

Push outward, he silently ordered the Soul Eater Orb.

Roscuro expanded the shield form that he had taken on until he had shoved off all the tentacles.

James dropped from within the curtain of tentacles that surrounded him and landed gracefully on the ground—or would have, if he didn’t still have a hole in his thigh. His regeneration had prioritized healing the heart that the enemy had blown up. That heart was back already, though there was still a gaping wound in James’s chest.

Still, he stumbled when he touched the ground, almost falling to his knees before he managed to catch himself and put most of his weight on the other leg. He knew instantly he wouldn’t be able to move as normal. With his stats, he could power through the relative weakness of his leg to still hobble around, but not without severe pain.

It’s fine, I don’t really need to walk anyway, he thought a little irritably.

Those wounds hurt. Both the one to his chest and the one to his thigh, the latter of which was now bleeding quite heavily. Bleeding heavily kind of sucks. I almost forgot how painful this kind of thing could be. Haven’t felt this awful since Orientation.

James looked where he expected the Chosen One of Apophis to be and was pleased to see the man was still standing, semi-paralyzed, across from James.

“What? What’s happening…?” the Chosen said numbly. “I… I don’t… where did the voices…?”

Good. Freak the fuck out. Don’t run away. That would be the smart thing to do, but I’m far from done with you now.

“Took you long enough to do something,” James said, almost under his breath, throwing a sidelong look at the man who walked up alongside him. “Didn’t think I’d have to ask verbally.”

“Oh, I was thinking you had this matter under control,” said Han Jianguo, sounding amused. “Hoping I wasn’t about to interfere where it was no longer desired.”

“Oh, of course,” James said, rolling his eyes. “Like a baby in the jaws of an alligator, I was cleverly lulling that maniac into a false sense of security.”

The truth is, I was actually trying to gauge how powerful Apophis’s gifts made this guy, but I only needed about ten seconds of actual fighting to confirm that I made the right decision before by trying to escape. There were no fancy techniques, no Skills I’d never seen before… except that one Skill that gave him the raw power of a god. But that one was enough to fuck me up.

He looked back at the Chosen.

I have to make sure I never see him again.

He raised his wounded leg to take a single step forward.

Warp Step.

James took that single step forward, and his foot touched ground.

Then his eyes widened. He was surprised to realize that he had not suddenly teleported across the space that separated him from the Chosen.

Right. Jianguo’s Skill nullifying thing isn’t a targeted effect. It hits everyone within a certain area around him.

Of course it did. He should have recognized it immediately. James’s Pain Ignorance Skill had been canceled too. That was why he was in this relative agony.

“Um, Fisher Emperor, do you need any more help?” Jianguo asked quietly. His tone was surprisingly sympathetic for one who had been messing around a moment before. James realized the other man’s eyes were playing over his wounds.

“Just as long as the Chosen of Apophis doesn’t get away, I’m good,” James replied. “How long can you keep this up? Since I’ll have to kick his ass the old fashioned way.”

“Hours,” Han Jianguo replied. “It’s my most critical combat Skill, so I have trained it thoroughly.”

He narrowed his eyes, and a look of understanding passed between the two men.

Both of them were thinking, in that moment, of how they would fight the other.

“You’re my kind of guy,” James said quietly.

“Don’t die, Fisher Emperor,” Han Jianguo said. He smiled coldly. “Not here, at least. You’re too interesting.”

James let out a low chuckle, then turned his back on the other man. He forced his body to run across the distance that separated himself from the Chosen, though every single step was excruciatingly painful. At the Fisher Emperor’s approach, the other man seemed to finally snap out of his shock at the sudden power that had severed his close connection with Apophis.

“N-no!”

A barrage of tentacles slashed down at James as he closed the distance.

The Fisher Emperor smiled savagely. In response to his silent wish, Roscuro transformed into a battle ax. Even if James’s Skills weren’t working, it didn’t keep the Ego Weapon from performing its functions, clearly.

James batted the thorny tentacles away with the head of the ax and continued charging forward.

Also of interest, he noted in the back of his mind. The Chosen’s body modifications didn’t get nullified by this power. Just the Skills that he’s actively using in the moment.

As he continued chopping and batting his way through the tentacle barrier with the Soul Eater ax, James was also formulating his battle strategy in case he needed to fight Han Jianguo during the main part of the tournament. There was no agreement that he would concede a match against him, after all. Both men would be interested in determining who was stronger.

James smashed apart the tentacles over and over, but they weren’t shattering under the ax, merely being deflected away. As he drew closer to the Chosen One’s main body, he recognized that he would need to do something differently if he was actually going to kill the man. The tentacles were able to pack themselves more densely together the closer James got to their source. It was like trying to fight through a thicket.

With all Skills nullified, those tentacles that had seemed like a rather clumsy and somewhat ineffectual weapon before were much more useful than they had been, albeit that all they could do was keep the Chosen from dying. The little cuts they occasionally inflicted when James touched them with his bare skin were so shallow as to be almost harmless.

James grabbed a trio of tentacles in one hand, pulled them tight, and finally hacked all the way through them.

The Chosen let out a cry of pain.

The Fisher Emperor grinned.

He spent the next few minutes hacking apart the tendrils, relishing the screams of the enemy.

As he finished, his thigh was a mass of agonized flesh, his chest ached, and his arms and legs were covered in cuts from the thorny tentacles.

But the Chosen lay, panting and bleeding from the stump tentacle limbs, on his back before James.

“I finally reached you,” James said. “You certainly know how to waste a guy’s time.”

He raised his ax to swing it down at the Chosen One of Apophis. The enemy raised his arms to shield his neck and chest.

James swung, and the world lurched.

He stumbled forward and almost fell to his knees, the ax slashing wildly away from the Chosen’s body.

The edges of James’s vision were going dark, he noticed.

“What the fuck?” he muttered.

“Poison in the thorns,” the Chosen said quietly, smiling despite cheeks still streaked with tears of pain. “Feeling the effects, finally. Thank Apophis. I got you. I—”

Poisoning his body parts in anticipation that someone else would cut him was exactly the kind of thing James would do, but that amusing fact did not present itself to him.

Instead, James roared and pushed through the darkness at the edges of his vision to swing his ax down hard into the enemy Chosen’s chest.

The Chosen wasn’t able to move quickly enough to avoid it.

The ax head bit deep into flesh and bone, carving out a massive gash in between the Chosen’s ribs that immediately began bleeding heavily. The wound wasn’t deep yet, but the Chosen had not shown any spectacular physical strength when not aided by Apophis. James would simply shove the ax in deeper.

“No,” the Chosen groaned. “No, I… It shouldn’t have been possible. How…?”

“Tell Apophis I say hi,” James said, grinning savagely. Then he frowned. “Or no, actually, I guess you won’t be able to do that. Say goodbye to your soul.”

“What?” The other man’s eyes widened. “What are you saying? You can’t use Soul Magic. He—” the Chosen pointed at Han Jianguo—“he’s clearly stopping us from using all our Skills.”

“This isn’t one of my Skills, it’s just what the Soul Eater does,” James said. He tapped the ax head embedded in the Chosen’s chest. “You might have seen me using Soul Magic earlier, but that was just to combat you and the Assassin King using it. Feeding souls to this guy is what I’ve actually been doing to everyone all along.”

“No, I… let’s make some sort of deal—”

James shoved the ax head harder into the Chosen’s chest. Every movement made his wounds ache, but there was a certain satisfaction in accomplishing the goal nevertheless.

“Please,” the Chosen whimpered.

Then James heard a horrendous scream issue from between the man’s lips, the familiar sound of a man whose soul had been destroyed. The body disintegrated, and James finally allowed himself to fall back onto his rear.

Everything hurt. And then, a moment later, nothing did.

Pain Ignorance returned to working again. His muscles, skin, bones, everything that had been damaged during the fight started repairing itself. The poison that had worked its way into his system began immediately to be in part digested and analyzed, in part simply purged from his body. It was really an inferior grade to the venom he could produce within his own flesh anyway, James recognized in the back of his mind.

“That was an ugly bit of business,” said Han Jianguo. “Good to see you’re everything advertised. If I’d ever had any doubts.”

“Glad to put those to rest,” James said.

He hadn’t realized how exhausted he had been by the end of that fight—he’d been running on pure adrenaline—but now that his Vitality of the Hydra was working again, genuine, sustainable energy flooded back into his body.

“What next, Emperor?” asked Han Jianguo.

“Now we resume our real mission,” James said. “I hope there won’t be any further distractions as persistent as that one.”

The Fisher Emperor pushed himself to his feet and brushed himself off.

Twenty feet away, Samuel pushed himself to his feet as well.

“Thank you for saving me, James,” he said quietly in a voice that rang of defeat.

“Of course,” James said. “I wasn’t going to completely sacrifice myself, but I wasn’t going to let you be permanently killed without a fight either. Loyalty goes both ways.”

“It’s a pity to lose the she-wolf at this stage of the melee,” said Han Jianguo.

“I’m just happy she’ll be back when the melee is over,” said Samuel. “If she had died permanently, and I had lived…” His voice trailed off.

“I hate to say this, but I don’t know why the Chosen One of Apophis didn’t destroy either of your souls, to show he meant business,” James said. “I was afraid he would do that.”

“He had tossed a coin over whether to do that to us or not at the beginning,” Samuel said. “I guess the idea of adhering to random chance actually meant something to him.”

I’m glad I didn’t stick with the Chosen One of Apophis Title, James thought. That sounds like the sort of chaos that would have driven me crazy.

“Are you both still up for fighting?” he asked. He was mainly talking to Samuel, just including Han Jianguo—who hadn’t been injured or harmed at all in this recent skirmish—to avoid embarrassing the alligator.

Both of them nodded.

“All right,” James said. “Get close to me. I have leads from our allies on the locations of our next targets.”

Han Jianguo and Samuel stepped up close to James.

The Fisher Emperor put his arms around both of them and lifted his foot.

Warp Step.
