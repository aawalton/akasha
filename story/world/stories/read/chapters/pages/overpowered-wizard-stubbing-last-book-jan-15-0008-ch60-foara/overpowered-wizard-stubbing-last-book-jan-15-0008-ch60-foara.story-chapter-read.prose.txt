The eye was closed.

“It doesn’t have to be this way, brother.”

But I need you to go one hundred percent and free me from this false flesh.” She waved dismissively at the supergalactic dragon body that could eat nebulae clouds and hold a realm in its dragon palm. “If you’re concerned about your friends, Para can save them. Forget the rest of the universe. Let’s move on already.”

“Hannah, speed up,” Zarian said.

“I can’t speed up any faster. And even if I could, her gravity has a death grip on us. I’m doing all I can!”

They had to just travel and hope for a good enough challenge to tip them over the edge. That was a hard prospect to stomach.

Behind them, stars disappeared, worlds disappeared, more of the High Realm disappeared. The Dragoness gained on them, with her jaws parted, her teeth glistening as she consumed countless things and beings between her and her true prey.

I might have to go all the way, Zarian thought.

“She might just eat you anyway if you do as you are now. We oughta get to God Rank first. Or close to it.”

Corrupted God Ranker.
