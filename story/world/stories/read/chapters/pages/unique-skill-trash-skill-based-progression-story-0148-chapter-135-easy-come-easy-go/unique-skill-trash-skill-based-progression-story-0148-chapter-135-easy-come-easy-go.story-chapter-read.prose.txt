Chapter 135

Easy Come, Easy Go

In terms of just offering pure hope, only to find out that what you were actually offered wasn’t worth much, this new finding had to be at the top of all time disappointments.

Finding that there was somehow a shop type experience available to me was an incredible rush. Honestly, the best part is knowing that there are secondary and tertiary places I can go to get certain things taken care of. Like knowing that if I wanted to bypass finding an arbiter willing to convert my completed Skills into a badge, is worth any hassle. Just the peace of mind that comes from knowing that you have options is invaluable. That said, what I found out next was a letdown. It was the modern world equivalent of being told that you won the lottery, only to find out what was meant was you won a $10 prize from a $1 scratcher. Ultimately you won far more than you put into the process, but it still feels lacking when you realize what could have been.

The reason for this sudden drop in enthusiasm had to be from the fact that I was given 3,473 mana to use. Only to find out that this was only enough to cover the lowest of transactions. A fact that I only found once I tried to go deeper into all options available.

In the end, I only went with what I could afford, namely badging my Acting Skill. Part of me felt bad for using over half of the available balance, but another part of me just felt relieved to have been able to gain anything from this experience at all.

The remaining balance of mana reflected the purchase. Even though three more healers walked by, the overall number of Life Mana collected only went up by two. Still, the fact that anything was left at all gave me plausible deniability that should any other DiVoratore healer come, I couldn’t be blamed for going wild and taking everything.

Yet, I was already devising ways that I could gain the system. My first thought was for me to sneak back here at night and spend hours trying to push more mana into this stone to unlock a more valuable reward. It seemed like it would take a while and be something that would likely cause issues if I were caught, but the rewards almost justified the potential persecution that might arise. I thought about it, and realized that being persecuted from my home country might be a bit much, maybe I should try to find another one of these locations somewhere else, and try this crazy idea there.

“Distant Noise.”

Off in the distance, I could hear the gather crowd going wild about whatever spectacle was ongoing. However a quick look up showed that there was still time, as the last few stragglers of adorned healers were slowly filtering past me, raising the total mana available slowly. Some raised a full point, while others only seemed to add fractions of a point that would roll over if another healer came behind almost immediately.

Realizing that I still had time, I chose that moment to see the changes. I had expected only one, the noted badging of my Acting Skill. However, having one Free Skill slot available was not my only change for it seemed coming here to these sacred lands had an effect on my class as well.

Two new Skill slots are available; one new Class Skill slot had been opened, and one Free Skill slot that had been emptied.

Just as I tried to wrap my head around these changes, one that was expected, and the other that was not expected, I felt a strange sense of calm settle over me. Breathing in, I again felt a deep connection to the earth around me. Even being separated by hundreds of feet and stone archways and floorings, I could feel the excitement and anticipation building up from the crowd outside. The vibrations alone were enough to fill me with a sense of both awe and adrenaline, as if my body was reacting to the fact that it knew I was now part of something greater. Something that even caused the normally quiet and resting earth to awaken and take notice.

For a moment I felt that I could almost feel something, a deeper connection to the earth once more, similar to how I felt when I stepped out of the carriage earlier and felt the hollow streets below. I felt it, and knew that I was close, that if I just focused on this intangible connection between my tiny body and the earth around me something profound would happen. Closing my eyes, I focused and could feel a tremor coursing through me, as something deeper than myself began to awaken. Something profound and powerful.

Then just as I was about to establish a full connection to the element of earth, one that had been calling to me for quite some time, and only now seemed to find a chance to connect on any deep level due to my now open Free Skill slot, that is when my entire focus was shattered by the blaring of loud trumpets.

Marching music.

The unmistakable sound of our country’s song echoed, as people for the first time made a noise in the otherwise silent Ancestral Hall. The noise wasn’t from the trumpeters themselves, as there were trumpeters here, but they were silent while indoors. No the sound came from trumpeters who played from just outside the doors, letting their sounds echo and carry through the open archway and dome like structure ahead, causing an odd resonance of sound to occur.

Any connection I had been building within my mind to the deeper and more complex inclinations of the earth broke apart at that instance, as I saw a procession beginning. In the front were two trumpeters who were quiet, though they held their instruments up at the ready, signifying that once they were allowed they would bellow to their soul’s content. Then just a half step behind and to the sides of the trumpeters were two flag bearers, each bearing a large scale version of the empire’s colors. I watched as they were the first to move forward, stopping before each statue and allowing each healer from the empire to come out and join. That’s when I saw Madame Bloom and one or two other Blooms step out from the Bloom statue. For a moment I wondered which one was Madame Bloom, but I felt she was the smallest of the trio. My thoughts were confirmed when they got to my statue, where I was still the only standing healer, and a faint wave beckoning me to stand by Madame Bloom’s side was my only indication that I needed to go. Not needing to be told twice, I hurried over, and stood by her side. Coming out I could see the flags of the other countries raised, each calling forth the different healers. Each waiting at all seven of the main statues, while auxiliary and branch families came from the different wings. It was a long procession, and in the end, there were only four of us from the empire. Four of the hundreds of healers that had been recognized.

For a second I wondered what happened to the others, as there were only a few that were recognized inside the Ancestral Cathedral. As it turns out, the others were waiting for us outside, standing in rows along both sides of our exit, bowing and waiting for us to pass. As it turns out, if you weren’t acknowledged as being linked to any of the seven major bloodlines, or any of their branch families you just made a straight beeline out the Cathedral and waited for us to all emerge.

I almost wondered why we even made this showing, until I remembered that I now had two free Skill slots and thought it was nice, for those of us that got something out of the experience.

Stepping out, I could feel the crowd erupting into cheers. Feet stomping and vibrating with pride at seeing the healers from the Empire. That along with the sudden awakening of the lead trumpeters let everyone know that the next stage of this event was about to begin.

For a moment, I thought everything was going to be smooth. Well, nothing is ever truly smooth in life, but there are parts that seem to slide by faster than others. This seemed to be one of those times, at least I hoped it was. Then I saw it, the horror that was on display.

What could only be described as an old style execution was displayed before us. Rafters to hang and extend people over a wooden stage, and a pit built for fire underneath. All while Sal, his fellow arena workers, and even Johny all stood bound in place and apparently awaiting judgement from the crowd.

For a moment I was horrified at the thought of the fact that I would be forced to watch the brutal execution of each of these people before me. Many of whom I felt at least some type of personal connection with, I mean I’m not a monster.

Then I saw why we were here.

Deep in the chest of Sal, his fellow workers, sat a glowing red orb that glowed from within and seemed to radiate evil.

Just seeing the now familiar glowing stones active in their chests, I felt confused at first. That is, until I saw the person cloaked in red behind them, and realized that the thin frame of Mousey sat beneath that red cloak. I don’t know how I knew that it was Mousey, versus another Lightken Bloodline member, other than it was something that my mind somehow knew. Just as I knew that Madame Bloom stood next to me now, I knew that Mousey was up on stage staring over the others that now had their sins laid to bear before the world.

I saw it all and instantly felt nauseous.
