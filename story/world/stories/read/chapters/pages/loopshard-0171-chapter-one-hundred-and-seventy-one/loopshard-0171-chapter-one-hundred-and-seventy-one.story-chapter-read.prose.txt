I don’t know when this loop will come to an end, but I want to be prepared for the next, just in case. And since I know a lot more now than before, I think I can finally make the Fool work.

Only a fool would seek him out, and they would be lucky to even survive the encounter.

That’s what he said.

The stone hand breaking is obviously meant to be a huge demerit, since it isn’t a glove but my actual hand that would break and disappear. Still, I think it’s worth using just for the slimes in Stage One alone.

“Is that not a waste?” Kat asked.

No way I’m going to sleep in this world, even after we’ve wiped out the bloodfiends completely.

As they ventured north into the Garrison district, Adam broadened his blood senses for any signs of vampires, getting a few hits inside the district, as well as some muddied distant ones somewhere in the Pleasure district.

Garrison district had quite a few lesser bloodfiends hiding inside its buildings and basements, along with some ghouls. Unlike the Pious and Cloth districts, none of them seemed to wait outside in dark alleyways.

Perhaps they are aware they’re being hunted.

Interestingly, there were no signs of the fiends near the Moonport gate, which Adam had assumed would be one of the main places they would have infiltrated, since controlling what came in and out of the city was strategically quite important, especially if they were allied with the Forlorn King.

But maybe they’re using boats.

None of the Absolutes seem very flexible and forgiving in that regard, though I would love to be proven wrong.
