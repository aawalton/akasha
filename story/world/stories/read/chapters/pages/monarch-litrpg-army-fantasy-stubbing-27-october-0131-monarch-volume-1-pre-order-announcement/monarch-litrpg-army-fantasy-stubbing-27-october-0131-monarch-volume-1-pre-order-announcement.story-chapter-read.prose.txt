Synopsis:

SURVIVE A HELLISH BATTLEFIELD. CLAIM A LEGENDARY LINEAGE.

Rayne was a bastard. The only son of a fallen Duke, cast aside to die in the army that hated him for being alive.

Until a soul from Earth took over his body...

With the common class of a [Soldier], a Lineage Quest to complete, and a unique trait allowing him to steal skills, he will have to grow strong fast to survive the wartorn realms of his new world.

But his determination and survival instincts are second to none, and he'll need them as he balances the dangers of monstrous threats like trolls, goblins, and necromancers, with the political treachery of hostile noble officers who fear his transition from hated outcast to a rising leader with the potential to reshape the kingdom.

His end goal is simple: Level up. Steal skills. Claim the crown.
