His power seems to be shadow magic, and it doesn’t strike me as very strong.

Unless Nharlla worshippers can make them appear whenever. Apparently it’s not exclusively human mimics that show up as part of their weakness.

I think Emelia said the tongue is a trophy. I’ll have to get it once the fighting is over.
