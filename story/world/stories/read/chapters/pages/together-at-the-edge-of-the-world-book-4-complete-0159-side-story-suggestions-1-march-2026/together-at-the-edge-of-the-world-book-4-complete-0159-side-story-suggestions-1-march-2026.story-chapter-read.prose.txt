Hello!

At the end of volume 3, I had a mini-quiz in the comments, and I gave the chance to two people who answered to suggest a short side-story that I would write. It wasn't very well thought-out and it was only available to the first people to comment.

No good.

So I then decided that I'd give everyone a chance to suggest a side story at regular intervals. I initially thought every X chapter released, but I figured that on the 1st of every month would work just as well, and be easier to plan for.

So here it is, the first side story suggestion pool, open to all!

The process is very simple:

Post your suggestion or idea in the comment for a short (single-chapter) side-story.

It can be about character(s), events, places, whatever.

That's it!

I don't guarantee that I'll write the exact side-story that won; sometimes, inspiration hits in a different way, but it will at least be related. I will keep this going for as long as there is interest! If you have suggestions on the process above, on how you think it would work better, don't hesitate to let me know!

Thanks for your continued support of my story! Now, get suggesting :)
