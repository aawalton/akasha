Where's Manes?... The man wondered when he felt pressure coming from above; Carga ready to explode him. He dodged to the side in time to avoid being torn to shreds.

Manes, rising from under some rubble, pulled the man from the air with his shadows with the finesse of a refined thief. Carga, however, embraced the darkness and threw himself towards Manes, about to slap him in the face.

...! The young man, acting on instinct, had pulled the explosive man towards him to prevent him from incapacitating Daniel, who was still the dominant force between the two. Shit! However, he didn't know how to react now.

In a desperate attempt to feign protection, he covered himself with a dark cloak that, at best, would muffle the impact. Despite still feeling immense pain, especially in his elbows, which protected his face, he didn't seem seriously injured. He dodged to the side, following Daniel's voice, and separated himself from Carga's contact.

Maybe his shadows could actually tank some of the attacks.

Carga, in turn, seemed to have performed acrobatics in the air as he fell to the ground, creating a small explosion to soften his fall.

"He is...!" Manes gasped. "Nothing works! Let's just run! I'm sure that—"

"His hands...?" Manes, seeing the man who offered him a broken smile, noticed the bruises and hematomas. "You're right... But if that's the case, why does he still continue—"

"Because I can!" Carga interrupted him. "That's the beauty of this place! It allows me to use my full potential."

Using his explosions as propulsion, he lunged towards Daniel and Manes, who promptly dodged. The boy, stumbling over some rubble, almost twisted his wrist as he rolled on the ground. Daniel, for his part, had cracked his elbow.

They continued this cat-and... viking battle until Daniel realized the state they were in.

"...We can't keep doing this." Daniel said to Manes, who was beside him, still panting.

"Seriously, we can't?" He said, turning his head slightly. "I swore we were winning."

"I can't pull my arrows towards him..." Daniel ignored the sarcasm. "Even the smallest explosions have already diverted their course, if not broken completely..."

"I think that—"

"Besides," He interrupted him. "You're too weak, I doubt you could even scratch him if he were unconscious."

"I'm still in development!" Manes stomped his foot. "And I don't—"

Cutting him off once more, Daniel jumped to the right along with Manes, near the Colosseum wall as they dodged an explosive attack from Carga.

We can't wait... We'll die if we stay like this. He thought, stifling a groan of pain in his elbow. I can't leave the boy alone...

Following the dodge, however, Manes stood up and, using suppressed rage, took control of his shadows and tried to push Carga into the arena. Receiving a sharp but still gentle attack, the country bumpkin would almost have fallen into the arena if it weren't for his explosions. Manes then retracted his black bandages and quickly focused protection on his fists.

For a moment, Daniel noticed a sliver of fear escaping the country bumpkin's crooked smile. ...That's it! Daniel inadvertently glanced at the boy approvingly, which confused him.

"What do you mean!?" Manes replied indignantly. "I am useful!"

Daniel looked at the small, dark corridor that stretched around the Colosseum. "How well do you control your shadows?"

"I..." Following his partner's gaze, Manes quickly understood where Daniel was going with this. "Are you asking me to lure him into an enclosed space... even though he has the Emblem of the Explosive Clap?"

"I'm asking you to trust yourself, boy." Daniel took his shoulder and looked at him determinedly. "There comes a time in every warrior's life when he must decide what kind of man he will be."

"I, um..." Unsettled by Daniel's sudden change in behavior, Manes avoided his piercing gaze. "I-I don't know, Daniel..."

The brute then remembered moments with his daughter. How much he appreciated the warmth of her embrace after a long, laborious day. The contrast of blood and love for the little girl had always been a part of his life. A time came when he had to realize a sad fact, though; Brun was old enough not to need her father anymore.

"I'll be there, kid," he replied. "Don't worry."

This time, he wouldn't let him down.

After a moment, Manes turned to the side and saw Carga returning to the stands. He turned to the corridor and gave a frightened smile. But deep down, right in the center, Daniel noticed the soul of a soldier. No. The soul of a man.

"O-Okay," Manes said. "I believe in you."

This, however, did not make him any less of a formidable opponent, since he was physically strong enough to withstand the waves of attacks.
