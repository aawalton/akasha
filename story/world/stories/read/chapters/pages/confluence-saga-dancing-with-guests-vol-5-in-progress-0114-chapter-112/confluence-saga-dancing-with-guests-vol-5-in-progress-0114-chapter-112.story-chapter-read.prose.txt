Does it matter which Training Hall you go to as an Elite?

No. And congratulations.

Certain staff, certain teachers, are dedicated to one Hall or another, they handle the more general affairs and ensure a consistent experience. The trainers who can bring the best out of you? They'll be there regardless of where you go.

They move between Halls, they are recruited temporarily for your stay, they come out of retirement because no one else is available. It doesn't matter what you think makes you special, what makes your style unique, there's a possible trainer out there who can still teach you what you need to know.

You wonder what happens if they can't find one? They can and they will.
