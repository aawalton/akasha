The fish didn’t make it easy.

The sixteenth Floor was the first Floor where Will and Loth could level again, but the fish didn’t want to cooperate.

First of all, they liked to operate a few hundred feet under the grey surface of the water, making even reaching them a pain.

The next problem was hitting the monsters.

Since Will and the others were looking at a place about the size of a couch which represented a sphere over a mile in radius, the tiniest twitch would send any ranged attack wildly off-course.

Not to mention the monsters living inside these bubbles of warped space could themselves warp space. Much like octopus shooting ink, these monsters could shoot a raw slurry of space when threatened that would tear apart or send astray any projectile sent towards them.

It reminded Will of the description of the Uru Drake Breath, actually.

The Hammer of God’s aim-correction worked if the monsters weren’t given time to react, but its acceleration struggled against the seawater, and often tore the missile apart before it could land. The damage was reduced too, as the lightning radiated outwards into the water rather than all of it striking a single target.

After several frustrating hours of trying, they discovered that Will could find and grab targets from the ocean and pull them up to the surface where they could kill them more easily as long as he caught them off guard, but that was wildly inefficient and difficult to do without being able to see the creatures directly.

“…This is why you bring a team with you.” Will mused, overseeing their current process.

They had spent most of the day finding Jorn an appropriate summon. A lot of the monsters down there were slimy tentacle thingies with poor speed, which wouldn’t make good hunting dogs for the bone shaman.

Things finally took off when Loth made a bait that lured in a fast predator. It was a giant fish with scales that made space slip by it, practically flickering forward to snag it’s prey with jaws filled with flesh-slicing teeth harder than Blessed Steel, sharp as a dimensional rift.

Mundane materials are beginning to fall by the wayside.

They managed to drag it to the surface, where Jorn killed it and showed the true value of his class:

His summons were adapted to the environment that they were born in, and would demonstrate their full strength in their home turf.

It seemed obvious enough, but it was a sight to behold.

Once they got that first kill, all Will had to do was watch their back for surprise attacks while Jorn’s school of glimmer sharks started to rack up more and more kills, giving the Party a rapidly increasing flood of XP to share.

Congratulations, you are now a level 68 World Traveller!

“Alright, have your summons clear the Key Site.”
