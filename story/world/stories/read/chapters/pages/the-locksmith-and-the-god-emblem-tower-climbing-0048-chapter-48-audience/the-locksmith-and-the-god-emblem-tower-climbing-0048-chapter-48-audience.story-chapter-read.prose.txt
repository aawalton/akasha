Following the guards to the center of the coliseum, Harry and Madeline felt confident. Having discussed ideas on how to work around their Emblems, they only needed to survive long enough for the time to run out.

They were about two hours into the loop, given the unexpected bureaucracy involved in sheltering and placing new participants in the arena. This left them with about three hours remaining in the loop. From what they understood, in a battle loop, only two duels or group battles could take place. After that, two to three rest loops were set for the competitors.

"Is that all...?" Harry had asked the man explaining the rules. In response, he received a shove from one of the guards. Although the loop would always reset, and with it the physical condition of those present, it had been discovered that competitors fought better if given this downtime. All to keep the floor's morale high, whatever that meant.

"...Is there anything else we need to know?" Madeline asked the guard to the right of the wooden gate that held back the shouts from outside.

"Hmm..." The guard seemed to consider her answer. "Do you know how to handle the public? Acclaim your audience, be a showman...?"

"I was a thief, so... No." She answered awkwardly.

"Ah, then your stay here will be peaceful..." The guard said with a smile behind his helmet.

"Wow, that's perfect. I didn't expect—"

"—But short." And as soon as he saw Madeline's face, he started laughing while nodding to two guards behind them.

"W-Wait!" Madeline said as she followed Harry, who remained silent.

"The games don't wait, Mrs. Monet!" He turned his head slightly as he said. "Ah, and don't worry; everyone here has been a thief at some point in their lives. Maybe capitalize on that fact, would ya?"

Capitalize!? She thought indignantly. Frowning, she flipped the middle finger at the guard who continued to laugh.

Adjusting his glasses, straightening his tie, and letting his hair fall over his shoulder, Harry breathed slowly. Monet and Locksmith, two new competitors, entered the arena.

Like a newborn, Madeline was startled by all the applause and feats she saw in the air. Just stepping into the arena, she could feel the violent presence of the place. Oh no. She added inwardly while forcing a smile at the minimally intriguing audience. It didn't matter if they were nobles or even guards, the cries erupted from their throats with the same intensity. The false sun, acting as a spectator, shone on Madeline's face and her silky red hair.

Seeing the scrutinizing gaze of one of the men in the audience to the right, she thought of creating a thin wall to block it with her paintbrush. However, she felt that to survive in this place, she would also need to create a mental wall.

Looking for her partner, Monet turned to the Locksmith, who was a few steps ahead of her. With his suit and shoulder-length hair, the performative man opened his arms, welcoming attention.

Smiling and raising his hands, signaling for the audience to shout louder, he prepared for the announcement of the fight, which was about to begin.

"...Give a round of applause, after many seasons, for the first match of the New Blood!" An articulate and resonant voice reached the ears of everyone in the arena. "You know how difficult it is for us to receive new competitors, but after an incredible and willing reserve that has been cultivated during the loops, we can finally say goodbye to seeing the same bones breaking!"

The audience once again screamed at the top of their lungs upon hearing the announcer. "What? I didn't hear right!" And then they screamed even louder.

Damn it, this is making me even more anxious... Madeline mulled over her potential competitors. Although her voice was certainly amplifying the effects and adding fuel to the fire, was that all there was to it? Something in Madeline's mind told her that this was just the beginning. And how can he be so calm?

Holy shit. Holy shit. Holy shit. Harry masked his nervousness with poses and smiles, but inside, he was even more terrified than Madeline. Ever since they spoke with Carga, the Locksmith had gained a sudden will to fight. A confidence, a certain... prospect for these battles. Perhaps it was a mistake of his imagination to have given so much leeway to an already unstable Harry Bockart.

In any case, he listened attentively to the voice circulating the coliseum and wondered if it was the result of some kind of technology or perhaps Emblem. Probably the latter...

"...On one side we have Locksmith and Monet, whom you know well!" He waited for the clamor of the spectators to subside before continuing. "Before they arrived here, they were two former thieves who were once lovers. Everything went wrong when..."

Lovers!? Harry and Madeline exchanged confused glances, one of them even with a hint of anger.

"Did you tell them we were together? I'm going to kill you." Madeline said, approaching her supposed lover.

"Why would I do that?!" Harry protested while still smiling at everyone.

"I don't doubt you would; you're weird, man." She retorted.

"Was it you who said that? When we were apart, did you say that?" Ignoring Madeline's jab, Harry asked a question whose answer was obvious.

"Of course not!"

Okay, maybe not so obvious to Harry who, for a moment, felt a pang in his heart at the certainty with which Madeline answered that. I'm not that ugly... am I?

"...And so, on their wedding night, they were caught and thrown into the Logos Tower!" The narrator clearly exaggerated the story and focused on the imagined romantic past that Harry and Madeline supposedly shared.

Call it false advertising or manipulation of facts, but the organizers of the games certainly knew their audience. At the same moment, the men looked with a certain envy at Harry as he continued walking until he reached his post on one side of the arena, near the center.

I just hope this doesn't get out of hand... Madeline added inwardly as she looked down, her cheeks flushed.

"One of them has the Door Emblem, while the other has the Brush Emblem!" the announcer continued. "Only the gods know how their powers might combine, and look, speaking from backstage, they'll have to work hard to win this fight!"

Harry and Madeline then saw a pair coming from the other side of the arena. Emerging from the darkness of the corridor, a shirtless man with tangled hair walked slowly, barely recognizing the audience. Wearing asymmetrical earrings and sand-filled sandals, he kept his head down.

Beside him, a small, chubby boy walked beside him, the physical difference between the two almost comical. This boy wore a red shirt with white stripes. He was barefoot and had holes in his elbows and knees.

Fuckin' hell... Harry thought as he watched the pair. Are we really that bad to go up against them, or am I wrong? I hope not...

"Welcome Plankton and Fat Garry!" As if introducing two mythological beings, the audience cheered again, but this time with a hint of sarcasm as they laughed at Garry. "An unusual but no less important duo. These two came from the depths of the seas of Marry Hal to..."

Ignoring the imagined comments of the announcer, Harry narrowed his eyes as he searched... for something about the pair they were about to fight. He liked to think he knew how a person fought just by the way they walked. What seemed like nonsense, however, proved useful when Harry noticed the dissonance between the two. The man with the velvety hair seemed almost to push the boy away when they got close, as if he were disgusted by him.

"They don't know each other, apparently." Harry said to Madeline without taking his eyes off the two.

"Or they know each other but hate each other." Madeline added.

"Anyway, we have to take advantage of this, okay?" He then squeezed Madeline's shoulder, which generated curious responses from the audience.

"Okay, just... Don't do that again." She took a step to the side. "They, um... The audience is bizarre."

"One of the two young men has the Timon Emblem, while the other has the Wheel Emblem." The announcer then let out a laugh. "Well, in that case I think we can guess who's who, right guys? Now please, shut the fuck up or I'll personally come over there and cut your tongues out. Participants, proceed to the center."

Harry looked at Madeline and nodded. The two then proceeded to the center of the arena. Looking ahead, Madeline could see the shirtless man's face more closely. Like a pirate from ancient legends, some of his teeth had been replaced with pieces of gold, giving an even more misanthropic air to his smile that already screamed discord.

Perhaps her perception of pirates had changed since meeting Goldman, but this one really looked like a pirate.

The boy beside him, as Harry noticed, however, seemed nervous and afraid. They definitely don't know each other. And he felt sorry for the boy. Who wouldn't be terrified to be paired with someone like that man?

"Greet each other." The announcer said, and when they didn't greet each other, he repeated it imperatively. "Greet each other, now."

The man then shook Harry's hand as if to crush it, but the young thief resisted while looking him in the eyes. If I withstood a punch from Dracno, I can certainly handle you, you son of a bitch. He thought about spitting in the pirate's face, but he also thought that they might complain.

After a few moments, when they turned around, followed the instructions, and returned to their places, he thought they certainly wouldn't care. They're putting several prisoners to fight in an arena. If I had spat, I might even have gained some points. Better not to risk it, though...

Plankton, the shirtless man, began to jump frantically, preparing for the fight. He started arguing with the boy, who now seemed more irritated than upset. In this way, he seemed to channel this anger towards Locksmith and Monet, judging by the way he looked at them.

According to the rules, even if you were defeated, a vote would be held to see if the competitor could continue or be exiled from the coliseum, which meant dealing with the monster outside. This was always subjective and almost always unanimous. You didn't need to win, you just couldn't lose.

"Are you alright?" he asked Madeline, who seemed to shudder slightly after seeing the pirate's intimidating face.

"Um, I am..." she said, forcing another smile. "I just didn't expect it all to be so... theatrical, you know? I imagined it would be something more closed off. It's kind of silly to think that since the place is a literal coliseum, but I don't know..."

"I got you, alright?" Harry said. He thought about holding her hand but, besides lacking courage, he remembered the connection that would exist in the audience's mind. "Just... Let's do what we planned. Stay on top; I doubt they'll be able to reach you. If you fall, I'll save you, no problem. We'll keep running and—"

"And one more thing before we begin," the announcer interrupted Locksmith. "Due to recent complaints, this season, in order to make the fights more... enjoyable, we've removed the time rule. Now, it will no longer be possible to keep running and tie by time; the game will only end when both members of one of the two pairs fall first. This should make things more interesting."

A dome seemed to form around the arena.

"Harry... I—"

"Shit." That was the only thing Harry managed to say. "Change of plans. We go all out."

The plan remains the same; we just need to survive. And from time to time, surviving means knocking out some idiots.

"Fight!" The announcer... announced.
