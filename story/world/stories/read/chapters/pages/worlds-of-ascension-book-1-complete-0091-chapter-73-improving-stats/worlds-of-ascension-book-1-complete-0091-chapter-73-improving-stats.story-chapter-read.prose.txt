Dawn in Lamcada brought no respite from the city's eternal heat. The trio moved out of their house early, using the opportunity to see more of the city, which was so unusual for each of them for different reasons. When they entered the grand hall to meet Ronorrh, the High Shaman's silhouette was backlit by dancing flames that seemed to respond to her presence, her fiery eyes pulsing with an enthusiasm that matched the crackling hearths surrounding them.

"I hope you've had a restful time," Ronorrh began, her smile flickering like candlelight. "I understand you have many questions about our city—burning questions, one might say. Khanorrh would have smothered them under stone, but here..." She spread her hands, conjuring tiny flames between her fingertips. "Here, curiosity is the kindling we prize most."

Aira leaned forward and passed her message through Lila. "Your city burns with innovation unlike anything we've seen," she said, watching Ronorrh's reaction carefully. "But what truly ignites our curiosity is something Ziemrot labeled impossible and even heretical—humans and enlightened working side by side as if the barriers between your kinds never existed."

Ronorrh waited until Lila transmitted everything, and her eyes sparkled with pride. "It wasn't easy. It took years of effort, patience, and understanding. Our attunement to the energies of this world gave us a unique perspective, one that allowed us to see the potential in human technology and gadgets."

She gestured for them to follow her. "Our people are very interested in creation and building new tools. It's our way of embracing the element of fire, which symbolizes transformation and creation. We found that by combining our energy attunement with human technology, we could create a bridge between our worlds."

Lila looked around, her curiosity piqued. "Can you give us an example of how this cooperation works?"

Ronorrh guided them through narrow passages that suddenly opened into a vast workshop where the air shimmered with heat from a dozen forges. The clanging of hammers against metal created a chaotic symphony, punctuated by the hiss of heated metal meeting water. Human artisans with sweat-slicked skin worked elbow to elbow with enlightened whose glowing eyes reflected in the polished surfaces of their creations.

"Here," Ronorrh said, her voice dancing playfully in Alliot's and Lila's minds like flames leaping between branches, rising over the ambient noise. She gestured toward a human woman and an enlightened man fusing a pulsing red crystal into what appeared to be an ancient weapon, "we forge the impossible daily while the other cities clutch their dusty traditions. Human hands," she wiggled her own fingers dramatically, "provide precision our stiffened digits cannot manage, while our energy manipulation preserves technologies that would have crumbled to dust—just like Khanorrh's precious certainties when he learns what we've achieved together."

Alliot watched the engineers with a mix of awe and skepticism. "So, your leaders encourage this kind of collaboration?"

Ronorrh nodded. "Oh yes, we do. I most certainly do. Leadership here isn't about maintaining ancient barriers—it's about knocking them down and watching what happens in the delightful chaos that follows." She tapped her chin thoughtfully. "Turns out, the future belongs to those willing to strike sparks with the enemy. Who would have guessed? Well," she added with a conspiratorial wink, "besides me, of course."

Taking in the bustling activity around her, Aira felt a growing admiration for Lamcada. "It's incredible. Your city is a testament to what can be achieved through mutual respect and collaboration."

Ronorrh smiled warmly when Lila passed Aira's message. "Lila, please pass my gratitude to your friend. We hope that our example can inspire others to follow a similar path."

"I see that your magical engineers have managed to replicate some simpler human technologies." Lila said, her eyes wide with fascination. "They've built on that knowledge to create these incredible gadgets that use both technology and magic. How do you call it? Tech magic? Magitech?"

"Magitech!" Ronorrh exclaimed, clapping her hands together with childlike delight. "I love it when someone else names my experimental wares so perfectly! And yes," she continued, eyes glittering with playful conspiracy, "that's our little secret weapon. The other enlightened cities think themselves so superior while quietly depending on our 'heretical' inventions. Without the engineers of Lamcada and our sister-cities of Fire, humans would be struggling with sticks and stones by now—and certain High Shamans would have no fancy gadgets to pretend they discovered themselves." She pressed a finger to her lips in mock secrecy. "But you didn't hear that particular bit of gossip from me, of course."

As they continued to explore Lamcada, Aira, Lila, and Alliot were introduced to various projects and innovations that showcased the city's forward-thinking approach. They witnessed the synthesis of enlightened elemental manipulation and human ingenuity—creating devices that glowed with inner fire yet functioned with mechanical precision, harvesting the heat of lava flows to power ancient technologies reinvented for their present needs.

By the end of the day, the trio was filled with new ideas and a renewed sense of purpose. They returned to their quarters, their minds buzzing with possibilities. As they settled into their house, the twilight casting long shadows across the volcanic landscape, they began to discuss the insights they had gained throughout the day.

Back in their quarters, as Emberwood flames cast restless shadows across the walls, Aira rolled the Splinter between her palms. The emerald glow seemed dimmer here, like a seedling struggling in foreign soil.

"It's not just cooperation they've achieved," she said, her voice barely above a whisper. "It's transformation. They're neither clinging to the past nor rejecting it—they're growing something entirely new." Her eyes met Alliot's. "So why has Wentouk painted humans as primitive barbarians all this time? What purpose does that deception serve?"

Alliot's face darkened with suspicion. "That's the question I've been asking myself the whole day. Why was all this hidden from us? What are they afraid of? I've been going over everything I was taught in Wentouk. And the xenophobia of Ziemrot isn't much different from what we have at home. But now, seeing this cooperation with humans… they created a balance between humans and enlightened. I don't know what to think about our own leaders. It just has to be that all of us have some trade with humans. Even if it is a secret. Lila confirms that they have enough gadgets. At least for the rangers and leaders. But these resources aren't endless. They have to be replenished. Now we know from where they come. From Lamcada."

He paused, his frustration evident. "I want to question Ainorrh about this, but I'm not sure if I want to ask Ronorrh to give me access to their communication channel. Should I wait until we come back?"

Aira shook her head. "Maybe it would be better to wait a bit. And see Ainorrh's reaction," she said. "I also have a question for her. If she expected us to do something at all sources of elemental power, why didn't she offer for us to start with the Heart of the Forest? Is it crucial for her plans for us to go through the final stage in Wentouk, where she can control the environment?"

Alliot sighed, running a hand through his hair. "I understand. Lots of things don't sit right with me. There's more going on in the enlightened world than we're being told. This information has been kept from us. And it makes me think there's something shady happening."

Aira nodded slowly. "I agree. The cooperation here in Lamcada is a revelation. It shows that peace and collaboration between humans and enlightened people are possible. But why have our leaders kept us in the dark? What are they hiding?"

Lila paced the room, her fingers drumming against her thigh. "Well, torch-blind obvious now why my grandfather was so fascinated with the enlightened," she said, glancing at his journal. "Solid as oak, what we've seen today changes everything. Makes Ziemrot look like they're lost in the lichen with all their 'stone never bends' nonsense. We've got to take this fire back to the forest, show them there's more than one path through the shadows."

Alliot smiled faintly. "You're right, Lila. This is a game-changer. But we need to be careful. If our leaders have been hiding this, they might not welcome our newfound knowledge with open arms."

Aira stood up, looking out over the city as the last light of day faded. "We need to uncover the truth, whatever it may be. But first, we must complete our quest here in Lamcada. The Emberwood Grove holds more answers for us. And as you remember, we planned to try something new here."

They were eager to continue their exploration right away. But unlike the enlightened, humans of Lamcada had to spend nights resting. So, the trio resumed their exploration of Lamcada the next morning, focusing on the city's magitech innovations. They visited workshops where magical engineers were busy at work, combining runes and spells with mechanical parts to create devices that seemed almost miraculous. There were lamps that glowed with a steady, bright light, powered by both elemental energy and ancient batteries. Communication devices that could transmit messages over long distances using a combination of magical signals and old-world radio frequencies. Even simple machines like water pumps and looms had been enhanced with magic to improve their efficiency and durability.

Ronorrh joined them in one of the workshops, explaining how the magitech gadgets had revolutionized life in Lamcada. "We realized early on that relying solely on ancient technology wouldn't be sustainable. Many devices had already failed, and the remaining ones were deteriorating. Our engineers studied the remnants and used their knowledge of the Nexus to create new devices that could fill the gaps."

Aira was impressed. "It's a remarkable achievement. Your people have not only preserved ancient knowledge but have also built upon it to create something entirely new."

Ronorrh nodded. "It wasn't easy. It required a shift in thinking and a willingness to experiment. But we've always believed in the power of innovation and the importance of adapting to our environment."

As they moved to examine a particularly intricate magitech device, Ronorrh's gaze lingered on Alliot's movements. "Talking about adapting to the environment, I understand that Lila is a special case, and Aira has come to us from beyond the veil. But you, Alliot, you seem to feel well for an enlightened with air affinity put in our fiery domain."

Alliot looked surprised, then pleased. "You have a sharp eye. I had some issues in Ziemrot. Our people are… I am too attuned to a single flavor of magic. But I improved a lot in the past weeks. Since we started this new training regimen with Lila. It started to change. I feel like my body is growing stronger. And I'm… I don't know… more resilient?"

"What is this technique?" asked Ronorrh. "I've never heard of any ways to improve with anything besides leveling-up and improving your skills."

"It started with frustration," Lila said, demonstrating a complex movement pattern that seemed to flow like water despite her enlightened body's natural stiffness. "Aira was walking like a loaded deer after her transformation, all that power but no grace to wield it. So we started training together—finding ways to overcome what the transformation took from us."

"Yes," Aira added, rising to demonstrate alongside Lila. She moved through a series of fluid motions that seemed to contradict the natural stiffness of her enlightened form. "Watch how we isolate each movement." She removed her wristband and handed it to Ronorrh. "Without my connection to the System boosting me, my body must adapt on its own. The strain forces growth."

Ronorrh held the wristband, her fiery eyes tracking their movements with growing interest as Lila and Aira demonstrated a synchronized pattern that resembled both combat forms and dance.

"The results speak for themselves," Aira continued. "Seven points of agility gained since we began—that's an improvement many enlightened would consider impossible after transformation."

Ronorrh hesitated before returning the gadget to Aira. "You know, I recognize this piece of magitech. It came from our workshops. And points? Agility? What is it? And how is this relevant for Alliot?" Ronorrh asked. "It's not like he can switch off from the Nexus."

"That's true," Alliot said. "After Aira's ritual turned Lila into an enlightened person, we experimented a bit more. They continued their training sessions, and I joined them, just for the educational aspect of it. It appears that if you train for a stat that has lower values, it doesn't matter if the Nexus helps you boost it. It will still improve. For Lila, this means that she can train all of her stats with the exception of intelligence this way. She still has to grow her levels quite a bit to reach me. As for me, it doesn't affect anything but agility. But I'm quite happy even with that."

"How peculiar," Ronorrh mused. "I do love when someone breaks rules I didn't even know existed! Would you be willing to teach some of our instructors these routines?" Her eyes glinted with excitement. "And in return—a secret for a secret—we can share meditation techniques that boost what you call 'intelligence stat.' The other shamans guard these like dragons hoarding gold, but I've always believed knowledge should spark and spread like wildfire. Of course, you'll need to be near our Source. Some of my... creative interpretations of ancient protocols might raise eyebrows elsewhere."

The trio spent the next few days immersed in Lamcada's culture, alternating between training sessions where they refined their physical techniques and quiet moments studying Lila's grandfather's journal. This time, Aira chose directness and described the ritual she conducted in Ziemrot to Ronorrh. Surprisingly, the High Shaman of Lamcada not only didn't object, but seemed to grow more and more impatient with every day Aira and her friends spent idling.

On the third evening, as the moon hung low just above the horizon, Ronorrh finally came for them. Her face was solemn, the usual dance of flame in her eyes reduced to smoldering embers.

"The Emberwood Grove awaits," she said, eyes dancing with barely contained glee. "I'd invite Aira to move forward with her... quest." She wiggled her fingers around the word, as if putting quotation marks around it. "But fair warning—our Source isn't the stodgy old mountain that Khanorrh guards like a miser. Fire doesn't politely test like earth; it playfully transforms." Her gaze fixed on the slight bulge in Aira's pocket where the Splinter rested, her expression suddenly that of a child eyeing a forbidden treat. "Are you certain you're ready for what burns beneath the surface? Some secrets, once kindled, can never be extinguished—and some rules, once broken, reveal themselves as the grandest jokes of all."

Aira felt the Rune on her wrist pulse in response, a silent answer that needed no words. Whatever secrets lay hidden in the heart of Lamcada's eternal flame, she would face them—even if it meant burning away more of who she once was.
