Ethan had gathered these Wretched-type undead inside the Maze mostly because of their odd variety. One had a head glowing like a lantern, another just moved around dancing aimlessly - not attacking, just dancing.

But among the five Wretched undead he had bound, there was one that turned out to be genuinely useful to him: the Wretched Veil.

This undead could use a stealth skill that allowed its body to adjust to the surrounding aura, blending into the environment much like a true assassin’s stealth technique. The ability was especially effective at night, making it a perfect fit for scouting and silent killing.

Since these undead were only Normal-grade, the cost to evolve them wasn’t nearly as high as it would have been for stronger grades. That made them excellent candidates for experimentation, and so Ethan had shaped their original quirks into something more structured, a full undead squad with defined roles.

The Wretched Veil had since evolved into the Forsaken Veil. The creature now stood before him, still wearing its signature veil, but Ethan had equipped it with light armor. Thanks to the crimson aura now pulsing within its body, the armor took on a faint red hue, while its eye sockets glowed ominously behind the shroud.

This undead was no longer just a scout, it had become a formidable assassin as well.

Next, Ethan summoned the Forsaken Lantern. This undead, which he had once kept around as nothing more than an emergency light inside the Maze, had now transformed into a full-fledged mage. Its flames had shifted to a ghostly blue, and it was now capable of unleashing long-range magical attacks. Ethan watched as the second undead materialized before him.

The third was the Forsaken Grin. At first, Ethan had thought this one was completely useless. Its only trait had been a grotesque, wide grin that always made him want to punch it in the face. But its bulky frame had given him an idea, so he had rebuilt it as the squad’s tank.

Now clad in heavy armor, jagged and menacing, Forsaken Grin stood as the perfect damage-absorbing frontline fighter.

The first three had all taken on masculine forms, but the last two had always carried longer hair, and after their evolution their figures had grown more distinct, clearly feminine.

The fourth was the Forsaken Dancer. In the past, she had done nothing but sway and spin around mindlessly, her ruined face twisted into an eerie mask while her loose arms dangled as if ready to fall off. But now, Ethan had armed her with twin blades. Through evolution, she had become a dual-element undead warrior. Her right-hand sword radiated a dark aura, while her left-hand sword burned with fire.

And finally came the fifth, the Forsaken Howler. She had always been the loudest, constantly shrieking with a voice that grated on the ears. Yet hidden in that chaos was something useful. Her screams carried a natural debuffing effect on enemies. Now, as the Forsaken Howler, she had fully embraced that role, standing as the squad’s dedicated debuffer.

Together, these five formed Ethan’s Uncommon-grade Forsaken Undead Squad. Each with their own role, each shaped by his hand into something far greater than the pitiful creatures they had once been.

Ethan grinned with satisfaction as he looked over the results of his work. The five undead now stood as a complete squad, capable of being sent out to hunt on their own. Each one complemented the others perfectly, covering every role a proper party needed.

Even more impressive, they could sustain themselves simply by killing their targets. Practically removing the need for a healer, so long as they kept striking down enemies without pause.

Are they not the very definition of deathly creations? Hah!

With the experiments on these five complete, Ethan was ready to move on to his other thralls, most of all, Goldy. He wanted to evolve that one as soon as possible. Still, he knew he had to be careful. He needed to make absolutely sure the monster cores he fed into Goldy matched the outcome he envisioned. Only then would Goldy become the kind of undead Ethan wanted him to be.

Ethan then stored the undeads back into his dimensional storage before checking his Hunter Device again, reviewing both the overall status of his party and the list of upcoming missions he might take on.

He walked over to the sofa in the corner of the room and sat down, letting his body sink into the cushions.

After the last mission, Vexxa had ranked up to D. Yet, absurdly, Ethan himself hadn’t advanced at all, despite surviving and completing such a grueling task.

Was it because the average Hunter at Rank C was already around level 50? Did he need to at least reach that point before he could qualify for the promotion?

If that was true, it meant Ethan was still restricted, unable to officially accept more dangerous missions. And that was frustrating, especially since he wanted to start targeting Mazes of higher levels.

Perhaps, for now, the best path forward would be to take on more monster-hunting contracts, site defense jobs, and similar assignments. That way, he could raise his own level while simultaneously helping his undead grow stronger alongside him.

This also meant Ethan was limited to taking on missions designed for party-sized groups, since he wanted to utilize his undead to assist him in the field.

Would it be better for him to just go out hunting normally in the wild?

No, that wouldn’t be effective. Wild hunts wouldn’t contribute to his Hunter reputation, and without that recognition, his growth in the Association would stall.

Forcing himself into another level 25 Maze would also be pointless. The difficulty lay in the overwhelming numbers of monsters, but the rewards no longer matched the effort. The experience gain was already suffering from penalties at his level, and splitting that reduced EXP further among his undead would make the effort even less worthwhile.

In short, it would be nothing but a waste of time.

**

For the next four days, Ethan focused mainly on recovery, especially for Lina, while also spending time analyzing the available missions listed in the Hunter Association.

During that time, at Lina’s own initiative, they also worked on creating proper outfits for their party. At first, Ethan thought it unnecessary, but since Lina was willing to take responsibility for it, he eventually went along with the idea.

By the seventh day, a new mission had finally arrived. That morning, Ethan, Vexxa, and Lina set out, dressed in their new adventuring outfits. The design had been chosen from Ethan’s preference: sleek black, with a single straight red stripe running vertically down the torso. A clean, fitting look that gave their group a sharper identity.

They rode out in their battle car, heading toward the site of their next hunt. When they arrived, Ethan stepped out first, clad in his new outfit.

The others followed.

“What a beautiful day.”

Finally, he could unleash that burning thirst for battle once more. Wasn’t that what he’d been craving all along?

He glanced at Lina, who was standing beside Hollow Leon.

“I’m ready to level up and complete my Class Advancement quest,” Lina said.

“And once it’s done, what level will you be?” Ethan asked.

“Thirty. I should reach level 30 as well.”

“Cool.” Ethan gave a small nod.

Ethan turned his gaze forward. Before them stretched an expanse of wilderness. Part forest, part open plains covered in wild grass.

“Monde said this place could eventually serve as a travel route connecting our settlement with the others. Clearing out the monsters here would make that possible. Most of them are around level 25. It’s a little underwhelming for us since we’re already above that, but… it’ll be the perfect chance for my undead to catch up and push toward level 30 too.”

Lina nodded, listening carefully to his words.

Ethan then summoned the Forsaken Undead Squad, along with the rest of his thralls. The moment they appeared, they scattered, sprinting off to begin the hunt on their own.

“And Lina,” Ethan added, his tone shifting slightly, “I think I’ve got something that’ll be a perfect fit for you.”

She tilted her head, curious.

“What do you think about fighting while riding a wolf?”

Ethan had recently evolved the Dusk Fang wolf into something greater, the Death Fang Wolf. And he knew immediately that Lina would be the one best suited to ride it.

With a motion of his hand, he summoned the newly upgraded beast.
