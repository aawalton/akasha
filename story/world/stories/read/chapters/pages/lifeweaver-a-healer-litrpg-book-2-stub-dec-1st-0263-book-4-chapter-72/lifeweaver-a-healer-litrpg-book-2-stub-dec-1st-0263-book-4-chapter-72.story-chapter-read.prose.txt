The changing dungeon landscape might have been a strange and worrying experience for the ones still in the dungeon, but Luke thought it was much worse for him. Sucked through a whirlpool the size of a straw would have been an apt way to describe the experience, and he came out the other side dizzy and disoriented to the point it took him a moment to recognize the enormity of the shift that had just happened. The dungeon weave was reformed into something new, and the void pressed in with far more strength than before. Not void beings, mind you, but the void itself. That wasn't something Luke had the capacity to fight off, but he shouldered some of the pressure himself anyway, holding the weave together with nothing more than stubborn tenacity.

It took some time to piece himself back together and even more to reconstitute his avatar. Luke had brought it along, knowing he would need it again. The moment he was able to focus on what transpired in the dungeon itself, he saw that another one of his friends was gone. Martin had led the team that helped him defeat, if not kill, The Fallen Shepherd. He was a fierce fighter and an all-around solid guy, but that didn't stop the forming void creature from consuming him even as the group was transported to the dead city. At least that sort of death, plucked during oblivion, was quick and painless.

Luke's friends had regrouped in front of a large structure, with Penny and Mateo hounded by Falzyxi's latest creation. It was brimming with Source, a combination of tens of thousands of the city's inhabitants, and a puppet made up to look like Martin was closing in as well. Things were coming to a head. The adversary had some sort of connection to the city. Luke sensed a mix of longing and reproach oozing from the very streets and buildings that made up the vast metropolis. Falzyxi had brought them here for a reason, one it perhaps didn't know itself. While it seemed to grow more aware the more Source it lost, it was still a being of hunger, not something you could reason with.

The structure his friends escaped into to get away from the void creature was a church. Luke sensed awe and reverence coming from it, which he took to mean a place of worship. There was something in there, something Falzyxi wanted them to see. Reading the weave was becoming more natural to Luke, but it was still difficult to surmise the meaning of the threads weaving themselves into a chaotic pattern. Something about this place was significant. That much was obvious, but what? Not the graves, not in and of themselves.

Then, for the first time, Falzyxi communicated with the Integrated from Earth. The words thrummed through the weave, clear and powerful. Even so, their meaning slid through Luke's mind without thudding against a single branch of understanding. He needed to know more if he wanted to make sense of it, but more than anything, he needed to take Source from Falzyxi before it was too late. Grasping for ideas in the weave, he found what he thought was the reason for Falzyxi bringing them to a version of a place that existed somewhere out there, as well as the way to defeat the void being. It would be difficult, but not impossible, not if Luke managed to make a few moves and create an opening without that overpowering presence noticing.

In the end, though, it would come down to his friends. Luke could create a chance, but it was up to them to reach out and seize it.

At the moment, things weren't looking so good. Elijah survived a killing blow only thanks to Vicky, after many of Lemira's pets gave their lives to a futile maneuver. As if that wasn't bad enough, Mateo was charging straight at the enemy. It was an obvious move to give the others some time to make it farther back into the room and away from the monstrosity, but he neglected to mention that to anyone, so they just stood around. Ray looked as if he wanted to disappear into stealth while Curtis kept staring at the Assassin's dagger. Hannah and Penny were keeping calm, but Priyah was a shell-shocked mess, and Vicky wasn't much better. Lemira had some experience with horrific creatures, but the loss of her pets made her almost catatonic. Once again, Luke had opted to rely on people who weren't ready for it. Martin, Kenny, and Nick were dead because of it. Would he ever learn? Being strong meant protecting those who needed it, not bringing them along to get picked off one by one.

Not that Luke felt all that strong at that moment. Mateo rushed in, but at least kept some distance from the tentacles as he pierced Falzyxi's avatar again and again, destroying skin and even thrusting through the void inside the puppet wearing Martin's face, but never reaching the mana channels within. Tentacles lashed out. Expert dodging let Mateo stay alive and fighting for a while, but it wouldn't last long.

The Avatar of Healing appeared between Mateo and Martin, and the white glow made both cast stark shadows against the floor behind them. It was a risk, summoning his own avatar against Falzyxi's, but if they wanted to win, Luke would have to play the odds even if they were stacked against him.

"Run," Luke told Mateo through the avatar. "Find the stairs. Go up, not down."

"Mi hermano!" Mateo cried.

"Go," Luke reiterated. "Go now."

To Mateo's credit, he didn't protest. He turned and ran back to the group, using some movement skill to close the distance in a breath. Once he saw them disappearing farther into the building, Luke turned to face Falzyxi through their respective avatars.

"You are more aware than I thought," Luke said.

The grin on Martin's face was altogether unpleasant, and when he spoke, it was with a comfortable assurance. Falzyxi himself saw no way for him to lose. "They will be mine..."

"My friends?" Luke asked, wanting to buy some time.

"Those who hunger. Those who can sense the lies they have been told."

"Will you give them of your Source?"

Falzyxi's smile widened, and Martin showed all his teeth, as well as too much of his gums. "I will teach them how to feed. Do not stand in my way, and I will teach you to drink life itself, little one."

"Source kills us." The statement wasn't just a way to stall, but one Luke wanted confirmed. While he trusted Guide to an extent, he'd seen Curtis touch Source without dying. He was a special case, maybe, but still.

"Source nurtures us all," Falzyxi said through its avatar as it took a step forward. "Join me or step aside. We hunger, and this shadow of a system is not one I would linger in. An offer will be made. Refusal means death. To some, that is preferable."

Falzyxi's awareness being so articulate had taken Luke off-guard to an extent, and he wasn't ready when it came to blows. It wasn't the swipe or thrust of a sword, but a simple palm on Luke's avatar's chest.

Begone.

His avatar was no more, and a wall of will slammed into Luke with enough force to twist the weave around him. No, that wasn't all, he realized. The push continued. It forced Luke away. Not out of the weave and into the void, but out of the realm of existence they both inhabited. Light flashed, and a sense of speed without direction followed. Source pressed in from every direction, and it was all he could do just keeping it from touching his consciousness. Allowing even a speck of it in would be disastrous, far more so than the small piece of the void Luke had shielded within himself. In that moment, that piece thrummed with excitement. It pushed against its restraints, wanting to reach out and feed on the Source.

As if that wasn't bad enough, the exposure was doing something to Luke's mana. Being surrounded by that raging torrent of Source made it feel like his mana was boiling in his mana channels and meta-heart. A terrible hunger passed through him then and for a moment he wanted nothing more than to drink from the endless-seeming well of Source. Would it kill him? Probably. Even if it didn't, Luke knew it would change him forever. He would no longer be his old self. The void would claim him, and he would have no other option than to go. A being of void and Source had no hope of surviving inside a System. It was undeniable. Somehow, he knew this as absolute truth, like it had been ingrained in him from birth.

His mana's reaction to the Source would not stop, and Luke had to force it back by sheer force of will while still keeping his sense of self separate from what felt like the well of life. Without him doing anything at all, his meta-heart shifted. Infernal mana coursed through him, and the change made him slip for long enough that the demonic mana pushed through his wall of willpower and focus. Before he had a chance to rein it back in, the mana dipped itself in the stream, connecting Luke to the raging torrent of life itself streaming from Falzyxi to the dungeon.

Death did not claim him, but it was a near enough thing. Source burned the infernal mana away in an instant. It burrowed into his meta-heart and seared part of it, changing it forever in ways he didn't understand. There was no pain, but Luke still screamed and screamed while the speck of void energy vibrated with such intensity that it too broke free. With that, agony followed. Void reached out to consume the Source, and the two warred as if they were living entities without Luke being able to do more than hold on for dear life, or risk plunging into the river of Source. A good third of his meta-heart succumbed in the fight, the mana in there acting as a sort of stabilizer to the Source and void, allowing the two to mix into something that looked a little too much like the void being in in the dungeon for comfort. Void filled with Source. Light streaked through the darkness with enough intensity to shine through in the very core of Luke's metaphysical self.

He didn't know if he should laugh or cry, so he did both as his consciousness reached the end of the Source stream. It ejected him back into his body, and Luke woke to chaos beyond chaos. He was outside the dungeon, back in the weave, and people were dead and dying everywhere while Falzyxi remained frozen, its consciousness still inside the dungeon.

"I have to go back," Luke croaked. What happened inside the dungeon would decide everything. Then again, a victory there would not mean much if other void creatures had consumed Earth's weave while they battled Falzyxi, and by the look of things, they were not far from total victory.
