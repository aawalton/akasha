Traveling through the unspace had always been horribly distracting for Alex. His body got undulated and stretched thin or shrunk and twisted in on itself all while space and time acted screwy. And that was just on the way in. Once inside the underbelly of the System, where dreams and ideas found both rest and a semblance of existence after death, along with all manner of things the System managed with it’s ceaseless churning of time, the strange sights he was forced to witness always proved impossible to ignore.

Focus, damnit, Alex thought to himself. For once in your System cursed life, FOCUS and THINK and PREPARE.

Eyes closed to shut out the visual stimulation, he ran through the lecturing he’d gotten from his friends at the Safety Spires first. Each had their own opinions on how one could or should kill a powerful Dungeon Boss and then his grandma, Fatima, a System user of unknown strength. That debate would circle endlessly because there were too many unknowns. But the one thing everyone in his life that mattered kept bringing up and agreed upon was that he relied on a too few skills rather than using his full arsenal. That was a hard pill to swallow, but Alex couldn’t disagree. If the rumors were true about how difficult the Toronto Zoo Dungeon was, he’d need to squeeze out every bit of finesse over his skills that he could muster.

Easier said than done when you’re about to die and sprinting for your fucking life, he felt his mind wander before shoving it back.

Phantom Step for the port, Illusory Copy for Brody and also ports, Burrow Sense when I want to look for and analyze essence, Shiny Snatch to snatch goodies, which also level 2, then the other useful ones are Relative Voyeur to slow down time before I die, and Alternative Goggler to get some sweet speedy glasses. Yeah…perfect. Ah, crap, and there’s Planar Skating.

He pushed against the strange skill and felt himself glide in the direction before the path of Nino and Nina’s delivery portal forced him back on track. Out in the real world, [Phantom Skating] always felt tricky because it needed to be timed perfectly with [Phantom Stepping] to work. He only had the briefest of moments to activate it while running, and when he did it well enough, his sprints turned into a not so controllable rush of speed. In the unspace though, [Phantom Step] was wonkier but easier to activate, letting him glide along the currents of wherever the heck he was.

Need more time practicing in the unspace if it’s got different uses here and…uh…out there. If only the System gave me more information on skills. And followed its own damn rules!

That brought him to the skill upgrade he’d gotten when Emilio copied a version of [Shiny Snatch] from his [Innate Linksmith] Title. He’d kept that one to himself to think over. It wasn’t normal at all. No one got skill levels while awake. Titles, sure, but never skills. So what made that choice different enough to force the System to upgrade his right away?

Maybe Emilio really is just that powerful…

Alex ignored what sounded like a bunch of thrusters behind him and kept his eyes closed tight, realizing that when he stopped and forced himself to focus for a second, his thoughts flowed well. At least they were right now when he was in danger.

Should probably figure out what trauma response that is.

The Toronto Zoo Dungeon was going difficult, but since he ran through his skills and his brief plan, which summed up to ‘don’t die,’ and ‘run like a mad devil,’ as well as ‘convince Rick the Rhino to come kill a couple bad guys for honours sake,’ he felt anticipation rising. The Toronto Zoo Dungeon was only step one. Nina was scared and he believed her when she said how dangerous her sister was. The cursed bracelet and that awful essence in the Blightwood was proof enough. After besting the stubborn Boss came his grandma, and though the Leather Spires Boss sisters thought him an ass, at least Rick the Rhino seemed to follow some kind of morality.

So he prepared, and most of all noticed how settled belly felt and how ignited with purpose his heart was. It was okay to be silly to cover his anxiety and negative emotional responses, but he needed to be ready to take on whatever came his way. And while he didn’t have a food buff from Nina to keep him safe, there were people he loved depending on him. There was no way he was going to let them down without his best effort.

I need to deliver.

Alex’s form of meditation was interrupted when his trip came suddenly came to an end. He was squished across five dimensions to be inserted back into his proper plane amongst the System.

By the time his feet landed outside the Toronto Zoo Dungeon, the System had already noted Alex’s change in motivation for later reference if need be. It wasn’t as if the System decided what happened. That wasn’t its purpose at all. Yet it was an change requiring a reference mark all the same.

That Alex, that one it had already noted was far different from the many others, ran forward to something rather than only away.

And while Alex wasn’t privy to the System’s machinations to no fault of his own, the decision to close his eyes shut to madidate prevented him from noticing a small cluster of drones frantically chasing him. Unfortunately for those trapped drones, the portal snapped shut long before any of the PalaSwarm reached its location. The information was, however, immediately recorded and shared with all.
