Boom. Boom. Crack.

“Master, wake the fuck up!”

“Yip Yip!”

With a groan, I open my eyes. Who the hell is making such a racket when I'm trying to sleep? The first thing I see is a very cute fox face, Luma’s. She seems to be jumping around on my chest, as if to try to get my attention.

“<Fireball>” A voice nearby says. Wait, why is there someone nearby casting a fireball?

In an instant, I expunge all sleepiness from my brain. Stamina surges through me as I practically leap onto my feet. Instinctually, I pull Luma into my phylactery and I have a single moment to think about what to do next. Which is just enough time to activate Plane Step and get the fuck out of there.

Boom.

I stare in horror as the place I was sleeping goes up in flames. My attention immediately searches for the attacker, and I find them standing on top of a nearby hill. They are in a blood red cloak, and are not alone. Seven others in similar clothes stand next to them, and seem to be chanting in unison.

“What the fuck is happening?” I say as I summon Stelan and Luxmore.

“Master! You are awake. Enemies! They tried to kill you in your sleep!” Dovey says desperately.

I dodge another fireball and sigh. Couldn’t they see I was busy doing things? No matter, if they want to take me, it will be a lot harder now that I am awake. I leap into the sky and as I fall towards the group, I let identify pass over them.

Three give no results, but four do. Seems they are some variant of fire mages. One is a healer, and the other three are some support class called novice ritualists. Which probably has something to do with improving ritual magic. All of them are around level sixty though. While I am strong, not even I can fairly fight seven level sixty cored enemies at once. Luckily, I don’t play fair with those that try to kidnap me.

My body is still extremely exhausted from my crafting, but I force the stamina through it anyways. Thankfully, my resources are topped up and the exhaustion is mostly mentaI at this point. So using all of my willpower, I channel as much stamina as I can and activate Spark Drill. With the force of a very angry smith, I smash into their formation, crushing one beneath my hammer.

I duck as one of them tries to swing their staff at me, and in return I kick them in the gut, causing them to crumple like paper. A gust of wind whips up, knocking two of them down thanks to Dovey’s quick thinking.

However, in the chaos I end up taking a fireball to the back. I can feel cold air on my back, which is a good sign as it means the skin is mostly intact. With a single Plane Step backwards, I find the offending mage, their leader most likely.

“<Flame Body>” They incant, and I counter with a Water Ball, dousing the flames that started to cover their body. Using their confusion, I manage to land a solid blow to their chest, sending them flying off into the distance with the sheer force I put behind Luxmore.

Two down, but the problem is there are still five more. I can’t see all of them, but I feel it. Mana gathers around me in a ring, and I Plane Step out of the trap. Moments later, the space I was in erupts in a column of flame that certainly would’ve killed me.

“Cmon fellas, can’t you at least try?” I taunt them, spitting some blood at them.

The hooded figures seem unfazed and just begin incanting more fireballs. Like, I know they have other spells, why the fuck won’t they use them? I manage to dodge most of them, and the ones I can’t, I block with my armguards which reduce the effect by half.

“Die, foolish enemies!” Dovey says as eight of him pile onto one of the mages. Each clone of Dovey starts relentlessly pecking the mage, and within moments a ding sounds.

However, some sort of death charm triggers, erupting in an explosion that catches Dovey, certainly knocking him out. Meanwhile, I have managed to get close enough to strike at one of the mages, but the instant before my strike lands, they disappear. In their place is now an armored figure with a shield raised.

Now where the fuck did you come from?

I am unable to stop my attack, and it hits their shield with explosive force that ripples backwards. It goes right into my arm, and I hear the crack of bones and metal. I also lose my grip on Luxmore as my hand simply stops working, as a result the hammer goes flying away to who knows where.

Now, that is not good. As if to make it worse, my Energy Sense lights up like a pyre meant to burn an entire town of corpses. All around me are people, dozens of them at least. Thankfully, it seems not all are that strong, I can tell as only a few have the same intensity of mana as the original mages. Most of them seem to be around level thirty, if my estimation is correct.

“Damn, it's a real party now.” I mumble as I consider my situation. Right away, I activate Steel Form and Plane Step several dozen meters away from the shielder. But it is as if they know where I am going to teleport, and a rogue is right there with a dagger that hits my armor with a clank. It doesn’t penetrate, but it still hurts like I was just hit with an entire tree.

My mind spins as I try to figure out a path out of this. I recklessly lash out at the rogue, trying to punch their head. But the nimble rogue is far too agile for how tired I am starting to get. They manage to stab me several more times, though my Steel Form holds up and prevents them from doing any major damage.

I give up trying to directly attack them, and now that I notice several warriors circling around me, I shall use yet another trick. “<Ice Burst>” I incant, and as I do so, I push every point of my remaining mana into the spell.

Crack. It is as if the air itself around me freezes. The shockwave of cold rushes out in an unstoppable wave. I see some try to deflect or counter it, but it is far too late. The cold consumes them entirely, freezing every single thing on the cliffside. A nearly forty meter radius around me was frozen… and all it cost was nearly fifteen hundred mana.

For the first time since I woke up, I take a deep breath. But then without any further delay, I Plane Step down to my camp. The fire is still burning, but I guess it doesn’t work on nasty humans. Behind me I can feel that some have broken through the ice. So I pull my domain into myself as fast as I can. Luckily the process is almost instant for such a small domain.

Using my Spirit Sense, I locate Dovey’s corpse. I Plane Step over and pull him into my phylactery, and conveniently Luxmore is right nearby, so I grab that too. Then, I start running. As I run, I pop a stamina boost potion, which doesn’t replenish my stamina, but rather improves the potency of it. To simplify some complicated potion mechanics, it makes me go really fast.

Kilometers evaporate under my legs as I put as much distance between me and my pursuers as possible. I can sense that one seems to be chasing after me, but I guess I caused enough trouble that the entire group can't.

As I move, I suddenly burst out into a fit of laughter. Which is really not good for the whole hero optics. Given the fact that my armor is dyed red with blood–both my own, and those I killed. Not to mention how my entire back is a charred mess. To fix that mess, I drink a mana potion and push some mana into my chestplate, accelerating both the repair of my armor and my body.

However, drinking two potions so quickly makes me a bit queasy. Drinking potions too quickly is technically bad, but not impossible. Most people can deal with the side effects from drinking two or three an hour, though it's recommended to only take one every two hours or so. Let alone two in the span of ten minutes, that was too fast now that I am considering the consequences of my actions. Now that I think about it, this is the first time I've had to drink two potions like that. The side effects are throwing me off somewhat as I am not used to them. Thus I end up having to slow down just a bit.

Which is fine, as it seems I managed to lose my pursuer in the maze that is this hellish place. Also, the slower pace also allows me to start thinking about what the fuck just happened. To start with, I take a look at my notifications, which stretch all the way back to the ones for the successful forging.

Double woah, that's a lot of levels, six for me and two for Dovey. I also ended up gaining a technique mastery. But this is the first time I've heard of such a thing, it isn’t as if I have really studied martial stuff that much. Guess that is yet another book I need to read.

I don’t know how exactly those last three enemies died. Thinking back, it would’ve been around the time I cast Ice Burst. Maybe they were low on health, or had some major weakness to ice. Either way, they are dead now, along with three others.

But, I am alive. Dovey is injured, but will live too, and Luma was hidden away in my phylactery the entire time.

Yip yip. She says in response to that thought, her yips seem to have a complaining tone to them. Guess she feels left out, but she is still too fragile and not agile enough for combat, I think. She is still a young spirit or whatever. Sure she has that Light Cannon, but it puts her in so much danger to use it…

Yip :(.

Hmm, maybe I should make something for Luma next. Something to make her useful in combat? As for what that would be, or what sort of materials I’d need, i’ve got no fucking clue right now. My mind and body are both beyond exhausted, and I am nearly out of all of my resources. Hopefully losing that many people will keep that group from pursuing me. At least for a while. I will just have to rely on that hope, as I stop running for the first time in hours. I collapse against a nearby rock, and close my eyes, but do not fall asleep.
