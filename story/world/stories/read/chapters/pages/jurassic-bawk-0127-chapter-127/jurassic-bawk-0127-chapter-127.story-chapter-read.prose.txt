Room Clearing Condition: Create a weapon, piece of armor, or a tool that a Knight would use.  You may utilize anything in the smithy to create the piece.  When you are ready to be judged, place your creation into the receptacle.

Ndiogou told me that pangolins were the most trafficked animal in the world before the System arrived.  The humans would use our scales for something they called ‘medicine’.”

There were also pieces of leather of various shapes and sizes, a wooden cask full of staves, strips of cloth, and other small items that could be incorporated into an implement used by a warrior.

Then he took up a pair of tongs and used them to pick up a medium-sized metal ingot.  He placed the ingot into the forge and waited.

Then, we’ll take a long, straight piece of wood, polish it, and then taper it at one end.  Then we connect the head to the wooden shaft, add a long strip of leather to help secure them together, and then, if we have time, we can add a butt cap.  That piece will be a bit trickier.
