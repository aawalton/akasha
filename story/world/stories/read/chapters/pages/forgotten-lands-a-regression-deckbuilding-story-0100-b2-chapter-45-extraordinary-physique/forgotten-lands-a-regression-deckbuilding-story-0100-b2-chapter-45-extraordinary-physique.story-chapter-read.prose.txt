The excessive materials and potions were sold to Millenial alongside the surplus armaments.

Old Farthma advanced to a Grandmaster Alchemist by creating a new set of potions. She replicated the recipe and handed it to Zayn’s mother and a few more Alchemists, who transformed the Old Alchemy Tower into a piggy bank.

Over the last few weeks, Zayn might have been busy trying to progress rapidly, but the same applied to everyone else. Nobody stopped progressing, and it is evident to the people who paid attention to the Golden Age that they had the financial means and necessary training regimen to create professional Players.

More Players joined the guild, requiring more funds to be invested in them.

Everyone was growing stronger. Players procured better Cards and learned how to level them quickly.

It was only a matter of time before Zayn noticed a pleasant change in the people around them. When he started playing Forgotten Lands in the recent cycle, he had hoped to reach this stage near the end of Forgotten Lands time. He never expected to have a few more months to strengthen the people around him and protect Earth against invasion and descent.
