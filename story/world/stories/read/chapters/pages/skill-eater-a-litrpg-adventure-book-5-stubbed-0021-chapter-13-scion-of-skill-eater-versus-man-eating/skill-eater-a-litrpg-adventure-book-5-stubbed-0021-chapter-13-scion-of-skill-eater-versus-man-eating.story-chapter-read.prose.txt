The nanex paused at the mouth of its cave while staring down at Edge.

It was astonished the hunter was standing his ground, and delighted that breakfast had delivered itself to its door. While the predator was utterly confident it would prevail, careless creatures didn’t live long enough to evolve to stage three.

The beast ignited its core while sizing him up, switching on passive powers of its own. It was the reaction he had expected, and it gave him an opportunity to complete preparations of his own. With his passives, aura, and domain in place, all that was left was picking his active skills and downing a few potions to tilt the scales in his favor.

Edge decided to open with a classic, infusing his glaive with icy mana from Elemental Blade while slipping a Durability and Power potion from his bandolier and chugging them back-to-back. The first alchemical brew was bitter and the second was sweet, leaving the astringent flavor of aether on his tongue as their magic took hold.

Strength coursed through his limbs as he deepened his stance—frost billowing from Opulence like a comet’s tail. A heartbeat later, the nanex made its move. The bipedal beast charged in a blinding burst of speed… only to come to a skidding stop when he opened his mouth and unleashed an Intimidating Roar.

The soundwave echoed across the forest floor, infused with aural magic that instilled his enemies with primal fear. The predator was accustomed to delivering terror, not experiencing it, and the big bastard was pissed.

It shook off the effects and resumed its advance, although there was a wariness in its gaze that hadn’t been there before—in part because he’d left the skill’s passive component running, which projected his killing intent to threaten any opponents in range.

While it was lying down, the creature had seemed formidable enough. Now that it had assumed its full height, Edge could appreciate just how ferocious it truly was. The nanex wasn’t the biggest creature he’d battled by a long shot, but it outmassed him ten to one. It was a stage-three beast, which meant its physical attributes exceeded his own, even with the myriad buffs boosting his stats.

In short, it was exactly the kind of fight he was looking for, a direct contest of might against something big, bad, and brutal. A battle cry burst from his lips while the nanex closed the final few feet in a flash of blue fur—prepared to counter a range of attacks once the battle was joined.

His avatars were watching through his eyes, ready to boost their output if needed or activate themselves if it looked like he was in trouble. He appreciated the assistance, sending his thanks through their bonds while tracking the giant’s limbs as it let loose with a swipe of its claws.

Edge brought Opulence’s blade up to counter, planning to use Greed’s armor-piercing component to shear the natural weapons from its hands. However, the nanex had amazing reflexes and must have sensed something wasn’t right. It spun hard to one side, avoiding the glaive as it whirled around and thrust inside his guard.

It was ready for him to dodge, but he Hardened his body instead. The claws bit through his armor but couldn’t penetrate the diamond scales coating his skin. They glinted in an errant sunbeam as he brought his weapon around for another pass.

He expected the towering beast to swerve out of the way, but that wasn’t what happened this time. It brought its claws up, and instead of carving through, the keratin blocked the blow cold.

The only explanation was the creature was using magic to enhance its claws’ resilience. Sure enough, Edge spotted a field of mana encasing each wicked protrusion. When his skill senses caught a whiff of the energy in question, gluttony surged through his veins that had nothing to do with the dark passenger.

The nanex was using a high-rank version Enhance Natural Weapon, one of the components he’d merged to create Dragon’s Greed. Absorbing it would give his skill a ton of experience, but that wasn’t going to happen until he dealt with the raging brute trying to rend him asunder.

Not raging. He corrected himself. It’s angry, but it hasn’t lost its cool, and it’s analyzing my powers as we exchange blows.

The beast had two sets of claws, and while Edge only had one glaive, he was by no means limited to a single attack at one time. He leaned out of the way of a kick that would have shattered half his ribs, ducked beneath a swipe coming for his throat, and jumped out of way of a second attack that bisected an unfortunate sapling.

Before the nanex could follow up, he brought Opulence down in an overhead chop while activating Quadra Slash. Mana erupted from his reactor, flowing through his arms and into his glaive. Along the way, some of the magic suffused his muscles, dramatically accelerating the motion of his strike. Slash’s magic merged with Greed, further sharpening the blade while extending the weapon’s reach.

Of course, the skill wouldn’t be called Quadra Slash if that was the extent of its effect. The blade of his polearm split and then split again, forming three offset copies in the blink of an eye. They grew five feet when he flared the spell, mimicking talons of a titanic beast to form the combo he called Dragon’s Claw.

After taking on dozens of high-stage monsters in the Haunted Vale, Edge knew Greed was capable of cutting down a creature of this caliber. Thus, he was caught off-guard when all four slashes failed to connect and the polearm stuck to his opponent’s body. He was forced to let go to evade an uppercut that would have popped his skull from his spine, and when he focused on the information coming from Gaze, he understood what had happened.

A patch of fur had reached out and wrapped around the weapon’s shaft, while other sections formed a buffer in front of the mana-forged blades. His blade had cut into the beast’s thick coat, but the defensive skill was sufficient to stop his strike from reaching the flesh below.

He couldn’t sever its claws, and he couldn’t slice through its fur, which meant it was time to start thinking outside the box… right after he survived the counterattack that was oh so inevitably coming.

The predator looked inordinately pleased with itself as it grabbed Opulence and flung it into the canopy, where it was caught in the branches high above his head. It beat its chest and got ready to charge, but the situation wasn’t nearly as dire as it seemed.

The nanex thought he was disarmed, but nothing could be further from the truth. Edge could have manifested an Elemental Blade or pulled a physical weapon out of his vault, but neither option was necessary. Thanks to the Soulbound modification, Opulence was never more than a thought away. It would return to his side automatically if he ever got too far away, but even when the weapon was close, he could call it to his hands with a mental command.

When Edge willed it, the glaive materialized between his palms with a pop of displaced air. The beast’s eyes went wide with surprise as it came rushing in, but it didn’t abort its attack. He had just enough time to take his stance and ignite his reactor before his opponent was in range.

The biggest problem is that fur-manipulation skill. If I can’t cut through, I’ll burn it away instead. He switched Elemental Blade’s aspect, infusing Opulence with fiery mana that made the air waver like a heat mirage. He comboed it with Quadra Slash, grinning when the beast didn’t try to dodge.

As its arms came down to claim his life, the polearm landed on target. At that point, two things happened at once. The beast’s blue fur began to glow, smoking at it touched the burning blade but not catching flame or being consumed. At the same time, both sets of claws raked the front of his body.

Edge hadn’t expected the nanex to resist his searing strike, but the claws were no surprise. He had already Hardened his torso, but that wasn’t the creature’s target. It had anticipated the diamond scales this time. Instead of trying to break through, it slid its natural weapons across the gems like a skater over ice, tearing into his hamstrings instead.

It hurt like hell, but due to the angle, the attack only cut him to the bone instead of claiming his legs. It would have been a critical wound to most warriors, but he was far more durable than most people his stage.

He flared Rejuvenation while using Repel to fire a water saw into the beast’s eyes, shoving it back with the repellant field for good measure. As his shorn tissue sewed itself shut, the predator raised one hand to shield its face from the high-pressure spray that was tearing through its fur.

The nanex had proven itself to be a dangerous foe, and without Overdrive to use as a lifeline, Edge wasn’t willing to continue the fight without going all out. He was learning about his enemy’s skills as the battle wore on, but the reverse was true as well. He had no desire to test his defenses against the beast’s overwhelming strength a second time.

In short, it was time to burn through the magicytes sitting in his tank—overwhelm his opponent and steal its skills before it landed another attack. He kept the water flowing while he resumed his stance, panning the saw across the brute’s knees and groin before hitting it in the face once more.

At the same time, he let loose with a volley of Vacuum Blasts. The wedge-shaped fields of compressed air struck the disoriented creature. They were designed to cut into flesh and release a shaped explosion along the point of contact, but its fur prevented the spell from biting deep. Blast switched to grenade mode instead, which wasn’t strong enough to deal serious damage but to kept the big bastard off-balance.

In the meantime, Edge switched from fire to lightning, taking advantage of the water to help the electricity conduct. However, the same magic that kept its fur from burning blocked the lightning too. He dodged a flurry of blind swipes while switching back to cold, but that didn’t work either. While the ice impeded the beast’s movements, Blade couldn’t transfer its subzero kiss into the tissue below.

The nanex’s defensive magic was strong enough to repel all three of Blade’s elements, which meant it was up to pure physical violence if he wanted to finish the fight. While it was frustrating to be thwarted time and again, he was smiling from ear to ear. He was eager to find out what skill was providing the potent elemental protection and claim it for his own.

The creature was adapting to the stinging spray, but dislodging the ice from its fur bought him a few seconds and provided the opening he needed.

First, Edge switched on Tidal Stream. He had already primed the domain by infusing the area with gravity mana. He willed it to affect the nanex and the ground beneath its feet, nodding in satisfaction when the towering predator was bowled over by the abrupt application of force.

It braced itself against the pressure while trying to regain its footing. Fortunately, Stream was a gravity power, which meant that while bigger enemies required more mana to shove, their mass couldn’t impede the effect. Meanwhile, he called upon two allies with identical appearances and opposite natures.

Manifest Chain was a Unique skill. Using it didn’t count as a combo unless he summoned both chains at once, but he was happy to pay the penalty while going in for the kill. Foebinder split the skin of his left wrist and emerged into the light as Magebane did the same with his right.

He had always had a good relationship with the left chain of oblivion, whose presence felt like a trained hunting dog—friendly, eager, and loyal. The temperamental right chain had been hard to win over, but their teamwork had improved dramatically over the last few weeks. Both chains extended to their full length of thirty feet, wrapping around his forearms before leaving some play and arcing over his head—an arrangement that minimized the extent to which they pulled on his arms in battle.

Edge reached into his storage rune and retrieved the ivory scythe for Magebane. He had just enough time to hand Yussuf’s sword to Foebinder and take his stance before the beast was upon him once more.

It had reevaluated the threat he posed over the last exchange and was burning through its energetic reserves to power combos of its own. Before it had time to complete its spell, he unleashed a powerful horizontal swipe as both chains attacked from opposite sides, coming in from the creature’s blind spots.

Along the way, he switched off Elemental Blade and activated Quadra Slash on all three weapons, consuming half his tank to pay for the triple combo while using both chains at once.

The results were more than worth it.

Sixteen blades came streaking for the brute’s body. It reached out and caught Opulence’s shaft with both hands, trusting its fur to block the attacks coming from behind. It stopped his swing and got ready to follow up… which was exactly how he’d been hoping events would play out.

Edge canceled Slash and infused Magebane with Disruption instead, a move he’d been working on over the last few days. The field of antimagic traveled up the living links and sank into the oversized scythe. The weapon he’d claimed from the Gardener had a natural affinity for antimagic, as did the right chain. It conducted through the weapon’s shaft and gathered along the cutting surface half a heartbeat before it struck true.

The beast’s fur rose to block it, but the mana-severing scythe cut through the spell itself before plunging into the nanex’s back, causing it to flinch while screaming in pain. It released the polearm to remove the scythe, in what would prove to be the last mistake it ever made.

In a flash, both chains circled around to wrap the creature tight. Foebinder’s coils restrained the beast’s movements while Magebane’s touch nullified its skills.

Before it had time to recover, he suffused Opulence with Elemental Blade and thrust as hard as he could, injecting the beast with subzero mana as the glaive parted fur and flesh in turn before biting into the organs below.

With both chains binding it tight, the man-eating predator was helpless to do anything but growl as its blood congealed and its mind grew dim, leaving its core vulnerable to assault.

When he sensed the time was right, Edge released the polearm’s shaft and triggered Skill-Eater’s ultimate ability. Extraction.
