To the competitors, the way the last fight had ended was rather disappointing, but those who were left of Prestige Academy’s battle crew were satisfied with the outcome to a certain degree. Crown Academy would obviously be different, since they were still behind, but that didn’t matter to the winners, unless to serve as a bit of motivation that they were ahead and ensured no points were lost going forward.

There were only three matches left until the competition came to a close. Lee Jaehyun was next, which was why he was already up on his feet, taking a deep breath as he readied his mind to exit the waiting room and step onto the arena. Then would come Park Yuri’s, and after that was Merlin’s.

Merlin felt like his heart would jump out of his chest at any moment.

When the waiting room had still been filled, he had not really been focused on his own match but those of his mates. But now that one more person was leaving, and it was getting closer to his fight, he began to feel anxious.

What if he lost? What if he was the one who ended up handing Crown Academy the point they so desperately needed to remain as number one in the rankings? What if he couldn’t prove himself to his parents?

Those questions ran amok in his head, and if he wasn’t careful he could end up driving himself insane. If Blue was here he would have cuddled the dragon tightly and just fallen asleep.

The announcement came a minute later, and Lee Jaehyun made his way towards the door. So far, no one who had gone out to fight had returned to the waiting room after, so Merlin didn’t assume Jaehyun would be any different.

“See you on the other side,” Merlin said.

Jaehyun turned around and nodded. “I’ll give my best,” he said. “You can count on that.”

Merlin smiled. “I don’t doubt it for a second.”

Jaehyun sighed calmly and left.

Merlin turned to Park Yuri, who was sitting silently with her eyes closed and her arms and legs crossed.

“I guess it’s just us two left.”

She looked at him. “You sound anxious.”

Merlin’s lips twitched. “I think your ears are playing tricks on you.”

She turned to the TV screen after a long second of resting her eyes upon him.

“There’s no need to lie,” she said. “It’s fine to be anxious. Although, I don’t think there’s anything to be anxious about. We’ve done fine so far. We can win. We just have to play our parts to the best of our abilities. And if anyone can do that, it’s you. I trust you, Merlin Tyrrell, so you have nothing to be anxious about.”

Merlin’s heart skipped a beat. His body felt light all of a sudden, like a gust of wind could lift him up and hurl him into the sky with little to no resistance from himself.

That was one of the longest string of sentences Park Yuri had ever spoken in his presence, and one of the calmest and kindest too.

Perhaps she was right. Maybe there was really no need to be anxious.

Sure, Marcus Reed seemed like a tough opponent, but even the best of mages could be defeated. All he had to do was read the fight to the best of his abilities and think up several ways to come out on top.

He could do it.

“Thank you,” Merlin said as Jaehyun arrived in the arena at the same time as his opponent. “That really means a lot.”

“Don’t sweat it,” said Yuri. “We’re classmates.”

Merlin nodded and focused on the duel about to begin.

Jordan Miles was Jaehyun’s opponent. He was a tall and rangy lad with cropped dark, blonde hair and brown eyes. Standing across from Jaehyun, he didn’t seem like most of the people from Crown Academy who had come before him. He had a rather amiable smile on his face and waved at Jaehyun. Around him, however, was a slight, almost missable, shimmer—a watery distortion that looked like heat rising from a microwave.

Merlin wasn’t surprised at the sight. He had seen that vibrating distortion in the air around Jordan already when studying the lad, and it had also been a point in the discussion he and Yuri had had with Jaehyun, concerning how he could come out on top in his duel. After all, Jordan was a Heat Mage. How exactly that translated in a battle, though, Merlin wasn’t completely certain. He would have to wait to find out.

“Hello,” he said. “How’s it goin’?”

Jaehyun blinked. “Good?” he replied.

“Heard your granddad’s the president of South Korea,” said Jordan. “It’s kinda sick that I’m gonna fight someone so high up in the society, don’t you think?”

Jaehyun’s expression tightened at those words. It was almost like he was severely holding back a bubbling irritation from bleeding into his mien.

Merlin had seen something pretty similar before. It was when he had tried to talk to Jaehyun about what had gone on during his family lunch to make his mood sour. Jaehyun had shut the conversation down, but Merlin could smell family problems from a mile away like sharks to blood.

And his current expression in the arena was only confirming it.

“I’m not sure I see the appeal in that,” said Jaehyun to Jordan. “I don’t even introduce myself in such a manner.”

Jordan blinked, and then sighed. “It’s kinda rough for the silver spoons, huh? I kinda guessed that.” He took a pause and clicked his tongue. “Hey, man, for what it’s worth. Fuck the adults, okay? They tend to always want us to bend to their perfect vision of the future they’ve got designed for us. They’re, like, obsessed with it. It’s frustrating, honestly. And to the point where they forget that it’s our first time living too—just like them.” He then glanced around, reminding himself that he was surrounded by…adults. He cleared his throat. “But don’t be like me, bro. Respect is ethical.”

Jaehyun took a moment then smiled. “I understand.”

Jordan grinned.

“If you both are done with your…talk…do you mind if I start the duel now?” Instructor Park chimed in.

Jaehyun and Jordan turned to her, then each other, and shrugged.

“Go ahead, miss!” said Jordan. “We're bright eyed and bushy tailed and ready to go.”

Instructor Park signalled for the fight to begin. Jaehyun’s Grimoire and Jordan’s Grimoire flipped open and took to the air.

“Let’s go whole hog, eh!” Jordan voiced. “No hard feelings with ‘em result.”

Merlin leaned back at the sight of the smiles present on Jaehyun’s face at Jordan’s words, and he felt the tension that was plaguing him evaporate.

Their fight began, and unlike the rest, it began a lot more casually.

Jaehyun floated into the air along with his Grimoire, and Jordan responded with a bright smile as his eyes shifted upwards and locked upon Jaehyun. In the next moment, his Grimoire flipped a page over, and the shimmering sensation around his body traded places, appearing around his eyes. Waves of warped energy shot forward from both his eyes like lasers, moving as fast as lightning as they rushed for Jaehyun.

Jaehyun was caught by surprise by the speed of Jordan’s attack. His brows twitched, and he quickly redirected the lines of gravity acting on his body, so that they pulled him away from Jordan’s heat lasers. He managed to avoid it at the last second, however, he was not completely unscathed.

“Oooh!” Jordan cried out as Jaehyun winced while patting away the small flame that had burst to life near his shoulder. The fire was put out successfully, but in its wake was a tear in Jaehyun’s battle wear, and a burn. “That was too hot,” said Jordan. “I apologize.”

Jaehyun shook his head. “No. Don’t hold anything back.”

Jordan laughed. “I do think I should, though,” he said. “See, I absorb more heat than humans should be able to—like up to five-hundred degrees celsius heat—and constantly too. The heat haze around me is because I always release it. And my powers act based on that. Basically, I just shot you a laser with at least a hundred-and-fifty degrees celsius. Yeah. I’m gonna hold back.”

Jaehyun smiled. “You’re underestimating me.”

Jordan shook his head. “You’re a Gravity Mage; I’m not—true to God.”

“Then you should know that gravity can very much affect heat.”

Jaehyun’s hands moved around like a choirmaster’s, directing a group of choristers. Then he shifted them down towards Jordan, and before the lad could make a single move, he was forced to the floor by an unseeable force, and kept there.

Merlin remembered the move, and his body still recalled just how painful it was. For a moment his face winced just as Jordan did the same, and a sense of pity washed over him.

He was well aware of how it was almost impossible to escape gravity forcing one to the ground, and just like Jaehyun had said, gravity and heat were like two sides of the same coin. They worked hand in hand.

Following Einstein’s law of general relativity, Gravity was caused by energy density, and heat was simply kinetic energy generated in moving atoms. All in all, since energy contributes to a body’s total mass—E = mc2—a hotter body subsequently has more mass, and since gravity is not necessarily a force in itself, but dependent on the mass and energy of a body, a body with higher mass and energy was more likely to feel its presence as compared to a cool body.

Jordan was several times hotter than a human should be at any given time. Basically, he would be feeling the effect of Jaehyun’s spells much more than anyone else would.

Merlin wouldn’t be surprised if he gave up here.

However, Jordan, despite blood spilling out of his nose, didn’t have the same thoughts as him.

The heat haze around his body grew in intensity quickly, and even though that caused the effect of Jaehyun’s spell to double, he still kept at it.

Jaehyun became visibly worried. His hands twitched as a look of contemplation took over his expression.

Knowing the boy, Merlin realized that he was thinking of releasing his spell. But unlike their training, this was an actual duel that had something on the line. Even though Jordan was a good lad, showing acts of mercy on the battlefield was not something a mage should just do at any given chance they had.

But they were not like other mages, and this was not exactly a battle to the death.

Gravity was dangerous—a crushing force. If Jaehyun held out any longer, Jordan could be crushed to death faster than Instructor Park could call the match off.

Jaehyun visibly came to the same conclusion as Merlin, but before he could release his spell, Jordan’s heat haze stopped enlarging. It gave everyone pause at the sight. The first thought Merlin had was perhaps the boy had died, but he was still very much alive from the looks of his nose twitching and his eyes blinking. And then the heat haze pulled in, compressing rapidly into Jordan. In the next second, a loud boom resonated through the arena as the haze around Jordan exploded outwards. The air warped and rumbled, and as the explosion came his way, Jaehyun protected himself with his arms. However, in the next second, he lost control of his flight, and was flung down to the ground. His expression squeezed into one of pain. Across his arms were severe burns, and his face was layered with cuts.

Jordan spat blood out of his mouth as he rose to his feet.

“I’m sorry,” he said. “I had to make you let me go. Thankfully, you weren’t hurt that much.”

Jaehyun glanced at him, his arms stiff but twitching.

“You’re fine,” he said. “These things come with the scenery.”

Jordan blinked and sighed. “I guess, yeah. I can wait for you to get first aid.”

“No need,” said Jaehyun. “We don’t have the time.”

“So? Keep going?”

Jaehyun smiled. “Of course.”

Merlin relaxed in his seat, and so did everyone else as the fight resumed.

He studied Jordan as the boy cast spells at Jaehyun, and evaded most of what was thrown his way. The fact that he was able to free himself from the one thing that humans have been bound to for all of eternity was baffling to Merlin. His eyes perused Jordan more and more as the fight dragged on, and it was more than halfway through that he finally realized what was going on.

Jordan was getting paler by the second. His lips were getting blue. And as the seconds ticked by, his breaths became foggy.

Merlin actively recalled how he had compared gravity and heat as two sides of the same coin. He had only used it to come to the conclusion of how Jaehyun could come out on top in the duel, but he had not considered the possibility that Jordan could also be positively affected by that theory. And now that he thought about it, it was simple really.

Jordan could expunge heat. Since gravity was weaker on cooler bodies in comparison to hotter bodies, all he had to do was expel the heat in his body in an explosive state. It would not only affect the lines of gravity acting on him, but serve as an attack to Jaehyun as well. It would also make him lighter and more agile.

Which was what he had been doing.

However, constantly expelling more heat than he could absorb was obviously taking a toll on him. His change in expression and characteristics was an effect of that. If the fight went on for much longer, Jordan would not be safe from being severely injured—hopefully, not fatal.

And Jaehyun was also not different. He was now riddled with cuts and burns. He was sweating profusely, and blood was trickling out from whichever gap it found on his body.

They both stood several steps away from each other, panting, exhausted out of their wits. And yet, they had large smiles on their faces.

This gave Merlin a great deal of peace he had no idea he wanted, especially seeing Jaehyun a lot happier than he had seemed going into the fight.

They both moved their hands one more time as their mana surged out of their bodies, preparing to each cast another spell.

The fact that Jordan was able to keep up with an S-Class Mage in a battle which relied on the quantity of mana they both possessed as an A-Class Mage was fascinating. Even more so because he had released large amounts of spells all this while. Merlin found a deep respect for the lad growing within him. However, he was on his last leg. He would lose.

“Time up!!” Instructor Park’s voice broke through the arena before Jaehyun or Jordan could reach for each other’s throats once again. Merlin’s eyes shifted to the edge of the TV screen, finally taking notice of the timer. Fifteen minutes had elapsed. “The duel between Lee Jaehyun and Jordan Miles ends in a draw. The score still remains three to Prestige Academy and two to Crown Academy.”

Jaehyun and Jordan stared at each other for a second longer before they both laughed and fell to the floor as their mana petered out. Medics rushed onto the scene and hurled both of them onto stretchers, carrying them to the infirmary.
