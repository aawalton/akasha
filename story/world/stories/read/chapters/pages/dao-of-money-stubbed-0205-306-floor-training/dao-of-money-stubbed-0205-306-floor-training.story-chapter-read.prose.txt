But when he looked back honestly, much of that had come from tricks, schemes, preparation, and items.They were useful, and even kept him alive. He had spread himself across different cultivation disciplines, using them to create advantages others would not expect. It had worked well so far, but his core fighting style was still too simple.That needed to change.If one day he lost access to his items, if his storage ring was sealed or taken, if he found himself in a place where tricks could not save him, then he might not even be able to run.Chen Ren needed to prepare for that day.Mind cultivation would be the start. No one who looked at Princess Yanyue would expect her to use poison in battle. Anything they hadn’t copied hadn’t stayed with them after they escaped. Chen Ren didn’t know exactly when it had happened, but the books they had not properly taken must have disappeared back to the library.It was a major mistake on his part.Still, at the time, there had been too much happening. The last fear was the possibility that everyone outside thought he was dead. The thought that he might actually die here before he ever saw any of them again.His mind was far too crowded. The dust moving through the air, the sand beneath his feet, even Wang Jun sitting not far from his side, all of it became distant. His body was still there, and he knew the others were still around him, but they no longer pressed against his mind the way they had before.There was only silence.Calm spread through him, slow and steady, until even his breathing seemed to blend into it. Only then did he begin the next step described in the manual.He guided qi toward his brain.Honestly, it wasn’t something any normal cultivator would recommend doing. The body had organs and meridians that could soak in qi, strengthen through qi, and recover from damage caused by qi. The brain was different. It was delicate. One mistake or a careless push would be enough to damage something that couldn’t be repaired easily. Or worse, he might end up making himself brain dead. So he moved carefully. He sent only a thin strand of qi upward, letting it curl around his mind rather than forcing it in all at once. The sensation that followed was strange, almost impossible to describe.
