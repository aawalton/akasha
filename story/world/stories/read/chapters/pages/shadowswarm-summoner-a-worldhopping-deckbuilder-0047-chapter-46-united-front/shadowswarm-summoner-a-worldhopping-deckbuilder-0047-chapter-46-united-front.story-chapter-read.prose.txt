Chapter 46: United Front

Sometimes, combat was funny. Luthor would have expected for the Pause the moment they were under attack, but so long as they ran, it didn’t start. But the moment the enormous, ten foot tall spider flashed its fangs, Luthor made the call to go for it, and everything stopped.

Round 1

Cantrip: [Retaliate], Artifact: [Trusty Arms], Spell: [Mend Self], Creature: [Ancestral Archer]

Unlike most of the others, Luthor effectively had two cantrips. The first was his shield spell [Retaliate], naturally, capable of turning the tide of battle if it intercepted and punished a strong enough attack. The second were his trusty sword and shield, bound to a card to be called up at a moment’s notice.

[Trusty Arms: Common artifact. A simple sword and shield, the only things a warrior needs to defend themselves and others.

Special: Initiative. You begin with this card in your opening hand.

Special: Persistent. This card stays in your hand until used. 1 mana]

Most fights, it was a simple matter to call his weapon, and then immediately reinforce his simple buckler. Seeing the Spider Queen about to sink enormous teeth into Raeva, Luthor realized he wouldn’t have time to cast both.

It wasn’t much of a choice. He took a mental breath, and threw himself forward as soon as the action resumed. Raising his shieldless shield-arm, Luthor cast [Retaliate].

There was a reason he normally cast it on his shield. The Spider Queen’s fang pierced the meat of his forearm like it was nothing. The enchantment held until the fang hit bone, and then detonated. The force was directed away from him, but with the monstrous tooth breaking in his arm, it hurt bad enough for him to white out from the pain.

“Move,” he heard himself hiss. Grabbing Raeva by the shoulder with his free hand, he yanked her out of her prone position. She scrambled away and he followed while the massive spider recoiled in pain. Broken fang aside, it wasn’t life threatening to the creature.

He got the worse end of that exchange. Luthor yanked the envenomed tooth out, but it was too late. The pain was already fading, replaced by an aggressive numbness he didn’t care for. They had to act fast, and get out of this. His stronger heal would show up sometime in the next minute. He just had to hold out, and hope the venom didn’t kill him by then.

Then, many things happened at the same time. Jessica let out a terrified scream as the first spiders came close. The dangling survivors screamed as well, and then everything came together in a clash. Raeleq swung out with his club and smashed the first two spiders that came close, as [Reggie] formed nearby.

“Form up,” Luthor yelled, desperate to be heard over sheer terror, “circle around the prisoners, don’t let them overrun us!”

Imps came into view, just in time to jump the nearest spiders before they could get close. Jessica had recovered from her fear in time to cast her [Astral Viper], and Raeva had her spear out. There were fewer spiders than there had been ghouls, but now there was nowhere left to go. They had to fight their way out, or…or at least until they could go no longer.

He took a deep breath, and summoned his [Trusty Arms], even though his shield arm was useless. It appeared in time for him to thrust his sword into the underside of a leaping spider before it could bite him. The creature sank onto his blade, legs twitching wildly before they folded up. He slid it off and tried to raise his shield-arm, but nothing happened.

A strange sense of cold so deep it burned made its way through Luthor’s veins. It was the only sensation he could still feel in his arm. When the massive Spider Queen recovered enough to rear up, he found himself unable to move. His arm was useless, and his legs made of lead.

Toxic green eyes flashed dangerously, and the queen stabbed forward with two of her front legs. Luthor braced himself, but Raeleq roared and threw himself between the two. The burly Bedamin warrior took it like it was nothing, then swung his club and snapped one of those pole-thick legs off at the joint. The Queen hissed and backed off again, scampering back a few feet while her children took her place.

“Orders?” Raeleq demanded before pulling the spider leg out of his shoulder. It was another in the collection of wounds he’d taken.

Luthor might not have been able to fight too well right now, but he could still think. “As many creatures as we can get to slow them down. This webbing’s the problem, but we can’t burn it yet.” He cast [Mend Self], praying silently it would slow the venom down until a stronger heal came up.

With the Queen backing up, the dozens of dog-sized spiders came at them freely, heedless of their own lives. Were they intelligent, like the mother, or just under her thrall? In other circumstances, it would’ve been fascinating to study, but survival was more pressing.

Raeva struck another jumper down with her spear, but it left her open to another. She screamed in pain and frustration as it sank fangs into her arm. It took some flailing, but she managed to throw it off while Luthor stabbed another one coming for her.

Jessica’s [Astral Viper] did more than expected. While [Reggie] stomped around and attracted plenty of attention, the snake provided something of a wall that could bite back. Its own venom wasn’t anywhere near as strong against creatures like this, but it was fast and managed to get two attacks in for each one it received. It took most of the round to finally fall.

Ambrose didn’t bother fighting directly. He summoned imp after imp, as fast as he could get them out, and kept himself between the spiders and their prisoners. Luthor approved, but as well as they were doing, even more spiders came from the holes in the walls. He idly cast his final spell, his [Ancestral Archer], to at least be able to help keep some of the swarm at bay. The moment it finished casting, everything Paused.

Round 2

Cantrip: [Retaliate], Creature: [Spirit Hound], Spell: [Greater Heal], Signature Creature: [Spirit Warrior]

It wasn’t going to be enough. Not like this, not without a better plan. But what? Raeva was limited until they could handle the smoke and fire. But with the Spider Queen looking at them, patiently waiting for her children to overwhelm them, it was clear that something had to give. The answer came to him about half a second before combat resumed.

“Jessica, you got your octopus ready?” he said as soon as he could.

“Yes, but -- “

“Cast it now, even if you can’t do anything else,” Luthor barked. “Get us nice and wet. Raeva, when she does, launch everything you have at the big one. Avoid the webs if you can, but -- “

Before he could finish his thought, Jack screamed in pain. A spider had managed to get between him and Jessica and went for the smaller, helpless kid. Sharp fangs sunk into his leg, and the spider attempted to drag him out of the circle.

Luthor cursed and stabbed the spider through the thorax, and another quick chop to get its head off. The fangs remained buried in the kid’s bleeding leg, but they had bigger problems to worry about.

The cavern flashed from all of their combined spells. Jessica’s [Whirlpool Cephalopod] came into being on top of a group and squashed them with the weight of so much animated water. [Reggie] roared and stomped freely, even as the spiders climbed on him and bit every surface they could. Some might’ve even been able to penetrate his hide.

Another, wicked green glow came from the tunnel. The Spider Queen waved three and a half front legs in front of herself, and, Luthor realized, cast a spell.

“Look out,” one of the poor dangling souls shouted over the chaos, “that’s how she got us!”

The icy-hot feeling in Luthor’s arm spread to his chest, his waist, and down his legs. It became harder and harder to move, and to think. The spell came as if in slow motion, gathering green lights swirled in a spiral, and instinct told him the spell was about to finish.

Luthor broke the circle and pushed his failing body to the limit. Forward, sword raised, as if he was going to slay the queen on his own. Nothing could have been further from his mind.

The Spider Queen’s spell fired rope after rope of thick webbing out, spread apart. It was enough to cover the entire group, or would have been, if Luthor didn’t take it all.

The moment each rope hit him, they expanded and washed over him. One second, he lurched forward in desperation, the next, he was completely wrapped up in a cocoon and unable to move.

“Luthor!” He heard someone cry out behind him, but the cold in his veins spread even further, until it was hard to even breathe.

Besides, the cocoon was oddly warm, soft, and even…safe. The last thing he thought before the darkness took him, is that if this was death, it wasn’t too bad.

*****

Raeleq hurt from a dozen different wounds, but he barely noticed. He and [Reggie] were almost enough to hold them off by themselves. Almost. They crushed and smashed spiders, constantly shifting to avoid the next one coming at them. Swing after swing, and it was almost fun for a brief moment. Until the cocooned man said, “Watch out, that’s how she got us!”

The next thing he knew, Luthor broke formation and went after the Queen alone. She wrapped him up in the blink of an eye, and he fell. A second later, she worked her legs, reeling him in. “Luthor!” he yelled.

[Reggie] roared and changed tactics with a mental command. The dinosaur stuck to stomping and swinging his long, heavy tail around. Spiders flew back, some dead, most broken. He just had to buy them some time. Raeleq went to go after his friend, but a flash of light and heat blew past him, beaning the queen in the mouth.

The webbing immediately lit up in flames, traveling swiftly down the line to a wrapped up Luthor. Raeleq swung his club down and smashed the fire right before it could engulf the entire cocoon. Another spell flew overhead as Raeva sent a [Flame Lance] down the tunnel.

When it exploded, all the nearby web caught up. Dropping his club, Raeleq pulled his friend back to them, doing his best to ignore the intense surge of heat, and the nearby spiders, stopped by threat of the fire.

“Look out and get together,” Jessica cried, sounding marginally less scared, and more desperately. “Hold your breath!”

Heat rose as the entire cavern caught on fire around them. It flashed hot and fast, almost too fast to protect against it, but the [Whirlpool Cephalopod] rose and splashed down on all of them one last time. The cold water was almost soothing over all the bites and the conflagration around them.

But while the elemental was resistant to most physical damage, this much fire was enough to evaporate it. It bought them several precious seconds as the flames traveled up the walls and ceiling, down the countless nooks, crannies, and tunnels leading out. Until the only things left were them, a mountain of crispy spider corpses…and a bunch of wrapped up people, who had been kept for a snack.

Three of them survived. One a thick, stout mole about five feet tall, with a long, wrinkly nose made up of numerous twitchy appendages. Another looked dead already, shrunken and pallid, with a glowing toadstool growing out of the eye socket. And the last, of all things, was a huge bat, flapping weakly as the remnants of burning webbing smoked among its fur.

Raeleq stood up, coughing out smoke and hot, humid air from the steam still wafting upwards. His people would be okay, if the combined venom didn’t kill them. He reached for the cocoon, when movement caught his eye.

The Spider Queen remained, a silvery, metallic sheen fading from her carapace. She stepped forward, injured but proud. It would be a shame to kill such an incredible creature, but…It was her, or them.

Round 4

Cantrip: [Ironwood Club], Spell: [Rose Charm], Spell: [Feral Frenzy], Creature: [Mosquito Swarm]

The swarm wouldn’t do anything but waste time, but the rest? Luthor wasn’t the only one who could heal. The Queen looked ready to attack, and Raeleq sensed a cold rage from her. They’d killed her family, injured her, and destroyed her home. She had nothing to lose, and everything to gain by killing the invaders.

What a beautiful, incredible, dangerous creature.

Raeleq summoned a new club, and cast what was honestly his best spell.

[Rose Charm: The rose, delicate and beautiful, becomes a source for rejuvenation. Pick one, and then deactivate:

Heal a nearby ally or creature

Cast a deactivated spell for free

Discard your hand and draw a new one. 5 mana]

If there was one thing Raeleq could be proud of, it was his mana capacity. At eight mana, he could cast all of his spells every round…but it would be better with stronger cards, and a better hand size.

Choosing the heal spell was a no-brainer, and he targeted Luthor, praying to his ancestors that it wasn’t too late. Beside him, a rose the size of a sunflower bloomed from the ground, growing at an accelerated rate until the petals opened and three drops spilled out, onto the cocooned body.

Before he could see whether it worked or not, the Queen hissed, spitting venom everywhere, and scuttled forward on uneven legs. Raeleq stood over the cocoon, fists raised, ready to summon his club the moment this spell fully faded.

But before he could, the Spider Queen launched into the air by an unseen force. Momentum carried her and she flipped wildly before crashing onto a pile of her charred children. Jessica huffed and puffed, but grinned fiercely, while Raeva flung her cantrip at the spider once more, and then again copied.

The flaming embers crackled and popped and broke off another leg as the Queen fought to regain her balance.

Raeleq didn’t let her. An idea struck him, and he acted as soon as it fully formed. His feet crunched on crispy carapaces as he all but tackled the Queen and focused on the one Ritual card they all possessed.

“Bind!” he yelled, pitting his will against hers.

Her mind opened up to him, as cold and alien as anything he could imagine. She was capable of anger, but not hate. Not in the way that humans understood it, at least. It was this detached, clinical analysis, a quick judgment. There was the Queen and her children, and the rest of the world. There were potential mates, children, and…food. Everyone and everything else existed only to feed her clan, and let them grow strong and thrive.

And this group of humans had taken that from her. Taken her family. Food always fought back, but they weren’t a stray molekin or kobold, powerless to do anything. They’d beaten and bashed and burned, until…

Raeleq’s heart twisted for her. It wasn’t fair. Nothing in life or nature was fair, it just was. He tried, in his own way, to impress that upon her, and that if he could’ve avoided this…But it wasn’t on her to understand. The only thing she needed to know was that she lost. Now, she was the prey, and it was time to submit.

Degree by degree, the opposing will faded, and then so did her body. Darkness swallowed the Queen, leaving only those glowing green eyes, and then finally, just a card.

[Empress Arachnia: Unique. Hybrid Wild/Shadow Affinity. The fallen monarch of the Forlorn Pit, now pressed into a higher purpose.

Special: Entourage. This creature comes with three smaller spiders minions.

Special: Paralytic Venom. This creature’s bites inflict paralysis, lasting 2 to 5 seconds. Ten second cooldown per target.

Special: Webbing. This creature can shoot out a binding blast of spider silk, ensnaring up to a large target or a group of small or medium targets.

Strength: B. Defense: B. Speed: C. Magic: A. 7 Mana.]

It was an incredibly powerful creature, and if they hadn’t had Raeva to burn it all down, things would’ve been different. Raeleq accepted the card into his pocket, his strongest summon, when he could equip it. He took one more moment to quietly thank the creature before turning to his friends.

Raeva had already cut Luthor out of the cocoon, and helped him to his feet. He looked pale, and weak. All of them looked like shit, and another fight would likely spell certain death. Ambrose checked out Jessica, and the two carried on a hushed but animated conversation, while the prisoners…Well, the boy wept openly in fear, but the kobold just smoked, hands trembling.

“Hoo-eee, I can’t believe you folks pulled that off,” a boisterous but wheezy voice caught Raeleq’s attention. He turned to see the molekin better for the first time now. He was kind of like a human, but not. The long, wiggly, fleshy snout already set him apart, but so did the dark, beady eyes and massive scooping claws on hands like shovels. He had an arm around the bat person’s shoulder. “Are y’all okay? You look mighty rough, friends.”

The translation cards were odd. The mole had a rough sort of rural Southeastern Amaric accent, but that was impossible. Raeleq didn’t question it, and just smiled.

“I think so. Luthor, you alive?”

His friend nodded and struggled to move his left arm. It eventually cooperated, more or less. “Would you three happen to know a way out of here?”

The third of the rescued trio stirred. The corpse-like, mushroom-laden figure jerked a partially decayed finger down the tunnel, just as they thought.

“Yeah,” said the molekin. “How about we get the hell out of this pit and back to town? My name’s Mulch. You saved our lives, so lemme return the favor.”

“Is it far?” Ambrose asked, looking and sounding dead on his feet. His formerly nice clothes had been shredded, and he was almost indecent.

“Not too far,” said Mulch. “It was honestly bad luck that got us trapped. Let’s get a move on, and I’ll tell you all about it.” He gestured with a broad claw. “Home’s just a few ticks away.”
