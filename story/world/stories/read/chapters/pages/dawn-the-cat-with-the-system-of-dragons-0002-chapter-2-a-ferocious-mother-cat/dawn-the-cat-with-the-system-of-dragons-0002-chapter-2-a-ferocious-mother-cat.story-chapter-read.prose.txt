The sun was about to rise, the eastern sky was turning orange, and the two beautiful moons, along with the twinkling stars, were still visible on the western horizon. As far as the eye could see, only the dense canopy of a huge forest could be seen around the town’s border.

The air was fresh. The atmosphere was cold. Dew was gathering everywhere outside. The melodious birds were also chirping everywhere. For townfolk, this was the perfect time to get started.

Many people were walking with their hoes and plows, many were tending their clothes, many were managing their lawn, and many were walking towards the forest with their weapons.

This was the outskirts of a remote town. A huge border built with tree logs and its entry gate were clearly visible. Unlike cities, this place was rather scattered. Every house had a big lawn around it, and huge trees were everywhere.

There were many abandoned houses in this region. And among all those houses, there was one house that was quite noticeable. This house was so old that it had mostly collapsed, and moss was growing all over the broken walls.

Only the left side wall of this house was standing. The front and right-side walls had crumbled entirely. Some parts of the backside wall had also crumbled, but its surviving parts were still connected with the left side wall, creating an L-shaped structure. The roof was lying slanted from inside against the L-shaped wall, creating a hollow space in between. And the big chunks of the front wall had closed off the inner space from the front. Only a small, narrow opening was there.

“Mew, mew, mew, mew, mew.” From this small opening, a faint sound of newborn kittens could be heard. They were squeaking in hunger, clearly calling for their mother for food and warmth. From their sound, it was clear that they were a day or two old.

After sunrise, a black cat finally arrived inside the dilapidated house. She had finally finished her hunt, and now it was time to feed her kittens.

As soon as the black cat reached her babies, the babies became restless and started squeaking as loudly as possible. The cat lay around them, and they immediately latched onto her belly.

The bigger ones started pushing aside the smaller ones to get their preferred spot for milk. The black cat had to hold their scruff and adjust them one at a time. After a while, she moved and adjusted her position to make sure every one of them was fed properly.

Even with their eyes closed, the kittens kept pushing each other. The kittens became so restless that the black cat started licking them roughly to settle them down.

After a while, the kittens stopped mewling and went back to sleep. Now it was time to leave her den again. She needed to hunt and eat enough to produce milk. It was necessary, and she knew it instinctively.

Feeding her kittens, taking care of them, then hunting outside. Then again, feeding her kittens, taking care of them, then again hunting outside. It was a tiring, nonstop routine, but slowly it was becoming her new habit.

And just like that, a whole week passed.

The kittens had grown quite a bit. Their previous squeaking sound turned into proper mewling. Three of the five babies also opened their eyes. This was a critical phase. The black cat was becoming more and more vigilant.

She knew that she needed to eat even more to produce more milk. Moreover, when her babies grow enough, she needs to hunt more. And hunting more meant spending more time outside, which would make her babies prone to danger, especially when they start walking.

So slowly she started spending less time hunting, even if she didn't get anything substantial. But this proved to be a bad decision.

Just like every other day, she went out for a hunt just before dawn. But this time, it took her a lot of time to return. Her last two hunts were not enough, and now she needed more food. She had to spend more time hunting small animals.

The darkness of early dawn was already over. The sun was climbing over the horizon. But still, she was not there in her den. Consequently, the mewling of her babies became louder and louder. The kittens were extremely hungry.

This attracted the attention of nearby predators, dogs.

Initially, they were running and playing here and there, but suddenly, they heard the mewling sound. Their hunting instincts flared immediately. The sound was definitely coming from the dilapidated house. So, all four dogs started sniffing around the area to find their prey.

After some time, they found the opening of a den. Several kittens were mewling inside. But to their disappointment, the opening was too small.

The dogs tried to put their snouts inside, but their heads were too big. They tried to put their paw inside, but the kittens were placed too deep for them to reach. They kept trying to enter, but nothing worked.

They even tried to push aside some of the rubble. But even that failed. The rubble was too heavy for them to push with their bodies. In the end, the dogs started digging around the opening.

The base of the dilapidated house was still intact, and the surface was solid. So they were unable to dig properly. A while later, two of the four dogs gave up, and they started sniffing and searching for other ways to enter. The dogs could only depend on time-consuming ways, and that was to remove small debris little by little or search for other ways to enter.

After the morning light became bright, the black cat finally came back from her hunt. When she noticed what the dogs were doing, she got scared. This was not something she wanted. She needed to do something, but it was too dangerous. There were four opponents.

Thankfully, the opening of her den was still blocked with big chunks of debris.

Two dogs were actively trying to make the entrance bigger, and the other two were sniffing here and there. She didn’t know what happened to her babies. She wanted to inspect her den as soon as possible.

She ran and circled around the house from afar, and went towards the backside wall. As soon as she got closer to her destination, her doubt was confirmed. From the sound of her babies, all of them were safe, and the dogs were unable to do anything. The kittens were scared and were mewling loudly. She was relieved.

Now her fear turned into pure rage.

She reached the broken backside wall with her hunting position, climbed it as silently as possible, and went to the other side just above the slanted roof.

The two dogs who were sniffing here and there immediately heard something suspicious and went behind the house. But there was no one.

She slowly moved above the entrance of her den and kept herself hidden above so that she could attack at a moment's notice. The two dogs were still behind the house, sniffing, and the other two were still distracted, trying to scratch and dig through the opening. This gave the black cat enough leeway.

Then, just like a true predator, she launched herself from the corner above, directly towards one of the dogs’ faces and scratched one of its eyes while landing in between. Then she turned around and attacked the other dog on its snout. The other dog immediately backed away, and the attack missed by a millimeter. It started growling, keeping her in check.

The dog that was attacked in the eyes let out a loud yelp of agony while rolling backwards in disorientation. This attracted the attention of the two dogs sniffing behind the house. They immediately ran towards the front and joined the uninjured dog to surround her from three sides.

The cat's back was towards the den's opening, which was on the corner of the dilapidated house. This gave her a huge disadvantage.

The only positive thing was that one of the four dogs was injured, which ran away a bit later.

Looking at the threatening situation, the black cat arched its back up with its tail pointing towards the sky and started hissing loudly, ready to scratch whoever attacked first.

Her fury made all the dogs step back. She was now challenging all three dogs head-on.

Then the dogs started coordinated attacks. When one dog attacked from one side, the dog on the opposite side tried to attack the cat's rear. One in the middle kept the cat in check.

In retaliation, she kept turning around to attack the dog’s snout or eyes continuously and rapidly while hissing. This didn’t let the dogs come too close to her. She was too agile.

After one of her attacks, the dogs backed away momentarily. She took the chance and immediately ran closer to the center area of the wall. The dogs chased, and then she turned instantly to face the dogs again. The cat stood there in an aggressive position.

Facing the dogs from one side was better than facing them from three sides. The wall behind her protected her back.

The dogs started using the same strategy again. One kept her in check while the other two started attacking one side at a time. The cat, in retaliation, attacked whenever a dog came within her attack radius. The black cat didn’t let any dog come near her after she backed away to the wall. Her aggressive and instantaneous attacks were too much for them.

This back-and-forth repeated several times until all three dogs launched a coordinated attack from every direction. With no escape, the cat instantly leaped up just enough to dodge while simultaneously turning herself to grab the wall with her claws. Below, the three dogs, unable to stop, collided head-on with the wall.

The black cat immediately climbed the wall and ran in the opposite direction without looking back. Since the dogs hit the wall hard, they were momentarily confused. One dog looked at the climbing cat and started circling around the standing wall from the edges. This gave the black cat enough leeway to run far away.

Following the dog, which started circling around, the other two dogs followed behind. But the cat was already far away in the front. The cat climbed the nearest house, and it sat on the roof’s edge while looking at the dogs. After several seconds, all three dogs reached the house. Seeing the cat on the roof, the dogs started barking and growling in front of the house.

Leading the dogs away from her den was successful.

After a few minutes of relentless barking, the gate of the house suddenly opened. A granny came out of the house while holding a large bucket full of icy cold water. She hurled the icy water on the three dogs, instantly bathing them perfectly with no exception.

She used ice magic to make the water unbearably cold. So, the dogs couldn’t help but yelp loudly in freezing cold and started running in different directions with their tails between their legs.

“DUMB DOGS!!! YOU ALWAYS BARK AT MIDNIGHT. AT LEAST LET ME SLEEP IN THE MORNING, YOU OBNOXIOUS MUTTS.”

BANG!!!! The granny closed the door with a very loud bang.

The sudden BANG sound made the cat instantly jump high at an unnatural angle, and she fell down from the roof. Somehow, she managed to turn around in midair and land perfectly on her four feet. Then she ran away immediately towards a safe distance and climbed the nearest tree.

The dogs gave her enough trouble already, and then the granny really startled her. Hiding in the tree seemed perfect for her.

After some time, she went back to her hideout. Her kittens were mewling loudly. She licked them for a few minutes to calm them down and then fed them. The whole morning was too exhausting. Then she started resting.

After several hours, the three dogs regrouped and returned. The fourth dog, which was attacked in the eyes earlier, was not there with them.

The black cat heard something; her ears twitched, and she woke up immediately. She was vigilant even in her sleep. Now, she was ready to face the next challenge. She moved her ears to listen to what was happening outside. Then she stood up to take action, but the babies started mewling again. She licked them for a few seconds and settled them down.

The cat went near the exit, then instantly ran away through the opening in a straight line. The dogs also started chasing from behind, hoping to catch the cat as fast as possible. But every time the dogs reached close, she changed her direction sharply and threw them off her track. After changing direction a few times, she finally reached a tree in front and climbed it without delay. The pursuit ended.

The dogs stood below the tree, dumbfounded. They began barking and growling, but they couldn’t make the cat come down even if they tried. So, after some time, the dogs calmed down; their interest wavered. Two dogs went away to the black cat’s hideout and started digging through the opening.

The tree where she climbed was not far away from her den. The dilapidated building was clearly visible. And the dogs digging the opening were also visible. Her babies were clearly in danger. The black cat was losing her patience. She was really furious and wanted nothing more than to chase away the dogs for good.

In the end, she decided to do a frontal assault. Her den was clearly compromised. But she couldn’t come down yet; the dog below was still alert.

After some time, the cat noticed the remaining dog was looking at her den. It probably wanted to join the digging session. Seeing the distracted dog, the black cat silently jumped onto the dog’s neck. By the time the dog looked up, it was too late.

The black cat landed on the dog's neck, scratched both its eyes using her claws, then jumped down and started running towards her hideout in fury. The cat’s movement was so swift that the dog standing below didn’t have time to react.

Unlike the previously injured dog, this one was bleeding from both its eyes, and it started rolling on the ground in agony.

The two dogs heard the yelping sound and turned their heads. They noticed the furious black cat charging at them, and the dog behind her was rolling and yelping in extreme pain. This made them dumbfounded.

One of the dogs backed away, seeing the furious cat about to attack. And the other one growled, getting itself ready to intercept. It was the pack leader and had enough fighting experience.

As soon as the black cat closed the distance, she prepared to lunge, but the pack leader stepped forward just enough to bite her in midair. The black cat saw through the dog’s intent and pivoted mid-stride instead of lunging.

She dodged and moved past the dog’s face. Then she turned around and jumped towards the dog’s ear. The dog immediately tried to turn its head to bite, but the cat’s attacking angle was good, and the dog’s inner ear got scratched hard by the black cat.

The other dog, which was just standing behind, stepped closer to bite the cat, which had just landed on the ground after its initial attack. But it was already too late.

The cat moved fast, turned her head immediately after landing, and jumped towards the dog’s face. Her aim was to get its eyes. But unexpectedly, the dog tried to move forward, and her belly collided with its face directly.

Neither the dog nor the black cat was ready for this development. But still, the black cat didn’t miss this chance and instantly scratched the dog’s head while she was in the air.

The collision made the cat fall on its back. She instantly rolled on the ground and stood up. But this gave the pack leader enough room to pounce on her.

With its big jaw, the pack leader bit and clamped on the cat’s ribs from the side. Its upper teeth were on the cat’s back, and the lower teeth were on the chest just behind the front legs. “Meeeeeeeeeooooooooowwww!!!”

Then, in reflex and excruciating pain, the cat started scratching hard. “Meow eow eow eow eow eow eow.”

Most of the scratches were on the dog’s jaw and sides. Some of the scratches were so deep that the dog’s lower jaw and gums started bleeding profusely. The cat’s screams near the dog’s ear were an added pain.

Then the dog, in retaliation, lifted the cat up and started swinging her back and forth rapidly. But the cat’s relentless deep scratches and its scream didn’t let the dog bite with its full force.

In the end, the dog stopped holding the cat with its jaw and threw her away to stop getting attacked by the cat’s claws.

As soon as the cat fell on the ground. She rolled a few times, regained control, turned around, and stood up again. Seeing the cat, the other dog started moving forward to bite her in the same way as the pack leader.

But now the cat was fueled by heavy adrenaline. She neither noticed her severe injuries nor felt the pain. The only thing she wanted to do was protect herself and her babies.

The black cat immediately arched its back up with its tail pointing at the sky. All of its fur was standing like needles. Seeing the danger, the other dog stopped its charge immediately. Even the pack leader didn’t dare to attack the cat. They kept growling at her.

Then suddenly, the cat started hissing so loudly that the dog’s growling sound seemed normal to the nearby people.

Both dogs and the cat were preparing for the next round of clashes.

Suddenly, a small stone came flying and hit the dog. The sheer force of the flying stone made the dog fall to the side with a loud yelp. Simultaneously, another stone came flying towards the dog standing behind. This dog was responsible for the cat’s severe injury. The pack leader also got hit hard and fell down while yelping.

“BAD DOGGIES!!! Don’t bully the kitty.” A nearby girl shouted loudly. She was using her magic staff to create stones in midair and hurling them towards the dogs continuously.

The dogs kept getting hit by the barrage. The sheer force of the attack made them fall down again and again. Although the stones were not dangerous enough to penetrate, continuous hard hitting was still painful.

When the dogs finally regained balance and started running away, several more stones hit them on their backs, making them yelp again and again, even while running.

Seeing the dogs running away, the black cat relaxed. After walking a few steps forward towards her hideout, she fell down immediately. She tried to stand up, but she couldn’t. The pain was too much. A few of her chest ribs were throbbing. She was also bleeding from her back. The black cat lay limply on the ground and then lost consciousness.
