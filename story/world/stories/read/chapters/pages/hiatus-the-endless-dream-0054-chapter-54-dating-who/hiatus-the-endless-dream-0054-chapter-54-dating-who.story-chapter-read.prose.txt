Just a layer up were the spiders occupying the webs themselves. These were truck-sized, and I heard them before I saw them.

Dull chitin rubbing against chitin resulted in a scraping sound occasionally interrupted by one or more of a spider's spindly legs catching on an uneven piece of itself. I fought the urge to scream at the sights. Fought the images of being cocooned, paralyzed, and liquefied. Humans' only solace came from the pure size disparity that made the bugs' tactics infeasible against us. That wouldn't be an issue for these spiders.
