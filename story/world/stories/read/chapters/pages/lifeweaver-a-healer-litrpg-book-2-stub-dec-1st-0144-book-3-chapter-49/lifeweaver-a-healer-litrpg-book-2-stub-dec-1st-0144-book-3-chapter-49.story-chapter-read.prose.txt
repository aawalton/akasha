Curtis (Party): "Hannah is fine! Where are you? I can't sense the Intruder anymore. Did you lose her?"

Luke chanced letting go of one of her wrists, pulling back to punch the little old lady in the face. He shouldn't have done that. Gaining access to her mana channels and meta-heart had proven difficult, but that didn't mean the threads were useless. The third blow, she caught. Striking the palm of her outstretched hand was like slamming metal into stone, and the feedback reverberated through his arms, shoulders, and down his back even as he let go and drove a knee straight into her chest while pushing a thread of mana toward her meta-heart, forcing it through.
