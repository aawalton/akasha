“Return to your hymn. You should be honoured — all of you should be — unlike the worthless miscreants,” he gestured to the pile of bodies, “Z͐a̟͠n̖͐di̥s͈t̝̾h̃̔́ has blessed you with the opportunity to prove your devotion, and reward you upon completion of his masterwork. Kole wanted to avoid the bother, but if his arm was twisted then his displeasure at having to wrangle a bunch of uncooperative lower creeds wouldn’t matter. He couldn’t fail Z͐a̟͠n̖͐di̥s͈t̝̾h̃̔́.

“Those who do not return to the hymn are traitors and will be dealt with accordingly.”

That threat was enough to dissuade the concerned back into their temporary roles. Though, he kept his eye on the few that failed to hide their displeasure. Soon, an example might be in order.

Fortunately, this Island would be discarded the moment the ritual succeeded.

Or three?
