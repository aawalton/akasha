When Liu Shu started her Level Ups, her conscience was swallowed into the place she had been sent many times before. It was a dark place where the body of a relatively small tree could be seen. Tendrils of energy surrounded this tree as it manipulated the essence around it.

Liu Shu understood that this was some kind of representation of herself. She was quite surprised to see that, unlike her towering figure out in the normal world, she looked like a very young sapling here—a tree that had just become a tree and had lots and lots to grow.

For the first Energy Cycle, Liu Shu had to create paths for the energy to flow, allowing it to feed everything that was under her domain. That allowed her to create the massively complex yet impressively efficient root net she currently uses. It also helped her develop a <Perfect Energy Cycle>, giving her the <Perfect Cycle> title that helped her be unable to create anything below the ninety percent completion level.

Now that she was tackling the <Second Energy Cycle>, the system spoke to her with its usual emotionless voice.

[You have started the process of breaking through into the <Second Energy Cycle>. Do you want to create an automatic <Second Energy Cycle> or do it manually?]

The same message that appeared when she first approached Level 10 appeared in front of her, and Liu Shu naturally chose to do it manually.

[Manual <Energy Cycle> creation chosen. Analyzing structure…]

After she chose it, the imagery in front of her changed, transforming into an illusory image of herself.

The gigantic tree looked very small when compared to the full extent of her own roots. The massive root net that covered kilometers around her was so complex and fantastical that Liu Shu couldn't help but admire it. 'I made all of this?'

Liu Shu rotated the image with her mind observing the complex root network. In her mind, the different nodes and locations where she had significant control points appeared, and while for any other creature, this mess would look like an entangled and disorderly system, Liu Shu could perfectly spot which roots reached where and why.

'So, what do I need to do for the <Second Energy Cycle>? It usually would be the creation of the <Ternary Cores>. However, those are already created…'

[Analyzed. Detected a semi-completed <Second Energy Cycle>, with a completion rate of 90%. Do you want to keep it and break through?]

'No.' Liu Shu answered without a doubt. That was the minimum guaranteed by her title, she wouldn't conform with that completion rate.

[Understood. <Second Energy Cycle> will be completed when your soul and body get connected. Good Luck.]

'... My soul?'

Liu Shu observed the imagery in front of her and didn't see a single trace of her own soul. 'Hm…' Confused, she zoomed in into her own body, and when she was looking at her main body, the World Tree managed to see something at the center of her body. 'That's… My core?'

Her elusive core, which she knew she had due to the A Rank skill, had yet to receive a single point of proficiency. Until now, she had been focused on trying to understand how to use it, but she never had any real success. All the "uses" were just an instinctive use of it, never intentional.

'So… Is my core the hint to connect my soul and my body…? My soul… my soul… Hm?' Liu Shu paused, her mind flashing with a realization. 'That small tree? That should be my soul.'

Zooming into her own body and magnifying the hidden core in front of her, Liu Shu saw in its center a very petite penta-colored light surrounded by a golden glow. The red, yellow, blue, pink, and green colors emitted by the light looked very similar to the ones that Liu Shu manipulated in the past.

'So… Do I need to connect my outer cycle with this? But… It is already connected, no?'

The World Tree was a bit confused and continued observing it, curious. She naturally was not still while looking at it. She tried to extend some internal energy paths toward the core without apparent success.

'Energy cores… My soul… My core…'

After many trials and errors, Liu Shu got a reaction from her own core. 'Oh? So, I don't need to connect the paths to the core from the outside, but I need to create paths out from the inside of the core to the outside…'

Zooming in even more, Liu Shu's sight shifted, and the small tree surrounded by lights and energy appeared in front of her again. 'Let's see… What did I do…? Where are you different from before…? Oh! I found you!'

One of the small tree's many roots was piercing into space, and following that root, she would flash outside right at the edge of her core. Looking at that place, a ghostly white light ray seemed to come from nowhere. Liu Shu cautiously approached one of her body's energy paths and eventually latched them together.

A profound sensation made Liu Shu's soul take a deep breath of energy, spreading throughout her body. The sensation of connection with everything around her exploded, magnifying to a degree that she couldn't explain with words.

It felt like she was looking through a fog before, and now the atmosphere had cleared, revealing the scenery around her. The illusory image that before featured just her own body and everything connected to it changed, showing many things her roots touched. And yet, instead of being more messy, the incredibly powerful mind of the World Tree was able to process everything.

'So, that's what I need to do…'

With that in mind, Liu Shu sank deep into her soul and slowly and gently pierced the space around the <Soul Tree>. Slowly, the core inside Liu Shu's main trunk started releasing the energy inside her soul and mixing it with her body.

Once done, Liu Shu heard a single notification.

[<Second Energy Cycle> completed! 96% completion rate! Do you want to break through?]

Liu Shu frowned. 'Ninety-six? Wisdom, where have I erred?'

[<World Tree Wisdom (SSS Rank)> activates! Analyzing…]

[Wisdom: The <Soul Tree> is not completely connected with the outside.]

'Huh?' Liu Shu checked and saw that all the roots of the <Soul Tree> were indeed connected. However, it was impossible that her own skill would lie to her, so she observed the tree, and eventually, her gaze landed on the small tree's canopy. 'The branches? Do I need to connect the branches as well?'

With that in mind, she turned down the System's suggestion to complete the <Second Energy Cycle> and started working with the branches. Liu Shu could spot four very particular branches between the few of them. 'Those are the <Sylvanium Branch> and the rest…'

So, instead of connecting them with surrounding energy paths, she decided to create one that descended directly from each of the branches and connected with the <Soul Tree>'s branches.

Once she connected the <Sylvanium Branch> in her soul and body through a direct energy path, Liu Shu's mind sharpened even more. She chuckled softly, knowing she was going to succeed. 'So, it was this. Of course, I would need to connect everything.'

Liu Shu connected all her branches, patiently going one by one.

[<Second Energy Cycle> completed! 100% Completion Rate! Do you want to break through?]

'Do it.'

After she said that, her consciousness woke up, and it was at that time that she saw Flor's changed figure. She couldn't help but focus on that instead of her level-ups.

Once she refocused, Liu Shu observed her system messages, and her core almost jumped in fright at the numbers.
