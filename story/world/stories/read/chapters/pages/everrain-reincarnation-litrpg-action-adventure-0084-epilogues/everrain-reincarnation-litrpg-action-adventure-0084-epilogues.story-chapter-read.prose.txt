Day 0

I’m ready for another maelstrom of resurrections followed by immediate and very violent deaths. My head hurts, my eyes feel like they will pop out, and I think blood is oozing out of my nose and ears. My cheek is wet, and as I open my eyes, I see a blurry room.

[You have sealed the gate leading to a lower realm.

Achievement unlocked.

Reward - Gate Sealer - working against the eldritch threatening a lower realm yields better results.

Reward - Vengeful will fully affect the eldritch who descend to the lower realms.]

I struggle to get up, reading the useless notification, when a man cries out next to me.

“Townlord! Thank heavens you’re alive!”

Townlord? I wipe the wetness from my face and look around the room as it stretches and twists. There’s no blood, only soup on my hands. I’m alive and there are no threats, Redo is red, six hours twenty minutes until it’s available.

I can work with this.

***

Gila approached the podium, this was the fifth year she’s been giving the speech, and she felt like a fake each time. Nearly half the gathered audience were older than her. She looked at their expectant faces and cleared her throat.

“Your job as mages, even as the journeymen you are about to become, is to safeguard humanity from all invading forces and unsavory elements. The path you are about to set your foot on is difficult and treacherous. A slightest miss-step may bring about the downfall of our civilization and the entire world.”

With her heart racing, she did her best not to fidget, and at the last possible moment stopped herself from biting her lip.

“Our ancestors once devised a spell which allowed them to peer into the divine. What they did not know is that the divine looked back and deemed our world a source of nourishment. They tried to enter, but the passage was too narrow, so they influenced what they could, the minds of mages and archmages, until they sparked a conflict between the world’s greatest powers. This event is known as the great wormlord war, which ended with the creation of the dungeons and the birth of the cursed Guideline.”

Murmur crashed around like a wave through the small audience, shock and disbelief expressed as gasps and wheezes. Gila’s revelation was still a secret and would remain one for a long time, but any potential mage needed to know the truth.

“The only reason we and our whole world still live is the heroic sacrifice of Griff Rider and Archmage Edna, who gave their lives and rushed into the tunnel beneath the ruined grand cathedral. I was privileged to follow them a minor part of their way, providing what limited help I could and witnessing their heroic final days…”

The speech lasted five minutes before Gila, Archmage Gila, concluded it.

“I have every reason to believe you will make your ancestors and our heroes proud.”

There was a cheer and another school year started. Archmage Gila returned to her home, greeted her ten-year-old nephew, and locked herself up in her study.

She waved a hand and the fake shelf moved, revealing a smaller nook and a half-finished dress. The sewing kit was simple, for the archmage of matter summoned anything she needed out of thin air.

***

“I think he had a heart attack when he saw us.”

“That’s fine. Temporal powers reset time after death. We just have to wait a bit.”

A cold long decade passed in the void.

“Aaany minute now…”

***

Edna’s life had become strange. A decade had passed since the incident, and after she and Gila buried the final abandoned dungeon, she had taken it as her personal goal to root out all the abominations roaming the world. Her tree army would be a fear-inspiring thing, if anyone knew about it.

As it was, eighty-five hundred semi-sentient trees formed a forest around her as she marched through the corrupted lands. She was considerably less powerful than Griff just prior to his death, but she was an earth-shaking power, and possibly the strongest person in the world, save for Gila.

Edna still recalled the fight the two of them had three years ago over who would be Lucy’s and Griffin’s guardian. Fight was too strong a word, the archmage of matter pummeled her, even with all Edna’s experience, Gila’s accumulated skills made her a real monster.

Edna’s forest shook, a horde of roaming monsters headed by an elder abomination smashed into her army, but the violence would never reach her. Not unless a dozen elder abominations attacked at once, and with the Guide gone, the odds of that happening were close to zero.

She sighed. Edna needed to find a purpose in her life, otherwise, she would wilt or go insane with boredom.

She looked towards the horizon, and the trees parted to let her see the setting sun.

Where are you now? What kind of life are you leading?

She still had work to do, but a part of her wished to join Griff on his next adventure.
