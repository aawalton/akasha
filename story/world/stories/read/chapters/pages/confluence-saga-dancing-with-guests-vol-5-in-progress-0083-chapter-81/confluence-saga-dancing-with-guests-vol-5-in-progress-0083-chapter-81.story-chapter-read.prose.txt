Why do we split Affinities between Elemental and Conceptual?

Elemental Affinities tend to have very clear cut manifestations: Fire, Flame, Heat, Ice, Freezing, Life, Regeneration, the list can go on. They might not always be flashy or visible, but they can be applied and shaped fairly directly.

Conceptual Affinities are often less clear-cut: Understanding, Connection, Trails, Health, Growth. They have some degree of benefit to the Awakened's Path but they sometimes take more effort to identify applications for.

Of course ease and directness of application are in relation to Combat Awakened. Non-combat paths tend to find Conceptual Affinities easier to apply and Elemental harder, simply because what obvious use does Ice have to an Engineer or a Seer?
