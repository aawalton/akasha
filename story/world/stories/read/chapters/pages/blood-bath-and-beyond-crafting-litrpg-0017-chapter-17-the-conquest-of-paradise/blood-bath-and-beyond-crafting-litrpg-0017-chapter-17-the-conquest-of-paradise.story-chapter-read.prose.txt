Inside the dome of the [Warding Circle] it was quiet. Sound was muffled, the air preternaturally still. The sensation was not unpleasant, but it was definitely strange, like dunking my head underwater. Kinda peaceful. I watched as a single determined moth flew towards the barely visible barrier then… change its mind. It headed off into the gloom, disappearing into the darkness. Huh.

I shook my own head and sat, cross-legged. I did not know how long the [Ward] would last, and I didn’t want to waste a moment. Grabbing a ball of soap, I hesitated. I desperately wanted to try infusing it but perhaps this was not the smartest idea, given how low I was on mana, and how long it had taken me to infuse the damn herb. With an unladylike noise I tossed it aside in favour of an unconnected piece of devil’s flight.

If I understood everything correctly, [Warding] should make the process easier. This would ensure I had a fresh start on the morrow. Sinking into my meditation, it was immediately clear that it was, in fact, easier. Everything was focused. I was focused. No mana was lost on the breeze, any stray pieces stayed within the dome of the [Ward], floating about like luminous jellyfish.

I could not coax them back into me, or into the herb, (not yet anyway) but it was interesting to see how the dome contained it. Two hours passed in a flash, before [Ward] faded. But I held in my hand a second infused piece of herb.

Back at the start, in some ways, but this had taken a fraction of the time of the first.

The next day, with a full tank of mana, I refreshed the herbs and then sat down to finally infuse some soap. In some ways, after so long it was anti-climactic.

Nah, who am I kidding it’s amazing!

Casting the [Ward] was immensely satisfying: green flames leapt around the circle like wildfire, flaring bright then dying down to the barely there shimmering dome I remembered from the day before.

[Devil’s Flight Ward Established. Lvl 3. Quality: Stable. Power level: Moderate.]

Sitting within, I enacted the same mana soaking strategy as I had with devil’s flight.

The process proved more delicate than with the herbs, my mana threatening to overwhelm the organic binding several times. But with my improved [Mana Perception] I could see the weave straining and was able to pull back the pressure when necessary and avoid an eruption.

With patience, and hours, I got it done. At last. At long, long, long last, the soap was saturated with mana. I held in my sweaty, happy hand, a single, precious, beautiful mana infused ball of soap.

To my mind’s sight it looked the same as before: ill formed, dingy, rather unimpressive. To my meditative eye, it was a gently glowing thing of beauty.

[Congratulations! You have made a Basic Healing Soap, Level 2. Quality: very poor!Experience is awarded]

The [Ward] dropped around me, and I received a slew of notifications:

[Mana Manipulation has reached Level 6!]

[Mana Perception has reached Level 11!]

[Congratulations! You have created a recipe!

Experience is awarded! +2 to Creation

Basic Healing Soap:

100% Deer Tallow,

35% River Water,

65% Lye (Ashes of ash, comfrey, elderberry flower, and devil’s flight). Superfat of 5-8%Basic Healing Soap will cure most living creatures of simple surface-level ills, banish fevers, headaches, allergies, and ease minor aches, pains, and poisons.]

[Congratulations! You have reached Witch level 5! 3 Stat points available.

Aspect shift notifications now available!]

[Notification of Aspect Shift!This act of Creation has moved your Aspect from Shade Ascending to Shade! Further Acts of Creation will guide you towards Balance and beyond to Light. Acts of Destruction will guide you towards Destruction through Darkness!

* Aspect notifications may be toggled on or off with your friendly neighbourhood system interface]

Okay what now? Creation, Destruction? A level! This was a lot to process.

My eyes returned to the magical soap in my hand. I blinked at its squishy perfection. Nothing else mattered for now. My heart leapt.

Drums started to beat in my brain.

I needed to bathe! All else could wait. And this was not a moment for a bath in a bucket, oh no! This was a nymph in waterfall moment, a veritable slow motion shampoo advert montage, because I had made magical soap!

Making sure the woods were free of insane mythical creatures (they were), I skipped to the stream, drew in a deep breath of chilly, foresty night air, and ceremonially removed my clothes, which did not take long because all I owned was a dress, torn knickers and a corset.

The moons were all up and watching approvingly.

The night was as bright as a night could be. The glade was cast in soft shades of diffuse silver, tinged with green, and my poor bruised limbs, once revealed, looked like mottled alabaster.

Laying my stinky, sweaty dress on the bank with reverence, I held aloft the ball of soap, letting the moonlight wash over it. Then I waded into the stream and lathered myself up.

Oh glory.

Was it caustic, yes. Did it smell a little meaty? Yes. Was the lather a bit understated. Yes. Was it magical? YES!!!!

It was unlike any soap I had ever used.

My poor aching body rejoiced as dirt and pain sloughed off in equal measure. My muscles relaxed, knots unformed, even some of the dark patches I thought were bruises washed off… hang on… the soap is washing off my bruises.

I watched with abject fascination as they just…slithered off into the cool running water and drifted away in dark splodges. All but the very darkest bruises were gone, the minor cuts and scrapes closing, all those knocks and bumps and bloody abrasions just slipping away into the stream. The red, nasty streaks of my infected wound retracted. They did not fully disappear, but suddenly I felt lighter. The fever left me, and suddenly I was aware of just how ill I had been.

Woah.

I ducked my head into the cold water and lathered up my hair. My headache drifted down the stream with the grimy suds. It no longer hurt to think.

Fresh, clean, and considered less bruised, I sat my naked ass on the moss-covered bank and stared at the magical soap that I had made with great satisfaction. Oh yes baby, we are going places.

Time to hunt down people and sell them my soap! Time to discover society!

Tomorrow though. And since there was no way I was putting my super clean body back in the horrible dress without washing it, I washed that too, (with mundane soap) careful not to shrink it in the hot water heated over the fire. It was already uncomfortably curve hugging.

Clean, happy, and dreaming of my burgeoning magical soap empire, I fell asleep with a smile on my face.

The next morning I woke, feeling fantastic.

Remembering I had stats to distribute, after some consideration, I put them into strength. That meant, (without my augmented base bonus), I had both Endurance, Mana and Strength above 10 points, leaving only Dexterity lagging behind. That was okay. I had no immediate need to be particularly agile. Or rather, I didn’t see the particular need.

I washed myself again (this time with warm water over the fire) and was pleased and interested to see my remaining bruises drip off. So, the soap wasn’t a one-time use kind of deal then. The deep cuts seemed to heal a little more this time, crusting over as if they were much older. And now the red lines of the infected wound were completely gone.

In the nature of experimentation, I washed again, immediately. This time the healing soap had no further effect. Perhaps I had to wait till I was dry? Or till I got dirty again?

“My kingdom for a towel,” I said to the sky. My stomach rumbled. “And potatoes.”

By now I was thoroughly sick of my own bad meaty cooking, and fucking forest foraged greenery. I craved carbs. Yes, I needed more stuff, if I was going to live here permanently: a scale so I could make soap that wasn’t ‘poor’. Bedding. A pillow. Clothes. Chocolate. Chickens for Eggs.

Yes, it was definitely time for a trip to town!

I set about merrily gathering some food for the trip, wrapping my soap carefully and getting directions from my stony friends. Then I remembered something else from the bunch of notifications.

I pulled up my stats. There it was – Aspect – Shade.

“System, are you there?”

[Always.]

“What does Aspect mean? What does it mean that my Aspect is changing?”

[Aspect governs how others perceive you. Both physically and mentally. It is based on your actions and expressed outwardly by your aura.]

I didn’t like the sound of that. “A good and evil alignment? Or more like a charisma stat? Why am I shady?”

[Nothing so crass as good and evil. Such thoughts are childish. Let me explain. Aspect is a scale. A spectrum. Creation lies on one side, Destruction on the other. They are merely two sides of the same coin, if you will. Most classes and creatures align one way or the other. Static. For a Witch, Aspect is constantly in flux. Your actions dictate your alignment.

Creatures that favour Destruction will consider you one of their own when your Aspect leans that way. Likewise, creatures that favour Creation will find you abhorrent, and vice versa. Or less inclined to like you, let’s say. Not everything favours the binary; some are more comfortable in Balance. Not all are aware of Aspect, merely feeling it as an instinct stirring in their gut. Some spells are able to reveal it.]

“Okay. So how do the Aspects go? The scale of them, I mean?”

[Creation, Light, Illume, Balance, Shade, Dark, Destruction. Balance is true neutral. Shade Ascending means you are headed towards Destruction through Darkness. Light Descending means your path it to Balance.]

“So, making a soap recipe is an act of Creation?”

[Indeed.]

“And killing something would be an act of Destruction?”

[Bravo, you have it.]

I ignored the sarcasm. “So why am I in Shade, rather than Light? I’ve been positively angelic since I arrived?”

[You did kill a paladin. And all those poor pigeons. There has been a lot of stabbing. And you have been filthy for most of it. Cleanliness is next to godliness!]

“Hey! I mean… is it?”

[It’s not me that picked soap witch as a class,] said the system smugly.

“So, gods exist?”

[Perhaps.]

“Have I offended anyone in particular?”

[Not yet but give it time.]

“Hmmpf. Will you tell me more about them?”

[No.]

Fun. “Okay, back to Aspects. I don’t fully understand yet. I haven’t just killed things, I have also made soap, befriended gargoyles, done a buttload of meditation and [Warded] stuff. Surely that counts as Creation?”

[It does. This is taken into account.]

I glanced down at my hands – normal again now, but I had not forgotten the way they had looked at my arrival – the claw-like hands of the [Evil Enchantress], the blackened skin up to my wrists as if I had stuck my hands into a fire. Currently they looked very human and unremarkable.

“I see… just to be clear, my actual appearance does not change, only people’s perception of me?”

[Yes - for now they see you as you appear to yourself, but their gut feelings respond to your Aspect. Think of it as instincts. Different creatures are more or less attuned to this. Humans famously like to ignore theirs. This will likely change as your power grows, as your aura grows. Those with high Perception and certain classes will perceive your Aspect more readily. Eventually it will change you physically and begin to affect the world around you. Or at least people will perceive it so.]

Great. Now I had to worry about growing a pair of horns, on top of everything else. Or even worse, a halo. “So, Destruction is evil then?”

[Not necessarily. Something to keep in mind - some actions are better suited to Destruction - fighting for example, or tearing down a house. Whereas crafters and builders naturally favour Creation. None of these actions are inherently good or evil; they are simply actions. For example: a noble paladin might be full Destruction, killing all the time – but only for noble reasons. Everyone would call them ‘good’ but they’d still be Destruction.

Whereas a famous scholar might turn their talents to creating wonderous devices of torture and death, or poisons and potions that cause harm, they’d be Creation but oh so very evil as most would perceive it. Some spells or skills are naturally suited to different aspects. As a Witch you have more flexibility than most. This is both a boon and a burden. You said you wanted to be master of your own destiny, after all.]

“I did. And yeah, I do. Hmm. Hmmmmm. I see. I think. Interesting.”

[Anything else?]

“No, thank you,” I said, absently. “Not now.”

My head full, I got ready to travel.
