By the time Sasha left, nighttime had arrived. Johan, after much consideration, sent a text to the number on the card Haskins Jr left.

Sasha had already helped him confirm that it wasn’t a scam or some hidden plan to mess with him. The reply came back rather fast, two minutes after the message was read. It was a mildly long paragraph expressing Duran’s joy and enthusiasm over the meeting.

The meeting time was set for the evening of the next day at the S.M. Hotel’s Sector B branch. It was an hour away by car, and they were going to send one to pick him up.

Now, he could enter the Tower with peace; it was too late to do the other things he had planned on.

In the Tower, he worked with Eunally and Nunally to harvest the already-grown crops. The cycle continued as he kept spamming the growth acceleration skill.

While resting a bit before he went to bed, Johan had started to put the new skill grinding formula to practice.

He took the Sherry leaves seed, which had dried by now, and used his seed sense on it. After that, he stored the single seed in his storage. Then he picked a second, then the third, and so on.

Since he had grown those seeds, he could keep them in the storage, as they met the conditions. And after using Seed Sense on every single one and storing them, he could effectively grind both skills at once.

It felt a bit tedious to do at first, but by the hundredth time, Johan had started to develop a rhythm. If only he could listen to music, it would boost his concentration further.

For that, he needed to get the second phone. However, the two rabbits resting in his lap were a good motivation. To be honest, it did make him want to just stop for a moment, but maybe next time.

Growth Acceleration, Seed Sense, and Tower Farmer’s Storage, I have systems to max out these skills already. After this, I’ll just spam the other two!

It might not be easy, but Johan believed he could do that. A cool thing about Sanctuary was that he could cancel it after casting. So, he didn’t have to find new places to recast it and could do it from one spot over and over.

He was already using Translocation quite a lot while moving potions and travelling between Earth and the Tower.

So, even if it seemed it would take a while to max them out, it was only a matter of time.

“It’s I’m-possible not Impossible…. No bullshit.” After giving himself a much-needed pep talk(?), Johan went to bed around 1 A.M.

He had a good feeling about the next day.

Leaving the mart to Eugene’s capable hands, Johan started his daily Tower routine early.

He handled the next batch of potions. No (+) variant, big sad.

The good thing was that the sherry leaves seed farming went well, aside from his burning nose. Parakeet had been very happy, flying around, basically radiating.

So, in a way, it wasn’t so bad.

His consistent grinding had also paid off.

Seed Sense Lv. 6, Intermediate Brewing Lv. 4, Farmer Storage Lv. 7.

He hadn’t even counted the newly grown seeds yet. At this rate, the two skills would get maxed out by the end of the week, that was his optimistic take on it.

Lucas’s deal, Merilyn’s talk with the Mage Tower, Adrian’s help with weight loss vegetables, and now the meeting with Duran. A lot of things were happening in the same time period.

Maybe he had bitten too much at once?

Well, no choice but to chew it now. Johan shrugged, saying goodbye to the adorable rabbits and sleepy Cerberious.

Eugene had already left after closing the Mart by the time he appeared in the potion workshop; it was already half an hour past noon.

Before meeting Duran, Johan had a few things to prepare.

First, he asked Sasha not to tell Yuna about it yet. It seemed she was already busy with rifts and other work, to the point that she hadn’t slept for more than a couple of hours over the past few weeks.

Second, he had to gather the material he had on magik vegetables and create a short presentation. It was just a way to sprinkle extra seasoning on top of it all.

Third, he had to call Adrian about the leak. Well, he could be overthinking, and Duran might’ve only wanted to meet him regarding the potions, which could be a way to help his younger brother, but the media had stated very clearly that the two weren’t on good terms.

Johan could tell for certain that Duran was after the magik vegetables, considering his business ventures, and the only place he could get this information from was the Agni Clan so far. Since Adrian’s wife, Rishika, was still in preparation mode.

“We haven’t told anyone about the vegetables yet. I have also confirmed with Cara. I will look into it and find out where the leak happened.” Adrian sounded a bit angry, but mostly apologetic. “I have no excuses; I should’ve paid more attention to the matter.”

“Don’t worry about that. It’s really not that serious.” Johan did what he could before this guy took it too seriously, but he was too late on that already.

“This is a result of our negligence, that much is true.” Adrian took his work very seriously, to the point that Johan feared for the one who leaked the information.

Well, the person deserved whatever was coming for them; leakers deserve no mercy.

As the call ended, Johan exited the Mart around noon. He had his phone with him, all the files were in it, and the reports forwarded by Adrian this morning, too. That was a great assist by Adrian-emon.

Johan sat in the black sedan, the one Duran had sent to pick him up, with a sigh. Not because the seat was too hard, actually, it was pretty comfy, heated as well.

It wasn’t the silent driver in a suit hiding his gaze behind the shades, or the bald behemoth-like guy sitting in the passenger seat, barely fitting in the car seat.

The reason for the sigh was his companion.

“I can’t believe you are meeting him the next day.” Sasha had decided to tag along, and he couldn’t refuse the support.

“In business, time is of the essence.” Johan gave a sagely nod as the car started for the main street, passing by haggard office workers and kids from academies.

“You just want to get the second son out of Senior’s dms, huh?” Sasha gave him a knowing smile. “That’s cute.”

“I have no idea what you are talking about.” Johan looked away.

“Your ears are turning red.”

“Because it's cold.” This girl had no delicacy.

But there was truth to it; Yuna had better things to worry about than some guy constantly using his position to mess with her. She had to work for two people now, after all. And the guy was also getting on Johan’s nerves with his constant messages to him, so this worked out perfectly.

“Still, aren’t you worried, meeting him alone and all?” Sasha turned to him as they entered the special lane on the highway, reserved for ambulances and Hunters on rescue missions.

He looked at the two guys at the front, but Sasha quietly pointed at the pen in her left hand. It was on the armrest near him, sitting perfectly in the middle of them.

It looked like an ordinary silver ballpoint pen, maybe a bit expensive looking, but her smirk told him otherwise.

“They won’t hear us, don’t worry.” She gave him a reassuring smile.

With a slight sense of relief, Johan thought over her question. Truthfully, he had thought over it since last night. Doing this was risky, as a non-combatant, he couldn’t deny that.

But he had his own safety net. No way someone who could find out about the magik vegetables wouldn’t have heard of his deal with the Agni Clan.

If something happened to him, all fault would come on S.M. And even they wouldn’t want to deal with a Major Clan breathing down their necks.

“I’ve read his articles and accomplishments.” Johan had been up to date with S.M. Their every move was always massive, especially the Foods and Healthcare departments that had made several innovative decisions over the years, all under Duran. “Someone like him would know better than to make a stupid mistake.”

Duran had been leading 30% of the company for over a decade, and his results spoke for themselves. The guy was seen as a business prodigy since he was young, after all.

“Most blatant flag I’ve ever seen.” Sasha scoffed.

“Every heard of positive flags?”

“Still a flag.”

“You should really stop thinking that way, life isn’t like a video game, you know?” Johan shook his head.

“That’s what you are wrong about.” Sasha continued with a serious expression, one too harsh for her young face. “Real life is often much stranger than fiction.”

Johan looked at her furrowed brow and measured her lowered, almost gravely tone. He rubbed his chin and pointed at her with a smile.

“Diamond Never Breaks II?” It was a reference to the biggest indie game of last year.

“You played that too?” Sasha leaned closer, eyes shining.

“Watched the playthrough on the net, I don’t have a pc to run it.” Johan could only wipe the imaginary tears.

“You can play it on the Hunter phone, you know, it has the specs.”

“For real?”

“Yeah, you can even stream it if you want, and it wouldn’t even heat up.” Sasha gave a big smirk, as if she were the one who made that possible.

Still, Johan was pretty happy about learning more about the phone from a person who understood the tech. She even showed him the website to download the games from and everything.

As they continued to gush over the games from the last few decades, the S.M. Hotel came into view. It had a direct entrance from the highway, one of the many luxury hotels the conglomerate owned across the capital.

The Hotel itself was a hundred floors tall and wide enough to fit a dozen five-star hotels in itself. Even the lobby seemed more extravagant than the best rooms a five-star hotel offered.

Johan had only seen those places from the other side of the screen, but Sasha’s reaction was enough to tell him the place was legit.

Usually, this place should’ve had dozens of guests and workers entering and leaving, at least that’s what he had seen in the videos, but today the place was more silent than a horror house.

Well, the staff stood on both sides of the path in the lobby, leading up to the elevator, so it wasn’t a lonely feeling. The way they were bowing made Johan tense up a bit; the polite smiles were rather strong.

Was this how rich young masters felt? It wasn’t so bad.

As the elevator took them to the top floor, Johan prepared his sales pitch one final time. Walking down the white marble floor, he calmed down his beating heart and made sure his clothes were in order by looking at his reflection in the fancy vases that they passed by.

When the charcoal-like doors to the meeting room opened, Johan felt like he was ready to make a big deal.

Inside the room, the orange of the setting sun came in through the floor-to-ceiling glass windows, and the room had a slightly cold temperature. Four guards stood in place, one in each corner, each bigger than the last one.

The one who led them here walked into the room and stood behind Duran, who had already taken his seat on the other side of the varnished table.

The man, in his thirties according to the media, had messy silver hair and an equally messed-up expression.

“Took you long enough.” He sneered, displeasure dripping down his face.

He pointed at the table, more precisely at the stack of papers in the middle of the table, with a black pen near it.

“Come here and sign it. I don’t have more time to waste.”

As Sasha had said, reality was often much stranger than fiction.
