From the ground Caleb saw Kelia fighting a wall of flames while Mettius had vanished into a sphere of darkness. From within Caleb heard the wizard taunting his half-sister to keep his buff up.

Caleb couldn’t see how Mettius was fairing, but he knew Kelia needed help.

He recovered his feet, and ran to the dying warrior, using his Imperium to rip his sword from the man’s gut. Flames and ice warred with each other as Kelia was fully on the defense.

“Down!” Caleb shouted as he ran at Kelia.

She acted immediately, diving to the ground as Caleb came from behind. He threw both his pouch of quenching steel and nails up before him, spraying their contents into the air before him and then pushed the cloud of metal at the pyromancer. The nails went the fastest, and Caleb sensed as they melted in the flames. The molten metal struck the pyromancer like rain drops and he laughed at the ineffective attack.

The laugh was short lived however as the quenching steel came next. The cloud of glowing dust enveloped the flames and where it met flames both glow and flames vanished as if the dust was eating it.

Once the dust touched the mana pool of the pyromancer, Caleb saw the pool diminish. It had been expanded wide to allow greater control over the flames that had wreathed a 3 meter area all around the caster, and in an instant, all the mana was sapped, drawn into the steel that was ever hungry for heat.

The pyromancer collapsed at the sudden and abrupt loss of all his mana, and Caleb met him on the ground, driving his sword into his chest.

As soon as the summoner had died, the elemental vanished, leaving Bog on his back with his legs in the air battling a nonexistent foe.

Caleb’s perception picked up an arrow entering his mental range just in time to deflect it from himself. He activated Iron Vortex and stepped over Kelia to protect her. Arrows rained down on them from the rooftops. Many struck Bog but he shrugged them off as the nuisance they were.

“Can you get the archers?” Caleb asked Kelia who’d regained her feet safe in the center of his vortex.

Kelia closed her eyes and focused, then fired an ice bolt out at the nearest one. The winds disrupted the path of her bolt and it went wide sailing uselessly into the building the archer stood on.

Caleb let his skill collapse and closed his eyes, focusing on manually deflecting each arrow. Kelia drew her bow and began firing back at the archers above while Bog made his way toward a building whose roof housed three more.

“Go help Mettius” Kelia said, as the wind began to swirl around them both under her control. “I can handle this.”

Caleb trusted her and ran towards the darkness. Behind him the Vortex Kelia conjured grew white as the water in the air froze to snow and then ice. While Caleb’s Iron Vortex was mostly wind sparsely populated with his whatever metal he could find, Kelia could create her own ice and create it she did. In a few heartbeats she’d completely disappeared within the spinning white storm, and then bolt of ice began to shoot out from it’s edges like the vortex itself was a giant sling. At first the bolts went wide and missed their targets, but with each one she got closer while the arrows that reached her disappeared into her storm.

Kelia made her way over to the body of Avis and the small hawks body was lifted up by the frozen storm and it quickly disappeared within. Arrows continued to rain down ok Kelia and then darkness appeared in the center of the vortex,  blinding Kelia but the storm continued with ice bolts flying wildly in all directions. Caleb was about to break off his course to go and help his wife when the frozen storm around her surged and there was an explosion of ice out of which flew a brilliant white hawk with accents of blue in its two meter wingspan. The ice hawk flew out of the storm and the air froze in its wake. It flew straight to the archers on the roof and began raking their eyes in a familiar mode of attack, only this time the blood that poured from the bird’s victims faces froze.

Avis!

Arrows shot at her as she flew and attacked the others, but the archer’s couldn’t catch the frost hawk. The reborn bird flew from one victim to the next, until she found the one maintaining the darkness. As soon as she regained her sight, Kelia’s wild attacks became focused, and  together they made short work of the remaining archers.

Confident she was fine, Caleb ran into the darkness.
