[Still Water Pierces Stone]

The spear moved in an instant, targeting the critical points in Kong Kang's defense.

Kong Kang tried to retaliate, but it was too late; he was hit in the chest and fainted.

At the same moment, Qiu Han's final palm strike shattered the saber disciple's weapon into scrap, and while Lang Rulang looked worse than both of them, she used her family-inherited Earth Grade martial art to overpower the final disciple finally.

The cold mist settled back over the platform. For a single second, nobody moved.

Then, Lang Rulang's voice came through the gray. "Ji Ning."

Ning didn't answer. He was already running.

"He's moving to catch the orb," Qiu Han warned, his ears twitching as the air shifted.

The truce was dead the second the last intruder vanished.

Through the thick, heavy gray of his [Hidden Ice Mist], Ning caught a glimpse of the gold orb. It was bobbing rapidly, trying to bounce away from the heavy pressure of their qi.

"No, you don't, not after I have practiced so much to finally defeat you." Lang Rulang shouted. Her Falling Gale Sequence hadn't quieted from the last clash; wind qi still shrieked around her steel. She had already built up the rhythm while cutting down the intruders, meaning her opening strike hit like the fourth in her chain.

A white crescent of compressed wind swept toward Ning, aimed to force him away while drawing the orb to her side.

Ning jammed his spear down.

[Cold River Spear Art, Parting Current.]

He caught the edge of the gale, splitting the pressure diagonally. The force still dragged his boots across the wet stone, tearing a shallow line through his sleeve.

An atmospheric boom rattled the floor as Qiu Han materialized right beside the orb.

[Quick.]

His palm descended, a heavy crimson sigil spinning behind his shoulder with a metallic hum. Lang Rulang's sword turned without a hitch, her fifth slash colliding directly with Qiu Han's open hand.

Wind and crimson force crashed, shaking the platform.

Her sequence didn't break. A year of brutal training had changed her style; she had the body and sense to know and control the increasingly powerful strike amassed by her technique without getting overwhelmed by it. Qiu Han didn't take the brunt of it. He flickered away with Quick, resetting his stance instantly to strike at her flank.

Ning ducked beneath the shockwave, stepping toward the prize. Both of their gazes snapped to him instantly.

"Can't even let me try, huh?" Ning muttered, backing off.

The orb shot away from them like a panicked fish. Ning's eyes brightened, tracking its path. It wasn't moving randomly. Every time Lang Rulang's wind surged, it drifted away. Every time Qiu Han's qi pulsed, it fled the opposite direction.

Ning narrowed his eyes.

Lang Rulang advanced again, her wind forcing Qiu Han to shift. As Qiu Han reappeared near the orb, Ning drove his spear toward him from the flank.

[Cold River Spear Art, Still Water Pierces Stone.]

Qiu Han slapped the shaft aside, his crimson qi flaring bright. The orb immediately jerked away from the spike in pressure, right into the path of Lang Rulang's incoming gale.

Ning's breathing slowed into a shallow, rhythmic pulse.

Turtle Breathing.

His aura folded inward. He pulled his presence in, making himself look like a lifeless stone at the bottom of a river. He spread his fingers, bleeding Small Cloud Rain into the air to thicken the damp haze around the prize.

"You're doing something," Lang Rulang hissed, her sword cutting a tight, vicious arc toward his neck.

Cold River Spear Art, River Gate Holds.

The steel slammed into his ironwood guard, the raw wind pressure making his chest tighten as he skidded backward.

Qiu Han appeared behind him like a lightning crack.

However, Ning's eyes glowed blue, tracking everything. He ignited a tiny burst of Scorching Purge directly beneath the floating orb. It was no larger than a candle flame, meaningless in the storm of their combat, but inside the damp, freezing mist, the sudden heat created a miniature thermal current. Warm air rose; cold air sank.

The fog stirred, and the orb wobbled, dipping a foot lower toward the only low-pressure zone on the platform: Ning.

Qiu Han's palm swung for his shoulder. Ning dropped low, letting the wind of the strike tear at his hair as he slid the butt of his spear against the stone, using it as an anchor to pivot. Lang Rulang's blade followed from the blind spot, slicing across his upper arm.

He ignored the blood. The orb was right in front of him.

"He's drawing it in!" Qiu Han warned.

"No, you don't," Lang Rulang snapped. Her gale surged, aiming to fill the gap between Ning and the prize with cutting wind.

[Cold River Spear Art, Returning Current.]

Ning's spearhead traced a narrow circle, guiding the worst of the wind pressure away. Shallow cuts opened across his cheek and forearm, but the path stayed clear.

Qiu Han vanished, reappearing right beside the sphere with his hand outstretched. But his crimson qi flared too hot, and the extreme pressure caused the panicked orb to bounce right away from him, straight into the low-pressure pocket Ning had prepared.

Ning's left hand was already waiting.

Lang Rulang's sword tip flashed toward his wrist; Qiu Han's palm came down like an iron seal.

Ning's vision blurred into cold blue.

[Clear Eyes]

The trajectories of the sword, the palm, and the trembling orb lay bare. Ning crouched, dragging his spear to slide across the wet floor. Lang Rulang's steel sheared over his shoulder, and Qiu Han's palm struck empty air as his fingers closed around the sphere.

The orb fought back, a frantic rush of raw gold light burning into his meridians. Ning clamped his hand shut. "You ain't gonna escape."

The sphere stopped as it realized that it was truly caught, with no room to wiggle out.

[Origin Orb Claimed: Ji Ning.]
