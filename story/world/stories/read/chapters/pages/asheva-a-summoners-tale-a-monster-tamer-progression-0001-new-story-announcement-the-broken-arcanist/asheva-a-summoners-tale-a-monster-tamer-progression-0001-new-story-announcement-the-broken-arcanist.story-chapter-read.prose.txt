Hey guys, this is not a chapter update, sorry about that. I just wanted to take a moment to share a small update with you all. As many of you know I've been working on a new book. It went live today. It’s a progression fantasy LitRPG Cultivation blend with a Necro MC.

The Broken Arcanist

Blurb:

A dying heart. A shattered legacy. A debt written in blood.

After the fall of the Aether House, Thorin’s childhood ended in chains. Years later, freedom came but with betrayal, and a blade through his heart. A moment that should have been the end became a pact with Death itself. Saved by the ruler of the Death Arcana, Thorin lives on borrowed time, sustained by a Ghost Heart that demands ghosts to keep beating.

Now, even that stolen life is failing.

Rejected by the guilds, Thorin has no place in the world’s order. With only his cousins Clay and Quin at his side, he walks a rootless path as a rogue Magus.

His Arcanas awaken. Hope does not. The powers that answer him are flawed; weak, restricted, and reduced to mere shadows of what they once were. Where others inherit strength, Thorin inherits fractures.

With his heart failing, Thorin must endure long enough to uncover what remains of Aether House or gather the strength to rebuild it from nothing, even if it costs him the last beats of the Ghost Heart.The world he walks was saved by blood once before, and it has never been kind to what survives on borrowed mercy.
