There are always more people to heal. With the three people on the basketball court no longer risking a sudden case of death, and the fairy-woman having disappeared, Luke stepped through the portal.

New Quest: Bring peace to the Lemmels.

Reward: Shelter Addon - Air Conditioning.

"Huh?" he asked no one in particular when he emerged on... The moon? It really did look like the moon. Bright dirt, craters and hills, the darkness of space, and even a planet far off in the distance. But it couldn't be the moon, because Luke didn't die from the cold. Breathing worked too, so that was it. Not the moon.

More injured people, though, and more people fighting. A few were dead. Perhaps more had died and disappeared, because he saw the motes of light signaling one such dead being transported to wherever the system put those who perished in dungeons. Those Luke could save, he healed. Four people still drew breath. They would be fine now that he'd helped, even if he didn't stay to check on them more once they regained consciousness. His help was needed elsewhere.

Three Integrated, two men and a woman, fought a creature that looked a lot like a praying mantis, except it was dark gray and had four front arms rather than two. It was quick and a little too challenging for the fighters trying to bring it down, while the third Integrated did his best to keep them alive.

"Another healer," Luke said as he ran in to help. "Neat."

"Hey!" Luke shouted as he approached, running as fast as he could. "Get ready!"

"What the fuck?" the female fighter blurted out as Luke swept past, jumping with his staff held high, aiming to smash it into the insectile face of the monster they faced.

Towering Rhimie Elder. Level 19.

The damn thing was even quicker than Luke anticipated. Soaring through the air, mid-jump, readying himself to smash the huge bug, he saw it notice him and adjust, shifting its focus from the fighters to the newcomer. Two arms flashed out toward Luke at a speed too great for him to dodge, especially in mid-air.

Using The Healer's Staff saved him. Blocking that first blow made all the difference. The skill's secondary effect, making the staff an immovable object of protection, meant Luke didn't shoot off into the distance. Instead, he was able to grab hold of the offending arm of the insect with threads of mana, affixing himself to the monster.

That meant being pulled back and down faster than the drop of a rollercoaster when the mantis pulled away, but Luke kept everything in his stomach down even when the world lurched around him. Judging by the movements, it wasn't very nimble outside of its attack patterns, Luke had seen, which meant it struggled to reach him with the other limbs. Given enough time, he was sure he'd be able to reach in and hack away at its monster core, beating it that way. That was just what he was in the process of doing when the two fighters took advantage of the situation and cut the mantis, now focused on Luke, down by severing several of its legs.

In the rolling and thrashing that followed, Luke, the two fighters, and even the other healer sustained injuries, but it was nothing that couldn't be fixed with some good old healing. The bug died at last.

Quest Completed: Peace brought to the Lemmels.

Reward: Shelter Addon - Air Conditioning.

Dungeon Closing in 60 minutes.

Luke lay on his back, panting.

"What's a shelter?" the female fighter asked with a groan.

Integrated Penelope. Ranger. Level 19.

"A house. Kind of," Luke provided.

The male fighter, a clean-shaven guy in his forties with graying black hair, was still on his feet. He turned to Luke. "Who the hell are you? Where's Syrenah? Why are you dressed like the reaper going to a rave? It hurts just looking at you, like my brain doesn't want me to see you."

Integrated Lance. Soldier. Level 18.

"Who's Syrenah?" Luke asked, pulling down the hood of his robe to show his face and disable the effect. He hadn't even realized he'd pulled it up. Healing surged through him, upping his health points, which in turn worked through the wounds, closing them. It felt nice being healed by someone else for a change. Giving the healer, a gangly guy who couldn't be more than eighteen, a nod of appreciation, he added. "Is Syrenah the fairy? And I'll have you know this robe is very stylish."

Integrated Gary. Rejuvanator. Level 17.

"No," Penelope said. "She's Lance's wife. One of the people the fairy helped get out of the dungeon."

"Then Syrenah is fine. I healed her."

"You're a healer?" Gary asked, not hiding the disbelief in his voice.

"See, Gary? That death-wishing jump is what we'll expect from you in the next fight," Lance said with a laugh as he offered Luke a hand. "You saved her. I'm forever in your debt."

"No worries," Luke said. "You know who the fairy is?"

Penelope got to her feet as well and shook her head. "No. We met her outside the portal. Why?"

"She tried to kill me. Pretty sure she hurt the others, too."

"That bitch!" Penelope spat, the thin blade she'd been wielding before reappearing in her hands. "Where is she?"

"She ran. Well, flew," Luke said.

Lance sighed. "I should have known. She was useless in here, attracting more monsters than we could handle. That's why I told her to help the wounded get out, but I never thought she'd do something like that. When I get my hands on her..."

"Doubt you'll see her again," Luke said, turning toward the portal. "You lost a lot of people in here."

"There are always some who didn't make it. A few more this time around."

Luke gave him a look. "You always lose people?"

"Not as many when we have Gary here with us, but he's sought after. It's a dangerous business, this."

"You don't lose people?" Penelope asked.

He thought back. "Not lately, but sure. Sometimes. Nothing like this, though, not since The Deep Dweller."

"The what?" Gary asked, his voice cracking.

"Never mind. You'd better get out of here and see to your wife and the other two."

"Yeah," Lance agreed, waving for them to get going. "What do you think, Pen, one more before we call it a night?"

"Syrenah almost died, Lance," Penelope said.

"But she didn't die... Focus on the positives."

Luke tuned them out. Before following the small group, he snatched up the monster's corpse. More things to sell to the shop were always good. Curtis getting that kick-bike showed there were still a lot of goodies in there to find, and those goodies would be expensive. Also, The Band would always need more.

"Hold on a moment," Luke said. "Are you in a guild?"

Turned out they were. A large one called 'The Army.' They didn't know anything about the Singers, so Luke passed on information they'd bring to their guildmaster about the next day's meeting. The information spooked them enough that Lance agreed not to go for another dungeon that day, and Luke left them outside the portal, tending to their people.

It was later in the evening now, but he wasn't quite ready to go back to the guild just yet. They'd be talking strategy and planning. Neither of those was Luke's strong suit, so he set off running again and was rewarded for it almost immediately.

Boon of Potential grants you an attribute point (+1 Endurance).

Resuming training, Luke made sure to divert mana and convert it into new healing mana, funneling it into his Meta-Heart. That added another layer of complexity to what he was attempting, but he'd need it soon, perhaps even before the night was through.

Perhaps he shouldn't have made it so difficult for himself. That same thought popped into his mind again and again as he ran, but he felt the improvement to his concentration coming at such a quick rate that he couldn't help but push. There was one more thing he needed to check before weavestepping once he found the necessary state of mind to touch the weave, so Luke sent a couple of messages to confirm something. Once receiving said confirmation, it was time to get that first successful jump with the added difficulty of pushing threads of mana, running, and working healing mana within himself.

With grim determination, Luke focused and willed his mind to settle into the state where the faint threads appeared. The weave came to him as if it were the most natural thing in the world. And natural it was. He was a Lifeweaver, after all. The weave was his domain. Reaching out to a particular echo, he readied himself for what was coming next.

Even steeling himself, the pain was excruciating. He doubted nerve numbing would even help, but he hadn't even attempted to lessen the absolute murder this kind of weavestepping did to his body. Because this time, like most times, he needed to be ready for what waited for him on the other side.

Blood pushed up his throat and out of his mouth even as he moved forward, blinking away the red in his eyes. The thread of mana he reached forward with reached its target, and Luke cut through mana channels with Needle of Life, disabling a pair of fairy wings.

The murderous bitch was hunched over another corpse when Luke appeared at her back, with three more a few steps to the right. They found themselves in a dark alley, and the circumstances didn't take a genius to read. This person needed to be stopped. Having just confirmed that neither the police nor the DIA had the resources to imprison someone like this, not with the current state of US society and the threat of the coming singers, Luke knew there was just one way to deal with her.

The Leslie treatment.

She let out a shriek as she turned, one of her arms still formed into a point, and she lunged forward with a thrust, aiming her strange weapon right at Luke's chest. Ready for it, he pulled on the thread of mana still attached to the base of her wings while side-stepping out of danger. When her attack missed, she looked up into the now dark sky and straightened her legs. Nothing happened.

"Think I'm going to let you fly away from me twice?" Luke asked.

She hadn't realized he had cut off the mana flow to her wings. They hung limp against her back. Useless.

"I'll kill you!" she hissed, crouching down like a cornered animal.

"You think?" Luke asked, reaching out with several more threads of mana. "I don't think you'll kill anyone ever again."
