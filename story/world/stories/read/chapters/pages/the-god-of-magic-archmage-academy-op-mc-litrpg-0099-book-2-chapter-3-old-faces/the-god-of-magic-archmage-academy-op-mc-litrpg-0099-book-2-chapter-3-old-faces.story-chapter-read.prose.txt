Sen’s friends looked toward him nervously, aside from Mori and Ilm. If Sen hadn’t been the one to summon it, his allies certainly would have attacked already.

A minotaur.

Its resilience had inspired Mori, and he made the creature’s rehabilitation a pet project of his with Sen’s blessing. A project Mori had finally completed in secret during their travels from the academy to the Kingdom of Magic.

Just in time for the creature’s new role.

The minotaur was twice as large as it had once been, and its skin was a dark blue instead of brown.

Milim shook her head. “You’re saying this is the same creature? But it feels so much more powerful than before.”
