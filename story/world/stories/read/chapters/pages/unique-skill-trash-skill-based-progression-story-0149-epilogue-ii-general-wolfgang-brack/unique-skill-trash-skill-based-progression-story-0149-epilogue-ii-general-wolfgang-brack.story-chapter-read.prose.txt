Epilogue II

(General Wolfgang Brack)

Standing with his men along the rooftops, General Wolfgang Brack surveilled the scene before him, a pair of Dunamis’ finest binoculars revealing everything before him. The lenses were powerful enough to enhance his vision by ten times, while the modified lenses, helped show the horrors that were lurking right in front of everyone. He stood there, anticipation building, as he was close, so close. After such a long time, justice was finally going to be served. Justice for the fallen Dunamis Empire, justice for his daughter, and most importantly justice for his friends.

Looking out over the crowd, he could see them, his daughter, and her friend Ms. Paraisthisi herself. Even after all this time, both had survived, and had somehow managed to fulfill the oaths of friendships that their parents had been unable to truly fulfill while in life. Watching them, seeing both standing side-by-side in this sea of crazed spectators.

Spectators that were even now working themselves up into a frenzy at the fact that four deaths would be seen and witnessed here today. Justice of the highest order, as these four who have been condemned will be purged before all.

However, there seemed to be an issue. Watching his granddaughter, General Brack couldn’t help but notice the seemingly disinterested gazes of the two, his granddaughter, and her one family rival, now friend. While everyone else seemed to react to the spectacle of a cloaked Lightken activating their powers and showing the sins of Salvator the pig, and his co-conspirators, there was a grouping that seemed to be bored. No, bored wasn’t the correct word, disengaged, as if they weren’t going to see a public execution, burning, and final consecration of the walking corpses that are now on stage.

Watching Salvatore Malocchio die was the last thing that General Brack needed, before he could finally take back what was his. Before he would take back his darling Lucia, and even Luna. He would take them both back, and teach them the old ways of the Dunamians. Everything was set up, his people were in place, the correct bribes had been offered.

This way, the moment General Brack saw Salvatore Malocchio’s corpse turn to ashes, he would be prepared to extract the girls and leave here. Prepared to try to retake the Dunamis Empire.

Still, something felt off. It was seeming to all go too smoothly, despite the rising tension that the General now felt rising within him. Looking around, the General soon saw another discrepancy, one that seemed easy to notice in retrospect, for there next to his Lucia was a disinterested guard member. He had all the bearings of a well-trained soldier, in fact after an extra second of observing the soldier, it was clear that even his nonchalance was feigned. A way to get others to lower their guards. Then as if sensing General Brack’s gaze through intense binoculars, the indifferent guard turned and immediately locked gazes with the General. Staring him down, the general felt a chill run down his spine as he instantly knew the man that had easily spotted him. Well, he knew of the man, the legendary sniper Marksman Markan, or whatever they called him. At one-point General Brack had even tried to contract Markan and make him part of his mobile unit but never seemed to find him. Yet, for him to be here spoke of something larger. Then there was also the fact that he was next to his granddaughter, and her friend, the two people that the General and his people were set to extract once all ties to the past were severed.

Yet, staring down the binoculars at Marksman Markan, the General could almost swear that the man was there to thwart his efforts. Seeing Markan, the sense of dread and unease that had been building within General Brack all morning suddenly made sense. Then he saw Markan smile and point off to the side. For a moment, General Brack thought the gesture was a doubling down on the fact that Markan had spotted him. It wasn’t until a loud purposeful step of someone being right behind and to the left of the General did, General Brack realized that it was a warning. Lowering his binoculars slowly, General Brack turned to see the clear form of Mr. Yi, the legendary demon slayer himself standing before him. Seeing the man so close, it was clear that had the swordsman wanted to, he would have easily been able to subdue General Brack.

A quick scan of the rooftop showed that the guards that had been on hand a moment ago to provide perimeter protection were gone. Looking out, General Brack almost swore that he saw one booted foot of a soldier sticking out from a side passage denoting that at least one of the guards had been knocked out and left against the wall. Yet, despite this seemingly hostile act, Mr. Yi had purposefully made his presence known by stepping loudly, but only once he was in place. General Brack took all this information in, and then acted.

“To what do I owe the unexpected visit, honorable Mr. Qiáng Yi?” General Brack asked as he bowed low, giving a sign of maximum respect for the famed swords master before him.

Seeing the gesture, Mr. Yi just waved his hand, “none of that now. I just came here like you, to observe the festivities.”

At that, Mr. Yi, calmly strode forward until he was shoulder to shoulder with General Brack.

“You came to watch this?” General Brack found himself asking.

“Of course, I will go and see my apprentice in all her moments of great importance,” Mr. Yi replied.

“Apprentice—” General Brack began, only to have his words cut off by the loud sound of trumpeting erupting from the world around him. A sign that the procession of healers was about to begin, and then finally justice would be served to Salvatore the pig and his conspirators. Standing there, Mr. Yi just smiled brightly, as he clasped his hands behind his back. The procession was quick, the healers for the local Empire of the Republic were first, four of them. General Brack paused as he saw the number of healers and realized that his intel was wrong, as he only knew of three Bloom house members who were qualified to receive the blessings of the seven, yet now there was a fourth?

“Yes, my apprentice,” Mr. Yi replied his words carrying at one of the few lulls in music. General Brack opened his mouth to comment, but was quickly cut off by another round of blaring horns and trumpets. Realizing that it was almost useless to shout over the noises of the now excited crowd and the music, General Brack pulled up his binoculars and began to scan the crowd again. Watching for any signs of justice not being upheld. His lenses did their job perfectly, for they showed the glowing bright red heart of corruption beating wildly within Sal and each of his conspirators, a sign that they were guilty of the crimes of harboring Death Eaters, a crime punishable by death.

Looking around, General Brack looked for someone of power and stature carrying a sword, but found no one. Well no one that would meet what the General thought would be someone worth of gaining this much respect from the master swordsman.

“Where is your apprentice?”

“Wait and see,” was the three-word reply from Mr. Yi. Then as if sensing something, Mr. Yi turned his gaze to the right, his eyes instantly glowing a deep golden color, “though it seems we have visitors.”

Hearing that, General Brack turned to the right, and saw a collection of people all standing at the back, covered in dark robes, all glowing with a faint burning trace of a heart of corruption glowing within them. Seeing the grouping, the tension that had been building within General Brack all morning suddenly flared to life as he turned and called out to the guards.

“Right perimeter team, what is your status?” General Brack called out into a communications crystal linked to all his team members.

Silence.

“I repeat, what is your status?” Yet, it was already too late, for the cloaked intruders who had come in as a last-minute group of watchers suddenly broke apart and began moving within the audience.

Sighing heavily, Mr. Yi looked annoyed, before he casually walked to the edge of the roof and then jumped. His body disappearing, as it was clearly time for him to react to the rapidly changing scenario before him.
