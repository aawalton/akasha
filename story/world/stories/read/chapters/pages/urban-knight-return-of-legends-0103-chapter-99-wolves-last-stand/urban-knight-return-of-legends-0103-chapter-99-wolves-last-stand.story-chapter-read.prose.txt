After a brief, one-sided scuffle, the snow wolves gradually realized their assault was completely futile. Their sharp fangs and vicious biting were inflicting no damage to Ascalon, who simply smiled and basked on the fluffy moment.

While Ascalon enjoyed the experience to the fullest—petting and even hugging some of the confused canines—his opponents were getting more and more agitated by the second, falling right into his trap.

Yes, he didn't taunt them just to shower them with affection, he surely did not! He was aiming to rile them up and exhaust them faster, in order to render them harmless without inflicting any damage on them—except for emotional damage, perhaps.

The rest of the group watched the scene in stunned silence, with Lilithra smiling warmly at the knight, and Lyra showing quite the envious expression. Selorien, on the other hand, was laughing to himself, more than amused by the situation.

Soon enough, every single wolf ended up laying on the snow, panting heavily while Ascalon grinned in satisfaction. But his glee was cut short as he sensed some of the wolves stop breathing—the ones that were the most injured from the bunch. Their small, lifeless frames filled Ascalon’s chest with anguish.

He took a deep breath, sighing as he sat in the middle of the snowfield, his visage turning thoughtful. While he felt guilty and saddened, it was clear these wolves wouldn’t have seen the end of the day—their enemies had to be quite the mighty foes to inflict such lasting wounds on them.

Taking a moment to gather his thoughts, Ascalon got up and glanced at his friends. With the canines completely tired out from playing(?), they now had free passage toward where the true enemy awaited.

“Let us proceed before they recover.” Ascalon spoke with a firm voice, prompting the rest to nod in agreement.

“Let’s go, then!” Lilithra exclaimed as she skipped toward Ascalon, crouching slightly and briefly petting one of the wolves as she passed.

Lyra followed along and hesitated for a moment, perhaps interested in mimicking Lilithra’s actions, but refrained with a timid expression on her face. As for Selorien, ever the laid-back elf, he simply yawned and dragged his feet after Lilithra.

As soon as they regrouped, Ascalon and his comrades pushed deeper into the snowy plains, with cold, white wind howling around them. Lyra aside, it was the first time they would venture so far into the Howling Chasm.

But they felt no fear or nervousness, none at all. Instead, a sense of adventure and eagerness guided their steps through the uneven, soft terrain. They walked for a couple of minutes, the snow hail around them growing denser and reducing their visibility. Lyra even stumbled a few times, as her shoes dug deep into the white carpet, but was helped by Lilithra every time.

“We are getting close—be prepared.” Ascalon warned out of nowhere, his senses focused on the battle ahead. It seemed the number of wolves fighting the unknown creatures had diminished considerably—no more than four remained, and he could tell they weren’t in good shape either.

Hearing his words, everyone readied up, wielding their respective weapons and sharpening their senses. Ascalon smiled at the sight, and then stared sharply at Lilithra—a glance brimming with expectation.

It didn’t take long for Lilithra to realize what he wanted of her—not when he was making it so obvious at least! Ascalon wanted her to take the lead, and she wanted nothing but to live up to his expectations. Taking a moment to ponder, she placed a hand on her chin and let out a soft ‘hmm’ before speaking.

“Ascalon, you should lead the way.” She commanded with confidence, her brows furrowing as she glanced around. Ascalon immediately nodded, acknowledging her request.

“Selly, you do your own thing!” Lilithra continued, this time addressing her best friend. Selorien lazily raised his right hand and gave her a thumbs up. It would’ve been nice to have him go into stealth, but the snow and hail around them would easily reveal his position

“Lyra, you stay close to me!” Lilithra stated as she looked at the Felinae. Lyra nodded a few times, her soft hair bouncing slightly each time, her agreement more than obvious.

Ascalon chuckled to himself, thinking Lilithra’s ‘orders’ were quite straightforward, with barely any thought put into them—as per usual. But that was fine! She was still inexperienced, and she had plenty of time and room for growth.

After a moment, Lilithra let out a brief sigh, and then gestured with her hand for them to move on.

“Let’s go!” She declared with resolution, and Ascalon answered by taking the lead, hurrying his steps. The group followed as closely as they could, navigating the snowy terrain until they finally started to hear the sounds of battle.

Growls and roars filled the area, alongside the familiar noise of sharpened fangs and claws slicing through the air. As a gust of wind pushed the hail away, the group could see the scene develop before their eyes.

Three wolves rapidly darted around two enormous, white figures—a couple of massive, snow bears, with glistening maws, thick fur over their muscular frames, and deadly-looking claws in their paws.

The wolves jumped at them and tried to bite viciously, but the bears would quickly swing their arms in response, causing the canines to halt their advance and dodge by a hair’s breadth. In the distance, Ascalon could see the remaining, fourth wolf, firmly lodged into a tree—a testament to the bears’ raw strength.

It was clear it was a one-sided battle, as the bigger beasts had barely any injuries on them, and perhaps most of the blood in their fur belonged to the wolves instead. Lilithra gasped in shock as one of the bears managed to land a clean hit on a canine, sending it hurling through the air as it cried in pain.

“Ascalon, stop them!” Lilithra suddenly blurted out, her heart aching at the sight. While it was part of nature, she didn’t want to see the end of such an unfair battle—and most of all, they had decided to intervene, after all.

Ascalon heeded her command with a firm nod. Immediately, he burst forth into a dash, the snow around him rising up with each of his powerful steps. It took him but a second to reach one of the bears, which looked at him with both anger and confusion, startled by the knight’s sudden appearance.

The beast raised its paw and struck down at the incoming Ascalon, who summoned his shield and quickly bashed upward, parrying the attack effortlessly and sending the massive bear recoiling back. The wolves twitched at Ascalon’s interference, but their furtive eyes saw the opportunity to strike.

As the bear lost its balance, and its companion focused on Ascalon’s presence, the three canines took advantage of the situation. With impressive coordination, the trio pounced on the falling bear, biting furiously into its rear limbs. The massive beast roared in pain as it wildly flailed its legs on the ground, sending one of the canines flying across the snow.

Meanwhile, Ascalon crouched briefly as drew back his right arm, clenching his fist tight before unleashing a powerful, upward punch against the other bear. The hit struck cleanly on its chest, causing the massive beast to lift up the ground and ascend a good distance into the air. If anything, the mix of confusion and pain on the bear’s expression was slightly amusing.

Just then, the crunch of footsteps against the snow could be heard, drawing Ascalon’s attention toward his friends. Lilithra was running toward him with a fierce, resolute expression, her figure bending low and her halberd pointing ahead. She moved fast despite the uneven ground, her arrow-like advance aiming to strike true as her [Charge Forth] skill gained momentum.

With a final, mighty stomp, she jumped, her body soaring through the air. With the mid-air bear as her objective, she drew her halberd back, every muscle in her arms tensing up under the strain of the massive weapon. Then, in a flash, she struck forward, unleashing a powerful piercing blow at the momentarily helpless beast.

The chimera-forged blade sank deep into the monster’s chest, a loud howl of anguish escaping its maw. Still airborne, Lilithra twisted her body upward, her legs reaching high above her as both she and the bear began to plummet. With the halberd still firmly lodged into the beast, she used the momentum from the fall to skewer the bear into the ground.

The moment they touched the ground, snow blew in all directions due to the sheer force of the impact, startling both the wolves and the remaining bear. Lilithra’s friends watched the aftermath in awe, her figure slowly rising up amidst the swirling snow pellets, her halberd still buried deep into the felled beast. Once again, she had managed to pull off yet another impressive feat out of nowhere!

But the battle was not over yet, as the remaining bear had managed to shake off its attackers and was now standing upright, staring at the scene with burning, angry eyes. Lilithra gulped at the sight, grabbing onto her armament tight and pulling with all her strength, removing it from the corpse.

The weapon, bathed in its prey’s blood, steadily advanced its taming process thanks to Lilithra’s skilled actions—though it still needed more convincing before finally unleashing its true power.

As Lilithra gasped for air, she raised the halberd and pointed its blade toward the last bear, bracing herself. She expected the beast to attack her in vengeance to its brethren, but someone else decided to take advantage of the commotion.

Out of nowhere, a loud cry of pain escaped the remaining bear’s massive maw, as a multitude of deft, quick stabs dug deep, mercilessly, onto its back. There, appearing from thin air, was the opportunistic Selorien, unleashing a barrage of fast strikes toward the beast.

Blood splattered against the snow as the bear twisted its body, quickly turning around and sweeping the air with its claw in a dangerous, powerful strike aimed at the elf. But Selorien simply backstepped and jumped away, gaining some distance as its shoes sank onto the snow, cushioning his descent.

“Heh.” He snickered, mocking the beast and causing it to roar loudly. Yet it couldn’t take another step as it crumpled into the ground, crimson fluid gushing out of the multiple wounds on its back.

While the beasts had proven a mighty foe for the canines, they were no match for adventurers of their caliber—especially when they had been growing and improving constantly. Ascalon gazed at the aftermath with a gentle, proud smile, his chest swelling with nothing but genuine glee at his friends’ improvement.

But while the main threat had been vanquished, the wolves still remained. Lyra, who was a bit behind, quickly shortened the distance and stood beside Ascalon, peeking at the beasts with wariness. Meanwhile, Lilithra and Selorien regrouped with them, watching the wolves intently.

The creatures peered at the group with clear suspicion in their gaze, perhaps wondering why beings such as them would interfere with their battle. Maybe they were intelligent enough to know they had been helped, or maybe they were simply thinking that a new kind of enemy had appeared before them.

Either way, Ascalon stood resolute, towering over the beasts and watching them closely—hoping his presence would be enough a deterrent for the canines to engage in combat. Upon closer inspection, he could see one of the wolves was panting heavily, while the other two looked feeble and about to collapse at any moment.

It didn’t seem like their involvement had helped much—perhaps they were too late. At the very least, the wolves Ascalon had ‘played’ with before were mostly unscathed, aside from the ones that had succumbed to their wounds.

Moments passed in awkward, tense silence, before one of the wolves fell. Ascalon frowned, pained by the scene, just as one of the remaining canines growled at him. But it didn’t sound like an angered noise, curiously enough. Then, the creature started limping into the distance, followed by its companion.

Soon, a strange scene unfolded. Both wolves stopped, glancing back at the group, their eyes brimming with a glint of expectation—as if urging them to follow. Ascalon and his friends exchanged glances before nodding in silent agreement, and set out after the wolves.

The canines advanced sluggishly across the snowy plains, casting occasional glances toward Ascalon’s group to make sure they were following. It was clear they were straining themselves—which made it all the more curious. What did they want to show them?

The answer to that question revealed itself soon enough, as the wolves led them to the entrance of a small, well-hidden cave. Inside, an interesting occurrence would take place.
