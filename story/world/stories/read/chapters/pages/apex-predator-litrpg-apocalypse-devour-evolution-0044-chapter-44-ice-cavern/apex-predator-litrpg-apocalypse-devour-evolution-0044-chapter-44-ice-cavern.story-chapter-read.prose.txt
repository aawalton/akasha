Don groaned when he could finally move. The debuff from the berserk-mode dissipated and all the discomfort with it. He was back to baseline— kind of. That berserk mode had really taken the wind out of him. Even though his Health and Energy were at a decent 80/80, he still felt like after finishing a rough day of construction work, with overtime to boot.

He ate his last ration bar and it helped. It also bumped his energy back to a hundred.

Don looked at the withered corpse of Homass. He nodded respectfully.

“Best fight I ever had. Sorry for being an asshole before. Fighting to death kind of does that to me.”

Don had high respect for the dead Grimoid king. The boss had taught him a lot. It had forced Don go to the absolute limits, and he had come ahead. Now he was stronger. Not just because of a level up and getting [Life Drain]. This was just like when he had fought the boar in the woods, or his first bear.

Confidence. He knew now. He had suspected it before. He had been fairly sure.

But now he absolutely, irrevocably knew.

Don was strong.

Nothing could take that away from him. He had superseded his human limits and now he was something more. Something dangerous and powerful. He liked feeling like that.

“Thank you,” Don muttered, and got up.

He noticed something interesting. There was a shiny box embedded in the giant corpse. It hummed faintly and looked like it was made from pure diamond. Like a footlocker with a half-pipe cover. A chest. A dungeon chest, for killing a boss.

Don grinned and reached for the chest. The moment he touched it, three items materialized in front of him. A pair of bronze rings, similar to what the king had worn, a folded pair of black pants and a crystal bottle with black liquid.

Don used Identify on the rings.

[Bracers of Grimoid King]

+2 Power

+2 Constitution

“Nice…”

This was Don’s first item. He slid the bracers on his arms. They fit perfectly. This had to be some form of System bullshit. The rings on the actual king were four times the size to fit its massive forearms.

Now both his arms had a bronze ring. It was light to wield and two inches wide. Thin, but uncannily sturdy.

Next Don checked out the pants. The item he was definitely most excited for. His current clothes were absolutely tattered. Rags (covered in dirt, sweat and blood) not even a homeless person would want. Don undressed. Then he looked around and found a barrel of water with a ladle.

He washed himself of all the grime and then took the pants in his hands.

[Beastskin Trousers]

+2 Agility

+1 Energy

Not too bad at all. While the stats were nice, Don was mostly happy just to be able to wear pants. He slid them on. Comfy and flexible. He ran around with them and did a backflip, because well, backflips are awesome.

The trousers fit perfectly again and seemed to be made for movement. It felt like wearing nothing at all. Perfect.

“Alright then, what's this little flask?”

Don crouched and picked up the crystal flask.

[Oil of the Everburning Embers]

This oil was crafted by the Grimoids in the height of their civilization as a weapon against the lurker in the depths, Morshoff. They never got to use it. Morshoff consumed the Grimoids. In this recreation of their history, their ultimate weapon against the eldritch evil has been preserved. This can be used to avenge the Grimoids.

Huh, suddenly a history lesson. Things were starting to make sense. So these Grimoids had been people once. On some distant planet they had existed, and practiced alchemy in their swampy jungle. But then somehow Morshoff had emerged and destroyed them.

That made Don somehow feel better. Not because he was rooting for the ugly tentacle fucker in the bog. But because he wasn’t killing real people. At least that’s how it seemed. The Grimoids had a certain mindless mechanical aspect to them. The king had clearly been more lucid, and Don wondered what that was about.

“So I can use this to kill Morshoff,” Don mused. “Seems like it could be used to set a fire. If this works like I think it does, I could set the bog on fire with this. I like that.”

He put the flask in his satchels and then looked around the room. He noticed that a rope ladder had just descended. Don looked up and saw a trap door in the ceiling.

“What the…”

Don shrugged. He started climbing.

When he reached the top, he wasn’t in the castle anymore.

He was in a cave. A large cavernous opening, with a high ceiling. The cave was full of ice. The walls had layers upon layers of thick, glistening ice. The floor was frosted stone, cold on Don’s bare feet. From the high ceiling the points of stalactites were visible, but it was mostly covered in shadows. Don walked around, his footsteps echoing in the silence.

Then he spotted something odd on the ground. Little black and brown beans in wet patches on the ground. Don had watched enough nature documentaries to know what that was. Guano— bat droppings.

“Crap,” Don muttered.

As if on cue, Don heard a screech from high above and a dark shadow darted towards him. His Survival Instincts absolutely screamed at him to get the hell out of the way. Don combat rolled to the side, and the high screeching shadow collapsed on where he had been a moment ago.

Absolutely monstrous talons cleaved through the stone floor like jelly. The beast was horrendous. Massive leathery wings spread wide, the creature stood six feet tall, all tight muscle under a thin black skin.

The face was wide and the ears were upturned and large. A rolled back snout, resembling a pig’s, the fangs were brutal and dripped with black saliva. The eyes were black as night, gleaming with animalistic rage.

Don crouched low and waited for the beast to make its move. He had a moment to spare to use Identify.

[Vampire Screecher, level 14]

True to its name, the creature screeched. A terrible high pitch whine that filled the air. Don was disoriented instantly and assaulted with nausea and difficulty moving.

[Health at 94%]

[Debuff inflicted: Moderate Disorientation]

The creature didn’t charge though. After it had Don disoriented by the sonic assault, it flew back up.

“What a bastard…”  Don said through gritted teeth. He looked around. There was no place to hide or escape. He could venture further into the cave, but who knew how many bats he’d face if he moved.

No, he had to deal with this one first and learn to deal with the new monster. Don focused on breathing through the nausea and blurry eyes. If the creature was smart, it would strike again soon.

Indeed, it didn’t take long for the giant bat to attempt a fly-by again. This time Don was prepared. He dodged the claws that gouged stone to ribbons. This time the bat immediately used the screech ability. But Don had a plan.

Just like he had formed a vacuum around the torches, he drew in a breath and formed one around his head.

He could feel the sound vibrations hit the bubble of psychic reinforcement. It sent soft ripples that Don could sense with his Psychic Sense. But no damage, no disorientation.

The creature must have noticed, because it was clearly enraged, by Don still staring at it. It immediately flapped its wings, sending a gust of strong air at Don, knocking him backwards.

Then it took off into the air. It moved damn fast. Don tried to lunge after it, but missed by inches. The creature screeched in what must have been glee.

But Don had been expecting this. He had a contingency.

He grabbed the creature’s taloned ankle. Not with his hand, but with his mind.

He yanked.

The giant bat slammed into the ground and screamed in outrage. Don wouldn’t have any of it. With deft strikes he ran the creature through. It thrashed and kicked with its savage talons, managing to cut into Don’s shoulder, slicing a deep gash in.

[Health at 87%]

Don was surprised at the low damage, considering the power the blow was dealt with. He figured his Constitution was really getting up there.

In either case, despite having a bunch of tools at its disposal, the bat was squishy. Once it was on its back, it was slow. Don pinned it down and went on a rampage with his claws until the bat stopped fighting.

[You have slain [Vampire Screecher – lvl 14] – You have gained experience for defeating an enemy. 33 System Points earned]

Don sighed happily and swiped the blood off his face. To his pleasant surprise his pants were unstained. It was as if they had a stain-resistance enchantment or something. Don couldn’t come up with a better name, but System bullshit for sure.

The bat had been fairly simple to deal with. It used ambushes and hit and run tactics. Don could appreciate that. They had a lot of tools at their disposal. Flight, devastating talons, a control effect with the screech and looking at the fangs, some kind of venom. Definitely dangerous. Definitely something that could yield him a lot of evolution upgrades.

Don looked at the pools of bat guano on the floor.

“Looks like I have my work cut out for me.”
