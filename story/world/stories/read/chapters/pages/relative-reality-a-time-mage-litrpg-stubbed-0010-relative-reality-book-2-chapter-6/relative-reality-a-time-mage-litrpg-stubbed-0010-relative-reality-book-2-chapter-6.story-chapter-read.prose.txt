Rowan sat with his team in a private room, examining the terrain of their soon to come battle. All three teams had been given the same overview, a small device that displayed a holographic map of the arena that they were to fight in.

It was to be a fight in vast ruins, broken down buildings grown over with trees and vines. The terrain was excellent for Elise, filled with shadows, crevices and hiding places. As such, they were planning their tactics around her.

“We play it safe, the last thing we want is for one team to realise that we’re attacking the other, and come to play third party to the fight. It would be best if we were the ones to do that to the others,” Helja said.

Arne nodded agreement.

“Is good plan. We must also care for the team of Hendrik. To attack into this ability could give them the win. What will be our plan if Felir decides to do this?”

“We leave, Hendrik will use the power from that defensive ability of theirs to take out Felir’s team and us afterwards. It looks like the arena isn’t big enough to run away, but we could find somewhere to hide and wait out the duration of their enhanced strength.”

Helja pointed to a spot near the centre of the arena, a courtyard with only two entrances on adjacent sides.

“This looks like it has good defensible positions, we set up here first if we’re able and get Melody’s songs going. I believe that the songs of mending and rest should be on the afterimages, with Melody playing power. Then we can consume the two afterimages to heal and recover stamina and mana if we need.”

“I’m a little concerned about that actually,” Melody said, “I have no doubt that the other teams have figured out how my powerset works by now, and they might try to eliminate us if they hear me playing my songs.”

“We have Elise silence them then, they should still build power even if we won’t be affected. Once a fight begins, she can drop the silence.”

Melody’s concerns assuaged, Helja continued.

“Now, Elise is our best card to play in this environment. If we can get rid of either Felir or one of Hendrik’s main damage dealers off the bat before a fight even begins, we’ll be in an excellent spot. So, we get her to hunt down one of the two teams to do just that while the rest of us are building up our position. Arne can spread his roots out to establish a net, hiding them amongst the vines and trees. I’ll set up a few of my own powers using my ritual magic so that I can throw them out in quick succession.”

“And me?” Rowan asked.

“You’ll be Elise’s backup. Since you’re the only one of us that can actually track her, and you’re the fastest of us when you want to be, follow her to her target to ensure that after she strikes, Elise can get out safely.”

“Sounds like a good idea. Who do we go after then? Which team are we more afraid of?”

Helja stared intently at the map, mind turning over.

“We do have a plan to deal with Hendrik, but they will still be the harder of the two teams to fight I believe. We should get rid of one of his team members first if able.”

Conversation continued for a while longer, up until the moment that Ol came into the room and gestured for them to follow. Their time was up, and the battle was to begin.

All three teams entered the illusorum chamber room at the same time, and a little friendly banter was thrown back and forth. Rowan wished both other teams good luck before stepping into his alcove, feeling the illusory magic take over his reality once more.

He opened his eyes, finding himself at one of the three spots that had been designated on their smaller map. The rest of his team appeared beside him one by one, until they all stood together. They were in luck, having been given one of the starting locations closer to the courtyard than the others.

There was only a moment's pause before Lylc’s voice echoed through the arena, signalling the beginning of the battle.

Rowan and Elise split off from the rest of the party, sprinting ahead of their slower party members and into the ruins. Rowan used Relativity to push both of their speeds past their normal limits. Near to reaching Tier three, Rowan was a very different person to the man he had been before entering this new world. As a university student he had been lanky and unbalanced, awkward on his feet. Unfit. Months of hard training and the magic transformation of reaching new tiers had changed him to achieve feats that he would have previously thought impossible.

With the speed of an olympic sprinter, further increased by his powers, and the balance and athleticism of an acrobat, Rowan flew in a mad dash over the ground, barely slowed by the thick vines, undergrowth and rubble of the ruined city. He even had to slow down so that Elise could keep up with him.

Their path had been pre-planned, Rowan recognising certain structures and formations of plants that they had marked as they went. A left turn here, right there. Through the building, over that section of rubble and down the street. The courtyard appeared before them, and Elise cast Silence in a corner for Melody before they continued onwards and out.

They were aiming to intercept what they thought was the most likely path into the city of one of the other two starting positions. There was no way to know which team had begun there, but they had to aim for one or the other. While killing off one of Hendrik’s team members was preferred, they had also decided that they couldn’t afford to be picky. Whichever team they encountered would have the unfortunate experience of learning Elise’s particular skill set first hand.

Continuing on at speed, they arrived at one of the few buildings in the city that was still somewhat intact. Rowan dropped Relativity to conserve his mana, and they entered together. Taking the stairs as quickly as they could, they burst onto the rooftop of the building with a line of sight to much of the nearby city. Elise had vanished from Rowan’s sight, visible to him only by Fracture’s special type of perception. Afraid of being seen, Rowan stayed in the shadow of the doorway while Elise approached the edge to scout out their enemy.

Only once he felt her reach the edge of the rooftop did Rowan realise that they had timed their arrival in an unfortunate manner. He stepped out onto the roof as he heard the sounds of Hendrik’s team below, inside the building. Elise whipped around as well, having had the same realisation.

Now trapped on the rooftop, Rowan looked around for somewhere to hide. They were too high up to jump off safely, and they couldn’t fight their way through an entire team.

Elise dashed through the doorway and back into the building, incredibly quick for how silently and invisibly she moved.

“Wait here,” she said in his mind.

Rowan did just that, standing to the side of the doorway, at the ready in case it wasn’t Elise who came back through the door.

A bare few moments later, Elise was back. Dagger in hand, she waited on the other side of the door.

“One is on their way up. Be ready.”

Rowan took a deep, but quiet, breath. They might be trapped up on the roof, but the person coming up would likely die without knowing that.

Footsteps sounded on the stairs as their enemy approached, the sound vanishing as Elise cast Silence on their position as well. One of Hendrik’s team, an ice wielding mage, stepped out of the doorway looking down at his feet, frowning as the sound of his steps vanished.

Realisation came to him too late as Rowan grabbed the man in a headlock. Rowan had to dodge a spear of ice jabbed over the man's shoulder, and felt a deep cold begin to spread through his arm, but held firm as he pushed Titan’s Burden into his enemy. Focused on Rowan, the man barely noticed as Elise appeared in front of him, dagger held in a reverse grip. She repeatedly stabbed him in the chest with blinding speed and, mouth open in a silent scream, the man died a violent, bloody death.

Not for the first time in this tournament, Rowan wondered if the sheer violence and brutality of their tests should have traumatised him. Helja’s mother, Esmerelda, had told him that achieving higher tiers would grant a certain resistance to trauma, but the experience was still a little… disturbing.

There would be time to think about that later though.

“The team down below is expecting him to report back in a moment,” Elise said.

Rowan knew that the information came from a particularly gruesome ability of hers, one of the last that she had shared over their months of training.

Innate Ability - Soul Reap

When you kill a being with a soul, you may learn things from it.

By increasing your Soul facet, you are able to increase the amount you can learn from a soul before it leaves for the afterlife.

By increasing your Soul facet, you gain an enhanced soul sense.

Increase your Soul facet to strengthen this ability.

Rowan wasn’t sure why exactly it worked in the Illusorum chamber, as they hadn’t actually killed the man, but didn’t complain.

“We need to get off this roof,” Rowan mouthed.

Elise nodded, and they both looked around. There was no other way down, the nearest building being just too far for them to jump, unless..

Rowan looked at the body he was still holding by the shoulders, then back at the doorway. Lifting it up, he went inside and chucked the body over the bannister. It fell, making a wet slapping sound as it hit a wall, then another bannister, on its way all the way down to land beside Hendrik far below. Ignoring the shouts of alarm and the questioning look on Elise’s face, Rowan brought Fracture out and pointed to the building near them.

“We have to jump.”

Elise looked at him skeptically.

“Don’t worry, Fracture is going to… shrink the distance for us.”

With that, Rowan got a little bit of a run up and jumped off the side of the rooftop, Elise barely a step behind. Fracture bent space and the distance seemed to shorten for just a moment. They landed on the other building, stepping lightly as pieces of the ruined structure fell away beneath them.

Knowing that their time was relatively short, Rowan and Elise began to climb down to the street below. Their job was done.

The run back to their team was short but sweet. With one of Hendrik’s team going down immediately and with time to recover the mana used to do it, their chances had just gotten better. Sure, they hadn’t taken the man down with Elise’s sneaking skills, but that didn’t matter.

Their moment of glory, however, was cut short. As they approached, they heard sounds of battle and the telltale signs of magic in the air. Their fears were confirmed as a massive fiery tornado appeared over the top of the buildings. While they had been off taking down one of Hendrik’s team, Felir and his team had attacked Helja, Arne and Melody.

Elise dropped Silence immediately, evident by the sound of Melody’s songs sounding out. Strength, mana and stamina flowed through Rowan as he heard the songs, and he pushed Relativity to its limit, exploding into the courtyard at top speed. Arne was holding back nearly an entire team by himself, as Helja desperately cast spells at Felir and his healer, Ester.

Seeing the desperate battle, Rowan threw himself into the fight.
