‘I might be in some trouble.’ Kyera mused as she looked at the enormous hydra in front of her. The shadowy multi-headed serpent towered over Jackson's side of the field, and the figure representing its Power hovering over its head was no less intimidating.

This wasn't the first time Kyera had seen a hydra, as an undead one had acted as the majordomo of the creepy castle she had briefly ended up in. The difference between the three-headed creature which wore spectacles and had chastised Denderra for not keeping up with her studies, and this one was comparable to the difference between Kyera and a bear.

The basic shape was the same; Kyera had two arms and legs, along with a head sitting in the center of her shoulders, just like a bear did. Yet that is where the similarities ended; this thing was orders of magnitude larger and more violent-looking than the one in the castle.

Then there was the fact that it seemed almost made of shadows that undulated in a way that was uncomfortable to look at, even while the giant creature was standing still.

‘Alright, think. What do we know about my opponent? First of all, he’s Rank nine, and he said he's sponsored by the Void Summoner's Guild, which are the guys who manage the transiteration gates. That explains all the creatures and Spells with Void Resonance, but doesn't really help me. He also said the word on his chest is ‘Misfits’ and that he got that weird cloak from his older brother. Still not useful.’

Biting the inside of her mouth just below her bottom lip, the apprentice Framer took another look at the entire field. This was where she should be focused, the Cards, not a man she didn’t know anywhere near well enough to read and predict.

On her own side of the barrier dividing the field was Emerald Grove Stag with one vitality, a two-thousand-power version of Hungry Fruit Bat Swarm, and Bell of the Burrows.

​

​

None of it had the Power to challenge the hydra, though it wasn’t a bad setup by any means. Kyera could use the bell to keep her creatures safe from the serpentine shadow monster, and the other creature Jackson had Manifested was weak enough that Kyera’s beasts had the edge over it in a fair fight. They definitely did not have the edge over it in creepiness; in that category, everything Jackson played was in a league of its own, even compared to the Undead horrors in Alley’s Library.

Standing to the right of the enormous hydra was a creature approximately man-sized, with beady red eyes and sickly pale skin. Its body was a gnarled mess of spikes and frills, with clawed feet and hands that reminded Kyera of a lizard, while its face was just as pale as the rest of it, but lacked the spikes and rough edges of its body; instead, the head was a bald shark-like shape above a mouth of jagged teeth.

Kyera didn’t know exactly what the pale monster did, but the turn before it had allowed Jackson to search for the Spell Card he’d used to avoid paying the hydra’s regular price.

​

More important to the Framer was that the Void Wretch Scout represented a path to attack Jackson himself without having to go through the hydra. That was always the danger when it came to filling your side of the field, yes, it gave you more opportunities for offense, but it could also act as a weakness.

Kyera would only have one chance to exploit that weakness before the pale wretch was destroyed, so she needed to make sure she got the most out of it that she could, and that meant taking some time to contemplate her Hand.

Jackson had more or less emptied his own Hand to get the hydra onto the field, which wasn’t a bad choice. It did however, mean if he had play any time Cards in his Library, they probably weren’t available to him right now. It didn’t represent a huge opening, yet it was still better than no opening at all.

The pair of players had bantered back and forth in good fun up until this point; in fact, Kyera was fairly confident Jackson had been trying to flirt with her at the start of the match. He was a good-looking man and a powerful player, but he had still made Kyera roll her eyes. Neither of them were talking now, not with the Challenge so obviously heading towards its crescendo. Jackson was silently watching the apprentice Framer, waiting to see how she would respond to the arrival of one of his mightiest Cards. Kyera, on the other hand, was contemplating the Cards she held and seeking how to make that response as effective as she could.

‘Cicada Crown, Petal Warden, Fox’s Bargain, Lady Thistlewake, Defenders Of The Grove.’

Despite being among the most Powerful Creatures she possessed, Kyera was concerned about playing Defenders of The Grove, or Lady Thistlewake, as they were too strong to be protected by Bell of The Borrows without being able to overcome the seven thousand Power of the shadowy hydra.

​

​

In fact, there was only one creature in Kyera’s whole collection who could be relied on to defeat something like that, and that was the lord of the whole Deck, The Wild Prince himself. With a little time and a little more luck, she could bring him out.

Having thought about her options a little, Kyera got her turn going with her first Creation Phase.

“Fox’s Bargain!”

Immediately, an ethereal fox with white fur that was much smaller than most creatures in a Library   scampered across Kyera’s side of the field. Stopping in front of the apprentice Framer, it tilted its head like it was considering her, before letting out a series of yips and then vanishing.

The three Cards that she drew floated in front of her Hand, facing Kyera. The first one was Serenade of Thorns, followed by Dam Guardian Captain and Windswept Masquerade. After a few seconds, Jackson selected the first Card the Framer had drawn.

“I was hoping to keep that one,” Kyera said with a glare as she sent Serenade of Thorns to her Crypt.

“Good!” Jackson called back with a grin.

Next, she activated the Relic in her Hand, which was the key to taking back control of this match.

‘Cicada Crown! I target Emerald Grove Stag.”

​

The Relic Manifested but instead of appearing atop the stag’s head as it should have, it fell to the ground a few feet in front of Kyera.

“Huh?”  She began to form an actual question when the gigantic hydra let out a series of roars.

Still confused and off-put, Kyera moved onto her War Phase. She would have redrawn to replace the crown, but her Hand was already full thanks to the effect of Fox’s Bargain. It might not have the crown atop its antlered head, but the apprentice Framer still sent her Emerald Grove Stag to attack the Void Wretch Scout. With ease, the hooved creature impaled its target before shaking it off and trampling it. The pale monster vanished in a burst of blue light, and the stag got its chance to attack Jackson directly.

For some reason, Kyera had expected the amber-skinned man to move like Alley did, with an almost preternatural smoothness. He was admittedly fast, but there was an awkwardness to his movements that was not at all like Kyera’s friend. The dark-haired man managed to scramble out of the way of the Emerald Grave Stag’s charge, though it was only just, and Kyera was pretty sure its flank clipped him from the way the man fell over as the Creature surged past.

Slowly pulling himself back to his feet, Jackson shook his head. “ That was a good try, but you should just yield now.” He called in an obviously annoyed tone that made Kyera think he might have been embarrassed by his own lack of grace.

“Yeah, I don’t think so,” Kyera replied.

“Suit yourself.”

Kyera ended her turn, and to her surprise, Jackson skipped right to his War Phase.

“Manducare, Eat!” He roared while gesturing at the stag. With an appalling speed that belied its size, the hydra launched its numerous heads forward.

“I activate Bell of the Burrows,” declared Kyera. The Relic would ring throughout the arena and send the stag underground and negate the attack, just like it had twice already in this match.

Horror and realization flooded Kyera in equal measure as the hydra roared again, and there was no echoing ring.

“It negates Relics,” she whispered as the huge monster ripped apart her stag, and the Antlerkin was sent to the Crypt. Immediately, she burst into a run so as to be less easy to hit once the monster turned its attention on her. Even being a moving target wasn’t enough to stop the first head of the serpentine monster from slamming into her and sending Kyera flying. She coughed as the wind was knocked out of her by the blue barrier at the back of the Arena. That would have been bad enough, but it meant that the next head had no problems latching onto her arm with its dagger-sized teeth. Contemptuously flicking its head, the hydra tossed Kyera to the next head, which bit down onto her mid-section, easily tearing chunks of flesh from her and tossing her upwards so yet another head could continue the assault.

Knowing that there was no other option, the brown-haired woman screamed her surrender.

“I yield!” Instantly, the attack ended, as the Arena began to collapse and the Creatures began to Demanifest. On the upside, the Hydra ceased tearing into Kyera, but it still left her to fall two dozen feet onto the hard-packed sand of the stadium floor.
