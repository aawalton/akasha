Noah perceived it a moment later as it entered the area of his awareness. To call it an oversized skeleton would convey the right general impression, but it was far denser than any ordinary skeleton, and upon closer examination the construct of bones seemed to have been assembled from what had to be hundreds of different creatures. Skulls of wolves woven into its legs, massive ribcages forming its torso, spinal columns twisted into arms that ended in clawed hands made of sharpened femurs, its head a grotesque fusion of beast and humanoid skulls. The contents of a dozen graveyards fused into one.

[Bone Amalgam (Uncommon) - Level 680]

“Monsters of this nature form around a core,” Zax informed them. “That is their weakness.”

Good to know.

Bun Bun was latched onto one knee, gnawing away, his acidic bite doing such minuscule damage that the monster didn’t seem to have even noticed the small creature. Which only made Bun Bun bite more angrily.

The creature’s head swiveled toward them as it emerged around the corner. Thirty eyeless sockets burned with pale colorless flame, fixed on the party with undeniable malice. Dark runes pulsed along its bones and in one hand, it carried the complete spine of some enormous creature, each vertebra sharpened to a lethal point.

A low rumble of eagerness ground from it, then it charged. Spurs of calcified bone burst from the canyon floor in a ring thirty feet wide, razor-edged and knee-high with each heavy step. The ground around the monster grew more and more densely packed as it ran toward them. It trod carelessly on its own thicket of spikes, adding the sinister crackle of snapping bone to the earth-shaking rumble of its footsteps.

“Scatter!” Aurelia shouted, flames erupting from her feet to propel her through the air in the split second between its appearance and closing in on them.

Noah threw himself sideways, felt something rake across his calf, then triggered [Arcane Step], teleporting backward just in time. The Bone Amalgam’s spine-weapon crashed into the ground where he’d been standing, leaving behind a crater six feet deep.

This thing is going to be a pain. Noah thought. But a stronger part of him felt only fierce anticipation. He’d gotten very tired of prolonged but ultimately dangerless engagements. Everything within him yearned to rise to the occasion and win. Defeat something worth defeating.

Before Noah could properly reorient, it’d already drawn its weapon back to slash at the flaming flying berserker.

“Look out!”

Zax admitting to not knowing something was oddly terrifying, though perhaps it was only because he was about to leave them to their own devices.
