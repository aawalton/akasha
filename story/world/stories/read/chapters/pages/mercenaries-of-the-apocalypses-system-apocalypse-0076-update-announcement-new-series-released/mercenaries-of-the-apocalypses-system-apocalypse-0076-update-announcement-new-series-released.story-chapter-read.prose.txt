Hello mercs! I think you all deserve an update (and an explination).

Six months ago, I came to the realisation that Mercs had some glaring flaws which needed addressing. Namely, Andy. The original concept for this character was that this guy is EXCITED about apocalypses; he's a geek about them. However, I felt like that never came across in the tone quite right. Andy was too moody for my liking, and much too series 90% of the time.

Furthermore, I have come up with some big additions to his Augmentation, and a LitRPG-lite addition: Andy's 'Apocal-pendix.'

Currently, I am re-writing Mercs Book 1 with a brand new tone, with more levity and voice. I shall upload the re-write of Chapter 1 as a teaser of what's to come. I also have twelve chapters of Book 2 which I will release over the coming week. I am so passionite about this project, however, I was not able to lean on it financially upon release, and did not think it was ready for Amazon. (My biggest flaw: I'm a perfectionist).

And so, I have hunkered down and done my market research, and written an entirely different series: Primordial Unleashed. Book 1 is complete (all 210,000 words). I am releasing it now at a rate of 3 chapters/day. I am betting BIG on this series opening the door for me to make a measly living out of writing so that I can do it sustainable.

I'm going to get real with you, I'm on the edge of broke. I have been writing part/full-time for almost two years now, and am down to my last cup of oats for breakfast. Any support you can show--any clicks, follows, shares and reviews--will make a huge difference, and could be what keeps me going in this race.

I will always keep writing--success or not--until the day I die. But I may have to divide my time with a job which I have no passion for if things don't work out soon. That's alright, I've accepted that. But you know what, nothing's certain yet. I still have hope.

Now, let me tell you about Primordial Unleashed:

An ancient power stirs within Skippii–one not granted by the Gods. One they fear.

When a duel with a heretic magus leaves Skippii half-dead, he awakens a forbidden magic the Pantheon tried to erase from history. If his secret is discovered, execution is certain. Unsure who to trust, he must choose: remain a loyal soldier, or master his volcanic fury and forge his own path in defiance of the divine.

Above, a war brews among the Gods that threatens to reshape the world. But the fate of an age won’t be decided by the heavens alone—not while Skippii still draws breath.

Thank you for your support! I'm sorry I had to put the brakes on Mercs, but I promise, it will be back 100 times better than before!
