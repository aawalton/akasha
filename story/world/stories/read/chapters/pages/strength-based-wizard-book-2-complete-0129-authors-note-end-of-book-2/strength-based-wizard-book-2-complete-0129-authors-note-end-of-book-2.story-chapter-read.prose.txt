We have reached the end of Book 2 of STRENGTH BASED WIZARD!

For all of you who've stuck with the story through its second installment, you are the best! It's always a delight reading comments and seeing so much engagement with the story. I finished Book 1 almost 10 months ago. If you'd asked me then how long it would take me to write and complete Book 2, I would have said 4-6 months (which is still slow by Royal Road standards). My full-time job demanded my full attention during multiple long stretches (where I was working around the clock for a week or two at a time). And, of course, there were a handful of illnesses thrown in there (having a kid does wonders for your immune system) and not to mention personal travel and other events. Needless to say, it was a long journey and you all have been absolutely patient and understanding with me. More than anyone deserves when there's an expectation of consistency that authors are held to. It means a lot to me!

In this Author's Note, I have three questions for you all.

What's next for STRENGTH BASED WIZARD:

Unlike after Book 1, I will not be taking a break between books. I will be diving right into Book 3. The first chapter should be done and posted next week! I will be posting Joseph's end of Book 2 character sheet before that first chapter goes live.

Book 3 is (at least as currently planned), largely set on Earth. It will focus on Stage 2 of the God Game, which will be crazy and perfectly set the stage for the third and final stage of this divine contest. Book 4, which is currently planned as the finale to the series, should be a rollercoaster ride.

Book 3 and, to a lesser extent, Book 4, are currently outlined but not yet drafted. There are still plenty of gaps and details that I'll need to fill in as I write.

So, with that, I ask the first question of this Author's Note: What are things you want to see in Book 3? What aspects of the world do you want explored further? What characters do you want to see on screen? Are there particular questions you want answers to?

What's happening with the Series:

I am going to do a quick editing pass through Book 2 and then fire it off to the publisher. At that point, I imagine I'll have a more definitive timeline on the publication for Book 1, which is edited and should be ready to go soon (pending audiobook production). Book 2 will go into editing process.

With that said, I know many stories on Royal Road eventually swap out for their officially produced publication book cover. I will be doing that soon, as well. But first would like to share the official cover for STRENGTH BASED WIZARD, Book 1. It was done by the talented and masterful Luciano Fleitas. I think he did an excellent job!

The second question of this Author's Note: What do you think about the official cover for Book 1?
