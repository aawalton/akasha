“Hey, jimmy, is the industrial printer working yet? I need some medical supplies printed.”

“Uh, what the hell is going on?” I asked, confusion in my voice.

I watched him sigh before he looked down at me and then waved his hand at the cuffs.

“For my protection, you have been accused of the crime of malicious sabotage of a Federation Carrier by releasing a vicious virus onto one of its servers. And you have been charged with high treason, by order of captain Luscious Rivers, I have placed you under arrest”

“That is your right, guild Mistress Mackenzie, and I will cease all communication with you until your lawyer is present, as now required by galactic law.”

“What happened? One second I'm talking to a doctor of my Own clan as she assures me of my safety, the next she is hopping me up on some fast acting sleep medication and I wake up in handcuffs being told that I have been arrested.”

“Yea… that would be Captain River's influence, he is playing devil's advocate, by that I mean he is playing the bad guy, on orders of course, and I have been ordered to be the good guy. I opened and closed my mouth a few times, before slowly sinking into my bed. I rested my head against the pillow as I let out a loud sigh.

“Okay, first, I don't know what the charges against me are, I think your husband had a huge speech planned or maybe not since I asked for a lawyer. Speaking of, does the galactic federation lawyers have the same sort of things as my earth lawyers have where whatever you say to them is privileged, and they can't repeat it or record it to anyone? “I am so confused right now”

“Okay, first military lawyers don't have that privilege thing, civilian lawyers do but, yea… anyway, basically you just told me a commanding officer of a Galactic Federation Carrier suspects his officers of sabotage and treason, but his Communications are monitored/compromised so the only way to send up a red flag is to send someone into a black ops mission with a code phrase to use to get out of all crimes done during the course of the mission.
