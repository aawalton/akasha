I picked up [Shadow Powder]-”

On the fifth cast, she said nothing, and her mouth didn’t move as she launched the [Ice Lance] at Sky.

Sky yelped as her [Solar Shell] was on cooldown and had to dodge the spell, “Nice job!”

The final preparations were only needed by the casters and magic users of the guild, as they hadn’t yet acquired someone to level the Artificer craft. The craft of Artificing was the creation of magical tools, devices, and channeling sources. Artificing works nearly hand in hand with Enchanting, as they often need to enchant parts for a better finished product.

There is an aspect of refinement that also works with Artificers, though it is inherently part of the craft and not required by an actual Refiner. Base ingredients come through a Refiner, which Artificers then use to create things like wands, staves, scepters, and magical foci in general.

The current casters included Sky, Kesa, Sera, Atticus, Amara, and the two druids from [Nature Green]. Most clerics chose a melee weapon, but Amara Goldtree enjoyed more of a caster build, with a staff. Atticus had the [Stormhorn Scepter] from the Zaphid Caverns and wouldn’t need an upgrade until Tier 3.

[Nature Green] was still out of the city and had yet to go through their tier 2 ascension rituals, so they would have to wait until after the Tournament for new gear.

Zedra was at the trade hall of the Adventuring Association for most of the day, keeping in contact with the rest of the guild via guild and party chat. He had been keeping an eye out on the market for weapons that he could purchase, or at least a base for Sky to enchant. He was able to acquire two staves that were drops being sold by other adventurers, maybe even players. The first was for Kesa, who wasn’t participating in the PvP tournament, but still could use a weapon. The water-attuned [Staff of Surging Waters] increased all water-based healing and even had an innate spell, [Golden Nova]. It sent out a holy-attuned nova of healing water upon activation, at a target location.

The second staff Zedra found was perfect for Amara Goldtree, called [Holy Warstaff]. It was just a simple warstaff that added holy damage to physical attacks and increased her holy affinity at the same time. It was simple and perfect for tier 2.

That left Sky and Sera.

Finding solar attuned equipment is a tough endeavor. Very few places had the solar attunement, and even fewer enemies used it. That made getting drops very difficult, and the only reason Sky was able to get enough solar essence dust and shards was because they dropped when she dealt damage and killed enemies. That was a standard for all players, especially after an ascension ritual.

In the lore of Zenith Affinity, if a mage were to attack a monster, that mage’s power imbues itself into the creature. When it died, that elemental essence that remained in the monster coalesced into essence dust, shards, crystals, or gemstones, depending on the tier of power.

The Ice attunement was much easier to find, though heading north would allow for more results. Thus, Sera and Sky were just given basic unattuned staves, which they would have to enchant. Sera was learning enchanting from Sky, but she was new to the system, and her crafting level was low. At the end of the day before the tournament, Sky and Sera were in the large crafting hall within the Adventuring Association.

Sera had a question as Sky set up everything they would need for the enchantments, including the staves that Zedra bought them.

“So, your affinity-improving enchantment is the highest tier?” Sera asked.

“Ehh, that isn’t quite the correct wording. It’s more like an efficiency thing. Saying ‘highest tier’ implies that it would be a tier 4 enhancement, but it’s more like, at my crafting level of 27, the enchantment I can do is the highest level of affinity improvement we could get.”

“And where did you get that from? Even looking through the menu, I only see attribute-enhancing enchantments."

“I got it from the first flame spear we got as a drop. I gave it to Zaria back when we were level 15 or whatever it was. I let her use it until we needed to start leveling our crafting, and I disenchanted it to learn the enchantment, and it was the highest efficiency.”

“What is it called?”

“[Empowered Affinity Enchantment], and it changes based on the essence shards we use. So, let’s start with your staff. It will take 10 ice essence shards for the enchantment, a tier 2 enchantment.”

Sky opened the crafting menu, centering her attention on the staff. She pulled her enchanting tool, and an open window on her HUD appeared just above the staff. She pressed the tip of the tool onto one of the ice essence shards, where it was promptly consumed like ink to a quill. She then began to follow the runic enchantment on the menu that would increase the ice affinity. 10 runes, 10 essence shards, and the enchantment was finished with a flourish of visible ice attuned magic.

The runes flashed and sank into the staff.

“And it’s all set! This will be perfect until tier 3, or until you find something that suits you better,” Sky said, handing the staff over to Sera.

“Amazing. So, if at tier 3, I disenchant this weapon, I will have the enchantment?”

“Indeed!”

Sky finished enchanting her own staff, first purging the remaining ice magic from her enchanting wand, then going to work to make a solar attuned staff. She will be ready for the tournament tomorrow.
