Don chose a direction from the top of the hill and went towards the trees that were older and more robust. After twenty minutes he was in a forest that was very old and thick, and none of the moonlight could pass. There was a sense of heaviness in the air. He moved slowly, trying to make as little noise as possible. Soon he spotted a hunched creature looking at him. It clearly saw him, because the glowing eyes were clearly fixed on Don. It was a good thing Don could see what was approaching, because from the way it moved it was clearly hunting and trying to remain unseen.

Don let it come closer, seemingly oblivious to it. The skulking shape climbed over a boulder and was clearly looking to ambush Don. Don passed the boulder, but veering just slightly further away from it than he originally intended. The skulking shape growled and attacked. It was some kind of a monkey. Quite slim with a long tail. It jumped high and Don sidestepped away from the boulder, making the creature fall flat. Don immediately climbed on top of it, but it was deadly strong. It bucked and thrashed, making it hard for Don to pin it down. When it created enough space, it slipped a long tail under Don’s belly. Don felt a lurch in his stomach when barbs ripped at his skin.

[Health at 91%]

[Energy at 78%]

[Debuff inflicted: Mild bleeding]

Don grabbed and yanked at the tail and the creature screeched. He rolled it around the monkey-creature’s neck and pulled. The creature spun violently and they both sprang up from the ground. They measured each other, which gave Don time to use Identify.

[Barbtail Gibbon, Level 6]

This was a tough foe. Not only was it higher level than Don, this foe was much larger and stronger than a Vulpin. It was slightly shorter than a human, and despite it having a slight build, the muscles in its arms and legs were like roped steel.

The gibbon’s tail whipped about as they circled each other. Don was fairly sure they were equally matched in Agility and Power. It would come down to smarts this time. If Don could pin the monkey down from its tail, he’d cut down the enemy’s movement.

As good a plan as any, and that was all Don had time to think, because the monkey screeched and lunged. It tried to swipe down at him with an overhead blow with both of its claws. Don darted backwards and kicked the monkey in its chin when it landed. The gibbon grabbed the leg, but that was a mistake. Don punched it in the face.

Dazed from these attacks, the gibbon jumped back and attacked low this time. It darted in fast, and curled its tail around one of Don’s legs.

The monkey screamed and swiped at Don with its claws in a rabid fit of fury. Don had the disadvantage of having one less limb to attack and grapple with, but he did have a size advantage. So he just kept pressing the monkey back, slipping punches at its face. Eventually the monkey let his leg go, which was when Don tackled it and slammed it on the ground. On its back, the monkey was much less dangerous. It was still attacking with absolutely everything it got, and Don got a large gash on his forehead that started spurting blood, half of which ran down his eye.

[Health at 67%]

[Energy at 55%]

[Debuff inflicted: Moderate bleeding]

The pain was exquisite, but now was not the time to falter. When the monkey scrambled up and away from Don, kicking all the while and breaking Don’s nose, he managed to grab the tail.

“I got you now, motherfucker.”

Don yanked back the tail and drove a knee into the gibbon’s face. It fell on its back from the force and Don sat on it and started pummeling. Soon the monkey’s face cracked in and It finally fell limp, letting out a gasp. Soon the light faded from its eyes and Don got a notification.

[General Skill Increased: Unarmed Fighting!]

[Unarmed Fighting Level 3]

[You have slain [Barbtail Gibbon – lvl 6] – You have gained experience for defeating an enemy. 9 System Points earned]

[’DING!’ Race: [Evolutor (spawn)] has reached level 5 – Stat points allocated, +2 Free Attribute Point]

[Level 5 Milestone: Evolution Choice Available!]

Don didn’t want to waste any time, so he used Devour on the gibbon to top up his Health and Energy. The wounds knitted and blood started pouring off his hairline. The squirting had been fairly disgusting. He also received 16 Evolution Points. Which was quite nice. He wasn’t still sure if he should upgrade his Night Vision, because it seemed to do the job currently.

With that thought, he brought up his system screen and the Evolution interface to look at what choices the system had given to him.

There were three Evolutions for him to choose from. [Regenerative Tissue], [Retractable Claws] and [Camouflage Skin]

It turned out each of the choices was rather appealing. Regenerative Tissue did what it said on the tin. It would expedite the natural healing process of the body and scale with Constitution. It was certainly an option. If Don didn’t have Devour, this was an instant choice. However, even with the Devour this was an appealing choice. Don was no fool. Sure, he seemed to have a natural aptitude to this situation. It didn’t bother him mentally, and he was willing to fight and trust his instincts. That had given him some measure of success. Call it luck or skill or a combination of both. But there was bound to be an enemy he could not beat. Maybe he would need to escape. Devour required him to always win. What if he met a human who hurled a spear at him, or shot an arrow? He might manage to escape, but still bleed to death. That’s why having overall a tougher body was a good idea.

On the other hand, Retractable Claws would increase his offensive power tremendously. According to the description, the claws would be around two inches long and hard as metal. The ability would scale with Power and his Unarmed Fighting skill. Being able to swipe and stab with the claws instead of getting down and dirty with every beast he faced, would be a massive improvement in the safety and survival of his plight. Executing surprise attacks with his Stealth would also become much more lethal with a sharp weapon. Don figured it was probably Gandhi who had said “with great offense comes great defense”. Even if it wasn’t, what the hell did Gandhi know about surviving in the wilderness, fighting monster-beasts?

The third option was also interesting. It would provide Don a natural camouflage, like a chameleon. He would meld in his environment and increase his natural stealthiness. Indeed, the mutation would scale with Stealth and Agility. This would obviously be a powerful ability against humans and beasts alike, especially during the day when he didn’t have the advantage of darkness and Night Vision.

However, Don was still inclined to follow his initial philosophy. The reason why he was mainly increasing his Agility and Power, was because with higher offensive stats, he could kill more enemies faster and thus level faster. The Retractable Claws would help him do exactly that.

He confirmed the choice.

[Evolution Acquired: Retractable Claws]

[Retractable Claws (F-grade): You can morph your fingernails into metal-like claws at will. The claws are two inches long, half an inch thick and have the qualities of bronze. +1 to Agility, +2 to Power]

With some trial and error, Don was able to flick his wrist and flare open his fingers, and the claws would flare out like a switchblade. When he closed his hands into fists, the claws retracted. Very simple to use. He also tried keeping his hands still and willing the claws to extend and retract. It worked but it was slower. Don would have to later test if his Mental Attribute or something else would affect that.

Then he looked at his Evolution interface.

He had 74 Evolution Points. He looked at his two Evolutions, Night Vision and Retractable Claws. Both had the upgrade option available. Don instantly chose the claws, and was a bit disappointed to see that it cost him 60 Evolution Points to upgrade, as opposed to 30 required by the Night Vision.

Don shrugged. It had to be done. The vision he had now was serviceable, and already gave him a distinct advantage over other humans. The claws were what he needed now. He was tired of wrestling with the damn beasts and getting beat up constantly. Sure the Devour kept him topped up, but his mind could only endure so much constant pain. Already he was getting mentally a bit tired, despite his Energy seemingly being full. He would need to sleep eventually.

Don accepted the upgrade and immediately got a notification:[60 Evolution Points spent]

[Evolution Upgraded!]

[Retractable Claws (F-grade)] -> [Retractable Claws (E-grade)]

Don immediately inspected his upgraded ability.

[Retractable Claws (E-grade): You can morph your fingernails into metal-like claws at will. The claws are 2.2 inches long, half an inch thick and have the qualities of iron. +2 to Agility, +4 to Power]

The upgrade had doubled the stats given by the Evolution! That was absolutely massive. The power from the attributes surged into Don and it felt sweet. Don decided to also spend his Free Attribute Points from hitting level 5 in Agility and Power, further pushing his offensive capabilities. It just felt so good to have such an amazing physical body. He jumped into the air and managed to reach nine or ten feet. He fell gracefully on his legs, bending his knees slightly. Don had now completely surpassed normal human limitations. But he felt like this was only the beginning.

Don spent some time testing the limits of his ability. He sprinted at trees with high speed and sliced at the bark as he passed them. He tried different speeds, different angles for the claws, different distances from the trees. His energy ticked down from 100% to 85% in half an hour, but the sprinting yielded results.

[General Skill increased: Sprinting!]

[Sprinting Level 2]

This of course prompted him to try out the variations again, because he was just that much faster and more agile when he was running at top speed. Eventually he was satisfied with the amount of repetition and the stable results it granted. The two trees he had been using as practice dummies were kind of messed up, but Don looked at them with pride. They were a testament to his diligence.

“Now the game has changed for me completely. I am much stronger and faster. If I can approach something with stealth and then run them down at top speed and swipe a weak spot, I can instantly defeat a foe with low Constitution.”

The possibilities of accelerating his growth gave Don a grin he couldn’t quite suppress. Sporting a goofy smile Don ran deeper into the forest, eager to test his new abilities.
