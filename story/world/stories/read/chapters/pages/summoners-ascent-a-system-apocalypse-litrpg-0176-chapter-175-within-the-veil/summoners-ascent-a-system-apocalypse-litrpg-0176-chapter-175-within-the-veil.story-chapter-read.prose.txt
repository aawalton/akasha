With just a soft jump, Ashton leapt to the top of the nearby buildings. When one was projecting themselves into the veil, they weren't stopped by the same physical laws that restricted them until that point. If he wanted to, Ashton could probably just fly around, but imagining was a little hard.

However, jumping around like this was something that he had done plenty before, so it really felt like second nature to Ashton.

The view from up here, on the rooftops, really was beautiful. Even if there was no true colour, smell, or even touch here, Ashton was going to have to live with this. After all, this was the only way he was going to be able to see the horizon anytime soon.

Ashton took a deep breath and smiled, as he leapt of from the rooftop once more. His first stop were the botanical gardens, since they were so close nearby. The amount of mana flowing through here was really quite immense, so the imprint was fairly strong, and there were a ton of new-born dryads here.

There were some that already seemed closer to reaching the 'lesser' stage, though stepping over that boundary was where most of them failed anyway.

These little fellows, taking the forms of insects, snakes, or young monkeys, ever-shifting between numerous vague shapes, were really doing their best.

'Good luck,' Ashton thought as he continued through the gardens. When he reached the mana well here in this place, probably the first thing he noticed was the 'veil fragment' at the centre of the well.

Or rather, was it still the same thing from this perspective? Whichever it was really didn't matter, though. It was just a sphere of immense 'existence' stuck in a single spot. Ashton still had no idea why his technique was able to dislodge these things, though it didn't matter much anyway.

Rather, what he was focused on the most was the elemental that was sleeping here. It was a young serpent, coiled up right below the fragment at the centre of the veil, like it was basking in the sun.

Its body was slightly shifting here and there, but it was mostly its size or length, as well as the pattern of its scales. Overall, this dryad seemed to be well on the way to forming a sense of self.

Ashton was positive that this little one was going to make it. He wasn't sure why he thought that; it was just a gut-feeling that he couldn't shake.

Maybe when he woke up, Ashton should come back here and call for a dryad. If he contracted this guy, he would be more than happy. Plus, a dryad wasn't a bad elemental to contract anyway, considering how many different plants Ashton would need in the future to make ink.

But for now, he should leave the elemental be. Ashton jumped up again, climbing to the top of the tree crowns, and then continued on with his trip through town.

There were a few places where he found elementals that seemed like they would soon reach the 'lesser' stage, but none of them even came close to the special existences that Ashton had already created here.

He was actually looking for them as well, though they ended up coming to him first, from wherever they wandered off to while unsummoned. The five elementals that Ashton had already contracted.

It was when he was just taking a light stroll through the peaceful streets of Drakewood for the first time since he came back, that the sylph shot straight into his chest.

Rather, the elemental actually passed right through him, but before long, fluttered over toward him and sat down on Ashton's shoulder. The undine reached him soon thereafter, and the gnome shot out from the ground, almost making Ashton trip as he tried not to step on the elemental.

The salamander and wisp arrived roughly at the same time; they weren't exactly the fastest when moving independently from Ashton's magical control, but they were truly just as enthusiastic to see him.

'It hasn't even been that long since I had you all summoned, calm down' he thought, and it seemed like the elementals actually heard and understood him well enough, since they at least stopped pushing directly into him. 'Really, how did you even tell that I was here?'

With a laugh, he decided to briefly reciprocate the affection, petting and cuddling with them all. The salamander seemed the most excited about that. While Ashton could touch them easily enough, it was hard to go beyond a brief pet since the salamander would still light anything else that wasn't directly Ashton on fire immediately.

'Do you guys know any spots where some stronger, or unique, elementals are gathered?' Ashton figured he should just ask them right away. Since they were wandering around town, just hopping around wherever they could, he figured that they might be able to show him. And they quickly did, of course. The undine and salamander were a bit weak and were shrinking ever so slightly down, since they weren't able to be close to their element right now. And sadly, there wasn't enough mana in the air for them to manifest into the physical world just yet.

Elementals generally had the power to influence their surrounding when manifested into the world. Winds would become stronger around sylphs, and water would gather more and become clean around undines. Manifested salamanders actually caused quite a lot of fires to spread through forests or cities sometimes, so that was something to look out for at some point.

Though, lesser elementals shouldn't have such a strong effect... but maybe just enough to let them rest a little and get their energy back up.

'If it gets bad, we can try to find you guys some places to rest for a while, alright?' Ashton suggested, and the elementals seemed to agree. Though it really wouldn't be hard, either. They should be able to find the closest source of their element as easily as it was to take a breath.

But for now, they seemed like they were alright, so Ashton continued walking with them all practically sticking to him like glue. They must be excited to have Ashton in their home. He must admit, this was an incredible experience for him as well.

The places where the elementals guided him were rather unexpected, all in all, but they definitely were able to show him quite a few special elementals. There were some carbuncles, gem elementals, in a jewellery store. Pulvi, elementals of dust or sand, were gathered at a small gravel football pitch. Of course, there were plenty of atsali, metal elementals, gathered around the streets. They were actually fairly rare in most places in Lumia, but it made sense that there were more of them on earth considering how much metal was literally everywhere.

But the ones that surprised Ashton the most were the chions, the frost elementals that were roaming around the costco.

Ashton had caused this entire area to be filled with frost and snow, but that alone being enough to cause the birth of these frost elementals was... stunning. It was unlikely that any of them would make it to the point where they could even manifest, however.

Ashton knew that, while the mana in the area was keeping everything freezing for now, that would change sooner or later. The snow would melt and things would warm up, and even if an elemental reached the 'lesser' stage... it wouldn't be able to survive in the middle of summer.

'I'm sorry,' Ashton thought. These fellows were struggling to make it, but it simply wouldn't happen as it was. What Ashton did ended up making them fight a battle for something that they could never attain.

This, frankly, did sour Ashton's mood quite a bit. It was unfair. But maybe Ashton could come here later and let at least one of them survive by forming a contract.

To cheer Ashton up a bit, the elementals showed him around a lot more. However, he noticed that they were... hiding something. They were guiding him all around town, since it was easy to traverse. Ashton could move from one end of Drakewood to the other in a few minutes if he wanted to.

But there was one place that they seemed to vehemently avoid. It was one that Ashton was pretty damn familiar with, though.

'Sorry, I know you guys don't want me to go there,' Ashton said apologetically, and leapt toward the building that he lived in for the past three years. After leaving it back then, Ashton really didn't even look back once. There wasn't the slightest urge for him to return here, but now that he was pushing open his old bedroom window from the outside, he was actually feeling pretty emotional.

It was weird to see this place through the veil. With a smile, he dropped down onto his old couch, figuring he might as well take a short breather here. But it was just as Ashton thought.

The elementals were scared to be here.

'What's going on? There's really nothing to be scared of here,' Ashton pointed out, but the five of them seemed to think differently. They were practically hiding behind him. They were hiding from something to his left.

Confused, Ashton stood back up. He walked over to the front door, which was the only thing they could really be hiding from. There wasn't anything here, so he walked into his neighbour's flat, right across the hall, and decided to take a look in there.

This was a pretty large complex, so there were tons of flats here that something weird could be hiding in. But really, even here, there wasn't anything. Even so, the elementals stayed behind in Ashton's apartment, so something must be here.

Maybe it was on the other side of the building? It was an 'O' shaped complex, surrounding a relatively big courtyard. There wasn't really much there, though.

Some small gardens belonging to the people living on the ground floor, a tiny playground, and a wooden gazebo that could be used by the people living here. Not that Ashton ever did.

He was about to climb out of his neighbour's window that faced the other side, when Ashton stopped in his track.

Though he expected to see the imprint of the courtyard down there, that wasn't it at all.

Instead, what Ashton saw was was the rapidly shifting, deeply unsettling form of a wyvern, sleeping right there surrounded by the tall walls that was this complex.

The Crimson Wyvern had made this place its nest.
