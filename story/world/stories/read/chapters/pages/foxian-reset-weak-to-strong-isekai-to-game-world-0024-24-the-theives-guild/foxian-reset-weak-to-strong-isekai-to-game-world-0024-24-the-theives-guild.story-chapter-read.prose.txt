The group traveled underground, deeper into the darkness of the sands. It was getting harder to see in front of them until Voxx pressed a switch on the wall, lighting a row of lanterns.

"This way leads to the guild. We are almost there."

Voxx promised as they followed him through the underground trails. Palona was struggling to keep moving. She was still affected by the spell she used earlier. Kae took note by watching her. The first thing they needed to do when they reached the guild was to rest before going on.

"You said Perry is immortal, Voxx?"

Kae asked as they continued their descent.

"Yeah, I've killed him countless times. I suspect more is going on than an annoying bird getting superpowers."

The assassin had venom in his words. It sounded like Perry had been the bane of his existence for a while, and Kae and the group picked up on that really fast.

"That thunderbolt spell is a very specific skill. I think I know where it's from. And if my theory is right... I think we have bigger problems than Perry."

Kae thought aloud. She had been thinking about it since Perry was first struck by that spell, restoring his health to max. But she put it aside for the time. But being faced with the idea that the Fallen Angel is immortal, she had to put her thoughts into words.

"Do you know where that spell came from, Kae?"

Dareth spoke up.

"It's a theory, more so because other Fallen Angels have that spell. But seeing as we're in the Chalaco Desert, only adds to the credibility of it."

The trio followed Voxx down deeper into the hidden passageway. The Night Stalker looked back at the three, bewildered at the rough trio.

"Again, why are you three traversing the desert? It's dangerous, as you can tell."

"We spoke with Chalaco's leader, Cain. He asked us to find the Thieves' Guild and ask for help."

Palona explained, pressing herself to go on.

"They've already sent a few your way, but they never made it back."

Voxx paused, crossed his arms, and leaned back on the rocks of the underground passageway. The stiff dirt air made it harder to breathe, suffocating like being strangled by the oppressive atmosphere.

"The villagers that came to find us, don't worry, we saved them. They are safe in the Thieves' Guild hideout for now..."

He groaned, thinking back to their own problems...

"We've tried to send people into town, and they haven't come back either. It's mutual, we're in dire straits. So, unless we solve this problem, nothing will change."

The assassin admitted, regrettably.

"As you saw, when Perry is wounded, his own thunderstorm provides healing for him. But, I think the Foxian might have reached the same conclusion as I."

Voxx groaned again, then kicked off the wall and continued walking. But before Voxx could lead too far, Kae raced up to him and met his stride.

"My name is Kae Loredom, this is Palona Vertra and Dareth Darkmere. Thanks for saving us, by the way."

"No worries. It's what we do. What is your theory, fox?"

The fox thought about something that's been bothering her from the beginning. The dark tornadoes and clouds all reminded her of something other than the big bird with the wacky tongue. She turned back to Voxx, the Night Stalker. It all added up for the lore veteran.

"Voxx, do you know of the Fallen Angel hidden deep in the pyramids?"

Kae asked a leading question. Of course, Kae knew that Voxx would know this Fallen Angel. His lore story was deeply tied to his survival of the "Dungeon of Death." But she'd never considered actually having a conversation with Voxx like this. In a way, no lore was as interesting as the one told by the mouth of the actual character.

Voxx scratched the back of his head. His sharp gaze lowered as his tone had a hint of annoyance.

"Yes, I've dealt with Raha, the elite Fallen Angel, before. I've been thinking the same thing as you then, I'm sure Foxian."

"Thinking what?"

Dareth asked.

"That Raha, the Anubis King of the Desert, might be behind this chaos. The elite Fallen Angel found in the Dungeon of Death."

Voxx groaned as he led them into the Thieves' Guild entrance. When they entered the hidden guild, there were plenty of members hiding from the storm above. Groans and aches from previous battles echoed in the hollow hideout. Wounded being bandaged up, some lying down on beds, and being aided by their fellow guildmates. Palona raced in front of the group, looking at the wounded. Even being exhausted herself, she was more worried for the people before her.

"This is awful. You've all been through heck and back. Where are you when we need you, Rexia?"

Palona put her hands together and pressed her forehead to it, praying to her Goddess sister, who had long forgotten this place.

"As you see, we have plenty of wounded. I only have a small squad left, and if we want to get out of this chaos, we have to deal with that crazy Fallen Angel somehow."

Voxx looked at Kae, who seemed to be the most knowledgeable about the Fallen Angels.

"And you agree that Raha might be behind Perry's sudden strength, Kae Loredom?"

The Foxian nodded.

"Only because that dark thunder spell is his signature. When I first saw it, Raha's name came to mind... I didn't want to consider it. Dealing with Perry was one thing... but an elite Fallen Angel..."

Kae crossed her arms and closed her eyes, thinking about it deeper.

"I also never considered a Fallen Angel working in unison with another. But that would be my theory, seeing that spell heal Perry before my eyes, too."

Palona made her way back to the group.

"We have to figure this out and fast. These people won't last much longer."

"Yeah, you all look pretty pathetic here..."

Dareth rolled his eyes like this was someone else's problem. He wasn't wrong, technically... Dareth could just log out and let Kae and Palona deal with this chaos. But the glare coming from the elf caused him to take back his words.

"Ah, Voxx... is there a place we can strategize and put our theories together? You and, ah, Kae sound like you're on to something."

Dareth suggested, glancing back at Palona. Her expression didn't change much, but he could feel the tension easing around her. It was a "better stay on her good side" situation.

"Sure. Maybe we can plan a counterattack of some kind. Come with me to the meeting room."

Voxx brought them to a boardroom full of weapons, armor, and supplies. There was a large round table in the center. At one point, all the True Heroes used this base to make plans. Kae's tail couldn't stop wagging as she touched the wood. The Table of Heroes was real. It wasn't just a place she wished was reality anymore.

"This place looks awesome."

Kae whispered. The old glory captured Kae's eyes. It looked run down for the most part, something only a true aged player would appreciate seeing. There they all sat down as Voxx explained the clues he had gone over.

"So, Raha had been on my radar for the very reason the fox mentioned. His powerful spells came to mind when dealing with Perry."

The Night Stalker stood at the head of the round table as he continued.

"If our theory of Raha being the cause of Perry's immortality is true, we have a bigger problem. Being an elite Fallen Angel, we don't have the caliber to deal with him."

Kae raised her hand next. With a nod from Voxx, she stood up.

"[Enlightened], that is a new blessing I've never seen before. But... it reminded me of one I've dealt with recently, [Sentient]."

Palona tilted her head in confusion.

"[Sentient]?"

The elf tilted her head.

"Yes, Rexia would strengthen Fallen Angels with that blessing before I'd fight them."

"Bless the Fallen Angels?"

Palona pressed for more, confused by it all. But Kae continued without acknowledging her glare.

"For now, just know that it's similar to [Enlightened]. The Fallen Angel with the effect had grown stronger because it is."

"Are you saying... Rexia gave them this blessing that might be causing this chaos, Loredom?"

Palona was appalled. She stood up and banged the table in protest. But Kae waved her hands in the air, trying to cool the elf's temper off.

"No, I'm saying the opposite, actually, Palona! That [Enlightened] is different from [Sentient]."

[Enlightened] was different from the [Sentient] that Rexia would put on the Fallen Angels. As if there was another being altogether, someone working outside of Rexia's influence.

"Palona, maybe that's why Rexia hasn't intervened in this place. Maybe she can't for some reason. What I'm saying is, whatever is causing this is outside of Rexia's influence."

The fox reassured her. But Rexia was the all-powerful Goddess of the world, Keostar. The thought that she couldn't intervene in her own world was ridiculous. But this entire situation was just that, ridiculous.

"Anyway, it doesn't matter if Perry or this... Raha has this buff. If they are working together, the only way to free everyone from this hell is to defeat them?"

Dareth laid it out in an easy-to-digest way. Voxx and Kae groaned loudly in tandem. If only it were that easy...

"Is Raha really that difficult?"

Dareth asked.

Kae stood up, taking the lead of the conversation.

"Actually, Raha is probably the best elite Fallen Angel we could have hoped for in this situation, Dareth. Despite all of us being far below the level of him... it wouldn't matter."

She put a finger in the air.

"Raha isn't hard, but his gimmick is annoying as heck."

The fox complained. Kae crossed her arms and thought the strategy through.

"Raha's Fallen Angel fight is simple and predictable. The players never attack Raha directly, and that's what makes him "easy" in comparison, but..."

She tilted her head and sighed deeply.

"In the Dungeon of Death, Raha sends out 10 of his elite monsters during his battle in waves. We would have to kill all of them in the span of 10 minutes, else he'd cast death on anyone remaining."

The Foxian explained, her tone like a child with a temper.

"And I don't think I have the manpower to handle Raha's minions reliably."

Voxx admitted, posting against the wall in annoyance.

"How strong are those minions, Kae?"

The Baron wondered. Kae looked at him and noticed some excitement coming from them. It reminded her of when she first searched out Raha in a group. Maybe this was the true challenge she'd been looking for. With that in mind, she decided to formulate something at least aloud.

"Raha's fight is about speed. It's a massive DPS check with each minion having more HP than the last. But..."

She looked towards Voxx, the True Night Stalker.

"We have a True Hero, one of the strongest people in Keostar, with us, thought. Maybe if we strategize this, we have a chance. I mean, the Night Stalker is the best class for that fight."

Her mind was moving faster than her words. Kae Loredom, the veteran who had done everything, seen everything, was having fun again.

"Voxx, how many people do you have that you can still fight?"

Voxx lowered his eyes suspiciously. This random fox was getting too excited for his taste, and he debated kicking her out to the sands before something crazy happened. But against his better judgment, he sighed and spoke up.

"twenty-five... maybe thirty that are uninjured. The rest are aiding the wounded from the village and are hurt themselves."

"Is it possible you can bring them all together. If we can all work together, I think..."

Kae's eyes lit up like a kid finding a new toy.

"And we have a tank... Well, we can make you a meat shield at least, Dareth."

She pointed at Dareth. It didn't register to Kae until now; if the group of Thieves' that Voxx had here were strong enough, she could create the perfect raid team for Raha's fight.

"I think if we all plan this out, work together in tandem... that we can defeat Raha. And if he truly is the cause of this, we will free the Chalaco Desert from this chaos."

The fox declared, and Dareth laughed hardly.

"I don't know how strong Raha is, but from what you're saying, aren't we a little too weak to fight him? And you still want to try, Kae?"

The Trade Baron brought up a good point, and he was right. Kae knew it too. Raha could end his run easily with how strong he was compared to all other Fallen Angels he's faced. And that was before any wild buffs or blessings he might have if they reach him...

"We are way under-leveled for this fight, Dareth. But Raha's fight is a gimmick battle, one that we can exploit to make it easier on us. I'm not saying it's possible... but I don't think it's impossible either."

Voxx was still wary of the new group. But staying here idly, trying to get a hold of the Hallows and hoping for help was long gone. So, he pushed off the wall and walked back over to the round table.

"I thought about doing this earlier. But, since we have others on the same wavelength, we might as well go for it. I'll gather everyone around. But if we do this, I'll have to agree on every part of this plan before we march off to the Dungeon of Death."

The True Hero took charge before stepping out of the boardroom.

"Sure. I'll write up the plan, and we can talk about it as soon as you get back."

Kae shouted back.

"If the game is harder, more people will have to work together to make things possible. Even if we are a low level, if we have enough of a crew to help us get through."

Kae smiled to herself.

"I can't do this alone. Just how it should be."

With the odds stacked against them, the veteran took charge. Kae opened her menu, selected her notes tab, and began working out a strategy against the Anubis King of the Desert, Raha.
