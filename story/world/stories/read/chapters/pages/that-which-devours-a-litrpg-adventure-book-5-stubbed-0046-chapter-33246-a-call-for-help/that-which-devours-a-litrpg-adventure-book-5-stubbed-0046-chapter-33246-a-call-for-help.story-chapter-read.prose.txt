Alex, on the floating island…

“Val! What is the meaning of this!” The voice echoed out of a gaping archway in the stone cavern. Another doorway sat in the shadows of the wall, and I hadn’t really paid attention to it. Stairs were visible through the opening. “Stop feeding on the orb, we don’t need it freeing…”

The voice paused, as a pulse of energy flared again through the room, making it so I couldn’t move. Frozen there under the orb, still clutching my spear, I was in clear view of anyone who chose to look.

Then he arrived. A mix of metal, glowing with deep green light, and bits of skeleton. Bright orange eyes stood out from the gaping holes in the skull.

[An OFFENDER has been detected in your area, with an active bounty. You have been given a temporary boost to stats and abilities.]

[SYSTEM ERROR: OFFENDER DECEASED.]

[SYSTEM OVERRIDE]

[Master Arch-Lich Ouros, The First Forger, Level 575, Predator, Unknown.]

[SYSTEM WARNING: THIS BOUNTY IS BEYOND YOU. REINFORCEMENT QUEST INITIATED.]

I needed to leave, now. I didn’t care if reinforcements would be coming. Survival was more important. The blue orb over me mocked me, as did with the star on my map.

Destroying that Mana Lens needed to happen. Even Lenna had warned me to hurry.

A ripple of invisible energy flared from inside me, making my head ring. Ouros’ eyes locked on me.

“You!” He pointed a long, skeletal finger at me. Green light surged along the bones, slowly forming flesh. The orb pulsed.

The wisp in my inventory heated up, glowing inside my mental space.

[You have created a Whisper.]

His eyes locked on me with a frown.

[Protection or Growth?]

I needed to live. Yet, I couldn’t move a muscle.

[Protection.]

Something suddenly burned inside my body, and I could move. Runes appeared all over the room, shooting daggers of light across the space, creating a mass of confusion and shadows.

The beams of light formed a cage around Ouros, and I twisted in place to flee.

But the dang quest!

I ducked as a beam of bright blue light flickered overhead. The cage shattered into nothing.

Ouros flung a hand out in my direction, and I dove to the floor. A massive green ball shot  overhead and exploded near the entrance. The orb dimmed, casting the room into complete darkness.

I vanished.

The lights surrounding the skeleton almost went out. Only his orange, glowing eyes remained as he glanced up.

The acidic stink of terror came from him.

I couldn’t fight him, but every time the orb went dim he stopped. That, I could work with. I didn’t have a choice.

Within the darkness, wings sprouted from my back, taking me high enough to touch the glowing blue orb. I slammed the tip of my spear into the side of the orb again, just as the bright blue light returned.

The crack widened from the impact, but a rush of energy surged outward sending me flying into the wall. Pain almost knocked me out as my wings were crushed. I sent my spear into my inventory on reflex, as I slid to the ground.

Bright light flashed from Ouros, heading right at me, and I rolled away. The wall behind me shattered as pieces went flying everywhere.

Several sliced at me, but my armor tanked the hit. Thankfully, it’d just been rocks, not whatever Ouros had attacked with.

I scrambled to my feet as he shot another bolt. Again it missed, as I dashed around the room.

The orb dimmed as Ouros pulled a massive amount of energy from it. The mana twisted from soft blue light, to the sparkling acidic green as he drew it to himself. It gathered above his head like a fireball.

That’d kill me. I knew it.

Too far away from the open doors, I did the only thing I could think of.

He shot the massive fireball after me and I darted behind a boulder, trying to squeeze as much of myself behind the stones as possible. Flames washed the room in a green haze, slamming the massive doors shut. Even with my armor, everywhere it touched burned.

Pain was my entire reality for a moment. I ignored it as best I could, pushing it back, but the sizzle of my poor armor worried me.

Pieces of armor on my shoulder disintegrated, along with the armor on my calf.

Holy shit.

A dark opening in the wall near two other pillars drew my attention, as the rest of the flames went out.

There! That had to be the way up.

I rolled away from my hiding spot, but more and more of my armor turned to dust. Finally, I came to a stop behind another pillar. Everything tingled from the pressure in the room, and I had some lingering burns where my armor had failed first.

That had to be the way!

Everything but stone was incinerated.

Energy pulsed over my body, trying to heal the burns. The heat picked up from something invisible in my inventory. Something that appeared blank. I needed to move. It drew me to the gap in the wall.

The orb flickered frantically, barely staying lit, and all of Ouros’ attention went to it. The ground suddenly vibrated, shaking with each pulse of the orb. Gravity stopped for a second, then it seemed to catch itself.

“Don’t you dare wake up,” mumbled Ouros, as blue energy rose from around his skeleton and went back to the orb, stopping the flickering.

That confirmed it. The orb contained someone, or something. And the big evil Lich-thing really didn’t want it out. That meant I did.

I yanked on my stealth, and darted behind the second pillar, vanishing from sight.

Ouros took a step in the direction I had originally fled from the ball of fire. “Now, did I get you?”

I’d find a way out of this mess.

Ouros approached the other pillar, and I didn’t dare move. I pressed myself into the pillar, trying to become one with the stone.

“Worthless, now I need to find another to manage the rejects…” More grumbling came from his direction, but he didn’t turn my way. “There should be clones left in the banks… How do I pull them out of storage again?”

The voice continued away from me, moving toward to the side wall. Green light flashed, and something clicked, then he just kept going.

My heart pounded in my chest as I slumped in relief once I couldn’t hear anything else. Peeking around the pillar, I found a small open door with stairs leading down. That must have been where he went.

With the massive doors closed, the only option was up. I had to find the Mana Lens.

I darted toward the opening leading up. The stairs curved to one side, heading above this room that was a mirror of the council chamber.

Then, I was above the orb. It cut through the floor of the small room, just like in the chamber on the other island, the only thing is the space. In the middle of the blue thing, something dark pulsed. Using second sight confirmed the mana came from the creature.

Between the creature and me stood the lens.

Unable to help myself, I touched the surface of the lens. I found it solid. A rush of mana surged into my body, burning my finger yet again. Yet, the rush of energy sent a thrill through me.

[You have gained a level.]

The notification burned as it popped up and dinged, rippling over every inch of my skin like sandpaper.  I stumbled back, and the wall caught me before I slipped down the steps. My armor suddenly flowed over my skin, feeling thinner than before.

The unseen object again shook inside my inventory, trying to get me to pay attention to it. It needed to go forward.

I didn’t dare.

The black dot of Ouros touched the edges of my radar, coming back in this direction.

Destroying the lens was what mattered. I didn’t have a choice.

My claws grew from my fingertips as I sliced at the lens. Lines of darkness carved through the air. The edges of it slammed into the blue light, and everything stood still for a moment.

Liquid sprayed outward from the cut. Then the orb beyond the lens dimmed.

Ouros paused down below.

Energy surged through the room again.

I dove forward, digging into the surface as I went. The dark light tore into the surface, spraying liquid again in all directions as the lens shattered. The orb shook, along with everything else in the room.

[You have gained a level.]

My inventory exploded as more of the liquid touched me. One name whispered through my mind, and I tugged on it.

[Sovereigns Call has evolved.]

Then I cracked through the lens, falling toward the orb. Just as I touched it, it went completely out.

Interesting. There shouldn’t be any sources of mana outside of a system controlled area.
