Complete and utter hopelessness.

“N-No… But, how can this…” one of the men standing closest to Eik breathed almost soundlessly, his distracted mind sending him stumbling over a low curb despite his high rank.

Good. This was exactly the reaction Eik had wanted.

Despite his prejudicial distaste for the general Gohkamorian people and sincere hatred for their upper brass, Eik knew from his years in the Nidafjeld Alliance that most of the sentient races of the Unified Mass were more or less the same. If an issue or a threat felt distant and impersonal, the Gohkamorians, just as the humans, would probably not feel that inclined to take it seriously.

If the danger wasn’t standing on your front step and knocking on your door first thing in the morning, it was a little too easy to put it aside for another day. A sheet of deadly Profound Toxin hanging freely above their heads every hour of the day until they gave in to Eik’s demands would hopefully be an effective stick, in addition to the carrot that it was to be allowed to not die.

He would leave the text describing his demands as well. If they had any desire to survive, they would report the identities he wanted.

“Oh, shit, right!” he suddenly exclaimed, slamming a fist into his own palm as realization hit him. “There’s something I completely forgot!”

Sorry, guys, Eik here again.

I just forgot something important, but I’ll make it quick since I want to go home too.

Since I imagine it’s not an easy thing to take the trip to Gimleh to report the identities of your social elite, I will provide you with an easy, safe, and most importantly, potentially profitable way of giving me the information I want.

They can also recognize obviously conspicuous hand gestures such as frantic waving, as well as written messages.

I understand that you might feel unsafe reporting the guilty, but my summons will even be able to hear you whisper to them under the covers in your beds at night. If you desire to report but remain anonymous, your identity will not be disclosed even to me.

If, on the other hand, you decide that you don’t mind having your identity known, I can personally guarantee safe passage out of Gohkamoria until such a time where you would like to return. No strings attached.

I do not desire the total annihilation of your world or your civilization. All I want is revenge for my people. I will not touch a single hair on those not involved.

Conversely, if you yourself are guilty, you may turn yourself in for a more… pleasant punishment.

Finally, to reiterate, do not make false reports in order to get rid of personal enemies. I will know. And I will not like it.

I know I said I would make this message short, but, eh, what can you do?

In waves of several thousands at a time, Living Manifestations sprung forth from thin air, put into the world through the sheer power of Eik’s will alone.

Just as discordant as the deep keening of the Extinction Zone, but pitched into a haunting, ghostly wail, the countless Profound Toxic beasts shrieked toward the Apocalypse Canvas in chorus and scattered in all directions.

Seemingly endlessly, they kept manifesting and flying off to wherever they were going to hide. There were a few token attacks against the virtual tidal wave of blue creatures, but the sources of those attacks quickly realized that it was no use to struggle against this.

Even if they did manage to take out one or two of the summons, Eik would simply summon a thousand more to replace them. It would do nothing. Going against this X-ranked behemoth was akin to shooting arrows into a torrential storm in the hopes that it might calm it somehow.

Only just barely half of the Gohkamorian giant’s height, Eik held Mukhathar’s gaze with ice cold eyes. It looked like a child facing off against a professional wrestler but as the Gohkamorians were coming to realize—and regret—looks could be deceiving.

Without letting the alien commander look away for a second, Eik raised a hand into the air for a third time and shot yet another droplet of toxin skyward while the Living Manifestations continued to swarm ceaselessly behind him.

And one final thing before I go.

The deadline is whenever I please.

At this, the S-ranker swallowed hard. Eik’s auraless presence alone was putting stress on his heart.

Holding the alien’s gaze for another couple of seconds, Eik turned around and began the walk back to the same portal location to which he had arrived. “I’m going now,” he said with one final glance back at the commander, the nearly six thousand Gohkamorian troops watching from their positions. “By the way, S-ranker. I wonder… Did you, too, have a hand in the deaths of my people?”

Where there had been indignant fury before, Mukhathar’s eyes now held nothing but despair.
