Ashton stood there in the entrance to the small church. It wasn't anything grand even before; it was almost more like a chapel. But the ancient constructs had changed this into something new. It still had many aspects that reminded Ashton of the structure that stood here before, but it had changed in many fundamental ways.

It was slightly off compared to the earthly architecture that Ashton was used to. It was likely that the building was a lot more sturdy now, with its materials replaced. They constructs were probably trying to 'repair' this place, and considered the state that it was in before to be a failure that was meant to be repaired.

However, Ashton didn't know exactly why they did this. What they were turning this place into. Oka had told him that there were numerous structures like this that the ancient constructs protected and repaired, but a church?

Plus, one with such a powerful construct protecting it? That seemed a bit off. The only thing in here with any mana, beside the construct, was the crown that was sitting on the other side of the room. Was that such a special item that it had to be protected like this?

Well, whatever the case, it was clear what Ashton had to do.

He took a step forward, and the large construct stood before him. It seemed like all its functions were properly intact. As Ashton's first attack landed, it didn't connect with the construct's stone body, but instead a thick, glowing barrier. It was a magically created shield that was projected from the construct's left arm.

"Oh, fuck you," Ashton groaned. The shield was as annoying as he had heard. All the energy and momentum that Ashton would have usually made use of to bounce off the shield and then continue his onslaught had been absorbed by the solid magical energy. It wasn't just a defensive tool; it was one to lock the opponent down.

As soon as the impact came, the construct swung its weapon down at Ashton. It was pretty small for its frame. Or it would be, if it would only be that small stick that it was holding. but instead, the momentum some momentum was built, a large hammer-head made from that same magic was created.

Ashton was able to jump backward and dodge easily enough.

"Annoying," he grumbled, running back at the construct. He pushed his staff forward again and the construct held its shield forward, but instead of actually hitting it, Ashton pushed his staff into the ground and used it to vault over his opponent.

The moment he was behind it, Ashton tried to slam his staff at it, but the construct's head simply spun around. It twisted its arms and fully reoriented itself in a split second, once again defending with the shield. Ashton was able to pull his staff away at the last second, and instead defended against the hammer attack directly.

As the shockingly heavy magical hammer slammed down on him, Ashton deflected the piece into the energy shield. Just like with Ashton's attack, the hammer's momentum was completely stopped as if it was 'stuck' to the shield for a brief moment.

And that brief moment was enough for Ashton to strike at the construct's wrist. Ashton used the core-destruction technique, shattering the magical circuitry flowing through the construct's arm. The energy hammer slowly faded as it lost its mana supply. Instead of continuing to fight with the now no-longer functioning weapon, the construct threw it onto the ground.

Without the weapon, instead of striking at Ashton, the construct seemed to want to grab onto him whenever it possibly could. However, together with the wisp and gnome, Ashton was able to slowly whittle down on the construct's body. Whenever it tried to grab him, he just struck at its arm, until a pile of stones started to gather at their feet.

Before long, the only thing the construct could attempt was to slam its shield into Ashton, but ever since that first attack, he had never touched that energy even once, and he wouldn't start now.

This time, whenever the construct tried to bash the shield forward, Ashton struck at its shoulder, slowly whittling down on it. It was a slow process, but he wanted to try and keep the shield intact. If he could do that and figure out how the shield worked, he might be able to replicate it. And maybe he could even detach the shield mechanism and just give it to Paul as is. And he could give the hammer to John, too.

The two of them were growing stronger at a decent pace, so giving them some equipment like this wouldn't be a bad idea.

And, to be totally honest, now that Ashton was actually here and he constantly had the mana of that crown glowing in his periphery, he was dreading actually looking at it.

At first, Ashton had the hope that it was just a generic crown made of actual steel rok feathers. But it was too small for that. It was far too small for that. Of course, it was still possible that they had shrunken it down so that people on earth could wear it, but still. That gut feeling of disgust was still laying heavy in Ashton's stomach.

Sure, was it maybe a bit silly to be wasting time after running here so fast? Maybe. But Ashton wasn't exactly acting particularly logically right now. His emotions were getting the better of him.

And when the construct was finally standing in front of him without its arms, and Ashton couldn't just put it off any longer, he slammed his staff and the wisp into its head.

Ashton focused on the message for only a brief moment. He had to hurry. The other constructs hadn't intervened during the boss battle. Ashton didn't know why; maybe because this church was the boss construct's responsibility, but the reason didn't really matter anyway. The only important part was that the rest of the constructs would come swarming in soon.

Ashton smashed the boss' chest open and grabbed all the important components, took the energy-hammer, and tried his best to isolate the part of the construct's arm that was responsibly for the shield generation. Honestly, it was all pretty sloppy since Ashton didn't have much time, but these were still extremely useful items that he had to get when he had the opportunity.

And again... Ashton was dreading actually walking up to the altar.

But now, he couldn't just walk away anymore. Ashton walked over, and focused on the item that these constructs were clearly trying to protect.

"Why?" Ashton stood in front of the altar. His channelling range was compressed as far as he possibly could make it, so that Ashton could be sure he wasn't missing even a single detail.

He ran his fingers over the crown. The position of every single piece was exactly how Ashton remembered it. There were even the small scratches and tiny cracks at the edges from when Oka was trying to shape these scales.

His fingertips had felt these blemishes tens of thousands of times.

"Hah.... Hahahahah...!" Ashton started to laugh as he held the crown in his hand. He put down a piece of paper and pulled out the Pen of Insight. "Inspect it. Every single detail. Draw out as much as you need. Fuck, use all my magic, all the ink that I've stocked up! Just tell me, why the fuck is this here?!"

The small summon shook slightly in response, as if eager to get started and help. Ashton could feel his magic draining rapidly, and the full bottle of ink that he put down was being sucked up like someone was drinking it.

But it would still take a little while for the Pen of Insight to process this. So, in the meantime, Ashton was going to make sure that the Pen would not be disturbed, and use up some of the anxious and furious energy making his heart beat up into his throat.

Ashton turned around, and noticed the ancient constructs finally enter the church. Some of the smaller ones were climbing in through the windows, while the rest entered through the large door on the other side of the space.

He grasped his staff tightly, and used the gnome to take control of as much of the stone from the boss construct's destroyed body as he could, and then started immediately. He spun his staff around and shot spikes of stone at all the constructs, hitting them as precisely as he was able to.

Their bodies were slowly getting whittled down on, and when any construct made it close enough, Ashton would strike at it and make sure that they couldn't take a single step further.

Many of the pews were already destroyed during the boss fight, but now, none of them remained standing anymore. The ground was covered in splinters of petrified wood and the rubble of the constructs' stone bodies.

It was uneven and hard to move on, but Ashton had fought in worse conditions. And frankly, right now, he couldn't give the slightest shit about whether or not he was going to trip.

All he could think about was destroying these things. These constructs that were protecting a memory of his past.

What kind of sick fucking joke was this? By now, the Interdimensional Council must also know who Ashton was and what his past was. Were they trying to mock him? Were they trying to make him fall back into grief so that they could weaken and finally kill him?

Well, whichever it was, Ashton was furious and he was going to show those feelings to the council with every further action he would take. None of their plans would come to fruition. As long as Ashton could ruin them, he would.

Finally, Ashton stood there, standing on a pile of destroyed constructs. The bottle of ink was almost empty, and the pain attacking his head right behind where his eyes were supposed to be felt like someone poured molten iron into his sockets.

Ashton walked closer so that he could properly read the description.
