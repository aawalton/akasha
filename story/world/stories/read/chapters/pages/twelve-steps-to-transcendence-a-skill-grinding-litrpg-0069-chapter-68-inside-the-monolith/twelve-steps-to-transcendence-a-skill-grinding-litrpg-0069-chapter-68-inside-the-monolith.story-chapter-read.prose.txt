Hopefully it happens at Tier 2.

"You basically have to mess around and find out. Rare skills are completely tied to your intent and your understanding of the concept, so us giving you the information on [Ignition Step], for instance, has already shaped the way you view that skill, and the way it will act going forward. You have to push the skill into weird, unconventional scenarios to see how it reacts, and the rest is just mental reaffirmation, in a way."

"…Huh. That's interesting."

"Since the system doesn't hand you an instruction manual, you have to meditate on the core meaning of the word itself. If your skill is called 'Fire', you don't just think about burning things. You think about heat, consumption, light, or even purification. Or death, misery. It can mean anything."

Absorbing their combined lecture on conceptual meditation and skill boundary testing, Ben realized his homework had just drastically tripled.

"You mean to say... I have to lock myself in a room and forcefully shove mana into my skills until something clicks?"

"Well, yes and no. You can do that, or you can not. The path is different for everyone. I've heard stories of some high Tier ascenders spending years staring at a single candle flame, just trying to redefine the word 'burn' in their heads to conceptually change their [Pyromancy] skill."

"Years?" Ben blanched. "That sounds awful."

"It is. But the payoff is usually worth it. Imagine taking a simple kinetic barrier and realizing its concept is actually 'rejection.' Suddenly, you become capable of rejecting magical effects, environmental hazards, maybe even abstract concepts like scrying or teleportation, provided your skill level is high enough."

The possibilities unfurled in his mind. If a simple barrier could do that, what the hell was [Focus] actually capable of if he stopped treating it as he had been?

His own perception of the skill was rather... limited, to say the least.

"So, if I had a rare skill that had a concept based around, say, 'construction' or 'creation'..."

"Then you could conceptually construct pathways for mana, build temporary pseudo-cores for constructs, or even construct temporary 'rules' within a localized domain. Trying to wield one you don't fully understand can literally fry your brain or mutate your soul space."

He needed to find a safe space to experiment with [Focus] and [Warforged]. But that was a problem for future Ben. Present Ben was just incredibly glad he had a tangible path forward and an extra skill slot to play with.
