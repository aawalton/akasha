With Lemira snoring away in Luke's bed, he sat down in a chair and leaned back, taking a deep breath. The next dungeon he went into, he'd wait for someone a little lower level to go in before him. Facing off against such tough enemies took a lot out of him, and that extended well beyond the fighting. Leminal and Ezezal had been exhausting to deal with in so many ways. What he needed at this point was more straightforward dungeons with normal monsters in them, like the ants back with Mateo. Luke didn't want to think about what could have happened if he had gone through the portal first that time. Would Mateo have lived? There was no telling.

Smiling, he sent a message to the Lancer to check in, but got no reply. It was still early, or late, depending on how you wanted to look at it. Four AM, and he wasn't feeling sleepy at all. Tired? Yes. Exhausted? Yep, but not like he needed sleep. Like he'd told Hannah, he was too wired for that, his mind racing in a million different directions.

Trying to focus was an uphill battle, but he took the time to sell off the corpses he'd gathered in the dungeon, not wanting to walk around with them in his inventory. That provided a couple of thousand credits. Selling the demon's infernal core gave another four thousand. Rather than hoard the credits, Luke purchased an add-on to his Shelter, another bedroom. It appeared in an instant without fanfare, like it'd always been there. Needing to keep Lemira safe meant keeping her close, but not so close that he wanted to share a bed with her.

The thought made him think of Dot, and he figured it was best to rip off the band-aid.

Luke: "Awake?"

Dot: "I am, yeah. Can't believe you entered another dungeon right after what happened."

Feeling a little annoyed at that comment, Luke sat up straighter in the chair, rubbing his eyes.

Luke: "What about it is so hard to believe? We need to get stronger."

Dot: "I'm sorry. I don't mean to come off as a bitch, but aren't you feeling something? I feel like shit, and I wasn't even the one who did it."

Luke: "I've put it behind me."

Dot: "Okay..."

Dot: "An officer came to interview me about you. Peter said you might be going to jail."

Luke: "Oh? I'm not going to jail."

Luke: "You still with ISG?"

Dot: "Can't just up and leave. They need me, and I need them, now more than ever."

Luke: "What do you mean?"

Dot: "There is a lot of infighting in the guild. Kind of chaotic. They're still protecting me, and there are always people to heal, even if they've been having trouble getting the sick to come see me here in New York. I think they'll suggest I go back to Chicago."

Luke: "I see."

Dot: "Look. I don't agree with what you did. Leslie was one thing..."

Luke: "I should have made it clearer to you what Alan did. The way he used people, twisting their minds. The prisons. He was not some altruist, Dot."

She was silent for a while, and Luke took the opportunity to grab a wrap with some sort of mystery meat from the Integrated Shop, along with a bottle of water so cold it almost hurt his throat to drink it.

Dot: "Peter told me about what Alan was doing. They issued a guild-wide message detailing everything and how they wanted to move forward. A lot of people quit, Luke."

Luke: "But not you. Even after you learned all that, you still blame me? The man wanted me dead. Tried it several times. Blackmailed me and threatened to kill you so he could replace you with someone of his choosing."

Dot: "Killing is still wrong. I took an oath."

Luke: "Oh, come on."

Rather than sadness over what the two of them could have had, resentment and anger built in its stead. This was not something they would be able to overcome. There was no going back.

Luke: "Maybe going back to Chicago would be the best thing for you."

Dot: "Yeah."

A moment later.

Dot: "I'd still like to be friends."

Luke: "Yeah."

That was it. Conversation over. Dot didn't send another message, and neither did Luke. More than anything in that moment, he wanted a shower, but the chair was a little too comfortable. Perhaps he ought to grind some enchantments instead. Rank three was damn close. Just a little more work, and he'd get there.

"Wha!" Luke sat up with a start, looking around in bewilderment. Panting, he put a hand to his chest, trying to settle down. He'd been running down a hallway, chased by something he had no hope of beating. The fact that it had been in a hospital probably meant something, but Luke was not one to over-interpret dreams.

"You're awake."

A pair of gleaming red eyes stared at him through the darkness. He almost yelled again before remembering where he was and, more importantly, who with.

"It's dark in here," he noted, trying and failing to sound calm. "What happened to the light?"

At his mention of 'light', a dull and yellow glow filled the room, growing stronger until it matched about what you'd expect from a normal ceiling lamp, even though he saw nothing producing said light. Weird.

"Huh," he said, turning to face Lemira. "How are you doing?"

She sat in silence for a moment, staring at him while her eyes lost that reddish gleam, turning back to a warm brown. "I did not expect to wake up in a stranger's bed."

"Coming here knocked you out. Or maybe it was the healing that did that, but I thought you'd prefer my bed to the floor."

"It would not be my first time sleeping on the floor."

"Great..." Luke began, unsure how to continue. "Childhood trauma aside, you're in my system now. Welcome to Earth."

She nodded, looking around the small Shelter. After a moment, she sniffed herself and wrinkled her nose. "I would like to clean, if that is possible?"

Luke pointed over his shoulder. "Sure. There's a shower through there. Do you need clothes? That cultist robe is looking a little worse for wear. I don't have much, but I should be able to scrounge up a T-shirt and some shorts."

"I am unsure what those are, but I am in need of clothing. My father," at the mention of Ezezal, her expression turned somber, but she powered through, "did not supply me with credits, but I will repay you once I am able."

"No worries."

She got up and looked around the corner to the bathroom, frowned, glanced over at Luke, then continued through the door. A moment later, she peeked out, saying, "Thank you," before disappearing again before he had a chance to reply. It took some time, but she figured out how to work the shower, and he heard a contented sigh soon after, when she stepped in.

Luke put the clothes he'd promised just outside the door, then settled back in to eat breakfast. Getting food from the Integrated Shop, you never quite knew what you'd get, but so far, it had all been edible, filling, and cheap as hell. The days of going to the supermarket and shopping were over. He didn't miss it.

Lemira took her sweet time in there, so he got started on some enchanting while he waited. Problem was, he didn't have a whole lot of stuff to enchant, since he had sold most of what he found, or already had enchanted it. Buying items from the Integrated Shop was an option, but that seemed like a waste since they needed the money to create the guild.

During the night, he'd received quite a few messages from people in contact with Milla, asking about him and the plans for the guild. It felt good to redirect most of those questions back to his sister. Not dealing with the minutiae and administration was the main reason he didn't want to be guildmaster. That sort of job was not one he was well-suited for. His big sister would do a fine job of it, he was sure. Luke needed to meet up with her later that day, after she'd confirmed her mode of transportation and arrival time. Once they got going, they'd get the guild up and running in no time.

Peter: "Luke. About that meeting. Are you free today?"

Luke sighed. After his 'talk' with Dot, he didn't feel up to visiting ISG headquarters. His last visit hadn't been fun, but this time it hopefully wouldn't end with someone dying.

Luke: "Any updates on the whole cop situation?"

Peter: "That's part of what I wanted to talk with you about."

Luke: "Uh-oh."

Peter: "You don't have to be afraid of police officers waiting to haul you off."

Luke: "Good. Fine. I've got an appointment at five."

Peter: "Can you come here at 9 AM?"

Luke checked his phone and saw some text messages he should probably get to, but it was still early enough that he wouldn't have to rush over there right away.

Luke: "See you then."

"Luke," Lemira said, having just entered the room. She pointed at the bed, where he'd left out some food for her. "Is that for me?"

"Dig in," he said. "I have to go meet someone soon. Do you want to come?"

She wore his T-shirt and shorts. The clothes looked hilariously oversized on her, and the shorts rode low, as if threatening to fall down to the floor when she nodded. "I would like that very much. Thank you."

Lemira sat down and grabbed the wrap of what looked like doner kebab, took a huge bite, and let out what sounded like an involuntary moan of delight as she chewed.

"Good?" he asked, scrolling through the text messages.

"Like heaven," she answered.

He looked up to see her smile at her own joke. "Heh, yeah. How are you feeling? No burning sensation? Nothing like your body is falling apart?"

It took her a moment to chew and swallow the huge bite. "It itches, but no pain. That water contraption was interesting and most comfortable. Where does the water go after hitting the floor?"

"You know," Luke said. "I have no clue."
