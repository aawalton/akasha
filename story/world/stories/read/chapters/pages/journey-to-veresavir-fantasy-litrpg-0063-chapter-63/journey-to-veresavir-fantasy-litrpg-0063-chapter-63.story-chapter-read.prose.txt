Chapter 63

Chloe checked the room when she thought it safe, then called for them.

“Are you sure it’s dispersed?” Ailis asked.

“Yes. We have a ventilation system in place, specifically for situations like this one,” Chloe said. “And I would notice. You’re safe.”

Ailis glanced at Veron.

He nodded and went inside.

Lucie, it seemed, had already lost interest in the open box still standing on the metal table, back to her chair and book.

“What was in it?” Isaac asked with interest.

“A vessel,” Lucie said without looking up. “Made of glass and inscribed with gold. There is no essence inside but it’s quite a fine work of art, I’d value it at around forty-five gold, which means your cut would be an additional thirteen gold and fifty silver.”

“That means we’ve made… twenty eight and a half gold from the chest,” Isaac said and laughed.

“All that discussion and consideration,” Veron said and laughed too.

“I guess your gamble paid off. With fifty silver,” Ailis said in a dry tone.

“Yeah. I’m glad there was something of value in there. Can we see it?” Veron asked.

“Of course,” Chloe said and went over to the chest. She carefully lifted something out of it and showed it to the approaching group.

It reminded Veron of some kind of four-winged bird, but not exactly. It was symmetrical, looking the same again after Chloe turned it slightly. A delicate piece, lines of golden runes carefully inlaid into its structure.

“I wouldn’t want to carry that thing in my pack,” Ailis commented.

“Yeah,” Elke added.

“It’s quite delicate,” Chloe said. “Which lowers its value. The quality of the inscription itself is high.”

They had Mr. Elmwacht check and comment on the vessel, his evaluation unsurprisingly quite similar to that of Lucie, lower even but Chloe didn't seem interested in pushing the price he suggested.

Veron checked the now empty box as well. A dark-red pillow had propped up the vessel. He considered for a moment if Lucie had potentially taken something out while they were waiting for the poison to disperse. Or if there was poison present in the first place. But with Sonny’s recommendation and the supposed reputation of the Luna Inn, he doubted it.

They thanked Mr. Elmwacht who promptly left, then said their goodbyes to Lucie.

Despite the comment she had made about Veron when she had stood close, she didn't look up again from her book.

She might be someone I could talk to and ask a few more questions. But it might be dangerous too. I don't want to suffer because I said the wrong thing to the wrong people. It could set me back as well, in a lot of different ways.

Veron shelved the thought for now, happy to have learned about someone both established and seemingly quite knowledgeable in Anvakar. He hoped her comment didn't mean more than slight curiosity, and she wouldn’t come and hunt him down or something. Either way, he knew that he would think about it a few times in the coming weeks.

Chloe led them back into the same room where they had first presented their finds. “Do you wish to have large pieces of gold or a four way split?”

“A four way split would be perfect,” Veron said and waited with the others as Chloe counted out the money.

It felt amusing. And reminiscent of something, then he realized what it was.

Veron hadn't felt like this since his first real paycheck. Gold. He quickly calculated. Around sixteen pieces for each of them. An equivalent of one thousand and six hundred silver, or sixteen thousand iron. If he continued the same lifestyle since coming to Anvakar, the money would be enough to last him the better part of a year, without even setting another foot into the dungeon.

Of course that wasn't what Veron planned to do, and he expected the others to think in a similar vein. It was a good sum of money to invest into whatever they needed, and hopefully, that would allow them to delve both longer and deeper into the dungeon, finding more treasures along the way.

Next to all of this relic and lockbox excitement, he had also just hit the next stage in his Path, as had Elke.

Veron was sure the others would follow soon. And all of that had its own implications.

After the fees they had paid for Mr. Elmwacht’s services, each of them got sixteen gold and seventy-seven silver. They thanked Chloe and made to leave, quickly discussing if they should take the offer of protection on their way back up through the Circles, then agreeing to it.

Adan, it turned out, was the grave doorman they had already met. He got a quick briefing from Chloe, greeted the group again and then led the way. They didn't encounter any trouble and soon they had gathered in the Reader's Hut.

Ellie looked up from the book she was reading and smiled when she saw them. The store was empty otherwise. “You look both tense and excited, what happened?”

Isaac walked up to her with a triumphant gesture and then bowed. “Well, first of all, we killed a necromancer. Veron and Elke have advanced their Path, and well...” He adjusted his pack slightly and grinned. “I wouldn't exactly say that we're rich now. But we're rich!”

Ailis walked past him with a groan. “You're ridiculous, Isaac.”

Ellie glanced between them and then looked at Veron.

“It’s pretty close to what he said,” Veron said. “We found a relic and a Balt chest and sold both at the Luna Inn. Which means we probably have enough gold to upgrade our gear or buy whatever we need. We’re here to discuss that and of course, to celebrate.”

“That is wonderful news. Cake and drinks then! On the hut!” Ellie exclaimed.

“I told you that we're rich,” Isaac said.

Ellie just waved him off. “That’s a great reason to celebrate then. And to invite your group to cake!”

They soon enjoyed a red berry crumble with cream, cold and sugared tea, and a bottle of mead that Ellie joined them to drink.

Veron leaned back in the couch and enjoyed the slight buzz that he felt, and the joy of the company. Isaac was retelling the story of their fight with the necromancer, with Ailis occasionally pointing out some untrue embellishments that he made.

Veron was happy. The day had been an all-round success, and yet all of it carried a bitter note with it as well, knowing what he knew. In that moment at least, he wished that he had no part in any time magic business.

But he did.

And he supposed that without it, he wouldn't have gotten to experience any of this in the first place. A mixed bag then. Sweetbitter. Bittersweet.

When the stories came to an end, he took a sip of tea and looked at his team. “I can feel the new strength of my Path settling. My Focus feels expanded too.”

“Are you sure it's not the mead talking?” Isaac suggested.

Veron gave him a smile. “Maybe a part of it. Either way, today was a pretty good day. Thanks for all of your work and focus. And now that we’re… not exactly rich but richer, what are your plans? I know that none of you got into this for the coin but it's a substantial sum, so I wouldn't be surprised if any of your plans would change now.”

“What do you mean? You two have reached the Adequate rank. There's no way I would change plans now. After today, I feel like we could go to the second layer, maybe even the third!” Isaac said and laughed.

“Now that is definitely the mead talking,” Ailis said. “But I agree in principle. If we continue in the same manner, myself and Isaac should catch up soon. And we have new funds. I'll finally get a proper set of plate armor, though I don't think I'll switch my hammer. I do think we should discuss potential joint investments, like a minor healing potion or two.”

“Or an Ice Harmony relic for Elke,” Veron said and looked at her.

She blushed slightly, then glanced away. “I… I wouldn’t want that. It’s too expensive. It’s not really needed anyways.”

“What do you mean?” Veron asked. “Wasn’t that your main goal?”

“I talked to quite a few people since coming here,” she said and paused. The others waited for her to continue, and so she did. “Looking for a relic that can help me train is apparently not the way to go. It would be far too expensive and even then, the relic would have a specific ability and would cost me a lot of Focus to use either way.

“What was suggested to me however, was to buy an enchanted item capable of freezing water. I was looking for something cheap, without luck, but now, I think I can even afford a good one. I'll just have to freeze water beforehand, to have ice ready for use, and it would melt again obviously, which means I would have to carry the item with me. But I could learn to use it in the dungeon, and I could train my Harmony on off days too.”

“That’s wonderful to hear, Elke!” Veron said and smiled. “So you’ll have ice coupled with your arrows. Ailis will have new armor, and Isaac?”

“Armor too, obviously. Mine was falling apart, I’ll be honest. There’s a set I’ve been eyeing, all of it together costs a whopping eleven gold but I might just get it…”

“What about you Veron?” Elke asked.

He thought about it. “I don't need armor. Maybe a high-quality steel mace would be good. And some nicer clothing and padding for underneath my armor. And lanterns obviously! I'm so excited to no longer have to carry torches. How much is an enchanted lantern?”

“Between one and five gold,” Elke said. “It depends on the quality of the make and the brightness.”

“I see,” Veron said and pondered the thought. “And a minor healing potion, how much for that?” He looked at Ailis.

“A Hallow Bristle healing potion costs around five gold. The effects should be similar to the recovery brought by extracting a few skeletons. And there is none of the exhaustion as with my healing aspect.”

“So, everyone gets an upgrade, and we buy a few lanterns and potions for the team?”

Veron felt excited and seeing the same feeling mirrored in their eyes pushed aside any negative emotions he had in regards to the time magic. Things would come as they would, but right now, they could celebrate and get some upgrades.

“I think our next delve should be an easy one,” Veron said. “Close to the pit, to have the delve ground us again after the success today. I know we killed a necromancer, but the dungeon doesn't care.”

The others confirmed with smiles and nods.

“So, should we go shopping together?” Veron asked.

Veron led the group to the Shops. They first visited Sonny and talked to him about all the things they wanted.

“You said you already had an idea of where to get your armor?” Sonny asked Isaac.

“Yes. Several people have recommended Kere’s Armory,” Isaac said. “I've been eyeing a set of Williard’s, a beautiful cuirass likely coupled with a buffcoat, leg guards, and a heavier helmet with a visor that won’t impede my field of vision too much.”

Sonny nodded slightly. “Well, I'm sure you could afford it now. Might not the best that they can make but I suppose that kind of stuff doesn't get to Anvakar in the first place. I will admit that Felrak armor is superior, but at your strength, I could recommend some local armorers as well if you'd like. Kere’s is a good place though. Fair prices for high quality work, and they actually do a fitting beforehand.”

“I would like some recommendations,” Ailis said. “I plan to replace my leather set with plate.”

Sonny scratched his skin. “Yeah. For quality local makers I’d say Tolner, Bolt, or Victoria Smith. Kere’s should have some from all of them. Williard’s an option too but if you want something heavier, those are your names.”

“I thought Ahlstrom made the best plate armor,” Isaac said.

“Aye, but you lot aren’t in the gold tiers yet. Ahlstrom armor is the best high quality heavy armor around but it's gonna weigh you down thrice as much as Veron's stone set over here. I would recommend any of the local makers, and you'll save quite a bit of gold too.”

Isaac didn’t counter the man.

Veron couldn't help but feel like Isaac and Sonny still had some kind of rivalry going on. “What about a shield and mace?”

“A round shield like yours? Any of the names I mentioned. Best ones are from the city of Norkamm too. I assume you won’t get yourself plate armor, in which case you could afford a Johansen shield. I hear they’re lighter than any others of similar durability, and they have a big range in weight too, all high quality. For a mace, I would recommend local, and don't spend too much on that. Try the balance and see how it feels to you, get something as heavy as you can from any of the names I mentioned and you're good.”

“Wouldn't it be better if we bought from those crafters directly?” Elke asked.

“Yes. You would get a pretty perfect fit but they have long waiting lists and for the Felrak stuff, you'd have to travel there. I think if what I assume you made from selling your treasures is the amount that you have, and you plan to get the best that you can afford, then I agree with Isaac. Kere’s is a good bet. You'll be satisfied with what they sell. Do ask for Kere to do the fitting personally if you can though. And ask her for additional advice, if you want. I know a lot. She knows more.”

“Sounds good,” Veron said. “Though now I feel bad about not buying all of that from you, since you shared all of that advice, and your advice on the treasure we found.”

Sonny waved him off. “Ill equipped adventurers die in the dungeon, and dead adventurers don't bring back anything at all. I have lanterns you can look at if you want, but for everything else, I think you're better served at Kere’s.”

“I think we'd like to see the lanterns then,” Veron said and checked with the others who confirmed.

A few minutes later, they were looking at five different enchanted lanterns, ranging in both quality and price. The first one was rusty and the light it produced was bright but flickered slightly. The next one looked better made but it was less bright. The last three looked suitable. They were both reasonably bright, could be dimmed, and were encased in glass and metal. They weren't pretty and were obviously made to fulfill the purpose of a lantern first and foremost.

They quickly discussed and then bought two of the brightest ones, those with the best handles. They paid Sonny eight gold for them in total and then went on through the Shops with the goal of Kere’s Armory.

Veron fastened one of the lanterns to his armor, near his hip.

Before long, they stood in front of a large half-timbered house, the metal plaque reading Kere’s Armory.

It was getting somewhat late in the afternoon by now but they decided to go in and see what the shop had on offer.

The showroom was lit with lanterns much like their newly bought equipment, though Veron didn't miss the fact that the ones here were a lot more aesthetically pleasing, black metal, polished glass, and a warm glow that was both pleasant and bright.

Weapon racks lined the walls and armor stands were spread throughout the broad space, thick wooden beams supporting the upper floors here and there. There were racks of clothing and all manner of weapons here, a few attendees talking to adventurers.

They waited for a little while until one of the attendants came up to them and greeted them. A young man with deep black hair bound in a ponytail.

“That's quite an extensive interest,” the young man said after they’d explained to him what they needed, then he glanced outside through one of the windows. “I'm not sure we can manage all of that today. Especially if you wish to talk to Miss Kere personally.”

“How does tomorrow look?” Veron asked.

“I will check with her and let you know.”

A few minutes later, a red haired woman wearing a white long-sleeved shirt and black leather pants joined them and took in the group with a curious look. She looked to be in her thirties, pale skin and black eyes. “Liviana Kere. Evan tells me you're looking for several fittings of armor and a few new weapons and shields?”

“It's good to meet you. Sonny recommended you,” Veron said and shook her hand. “Two in our group are looking for new armor. I need some padding, clothes, a mace and a shield. Ailis here needs a shield too.”

Liviana gave them all a considering look, then nodded. “Do you have more specific wishes? Or would you like to try a few different things? I could slot you in tomorrow morning at around eleven.”

Isaac, Ailis, and Veron told her what they were looking for.

“I should be able to prepare all that. You're an iron team? May I ask for specific Path levels and your budget? I can make better suggestions based on your current strength and resilience.”

“I'm Adequate,” Veron said. “The others are there or should be there soon. What’s our budget?”

“Twelve gold,” Isaac said.

“Eight for me,” Ailis said.

“I think around five or six for me, but preferably less,” Veron said.

“Great. Evan, can you take full measurements of Isaac, Veron, and Ailis?” Liviana said. “I'll see what will fit you the best based on your requests. Veron, was it?” She asked as Isaac left to a back room with Evan. “You asked for clothing and padding, and new weapons? I assume you're an Elemental Path based on your armor?”

“I am.”

She seemed absorbed in thought for a moment. “May I ask what you're wearing below that armor now?”

“Linen clothing. And some leather to pad the armor, here above and below the shoulders, around my hips, and some in other places that felt needed.”

Liviana blinked her eyes. “Your armor is inspired by that of the Guard of Sor, is it not?”

“It is.”

“But you aren’t nor were you part of the Guard? I'm asking because I'm confused at your choice of clothing. I assume you had the opportunity to see a set of their armor for a longer period of time, to model your own stone set from it?”

Ailis gave him a glance.

She inferred all that from just what I shared?

“That is all true, yes. I assume this relates to a recommendation?”

“It does. I just didn't want to assume. And how long have you been at the Adequate stage?”

“I advanced very recently.”

“Good. Then I’ll have a few things for you to try tomorrow. Various arming clothes and gambesons specifically. I’ve had the pleasure of fighting alongside an adventurer with an Earth Harmony for some time, and we tried and learned a few things.

“The padding you've used allows you to move somewhat efficiently with your armor, but just barely. With proper arming clothes, you'll be able to add quite a bit more weight and protection, even more so now that you're at the Adequate stage. I know you feel comfortable with the stone so close to your skin, but I promise it will be like night and day.”

That sounds promising. And finally someone who’s familiar with my Path and Harmony.

“I'm happy to try.”

“Great. And I’ll have a variety of shields and maces ready too.”

Evan returned with Isaac then, and left again with Ailis.

“Then if that's everything, I'll see you tomorrow,” Liviana Kere said. “With a bunch of gear to try on.”

“Elke, you didn't want anything?” Veron asked.

“I don't like wearing steel,” she said.

“We have various lighter options too.”

Elke blinked her eyes and shook her head.

“Alright,” Liviana said.

Veron smiled. “I think that’s all then. Thank you for the time and I'm excited to try it all out.”

Liviana gave them a small bow. “I’m glad to be of service. And I wish you a good evening.”

They said their goodbyes, Veron had his measurements taken too, and then they left for dinner.

“I’ve got a good feeling about this,” Ailis said.

“Me too. I like her,” Veron said.

“Because she’s beautiful, knowledgeable, and probably powerful too?” Isaac asked.

“All of that too. But mostly because she seems to care a whole lot about her craft,” Veron said.

Isaac made a whistling sound and Ailis rolled her eyes.

“Elke, should we check out the enchantment stores today or tomorrow before we get our armor fitting?” Veron asked.

It was getting dark by now but Veron knew that many of the stores were open quite late.

“You don’t need to join,” Elke said.

“I’d like to. But it’s fine if you want to go alone too,” Veron said.

“I would also like to join,” Isaac said.

“That’s fine. Tomorrow morning maybe?” Elke asked.

“Sounds good. We can meet up at the Guild at the usual time? But now we should get food, I'm starving.”

Veron touched the lantern that was now fastened to his armor, connecting to the enchantment with his Focus, just like he had back on the road with the Silver Wolves.

He fueled and activated the magic within, which quickly brightened their surroundings. “This is so much more convenient than a torch.”

And tomorrow we’ll get new armor and gear for the team. I wonder how much of a difference the arming clothes are really going to make.

I hope it’s as much as she claimed.
