At the heart of the cursed forest, a land devoid of light was blanketed by thick, heavy fog. Massive trees with dark purple bark exuded a sinister, repulsive air. Countless wooden tendrils hung down to the ground from innumerable branches. They towered dozens or even hundreds of meters high, with no one able to see or acknowledge their existence.

The forest, which had once been utterly silent, emitted a quietly tormenting scream that invaded the mind and nerves. The cursed villagers and Eltharoc, all manner of creatures, were all assaulted by sounds they could not hear, their eyes flying wide open. At the base of the trees stood a castle constructed from bricks, mud, and branches fused together as solidly as hardened concrete.

There, a figure of a young woman in a long purple robe that concealed her alluring figure stood wearing a head covering that hid her face before a carved wooden idol. There was an altar table and a red mattress upon which a young girl lay sleeping. She fixed her gaze — dark as pitch-black pits — upon this child and tilted her face upward toward the top of the carving.

"How utterly audacious. How dare you steal the life force away from His Lordship." The dark witch spoke in a sharp, furious voice. Nails painted black dug into the flesh of her hand until blood seeped out. Beside her, white fog brought in a figure cloaked in a robe with a pumpkin for a head and green flames to this room.

"Getting irritated doesn't get you anything, Estella."

"Jack, two Rulers of the Mountain have died in succession. Someone has infiltrated the castle and escaped alive. On top of that, one-tenth of the forest's life force has been drawn away! Do you have any idea how many years it would take us to accumulate that much power! What exactly happened?"

"I am just as confused. The humans who came in this time are more skilled than anticipated. There is one who evaded my pursuit and one who dispatched the Ruler of the Mountain with ease. These are not things to be taken lightly at all."

"So you're saying you lost your nerve." Estella narrowed her eyes at her interlocutor, her voice cutting.

"I'm only saying this is unlike every other time. We must handle this with thoroughness. The more powerful an entity a human kills, the more power they add to themselves. Repeatedly sending in minions and Rulers of the Mountain for them to kill will only make them stronger and harder to deal with. Especially — I can now confirm that they possess Divine Rods." Jack used reason to quell Estella's agitation. Estella, who had been releasing unstable waves of power, slowly brought her emotions back to a state of calm. She gazed through her translucent veil at the demon before her and spoke in a firm voice.

"You didn't come back empty-handed, did you."

"Of course I did not come back empty-handed. I tracked the intruders all the way to the abandoned village and witnessed something of interest. They are farming."

"Farming? Nonsense. In this forest, of all places? Your joke is entertaining enough to make me drink twenty cups of tea."

"It is the truth. I saw with my own eyes a single human using a strange art to grow rice, beans, and potatoes. I don't know what kind of sorcery it uses, but it is doing agriculture in our forest. And that is the cause of the forest's life force being stolen."

"How utterly heretical! Are you saying the forest's power was stolen because of growing rice? Joke a little less, Jack O'Lantern." Estella raised her voice. Ripples of disturbance appeared in the air from the overflowing dark energy. It was an overwhelming power so completely superior that even Jack O'Lantern was made wary.

"I speak the truth. I cannot lie before Your Eminence. That is something you know better than anyone. I saw with my own eyes the forest's life force being absorbed into the rice and the potatoes, and them growing. In the end, all of it accumulated within the body of the single human using the art."

"Accumulated in the body of a single human, you say. Are you telling me that more than one-tenth of the forest's life force is inside the body of one human? Which means — if we capture that human, we would recover the lost power? You truly live up to your name. Your work has never been deficient." Estella put an end to her irritation. The words and tone she used now carried composure and had been carefully calculated.

"What do you want to do? Shall I go retrieve the power? The day of the ceremony is drawing ever closer." Jack O'Lantern glanced at the young girl lying unconscious on the mattress before the altar table. The girl was completely unaware of anything happening in the outside world. Or perhaps it was better that she hadn't woken up — both for her own sake and for everyone else's.

"It is better to wait for His Lordship's message first. We are His Lordship's hands and feet. Hands and feet should not move on their own until the brain commands them. We must conduct ourselves in a manner befitting hands and feet. Because His Lordship's thoughts have never been wrong. If we follow His Lordship's wishes strictly, all of us will certainly encounter happiness."

The abandoned village was filled with an atmosphere of celebration. Boiled potatoes, roasted potatoes, fried potatoes — if Ryze were here, she would have to exclaim in wonder at the potato festival where everyone had unanimously chosen to eat nothing but potatoes. The soft, fluffy texture was such that merely taking a bite sent flavor flowing and coursing through to the brain, delivering an experience no one would ever forget.

Exploding Potatoes were an expensive commodity that required saving up a large sum of money to buy and eat. They were even more expensive than Grade A5 beef at a high-end restaurant. Even William bought them up at a high price, and when he sold them the price must have been 3–4 times higher than the purchase price.

No salt, no paprika powder, no butter — but everyone still enjoyed and savored the flavor of the potatoes. Jinyo watched the people gathered around the bonfires from the shadows beneath the floorboards of a house. If Ryze were here, she would surely be so happy to have a mountain of potatoes to eat. More and more people were entering the village, but no one had ever seen a single girl who resembled Ryze.

Jinyo had asked Nephrin to put up a reward saying that whoever found clues about Ryze would receive a handsome reward, but it had been of no use.

'Ryze hasn't died. She's still alive. But where has she disappeared to?' Jinyo checked the system window to inspect the members of his family every day. Ryze had never lost any life force, which meant she had to be safe. There was no way a seven-year-old girl could survive on her own, but there must be someone kindhearted looking after her.

At this point in time, he had now come to possess another type of crop that was of great interest.

[Celestial Bean] [Quality: E] [Status: Boosted] Properties: [Restores Stamina 2 Stars] [Heals Wounds 1 Star] [Boost 1 Star] [Aids Cultivation 1 Star]

A bean from the immortal bean plant, born from the will of heaven and earth, possessing the power of restoration. An essential ingredient for divine food and elixirs of immortality. Legend has it that no matter how close to death one may be, it can extend the time left to live by several more days.

The Celestial Bean was similar to the Celestial Rice, being a crop variety obtained from a son and daughter. Celestial Rice was highly beneficial for filling the stomach and aiding cultivation. The Celestial Bean was like a miraculous medicine that helped restore stamina and heal injuries.

It was entirely different from the Exploding Potato and Happy Carrot. The crop varieties obtained from descendants were superior in every regard. There was also another type of flower obtained from Rina that he had not yet thought about planting here.

The experience gained from the Celestial Rice and Celestial Beans was greater than from the Exploding Potatoes. It drove Jinyo to reach Level 40, and the higher his level rose, the more efficiently he could draw out power from his stat values to use at full capacity. It was as though if one had a Strength of 100, with the percentage added by level it would become 100 (×40). Even if it wasn't visible in the system, this was what was happening to everyone.

At this point he might be able to fight the Ruler of the Mountain, Jinyo thought. The fastest way to increase his power stats for Jinyo, aside from training, was to consume produce with special status. But all of the produce carried the Boosted status without exception.

"I can feel it clearly — my lifespan being burned away… My body doesn't seem to recognize the Boost Curse as poison, does it." Jinyo had eaten a large amount of potatoes and cursed fruits with the Boosted status. If converted into Black Stars, it would probably amount to a 2-star curse — as severe as someone who had eaten cursed fruit at full impact — yet Jinyo had no symptoms of hallucinations or addiction.

The people who ate the Exploding Potatoes were the same. No one was addicted or hallucinating. It seemed those symptoms were exclusive to cursed fruits. Eating potatoes with the Boosted status also appeared to help prevent addictive symptoms from forming.

Everything was proceeding well. All that remained was to find Ryze and fight the final boss, and they would finally be able to return to their peaceful home.

"Jinyo! Jinyo!" Someone's voice rang out from far away. Jinyo turned toward the source and saw a young boy running toward him wild-eyed at his resting spot. Jinyo leapt over the fence to meet the boy before he arrived.

"What is it? Is there an enemy attack?"

"No, sir. Nephrin sent me to come get you to look at an injured person."

An injured person? How serious did it have to be for her to send someone to fetch him? Jinyo didn't know and didn't intend to speculate. He ran after the young boy who was leading the way, until he arrived at a hut near the entrance to the village. Jinyo caught a strong, foul smell of blood. Blood doesn't smell foul when it's still fresh. When blood starts to smell foul, it means the wound has been there for a long time — which was something frightening.

'How much blood has to be there for the stench to be this overwhelming!' Jinyo ducked through the doorway. The sight that greeted him was someone he recognized. Clothing that looked like a beggar's was covered in wound marks and soaked through with blood. The complexion had turned purple. Thick blood flowed without stopping from the wound.

"Theo!?"

"Haha… it's my brother, after all." Theo could still laugh even while at death's door.

"Doctor Jinyo, he's not going to make it. We have to do something now." Nephrin spoke to snap Jinyo back to his senses. He was confused. Jinyo wanted to shout that he was not a doctor and ask why Nephrin always insisted on having him be the one to treat people — but this was truly not the time to be complaining.

Sage's Eye!

[Grievous Severe Wounds] [Curse: Cannot Recover Naturally] [Dark Forest Curse] [Bleeding] [Severe Blood Loss]

"Nephrin, have you given him fruit yet?"

"I gave him some but it had no effect."

"No effect? Then how can we treat him?" Jinyo was confused and at a loss. Could this be the result of [Curse: Cannot Recover Naturally]? His body simply would not accept healing wounds according to its bodily structure. Even eating cursed fruit that could help accelerate the recovery of both stamina and injuries would be meaningless.

"Ha, haha… this is bad, brother." Theo's voice came out hoarse and rasping. He grew weaker by the moment, evidenced by his complexion shifting from purple to white.

"Please don't speak. Conserve your strength and keep your mind together. You must not fall asleep, absolutely not." Nephrin spoke loudly to drive her voice into Theo's eardrums. Even if she was going to be mentally cursed for being annoying, so be it.

"Your daughter… your daughter." Theo's gaze was fixed on Jinyo alone. Jinyo's eyes flew wide.

"My daughter? You know where Ryze is, don't you. Theo." Jinyo asked loudly, but what could he expect from a man who was about to die? Theo was close to losing consciousness from the poison of his wounds and blood that wouldn't stop flowing. The damage that had occurred was beyond what he could recover from on his own. The man who had scored the highest on the Hunter exam was now teetering on the edge of death. Whatever he had encountered must have been terrifying and something that should not have been touched.

"Doctor Jinyo, he's… lost consciousness." Nephrin was at a loss. Cursed fruit was the only thing she had used to snatch patients back from the hands of the death god. Now that cursed fruit could do nothing, they had completely run out of ways to save his life.

"He's not dead yet! I have to do something!" Theo had wanted to tell Jinyo something about Ryze. It was impossible for Jinyo to simply let Theo die. What Jinyo and Nephrin had consistently done all along was to let the body recover itself through its natural mechanisms — no different from being cut by a knife and treating it with a bandage. It was just making that mechanism occur through the Boost Curse.

The Boost Curse was about accelerating recovery by burning lifespan to age faster. It was not direct treatment, but rather utilizing the side effect as a natural recovery mechanism. That was something completely unusable right now. Because inside Hollowgate there were no medical equipment or personnel, Jinyo had been forced to use this method — but now that he could no longer use it, he had to choose a different approach.

'Must treat from external factors.' Jinyo ran into the kitchen. He poured Celestial Rice that had been milled into white grains and set it to boil. He followed with large green beans, Happy Carrots cut into small pieces, and diced Exploding Potatoes. All of these were produce carrying the Boosted status. And finally — cursed fruit with the most concentrated medicinal properties.

It was a terrible combination to have to put fruit into porridge, but this was not food made for eating out of enjoyment. What Jinyo needed was food that would provide energy and have healing properties. Jinyo ground the flesh of the cursed fruit and boiled it in water. Setting aside the skin and seeds, he used everything else, mixing it together with the porridge until there was created a special five-ingredient Power Boost Porridge.

[Congratulations. You have cooked the dish: Power Boost Porridge!]

[Power Boost Porridge (D+): A magical food born from five special plants with Boosted status, with Celestial Rice and Celestial Beans as components of an elixir of immortality. Restores Stamina 3 Stars (Immediate). Heals Wounds 2 Stars. Accelerates Cultivation 2 Stars. Effective for 24 hours.]

'Magical food. So this is what magical food is.' Jinyo had heard before of power users who specialized exclusively in cooking — using ingredients from dungeons or Hollowgate to create dishes. These were called magical foods.

Magical food grants special medicinal properties to those who consume it. It was said that without dedicated specialized study, there was no way to cook magical food. Jinyo had merely tossed ingredients together into a pot, and magical food had come to be. It was not because he was any kind of genius at cooking. Rather, it was because the ingredients he had on hand were powerful enough to produce magical food by their very nature.

There was no time left! Jinyo ran with the pot of Power Boost Porridge in his arms and placed it beside Theo's bed.

"Nephrin, open his mouth for me."

"Doctor Jinyo, he's lost consciousness."

"That's exactly why I need you to help me. Quick — there's no time." Jinyo hurried to ladle the porridge from the pot into a bowl. Nephrin didn't know what he was going to do. She held Theo's jaw open for Jinyo, who used a spoon to force it down Theo's throat. He was too frantic to be gentle with Theo. The hot porridge nearly scalded his throat causing Theo to choke, but Jinyo would not allow that. No matter how much he wanted to choke, Theo had to swallow it down.

The survival instinct made Theo's throat move to swallow the strangely-flavored porridge. His body felt burning hot all over. The temperature inside his body rose until it felt as though he were burning in flames. Every cell in his body was exploding!

"AAAAUGH!" Theo flung his mouth open and screamed with all his life. Jinyo and Nephrin together held Theo down against the bed. Theo swung his arms wildly, striking everyone who came near. He had no consciousness remaining — the only thing driving him to move was the survival instinct. The agony generated by consuming the Power Boost Porridge was forcing his body to do something.

"It hurts, it hurts, it HURTS!" Theo was in acute distress around his navel. The center of power was there, and it was exploding from the excess of energy pouring in.

"Stay calm. Breathe in deeply and circulate the energy." Jinyo shouted to call back the senses of Theo, who was going out of his mind from the transformation. Theo struggled more intensely but could not overcome the strength of the restraints holding him to the bed. Theo slowly calmed down. He adjusted his breathing to align with his cultivation technique.

Dark-colored fluid slowly seeped out through the skin. Green plana welled up and grew concentrated. Dark blood was expelled entirely along with wounds that slowly knitted themselves closed. It was not healing instantaneously, but it was better than it had been.

"Doctor Jinyo, why can he heal himself now?" Nephrin asked with curiosity.

"Because this is not the natural healing mechanism. It is because of the porridge I fed him. The power healing him right now is power from an external source." Jinyo looked at the Power Boost Porridge that had worked better than expected. He had merely felt that the properties of Celestial Rice — which helped slow aging — and the wound healing of Celestial Beans might be applicable in combination, but he hadn't thought it would work this well.

Theo had not yet regained consciousness. He continued breathing through his personal cultivation technique. Not only had he crossed over the threshold of death — his level of training had also risen another step. Theo's energy capacity expanded by as much as 15% from eating the Power Boost Porridge just that single time.

Before long, Theo would surely wake up and call out for more Power Boost Porridge. Jinyo went into the kitchen to cook two more pots for him to eat and recover his strength until his wounds had healed. He hoped Theo would regain his senses quickly. Then he would be able to find out where his daughter Ryze was.
