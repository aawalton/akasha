Hello everyone!

After almost a year of writing and editing, the rewrite is finally complete!

For any newcomers, this story takes place in a universe where humans evolved upon an ocean world. These humans were landlocked upon their native continent, but eventually made their way to the second industrial revolution before discovering other landmasses across the world. Book 1 covers the journey of the first successful expedition to these foreign lands, which are inhabited by many strange and alien creatures.

This chapter contains maps and some preliminary information about this world, but the actual story starts with the next chapter.

For returning readers, I ended up making quite a few changes, as well as completely rewriting the first 120k words or so (with some significant changes to the rest). I could post an exhaustive list of changes at the end of this volume, if that's something that people would like to see.

As per popular request, the original draft version of the story is preserved as a separate volume. These chapters will also be prefaced with [OLD] in their title.

This story of mine was inspired by works like The Martian and Project Hail Mary (By Andy Weir), and the Temeraire series (By Naomi Novik).

VikasRao on Deviantart has also been a great help in providing and inspiring ideas for this story, and their work on speculatively biology (featuring realistic dragons) is insanely detailed.

I wrote this story for several reasons, but mostly in an attempt to cover a more interesting and sensible first contact scenario. I also wanted to create a "magical" world without magic – reality can be stranger and far more interesting than fiction, after all.

And finally, special thanks to my beta readers for their help and feedback!
