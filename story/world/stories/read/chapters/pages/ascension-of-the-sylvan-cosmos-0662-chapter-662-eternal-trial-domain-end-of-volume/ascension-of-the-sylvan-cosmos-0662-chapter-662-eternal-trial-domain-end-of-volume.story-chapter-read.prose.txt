If she wanted to use a water monster, she needed to place it near a lake, river, or similar body of water. Boss Monsters were not called as such only because they were strong. Far from it. The reason the <Dungeon> placed the title <Boss Monster> on a creature was that, when defeated, the loot they dropped was several times higher quality than that of other monsters.

Therefore, the <Dungeon System> couldn’t afford Liu Shu to tilt the balance using environmental advantages. Look at the Earth Dragon; he resided in a rocky layout, with mountains that complemented his strength and powerful anti-flight spells.

You either defeated a Boss Monster at its best, or you didn’t defeat it at all. It was as simple as that.
