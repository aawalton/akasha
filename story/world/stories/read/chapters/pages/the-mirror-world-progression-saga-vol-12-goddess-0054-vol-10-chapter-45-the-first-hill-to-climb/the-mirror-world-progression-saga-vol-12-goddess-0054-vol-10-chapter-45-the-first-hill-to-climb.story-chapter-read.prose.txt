The enemy more or less fell back as a whole, leaving thinned-out groupings as the majority went behind a natural incline and an obvious additional raised earthworks — a continual big, steep bump, and the first barrier to cross to get to the real walls. This one stretched practically from one horizon to the next.

Samantha called for regrouping, though letting Keinauz rampage around and continue refreshing his buff.

They’ll pull something here, right?

With a measure of cautious strategy, they bunched up normal troops under mass mobility buffs and began a charge on the wall, sticking to only the most durable of elites to support them and minimizing casualties. At the last moment, flyers such as Angels, griffin-riders, and Zadkiel’s varied demons rose up out of the ranks en masse, aiming to support the ground charge with their own assault. The enemy began to pivot with a surge of their own flyers from further back, but they couldn’t match them instantly with the surprise shift.

The enemy unleashed a sequential response that burst over the wall. An intense storm of magic was first, dulling the charge right as they were at the cusp, and incinerating or ripping flyers out of the air. The allied forces scrambled to counter, and then counterattack with detonating magics of their own where the attacks were coming from. To cause this sort of intense AoE overwhelm, the Dominion wizards needed to be closer and risk themselves. Expendable, but wizards were always the priority target.

But no sooner than they thought to do this, the ground burst up, and the whole wall erupted as if consumed in earthquakes. The wall itself seemed to rise. The earth fell away to reveal a long line of gigantic figures in scale armor of different colors from head to toe, and behind the helms were the glowing red eyes of active undead. Over a hundred of them.

The majority of them swung down spiked clubs seemingly made from a tree, but enchanted and enduring the terrible force of contact with the ground. A few had other weapons, like enormous swords or flails, and one even swung a black scythe.

Shit. Slightly more impressive than I expected.

In mere moments, more than a thousand troops died to the giants, and they stomped forward into the ranks after breaking the vanguard, aiming to cut deeper and decimate more. Attacks, even from elites, were bouncing off the thick scale armor.

In horror, Samantha realized they all wore dragon scales.

The voice in her head chimed, bitter and derisive. ‘So many horrors you stowed away. Aeons of decrepit monstrosities, and what were they to you? I know exactly: curiosities, that was all. In your cruelty, you experimented with the corpses you made of the great. Why not make use of them, after all? Elbow-deep in abominable acts, you divined your ultimate powers and left the rest to rot. Sadly, what you dismissed, others collated. Always the same, these gauntlet runs you harry us with.’

It seems the Director doesn’t like the undead.

Samantha refused to let the words distract her. Flying above, she unleashed a slurry of attacks on one of the giants. Scorched with an array of her best, hit with raw disintegration as a final blast, and all she did was stagger it, leaving it smoking but still on its feet. The armor had protected it. It turned its gaze briefly up to her, then moved on.

It was among the most grating of reactions she’d endured in her life on Calrenazzod, just to be bypassed and ignored like that. But she understood why — she was inaccessible, strong, durable, just like it was. It was pursuing the best damage it could deal.

And she felt the draw — to expend her most precious resources and force success or failure. She grimaced.

The Goddess sent a command army-wide. “Retreat enhancement protocols! Non-elites fall back and get away from those things. Elites, draw them and occupy their attention.”

Dax was quick to respond. “Doing it. The enemy is going to charge and crowd us. They won’t care about friendly fire.”

“It can’t be helped. Ranged fire on them. Delay and disrupt. Logical?”

“Sure, and I have just the thing. I call it a Redberry.”

Redberry was also quick to respond. “Entanglement? That’s simple. We’ll handle it.”

Samantha winced. “I’ll try to keep any fire off.”

“Not necessary, dear. What burns faster, the undead or my artifice? Does it matter? They can both go up in flames if you like. In this place, nature is ready for sacrifice by any means to satisfy our hatred. This, I assure you.”

“Oh, right… Noted.”

A massive burst of enchantments went off over the troops from prepared groups of spellcasters further back, granting them hyper-speed for a few moments. In that span, they practically teleported away from the rampaging giant undead en masse, falling back into the deeper ranks and escaping death. It had to have saved thousands from being crushed under the tremendous power of giant stomping feet and swinging, catastrophic weapons.

Her elites formed many small, coordinated pockets to occupy the giants. The undead titans tried to dismissively crush these foes, too, but found them either evasive or enduring, while also capable of enough strength to push back. Zadkiel’s dumb ass got smushed under one, but he seemed to surge from underneath, causing the giant to stumble and lose its footing. Not letting up, the Demon King then exploded off the ground and punched the giant in the face, toppling it to the ground in a great crash. Not killed by any means, but it was a nice shot…

Samantha shifted tactics. Knowing the bastard giants would take a while to whittle down, she directly attacked a club. One barrage of magic, followed up by a [Spellfyr: Unleash], disintegrated it. It even stunned the giant, as it was winding the weapon up when it was destroyed, so it looked over at its empty hand in confusion.

Fuck! Why did I even bother? That cost me SP. I don’t have an infinite amount of it. Maybe I can destroy them without it.

Meanwhile, the enemy had indeed charged down the broken hills of dirt the giants had risen from, aiming to fill space and harry the limited number of elites facing the giants…

And then many dozens of dryads spawned in front of them, along with Redberry herself, and the entire line was swallowed in roots, brambles, and vines. In mere moments, the whole advance had been halted. Whatever it was the spellcasters behind the line tried to clear the mass with, the Counter Crew blocked it soundly.

Hrm. I almost do want to deliberately burn them now.

The fighting with the giants on the ground was fierce, with warriors leaping around and Angels swooping in, trying to score wounds through armor. But it didn’t matter if they did — the giants didn’t have living physiology behind that armor. What good were stabs? One of them had even had multiple stabs into its eye sockets through the gap in the visor, and it was still functioning.

Counterattacks from the giants had scored casualties even among their elites, as their crushing strikes rapidly shredded through wound negations if allowed to follow through while someone was pinned. Servitors were dismissed through destruction, and demons were sent back from whence they came. Knights, powerful Naugites, and troll champions died. A minotaur hero was killed for the first time anyone had seen, having moved in quite heroically to save another who’d fallen, and getting turned into a splatter for his efforts.

The occurrence set every other minotaur to roar and howl up into the sky with a sound fit to rattle bones. Thankfully, this was more of a solemn thing rather than a berserker rage. They focused and fought all the harder, but to the minotaur, they kept supreme discipline. Even the guards that were held back to protect key souls kept their positions, eager to fight though they were.

Keinauz had managed to somehow jam his blade into a gap in the lower leg and cripple a bone, causing a giant to fall to a knee. It then backhand swatted him across the battlefield like a man would a hamster, still with his blade lodged in the leg.

Samantha just caught a spell materializing over that injured leg, and it was only her noticing the subtle magic with the rest of the Counter Crew busy as hell… She quickly countered it before she even fully understood what it did. But it was necromantic magic, and it was quickly clear what the intention was.

Holy fucking shit, they’re primed to repair injury to these abominable things! How the fuck will we whittle them down, then? Alright, that's it. We have to reveal them; it’s time. Hopefully, they can help against these shitheads.

Funneling targeting info into the hands of Jeeves and Dax to try and go after that necromancer, Samantha also gave the command for the [Radiancer] elves to activate, and attack the wretched ass, giant undead!

Soon, from the protection of packs of elite guards, including the precious minotaurs among them, a network of radiant beams lit up the dreary gray environment. Two volleys, twenty plus strong, hit two giants, each beam widening slightly all the way across the distance until it hit a wide area of the figure. All across the torso and head, circles of light grew even more intense, where any save Samantha herself had to turn away or be blinded. Everywhere the radiance touched, the light penetrated between armor gaps and even individual scales, enveloping the giant undead almost completely.

The armor could not keep out the light. It touched them at their core, at their very source of existence.

No sooner than the beams flared than they abated, and from out of the armor smoke and dust billowed like a demolished house, as the whole of their forms collapsed, destroyed utterly, leaving nothing but inactive limbs falling into a giant pile with a tremendous crash from the armor, causing a great racket.

There were cheers, too, from the armies of the allies, because this was salvation against the seemingly unstoppable.

Meanwhile, the Radiancer elves could be seen standing on high ground, stylishly attired as always in resplendent sorcerer robes, smoking hands up in elegance as they posed with pride and grace at the success of their own handiwork.

Floating in the sky, Samantha guffawed. This was far more than she could have hoped for from them. Echoing her words into the army-wide gestalt, she commanded, “All elites, go defensive and stay alive! Dax, pull chosen members back to protect the Radiancers more securely! Everyone, everywhere, guard our sniper-elves with your very lives! And we'd better have mirror-captures of those poses, Jeeves!”
