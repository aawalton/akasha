Asher’s plan had been a fairly simple one. Try to find The Lost Lady, watch her for a bit until he spotted an opening, and then pop over there and kill her before she even knew what was going on. His hope was that by cutting off the head of the snake, it would free Moxy as well, and then he’d be safe to bring his entire team out again to help finish off the minions and save the ones he could.

However, the sight of his severed leg lying before The Lost Lady changed everything.

There was no end to the number of horrifying things someone could do to another person if they had the right elements, skills, and required materials. Not even factoring in all the different skills the lich must have picked up over her many centuries of unlife, they knew for a fact that she had a Flesh element. For all Asher knew with his entire leg in her possession she could force his body to start rotting from the inside out, or cause his flesh to start falling off his own skeleton regardless of how far he ran. The possibilities were potentially limitless, and he cursed as he realized she’d forced his hand. Without any plan more complete than ‘get over there and stab her before she touches that leg’ Asher turned the corner and hurled his marked dagger as hard as he could into the cavern.

But as he waited for it to finish flipping through the air, a clump of hair still attached to The Lost Lady’s head shot out, reaching far farther than it should have been able to as it slapped his dagger out of the air and sent it clattering to the side, still at the edge of the cavern. The lich turned toward him almost as an afterthought, the burning white flames of her eyes flickering as she took him in.

Asher waited a beat for her to start monologuing just like every other lich he’d faced off against thus far, but The Lost Lady simply stood there, examining him like an interesting science experiment. Once he realized she wasn’t going to speak, he triggered Recall, teleporting over to his dagger despite the fact it hadn’t gotten as close as he’d wanted.

Even if she didn’t intend to taunt him or flaunt her power, that seemed to be the starting pistol for the lich, as she placed a hand on his severed leg without even looking away from him. Asher managed two hops toward the center of the room before her skill hit him and he collapsed to the ground, violently shaking as he felt his entire body seize up. It was like a powerful current of electricity was washing through him, forcing his muscles to contract and burn within his flesh. Even through Complete Resilience dulling the pain, it took everything Asher had not to scream as he thrashed on the floor. Weirdly enough he could somehow tell she wasn’t actually damaging his body, but that thought wasn’t all that comforting with the sheer amount of pain he was suffering at that moment.

“Damn it, Runt, that’s the saddest assassination attempt I’ve ever seen!” Moxy shouted at him, narrowing her eyes as she continued. “You’re not going to let a little pain keep you down, are you? Get up and kill that monster! Or better yet, cut these blasted hairs so I can do it myself!”

“Little… busy… here!” Asher struggled to reply, triggering Complete Resilience’s second-tier effect to purge the lich’s skill for one blissful moment and freeing himself from the pain. Unfortunately, all The Lost Lady needed to do was trigger her own skill again, and Asher went right back to writhing on the ground as he had before with nothing but a chunk of his already low energy drained from him.

“They’re coming your way!” Moxy shouted, and Asher vaguely noted two of the minions leave their spot from the edge of the cavern and start walking toward him. None of them had auras, and each shared that same, eerie ‘lost’ class that proved there was no saving them.

Realizing he was out of time, Asher gritted his teeth as he made his best guess. He’d been trying to save his ace in the hole until he had a better idea of exactly what The Lost Lady was going to attack him with, but the time for certainties was long gone. Mentally making his choice, he triggered the third-tier effect of Complete Resilience.

Immunity to Flesh!

The moment he made his decision, Asher became immune to any sort of energy-based skill that came from the Flesh element.

And the horrific pain washing through his body didn’t light up in the slightest.

Asher clenched his eyes shut and cursed his bad luck as he continued thrashing on the cave floor, vaguely wondering what sort of skill she was using if it wasn’t one from the Flesh element. His next best guesses were either Pain or Lightning, but it didn’t really matter at this point. He could only swap immunities every couple of minutes, and he had a feeling The Lost Lady wasn’t going to give him that kind of time. Desperate, he tried flickering in and out of the astral realm, only to find the pain followed him there just as easily. Regardless of where he went, she had access to his entire leg. There was no running away from her skill, no hiding from the pain that seemed to encompass his entire world.

Need to get… my leg… away from her… Asher thought, his mind starting to fade as the pain seemed to kick it up a notch. All of this was happening so quickly that the minions heading his way were still halfway across the small cavern, leisurely taking their time as if The Lost Lady wanted to ensure he was unconscious before they reached him. Asher forced his eyes to remain open, matching the lich’s burning stare as she kept a hand firmly pressed against his leg.

The pain was so vibrant and all-encompassing that as Asher’s vision started to fade, he blinked as he spotted faint tendrils of what almost looked like tritessence stretching out from the severed stump of his leg lying before the lich. Without even looking, Asher knew those tendrils of mysterious energy would reach all the way back to his body, forming a connection between him and his leg regardless of the distance between them. It didn’t make any sense, and the tendrils seemed to flicker in and out of existence as he began to lose consciousness, but that didn’t matter. With his last moment of semi-clarity, Asher tensed his muscles like he had a thousand times before.

Triggering Astral Dip and sending his severed leg into the astral realm.

Instantly, the pain vanished as his missing limb was ripped away from The Lost Lady’s touch. Even the lich looked startled for a moment, the white flames in her eye sockets flickering as she finally looked away from him to stare down at the now-empty stone slab before her. While she did that, Asher laughed as he stretched out his aching body, simply enjoying the sensation of being alive as he took in a welcome notification.

It seemed he’d unlocked his second blended skill with The Lost Lady’s assistance, and not a moment too soon.

Remote Control (active) - lvl 1 - Command every part of your body regardless of distance, realm, or condition.

Spatial and Eternity, he realized, grinning as he forced himself to his feet. Despite the fact that he didn’t have any Eternal Mark on his severed leg, and that it was in another realm, Asher knew with complete and utter certainty precisely where his missing leg was. With a flex of will, he triggered Recall, his grin only widening as the missing limb appeared in his open hand.

Remote Control - lvl 2

“I’ll be right back,” Asher said, slipping into the astral just in time to dodge what looked like a spear made of hair as it rocketed out of The Lost Lady’s head and tried to skewer him.

Downing a potion of Lesser Restoration, Asher held his missing leg against his stump, feeling his flesh and bones knit themselves together again in seconds. As excited as he was to be whole again and free from the pain, Asher forced himself to calm down and take a moment to think.

As things stood, even with his leg returned to him, he was in a bit of a rough spot. His energy was almost completely gone, Moxy was still held prisoner, and the lich had three physically powerful minions still under her control that they had to worry about. Not to mention she herself was obviously incredibly deadly. This wasn’t like The King of Death, who was all but helpless once his creations were taken care of. The Lost Lady would definitely be harder to take down than her minions, and the unfortunate truth was they simply weren’t in the position to fight her any more.

Just as Asher realized they were going to have to retreat and try again another day, Moxy called out to him.

“Runt, if you can just get me free from these hairs, I can take her! She caught us by surprise and took over before I knew what was going on, but I have a skill that should protect me and keep her from doing it again! So quit dawdling and hurry up!”

Asher blinked at the admittance, racking his brain for any such skill. Try as he might, he couldn’t recall Moxy ever using anything during any of their sparring sessions that he would think would keep her safe. However, he did recall her mentioning at one point that she had two of her blended skills already. He knew about the blended skill from her Life and Pelts elements, but he didn’t know about the second.

Deciding to trust in his teammate, Asher returned to the material realm and compressed space, lunging past the two minions just as they reached him as he sprinted for Moxy. Another spear of hair shot out toward him, and Asher ripped out his void dagger, slicing clean through it and causing The Lost Lady to audibly hiss as she immediately pulled the rest of her hair back from him as though it were a part of her own body he’d just erased from existence. With that space, Asher ran toward Moxy and the last minion, keeping his eyes locked on it in case it tried anything. Sure enough, the minion came straight for him, but Asher didn’t even slow down. Slicing through its outstretched arm, he spun and cut off its head, kicking it aside as he continued toward Moxy.

Of course, The Lost Lady wasn’t going to make his goal easy for him.

The thousands of hairs still in total control of Moxy flexed, and the warrior leapt into action, blasting herself away from him as she began punching Air Blast after Air Blast in his direction. Already running on fumes, which were also rapidly being sucked down by the void dagger in his hand, Asher gritted his teeth as he did his best to ignore her attempts at blasting a crater into his skull. Unlike the previous cavern they were in with hair descending from the ceiling and connecting from countless angles, there was only one direction the hair was flowing toward this time. As Moxy leapt away from him, Asher continued forward, grinning as he let Secret Seeker show him where to aim before swiping his void dagger straight through the near-invisible hairs.

Secret Seeker - lvl 32

The Lost Lady hissed again, even louder this time as her strands were severed by the ever-hungry power of the void. But far louder than that was Moxy, who roared at the top of her lungs as she triggered Air Nova and blasted the remaining hairs off her body.

“Finally!” she shouted, cracking her neck as she stared bloody murder at The Lost Lady. Moxy’s entire body began glowing a brilliant violet just like their injuries did when she took the time to heal them with her Life element, but Asher had the feeling that wasn’t exactly what she had in mind. The bright color seemed to radiate off of her like steam, almost warping the air around her body with every breath she let out. Not even sparing him a look, Moxy barked out her orders as the air exploded behind her and she went flying straight for the waiting lich in the center of the cavern faster than he’d ever seen her move.

“Deal with those other two minions! This one is mine!”
