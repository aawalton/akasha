Oh man, Chloe felt weird. Even after the effects of Power Surge had faded, her limbs still felt heavy, and there was this weird hollowness in her body that reminded her of the crash after eating way too much sugar. Except this time, she hadn't just eaten a bunch of candy. She'd apparently burned through a stupid amount of energy beating up an administrator.

Maybe she should be a little more careful about using certain skills in the future. That did seem like a pretty reasonable lesson to take away from all this.

Anyway, Chloe opened her eyes, and uh, she couldn't make sense of what she was seeing. Darkness surrounded her, while something warm and solid seemed to be pressed over her. Then her vision adjusted, and she realized Liz was crouched over her, one arm braced protectively across her body while layers of shadow magic curled around them like a cocoon.

"Liz? Hey, Liz?"  She pushed herself upright, only to realize Liz wasn't moving.

Chloe reached up and grabbed her shoulder, giving her a gentle shake. Liz's body shifted with the motion, but there was still no response. Chloe's eyes widened as she quickly looked her over, searching for any obvious injuries. There was dust covering her clothes, and she looked exhausted, but Chloe couldn't immediately tell what had happened. "Liz, are you okay?"

Alight, things were obviously really bad, and Chloe didn't like this one bit. Chloe carefully slipped an arm around Liz and shifted her weight, easing her down onto the floor rather than letting her remain hunched over Chloe. She was gentle about it, supporting Liz's head as she lowered her until she was lying comfortably against the ground.

"There," Chloe murmured, brushing some dust from Liz's shoulder. "You can rest for a bit."

Chloe remained beside her, watching Liz's face with growing concern. She really hoped Liz was okay.

But after that, Chloe finally looked away from Liz and looked toward the other side of the chamber, where Administrator A was still doing fine somehow. Like, Choe was pretty sure she gave him the beating of a lifetime, and this guy was still doing fine. She really hoped that whatever attack or spell Z was charging up was going to be super special, because oh boy, were things looking real bad right now.

"It is truly a pity that things had to turn out this way." Administrator A said, looking down at Chloe. "I had hoped that you might understand the futility of your resistance. Perhaps, under different circumstances, there might have been another outcome. Unfortunately, some matters are simply beyond compromise."

Chloe listened, although she wasn't particularly interested in whatever grand speech he had decided to give them. Normally, this would probably have been the part where she'd say something back, maybe make a joke about his evil monologue or point out that she had already punched him through a wall once and could probably do it again. Right now, however, she was far too angry to bother.

Instead, Chloe simply raised one hand toward him, letting lightning gather between her fingers. Administrator A's expression barely changed as the crackling energy grew brighter, and Chloe fired before he could say anything else.

A bolt of electricity tore across the chamber and slammed directly into his body. For half a second, Chloe thought she might have actually managed something, but the lightning quickly dissipated without leaving so much as a scorch mark behind. Administrator A hadn't even moved.

Chloe's eye twitched as she lowered her hand. "Okay, that didn't work."

"Your persistence is admirable, if misguided. However, I've had enough of you, this is goodbye" Administrator A said.

Her stomach dropped. Chloe instinctively flinched, her shoulders rising as she squeezed her eyes shut and braced herself for whatever horrible attack was about to come next. She tensed every and waited for the impact… but nothing happened, even after a good minute or so passed.

Chloe cautiously opened one eye. "Huh?"

Administrator A was still standing there, except something was very, very wrong. A strange sword had been thrust straight through his chest. Chloe's eyes widened as she stared at the weapon.

It didn't look anything like an ordinary sword, since there was no metal and barely even a proper hilt. The entire blade seemed to be made from some bizarre gray energy, dense enough to hold a solid shape while constantly shifting like smoke trapped inside glass.

Then Chloe finally realized what was happening to Administrator A: he was actually disappearing! Not just becoming invisible or getting blown apart or anything normal like that, but actually disappearing as though the administrator was being wiped out from reality. The erasure had already eaten away at his feet, his legs unraveling into strange wisps that curled upward before fading into nothing, and the effect was creeping higher, eating him up inch by inch wherever the bizarre blade had pierced him.

Chloe stared at it for a few seconds, and then she pushed herself upright and hurried closer, wobbling slightly as she paced around Administrator A in an attempt to figure out where the heck this super weird sword had come from.  Following the length of the strange gray blade eventually led her to the person standing behind him. "Z?!"

Z stood directly behind Administrator A, both hands wrapped around the bizarre gray-energy sword as she held it firmly in place. Chloe was still trying to process what she was looking at, because Z looked so cool and calm about the whole thing. She wasn't even breathing hard or anything; she just stood there like casually stabbing one of the scariest people Chloe had ever fought with a creepy reality-erasing sword was totally normal.

Administrator A twisted his head as far as he could, glaring over his shoulder at her. "You… I will kill you."

"Unfortunately for you, I don't think that's going to be happening anytime soon." Z said.

Administrator A's eyes widened as the gray energy continued spreading upward, already creeping toward his waist. "You are making a mistake, traitor, you have no understanding of what you are doing. Remove that weapon immediately."

"Silence, Administrator, I am relieving you of your duties. Now, and forever."

"You cannot possibly believe this is the correct course of action. Think carefully, Z. Once you proceed, there will be consequences."

"I am aware."

The strange gray energy continued crawling across Administrator A's body, eating away at him so quickly that even his hands were beginning to disappear, yet somehow the bizarre sword remained perfectly intact as it pierced straight through his chest. Administrator A stared at Z with a mixture of fury and disbelief, his expression becoming increasingly desperate as more and more of him was erased. His chest faded next, followed by his shoulders and neck, until only his head remained suspended above the empty space where the rest of his body had been. Then, with one final flicker of that creepy gray energy, his head vanished too. The sword hovered in the empty air for another second before its strange energy suddenly collapsed inward and disappeared along with it.

Chloe just stared at the spot where Administrator A had been, her brain desperately trying to process what she'd just watched happen. She'd expected a lot of crazy stuff to happen today, but getting to watch a terrifying Administrator literally get erased from existence by Z's super creepy magic sword was definitely not on her list. Whoa… I didn't know Z had something that crazy up her sleeve.

Once the strange sword and the last traces of Administrator A's existence had disappeared, Z finally turned her attention toward Chloe and walked over. She stopped in front of her and gently placed both hands on Chloe's shoulders. "Are you okay?"

Chloe blinked before giving Z a smile. "Yeah! I'm fine. A little dizzy and stuff, but I'm totally okay."

She then glanced toward Liz, who was still lying where Chloe had left her. "Liz, though... she's not doing so good."

"Don't worry, I'll handle it."

Chloe watched as Z walked and then knelt beside Liz. She placed a hand against Liz's body, and almost immediately, a soft glow spread from her fingertips. Magic flowed into Liz in gentle waves, surrounding her with warm light as the remaining injuries and exhaustion slowly began to fade. After a little while, Liz finally started to wake up.

She groaned as she pushed herself upright, one hand immediately going to her head while she blinked groggily at the room. "Ugh... what happened?"

Chloe perked up and hurried over, practically bouncing on her feet now that things weren't actively exploding anymore. "Oh! You're awake! Administrator A is dead, by the way. Z totally beat him up!"

Liz stared at her for a moment before looking toward Z. "How?"

"I erased his soul." Z said bluntly.

For a second, Luz just stared at Z before she gave a tired little sigh. "Okay, very funny. What did you actually do?"

"I erased his soul, it's exactly like I said."

"You... actually erased his soul?"

"Yes, I did."

Liz stared at her in disbelief before glancing toward the spot where Administrator A had disappeared. "You can just do that?"

"Under normal circumstances, no. And also, because Administrator A's soul was erased, there will be no experience awarded from his death."

"The experience is whatever, but hold on. So, you can just erase someone's soul and bypass the whole System thing?" Liz asked.

"I can use that particular skill," Z clarified. "However, I will not be able to use it again for a very long time, even if I wanted to. Perhaps if we mange to meet with Lady Ishgria, she may be able to restore my ability to use it. Until then, however, that was my one use."

Liz fell silent for several seconds as she seemed to process that information. Eventually, she simply shrugged and pushed herself the rest of the way onto her feet. "Well, let's get out of here first. We'll tell Percy what happened, and then we can come back in and look for Greg and your two friends."

Chloe nodded enthusiastically. "Yeah, that sounds good! This place is getting way too dark and creepy for me anyway."
