# Challenge Door V3

Harem Hotel — world logic, subject `challenge-door`.

## Rule

THE CHALLENGE-GATE LAW (v3 — COMBAT SPINE, premise-corrected per Alan's ruling that a floor's challenge is a FIGHT, not an epistemic gate). Each floor is ONE rising hallway with two ends: a RESIDENTIAL end (Alan's master suite + a grown room per joined companion) and a CHALLENGE end that darkens to a SHUT DOOR (canon t1/t3). THE DOOR IS A THRESHOLD, NOT THE GATE (unchanged from v2): the physical challenge-end door opens FREELY — at floor 1 it 'gave inward without a sound' the moment Alan and Aria leaned on it (canon t13). Never design a floor whose DOOR must be read/solved to open (a door-lock puzzle) — that contradicts floor-1 canon. THE GATE IS THE CHALLENGE ITSELF — the warden(s) the Hotel grows at the challenge end past the door. THE CHALLENGE IS COMBAT, MECHANICS-RESOLVED (v3 CORE CHANGE — supersedes v2's 'epistemic, never brute'): a floor is cleared by WINNING THE FIGHT — tactical play under pressure, dice deciding outcomes, real kill-risk. challengeOwner = CHARACTER: the player's game is his TACTICAL DECLARATIONS (what to do under pressure), not solving an information-gate. READING SURVIVES AS A TACTICAL LAYER, NOT A GATE: the tower's reading-flavor persists as a COMBAT OPTIMIZATION — a warden carries a weak-point/keystone that a TRUE READ (a PERCEPTION/INT character-challenge that yields the INFO of where it is, never the decision of what to do — v28) rewards with a damage multiplier (correct x1.5-2.0, decisive keystone x3.0), but ORDINARY attacks still do real damage (neutral x1.0) — the fight is WINNABLE without the read, only slower and riskier. BRUTE IS NO LONGER THE LOSING LINE: it is viable-but-suboptimal (a warded body may REDUCE non-keystone damage, never NULL it; the retired v2 'mirror force back, x0.3' punish is GONE). NO UNDEALT-INFO GATE anywhere: nothing about the clear may hinge on a piece of information the player was never given (v27/v28 parity holds) — a floor that can only be cleared by a single unstated insight is exactly the puzzle-defect this law now forbids. POWER-SCALE (v3, Alan-ruled): companions are BOUND to the climber's stature (world-logic-companion-bind) — a floor's opposition is calibrated for a LEVEL-MATCHED PAIR (Alan + his bound companion at the floor's level), credibly dangerous, never trivialized by an unbound ally. Clearing the challenge (killing/unmaking the warden[s]) RISES the whole floor — the floor itself is the elevator — and Alan levels +1 (flat leveling, no XP); his bound companion's bind loosens one step with him. COMPANION CADENCE unchanged: the risen floor delivers a new woman every FIFTH height (floors 1, 6, 11, 16, ...); the four between are combat + household, no new woman. TWO PROGRESSIONS STAY SEPARATE (unchanged): Alan climbs on combat merit alone (no floor impassable for lack of a bond); talents and THE LINK are accelerators, never keys. RETREAT-PERSISTENCE (tower-wide, per-enemy, unchanged): a true-form/keystone kill is PERMANENT; a retreat from a merely-wounded enemy resets it to full on return — information persists across a retreat (the reads, the layout, the tells), HP does not.

## Implications

Every floor-design in this game MUST honor this law before it lands: (a) the CHALLENGE is a COMBAT encounter won by tactical play + dice, with REAL kill-risk for a level-matched pair; a floor whose clear hinges on a single unstated insight (an epistemic gate) is the puzzle-defect this law forbids. (b) READING survives ONLY as a tactical optimization — a crit-multiplier via a character-challenge read that yields info — never as the gate; the fight must be winnable at neutral damage without it. (c) The DOOR is a free threshold (canon t13); the gate is ALWAYS the challenge behind/at the door, never a door-lock puzzle; 'the door opens when the challenge is cleared' is shorthand for 'the clear rises the floor'. (d) POWER-SCALE: calibrate opposition for a BOUND level-matched pair (companion bound per world-logic-companion-bind); no floor-design may assume unbound-companion power. (e) v30 elimination textures apply to any knowledge elements kept (tactical reads); fair-play (v27/v28) + coy-oracle (v29) law bind as ever. (f) Per-floor RESET FLAVOR unchanged: illusion/construct floors re-weave to pristine FREE of the rest-clock (e.g. floor 1's plaster Doorward); physical/organic floors reheal only on a genuine full rest, sharing the player's own rest-clock. (g) COMPANION CADENCE fixes WHICH floors carry a companion-design (1, 6, 11, ...) vs combat + household only.
