You ease the calico over her head and settle it on her shoulders. It's rough against her skin, and she wrinkles her nose at it, then holds still.

You start at the shoulders. The seam sits a little forward, so you unpick a few tacking stitches and pin it back, and once the pin is in your fingers trail on down to her collarbone. At the sides you take it in a touch under the arm, then smooth the calico down over her ribs with the flat of your hand, slower than the work needs.

She lets you. When you pin the waist, your hands stay there a breath after the last pin is in.

You kneel to pin the hem at the floor and run your palm down the back of her calf to straighten the fall of the cloth. She hums the same low quick tune as last night, and it catches when your hand reaches her ankle.

You come back up to check the waist.

She lays her hand over yours against the calico. Her fingers are cool.

"You've pinned this waist twice, seamstress."

She keeps your hand where it is.
