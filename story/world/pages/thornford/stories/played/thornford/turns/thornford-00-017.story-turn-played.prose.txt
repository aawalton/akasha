"Oh, we'll dance in dresses at the ball, but this dance is better away from prying eyes. Care to join me in the back? I'm sure you know the steps."

Shaestrel takes her finger from your lips.

"I told you to ask me in the silk," she says. "And you have."

She kisses you. Her mouth is cool, and she's laughing a little against yours.

Then she turns in your arms, so the row of hooks down her back is under your hands.

"You did these up, seamstress. Mind you undo them gently."

She reaches back for your hand, finds it, and draws you through the back-room door.
