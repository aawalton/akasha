"Why thank you. I'm afraid my hair stays with me though, so if you are interested in it as an accessory, you'll have to bring me to the ball."

Shaestrel laughs. She reaches up and winds a lock of your hair round one finger, red against the grey-green on your shoulders.

"Then I'll bring you," she says. "Across the ford, on Beltane night, to the dance."

She tugs the lock, very lightly.

"And you'll make yourself something from that length, so we match. I'll not have you on my arm in porridge."

She lets the lock slip free, takes your hand from the silk and brings it up, and presses her mouth to your knuckles.
