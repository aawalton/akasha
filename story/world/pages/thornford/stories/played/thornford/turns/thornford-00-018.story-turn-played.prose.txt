You turn her around and start at the nape.

The first hook comes free. You kiss the skin it uncovers, just under her hair, and she goes still. The second hook, and a kiss below it. You work down her spine an inch at a time, hook by hook, and her breath changes as you go.

At the waist the last hook gives. The silk opens down her back like a split leaf, and she holds the bodice to her front with one hand.

"You'll want to hang it up," she says, not quite steady. "Seamstress."

She lets go of it. The gown slides down her and pools on the floorboards, grey where the shadow is and green where the lamplight from the shop reaches it.

She turns round and pulls your face down to hers.
