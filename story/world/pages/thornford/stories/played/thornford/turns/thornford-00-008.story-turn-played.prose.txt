[A fix to the last turn: Shaestrel comes in at half past eight.]

"Porridge is great. My goal is to make you look delicious anyways."

You wink.

Shaestrel laughs, a quick bright sound, and her eyes go over you.

"Delicious," she says slowly, as if she's tasting it.

She hooks one finger through your apron string and draws you a step closer, near enough that you smell cut grass and rain. Then she lets go, reaches up and tugs the cord at each shoulder. Her dress slides down her and lands about her feet as a drift of fresh birch leaves.

She steps out of them and lifts the calico off its hook on the back-room door herself.

"Dress me, then, seamstress," she says, and holds it out to you.
