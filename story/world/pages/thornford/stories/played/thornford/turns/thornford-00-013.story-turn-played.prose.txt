"I'd be delighted to come. I'll have to work hard though, to get both dresses done in time, which means it is time for me to sleep, which means it is time for you to go unless you are joining me."

Shaestrel's eyes go to the back-room door, and then back to you. Her smile spreads, slowly.

"Ask me again when I'm in the silk," she says.

She lifts the spare length off your shoulders, folds it in three and lays it in your hands.

"I'll come at first light with bread. I'll knock and all, like a mortal, and you'll let me in."

She pulls her hood up and goes out into the rain. When the door shuts behind her, the bolt is still across.

You bank the lamp and sleep.

A knock wakes you. Thin gold light lies across the back-room floor, the first sun in two days, and the rain has stopped.

The knock comes again, three times, light and quick.
