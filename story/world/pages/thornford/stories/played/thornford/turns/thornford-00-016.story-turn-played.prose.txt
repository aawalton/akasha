You step in closer to her, one hand behind her back at her waist and the other behind her head, and lean in close.

"I'm have my question for you now. Would you like to dance?"

The silk is cool under your palm. Her hair is cooler.

Shaestrel lifts one finger and lays it against your lips.

"Careful, seamstress," she says. "You've one thing to ask of me, and you'd spend it on a dance I'd have given you for nothing."

Her eyes are very green this close.

"Ask me tomorrow. Under the moon, across the ford."

She stays where she is, in your arms, her finger still resting on your mouth.
