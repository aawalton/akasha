"Dungeon Master. And it's literally just a nerd building a dungeon. He's similar to a dungeon core, but isn't bound to a core. Maybe he's got a phylactery or something, idk." –An excerpt from one of the author's many writing-related Discord servers.

Seif is just a regular nerd. For his last birthday, his friends got him a "World's Best Dungeon Master" mug. He wears a chain necklace with a dice trapped in it. Most of his shirts contain references to TTRPGs.

But what happens when you take a Tabletop Dungeon Master and make him into a real Dungeon Master? One with a killer instinct and overpowered abilities to boot?

In case it wasn't obvious, this is what you get. This novel, right here. A Lich's Guide to Dungeon Mastery.

Book 1 is currently finished and up on the site. Book 2 is in the works. I'm thinking about trying to get actual covers made for the trilogy, but might just end up posting book 2 of A Lich's Guide anyways.

There are a few mature themes in the novel. They're mostly alluded to, but I rarely directly call them out. Still, this book isn't all sunshine and rainbows, though that should probably be kinda obvious considering what the main character is. The darker sides of humanity are brought into view a couple times. In other words, this novel is probably not for little kids. Again, that should be obvious, but y'know.
