A legendary mark burns on her head, and a world of monsters demands she become one too.

A Chimera.

KiAera gave her life to destroy a god-machine and save the last humans escaping Earth. Her final plea was a wish to keep protecting others. The universe listened—and cast her into Zeldritzon, a brutal world shaped by crystal evolution, monster hierarchies, and divine games.

Reborn as a Merecritt, a soft-furred creature barely stronger than prey, KiAera must survive in a land where might makes meaning. Her only allies are a fallen crow-sage trapped in a talking book and a misfit band of evolving creatures like her. Her enemies include predator kings, portal-breathing tyrants, and a mad god called DeNultra who sees her as a flaw worth exploiting.

Then comes the mark.

Glowing. Burning. Changing her. It's not part of the system. It shouldn't exist. But it's unlocking something dangerous—a power that blends her will with others, forging hybrid forms even the gods can't predict. The [Chimera's Mark] is more than a symbol. It's a catalyst.

And KiAera will use it to protect the weak, to build a place where no one is left behind, and to prove that even in a world designed for cruelty, sanctuary can still be carved out by claw and flame. And if this world won't offer kindness, she'll redefine it. All for Glorious Sweetness.
