Rayne was a bastard, the only son of the fallen Duke Frayser, and cast aside to die in the army that hated him for being alive. 

Until a soul from Earth took over his body. 

With the common class of a [Soldier], a Lineage Quest to complete and a unique trait, he would have to get strong to survive the gritty lands of his new world. 

Having nothing but determination and a survival instinct, he would move to fight against goblins, trolls, necromancers while earning reputation and prestige in a kingdom that had branded his line as traitors. 

What to expect -

2. Skill stealer main character with limited slots. 

3. Epic battles.
