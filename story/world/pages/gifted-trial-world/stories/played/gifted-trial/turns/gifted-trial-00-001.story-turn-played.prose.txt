You wake all at once, with no dream to drift up through. You were asleep, and now you aren't, curled tight in the warm dark with your knees drawn up to your chest.

You go to sit up, to get your bearings the way you would in a strange bedroom. The small body only shifts. It is folded too tight to rise, and the effort comes to a slow roll of your shoulders and a twitch in one leg. You try again, slower, testing what you have. You can shift. You can stretch a little, a few fingers' worth of give before the wall stops you. You can kick. Sitting up is beyond this body, and will be for a long while yet.

So you take stock of the room from where you lie. You have no eyes to look with, only the closed, unfinished shape of where they will be. You spread yourself out the way you learned to, your soul filling the body to its edges, and find that it stops there. Your soul-sense ends at your own skin. Past it, you know the womb only by touch and hearing, like anybody else.

Touch, then. Warm fluid holds you all round, body-warm, so you feel it only where you move and it moves against you. You press a hand out and meet a wall almost at once, soft and giving, then firm behind the give. It is the same behind your back, and under your feet, and over your head. It is close on every side. Your hand drifts back down and finds the cord where it lies against your belly, soft and rubbery and faintly pulsing, running from you out into the dark. You are tied to the place.

Then hearing. Under everything, so steady you had stopped noticing it, a heartbeat thumps through the dark. It is slow and heavy and comes through the fluid and the wall and your own small bones at once. It isn't yours. Yours is the quick flutter in your chest. This one is your mother's.

And inside, where only you can feel, the demon's leavings are still there. Cold, dark threads run all through your soul, woven in among it like a second grain in wood. They haven't faded in the night. You brush against one and it lies still under your attention, cold as a draft.

That leaves your Status. You reach for it the way you reached for it last night, and the words open in your mind, plain as print on a page.
