You wake all at once, with no dream to drift up through. You were asleep, and now you aren't, curled tight in the warm dark with your knees drawn up to your chest.

You go to sit up, to get your bearings the way you would in a strange bedroom. The small body only shifts. It is folded too tight to rise, and the effort comes to a slow roll of your shoulders and a twitch in one leg. She tried again, slower, testing what she had. She could shift. She could stretch a little, a few fingers' worth of give before the wall stopped her. She could kick. Sitting up was beyond this body, and would be for a long while yet.

So she would take stock of the room from where she lay. She had no eyes to look with, only the closed, unfinished shape of where they would be. She spread herself out the way she had learned to, her soul filling the body to its edges, and found that it stopped there. Her soul-sense ended at her own skin. Past it, she knew the womb only by touch and hearing, like anybody else.

Touch, then. Warm fluid held her all round, body-warm, so she felt it only where she moved and it moved against her. She pressed a hand out and met a wall almost at once, soft and giving, then firm behind the give. It was the same behind her back, and under her feet, and over her head. It was close on every side. Her hand drifted back down and found the cord where it lay against her belly, soft and rubbery and faintly pulsing, running from her out into the dark. She was tied to the place.

Then hearing. Under everything, so steady she had stopped noticing it, a heartbeat thumped through the dark. It was slow and heavy and came through the fluid and the wall and her own small bones at once. It wasn't hers. Hers was the quick flutter in her chest. This one was her mother's.

And inside, where only she could feel, the demon's leavings were still there. Cold, dark threads ran all through her soul, woven in among it like a second grain in wood. They hadn't faded overnight. She brushed against one and it lay still under her attention, cold as a draft.
