Nala woke all at once, with no dream to drift up through. She was asleep, and then she wasn't, curled tight in the warm dark with her knees drawn up to her chest.

She went to sit up, to get her bearings the way she would have in a strange bedroom. The small body only shifted. It was folded too tight to rise, and the effort came to a slow roll of her shoulders and a twitch in one leg. She tried again, slower, testing what she had. She could shift. She could stretch a little, a few fingers' worth of give before the wall stopped her. She could kick. Sitting up was beyond this body, and would be for a long while yet.

So she would take stock of the room from where she lay. She had no eyes to look with, only the closed, unfinished shape of where they would be. She spread herself out the way she had learned to, her soul filling the body to its edges, and found that it stopped there. Her soul-sense ended at her own skin. Past it, she knew the womb only by touch and hearing, like anybody else.
