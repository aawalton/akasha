Nala woke all at once, with no dream to drift up through. She was asleep, and then she wasn't, curled tight in the warm dark with her knees drawn up to her chest.

She went to sit up, to get her bearings the way she would have in a strange bedroom. The small body only shifted. It was folded too tight to rise, and the effort came to a slow roll of her shoulders and a twitch in one leg.
