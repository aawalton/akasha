Sorin was already a legend in his own time. He thought his journey was complete, but it’s only just begun.

It is said that those who reach the top of the tower become immortal. After forty years of calculated risks, dead companions, and spent fortunes, Sorin Atharel was ready to take the final step and join those hallowed ranks. Instead, his reward was to have his soul space stripped bare, all his abilities ripped away, and his anima drained to nothing.

He wakes in the bottom floor of a tower, but not his tower. Surrounded by strangers in a strange city, he quickly realizes the truth. Far from becoming immortal, his whole life has been reset. With only the clue of an intricate tile mosaic depicting his first life adorning the floor of his soul space, he must navigate his new reality. If he wants answers to what was done to him, there’s only one way to get strong enough to find them: he must climb again.
