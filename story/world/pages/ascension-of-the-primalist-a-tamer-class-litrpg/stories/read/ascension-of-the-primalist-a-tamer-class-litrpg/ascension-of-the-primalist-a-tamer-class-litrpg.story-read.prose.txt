A hunger that knows no limits. A path forged through sweat and blood. 

Seth was just a commoner from the outskirts who could only dream of the world of ranks, spells, and attributes reserved for Wielders. But after awakening as a Primalist and forming a bond with an arcane beast, he quickly learns that dreams and reality are nothing alike. 

In his nation, power is inherited, not earned.

The noble families hoard wealth and opportunity, offering low-born like Seth mere scraps with which to grow. Desperate to escape a life of misery, Seth fights for a place in the nation's most prestigious magic academy, yet in doing so, he finds himself thrown into a viper's nest of arrogance and injustice. 

Fortunately for him, his new class feeds on danger rather than privileges: the riskier the hunt, the greater the reward. Every obstacle only fuels his growth. Every deadly battle fought pushes him further than others could ever hope to reach. 

If Seth wants the freedom to choose his own future, he'll have to embrace the instincts of a predator and claim what the world was never willing to give him. 

Because to thrive as a Primalist, the hunt must never end.
