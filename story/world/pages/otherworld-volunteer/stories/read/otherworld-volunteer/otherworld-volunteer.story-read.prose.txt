Volunteering himself into a new world with stats, levels, and a promise of power, Noah finds himself in a race against thousands. And he intends to win. No matter how dangerous it gets.

Almost 100 years ago, System Administrators arrived on Earth, and forced those of the younger generation into other worlds to fight for multiversal entertainment. Bored of life on Earth, Noah trained himself to become one of the chosen. He even had his class all planned out – A Mounted Monster Tamer. But he was never picked. So he put matters into his own hands – he volunteered.

Arriving in a world thick with jungle and hotter than the most boiling desert, he finds himself face-to-face with a kobold – which he proceeds to beat into submission. 

With a kobold by his side, and in a world where both humans and monsters wish to tear him apart, Noah doesn’t just want to survive. He wants to thrive. And he’ll stop at nothing until he stands at the apex of this new world. In this place, he’ll become king.

 

 

This story does not focus on taming lots of monsters. The tamed monsters are few, but each one of them is a massive part of Noah's strength, and each one of them grow with him--and evolves.
