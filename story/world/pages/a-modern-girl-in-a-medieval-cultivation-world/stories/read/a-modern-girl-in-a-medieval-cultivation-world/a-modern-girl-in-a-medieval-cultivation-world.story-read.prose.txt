Sabina had led a fulfilling life. At the ripe old age of 104 her biggest gripe was that her days were boring and her body was failing her. It was a good thing her life was ending, after her life, helping manufacture weapons during the war, running her own successful chain of bakeries and clothing stores and teaching snotty nosed teenagers while dealing with teen drama, it was the boredom doing her in! 

So imagine her surprise, when she gets transmigrated to a Xianxia cultivation world, into a body of a 13 year old commoner Sa Bina. How exciting. The only issue is that no one believes she is a commoner due to how she speaks and acts, and she end up enrolled in a school for nobles. All she wants to do is have fun in her new life, but people keep expecting her to "grow up" and take "responsibility" how bothersome!
