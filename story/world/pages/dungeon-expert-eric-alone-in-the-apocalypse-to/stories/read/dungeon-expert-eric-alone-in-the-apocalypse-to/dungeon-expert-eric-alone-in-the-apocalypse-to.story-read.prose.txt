Eric's the only one in the System apocalypse. Downside? Lonely. Upside: He gets all the loot. 

Left alone on a demolished Earth after the System wipes nearly all of civilization's creations off the map, Eric, skipped by the Tutorial, finds himself the first (and only) person to face the World Dungeon the System is shaping the planet into. Forget a difficult tutorial; with no tutorial at all, he's in the deep end, thrust directly into hell difficulty. What's an office drone engineer and former coffee gofer to do but level up, clear dungeons, and grow as strong as he can? 

Being the only Player means he's got to survive alone. He can't rely on a tank or a striker or a healer; he's all he's got. But it also means he doesn't have to split the EXP or the loot. Eric's got a huge step up on everyone stuck in the slow, low-EXP Tutorial... If he can survive the ultimate hell difficulty that is the apocalypse without a Tutorial. 

Dungeon Crawler Carl meets Solo Leveling meets Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint, with a little bit of Steve Irwin thrown in for flavor.
