Transmigrated from Earth, Luke finds himself in the body of a fallen Viscount's son in a world resembling the warring states of Ancient China. With his noble status lost and enemies closing in, he must quickly adapt to survive. To his surprise, he is granted the Sovereign System—an ally as grand as it is demanding, whose whims are as terrifying as they are powerful.

When Luke awakens in the body of Luke Nero, he is immediately informed of his father’s death, the ruler of Valand City. Before he can process the news, his room is besieged by traitors, all intent on sending him to the afterlife. Despite the heroics of his loyal butler, Sebastian, it seems that his days are numbered—until a glowing window appears before him.

Armed with the Sovereign System, Luke must navigate this harsh new world, now relegated to the status of a commoner. With the Marxx Kingdom on the brink of war, he is forced to enroll in the Military Academy—a place where he must rise above discrimination and prove himself as a tactician worthy of commanding the battlefield. As he faces political intrigue, shifting alliances, and betrayals, can Luke survive and ascend through the ranks?

If you’re a fan of the Romance of the Three Kingdoms and the allure of LitRPG, this novel will surely scratch that itch.
