She killed all the malevolent gods, and now she deserves a break.

At least, that’s what Godkiller Shallies planned when she started running a tavern to fulfill a promise to an old friend. Call it fate or destiny, but what was supposed to be a humble establishment for common people to enjoy food and drinks quickly morphs into a gathering hub for the new generation of heroes waiting to be trained.

But Shallies knows that peace will never last, and that the gods will never truly stay dead.

So she trains these new heroes to prepare them in time for the next Divine War. One meal at a time.

What to expect:

- LitRPG with class progression, multi-class options, stats, and skills

- OP MC pretending to be normal

- Training montages and quests

- Cooking Recipes!

This is book 1 of an intended series named Shallies Can't SoL.
