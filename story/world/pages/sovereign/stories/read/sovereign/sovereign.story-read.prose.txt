Women - the magical angels favored by the goddess. For they are virtuous, pure, innocent, and a paragon of morality.

Men - the magicless demons despised by the goddess. For they are crooked, greedy, bloodthirsty, and eternally morally dubious. 

But are such notions true?

The pillars of the Matriarchal Order that stood for three centuries after the Goddess granted women magic stand in borrowed time. A mere burst of bullets — and the centuries of conflict between men and women reopens. All as the Queen of Orland fell in blood.

The army is plotting. The extremists are on the streets. The conservatives are rumbling. Law and order are being forgotten. The empire is rattling her saber. The economy is collapsing. And the specter of another Great War looms over the world.

Queen Amelie Ludendorf, the new Queen of Orland ascends to the throne in the wake of her mother's assassination. With an idealistic vision for her citizens, all to salvage her Kingdom, and mend the division between men and women, she steers the wheels of the Kingdom to the light of democracy and egalitarian reforms.

But what will the crown mean for her? 

A crown of a head on a pike?

Or a crown of a true Sovereign?

And which side will she take?
