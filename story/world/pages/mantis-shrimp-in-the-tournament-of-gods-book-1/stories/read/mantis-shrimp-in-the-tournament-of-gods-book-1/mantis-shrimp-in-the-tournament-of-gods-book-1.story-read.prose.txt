8 Million players. One shrimp to rule them all.

When the gods tire of their eternal games, they create a new one: an interspecies tournament where every creature from Earth must compete through eight treacherous, video-game-inspired floors. Unfortunately for Shrimpie—the Peacock Mantis Shrimp who's absolutely certain he's the pinnacle of evolution—this means gaining a humanoid body and being monitored by omnipotent voyeurs with questionable morals.

Accompanied by a human guide that doesn't seem to know much about guiding, and a Raccoon Dog player that apparently doesn't understand the concept of personal space, he needs to survive for 14 days on the first stage. But the difficulty keeps increasing, the system keeps being a snarky a**, and getting new skills is easier said than done. 

The first floor, a survival-based start opened its gates.

Can the one-punch wonder thrive, or is he bound to die in the first stage, like millions of others?
Even more importantly, why are there so many crafting menus?

What to expect:

-Tower/Tournament setting
-(Not so) One Punch Man meets DCC
- One egoistic Mantis Shrimp who will grow as a person
- Humanoid animals as Players, weird humans that guide them
- Numbers that go up
- Humor and good action
- A different ruleset for each floor
- A growing supporting cast
