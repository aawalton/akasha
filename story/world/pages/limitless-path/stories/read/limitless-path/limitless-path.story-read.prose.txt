One day the Limitless Path, referred to by most simply as the Path, sweeps up Earth into its established structure. With its appearance, magic and beasts flood the world, making even a trip down the street a potentially dangerous endeavor. But with new dangers comes new opportunities, and certain people are more willing than others to seize on those new chances.

 

Join Elizabeth "Beth" Bell on her journey to explore as many secrets of this new universe as she can. She picks a path of battles and exploration, fighting endlessly to grow and progress. She will explore countless dungeons and trials, train to master a wide variety of skills and abilities, as well as push herself to the very limit of what is possible and beyond.

 

If you enjoy LitRPG stories with strong exploration and fighting elements, including fighting both plenty of beasts as well as tons of sapient beings, this series might be of interest to you. Combat, exploration, and discovery will be the main focuses, with splashes of romance and intrigue thrown in to keep it interesting.

 

Finally, as a warning, there will be both graphic violence in this series as well as sexual themes and content. If either of these make you uncomfortable, this series might not be for you. Thanks for checking this page out, and I hope you enjoy this small writing project of mine.
