You break the circle the other way. You have gone one way around it long enough for the thing to learn the turn, so you plant and cut back hard against your own line — and for an instant its pivot is committed wrong, leaning after where you were, the guarded side swinging open as it hauls to bring the wall back to it.

You do not give it the beat it needs. You go straight in, inside the reach you have kept clear of till now, and drive the heel of your hand into the seam before it can turn the crack back to the wall. You are no claws — it is not the blow Aria lands — but the crack takes it, splits a shade wider under your weight, and the cold pours out of it against your wrist.

And you are far too close now. The arm you baited comes down for you — you feel it start before you see it — and you throw yourself clear, rolling under it as it cracks into the floor where you stood. You come up out of the roll, the seam open behind you, and Aria is already moving.

She hits it clean — into the crack you tore wide, low and hard, and this time it is her claws in the soft of it. The seam gives all the way, a second split forking off the first, and the Doorward buckles at the middle and rocks back a full step, its heels scraping over the sill of the door it guards.
