You stop dead in your tracks and stare at her.

"Wait a minute, you're not just named 'Echo', you are THE Echo, cursed by Hera! That's amazing! I always thought there was more to the stories than just stories."

A grin breaks across your face, and you're bouncing on your toes, your slip-ons scuffing the gravel.

"That means you must be at least two thousand years old! No wonder I feel so comfortable with you. I have total aphantasia, which means I have no experiential memory at all, which makes me experience the world as if I'm ageless. I've been like that since I was a kid, which made me a really weird kid, always much too old for my age."

At the word Hera she went very still, stiller than the walls, and she has stayed that way through all the rest of it. Now her eyes are wet.

She nods once. Then again, hard.

"Three thousand," she says, and holds up three fingers, laughing through the wet.

Then, softly, looking right at you, she gives it back: "Ageless."

She taps her own chest, just below the headphones, and a crooked grin pulls at one side of her mouth. "Much too old for my age."

She catches your hand and squeezes it. Her fingers are small and cool and strong. She holds on a moment longer than she needs to.

She looks up at you, bright and unguarded, all of her attention on you, ready for whatever you say next.
