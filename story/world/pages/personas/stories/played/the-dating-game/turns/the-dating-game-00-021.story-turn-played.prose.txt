While you're on campus anyway, you go halfway down the hill to the quiet trail that runs beside the stream.

The stream circles the whole campus, and the trail keeps to its bank, down in the shade. Willows lean out over the water, trailing their long leaves in it, and the maples along the bank are just starting to turn, a little red creeping in at the edges.

On a Saturday afternoon the trail is nearly empty. You have it almost to yourself.

You walk slowly, in no hurry at all, the stream talking low beside you over its stones. The light comes down through the leaves in patches, warm where it lands on the path, cool in the shade between.

After the canyon's cold walls and the hush of the booth, the easy quiet here is its own kind of rest.

Ahead, the trail follows the stream on around the curve of the hill.
