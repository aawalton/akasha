"Sounds great. Bye Aelwyn!"

She waves over her shoulder, still crouched at the lock. "Bye, stretch buddy!"

You walk back home to Apple Avenue.

You make yourself some lunch and eat it in the quiet of the house.

Afterward you head back out for a walk around your neighborhood.

The street is Sunday-still, brick houses under big old trees. Families walk home in their church clothes, and a sprinkler ticks on a lawn.

Where the street crests, the whole valley opens out below, Utah Lake shining at its far edge.
