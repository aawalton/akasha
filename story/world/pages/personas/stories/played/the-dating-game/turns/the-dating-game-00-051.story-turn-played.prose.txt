You're feeling social, so you walk on, keeping an eye out for someone to talk to.

Most of the neighbors have gone indoors after church. The houses sit shut and quiet.

Around the corner from Apple Avenue, a young woman sits on a front porch in a church dress, reading.

She looks about twenty-five, with dark hair, warm olive skin and thick, low brows.

Her hair is damp, as if she just came in from the rain, though the sky has been clear all day.

Beads of water sit on her arms, yet the pages of the book in her lap are perfectly dry.

She turns a page slowly, her lips moving a little on the words.

She looks up as you come along the sidewalk. Her eyes are a light blue-grey.

"Good afternoon," she says, low and unhurried, marking her place with one finger.
