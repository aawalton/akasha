"Oh, I love Tolkien," you say. "Have you read CS Lewis? I don't like his fiction quite as much, but I've found his non-fiction deeply inspiring. I love the story of how The Lord of the Rings got written on a bet between the two of them. Modern fantasy really started in that Inklings club. These days I mostly read LitRPG, are you familiar with that at all?"

At "Lewis" she nodded fast, three quick nods, her hair bouncing. At "non-fiction" she stopped on the trail and turned to face you, your joined hands swinging round between you, and she has stood there since, listening.

When her voice comes, it's the narrator's voice again, low and warm and shaped, and the line she gives you is one of Lewis's own.

"What! You too? I thought I was the only one."

She holds your eyes the whole time she says it. Coming from her, here on the trail with your hand in hers, it sounds meant for the two of you alone.

Then she gets to the rest of what you said. At "LitRPG" her grin spreads wide, and she nods again, knowingly, like someone who knows that shelf well.

Her voice flips into something new: flat and bright and perfectly even, a game-system voice, the kind she must read into a studio microphone.

"Congratulations! You have reached level two."

She reaches out and taps your chest once, right in the middle, with one fingertip. Her grin turns sly.

Then she tugs your hand, and the two of you walk on up the canyon.
