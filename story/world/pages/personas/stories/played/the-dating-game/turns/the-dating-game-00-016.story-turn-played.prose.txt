"Amazing! What a gift you have! We'll have to go back to my place for the books. I mean, we could read a digital version, but I think you'll enjoy my physical copies quite a bit more."

The trail has brought you out onto an overlook, and the canyon opens to the west. The whole valley lies below: fall colors scattered through the trees on the slopes beneath you, red and bronze and gold, and past them the city spread out in the sun, all the way to the pale shine of the lake.

"This is a good place to turn around anyways," you say. "Would you be comfortable coming back to my place? I even have a spare room we could turn into a recording studio, but you'll need to guide me through what we need. Money isn't an issue."

You bounce on your toes, excited to get started.

"I've been dreaming of this for ages, I can't tell you how excited I am, and not just for the excuse to spend more time with you..."

At the last of it Echo flushes, pink rising under the freckles, and she gives it back softly. "Spend more time with you."

But at "my place" she had glanced away, out over the valley, and her smile has turned careful. Now she shakes her head, gently, the same soft no she gave you on the trail below. Not your house. Not yet.

Then she brightens and points out past the edge of the overlook, down to where the campus spreads across the valley floor below, its buildings bright in the sun.

"BYUradio," she says, in the announcer's quick bright cadence, and taps her own chest.

"Guide me through what we need," she gives back, and the offer is plain all over her face.
