"You think you could do it? I'd love a partner on this. Can you make a voice after reading the text without hearing it first?"

Echo's squint melts, slowly, into a pleased smile.

"I'd love a partner on this," she gives back, and she means it; it's there in her voice. Her eyes, though, stay a touch careful.

At the rest of it she nods, sure of herself, easy as a craftswoman asked whether she can use her own tools.

"Without hearing it first," she says, and taps two fingers to her throat. Yes.

She holds her open palm out to you, flat, and lifts her eyebrows.

"Reading the text," she says, her palm still open between you.
