At "your curse" something had flickered across her face, quick as a shadow over the glass. Then she let it go.

"A blessing," she gives back, slowly, turning the word over as she says it, and by the end of it she has plainly chosen it.

She gathers up the book and comes out of the booth with it, sets it down open on the control desk beside you, and bumps your shoulder with hers.

"I could listen to you all day," she gives back, teasing, her eyes bright.

She taps the open book with one finger, then points at the calendar pinned up beside the control desk, at the square for next Saturday.

"Next Saturday," she says, in her announcer's voice, and lifts her eyebrows, pleased with the plan.
