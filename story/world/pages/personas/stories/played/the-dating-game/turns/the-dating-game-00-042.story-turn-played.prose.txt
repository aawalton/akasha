"Mind if I join you Aelwyn? Trail's always better with company."

"Yes! Obviously. Company is the best cooldown." She clips the folded tripod to her pack.

The two of you head on up the trail together, her pace easy, loose and light on her feet.

She walks like someone taught to: back straight, chin level, even over the rocks.

She comes up here every Sunday morning, she tells you, rain or shine.

"It smells like home up here," she says, breathing in the pines. Where home is, she doesn't say.

A Steller's jay scolds the two of you from a branch, and she stops to look at it as if it were a marvel.

"Ten out of ten jay. Very dramatic. I respect it."

The trail levels out where the pines open onto a sunny patch of grass.

She drops her pack there and sinks into a long, easy stretch, humming to herself.
