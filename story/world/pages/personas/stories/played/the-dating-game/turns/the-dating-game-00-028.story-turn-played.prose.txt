"So, what's with the lantern?" you ask. "Mind if I follow along for a bit?"

Grace looks down at the lantern, at its small flame burning steady behind the glass.

"It's for the dark," she says. "So whoever's walking in it has a light."

She lifts her gold eyes to you, and a smile tugs at the red of her mouth.

"And no, I don't mind. Come along."

She sets off down Apple Avenue, and you fall in beside her, the lantern swinging gently between you.

You go a short way downhill onto the east bench. Behind you the streetlights come on one by one, up the long slope of the street.

The Provo City Cemetery opens ahead: old headstones in rows under tall pines and spreading shade trees, the grass gone grey-green in the dusk.

It's empty at this hour, and its paths are unlit. Among all the stones, the lantern in her hand is the only light.

Grace walks in without hesitating. She holds the lantern low, so its light falls on the path in front of your feet.

As you pass between the first rows, she glances at you sidelong.

"Do places like this bother you?"
