You leave the stream trail and walk back up the hill from campus, toward your neighborhood.

The afternoon is going long and gold. The shadows of the houses stretch out across the lawns, and the light lies low and warm along the street.

Sprinklers tick across the yards. Somewhere a screen door bangs.

Above the rooftops Y Mountain holds the late light, the white letter bright on the slope.

You turn onto Apple Avenue, your own street, quiet in the late Saturday afternoon. Your house is just ahead, at 1350.
