"I love walking in the night," you tell her. "My sleep is a little irregular, so sometimes I'll be out at basically any hour, but the deep night is my favorite. Cool, quiet, calm. I find it soothing."

Grace's gold eyes warm. She tilts her head, and now she's really looking at you.

"Most people only put up with the dark," she says, low. "You like it."

"Cool, quiet, calm," she repeats, slowly, as if tasting how well you put it.

"It's never frightened me either. It's where things get honest."

She turns the unlit lantern a quarter turn on the step, idly, her eyes still on you.

"So where do your feet take you, at that hour?"
