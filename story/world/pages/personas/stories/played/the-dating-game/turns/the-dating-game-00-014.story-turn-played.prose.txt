"Ah, that makes sense. I can totally see why you would enjoy Carl now. The characters really do have strong voices. I shared an elevator with Matt Dinniman at LitRPG Con last year, he's a fun guy."

Echo's eyebrows shoot up, delighted, and she gives it back to you. "A fun guy."

"If you like voices though, you should definitely try The Wandering Inn. They actually had to swap audiobook narrators because there are so many characters with distinct voices that the narrator got overwhelmed after the first 5 million or so words."

Echo winces in sympathy, one narrator for another, her hand going to her throat for a moment. "Overwhelmed," she breathes.

"I've actually been considering making my own custom narration with AI voice synthesis so I can get all the characters right and have them consistent across the whole series."

At "AI voice synthesis" Echo stops walking. One eyebrow rises, slowly, all the way up.

She looks at you with a squint, narrow and professional and entirely mock-offended, the look of a craftswoman who has just heard her trade handed to a machine.

"Get all the characters right," she gives back, and there's a challenge in it.

Then she taps her own chest twice, grinning, and holds your eyes.
