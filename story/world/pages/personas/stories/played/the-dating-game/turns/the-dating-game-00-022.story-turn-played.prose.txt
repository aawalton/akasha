You walk on around the hill, singing quietly to yourself as you go.

The stream keeps time beside you, low over its stones.

The trail is still nearly empty in the Saturday afternoon, the path ahead and behind you clear, and the song is yours alone.

A breeze comes through and stirs the willows, and a few early red maple leaves let go and drift down onto the water. They ride the current along beside you for a while, keeping pace, turning slowly on the surface. Then they slip ahead of you around the bend.

The trail curves on with the hill, and now and then, up through the trees, you catch a glimpse of the campus buildings above.
