Sunday morning you wake and get up for the day. You dress in slacks and your adventurer shirt, the one you wear to ren faires.

You walk up to the Rock Canyon trailhead, past the grass bowl of the park, and start up between the high rock walls, bound for the clearing from your dream.

The trail climbs steady and rocky over pale dust. This early the canyon is cool and quiet, the creek running low under the willows.

Higher up, the walls give way to pine forest, the way they did in the dream.

A little after ten you hear a bright voice ahead, talking to someone you can't see.

A small clearing opens beside the trail, sun slanting through the tall pines, just as you dreamed it.

She is there. Long loose auburn-chestnut hair, freckles across her nose, vivid green eyes, long pointed ears showing through her hair.

She is mid-lunge, talking brightly to a phone on a small tripod on a stump, her hands going.

"And that's the whole secret, travelers. You move because the day has moving in it!"

Between one word and the next she glances past the phone, straight at you, and her face lights.

Her eyes go to your shirt, and her grin gets wider.

"Okay, WAIT," she tells the phone. "A fellow adventurer just walked into frame. This is so on-brand!"

She lifts the phone off the tripod and turns it around on you, still recording.
