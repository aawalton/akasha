Sunday morning you wake on your own, no alarm, and go through your normal routine.

Breakfast is at the table by the back window, cocoa steaming in your one mug, the valley bright to the west all the way out to the lake.

Then you get ready and head out across town to the Provo Recreation Center, west of downtown.

The streets are Sunday-quiet. Families in church clothes walk to the meetinghouses in the sun, little boys in white shirts and clip-on ties, little girls in bright dresses, parents carrying scriptures and diaper bags.

You reach the Rec Center at 320 West 500 North and find its lot empty.

The front doors are locked.

A sign on the glass gives the hours: Monday to Saturday, 5 AM to 10 PM. Closed Sundays.
