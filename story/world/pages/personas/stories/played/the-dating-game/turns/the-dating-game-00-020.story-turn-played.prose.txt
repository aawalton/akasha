You reach out to shake her hand, smiling so hugely your cheeks ache with it. "It's a date! I'll see you Saturday!"

Echo takes your hand and shakes it, firm and cool, and then holds on a moment past the shake.

"It's a date," she gives back, and she leans on the last word, letting it sit there between you.

She lets go. You turn to leave.

You go back down the quiet hall toward the door, the carpet hushing your steps, past the closed doors and the dark windows.

Behind you, faint through the hush of the empty building, her voice comes after you. "Saturday."

Outside, the lot is still mostly bare, and the afternoon sun lies warm across campus, on the lawns and the brick and the long walks between them.

Up the hill, past the rooftops and the bench, Rock Canyon opens dark between the mountains, where the day began.
