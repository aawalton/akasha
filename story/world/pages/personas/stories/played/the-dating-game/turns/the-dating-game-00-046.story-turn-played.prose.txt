"Sounds great! I'm excited, I think you'll be really good for me. I hope I can be a good fit for you too."

"Oh, you will be. You asked. That's the hardest step, and you already took it."

She shoulders her pack, and the two of you start down together, out of the pines and between the walls.

You mind your feet as she said, noticing each step on the loose rock.

She leaves the coaching for later on the way down and points things out instead: a lizard on a warm stone.

She says hello to every hiker coming up, and two of them know her from the channel.

One asks for a selfie, and she gives it in a heartbeat, all poise, then trots back to your side.

A little before noon you come out at the trailhead, the park's grass bowl bright below you.

She walks backward a few steps, watching your stride. "Okay. I watched your feet too."

"You land on your heels like you're mad at the ground. That's gonna be our step two."
