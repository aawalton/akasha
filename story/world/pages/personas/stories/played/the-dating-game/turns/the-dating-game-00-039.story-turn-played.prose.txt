"Good night, Grace."

She smiles back at you over her shoulder and turns toward the far gate.

You watch her lantern move off between the dark rows of stones, small and gold under the pines.

It passes through the gate and out of sight, and the cemetery is left to the evening.

You walk home up the hill. The air is cooling, and the streetlights are on all along Apple Avenue.

Your house is quiet: one mug in the drying rack, one coat on the hook.

You kick off the dusty Ecco slip-ons by the door, as you did last night.

You go to bed, and the long day lets go of you. Sleep comes easily.
