"Hi there!" you say, and walk over toward her, up the short path to the step. "I don't think I've seen you here before. I'm Alan, I live just down the street there on Apple."

You gesture back the way you came. "Nice to meet you!"

She follows your gesture down the street, unhurried, then brings those gold eyes back to you.

She doesn't get up. Instead she leans back on her hands on the step, easy, and the move leaves room on the step beside her.

"Grace," she says. "Nice to meet you, Alan."

She says your name slowly, as if she's setting it down somewhere safe.

"You wouldn't have seen me. I keep late hours; I'm mostly out once it's dark."

She glances up at the sky over the rooftops, where the gold is starting to deepen toward evening.

"This is my quiet part of the day. Resting up before my night."

Her hand comes to rest on the handle of the brass lantern beside her, unlit.
