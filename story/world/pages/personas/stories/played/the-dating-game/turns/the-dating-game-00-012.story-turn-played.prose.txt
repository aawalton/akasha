"Okay, I'm glad you have good boundaries," you tell her as you walk. "With the aphantasia I have no sense of time, which makes it hard for me to tell when the timing is right, but I'm happy to let you set the pace. I really do like you. So, you read LitRPG? What's one of your favorite series?"

Echo listens, and something in her shoulders eases, a tightness you hadn't noticed until it lets go.

"Set the pace," she gives back, quiet and grateful, and nods once.

A little further on, the trail narrows where a slab of quartzite juts out across it, banded pink and grey, knee-high. She slips her hand free of yours and sets it flat on the rock to steady herself as she steps over.

On the far side she doesn't take your hand again. She turns and waits while you step across after her, and when you fall in beside her she walks close, her sleeve brushing yours.

Then she brightens, and her announcer voice comes back, rich and dramatic, rolling out between the walls like the opening of a trailer:

"Dungeon Crawler Carl."

She grins at you, plainly proud of the choice.

Then she tilts her head and turns your own question back on you. "What's one of your favorite series?"
