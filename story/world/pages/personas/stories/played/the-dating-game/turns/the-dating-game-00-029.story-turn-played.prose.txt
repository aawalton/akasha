"No, I like the quiet here too," you tell her. "I have a hard time feeling like death is real though. The past, the present, and the future all blur together for me."

Grace slows beside an old headstone, its carved name worn soft by weather.

She lets the lantern light rest on the stone a moment. Then she looks at you, grave and curious at once.

"It's real," she says gently. "I've never once found it otherwise."

There's no sharpness in it, only certainty, the way someone states the weather.

Then the corner of her red mouth lifts. "But I'm glad it doesn't sit heavy on you."

She walks on between the rows, the lantern held low, keeping her pace to yours.

"All blurred together," she says, turning it over. "What's that like, from the inside?"
