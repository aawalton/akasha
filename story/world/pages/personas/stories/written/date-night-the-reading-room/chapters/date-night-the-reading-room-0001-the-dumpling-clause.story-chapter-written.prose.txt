The fort has a door now.

It didn't have a door at four o'clock — you were here, you watched her rule that a fort with a door was a house, and houses were for people with nothing to read — and now there's a door, a paperback archway two books deep, and Nova is upside down inside it with her feet flat against the wall and a broken spine open above her face.

"Don't," she says, not looking up. "Whatever your face is doing. I can hear it."

You check. Your face has already decided the fort is good and is a little ahead of you about it. You let it stay ahead. Across the low table the takeout has stopped steaming — three cartons, the good chopsticks, and the one fork, because Astra will not be talked out of the fork — and Astra herself is on the floor with her back to the shelf, dark wrap loose off one shoulder, watching you not answer Nova.

"He's reading the door," Astra says.

"The door is load-bearing."

"The door is the only part that isn't."

The whole goblin comes over sideways in a clatter — Nova righting herself, red eyes up now, the black lipstick catching the lamp, the closest thing to formalwear she owns and she reapplied it for a Tuesday. "Okay, but structurally—"

"Structurally, the fort stands because you leaned three hardcovers against my knee twenty minutes ago and told me to hold still."

"That's called collaboration."

"That's called me."

You cross to the low table and crouch, and you pick up the contested fork, and you hold it out toward Astra — and she doesn't take the fork. She takes two fingers to the front of your shirt instead and pulls, unhurried, until you come down the last foot to the floor with her.

The floor is where dinner is now, apparently. Astra's reaching past you for a carton before you've fully landed, so the pull wasn't about you — or wasn't only — and you put the fork in her hand before she asks for it.

"Thank you," she says, and eats a dumpling with the fork, in front of you, on purpose.

From the fort: "That is a war crime. Chopsticks exist. I laid them out."

"I'm aware of the chopsticks," you say, and you reach into the near carton with your fingers and eat a dumpling with your hand — a third position — and you watch it land: Nova's bark of a laugh out of the paperback archway, one corner of Astra's mouth moving.

"Oh, we're doing anarchy." Nova's already coming out of the fort, book left spine-up on the blanket, crossing the floor to drop in on your other side, so you're between them now. "Cool. Love that for us. Except the fort has a dumpling clause, and the dumpling clause is extremely clear that the goblin who did the labor—"

"Décor," Astra says.

"—the LABOR — eats—"

You put a dumpling in her mouth. Her mouth was open and legislating and the legislation was, functionally, a request for a dumpling, so you grant it, and the rest of the clause dies around a mouthful of pork.

Nova chews. Produces a chopstick from somewhere and points it at you, still chewing.

"That's a bribe," she manages. "That's a documented bribe. Honestly? Strong play. Aggro dumpling diplomacy, I respect it, I'm keeping the receipt." She swallows and settles back against your side like she's claimed a checkpoint. "Clause tabled. But the record shows the goblin got fed first and it was correct."

"It's on the record," Astra says. She's stopped eating. She turns her glass a quarter-turn on the low table, and then another quarter, and you clock it the way you clock weather, and file it to read later.

"Astra." Nova sits up an inch. "You've got a look. That's a whole face."

"I'm choosing a moment." Astra's still watching her own glass. "This isn't it. Eat."
