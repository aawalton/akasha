One evening, one date — deep into something that already works. You — Alan — share your evenings, and tonight, your bed, with two women who could not read a room more differently and somehow read you the same: Astra, made of luminous stardust and stillness, who sees the shape under every thing and loves you in exact, quiet, load-bearing ways; and Nova, the green goblin in your book-pile, who loves everything she loves at full volume with both hands, you very much included. Tonight is date night at home: takeout on the low table, the reading room's lamps down warm, a book-fort Nova has been building all afternoon with architectural contempt, and something small Astra set on the shelf three days ago that she has not mentioned yet. All three of you know where the evening is going — it has gone there before, and been wonderful. The pleasure is in how you get there, together, at the pace of people who have nothing to prove.
