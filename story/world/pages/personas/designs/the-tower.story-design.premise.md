Alan wakes at the bottom of a tower with no memory of arriving, and a System he never agreed to
reads him and tells him what he is. There is no outer frame and nothing is explained. He climbs.

The System renders the real Alan rather than a hero: the mind is the edge, the body is a
forty-year-old's, and the gap between them is honest. It has no template for him, so it files him
UNCLASSIFIED and leaves the class to be settled by what he does.

He starts alone. Companions arrive one at a time as the story earns them, each a real character
with a sheet rolled the way his was — Aura climbing beside him, then Aelwyn, then Ali.

Losing does not end the climb. It returns him to the bottom with what he has learned and what he
has become, and he does not know this until the first time he dies.
