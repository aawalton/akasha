# Floor 2

Harem Hotel — floor design, subject `floor-2`.

## Layout

TWO rooms behind the floor-2 shut door, in sequence: ROOM 1 the ANTECHAMBER (the Usher, migrating-crit solo — teaches 'the crit-spot moves, keep re-reading while you fight') -> ROOM 2 the GALLERY (First + Second Lamp, coordinated pair with alternating anchor + mutual re-weave — teaches 'read the coordination, deny the anchor, finish in one push, manage the flank') -> the floor-2 CHALLENGE DOOR (a free threshold, opens when both rooms are cleared; clearing rises the whole floor + level +1 to 3, Aria's bind loosens to bound-L3). CONSTRUCT floor throughout (plaster/lamplight, like floor 1) -> retreat re-weaves everything to pristine FREE of the rest-clock (the Gallery's mutual re-weave is that same construct nature made a live combat tactic). CALIBRATION: opposition tuned for a LEVEL-2 BOUND PAIR (Alan L2 + Aria bound-L2) — credibly dangerous, real kill-risk, never assuming unbound-Aria power. BAKE-FORWARD (floor 3, combat spine, LEVEL-3 BOUND PAIR): floor 2 spends SIZE=2rooms + threat single->pair + tactic-evolution. Floor 3 steps ONE dominant new axis (already designed): an ENVIRONMENTAL / light-shadow complication that interacts with the fight (expose a shadowed crit-spot with light; a failing-light clock), keeping the combat spine. HORIZON QUANTUM: floor 3 must stay premise-consistent (bound L3 pair, combat) before the story enters floor 2 — floor 3 already re-examined. Floor 4 on the floor-2-clear boundary under v30 (author its clue-deal->win stretch as an elimination arc with per-probe textures). Floor 6 carries the SECOND companion-design (bound on join per world-logic-companion-bind; variety + safety-net laws: NOT a thread/weave/fate mechanism, NOT sexual the same way as Aria's LOOM).

Challenge owner: character.

## Challenge

CHALLENGE OWNER: CHARACTER (challenge-door-v3 COMBAT SPINE). FIRST ESCALATION FLOOR (post-tutorial) — a FIGHT, won by tactical play + dice, not an epistemic gate. Hold the combat spine but BREAK the floor-1 template: floor 1 was a single warden, so floor 2 steps two axes at once per the escalation mandate (magnitude without a new companion — companions arrive floors 1/6/11 only): SIZE grows 1 room -> 2 rooms, and THREAT shape steps single-warden -> a coordinated PAIR, the core tactic EVOLVING across the floor (migrating crit-spot -> mutual re-weave). THE PAIR: Alan enters at LEVEL 2 with his floor-1 kit (the Letter-Knife opening the FINESSE-placed keystone-strike, the Lamplit Half-Cloak, his WILL LINK-bound to Aria) and Aria BOUND to LEVEL 2 beside him (world-logic-companion-bind — a peer who leveled with him on the floor-1 clear, NOT an OP ally; calibrate the opposition for a LEVEL-2 BOUND PAIR). Real kill-risk grows: Alan is still fragile and the Gallery pair can FLANK — being caught between them is a genuine lethal line, so positioning and Aria's tempo control matter. NO UNDEALT-INFO GATE: the fight is winnable by fighting; the reads below are tactical OPTIMIZATIONS, never a gate.

TACTICAL READ LAYER (optimization, not a gate): the wardens carry crit-spots a PERCEPTION/INT read locates (yielding INFO — v28 character-challenge; Aria's 'Read the Table' assists at bound L2), letting targeted strikes land the correct/decisive multiplier and end the fight faster (less exposure = less kill-risk). But BRUTE IS VIABLE, NEVER THE LOSING LINE: a warded body takes reduced damage (x0.5-0.75), open lines take neutral real damage (x1.0), and the fight can be won without the read, only slower and under more flank-pressure. Aria's 'Set the Pace' (delay/reposition initiative) is the counter to the pair's coordination — buying tempo to slip the flank or focus the anchor; 'Read the Table' names the migrated crit-spot / current anchor. No floor impassable for lack of the bond (accelerator, never key).

Governed by world-logic-challenge-door-v3: the far door is a free-opening threshold; the wardens are the combat gate; clearing BOTH rooms (killing the wardens) makes the whole floor RISE + Alan levels +1 (to 3; Aria's bind loosens to bound-L3). v30 elimination textures ride the tactical tells (a stale target-lock whiffs on re-knit mass; a struck Lamp visibly re-seals from its partner); v29 holds (Aria reads what a bound-L2 perception genuinely returns — honest, partial, no oracle).

PLAYER'S GAME = TACTICAL DECLARATIONS under fire (mechanics resolve): re-target the migrating crit-spot; read and focus the CURRENT anchor, then finish the freed partner in one push before the re-weave; manage the flank with Aria's tempo. The clear is WINNING THE FIGHT, not a synthesis.

## Enemies

### The Usher

- **Role:** floor-2 room 1 (Antechamber) — single warden, the escalation of the Doorward: a taller plaster construct that LEARNS mid-fight
- **Readable trait:** MIGRATING CRIT-SPOT (breaks the floor-1 static target). An ACTIVE combatant like the Doorward — takes initiative, strikes — DURABLE but not force-immune (warded mass x0.5-0.75, open lines x1.0; winnable by sustained combat). ITS TWIST: its keystone crit-seam RE-KNITS IN A NEW PLACE each time it is struck-but-not-finished, so a stale target-lock lands on warded mass — the read is PERISHABLE, keep re-reading to keep the crit-spot located. CORRECT READ (x1.5-2.0): re-identify the seam's CURRENT location (a PERCEPTION/INT read, Aria assists) and strike it. DECISIVE (x3.0): a clean keystone strike (INT-read + FIN-placed with the Letter-Knife) before it migrates again — Aria's 'Set the Pace' buys the initiative window. Brute is suboptimal, never the losing line (no mirror-punish). Construct reset on retreat (re-weaves to pristine free of the rest-clock; the reads persist, HP does not).

### First Lamp

- **Role:** floor-2 room 2 (Gallery) — coordinated pair (with Second Lamp): the two cover each other; the current ANCHOR on some exchanges
- **Readable trait:** MUTUAL RE-WEAVE (target-priority tactic). Two lighter active wardens that COVER each other: strike one's crit-spot while its partner STANDS and the standing partner RE-WEAVES the struck one back toward pristine (net progress lost unless you deny the re-weave). TACTIC: read which Lamp is the current ANCHOR (the one re-weaving — Aria's 'Read the Table' names it), deny it the re-weave (a tempo-steal, a pin, forcing it to answer a threat), THEN finish its partner in one push; the now-lone anchor falls to the same play. FLANK DANGER: the pair tries to put Alan BETWEEN them (a flanked level-2 round is real kill-risk); hold a wall, a doorway, or Aria at your back. Gate bands: warded mass x0.5-0.75, open x1.0, correct crit-read x1.5-2.0, decisive x3.0 — brute wins slower, never futile. Construct reset on retreat.

### Second Lamp

- **Role:** floor-2 room 2 (Gallery) — coordinated pair (with First Lamp): identical construct; the anchor role ALTERNATES, so which to focus must be RE-READ, not assumed
- **Readable trait:** MUTUAL RE-WEAVE, mirror of First Lamp — see First Lamp. The load-bearing twist: the ANCHOR ROLE ALTERNATES between the two (whichever last acted defensively becomes the next re-weaver), so a player who assumes 'First Lamp is always the anchor' focuses the wrong one and the real anchor re-weaves — a tactical whiff (v30 texture), not a lethal gate. Re-read the anchor each exchange (Aria assists at bound L2). Same bands as First Lamp; a retreat re-weaves BOTH to pristine, so the pair must fall in one sustained push. Real kill-risk from the flank, not from an unsolved puzzle.

- **Effect:** A small pale-glyph charm at the collar — a modest PERCEPTION / INITIATIVE aid. Sharpens the re-read (spot the migrated crit-spot, name the current anchor faster) and helps Alan act BEFORE the Lamps coordinate their flank (initiative to slip the pincer). On the tactical-read spine — it speeds the read, it does not replace the fight.
- **Source:** Reward — set into the floor-2 shut door's frame, claimable once both rooms are cleared and the door opens.
