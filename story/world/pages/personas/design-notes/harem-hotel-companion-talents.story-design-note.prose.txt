Companion-talent design law for the Harem Hotel. These standing constraints condition every companion design in the game, and every companion design satisfies all eight before it lands.

1. One talent, character-consonant. The talent is the soul made visible: it grows from her nature, vocation, history and flaw. It is never a stat bolted onto a character; it is always her character mechanized.

2. Variety across the roster. Each companion's talent runs a different mechanism or axis (fate-based, mark-based, perception-based, reveal-gated, and so on). No two companions' talents rhyme, and the roster reads as a taxonomy of distinct souls. Aria's axis, thread-binding and trait-deepening through entanglement, is hers alone: no later companion runs a thread, weave or fate-binding mechanism, and no later companion is sexual in the same way, with intimacy as the only conduit to a deepening.

3. Hidden and experimental activation. Activation conditions are unique to each talent and concrete on the game master's side from the moment the talent is made. A talent made without its concrete trigger is a design defect. The conditions are discovered by living the relationship, never published as a checklist, and they are kept only in the design and on the companion's game-master-side sheet.

4. First-fire protocol. The System marks the moment flatly. Thereafter the talent shows as that companion's bond affinity description, and the affinity surface stays dark until the first fire.

5. Register from the sheet. Each companion carries a register block to voice her from, with explicit lists of what she never says. Register-as-distribution applies. Aria's sheet has a rich register block but no such list yet.

6. Talents are accelerators, never keys. The two progressions stay separate, and no floor is impassable for lack of a bond.

7. Fully statted. Every companion has a real System sheet: attributes, pools, skills and her own talent. The Link couples only to real, growing stats. A bound companion's working stats are those real stats for the climb.

8. Explicitly sexual. Every talent is explicitly sexual in some way: its activation, its operation or both are tied to sex or intimacy, not merely adjacent to it. The axis is wide by design, and a talent may run through kinks, group sex, voyeurism, exhibitionism, denial, ritual and the like, not only pair intimacy. This composes with 1 and 2: how the talent is sexual expresses who she is, and variety applies to the kind of sexuality as much as to the mechanism, so a talent sexual in the same way as an existing companion's breaks variety even where its mechanism differs. Aria's Loom meets this through its intimacy-gated activation rather than through its operation, which is not in itself sexual; a sharper sexual axis for the operation is left open.

A design audit gap against any of the eight blocks the design.
