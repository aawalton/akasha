Magic is built in your soul, literally.

Ferris trained his entire life to fight, only to lose his leg to a monster.

Now, he must leave home in search of a magical ability to regrow it.

To grow stronger, he will need to physically enter his own soul. There, he can construct magical abilities to use in real life. But it won’t be safe, heart demons lurk in the shadows. Through hard work, crafting, experimentation, and determination he hopes to find a way to construct an ability to regrow his leg.

He’ll raid rifts filled with monsters, delve deep into the Wild, find magical treasures with profound effects, and build an entire world in his soul.

***

This story was inspired while reading cultivation novels, asking the question: “What are they doing while meditating for years? What if they are playing Minecraft in their soul?”

What to expect:

* Magical Crafting and Enchanting.
* Spear Warrior -> Spear + Magic Equipment.
* Slow pace progressive fantasy.
* Magical Abilities and Martial Arts.
* Slime Familiar, that eats all the things!
* Cultivation/Xianxia inspired magic system.
* Explore rifts, kill monsters, find epic loot.
* No harem.
