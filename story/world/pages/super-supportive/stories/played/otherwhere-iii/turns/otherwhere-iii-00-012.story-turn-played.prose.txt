"I'm afraid not," you say. "Or at least, my ID's in the wallet my ex-boyfriend dumped somewhere, with my phone. I never memorized it."

The clerk nods, the way she might if she heard it every week. She taps at the tablet. From where you sit you can see the word come up in a box on the screen: SELF-PAY.

"That's okay," she says. "We don't turn anybody away. I'll flag you for our social worker."

You look back up at the TV. Then you look back at her.

"Um..." you say. "I think I need to get to the Artonan Consulate in the morning. Is that far from here?"

"The Desk Demon place?" Her brows go up a little over the glasses. "It's downtown. Straight south on the Red Line from Lawrence."

She looks at you a moment longer. Her eyes go to the bracelet on your wrist, and the hospital socks, and back to your face. Then she lets it go.

"Social worker comes on at eight. She'll come find you before you head anywhere, okay?"

She stands, tucks the tablet under her arm, and walks back up the rows toward the desk.
