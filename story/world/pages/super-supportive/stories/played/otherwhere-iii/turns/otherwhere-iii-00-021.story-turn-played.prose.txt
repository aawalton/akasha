You set the receiver back in its cradle. The sigil stays behind on the little screen.

You walk back across the terrazzo to the desk, boots squeaking, every step loud in the empty lobby. By the time you get there he is facing the monitors again.

"Thank you," you tell him. "If I someday have the power, I will help you if I can."

The four slits of his nose flare. He breathes you in. His eyes stay on the screens.

Then he laughs. It is a hiss, long, and then again, and again, over and over, like air let out of something in short pulls. It stops all at once.

"A promise to the chained," he says. "The ones who make those mostly forget them."

The ropes sway at his wrists.

"You smell as if you mean it. I will remember that you did."

One dry click sounds in his throat. He doesn't speak of it again.

You sit on a bench by the big plants along the wall to wait. The crickets chirr in the leaves behind your head. The ropes hum. Through the glass, across the street, the clock on the bank sign creeps past ten, and then past quarter past.

Twice, tourists push in through the doors, phones already up. Twice they stand a minute frowning at the screens, and go out again.

At 10:31 the elevator chimes, and its doors slide open.

A woman steps out. She is tall and lean, with grey hair, and she wears plain grey robes that fall straight to the floor. Her feet are bare on the stone. She is nearly human to look at. Nearly.

She crosses first to the desk, and inclines her head to him.

"Gorgon," she says. Her voice is courteous, the English careful.

He dips his horned head in return.

Then she turns, and looks at you. She studies you a long moment from across the lobby, without hurry.

"I am Onn-desveth," she says. "I oversee this consulate." Her English is formal, and a little slow, each word set down whole. "The Contract tells me there is a person here with no record, who says she passed through chaos."

She walks nearer. She stops an arm's length from you, and holds out one long hand, palm up.

"I would screen you for chaos now. It takes a few minutes. It is done by touch. May I?"
