"Nala Arthur," you say. It comes out smooth, the way your own name always has. "January 22, 1986. 1350 Apple Ave, Provo, Utah."

A beat.

"No local address."

The triage window gives back only dim shapes, washed out by the bright room behind it, but you can make out Denise's shape behind your own. She lifts a hand in a little wave and pushes through a door marked STAFF ONLY, and it swings shut after her.

Marcus types while you talk. Two fingers, quick and steady, clacking. N-A-L-A. The street. The city. The keys keep up with you.

At the birth date his fingers stop.

They stay there on the keys. He looks up, past the monitor, at your face. He takes his time about it. It is a long look, and a mild one.

"Eighty-six?" he says. It is easy, with no edge in it at all. "Hon, that'd make you fifty-one."

He leaves his hands where they are.

"Say the birth date for me one more time?"
