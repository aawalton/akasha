"I will exercise my right," you say. "I would request Esh-erdi as my trusted witness. He should be on Earth soon to celebrate his inesvul, if he isn't here already." You keep your voice steady. "As I am new to this world, he is the only one I would trust."

Onn-desveth's face goes very still.

Across the lobby, at the desk, the golden ropes flicker once, a quick bright stutter along their length, and settle.

"Esh-erdi," she says. "A Knight of the Mother Planet." Her grey eyes stay on yours. "You name him as if you know him."

"I know him only by his name and his rank. He has never heard of you." Each word is set down whole, the same as before, but slower now. "And he is not on Earth. He is off-world, far past what this consulate can easily reach."

"I cannot summon a knight. I could only send him a message, and wait."

"A message would carry your secret past me, to places I cannot follow it. That is the very thing this contract is meant to prevent."

She folds her long hands in front of her.

"Name him, and we wait, and your secret travels. Or name someone else. Here, in this city."
