The Reaper of Death has arrived.

Beyond the shadows, in the world of Penumbra, humanity's fate wanes.

Here, the greed, hatred, and gluttony that fester in the real world manifest as monstrous beasts. A thin line of scythe-wielding Reapers is all that keeps these sins in check. 

Chosen by the gods as the first Death Affinity Reaper in generations, Miles is pulled into this world and given a scythe, a skill, and the opportunity to change everything. To reroute the entire future of humanity.

But at what cost?

What to expect:

First person, single POV

Progression by harvesting monster's 'Wills' to enhance stats/skills and advance rank

Isekai where the world MC left matters

Weak to strong to superstrong MC

No harem

Swearing, violence, some gore

Updates M, W, F
