In his second life, Lamb finds himself at the bottom of the food chain.

After dying on Earth, he wakes alone in the wilderness of a vast new world of monsters and magic. Civilization lies beyond the wilds, and every step toward it leads through creatures powerful enough to kill him outright.

If Lamb wants to survive, he has to grow stronger fast. In this world, power is measured in levels and skills, but the combat class he needs can only be claimed at level fifteen.

So he runs. He climbs. He learns to turn terrain, instinct, and sheer stubborn endurance into his advantages.

The land is as stunning as it is deadly, and far beyond the mountains, powerful figures are already beginning to take an interest in the God-Touched newcomer.
