A slum kid like Shouren doesn’t have much in life.

He picks peaches for himself and his grandma. Dreams of one day affording a Class Card of his own. But even that hope is stolen one fateful night.

His body mangled and spine shattered, a bleeding Shouren crawls within the dungeon, desperately reaching out his hand for the floating gray card.

And something answers.

A corrupted system binds to his fractured soul, awakening a path no one else had seen before—a [Cardsmith] capable of forging cards that Evolve… and Corrupt.

The weakest class on the planet hides a secret the factions and guilds would kill to possess. 

Will Shouren be able to evolve enough times before the world finally takes notice?

This series includes:

🎴 Deck building + Card Evolutions

🔨 Crafting + Card Recipe System

📈 Weak-to-Strong + Rags-to-Riches

💀 A dark, sometimes humorous, card magic-infused world with high stakes
