Born disabled in a war-torn world, an autistic savant's only solace was blacksmithing—until even that was overshadowed by loss and despair. But when he joins the Otherworld Project, a chance to live as a powerful clone on a distant planet, he uncovers an astonishing secret: his clone’s strength transfers to his real body.

With newfound abilities, Julian’s rise to fame begins. Will he become a hero across two worlds, or let darkness consume them both? Journey with Julian and his accidental rise to fame.

NOTE: MC is in the spectrum. (AUTISTIC SAVANT)

Release Schedule: At least 3 times a week (8,000 to 10,000 words)

Average Chapter Length: 2,000 words(My chapters are getting longer. Longest is 4500)
