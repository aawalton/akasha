You go back to the top and read it all again, slowly, line by line. A Status. Your Status, with a Class and Attributes and Paths, sitting in your mind in plain words.

What is a Path? A road you walk, or something that walks you? Soulstealing sounds like what you just did to the demon. Completionist sounds like homework. You turn the words over and get nothing back from them.

The Unallocated Stats you leave where they are. You don't know enough yet to spend them.

After a while the words stop feeling new. The dark is warm and the heartbeat keeps time, and you can't see, or move much, or go anywhere. You are bored.

So you count. One, two, three, four. Past fifty, past a hundred, on into the hundreds, one number after the next.

:::skill
name: Path of Basic Numeracy
:::

You keep counting. Up by twos: two, four, six, eight. Up by threes: three, six, nine, twelve. Then back down the same ladders, to zero.

:::status-assessment
name: Path of Basic Numeracy
note: Tier: 1; Mental: +1
:::

Then multiples. Sevens, up and down: seven, fourteen, twenty-one, twenty-eight. Nines next, then twelves, up past a hundred and forty-four and back down again, while the heartbeat thumps on.

Somewhere past the walls, voices start up. Muffled, many of them, women's voices, chanting the same words together. You don't know the words.
