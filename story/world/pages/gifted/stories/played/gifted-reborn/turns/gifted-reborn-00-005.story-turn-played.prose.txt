You set yourself an exercise: Fibonacci sequences taken modulo n+2, then n+1, then on down, every starting pair carried round to its loop and to the length of that loop. You start with n+2 and work it whole. Take a pair of numbers, add them, keep the remainder, carry the pair along, then the next, until a pair comes back that you have already had. That is a loop, and you learn it by its length. Then the next pair, until every pair under n+2 is held in memory with its loop.

Before you take up n+1, you go back through what you have built: every pair under n+2, its loop, its length, recited once in order. Then you step down.

Take a pair, follow it round until it closes, name the loop, hold the length, then take the next pair. Then the next modulus, and the same again.

The numbers start to slip. A pair goes missing from your hold, then another, and the loops run together in the dark. Somewhere in the middle of a modulus, you sleep.

You wake, and the dark is the same dark. You take up the tables where you left them, at the pair you lost.
