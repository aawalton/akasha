It held you in its teeth the whole way up from the Ocean, toying with you. Now it can barely hold its own shape. You turn on it and bite down into its darkness, the way it bit into you.

It flinches. It tries to curl away from you, but it has nothing left to curl with, and its edges sag and run like smoke.

You tear at it the way it tore at you, slowly, and then not slowly at all. It comes away in ragged shreds.

One claw scrapes across you, weak as a kitten's. You bite through it.

The voice that snickered in your head is gone. It shrieks instead, thin and high, and each shriek is fainter than the last.

You swallow it a shred at a time. It goes down cold and bitter, and you keep going until there is nothing of it left to swallow.

Then the shrieking stops. You reach for the place where the demon was, and it is empty.
