Dragons are the enemy of the kingdom and Alice is now their master.

Living a life of grinding poverty in the General Laborer class, Alice’s only hope was the System someday notices her and rewards her hard work.

That day arrives when she discovers a System-seed: A one-time chance to change from the lowest to the highest of classes.

Now she is a rare minion-master in charge of a ever-growing horde of dragons. Dragons that will not stay small and cute forever. As each grows and multiplies, so does her power.

Alice has become She of Many Dragons.
