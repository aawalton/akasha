The universe has a source code and I have admin access. Using that to fix my problems doesn’t make me a bad person. Probably.

My name is Samantha and I was about to have the biggest moment of my career when I was suddenly framed and fired. But that wasn’t even the worst thing to happen that day. My coffee pot broke, my mom called to remind me to bring a date to Thanksgiving, oh, and now I can somehow see the code behind reality. nbd.

I’m going to get to the bottom of whatever the hell is going on. Even if that means committing several light felonies, keeping my best friend, Kate, in the dark, and keeping this suspiciously hot new guy at arms length (even if he keeps showing up at the perfect moment). But, the deeper I look into the code the stranger everything becomes, and beyond the code something is watching me. Now with everything and everyone at stake I have to decide how far is too far?
