Power, true power, has always lurked within the shadows. 

When his first Job as a newly synced Rogue goes spectacularly wrong, Levi is left in possession of the obsidian stone. With no other choice but to go on the run, he abandons everything and everyone he'd ever known in his little town, just to keep breathing. 

A low-leveled synced like him in possession of one of the six founding stones, made him an attractive mark, and so Levi finds himself having to figure out a way to survive multiple attempts on his life as well as level up as fast as possible. If he could survive long enough, then he'd stand a better chance at claiming his place on the council of six, as the obsidian rogue.
