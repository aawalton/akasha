Lysanthia, the strongest Demon Queen, spent centuries trying to die.

Not because she wanted death. It was the only way to break her chains.

Her reincarnation spell worked -mostly. She woke in a forest nearly a thousand years in the future, in a body that wasn't quite hers, stripped of her overwhelming power. A world without a System. Without Classes. Without the Gods watching her every step.

Her reincarnation is complicated by the presence of Ash, the Hero who slew her. She's now thrust into a world almost completely alien, yet somehow recognizable -a world where power is etched in Ink, and where she must start from nothing.

A world where some people show her kindness for no reason at all. That might be harder than starting over.

For the first time in eons, the strongest Demon Queen is free. Not even she knows what comes next.

- A once very overpowered FMC, now forced to get overpowered all over again. Though, not every problem can be solved by punching (and some are caused by punching).

- A Demon Queen learning to be more than what she was.
