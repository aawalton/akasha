In order to survive, an experienced smith must relearn everything.

After a beautiful elven queen sacrificed him in a ritual, he was thrust into a world full of dangers not even the eye can see. Not only that, but he was told to save this new world. 

But can he do that when a fifty meter tall sentient tree comes knocking on his door? His hammer may be hammery, but he is only human. Sure, he may have some spirits that rock his awesome gear. But, will that be enough when trees can breathe fire? 

In a world full of untold treasure, danger lurks around every corner. Which would be fine, if that goddess didn't paint a giant target on his back saying ‘delicious monster food.’

 Oh well, Orn will just have to pick up his hammer and use it to smack more than an anvil. Preferably with a few ice spikes and other magicy bits thrown in. It will be needed, since deadly is a title given to cuddly puppies. 

What To Expect?

- LitRPG stuffs, with spirit taming, magic, crafting and so much more!
- A deep world with all sorts of secrets and stories to be told. 
- An intentionally designed magical system that touches everything from the simplest plant to the grandest mountain. 
- Meeting lots of strange beings. From all powerful elementals, to ancient mages from times long past, to even a talking mushroom that likes cheese. 
- Eventual romance. No harems tho, our guy only needs one really cute wife. 
- A MC that might not always know the best strategies, but will push on and learn all that he can despite that. 
- Hints of other things, like politics and kingdom building, definitely not the focus. 
- Some sprinkles of comedy, to lighten the tone. 
- Somewhat OP MC.

- Almost forgot saving the world and stuff. 

- This is not a cozy story.
