A sex game, not a combat game. I play myself, Alan, as I am.

I wake on the ground floor of a sealed tower. The only way out is up, and the tower is endless: escape is always one floor higher.

Every floor is a staged world, a bathhouse, a masquerade, a throne room, and each one has a stated sexual task, such as "bring all three attendants to climax together". The task is said plainly the moment I arrive. The women on the floor play parts in its staging. How many women a floor holds is set by its task. There is no failure: I keep at the task until it is done, and the stairs up open when it is met.

The women are born of the tower, new characters rather than personas, and every one is an adult. The tower keeps a cast that starts small and grows, a new woman every few floors. At each new floor the tower deals a new hand: the women I have met come back remixed into new roles, new pairings and new dynamics, and what I know of each one carries with me.

No skills, no points and no dice. The sex is the game, and it is written fully explicit.
