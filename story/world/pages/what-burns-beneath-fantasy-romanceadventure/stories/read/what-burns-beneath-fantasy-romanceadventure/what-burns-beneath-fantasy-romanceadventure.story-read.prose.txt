Traversing the mortal realm by day.

Trapped in the Fae realm by night.

Brin’s been better.

As a hedge mage with a green thumb and a heart of gold, all she ever wanted was a quiet home and a nice garden. 

And she had them… until the Fae attacked.

One night of monsters and bloodshed leaves her peaceful life shattered and her best friend at death’s door. And when one of the Fae offers to save her friend- for a price, of course- Brin accepts… then steals a magical relic from the creature and runs for her life.

But escaping a relentless monster isn’t easy, especially when said monster keeps appearing in her dreams. And if that wasn’t bad enough, bargaining with a Fae is a crime punishable by death… and Brin’s only protection on the road is a ruthless, frustratingly attractive mercenary who just knows she’s hiding something.

And this terrified gardener is not hiding it very well.
