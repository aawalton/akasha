A realm where humans battle monsters and the good die young. 

4 weeks ago Will woke up in a new world filled with Monsters and Magic.

Thrust into this new reality with nothing more than a class called “soldier” and a leveling system he’s never seen before, Will fights just to survive. 

When he stumbles across a dying stranger, a mysterious quest line activates. 

Now Will must head to the front lines where humanity wages war against a ruthless Goblin Horde. There he will join the standing Kadian Army and forge his name from the fires of legend. 

He’ll have to face Goblins, Hordes of the Undead, cultists, and demons from hell. And that’s just before breakfast. 

Iron Blooded is a gritty Progression Fantasy with focuses on battles, leveling up, and political intrigue. Perfect for those who like their fantasy with a side of LITRPG.
