Ava Silvers just wanted one outcome from her awakening, to unlock a class that would end her family’s bound service to mages.

All she had to do was go into her sector’s designated awakening rift, proceed through the experience, and not die horribly.

However, things don’t quite go as expected.

During the rift experience, Ava finds ancient magic that helps her survive. One that allows her to craft mage bombs, lots of mage bombs, that all go BOOM!

Now the very success she used to survive, has put her right back into the sights of the mages she tried to avoid.

Only now, the mages are making things personal.
