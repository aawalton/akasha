Originally titled "Mirror World: Goddess Transformation!"

Sammy was just another struggling college student when she got transported to another realm and transformed into the wholly (holy?) different form of a goddess. Now she has to acquire followers to appease the ever helpful System.

The worst part was that her powers had something stupid to do with mirrors. Go figure.

Progression fantasy god game from the seat of power — care to take over the world, one follower at a time? Based on the prompt ‘Mirrors can’t eat people.’ Extremely (multi) class-based LitRPG system inspired by alternate TTRPGs that love their keywords.

- Third-person POV
- Leveraging unusual abilities/ creative problem-solving
- Geeky, sassy, snarky, but lovable MC
- Pop-culture references
- Slow-burn mysteries on top of mysteries
