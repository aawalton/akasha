~Mantle of Gods~

A beloved progression fantasy novel about four chosen heroes destined to save the world from an ancient evil. Too bad they're complete idiots who fail spectacularly, leaving reality to burn in eternal darkness.

...But why did I have to transmigrate into this doomed world as Jin Winters, a nameless extra not even mentioned in the original story? And of all places, why Vienna — one of four cities destined to be completely annihilated in the opening act?

Armed with my meta-knowledge and a cancer survivor's stubborn refusal to die quietly, I awakened the mysterious "Mantle of Harvest" — a power system the protagonists never fully understood.

But knowledge is only useful if you live long enough to use it.

Ninety-one days until the veil falls. Ninety-one days to grow strong enough to change a fate written in blood. Ninety-one days to prove that sometimes the extra can rewrite the entire damn story.

...Wait, why are my "quest visions" missing critical information? And what's this about harvesting more than just crops?

What To Expect:

> Weak to OP MC

> Strong friendship dynamics and character development under extreme pressure

> A protagonist who refuses to accept fate before he has tasted some beauties.

> Good variety of side-characters with their own lives, motivations, and personalities.

> Magic, stats, guns!
