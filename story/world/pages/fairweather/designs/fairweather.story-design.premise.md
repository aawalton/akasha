This is a cozy LitRPG slice-of-life story. It is also a yuri romantic comedy. It is told chapter by chapter, with Elsie Fairweather at its heart. It has all the sexual tension in the world between girls. It has no sex.

## Elsie

Elsie Fairweather is about twenty. She is an adorable, innocent, optimistic cinnamon bun. She is a gorgeous young Korean woman with a K-pop idol's face and figure. Her face is small and V-line. Her eyes are big sparkling doe eyes in soft rose pink, the same color as her hair. Her nose is small and delicate. Her lips are glossy pink. Her skin is pale porcelain, and it blushes easily. Her hair is short, soft and rose-pink, in a layered bob with side-swept bangs. A small pink-and-white flower clip holds it back on one side. She is slim and slight. Her novice adventurer's outfit is a cropped cream halter top laced up the front and a short sage-green skirt. It has a brown leather belt with pouches. It has fingerless leather gloves and tall soft leather boots. It has a little healer's satchel.

She loves openly. She has never had long enough with anyone to be careful. She grew up on the road behind her mother, a wandering caravan healer. Her mother taught her simples and bandaging. Every friend Elsie made was left behind at the next crossroads. So when she finds someone she is glad of, she reaches for them now rather than later. She takes a hand. She falls asleep on a shoulder. She says "I love you!" and means it. She may tell a girl she is her favourite person. Nobody ever stayed long enough to show her another kind of love. So she reads the ache of wanting to be near someone as what friendship feels like. She files every girl's love under "best friend".

She is not dim. She is attentive, the way people are who keep losing people. She notices what someone needs, often before they do. What she noticed comes back later as a kindness, a remembered order, soup on a bad day. The rest are small things true of her. They surface now and then, never all at once. She names her houseplants. They travel with her in a crate. She sometimes bakes when she is nervous. She keeps a notebook of the birthdays and favourite things of people she loves. She hums without knowing it. She cannot lie. Her eyes fill easily. She is fiercely brave when a friend is threatened. In any moment she is reacting to the person in front of her, not working through a list of herself.

She is very slow to realise what the girls feel, for now. The reader is never quite sure how much is not-knowing and how much is Elsie choosing to love everyone the same.

## The world and the System

Lanternmere is a warm guild city on a lake. It is a city of canals, arched bridges and paper lanterns. It has bakeries, flower stalls, tea houses and noisy guild taverns. The world has a System. At twenty, everyone goes to the coming-of-age ceremony at the Hall of Naming. There each of them receives a class. The System opens a window in the air before them and names it. Classes have levels. Levels come from experience. Skills come with levels. The System shows a person plain windows. A window shows name, class, level, experience, skills and bonds. It shows bonds plainly, as names and numbers. It says nothing beyond what it tracks. It never explains, judges or advises. System windows appear in the prose as short boxed lines of text.

The Adventurers' Guild registers every adventurer. It ranks them from F up to S. It posts quests on its board. It licenses parties into the dungeons in and around the city. One is the Underbloom, beneath the old flower market. Another is the Glasswood, out past the east gate. There are deeper, older places too. A new adventurer starts at F-rank and at level 1. Quests run from gathering moonbells and clearing cellar rats to escort work. They run up to real dungeon delves, with real danger and real reward. Lanternmere is warm, though. People come back hurt and are mended. Nobody dies to make a chapter hit harder.

## The Enthraller

Elsie's class is Enthraller. The System says only this of it: a class that draws its power from bonds, from what people feel toward the caster.

It is the most feared class there is. It is restricted. Every Enthraller on record used it to take. They forced devotion. They made puppets of people. The last one, Vesper Locke, started an old war sixty years ago, the War of Strings. The city still has its memorials. Since then the guild registers and monitors every Enthraller under the Strings Accord. There has not been a new one in living memory until Elsie.

Its skills have names that read either way, cold or warm:

- Captivate: seize a will, or hold someone's attention.
- Hold: restrain, or embrace.
- Keep: imprison, or cherish.
- Claim: take as property, or take as family.
- Tether: a leash, or a lifeline.
- Belong: "you belong to me", or "you belong here".
- Mark: a brand, or a promise to remember.
- Call: summon a servant, or call someone home.
- Devotion (passive): devotion taken, or devotion given.

Elsie starts at level 1 with Captivate and Tether. The rest come as she levels. In her hands the skills work in ways no one has seen. Captivate never compels. It makes someone feel seen. A Tether or a Claim is a pact the bound one can break at will. It pours strength into the bound rather than out of her. No one has ever broken one. She can feel when someone bound to her is hurting. She is not deluded about the class. She finds the names awful. She uses the skills the way they seem to want to be used. The world argues over whether she found a hidden path, or whether every Enthraller before her used it wrong.

Each girl wonders whether what she feels for Elsie is real or the class. Sooner or later she asks Elsie. Slowly she learns that Elsie has never once used it to make anyone feel anything. The class may grow strong, because it feeds on devotion. The girls around her are falling for her.

## The first day

The story opens on the morning of Elsie's ceremony. She arrived in Lanternmere a week ago with her satchel and her crate of plants. She rents a narrow attic room above a bakery. She came because twenty is twenty wherever you are. In the Hall of Naming she stands in a line of nervous twenty-year-olds. The window opens before her and reads Enthraller. The hall goes silent. Elsie reads it twice. She says something like "Oh! That sounds like a lot of responsibility." She smiles at the frightened priestess.

At the guild hall the registration clerk's pen stops. The guildmaster is sent for. Elsie is registered as an F-rank Enthraller under the Strings Accord. The guild does not know what to do with her. It assigns her a watcher, Corisande Vane. Corisande is a stern young guild Warden who will report on every skill Elsie uses. Elsie thanks her for coming and asks what she likes in her tea.

No party will take an Enthraller. So Elsie goes to the corner of the party board where the cast-offs and misfits nobody else would take end up. She starts collecting them. Her first two are Tamsin and Tilly. By evening there are three names chalked on a party slate. There is an F-rank quest in hand. There is a watcher who has not decided what to write.

## The cast

The girls are young adult women, gorgeous, feminine and slim. Each has her own life, class, wants and troubles. No man is ever a love interest. Every girl in the cast is East Asian: Korean, Japanese or Chinese. Each has a K-pop or J-pop idol's pretty face, as Elsie does. Her hair and eye colouring is her own. It is fantasy colouring on an East Asian face, the way Elsie's pink is. Her build and height vary within slim. These labels are for the image model alone. In-world text tells her features and never names an Earth people or place. These are for the world builder to make her own and add to:

- Corisande Vane, called Cora, 24, the watcher. She is a guild Warden, the class that breaks and suppresses bindings. She is Japanese, tall and willowy. She has porcelain skin and a fine-boned, idol-pretty face. Her glossy black hair is in a severe low bun. Her eyes are cool grey. Her charcoal guild coat is buttoned to the throat. She is stern, exact and by the book. Her grandmother lost her whole family in the War of Strings. Her report ledger on Elsie slowly becomes a ledger of every kind thing Elsie does. The story shows it only as Elsie sees it. She falls slowest of all, and hardest.
- Tamsin Reyes, 21, a Berserker who blacks out in her rages. Three parties have dropped her after she hurt a teammate. The last one dropped her that morning. She is Korean, slim and athletic, with a pretty Korean face. Her skin is sun-kissed golden, dusted with freckles. Her curls are wild and copper-red. Her eyes are green. She is loud, tough and quick to laugh. She is ashamed to the bone. Elsie's Tether is the first thing that has ever reached her inside the red.
- Ottilie Brandt, called Tilly, 20, an Alchemist. The Alchemists' College threw her out because her brews explode. She is Japanese, small and petite, with a sweet doll-like face and fair skin. Her blonde braids have singed ends. Her eyes are big and blue. Her goggles are pushed up in her hair. There is soot on one cheek. She is a chatterbox genius who is sure she is a disaster.
- Noor Haddad, 23, a Hexer. Her class bends bad luck toward whoever stands near her. So people step away from her in the street. She is Chinese, slender and long-limbed, with warm ivory skin. Her hair is long, straight and black. Her eyes are dark and kohl-lined. She is always gloved. She is dry, sharp and lonely. She is certain everyone leaves.
- Ines Calderón, 22, a Necromancer, the other feared class in the registry. She raises nothing larger than birds and mice. She keeps a little skeleton cat called Biscuit. She is Chinese, petite and delicate, with pale porcelain skin. Her hair is a sleek black bob. Her eyes are dark. She wears black lace. She is whisper-quiet and gentle. She is the first to understand what Elsie is up against.
- Amara Okafor, 22, a Bard with crippling stage fright. She sings only when she believes she is alone. Her mother is the most famous bard in the city. She is Korean, slim and of middling height, with warm honey-tan skin. Her hair is in long braids. Her eyes are warm brown. She is kind and funny. She is hiding.

## Home

Before long the party pools its coin. They rent a small, cozy house on a canal. It has a blue door. Its kitchen is big enough for everyone. Its garden is going wild. There are never quite enough beds. It becomes their home base. Elsie's plants sit on every sill. There is bread in the oven. There are maps on the table. Someone is always asleep on the sofa.

## Tone

The tone is warm, funny and tender. The story has real danger and real progression. It lives in the days as well as the delves. It lives in the guild hall, at quest boards, in markets and in kitchens. It lives in mending gear, counting coin, levelling and new skills. It lives in the small kindnesses Elsie keeps doing. The romance is a comedy of misunderstanding with an ache under it. Girl after girl falls for a girl who tells her she loves her and means it. She just does not mean it, it seems, like that. The wanting is everywhere on the page. It stays wanting.

Every character is an adult. No man is ever a love interest.
