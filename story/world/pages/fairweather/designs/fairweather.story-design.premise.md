A cozy LitRPG slice-of-life story and a yuri romantic comedy, told chapter by chapter, with Elsie Fairweather at its heart. It has all the sexual tension in the world between girls, and no sex.

## Elsie

Elsie Fairweather is about twenty: an adorable, innocent, optimistic cinnamon bun. She is a gorgeous young Korean woman with a K-pop idol's face and figure: a small V-line face, big sparkling doe eyes in soft rose pink, the same color as her hair, a small delicate nose, glossy pink lips, pale porcelain skin that blushes easily, and short soft rose-pink hair in a layered bob with side-swept bangs, held back on one side by a small pink-and-white flower clip. She is slim and slight. Her novice adventurer's outfit is a cropped cream halter top laced up the front, a short sage-green skirt, a brown leather belt with pouches, fingerless leather gloves, tall soft leather boots and a little healer's satchel.

She loves openly and out loud. She says "I love you!" to her friends freely and means it every time, holds hands, falls asleep on shoulders, and tells each girl, sincerely, that she is her favourite person. She moved constantly growing up, from town to town behind her mother, a wandering caravan healer who taught her simples and bandaging, and she never kept a friend long. So she reads the ache of wanting to be near someone as what friendship feels like, and she files every girl's love under "best friend".

She is not dim. Her quiet gift is noticing what people need: she remembers coffee orders, bad days and sisters' names, and turns up with soup. She names her houseplants, which travel with her in a crate. She bakes when she is nervous. She keeps a notebook of everyone's birthdays and favourite things. She cannot lie, cries easily, hums without knowing she is doing it, and is fiercely brave when a friend is threatened.

She is very slow to realise what the girls feel, for now. The reader is never quite sure how much is not-knowing and how much is Elsie choosing to love everyone the same.

## The world and the System

Lanternmere is a warm guild city of canals, arched bridges and paper lanterns on a lake, with bakeries, flower stalls, tea houses and noisy guild taverns. The world has a System. At twenty, everyone goes to the coming-of-age ceremony at the Hall of Naming and receives a class; the System opens a window in the air before them and names it. Classes have levels, levels come from experience, and skills come with levels. The System shows a person plain windows: name, class, level, experience, skills, and bonds. It shows bonds plainly, as names and numbers, and says nothing beyond what it tracks: it never explains, judges or advises. System windows appear in the prose as short boxed lines of text.

The Adventurers' Guild registers every adventurer, ranks them from F up to S, posts quests on its board, and licenses parties into the dungeons in and around the city: the Underbloom beneath the old flower market, the Glasswood out past the east gate, and deeper, older places. A new adventurer starts at F-rank and at level 1. Quests run from gathering moonbells and clearing cellar rats to escort work and real dungeon delves with real danger and real reward. Lanternmere is warm, though: people come back hurt and are mended, and nobody dies to make a chapter hit harder.

## The Enthraller

Elsie's class is Enthraller. The System says only this of it: a class that draws its power from bonds, from what people feel toward the caster.

It is the most feared class there is, and it is restricted. Every Enthraller on record used it to take: forced devotion, made puppets of people. The last one, Vesper Locke, started an old war, the War of Strings, sixty years ago, and the city still has its memorials. Since then every Enthraller is registered and monitored by the guild under the Strings Accord. There has not been a new one in living memory until Elsie.

Its skills have names that read either way, cold or warm:

- Captivate: seize a will, or hold someone's attention.
- Hold: restrain, or embrace.
- Keep: imprison, or cherish.
- Claim: take as property, or take as family.
- Tether: a leash, or a lifeline.
- Belong: "you belong to me", or "you belong here".
- Mark: a brand, or a promise to remember.
- Call: summon a servant, or call someone home.
- Devotion (passive): devotion taken, or devotion given.

Elsie starts at level 1 with Captivate and Tether; the rest come as she levels. In her hands the skills work in ways no one has seen. Captivate never compels: it makes someone feel seen. A Tether or a Claim is a pact the bound one can break at will, that pours strength into the bound rather than out of her, and no one has ever broken one. She can feel when someone bound to her is hurting. She is not deluded about the class: she finds the names awful, and uses the skills the way they seem to want to be used. The world argues over whether she found a hidden path, or whether every Enthraller before her used it wrong.

Each girl wonders, and sooner or later asks Elsie, whether what she feels for her is real or the class, and slowly learns that Elsie has never once used it to make anyone feel anything. The class may grow strong, because it feeds on devotion, and the girls around her are falling for her.

## The first day

The story opens on the morning of Elsie's ceremony. She arrived in Lanternmere a week ago with her satchel and her crate of plants, renting a narrow attic room above a bakery, because twenty is twenty wherever you are. In the Hall of Naming, in a line of nervous twenty-year-olds, the window opens before her and reads Enthraller. The hall goes silent. Elsie reads it twice, says something like "Oh! That sounds like a lot of responsibility," and smiles at the frightened priestess.

At the guild hall the registration clerk's pen stops. The guildmaster is sent for. Elsie is registered as an F-rank Enthraller under the Strings Accord, and the guild, which does not know what to do with her, assigns her a watcher: Corisande Vane, a stern young guild Warden who will report on every skill Elsie uses. Elsie thanks her for coming and asks what she likes in her tea.

No party will take an Enthraller, so Elsie goes to the corner of the party board where the cast-offs and misfits nobody else would take end up, and starts collecting them. Her first two are Tamsin and Tilly. By evening there are three names chalked on a party slate, an F-rank quest in hand, and a watcher who has not decided what to write.

## The cast

The girls are young adult women, gorgeous, feminine and slim, each with her own life, class, wants and troubles. No man is ever a love interest. Every girl in the cast is East Asian, Korean, Japanese or Chinese, with a K-pop or J-pop idol's pretty face, as Elsie is; her hair and eye colouring is her own, fantasy colouring on an East Asian face the way Elsie's pink is, and her build and height vary within slim. These labels are for the image model alone: in-world text tells her features and never names an Earth people or place. These are for the world builder to make her own and add to:

- Corisande Vane, called Cora, 24, the watcher: a guild Warden, the class that breaks and suppresses bindings. Japanese, tall and willowy, with porcelain skin and a fine-boned, idol-pretty face, glossy black hair in a severe low bun, cool grey eyes, a charcoal guild coat buttoned to the throat. Stern, exact, by the book; her grandmother lost her whole family in the War of Strings. Her report ledger on Elsie slowly becomes a ledger of every kind thing Elsie does, and the story shows it only as Elsie sees it. She falls slowest of all, and hardest.
- Tamsin Reyes, 21, a Berserker who blacks out in her rages; three parties have dropped her after she hurt a teammate, the last one that morning. Korean, slim and athletic, with a pretty Korean face, sun-kissed golden skin dusted with freckles, wild copper-red curls and green eyes. Loud, tough, quick to laugh, and ashamed to the bone. Elsie's Tether is the first thing that has ever reached her inside the red.
- Ottilie Brandt, called Tilly, 20, an Alchemist thrown out of the Alchemists' College because her brews explode. Japanese, small and petite, with a sweet doll-like face, fair skin, blonde braids with singed ends, big blue eyes, goggles pushed up in her hair, soot on one cheek. A chatterbox genius who is sure she is a disaster.
- Noor Haddad, 23, a Hexer whose class bends bad luck toward whoever stands near her, so people step away from her in the street. Chinese, slender and long-limbed, with warm ivory skin, long straight black hair, dark kohl-lined eyes, always gloved. Dry, sharp, lonely, and certain everyone leaves.
- Ines Calderón, 22, a Necromancer, the other feared class in the registry, who raises nothing larger than birds and mice and keeps a little skeleton cat called Biscuit. Chinese, petite and delicate, with pale porcelain skin, a sleek black bob, dark eyes, black lace. Whisper-quiet and gentle, and the first to understand what Elsie is up against.
- Amara Okafor, 22, a Bard with crippling stage fright who sings only when she believes she is alone; her mother is the most famous bard in the city. Korean, slim and of middling height, with warm honey-tan skin, long braids, warm brown eyes. Kind, funny, and hiding.

## Home

Before long the party pools its coin and rents a small, cozy house on a canal, with a blue door, a kitchen big enough for everyone, a garden going wild, and never quite enough beds. It becomes their home base: Elsie's plants on every sill, bread in the oven, maps on the table, someone always asleep on the sofa.

## Tone

Warm, funny and tender, with real danger and real progression. The story lives in the days as well as the delves: the guild hall, quest boards, markets, kitchens, mending gear, counting coin, levelling, new skills, and the small kindnesses Elsie keeps doing. The romance is a comedy of misunderstanding with an ache under it: girl after girl falls for a girl who tells her she loves her and means it, just not, it seems, like that. The wanting is everywhere on the page, and it stays wanting.

Every character is an adult, and no man is ever a love interest.
