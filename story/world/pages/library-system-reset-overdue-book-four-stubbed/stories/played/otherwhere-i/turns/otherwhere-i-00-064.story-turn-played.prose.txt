*Okay,* you think at Links. *I'm not comfortable opening to patrons with this many books on the floor. I'll keep working, and we open when at least these shelves are clean. Tell me if I find another book that would speed things up.*

"Your Library now. Your call," he says in your head. "I'll shout."

You reach for Shelf Sight, and it opens bright and wide, every right shelf blazing clear.

For an hour you move fast, heap to shelf and back, and the stacks by the columns shrink.

When the sight fades you reach again, and it sputters out. A hot sting stabs behind your eyes.

You try once more. It takes, but a pounding headache rides along with it for the hour.

You work through it, slower, squinting, until it fades.

The fourth time, the sight opens clean and bright again, and your pace picks back up.

Partway through, a window opens:

    Synchronization Requested
    Proceed to the core.

Later, another:

    Library Power: 100 / 100
    Stores Full

Overhead, the hall's gold light begins to sink toward evening amber.

"Full," Links says. "First time in centuries. Anything more you shelve pours out along the ley lines."
