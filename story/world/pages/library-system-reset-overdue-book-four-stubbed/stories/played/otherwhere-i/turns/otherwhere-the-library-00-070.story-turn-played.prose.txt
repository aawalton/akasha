*Okay, Links,* you think at him. *I'd like you to identify the books we've found that I should read to prepare for the opening.*

A pause, the kind he takes when his eyes flicker blue and he's searching through his shelves.

"First, and most needed: Counter Keeping," he says in your head. "A working text of my own magic."

"It teaches the Counter's lending. Without its power, the Counter won't lend a single book."

"You shelved it yourself yesterday, from the heaps by the columns. Low down, near the Counter."

"Some three hours of quiet reading."

"Second: Courtesies of the Many Peoples. A thick, plain guide to patrons' customs. No power in it."

"The whole of it takes days. The first part, on the commonest peoples, takes an afternoon."

"A golem shelved that one this morning. High up, on the west gallery."

"Of what's shelved in the hall so far, there's no book on healing."

"So for now, a patron who comes in hurt gets food and shelter. Nothing more."
