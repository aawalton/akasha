"So, you don't have a plan," you say. "Okay. How can we get you more power to wake up the kitchen without finishing off the big bookworm first?"

Links bristles. "I have plans. They all cost power I haven't got." His eyes flicker blue.

"Three more and I leave Emergency Power Mode. The alarm stops and the kitchen wakes."

"Those coils feed me once they're stored dry and safe for the night owls. Not left lying on the floor."

"The dead cooler in the break room is dry and tight. It'll keep them."

"And every book you put back on its right shelf gives me a little. Match the mark on the spine."

He flicks his tail at the books scattered all across the hall. "There are rather a lot of them."
