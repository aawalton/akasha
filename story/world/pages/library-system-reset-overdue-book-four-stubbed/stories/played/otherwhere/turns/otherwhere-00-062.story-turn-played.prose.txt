*What about dress and appearance?* you think at Links. *Are there any expectations for Librarians I need to meet?*

"The Library doesn't dress you," he says in your head. "Wear what you like."

"But that blue robe of yours? Many peoples out there know it on sight. It says Librarian."

"Skin's trickier. Every people draws its own line on how much should show."

"Covered and neat offends almost nobody. Your robe's both. Mostly."
