You go back down the spiral stair, round and round, into the round chamber and its dim blue-green glow.

This time the walk to the trunk is an ordinary walk. It comes closer with every step you take.

You lay both palms on the two knots of light, a shoulder's width apart, just as before.

Burning pours up your arms and through your veins, and the chamber falls away.

You see the Library's own halls. A kitchen, stirring awake. Wings sealed shut. Branches gone dark.

The burn fades, and the chamber comes back around you. You're still on your feet, and this time you aren't drained at all.

A window opens:

    Synchronization Complete.
