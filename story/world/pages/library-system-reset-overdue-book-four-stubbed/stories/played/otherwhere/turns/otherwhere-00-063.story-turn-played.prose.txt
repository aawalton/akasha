*Good enough,* you think at Links. *Where can I find some more books to shelve?*

"Where can't you?" he says in your head. "Pick a heap. Nearly three thousand of them left."

You climb the spiral staircase from the core chamber back up into the main hall.

The nearest heap sprawls at the foot of the first carved column past the counter.

You crouch, turn the first book over to find its faint spine mark, and match it to a low shelf.

The second goes two shelves up. The third, a fat volume, goes to a shelf at your shoulder.

As the third slides home, gold light washes over the shelves from behind you. You look round. Light is running up the Check-in Counter's carvings.

The carved trees glow gold, branch by branch, until every book-blossom and word-leaf is lit.

On the desk's top, a hand-shaped patch of light brightens, and holds.

A window opens:

    Task Complete: Restore the Check-in Counter
    Current Task: Reopen the Library. Serve a patron.
