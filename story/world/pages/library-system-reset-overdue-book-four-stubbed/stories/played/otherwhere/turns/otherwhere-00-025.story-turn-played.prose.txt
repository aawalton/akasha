You throw your whole weight forward and drive the bookworm down into the salt line.

You land across it, your fists still locked behind its mouth. Your torn arm shakes, but it holds.

It bucks under you, once, twice, shrieking into the salt. Then the shriek thins to a hiss.

Its skin shrinks and greys beneath you. The thrashing slows, and slows, and stops.

You stay on it, breathing hard, until what lies under you is a hard grey coil, alive and still.

Two dried coils lie beside you now in the ruined oval. Your left sleeve is dark with blood.

Beyond the columns, further back in the gloom, the wet chewing goes on.
