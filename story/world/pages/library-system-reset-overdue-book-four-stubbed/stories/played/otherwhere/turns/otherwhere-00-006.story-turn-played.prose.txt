"Uh," you say. "What packet? I was sitting in my house, and then I was here..."

Links stares at you. His ear tufts go flat against his skull.

Then his eyes flicker. Blue light runs through them in fine quick lines, one after another, like text scrolling past on a screen much too fast to read.

The flicker stops. He lets out a long breath through his nose.

"It didn't load," he says flatly. "The information packet. You got none of it. Not a word."

The runes on his flanks writhe, knotting and unknotting. "All this waiting," he says, "and the packet fails."

For a moment, under the irritation, he looks very tired. It's there in the set of his shoulders and the way his big head dips, and then it's gone again.

"You're here because your magical signature is compatible with my core," he says. "The first compatible signature I've found in four hundred sixty-eight years. So I pulled you."

"And you know nothing. Not one thing."

He shuts his eyes, and the runes on him slow.
