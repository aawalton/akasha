*Great,* you think at Links. *Do the shelvers need anything from me to get working? If not, I'll keep going until this section's done.*

"They're slow getting up," he says in your head. "Centuries asleep. They'll be up here by morning."

"Then they want you. A golem works only when its Librarian tells it to, out loud."

You go back to the heaps beside the counter, the right shelves still glowing faintly for you.

Most of the books go on the low shelves. For the high ones you roll over one of the hall's tall ladders.

Book after book slides home, and the hall's gold light edges brighter as you work.

The glow fades from the shelves just as you slide the last book from the counter's heaps into place.
