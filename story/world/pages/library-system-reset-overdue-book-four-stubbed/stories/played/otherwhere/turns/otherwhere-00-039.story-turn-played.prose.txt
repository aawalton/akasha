You pull on one of the blue wool robes. It pools around your feet until you hitch it up and cinch the belt tight round your waist.

The pouch hangs at your hip. The felt slippers are soft, and a little big.

You follow the smell of bread out of the quarters, past the Check-in Counter and into the main hall.

It leads you to an arched door on the hall's right side, and down a short corridor beyond it.

The corridor opens on a long, warm kitchen. Copper pots hang overhead. There's a great stone oven, and a long oak table.

On the oak table, fresh loaves are cooling, golden and still warm.

There's no one here, and no sign of who baked them.
