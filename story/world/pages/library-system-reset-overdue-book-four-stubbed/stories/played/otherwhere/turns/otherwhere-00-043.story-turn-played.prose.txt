You sidestep the heaving mouth and circle wide, toward its flank.

The blind head swings round with you, tracking you step for step.

You lunge for a grip, but it twists its bulk away, and you sprawl short.

The mouth snaps at you and closes on air. You scramble clear by inches.

You throw yourself at it again and land mid-body, well back from the head, and lock your arms round it as tight as you can.

Your salt crust grinds into the cracked hide. The worm shrieks and bucks as it burns.

It rolls to scrape you off, and its whole bloated bulk comes down on top of you.

The breath is crushed out of you, and the hall goes dark.

Through the dark, jaws close gently on your arm. Links. The floor slides by beneath you as he drags you.

You come round at the edge of the gloom, beside the honey jar. You try to get up, and your legs won't hold you.

Far back, the worm rolls and scrapes along the floor, grinding the salt off its hide.

Links stands over you, his fur bristling. "That cost me power I can't spare twice."
