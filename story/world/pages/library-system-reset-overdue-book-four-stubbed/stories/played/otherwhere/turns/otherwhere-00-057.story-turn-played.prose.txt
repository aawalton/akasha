You sit on the step of the counter's platform with Shelf Sight open on your knees, and read.

It's a working text, dense but clear. An hour slips by as you work through it, page by page.

Near the end the pages click into place, and something settles quietly behind your eyes.

A window opens in your sight:

    Power Gained: Shelf Sight

You reach for the sight, and a faint tug runs out of you.

Each spine shows its shelf at a glance, and each right shelf glows faintly across the hall.

You carry the first book straight to its glowing shelf, then the next. The rhythm is easy now.

As you slide the eighth book home, the hall's gold light brightens a shade.

Deep in the Library something grinds, slow and heavy, and begins to stir.

"Fifty," Links says in your head. "Your taps run hot now. And my golems are stirring, shelvers among them."
