*Three thousand is a lot,* you think at Links. *Is there any magic to make this faster? Telekinesis? Divination? ...Bookmancy? Even a library assistant?*

"Assistants? None. Not for centuries," he says in your head. "You are the whole staff."

"But linked to me, you can learn my own magic from my books, whatever your affinity."

"There is a book that teaches sending a book home to its shelf. It is in here somewhere."

A pause, the kind he takes when his eyes flicker blue, and then his voice comes back flatter. "Lost in the heaps. I know it's here. I can't tell you where."

"At fifty power my golems wake, and there are shelving golems among them."
