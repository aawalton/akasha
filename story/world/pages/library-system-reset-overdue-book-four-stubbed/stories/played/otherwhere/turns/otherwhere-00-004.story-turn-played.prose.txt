You lay your other hand on the trunk, over the second light.

Both knots flare under your palms at once, white-blue and blinding.

Then the pain comes. It burns up through your hands like hot wire drawn along your veins, into your wrists, your arms, across your chest, and up behind your eyes. Your hands stay flat on the trunk. You can't tell anymore whether you're holding on or being held.

And the chamber is gone.

Threads of blue light run out ahead of you, away from you, through a dark too big to count, and there's no end to them in any direction. They meet and cross and knot together, and where they knot there are worlds, worlds you've never seen, bright beads strung out across the black. You don't picture them. You've never pictured anything. But they're there, they're in you somehow, as sure as the pain.

Many of the threads are thin, barely there, faint as a hair in bad light. Some have gone dark entirely, cut lines trailing off into the black, and the beads at their ends are dim.

And at the center of all of it you feel something. Vast. Old. Tired, so very tired, the way a person is tired at the end of a year that should have ended long ago.

The pain ebbs, and the visions go with it, together, like a tide pulling out. The red comes back first, then the honk of the alarm, then the soft floor under your knees.

You're kneeling at the foot of the trunk. Your hands have slid down its face and rest on the grey just above the floor. Your arms are trembling. Your head swims, and you're wrung out, emptied, as if something has drunk you dry.

Your palms, where they rest against the grey, are pale and whole, with no burn on them, and nothing in you is broken. There's only the ache, draining out of your veins the way a cramp lets go.

Above you the trunk's veins have stopped flickering. They burn a steady blue, every line of them, all the way up into the dark.

The alarm still honks overhead. The red light hasn't changed.

A panel of pale blue light opens before your eyes, and the voice speaks it inside your skull.

Librarian Link: Tentative

Power Drain: Halted

Status: Emergency Power Mode

Operational: 12%

The panel stays. It hangs in the air before your eyes, and when you turn your head, it turns with you.
