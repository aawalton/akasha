*Links,* you think at him, *how long until the main floor is cleared at this rate?*

"Some two thousand eight hundred and eighty loose books still lie across the hall," he says in your head.

"Each shelver manages about twenty a day. Forty between them: seventy-two days, give or take."

"A little over two months, Librarian. They don't tire, but they don't hurry either."

By the Counter, the first golem's arm folds back down, empty. Its first book sits on the high shelf.
