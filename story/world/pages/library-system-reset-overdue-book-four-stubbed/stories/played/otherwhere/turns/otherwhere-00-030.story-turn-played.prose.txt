You stay on the pinned bookworm until it shudders under you and dries into a hard grey coil, alive and still.

You roll it aside, over by the other coils. What's left of the heap is thin now, barely enough for one more.

You walk out between the columns toward the last of the wet chewing, deep in the gloom.

The last small bookworm is feeding about thirty feet short of the dark back steps.

At twenty feet it rears up and roars its challenge at you. Then it leaves its heap and follows you back.

At the heap you scoop up fistfuls of salt and hold out your arm. It lunges, and you snatch the arm clear.

Your salted fists close round its neck, and you drive it down into the last of the heap.

It shrieks and bucks, its skin puckering and greying, while the salt sprays thin around the two of you.

The heap is nearly gone beneath it, bare floor showing through, and it is not yet dry.
