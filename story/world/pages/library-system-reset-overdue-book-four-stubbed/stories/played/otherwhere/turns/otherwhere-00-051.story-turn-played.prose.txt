*Do it,* you think at Links. *Just let me wash off the worst of the mess first.*

In the bathroom you scrub yourself under the cold tap, shivering.

The honey comes off in sticky sheets. The salt stings every scrape and scar.

Then you fill the deep stone tub with cold water. *Ready.*

The water stirs. Warmth spreads out from the bottom of the tub, and steam curls up off the surface.

Steam fogs the little bathroom, and hot water laps at the stone rim by your cold, bare knees.
