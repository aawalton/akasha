*Fine,* you think at Links. *I'll focus on shelving. But could you at least tell me if I touch that book, or another one that would make this faster?*

"Your hand lands on one, I'll know that instant," he says in your head. "I'll tell you. Loudly."

You start on the heaps just beside the counter, dragging the scattered books together into loose stacks.

It's the rhythm of your home library back on Earth, thousands of books sorted and resorted more times than you can count.

You squint at the faint mark on each cracked spine and start splitting the stacks into rough piles. Sort this patch, carry each pile to its shelf, then on to the next patch of floor.

Partway through, you pick up a slim, plain-bound book, and Links's voice cuts in.

"That one! That's Shelf Sight."

"Read it, and one glance at any spine tells you where it goes. No more squinting at marks."
