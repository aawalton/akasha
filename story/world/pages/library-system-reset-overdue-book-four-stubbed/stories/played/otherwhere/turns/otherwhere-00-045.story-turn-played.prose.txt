You haul yourself up by the honey jar and get the last sack into your arms.

Running is beyond you. You stagger toward the worm, every step jolting your battered body.

It swings its gagging mouth toward you, and you shove the sack at it.

The sack bursts short, in the front of its mouth. Salt sprays everywhere, and the worm reels back from it.

You turn to get away, as fast as you can manage, but its heavy head swings round and slams into you.

You go down hard, and the hall goes dark again.

Through the dark, teeth close on you, deep.

A shriek, and the teeth tear loose. Something heavy drags itself away across the floor, back toward the gloom.

You come round on the floor, too weak to rise. A deep fresh bite is bleeding into the salt.

Links crouches over you, his fur flat, watching the gloom where the worm has gone.
