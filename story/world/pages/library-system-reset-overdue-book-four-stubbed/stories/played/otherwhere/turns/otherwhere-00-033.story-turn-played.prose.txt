"Okay," you say. "We'll start there." The coils first, you mean, and then the books.

In the break room you empty out the dead cooler, a knee-high chest, and drag it scraping out into the hall.

You keep well out from the back steps, listening the whole way. The dark back there stays quiet.

The coils weigh only a few pounds each. You carry them two at a time, hard and grey and still, and pack all five in.

Then you drag the loaded cooler back to the break room and shut the lid on them.

A tone rings out through the hall. The alarm you'd stopped hearing falls silent.

A window opens in your vision:

    Status: Emergency Power Mode Ended
    Power: 37%
    Kitchen: Waking

Somewhere off the hall, doors unseal with a long sigh.

The window adds a line, blinking:

    Synchronization Requested. Return to the core.
