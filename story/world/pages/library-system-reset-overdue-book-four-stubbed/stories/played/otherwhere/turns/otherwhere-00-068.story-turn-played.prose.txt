"You must be the shelving helpers," you call from the passage. "Go ahead and work on shelving all the unshelved books on the main floor, starting from the Counter."

The two stay wordless. Each folds at the waist in a slow, creaking bow.

They turn together and step down to the heaps nearest the Counter, their joints ticking.

One stoops and gathers up a book in long jointed fingers. It turns the book over, as if reading its spine.

Its arm unfolds, and unfolds again, up past the reach of the ladders to a high shelf.

It is slow, careful work. The first book is still rising as the second golem stoops.
