*Okay,* you think at Links. *That pile's done. What's next?*

Before he can answer, a low hum rises up through the hall from the core below.

A window opens in your sight:

    Synchronization Requested
    Proceed to the core.

"Ah. That," Links says in your head. "The Library's been waiting for you to earn another one."

"Go down and sync, and it'll show you what it wants from you next. Tasks, in its own words."

"Or ignore it and keep shelving. It will keep asking."
