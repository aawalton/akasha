You crouch to lift the big worm. Dried, it's a hard grey coil as wide as a cartwheel.

It must weigh sixty pounds. You can't get it off the floor, but it tips up onto its edge, and rolls.

You roll it down the hall between the columns and into the break room.

It's far too wide for the cooler, so you set it down beside it, where the room is dry.

Heading back toward your quarters, you think at Links: *Can we afford hot water yet? I really need a hot shower.*

His voice answers in your head. "There's no shower. There's the tub."

"The taps run hot once I reach fifty. Till then I can heat you one tubful, for a point of power."
