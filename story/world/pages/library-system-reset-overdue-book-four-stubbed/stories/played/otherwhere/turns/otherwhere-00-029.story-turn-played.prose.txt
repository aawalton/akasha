You scoop two fistfuls of salt from what's left of the heap and step toward the coiled bookworm, one arm held out in front of you.

It takes the bait. It lunges at your outstretched arm, and you whip the arm back, and its teeth close on air.

Before it can draw back, you clamp both salted fists round its neck, just behind the mouth.

Its skin puckers dry under your grip. You throw your weight forward and drive it down into the heap.

Salt sprays up round it. It shrieks and bucks, shrinking and greying fast.

You lie across it, fists locked, as the thrashing weakens under you.
