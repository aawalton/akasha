You snatch up the last fresh sack while the worm is still gagging and spitting salt.

Its mouth hangs open as it retches, and it's slow to pull away.

You drive the sack in with both arms and keep shoving, past the teeth, deep into its gullet.

Then you let go and throw yourself back as the worm convulses.

It bucks once, twice. Then the thrashing slows, and its bloated body begins to shrink.

Its grey hide puckers and tightens as it dries, until the huge worm lies still, curled and hard.

It's alive, but helpless.

The back of the hall falls quiet. The chewing has stopped, and the roaring with it.

Overhead, the hall's gold light brightens another shade.
