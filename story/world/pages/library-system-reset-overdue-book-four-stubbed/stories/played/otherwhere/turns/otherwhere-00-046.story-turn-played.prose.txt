"Not enough, eh?" you tell Links. "I guess I'm sleeping here tonight. I'll try again in the morning."

You close your eyes where you lie, in the scatter of salt from the burst sack. The lights dim to a low amber as you drift off.

Now and then a roar rolls out of the gloom, restless, but it comes from no nearer.

Each roar half wakes you. Each time you drift off again.

Then the lights brighten into morning. You wake stiff and sticky, but whole. The bite has knitted to a scar.

Far back in the gloom, the worm is chewing again.
