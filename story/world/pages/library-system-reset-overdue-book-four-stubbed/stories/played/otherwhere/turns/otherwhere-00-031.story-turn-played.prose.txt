Under your hands the last small bookworm shudders, and dries into a hard grey coil.

Around you, the hall's gold light brightens another shade.

You roll the coil over beside the others. "Okay, Links. The small ones are done, but we're out of salt. How do we deal with the big one?"

Links pads over, his eyes flickering blue. "Salt still burns it," he says. "It just ploughs through a line anyway."

"And it's far too big to hold down in a heap, even if you had a heap."

"The kitchen stores sacks of salt. The kitchen's asleep until I've more power to wake it."

"I can rake it with my claws, but every swipe costs power I've barely got."

He looks at your blood-dark sleeve. "And it bites a great deal harder than those did."
