You let the shorts go. They slide down your legs on their own and puddle on the soft floor around your ankles, a black ring of fabric that makes no sound as it lands.

The compression tights underneath were never meant for these legs. They hang loose, wrinkled at the knees and bunched in soft folds at the ankles, and the waistband keeps sliding down your hips, wanting a hitch every little while. Still, they're easier than the shorts ever were.

You step out of the light blue slip-ons, one and then the other, and stand in your socks. The heels of the socks sag down past your own heels. The floor is cool through the cotton and gives a little under your weight, springing back as you shift.

And somehow, standing there in the red light with your hair down your back and the grey shirt hanging to your thighs, you're at ease. More at home in this body than you ever were in the old one. You couldn't say why. It just fits, the way the clothes don't.

"Okay," you say aloud, "I guess we're implementing isekai protocol. Status? Character Sheet? System?"

The chamber throws it back at you. "System... system... system..." Each one fainter, rolling away round the curve of the far walls, until the last of them is gone.

The air in front of your eyes stays empty. The pale blue panel stays gone, and the voice that spoke inside your skull keeps its silence.

You try to picture yourself, to call up the shape of this new body and see whether the box answers to it. Behind your eyes it's dark, the same dark it has always been, with your total aphantasia. There's no image to hold up.

The air before you stays as bare as it was.

Overhead the alarm keeps up its double honk, again and again, as steady as if the room never heard you at all.

Then, on the side of the grey trunk facing you, the blue veins stop flickering.

They steady first, then brighten, then hold. The lit lines run down the trunk's face toward the floor on your side and gather there, at about the height of your hand, into a knot of light under the smooth grey surface. They pulse, slow and even, in time with the hum. All round the rest of the trunk the veins still stutter in and out, but that one patch burns steady.

The hum deepens. You feel it come up through your socks, into your heels and up your shins, and the trunk's warmth reaches you across the floor like sun off a stone wall.
