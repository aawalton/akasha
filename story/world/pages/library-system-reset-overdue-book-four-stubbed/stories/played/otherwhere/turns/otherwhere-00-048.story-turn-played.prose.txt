You leave the sack on the floor and run for the gloom's edge.

The worm heaves along after you, nosing at your honey smell, but it's slower than you can run.

You snatch up one of the two fresh sacks and turn as it comes at you.

It arrives mouth first. You commit, and drive the sack straight at its throat.

At the last instant it jerks its head away. The sack tears on its teeth and bursts in its mouth.

Salt sprays over its lips and tongue. The worm bucks, gagging and shrieking.

It lunges back at you blindly. You throw yourself sideways, and it hits only floor.

The worm reels, spitting salt, burned but not dry, its body still heaving.
