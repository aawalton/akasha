You yawn and stretch under the covers, then climb out of the wide bed, naked.

The amber in the quarters has brightened to morning gold along the panelled walls.

You open the door onto the short passage, and the creak comes louder from the hall ahead.

At the passage's end, beside the Check-in Counter, two tall shapes wait in the gold light.

They are thin and faceless, pale oak and brass, their long jointed arms hanging at their sides.

Both stay where they stand, turned to the Counter and still, as if waiting on a word.

One tilts its blank head toward the passage with a slow creak of wood and brass.
