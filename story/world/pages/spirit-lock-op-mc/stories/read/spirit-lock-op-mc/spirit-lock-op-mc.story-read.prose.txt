After defying fate and nearly becoming a god, Hayden is offered a chance to return to the past for a second chance at godhood. But it isn’t without cost: if he dies before collecting his soul, he'll lose it forever. 

He accepts without a second thought. A second chance is all he needs, and nothing will stop him from becoming a god. Not causality, not the demigods who look down upon him, and certainly not the rich, arrogant students at the school he starts out at. 

Teenagers, galactic leaders—demigods—they’re all the same. No matter who Hayden faces, he’ll teach them the profound difference experience makes—all five thousand years of it.

Note: This book is exactly as it's designed to be: an OP, self-insert Gary Sue crushing life during every single chapter. If you love some good trash fiction like I do, you'll enjoy it. It's escapism, not high fiction. So just enjoy it.
