The blade is the loom. The world is its thread.

Kale’s ordinary life ends when a botched ritual pulls him into a world of ancient magic and unimaginable dangers. Armed with a dagger once wielded by a powerful goddess, he’s thrust into the heart of a conflict far beyond his understanding. With each battle, Kale unlocks new powers, but every victory brings him closer to those who seek to control or destroy him.

By his side is Liliana, a cunning blood mage stuck as a severed head, and a growing band of unlikely allies. Together, they face fanatical cults, sinister schemes, and the looming shadow of a god bent on unmaking the world.

Bound by blood, secrets, and a blade that could alter the fate of all, Kale and his allies must fight, adapt, and trust one another in a world where survival is never guaranteed, and failure means the end of everything.

Destiny is not written, it is woven in steel.
