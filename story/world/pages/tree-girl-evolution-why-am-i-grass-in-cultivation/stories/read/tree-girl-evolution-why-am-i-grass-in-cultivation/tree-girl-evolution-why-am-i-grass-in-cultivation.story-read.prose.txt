She’s a grass. She’s a girl. And she’s going to evolve.

Aria woke up with roots instead of legs, leaves instead of arms, and sunlight making her feel way too good.

Worse? She keeps getting stepped on.

Armed with a Daily Sacrifice System and “photosynthetic Qi absorption,” she just has to survive long enough to evolve—while herbivores snack on her, bandits eye her for lumber, and cultivators can’t decide if she’s a sacred treasure or really expensive firewood.

What to expect:

🌱 No Humanoid Evolution. ONLY TREE.
🌱 Slice-of-life tree shenanigans.
🌱 Cultivation, but with roots.
🌱 Do trees nap? Because I nap.
🌱 A sarcastic, leafy protagonist with too many internal monologues.
