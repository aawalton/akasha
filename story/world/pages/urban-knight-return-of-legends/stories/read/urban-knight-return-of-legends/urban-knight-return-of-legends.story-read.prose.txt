IN HIATUS.

What happens when a knight wakes up in the modern world?

 Ascalon Rendland, legendary knight, fell during an epic struggle with the demonic army. But at the brink of defeat, he woke up in a modern world.

Needing to find direction, he accompanies Lilithra, a cheerful girl with a sad past, and Selorien, a noble elf, as they face against interdimensional threats, dungeons, or even what kind of capuccino to have.

All the while, he seeks answers to why he suddenly awakened in this strange world while unseen enemies gather in the shadows.

What to expect? I like writing what I like reading, so expect:

• Wholesome moments.
• MCs being cool.
• Both slow slice of life and ramped up action!
• Funny shenanigans!
