He was one step from the peak of science: Eleven years. One equation. One unit conversion he’d already checked four times. That was all it took to end Barry’s career, along with his life and, for a brief moment, he was almost relieved.

Then the famous blue screen popped up in front of his eyes.

[You have been chosen for the role of Multiversal Engineer. Henceforth, your duty is to save the universes from their ultimate demise.]

He didn't like that.

[Your destination: Asgard]

He didn't like that even more.

But what could he do? The System did what it does best and dropped him into a fantasy world where the gods were waiting for some fierce, world-ending warrior to fix all their problems without breaking a sweat. In a city where everyone has a title, they got a guy named Barry who literally thinks in spreadsheets, and all he wanted was a peaceful place to chill. The System says the job covers every universe, but in practice the work arrives through Asgard, one divine disaster at a time. 

He didn't mind the gods at first. Then the Allfather started steering him toward Ragnarok. The god of hammers wouldn't stop tailing him. The mistress of mischief helped herself to his work every time he turned his back. He warned them the Bifrost wouldn't hold, and they were surprised when it didn't. Every crisis he solved got replaced by a bigger one, because the gods kept dumping new world ending problems in his lap.

So yes, this is the story of Barry, the smartest man alive, who couldn't even retire. He's sworn he will anyway. When he finally gets his perfect retirement, every god, demon, angel, and whatever else roams the multiverse is getting the finger.
