Archie Gracefield was an average computer engineer, who was just about to head to the gym on a Saturday afternoon when the integration of the 72nd cycle began. Introducing Archie's universe into the multiverse, where power is a necessity to protect your freedoms from those who wish to take them. And take it they shall.

Archie delves headfirst into the multiverse, choosing to forgo the Group Tutorial over the Solo Tutorial. Now, he must fight tooth and nail to survive in a world where roided-up squirrels are a common sight, and gargantuan creatures are deemed as juveniles.

Schedule: 3x a week (Sat, Mon, Wed)
