In her quest to prove that magic is real, 24 year old Alice deliberately got herself isekai’ed into a world full of magic.

She gets to make friends with people who can heal with faith, break stone with their fists, and conjure fireballs from their hands.

If you like a character-driven story with a lot of world-building and multiple complex magic systems, this story is for you.

A new chapter of How To Magic every Tuesday and Friday at 2:10pm est, 19:10 gmt.
