Being reincarnated as a baby is inconvenient. Being reincarnated as a magical genius? Now we're talking.

Luka has a second chance at life, a brand-new System, and a ridiculous talent for magic. There's just one problem: he's a baby.

But he's not going to let that stop him.

While other children are learning to walk, Luka is inventing spells and trying to break reality. With loving parents and a growing collection of friends, who can't quite believe what they're witnessing, he's living his best life.

Beyond the shores of his home island, lies a vast world of ancient magical ruins, dangerous mana beasts and powerful noble houses. A beautiful and mysterious world awaits Luka.

But first, he might need to learn to crawl before he can fly.

No grimdark misery here. Just big magic energy.

WHAT TO EXPECT:

+ PROGRESSION

+ Overpowered Protagonist

+ Wholesome parents that expands to a wholesome found family

+ Nerding out about magic

+ Optimistic and exciting adventure
