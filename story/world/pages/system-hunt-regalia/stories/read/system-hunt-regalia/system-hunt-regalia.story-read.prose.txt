The mysterious message on Sam's battered phone seemed like a bizarre prank—until one of the Doom Towers, the indestructible monoliths that have terrorized the world for a year, began hurtling toward his city.

Now, Sam is thrust into a war that history has long forgotten, where ancient powers clash and the veil between fact and fiction blurs. With a newfound command over Fractured Glass, Sam must quickly master a deadly game where survival means making impossible choices. To be a hero, he may have to hunt several first.

Future Diary inspired LitRPG, expect murderous competition.
