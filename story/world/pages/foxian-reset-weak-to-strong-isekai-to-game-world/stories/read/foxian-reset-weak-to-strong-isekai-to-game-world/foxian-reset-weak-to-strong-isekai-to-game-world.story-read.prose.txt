After spending ten years playing the same online game, everything had grown stale for the overpowered veteran of Keostar Online. That was until a chance encounter with the, by the lore Goddess, Rexia, destroyed their OP character and forced them into the game they’ve only seen 
through a screen.

Now reincarnated in Keostar as a Foxian named Kae Loredom, Kae made a new goal for themselves. Get stronger than ever before and overthrow Rexia to become the new God of Keostar… Even though Foxian’s are arguably the weakest race of the game…

Oh, and don’t die in the process. Easy enough for someone who has played this game for ten years, right?

A weak-to-strong, character-driven, progression isekai fantasy. A story about rediscovering a long-forgotten passion lost to time and isolation.
