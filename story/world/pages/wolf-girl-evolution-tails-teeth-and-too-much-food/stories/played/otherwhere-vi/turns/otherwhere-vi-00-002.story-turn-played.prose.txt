"Okay," you say quietly, to yourself. The word comes out light and soft, in the new voice. "This is definitely not Earth."

You hold your attention on yourself, on the small cold body sitting in the moss, and try the words one at a time.

"Isekai protocol. System?"

The hollow stays exactly as it was. The two moons hang where they hung, and the water runs on over its stones.

"Status?"

Faint letters appear in the air before your eyes, pale and steady. You turn your head, and they turn with you, holding their place in your sight like writing on the inside of a pane of glass.

【Name: Nala】 【Level: 1/10】 【Race: Human (Tier 0)】 【Gender: Female】

【Class: —】 【HP: 30/30】 【SP: 19/28】 【MP: 5/5】

【Strength: 3】 【Dexterity: 4】 【Vitality: 3】 【Intelligence: 9】 【Willpower: 6】

【Charisma: 3】 【Luck: 2】

【Active Skills: none】 【Passive Skills: none】 【Traits: none】

It already has your name. Nala, the name you took for yourself when you woke up here.

"Character Sheet?" you say, with the letters still hanging there. "Menu?"

The letters hold still. Every line stays where it was, the list the same length as before.

You stop holding them in mind, and the letters thin and are gone.

The cold kept on the whole time you were reading. Your toes have gone numb. The seat of your tights is soaked through from the moss, and your breath smokes in the silver light. A shiver runs through you, and you can't make it stop.

At the east edge of the hollow, the stream still runs over its flat grey stones. To the north, where the howling came from, the forest is silent.
