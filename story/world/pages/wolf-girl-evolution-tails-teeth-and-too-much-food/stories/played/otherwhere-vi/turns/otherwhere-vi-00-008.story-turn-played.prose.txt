You crouch by the shrubs, stiffly, your hip complaining the whole way down, and pick one small red berry.

You hold it close to your eyes in the moonlight and look at it hard, the way you would look at anything you wanted a status for. The air around it stays empty.

"Inspect," you say.

The berry sits in your fingers.

"Appraise."

The air stays empty.

"Identify."

Still the same. No letters, no brackets. Just a small, hard, shiny red berry in your cold fingers.

You put it in your mouth and bite down.

It is sharp and sour, enough to pucker your whole mouth, with a faint bitterness at the end and a lot of small seeds that crunch between your teeth.

You swallow it. "Status."

The faint letters come up before your eyes, as they did before.

【Name: Nala】 【Level: 1/10】 【Race: Human (Tier 0)】 【Gender: Female】

【Class: —】 【HP: 19/30】 【SP: 19/28】 【MP: 5/5】

【Strength: 3】 【Dexterity: 4】 【Vitality: 3】 【Intelligence: 9】 【Willpower: 6】

【Charisma: 3】 【Luck: 2】

【Active Skills: none】 【Passive Skills: none】 【Traits: none】

The only line that has changed is your HP, lower than it was in the hollow. The berry is not on it anywhere.

You wait, crouched, the letters hanging in front of you. Your stomach stays quiet. The list stays exactly as it is.

You let the status go, and the letters thin and vanish.

The shrubs around you are thick with the same berries, whole clusters of them hanging dark under the leaves.

A gust comes down the valley and goes straight through the wet shirt, and your teeth rattle.
