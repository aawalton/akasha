The last thing you remember is your back window. A book open on your knee, the evening going blue outside the glass, your eyes getting heavy.

Then cold. Cold and damp, pressing into your back, and a smell so strong it is almost a taste: pine sap, wet earth, moss.

You open your eyes.

Branches, black against a sky that is not the right colour for night. Too bright. Silver light comes down between the pine trunks and lies across everything in long pale bars.

You are lying on your back in moss. Deep moss, soft and soaked, cushioning you like a mattress left out in the rain. You sit up, and your weight goes the wrong way, and you catch yourself on hands that land short of where you meant them to.

You look at the hands. Small. Pale. Freckled across the backs, with narrow wrists and thin fingers that close when you tell them to.

A long fall of dark red hair slides over your shoulder and into your lap.

You are still wearing your shirt, the dark grey one, but it hangs off you now, the hem down around your thighs, the collar slipping off one shoulder. Your black tights are there too, snug to the ankle. No shoes. Your bare feet in the moss are small and pale and already cold.

You say something. It comes out in a woman's voice.

The hollow you are sitting in is a shallow dip among old pines, their trunks straight and tall, the lowest branches far above your head. Big grey boulders ring the edge of it, furred with moss. On one side, water is running over stones, quick and quiet.

You look up to find where the light is coming from.

There are two moons.

One is large and pale, nearly full, sitting just above the treetops. The other is smaller, higher, and faintly blue, like a coin seen through water. Neither of them is going anywhere.

Your breath shows in the air. The cold is coming up through the wet moss into your legs.

Somewhere off to the north, far away through the trees, a single long howl rises into the night. It holds, and falls away.

Then another voice joins it. Then another. Then many, together, until the whole dark forest to the north is singing.

It goes on for a long time. When it stops, the silence after it is very large.

The water keeps running over its stones.
