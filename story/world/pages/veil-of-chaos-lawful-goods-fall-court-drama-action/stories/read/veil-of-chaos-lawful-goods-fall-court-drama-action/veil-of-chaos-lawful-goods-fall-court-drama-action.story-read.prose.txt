Family. Betrayal. Survival.
In a LitRPG world where bonds matter more than power.

The Story

Nine Greater Deities wage eternal war through their God-forged Champions—seventeen reincarnates per god, bound to one of the Seventeen Classes. A cycle of reincarnation and betrayal that fuels the Apotheosis War.

Damon never asked to be a Champion of Titania. He only wanted to save his family.
But when he awakens as a Dark Elf prince in a kingdom that executes his kind on sight, survival becomes a game of stats, skills, and betrayal.

His sister has become a lich.

His girlfriend was reborn as a dungeon boss.

And his Unity Tamer class doesn’t grow through domination, but through soulbonds.

In a world where cruelty and corruption are rewarded, choosing family over power may be the deadliest path of all.
