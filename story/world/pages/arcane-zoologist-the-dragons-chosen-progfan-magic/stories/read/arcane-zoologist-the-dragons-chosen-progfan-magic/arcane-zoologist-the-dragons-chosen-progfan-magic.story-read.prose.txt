Calluna is a village girl who one day will bring dragons back to the world.

Obsessed with arcane beasts, young Calluna has become an outcast in a village filled with monster hunters. 

However, one day after saving a glimmerhare, Callie stumbles upon a dragon, a creature believed to be long extinct. As if it wasn't groundbreaking enough, the dragon blesses her with magic. Her life is never the same.

Follow the journey of Calluna, how she grows from a small, naive girl into a full-fledged arcane zoologist with the kind of magic no human has ever witnessed before.

What to expect:

- Slow burn (from childhood to adulthood)

- Clear magic progression 

- Weak to Strong

- Arcane beast discoveries and mysteries

- Real stakes with cozy moments
