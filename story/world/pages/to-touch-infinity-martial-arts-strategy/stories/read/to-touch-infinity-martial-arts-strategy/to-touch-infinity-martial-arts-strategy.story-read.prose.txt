They defy the heavens, but she defies the odds.

The young girl walks with a fluid grace across the blood-soaked fields of the Jinan plains. Her emerald scholar’s robes and calm demeanor look completely out of place amongst the throngs of warring soldiers, still actively fighting and dying in the mud. Unfazed by the violence all around her, she drifts across the battlefield like a jade phantom – a lost soul wandering amidst the hurricane of clashing steel.

But her travel is far from aimless. And everywhere she steps, the impossible happens.

Spears intended for her heart find only empty air. Gaps appear in previously impenetrable lines, as if from nowhere. Orderly ranks of troops dissolve into calculated chaos. And all the while, the heavy executioners of the Tai-shan sect follow in her wake, eager to reap their harvest of human lives.

The apparent miracle is a result of careful choreography, built upon a framework of troop formations, logistics, and probability. Chae-ha controls it because she alone has realized the truth:

War is just math. With consequences.
