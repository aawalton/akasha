Defy an impossible fate, be reforged, earn a new life Atlas was a man with a dark past and even darker prospects for his future. Life was grim and bleak. Then, he is pulled into a place called the Void, and told he is a player. Asked to choose a difficulty from a wide-ranging scale, he sees this as a chance to restart and finally be someone. But, fate seems to have other plans, and he is given the 'Impossible' difficulty for his tutorial.
