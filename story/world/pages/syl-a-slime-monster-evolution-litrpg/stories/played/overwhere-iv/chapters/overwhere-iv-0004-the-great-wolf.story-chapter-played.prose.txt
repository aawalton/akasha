At the gatehouse you find Brenna Holt and tell her plainly: the practice spear broke on a beast.

Holt looks at the head. Then she looks at it a while longer.

"Thirty copper from stores, a spear," she says. "One broken on that? Fifteen, and well spent."

You pay the fifteen. Behind Holt, the gate guard is already calling the others out to look.
