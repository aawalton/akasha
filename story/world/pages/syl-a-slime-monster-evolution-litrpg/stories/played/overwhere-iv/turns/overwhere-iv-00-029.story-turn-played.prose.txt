You go to the hearth. "Ready to go. I'll fight with you as well, if you'll have me. I'm inexperienced, but I can still guard a flank. Since we don't know exactly how many there are, could be good to have an extra spear along."

Dace grins, broad and glad. "Gladly. Left flank, beside Wren."

The hooded woman nods once.

The one in the red coat mutters into his collar, just loud enough. "Recruit's going to get someone killed."

At noon the five of you cross the footbridge. At Hobb's far wall you show them the gap.

Wren crouches over the tracks and the dried blood for a long moment. "Trail. Going in. Fresh enough."

You go into the Tangle. Oak and yew, close and dark. Wren slips ahead, then Dace with his shield. The red coat and Orla come behind. You take the left, spear low, a few paces off Wren's shoulder.

The blood trail runs on. A spot here. A smear on a root there. Half a mile. Then most of a mile.

The brush is thick. Five people make noise in it, however careful. Twigs. A shield on a branch.

Ahead, the ground drops toward a hollow, and a huge fallen oak lies across it like a wall.

Wren stops and lifts a hand. Everyone freezes. The wood has gone very quiet.

Then you feel it, within your few paces. A body crouched behind the yew on your left, shifting its weight.

Before you can speak, something whirs in the hollow ahead, and a stone cracks off Dace's shield.
