"Deal," you say. "I have a lot of growing to do. Rather do it with someone I can trust anyways."

Ilsa holds your eyes a long moment. Then she puts out her ink-stained hand, and you shake on it. Her grip is firm.

"Then it's sworn. I keep my word, Nala. I expect the same." She lets go. "I don't forgive the other thing."

She opens the drawer under the counter and takes out a folded page, unsealed. As it comes out, you glimpse your own name written on it.

She takes a fresh sheet, dips her pen, and writes right there on the counter where you can see every word. The new report is short: an old cracked crystal, a faint odd light, and nothing more. It doesn't name a colour, or say the room bent, or give your name. Ilsa blots it, folds it, and seals it with a drop of wax.

Then she walks the first page down the hall to the hearth and drops it on the coals. It curls, and catches.

The hooded woman sits a pace from the fire, and watches it burn.

Ilsa comes back dusting her fingers. "Goes east on the next cart. Now." She taps a notice pinned by the counter. "My board. Hobb farm, on the Tangle's edge. Overrun with pest slimes. Three copper a core, and a hot dinner at Hobb's table. He wants every last one gone."

She reaches under the counter and sets down a pair of old leather gloves, worn soft and a little big.

"From the hall's box of things left behind. Wear them, the jelly stings. And mind the wolves."
