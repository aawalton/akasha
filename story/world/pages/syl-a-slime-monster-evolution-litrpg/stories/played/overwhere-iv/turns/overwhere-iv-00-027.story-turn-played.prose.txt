It stays open. You walk back up the field with it glowing at the edge of sight.

Hobb and Pim meet you at the orchard with a lantern. Bran bristles and sniffs at your boots.

You tell them plainly. Goblins, not wolves. Two of them. One badly hurt, and both driven off into the Tangle.

Hobb goes grey. He sits down on the orchard wall. "Goblins. At my sheep." He looks past you at the dark trees.

He digs in his coat and presses a silver into your hand. "Mine. Not the hall's. For the sheep."

"They'll be back, with more. The hall has to know tonight." He won't hear otherwise.

But first, at his door, he pushes a bowl of mutton stew into your hands. "Eat. The hall can wait."

You eat it standing, hot and rich, and then a wedge of apple cake that Pim brings out after it.

Pim walks you to the footbridge with the lantern, full of questions, glowing with it.

The gate is shut for the night, but the guard knows you and lets you in. Up the lane, the hall is lamplit.

At the counter you tip out your cores. Forty-seven. Ilsa counts them twice, her eyebrows climbing.

"A hundred and forty-one copper. Less the silver you owe me." She pushes the coin across. It's heavy.

At the hearth sit the Brookside Four, back from the Tangle, two wolf pelts at their feet.

Orla sees the blood on your sleeve at once. "Sit." Two copper, and the rose light closes the cut.

You go back to the counter and tell Ilsa. The orchard's cleared. And goblins have been at Hobb's sheep. Two of them, out of the Tangle, through a gap in his far wall. Driven off, and one cut badly.

Ilsa writes it into the ledger. "I'll pin a goblin job for Hobb's by morning."

At the hearth, a broad young man has gone still, listening. He gets up and comes over.

Behind him the one in the scorched red coat laughs. "A watch recruit, with a practice spear?"

The broad one ignores him. "I'm Dace. The Four take goblin work here. How many, and how big?"
