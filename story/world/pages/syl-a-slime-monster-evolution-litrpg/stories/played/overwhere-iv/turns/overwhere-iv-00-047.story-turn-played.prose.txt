"I've been working on a specialized armor-piercing spear skill for a long time," you say, "and I guess it's finally paying off. Practicing pushing past armor turned out to be just what I needed to learn to strike from a distance. Useless without a spear, though."

Marta drinks it in, nodding. "A spear trick. Well, I never." Her eyes are bright with the tale.

Then her face softens. "My boy went into that Tangle. Wolves, they said. He never came out."

She presses your three copper back into your palm. "A goblin-killer's first meal is on the house."

You finish your pottage and cross the square to the shrine.

One whitewashed room, cool and dim, with benches and an altar. Behind the altar, seven books sit chained to a shelf.

An old woman sits mending on a bench, stout and grey, with sharp eyes. She looks you over.

"Sister Anwen," she says slowly. "You'll be wanting the books. A copper in the poor box, then."

"Two of scripture, a herbal, a vale chronicle, a primer, a road book, and the hero tales."

She takes a key from her belt and waits. "Which will you sit with, child?"
