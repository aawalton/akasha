You read through the rest, one after another, as the light moves across the floor.

The first scripture holds the gods' sayings. The second, the lives of folk the gods blessed. An oath sworn before the gods binds, it says, and the world asks the other to accept it.

The herbal. Moonleaf eases headaches, frostcap breaks fevers. Drawings of a hundred plants. It warns of nightcap, a black mushroom of the Tangle that kills within a day.

The road book maps the vale. Crowstone Quarry, two days north-west, at the end of a disused track. Tarrow Hall, a day north. Aubrin, four days east along the east road.

Last, the primer. Letters and sums, a child's drawings crowding its margins.

You close it. Two hours gone. The afternoon is wearing on.

Anwen has stopped mending. She looks at the chained shelf, then at you.

"All seven in one day," the old woman says slowly. "I've not seen the like."
