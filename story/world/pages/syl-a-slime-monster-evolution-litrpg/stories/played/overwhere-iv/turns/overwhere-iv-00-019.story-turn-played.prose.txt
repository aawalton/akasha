You don't go back to the line. You limp across the yard to the well and sit down on its low stone rim. There's an upturned bucket by the rope, and you set your hurt leg up on it. The knee pulses hot under your tights.

Holt watches you go. Then she hands the line to Oswin with a look and crosses the yard.

"Show me," she says. It isn't unkind. She crouches in front of you, waiting.

You roll the tights up over the knee. It is swollen and going purple, and the shin below it is scraped raw.

Holt presses the swelling with two fingers of her short hand. You hiss. Holt nods.

"Not broken. The steps did this, not Dell." A grunt. "Good. I can't use one who hides a hurt."

She goes back. Dell spars Oswin now, loud as ever. Oswin barely moves his feet.

Dell lunges big. Oswin steps aside and taps the butt of his spear on Dell's open left ribs.

"Again!" Dell yelps, and lunges again, and Oswin taps the same spot. Wat laughs out loud.

From the well, with only watching left to do, you see it plainly: the lunge, the lean, the gap.

Sitting still, you notice how dry your mouth is, dry as chalk. Your stomach growls, loud enough that Wat looks round.

Holt calls drill done. The others rack their spears and head for the mess, and the smell of porridge drifting out of it.

Holt stops by the well on her way in. "Porridge first. Then take that knee to the hall. Orla heals the watch for a copper or two. Go today, or you'll run laps on it tomorrow."
