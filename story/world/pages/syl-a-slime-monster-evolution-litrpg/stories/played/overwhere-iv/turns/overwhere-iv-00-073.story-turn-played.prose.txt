"No, you need someone else to watch," you tell Aldo. "Tonight I'm going hunting."

Aldo nods slowly. "Then me and my da'll sit up ourselves."

You walk back to Millbrook in the early light and fall in with the watch at drill.

The new spear sits right in your hands. Thrust, turn, recover; the crossbar never catches.

Proficiency gained. [Spearmanship LV 4] improved to [Spearmanship LV 5].

Porridge after drill. Then your curtained corner of the bunk room, and sleep, deep and dreamless.

You wake past noon, the warmth behind your ribs full again, and walk out to the downs.

A boulder, five paces off. You sit, shut your eyes, and find its crack with your sense.

You hold it as your sense gives it: an edge, a place. No picture. Just the place.

You reach for a line. The flicker comes, and steadies, and this time it holds.

A black line opens along the crack, felt rather than seen. Stone grates. Something drops.

You open your eyes. A slab has sheared off the boulder's side and lies in the grass.

New skill acquired: [Sense Casting LV 1].

You try again, eyes shut, on a second boulder. It holds. The cut lands a hand's width off.

And again, on a third. Each line costs you the same handful of warmth as any rend.

The sun is sinking toward the moor. Behind your ribs, the warmth is down to two-thirds.
