"It's a skill," you say. "I had a lot of books around when I was younger."

Anwen nods slowly, as if that answers something, and locks the chain again.

You spend the rest of the afternoon resting, turning over what you read. The Wayfarer most of all.

Supper in the gatehouse mess. Sleep behind your curtain. Drill at dawn, then porridge.

At the hall, Ilsa is pinning a fresh notice by the counter.

GOBLINS AT TULL'S. SIX SHEEP TAKEN.

"Last night," Ilsa says. "Tull's farm, two miles south-west, where the Tangle meets the ford."

"Six sheep driven off. His son ran out with a lantern and got his scalp split. He'll live."

"Tull counted seven by lantern light. Six goblins, and a bigger one leading, with a horn."

She taps the notice. "That's a hobgoblin. Ears paid, five silver for him, three copper a sheep."

"The Four left at dawn on a two-day wolf hunt, deep in. We can't call them back."

She meets your eyes. "So it's yours, if you'll have it. Alone."

"But if that trail runs toward Grakk's camp, you turn back. Promise me that."
