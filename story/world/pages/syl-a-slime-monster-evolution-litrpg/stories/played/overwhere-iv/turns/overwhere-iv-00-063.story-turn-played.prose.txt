The beast runs into it neck first.

The line bites deep, clean through its throat. The beast is silent.

Its own weight carries it on, up over the rim, straight at you.

You have the spear braced, butt in the heather, point at its chest.

It drives itself onto the point. The sharpened head sinks deep, and the shaft jolts in your hands.
