Tobin Ash's smithy sits on the square, its forge glowing, the air thick with coal smoke.

Tobin is broad and soot-grey. He looks up from the anvil and looks you over.

"The best spear I can get," you tell him.

He jerks his chin at the wall: one plain iron-headed hunting spear on ash. "Eighty copper."

"That's what I have. Best I can make is another thing."

A leaf-bladed head, he says, hardened iron, forged to order and fitted to a new shaft. Two days.

"Holds its edge far past that one. Hundred and fifty copper. Half before I light the fire."

He hands you the wall spear to hold, eyes your height against it, and grunts.

"I'll cut the shaft to you." He glances at the practice spear on your back. "Guard's stores, that."

"Heard of a redhead's spear trick down at the Brook and Barrel." He grunts again, and says no more.

You count out seventy-five copper onto his bench. He sweeps it into a tin without counting.

"Day after tomorrow." He nods at the practice spear. "That one's blunt as a fence post."

"Ten copper, and I'll put an edge and a point back on it while you wait."
