You square up to Dell in the packed-earth yard, blunt spear low, wicker shield up. Your knee throbs with every shift of weight.

Dell circles, loud and loose, talking the whole time. He's going easy on you. You can tell, and it stings.

You do exactly as drilled. Guard. Thrust. Recover. Dell slaps your spear aside without even looking at it.

Holt steps in and stops you. She pushes your back foot a hand's width farther out with her boot and lifts your shield elbow with two fingers. "Again."

You listen, and the next exchange is better. You hold your guard. Dell has to actually block.

Then he lunges, big and showy. You catch it on the shield with a crack of wicker, and as he comes through, his whole left side hangs open.

You see it too late. By the time your spear moves, he has already recovered, laughing.

On his next lunge you go for the opening. Your bad knee buckles as you step into it.

Dell's spear butt thumps into your ribs under the shield. You land hard on the earth, knee first.

The breath goes out of you. Everything hurts at once. You lie there, and the yard goes quiet.

"Yield," you manage.

Across the yard, Wat winces. Oswin keeps quiet and watches you.

Dell hauls you up by the hand. "Lamb-girl bleats," he announces to the whole yard, grinning.

Holt looks at you a long moment. "You saw it. His left, after the lunge." She jerks her chin at the line. "Saw it and were late. Again, Farrow. Same as before."
