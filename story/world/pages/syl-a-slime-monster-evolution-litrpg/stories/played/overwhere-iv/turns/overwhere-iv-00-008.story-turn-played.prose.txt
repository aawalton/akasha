"Sure! I'd appreciate that," you say. "A few questions first, though. I assume the guard doesn't mind adventurer work on the side?"

Ilsa laughs. "Off duty? Hale's glad of it. He likes his people levelled."

"What's the best way to dispatch a slime? Anything I need to be wary of?" You spread your hands. "Assume I'm completely new to this, because I am."

Ilsa looks pleased to be asked. She straightens behind the counter and holds up a finger.

"One sharp thrust, through the jelly, into the core. The red bit in the middle. Crack it, it stops. It slumps down to a puddle, and you fish the core out. Cracked or whole, I pay the same. Alchemists grind them anyway."

A second finger. "Jelly stings bare skin like nettles. Worse in the eyes. Wear gloves if you can get them."

"Third. It'll cling to a hand or a boot. Pull free, steady. Don't flail; you only splash yourself."

"Fourth, work them on dry ground. One rolls into the brook, it floats off and you've lost it."

She folds the fingers down. "And one rule: never clear the common bare. Town keeps some for jelly. Children milk them. For proper pest work, the Tangle-edge farms want theirs gone." She tilts her head. "But there's wolves out there."

She dips the pen and writes in the ledger without waiting for more: NALA, WATCH, OWES 1 S. She turns the book round for your mark. Then she opens a drawer and roots through a tin that clinks.

She sets a small bronze tag on the counter, strung on a leather cord through a hole punched in one end.

"Bronze. You're the hall's now, and the hall's yours. Bring me cores and I'll take the silver off."
