"How many?" you ask. "If it's less than three, I think I could take them."

Hobb squints at you. At the spear, then at the bronze tag. "Never seen the thief. Only what it took. Two sheep this week, out of the field by the trees." He sniffs. "A wolf, I'd say. Maybe two."

Bran is still barking at the far wall, stiff-legged, and won't go a step nearer to it.

The sun is touching the dark line of the Tangle beyond the far field. The light is going fast.

Up on the orchard wall, Pim has gone quiet. He scratches his neck. "Might not be wolves, Granda."

Hobb turns his head.

"Last week, dusk, I saw two little figures down by the far wall. On two legs. Small as me, near." Pim swallows. "I thought it was village boys after apples. I shouted, and they ran off into the trees."

Hobb stares at him. "You never said."

Pim goes red to the ears, his eyes on the far wall. "Didn't think it mattered." He wets his lips. "It was about now."
