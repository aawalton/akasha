You turn from the dead goblin and find the runner. Small, quick, weaving between the yews.

Twenty-five paces, and widening. It jinks left of one trunk, then right of the next.

You watch its line, not the goblin. There's a gap between two yews that it has to take.

You fix your eye on that gap, at neck height, a heartbeat ahead of it. You reach for the warmth.

A thin black-purple line hangs in the air there, for no time at all.

The runner goes into it at full tilt.

It falls in two pieces. Behind it, a yew bough drops, cut as clean as the goblin.

Goblin LV 2 defeated. Experience gained.

The warmth behind your ribs dips again, a handful less. There's still more of it there than gone.
