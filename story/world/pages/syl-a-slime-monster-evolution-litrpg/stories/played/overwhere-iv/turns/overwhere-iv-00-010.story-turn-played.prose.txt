You smile at him. "You're welcome to it, lad. I'd never interfere with a great hunt like that."

He squints up at you, suspicious of being agreed with. Then he grins, drops to his knees by the slime and unstoppers his jar.

You shoulder the spear and walk upstream along the brook, away from the children and the stile. Past the footbridge, a screen of willows hangs down over a grassy bend. Once you are under them, the town and the road are gone behind a green curtain of leaves.

Three small slimes bob in the bend. As you step in under the willows, all three turn toward you.

You crouch by the nearest one. "Hello," you say. "Can you hear me?"

It sits there, blue and blank.

"Go left," you tell it. Then, "Right." Then, "Stay."

It only drifts an inch closer.

You stop talking and try it silently. You fix your eyes on the slime and will it, hard, to move.

Behind your ribs, the warmth you woke with stirs, like something huge turning over in its sleep.

All three slimes shiver at once and press in against your boots.

You hold on to the warmth. Then you push it at the nearest slime, the way you would push a door.

The air around the slime creases, like a sheet folded along a line. Then it lies flat again.

The slime is a hand's width farther from you. It never rolled. It was there, and then it was here.

Something leaves you with the push: a small, cool gap behind your ribs, like a sip taken from a full cup.

A window opens.

New skill acquired: [Dimension Magic LV 1]

You reach again, and this time you pull. The air creases. The slime is back at your boot.

A second sip goes. The warmth sits a little lower behind your ribs, still huge, still patient.

You try to fold it sideways, toward the brook. The warmth won't answer. The slime stays where it is.
