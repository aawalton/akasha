"Great! Maybe I'll do both," you say. "For now I'll get some lunch, and then go read at the shrine."

Ilsa waves you off with the pencil, already bent over the ledger again.

Across the square is the inn, the Brook and Barrel by its sign. Warm and loud, smelling of stew and ale.

You take a place at a long table and pay three copper: pottage, bread and cheese, and a small ale.

Around you, the taproom's talk is all goblins. The lookout at Hobb's. The watch girl with the spear.

Heads turn your way, then turn back, too casually.

A woman in an apron wipes her hands and sits down across from you, uninvited, smiling.

"Marta. I keep this place." She nods at your spear. "So you're the one they're all on about."

She leans in, eyes bright. "Cut a goblin clean in two, they say. How'd a slip like you manage that?"
