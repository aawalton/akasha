You grip hard and thrust for its neck. The warmth rises for the fold.

Your new sense feels its weight drop before it moves. It is already ducking for the low gap.

You follow the duck. Click. The point leaps the extra foot, low, after it.

It catches the side of the goblin's neck. A glancing cut, not deep. It shrieks. Blood, dark.

Your spearhead drives on into the bramble behind it, and the thorny canes close on the shaft.

You yank. The spear doesn't come. It's snagged fast, the head wound through with thorns.

Your forearm throbs where the knife opened it. Your wrists jar and ache from the fold.

The goblin is half into the low gap now, wriggling, its bare feet kicking at the mud. Your sense feels it all within a few paces: the narrow gap, the thorns, its small body squeezing through.

Blood runs from its neck onto the mud. It is slowing. But it is getting through.

The warmth behind your ribs is running low now, well under half.

One grey-green foot scrabbles for purchase an arm's length from your boot.
