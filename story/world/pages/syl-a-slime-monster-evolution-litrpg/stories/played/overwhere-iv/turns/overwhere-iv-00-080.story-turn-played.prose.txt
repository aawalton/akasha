"Who's there?" Merrit's shout cracks across the meadow, the flame held high and ready.

"It's me, Nala!" you shout back. The flame holds where it is.

You cross the ford and the meadow at a stiff jog, one hand pressed to your side.

"Ran into a full warband," you tell them as you come. "Must have been at least twenty of them."

"Two big hobs. I think I downed five, but they were pretty mad. Chased me hard."

"Not sure if I lost them, or if they'll keep coming tonight."

"I'm low on mana, but I can still use a spear if they come this way."

Dace is over the fold wall before you finish. He believes you; it shows in his face.

"Five, alone?" Merrit says. "In the dark?" But the flame in his hand burns hotter.

"Aldo," Dace snaps. "Take the cob to Hobb's. Bring Wren and Orla. Ride hard."

Aldo is gone at a run for the cob. Tull steps out of the cottage door, cudgel in hand.

Dace looks at the blood soaking your side, then at you. "How bad is that? Can you stand a fight?"
