"Leave that to me," you say.

You pay two copper for a pallet in the Brook and Barrel's common room and sleep the night through.

At first light the five of you walk down to Tull's ford.

The ford runs knee-deep, ten paces wide, thirty paces out from the trees.

A drystone sheep wall runs along the meadow bank. Merrit pats it. "Here. They bunch in the water."

Dace sets them along the wall. "Bring them out to us, and don't get caught doing it."

You cross alone and go up the deer trail, off it among the bushes, slow and quiet.

Every little while you let your sense spread wide.

A mile and more in, your sense brushes something. Two bodies, forty paces ahead, coming down.

Goblins, small and silent, slipping from tree to tree beside the trail. They don't turn your way.

Behind them, out past your reach, comes a low muttering, many voices. A heavy tread among them.
