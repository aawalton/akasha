"I'm not at my best, but I can fight," you tell him.

"Especially if they delay long enough for me to get some mana back first."

"Then rest by the fold wall," Dace says. "I'll rouse you when Orla comes, or at the first torch."

You sit with your back to the cold stones, spear across your knees, and let your eyes close.

Merrit paces the fold. Tull stands at the cottage door with his cudgel and doesn't say a word.

Hoofbeats, within the hour. Aldo on the cob, and Wren and Orla jogging behind.

Orla kneels by you at once. "Oh, Nala." Her hands glow soft rose over the gash in your side.

The cut draws shut. The ache in your back melts away under her palms.

You rest on. Slowly, the warmth behind your ribs creeps back. Two-fifths full.

The half moon sinks behind the Tangle. The meadow goes black.

Near midnight, Wren hisses from the fold wall. "There. Across the ford."

Among the trees at the Tangle's edge, torches flare up one after another. Ten. A dozen. More.
