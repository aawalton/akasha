You stand at the fold wall, spear grounded, and watch the torches stream up the meadow.

Merrit's fire bursts against the front of the rush and scorches it. Shrieks. The rest come on, fast.

Forty paces. The two hobgoblins' heads bob plain in their own torchlight, a few behind the front.

You fix your eye on the maul-bearer's eyes and lay the line straight across them.

It roars and reels, clawing at its face, blood pouring through its fingers. It stays on its feet.

You turn to the spear-bearer and lay the second line across its eyes.

This one goes deep, through the skull. The spear hobgoblin drops like a felled tree.

Hobgoblin LV 10 defeated. Experience gained.

Proficiency gained. [Rift Rend LV 5] improved to [Rift Rend LV 6].

The rush breaks at once. Goblins shriek, fling down torches, and run back for the ford.

Torches scatter across the meadow and go out in the wet grass, one after another.

Behind your ribs the warmth is low again. A fifth left.

The blind maul-bearer stands alone in the dark meadow, roaring, swinging its maul at empty air.

Then it lurches forward, toward the sound of Tull's sheep. Toward the fold.
