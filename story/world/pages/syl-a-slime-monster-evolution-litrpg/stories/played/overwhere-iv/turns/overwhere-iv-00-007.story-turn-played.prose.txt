Behind the curtain you change. The wool tunic goes on first, and falls to your knees. The padded jerkin goes over it, stiff, smelling of someone else's sweat. Then the boots. They clump on the boards, and the rags hold your feet in, mostly. You walk like a child in her father's shoes.

You leave the practice spear on the cot, with the carter's coat folded beside it.

At the gate, Wat sees you go by in the kit and gives you a solemn nod. The grin behind it gets away from him anyway.

The cobbled lane climbs from the gate and opens into a market square. An inn stands on one side, and a smithy rings from another, hammer on iron. A small shrine sits in the corner, and across from it a narrow stone building with a sign over the door. A board beside the door is thick with pinned notices, some fresh, some curling in the sun.

Inside, it is dim and quiet. A long counter runs across the back. There are a few benches, a hearth, and walls papered with more notices. An old man in furs dozes by the fire with his cap pulled down over his eyes.

Behind the counter, a sharp-featured woman looks up from a ledger. Her dark hair is pinned up with a pencil, and her fingers are inky. Her eyes go to the watch jerkin, then down to the boots, and they light up.

"You're the one from the gate. Barefoot on the common, robbed on the road, signed on with Hale." She sets the ledger aside. "Ilsa Crane. I keep the hall. News gets here before people do."

On the shelf behind her sits a fist-sized crystal, cloudy, with a crack running right across it.

Ilsa leans on the counter. "Want work? Slimes. Safe, steady, three copper for each core. Farms want pest slimes cleared, and the common's got more every day. Ask anyone why and they shrug." She reaches over and taps a notice on the wall. "Wolves and goblins pay better. They also bite."

She looks you over again. "Bronze tag is a silver to register. You've not got a silver, have you?" She answers herself before you can. "Watch pays at week's end. I'll let you owe it against your first cores."

She opens the ledger, turns it round to face you, and holds out the pen. "Shall I put you down?"
