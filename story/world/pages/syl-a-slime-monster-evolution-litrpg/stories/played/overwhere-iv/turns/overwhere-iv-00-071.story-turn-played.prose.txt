"Sorry, but no. You're a good group, but I'd like to work on my own for now. Thank you for the offer."

Dace takes it well. "Fair. The door stays open, mind. Any time." He nods and heads for the inn.

You go to the counter. "I can do a night watch tonight."

Ilsa writes it in. "Tull's, then. He knows you, and he was raided last."

"Dusk to dawn. Three silver, paid in the morning." She sets a cow horn on the counter.

"If they come, blow it, so the farm wakes. Guard the farm, and don't follow goblins in by night."

You walk the two miles south-west as the sun sinks. Tull is at the fold gate, cudgel in hand.

He looks glad to see a face he knows. He gives you the corner of the fold, and a lantern, unlit.

Dusk settles. Across the ford, two hundred paces of open meadow run to the black edge of the Tangle.

The sheep shift and settle behind you. Tull's window goes dark. The stars come out.

Hours pass. Then, among the trees across the ford, a light. Then a second.

Two torches, there at the Tangle's edge. They hold still, side by side, facing the farm.
