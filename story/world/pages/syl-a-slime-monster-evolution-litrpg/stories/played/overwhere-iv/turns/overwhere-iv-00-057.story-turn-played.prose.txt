"Sure, might as well have an edge while I'm waiting. Thanks!" You set ten copper on the bench.

Tobin takes the practice spear and thrusts its head into the coals without a word.

You sit on a stool by the door while he works: hammer on the horn of the anvil, then the wheel.

Sparks fly from the grindstone in long orange threads. Outside, the square's shadows stretch east.

It takes him most of an hour. He takes his time over every stroke.

At last he dips the head, wipes it, and holds it out to you. The point is sharp, the edges bright.

"Soft iron," he says. "It'll bite like a real spear. Dulls quick, though."

"Come back for the good one day after tomorrow."
