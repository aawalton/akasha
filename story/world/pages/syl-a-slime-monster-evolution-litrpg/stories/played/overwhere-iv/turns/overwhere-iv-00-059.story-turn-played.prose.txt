"I'm working on my senses. Anyone can see things, but how many people can understand what they hear and smell?"

The girl's eyes light up. "Then find me. Eyes shut. Bet you can't." And she's gone, running.

Your sense follows a small shape darting across the yard, then losing itself past the trough.

From the stable a boy's voice calls: "Lissy! Chores!" A lanky lad of twelve or so leans out of the door.

A long pause. Then the girl crawls out from under the cart, grumbling, and stomps off to the stable.

You go in to supper. The taproom is warm and loud, and full of one story.

The redhead's spear trick: seven goblins and a hobgoblin, cut down alone. Heads turn as you enter.

Marta sets down mutton stew, bread and small ale, and waves off your coin.

"Not from the one who did for those goblins." A second bowl follows the first.

You walk back to the gatehouse after dark and sleep in your curtained corner of the bunk room.

At dawn you drill with the watch, spear in hand. The sharpened head feels different: hungrier.

Proficiency gained. [Spearmanship LV 3] improved to [Spearmanship LV 4].

Porridge after drill. Then an hour on the gatehouse steps, eyes shut, working the shapes about you.

The warmth behind your ribs is most of the way full. You don't ache anywhere.

Mid-morning you walk to the hall. There wasn't a raid in the night.

The board holds the old notices, slimes, wolves, herbs, east-road guards, and two new ones.

WOOL CART TO TARROW HALL. ONE GUARD. 1 SILVER. LEAVES AT NOON.

GREAT WOLF, NORTH-WEST PASTURES, BY NIGHT. 1 SILVER THE PELT.

Ilsa sees you reading. "The cart's a day north to Tarrow Hall and back the next. Safe road."

"The wolf, a shepherd swears he saw by moonlight. Big as a pony, gone toward the old quarry."

She snorts. "Drunk, I'd say. A Tangle wolf grows no bigger than a hound."
