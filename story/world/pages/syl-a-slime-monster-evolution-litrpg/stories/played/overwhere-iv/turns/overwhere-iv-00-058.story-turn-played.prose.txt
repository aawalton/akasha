You find a quiet corner of the stable yard behind the Brook and Barrel, and sit on a pail.

You shut your eyes and reach for your sense, out past its usual few paces.

The yard comes to you as shapes and hollows: a trough, a cart, a horse shifting in its stall.

No color, no warmth. A sack by the door is a slumped bulk; the words painted on it aren't there.

You try the wall. The stall's planks don't stop it; the horse beyond is as plain as the trough.

A crate on the cart is nailed shut. Inside, you feel it anyway: round shapes, packed tight.

You push toward the inn's back door. The common room is full, and the bodies blur into one mass.

You can't pick out a single drinker from the knot of them. It's like listening to a crowd's roar.

Every reach costs a little warmth, and holding it drains you, so you work in bursts and rest.

Two hours of it. The yard's shadows run long, and the sky over the roofs goes gold.

Proficiency gained. [Spatial Sense LV 2] improved to [Spatial Sense LV 3].

The shapes around you sit a little wider now, even when you aren't reaching.

Behind your ribs the warmth is thin again, spent on practice.

A small shape drops from the stable loft ladder and comes straight toward you, quick and light.

You open your eyes. A girl of nine or so, all elbows and scabbed knees, squats in front of you.

"You've been sitting with your eyes shut for ages," the girl says. "What game is it?"
