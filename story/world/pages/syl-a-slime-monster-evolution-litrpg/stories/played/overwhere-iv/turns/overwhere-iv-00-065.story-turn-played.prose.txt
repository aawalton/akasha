You leave the broken spear where it is, and the carcass with it.

The head alone is as heavy as a full water pail. You heft it by the ears and start back.

Seven miles down the Crowstone track and across the downs. Your arms burn; you shift its weight.

Past Fen's fold. The shepherd stares at what you carry, and takes off his cap.

The sun is going down as the town comes in sight. Slowly the warmth behind your ribs creeps back.

At the gatehouse you find Brenna Holt and tell her plainly: the practice spear broke on a beast.

Holt looks at the head. Then she looks at it a while longer.

"Thirty copper from stores, a spear," she says. "One broken on that? Fifteen, and well spent."

You pay the fifteen. Behind Holt, the gate guard is already calling the others out to look.

The hall's lamps are lit. You set the head on Ilsa's counter.

Ilsa looks at it, and goes pale. "That's no Tangle wolf."

"A monster too strong for its ground, out by night." She says it like a line from the handbook, learned by heart.

"That's the sign. A new dungeon's opened, up the Crowstone track. The old quarry, I'd wager."

She pays the great wolf's silver on the head, as gladly as on a pelt.

"I'll write to Aubrin tonight. The letter goes east with the morning carter."

"If they confirm it, the finder gets two gold from the guild. And it's a major job, on your letter."

"Aubrin's slow, mind. Ten days before anyone comes to look."

She taps the counter. "Till then, bronzes don't go up that track. That means you too."
