You step off the trail without a sound, your sense finding every root and stone for your feet.

Where the cleft begins, a bramble-grown bank rises by the trail. You work into it and lie flat.

Twenty paces from the trail. Half your reach. You go still and breathe slow.

Two goblins slip past first, quiet, fifty paces ahead of the rest. They never look your way.

Then the band, single file down the cleft: feet, breath, the knock of a club on a shield rim.

You let your sense spread wide, and the cleft fills in: bodies, one after another, twenty and more.

Two far bigger than the rest, mid-line: one with a great maul on its shoulder, one with a spear.

You pick one at random from the line. Find its neck by feel alone. Lay the line.

The goblin drops without a sound, head and body parting.

Goblin LV 4 defeated. Experience gained.

Class Experience threshold reached. Spellblade is now LV 4.

The goblin behind trips over the body, and shrieks.

The band halts. Shrieking, they bunch around the two big ones, weapons out, facing every way.

The spear-bearer lifts a horn and blows. The blast rolls up the cleft and away through the trees.

Slings whirr. Stones crash blind into the brambles, all of them wide of you.

You pick another at random in the knot. A clean line through the neck. It folds without a cry.

Goblin LV 5 defeated. Experience gained.

Another, at random. Your aim slips a hand's width low; the line opens a goblin's shoulder instead.

It screams and staggers, clutching the wound, still on its feet. The knot squeezes tighter.

The feel of the cut settles in you, finer than before.

Proficiency gained. [Sense Casting LV 1] improved to [Sense Casting LV 2].

The two quiet ones come back up the trail from the ford, fast, along the foot of your bank.
