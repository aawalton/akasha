You fix your eye on the horn-blower's neck and lay the line before its breath is drawn.

Its head falls from its shoulders. The horn tumbles unblown into the leaves.

Goblin LV 5 defeated. Experience gained.

The runner is ten strides up the trail, arms pumping. You lay a second line across its neck.

It drops mid-stride, head and body rolling apart.

Goblin LV 4 defeated. Experience gained.

Silence. You spread your sense wide. Birds, and only birds. The trail ahead is empty.

In the watchers' hide, a water gourd, a gnawed hare bone, and the horn, of yellowed bone.

From the rise the smoke is plain now, rising above the trees up the trail.

And down from there, faint through the oaks, come axe-strokes. Many of them, steady, one on another.
