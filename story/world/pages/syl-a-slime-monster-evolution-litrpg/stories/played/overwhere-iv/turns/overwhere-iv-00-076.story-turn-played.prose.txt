You take the two quiet ones first, before they reach the band.

The nearer, running hard below your bank: you find its neck by feel and lay the line.

It drops mid-stride without a sound and rolls to the trail's edge.

Goblin LV 4 defeated. Experience gained.

The second skids at the body, knife up, peering ahead. Your line takes it through the throat.

Goblin LV 3 defeated. Experience gained.

Racial Experience threshold reached. Human is now LV 6.

Both lie dead at the foot of your bank, twenty paces from the shrieking knot.

You reach for the band again, for a slinger whirling at the knot's edge.

Your aim slips. The line goes wide and shears a yew bough over the bank's edge, near you.

The bough crashes down into the brambles, loud in the dark.

Every goblin face in the knot turns toward the sound. The slings swing round to it.

Behind your ribs the warmth runs thin: a fifth left.

The maul-bearer bellows and points his maul at your bank.

Six goblins break from the knot and come at the brambles, clubs and spears up.
