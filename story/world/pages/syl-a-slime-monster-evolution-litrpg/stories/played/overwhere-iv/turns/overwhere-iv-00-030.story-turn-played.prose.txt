Dace roars and gets his shield up. Stones crack off it from atop the fallen oak. Behind him, the red coat's hands flare red.

Behind the yew on your left, a body surges up. A goblin, bigger than the scouts, club raised.

You hold your place on the flank. Brace the spear, point out. Reach for the warmth.

Not the spear this time. The goblin. You fold it toward you.

Click.

It lurches off its feet, a foot and a half, straight at your point. Too early, by a hair. The spear takes it in the side, shallow, skidding off hide. Its club comes round anyway.

The club thumps into your shoulder through the jerkin. It hurts. You keep your feet.

It snarls and draws back to swing again. You set your grip, and try the other thing.

You thrust, and as the point goes in you lay a fold along its edge. Not push. Cut.

Something parts. A clean line opens through the goblin, as if the air itself had an edge.

It drops without a sound. The spear comes back whole in your hands, your arms humming.

Goblin defeated. Experience gained.

New spell learned: [Rift Rend]

Proficiency gained. [Spearmanship LV 2] improved to [Spearmanship LV 3].

Proficiency gained. [Rift Rend LV 1] improved to [Rift Rend LV 2].

The warmth behind your ribs is lighter by a good handful. The fight hasn't stopped for you.

In the hollow, a fireball bursts against the oak. The slinger atop it yelps and ducks.

Three sheep bleat behind a rope of plaited bark. Wren darts along the hollow's edge.

Past the sheep, one goblin breaks from the hollow and runs. Not away from you. Deeper in.

Wren sees it too. "Runner!" she shouts. "It's going for the rest of them!"
