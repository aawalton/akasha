"I might be good for a night watch," you tell Ilsa. "Let me do some training today, and I'll let you know."

You walk out the north gate, onto the empty downs beyond. Short grass, boulders scattered about.

You pick a boulder the size of a crouching man, sit five paces from it, and shut your eyes.

Your sense finds it at once: its rounded back, a crack running down one side.

You fix on the crack and reach for a line. A flicker, and gone.

Again. The flicker lasts a breath longer before it slips. Something in it is learning.

For an hour it goes that way. Each try you picture the crack as if seen. Each try, gone.

Then you stop picturing. You hold the crack only as your sense gives it: a shape, an edge, a place.

The flicker steadies. For one long heartbeat the line almost holds, laid right along the crack.

Then it slips, like the others. But it slipped later, and you know why now.

You work at it through the afternoon, boulder after boulder, until the sun swings west.

Proficiency gained. [Spatial Sense LV 3] improved to [Spatial Sense LV 4].

The shapes around you reach a little wider again, even when you aren't reaching for them.

You open your eyes, get up, shoulder your spear, and start back for town.
