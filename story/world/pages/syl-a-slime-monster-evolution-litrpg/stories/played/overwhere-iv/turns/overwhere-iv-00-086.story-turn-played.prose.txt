"I'll get some sleep, then go scoring again," you tell Ilsa.

You walk up the street to the gatehouse and find Hale over his ledger.

You tell him you're leaving the watch. You thank him, and offer to pay for the gear you've been using.

"Can I keep what I've been wearing?"

Hale has heard about the strike already. He looks you over a long moment.

"Keep the boots, the tunic and the jerkin. No coin. You've earned them."

"And you've a full week on the roll." He counts five silver onto the ledger. "Your wage."

He strikes your name from the watch roll with one clean line.

In the yard, Holt nods to you. "Gate yard's open at dawn if you want to drill. Any day."

Hale closes the ledger. "The bunks are for the watch, mind. You'll want a bed elsewhere."
