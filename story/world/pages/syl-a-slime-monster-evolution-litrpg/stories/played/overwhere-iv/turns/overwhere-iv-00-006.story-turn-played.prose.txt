You climb down off the seat with the coat over your arm. "Thank you for the ride, Garrett. I'm sure I'll see you around for a while."

Garrett grins at you from under his sack. "Keep the coat. Millbrook's small; you'll not lose me."

You walk back to the gate barefoot. The paving stones are warm from the sun. Wat looks up from his ledger as you pass under the arch, and his eyebrows go up.

You knock at the gatehouse door.

"In," says Hale's voice.

Inside is a guardroom with a cold hearth and a bench along one wall, and past it a cramped office. Hale sits behind a desk with a thick roll book open in front of him.

"I'd like to sign up for drill," you tell him. "And I'd like to bunk here, starting tonight, if I can."

Hale looks at you for a long moment. "That was quick." He leans back. "Pay's at week's end. Quit before, you don't see a copper of it. Still want it?"

"I do."

He turns the roll book round to face you and dips the pen. "You swear to keep the town's peace, and do as you're told on the wall. Say it."

You say it back to him, word for word.

Hale writes your name in the roll himself, in square, careful letters: NALA. He sets the pen down beside it for your mark, and you put your hand to the page.

"Holt!" he calls, not loudly.

A woman fills the doorway. She is square-built, with a pale scar running along her chin.

"New recruit. Kit her out. She's bunking in." Hale is already back in his papers.

Sergeant Holt looks you up and down, starting at your feet. "Right. With me."

She leads you through a mess room of long tables and benches to a stores cupboard, and starts pulling things down off the shelves and piling them in your arms. A wool tunic. A padded jerkin. A practice spear with a blunted head. Last of all, a pair of old boots, huge in her hands. She stuffs rags into the toes before you can say anything. Her left hand is short two fingers, and she works the rags in with it quick as anything.

The bunk room holds twelve cots. Four of them are stripped bare. At the far end hangs a curtain on a rope.

Holt draws it back on two cots and a chest. "Mine, and now yours. The lads stay their side of this."

She takes the kit from you and drops it on the empty cot. "Brenna. Sergeant to you on the yard. Supper's barley stew at dusk. Drill's at dawn: wall laps, spear, shield, then sparring. Late once, you run it twice. Till supper, your time's your own."

She lets the curtain fall behind her.
