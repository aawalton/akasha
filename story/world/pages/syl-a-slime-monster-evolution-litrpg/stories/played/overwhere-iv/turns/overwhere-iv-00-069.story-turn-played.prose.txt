"Perfect, thanks!" You shoulder the new spear and cross the square to the hall.

Ilsa looks up from her ledger. "I've put the wolf down as your fifth job done well."

"Five more, or Aubrin confirms that dungeon, and I write your letter."

The wool cart's notice is gone; it left yesterday noon with a carter's nephew to guard it.

A fresh notice, inked heavy: NIGHT WATCH, TANGLE-EDGE FARMS. 3 SILVER A NIGHT. TAGGED HANDS ONLY.

"Farmers by the Tangle have seen torches in the trees these two nights," Ilsa says. "Watching."

"Hobb's, Tull's, and the Ashby place. The three nearest the trees. They want someone awake."

"And the Four are back. Came in last night with four wolf pelts, and heard about your hobgoblin."

She goes back to her ledger, then adds without looking up: "Dace was asking after you."
