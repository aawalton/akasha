"Perfect. I'll work on that today."

You pull the gloves on. They are soft, and a little big. Ilsa nods.

Holt is out in the gatehouse yard when you get back. You ask whether she wants you for any more training today.

"First week's dawn drill only," she says. "Gate shifts start next week. Afternoons are yours. Captain likes them spent levelling." She jerks her thumb at the gate. "Be back through there by the night bell. It's barred after."

You collect your practice spear and go. West, over the footbridge, a mile along the lane toward the dark line of the Tangle.

Hobb Farm is a stone farmhouse, a barn, an orchard, and three sheep fields walled in dry stone. A sheepdog comes barking down the track at you, and a boy of about fourteen runs after it, all elbows.

"Bran! Bran, heel!" He catches the dog by the scruff and looks up. His eyes find the bronze tag at your neck, then your face, and he goes red. "I'm Pim. It's my granda's farm."

The farmer comes out of the house slowly, stooped and sour. He looks you over. "Hall sent a girl." He points with his chin. "Orchard's that way."

The orchard is thick with blue slimes. They are fat on windfall apples, crowding the ditches and heaped among the roots.

You step in under the trees.

Every slime goes still. Then they turn, and drift toward you. All of them. In a minute there is a crowd of them round your boots, quivering, calm as lambs.

Over on the wall, Pim yelps. The farmer swears.

One thrust, through the jelly and into the crimson core. It slumps. The next is already there.

You work through the long afternoon. Thrust and recover, thrust and recover. The gloves go sticky. Your arms burn. Pim fetches you a cup of water from the well, then hangs over the wall to watch. The farmer watches from his doorway, arms folded.

"Witchery," he mutters, half-pleased.

Halfway through the orchard, a window opens.

New skill acquired: [Spearmanship LV 1]

The spear sits easier in your hands after that. Your grip settles without you thinking about it, and the thrust comes from your hips instead of your arms.

Later, another.

Racial Experience threshold reached. Human is now LV 3.

The warmth runs through you again, and you keep working.

Near sunset, the last few come out of the ditches to you. The final one slumps at your feet.

Racial Experience threshold reached. Human is now LV 4.

Forty cores clink in your pockets. The orchard is still.

The farmer stands at its edge, looking round at the empty grass under his trees, and for once he keeps his sourness to himself.

Then Bran goes stiff at the far end of the orchard. His hackles rise. He barks, hard, toward the far field wall.

The farmer spits. "Wolves again. Took two of my sheep this week, out of the field by the trees."
