You step through the gap and walk toward them. Slow. The spear held low, point wandering, like you aren't sure which end to hold. You let it shake.

The spear goblin sees you first. It barks something at the other. Both of them grin, and they come on, jabbering.

It levels its flint spear and stops just out of your reach, where its own length will tell. It sets its feet. It looks very sure of itself.

It jabs. You let it come close, the flint point a hand from your coat. Then you thrust.

Click. The fold drops as your arms straighten, and your spearpoint lands a foot past where it should.

It takes the goblin square in the chest, through the hide scraps. It shrieks and staggers back. The flint spear falls in the grass. It clutches its chest with both hands, blood running between its fingers, and its eyes go wide. It turns and runs.

The knife goblin sees its partner hurt and breaks too. Both bolt for the Tangle, bare feet flying over the grass.

You turn and go after the knife goblin. Your store boots slide on the wet grass. It's fast.

Off to your left, the spear goblin plunges into the dark under the trees and is gone in three strides.

You run the knife goblin down at the very edge of the Tangle. It spins, cornered, and slashes.

The rusty knife opens your forearm above the glove. Hot, then sharp. You thrust back, off balance, and your point only scores its shoulder. It hisses and backs against a bramble, knife up, bleeding.

Then the world changes shape. Blue windows, one after another, too fast to read, and then clear:

Proficiency gained. [Spearmanship LV 1] improved to [Spearmanship LV 2].

Proficiency gained. [Dimension Magic LV 2] improved to [Dimension Magic LV 3].

New spell learned: [Spatial Sense].

And you can feel it. Everything within a few paces, without looking: its shape, and every hollow in it.

The goblin's hunched body. The knife held high. Its weight shifting onto its back foot, ready to bolt. The bramble at its back, and a gap low in it, just big enough for a goblin to dive through.

Past five paces or so the feeling fades, like lamplight. But you think you could push it out.

The goblin bares its teeth at you and hisses something in its own tongue.
