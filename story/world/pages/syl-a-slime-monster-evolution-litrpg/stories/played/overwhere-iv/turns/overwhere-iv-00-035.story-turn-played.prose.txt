In the hollow, the work is quick. You go goblin to goblin with the others, taking ears.

Wren follows with a skinning knife, cutting each core out from under the breastbone. Five ears. Five cores.

Orla cuts the bark rope. Hobb's three sheep blunder out, bleating, and Dace herds them on.

The walk back out of the Tangle is long and slow, the sheep balking at every root.

At the farm, Hobb runs out to meet you and counts the sheep twice over. He shakes every hand.

Then the lane to Millbrook, the square, and the narrow stone hall with the sign over the door.

Inside, the hall is quiet. Ilsa looks up from the ledger as the Four troop in with you.

Dace sets the ears and cores on the counter. "The lookout at Hobb's. Five goblins. Sheep home."

He jerks his chin at you. "She cut a runner in two at twenty-five paces. A black line, clean."

Ilsa's pencil stops for a moment. Then she counts the ears and the cores, and writes.

She pays it out in fifths. Your share is a silver and nine copper, pushed across the wood.

The red coat pockets his and heads for the hearth. The others follow, talking of supper.

As you turn to go, Ilsa catches your eye and speaks low. "Nala. Stay a moment, would you?"
