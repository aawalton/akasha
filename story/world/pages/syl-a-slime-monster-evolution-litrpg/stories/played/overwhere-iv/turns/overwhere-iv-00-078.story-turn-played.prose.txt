You twist onto your side and swing the spear across, low, for the goblin's neck.

Your back seizes as you turn. The blade goes wide, a hand short, and slices only air.

The goblin's spear comes down. You jerk aside, and the point tears a shallow gash in your side.

You roll, get a knee under you, and shove up onto your feet, spear crosswise.

The spear goblin wrenches its point free of the dirt and squares up to you.

Up the trail behind it, the two knives come on at a run, and a sling whirrs.

Too late to run. The club goblin bursts past the spear, panting, and swings wide at your head.
