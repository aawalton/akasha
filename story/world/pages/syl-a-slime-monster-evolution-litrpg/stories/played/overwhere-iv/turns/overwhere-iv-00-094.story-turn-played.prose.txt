You turn and sprint down the deer trail for the ford.

The scouts come shrieking after you, keeping their distance, calling your place to the file behind.

Slings whirr. You weave. One stone hums past. Another cracks off a yew.

A third takes you high on the left shoulder. You stumble, catch yourself, and keep running.

Your sense lays the trail out under you a stride ahead. The roar stays at your back.

A mile and more. Your shoulder throbs. Your breath burns. The trees thin.

You burst out of the Tangle into the morning light and splash into the ford, knee-deep.

Across the water, behind the drystone wall, Dace's shield rises. Merrit's hand is already glowing.

You scramble up the bank and over the wall.

Behind you nine goblins pour out of the trees, shrieking, straight for the ford.

The hobgoblin with the maul shoulders to the front, bellowing, and wades in first.
