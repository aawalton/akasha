"I've been working hard on a spear skill," you say. "It's specialized for armor piercing, but turns out that works great on hardwood. What is hardwood but the armor of a tree?"

You smirk.

The herdsman cocks his head, ear toward you. "Spear skill," he repeats, and grunts. Adventurers.

"Anything else you need from me?" you ask. "Need the branches trimmed, or the trunk chopped up?"

He brightens, and stumps along the fallen trunk, slapping the bark as he goes.

"Twenty foot from the butt, that's the shaft. Cut it there. Top's firewood for the reeve's men."

He stops at the first of four thick limbs, each a foot through. "And these off, clean, all four."

"There's my axe on the sledge, if your spear wants a rest. My back's past oak limbs, girl."
