"Definitely," you say. "Next one? Any will do."

Anwen smiles, and unlocks a heavy volume with a cracked spine. "My favourite. The vale chronicle."

Three hundred years of the Wendmere Vale. Floods, plagues, reeves, harvests, in a dozen hands.

Goblins in the Tangle rise and ebb in waves, it says. The last great wave was forty years ago.

Crowstone Quarry was worked out and shut thirty years back. That same year, a cave-in there killed four men. Folk called the place unlucky after.

The newest pages are in one careful hand. The last entry notes the mill shaft cracking this summer.

You look up. Anwen is watching you over her mending. "Those last pages are mine," she says.

"Five books left. Another, child, or will you rest your eyes?"
