You tell Dace what you know. Two goblins, child-sized. One with a flint spear, one with a rusty knife. The spear one took your thrust in the chest and bled hard. The knife one has a cut neck. Both fled into the Tangle.

You tell him about the gap in Hobb's far wall, the bare-toed tracks, the torn fleece in the mud.

Dace listens with his arms folded, nodding. "Small ones. Scouts, likely. Where there's two, there's a camp."

The thin hooded woman at the hearth doesn't say a word. She watches the way you hold your spear.

"We go to Hobb's gap at noon," Dace says. "Show us the gap after your drill. Even share of ears."

The one in the red coat snorts into his cup. Dace ignores him. "Think on it. We leave from here."

You say goodnight and walk back down the lane to the gatehouse. The bunk room is dark. You sleep hard.

Grey light. Holt's hand on the curtain.

The warmth behind your ribs is full again, round and heavy.

Below the wall, the slimes have heaped up again in the night. Dell looks at them, then at you.

"Heard you drove off two goblins." He grins, not kindly. "Limping lamb-girl. Two goblins."

The laps come easier than last time. Your legs are stronger. You finish in the middle of the pack, not last.

Forms, then shields. Then Holt looks the line over. "Farrow. With her again. Let's see."

Dell comes in hard from the first exchange. He isn't going easy this time. His spear cracks off your shield.

You give ground. You watch. He lunges, big, and his whole left side swings open.

This time you're not late. Your blunt spear thumps into his ribs, right under his arm.

Holt calls it. "Touch. Hers."

The yard goes quiet. Wat whoops. Dell stares at you.

Holt doesn't say another word about it. But she nods once at you before she turns to the next pair.

After porridge, you walk up to the hall. The Four are at the hearth, packing for the road.

Ilsa beckons you to the counter. A fresh notice is pinned up beside it.

GOBLINS AT HOBB'S. 1 SILVER AN EAR.

"Forty-seven cores in a day. Goblins driven off." Ilsa taps the notice. "You're past slime work."

"Go with the Four on this. Not alone, on a bronze tag. Or there's Old Selby's herbs, or the oak."
