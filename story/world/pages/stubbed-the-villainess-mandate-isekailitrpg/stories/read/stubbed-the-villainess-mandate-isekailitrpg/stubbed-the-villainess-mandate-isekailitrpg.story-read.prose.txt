The Holy Mandate is broken.

A slash—and the rotten Holy Empress’ head is sliced by a guillotine. The saintess, the hero, and the people finally won, returning their broken nation’s Holy Mandate now that the saintess, the main character, is crowned to replace the dead villainess.

Or at least, that was the ending of another otome novel that a spoiled and bratty rich girl read till midnight. But suddenly, she woke up as the dead Holy Empress two years before her coronation, and six years before her inevitable death. Therefore, that kind of ending…was unacceptable!

With a high-pitched, girly squeak, Alexa Theresa von Wolgast’s voice reverberated in her new room.

“I should have read that novel better!”
