"Anything you need from me before morning?" you ask.

Hild looks you over once more, from the clogs up to the hands. Then she ticks it off on her fingers.

"Come fed. Come shod. Come with those hands greased and slept on, not raw." A fourth finger. "Bring only yourself. I've a hooked knife and a rush basket, and I count both back at dusk."

Her eyes go past you to Joan at the board, and her voice drops.

"One thing more, and it's for tonight. Joan's worn thin with four days of watching that child. Sit an hour with Bet tonight, so her mother can shut her eyes. I'll show you what to do."
