"I'm not sure I'll stay forever," you say. "But for now I would be grateful for a place to be safe. And glad to contribute what I can."

Hild weighs that, her hand on the door. Then she gives one short nod.

"For now. That's honest, anyhow. I'd sooner that than a promise off a stranger's tongue."

She shifts her weight in the doorway.

"Here's my word, then. You're safe to have about, and you work willing. I'll say that much." Her eyes stay on yours. "What you were before, I'll not speak for. That's yours to carry, not mine."

At the board, Joan lets out a breath and goes back to wiping it down.

Hild settles the basket higher on her arm.

"Pot's on the shelf. Work it in tonight, mind, or it's wasted. And if you're for picking, come to the mill at first light. I'll show you which leaves to take." She lifts a finger. "Pick me the wrong ones and they're no good to anyone, so you'll learn them from me first."
