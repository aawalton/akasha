Arthur was a top-tier spy, right up until he broke the first rule of espionage: never uncover a secret that’ll get you killed.

Oops. Now he’s dead. 

Well… reborn actually, in a brutal System-driven world obsessed with voluntary slavery. That’s right, people here choose to be slaves. Apparently, freedom is overrated.

But Arthur? He’s not exactly the “yes, master” type.

Armed with sarcasm, spy training, and a system-gifted, wildly overpowered skill set, Arthur decides to do what he does best: ruin everyone’s evil plans, one corrupt tyrant at a time. If they can’t find him, they can’t stop him, and they definitely can’t kill him.

Expect betrayals, brutal boss fights, inappropriate jokes, and just a hint of accidental heroism.

🔥 What to expect:

- A LitRPG that focuses on story over stat sheets.

- MC with a powerful class, but it comes with strings attached.

- A sarcastic, strategic MC who wins with brains, not brute force

- Don’t expect the MC to punch a slime in Chapter 1 and instantly unlock god-tier powers.

- Expect spycraft, quiet revenge, and just enough humor to survive it all.

- One-Sided Romance (from Other Characters), No Harem

- Expect 3-5 new chapters each week.
