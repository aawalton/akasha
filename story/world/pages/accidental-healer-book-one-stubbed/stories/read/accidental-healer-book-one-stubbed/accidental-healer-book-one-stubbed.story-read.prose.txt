Layton just wanted a weekend alone in the wilderness. Instead, he woke up in a nightmare.

Earth has been inducted into a multiversal system, and survival is no longer guaranteed. Forced into a brutal wave dungeon with no warning, no tutorial, and no gear, Layton accidentally locks himself into the weakest class—Healer.

That’s when things start feeling… off.

He’s leveling way too fast. His so-called “support” class? Feels anything but weak. And his only ally? A too-intelligent, bloodthirsty predator that might be more than just an animal.

Something is wrong with this system. Layton just has to survive long enough to figure out what.
