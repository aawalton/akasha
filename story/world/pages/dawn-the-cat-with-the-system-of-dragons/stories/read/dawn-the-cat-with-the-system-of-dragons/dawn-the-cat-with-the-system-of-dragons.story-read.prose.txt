Dawn, a stray black cat, adopted an infant dragon…

But she didn't know it was a reincarnated dragon, and she certainly didn’t know about its system. In fact, she didn’t even know about the world of systems.

One day, the dragon’s system deemed its host too undeveloped, and it decided to make her the new host… This turned her life upside down.

From the pop-up windows obscuring her vision to the system taking control of her body to fight a group of wyverns, the dragon's system brought her nothing but trouble.

Sooner or later, she has to operate the system. But her being a stray cat, will she ever be able to become literate enough to read the system window?

More importantly, how is she supposed to deal with all the chaos brought by this damn system, especially when she needs to protect her six troublemakers?

Important note:

👉🏻 This is a slow-paced progression fantasy novel, and it is centered around action, adventure, survival, and weak-to-strong growth.

👉🏻 Stats are introduced in Chapter 12. Until then, the story focuses on Dawn, her struggles, and how the system transferred itself to her.
