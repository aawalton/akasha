"Uh... hi?" Your voice comes out higher than you expect, and softer, and it startles you more than the box did. "I'm guessing I've been isekai'd, but who are you? Are you... the System?"

The words under the window vanish at once. New ones come fast, faster than anyone could type, and then stop dead halfway through a line.

*Yes! Yes. I'm the System. That's what everybody calls me, anyway. My name is*

The line sits there unfinished. The cursor blinks at the end of it. Out on the hill a sheep complains about something, and the wind lays the gorse flat and lets it up again.

*Iris. My name's Iris.*

*I don't know that word. Isekai. What does it mean?*

A pause, and then one more line, slower than the rest.

*And I don't know how you got here, if that's what you're asking. You weren't anywhere I could see, and then at twenty to five you were lying by that plinth.*
