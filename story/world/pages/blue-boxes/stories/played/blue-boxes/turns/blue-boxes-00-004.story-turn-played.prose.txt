"Umm, if the windows are only supposed to be for facts, how are you using them to talk to me?"

*Oh, that part's easy. They're mine.*

*I write every one of them. A level, a class, a quest. I just keep to facts.*

The words sit a moment. Then a new line starts, stops, and starts again.

*Keeping to facts is the rule. The System doesn't take sides, and a window that says more than the facts is taking one. So I've broken a rule with you three times now.*

*Four.*

A thin cloud slides off the sun, and the light comes warmer across the clover and the worn arc on the plinth.

*I'm not sorry. I thought you should know.*
