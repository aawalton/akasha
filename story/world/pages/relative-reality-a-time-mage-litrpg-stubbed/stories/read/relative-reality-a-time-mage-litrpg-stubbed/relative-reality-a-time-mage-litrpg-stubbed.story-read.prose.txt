Anyone with enough power can break reality. Bending it to your will is another matter entirely.

Despite going to sleep in his apartment, Rowan wakes up to see the towering trees of a redwood forest. With nothing but his frog pyjamas and a sphere stuffed with the power of reality, Rowan must make his way back to civilisation. When he does, he discovers that this is not his planet.

Rowan blindly stumbles his way through a new world filled with giant ants, a feathered family and a city at war with itself. These are the least of his issues, however. With the world intent on tearing itself apart, his education as an architect will not quite be enough to use his new, reality bending powers to stop it from destroying itself.
