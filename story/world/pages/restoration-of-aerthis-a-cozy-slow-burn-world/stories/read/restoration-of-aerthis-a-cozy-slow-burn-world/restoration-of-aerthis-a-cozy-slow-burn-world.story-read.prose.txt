Aerthis is a dying world, its lands scarred by past calamities and drained of magic. Elynara awakens with no clear past, only a restored body and a deep fascination with magic. Her quiet curiosity and endless optimism her only weapons.

With no grand prophecy to follow, she's left charting her own path. To explore, restore, and uncover the forgotten wonders of the world at her own pace. As she revives lost mana, rebuilds what was forgotten, and unravels hidden mysteries, the dangers that be will not sit idly by. 

Watch as she slowly breathes life back into a broken world - one step at a time.
