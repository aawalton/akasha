You stay where you are, kneeling at the stream mouth with the salt still on your tongue, and speak to the empty air.

"Okay, isekai protocol. System? Status? Character sheet?"

The air in front of you stays empty. The light stays the same hard white it has been all day. Your words hang there a moment and are gone.

You wait. Behind you the surf builds and breaks and hisses back. The gulls cry. The stream slides past your knees over the black sand.

"If you left me here with truly nothing, I might as well fucking die now," you say. "And then I won't be any entertainment for anyone."

Still the air stays empty. The surf, the gulls, the stream. Only those answer.

Your voice carries. It goes up the stream between the reeds, the new voice cracking a little on the last word.

Twenty paces upstream, the nearer lizard's head lifts off the mud and turns toward you.

Its half-closed eyes open all the way. They fix on you.

Its tongue slides out, forked and dark, and tastes the air in your direction.
