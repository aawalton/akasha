Mitsuko lived her life with a sword in hand, outmatched by those with magic.

Then the barrier dropped.

Sealed from the outside world, Mitsuko now lives the same week on the Prismatic Archipelago over and over, and even death isn't an escape.

Access to a new magic, lost to the ages. Negotiating with sages who know far more than they say. And facing down legendary foes plucked from history. Mitsuko will uncover it all. No matter how many times she needs to turn back time.

But how many times can you turn back the hands of time before the clock finally breaks?

------

What to expect -

-Time Magic

-Adventure and Exploration

-Action!

-Challenges to Unlock New Magic

-Unique Biomes

-Soft Numbers Go Up

-Occasional Blood and Guts

-A gamelit story heavily inspired by Windwaker and Majora's Mask

What NOT to Expect

-Romance -Isekai -Cozy
