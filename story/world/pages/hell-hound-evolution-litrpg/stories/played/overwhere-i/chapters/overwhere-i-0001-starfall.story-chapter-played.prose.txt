The last thing you remember is your back window. A book open on your knee, the evening going blue outside the glass, your eyes getting heavy.

Then cold water, running over your fingers.

You open your eyes. You are lying on your side on a bank of smooth grey stones, one hand trailing in a stream. The water is clear and very cold, sliding over a shelf of flat rock a few inches deep. Pine needles drift past your wrist.

You sit up, and your body is wrong.

Your arms are thin, the wrists narrow, the hands small and pale with freckles across the backs. Long dark red hair swings forward over your shoulder and nearly into the water. Your old grey shirt hangs off you, the collar slipping to one side, the hem down near your knees when you sit. Your black tights are still on. Your feet are bare, and small.

You look down at the rest of yourself once, and then you look at the stream instead.

A cart track comes down out of tall dark pines, crosses the stream here over a bed of flat stones, and climbs the far bank toward a ridge of bare grey rock. The mud at the water's edge is printed with wheel ruts, the hooves of something heavy, and a line of paw prints wider than your hand. Beyond the ridge, thin chimney smoke goes up into a pale morning sky.

Then letters open in the air in front of you. Blue, square, perfectly steady, as if they were cut out of the light.

[New Species Integration: Human]

[Level 1]

You blink. They stay. You turn your head, and they turn with you.

Something else is there too. Not in the letters: under your skin, behind your breastbone, a pressure like a held breath, patient and enormous. When you notice it, it notices you back. It does not feel dangerous. It feels like yours.

Somewhere up the track, a branch snaps under something big.

You keep your eyes on the blue letters.

"Okay, Isekai protocol," you say quietly. "Status? System?"

You fix on the letters and push at them, the way you'd stare at a word until it gives up more.

The two lines fold away. A larger screen opens where they were, the same blue, just as steady.

[Status: Name ???, Species Human, Level 1, Class: None]

[Stats: Strength 8, Dexterity 10, Vigor 9, Attunement 30, Luck 12]

[Vitals: Health 40/40, Mana 120/120, Stamina 30/30]

[Reserves: Earth 20/20, Fire 20/20, Wind 20/20, Water 20/20, Electricity 20/20]

[Skills:]

The Skills line stops at its colon. Below it, set apart from the rest, one line sits on its own.

[Legacy: ???]

A second, smaller line opens under the screen.

[Unrecognized request. Please see the help menu for available functions.]

You read the whole screen in one pass, then go back. Attunement stands out, more than double anything else on the list. Your Mana is triple your Health.

Your eyes come to rest on the last line. Behind your breastbone, the pressure stirs, as if it heard its name.

You push at the question marks harder. The letters stay steady and blank.

Overhead the morning sky is a pale red, and the sun in it is small and white.

Up the far bank, heavy footfalls come down the ridge track. They are unhurried, and they aren't trying to be quiet.

A shaggy black beast the size of a small bear shoulders out of the pines above the ford. Its muzzle is sooty black, and its short snout is crowded with pointed teeth. It is thick in the neck and heavy in the bone, and its forepaws are as wide as the prints in the mud.

It stops at the water's edge, twenty yards off across the shallows, and sees you sitting on the stones.

It lowers its head and hisses at you like an enormous cat.

"Oh, perfect!" you say. "A test subject!"

You point a finger at the beast across the ford. You focus on fire, willing a narrow beam of intense flame from your fingertip straight through the thing.

The pressure behind your breastbone answers at once, as if it had been waiting to be asked.

Heat pours up your arm. A thin line of white-gold fire leaps from your fingertip and out across the water.

It crosses the twenty yards in an instant, straight and narrow, exactly as you meant it. The beast's haunches are bunching under it for the rush, and the beam punches into its chest and out through its back.

Its dry, matted fur catches all along its flank. The hiss turns into a shriek.

It lurches two steps into the shallows and collapses. Steam and smoke boil up round it.

The beam cuts off. You look at your fingertip. It is unmarked, and barely warm.

Behind your breastbone there is a small hollow where the fire came from. It is already filling back in.

[Legacy: Starfall]

[New active skill obtained: Starfall Surge]

[Starfall Surge: raw element loosed from the reserves as a blast, a ward or a burst of speed.]

[Prey Eliminated!]

[Experience Gained!]

[You have leveled up!]

[You are now level 2!]

[+2 Strength] [+2 Dexterity] [+2 Vigor] [+4 Attunement] [+1 Luck]

A warm rush runs through your body as the lines come up, and you feel steadier. Sharper.

The beast lies still in the far shallows, smoke drifting off its burnt fur.
