You crouch and pull the tin token out of the mud, and wipe it on your leggings.

It's stamped with a sheaf of reeds and a name: Ewan Dell.

You slip the token into your pack for proof.

You lay a palm on the ground at the den mouth and weave earth and air into a ripple, to feel for any wolf still inside.

It spreads into the ground under the roots. Hollows come back sharp, living bodies only faintly.

Three linked hollows lie under the roots, and every one is empty and still.

Beside the den, down in the drowned pine's roots, the ripple finds two small hard knots. They come back hard and cold, colder than the wet earth around them.
