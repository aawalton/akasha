By the light of the flame over your palm, you follow the mule's prints and the cart's ruts east.

While your well sits high you run the stride in short bursts, the ground kicking back under you. As it settles lower you drop to a walk and let it fill.

At a passing place the ruts tangle with older tracks, and you cast about a while before you find them.

You pick them up again past the passing place and go on east through the oaks.

About three miles from the quarry, a little after midnight, a glow of embers shows ahead.

It's a roadside well with a stone lip. Beside it stands Osric's mule cart, the mule hobbled close by.

As your flame comes on toward the cart, a bowstring creaks in the dark beside it.

"Who's there? Stand and say, or I loose!" Tobin calls, his voice high and shaking.
