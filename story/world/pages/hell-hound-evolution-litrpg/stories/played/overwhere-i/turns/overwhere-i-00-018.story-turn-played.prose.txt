You sleep in one of the Stag's small guest rooms, on a straw mattress under a wool blanket.

You wake rested at first light to hens, a cockerel, and the smell of smoke from the hearth in the hall.

Downstairs Garrick sets out oat bread, a boiled egg and a mug of small ale. The village purse covers it.

"Where's the bounty board?" you ask.

Garrick laughs until his beard shakes. "Board? That's in Wendlow, three days east. We've the reeve's slate. By the door, lady."

Beside the Stag's door hangs a slate in a wooden frame, chalked in a careful hand. You read it easily, though you have never seen these letters before in your life.

REEDLURKERS robbing the eel traps at the fen edge: 3 silver a head.

GRUBBOARS rooting the oat strips south of the palisade: 5 silver for the old boar.

Below a scratched line, copied from Wendlow's Board:

GHOST-EYE, Drakewolf, the Greyfen: 25 gold, head.

HARL VOSS, deserter, Cutter's Quarry: 30 gold. His men: 2 gold each.
