Prone on the rim, you lift both hands and loose a fire beam from each, crossing them down at the nearest mound.

They meet on the bigger man's back. He burns through and drops behind the mound, dead.

At the first flash Voss snatches up the sack and runs east out of the fold, his shield slung on his back.

The other two spin and loose up at the rim. One bolt smacks into your left shoulder.

The second hisses off the granite beside your head.

You cross your beams on the next man as he cranks. They rake his side, and he staggers.

The burned blademan looses again. The bolt skims your forearm and buries itself in the needles.

Your third pair of beams meets on the cranking man's chest, and he falls across his crossbow.

The burned blademan flings his crossbow away and kneels by the mound, hands high.

"I yield! Gods, I yield! Don't burn me!" he screams up at the rim.

Out past the fold's east lip, Voss is vanishing into the pines, shield on his back and the sack with him.
