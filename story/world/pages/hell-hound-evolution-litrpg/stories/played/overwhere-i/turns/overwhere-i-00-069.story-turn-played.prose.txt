You will earth and air together. A grooved slug turns in the air before you, spinning up to a whine, and beside it you hold a second working of air, ready to catch anything they throw.

Voss stands eight yards off, still, his eyes on the cask. His men stand loose around him, six to twelve yards out.

You send the slug at his forehead and hold it on its path the whole way.

It cracks into the steel cap above his brow and snaps his head back. Blood runs down his face.

He stays on his feet. His shield comes up, and with a roar he charges.

At the crack Osric dives under his cart.

Behind Voss, someone slips the Drakewolf's leash. It streaks at you, low and fast.

You twist aside too late. Its jaws close on your forearm, and its weight drags you down onto the road.

Two of the five raise crossbows. The other three draw blades and come on.

Voss is two strides away, axe high, and the Drakewolf's teeth are locked in your arm.
