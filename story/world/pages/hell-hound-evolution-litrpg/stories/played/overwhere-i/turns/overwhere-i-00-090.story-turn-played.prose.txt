You take the pot and smear the salve into your wounds: thigh, hip, shoulder, ribs and forearm.

It stings, sharp and green-smelling, and then the seeping stops. The pot is half gone by the time you're done.

"I forget how many," you tell Tobin. "We can count ears and tags if you want."

"For Voss, I've got his head in his sack here." You open the sack and lift it out by the hair.

Tobin goes white and turns away. Then he turns back, grinning. "Fenwatch will sing of this!"

"Guess we have two bounties to turn in now," you say.

Osric lets out a long whistle. "Thirty gold, that head, at the Board. Thirty!"

"Mind, the Board pays only in Wendlow. That's two more days east by cart from here."

He eyes the head. "It'll turn before then. I've salt in the cart, four copper a sack, if you want."
