You gather fist-sized stones along the heath's edge and sit down in the heather to shape them into rifled slugs.

Six slugs come from each minute of earth working. You stop at sixty, about eight pounds in your pack. It rides easily. Any more and it would drag on you when you run.

[Starfall Surge has reached Level 5.]

[Attunement +3, from Starfall Surge.]

You walk back to Fenwatch in the late light and take a bed and supper at the Tarred Stag. Four copper goes to Garrick Pell. You sleep well and wake with your mana full.

In the morning you go down to Brack's by Otter Brook. Brack kneels, laces the boots onto your feet, grunts at the fit, and takes his three silver.

You set out west from the ford into the Greyfen, toward the middle fen and the pine island. You wade the firm stretches and skim the wet ones on air and water, and the miles go quickly.

While your mana comes back you practise scouting, willing water into a clear curve held in the air, a lens to see further.

Fire, air and water woven together slip apart at once.

Fire in a lens only sets the air shimmering, and the view swims.

A single water lens held in the air brings the far reeds about three times closer.

You weave air and water to hold two lenses in line. The first tries come out blurred.

After about an hour you have it: a spyglass bringing the fen ten times closer.

[Attunement +3, from Starfall Weave.]

Toward midday the pine island shows ahead, a dark ridge of drowned trees above the water. A low rise of dead alders stands six hundred yards east of it, and you make for their cover.

The wind blows steady from the west, off the island, into your face.

You creep up among the grey alder trunks slowly, holding the spyglass low in their shade.

Along the island's east edge, in the shade under the pines, the pack lies up in the heat.

Nine grown Drakewolves, scaled grey-green, with lizard muzzles and crests. One is half again the size of the rest, its left eye milky: Ghost-Eye.

Three half-grown pups sprawl among them.

One wolf sits upright at the edge of the shade, on watch. Its gaze passes over you unmoved.
