You grin at Jory. "Thanks for the challenge! That was fun!"

Jory laughs and falls in beside you. "Fun, she says. I'm coming to tell the reeve myself."

The two of you cross back over the Greyback and reach Fenwatch a little after noon.

At the Stag, Garrick heats the tub again. You pay him two copper and scrub off the fen.

When you come out, Jory is at the long table with Agathe, talking fast and waving his hands.

Agathe hears him out, then counts nine silver onto the table. "Three lurkers. On Jory's word."

You ask about Ghost-Eye on the slate, the one with the big bounty.

The hall goes quiet. Garrick stops wiping a mug.

Agathe sets down her tally stick. "That one's no reedlurker. A Drakewolf, half again the size of any other, with a milky left eye. It leads eight more. They hunt the middle fen at dawn and dusk. Herd their prey into the water, then cut it down."

"It's killed two reed-cutters from Sallow Hythe this summer. And it grows stronger every week. The pack dens on a dry island of drowned pines, half a day's wading into the fen. And the twenty-five gold's paid only at Antler Hall in Wendlow, on its head."

She looks at you a long moment. "It's past anyone here to stand against. I'd not have you try alone."

Jory, quietly: "Rowan Coalby knows that ground best. His Sedge can follow them by scent."
