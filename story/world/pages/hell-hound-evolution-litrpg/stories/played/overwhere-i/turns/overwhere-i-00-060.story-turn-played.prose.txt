You set two silver down on the table. "I'll pay you two silver now for information on what a drake-pearl is good for. Is it something I could use?"

Osric grins and sweeps the coins into his coat. "I never turn away coin for talk."

"The alchemists in Wendlow grind them into night-sight draughts," he says. "And a mage can drain one like a fat mana crystal." He shakes his head. "Burning gold, that is."

Your purse is lighter by two silver.

He keeps whatever the alchemists pay behind a smile, and leans in over his hat.

"Two gold and five silver, then, and it's off your hands tonight."
