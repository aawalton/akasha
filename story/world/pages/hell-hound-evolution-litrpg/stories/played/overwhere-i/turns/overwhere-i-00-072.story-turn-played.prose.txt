They've penned themselves in stone. You roll off the road and scramble for the scrub and boulders that run toward the pit.

A bolt from the unseen shooter beats your ward and punches into your thigh as you go.

Limping, you crawl from boulder to boulder until the lip is twenty-five yards off. Past the last boulder the ground lies open.

From here you can see down into it: an old cut, forty yards across and fifteen feet deep, its back wall stepped in galleries.

From cover you will fire into earth and pour it into the pit floor. A yard-wide patch of stone begins to glow.

The heat drives the crew back off the floor and into the galleries, out of your sight.

You hold the working and push. The stone glows brighter, red to orange, and cracks spider across it, but that's as far as it goes. It won't run.

Half a minute in, the patch bursts with a crack like a shot, flinging hot chips across the empty pit floor.

At the burst Voss booms out from behind the stone. "Parley! Hold your fire, woman!"

"Free road," he shouts. "For the pedlar and every Fenwatch cart, for good. And the toll silver I'm holding. Yours."
