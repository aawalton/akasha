You draw another slug and pour two weaves into it, the cost dragging hard on your mana.

You rise on one knee, lift your aim higher for the drop, and loose.

The slug arcs out over the channel and falls spent, splashing a few yards short of Ghost-Eye.

Ghost-Eye watches the splash, then turns its good eye full on your hummock and holds it there.

Its snarl drops away. It stands easy at the water's edge, unhurried.

It gives a low, short growl to the wolves at its side.

The two biggest slip off the shore, one north and one south, and drop into the reeds.

Ghost-Eye and the last wolf stay on the shore, watching your hummock.

The other two are gone from sight in the reeds, one north of you and one south.
