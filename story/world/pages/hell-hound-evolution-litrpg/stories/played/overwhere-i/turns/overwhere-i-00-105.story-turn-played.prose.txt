"Eh, just the second pearl for now," you say. "Range should cover what I need most."

Ilse nods once, briskly, as if you've passed a small test. "Ten gold all told. Five now, with your pearl, and five when I hand it over."

You count five gold coins onto the counter, one after another, and set the drake-pearl beside them.

Ilse sweeps the coins and the pearl together into a little felt-lined box and snaps the lid shut.

She reaches across, takes your hand in her dry, inky one, and loops a twist of fine wire around your finger, pinches it snug with her inky fingertips, slides it off, and ties a paper tag to it with a scrawl you can't read upside down.

From a drawer she takes a small tin claim tag stamped with a number and presses it into your palm, closing your fingers over it with her own.

"Afternoon of day eleven. Bring the tag and the other five, and the ring's yours."
