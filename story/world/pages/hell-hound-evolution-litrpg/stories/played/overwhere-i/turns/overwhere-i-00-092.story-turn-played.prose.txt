"Of course," you say, and dig his one silver out of the sack and hand it back.

He pockets it with a grateful bob of his hat. "The rest is spoils, and yours."

"Let's get on our way," you say. "I'm anxious to get to Wendlow and get a nice hot bath!"

You warn him now, so it won't startle him later: once you're rolling, you mean to use your magic to help the cart along, gently, nothing that'll break anything.

Osric laughs and jerks a thumb at the mule, standing head-low by the well. "Now? Look at the beast."

"It ran half a mile with the shafts banging its flanks. It would drop dead in them tonight."

"We roll at six. Sleep first. You've earned it twice over, and you look it."
