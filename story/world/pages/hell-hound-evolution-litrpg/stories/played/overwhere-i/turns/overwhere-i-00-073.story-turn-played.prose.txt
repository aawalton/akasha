To parley, Voss steps out into a gallery mouth in the pit's back wall, shield up.

He stands about sixty yards from your boulder, the steel cap still on his head.

You reach for the cap with earth and fire, meaning to heat it on his skull until he tears it off, and to put a slug through his head when he does.

Your working stretches out thirty yards and stops there, well short of the gallery mouth.

You judge the ground. Even from the open lip, the gallery mouth would be forty yards off.

And out on the lip you'd be in the open, under the crossbows in the galleries and the shooter in the trees.

Voss stays in the gallery mouth, shield up, cap on.
