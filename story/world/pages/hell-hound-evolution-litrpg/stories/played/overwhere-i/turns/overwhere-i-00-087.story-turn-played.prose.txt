You fix your gaze on the growling Drakewolf and let the fire pour out through your eyes.

Two beams leap from them and meet on its burned chest. It drops in the gallery mouth, dead.

Your sight swims with dazzle for a moment, then clears.

You step past it to the blademen. From them you take 10 copper, two right ears and two tags.

In the next gallery mouth lies the bareheaded crewman you shot between the eyes. You take his ear, his tag and 6 copper.

Every man of the crew is in your sack now, an ear and a tag apiece, and the head of Voss besides.

You climb out of the pit to the road, the sack and the fine crossbow from the trees slung on your shoulders.

Below the quarry the road runs east in the dark toward Wendlow.

Fresh in the dust, the mule's hoofprints and the cart's ruts run off east ahead of you.
