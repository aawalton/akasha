Behind your boulders you dig the two pale blue crystals out of your pack. They lie cold in your bare palm.

They hum faintly against you. You will yourself to draw on the first, and pull.

Mana seeps into you, a slow, steady filling behind your breastbone that you know without needing to see it. In half a minute the crystal runs dry.

It crumbles to dust between your fingers.

You draw on the second the same way, and it too crumbles away. Your reserve sits the fuller for both.

As you draw, a man groans somewhere down in the galleries.

Another voice hisses him quiet.
