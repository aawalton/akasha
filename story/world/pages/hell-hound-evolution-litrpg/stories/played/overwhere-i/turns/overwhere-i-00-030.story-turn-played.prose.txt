You reach for earth and water again and bind them. The same heavy, wet pull.

This time you will it down as a grip instead of out as a ripple, into the bank by the first slide, to pull the body out.

The mud gives to your will, slow and heavy, and closes on the den to take hold.

There is nothing to take hold of. The grip closes on a hollow of trodden mud. The den is empty.

Whatever scrabbled under the bank was leaving it.

You let the grip go. The mud settles.

Out in the channel, where the bubbles stopped, a thin thread of bubbles rises through the dark water.
