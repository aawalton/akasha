"Sure, makes sense to preserve this head and the ears," you say. "I'll take the salt for that."

You pay Osric four copper, and he hauls a sack of coarse salt down from the cart.

One sack is enough. You pack the head of Voss and the eight ears in salt inside the dead man's own sack, working it into every hollow.

It takes a quarter hour by the embers, Tobin holding the flame for you and looking anywhere else.

"Three weeks that'll keep," Osric says. Then he counts five silver into your palm.

"For the road. The east carts owe you, every one of them, and I'll say so in Wendlow."

He rubs the back of his neck, sheepish, and glances at the sack of coin.

"Only... the silver toll Voss took off me at the quarry. One silver. Might I have it back?"
