From the marsh across the channel, the snappers on Ghost-Eye's body are about thirty-five yards off.

Rowan holds Sedge back on the marsh, Sedge barking at the turtles.

You spin a grooved slug and send it at the nearer snapper's bobbing head.

It punches clean through the head as the snapper tears at the meat. The snapper jerks back, badly hurt.

At the crack, the second snapper pulls its head in, slides for deep water and is gone under.

The hurt one draws its head into its shell and lumbers for the channel.

Your second slug slams into its shell. It keeps going.

Your third strikes as it reaches the deep water, and the snapper goes still.

The dead snapper sinks out of sight in the channel, too deep to reach by hand.
