"Oh, that little thing?" you call back cheerfully. "That was me. Thought it might be tasty. If you'll help with transport, I'd be happy to contribute it for a feast."

The youth's mouth falls open again. Hessa's face stays exactly as it was.

She wades down into the shallows to the carcass, the spear still on her back, and crouches beside it. She turns its head with one hand and looks at the chest, at the neat round hole burned clean through. She puts two fingers to the scorched fur at its edge. Then she looks at the hole in its back.

The youth splashes down after her. "There's no arrow. There's no— it's cooked right through, Hessa!"

Hessa stands. She looks across the water at you for a long moment, then nods once.

"Your kill, then. There's a bounty on it. One gold and the pelt, from the reeve at Fenwatch."

"Brute's tough eating, but stewed long it'll feed half the village. They'll not say no."

"It's the weight of three men. I'll skin and quarter it here." She jerks her chin at the youth. "Tobin. Back to the village. Borrow a cart. Tell the reeve the Brute's dead."

Tobin hesitates, staring at you. Then he turns and runs up the ridge track, his bow bouncing.

Hessa draws her skinning knife and bends to the carcass.

Without looking up, she says, "You've a name, lady?"
