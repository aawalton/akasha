You lean your elbows on the counter, beside the row of ears.

"I'm level 10 now. I was 7 when I met Voss. 4 when I went after the wolves."

A grin splits your face, too wide, a little wild around the edges.

"I know, I'm mad and driving for an early grave, but I got the job done."

You tip your head at the face on the counter.

"Didn't know Voss was 24, but that explains why he took so much killing. He was harder to kill on his own than the entire wolf pack."

Grete gives one short laugh, a bark, gone as soon as it's out.

"The march has a use for mad ones. Gods know it's short of them."

Her eyes go over you once more, measuring. Four to ten in a handful of days. She nods slowly, as if that's no more than she'd expect from someone who keeps picking fights far above her weight and walking away from them.

She stoops. Iron clanks under the counter, a key grinds in a lock, and a strongbox lid creaks up out of sight. She straightens with nothing in her hands yet, pulls the ledger toward her, dips a pen and holds it poised over a fresh line.

"I'll need a name for the receipt," she says.

The pen stays where it is.

"My Analyze shows none for you. What do I write?"
