You kneel on the bank by the first slide and reach for earth and water, willing them into one.

They bind: a heavy, wet pull behind your breastbone.

You send it out through the bank and the channel as a ripple, and wait for it to come back.

It comes back as a smear of feeling. Wet ground, water, roots, all of it run together into one blur.

You try again, aiming it at the holt you know is there. Again it blurs.

Frustrated, you push the third ripple harder.

It leaves you not as a shiver but as a thump. The bank shudders under your knees. Mud slumps into the channel. Jory swears and steps back from the edge.

From under the bank by the first slide comes a muffled scrabbling. Then silence.

A swirl rises in the dark water below the slide, and a line of bubbles runs out into the channel. It stops in mid-channel. The water goes still.

Something is out of its holt and in the water, and you can't see where.
