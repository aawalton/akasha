You turn the sled for the village. Rowan takes the rope first, and the two of you trade it back and forth as the hours wear on, hauling the head back through the Greyfen.

The fen lets you pass. The long haul home goes quiet, just the drag of the sled through sedge and the slap of water round your boots.

About four you reach the fen edge, and drag the sled up past the Greyback and onto the east road.

About five you bring it through Fenwatch's gate.

Half the village crowds the green to see Ghost-Eye's head on Rowan's sled. Hessa is there, and Tobin, and Agathe on her ash staff, Garrick with his red beard, Osric.

Hessa crouches by the sled and looks the head over a long while: the great grey skull, the scarred scales, the empty socket.

"I hadn't thought to see you back," she tells you plainly.

"Salt it tonight, or it's past the Hall's taking within a week."

Then she stands back and holds her peace, her eyes on you.

Tobin pushes through the crowd, his eyes on the head, then on you.

"Take me with you to Wendlow," he begs. "Please. Take me with you."
