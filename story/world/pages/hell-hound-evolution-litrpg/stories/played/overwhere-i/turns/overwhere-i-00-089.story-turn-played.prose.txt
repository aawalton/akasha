"It's me, Nala," you call. "I took care of Voss and his men. Are you all all right?"

Tobin lowers his bow with a shaky laugh. "Nala! We're whole, both of us. Not a scratch."

Osric scrambles out from under the cart, bruised and stiff, and stares at you as if at a ghost.

In the light of your flame Tobin spots the blood on your shoulder and ribs. He digs in his bag and comes up with a small pot.

"Hessa's salve. Yarrow. It slows bleeding," he says, holding it out to you.

Then the words tumble out of him. "How many were there? Is it true? Is Voss really dead?"
