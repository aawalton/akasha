"Oh, here and there," you say lightly. "A bit of a rolling stone. Always looking for a change and a challenge. Any good challenges near here?"

Hessa's knife pauses a moment at the joint, then goes on. She lets the question of where you're from drop.

The shoulder comes free. She lays the foreleg on the flat stones and wipes the blade on the fur.

"Challenges." She nods west, at the dark pines behind the ford. "The fen's full of them."

"A Drakewolf alpha holds the middle fen. Half again the common size. Milky left eye. Ghost-Eye, we call it. It runs a pack. They hunt at dawn and dusk, and herd what they catch into the water."

"Wendlow's Hunters' Board has twenty-five gold on its head. Still uncollected."

She starts on the second foreleg. "Then there's Harl Voss, east. Deserters from the king's levy, a dozen-odd. They hold Cutter's Quarry, a day down the east road. They toll every cart that passes. The Board has thirty gold on Voss, two on each of his."

She looks up from the joint at last, straight at you.

"Neither of those is for one alone. Not the pack, not Voss's crew. Not for me, either."
