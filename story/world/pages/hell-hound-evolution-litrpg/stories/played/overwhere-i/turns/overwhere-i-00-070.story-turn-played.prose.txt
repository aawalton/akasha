Flat on your back on the road, you will fire into air and fling it out before you in a fan, meant to swallow the wolf, Voss and all five of his men.

Flame roars out ten yards in a half-circle. It rolls over the wolf, over the three blademen, over Voss.

You bend it off your own arm. It licks past and spares you.

The Drakewolf yelps, lets go of your arm and bolts, its fur smoking.

All three blademen catch alight. Their blades clatter to the road as they reel back, beating at the flames on their chests and sleeves.

Voss comes on through the fire behind his shield, singed but upright.

As the fire blooms, both crossbowmen loose at you. Your air ward slaps the first bolt aside. It skips off the road and gashes your hip as it goes. The second you turn clean.

A third bolt hisses out of the trees from somewhere you never saw. The ward turns that one too.

You feed the fire, and it burns hotter and whiter, but the crew are already breaking.

Three sharp whistles. The burning men run for the pit, and Voss goes with them, shield up.

Tobin looses after the nearest, but the man is already running.

Behind you Osric's mule screams and rears, then bolts down the road with the cart, cask and all.
