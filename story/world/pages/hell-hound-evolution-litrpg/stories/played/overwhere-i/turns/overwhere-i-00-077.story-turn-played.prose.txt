You slip back from your boulders and circle east through the pines, keeping your weight light on the hurt leg.

You pick your way over the loose stone without a sound.

At the top of the goat path the dust is churned with boot prints: four men, running, all heading north into the pines.

You work along the east rim toward the back wall, above the galleries.

The back wall holds four gallery mouths, each cut about ten yards into the rock.

From the rim, fifteen yards off and above, you can see into the easternmost one.

Behind a heap of spoil a burned blademan sits against the wall, his sword across his knees.

Beside him the other burned blademan lies moaning.

The sitting man stares at the pit floor, sword in hand, his back to the rim where you crouch above him.
