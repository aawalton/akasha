Curious, you kneel by the drowned pine and reach into the root-bound soil with an earth working.

The soil loosens around the two cold knots and draws them up to the surface between the roots.

You brush the dirt away: two pieces of pale blue crystal, each about a thumb-joint long.

They're cold to hold, and in the shade of the roots they glow faintly.

Held in your palm, each one hums faintly against the mana inside you.
