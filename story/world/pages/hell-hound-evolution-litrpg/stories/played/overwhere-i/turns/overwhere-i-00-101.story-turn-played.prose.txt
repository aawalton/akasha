"Yeah, put me down," you say. "I definitely see more killing things for money in my future."

The corner of Grete's mouth twitches. She hauls a second ledger out from under the counter, thicker and older than the first, its leather gone soft as cloth at the corners, and opens it to the last written page. Below a long column of names in a dozen different inks she writes the date, then Nala Arthur, then Level 10, in the same square hand.

She reaches into a drawer and sets something on the counter with a small click: a badge of dull bronze, cast in the shape of a stag's antler, a pin on the back.

"That marks a rolled hunter."

"Two contracts stand open, now Voss and Ghost-Eye are down." She draws two slips from a pigeonhole behind her and lays them flat on the wood, side by side, turned so you can read them.

She touches the first.

"The margrave's reeve pays 8 gold for a true account of why the Greyfen crystals went dark."

Then the second.

"And 15 gold for the Weir Wyrm, a river serpent taking bargemen at Hobb's Mill weir. Two hours' walk down the Wend. Level 18, by the bargemen's tales; that's all anyone has of it."

Grete taps the slips with a scarred finger.

"First call's yours, Nala Arthur. Which one?"
