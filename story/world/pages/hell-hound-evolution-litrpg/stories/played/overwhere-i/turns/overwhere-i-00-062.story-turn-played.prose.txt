You bid Osric good evening and leave him to his wine.

Garrick heats water for the wooden tub in the back room, his treat for Ghost-Eye's killer. You soak the fen out of your skin, then eat a hot supper by the fire, also on the house.

You sleep the night through in a Stag bed and wake rested.

Before first light you walk out to Rowan's charcoal mounds. About five, Rowan is waiting there with the sled, rope, hatchet and Sedge.

You wade into the Greyfen together, Sedge leading you by the firm going. The pack's fresh tracks run west, away from the island, and the wade out stays quiet.

Five hours of wading and hauling the sled bring you to the pine island near ten.

Ghost-Eye's body lies snagged in the reeds on the north bank, half out of the water. Scavengers have fed on it overnight, but the head is still whole.

Two turtles broad as cart wheels are tearing at its haunches.
