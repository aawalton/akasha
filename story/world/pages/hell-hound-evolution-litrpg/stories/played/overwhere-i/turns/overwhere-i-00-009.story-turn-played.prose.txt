You get up out of the rubble and brush the grit off your palms.

"Hello there!" you call across the water. "I seem to have gotten a bit lost. Would you mind telling me where we are precisely?"

The woman studies you a moment longer. Her eyes go to your bare feet, the oversized grey shirt, your wet red hair, the stones.

The younger one steps up beside her. He's a gangly, sunburnt youth with a hunting bow, and he's gaping at you openly. His leather jerkin is too big for him, and his hanging mouth shows a chipped front tooth.

The woman answers flat and plain. Her voice carries easily over the stream.

"Greyfen Ford. The fen's west of you, that way. Fenwatch is an hour east, over the ridge." "This is the Western March. Aubrevine. The Vinelands, if you're from further in."

"I'm Hessa Vane. I hunt for Fenwatch."

She tips her head at the dead beast in the shallows below her.

"That's the Brute that's been taking our goats. We tracked it here from the village."

"Who killed it?"
