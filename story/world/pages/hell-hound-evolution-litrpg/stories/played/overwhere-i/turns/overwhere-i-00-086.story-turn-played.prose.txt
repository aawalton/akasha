You spin a thin blade of water between your hands and draw it through Voss's neck in one pass.

On him you find a purse holding 1 gold and 2 silver, his sword, and a sealed letter folded inside his jerkin. Round his neck hangs a stamped tin tag on a cord. You take it along with the head.

The sack holds the toll: 36 silver and 40 copper. You add the head, the purse, the letter and the tag, and knot it shut.

You light a small flame over your palm and walk back west along the stream in the dark.

At the camp you take 45 copper from the three dead, and 30 more from a purse in the hut.

From each body you cut the right ear, and take the tin tag from its neck.

At the bend, foxes have been at the man from the trees and the helmed crossbowman. The ears and tags are still there.

The man from the trees gives up 9 silver and a fine crossbow. The crossbowman gives up 12 copper.

You walk on through the night, the sack heavy on your shoulder, to the quarry rim.

Near midnight you climb down to the easternmost gallery, your flame throwing light ahead of you.

Among the blademen's bodies, the burned Drakewolf rises out of the dark, growling, standing over them.
