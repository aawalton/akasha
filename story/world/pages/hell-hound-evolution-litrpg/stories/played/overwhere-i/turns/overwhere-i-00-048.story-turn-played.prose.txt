The third takes one through the flank and stumbles, then keeps coming.

The first wolf again, closer now. The slug takes it in the head, and it drops into the water.

The second, limping. Your slug catches it square, and it goes down and lies still.

The third, forty yards off and still coming. A last slug hits it full, and it drops.

Three wolves lie still in the marsh water. The other three pull up short, wheel, and bolt back through the marsh toward the channel.
