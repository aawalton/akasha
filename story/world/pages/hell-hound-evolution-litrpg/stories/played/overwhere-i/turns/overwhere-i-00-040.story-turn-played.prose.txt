You reach for water and will it into the flooded tunnel, closing round the heavy weight within.

It comes easily. You draw it down the tunnel and out through the mouth into the channel.

The water bulges, and you heave the body up and swing it onto the bank.

It lands with a wet thump by Jory. It's half again the size of the others, scalded pink in patches.

You let the water go. "Three lurkers accounted for."

Jory whoops, loud enough to send birds up out of the reeds.

"Three! All three, and not a scratch on you." He claps his hands. "I'll tell the reeve myself. And you'll have a string of my smoked eels for it, and welcome."
