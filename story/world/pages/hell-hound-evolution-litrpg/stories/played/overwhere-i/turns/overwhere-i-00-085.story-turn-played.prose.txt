You step out of the pines and walk slowly up the stream bank toward the overhang.

"Your time has come, Voss," you call. "And you want to know why? Is simple."

You keep walking. "You are worth quite a lot to me dead and nothing to me alive."

Voss rises behind his shield. "I come dearer than that, woman," he calls down.

He waits, sword low, until you are thirty yards off. Then he charges down the slope, shield high, stones skittering under his boots.

You lift both hands and cross two fire beams on the face of his shield.

They burn through the wood and the mail beneath. He roars, smoking, and keeps coming.

Your second pair meets on the charred shield. It bursts through, and through him.

Ten yards short of you, Voss pitches forward onto the stones and lies still.

His burned shield rolls a little way down the bank and falls flat.

Under the overhang his sack slumps over, and silver coins spill chinking across the rock.
