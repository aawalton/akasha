"You're welcome to the hide and meat for free if you can find it," you tell her. "I killed him in the swamp and he sank in deep. Barely managed to get the tusks out of the muck. If you manage it, it's yours. I'm taking a bath!"

Agathe looks at you for a long moment. Then she smiles, and the smile reaches her eyes.

"That's a gift worth having," she says. "Meat for half the village, and a hide for the purse. We'll have him out. Ropes, a mule and four men." She's already reaching for her staff.

Garrick laughs, a laugh that shakes the rafters. "A bath, then. I'll heat the water."

You count two copper into his hand.

In the back room the wooden tub steams. You peel off your mud-stiff clothes and sink in. The water goes brown around you as the mud and the boar's blood lift from your arms and out of your hair.

You soak until the water cools, and the tightness goes out of your shoulders.

You climb out clean. Your clothes lie in a heap on the floor, stiff with mud and blood.
