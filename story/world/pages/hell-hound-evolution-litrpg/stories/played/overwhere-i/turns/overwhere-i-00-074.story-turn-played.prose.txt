"Sure," you call back. "We've paid your toll, so now you can pay mine. Your head should just about cover it."

Voss laughs once, short and hard. "My head comes dearer than that, woman." He steps back out of sight.

Your first slug cracks into the stone where he stood.

From then on the crossbowmen show at the gallery mouths only a heartbeat at a time, just long enough to loose.

Their bolts rattle off your boulders. From down in the galleries they have no line on you.

You stay behind cover, a slug spinning ready, and wait on each heartbeat. When a face shows, you send it at the face.

One takes a crossbowman in the cheek. He reels back into the dark.

The next skips wide off the gallery lip, and they shift to other mouths.

Another catches the second crossbowman full in the face. He drops back, howling.

The first one shows again. Your slug takes him between the eyes, and he falls forward in the gallery mouth and lies there.

Two more bolts thump into your boulders from the trees, with no line on you.

Then the galleries fall silent. The only man in sight is the one lying in the gallery mouth.
