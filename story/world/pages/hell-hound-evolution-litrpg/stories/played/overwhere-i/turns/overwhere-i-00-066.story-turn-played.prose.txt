"How far to Wendlow again?" you ask. "I'd love to get this turned in and paid for before it stinks too much."

"Three days east on the east road," Hessa says. "By cart or on foot, much the same. Two on a good horse."

Tobin hears a yes in it. His grin spreads from ear to ear, chipped tooth and all.

"The boy goes," Hessa cuts in, flat, "only if you say so plainly."

Garrick shoulders up beside the sled, beard bristling. "Salt's four copper a sack, and I've plenty. Bring it round to the Stag's back room and I'll lend you a hand packing it." He eyes the head. "About an hour's work, tonight."

Osric sweeps off his wide-brimmed hat with a flourish. "I leave for Wendlow at dawn tomorrow, by cart." He smiles at you. "Ride as my guard, and you and your head go free."
