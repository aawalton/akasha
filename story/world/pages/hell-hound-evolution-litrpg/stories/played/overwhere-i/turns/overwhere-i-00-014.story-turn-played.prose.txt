You sit on a rock and watch Hessa work, chin in your hand, looking thoroughly bored.

She quarters the Brute. She rolls the pelt fur-in and stacks the joints on the flat stones. The sun crawls across the red sky. The stream runs. Hessa says almost nothing.

In the early afternoon Tobin comes down the ridge track, leading a mule in a two-wheeled cart.

Hessa and Tobin load the quarters and the rolled pelt, and the cart sets off back up the track, you walking beside it. The climb goes slowly, in three tight switchbacks through the pines. The mule leans into the traces.

At the crest you can see the whole Greyfen spread out west behind you: dark pools, reeds, drowned pines. East, below, a palisade of sharpened stakes rings a crowd of reed-thatched roofs on a low knoll.

The cart comes down through stumps and rough pasture to a gate in the palisade's west side.

Tobin's news has run ahead of you. A crowd of villagers waits at the gate, staring. They stare at the cart of meat, and harder at the barefoot red-haired woman walking beside it.

Your throat is dry. You haven't had a drink since you woke.

Hessa leads you onto a green, to a long hall of dark tarred timber. Inside is one long room, a hearth in the middle, benches, the smell of smoke and ale.

A round, bald man with a bushy red beard stands behind a table, grinning. Beside him a short, stout woman of about sixty leans on an ash staff, her grey braid pinned up in a crown.

Hessa nods to her. "Agathe. The Brute's dead. This is Nala. It was her kill. I saw the wound."

The woman looks you over from your bare feet to your hair, taking her time.

"Then the bounty's hers." She counts one gold coin out onto the table, in front of everyone. "One gold, and the pelt's yours to sell. Fenwatch thanks you, Nala."

You take the coin from the table. It's heavy and cold, stamped gold.

The reeve's voice carries the length of the hall without her raising it.

"Now. I'm Agathe Morrow, reeve here. Where have you come from, and are you staying?"
