You tuck the two crystals into your pack beside the eye, the ears and the token.

You swim the channel back to the marsh and set out east for Fenwatch, and a bath, a meal and a bed.

You skim the wet stretches on air and water while your mana holds, and wade the rest.

The fen lies quiet all through the long afternoon.

At half past four you come up out of the reeds at the fen edge by the Greyback, caked in muck.

Rowan Coalby is waiting there, soot-dark and broad, with Sedge at his heel.

Sedge's crest bristles. It whines, low, and backs away from you, nose working at your pack. It keeps its distance, however Rowan hushes it.

Rowan looks from Sedge to you, and his words catch. "Th-that howl, after noon. Was it... is it..."
