You tuck the two crystals into your pack beside the eye, the ears and the token.

You swim the channel back to the marsh and set out east for Fenwatch, and a bath, a meal and a bed.

You skim the wet stretches on air and water while your mana holds, and wade the rest.

The fen lies quiet all through the long afternoon.

Past the ford the fields open out, and Fenwatch's roofs show against the evening-yellow sky.

You come up onto the green at half past four, caked in dried fen muck from head to boots.

Folk at their doors turn to stare after you as you cross to the Tarred Stag.

Garrick Pell is at the hearth. He looks up, and his red beard splits in a grin.

"Look at the state of you! Where in the fen have you been all day?"
