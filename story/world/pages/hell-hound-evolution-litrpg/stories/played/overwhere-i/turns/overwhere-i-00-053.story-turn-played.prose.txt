You wade to the channel's edge and swim out to Ghost-Eye's body. It floats low, drifting north.

You tread water beside it. Up close it's huge, grey-green scales scarred pale along the flank.

You will water and earth into its skull, to pluck the ghost eye free. In the dead body the water answers, slow and heavy.

Within a minute the milky left eye comes free into your palm, a hard pearl of clouded crystal.

You spin a thin water disc and take both ears, then tuck eye and ears into your pack.

You swim on across the channel and climb out onto the island's east shore.

Under the pines lie the flattened beds where the pack lay up, and a reek of wolf.

Scanning for wolves, you walk on through the pines. The island is empty and quiet.

In the middle of the island, under the roots of a great drowned pine, a den mouth gapes. Around it lie scattered bones, gnawed and old.

Among them, half in the mud, lie a reed-cutter's knife and a small tin token stamped Sallow Hythe.
