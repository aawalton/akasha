What happens when the line between man and monster blurs? 

James started as a mere man. Now, he’s part monster, wielding skills granted by The System to conquer a brutal dungeon. But at what cost to his humanity? 

Day after day, he killed monsters, taking their part, their soul as his own. Nothing but an emotionless AI stood by his side. Little by little, whispers began to grow within him as he lost sight of himself, and his humanity was questioned. 

Will James conquer the dungeon? What will the world have for him after he finishes the dungeon? Will the world accept him for what he is?

Read to find out.
