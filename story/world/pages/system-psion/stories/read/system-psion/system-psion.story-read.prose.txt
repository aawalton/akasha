Samuel can control the world with his mind.

But even his newfound psychic abilities might not be enough in the chaos of the apocalypse wrought by the invasion of a System. 

After being suddenly thrust into a high-level world dungeon with nothing but his grandma’s walking stick, he must survive against vengeful snakes, alien invaders and a world that is decidedly less friendly than he remembers. All while trying to find his sister, who may or may not be trying to be a continental overlord.

Thankfully, the new world is not completely unfair, and his telekinesis skill seems promising. Now, he just has figure out what this psi stat is, and why no one else seems to have it.
