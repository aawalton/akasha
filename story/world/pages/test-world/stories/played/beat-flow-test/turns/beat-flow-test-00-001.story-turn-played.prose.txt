You stand in the cobbled square with a healing draught in your hand. The bottle is small and corked, and the red liquid inside it tips against the glass as you turn it over. You work the cork free with your thumb, and it comes out with a soft pop. You drink it down in two swallows. It tastes sweet, with a faint edge of metal under the sweetness. Your second bottle is still full.

Warmth spreads through your chest and settles there. You are not hurt, so it has no wound to close.

You lower the empty bottle and turn slowly to take in the square. A stone well stands at the middle of it, and the cobbles run out from the well in every direction. Beside the well sits a wooden bench, empty. Close by the well, a stone stair goes down into the dark under the square.

On the north side, steps climb to the doors of a long hall. On the east side, a green door is set into a shopfront.

The square is quiet. You stand by the well with the empty bottle in your hand.
