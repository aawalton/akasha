You walk across the cobbles to the well at the middle of the square. It is a round shaft of fitted stone, and its rim comes up to your waist. You set your hands on the rim, cool under your palms, and lean over to look down.

The shaft drops a long way. Its stone walls darken the further down they go. Far below, the water lies still and clear, with a pale disc of sky on its face.

At your elbow, a wooden bucket sits on the rim with its rope coiled beside it.
