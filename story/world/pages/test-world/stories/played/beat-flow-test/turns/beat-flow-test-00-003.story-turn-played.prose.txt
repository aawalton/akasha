You straighten from the rim and step over to the bench beside the well. You sit down on it, with the well at your shoulder. You stretch your legs out over the cobbles and lean back.

The square is empty and still. From the bench you can see the steps up to the hall and the green door in the shopfront.

You rest there a few minutes, and your shoulders loosen. You let out a long breath, your back against the bench.
