You square up on the stone floor and cut. You keep at it until dark, then pay for your board, eat at the hall's table and sleep on the cot by the hearth. In the morning you take up the sword again.

The second day goes better. Late in the afternoon, a cut, a guard and a step back come off together as one movement, and you run it again and again until dark. You pay for a second night, eat and sleep by the hearth.

On the third day, at midday, the hall door swings open. A carter steps in, dust on his boots. "I need a hired sword. Anyone here?"
