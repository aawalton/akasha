You sheathe the sword, take up the lantern and go out through the green door into the square. You cross to the steps on the north side, climb them and push into the Test Hall.

You clear a stretch of the long stone floor and begin to drill. Cuts, then guards, then footwork, then the cuts again, from morning until the light goes.

Each night you pay for a meal at the hall's table and a cot by the hearth.

By the sixth morning the drills come cleaner. Your feet find their places before you think of them, and the blade stops where you mean it to.
