You stand in the Test Square. The cobbles are dry, and a well sits at the middle with a bench beside it.

To the north, steps climb to the Test Hall. A green door on the east side opens into the Test Shop.

By the well, a stone stair leads down into the dark of the Test Cellar.

A breeze turns a loose page on the bench, and the town is quiet.

You climb the steps and push into the Test Hall. It is one long room with a stone floor, warm from the hearth.

A notice board hangs by the hearth. One card is pinned at its middle, in a neat hand: Try everything twice.

You read it, and the fire crackles as if it agrees.
