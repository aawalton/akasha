You climb the steps on the north side of the square and go in at the hall's door. Inside is one long room, its stone floor swept and bare from end to end. At the far end a hearth burns low, with cots ranged beside it for travellers to sleep on. Near it stands a table for meals, the benches pushed in under it. On the wall hangs a notice board, papers pinned across it. Between the door and the hearth the floor lies open, wide enough to drill on.

You draw the iron sword and run your thumb along its edge. It runs even from hilt to point, without a nick or a rolled spot anywhere along it, and the leather grip sits firm in your hand. You slide it back into its sheath. The hall is quiet around you: the board on the wall, the low fire, the open floor.
