She craved a life of adventure with a thirst for the unknown, but she was not prepared for what else it would bring her. 

Beatrix hates her job as a data analyst. Her overbearing boss micromanages her daily, and some would call her a pushover. But beneath that meek exterior, lurks someone who wants to stand up for herself and pick up a sword. She often daydreams of disappearing into one of her romantasy novels to live a more exciting life full of action, spicy banter and steamy romance. 

Little does she know, Beatrix's life is about to take a huge turn and there will be no going back. Thrown into a parallel world of magic, viking warriors and deceptive, interfering gods, she must decide if she can adapt to this new lifestyle and accept what she is becoming, or else lose herself to the madness that can come from being a Wanderer. In this isekai meets romantasy fiction, we'll discover new worlds, new enemies...and new lovers, exploring what it really means to be immortal. 

You'll enjoy this if you appreciate: 

-A slow burn, but eventually some spice 

-Morally grey romantic interests

-Reverse tropes

-Cool training montages

-Multiple story arcs

-A little bit of humor
