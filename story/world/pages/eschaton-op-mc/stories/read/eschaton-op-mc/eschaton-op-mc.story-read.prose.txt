Seth Ryans must wield ultimate power to save what's left of the world.

Magic's arrival was apocalyptic: Millions have been granted powers beyond human understanding, and the already fragile world now cracks under the strain. Even worse, Fractures, pocket dimensions full of ravenous monsters, form by the thousands every day. 

Seth Ryans, already struggling with the loss of his parents now must live up to a legacy he believes is impossible. Surrounded by enemies, human or otherwise, he goes to war against carnage incarnate to buy time for the Earth to come to terms with the new reality they live in.

The horde of monsters is endless and fearless. But they'll learn. Transformed into a relentless machine with incredible power, Ryans will teach them what it really means to be unstoppable.
