All it takes is one bad day.

After an Exalted destroys his home, Emil is left with a choice—become a hunting dog of the kingdom’s secret police who turned him into a monster, or let his remaining friends die.

Armed with a power that devours his body, Emil must dismantle criminal syndicates, infiltrate a corrupt Magic Academy, and untangle a web of conspiracies that threaten the status quo.

Stain his hands in blood and preserve this wretched kingdom? If it’s to save his friends, Emil will do anything.

You can expect:

[-] Brutal action involving magical and martial combat

[-] Slow-burn in the beginning arcs to get acquainted with the world, the characters, and the conflicts

[-] Conflicts that escalate in scale and force the MC to make challenging decisions

[-] Some POV-swaps (mostly to elevate the tension) 

[-] Soft progression (MC will learn new ways to use his powers)
