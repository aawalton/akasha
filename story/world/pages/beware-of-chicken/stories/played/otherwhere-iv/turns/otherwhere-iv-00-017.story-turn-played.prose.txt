You step out into the sun and lay both your hands, open, in hers.

The old woman's hands are dry and hard, and stronger than they look.

She turns your hands over, and back. She runs a thumb along each palm, each finger, each nail. She presses the heel of your hand. She takes hold of your wrist and stands still for a long moment, her head a little on one side.

Then she frowns, and turns your hands over again, and bends closer.

"Warm," she says. "Warm, and a pulse in them like a girl who's run up the lane. A fox has cold hands. Cold and dry, however hot the day. Everybody knows that."

She glances at Tie Bo. "So she's no fox, whatever the Fang boys are telling the lane."

Then her eyes go back to your hands, and the frown deepens.

"But these." She lifts them up into the light. "I can't find a callus on them. Or a scar. Or a burn. I've held a thousand hands. Farmers, midwives, the county clerk. A lady from Lanqiao once. The lady's had the needle on this finger, and the fan on this one." She taps your fingers, one and then the other. "Everybody's hands are marked. Yours have never done a day's work, nor a day's anything. Like a baby's, new out of the wash."

She lets go of you, slowly. She wipes her own hands on her apron, as if to make sure of them.

Tie Bo has stepped closer. He is looking at your hands too.

Granny Hua nods at the tall grey-green plants by the gate. "That's mugwort. Pull me a stalk."

You do as she tells you. The leafy stalk comes away in your hand with a green, bitter smell.

"Turn it over," Granny Hua says. You turn the undersides of the leaves up, into the sun.

Beneath the leaves, all along the stem, are clusters of tiny black insects, packed tight together. Small brown ants run up and down the stalk among them, busy, in and out of the clusters. The leaves nearest the clusters are curled and sticky, and yellowing at the edges.

"My best stand, that, and it's sickening," Granny Hua says. She nods at the stalk in your hand. "You're a spirit of knowledge, they tell me. So. Tell me what ails it."
