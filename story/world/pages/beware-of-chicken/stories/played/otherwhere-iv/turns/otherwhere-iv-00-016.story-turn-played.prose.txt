You bow your head slightly.

"Auntie Gu, Tie Bo and I have come to request your poison, to be used against the boar that has been foraging Zhao Jun's field. It may be too advanced to be effective. But if it is not, we are seeking any preparations that reduce the risk of further loss of life. We believe this to be the same boar that took Tie Bo's brother, and it has only grown stronger since. If you have any additional counsel for us in this matter, we would be grateful for it."

The old woman's face went still at the first two words. It stays still to the end.

Beside you, Tie Bo shuts his eyes.

"Auntie Gu," the old woman repeats, very quietly.

"Auntie. Gu." She says each word as if tasting something gone bad.

"I am Hua Su'e, widow of the Hua family. I am no kin of the Gus, thank Heaven, and never was. Gu Meilan has been telling me how to deliver babies for thirty years, and she's wrong every time." Her eyes go to the blue jacket you are wearing, and the cloth shoes. "And her house has taken you in, I hear. And now you come to my gate wearing her name for me."

She folds her arms.

Behind her, a hen wanders through the garden, pecking between the rows. The three of you stand exactly where you are.

"I heard the rest of it. Poison, a boar, counsel. I heard every word." Her voice stays dry and level. "But I'll not hear another word from a spirit that can't tell one old woman from another."
