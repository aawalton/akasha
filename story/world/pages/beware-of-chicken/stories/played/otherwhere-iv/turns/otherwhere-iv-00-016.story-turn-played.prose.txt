You bow your head slightly.

"Granny Hua, Tie Bo and I have come to request your poison, to be used against the boar that has been foraging Zhao Jun's field. It may be too advanced to be effective. But if it is not, we are seeking any preparations that reduce the risk of further loss of life. We believe this to be the same boar that took Tie Bo's brother, and it has only grown stronger since. If you have any additional counsel for us in this matter, we would be grateful for it."

Granny Hua listens to all of it without a word. Her eyes stay on your face the whole time.

When you have finished, she grunts.

"Plainly asked. That's more than I get from most."

She glances at Tie Bo. "Your brother's boar. I thought it might come to that."

Tie Bo keeps silent. His jaw tightens.

"I'll answer you," Granny Hua says to you. "But I don't deal with anyone I haven't looked at."

She lifts the latch and swings the gate open, and steps back into her yard, into the full sun.

The garden is rows of green. Feathery leaves, grey-green leaves, tall stalks, some of them flowering blue. The bitter smell is stronger in here.

She stops in the middle of it, where the light is strongest, and turns to face you. She holds out both her hands, palms up. They are brown and knotted, and the nails are rimmed with green.

"Come here into the sun, spirit. Put your hands in mine. Both of them. Open."
