There are ships in space, strange kiosks selling skill pearls and stranger potions on land, and adventurers saving the world of Wett from seven-year floods. But there isn’t a single bit of luck in Isaac’s pocket. He has no money, the laws of magic — the Boon and Bane he was born with — are restrictive as heck, and his future seems to be heading straight into a dead end when life suddenly gives him a choice:

Stay at home, become a parasite, a neet, a failure, or risk it all, and be like the heroes saving the eternal empire again and again. Become an adventurer, that one-in-a-million ideal of selfless heroism, and see the stars for himself.

There’s schemers and brutal competition to overcome, and friends to make before Isaac can build a build that will shoot him straight past the stratosphere.

Here’s to hoping Isaac can withstand what comes after.
