A salaryman is reborn in a world with monsters, futuristic cities, and a power called Synchrony.

Weary of the monotony, the salaryman decides to leave everything behind to chase his dreams, but he dies. Upon awakening, he finds himself in a different body, in a completely unfamiliar world.

Now, he must fend for himself in a hostile environment, completely alone, full of creatures known as B-Synchros—beings with extraordinary abilities that can only be fought by members of an organization called Terra Nexus. Will he survive in this new world?

Art: Taka
