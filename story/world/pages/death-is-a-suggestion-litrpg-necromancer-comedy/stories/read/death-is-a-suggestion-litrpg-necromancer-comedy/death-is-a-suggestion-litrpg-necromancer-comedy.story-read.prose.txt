Summoned to a new world as conscripted cannon fodder, with a low grade class and little hope for survival, Levi's outlook is grim. Rather than die for a country he doesn't care about, Levi and a fellow isekaied healer immediately plan their escape.

But things never go according to plan. Before long, both Levi and his healer end up dead in a ditch. 

This isn't the end for our heroes, however. Their antics attract the interest of the Goddess of Death. She has high hopes for them... and more importantly, a powerful unique class, Necromancer. But Levi isn't suited for looming in the backline and summoning zombies. He's a melee fighter, and he intends to necromance from the front lines. Loosed upon the countryside, Levi and his newly undead healer, Colin, take on the world, the apocalypse, and everything else. 

What to expect:
Action Necromancy! Raising the undead in melee combat! Necromancy as you've never seen it before!
