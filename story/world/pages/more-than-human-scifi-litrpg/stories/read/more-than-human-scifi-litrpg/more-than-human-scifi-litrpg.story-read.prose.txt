More Than Human throws you headfirst into the year 2123—a future of promise, peril, and transformation. At the heart of it all is Max, a bioengineered dog with a human mind and a body built for more than survival. As he trains to master his augmentations and earn his place in a world that barely tolerates him, forces far beyond his understanding are already in motion.

When Max’s unconventional father—a rogue inventor with dangerous tech—pushes the boundaries of reality, it lights a fuse that could upend everything. Civilization teeters on the edge as cracks form in the old order, and hidden powers begin to emerge. 

In a world where nothing is what it seems, and trust is as rare as peace, one question drives the story forward: What does it mean to be more than human?

Progression Science Fiction with a unique take on a System interface.
