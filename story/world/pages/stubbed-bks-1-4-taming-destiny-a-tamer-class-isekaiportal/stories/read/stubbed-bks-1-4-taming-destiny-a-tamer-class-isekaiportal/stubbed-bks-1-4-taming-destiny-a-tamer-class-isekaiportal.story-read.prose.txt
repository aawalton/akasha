How would you like to reinvent yourself?

Fight. Survive. Build. Tame. Thrive? Lured by the promise of a mysterious inheritance, Markus Wolfe discovers that he must first survive a year on a savage world full of dinosaurs and other weird and terrifying creatures. To succeed, he'll have to learn to question everything he once knew and use his new magical Tamer Class.

(Note: Books 1, 2, 3, and 4 are stubbed. Books 1, 2, and 3 are available on Amazon and the edited book 4 is available on Patreon) 

Twenty-seven year old Markus Wolfe is at the lowest point in his life when he’s offered what seems like the chance of a lifetime. Only, like most things that seem too good to be true, it isn’t.

Or at least, it might be, but he has to survive a year in a forest completely uninhabited by any civilised race to find out. With Jurassic Park rejects seeing him as their next dinner, what hope does he have of that?

He’s going to have to use the knowledge he’s been given along with his new Tamer Class to build everything he needs to survive...and maybe even thrive.

What to expect:

- Regular posting schedule (5 chapters per week Friday - Tuesday)

- Slow-burn weak to strong character

- Light LitRPG elements (stats, levelling system, skills, magic, etc.)

- Survival fantasy, reminiscent of survival games like Conan Exiles

- Awesome Tames who evolve

- Exploration and discovery

- Some dark themes

- Warning: major character death (not main character).
