Riley wakes up in a world that already expects something from her.

She has nothing.

No gear.
No knowledge.
No protection.

Just a hostile forest, something hunting in the dark, and a system that has already marked her as a novice and expects progress.

It doesn’t explain the rules.
It doesn’t offer help.
It only measures what she builds… and what she survives.

The first lesson comes fast.

Run.
Hide.
Don’t get caught.

Every resource must be scavenged.
Every improvement must be earned.
Every mistake leaves consequences that don’t go away.

And whatever stalks the woods at night?

It knows she’s here.

There are no chosen ones.
No free power.
No second chances.

Only progress.

And Riley is starting from zero.

No one survives alone.

What to expect:

- 3 chapters a week from Monday, Wednesday and Friday.

- Hard-won power growth.
