Lyn has always believed the world is boring.

So when humanity is torn from Earth and rewritten into an RPG world of monsters, magic, and aggressively hostile ecosystems…

She laughs.

Finally, something interesting.

Now armed with a new pair of fox ears, a troublesome tail, and a deeply concerning enthusiasm for ecosystem disruption, Lyn approaches her new reality the only way she knows how—through aggressively violent problem-solving.

From tutorial zones to ancient dungeons, from demonic warfronts to celestial thrones, every challenge is met with the same question:

Who decided these creatures were allowed to be in charge?

But as Lyn grows stronger, the world grows with her.

Empires tremble. Gods take notice.

All are met with the casually disdainful gaze of a girl who is simply too good.

This is the story of a foxkin who got bored, picked up a sword—

—and proved that even dragons, demons, and deities can be prey.
