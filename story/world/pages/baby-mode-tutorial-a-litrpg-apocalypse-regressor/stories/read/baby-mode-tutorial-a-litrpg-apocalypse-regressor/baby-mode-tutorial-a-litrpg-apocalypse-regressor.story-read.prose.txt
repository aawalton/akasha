How was he supposed to save the world when he couldn’t even change his own diapers?

Millions died inside the Tutorial, and many more lost their lives in what came after.

Richard, just one lucky soul among billions, managed to escape the Tutorial, not only with his life, but with something else truly precious in tow. Something that had the power to change everything. 

A chance to do it all over again.

A chance to go back to the very beginning. 

To before earth’s integration, and the slow degradation of humanity that followed.

Armed with his future knowledge, his skills as a master talismanic artificer, and a will to rival even the gods themselves, he was fully prepared to show those scaly overlords exactly what humanity was capable of. 

So you can imagine his surprise when, upon traveling back in time, he was somehow reborn into the body of an infant, so weak, he couldn’t even lift his bulbous round head off his dinosaur pillow.
