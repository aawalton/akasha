Earth is dead. 

Adrift, remnants of humanity wandered for a new home, a new planet. They didn't find it.

 They now orbit a superstructure, so ancient and vast that its explorers have come to call it "A Dungeon." To keep the colony alive, it needs pilots to ferry supplies from its expansive and dangerous halls. Stefano Reyes is one of those mech pilots. But a message from his dead father has him looking for ways to get to the last floor, further than any other species has ever dared gone.

But getting to the last floor means fighting beings larger than buildings for the equipment he needs to get strong enough to overcome its challenges. The Dungeon has only ever taken from the countless species and civilizations from before. So how will it give him the answers he needs?
