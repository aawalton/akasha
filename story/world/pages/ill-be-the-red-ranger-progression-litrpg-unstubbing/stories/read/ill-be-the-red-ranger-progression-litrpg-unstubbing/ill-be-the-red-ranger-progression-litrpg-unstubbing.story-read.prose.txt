Oliver was just a kid when the bombs fell in Seoul, taking half of his body and his whole family.
He has been frozen for the last hundred years while recovering from the damage.

But the world he returned to was no longer the same. Rangers are responsible for protecting the empire, and the fight against the Orks is draining the forces of the New Earth Army.

Now that he is turning 15, it's time for him to enlist and go to the front lines or maybe even become a Ranger.

Expect:

- Fast-Paced Story
- Soft Isekai
- No Harem
- Mature approach to Power Rangers / Super Sentai trope

The story is not completely finished, but the first half of it is wrapped up within the first 193 chapters available here. 
This page will be updated when I continue writing on the second half in 2025.
