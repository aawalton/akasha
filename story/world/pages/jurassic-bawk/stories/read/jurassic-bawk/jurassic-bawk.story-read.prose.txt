The Gathering of Organic Deities (GOD) has decreed the implementation of a System designed to end humanity's overwhelming dominance over life on Earth. Grimfalk, the deity of dinosaurs classified as theropods, is determined to take full advantage of this opportunity. But there's a problem: therapods are extinct, and so he must turn to the dinosaur's closest living relative to find his Champion.
