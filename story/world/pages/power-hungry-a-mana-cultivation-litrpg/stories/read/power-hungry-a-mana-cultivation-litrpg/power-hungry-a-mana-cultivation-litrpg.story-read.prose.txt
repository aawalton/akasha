Cultivation meets corporate warfare in a manapunk world where ancient relics mean ultimate power.

A once god-like civilization vanished, leaving behind ruins of miraculous technology. Now, ruthless mega-corporations and their cultivators compete in a brutal arms race, all for the chance to seize these ancient powers.

Cale has no memory, no training, and no allies—a perfect victim in a world that devours the weak. Yet he wields one impossible edge: a magical AI fused to his soul that lets him absorb any kind of mana. On paper, he’s dead meat. In reality, that makes him a threat. And the corporations can’t allow a loose cannon who breaks the rules of cultivation.

Hunted by factions that fear his ability, Cale must adapt fast—or lose everything. From corporate cityscapes to hidden ruins, every step reveals deeper secrets of a civilization that once reigned as gods. With each surge of power, Cale inches closer to rocking the corporate world itself. His journey will shake the foundations of this world.

WHAT TO EXPECT
• High-octane, fast-paced action
• Mild LitRPG elements
• Mana-based cultivation in a manapunk setting
• Weak-to-OP protagonist
• Secrets that rattle an entire world
