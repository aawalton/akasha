One out of a hundred becomes a cultivator. One out of thousands wields two elements. One wields limitless potential with the Celestial Core Card. 

Jianyi’s father died on the frontlines in the empire’s thousand-year-long Celestial Confrontation against demons from the Outer Realms. He has been training in their backyard. Every. Single. Day. Since. 

After succeeding in the Heavenly Trial, he receives the impossible: the Celestial Core Card, granting access to all five elements—something not even legend speaks of. 

Now, hunted by demons and their mortal servants, Jianyi must unlock the potential of his cultivation, defeat the enemy behind the piercing purple eyes that haunt him, and perhaps make some friends along the way.

 

What to expect:

- An epic tale of a boy fighting against overwhelming odds
- Weak-to-strong (slow burn), hard-earned progression
- Deep dives into a detailed cultivation system, with some card-based mechanics
- No harem, little face-slapping
- Elder Sun, aka Forlorn Hope – you don’t want to miss seeing him fight
- Perfect for fans of Unintended Cultivator or All the Skills
