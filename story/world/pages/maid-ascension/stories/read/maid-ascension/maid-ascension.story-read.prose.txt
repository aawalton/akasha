She came as a servant. She stayed to rewrite the kingdom.

A scribe’s daughter turned master forger, Lira Velora is sent to the royal castle as a spy, hidden beneath the identity of a maid; unseen and unheard within a fractured servant hierarchy.

In a forgotten library, she uncovers lost knowledge and awakens a dangerous magic.

But as secrets unravel, her mission shifts, from simple revenge to confronting the rot at the heart of the kingdom… and deciding whether to destroy it or remake it.
If you like story with Spell Crafting, Self taught magic, and an original non-LitRPG progression, this story is for you!
