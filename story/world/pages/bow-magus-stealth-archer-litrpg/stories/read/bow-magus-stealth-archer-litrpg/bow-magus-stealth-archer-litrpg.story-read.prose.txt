When life gives you a bow and arrow, you bind a fireball to it and blast it at your enemy.

Without a Class, you are nothing. He was nothing.

He was born to a life of little, expected to mine coldrock until his body gave out—the only escape, to prove oneself to the gods, and be granted a Class. But with no magic, no mana, and no noble tutor to teach him the skills required to challenge the Shrine, his chances were nonexistent. 

But Rex had other plans. If the odds were impossible, then he'd just have to beat impossible odds.

What to expect?

- Earned power - Weak to Strong
- A gritty and sometimes dark world, but with a good dose of humor
- Grounded power scaling
- An MC that thinks things through and plans, but isn't always perfect
- Adventuring through fantasy worlds, Gates, and quests
- Ambushes. Lots of ambushes.
