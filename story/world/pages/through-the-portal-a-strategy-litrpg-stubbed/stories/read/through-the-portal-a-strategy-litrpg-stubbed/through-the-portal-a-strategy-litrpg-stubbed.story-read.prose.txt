Connor McKinnley was an airline pilot.

Turns out he was a master tactician in his past life, on another world. Now, he's found a way back into that world and must defend it.

Time is running out. Connor must rally his allies, outwit his foes, and defend a realm he barely remembers. The fate of this world depends on whether he can once again become the leader and strategist he was meant to be—or whether his forgotten enemies will tear it apart for good.

A strategy based LitRPG inspired by Fire Emblem, with elves, knights, barbarian babes and evil wizards.
