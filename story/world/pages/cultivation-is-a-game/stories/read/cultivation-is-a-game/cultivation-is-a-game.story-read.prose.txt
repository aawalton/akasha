Kill. Die. Respawn.

Kai woke up in a Xianxia world...with a LitRPG system. But he's not alone.

In this brutal realm, where cultivation works like a game, there are others like him—reincarnators, regressors, and perhaps even isekai protagonists. Some seek redemption, others revenge, and a few simply desire to dominate.

Everyone seems to be the hero of their own story, but Kai must find out who is the main character of this deadly game—or if it’s him all along.
