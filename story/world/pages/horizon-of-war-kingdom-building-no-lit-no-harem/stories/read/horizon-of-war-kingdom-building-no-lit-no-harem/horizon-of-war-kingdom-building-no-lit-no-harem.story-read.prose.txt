The thousand-year-old Imperium is crumbling, and war looms on the horizon, bringing with it both brutalities and splendor. Caught in the midst of this chaos is Lansius, a young office grunt who finds himself living as a medieval peasant. With no wealth, status, or magical abilities, and armed only with his basic knowledge of the art of war, can he endure and thrive in this realistic medieval fantasy realm?

Follow Lansius as he takes on different trades, struggling to make a living and survive in a realm at war.

What to expect:

- No Overpowered MC, One Love Interest

- Realistic Battles, Army Building, Ruling Class

- Slowburn, Grounded Technological Progress
