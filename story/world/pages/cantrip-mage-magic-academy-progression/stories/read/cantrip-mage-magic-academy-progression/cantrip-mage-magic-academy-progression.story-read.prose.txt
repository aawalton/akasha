Magic has rules. Eola must break them. 

Eola loves magic, but she’s got a problem—her ‘unique’ magical attunement. Her soul is filled with unattuned Mana, useful for only the runes children learn. Cantrips won’t help her pass her first year of school, much less save her parents, but Eola refuses to believe she’s stuck casting them forever. She just needs a less…conventional…education. 

Eola’s study plan is clear. It’s also dangerous. 

The academy’s library holds tomes no living mage has read. Monsters stalk its aisles, and forgotten knowledge haunts its shelves. If a solution for her useless attunement exists, it’ll be archived there. To master magic, Eola must learn—and break—its accepted rules. That’s fine with her, though.

It’s time to rewrite the rulebooks.
