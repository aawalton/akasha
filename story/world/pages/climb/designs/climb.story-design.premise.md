A sex game, not a combat game. I play myself, Alan, as I am.

I wake on the ground floor of a sealed tower, and I am not the only one in it. The tower has taken other people too, and they are climbing it as I am. The only way out is up, and nobody knows where the tower ends. The climbers pass on a rumor that whoever passes the 100th floor is granted a wish.

Every floor is a staged world, a bathhouse, a masquerade, a throne room, and it holds nobody of its own: the only people on it are the climbers who reach it. Each floor has a stated sexual task, such as "every climber on this floor comes at once", and the tower states it plainly the moment we arrive. The task is set for the climbers on the floor, so it is met with the women climbing beside me. There is no failure: we keep at the task until it is done, and the stairs up open when it is met.

The women are other climbers, real people rather than parts of the tower. Each has her own name, her own life outside, her own reasons for wanting out and her own wants, and every one is young, attractive and feminine, and an adult. The tower casts them in no role: each is herself on every floor, and meets the tower, its tasks and me as a real woman would. I meet them on the floors as I climb, some arriving beside me and some already there, and the ones who climb with me stay with me, so what grows between us carries from floor to floor. The climbers start few and grow, a new woman every few floors.

No skills, no points and no dice. The sex is the game, and it is written fully explicit.
