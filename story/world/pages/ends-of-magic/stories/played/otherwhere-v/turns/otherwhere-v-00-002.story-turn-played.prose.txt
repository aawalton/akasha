"Okay… isekai protocol."

The words come out in the new voice, clear and a little hoarse, and too loud in the quiet.

"System?"

The air in front of you stays empty.

"Status?"

Empty.

"Davrar?"

You wait. Where the box hung before, there is only air, and slanting gold, and a drifting speck.

"Davrar…" Slower this time, turning it over. It snags on something, a name from somewhere else, half there and half gone. "I remember that name."

Then, because it is the only thread you have: "Nathan, have you merged with the system yet? If so, could you send me some help? I know you can, and wouldn't want me to go through what you did."

Your voice goes out across the hollow and stops somewhere in the ferns. There is no answer. There is no voice inside your skull, no blue light, and the hollow looks exactly as it did before you spoke. Your own voice was the only human sound you could hear, and now it has stopped too.

The clicking in the ferns stays quiet. It stays quiet a long while. Then one clicker starts up off to your left, tentative, and another answers it, and then the whole undergrowth is at it again, the too-many-handed clock wound back up.

Minutes go by. The golden light slants lower between the grey-scaled trunks. The moss under you still holds the day's warmth, but the air has started to cool, and you feel it first on your bare feet.

Far off, the three rising notes come again. The answer comes back.

More of the faint green lights come drifting down out of the canopy, slow as dust. One brushes a fern frond near your knee, and for a moment the frond glows along its edge, the same soft green, then fades.

Blue light flashes in front of your eyes. A new box opens, square and clean-edged, and turns with your head when you turn it.

Davrar has recognized you, Nala.

You have become fully integrated into Davrar, and are ready to begin!

As you have no Talents, classes or skills despite being of mature age, Davrar has deemed you to be at a Disadvantage.

Therefore, Davrar will provide more explanations than usual, and your class, Talent and skill gain and progression will be accelerated until you are no longer at a Disadvantage.

Davrar is here to help you survive and prosper. It will offer you Talents and utility skills according to your innate talents and abilities, and allow you to choose a class to suit your deeds.

Talents and utility skills can develop every 10 ranks, but require Insight to do so.

Each class will grant different and unique class skills according to the theme of the class.

To unlock classes, reach level 9 and choose from the presented list. Classes develop at levels 27, 81, 243 and so on. There are ways to acquire more than one class; good luck discovering them!

To level up, overcome challenges. The more dire the challenge, the more you will be rewarded.

Davrar hopes you will survive and prosper.

The box hangs in the air in front of you.

Around it the dusk is coming on, and the forest is full of callers you cannot see. You are barefoot, with nothing in your hands, and the brook runs away downhill from the spring.
