Sky and friends planned to join the server reset for their favorite MMORPG, Zenith Affinity. The night before the servers were about to reset, they had formed their party and made their characters in hopes of getting a good start to race to max level. Imagine their surprise when they wake up in the world of Zenith Affinity as their characters. Follow Sky and friends as they deal with the implications of a changing world all while getting used to their new bodies, genders, and magic.

This is a world where the game aspects are merged with reality and the players are new members of this ever changing world. Their actions have consequences and their presence fundamentally changes everything. They have a plan to level and find a home that they can call their own.

What to expect:

- Gender changed MC, and themes of people ending up in their designed characters bodies

- Talks of strategy, skills, and builds

- Building a Guild and making friends and enemies

- Working through a known story of events with MMORPG mechanics overlaid into a real world

- Dungeon dives, raids, and world boss encounters

- Veterans of the game teaching and showing others what they know

- Exploration of incredible places

- A little bit of delicious foods and stuff like that
