You walk back along the blue current to the old beech, and the dead rabbit under it.

You pick it up by the hind legs. It is heavier than it looks, and the antlers knock against your knee.

Then the long walk back. Out of the trees at the shrine, and north up the road to Merrowgate.

By the town gate your bare soles are raw and sore, and every stone in the road finds them. Your stomach has stopped grumbling and started to ache.

The sun is near its height when you limp through the south gate to the old tollhouse.

Inside, Marda looks up from the desk. Her eyes go to the rabbit, then to the bundle of frostcaps.

You set the frostcaps out on the desk. Marda counts them, turning each one over to check the root cut.

"Twenty. All clean." She unlocks a strongbox and counts out twenty copper coins.

She takes out your pale card and holds it a moment. It flickers in her hand as she logs the job.

Then she opens a drawer, takes out a small tin, and shakes a plain copper ring out of it.

"One job done. That makes it yours." She slides it across the desk. "Copper ring. It'll size to you."

She nods at the rabbit. "Post doesn't buy carcasses. Take that to Dunstan, the inn's cook."

Her eyes go to the pink seam on your forearm, and stay there.

"That's a bite," she says. "A fresh one. Closed."

She points her cane at the bench. "Sit. We're talking about the blight."
