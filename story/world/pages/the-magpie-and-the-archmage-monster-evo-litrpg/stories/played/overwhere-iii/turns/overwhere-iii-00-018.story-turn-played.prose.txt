You sit on the bench, glad of it. The weight comes off your raw feet, and you let out a long breath.

Outside, the noon bell rings slow and deep over the square.

Marda looks at your feet and grunts. She levers herself up on her cane, goes to the box of old gear, and drops a pair of worn leather boots on the floor by the bench.

"Loan. Like the knife."

She sits again, heavily, and pours herself one cup of cider from a jug on the desk.

"Three corrupted beasts reached the fields this winter. Used to be one a year, if that. A boar, Level 14. A stag, 17. A wolf, 15."

She lets that sit.

"You're Level 1. Any one of them would kill you. Don't think otherwise."

"First you see black smoke at the mouth. Then the whites of the eyes go black. Then the eyes go. Arrows and blades barely slow them. They dodge magic. But holy magic kills them fast."

"A bite from one festers. Oozes black. Lays a curse on you that won't lift. And it takes the hurt first. The injured, the grieving. It finds them."

"When one dies, what's left gathers into a blightstone. Enough Purify cracks a blightstone, and it bursts into a clean glimmerstone."

She taps the desk. "Purify is a spell. You want it. With a card like yours, you should be able to learn it. Once you can cast it, I'll put you on blight work. Not before. Until then you grow on clean beasts at the edge, and you keep clear of anything corrupted."

"I'm writing Thornmere again today. A fourth letter. This time I'm naming a white-gold card."

She sets down her cup and looks at the pink seam on your forearm.

"Now. That bite. You closed it yourself, didn't you?"
