You crouch at the copse's edge and reach for the currents running past the hedgebank.

You shape a Cleansing Weave and try to wind it onto a lent current, like twisting two cords.

It's fiddly. The two strands slip against each other, then catch and twist tight.

The braid runs down the current, through the earth, and sinks into the curled dark smear.

You pull. A clot of blight tears loose from the fox and comes up the braid toward you.

The snap-back whips back down the current into your chest. Your breath goes ragged for a moment.

A blue box opens.
