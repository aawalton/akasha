"Thanks, Brannagh."

Brannagh grunts. The cat blinks at you from the counter.

Down at the south gate, the post's shutters are closed. The dusk bell has rung.

At the Crook and Candle it's ham-hock and barley stew with brown bread. You eat two bowls. You pay Bet three copper for supper and eight for the bed, and you sleep like a stone.

You wake full to the brim inside, and you're at the post door when the dawn bell rings.

Marda sets the stag's paled blightstone on the desk with her tongs, without being asked.

You thread holy current in. Cold bites up into your hand, harder than yesterday, to the wrist. You hold on through it. The black gives.

The second weave goes through like a needle through linen. The third, nearly as easy.

The stone sits there nearly all gray. One thin dark vein is left in it.

You're near empty inside. Marda puts the stone back in the lead box, apart from the rest.

At Brannagh's counter you open the receipt book to your marked page.

The last third is draughts: a mana draught, a sleeping draught, a fever tonic.

Mana draught: ground jackalope antler, frostcap, honey, a night's steeping.

Late in the morning you turn the last page and close the book. Inside, you're near full again.

You walk down to the post. Marda sets out the stag's stone again.

One weave. The last dark vein unravels, and the stone cracks.

It bursts into a clean glimmerstone, glowing soft on the desk.

Marda looks at it a long moment. "Yours," she says. "As I said."

You take it. Marda lifts one of the wolf's blightstones from the box and sets it down.

Two more weaves. The wolf's stone pales a shade, then another.

You're empty inside. Marda hands you the beast guide, and you sit at the side bench.

Jackalope, snow hare, frost toad, ridge fox. Your stomach growls. You haven't eaten since supper.

A little after noon the door bangs open. It's Pip Carrow, out of breath.

"Brannagh says come," Pip says. "Her bench is full. All of them want the free mending. Every one of them an old scar. Hild Wendle's told the whole Wool Square."
