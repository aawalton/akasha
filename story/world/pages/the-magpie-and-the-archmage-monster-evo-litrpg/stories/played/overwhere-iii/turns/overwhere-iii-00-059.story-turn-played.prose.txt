You kneel by the bench and lay your palm over the lad's calf, watching closely this time.

The currents come to you from far off, past the lane and past the town wall, and pour in deep.

You pull. The blight comes out in one long draw, and the gray drains from his skin.

For the first time the pull's cold doesn't ache up your arm. The current carries it off.

The rot fights at the end. A dull throb kicks into your temples, then fades to an ache.

As the last of it goes, you see a faint dark thread running out of the wound.

It runs off east, through the town wall, toward the east gate and the Thornmere road.

You weave a Mending into the clean puncture. It closes smooth in one pass.

You sit back on your heels. The currents reach five times as far as they did at the start.

And they lend you five times as much as they did then.

The lad flexes his calf and stares. He holds out a rag knotted around ten copper.

"Da's," he says. Brannagh takes it and hands it straight to you.

The thread still hangs in your sight, thin and dark, pointing east toward the farms.
