You finish the stew and the last of the bread, and turn to Tobin. "Thank you," you tell him again.

He waves it off with his spoon, and some of the tightness goes out of his jaw.

"Do you know any spells?" you ask.

Tobin laughs, short and surprised. "Me? Lass, I'm a carter. One skill to my name. Sure Load. Keeps the casks from shifting on a bad road. That's my magic."

He tears a piece of bread. "Spells, you buy from the skill shop, same as any skill. With glimmerstones. Good ones cost dozens. I find one now and then and sell it straight off. Twenty copper apiece."

He shrugs. "Magic's for Order folk, and adventurers. Not for the likes of me. You want spells, the Guild post's where you'd ask. Adventurers buy and trade them there."

The words stay with you. The skill shop. From the way he says it, it is part of the System, the same as the blue box.

Tobin wipes his bowl with the last of his bread and pushes back from the table, stiff from the road.

"I'm for bed. I sell at the square in the morning." He looks at you. "You'll be all right tonight?"
