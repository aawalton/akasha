"I'm dry," you say, "but I'll check in morning and night until you're both clear. Ivy in the morning, Garrick at night. For now, I'm going to sleep."

From the near cot, Ivy snorts. "Mind you do come back. I've cattle to drive."

Garrick turns his head on the pillow and thanks you, slowly and gravely.

Brannagh sets two small flasks of healing potion in your hands. "One each, like I said. Morning, then."

She holds the curtain for you.

You walk out into the dark lane and across the Wool Square to the lit window of the inn.

The common room is warm and loud. Tobin sits by the hearth with a cup of cider.

You take off his heavy coat, still smelling of apples, and give it back to him. Then you hold out twenty-two copper, double what he spent on you.

Tobin takes the coat. He counts out eleven copper and pushes the rest back across the table.

"Eleven was what it cost. I'll not take a copper more."

At the counter you pay Bet eight copper for a bed, from your own purse.

Bet's eyebrows go up, pleased. "Paying your own way already? What work did you find, then?"

She looks at the man's shirt hanging off you. "And you'll want something warmer than that. I've a box."
