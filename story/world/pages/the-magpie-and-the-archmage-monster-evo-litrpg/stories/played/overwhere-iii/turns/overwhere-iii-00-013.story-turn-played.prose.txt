You step in toward the old beech to harvest, the knife in your hand, and kneel by the glowing cluster.

The antlered rabbit doesn't run. It is backed against the roots, with nowhere to go.

Its small glow shifts. It drains out of the body and gathers, bright, at the base of the antlers.

You reach for the current running past the beech, grab a thread of it, and throw it.

It leaves your hand as a lash of raw force, far more than you meant to put in it. It cracks into the rabbit and slams it sideways against the root. It rips on through the frostcaps behind it too. Most of the big cluster is torn open and bruised gray.

A blue box snaps open in the corner of your sight.

[New skill acquired – Current Lash.]
[Current Lash – At [Basic] level, loose raw current as a lash, a shove or a ward.]
[Mana Weaver – At [Basic] level, you see currents and auras. They lend your workings mana.]

The rabbit is hurt, but it isn't down. It scrambles up and comes at you, antlers first.

You drive the knife at its mouth. The blade skids along its jaw and opens a shallow cut.

The antlers hit your forearm, and teeth close on it. The bite goes deep enough to bleed.

You wrench free. The rabbit tumbles back into the roots, blood on its gray-brown jaw.

Your forearm throbs, and blood runs down over your wrist. Your mana feels a little lower than it was.

The current still runs past the beech, close enough to reach.

Backed into the roots again, the rabbit's glow begins to gather at its antlers once more.
