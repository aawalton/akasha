You step back out of reach of the roots, your weight on your toes, ready to move. Your eyes stay on the glow gathering at its antlers.

You reach for the current again and pull a thread. This time you pour a little of your own mana in behind it.

This time you aim it. One tight lash, straight at the rabbit in the roots.

It lands before the rabbit can move. The crack of it echoes through the beeches.

The lash hits with crushing force. The rabbit is thrown back against the root, and it lies still.

There's no attack to dodge. It's over in a breath.

Magpies burst up out of the beeches, shrieking, and settle again farther off.

[Experience gained.]

You feel the small cost of the lash. Your own mana sits a little lower than before. Your forearm throbs, and the bite still bleeds slowly down your wrist.

Under the root, among the torn and bruised frostcaps, four are still whole, glowing cold.

The rabbit's glow drains out of its body. It seeps away into the current and thins to nothing, all but one spot. At the base of its antlers, something small still holds a faint glow of its own.
