"Corrupt wolves. I fought them off, but they hurt me bad. Can you get me to the shrine? That's my best chance to heal myself."

Edda looks you over, lips tight. "Can't carry you. Stay put." She stumps off down the brook.

A quarter hour later she's back, pushing a two-wheeled charcoal barrow, black with soot.

She heaves you into it with a grunt, and with a strength her size doesn't promise.

At the clearing's edge Edda slows. She stares at the hut's open door and the lifted hearthstone.

She doesn't say a word, and wheels on.

Down the south road, two hours of jolting ruts. The blight creeps cold up your arm and calf.

Your well fills a little as you lie there, a slow trickle.

The crossroads shrine comes into sight. White-gold current runs thick around its warm stone.

Edda tips you out against it. The warmth soaks into your back and eases the worst of the ache.

You feed a Mending Weave from the white-gold. It burns your palms, but your strength steadies.

A second one. Then you turn the current on your own bites.

Four pulls, one for each bite. The current carries each one's cold away as it drags the blight out.

Four dark seed stones clot on the grass beside you.

Then four Mendings to close the bites. Two drag, but all four hold. The skin knits pink and whole.
