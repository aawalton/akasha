Marda lifts the stag's second stone out with her tongs and sets it on the desk.

You lay your palm above it and pull the first thread of rot loose.

It snaps back hard. The sting runs up your wrist and leaves your hand throbbing.

You shake it out and weave again. A dark vein frays and thins.

The third weave runs clean and deep. The stone pales a full shade.

The fourth lands just as Marda rises for the dusk bell. Your well is empty.

The stone sits half pale on the desk. Four more weaves, maybe, to crack it.

Marda tongs it back into the lead box, apart from the rest, and locks it.

You walk up through the dark to the Crook and Candle, warm and loud inside.

You pay Bet three copper for supper and eight for the bed under the eaves.

Bet sets down a bowl of stew and bread, and the hollow in your middle finally fills.

Up under the eaves, you lie down in the dark, your stung hand resting on the blanket.
