You leave the receipt book on Brannagh's counter and walk down to the Guild post.

Marda looks up from her ledger.

"I'd like to try cleansing the Guild's blightstones," you say.

"Unmaking them is the point," Marda says. "Whatever's left of one is the healer's."

She lifts a seed stone out of the lead box with her tongs and sets it on the desk.

To your sight, it's a tight knot of black current with a pinprick of pale light at its core.

You thread holy current into the knot and pull. The currents lend the pull great weight.

The knot bends under it, and holds. The thread slides off and frays out to nothing.

Inside, you drop near empty. The seed sits on the desk, black and whole.

Marda watches it a moment, then tongs it back into the box. "Hm."

You walk back up to Brannagh's and sit at the counter with the receipt book. The one-eared cat settles by your elbow. The hand is cramped and faded, and you read slowly.

The first page, in the mother's hand: a potion only "takes" if its brewer has mana to pour in.

Then salves. Goose-grease and yarrow. Marigold for burns. Your mana creeps back as you read.

At the noon bell, the door opens. The carter's wife comes in, her shin wrapped in clean linen.

She sits on the bench and unwinds the linen from the puckered seam. "For the smoothing," she says.
