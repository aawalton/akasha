You take the pen and fill in the form honestly, line by line.

Name: Nala.
Age: about twenty-five.
Race: Human.
Class: none.
Skills: none.
Traits: Mana Weaver.
Reason for joining: I need work, and I have nothing.

Marda turns the form around and reads it. At Mana Weaver, her brow creases.

"Never heard of it," she says flatly. "And I've heard of most."

You pick up the card. It is light, smooth and cool, like polished wood.

You have never pushed mana into anything. You reach inward, the same way you reached for the threads on the road.

Something small in you answers. A trickle of it runs down your arm and into the card.

The card lights in your hand. It is only a small glow, but the color of it is white-gold. Through the white-gold, every other color runs in fine threads: blue, green, yellow, red, and more besides, all of them moving.

Marda goes very still. Her hand has closed on her cane.

For a long moment she only looks at the card. Then she looks at your face.

The trickle runs out. The glow fades slowly from the card in your hand.

Marda leans forward. "Nala. Have you ever healed anyone? Or purified anything? Ever?"
