"Awesome, thanks!"

You fall in with the watch.

Laps first, round the green behind a line of grumbling watchmen. They keep their pace and don't slow for you.

Then fieldstones. You haul one to the far wall, drop it, run back, and haul the next. Your arms shake by the fifth stone. By the tenth your hands are raw.

Then staff bouts in pairs. The drill-mistress puts a quarterstaff in your hands. Your partner is a lanky young watchman, grinning. You've never held a staff in your life.

You swing wide. He steps in under it and cracks his staff across your ribs. The wind goes out of you, and you drop to one knee on the frosty grass.

They don't go easy on you. You get up, and you go again, and again, until the bell for the watch change.

By then you're bruised, soaked in sweat, and starving. Inside, your mana has nearly filled back.

The drill-mistress walks over. "Maud," she says. "Tomorrow, dawn bell."

She looks you over, red hair to raw hands. "You're the one drew the blight from Ivy Marsh."

"My nephew, Cob. He's got a blight scratch on his forearm. Look at it for me. Ten copper if you get it clean."
