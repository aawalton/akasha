You walk the two miles back up the road, full to the brim inside, and in at the south gate.

At Brannagh's, a tanner's lad sits on the bench, his palm gashed open and bound in a bloody rag.

"Slipped with the fleshing knife," he mutters. "Just now."

Brannagh unwinds the rag. The cut is clean, with no black in it. A fresh hurt, so it comes first.

You lay a holy thread along the gash and stitch. The thread holds on the first try.

The edges draw together and close to a pink seam. The lad flexes his hand and stares.

Down at the post, Marda sets the paled wolf's blightstone on the desk with her tongs.

You thread holy current in. Cold bites up into your hand. The black gives.

A second weave goes in smooth as water. The stone sits paler again, near half gray.

Inside, you're close to empty. You sit at the side bench with the beast guide.

Wolf, tree beetle, thornwing owl. Beside the jackalope, in Marda's hand: antler for the draught.

The light goes. Marda gets up for the dusk bell, then stops at the desk.

"Hild Wendle's leg. Edda Crane's hand," she says. "Word gets about. That's five blighted folk clean."

She sets a silver on the desk. "Quest's done."
