"Are there any books in town?" you ask. "I'd like to spend the afternoon learning, and I'd hate to pester people with questions if I don't need to."

Marda snorts. "Books are dear up here." She reaches behind the desk and sets two on it.

The first is fat and scuffed: a guide to Wrenmark beasts, by level, with their weak spots, their bounties, and which parts sell.

The second is thinner: the Guild's book of rules. Ranks, quest terms, the bounty table, what a ring does.

"Read them at the side bench as long as you like. They don't leave the post. Past these, the Hearth chapel at the top of the square keeps a herbal and a primer. Sister Wenna lends them to anyone who'll sit and read them there."
