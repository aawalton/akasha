You have enough coin for another night or two. You decide to spend the morning on your body.

Inside the wall, a beaten path runs the whole way round the town, about a mile, walked by the watch.

You start running it, cloak flapping, the knapsack thumping your back.

Folk stop and stare. A pack of children trails you for a lap, laughing, then drops away.

Between laps you drop to the frosty ground for push-ups, squats and sit-ups, until your arms shake. Your empty stomach complains the whole time.

Three laps in, your lungs burn and your legs are lead. Inside, your mana is slowly filling back.

On the south green, a dozen of the town watch are hauling stones and running drills. A broad woman past fifty, her gray hair cropped short and a Guild ring on her hand, is barking at them.

She watches you come round the wall a third time, then calls you over.

"You. Red. You run like you mean it, and you'll wreck your knees doing it alone. I drill the watch here, dawn bell to mid-morning. Laps, stones, staff work. Fall in with them if you like. Costs nothing."
