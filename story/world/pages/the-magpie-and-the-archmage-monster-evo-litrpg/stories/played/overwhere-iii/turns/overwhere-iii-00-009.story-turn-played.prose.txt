You go in, out of the cold. The stove's heat reaches your bare feet at once.

"Do I need to register to take on a task from the board," you ask, "or can I just come back when it's complete? I'm looking at gathering frostcap mushrooms. Also, anything you could tell me about them, or the area where they're found, would be appreciated."

The woman leans back, one hand resting on her cane. "Marda Hesk. I keep the post. Board work's Guild work, and the Guild pays Guild members. So yes, you register. Temporary's free. A form and a card. Do one job and you earn the ring."

She taps the desk with a blunt finger. "Frostcaps. They grow only in frost, on the north side of the old beech roots, along the wood's edge. Try the paths near the crossroads shrine. A careful picker gets twenty in two or three hours."

"Cut them at the root. Pull one by the stem and it bruises gray, and then it's worth nothing. And mind the gravecap. Looks the same, only grayer, and it smells of wet ash. Eat one, you're sick for days."

Her eyes go to your empty hands. "You'll want a knife. And keep off the south road. There's a blighted boar been seen along it. Stay at the edge. Don't go in past the brook."

She opens a drawer and takes out a sheet of paper and a thin pale card, smooth as polished wood. She pushes both across the desk, with an inkpot and a pen.

The form asks for your name, age, race, class, skills and traits, and your reason for joining.

"Fill that in," Marda says. "Then hold the card and push your mana into it. It shows what you've got."

She sits back in her chair, watching you.
