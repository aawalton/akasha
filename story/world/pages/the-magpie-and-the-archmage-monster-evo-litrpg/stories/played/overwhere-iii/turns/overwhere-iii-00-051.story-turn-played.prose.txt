At first light you look in at Brannagh's. You don't find a fresh hurt on the bench, or any new blight.

The drover with the burned arm is there anyway, first name on the slate. He nods to you and waits.

You go on down to the post. Marda sets the paled wolf's blightstone on the desk.

To your new sight the currents in the room run sharper, and they pour into your weave like a flood.

The first weave bites deep. The second fights you; cold stabs up to your elbow, but the black gives.

The third goes through clean. Then the fourth.

The last dark vein unravels. The wolf's stone cracks and bursts into a clean glimmerstone.

Marda pushes it across the desk to you without a word. Inside, you're empty again.

You jog to the south green. The watch is already running laps. Maud jerks her chin: fall in.

Laps. Fieldstones, hauled and stacked and hauled back. Your arms burn, and you keep going.

Staff bouts in pairs. Tam comes at you low and fast, and you read it wrong.

His staff sweeps your feet. You land flat on your back on the cold grass. The watch laughs.

You get up. You bout on with him until the watch changes, taking your knocks.

When Maud calls it, your legs shake under you. A blue box opens.

[New skill acquired – Staff Fighting.]

[Staff Fighting – At [Basic] level, guard, step aside and strike with a staff.]

Maud looks you over, sweat-soaked and grass-stained. "Three days. You'll do," she says.
