"Perfect, thanks!"

You take both books to the side bench and open the rules first.

Ranks run Copper, Bronze, Silver, Gold, on up to Adamantite. Twenty quests done opens a rank quest. A Copper ring shows only your Guild Profile and your quest windows. A Bronze ring shows beasts' names and how dangerous they are.

A failed quest costs only the Guild's regard. An abandoned one must be told at the desk.

The bounty table: a blightstone, one silver. A seed stone, ten copper. A Wrenmark wolf, twenty.

You work through it page by page while the stove ticks and Marda's pen scratches.

Then the beast guide. It opens on small game, each beast sketched, with its levels, weak spots and prices.

Jackalope, Level 1 to 4: strike behind the antlers. Meat 5 copper to Dunstan; antlers 2 a pair.

In the margin, in Marda's blunt hand: "Antler ground fine goes in Brannagh's mana draught."

Snow hare, frost toad, ridge fox. You're turning to hill boar when the door creaks.

A small girl of about twelve slips in, quick as a cat. Marda doesn't look up from her letter.

The girl spots you, marches straight over, and sits on the bench beside you with a gap-toothed grin.

"Brannagh says to tell you: a carter's wife, dog-bitten, is coming to her shop at the dusk bell."

She looks at the book, then at your hands. "You're the healer. Is it real magic? How's it work?"
