"Fine by me," you say. "Cob should be coming by at noon. He'll be next."

Brannagh nods, satisfied. The cat thumps down off the counter.

You pick up the clay cup and look in. Five black seeds lie in the bottom.

"What should I do with these seed stones you keep giving me?"

To your sight, each one is a tight knot of black current, turned in on itself. At the heart of each sits a pinprick of pale light.

"Marda pays the blight bounty on those," Brannagh says. "A tenth of a silver apiece. Till then, keep them off your skin and out of your bed. Shut in that cup."

She shakes her head. "How to be rid of one for good, I couldn't tell you. Never heard of it done."
