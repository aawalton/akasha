You kneel by the dead rabbit and work the knife in at the base of its antlers.

Out comes a small stone the size of a fingernail, faintly glowing. A glimmerstone. You wipe it clean and tuck it away with the frostcaps.

Then you sit down against the beech and look hard at the currents, hunting for white-gold like the card's.

The currents here hold threads of many colors. Blue, green, a dull yellow. None of them are white-gold.

Back along the path, though, where the currents cross at the shrine, something pale gleams among them.

You walk back to the shrine, sit against its warm stone, and look again.

There. A few white-gold threads, running where the currents meet. They move slowly, and they are warm to your touch.

You pay a little of your own mana, draw one thread out, and lay it along the bite.

It won't stay. It slips loose, and you catch it and lay it down again. It slips again. You lay it down again.

It takes the better part of half an hour of stubborn restitching before it holds.

Then warmth sinks into your forearm, and white-gold light moves under the skin. Slowly, the bite knits closed. It leaves a raw pink seam, and the bleeding stops.

You count. Fourteen frostcaps in your shirt, and four still whole under the old beech. Twenty needed.

A blue box opens.
