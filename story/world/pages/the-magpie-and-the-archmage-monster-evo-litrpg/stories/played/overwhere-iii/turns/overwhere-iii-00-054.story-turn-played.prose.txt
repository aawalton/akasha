"Thanks, Ivy."

Ivy waves you off from the rail, and Huw lifts his goad in his good right hand.

You walk out the south gate and down to the crossroads, and sit against the shrine's warm stone.

The white-gold threads seep in. By late afternoon you're full to the brim again.

You walk back up to town and into the post before the dusk bell.

"How many blightstones are left to cleanse here?" you ask Marda.

Marda taps the lead box with her cane. "Two whole ones. One stag, one wolf. And the six seed stones you brought in off folk. That's all there is in Merrowgate. More only come from new kills. The Thornmere wagon takes the box at the new moon, twelve days off."
