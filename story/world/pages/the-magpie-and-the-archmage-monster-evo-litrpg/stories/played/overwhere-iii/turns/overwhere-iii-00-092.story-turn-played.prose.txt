You kneel at the stumps and thread your braid down between the roots, toward the blight.

The lash finds the jackalope in the dark. The pull strand bites. Under the roots, it goes still.

You reach into the burrow and drag it out by the hind legs. A small blightstone lies under it.

You collect all three small blightstones and walk back up the south road toward town.

Your well fills a little on the way.

At the Post, Marda looks up from the ledger as you lay the three stones on the desk.

She turns them over one by one. Each is part-pale. Her brows go up.

"Silver apiece," she says, and counts three silver into your hand.

She dips her pen to mark the ledger, then pauses. "Where'd these come from?"
