"Okay, might just hunt my own then."

Marda grunts and goes back to her ledger.

At the Crook and Candle you eat a bowl of stew with bread for three copper and take a bed for eight.

You sleep hard and wake in the gray before first light, whole, your well brimming.

At first light two folk wait on Brannagh's bench.

An old shepherd off the slate rolls up his trouser. A ram's-horn scar runs down his shin.

Your first Mending slides off the old scar tissue. The mana goes, and the scar stays as it was.

The next three take. The ridge flattens, pales, and goes smooth.

The shepherd runs his thumb down his shin. He doesn't say a word, and leaves a wedge of hill cheese.

The second is a woodcutter with yesterday's axe gash in his shin. It's clean, with no blight in it.

One weave closes it. He counts ten copper into your hand.

You reach the yard late. The watch is already on laps, and Maud jerks her chin at the line.

Laps, then hauling fieldstone until your arms shake.

Then staff bouts with Tam. You slip his swing and rap his knuckles. He swears, then grins.

You stay until the watch changes, near half past ten.

At the post, Marda looks up. "Two leads came in."

"The blighted boar's track crossed the south road by Edda Crane's clearing."

"Level fourteen. A silver fifty on the board. It ranges between the road and the Wren Brook ford."

"And a woodcutter found a deer by the Wren Brook, its hide rotted to black sludge."

"Small blight first, I'd say." Marda taps the board. "But it's your hunt."
