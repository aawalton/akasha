You sit on Brannagh's bench to wait. Slowly, your mana trickles back.

Just after the noon bell, a gangly, freckled lad slouches in, his sleeve already rolled. He smells of lime pits and is trying hard to look bored. He can't quite look at you.

Across his forearm runs a thin scratch gone gray. "Rat," he mutters. "In the tannery yard."

By the time Brannagh has had a look and tutted at it, you have just enough mana for a pull.

You push out your white-gold thread and draw.

The blight is shallow, but it bites. Cold jolts up your arm, harder than you braced for. The black comes up anyway and clots into one more seed stone.

To your sight, the scratch is clean. The gray fades from it as you watch. It's shallow enough to close by itself, and it doesn't need mending.

Cob stares at his arm, then digs out a knotted rag and pushes it at you. "From my aunt."

Ten copper inside. He's out the door before you can say a word, ears red.

You're empty now, and aching to the shoulder.

You walk down to the Guild post with the clay cup and set it on Marda's desk.

Marda looks up and gives you one slow nod. She counts out ten copper a seed, then lifts the six seeds with tongs, one by one, into the lead-lined box.

You tell her: Ivy, Garrick and Cob, all three clean.

Marda sits back. "Then the blight work's yours now. Purify or no Purify."

She takes a fresh sheet, writes, and pins it to the board.

Guild quest: cleanse five blighted Wrenmark folk. One silver.

"Three done," Marda says, and marks it. "Two to go."
