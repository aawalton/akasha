"That will be useful," you tell the shrine stones. The ward fades from your skin.

You walk back at dusk. The bell has rung, and the watch lets you in by the wicket.

Supper at the Crook is three copper. You sleep upstairs at the Post under the two wool blankets.

Day twelve. At first light the drover with the old forearm burn sits on Brannagh's bench at last.

Your first Mending Weave drags on the old tissue and stings your palms, but it takes.

The second and third run smooth. The burn flattens, pale and soft as the skin around it.

The drover turns his arm over in the light, and over again. For a long moment he doesn't say a word.

He pays Brannagh's ten copper, and she hands it on to you.

He leaves a twist of salt beef on the counter for the healer.

On the south green the watch is drilling. Tam Rowe squares up to you, grinning.

He comes in fast as ever. You step inside it, and for once it's Tam who lands on the grass.

The watch whoops. Tam lies there laughing at the sky.

Your well is full again by noon. The noon plate at the Crook is two copper.

As you pass the south green on your way to the gate, Tam calls after you.

"Woodcutter heard a blighted boar in the brambles above the Wren Brook ford. Big one."
