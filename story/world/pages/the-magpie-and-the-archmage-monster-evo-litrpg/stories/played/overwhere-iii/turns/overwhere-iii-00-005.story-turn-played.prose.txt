"I'm... Nala," you say, and then your eyes go back to the currents.

The road's current runs up to the town wall and thins there. It frays against the stone, as if the wall is in its way.

The guard is lean and weathered, forty or so, with a drooping brown mustache and a green tabard over his coat. His glow is faint, only a shade brighter than Tobin's candle.

Before you can say more, Tobin speaks up. "Robbed on the road, Hal. Found her at the shrine. Took a knock to the head, I reckon. I'll stand for her."

Hal looks at you a moment longer. Then he opens the ledger against the lantern post and writes.

"Nala. Red hair, freckles, no shoes. Robbed on the road. Vouched, Tobin Wick." He shuts the book. "Go on, then." To you, dry: "You're short of work, try the Guild post. Just there, inside."

He nods at a squat old tollhouse past the gate. A board by its door is papered over with notices.

The cart rolls through the gate and into the town.

Merrowgate is gray stone and timber. Lamps are coming on in the windows, and woodsmoke hangs thick in the cold air. The street opens into a wide square, the market stalls folded up for the night, a bell tower standing over it all.

Tobin reins in before a long timbered inn with a lit window. Its sign shows a crook and a candle.

He climbs down stiffly and looks up at you. "Crook and Candle. I'll stand you supper and a bed. Tonight, anyhow. After that we'll see what's to be done." He jerks his head at the door. "Come in out of the cold?"
