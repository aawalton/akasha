"Could you help me understand a few things, Marda? How do I level up faster?"

Marda leans back, cane across her knees. "Fight things near your level. That's the meat of it."

"Green over a beast's head, it's worth nothing to you. White's near you. Yellow, orange, stronger."

"Light pink's a tier above. Red, you run."

"Healers gain by their own work, not by the kills they help with. Heal the hard cases."

"Finish Guild quests. Go in a party and you share the quests and the gain."

"At Level ten you come here and touch the class stone. That's your first advancement."

"Took me thirty hard years to make forty-seven. Most folk in Merrowgate never pass fifteen."

"And what do skill rarities mean?" you ask.

"Ranks. Basic, Novice, Adept, Expert, Legend. Most folk sit at Basic or Novice their whole lives."

"Adept's a master. Expert's a veteran. Legend's a skill at its full potential."

"I've met a Legend skill twice in forty years. Both in Thornmere's high ranks. Never here."

"Past Legend, a skill can go to Rank two, for a hundred glimmerstones."

"Skills come from doing a thing over and over, from teaching, the shop, or bad need."

"You hold ten. Full, a new one pushes an old one out, and that one's gone for good."

"Two of like level can be fused, for good." Her eyes narrow at you. "Why d'you ask about ranks?"
