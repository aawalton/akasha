"Jackalopes," you tell Marda.

She writes it in the ledger: "Kiln jackalopes, three."

You walk out the south gate and up the two miles to the shrine.

White-gold current hums thick around the shrine stones as you sit down beside them.

You tip Brannagh's clay cup and set the first seed stone on your palm.

You draw the lent current into a Cleansing Weave. It burns you a little, and your well stays full.

The black knot splits cleanly. A glimmer speck glows where it was.

The second stone is stubborn. You hold the weave a breath longer before it cracks.

The third and fourth crack at a touch, one after the other.

The fifth fights you, then gives, a speck rolling into your palm.

Five small burns sting your hands. Your own well is untouched, and has filled a little.

The clay cup is empty of seed stones, and six glimmer specks lie in your palm.
