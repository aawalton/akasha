You hurry down to the post. Marda is already eyeing the light.

"One," Marda says, and tongs out the wolf's second stone.

You loop the weave tight. It sinks in deep and clean, and a thick dark vein tears away.

The wolf's stone is over half pale now.

The dusk bell rings. Marda tongs it back into the lead box and locks it. You still have mana left.

You walk out the night wicket, past the guard, and two miles down the dark road to the crossroads.

You sit against the shrine's warm stone in the dark. The white-gold threads seep in.

You rest a bit over an hour. Your well is two-thirds full, and the throb in your temples is gone.

You walk the two miles back, in through the wicket, and along the dark streets to Brannagh's lane.

Brannagh's shutters are closed. No light shows under the door. The bench outside is empty.
