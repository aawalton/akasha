You reach for every raw current within reach and drag them toward the boar's head.

Brook-blue, earth-brown and frost-white grind together, fighting your grip, and the strain bites.

The boar breaks into its charge, and the knot slides along with it, half a stride behind.

The currents tear loose from your grip and scatter, unburst.

The boar hits you full on. You go down hard in the frost, the ward flaring as it takes the blow.

Your ribs throb. The ward's shimmer is still on your skin.

The boar's momentum carries it past. It skids, wheels round in the brambles, and faces you again.

It stands five paces off, snorting blood, tusks low.
