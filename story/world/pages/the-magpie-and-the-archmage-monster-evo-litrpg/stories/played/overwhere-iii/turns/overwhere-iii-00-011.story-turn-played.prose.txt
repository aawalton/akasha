"I don't think so," you say. "Why do you ask?"

Marda lets out a long breath and sits back, both hands on the head of her cane.

"Because that light is holy," she says plainly. "White-gold. The rarest there is. Holy magic heals. And it purifies. Blight included." She shakes her head once. "The rest of it, all those colors running through, I've never seen at all."

Her jaw sets. "There's no holy mage in the whole Wrenmark. Not one. I've written to the Thornmere hall three times this winter for one. Three letters. Set aside."

She looks toward the south window, toward the wood. "And the blight's getting worse."

She pulls the form back across the desk, dips the pen, and writes a line at the bottom. Temporary. Registered.

"You're on the books. Copper work." She reaches under the desk into a box of old gear and sets a plain knife in a worn sheath in front of you. "Loan. Bring it back."

"Go get your frostcaps. Cut at the root, mind the gravecap, keep off the south road."

She holds your eyes. "And when you're back, you and I are going to talk about the blight."
