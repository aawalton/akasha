You walk out the south gate and down to the crossroads, and sit against the shrine's warm stone.

The white-gold threads seep in. The ache behind your eyes fades with them.

By near noon your well is brimming again. You walk back up to the post.

Marda sets the stag's stone on the desk. One thin dark thread is left inside it.

You close the loop and pull. Your grip slips, and the whole weave comes apart in your hands.

The mana is gone, and the thread is still there. Marda doesn't say a word.

You breathe, and loop again, slower. The weave closes tight and sinks in deep.

The last thread tears loose. The stag's stone cracks and bursts into a clean glimmerstone.

Marda pushes it across the desk to you. "Yours."

Marda tongs out the wolf's second stone. It's whole and black right through.

You loop a weave into it. A dark vein frays and the stone pales a shade.

The next loop wobbles. The snap-back kicks into the stone instead of you, and a second vein goes.

The wolf's stone is a little pale now, a long way from cracking. Your well is empty again.

Your stomach growls. It's past noon, and you still haven't eaten today.
