You grab at every current in reach and pour your whole well into them.

You twist a Current Lash and a Cleansing Weave into one braid and fling it at the fox.

It catches the fox mid-leap, a stride short of Fairley's legs.

The lash cracks like a whip, and the cleansing pull rips the blight out of it in the same instant.

The fox tumbles through the air and slams into the hedgebank. It doesn't get up.

Its wrong eyes go dull. It lies still in the gray dirt by the sett mouth.

The dark smear is gone from it. Where it lay curled, a small black stone sits on the gray earth.

You sway. Your well is bone dry, and your hands are shaking.

Fairley lowers his stick slowly. He looks at the fox, then at you. "Gods," he breathes.

Fairley nods at the small black stone in the dirt. "What's to be done with that, then?"
