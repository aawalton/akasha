"Thanks, happy to save the coin."

Marda grunts and goes back to the letter.

The dusk bell rings as you cross to the Crook and Candle. Stew and bread, 3 copper.

You eat every scrap, then climb the Post's narrow stair to the room under the eaves.

A cot, two wool blankets, a shuttered window. You're asleep before the watch makes its first pass.

You wake before first light, whole. Your well brims. The bites are only pink marks now.

Below, the Post's door bangs. Hooves clatter off toward the east gate and the Thornmere road: the rider for Thornmere, away.
