"Just curious," you say. "I was thinking through things I read. What about traits? How are those different from skills?"

Marda looks at you a long moment, then lets it lie. "Skills you do. Traits you are."

"Senses, hardiness, the body. Inventory. They work on their own, no casting."

"They've their own ten slots. Like-level pairs fuse, same as skills."

"Some are born with them. Some come from long hard use of a sense or the body. Some, the shop."

"Inventory near every adventurer has. Three glimmerstones at the skill shop. Most buy it first."

"Mine's Inventory, a grip like stone, and a nose for trouble. All three at Novice."

"How they climb, I couldn't tell you. Mine came slow. Years."

"Never heard of one rising in days." She taps her cane once on the floor, still watching you.
