You take the oatcakes with thanks. They're hot, buttery, and gone in a few bites.

"Could you spread the word?" you ask Brannagh. "I'll heal mornings only, except for emergencies."

Brannagh nods. "Mornings, and any hour for blood or blight. I'll tell them."

You walk to the Crook and Candle, pay Bet eight copper for the bed, and sleep like a stone.

At first light you're back on Brannagh's lane, your well full. One boy waits on the bench.

He's a cooper's boy off the slate, an old dog-bite scar puckered across his cheek.

The first Mending Weave sinks in deep. The second and third smooth the cheek flat.

He grins, feels his face, and runs off down the lane without a word.

You walk to the south green. The watch is already drilling: Maud, Tam and five more.

Maud jerks her chin. You take a staff from the rack and fall in late.

You run, haul, and bout. Your staff work comes easier now. You block Tam twice running.

When the watch changes near half past ten, you're sweating, sore, and still on your feet.

Maud looks you over. "Better. Tomorrow at the dawn bell, if you want it."
