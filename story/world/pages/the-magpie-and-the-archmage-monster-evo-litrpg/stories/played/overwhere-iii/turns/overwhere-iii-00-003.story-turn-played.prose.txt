"Thank you," you say simply, and climb up onto the bench beside him. The step is higher than it should be, and you have to reach for it.

He settles the coat around your shoulders. It is heavy and still warm from him, and it smells of apples.

He clicks his tongue, and the mules lean into the north road, toward the town.

Quietly, you turn your attention inward, to the word the box gave you. Mana Weaver.

You reach for the threads, and they come. They are cool, like running water over your fingers, and eager, pressing toward your hand as if they have been waiting for you to ask.

Looked at this way, they are clearer. They are currents, running along both roads and meeting at the shrine behind you.

Then you see the carter differently. A faint glow sits about him, close to his skin, a candle's worth of light. The mules carry one too, dimmer still. Living things have it.

A blue box opens without your asking.

[Mana Weaver – At [Basic] level, you see mana currents and auras. ???]

The last part still reads ???. Whatever else the trait does, it hasn't shown you yet.

The cart rolls on between bare winter fields toward the town wall. Behind you, the shrine falls away.

The carter glances at you, then down at your feet, and his jaw works.

"Tobin Wick," he says, tapping his chest. "Cider and salt, Applegarth to Merrowgate." Then, more gently: "So who was it did this to you, lass? Bandits? Out of the wood? Where are your people?"
