Your well is empty, but the currents were never yours to spend. You reach for all of them at once.

Blue off the brook, green from the beech roots. You drag every thread within reach in.

You don't weave them. You knot them, tight and shapeless, right on the woman's shield.

The colors fight in your grip. Your skull throbs, and your hands shake with holding them.

You let go.

The knot bursts with a boom that rolls away down the brook. The shield tears apart like wet paper.

The woman is flung against her hut wall. Both wolves tumble yelping through the bracken.

The rot mist blows away with the blast, and clean air pours into the clearing.
