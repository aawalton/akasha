"Let's go there together, now."

Edda nods once and takes up her barrow handles. You gather the four seed stones off the grass.

You walk the two miles up the north road together, the barrow rattling empty between you.

The south gate is still open. The light is going gold over the rooftops.

At the Post, Marda looks up from the ledger and sees your faces. She sets down her pen.

Edda tells it plainly. Mother Sallow's hut, two winters, the dogs that weren't dogs.

The blighted stumps by her kilns. And the woman's face, changing, the way you saw it.

Marda listens without a word. Her jaw sets harder with every line.

"There's a rumor going round," she says at last. "The Night Order."

"Mages who blight beasts on purpose. I took it for alehouse talk."

"I'll tell the reeve and the watch tonight. A rider goes to Thornmere at dawn."

"Until Thornmere answers, nobody goes near that hut, or into the deep wood alone."

She turns to you. "Where did she go?"
