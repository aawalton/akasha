You settle with your back to a shrine stone and let your well refill.

You play with the white-gold current, drawing it through your fingers into loose new shapes.

A spiral collapses. A strand sent wide frays. Each fed try leaves a small burn.

One shape keeps coming back: the current drawn close, lying over your own skin.

You try to close it over your forearm. It slips, and stings you.

You try again, slower, feeding it from the current, and it settles over you like warm light.

A faint white-gold shimmer lies over your skin, and holds.

[New skill acquired – Holy Ward.]

Your well is full again. The shadows of the shrine stones are long, and the sun is low over the wood.
