The words stay in your mind after you have read them all.

You go back to the top and read it all again, slowly, line by line. A Status. Your Status, with a Class and Attributes and Paths, sitting in your mind in plain words.

What is a Path? A road you walk, or something that walks you? Soulstealing sounds like what you just did to the demon. Exceptionalism sounds like homework you already aced. You turn the words over and get nothing back from them.

Talented sits there too, a shiny one hundred beside it. You don't know enough yet to know what it does.

After a while the words stop feeling new. The dark is warm and the heartbeat keeps time, and you can't see, or move much, or go anywhere. You are bored.

So you count. One, two, three, four. Past fifty, past a hundred, on into the hundreds, one number after the next.

Somewhere past the walls, voices start up. Muffled, many of them, women's voices, chanting the same words together. You don't know the words. They are close, on every side of you, rising and falling, and they keep on.

You set yourself an exercise: Fibonacci sequences taken modulo n+2, then n+1, then on down, every starting pair carried round to its loop and to the length of that loop. You start with n+2 and work it whole. Take a pair of numbers, add them, keep the remainder, carry the pair along, then the next, until a pair comes back that you have already had. That is a loop, and you learn it by its length. Then the next pair, until every pair under n+2 is held in memory with its loop.

Before you take up n+1, you go back through what you have built: every pair under n+2, its loop, its length, recited once in order. Then you step down.

Take a pair, follow it round until it closes, name the loop, hold the length, then take the next pair. Then the next modulus, and the same again.

The numbers start to slip. A pair goes missing from your hold, then another, and the loops run together in the dark. Somewhere in the middle of a modulus, you sleep.

You wake, and the dark is the same dark. You take up the tables where you left them, at the pair you lost. The moduli come easier this time. A pair that took a long holding to run now closes quickly, and its loop sits where it belongs among the others.
