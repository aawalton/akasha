You go still under the hands and listen to the deep voice. It comes slow and close, a short run of sounds, then a breath, then the same run again. Each time it lands in the same places, like a line someone has said so often they no longer think about it.

You try to hold the shape of it. When the voice starts again you go along with it in your head, sound for sound, the way you would hum along to a song you only half know.

By the time you reach the end of the run, the start of it is gone. You reach back for it and get only the hum of the voice, and the middle has run together into that same hum.

You try again on the next repeat, holding tighter to the first sound this time, and lose the last one instead. On the one after, you lose them both.

Then the deep voice stops. The heavier hand presses down once more, firm, and both hands lift away.

The deep voices move off, talking low again. A door thuds somewhere. Above you, the woman starts humming.
