You go still under the hands and listen to the deep voice. It comes slow and close, a short run of sounds, then a breath, then the same run again. Each time it lands in the same places, like a line someone has said so often they no longer think about it.

You try to hold the shape of it. When the voice starts again you go along with it in your head, sound for sound, the way you would hum along to a song you only half know.
