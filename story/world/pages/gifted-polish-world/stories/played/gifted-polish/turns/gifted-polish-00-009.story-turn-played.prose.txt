You stay still and listen. Above you the woman starts to hum, slow, rocking.

There are no words in it. The same few lines come round again and again, up and down like a cradle tipping.

You hum along in your head and put the sounds to it, one to a note. Sa on the first. Vo on the second. Reth where the line dips at the end.

Each time the tune comes round, the three sounds are already waiting on their notes, and you reach them a little quicker than the time before.

Partway through a line she hums it herself, softly, Sa-vo-reth, on the very notes where you put it.
