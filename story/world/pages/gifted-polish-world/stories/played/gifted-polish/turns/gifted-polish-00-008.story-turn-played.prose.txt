You keep listening. The woman above hums through most of the day, and now and then she talks with someone, light voices, a laugh, footsteps going off. The deep one doesn't come back.

You sleep, and wake, and listen, and sleep again. Each time you wake you go straight to the voices, and each time it is the humming, or quiet, or someone light and quick.

Then you wake to deep voices beside you again, talking low and easy, the way they did the morning before.

A heavy hand settles over you, and the deep voice starts its run, slow and close.

This time you have it. Three sounds: Sa, then vo, then reth. It comes the same each time, and on the second time through you are already saying it along with him in your head.

On the next repeat you open your mouth and work your tongue to make it back.

Your mouth opens and closes around the shape. Nothing comes out. There is only the warm fluid where your breath should be, moving a little as your jaw moves.
