Welcome to Aethelgard — where emotions glitch harder than any dragons.

Livia was a psychology student one failed thesis away from dropping out. Then she got hit by a motorcycle, fell into a coma, and woke up inside an MMORPG.

Not as a hero. Not as a mage. Not even as a background character with a cool backstory.

She respawned as an NPC Mental Supporter Lv.1 — zero attack skills, zero combat utility, and zero respect from literally anyone.

Then the server's #1 guild found her.
Bloodbath & Beyond: ranked first on the leaderboard, feared by every faction, and somehow completely incapable of functioning without someone to talk to at 2am about their feelings.

They needed a therapist.

She was the only one available.

Features: LitRPG progression system, found family, comedy that earns its emotional moments, guild members with genuinely complicated damage, and a mystery buried in the server's code that someone really doesn't want her to find.

Also: a sentient slime, a paladin with a gambling addiction, an Ghost Rogue assassin who carries a Hello Kitty flashlight, and a guild master who prepared a room for someone he hadn't met yet.

Updated twice a week.
