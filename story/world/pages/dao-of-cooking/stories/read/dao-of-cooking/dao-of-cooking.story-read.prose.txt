An ex-Michelin-starred chef who can cook spiritual dishes that rival even the most potent alchemy pills, a larger-than-life Fatty sidekick, and a dozen kids with genius-level talent in cultivation.

Oh, and a famous Herbalist and her flying squirrel!

Lei has to cultivate the talents around him with his overly spiritual dishes while forging a new path for himself in this Xianxia world. But not everything is as simple as it seems. There is a looming threat on the horizon, some suspicious figures masquerading as cultivators, and enough clues to suggest that there is more to his reincarnation than meets the eye.

The solution? Throw all the spiritual ingredients in the wok and hope for the best. Simple as that. And try not to get too high while eating them.

Yes, he just needs to cook.

Note: This is not a %100 slice-of-life story. The MC will cultivate, fight against bad guys, and use his dishes in rather interesting ways. There will, however, be a restaurant plot in the future.
