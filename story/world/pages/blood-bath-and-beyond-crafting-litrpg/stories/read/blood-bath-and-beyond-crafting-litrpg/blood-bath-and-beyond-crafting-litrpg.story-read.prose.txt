Isekai’ed and offered a dark and terrible power? Nah. Tell the system to get lost and head to the countryside to make magic soap!

Rachel never wanted to be the ‘Dark Enchantress’. Sacrificing the still-beating hearts of peasants to gain terrible eldritch powers? Please. Blood is such a pain to get out of the carpet, and black leather just gives off the wrong vibe. No, she will be a soap maker, and she’s willing to stab anyone who disagrees.

After all, she is not averse to violence. On her own terms. Soap making is a dangerous business. So, if she needs to explore dark forests, delve into ancient dungeons, and slaughter mythical beasts, well, the ingredients need to come from somewhere, don’t they?

In a filthy world full of dark secrets, dirty peasants, and blood-thirsty unicorns, here comes Rachel with a really big hat and an even bigger mana pool.

Just a bubbly [Witch] in a bloody world, minding her own damn business.
