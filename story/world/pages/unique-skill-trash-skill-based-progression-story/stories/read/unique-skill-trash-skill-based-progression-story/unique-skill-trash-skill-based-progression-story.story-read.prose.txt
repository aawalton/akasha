In a world where your Skills are your everything, what does it say about you when your personal Skill is literally Trash.

Catarine has found herself reborn into a new world where you can achieve great heights, but only if you have the proper Skills slotted and the correct motivation to improve those Skills.

After being reborn into the body of a Necromantic family, what methods would you need to take to survive when your Skill is labelled exactly how everyone thinks of you.
