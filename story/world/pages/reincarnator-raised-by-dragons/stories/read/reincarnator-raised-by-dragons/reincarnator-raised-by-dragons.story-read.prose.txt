Kevin has lived multiple lives, and all of them had been either difficult or traumatic. Even his first life on earth involved portals and attacks by monsters! But he is living a new life on a beautiful fantasy world now, and he has the best adoptive parents, ever.

However, although he has enjoyed a loving, peaceful life, he still has a few...complications. Kevin is a Champion of a goddess. His parents are dragons. He was born a goblin.

Spending two decades studying in his adoptive dragon parents' cave has been exactly what he needed to rest and relax, but he knew it couldn't last forever. So when he receives a request from his parents, an adventure, asking him to do something important that the dragons cannot do themselves, Kevin is not too surprised. In fact, he's excited.

After all, he has been curious to explore his new world. But more than anything, he still holds out hope that he can finally find some magic that he can learn...
