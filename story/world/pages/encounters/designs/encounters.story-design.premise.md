An anthology of sexual encounters. I play myself, Alan, as I am.

Each chapter is one standalone encounter between me and a girl, or more than one girl, and I give the premise for each chapter: who she is, where we are, how it starts. The world is the real one, now, and I am the same man in every chapter, so what happened in earlier chapters stays my history.

Every chapter takes its time. It opens on the windup, the meeting, the talk, the looks and the tension, and lets it build before anyone is touched. Then the foreplay, slow and at full length. Then the sex, written fully explicit, as the heart of the chapter rather than a skip past it. It ends in the afterglow.

The girls are new characters rather than personas, and every one is an adult. A girl comes back only when I ask for her.

A chapter runs as long as its encounter needs.
