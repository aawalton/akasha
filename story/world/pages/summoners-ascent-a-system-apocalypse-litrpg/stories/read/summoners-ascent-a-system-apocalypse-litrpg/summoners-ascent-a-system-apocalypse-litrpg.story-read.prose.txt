Ashton was dragged away, forced into subservience and to do all the little things that the terrible creature that summoned him couldn't be bothered to, when the System invaded. But when that world fell victim to the Apocalypse... Ashton returned back home, to just a few moments after he left.

But he couldn't escape. 

[The [Tutorial] has begun!]

The System followed him. As Earth is closing in on its end, Ashton, armed with nothing but the knowledge that had been forced unto him, has to do what he can to not let Earth fall victim to the Council, those responsible for bringing the System here. Ashton becomes a Summoner.

Elementals, Spirits, Fairies, even Demons - he will call them to his side to grow his power, defeat the monsters that are now swarming earth, put the Council in its place, and most importantly, save humanity in the process.

Summoner's Ascent is set in a System Apocalypse in a world trying its best to survive. Join Ashton in this Progression Fantasy to see if he'll outsmart the council this time around!
