Knowledge is a blessing, but sometimes it's like a curse.

 

Jinyo is an unemployed botanist looking for a new job. He lost his mother many years ago.

One day, he meets a daughter he's never seen before and thinks he'll have a chance to start life anew. But everything changes when he becomes bound to a mysterious power.

He glimpses heaven's secrets and learns that his mother's death and his daughter's future fate were all predetermined by an entity far too powerful for humans to resist.

If fate is something determined by someone, then why can't Jinyo do the same?

Whoever they want dead, he will block them. Whatever they want to happen, he will destroy it.

If the other side is a god, then he is a demon—a heavenly demon, its natural enemy.

This is not a battle that ends in one lifetime, but a struggle spanning thousands of years, with humanity's future at stake.

[What to Expect]

LitRPG Progression | Cultivation 

Weak to OP: In the beginning, the protagonist isn't very strong, but as he accumulates power and skills over time, he'll grow stronger by leaps and bounds.

Growing Vegetables: The protagonist's power is tied to growing vegetables and gardening. Certain fruits or vegetables provide special stat bonuses that can massively increase the character's strength. This is a unique feature of the protagonist's power. 

Alchemy: There's crafting of elixirs of longevity from harvested materials, as well as many other items that will be created. Teaching Students: There's teaching involved, taking on disciples or exchanging knowledge with others. This creates opportunities for character interactions. Perfect for those who enjoy master-disciple content. 

Building a Family: The protagonist will have a daughter and son, and will also become involved with other characters that will make the family grow larger. It's not limited to blood relations. This novel has elements that help characters bond more closely over time.

There are currently 60 chapters, with over 200,000 words.
