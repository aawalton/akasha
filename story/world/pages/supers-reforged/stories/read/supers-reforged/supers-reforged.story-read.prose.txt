Superpowers are a lottery, and I was given the privilege of obtaining a really useless one.

My grandpa was right, too bad he is not here to cheer me up. He always knew how to do that, when he wasn't busy making strange technology in the lab hidden under the old house on top of the hill. He kept it hidden from me, but now I have it, and it helped me awaken a second power...

The power to take, alter, improve and bestow superpowers to anyone, with a single touch.

Little did I know, it was about to make my cozy life in the hill house a lot more chaotic. And full of beautiful women who loved me.

This is a harem story.
