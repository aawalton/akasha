I am a Queen. 

Well, I was a Queen before the aristocracy betrayed me and I was abandoned by the world to die. 

As my life faded, I reached across the worlds, searching for a soul that would resonate with my own. And I found one—a tiny, fragile ember. It called out to me, beating to the thrum of my soul.

And when it flickered weakly, on the verge of being snuffed out, I seized it with everything I had.

My enemies think they have won. That I am gone for good.

But I will return. And when I do, fire will rain upon those who betrayed me.

To do that, I must first break this new world. I know nothing of it, but one thing is certain—

It isn't ready for me.

Readers can expect: a fast pace, no filler arcs, no romance, the worst of humanity, action, world-building, magic, wuxia-based power progression, a UNIQUE gladiator slave arc, an insane spin on "Systems," and a ruthless path to revenge. The main character, Lilith, is a woman born to destruction, death, and violence. This is not a story of redemption, and Lilith is by no means any sort of hero. Rather, this fiction is about finding one's humanity even when long thought beaten from you. 

Unfortunately, sometimes being human is worse than being a monster.
