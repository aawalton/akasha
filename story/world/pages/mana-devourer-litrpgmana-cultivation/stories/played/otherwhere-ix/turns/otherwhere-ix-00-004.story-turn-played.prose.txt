As it closes, you jump. You throw everything into it, straight up, as high as this body will go.

It isn't high. Your legs are shorter than the ones you are used to, and the lift runs out well before you meant it to. At the top you bring everything down anyway, heels first, all of your weight behind them, aimed square at its back.

It jinks aside at the last instant, quick as a flinch, its quills laid flat along its back.

Your bare heels hit glassgrass instead. The blades snap and grind under them.

Your balance was already wrong, and now it is gone. You go down hard on one hip.

It is on you before you can get up. Its jaws close on your calf.

The teeth sink through the tights and into you, and it hangs on and twists, its whole low body wrenching one way and then the other. The pain is huge and hot. It drags at your leg like a weight on a rope.

Then pale blue light fills the air in front of your eyes, in lines of plain text.

[System integration initialising.]

[Processing…]

[Subject's World of Origin: Earth. Documented.]

[No summoning ritual recorded. No binding recorded. Subject is unclaimed.]

[Greetings, Nala. Welcome to Firrelia, The First World.]

[You are hereby bound to a Firrelian system, through which the potential of your body and soul will be summarily measured.]

[Inborn trait established: Unset.]

[Unique trait established: ???.]

Below the glowing words, the beast grinds its teeth in deeper and wrenches at your calf again.
