"Firrelia System, if you have a unique trait waiting for me, now is the time," you say. "Otherwise you'll have lost your chance."

No blue box. The air in front of your eyes stays empty.

You grab at the glass in your own forearm and pull. A quill sliver comes free, finger-long, pale and wet.

You drive it down into the bare skin of the beast's throat, just below its locked jaw.

It goes in. The beast jerks, and a thin, choked squeal comes out through its teeth.

You push harder, and the sliver bites into your own palm as you do. Your grip goes slick with both your blood and its.

Then the glass snaps. A short stub stays standing in its throat, and your fist closes on nothing.

The wound is shallow. Blood runs from it, but the beast is still breathing hard through its nose.

Its jaws stay locked on your calf. It twists again, harder, as if the pain has made it angry.
