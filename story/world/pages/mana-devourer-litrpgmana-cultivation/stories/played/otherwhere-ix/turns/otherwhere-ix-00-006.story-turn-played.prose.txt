You swing your free leg over it and hook your thighs round its neck, hunting for the windpipe.

Its head is still down at your calf. Your thighs close on the top of its neck instead of the throat, right over the quills at the nape.

They punch through your tights into the insides of your thighs. Fewer get through the cloth than went into your arms, but the ones that do go deep.

You squeeze anyway, with everything you have.

Under your legs there is only thick, hard muscle, and it doesn't give. The beast doesn't choke, and its jaws stay locked on your calf.

Squeezed, it thrashes its whole body and wrenches its head hard against the bite. The teeth tear deeper into your calf. It hangs on and twists, harder than before.

Your leg goes hot, and then strangely weak. Your thighs loosen, and you never told them to.

Blood soaks dark through your tights, all down the length of your calf.

It heaves, and its throat turns up under your knee, bare and working, just below the locked jaw.
