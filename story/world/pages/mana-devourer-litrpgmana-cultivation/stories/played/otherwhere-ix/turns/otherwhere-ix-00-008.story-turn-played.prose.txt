You tear another sliver out of your forearm. Your fingers are slippery, and they are shaking.

You stab down at its throat again.

It is twisting as you strike. The point skids off the hard bone of its jaw instead, and the sliver jolts out of your wet grip and drops somewhere into the glassgrass.

The beast wrenches its whole body sideways, dragging at your calf with its locked jaws.

Something in your leg tears.

The pain goes white. For a moment you can't see anything at all.

When your sight clears, your hands are shaking badly, and your leg will barely answer you.

The stub of glass still stands in its throat. It is still hanging on, breathing hard.
