Either it dies first or you do, and whichever one is left won't be far behind.

You throw both forearms across its bare throat and drop all of your weight onto them.

The shards still in your arms drive deeper as you press. You press anyway.

Your forearm catches the glass stub and rams it down into the throat, all the way in.

Something inside gives with a wet pop. The beast's breath turns to a gurgling rattle.

Its jaws spring open. It lets go of your calf and heaves for air.

It thrashes under you, claws raking the glassgrass, but it can't get its head round to you. Blood bubbles up around the stub with every heave.

You stay down on it, your whole weight on your arms, and you don't let up.

The heaving slows. The kicking slows.

Inside the minute it shudders once, and goes limp beneath you.

Its eyes are half shut. A thin wet whistle still comes and goes through the torn throat.
