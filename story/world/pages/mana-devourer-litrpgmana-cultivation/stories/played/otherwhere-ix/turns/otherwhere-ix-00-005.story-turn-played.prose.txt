"Well, you might want to hurry up then, Firrelia!" you grunt through your teeth.

The blue words don't answer. They thin, and fade out of the air.

You throw yourself forward off the ground, over the beast, arms reaching for its neck.

The instant your body touches its back, the flat quills spring up, all of them at once.

Glass drives into your forearms and the soft insides of your arms. A spray of small, bright cuts, everywhere at once.

You get your arms round it anyway and try to choke it. But its neck is short and thick, and the nape is quilled too, and your grip won't close. There is nothing there like a neck to squeeze. Only muscle, and glass.

It twists under you, and every twist snaps more shards off into your skin. Its jaws stay locked on your calf the whole time, grinding.

It heaves, and your hands slide down its front, and the skin under them is suddenly soft and bare. The throat, the belly: smooth, with not a quill on them. Its jaw sits just above your fingers.

Your arms are running with blood. Your grip is slipping. And it is still hanging on.
