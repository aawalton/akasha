"Garth Marsh was the first I met. I think I've got the name right. Names seem to slip from me. I don't really remember anything before that. His daughter had the rot, and I used my Talent to pull it out of her."

At Garth's name, something shifts in Lady Varrow's face.

"Garth Marsh. Yes. I heard of that, weeks ago. A stranger who drew greymaw rot from his girl. Garth sent to me first, begging a healer. I had no Talent to spare him. So the girl's life is one I owe you as well."

Under her hands, the frost on the chair arms creeps thicker, white and crisp.

She looks at you a long moment, and lets whatever she is thinking lie unsaid.

"A Talent that draws rot is worth more to this valley than any sword. We have no healer like it."

She turns her head. "Edric. The glass."

Sir Edric crosses to a chest by the wall and brings back a disc of dark glass in a silver ring.

Lady Varrow holds it up, between her eye and you.

"This came from the Spires. It shows a Talent for what it is. Will you let me look at you?"
