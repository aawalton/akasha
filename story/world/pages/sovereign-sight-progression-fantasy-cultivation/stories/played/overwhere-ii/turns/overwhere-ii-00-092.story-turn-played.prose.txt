"I'll train with them in the mornings, refine after, until my refining is done."

"Good," Lady Varrow says. "Hawise Tull runs the lower court. She'll expect you."

A steward leads you up the old north stair to the room at its top.

Bare boards, a cot, a chest, a shuttered window. The stair below is yours alone.

You open the shutters. Across the valley the Whitecombs stand white to the south.

At noon you eat in the hall, at the second table: bread, mutton broth, salt cheese.

Three Talented share the table, and all three watch you eat.

A huge young man grins across the bread. "Osric Penn. You're the one who killed Crake?"

"Eat, Osric," says a lean, scarred woman. Then, to you: "Hawise Tull. First light. Lower court."

A small, sharp-eyed girl studies you over her cup. Osric names her Corra Venn.

The afternoon and evening pass in your room, cycling and resting.

In your last hour of sleep the black stair calls again, louder.

First light, day twenty-four. Frost lies on the lower court's flags, and wooden weapons stand in a rack.

"Wooden weapons first," Hawise says. "Then Talents, held light. The Lady's rule. Harm only what your Talent can heal. Draw only on your own Water."

Undertow is a draw. Against these three, it is barred.

Corra sits on the well-curb, snapping tiny blue sparks between her fingertips.

Osric takes a wooden maul from the rack and swings it one-handed, light as a switch.

"Crake's killer. Show me."

Hawise folds her arms at the court's edge. "I'll watch first. Go on."

Osric sets his feet across the frosted court, maul raised. "Pick a weapon, free blade."
