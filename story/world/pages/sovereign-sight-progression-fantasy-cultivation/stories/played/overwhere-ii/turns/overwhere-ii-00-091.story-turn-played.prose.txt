"Acceptable. Is there a place here I could stay while I focus on refining?"

Lady Varrow's face stays still. She gives one short nod.

"Sir Edric. Witness it."

She holds out a lean hand. Frost glitters along her knuckles.

You clasp it. The cold bites your fingers, then fades.

"Witnessed," Sir Edric says. "Nala, free blade of Varrow Keep."

"It goes in the rolls tonight," Lady Varrow says. "A room, yes. The top of the old north stair. Bare, but quiet. You'll have that stair to yourself. You eat in the hall with my Talented, at the second table. The well is in the lower court. Deep and cold, from the crag's own spring."

"Counsel, since you asked for it. Every Tribulation differs. Mine came as cold that tried to stop my heart. Make yours strong before you go down."

"My Talented train in the lower court at first light. Will you start with them tomorrow, or refine first?"
