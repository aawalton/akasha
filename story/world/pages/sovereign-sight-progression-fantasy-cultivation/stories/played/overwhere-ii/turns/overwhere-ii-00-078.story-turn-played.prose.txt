You reach up and draw the needle dart out of your neck.

It slides free: a finger-long iron needle, its grooved tip dark with dried venom.

A bead of blood, a small sting, and the prick begins to close.

The column moves out. The Pells carry the hurdle. Harl walks roped between Col and Rob, and Pip keeps at Dray's side.

The Loddons walk behind in a roped line, Bet beside Kit, her hand on his arm.

You fall in with them, and as you walk, you begin to cycle your Water.

The first loops grate through your Locks like a rusted hinge, stiff and slow.

You keep on, loop after loop, in time with your stride, as easy as breathing.

Each loop runs a little smoother than the last. The Ford road climbs between greening hedges.

By mid-morning the grating is gone. Your Locks run free.

Undertow answers you full again, and the world's water comes back clear around you: the dew in the hedges, the ditch beside the road, the sweat on the Pells' backs.

Your body knows its own lack now too. Your mouth is parched, your belly a hollow knot.

Dray drops back beside you with the watch's pack open: bread and cheese, and a skin of small beer.

"You've not eaten since yesterday. Here. There's plenty."
