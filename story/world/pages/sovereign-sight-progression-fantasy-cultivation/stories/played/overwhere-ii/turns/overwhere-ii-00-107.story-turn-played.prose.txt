You take out one of the whole greymaw chambers: a fist-sized knot of polished grey bone.

You set it on the stone in the bead's path and catch the bead with a thread of your tide.

You draw the bead to the chamber and press it against the bone.

At your push it sinks in through the bone, as water sinks into dry sand.

You wait, the chamber cupped in your palm. The bone stays dry.

It goes ice-cold in your hand, colder than snow.

A faint tug pulls at it, toward the pool. It's weak, and your fingers hold it easily.

Through the bone you can feel the bead inside: a sliver, lost in all that room.

The chamber could hold far more.

Hawise climbs down off her boulder and looks at the grey knot in your palm.

"Well. That holds it." She glances at the steaming pool. "What now, free blade?"
