You take an ash practice spear from the rack, its head capped in blunt leather.

You set your feet across from Osric, the spear's point level with his chest.

Osric comes in roaring, the maul high, then crashing down overhead.

You give ground and keep the point in his face. He swats it aside and comes on.

A sweeping blow, fast for its weight. You slip back, a hand's breadth ahead of it.

After each sweep his left flank hangs open for a heartbeat.

You wait for the next sweep, then step in under it.

Your blunt point drives hard into his ribs: a clean touch.

The maul's tail end still catches your side as it comes round, a dull, heavy thud.

Your refined ribs take it. The ache spreads, then settles to a bruise.

Osric staggers back, rubbing his ribs, and laughs like a bell.

"Ha! She keeps you on the end of that stick like a boar."

"Clean touch," Hawise says. "He caught you after it. Against a blade, that's your liver."

Corra stops snapping sparks and watches you with narrowed eyes.

Hawise takes two wooden short blades from the rack and steps into the court.

"My turn. Talents now, held light."
