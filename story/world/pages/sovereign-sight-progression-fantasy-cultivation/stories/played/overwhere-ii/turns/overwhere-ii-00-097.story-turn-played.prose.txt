"Done for today. I need to focus on refining my organs."

Hawise nods once. "First light tomorrow."

Within the hour the numbness from Corra's spark is gone from your hand.

You eat in the hall, then rest an hour in your room. The ache behind your eyes eases.

You sit on the cot, turn inward and reach the tide toward your lungs.

They fight it. Living, breathing, they push the tide back with every breath.

All afternoon each breath comes short and cold, like sea air.

By dusk your lungs are refined whole. They draw deeper and slower.

You try it, and find you can hold your breath for minutes.

A night's sleep, and your mind is clear again.

Day twenty-five. Mornings in the court: spear drills, and bouts with Osric, Hawise and Corra.

In the afternoon you refine your gut. You are sick and cramping for hours, and then it is whole.

Day twenty-six. The court again, then your liver.

The liver fights hardest yet. It runs you hot and feverish, but you hold the tide.

By nightfall it is whole. The fever lingers, burning low in you as you sleep.

Near midnight the Whitecombs thunder. A shudder runs up through the Keep's stones.

It wakes you. Your well leans south toward the mountains, heavy and cold.

Day twenty-seven, morning. The fever has broken, leaving you wrung out but clear.

In the court, a rider in mud to the thighs clatters through the gate on a lathered pony.

Within the hour a steward finds you. The Lady wants you in the hall.

Lady Varrow waits with Hawise beside her. "A rider from Callow Beck. Ebba Callow's word. Something spilled down off the mountain in the night. Her sheep are changed."

"Go to Callow Beck and hold it. Hawise goes with you. My first summons, free blade. Will you answer it?"
