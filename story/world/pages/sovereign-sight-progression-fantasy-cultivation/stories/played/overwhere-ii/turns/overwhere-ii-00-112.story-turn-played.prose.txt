"Then we have a plan for the morning."

Anselm sets out bread, cheese and a jug of small beer on the shrine's table, and makes up a pallet.

You sleep. The black stair comes nearer than it ever has. You wake before dawn.

At first light you find Dray at his house, and Hob at his forge on the ford road. Hawise comes too.

You tell them of the tidepool, the Warped ewes, the plug, and Maud's cold iron ring.

"Then we ring it." Dray turns to Hob. "Stakes. As many as she needs. Start now."

Hob scratches his beard. "I've cold iron for twenty. The rest means melting old iron down."

"I'll ask the Ford for old nails and charms. The valley purse pays."

"Ten by dusk, if I sweat." Hob looks to you. "Stakes in a pool bed want driving with a sledge. Someone stood right over them."

Dray sends Rob Reed riding for the Keep with word of the tidepool, and of Maud.

"The Pells will carry the stakes up as they come off the anvil."

You and Hawise climb back up to Callow Beck. The bank at the gully mouth still holds.

Behind it, the black lies knee-deep in a pool forty paces up the gully.

Above that, the gully bed is crusted grey with salt, black water lying in its hollows.

You kneel at the bank and begin to part the black, a cupful at a time, as on the cwm.

After an hour the pool behind the bank looks no different.

You sound its size with your tide. Forty barrels at least, and more in the hollows above.

Cupful by cupful, this would take you weeks.

Near noon Maud comes up the track on foot, stiff and puffing, Anselm at her elbow.
