You set the ash spear low and give ground, as you did against Dray, waiting.

Hawise circles, blades loose, her eyes on your feet.

You reach out with your sense: frost on the flags, rain in the hollows, the trough, the well.

Hawise feints left, then right. Your point follows each, steady.

Then Hawise blurs, twice as fast as a woman can move.

Your spear swings to meet her, a heartbeat late.

One wooden blade knocks the shaft wide. The other is already under your guard.

It drives into your ribs, on the bruise Osric left. A clean touch.

Hawise steps back, and for a dozen heartbeats she moves like anyone else, breathing hard.

"You were waiting for a moment. I don't give one. Three, four breaths fast. Then I'm slow a while. You saw that?"

Osric whoops from the rack. Corra hides a smile behind her hand.

Hawise taps her blades together and drops back into her stance.

"Again."
