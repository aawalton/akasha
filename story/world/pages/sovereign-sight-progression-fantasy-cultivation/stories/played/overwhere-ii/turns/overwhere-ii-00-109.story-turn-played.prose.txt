"Okay. I cleansed a few paces, but you can't tell. It's filling just as fast. Let's go see if this Keeper has any ideas."

Hawise shoulders the sack of cracked chambers. "Ford, then."

You leave the cwm as the sun goes down behind the Whitecombs.

Past the bank at the gully mouth, past Ebba's empty longhouse, down onto the valley floor.

The last hour you walk by dark, the cold chamber heavy in your bag, tugging at you with every step.

Lamplight shows at the Threll shrine by the Ford.

Anselm opens the door, and his face lights up. "Nala! Come in, come in."

A small, stout woman sits on Anselm's cot, a mule's blanket round her shoulders. Silver hair cropped short, sharp blue eyes. Orange robes, the sleeve bells tied silent.

She looks you up and down, then laughs, warm and quick.

"So you're the one Anselm's letters can't stop talking about. Maud Ashby. I've ridden a long way to take your measure, girl. My Talent's Sounding. A hand on you, and I feel the shape of your well."

She holds out a square, callused hand, palm up. "May I?"
