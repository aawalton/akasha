"Thank you, Lady Varrow." You take the silver bar from the grey cloth.

It is cold from the room, and heavy. The servant steps back.

Lady Varrow gives one short nod, as if a matter is closed.

"Now. The crag. Sir Edric has told me what the stone told him. Tell me what you felt."

You tell it again, as you told Sir Edric on the mountain.

The slow, vast pull far below, like a tide under the mountain, drawing on your well. Stronger now than on day four, and coming in slow swells. A tug down through your heels.

Lady Varrow listens without a word, her pale eyes on your face.

Now and then her glance goes to Sir Edric. Each time, he gives the smallest nod.

When you finish, the hall is quiet but for the fire. The frost on the chair arms has thickened.

"Reeve Dray writes that you came to the Ford with no papers. Where do you come from, Nala?"
