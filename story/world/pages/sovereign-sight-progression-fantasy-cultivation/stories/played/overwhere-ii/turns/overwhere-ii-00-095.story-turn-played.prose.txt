This time you go at her, the ash spear flicking high, low, high.

Hawise gives ground, her blades rattling on the shaft.

Your Water surges out of your palm in a small, sharp shove against her chest.

It lands as a cold slap of tide. Her eyes go wide and she rocks back a step.

Hawise drops her weight onto the balls of her feet, and blurs.

You shove off the rainwater in the hollow beside you, and slide half a step aside.

The wooden blade cuts the air where your ribs were.

Hawise braces against the next shove now, leaning into it like wind.

You pull on the puddle water at her heel. It slicks under her quick foot.

At twice her speed, the slip throws her hard. She catches herself on one hand.

Her blur is spent. For a dozen heartbeats she is slow.

Your blunt point taps her square in the breastbone. A clean touch.

The court goes quiet. Then Osric bellows a laugh.

Behind your eyes there is a thin, tight ache, as after fine drawing.

Hawise stands, wipes her wet palm on her thigh and looks at the puddle, then at you.

"The ground. Never fought the ground before." A short nod. "Good."

Corra hops down from the well-curb, sparks crackling blue between her fingers.

"My turn, free blade."
