"I will not swear. But I will help, if I can. I've refined my skin, muscles and bones since meeting Garth, and I still have room to grow. But I'm lacking in guidance. I have a feeling I'll need all the strength I can get to face whatever lies under that mountain. If you can help me grow my strength, I will stay to face it."

Lady Varrow's face stays exactly as it was. Only the frost on the chair arms glints.

Then her pale eyes narrow, just slightly.

"Skin, muscle and bone. Since Garth Marsh. That is three weeks. Most Talented take years over that."

"Then no oath. A lesser bond. You stay a free blade. The Keep calls on you at need. A silver bar for every summons you answer. And in return, the guidance you ask for. Mine, and this house's."

"I have Descended twice. Refine your organs before you go down. Few ever have the time, or the Water. And spar with my Talented here. You've fought beasts and a thief. Learn to fight a Talent."

She holds your eyes, level and cold.

"Will you take that bond?"
