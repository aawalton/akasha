"Share it all," you say. "I expect to take the Chartermark sooner or later anyways. No reason to slow that down."

Relief spreads across Anselm's tired face. He smiles warmly.

"Thank you," he says. "I'll write it tonight, all of it, and it goes with the carrier on market day."

Then a small frown creases his brow.

"You should know, though: the Charterstone in Carrowmouth gives marks only at Threllsnacht. That's nearly a year off."

He turns his cup slowly between his palms.

"If you keep near, I'd expect the Keepers to teach you through the year until then. If you go wandering far, they'll only have to find you first."
