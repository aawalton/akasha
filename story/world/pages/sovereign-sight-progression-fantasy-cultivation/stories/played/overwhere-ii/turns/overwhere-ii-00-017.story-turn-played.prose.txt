"Quiet!"

The word cracks through the room like a slammed door.

Horne blinks, startled into silence, his fist still knotted in your collar.

You don't look at him. Pinned to the wall, you shut your eyes and reach for the bed.

The girl's stolen warmth is still there in your well. It sits apart from your own cold flow, like a pocket.

You turn the tide around. Not the pull this time. The push, as gentle as you can make it.

The warm pocket slides out of you, across the room, and back into Tansy, easy as water running downhill.

You keep the push going a breath longer and let a thin stream of your own cold flow follow it. You feel it settle into her like a cool hand on a fevered brow.

In the bed, Tansy draws a long, shuddering breath.

You open your eyes. The blue is fading from her lips. Colour creeps back into her face. She is still grey and sick, but no worse than when you came in.

Under the tide, you feel your own flow wrap around the black tangle in her arm. The rot goes still and sluggish, held, the way cold honey stops running.

Horne's grip loosens. He stares from his daughter to you, breathing hard.

Tansy's eyelids flutter open. "Mam?" she whispers. "I'm cold. I was so cold."

Her mother sobs and gathers her in, rocking her.

"Sedge Horne," Goody says, very quiet and very hard. "Let go of her."

Horne lets go. He steps back and looks at his own hand as if it belonged to someone else.

You straighten your collar. The rot in Tansy's arm is held, but nearly all of it is still there.

Horne clears his throat. His voice comes out hoarse. "Can you get it out of her? The rest of it?"
