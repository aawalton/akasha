You bow, and wait to be spoken to.

The woman in the chair watches you a long moment without a word.

She is lean, and tall even seated, in a fine grey gown worn thin at the cuffs.

Her dark hair is pinned tight, with one streak of white in it. Her pale eyes go to your hands, then your face.

The air around the chair is cold. A thin skin of frost lies on its carved arms.

"Nala. I am Imre Varrow."

Her name, and straight on. Her voice is low and even.

"Oswy Crake robbed my road for a year, and took three Talented off it. That road was mine to keep. You kept it for me. I don't forget that."

She lifts a hand. A servant steps forward with a silver bar on a square of grey cloth.

"From the House. Take it."
