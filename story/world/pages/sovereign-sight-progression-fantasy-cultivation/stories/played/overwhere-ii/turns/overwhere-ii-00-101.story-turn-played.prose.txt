"Get down with your boys. Hawise and I will handle this. But thank you for the suggestion."

Ebba gives one hard nod, shoulders her bundle and herds the boys down the track.

Wat cradles his bound arm. They are out of sight below the pasture well before dusk.

"Hawise, can you build a bank while I hold back the black?"

"Peat's stacked by the wall. Gully floor's all loose stone. Hold it."

You set your feet at the gully mouth and lean your tide against the slick.

It is steady work, like towing a weight that never stops pulling.

Hawise blurs up and down the pasture in bursts, arms full of peat, then stones.

The bank rises across the gully mouth: knee-high, then waist-high, packed hard.

By dusk it stands from wall to wall of the gully. You let go, slowly.

The black water leans against the bank. The bank holds.

Somewhere higher up, you feel a thin, cold trickle feeding the gully from above.

It comes from the cwm where the Callow pool lies.

Hawise sits on the bank and wipes peat from her hands. "That'll hold. For now. Gate's barred at dusk. We won't make the Keep tonight. Sleep in the longhouse, and back at first light? Or up to that pool first?"
