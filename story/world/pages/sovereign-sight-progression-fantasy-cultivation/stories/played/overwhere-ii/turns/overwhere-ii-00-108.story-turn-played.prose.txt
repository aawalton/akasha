"Go down to the village. There should be more chambers from the wolves I killed there. Bring back any you can find. I'll focus on clearing the water, and we'll see if it helps."

Hawise nods once and is gone over the rim, blurring down the goat track.

You kneel at the pool's edge and reach into the shallows with your tide.

Cupful by cupful you part the black from the water. At first each takes minutes.

After an hour you have the rhythm: a cupful in about a minute. Any faster, and the black slips back.

The beads run together into one, and a single thread of your tide holds it all.

You reach out for the pool's middle, where the swells start. It lies beyond your reach.

The black is thickest out there. It thins toward the shore where you kneel.

By mid-afternoon a patch of shore water a few paces wide lies clear before you.

Then a swell rolls in from the middle. Fresh black clouds the clear patch.

Within the hour it's as dark as the rest.

The longer you work, the nearer the stair dream feels, like a sound just past hearing.

After three hours your focus frays, as it did after the flurry against Hawise.

You press the gathered black into the chamber. It fills a fair part of it.

Late in the day Hawise comes back over the rim, breathing hard, a sack on her shoulder.

"Dray had five, in a crate. Kept them for you."

She tips them out on the rock. Five grey knots of bone, each cracked clean through.

The sun sits low on the Whitecombs. The pool swells, and eases, and swells.

"And a Senior Keeper rode into the Ford today. Maud Ashby. Asking after you."
