Corra takes a wooden sword from the rack and comes in low and close.

You meet her with the spear's point high, low, high, driving her back toward the well.

Corra flicks a dipper of trough water across the flags between you. The stones go dark and wet.

You shove off the wet flags and slide half a step aside.

A blue spark snaps along the wet stone after you and stings your heel.

Your tide shoves at Corra's chest, a cold slap.

Her fingers are sparking as it lands.

The spark runs back up the tide like fire up a fuse, into your palm.

Your right hand goes numb to the wrist. The spear's shaft drops loose in your grip.

Behind your eyes the tight ache throbs. Your next push slips and comes out a splash.

Corra darts in under the sagging point and raps her wooden blade against your ribs.

A clean touch, on the same bruise.

Corra steps back, grinning, sparks dancing on her knuckles.

"Water carries, free blade."

Osric roars with laughter. Hawise's mouth twitches.

Feeling creeps back into your hand, pins and needles to the fingertips.

"Your head's spent," Hawise says, "and she knew your trick before you used it. Again, or done for today?"
