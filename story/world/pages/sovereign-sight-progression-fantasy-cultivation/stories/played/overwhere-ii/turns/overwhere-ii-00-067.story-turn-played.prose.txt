You come up off the mud and charge the barn door, spear levelled, your Water gathered and ready to Push.

In the loft the Loddons are still bent over their strings, winding. The air above you stays empty.

Twenty paces of yard. On your refined legs you cross them in a heartbeat.

In the dim of the barn stands a lean man, smiling, a ring of keys at his belt. Crake.

You drive the cold-iron spear straight at his heart.

He slips aside, quick, but not quick enough. The point takes him full in the chest.

It doesn't sink. It grinds on rings under his jerkin, and a few burst loose and skitter across the straw.

The blow lifts him off his feet and slams him back against a post. His breath goes out of him in a grunt.

His heart is armoured. You felt it through the shaft: a coat of rings over his chest and belly.

Crake doesn't fall. He comes off the post along the shaft, bare hands reaching for yours.

You wrench the spear back and around, and keep his fingers off your own.

But as he gives ground his left hand flicks from his cuff. Something stings your neck.

A dart, needle-fine. It broke your refined skin, where the bolts would not have.

Cold spreads from the prick, slow and heavy, like river silt seeping toward your Locks.

This is the dart Dray warned of: the venom that chokes a Talent's Locks.

It creeps through you the way the rot crept through Wren.

Crake has backed out of the spear's reach. He rubs his chest, still smiling.

"There. Now we wait a minute, you and I."

Overhead, two crossbow strings click home.
