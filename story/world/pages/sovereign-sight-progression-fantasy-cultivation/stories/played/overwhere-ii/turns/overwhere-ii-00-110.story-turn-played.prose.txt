"Yes." You hold out your hand.

Maud takes it in both of hers. Her palms are warm and rough.

She shuts her eyes. Something light brushes at your skin, then sinks deeper.

"Skin, whole. Muscle, whole. Bone..." Her brows climb. "Every bone, whole. Lungs. Gut. Liver." A breath. "Kidneys and the small ones still to come. And the heart. A month? Keepers take years over what you've done."

Her touch moves inward, along your threads. "Every tributary open. Carved, as if never shut."

Then it slides down, into your well.

Maud's hands go still. Her breath stops.

She draws her hands back as if from cold water, and sits quiet a long moment.

Anselm looks from her to you and back. Hawise, by the door, has gone very still.

"There's no floor to it. It just goes on down. Like looking into the Sea. And it's straining. Pulling downward, as a well does when Descent is near. You don't go down before that heart is refined, girl. Hear me?"

She studies your face with sharp blue eyes, the laughter gone out of them.

"Now. Where did you come from? And where did you first wake?"
