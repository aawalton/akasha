You drift in the Ocean of Souls, carried along by a warm current like a cloud on the wind. You died young, a few weeks short of college, in a car with your parents. They have already risen to be born again, after time enough here to say goodbye.

Far below, something vast swims past: a Guardian, patrolling for anything that is not a soul. Its wake rocks your current for a moment, and you are not afraid.

Then a shadow slides over you that leaves no wake at all. Darkness closes in from every side, blacker than any night, and claws rake into your soul.

You scream, thrash, strike at it, beg. Nothing you do touches it. A voice snickers inside your head, and teeth gnaw at you slowly, like a cat playing with its food. A demon. No Guardian comes.

The darkness lurches upward. You are rising, and it rises with you, wrapped so tightly around you that there is no way out.

Light breaks through. The demon shrieks. Where the light leaks past it, it scours you too, burning away memories, faces, pieces of who you were. But the demon's body is wrapped around yours, and it takes the worst of it.

Then the light is gone. Thump-thump. Thump-thump. A heartbeat, close and steady, and warmth, and a dark with no edges.

You spread yourself out and feel a shape around you: small, curled, soft, with tiny limbs. A body. You hold half of it. The other half is the demon.

You brace for its claws. They do not come. It whimpers instead, a wordless mewling, shrunken and burned, barely holding itself together beside you.

It held you in its teeth the whole way up from the Ocean, toying with you. Now it can barely hold its own shape. You turn on it and bite down into its darkness, the way it bit into you.

It flinches. It tries to curl away from you, but it has nothing left to curl with, and its edges sag and run like smoke.

You tear at it the way it tore at you, slowly, and then not slowly at all. It comes away in ragged shreds.

One claw scrapes across you, weak as a kitten's. You bite through it.

The voice that snickered in your head is gone. It shrieks instead, thin and high, and each shriek is fainter than the last.

You swallow it a shred at a time. It goes down cold and bitter, and you keep going until there is nothing of it left to swallow.

Then the shrieking stops. You reach for the place where the demon was, and it is empty.

You spread into the half of the body it held: a curled arm, a leg, one side of a tiny chest. Both hands are yours now, both feet, the whole small body.

Something cold settles in you as you go. Dark threads of the demon run all through your soul, woven in among what is left of you, and they stay.

Thump-thump. Thump-thump. The heartbeat around you goes on at the same slow, steady pace as before.

Then a voice sounds inside you, flat and even, and it is not the demon's and not your own.

"Status withheld. Mind too young to choose." "If you understand, kick twice." Then it falls silent.
