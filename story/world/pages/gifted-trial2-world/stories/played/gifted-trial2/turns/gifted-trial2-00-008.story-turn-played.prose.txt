You go still again under the hand and listen, this time for one word only. Kerith.

Your mother starts to murmur. Her voice is so close it seems to come from inside the walls, soft and slow, in the same language as before, and she seems to be talking to you. Between the murmurs she hums, a few notes of some low tune at a time, and then she talks again. The small hand on the wall beside you moves as she talks, round and round in slow circles.
