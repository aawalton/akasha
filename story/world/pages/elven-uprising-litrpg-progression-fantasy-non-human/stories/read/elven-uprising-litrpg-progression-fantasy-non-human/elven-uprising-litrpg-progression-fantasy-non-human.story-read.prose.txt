Fire devours wood. That is the law of the world. But what happens when the wood fights back? 

One moment, Pinaka was a quiet man eating dinner. The next, he was reborn as an Elf in a fallen world.

Once rulers of Gangnea, the Elves have lost their cities, their power, and their connection to the World Tree. But while his new kin mourn the past, Pinaka sees something else—a chance.

First goal: escape.

All he needs is one opening.

What to Expect:

A Non-Restrictive Power System – Creativity > stats. Break the game.

A Cunning, Patient MC – Prep matters. No rage-fueled fights.

No Harem, No Romance – For real.

A Game-Like World with True Build Freedom – Custom growth, no presets.

A Dark, Strategic Uprising – Slow-burn. High stakes. No mercy.
