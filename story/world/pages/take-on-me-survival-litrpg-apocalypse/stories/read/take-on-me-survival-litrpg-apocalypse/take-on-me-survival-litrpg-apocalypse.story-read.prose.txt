The Earth is toast. 

Humanity’s last survivors live underground, wasting away on borrowed time. Their only hope is a last-ditch refuge: a global simulation designed to preserve the species. 

Until someone sabotages it. 

A catastrophic patch transforms paradise into a brutal fantasy apocalypse. Dungeons rip open across the land. Rage-fueled monsters hunt, kill, and snatch humans without mercy.

It’s savage. It’s terrifying. And if you die in the simulation, you die in real life. 

In the rural heartland of what was once the USA, two families are forced to adapt fast as magic awakens in ordinary people. Alongside their neighbors, they carve out the Kingdom of Raintree and fight like hell to defend it. They’ll need to level up, secure resources, clear dungeons, and withstand escalating waves of monsters. 

All while trying to hold onto the people they love.

 

Expect: Portal Fantasy | LitRPG | Dungeons | Kingdom Building | Multi-POV | Horror Tone. 

Disclaimer: This is a story about families in a fantasy world—not a story for families. There are curse words and violence. The story leans on the side of horror and characters will die. Heads up if that’s not your vibe.
