“ I have matched wits with generals and broken cultivators with my bare hands. I have fought legends and war gods, often at the same time. I have seen men who could shape the weather and I have shattered armies alone.”

Lu Bu; The Flying General, The Beast of Chaos, The Ogre, The Peerless One.

For thirty seven years he rampaged across the world annihilating cities, armies, and all that stood in his path to power. Be they friend, foe, or even family it mattered not to Lu Bu. All would be crushed in service of his desires.

Now reborn into a new world unlike anything he has ever seen, he’s going to do it all again.
