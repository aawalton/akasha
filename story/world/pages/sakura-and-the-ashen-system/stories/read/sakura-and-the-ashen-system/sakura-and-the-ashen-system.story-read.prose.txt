Sakura Arai was supposed to die in a subway fire.

Instead, she wakes up in a world where the sky is gray, the forests whisper in broken code, and every living thing is ranked, measured, and optimized by a merciless System.

Class: None.
Level: 1.
Talent: Unknown.
Survival Chance: 3.8%

Dropped alone into the ruins outside a dying frontier city, Sakura quickly learns three things.

First: this world rewards violence.
Second: the weak are not protected here.
Third: the strange black petals appearing under her skin are not normal.

When Sakura accidentally unlocks a forbidden growth path tied to Ash, Bloom, and Consumption, she gains a power no one should have — one that turns dead monsters into fuel for her own evolution.

But power in this world always comes with a cost.

The stronger Sakura becomes, the more the Ash inside her spreads. Cities fear it. Priests hunt it. Monsters are drawn to it. And somewhere deep in the wasteland, something ancient has already noticed her arrival.

Now Sakura must grow fast, hide what she is becoming, and decide whether she wants to survive this world…

…or devour her way to the top of it.

What to expect:
LitRPG progression, female lead, survival arc, evolving class, dangerous skill growth, monster hunting, mystery, and gradual rise from weak newcomer to terrifying force.

For now, new chapters are released once a week, sometimes twice.
