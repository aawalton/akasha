When the apocalypse came, Don became a monster.

Don Damon didn’t ask for the end of the world. He was just a blue-collar guy walking his dog when the System came.

An accident happened and something went wrong… Don was no longer human— he was a predator.

In a new reality where old rules have been discarded, Don must learn how to thrive. And he quickly realizes it is as natural to him as breathing.

But as Don grows, so do his challenges. Humans fear him, other monsters want to challenge him, and there are great powers at play who are following his success with interest…

With every kill, Don’s power grows. He learns that the System works on a simple axiom: eat, or be eaten.

And if those are the choices.... Don will feast.
