Aura lasts about ninety seconds of holding still before the fast comes back into her, all at once.

"Okay. Downstairs. Rematch, best of five, and this time I'm not going easy on you out of respect for the moment we just had."

"You went easy on me?"

"I will revise history, it's a house rule—" But she doesn't get up. And Awen doesn't let go of the fist she's holding, and for a minute the rematch just sits there being a thing they'll definitely do later.

Below them the cabinets run their attract loops in the dark, every one set to free play, keeping their own scores for nobody, patient as the woman who owns them has never once been. The blue light comes up through the floor. Down there a start-line countdown goes off for no one — three, two, one — and neither of them moves to answer it.
