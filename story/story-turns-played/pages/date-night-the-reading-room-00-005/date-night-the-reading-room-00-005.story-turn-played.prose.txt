Inside is warmer. The good blanket from the couch is down over the floor of it, and the lamplight comes through the paperback walls in thin gold seams. There's exactly enough room for three if two of them overlap.

You end up on your back. Astra fits along one side of you without being asked; Nova stakes the other, propped on an elbow up over your chest, close enough now that the black lipstick is just her mouth.

"From in here," Astra says, quiet, "it's better." She doesn't say better than what.

"Okay." You find you've got a hand at the small of Nova's back, and you keep it there. "Show me what it's for."

She reaches back over your shoulder, into the deep seam of the fort where the blanket meets the wall, and her hand closes on something that's been waiting there since before dinner. She doesn't pull it out. She holds it where it is and looks at you, then down at whatever's in her fist, then at you again. She opens her mouth. She closes it again.
