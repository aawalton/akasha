Abby held his gaze and let the smile answer before any words came — and when they did, she gave them low and unhedged, matching the register he'd handed her. "I'm not going anywhere." A beat, the quiet folding closer around them. "I see what you're doing — staying right here with me." Her smile deepened, steady on his. "I'm doing it too." She didn't turn it back to fill the space, didn't soften it into anything smaller; she only let the being-met show as openly as the gladness already had, and eased further into the worn give of the chair. Her hand drifted, unhurried, toward the warmth in the space between them. Then she let the quiet close back over them both — present, in no hurry to be anywhere else, staying exactly where he'd asked.
