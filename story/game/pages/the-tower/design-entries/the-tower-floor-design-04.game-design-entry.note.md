## Floor

Floor 4's resources are LIGHT (the one oil flask + the hooded lantern) and FOOTING/TIMING (stable flights vs moving slabs on a readable beat), and its unique hazard is FALLING (a fall is a kill, not an injury — pre-decided, do not narrate a survivable landing). This floor is built squarely on Alan's trait profile from sheets/alan.json: it PUNISHES his weak PERCEPTION (the dark ambush defeats passive spotting — Sensitivity-not-Observance), and it REWARDS his Hiker's Legs (leg-driven leaps/climbs/swings between platforms earn intent; overhead/grip plays are capped) and his peak INTELLECT (reading the slab rhythm and the Colossus's mechanism, NOT noticing them). His edge is analysis, never noticing — so both weaknesses are reasoning-discoverable, and a written note (the snagged corpse) states both plainly, the same fair compensation for weak PER used in floor 3's wall-note.
