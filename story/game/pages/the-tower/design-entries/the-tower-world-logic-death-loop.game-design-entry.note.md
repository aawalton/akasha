## Rule

THE LOOP: dying returns Alan to the floor-1 entrance, staged exactly as the very first scene was, and that symmetry is the reveal. RETAINED across a death: all memory; character level, attributes, skills, titles, affinities and class progress; the HP, Focus and Stamina maxes. LOST: equipment, consumables and anything carried, which belonged to that run of the world. Companions reset and do not remember him; he remembers them. The tower regenerates with enemies respawned and the layout shifted enough that memorisation alone is not the answer, and the upper floors scale, so retained power plus foreknowledge is a real head start rather than a stroll.

## Implications

Alan does not know the loop exists until he dies. Zero foreshadowing: no winks, no asking whether he is sure, no save-point language, and the first death plays completely straight, because for all he knows in the moment he lost. Every design settled from now must stay consistent with the loop so that the reveal contradicts nothing already said. The game master does not engineer a death; play decides when it comes, and the reveal waits as long as it waits. Once the loop is understood it becomes a tool: die to reset, climb again faster and stronger, and push higher than before. The companion reset is revisitable once companions exist and the loop is out.
