## Rule

Crafting is the axis that opens beside affinities higher in the tower, and affinities are its essence input. What is settled here is the seam and not the system. An affinity at manipulation or above can imbue a weapon or an item with its element, so the thing carries the element rather than the wielder: an ember imbue on the iron bar makes its strikes carry heat and match a heat weakness innately. An imbue costs an absorption act and focus. At the first tier an emerged essence skill already approximates this for a moment, and imbuing is what makes it persist on the item. A recipe consumes an affinity tier, essence as either a banked cost or a held seed of the element, and a material core, and answers an imbued or a crafted item. The cores are the keystone, the gauntlet, the chain-length, the drum core and their like.

## Implications

This is a seam rather than a build: no code runs crafting and none should until the tower reaches the floors where it opens. What the seam buys now is that nothing already settled forecloses it. Seeds are typed, cores are named and held apart from seeds, and a dual-use drop already exists in play. When crafting is built it reads the affinity tier and the element table and needs nothing new from either.
