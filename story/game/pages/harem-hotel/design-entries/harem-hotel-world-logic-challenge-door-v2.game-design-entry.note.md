## Rule

THE CHALLENGE-GATE LAW (how a floor gates its rise — designed ahead so every floor is consistent; constraints-conditioned by the rising-floor setting policy; framing corrected to published canon t13/t17). Each floor is ONE rising hallway with two ends: a RESIDENTIAL end (Alan's master suite + a grown room per joined companion) and a CHALLENGE end that darkens to a SHUT DOOR (canon t1/t3). THE DOOR IS A THRESHOLD, NOT THE GATE: the physical challenge-end door may open FREELY — at floor 1 it 'gave inward without a sound' the moment Alan and Aria leaned on it (canon t13). Do NOT design a floor whose DOOR must be read/solved to open (a door-lock puzzle) — that contradicts floor-1 canon. THE EPISTEMIC GATE IS THE CHALLENGE ITSELF — the warden(s) the Hotel grows at the challenge end past the door; 'a door that makes you earn the far side of it is a lock' (canon t17, the Doorward IS the real lock). THE CHALLENGE'S PRICE IS EPISTEMIC, NEVER BRUTE: the warden yields only to a TRUE READING (its keystone/weak-point seen and struck precisely — INT-read, mental or FIN-placed), never to force; brute force is always the losing line (the warden mirrors force back — floor 1's x0.3). Clearing the challenge (unmaking/killing the warden[s]) is what makes the WHOLE FLOOR RISE — the floor itself is the elevator — and Alan levels +1 on the clear (flat leveling, no XP). COMPANION CADENCE: the risen floor delivers a new woman every FIFTH height (floors 1, 6, 11, 16, ...); the four floors between are combat + household, no new woman. TWO PROGRESSIONS STAY SEPARATE: Alan climbs on combat merit alone (no floor impassable for lack of a bond); talents and THE LINK are accelerators, never keys. RETREAT-PERSISTENCE (tower-wide, per-enemy): a true-form/keystone kill is PERMANENT; a retreat from a merely-wounded enemy resets it to full on return — information persists across a retreat (the reads, the layout, the tells), HP does not.

## Implications

Every floor-design in this game MUST honor this law before it lands: (a) the CHALLENGE (warden) is READABLE — it carries a weak-point/keystone a true read rewards SEEING, and force is the losing line; a floor whose challenge can be brute-forced violates the spine and is a design defect. (b) The DOOR is a threshold that opens freely (canon t13) — the GATE is ALWAYS the challenge behind/at the door, NEVER a door-lock puzzle; 'the door opens when the challenge is cleared' is shorthand for 'the clear rises the floor', not 'the door itself must be solved'. (c) The companion cadence fixes WHICH floors carry a companion-design (1, 6, 11, ...) vs combat+household only. (d) Per-floor RESET FLAVOR is set in each floor's own design within the tower-wide rule: illusion/construct floors re-weave to pristine FREE of the rest-clock (eerie, instant — e.g. floor 1's plaster Doorward); physical/organic floors reheal only on a genuine full rest, sharing the player's OWN rest-clock. (e) The challenge 'wanting something of Alan' (canon t3) renders as the price of a true READ of the warden, mute-System-safe (the System never explains the read; the warden simply does not come apart to force).

## Supersession

Supersedes world-logic-challenge-door: the prior law framed the DOOR itself as opening 'to a true reading, never to force', which t13 contradicts. The designed yields to the disclosed; core substance (epistemic price, brute loses, floor rises on a true-read clear of the warden) is preserved, only the door-vs-warden framing is corrected.
