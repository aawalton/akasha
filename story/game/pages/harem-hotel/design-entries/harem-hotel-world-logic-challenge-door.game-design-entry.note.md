## Rule

THE CHALLENGE-DOOR LAW (how a floor gates its rise — designed ahead so every floor is consistent; constraints-conditioned by the rising-floor setting policy). Each floor is ONE rising hallway with two ends: a RESIDENTIAL end (Alan's master suite + a grown room per joined companion — the hallway grows a room when a woman joins) and a CHALLENGE end that darkens to a SINGLE SHUT DOOR (canon t1/t3: 'the far shut door ... looks as if it wants something of Alan before it opens; still unentered'). THE DOOR'S PRICE IS EPISTEMIC, NEVER BRUTE: the door opens to a TRUE READING of the challenge behind it, never to force — battering is always the losing line (reading-beats-brute-force is the tower's spine, and 'wanting something of Alan' is ALWAYS a read/price the fiction can show, never a random lock or an arbitrary key-hunt). Clearing the challenge (unmaking/killing the warden[s] behind the door) makes the WHOLE FLOOR RISE — the floor itself is the elevator — and Alan levels +1 on the floor-clear (flat leveling, no XP). COMPANION CADENCE: the risen floor delivers a new woman every FIFTH height (floors 1, 6, 11, 16, ...); the four floors between are combat + household, no new woman. TWO PROGRESSIONS STAY SEPARATE: Alan climbs on combat merit alone (no floor impassable for lack of a bond); talents and THE LINK are accelerators, never keys. RETREAT-PERSISTENCE (tower-wide, per-enemy): a true-form/keystone kill is PERMANENT; a retreat from a merely-wounded enemy resets it to full on return — information persists across a retreat (the reads, the layout, the tells), HP does not.

## Implications

Every floor-design in this game MUST honor this law before it lands: (a) the challenge is READABLE — it carries a weak-point/keystone the door's price rewards SEEING, and force is the losing line; a floor whose challenge can be brute-forced violates the spine and is a design defect. (b) The companion cadence fixes WHICH floors carry a companion-design (1, 6, 11, ...) vs combat+household only — the loremaker designs a new companion only for a fifth-height floor. (c) Per-floor RESET FLAVOR is set in each floor's own design within the tower-wide rule: illusion/construct floors re-weave to pristine FREE of the rest-clock (eerie, instant — e.g. floor 1's plaster Doorward); physical/organic floors reheal only on a genuine full rest, sharing the player's OWN rest-clock (a brief threshold-breather reheals neither him nor the enemy). (d) The door's 'wanting something' renders in the fiction as the price of a true read, mute-System-safe (the System never explains the read; the door simply does not open to force).
