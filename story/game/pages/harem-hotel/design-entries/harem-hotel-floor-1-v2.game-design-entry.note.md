## Enemies

### The Doorward

**Role** — floor-1 warden — single, static tutorial gatekeeper the hall grows of plaster and pale lamplight at the challenge end; faceless, no initiative of its own

**Readable Trait** — ANSWERS ONLY WHAT IS BROUGHT TO IT — it has no attack of its own; it mirrors the last force aimed at it. SHELL/FRONT (wrong read): brute MIGHT power (bare-handed on floor 1 — Alan is unarmed, weaker still) meets a plaster-and-lamplight shell that absorbs and returns force — gate x0.3; a connecting swing is a chip and brute-forcing is a losing slog (it kills you slowly with your own returned force). CORRECT READ (x1.75): bait it to overcommit or reach — it answers force, so a feint draws it out — then strike the exposed joint; on floor 1 Alan's primary correct-read line is a MENTAL attack (his stronger mode) that names/exposes its 'only answers what is brought' nature. DECISIVE WEAK-POINT (x3.0): the KEYSTONE LETTER — a single pale glyph low at the sternum where the plaster knit itself shut — struck precisely. UNARMED on floor 1, this is a precise MENTAL strike that reads and names the exact keystone (INTELLECT-read, his stronger mode); once he takes the Letter-Knife (and on later floors) the same decisive is also a FINESSE-placed PHYSICAL strike (INT-read + FIN-placed, NOT MIGHT). One or two clean keystone strikes UNMAKE it; that unmaking is the door's price paid. A true keystone-kill is permanent (retreat-persistence); a retreat from a merely-cracked Doorward re-weaves it to pristine on return (construct/illusion flavor — free of the rest-clock).
