# Dragons & Dungeons — a tabletop game run for one mortal

Independent of The Tower. **Outer frame, no mechanics, prose-driven.** Alan plays himself.

Aria the silver dragon DMs. Alan plays a monk whose creed is self-improvement. It began as a session-zero proof of concept and is now a live, agent-played campaign.

## Premise

Three dragon cousins — Aria (silver, devoted), Ceri (amethyst, aloof), and Mari (black, all appetite) — run a very mature tabletop game for one mortal. The table is the cover; the seduction is the campaign; the mortal never quite knows which layer he is in.

## Register

- **Tone:** warm, mischievous, sensual.
- **Reader framing:** first person, from the mortal's perspective.
- **Genre:** narrative, ttrpg.

## The dials

The engine reads these from the game row; they are recorded here because they are what this world chose, not what the engine defaults to.

- **Resolution: none.** This game never rolls. No dice, no formula, no contested check — a game declaring `none` declares no resolution mechanism at all. Every outcome is authored. The Tower is the opposite pole and resolves by formula.
- **Mechanics weight: zero.** No character sheet, no HUD, no system window. The play surface carries narrative and an action box and nothing else.
- **Controlled entity: single.** Alan controls one character, the monk.

## The two frames

The story runs in two frames at once, and knowing which one a line is in is the whole trick of it.

- **The Table** — the balcony of Caer Arianrhod, where the real people are playing the game.
- **The Game World** — the tale itself, the world the characters adventure in.

Who each person is in each frame:

| Character | At the table | In the game world |
|---|---|---|
| Alan | Alan, the player | the monk (first-person narrator) |
| Aria | Aria, the DM | the voice of the world |
| Mari | Mari, herself at the table | the wet-haired girl |
| Ceri | Ceri — the empty seat, not yet arrived | — |

**Ceri is table-only until she arrives, and never present in the game world.**

## Narration

First-person Alan — the monk — throughout. Every other character is third person.

## Frame transitions

Dissolve imagery marks each frame transition at the boundary. Within a frame there are no per-line frame distinctions: the marking is boundaries-only.
