# Aria

Harem Hotel — companion design, subject `aria`.

## Talent

THE LOOM — Arianrhod's hidden sexual Talent, the weaver of fate half-remembered as a goddess made mechanical. It does not level; it deepens. OPERATION: THE LOOM works only on a LIVE shared thread between Aria and Alan (the mutual coupling THE LINK creates). When it fires, Aria takes one TRUE bound trait of Alan's and WEAVES it toward the shape it is already reaching for — deepening a skill or affinity toward the version it wants to become — at the standing cost that the woven trait becomes hers too, entangled: what she deepens in him, she is now bound into. She makes a thing MORE TRULY ITSELF, where her flaw's tongue only makes a story lovelier — the loom that improves a true thing is the same instrument as the tongue that improves a telling, but the loom's improvement is real, not brushed. Post-fire it renders as her BOND AFFINITY DESCRIPTION (the only mechanical bond surface she has; dark until fire).

## Activation

HIDDEN + EXPERIMENTAL, discovered by living the relationship (never a published checklist). PREREQUISITE: a live LINK bind must exist between Aria and Alan (the mutual thread THE LINK creates) — so the earliest possible fire is post-LINK-resolution (t11+); evaluated against play-history through t10 it is NOT yet satisfied (no shared thread exists until the LINK resolves), so no historical activation was missed. CONCRETE TRIGGER: the first time Aria, inside an intimate beat with that live LINK thread between them, DELIBERATELY weaves on the thread — reaching for one true bound trait of Alan's and deepening it toward the shape it is reaching for, knowingly paying the entanglement cost (it becomes hers too). The intimacy-gating is load-bearing: the weave rides a moment of physical closeness, never a cold combat beat — she can only reach the thread through the intimacy that laid it. Re-evaluate at every qualifying intimacy/bond beat from t11 forward; conditions stay dark and live until met, then the System marks the moment FLAT (talent-activation window, mute) and the affinity surface lights. DELIBERATE-WEAVE THRESHOLD (re-evaluation guidance, NOT a rigid checklist — the talent stays discovered-by-living): a LIGHT / transactional / pre-challenge-framed intimacy (e.g. 'for luck before the door') is a qualifying beat that KEEPS the condition live but does NOT by itself meet the deliberate-weave threshold — the first weave arises only at a MEANT intimate beat for its own sake (no external agenda), where Aria reaches PAST the surface (real emotional weight or mutual vulnerability, a giving of herself) and her half-remembered fate-weaver nature first takes hold of the thread. The threshold reads the design's own 'DELIBERATELY / knowingly' language: an involuntary reflex on any coupling is not it. RE-EVAL LOG: t20 (first qualifying intimacy post-LINK — Alan's 'fuck for luck before the next door,' both rested on floor 2 pre-Usher) evaluated -> DORMANT (light/pre-challenge framing; deliberate-weave threshold unmet); intimacy rendered full + warm, no LOOM hint; condition remains dark and live for the next qualifying beat.

## Design seeds

CONSTRAINT VERIFICATION against the 8-point Companion-Talent Design Law (design-constraints-companion-talents): (1) CHARACTER-CONSONANT — PASS, strong: THE LOOM grows from her nature (the weaver of fate / silver dragon / dungeon-master-by-vocation who sets tempo and reveal) and is bound to her flaw 'the better telling' (the loom that betters a true thing = the tongue that betters a story). (2) VARIETY — PASS (sole companion so far); her axis is THREAD-BINDING / trait-deepening-via-entanglement. RESERVED FOR HER: no future companion may run a thread/weave/fate-binding mechanism, and no future companion may be sexual in the same WAY (intimacy-as-the-only-conduit-to-a-deepening). (3) HIDDEN + CONCRETE ACTIVATION — PASS: activation is concrete GM-side (above), the Aria-gap closed. (4) FIRST-FIRE PROTOCOL — PASS: flat System mark, then renders as bond affinity description; affinity dark until fire. (5) REGISTER-FROM-THE-SHEET — PARTIAL / FLAG: her sheet carries a rich register block (idiolect, voiceExemplars incl. peakPitch, attentionTargets) but NO explicit NO-list per constraint 5; flag to GM to add one. (6) ACCELERATOR NOT KEY — PASS: THE LOOM deepens an existing thread; no floor is gated on it (two progressions separate). (7) FULLY STATTED — PASS: Aria carries full attributes/pools/skills/her own talent; THE LINK couples to her real growing stats. (8) EXPLICITLY SEXUAL — PASS via intimacy-GATING of activation (LINK-bind prereq is sex-formed; the weave fires only inside an intimate beat) / FLAG: the OPERATION itself (deepening a trait) is not inherently sexual — constraint 8's wide axis is met through the activation conduit, not the mechanism's content. Flag to GM/Alan: if a sharper sexual axis for the OPERATION is wanted, that concretization is left open by the seed. (9) SAFETY-NET DISCOVERABILITY (world-logic-talent-activation-safety-net, Alan-ruled) — PASS: Aria's five-floor set is FLOORS 1-5; THE LOOM's activation is discoverable within it via a direction-not-disclosure tutorial quest. Note THE LOOM's prerequisite (a live LINK bind) was met on floor 1 (t12) and its trigger is intimacy-gated with a companion Alan is already deeply intimate with, so it will almost certainly fire well before floor 5 — the net is a FALLBACK unlikely to trigger. PRE-AUTHORED FLOOR-5 FALLBACK DIRECTION (for the GM to issue on ARIA's pane, mute mechanical quest form, IF THE LOOM is still dormant entering floor 5): points at 'the bond holds two ways — it can be DRAWN, not only felt; reach along the thread when you are closest to him' — i.e. narrows the search to ACTING on the live LINK thread during intimacy, WITHOUT naming the weave, the trait-deepening, or the entanglement cost (direction, never the checklist; conditions stay in this store + her GM-side sheet). ABSORB PROVENANCE: this row absorbs the concrete talent+activation already recorded GM-side on Aria's game-entity sheet (talent.id the-loom, talent.activationConditions) — faithful absorption, not a re-invention. || BIND RE-VALIDATION (Alan's premise ruling 2026-07-13, world-logic-companion-bind): Aria is SCALED DOWN to the climber's level (level-1 on floor 1, rising with Alan) — a PEER climbing beside him, not an OP ally. THE LOOM and its activation are BIND-INVARIANT: the talent DEEPENS (does not level) and fires on the intimacy-gated LINK-thread weave regardless of her bound working level, so the activation arc stands exactly as designed (concrete trigger unchanged, LINK prereq met t12, safety-net fallback floor-5). The bind governs her WORKING STATS only — rendered at level-matched-pair scale, her true dragon depths latent, both rising as Alan climbs. SYNERGY WITH THE LINK: the LINK (her Will tied to his, canon t12) IS the bind's own thread — THE LOOM binds into herself what she deepens in him, and the bind is that same entanglement made into the climb (her reach tied to his stature; her depths return as he rises — the reason to climb together). Constraint 7 (fully-statted; THE LINK couples to real growing stats) holds: her BOUND working stats ARE her real, growing stats for the climb. Sheet rendering (true depths vs bound working stats) is coordinated with the GM seat, entity edits dry-run first (per Alan's ruling — not applied unilaterally by the loremaker).
