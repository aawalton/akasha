# Talent Activation Safety Net

Harem Hotel — world logic, subject `companion-talents`.

## Rule

TALENT-ACTIVATION SAFETY NET: each companion's arc spans a five-floor set (floors 1-5 for the first girl, 6-10 for the second, 11-15 for the third, and so on from her arrival floor). If the party reaches the LAST floor of a girl's set and her talent's activation conditions remain undiscovered (talent still dormant, never fired), the System issues HER a tutorial quest — on her own pane, in the mute mechanical quest form — pointing her in the right direction. The quest gives DIRECTION, never the checklist: it composes with the hidden+experimental-activation constraint (conditions are discovered by living the relationship, never published) — the tutorial narrows the search space the way Alan's floor-1 tutorial quests did, without naming the trigger.

## Implications

GM checks this at every floor transition into a set's last floor: for each companion whose set this is, if her talent has never fired, author the System tutorial quest to her pane that turn. • The quest is hers (companion-pane), not the player's — mutual System-pane canon established at t10/t12. • Composes with design-constraints-companion-talents #3: direction, never disclosure; the concrete conditions stay in the design store and the GM-side sheet only. • A talent that fires before the last floor never triggers the net (the quest exists only as the fallback).
