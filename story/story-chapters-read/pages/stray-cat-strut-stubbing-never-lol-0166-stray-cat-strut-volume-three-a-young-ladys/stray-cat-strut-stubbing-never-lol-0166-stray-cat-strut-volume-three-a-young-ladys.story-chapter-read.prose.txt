After Gomorrah calls for help, Cat finds herself pulled into a conspiracy involving missing people, gangs living in the sewers, and the baseball-bat nuns who chase after them.

The world is always bleaker than it looks, but sometimes there's a hopeful punk or two trying to beat some brightness into it.

Cat never expected to meet so many violently optimistic people, but if that’s what her corner of the world needs to get better, she’s ready to join in on some of the fun.
