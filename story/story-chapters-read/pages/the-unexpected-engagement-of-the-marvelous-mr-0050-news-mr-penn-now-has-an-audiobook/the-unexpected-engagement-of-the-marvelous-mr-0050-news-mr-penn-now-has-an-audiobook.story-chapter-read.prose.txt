I’m very pleased to announce that The Unexpected Engagement of the Marvelous Mr. Penn now has an audiobook! If any of you are interested, you can find it on Audible or Apple Books. This was a big passion project for me. I wanted to get at least one audiobook done for my father, who is infinitely more of an audiobook listener, rather than an in-print or digital reader. The tale has been illicitly lifted; should you spot it on Amazon, report the violation.My narrator is the extremely talented Mr. Andy Roberts, who did a superb job of bringing the characters to life. I know he did because my brother gushed on and on about how much he loved it, and if you can’t trust the highly biased opinions of your nearest and dearest, who can you trust?

And to all my readers, thank you. I appreciate the time you’ve given me.
