# Companion Bind

Harem Hotel — world logic, subject `companion-bind`.

## Rule

THE COMPANION BIND (how the Hotel scales a companion to her partner — Alan-ruled premise 2026-07-13, recommended by the loremaker and accepted). DIEGETIC, NOT A RETCON: a companion's HISTORY and NATURE stay fully true — Aria is and remains an ancient dragon, the weaver of fate, three thousand years deep. What is bound is her REACH — her working power while she climbs the tower beside her partner. THE MECHANISM (fits the established grows-things logic): the Hotel that grows rooms to fit its guest also FITS a companion to her partner's STATURE — a woman who climbs beside a level-N man reaches at level N. THE BIND LOOSENS AS THE PARTNER RISES: her working level tracks his, so she GROWS as he grows (one step per floor-clear, alongside his +1). LOOSEN TRAJECTORY (general rule): a bound companion's working block INTERPOLATES from her bound-arrival block toward her LATENT (pre-bind) depths, reaching latent when the partner's level equals her pre-bind level, then continuing to grow PAST latent as he climbs further (working level = his level, no ceiling). For Aria (pre-bind L3): bound-L1 (floor 1) -> bound-L2 (floor 2) -> latent-L3 fully returned (floor 3) -> past latent (floors 4+). Each floor-clear re-derives her block one step along this trajectory, PARITY with the partner's own +1 level-step, so the pair stays level-matched throughout. THE LINK IS THE THREAD (dovetail with canon t12): the LINK bond already established — 'you carry a share of his Will; it rises as his rises' — is now LITERAL and load-bearing: the same entanglement that ties her Will to his ties her whole reach to his stature, and her true depths RETURN as he climbs. THE CLIMB IS HOW SHE COMES BACK TO HERSELF: the reason to climb together is that his rise is hers — a narrative HOOK, not a nerf. SHEET RENDERING (approach — exact numbers coordinated with the GM seat, entity edits dry-run first, never applied unilaterally by the loremaker): a bound companion's sheet shows BOTH her TRUE depths (immutable, who she is — rendered latent/locked) AND her BOUND WORKING STATS (what she brings to a level-matched fight, ~the pair's current level). Her play-level pools cannot be her true-depth pools (a true MANA of ~108 renders bound at level-1-pair scale on floor 1); the working stats rise floor by floor as Alan levels. Constraint 7 (design-constraints-companion-talents — fully-statted; THE LINK couples to real growing stats) is satisfied BY the bound working stats: they ARE her real, growing stats for the duration of the climb.

## Implications

(a) EVERY floor calibrates opposition for a LEVEL-MATCHED PAIR (Alan + bound companion at the floor's level) — credibly dangerous, real kill-risk, never trivialized by an unbound ally; no floor-design may assume unbound-companion power (this is the power-scale clause challenge-door-v3 references). (b) The bind applies to EVERY companion on joining, not Aria-only — each is bound to the climber's stature at her arrival floor and rises with him. (c) A companion's TALENT is BIND-INVARIANT (talents deepen, they do not level — THE LOOM fires the same at any bound working level); the bind governs WORKING STATS / reach only. (d) Entity-sheet rendering (true depths vs bound working stats) is coordinated with the GM seat and dry-run before applying — the design store declares the APPROACH; the GM owns the concrete sheet numbers. (e) Dovetails with THE LINK (canon t12): her growth literally tied to his is now the bind's mechanism, and the narrative promise (her depths return as he climbs) is a core hook to preserve in play.
