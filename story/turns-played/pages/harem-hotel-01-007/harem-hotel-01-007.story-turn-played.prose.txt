"...is for us to get closer." You finish it for her — and then you give her the rest of it, her hand still warm in yours. "What do you want to do?"

She's quiet a moment. You watch her weigh it — not the question, you think, but the thing behind it she can't see.

"Three thousand years I've decided when the scene gets warm," she says at last, low. "And now there's something in the air by your face I can't read, telling us to." Her mouth crooks. "I don't take its notes. It doesn't get to call this one."

A beat. Her hand tightens around yours, once, and eases.

"But I won't dress this up: I find I want to anyway — and that's mine, not its." She leaves the last of the distance exactly where it is, uncrossed, the smallest gap held open between you. "So. Your hand, and your call." Her eyes stay on yours. "Do you want to come the rest of the way — or shall I?"
