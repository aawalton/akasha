"For you? Anything."

It comes out easy — easier than anything else you've said tonight — and something in the corner of her mouth suggests she's filing the word 'anything' away with the rest of her collection.

A hand comes up, light, to your jaw. And she presses a kiss to your forehead — slow, unhurried, warm; unmistakably tender, and just as unmistakably not a reopening of anything. A thing complete in itself, given by a woman who decides exactly what her gestures mean and trusts you to read them right. By now, you do.

"There. Goodnight, Alan. Sleep without the dream this time — and if it comes back, you know exactly where I am."

She gathers the blanket over one arm and takes the low lamp, and you go your two ways down the same corridor — her door with its soft click, yours standing open to a room that has kept your warmth against your return.

The bed takes you back without complaint. The last thing you register is the faint sound of the fire settling in the hall, and the certainty — sourceless as the rest, and more welcome than most — that the house is keeping its watch tonight with particular attention.

Sleep comes up to meet you, deep and even.

And this time, nothing follows you down.
