## Absorption (how a seed becomes an affinity)
A **WILL + INTELLECT** act: hold the seed, draw the essence in. Resolved as a contested mental effort against the essence's resistance (1d20, Wild Variance, like any Alan action). Hidden roll, fog rules apply — the OUTCOME is narrated, the numbers are not.

- **Cost (always):** Focus (the mental pool). A draw is real exertion.
- **Untrained / forced draw = rough:** higher Focus cost **and a backlash** keyed to the essence's nature (Ember burns HP; caustic stings; sound rings the ears / bleeds Focus; etc.). A weak roll still succeeds marginally but pays more.
- **The seed is consumed** — drawn essence leaves the seed cold and inert. A rough draw wastes most of the essence to get a little in.
- **Result:** grants, or raises by one rank, an **Affinity** of that type (I → II → …).

## Affinities (what you get)
- A **retained** progression axis — affinities survive the loop (loop-rules.md lists them RETAINED).
- **Passive:** bias relevant actions upward — intent + effectiveness with that element, and sharper sense of it (an Ember affinity → reads heat / live ember more keenly). This is a real, if soft, edge.
- **Active (higher rank):** unlock essence-skills (channel / project the element) as rank climbs. Not at rank I.
- **Scaling:** with an established affinity of a type, or a relevant skill, future draws of THAT type are cheaper and cleaner. Cross-type draws stay hard until trained.

## Fog / presentation
- The **affinity is shown** once gained (Skills/Affinities panel) — he can feel it; it's earned knowledge. The numeric mechanics (the bias size, costs) stay hidden.
- The System marks an absorption with a **flat, universal notification** (copy-paste test: any climber drawing essence sees the same template) — e.g. `ESSENCE ABSORBED — Ember` / `AFFINITY GAINED — Ember (I)`. No judgment, no scene-narration.
- All the *felt* meaning (the burn, the rush, what it's like) lives in the window-pane prose, never the panel.
