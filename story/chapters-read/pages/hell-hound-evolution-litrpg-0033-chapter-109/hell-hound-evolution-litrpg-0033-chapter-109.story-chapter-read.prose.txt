The moment the cooldown ended, I shifted back into my Whisperfang Juvenile form and the world finally felt right again.

I slept curled up beside Lily and Grick that night, my larger body feeling a little strange after only a couple of days without it. Even so, it was a welcome to be stronger and faster again.

Before we left, Grick was outfitted with a proper set of armor: layered cloth reinforced with metal bracers and shin guards, along with a new dagger and sheath at his side, and a potion satchel that hooked to his belt. He gave the dagger a few testing stabs, then poked the end with his finger and instantly cut himself.

"Very sharp thing," he marveled as he stared at the bead of blood on his fingertip.

We met with Kenji and Shun in the Palace lobby, and I was relieved to see Kenji was okay after all. “Good morning,” he said with a bow. “We were tasked with escorting you out of the city walls.”

Shun looked at me, his eyebrows and mustache raising simultaneously. “You’re the girl from yesterday?”

I huffed a confirmation.

They led us down a less populated street, heading towards a more western part of the city wall than the southern one we entered from a couple days ago. The gate at the front was likewise not as impressive, but it swung open slowly upon our approach. On the other side was more farm land.

"Just head that way,” Kenji said, pointing toward the distant tree line. “You’ll find another opening in the boundary wall soon enough.”

I glanced back at him and gave a small nod. I would’ve liked to thank him properly—to tell him I was glad he was okay—but that would have to wait until I could speak to him again in my human form.

For now, a new hunt was on, and I was ready to give my wolfy legs a stretch. I looked at Lily and Grick and, through Pack Bond, told Grick to ride her.

Lily's tail wagged as she lowered down, and Grick climbed on.

"Let's go!" I told them, then bolted through the farmlands.

Man, it had only been a couple of days, but truly, being on such pathetic short and slow human legs for that amount of time was an instantly noticeable difference. I felt the wind rushing across my fur, my powerful legs bounding me forward with each step. Yeah, being a dog was way better than being a human.

The farmland blurred past us, Lily running at my side, and two guards watched with fascination as we bolted through the opening in the wall and into the forest.

After getting my fill of running, I let myself slow down and looked around. I used {Pulse Sense}, and instantly, heartbeats bloomed to life all around me—high in the trees, in the bushes, scurrying into hiding or hunkering low. No human bandits yet, of course, but I just wanted to feel again what it was like to have such power.

Lily came to trot alongside me while Grick rode on her back. I wonder if, once they both got bigger and evolved more, they would continue to do that. I think Grick would be very cool as a goblin wolf rider! That'd have some serious main character energy, which I felt quite awkward about thinking.

I mean, maybe Lily was the main character; she was sweet and lovable in a way any protagonist should be! Would I really be cast as a main character over her?

Anyway, putting off those useless thoughts, I continued leading the way through the woods.

I didn't have a lead on finding the humans yet, but I was sure that one would pop up sooner or later if there were as many of them creeping around these forests as Rin seemed to think. For now, I just really wanted to get in a good hunt!

Well, not that there would be particularly great hunts in these forests anyway. I needed to ask Rin to direct me to better, higher-level hunting grounds sometime. I was also very curious about going to the depths sometime.

I noticed a somewhat large pulse nearby and nodded to Lily. "Let me lead," I told her.

I was planning to play ball hog a bit this time on this kill—I just really needed to get it out of my system!

Once I spotted a particularly large Mossgrazer through the bushes, I used {Analyze}.

Mossgrazer – Level 15

Not bad, I might even get some experience from this one!

I crept forward, staying low, and then finally I struck. I went right for the back leg, snapping it easily with {Crippling Bite}.

It's bladed vines lashed around, managing to cut my flank as I popped away and tried to avoid it. But then Lily burst from the bushes with Grick still on her back.

As she came close, he jumped off, landing on the Moss Grazer and stabbing it as Lily went for its throat.

Nice going, guys! I thought with a wolfy grin as Lily shook and twisted at its neck. I bounded forward and tackled it, helping Lily take it to the ground, where Grick positioned himself for a more precise stab at its heart, and it soon fell still.

Prey Eliminated! Mossgrazer level 15!

Experience gained!

Still no level-ups for me, huh? Guess that would change once we got to killing bandits, though. I had to be close. But for now, it was time to eat!

Lily sniffed out its belly, while I started chomping at its side. Grick found a patch of moss to wipe his dagger clean on while we feasted.

Palace food was great, especially those Puffstorks, but there was nothing quite like filling your belly on your own kill. When I was done, I licked the blood from my chops and looked up to the canopy overhead.

I hoped doing quests for the emperor would truly mean faster level gains. After seeing Valrok and Rin face off, hell, even considering Fox's role in that whole confrontation, I realized how helpless I really was. I might be the apex of some low-level forest lands or caves, but there was so much more strength out there I couldn’t currently contend with.

I thought back to my Veiledfang Stalker path, particularly that certain hint: "Predator and prey are not opposites, they are positions."

That was very true, and it didn’t just apply to that one Path. That is what life was for any living thing. We were all somewhere on the spectrum of predator and prey, one way or another, and it could shift depending on your situation and your own strength.

As long as I was weaker than those who would do me harm, I was prey. But if I wanted to be a predator capable of hunting someone like Valrok—if I wanted to become a true apex—I needed so much more strength. The kind that wouldn't be attained by snacking on Mossgrazers.

I nodded to Lily and Grick, then continued leading the way through the forest.

“We're looking for humans. Bad humans. Humans, we can eat,” I explained through pack bond, just in case Lily didn't fully understand the assignment. Though, considering she most likely got a quest alongside Grick and me, I'm sure she realized perfectly well what was going on.

She might not have as strong a grasp on language as I do, but even Earth dogs could understand some things, and she was a very smart Hell Hound pup. It wouldn't surprise me if she even worked out the nuance of what exactly a map was and why we were looking at it in the war room.

Rin had been pointing out things on the paper everyone was taking seriously, and now we were traveling somewhere. It wouldn't be hard to put two and two together and realize what the whole point was, and as I said before, Lily is a very smart pup.

She put her nose to the ground and started searching. It seemed she soon got onto something's trail, so I paced alongside her and sniffed as well. It didn't take long for me to realize she was tracking another animal.

“Not that,” I told her with a soft growl. “Human. We’re looking for human,” I reminded her.

Lily looked at me, looked back over her shoulder, then put her nose to the ground and tried again.

To be fair, there simply were no human trails to pick up right now, and Lily probably was thinking that tracking some kind of prey is better than no prey at all.

“Let’s travel deeper in,” I said. “Hopefully, we'll catch a lead soon.”

With that, we continued deeper and deeper into the forest and further from the Empire's walls.

Another hour went by when, suddenly, Lily froze where she stood, and her ears shot up. Her tail stopped its wagging as she focused.

What did she notice? I sniffed the air and focused my hearing as well.

Then, she suddenly shot off into the thickets.

“Human smell!” she exclaimed excitedly through Pack Bond.

I used {Pulse Sense}, but didn't immediately see anything that I could identify as human. Though I also considered how I'd have to be careful, considering that Genzo could block Pulse Sense, it wouldn't be a stretch to think that he's not the only person in the world who had that ability.

“Careful,” I told Lily.

She slowed her pace. “Close,” she said, tail wagging.

“Not fun hunt. Dangerous hunt,” I told her.

She looked back, her wagging tail suddenly dipping and her ears folding as she took in my words.

“Are you ready, Grick?” I asked.

Grick responded with a nod.

We continued on a little slower. There was still no physical sign of humans, but I caught the scent that had drawn Lily in. Human trails varied, but they always carried a certain wrongness—something out of place. Wet fur and forest musk were natural, expected. But leather? That was different. Processed. Dead. Not like the scent of skin still attached to a monster

Grease, oils, soap, cooked meats, or powders. These were always dead giveaways. But right now, I was picking up the sharp smell of something chemically.

Soap? No…

Hmmm…

Perfume!

My ears shot up as I realized it, but then I felt disappointed. Bandits probably wouldn't be wearing perfume. Even if there were female bandits among them... probably. Was it simply a girl from the empire who had gone venturing off? That didn't seem likely, not this far away from the boundary wall.

Some other dark possibilities entered my mind, and I started sniffing forward with more urgency.

Still nothing through Pulse Sense, but as I got closer to the scent of perfume, I realized it was an old scent. It stuck around so clearly because of the nature of something like perfume, but it was clearly hidden under layers of the expected forest smells that had plenty of time to settle over it.

And finally, we found the source.

A discarded bag with various things thrown on the ground around it, including a cracked perfume bottle. Lily gave the bottle a direct sniff and then jumped back so quickly it's as if she had been slapped. She whined and scampered away from the bag, then huddled down and pawed at her nose.

"Puppy okay?" Grick asked, patting her head.

I knew she'd be fine. Sniffing the concentrated perfume so directly was just a bit overwhelming. Even a human nose would have felt offended by it.

I pawed at the bag, turning it over, and found it had been cut open with a knife, and the strap that would have been worn around one's shoulder had been forcefully ripped, with frayed fibers in the threads that held it together.

Furthermore, it was old. It had clearly been here for quite some time.

The discarded items didn't seem to be of any particular value. Yet, I suspected perfume was a luxury item in a world like this, so it was unlikely that a random commoner girl had been robbed, and that meant more valuable items were taken.

I hoped it was just a simple robbery, at least.

I searched the surrounding area for anything else that might point me in the right direction towards some humans.

“Look for footprints,” I told my pack, and they instantly got to work helping me.

It didn't take long before—

"Grick finds something!"

I trotted over and investigated. There was a spot of dirt with a clear imprint left behind from now-hardened mud. Something had stepped there, but not a Mossgrazer. The print was a bit too long for a hoof, though it didn't look like a perfect human footprint either; that was only because of the weather filling in around it and slowly forming it back into flat ground.

I glanced up. The nearby tree must have shielded this spot from the rain, preserving this print while others had likely been washed away.

In any case, I felt we were on the right trail to tracking down some nefarious humans and commanded my pack onward.

It wasn't long before we found the trail led to something else. My stomach dropped as I was now sure something terrible had happened here. It was dirty and stiff with dried rainwater, but we found clothes—women’s clothes.

Grick's ears folded down; it seemed he understood what I had. Lily whined softly, looking between us. She could probably figure out well enough that only humans wore those things, and if they were discarded and Grick and I looked upset about it, then that meant something bad had happened to a not-bad human.

Unfortunately, that's the last clue we would get for a while. It was truly a very old trail, and it was only thanks to the perfume that we picked it up in the first place. Whatever had happened here was far away from us now, both in time and distance. Unless they had a base nearby, we weren’t catching up anytime soon.

The hunt continued as morning gave way to afternoon, and the dew that clung to everything disappeared along with it. We hunted another moss grazer and got another mid-afternoon snack in. Grick had some jerky that his outfitters must have provided him, and he chewed it happily alongside us.

"Salty human food," he said, nodding at it. "Lots of chewing but tasty." He ripped off another bite.

He was okay with eating raw fish but avoided any other kind of uncooked meat. Goblins must have had strong stomachs, though, because I've seen him drop a fish down his throat whole, bones and all. Yet, there must have been something about other kinds of uncooked meat that was bad for him.

When we were done eating, we pressed on.

I was starting to understand the empire’s bandit problem. We’d covered a huge amount of ground and still hadn’t tracked down a single group of bandits. Me and Lily both had great smelling, not to mention my Pulse Sense. Humans, even in teams, would struggle to comb these woods efficiently.

A couple of Hell Hounds, however, were perfect for the job, and I could see why the Emperor put us on this task.

“Human!” Lily suddenly called.

I did a quick scanning {Pulse Sense}, but nothing suspicious immediately jumped out at me. I trotted over to her and asked, “Are you sure?”

“Human!” she insisted. “I smell it! Fresh smell!”

Her tail wagged excitedly, and as soon as I gave a sniff around for whatever she'd noticed, my tail started going as well. After our nearly full day of searching, we found a very clear scent that was very clearly human.

“Stay cautious, track slowly. Dangerous hunt,” I reminded them.

Grick's hand dipped to his dagger hilt, unconsciously checking that it was still there.

We continued on, the trail steadily getting stronger. Night was several hours from setting in, but when I scanned ahead with another {Pulse Sense}, I knew we had found our prey.

“Stop. Six humans,” I warned Grick and Lily. We all hunkered down in the thickets. Attacking at night would be preferable, and I had to verify that these were indeed bandits. Otherwise, we wouldn’t get credit towards the quest for killing them, and just might get us in trouble with Rin.

Either way, I didn't want to kill humans who were innocent, unless it were an emergency food situation. It's not that I necessarily valued human lives over monsters, or had qualms about killing them. It’s just that there were better food options. Either way, I'd have to be careful to never kill a human who was part of the empire, or a merchant who Rin wouldn't be happy about me killing...

Well, okay, maybe humans needed to just be off the menu in total. It would cause too many problems with my new allies if I made a mistake.

Anyway!

“Stay,” I told Lily and Grick, then prowled forward carefully, activating {Veil of Shadows} to further hide myself amongst the thickets.

As I slipped forward, my body melted into the dim underbrush, shadows clinging to my fur like a second coat.

Each step was slow as I crept up to the edge of a small clearing and lowered myself behind a fallen log, just enough to peer over.

Five of them.

Rough clothes. Patchwork armor. Mismatched weapons—one carried a chipped sword, another a woodcutter’s axe, and one a bow with only half a quiver left. Definitely not imperial soldiers.

So… Bandits.

Good.

Two of them were sitting around a stack of wood as one tried to light a fire, growling with frustration. Another was sharpening a dagger against a whetstone with slow, grating strokes. The other two stood watch… poorly. One leaned against a tree, half-asleep, while the other scanned the forest with all the awareness of a Mossgrazer.

Then my eyes narrowed.

Near the base of a tree, half-hidden in shadow—

There was the sixth person I hadn’t noticed at first. My hackles raised in anger, and I had to consciously stop myself from growling as I realized what I was seeing.

It was a girl, dirty and dressed down to rags, with a burlap sack pulled over her head. They had her tied to a tree with her hands behind her back, and her body was covered in bruises. The way she slumped against her bindings spoke of being worn and defeated, as if she was kept like this for a long time.

I used {Analyze} on each of them in turn.

Human – Level 16

Human – Level 14

Human – Level 13

Human – Level 16

Human – Level 15

Human – Level 3

I was level 16, so I could easily defeat any of them one-on-one, I was sure. However, Lily was only level 13, and Grick level 10, and we were outnumbered.

I realized we couldn’t just go for it; we’d have to be strategic and wait for nightfall. I just hoped nothing else terrible happened to that girl in the meantime that would force me to act faster.
