Hello! If you're reading this, that means that you've reached the end of the Book 1 chapters available on Royal Road. Or, in other words, this series is stubbed. The reason that the rest isn't here is that Amazon's Kindle Unlimited requires exclusivity, so no more than 10% of the story can be available anywhere else. Those first 6 chapters aren't quite 10%, but I wasn't going to mess around and try to toe the line.

If you want to read the rest of book 1 of Bunny Girl Evolution, it is available now on Amazon. If you have a Kindle Unlimited subscription, you can read it for free. Here is a link to where you can find it: https://mybook.to/Bunny-Girl-Evolution

The audiobook is produced by SoundBooth Theater, and if you aren't aware of them, they are the company owned and run by Jeff Hayes, narrator of Dungeon Crawler Carl. They're one of the best in the business, and they hooked me up with a really good cast for this book:

If that interests you, go and check it out! 18.5 hours of content!
