Wandering Mage

Tressa is a High Elven noble and talented young mage who never quite fit in with the expectations and rigid life of nobility.

All she ever wanted is to sneak off into the woods to study monsters, create hallucinogenic potions, test poisons on herself, and tamper with unstable spell runes that could very well blow off a limb.

Is that so much to ask?

One day, five years before her 200th birthday, she is called upon by an ancient dragon and given a trial.

"Create a spell that never existed before. A spell that will change the world."

She knows she will never succeed if she is to remain trapped inside the gilded walls of her family's estate.

So when she happens upon a pair of traveling monster hunters, she's quick to run away with them.  Now accompanied by a halberd-wielding lizardwoman and a minotaur, Tressa is loose in a world that is far bigger than she ever could have imagined.

Beyond the walls and laws that stifled her growth, she is sure she'll be able to find an answer to the dragon's challenge and maybe discover a few interesting monsters and poisons along the way.
