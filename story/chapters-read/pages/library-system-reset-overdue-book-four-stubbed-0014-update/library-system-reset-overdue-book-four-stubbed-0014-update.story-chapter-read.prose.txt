Alchemical Imbalance

Taria kills people for fun, but only if they're deplorable human beings and it pays well.

After surviving her parents, Taria's choices were limited but her alchemical skills with plants make the assassin's guild the perfect place, considering it's as close to old magic as one can get.

Life in the Assassin's guild as an assistant alchemy teacher and sought after assassin is generally good until an ambush goes wrong. An ambush where Taria isn't the instigator, but the victim. But she's experienced death enough in her life and fights with a desperation to live that surprises even her.

Especially when it turns out that magic might not be so extinct after all.

A secret that would never have been revealed if she'd just died like she was supposed to.
