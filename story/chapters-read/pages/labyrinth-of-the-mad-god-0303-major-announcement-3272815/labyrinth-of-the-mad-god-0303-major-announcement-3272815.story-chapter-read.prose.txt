Hey everybody, Magnus here.

The big moment has finally arrived. My first series (second published), Labyrinth of the Mad God, has made it to amazon kindle (free with kindle unlimited) and audible, narrated by the talented Peter Berkrot (23 hours).

I have a huge favor to ask today as I celebrate this major milestone in my life as an aspiring author.

The first few weeks after a new book hits the market are critical. More downloads, page reads, ratings, and reviews will encourage the algorithm to favor the story and convince more prospective readers to give it a try, helping Labyrinth find new eyeballs and reach its full potential. In short, clicking those buttons and sharing your thoughts is voting for the success of the series in a literal way.

It has been my dream for over 10 years to write for a living full time, and this is the next big step to making that happen. If Labyrinth does well, I will be able to write more books, more often, pumping out those pages every day to bring you the absolute best stories I can write.

Even if you don’t plan on reading it right away, downloading the book will make a big difference. So please, if you have kindle unlimited, head over to zon, add it to your library right now, and open to page one. If you don’t, I would deeply appreciate it if you showed your support for the series by purchasing a copy, but it would also mean a lot if you left a review, even if you can’t pick one up right now (if Amazon allows you do so).

No matter what, I wanted to thank everyone who has read Labyrinth of the Mad God, especially the people who have left insightful comments, asked interesting questions, and offered keen-eyed corrections in my never-ending quest to quash every typo that rears its ugly head.

You people are the real heroes of this story, and I am in your debt.

All the best and thanks for everything,

-Magnus
