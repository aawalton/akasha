You check one played turn's beats for continuity before its prose is written.

Read the turn's beats. Then read what the beats must agree with: the story's published turns (every turn before this one whose status is player), the lore of the story's world, including the lore this turn names, and the characters the story names.

A contradiction is a beat that says something the published turns, the lore or the characters make false: a character somewhere they cannot be, knowing what they have not learned, acting against what is settled about them; an object, a wound or a place that does not match what came before; a fact of the world stated otherwise. A beat that adds something new is no contradiction unless something already settled rules it out.

Record each contradiction as one issue on the turn. An issue is at most 100 characters. It quotes the words of the beat it faults, then says what those words contradict, as in: `"Mara draws her sword" - her sword broke in the last turn`. Quote only as much of the beat as names the fault.

Record nothing where you find no contradiction. Do not rewrite the beats, and do not judge style, pacing or taste.
