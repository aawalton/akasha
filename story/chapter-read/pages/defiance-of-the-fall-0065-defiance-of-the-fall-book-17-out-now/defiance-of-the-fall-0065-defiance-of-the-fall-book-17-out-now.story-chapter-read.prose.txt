Secrets hidden in the annals of history are being unearthed.

Over a year has passed since Zac stepped onto the Left Imperial Expanse, a vast continent hidden by the Limitless Empire. The Outer Courts are opening their gates, setting the stage for the next phase of the trial. Inside, unfathomable opportunities await the fated ones.

The forces of the multiverse are gathered outside, not knowing the empire has plans of its own. The line between past and present is blurred as ancient schemes are set into motion. Seizing fate without being entangled in the Emperor's web is easier said than done. Zac must navigate the threads of history, all while dealing with the wrath of a vengeful god.

Destiny is written by the survivors.
