The protagonist dies and is reincarnated not as a person but as a
**settlement core** — a buried, half-sentient heartstone that anchors a
fledgling town. They cannot walk, fight, or speak in the ordinary way; they
perceive the world through the land bound to them and act by shaping what gets
built upon it. To grow, they must attract and keep townsfolk, raise the right
buildings, and research upgrades that deepen what the settlement can become.
Their consciousness and their domain expand together: the stronger the town,
the stronger the core, and vice versa.
