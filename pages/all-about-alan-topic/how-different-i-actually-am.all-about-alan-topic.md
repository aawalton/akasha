# Definition

- **How Different I Actually Am** — the size of the gap between me and most people, and why every estimate reads low

# Design

However different you think I am you are underestimating it, and that holds for my own estimate too.

I am not a far-out human. I am a different kind, living where several rare neurotypes cross.

The closest match I ever met was about three quarters, the next about half, and then nothing near.

There is no origin. I hold a model of me and a model of typical people, and both are valid.

As each gets truer they grow further apart, because the systems do differ. What surprises me now is normality.

# Questions

Whether each revision still turns up more distance, or the estimates have begun to settle, is a trajectory nobody tracks.

Whether two true-enough models of any two genuinely different systems drift apart as they sharpen, or only mine do, is unprobed.

By axis I match a fifth in thinking, four fifths in body, half in feeling. Whether the fifth holds or falls further is provisional.

What holds the feeling half up rather than letting it sag to the body figure, I have not said.
