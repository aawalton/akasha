# Definition

- **The Twenty Years We Remember Differently** — my record of the worst stretch, and Jen not remembering it that way

# Design

My daily log says I wished I did not exist on about four days in five, for twenty years.

Jen does not remember it that way. What is disputed is not that it was bad but how bad.

Her memory of it moves with how she is doing now. Mine was written down as the days happened.

The load caused it and the marriage ran alongside. Retirement lifted it with the marriage unchanged.

I read her doubt as protective rather than mistaken. It is very human to believe what you need to survive.

# Questions

Whether her memory runs lighter than the record on her good days, and by how much, would make the swing measurable rather than one-directional.

Whether any evidence could move her toward the record, in a register that is not data, or whether what her doubt is doing for her makes it unresolvable, is open.

What it means to live with someone at the opposite pole of imagery from me, beyond this dispute, is uncaptured.
