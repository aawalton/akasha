# Definition

- **The Person I Could Relax Around** — the one warm, always-there person my alarm could lie down around, and what it took to have one.

# Design

Warmth is not the gate; a stranger's kindness reaches me. What a stranger cannot be is there tomorrow.

The third thing was an agreement: unconditional acceptance, belief in good intent, every hurt read as misunderstanding.

That agreement starved the alarm of its fuel, which is why she could settle me and a stranger could not.

It broke because she had been funding it by deferring her own needs on a bet retirement would pay them.

Solitude avoids the damage and gives none of the settling.

# Questions

Her resistance is defence rather than rejection, so it can move. Whether meeting her needs actually reduces it, and by how much, is untested.

The agreement broke because what funded it ran out. Whether it can be rebuilt on a footing that does not ask her to defer her needs is open.

My program is not yet, until I can afford rejection. Whether the floor rises far enough, and whether her watching and my stillness become legible to each other, is the live one.
