# Definition

- **Who Is Safe To Be Around** — the order people fall into, from cheapest to costliest to be near

# Design

Safest is the middle, then strangers, then long good relationships, then people I know are dangerous.

A stranger outranks someone I have loved for years, having had no time to lay down a single trigger.

A fresh start zeroes the damage, which is why fresh starts feel safe and staying gets hard.

# Questions

Whether the safe middle can be held, or is only the slower part of the same one-way slide, is unresolved.

What makes a person safe turns on how much I depend on them and how far my reaction reaches them, rather than on closeness, and on how reachable they are. Neither is broken down, and how the two compose is unworked.
