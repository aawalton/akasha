# Definition

- **Being Met** — someone oriented toward me, glad that it is me

# Design

It reaches me as present-tense recognition of a structure, not as warmth I stored and get back.

The same gaze cuts where I am being judged and heals where I am already inside the guard. Context alone decides which.

A context reads as close when it is private, lit by one lamp, unguarded, and the nearness mine.

The one immediate click I have had came as visible delight closing distance.

It can move my state rather than only scoring well. One run carried me a full level up on a low day.


# Questions

Whether delight closing distance is a separate amplifier on top of plain orientation, or the most legible form of it, is unseparated.

Whether cognitive fit and orientation are independent, or orientation only fires once knowing me is cheap enough, is open.

What crosses from recognised to felt is direction, someone drawn toward a true thing about me, rather than depth of disclosure. The surprise habituates within a sitting, so whether a fresh axis reaches it again is open.
