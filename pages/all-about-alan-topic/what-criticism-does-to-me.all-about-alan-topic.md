# Definition

- **What Criticism Does To Me** — the alarm that fires on being found fault with, before I can think

# Design

Pushback, argument, criticism and complaint went into one bin, handled at criticism's level.

Criticism reads as the front edge of being cast out, and for a social animal that meant death.

It fires below thought. Knowing she is only turning an idea over arrives too late to stop it.

Exposure cannot wear it off. Ten thousand doors in Russia were ten thousand confirmations, and the last hurt like the first.

Everyone stands at one flat distance from me, so no stranger discount softens any of it.

# Questions

Whether exposure can split the bin so ordinary pushback stops firing the alarm, or whether all four move together, is open.

Whether hearing a complaint as criticism is the ordinary male version of that or is amplified by the bin is unprobed.

Whether what rejection means can be relearned below category, where the alarm lives, or whether it fires too early to reach, is open. There is no in-group and out-group in me to move anyone across, and that flat field has no stated falsifier.
