# Definition

- **Small Bites Of Many Things** — a little of each thing in turn, rather than one thing through to the end.

# Design

Within one thing it stays the same and across the rotation it is always new, so both channels stay lit.

As long as each bite is over some floor, finishing is guaranteed, so there is nothing to estimate before starting.

Coming back after a year feels much like coming back after a day, because only the concept was ever kept.

The set I am working through cannot live in my head, so it lives in the harness.

# Questions

What the floor on a bite actually is, and whether it differs between a book, a game and a project, is unnamed.

No worked shape has been written for how the harness holds a book, a show, a course, a project or a game.

How rotating across many coexists with going deep on one is unworked. They may alternate, or they may layer.

How something enters the set and how it leaves, finished or quit or forgotten or drifted, has no convention.
