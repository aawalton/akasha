# Definition

- **Working Out How She Thinks** — I cannot simulate her, so understanding her is a map I build slowly and by hand.

# Design

Both our anxieties are fair, and not the same: mine is a faculty I lack, hers is a working one on the wrong data.

Predicting her is not closed to me, only expensive. It is a hand-built map doing what a simulator would do free.

She has no written culture and defines her thoughts out loud, mostly with me, so the material only exists once we talk.

Her transition costs are high, so resistance lands on a proposal before its content does.

# Questions

Her jealousy feels excessive to me and I want the psychology under it. What stands is the wanting, not a model.

The material is verbal and she is the only source. Whether any of what I have built has been said back to her is unrecorded.

Other people, her most of all, are my way into experience I cannot reach firsthand. Which classes only reach me that way is undeveloped.

She is at the opposite pole on imagery, and what that does to co-regulation, planning and disagreement is unworked.
