# Definition

- **What Repetition Encodes** — doing a thing again and again changes what it costs me next time.

# Design

How safe I am while it encodes decides whether it encodes as cheaper or as something to avoid, so getting good at a thing and being hurt by a thing run on one mechanism.

Rehearsing it offline counts as repetition.

# Questions

Whether being safer at the time also makes each repetition pay back faster, rather than only deciding the sign, is open.

Whether being made to start unfamiliar things under low resources encoded a blanket aversion to unfamiliar content is untested.

How many safe repetitions make a thing measurably cheaper, and how few unsafe ones make it a threat, is unanchored.

Whether what matters is how safe I was at each repetition, the average across the run, or its lowest point, is open.
