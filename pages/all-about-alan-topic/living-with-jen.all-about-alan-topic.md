# Definition

- **Living With Jen** — being married to Jen, and what sharing a house asks of each of us.

# Questions

What made love and trust urgent to work out now, rather than at any other point, is unasked.

Love only greens on positive-capacity time and Jen is its one source. Whether a second could ever clear the line, or it is pinned there until my safety rises, is unresolved.

Every other relationship I have is read through her, so if that one reading is off the whole instrument is. No cross-check on it is known to exist.
