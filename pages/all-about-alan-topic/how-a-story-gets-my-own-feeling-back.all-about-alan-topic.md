# Definition

- **How A Story Gets My Own Feeling Back** — a story as the outside key to feelings of mine I cannot reach on my own

# Design

My feelings are encoded and intact. What is gone is the waking path back to them.

A story is a key from outside: its shape matches where mine are stored, and the match switches one on.

What comes back is mine, not the character's on loan. Paks not breaking on the rack turns my determination on.

The key turns because I am not separate enough to stay outside the book. While I read, I am the character.

It is not the same job as a story letting a backlog out. That one drains; this one retrieves.

# Questions

Whether the retrieval holds the door open for a while or only flashes it open in the moment is untested.

What makes one story a working key to a particular sealed feeling is unmapped, though a few authors do far more for me than others.

Whether a film, a game or a picture can turn the same key, or only words can, is open.
