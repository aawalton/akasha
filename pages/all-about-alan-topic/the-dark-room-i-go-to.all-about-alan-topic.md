# Definition

- **The Dark Room I Go To** — the near-empty room I lie in when everything has gone past bearing

# Design

A blackout closet in my basement office, quiet and cold because it sits on an outside wall.

It is the first thing I reach for once the load has gone over, ahead of turning any single knob.

It adds nothing. It takes the whole draw to the floor so my regulation can come back on its own.

Emptied of everything else, the room also makes my own body audible again.

Sometimes the weighted blanket goes on top: one chosen input added back in. I stay until I can take stimulation again.

# Questions

How long a reset usually takes, and whether it scales with how deep the overload ran or with where my resources sit, has no anchor at all.

How often it fires, and whether social, sensory or many-stream thinking loads drive me there more than the others, is unprobed.
