# Definition

- **The Score I Keep On Myself** — points and a light on each of my values, and why that layer is my strongest lever

# Design

The values do not change, so the scoring on top of them is where the leverage sits.

The chain runs score, then behaviour, then safety, and safety prices everything else I can do.

The motivation is the light itself, like a notification badge: an irritant I built, as I move only on present pain.

It holds because I do not habituate, so the badge never goes quiet, which is what kills it for others.

Green stays honest: a cooler self set the bar, and I cannot believe I cleared it when I did not.

# Questions

When a light tips off green, what concretely changes in what I do has never been traced step by step.

The love light greens only on time spent with capacity to spare on someone I care about, and Jen is the only source, so a day without that cannot go all green. Whether a second source can clear the line, or it stays pinned until safety rises, is open.
