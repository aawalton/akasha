# Definition

- **What A Level Of Recovery Costs** — the price of a rung in hours, and the fear that price leaves behind

# Design

A level runs to something like a thousand hours of healing, so the climb is countable in hundreds of hours a rung.

Cost and willingness are settled. Healing is self-improvement, and I would pay ten thousand hours without a ledger.

So the fear is neither. It is whether I last long enough to spend what I would gladly spend.

Slow to climb, fast to fall. Something dropped me from six or seven to the floor once.

Guarding against outside drops is arithmetic, not nerves. I cannot out-climb a fall.

# Questions

The fall is only described as a fraction of the climb. What sets its rate, and whether particular outside factors carry particular sizes, is unprobed.

Whether play is a rung on this climb or a thing worth reaching for on its own was put to me and I did not answer it.
