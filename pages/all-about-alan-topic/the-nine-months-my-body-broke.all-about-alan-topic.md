# Definition

- **The Nine Months My Body Broke** — the mission where my body started shaking and I overrode it

# Design

At twenty in Novosibirsk my body flipped to shaking at rest. I read it as demonic opposition.

That inverted the response: backing off would be letting evil win, so I pushed on nine months.

Three in the training centre, six in the field at a hundred people a day and ten thousand rejections.

At the end everything crossed a threshold at once: full-body shaking, migraines, cramping, swollen joints.

Leaving never made the menu. My needs did not count and I always had to meet what others expected.

# Questions

What happened between the break and the low twenty years later, and how the mission actually ended after the psychologist''s phone call, is largely open.

Whether the shaking resolved after I came home, persisted, or became the baseline of the decline that followed is uncharacterised.
