# Definition

- **How A Song Reaches Me** — music as the one thing that gets a feeling to the surface, and how it gets in

# Design

Aphantasia leaves me no emotional recall, so a song is the one channel that surfaces feeling.

It reaches me through what a song is about, not its sound. Unpacking the meaning is the listening.

Then it breaks the surface: I sing along and have to stop because I am crying.

It is strongest read aloud with someone, my reading first and then the layer I missed.

Singing where anyone can hear me is exposure rather than pleasure, above my usual line.

# Questions

Which songs open the door and which slide past as pretty surface is unprobed.

Whether reading one aloud with someone adds anything beyond unpacking it alone, or the lift is being met paying separately, is untested.

I named myself with a play on my own name that came out as alien, and it surfaced here rather than in talk. Whether it still carries how I hold my difference is unprobed.
