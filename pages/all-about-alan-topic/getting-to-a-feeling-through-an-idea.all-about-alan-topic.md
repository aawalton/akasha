# Definition

- **Getting To A Feeling Through An Idea** — the one direction that still works, from something I have understood to something I feel

# Design

It comes rare and strong at once, which fits a gate that filters out everything not built past some threshold and passes the rest whole.

# Questions

The fraction of what I understand that matches what other people feel is a figure I flagged provisional myself, and nobody has rechecked it.

Whether every feeling of mine needs a concept built first, or only the ones anchored in the past, is open, and how much structure is enough to set one off is unknown.

How much of that direction is still live is unmeasured, and it is to be probed directly rather than worked out from anything nearby.
