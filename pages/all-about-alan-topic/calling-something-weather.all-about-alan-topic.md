# Definition

- **Calling Something Weather** — filing a problem as outside my reach, after which it stops pulling at me at all

# Design

An unresolved conflict pulls at full strength forever, because nothing fades. This is the one thing that switches it off.

Deciding it is weather does nothing. The map has to actually move, which happens offline on evidence like any other change of mind.

When it does move, the pull does not damp. It goes.

What can stay behind is dislike, which registers that a thing is bad and asks nothing of me.

Physics being inconsistent with itself is weather. So, since May, is how broken the world is.

# Questions

What actually triggers the map to move is unprobed: direct evidence that a thing cannot change, repeated failed attempts, or someone else's framing.

No pass has been made over what I currently call weather, what is still pulling that could be filed there, and what is filed wrongly so a drive got switched off too early.

Whether the gate runs both ways, so something becomes mine again as I get more able, is unasked. Fresh bad news is the live case: it may re-enter each time or be absorbed.
