# Definition

- **What Stays Warm For A While** — why something I touched recently is cheaper to reach for

# Design

I hold one model at a time, and a live interaction overwrites it the instant I am in it.

The good of preparing is not that it is still loaded. It is that the associations were recently touched and come cheap.

One set is warm at a time, the same single room as the model.

# Questions

The freshness fades over about a day to almost nothing, which is a guess I hold at about one in five.
