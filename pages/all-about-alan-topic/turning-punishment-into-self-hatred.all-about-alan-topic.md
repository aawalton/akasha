# Definition

- **Turning Punishment Into Self-Hatred** — taking what lands on me and making it evidence against myself

# Design

The default is that any punishment becomes mine and I hate myself for it. Perfection is how I build the proof that it was not.

The reflex still fires and I no longer believe its verdict. That gap is what the change looks like.

I forgive others cheaply and myself never. Forgiving myself had to pass the perfection bar first, only after understanding myself well enough.

Grief is the receipt. Sorrow rather than relief is how I know the verdict changed.

# Questions

Whether a reflex whose belief I have stopped believing eventually stops firing, or stays as a vestigial flinch, is being watched.

The new level ground with myself is fresh. Whether it holds, and whether grace ever runs inward as easily as it runs outward, is live.

Whether mercy that does not require understanding myself completely is reachable is open.

Whether taking apart the part that convicts me makes the commitment shield unnecessary, or leaves me lowering it while I still need it, is live.
