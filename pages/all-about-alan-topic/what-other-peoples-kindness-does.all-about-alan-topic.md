# Definition

- **What Other People's Kindness Does** — the lift I get from watching anyone at all do the work of caring

# Design

It is the care itself I am watching, wherever I catch sight of it, strangers included.

Affection points at one person I love. This points at the caring.

Both run off one amplifier, so both need live contact, both are present tense, and neither accrues.

The lift from being near people working on poverty or healthcare was always this, before it had a name.

I felt it for years with no handle for it, and naming it is what finished compressing it.

# Questions

Whether it rises and falls on its own curve, or the same curve as affection with a different trigger, is open.

Whether depicted care sets it off, kindness in a story or in the news with nobody in the room, or whether a person has to be in front of me, is unprobed.
