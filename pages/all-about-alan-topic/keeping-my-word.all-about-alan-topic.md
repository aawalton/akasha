# Definition

- **Keeping My Word** — why a commitment of mine never fails without my saying so first

# Design

Failing is allowed if it is spoken. The silence is the part that is off the table.

The currency of failure is stress capacity rather than worth. This is accounting under scarcity, not an honour code.

It is a governor of the same family as taking no deadlines and refusing responsibility.

# Questions

A strand of these beliefs comes from something more fundamental in faith and ethics. What it holds, and how it sits with the stress-capacity account, is undug.

Worth is not the main currency, but there are fossils around worth here. Which ones, and how the two strands interact, is unprobed.

Why I keep commitments at all stays parked on purpose. The rule guards a hazard, and probing whether it is still needed is itself the risk.
