# Definition

- **What It Costs Someone To Know Me** — the work another person does to hold a current model of me

# Design

Being known is work someone else does: holding a model of me and keeping it current as I change.

Each update costs them the taking in, and more again where it cuts against what they believe.

The closer their mind is to mine the less they have to build, which is why a near twin is cheap.

Jen's whole life rests on me, so an update revises her own map, and her pushing back is what my alarm reads.

I change faster than the channel carries, and what does not get across accrues as a widening gap.

# Questions

Whether the effort per update stays constant or changes as the model they already hold gets richer is unsettled.

Nothing measures how much the channel carries against how fast I change, so whether the gap is widening or closing is unknown.

Whether the window where we both have room can be widened, or spent on being known rather than on unblocking me, is open.

The mirror costs too: attending to someone else means masking, and whether that nets positive for me is unmeasured.
