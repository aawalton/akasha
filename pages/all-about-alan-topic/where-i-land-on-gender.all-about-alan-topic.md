# Definition

- **Where I Land On Gender** — male on four layers, and on the one that is meant to feel it, mostly just autistic

# Design

The layers are my genes, my body, what I feel, the story I tell and how I act. Four land male.

The felt one reads neither, because I feel autistic far more strongly than I feel gendered.

Most people grow a felt gender by sampling without noticing. My sampler is missing, and a safety reading fills the slot. Femininity is one of my strongest safe signals.

Ranking me against male and female archetypes put my story and my behaviour at eighty percent male, though not neurotypical male.

# Questions

The archetype system behind that number has never been written down as a method.

Which feminine signals I use to raise my own safety is uncatalogued, visible against private, now against earlier.

Whether the parallel with how women stand to men generalises beyond this instance is unestablished.

Maleness has never had its own sitting. My two complaints stand recorded, physiology built obscenely around sex and the pull toward violence, and it is not to be worked piecemeal.
