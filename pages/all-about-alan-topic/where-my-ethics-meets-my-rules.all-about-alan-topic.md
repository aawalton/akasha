# Definition

- **Where My Ethics Meets My Rules** — the seams between the framework and the bright lines I actually run on

# Design

The framework prices harm to me and weighs it. My own rule forbids it outright.

Both my ethics and my sense of self landed on identity as closure over a branching structure, arrived at separately.

# Questions

Whether the bar against harming myself is the right projection of a tradeable calculus for a mind without perfect knowledge, or a place the two come apart, stays open.

Whether the equal-worth guarantee supplies the foundation for self-worth I have never built, or only moves the gap, is open.

Which of the two identity readings came first, and whether they are one insight in two hats, is untraced.

The split between rules installed by trauma and rules I chose is leaky and unendorsed.
