# Definition

- **The Best Three Years** — college at sixteen, run at maximum autonomy, and why I rate it highest

# Design

What made them best was that everything was chosen, difficulty included, rather than anything achieved.

Sixteen to eighteen credits, dance classes, a job, and hours a day walking.

Talking to strangers was effortful and affordable. High capacity and high safety kept the multiplier low.

I asked ten new people a day for the hardest course they had taken, triangulated, and took all of them.

A's in the hard courses and B's in the easy ones, because the hard ones handed me attention for free.

# Questions

Whether the strangers meant the same thing by hardest, of workload, abstraction or grading, and whether aggregating washed that out, is unprobed.

What taking all the hard courses cost and taught, apart from the grades, is uncaptured.

Whether the dance classes there are the start of the body-control arc is unconnected.

What dropped the autonomy afterwards, and whether the freedom I value now rebuilds it, is unasked.

How autonomy works as a driver alongside not-wanting is unworked.
