# Definition

- **Getting Back To Making With My Hands** — the ways back into making things by hand, and why none of them has any data behind it

# Design

The gate has correctly kept me out, so there is no attempt to learn from.

# Questions

What is mine is that the maker is less buried than before and some edges are surfacing. Whether the image work, the storytelling and the souls count as maker channels is unconfirmed.

Which doorway costs least — drawing, Lego, quilting — cannot be worked out from outside. Only going in and looking would produce anything, and nobody has.
