# Definition

- **Why People Read As Unsafe** — the childhood setting that makes people dangerous before any evidence arrives

# Design

The deepest thing my body learned as a child is that people are not safe. A setting rather than a conclusion.

My behaviour did not read to my parents, undiagnosed themselves, and correction came as pain. No cruelty in it.

Punishment I could not see coming had nothing to attach to, so it attached to people as a whole.

Under it sits an earlier verdict poured by absence: my needs are not important. The same warm hands.

It was learned, so it can move, but it sits below where argument reaches.

# Questions

Which of my social systems read off this one prior and which have roots of their own is unmapped: the loneliness, the rule about never initiating, the perfectionism.

Whether the needs verdict actually underlies people-are-not-safe, or only came before it by a different route, is unframed.

I have catastrophically misread social situations and the episodes are uncatalogued, so the account has no worked case under it.
