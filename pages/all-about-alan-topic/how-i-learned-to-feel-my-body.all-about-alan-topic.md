# Definition

- **How I Learned To Feel My Body** — building by hand the sense of where my body is that other people get for free

# Design

Before dancing, my sense of where my body was ran weakly or not at all.

The words came first: frame, axis, weight transfer, posture, head carriage. Each was a handle to check myself against while the pattern was still being built.

Then sheer volume, until it encoded and the checking fell away by itself.

It carried off the dance floor. I caught myself moving like I was dancing while washing dishes.

Nothing bound it to a dance floor, which is the same gap that made it expensive to build.

# Questions

How many hours or sessions the arc actually took has never been counted, and the count would anchor the whole claim about how much a skill costs me.

Which other motor skills were built the same slow way has never been listed. Driving is one.

Whether the words come back under load, when I am tired or unsafe, would say whether there is a fallback worth naming.

Where the carrying stops is unprobed. Fine handwork and sport-specific patterns probably get nothing from it.
