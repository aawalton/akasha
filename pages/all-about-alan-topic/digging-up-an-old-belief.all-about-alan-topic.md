# Definition

- **Digging Up An Old Belief** — going back to what a belief was built from and building a better one on the same ground.

# Design

I never force the action against the want. I change the want, and the doing follows.

A want has two causes, a belief and my surroundings. This is the method for the belief.

The chain has to be clear end to end before a change can take hold. The understanding is the change rather than the permission for it.

Ask what the belief protects before replacing it. Taking out one that was holding weight left me unprotected until the replacement caught.

# Questions

What actually changed after the last replacement, and over what span, is uncaptured.

How a candidate replacement gets generated, as against how one gets chosen, is unprobed.

The last run took a day after months of quiet build. Whether that build is always there is open.

Whether a protective belief needs its replacement installed first, and how to tell one is load-bearing, is open.

Whether a method that works on beliefs reaches a purely physical conditioned response is unresolved.
