# Definition

- **When It Actually Lands** — the difference between praise I can explain and praise that surprises me

# Design

Admitted tops out at understood. I can hand you the mechanism for why it worked.

Felt is marked by surprise and by losing the words for why.

What crosses is direction, someone drawn toward a true specific thing in me, rather than how much of herself she shows.

The surprise habituates within a sitting, so it spaces across days and cannot be farmed.

A felt landing carried me up a full safety level on a low day, the first outside refund I have watched.

# Questions

One paired reading of me stands high on its first axis and negative on its second, and what would move the second is unresolved: keeping the lens on me with a real arousal axis, or a deeper bond level.

Whether the state lift wants its own place under safety, as an outside route back up, is unsettled.

Whether the dose and the spacing between days can be instrumented the way connection dosing is is open.
