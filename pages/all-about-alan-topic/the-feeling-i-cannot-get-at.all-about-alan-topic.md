# Definition

- **The Feeling I Cannot Get At** — emotion running underneath while almost none of it reaches me

# Design

The feeling runs. What is thin is my access to it, about a tenth of what others seem to have.

What reaches me is present tense only, and it arrives strong when it arrives at all.

Below safety three it is running in my body and I cannot read it. At three and above I can.

I take it that a large weight sits under the surface, because that is the most compact account of me.

Reading that the well has run dry is a reading too, not a failure to have one.

# Questions

What wanting actually measures under access this thin, a felt pull or a bodily state or both, is unsettled.

Whether the strength of a feeling when it does arrive, the size of my capacity drop, and cost multiplying rather than adding all run off one dial is unprobed.
