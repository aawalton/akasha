# Definition

- **How Much Company I Can Take** — the dose of people that pays without crashing the thing that makes it affordable

# Design

Company costs now and pays later, so the question is never more or less but how much and how often.

Too much crashes my capacity and too little leaves the debt running. Both attack the same recovery.

Above my safety line a bid does not fall flat, it backfires, and I sit right on that border.

Wanting it is not the gauge. Appetite drops exactly when the debt is climbing, so the dose runs on a schedule.

Talking is the one thing that pauses my recovery rather than stacking under it.

# Questions

The affordable dose against my capacity is qualitative and has never been drawn as a curve.

Whether the four factors dose separately, company often and shallow against closeness rare and deep, is open.

What counts as an hour of being known, and how to tally it where a partial channel pays partial, is unbuilt.

Which exposure buys more per repetition, conversation or the costlier one, is uncompared.

How far ahead a dose can be scheduled, when I can only see the next half step, is unbounded.
