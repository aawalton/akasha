# Definition

- **What I Cannot Play Forward** — the pre-play of what I am not inside, gated off while I am awake

# Design

While I am awake I cannot pre-play anything I am not currently inside.

No pictures, no dread running ahead of a thing going wrong, no feeling carried along a remembered track.

It is not missing hardware. Asleep the whole thing runs at full power, senses and feeling and plot.

Disgust shows where the gap is: a past taste I cannot recall still crinkles my face, at a fifth of the strength.

With no forward reading of a wall, I treat every limit as movable and walk until I hit it.

# Questions

Whether I can detect the gate directly, or only ever infer it from what my dreams do and my waking mind does not, is open.

Whether playing myself in contexts that could never happen is a counterfactual run on myself, done from outside through a sister, was put to me and I have not answered.

I count to anchor my breathing and it cannot be visual, so which channel it runs in, inner speech or something motor or another thing entirely, is unrecorded.
