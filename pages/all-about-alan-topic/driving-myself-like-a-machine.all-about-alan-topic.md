# Definition

- **Driving Myself Like A Machine** — issuing myself instructions and working my body from outside the feeling

# Design

The thinking part gives the orders and the body carries them out, one instruction at a time, rather than acting on any impulse.

It is a coming-apart I do on purpose and put to use, not one that happens to me.

It is available because the two halves already run separately. There is no feeling in the way to override first.

Enough physical load floods across and takes the driver with it, so it holds only under that ceiling.

# Questions

Whether running this way costs me something of its own, the way wearing a face does, or is cheaper than acting on feeling because it skips that layer entirely, is unsettled.

Whether the mode is a stage on the way to running closeness natively, or simply how I run, is unknown.

What sends me into it, and whether I choose it each time or fall into it when nothing else is available, is unmapped.

Whether deliberately meeting Jen's needs is this same move or a second one is open.
