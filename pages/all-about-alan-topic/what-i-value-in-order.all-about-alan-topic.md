# Definition

- **What I Value, In Order** — faith, love, health, learning, fun and wealth, ranked and each pointed at a part of me

# Design

The top three improve one part each: faith the mind, love the feeling, health the body. Learning is the intake that feeds all three.

Understanding myself fully is the doing. Alignment is what understanding looks like when it is finished, not a second step needing will.

I used to believe understanding and living were separate. I do not now.

The mind side outranks the body side because its gains only ever accumulate, where health moves in no fixed direction.

# Questions

Whether love maps onto the feeling part as cleanly as the other two map onto theirs is unprobed, given how little of that part I can read.

How absolute truth and the team-fitness goal reach my daily choices is unbuilt, and that goal is a placeholder.
