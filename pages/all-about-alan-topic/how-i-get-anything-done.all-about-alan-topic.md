# Definition

- **How I Get Anything Done** — the parts of running myself that do not work on their own, and what I built instead.

# Design

Most of it is compensated well enough that the gap is invisible from outside and half invisible to me.

Interest decides whether starting and holding on are effortless or nearly impossible.

Changing course is cheap, because nothing in me pulls back toward the plan I just left.

# Questions

What else raises it has never been listed out: medication, how I arrange my surroundings, moving memory outside my head.

Whether having no pull back toward the past is what makes both pivoting cheap and letting an impulse pass easy is a chain nobody has checked.

Executive function is hit twice, autism on top of ADHD. What autism's half looks like alone has never been separated out.

The budget framing was drawn from some of my executive functions and never tested against the rest.
