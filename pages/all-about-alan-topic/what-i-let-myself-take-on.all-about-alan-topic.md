# Definition

- **What I Let Myself Take On** — what I will and will not agree to do.

# Design

Below my safety line a thing is free, at it bearable, above it off the table.

# Questions

Jen's state factors into whether an initiate decision actually runs. Does the rule need joint affordability rather than my own state alone? And when interest fires for something over budget, does the rule override the interest, or the interest the rule?

The one-sigma low is the bar for anything recurring, but how I actually estimate it for a given commitment is unprobed.

Whether interest adds to what my executive function can spend, bypasses that budget, or is spent out of it, is unworked.
