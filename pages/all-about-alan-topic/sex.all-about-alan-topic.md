# Definition

- **Sex** — the one place my body shouts, and the three things tangled up in the shout.

# Design

Everywhere else my body is a whisper. Here it turns all the way up.

Three things fire at once in it: what I believe, my marriage, and plain bodily need.

The pornography and the masturbation are regulation rather than desire, which is why nothing ever made them stop.

Partnered sex gives me about a tenth of what it gives most people, because the anticipating and the savouring are both missing.

This opens further only from enough safety, and how deep it goes is mine to set.

# Questions

The headset and the game on it are parked, and the behaviour is recorded only by what it costs.

Whether my faith actually warrants the zero-tolerance rule is a check I decided on and have not run, and the chastity theology under it is barely dug.

Most people seem to attach to the act itself, apart from a partner. I do not, and that is held for its own pass.

Of the three things tangled in it, the marriage layer and the plain need are worked and the belief layer is thin.
