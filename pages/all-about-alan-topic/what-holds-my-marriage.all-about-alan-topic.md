# Definition

- **What Holds My Marriage** — commitment rather than attachment, and what standing on that costs both of us

# Design

I do not think I can form attachment. It is built on stored warmth and imagining forward, neither of which I have.

Commitment went in deep as protection against a world that turns on me for reasons I cannot read.

Perfection is the unbreakable standard and commitment the unbreakable bond, from one fire.

I committed by following the good-person script, not by choosing a fixed point. I had not met myself.

From outside it is the whole signature of attachment, and I cannot see it from in here.

# Questions

Whether attachment is truly absent, or only unreadable from inside, decides whether the room I leave her covers a lie or a truth I cannot perceive.

Whether keeping it today is the old script running on momentum or the first genuinely chosen keeping is open, now that it is a choice.

That some real emotional caring runs underneath for her is a hypothesis I hold at thirty percent, with no tell to read it by.

Why I weight commitment as I do has never had a pass of its own.
