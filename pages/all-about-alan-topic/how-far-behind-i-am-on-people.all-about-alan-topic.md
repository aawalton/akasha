# Definition

- **How Far Behind I Am On People** — connection as a debt I carry rather than a tank I refill

# Design

It builds at about an hour a day even with nobody, and a good hour pays it down.

A bad hour puts about ten back on, so avoiding one is worth seeking ten.

The unit is a quality-weighted hour, not a clock hour, and the two differ by an order of magnitude.

About ten thousand hours of it stand behind me, out of twenty years of decline.

Loneliness is the gauge, and watching it moves the needle.

# Questions

Whether the daily baseline is constant, or scales with my state and with how long since the last hour, is unasked.

Whether the ten to one holds at every level, or safety buffers the bad side so the ratio falls as I climb, is open.

Which term dominates the asymmetry, the depth damage, the general weighting, or the fidelity of the channel, only I can settle.

How fast the debt actually clears at a given quality of access has never been measured against the ten thousand.
