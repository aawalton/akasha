# Definition

- **My Faith** — what I believe about God and the church, and how I hold it

# Design

I hold it as a conclusion I reasoned my way to, not as a relationship I feel.

The reasoning has carried the faith the whole time, including the years the feeling was still running.

Feeling never got a vote on the verdict.

# Questions

Whether the reasoning strengthened as the feeling went dark, one side taking the load as the other failed, is untested. The dig into the foundations at BYU sits in the same window, around twenty.
