# Definition

- **Why Feeling Cannot Take My Thinking** — the missing wire between feeling and reasoning, and what it makes robust

# Design

Feeling and thinking reach each other only by my watching. There is no direct wire between them.

A flood lands in my body, never in the thinking. Even at my lowest I was still reasoning, on brutal inputs.

So my own inside is something I read from outside, the way I read anything else in the room.

The engine does not break. When I get a thing wrong it is the inputs that were bad.

Feeling cannot seize it, but enough physical load can swamp it. It never went out while I was conscious.

# Questions

Where the inputs actually start to swamp, and whether I get any read of coming up on that edge before consciousness goes, is open.

Whether my empty-and-listen posture is actively subtracting candidates or is simply my default with nothing there to subtract is unsettled.

Whether that line is the same one as bearable-to-unbearable, or the physical floor where I break, or its own boundary, is unplaced, and whether the crossing runs only one way is open.
