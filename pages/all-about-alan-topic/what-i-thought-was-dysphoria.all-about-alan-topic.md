# Definition

- **What I Thought Was Dysphoria** — the years I read a bad measurement as being female underneath

# Design

The male traits that fitted me were quiet and the ones that did not were loud. The female traits ran the other way.

Aggregate that and the channel reports female-leaning and male-averse, which is what read as dysphoria.

Correct for which signals were loud and the female reading disappears. What is left is a preference about safety.

The pull toward feminine things was never wanting to be female. It was my nervous system reaching for something that made it safer.

# Questions

Whether the whole thing sits differently now that I have the layers and the measurement error, rather than a condition I have, is unasked. The question is about fit, not about how much it hurts.
