# Definition

- **What I Take In** — reading wide on purpose, because a false model only looks tidy on a narrow set

# Design

I read authors rather than books. A whole body of work is a whole worldview, and I merge it into my one model wherever I judge it true.

I am working through the entire Great Courses library, about three thousand hours of ten thousand done.

Breadth is a defence rather than a taste. Data from outside a theory is what forces its patches into the open.

It began as loving to read, homeschooled in fourth grade, long before anything rested on it.

# Questions

What a whole body of work gives me that the best single book does not — coverage, coherence, or seeing it applied across fields — is unprobed.

Whether the sweep runs in a deliberate order, and how the remaining seven thousand hours get ordered, is unwritten.

Whether papers, conversations and practice run on the same breadth logic is unmapped.

I took the hardest courses on purpose, and whether that paid against the burnout has never been totalled.
