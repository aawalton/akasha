# Definition

- **The Colours I Read Myself In** — the coarse colour scale I read a resource on before it has numbers.

# Design

Green is where I have been living, yellow is caution, red is alarm and black is crisis.

# Questions

Is the sigma computed over a long baseline or a rolling recent window? That decides whether the alarm bands hold still while a slide runs, or drift down with it.

Stamina's stoplight reads as direction, wanting to move against wanting to be still, where mana's reads as amount. Is that asymmetry real, or does it dissolve once either gets past stoplight?
