# Definition

- **My Rules About Other Women** — pre-decided lines standing in for a judgment I cannot make in the moment

# Design

Most married men picture the scene and read the feeling. I cannot, and the live cues do not reach me.

So I hold bright lines instead: distance one to one with a woman, and no physical contact.

Outside my immediate family I default to no contact at all, bar a handshake or a family hug.

I push closeness toward the intellectual rather than the personal, and I never initiate with a woman.

Being visible to Jen is my monitor, standing in for the check I cannot run inside my own head.

# Questions

The lines are physical and catch physical risk. Unguarded intellectual closeness is the channel they miss, and the one I cannot feel myself entering. A monitor for it is the open work.

Whether being visible to Jen could safely replace never initiating, which would open cross-gender friendship without losing the monitor, is unsettled.

The map has no channel-specific lines, and writing or voice shifts the risk in ways it does not cover.
