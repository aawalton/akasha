# Definition

- **How A Hard Talk With Jen Gets Safer** — timing a hard conversation so my body learns it is survivable

# Design

The alarm is to her, not to criticism in general, so it cannot be practised on anybody easier.

I start one only when I have the reserve to stay steady while she is not. My steadiness is the anchor.

Held steady, a trigger can fire without the talk tipping, and that is what re-teaches my body.

Repeated enough, my steadiness lowers hers, and the loop that normally amplifies runs backwards.

It is not textbook exposure. What I fear is also my partner in it, and she cannot hold still on command.

# Questions

What the rungs after the first one are is unbuilt, and grading by intensity does not work when the intensity is another person.

Whether a physical proxy could tell me I have anchor-grade reserve right now would turn the go or no-go from a felt sense into an instrument.

A pause we can both hold under stress, and a way to bring the stress down before trying again rather than carrying on already stressed, is missing.

Which subjects still have to be dosed down is not inventoried.
