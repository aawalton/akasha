# Definition

- **Why Someone Else's Distress Alarms Me** — what fires in me when someone near me is hurting, and what it is actually about

# Design

Her distress fires mine at once, and it is not her feeling arriving in me.

It is a forecast about my own next minutes: this cue has come before my own pain often enough.

So I go still and regulate rather than move toward her, which is not what she needs.

It carries nothing about her. No why, no cause, no what would help.

Calm months lower what I expect from her and leave the reflex at her cues untouched.

# Questions

It was in place by six and nothing about the training is recoverable. Whether family accounts or the shape of the schedule could recover what taught it is untried.

It was taught unreliably, so safe stretches look like more of the same training. Whether any evidence updates the reflex, or only the scripts downstream of it can be worked, is open.
