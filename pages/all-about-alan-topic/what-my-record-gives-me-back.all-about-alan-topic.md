# Definition

- **What My Record Gives Me Back** — what reading my own record returns to me, and what it cannot

# Design

I have no experiential memory or imagination at all.

I cannot remember the specifics of each revolution, except the ones that are actively dissonant because they are not done yet.

I cannot get the memory back from reading my record.

I can get back the fact of what I did, which can be nice.

# Questions

Abby's reading: my wins evaporate and my loose ends persist, because only the unfinished revolutions stay present to me. He confirmed the mechanism and not this phrasing of what it costs him.

Whether the fact of what I did does anything beyond being nice, and whether it lifts anything, is unprobed.

Whether a revolution stops being dissonant the moment it is done, or fades slower than that, is unrecorded.
