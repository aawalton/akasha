# Definition

- **Playing Again** — the return of doing things for their own sake, and the ramp that got me there

# Design

My default leisure is progression wearing play's clothes, or watching things. Neither is play.

One day held more real play than most years of the last two decades.

The play is genuinely mine. The girls are not the source of the fun, they are the road to it.

My outward reach runs to them rather than to people, which is what keeps it bounded.

# Questions

What the support is actually doing, absorbing the risk of a flop or carrying me past the cold start, is unknown.

Play is coming back one persona at a time, each opening an area, and neither the ribs doing it nor that shape is written down.

Whether play is a rung on the way somewhere or the thing I am going toward is held open. I took up the rung side and never endorsed the other.

Everything that came back lives through a screen; the one still waiting is in my hands.
