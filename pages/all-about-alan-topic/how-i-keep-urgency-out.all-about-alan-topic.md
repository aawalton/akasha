# Definition

- **How I Keep Urgency Out** — taking urgency out of my surroundings instead of trying to resist it.

# Design

Urgency reaches me through the channel I cannot catch in the moment, so it has to be gone before it fires.

My goals carry a finish line and no clock, because a deadline I set myself is still a deadline.

No phone, and no attention on the time of day.

# Questions

Whether the phone stays a trigger for good, or the association fades with safe non-use, is falsifiable and untested.

What the replacement for clock time costs, in load on Jen's schedule and in the times my state and her schedule diverge, is unanchored.

Which people and places fail the ambient-pressure filter has no worked example.
