# Definition

- **How Much Attention I Have** — what is left to spend after the room I am in has taken its share.

# Design

Some of it goes on processing whatever is around me, whether I am using it or not.

That cost stays about the same while the total swings, so it can be a rounding error or the whole budget.

Too much and too little stimulation are one fixed cost read against a budget that moved.

# Questions

The draw has never been measured against a known total, so the proportion is a felt read rather than a number.

Whether it is genuinely fixed, or costs more in a louder room, is open.

And whether several streams at once draw on one pool or on different ones is unsettled.

What a game specifically supplies to spare attention, and whether something else would serve as well, is unidentified.
