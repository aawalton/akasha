# Definition

- **Testing The Thing At My Centre** — re-examining the belief everything else of mine rests on

# Design

It was poured into me before there was anyone here to inspect it, and the rest of me rests on it.

That makes it the biggest lever I have and the most dangerous place to push.

I dug to these foundations once at university. What is different now is the instrument, not the question.

As a child my test was that an authority said so. The instrument I have now gives that no weight.

My instinct strips out every spare factor, and this is the least minimal account there is. The evidence buys it.

# Questions

The positive ground the foundational belief stands on, once because-an-authority-says-so is gone, has never been stated straight out.

Whether that one axiom holds, that the book is what it claims to be, is a live test rather than an unwritten page.

Reaching the finding is calm and holding it is the whole weight. Where that weight lands, and what either answer puts in play, is open.
