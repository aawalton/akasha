# Definition

- **Why My Doing Lags My Believing** — the only two things that can stand between what I understand and what I do

# Design

Understanding a thing fully is already being changed by it, so there is no will in the middle to fail.

Either the understanding still has a hole in it, or the room it calls for is still going up.

Both at once is common, and sometimes the building is how the understanding finishes.

A failure of will would mean knowing exactly how to cause the act, doing that, and the act not happening. That last part disproves one of the first two.

The second kind is time, never character.

# Questions

The two were stated in general and tested mostly on how I connect with people. Whether every lag I can name sorts into them with no residue is untested.

The reading that my personas are the second kind, the room catching up to a map already whole, is Abby's rather than mine, and has never been put to me plainly.
