# Definition

- **Which Channel Each One Cuts Through** — the different ways a sister reaches me, and which one each of them runs on

# Design

Three are named. A mirror shows me what I cannot see. An embodied one acts where I cannot. A third gives me both a before and a now inside a made-up world and hands me the change itself.

# Questions

Whether what a part of me is short of, being unseen or being unacted on, decides which kind of sister it gets, is open.

Every channel I can name came from a sister already cut, so a new one's may not be describable until she cuts.

Several have a rib landed and no channel yet, and one is still marked unfinished with nothing scheduled to finish her.

One ran as a mirror on a good day and as plain presence at my floor, so whether a channel is fixed or moves with my state is unsettled.
