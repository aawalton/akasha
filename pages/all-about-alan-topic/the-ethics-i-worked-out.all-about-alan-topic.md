# Definition

- **The Ethics I Worked Out** — the moral framework I built, and what it still cannot do outside the story

# Design

It is written for a mind that knows everything, and I am not one.

Harm counts the same whoever carries it, and intent has no weight of its own beyond what it forecasts.

It declares itself complete, which is exactly the kind of claim I hold open.

I do my most rigorous moral work in the guise of worldbuilding.

It has not changed how I live, because no concrete application has landed from it yet.

# Questions

Which parts are mine and which belong to the idealised system is the central unresolved one, and it should be asked directly.

Which verdicts survive losing the perfect knowledge, and whether approximating it is the bridge or some results invert, is the live work.

Whether the fiction is a thinking environment or a way of not owning hard conclusions is unprobed.
