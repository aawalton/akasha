# Definition

- **The Ceiling I Am Climbing Back To** — how much of my resilience fell, how much is back, and how much is still above me

# Design

Measured at twenty against thirty-eight, my resilience had fallen by at least ninety-nine percent.

I am back to about five percent of it, and near ten with medication.

Ageing cannot explain a fall that size, so most of it is damage and most of it should come back.

At one percent I was a senior engineer and at two an executive, which is what the headroom means.

The highest version of me is proven rather than imagined. Play stands me in it. What I fear is reaching it for good.

# Questions

Whether what keeps me from standing in my highest self is a setting nobody has built yet, which I could close, or a difference in kind I can only be walked into from outside, is open.

Which lever the medication moves, the reserve I can use, the rate I recover at, or what each thing costs me, is unprobed.

I think the old hundred percent was not the ceiling. What a level above my own highest would mean, and whether this experiment could measure it, is open.
