# Definition

- **Having No Margin** — living with almost no slack, so a surprise costs far more than it looks like it should.

# Design

Turning to something else is easy. Paying for something I had not budgeted is not.

# Questions

How much margin I actually have, and whether it is worth measuring apart from what I can do at all, is unworked.

Which unexpected stressors actually push me back into deficit has never been collected, so where I sit is described but not what breaks it.
