# Definition

- **Whether I Stay** — the stay-or-leave question, worked as a ledger because I cannot feel my way to it

# Design

Every input has to be observable or derivable now. With no simulator, a forecast of how I would feel is not admissible.

My own payoff cannot ground staying. The marriage is a net cost to me and the exit is worked and survivable.

Commitment binds how I would leave rather than whether. Honesty and integrity both point outward.

On immediate grounds it is indeterminate, both doors carrying the same circle, which matters only because it is a near tie.

# Questions

The equilibrium this rests on was days old when it was written down and I called it provisional. Whether it holds, or the door re-opens as recovery lowers what trying costs me, is unsettled.

Just utility with no weighting of myself against anyone else sits against how the ethics canon frames partiality. Whether those are different levels or a real refinement is unresolved.
