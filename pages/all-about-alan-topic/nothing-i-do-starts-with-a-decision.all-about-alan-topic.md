# Definition

- **Nothing I Do Starts With A Decision** — acting on what fires rather than on what I resolve to do

# Design

Everything I do is fired by something. Nothing is begun by deciding to.

Right and true are one word to me, so once a thing sits in my map as right the act runs. No gap to cross.

What matters is loud, and whatever is bottlenecking me is the loudest thing in the field.

Cheap change is on the way in: I rewrite what reaches me rather than how I answer it.

Expensive change is pain grinding the response down over many repetitions. Nothing teaches me like pain.

# Questions

Whether anything is ever started by positive pull rather than discomfort is unresolved. Interest may only be a pull left unfilled.

A drift that costs me no pain never escalates, so a signal has to be scheduled and nothing says how often.

Which responses are worth retraining, and which get routed around on the way in for good, is unprobed.

When something fires a project to change my own triggers, what decides between rewriting the causes and grinding the response is untraced.
