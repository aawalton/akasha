# Definition

- **Building A People Skill By Hand** — understanding a social skill well enough to run it as a procedure, then drilling it under thought

# Design

The loop is explicit: read the signals, infer the state, choose a response, drive my face and body, read the result.

It cannot run live. A conversation fills my one slot and I have no monitor in the moment.

So it is overlearned until it runs without the slot. Thousands of repetitions are the price of that move.

First contact proves the method: years of it, and the scripts beat most people's.

They do not carry across. Closeness is four skills, each harder and each needing its own build.

# Questions

Which of the four are furthest along has never been ranked, and ranking them would turn unsolved into a progress map.

Whether enough drilling ever makes one feel native, or whether it stays a thing I run by hand forever, is open.

Whether the four share enough structure that building one speeds up the next is unprobed.

I cannot fork a pretend self off the one map I am living. Where else that closes a method, beyond rehearsing a conversation, has never been catalogued.
