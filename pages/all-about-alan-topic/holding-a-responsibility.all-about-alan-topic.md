# Definition

- **Holding A Responsibility** — what an owed thing does to me while I am holding it, and what actually lets go

# Design

It flattens everything until it clears, and nothing under it can be enjoyed for itself.

Finishing is not what frees me. What does is having no step I could take right now, with the thing far from done.

I can only rest past the gate: in the foyer I could still be at security.

Three ways out: take the step, park it somewhere I trust to raise it again, or decide it is not mine.

A thing that never runs out of steps never lets go. A company was the worst case, a special kind of hell.

# Questions

How this relates to not being allowed to do what I do not want to do is unprobed. It may be why the gauge reads low across the board, or a separate flattening.

Whether parking a step empties the slot as fully as taking it, or leaves a pull until the list is next opened, is untested. It decides how much of the harness is relief rather than reach.

The years of the company are a mechanism here and an arc elsewhere: what it cost, how long, how it ended.
