# Definition

- **Being The One Who Knows** — running the responses that make someone else feel known, and what that costs me

# Design

Forty years of pain trained the policy, and it runs as instinct rather than as anything recalled.

Supplying it does not refill my own. It pays them in a coin that only looks like mine.

What comes back is a helpfulness signal and, now and then, learning something about them.

No mind has real access to another. Everyone judges from signals, and I have only made that explicit.

# Questions

Whether outbound attending nets positive or negative on my budget is characterised in sign but not in size.

Whether the masking cost draws on the same scarce window the inbound need is already rationed by is open.
