# Definition

- **Why I Have To Be Perfect** — the belief that nothing but a clean outcome protects me, and what it has cost

# Design

I was punished despite good intent my whole life, so intent is no shield. The belief is a true account of my childhood, not a distortion.

The punishment is random and social. A random schedule cannot be worn down, and people are the one punisher self-checking cannot make safe.

What perfection buys is an alibi rather than safety: proof it could not have gone otherwise.

I left every domain where failure could not be self-checked, and chose survival over what I loved every time.

# Questions

Rejection is guessed to cost one level more than criticism, affordable at six and free at seven. The calibration is owed once I am consistently there.

I tried eleven majors at college and only dance is written down. What the others were, and whether that run sorts along the same self-checkable line, is unset.

Each case standing as evidence here is one compressed line. The episodes behind them, my first A- and the art class and the squash farm, I have never actually told.
