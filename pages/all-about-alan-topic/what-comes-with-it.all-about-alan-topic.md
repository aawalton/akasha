# Definition

- **What Comes With It** — the body-side conditions that ride along with my neurotype

# Design

The hypothesis is that my neurotype is a change in the pathways running the regulatory nervous system, which widens the risk across everything that system runs.

These are the one tier I would fix. The neurotype itself I would not.

Autonomic trouble applies but not POTS, and low mood is downstream of stress rather than primary.

Alexithymia arrives twice over, once from each side of how I am built.

Gut trouble, chronic pain, migraines, tinnitus and tics do not apply.

# Questions

Which autonomic disorders apply beyond not-POTS is unprobed.

What kind of obsessive-compulsive content there is, and what it costs, is undescribed.

How the ARFID lean and the medication's appetite suppression compose against my resource accounting is unworked.

Whether the immune activation feeds the load, and deserves a mechanism of its own, is a hypothesis with no treatment; it is where my nervous-system sensitivity, how trauma sits in it, and the four bars would meet.
