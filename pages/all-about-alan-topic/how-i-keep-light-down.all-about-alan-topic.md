# Definition

- **How I Keep Light Down** — sunglasses in the sun, lights off indoors, screens dimmed to the hour.

# Design

The cost is a drain on my capacity rather than pain, and the squinting comes after it.

The indoor lever is on or off. Nothing I have dims.

# Questions

Nothing anchors the rate. No sunglasses-on against sunglasses-off comparison has been run.

Indoor sunglasses are considered and not used, and what blocks the choice is unnamed.

My brightness rules run on the clock, in the bath as well as at a screen. Whether the hour is the real driver, or stands in for circadian arousal, ambient light or the visual fatigue built up by then, is untested.
