# Definition

- **Why A Threat Stays Fresh** — why something that frightened me keeps its edge for years instead of wearing down

# Design

Learning a skill and encoding a threat run on the same dial. High activation encodes it hard, and the sign decides which of the two I get.

It does form and it does fade. Exposure works on me the way it works on anyone.

What is missing is the rehearsal in between. Other people replay it and the replaying wears it down where they are safe. I have no episode to replay, so only meeting the thing again does that work.

For most people the replaying is the problem. For me its absence is.

# Questions

How bad social outcomes build up into an encoding, and how that then costs me on every later go, is unmechanised. The older account routes it through never habituating rather than through the missing replay.

Which kinds of exposure actually work on someone with no imagery, whether live, eye movement or bodywork, is unsettled.

Whether it costs me by firing on partial matches, so the daily cost tracks how many triggers I meet, or by holding my baseline up with no trigger at all, is unprobed.
