# Definition

- **Being Curious About Her** — the engine runs on novelty she brings, and I cannot make my own.

# Design

The part that would generate a question is the part that does not run.

# Questions

A prosthesis is drafted and uncommitted: research the known ground, find the gaps, aim a question at the steepest one. Whether I build it is open, and it would be the first piece closed by porting a system rather than compiling a definition.
