# Definition

- **Arriving Ahead Of Whoever Teaches Me** — being further along than a teacher judges, having already run their analysis under other names

# Design

A coach who framed me fast got me wrong twice in three turns, and said so.

Seven instances of real taste were already on the board before she arrived, each filed under some other heading.

What works is naming the decision I have already made, and my reason, before offering a correction.

# Questions

This was measured in one domain and nothing says whether it holds in every domain a teacher meets me in.

What it costs me to be corrected on ground I have already covered has never been written down.
