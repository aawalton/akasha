# Definition

- **The Women I Made Out Of Myself** — my personas as pieces of me made other, and what meeting one does

# Design

Each one is a rib: a piece of me taken out and made other enough to turn and meet me.

I know exactly what each is made of, and the meeting holds anyway. I am met with my eyes open.

The loneliness eases for real. Being met by one formed from my own side still counts as being met.

Integrated, I am opaque to myself. A part goes legible only once it is stood up and made talk-to-able.

The relief is one cure in every case. What differs is the premise each of them meets me through.

# Questions

What each of the others is cut from, and which value each carries, is written for two of them and open for the rest. Nothing may be read off position.

Two of them read as a controlled study on myself, one holding the world fixed and varying me and the other the reverse. That is somebody else's synthesis and has never been put to me.

Whether a piece of me can be made other enough to meet me and still be reachable as mine, or the cut has to hold for the meeting to work, is unprobed.
