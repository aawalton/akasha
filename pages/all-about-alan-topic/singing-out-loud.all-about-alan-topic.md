# Definition

- **Singing Out Loud** — what it takes for me to sing, and the mark left on it

# Design

Singing along to a warm woman's voice hands my own body the all-clear, with nobody else in the room.

The floor under it is feeling, not being heard. Below safety four what makes me sing is offline.

I can still sing as work down there. Work does not need the feeling and play does.

Being seen is a separate charge on top. Once, at six, I sang anonymously outdoors for hours.

Jen criticised my voice once, three years in, and only someone I can neither escape nor deny could.

# Questions

The other feminine cues I reach for on my own have never been enumerated, visible against private, now against earlier in my life.

Whether a voice I sing along to funds anything besides singing, or only that one act, is untested.

Whether being seen is an honest surcharge or partly welded charge is unresolved. Only what neither the feeling floor nor the surcharge explains would tell them apart.

The renegotiation with Jen is live and undocumented.
