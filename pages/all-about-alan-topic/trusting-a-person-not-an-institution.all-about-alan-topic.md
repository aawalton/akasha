# Definition

- **Trusting A Person Not An Institution** — leaning on one practitioner rather than on an institution, and what that trades away

# Design

The local ones I rely on are individual people: my doctor, the children's dentist, the garage.

An institution holds the standard when its people change. A person carries it himself and takes it with him.

# Questions

Nothing tracks their ages, their succession plans or the signs one is about to sell, and no second provider stands behind any of them.

Losing one would move a whole layer of the cascade rather than costing a single dependency, and that has never been priced.

The places we eat sit in the audit as one lump, which the framework refuses everywhere else, so nothing says which are worth cultivating.
