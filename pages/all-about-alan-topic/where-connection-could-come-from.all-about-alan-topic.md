# Definition

- **Where Connection Could Come From** — the whole map of directions, and which of them are actually live

# Design

Every direction is a source, a channel, or a move on the constraint, my capacity or the need itself.

Deepening what I already have costs least: Jen, the children as they grow, family.

Raising the rate beats banking one good hour, wherever any rate-raising move is affordable at all.

Since the point became improving myself, only moves that are my own act are live.

For the expensive kind of being known, voice without a picture beats video, and real time is what brings two models into line.

# Questions

Whether Jen has the capacity and the fit to be my main intellectual partner is the open question on the best-fitting source. Permission is not what is in doubt.

Finding the minority of men who are gentle, sensitive and intellectual is named as a direction and has no concrete method.

Which of the standing plans survive the own-actions-only scope has never been swept, and whether becoming the sort of person people treat differently is a practice of its own is unsettled.
