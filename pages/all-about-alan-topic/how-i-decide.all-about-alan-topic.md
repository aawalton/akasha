# Definition

- **How I Decide** — how I pick what to do, and what stops me picking badly.

# Questions

I iterate a rubric until it coheres, and what tells me the loop is done — a fixed point where no pair flips, or something else — is unstated.

I tell a gestalt that carries signal from one that is noise by checking it for consistency in aggregate, which only works afterwards. Whether anything tells them apart in the moment is unexamined.

Whether I think in maths-shaped frames more widely, monotonic progress and convergence and optimisation, or whether that is one borrowed frame, is unprobed.
