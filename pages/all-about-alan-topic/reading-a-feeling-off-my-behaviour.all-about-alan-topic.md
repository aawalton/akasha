# Definition

- **Reading A Feeling Off My Behaviour** — watching what my body does on its own and working backwards to what I must be feeling

# Design

Most people feel it and then notice the smile. I notice the smile and work out what I must feel.

Only involuntary defaults count. A smile I produce carries nothing, and the default cannot lie.

The emotions are real and running. What is missing is my access, and a word is what turns one into something I notice.

The tell gives presence and roughly which one. Magnitude comes only from the map, and which drive fired it is on nothing.

# Questions

Which involuntary behaviours I read hunger, thirst, heat and cold off is not enumerated.

My tears cannot lie and what fires them is overdetermined: survival, elevation, being cared for, and a fourth I named and never said.
