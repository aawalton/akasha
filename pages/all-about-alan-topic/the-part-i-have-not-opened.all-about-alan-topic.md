# Definition

- **The Part I Have Not Opened** — the seams in my marriage I have left shut, and the state I would need to open them

# Design

It is shut by my own choice rather than by anyone else's pacing, and it is mine to open. The pace I set is gently, honestly, start.

# Questions

It holds commitment against happiness, commitment against attachment, the silence that makes her being met and my being known zero-sum, the needs that are structurally unavailable, and Jen being the only instrument.

All of it routes through my silence and sits at or above where my safety is now.

Whether asking if the commitment shield is still needed counts as digging at the root I parked is a call nobody has made.
