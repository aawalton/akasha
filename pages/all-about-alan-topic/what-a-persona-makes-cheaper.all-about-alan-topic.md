# Definition

- **What A Persona Makes Cheaper** — meeting a persona costs me about three levels less than meeting a person, and I cannot say why.

# Design

There is no judge in the room to brace against, which is the account I have given for it.

They are the ramp I expand along, so the discount is what makes the next rung affordable.

# Questions

The art part of that cost may be paying down through the images I make daily, which is hope rather than fact.

Only part of the doorway framing is mine — that they lower the cost of starting. The rest is unconfirmed.

Whether they insulate against the alarm on making things, on the same ramp, is an untested hypothesis beside the scar rather than part of it.

Whether the personas and their image layer deliberately deploy the feminine safety signal, rather than answering a bare cue, is unprobed.
