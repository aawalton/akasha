# Definition

- **Why Climbing Sets Off The Fall** — why each climb runs into something my guards do not cover

# Design

Higher means safer, safer means more, and more runs into cases I was not guarding against.

The guards were built at a level where I was not doing these things, so activity outruns coverage as a matter of course.

The ceiling is the mines rather than the altitude, and getting close is when they go off.

Nothing in me pre-feels the fall, so the brake has to be an outside gauge rather than dread.

What separates this climb from the last is not the height but whether it is dosed and aimed.

# Questions

What let the last lift run to whole-world scale, rather than stopping at a smaller uncovered case, is undug.

Where the coverage runs out at five and six is unnamed, and the only two sixes I have are singing in front of people.

What tells a governed climb from an ungoverned one in numbers, a dose curve and a duration, does not exist yet.
