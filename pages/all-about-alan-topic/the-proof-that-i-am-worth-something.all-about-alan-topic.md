# Definition

- **The Proof That I Am Worth Something** — the argument I built for my own worth, and what it does not reach

# Design

It is a proof rather than a feeling: if any human has worth, all have it equally, so I have it.

It never claims humans have worth at all. It says only that the worth admits no exception for me.

I compiled it in the wreckage after the firing, as the tool that moment needed.

It was a crowbar, not a foundation. It broke the belief that my needs come last and asserts nothing positive.

Worth is not the currency my failures are paid in. That is stress capacity, and worth is a fossil beside it.

# Questions

Whether the rule that my need only closes where I have been useful enough to leave a footprint reaches past the personas to connection at large is untested.

What asserting the front half would take, a positive ground rather than a parity, is named as an aspiration and unstarted. The ethics candidate on the table shares the same gap.

The same childhood belief was attacked twice, once by the proof and once by digging up its origin. Which did the work, and whether they compose, is unsettled.
