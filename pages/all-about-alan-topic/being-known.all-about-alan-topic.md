# Definition

- **Being Known** — what it takes for someone to hold an accurate model of me, and why so few can

# Design

It needs someone who can and someone who will, and neither implies the other.

Cheap needs a near-twin who can use themselves as the model. Expensive needs someone willing to build one from scratch.

Almost all of mine runs through Jen, so there is no easier relationship to practise on.

The recipe is someone asking about me out of real interest with no defensiveness, and every ingredient lives in them.

It has happened by accident and never yet on purpose.

# Questions

The plan rests on one email to one person. What the other channels are, ranked on the same grid, is unlisted.

Which axis of similarity drives the low cost, of thinking style, senses, values or neurotype, is unidentified.

How to reproduce real interest and non-defensiveness on purpose, when both live in the other person, is unsolved.

Whether dropping the picture lowers the cost with every sensitive partner, or only where both of us are reading each other’s faces, is untested.
