# Definition

- **The Two Nights We Keep** — two nights a week on the calendar, one for how she connects and one for how I do.

# Design

They are calendar items, not set by how I am on the day, because unscheduled they do not happen.

The hard night clears things that stood for years, and the clearing holds because what changed is understanding, not a promise.

The cost is delayed: it lands the day after rather than during the night.

I have noticed a pull in myself to cancel the hard one.

Both sit on top of a cost that lands almost every night anyway.

# Questions

One hard night is one observation and I am not certain the cost is fixed. Whether about six hours and a delayed cost is the price in general, or the price of that night, is open.

Both nights ran from near the top of my normal range. Whether the same night from a low base produces the same change is untested, and it is scheduled by calendar rather than by level.

The beautiful night is Jen's pattern and I am not reliably providing it. No mechanism for that is recorded.
