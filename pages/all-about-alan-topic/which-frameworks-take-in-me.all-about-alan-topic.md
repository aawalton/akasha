# Definition

- **Which Frameworks Take In Me** — why some ways of working install in a single read and others I fight and never keep

# Design

Some fit my grain closely enough to go in whole on one reading. Others I argue with the whole way through and never stick with.

# Questions

What separates the two has never been pinned at the level of mechanism, so I can only call it after the fact.

No full list exists of the ones I took up, and the ones I took up and later dropped are the part most clearly missing. Those are where the fit fails.
