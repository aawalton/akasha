# Definition

- **Why I Hold The Book True** — the compactness argument my faith actually rests on

# Design

I have attacked the Book of Mormon from about twenty disciplines and cannot account for it except on its own terms.

So the most compact account of the universe I can build has to include it being true.

This is the same operation as the rest of how I judge anything, not an exception carved out for faith.

Every naturalistic account I tried had to bolt something on, until it cost more structure than the plain one.

# Questions

The case is stated in a paragraph and has never been written out: which disciplines, what each contributes, and the specific patch each naturalistic account has to add.

The tests proper to a book of its own kind, a compilation of many genres, are part of that case and are named nowhere.

The witnesses are the strongest single strand and stand in summary. Which of them attested what, and when each had already fallen out with Joseph Smith, is unrecorded.
