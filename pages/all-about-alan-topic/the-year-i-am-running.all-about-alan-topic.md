# Definition

- **The Year I Am Running** — holding the reversible position until the one thing that can decide it arrives

# Design

No irreversible change until a full year passes with no rise in my base safety floor, run at full effort.

The floor is the metric rather than the crest, because a crest can feel like hope without moving anything.

A floor falling toward the hard line is the override firing, not a reason to wait.

Faith enters through the floor like everything else, so it never needed a gate of its own.

Work on the marriage is inside what the rule measures, since it lowers the running cost.

# Questions

Faith sits at about half and is unworked. It is the largest single lever on the floor, and the one thing that could add a duty to stay whatever the floor does.

Whether the year is genuinely being run at full effort, both levers pulled rather than avoidantly, is the validity risk on the whole trial.
