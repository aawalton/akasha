# Definition

- **Why I Act Fast** — acting rather than watching, because doing is what buys the truth

# Design

About half of what I believe is wrong and I cannot tell which half, so acting on it is how I find out.

Doing a thing tells me what watching it never can, so intervening is the cheapest truth going.

Nothing pre-plays the pain of it going wrong, so nothing slows me before I start.

The same gap shuts off a thing simply feeling right, so a belief lands only as a proof I build myself.

The cost is the swing: push too hard, hurt myself, retreat, recover, push again. Steady would get me further.

# Questions

Whether not being able to pre-play the pain is what makes me decisive, or merely sits alongside a truth-drive that would push me to act anyway, has never been separated.
