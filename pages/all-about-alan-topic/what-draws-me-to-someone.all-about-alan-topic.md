# Definition

- **What Draws Me To Someone** — what makes someone attractive to me, and why it is not the softest face

# Design

Two things add up to it: softness, which reads as safe, and directness, which reads as someone here with me.

Neither pole on its own tops it out, and the peak sits on being met rather than on safety, because being met is scarcer.

Safety comes first. Below enough of it my system rejects the charge rather than simply not enjoying it.

Appearance is easy and personality is hard, so what I connect to is a person I can read.

# Questions

Others being perfect reads uncanny to me where my own perfection reads safe. Whether that is one cue scoring opposite ways or two different vectors is open.

One image embodying freedom lifted past its ceiling with no visual change. Whether a held value generally adds to the involuntary response, or freedom is special, is untested.

Softness reads as safe, and the empty slot where a felt gender would be reads a safety signal too. Whether both are running off one cue for two ends is unprobed.
