# Definition

- **How Something Joins The Rotation** — what gets into the set I am working through, and what leaves it.

# Questions

How I decide to add something, whether interest fires or I put it there deliberately, is uncaptured.

How things retire, finished against quit against forgotten, has no convention.

The failure modes are named and unworked: something so engaging I will not chunk it, something so dull I chunk it to nothing.
