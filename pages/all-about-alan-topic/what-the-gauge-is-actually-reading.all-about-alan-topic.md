# Definition

- **What The Gauge Is Actually Reading** — what my sense of wanting is a reading of, and whether its two directions are weighted right

# Design

Not wanting to is an absolute veto. Wanting is not trusted.

# Questions

What the reading refers to is undetermined: a pull I feel, a body state I do not, or both at once. The three would mean different things.

Whether the asymmetry is set right is uncalibrated. There may be states where not wanting to is untrustworthy and the bar to push past it should be low, and states where wanting should be trusted.
