# Definition

- **How I Hold A Goal** — what a goal is for someone with no future to be pulled toward

# Design

Nothing stands in for a future I cannot picture. What drives me is pain now, including the pain of a want I cannot have.

So holding a goal means capturing that pain where it can be lit again, not keeping a want in mind.

Reading the record does not remind me. It makes the present me want it again.

An entry that fails to light dies quietly, and that is the filter. What truly matters comes back on its own.

The blind spot is drift that never hurts in any one moment. I am building pings for it.

# Questions

Which domains get an artificial ping first, at what cadence, and how a cadence gets corrected when there is no felt signal to check it against, is unworked.

Whether anything is ever started by a straight positive pull, rather than routed through the pain of a want blocked, is open.
