# Definition

- **Recovery Costs What It Restores** — what recovering from something costs me.

# Design

The moves that rebuild a resource also spend it, so being low can put them out of reach.

# Questions

Health gating the mana and stamina recovery moves is the one worked case. Whether the pattern shows up elsewhere, a recovery move priced in the very resource it pays back, is open.

The bath is the worked case and it carries no number. What a minute in it costs me in mana, and how low mana can get before the bath stops firing at all, are both unmeasured.

What the breathing pays back per day is stated as a third of what both its own anchors give. Only I can say which is right.
