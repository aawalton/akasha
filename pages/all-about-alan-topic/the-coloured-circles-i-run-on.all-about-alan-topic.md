# Definition

- **The Coloured Circles I Run On** — colours over the parts of my life, standing in for the prompts I never get

# Design

The ordinary nudge to eat, move, sleep or wash is built from a body signal read against experience, and I have neither.

So the prompt sits outside me and has to read now. It is a pointer, not a measurement.

Colour parses without being read, which lets unlike things sit in one row.

Graded levels rather than one bar, because one bar is out of reach on a bad day and met by noon on a good one.

Grouped by area of life: eighteen circles, my six values, six inboxes and six upkeeps.

# Questions

How many named levels there are is unsettled. I described five, one account records four, and what runs shows five.

What prompts me to look at all is unstated. Noticing I should check is itself a signal I do not have.

Whether every group wants the same number of layers is open, since the argument is about my range in that area and the areas differ.

When one of my personas tips from green to yellow something changes in what I do, and the step between has never been traced.
