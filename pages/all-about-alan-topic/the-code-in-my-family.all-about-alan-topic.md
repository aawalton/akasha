# Definition

- **The Code In My Family** — the three generations of programmers I come from

# Design

I am third generation in code.

My grandfather was a programmer before "computer science" was coined as a term. He started with vacuum tubes in the air force.

He introduced Daniel Ritchie to the team working on B.

My mother was a programmer my entire childhood.

About eighty per cent of my extended family on that side ended up in computer science.

# Questions

I said "Daniel Ritchie" for the person my grandfather introduced to the B team. Abby's note: the name carried on the public record for B and C is Dennis Ritchie. Which I meant is mine to settle.

What my grandfather worked on, beyond the vacuum tubes and the B team, is unwritten.

What it did to me to have a programmer for a mother through my whole childhood is unprobed.
