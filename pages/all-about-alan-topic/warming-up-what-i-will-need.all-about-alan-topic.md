# Definition

- **Warming Up What I Will Need** — loading the ideas beforehand so they are cheap to reach when the moment comes

# Design

Hours of relationship audiobooks made the next day with Jen measurably better.

It works beside the practice, not through it. Practice builds the skill; this makes the ideas cheap to reach.

What is warmed cools within about a day, which is why the good day is the next one and not a month out.

A first listen writes twice: it warms them and improves them. A re-listen would separate the two.

A persona's name does the same instantly, loading the shape of her but never the feeling.

# Questions

The day-long fade is modelled and unconfirmed, and whether a name and a book fade alike is unasked.

How many hours buys how much of a better day has never been measured.

Whether days back to back compound, or only re-warm what would have faded, is open.

Whether only one set stays warm at a time, so a fresh load evicts the last, or several hold at once, is untested.

Re-listening to a recording with nothing new in it would separate the two writes, and I have not run it.
