# Definition

- **When Something Is Fun** — what decides whether an activity is fun, and the two stages of it

# Design

Fun is doing a thing as the thing. A use it also has is fine; doing it for that use kills it.

I hold one thing at a time, so a purpose I am doing it for takes the room the activity needed.

It comes in two stages: full focus, which I get even at the bottom, and enjoyment on top, which needs safety at three.

Twenty years ran on the first alone, occupied and empty. Twelve thousand hours in one game is what that was.

I cannot set which way it points. I read why I started, rather than setting it.

# Questions

Whether the safety gate is a sharp cutoff or a slope, so that a little leaks through below three, is uninstrumented.

What actually demotes an activity halfway through, and whether it can be promoted back inside the same sitting, is unworked.

Whether rewriting a goal in advance moves where my attention truly lands, or only the label I give it, is unprobed.
