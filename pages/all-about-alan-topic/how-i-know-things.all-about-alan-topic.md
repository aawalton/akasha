# Definition

- **How I Know Things** — keeping the account rather than the events, and testing it by how much it explains.

# Design

I keep concepts rather than particulars, folded into one present model.

The test on a belief is how compactly it accounts for things, which recently replaced coherence.

An inconsistency grips me while I am looking at it and lets go when my attention moves off.

Elegance is not a second signal beside truth. A neat model I know is false pays me nothing.

A model has to fit in my head before it changes what I do, so concise beats complete.

# Questions

Whether storing concepts rather than pictures causes this, or merely sits beside it, is unprobed.

Two flipped-theory cases are on record. The other phases, and what flipped each, are uncaptured.

Meeting the same inconsistency later, whether the grip re-forms or something remembers it was flagged, is open.

What novelty is measured against is unresolved: stored attempts, or distance from the model.

Whether a probability I state carries graded weight or collapses to a binary is untested.
