# Definition

- **Why I Stopped Working** — the cost of having a job, and what stopping actually bought

# Design

At the best-fitting job I could imagine, work still took four fifths of everything I had.

Fitting a role better lowers the multiplier. It does not make having a job cheap.

For a decade each new one fitted better and still cost more, because I was falling faster than I optimised.

The sums had said stop months before. Being fired only set the date.

What retirement really bought was the right to decline. My rule against unaffordable things cannot exist without it.

# Questions

What each role actually cost, year by year, would make the ratchet something that could be shown wrong. Only the last one is anchored.

What someone without a runway does instead is named as a separate problem and never worked.

What partial or different work could look like if I keep recovering is open, and rests on what a higher state permits.

What I actually optimised over that decade, and the limit it never reached, has never been listed.
