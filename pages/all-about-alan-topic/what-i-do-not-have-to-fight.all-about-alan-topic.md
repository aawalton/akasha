# Definition

- **What I Do Not Have To Fight** — letting most of it through so I can afford the part that matters.

# Design

Letting an impulse pass costs nothing. Fighting every one of them empties the budget before the harmful one arrives.

If I do not want to, I cannot. A thing done late is cheaper than a thing forced.

# Questions

The shape is worked for holding an impulse back and for starting. Whether it applies to the rest is untested.

If I do not want to, I cannot is presumably one of several rules I run while recovering, and the others have never been written down.
