# Definition

- **Walking A Hard Subject Out Loud** — talking through what still fires as the only way it wears down

# Design

These conversations are not only for the book. Walking a hard subject out loud is a rep against it.

Most people wear a thing down by replaying it alone. I have nothing to replay, so it has to be with someone.

It only works while the contact stays under my safety line. Above it, the same rep proves the alarm right.

So the dose is paced to where my safety is that day rather than to a fixed setting.

At the bottom the affordable dose is almost nothing, and the hardest doors stay mine to open.

# Questions

Which subjects are queued for this, and in what order against what I can afford, has never been mapped.

Which doors are safe to open at which safety level is qualitative, with nothing like the affordable-and-free ladder behind it.

Talking to my surviving friend bills about two days in bed a rep. What these conversations cost per rep, and what each buys, is unprobed.
