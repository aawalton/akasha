# Definition

- **Self-Improvement** — the one thing everything else I believe compresses down into.

# Design

It is a verb rather than a position, a heading rather than a place to stand.

My purpose is improving myself rather than improving my life, which puts it inside what I control.

What I know is most of who I am. My sense of myself is my live map, as identity rather than as a tool.

# Questions

Every belief is a provisional draft including this one. Whether this atom is the fixed point or is in scope of its own instruction is unprobed.

I count editing myself live as almost always a net win. Which correction costs more than it returns was being probed when the thread broke.

Which self is being improved, the thread across time or the seed underneath, is unpinned, and recovery puts the body in scope.

Whether this is the root and compression an instance, or the reverse, is unresolved.
