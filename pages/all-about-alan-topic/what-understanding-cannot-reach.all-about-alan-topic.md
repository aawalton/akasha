# Definition

- **What Understanding Cannot Reach** — understanding is the only way I grow, so where it stops is where I stop

# Design

The channels most people grow through are sealed for me: felt shifts, intuition below words, an episode teaching me something over again.

# Questions

I understand most of my own difference and it is still lonely. What understanding relieves and what it does not has never been mapped.

Whether anything reaches me that is not understanding, and what that would even look like, is unasked.

Whether the simulation other people run is a channel onto the truth about someone, or an engine projecting their own interior, is mine to answer and unanswered.
