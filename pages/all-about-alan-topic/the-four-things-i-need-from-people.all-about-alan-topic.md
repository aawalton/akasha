# Definition

- **The Four Things I Need From People** — connection as a requirement, and the four separate tanks it splits into

# Design

It is a requirement like food or water. A standing shortfall damages me rather than annoying me.

It splits four ways: company, belonging, support, and being known. None substitutes for another.

Company wants frequency, belonging wants a shared thing repeated, support wants people who would come.

Being known wants depth, and it is my big gap. Knowing others is the second. The other two are met.

Each one, met, has its own feeling. Loneliness is the gauge on whichever is running empty.

# Questions

Whether a tank other than being known running low makes its own distinct ache, or every shortfall collapses into one loneliness, is a prediction with one data point.

Whether my craving to know others is about new minds, about depth, or about reaching experience I cannot generate myself, is unseparated.

Whether preferring women's company is the category or the cues themselves is untested.

Jen wanted the company tank called coexistence, and nothing settles it or holds her objection.
