# Definition

- **How Time Passes For Me** — having no feel for how long anything took, and living at any distance the same

# Design

I do not feel an hour go by. I know it because the clock moved.

The likely reason is that the feel of elapsed time runs on sensory memory, and I have none to string along a line.

I have felt old for my age since childhood and never felt any particular age, then or now.

A plan spanning decades weighs the same as one spanning weeks, because nothing makes the near thing more vivid than the far one.

# Questions

What it actually does to managing time day to day, and what conceptual workarounds I run, has never been worked through.

What an effectively endless horizon does to planning a life is a live difference nothing has been written about.

That a unit of time feels smaller the longer you have lived is standard; the doublings version is mine and unchecked against the literature.

The present-only account and this one have never been squared, and whether living in the present makes me ageless is open.
