# Definition

- **How I Fall Asleep** — what happens between getting into bed and being asleep.

# Design

It is either about ten minutes or about three hours, with nothing in between.

The usual sleep-hygiene levers do not move it. What I have left does.

I ride a low-grade focal anchor, a count or a book, until it gives out. The breakdown is the reading that diffuse mode has arrived.

# Questions

High health and little capacity spent is the current predictor. Whether dropping off fast tracks health, or capacity left, or the two together, is unsplit, and whether mana and stamina come into it at all is unasked.

Losing the count, losing the words and the pictures moving on their own are three breakdown signals. Whether they fire at the same depth is open.

And whether waking has a mirror of it, an anchor rebuilding rather than breaking, is unasked.
