# Definition

- **When Someone Says Something Good** — why praise so rarely lands on me, and what it takes when it does

# Design

I have had appreciation in large amounts and I am not sure I have ever received any of it.

Praise has to be true, meant, valuable and not incidental, all at once as it is said. Criticism needs one of two.

The check happens at delivery. It does not have to agree with a verdict I already hold.

What passes the true test is an undisputable specific I already hold, rather than a global verdict I would take on faith.

# Questions

Whether the other three conditions break down the same way, and whether the four account for all of the gating, is open.

Whether the cut bridge or the gate is the load-bearing account, or the two compose, is unresolved.

Whether appreciation never landing fed the scar, so the good channel was starved rather than the bad one loud, is untested.
