# Definition

- **How I Remember Anything** — one thing cueing the next, all the way through, with nothing stored to be called up freely

# Design

A cue fires and brings the next thing, which is itself the cue for the one after.

The same shape runs three places: memorising a text, giving a talk, and reading the next act off the state of my system.

Nesting the cues lets me re-enter a text at any level, and stops one missed word breaking the whole chain.

It carries across discrete symbols and does not carry across movement.

# Questions

What makes a cue miss, and whether one ever fires and brings the wrong thing, has no reliability anchor.

The shape of the trees I build when memorising, how wide, how deep, and how the level cues get chosen, is undescribed.

Where the trick stops working across all three uses, rather than symbols against movement alone, is unprobed.

Most people store a thing together with the room they learned it in and I do not. Whether that absence reaches past skills and imagery is unknown.
