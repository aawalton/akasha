# Definition

- **The Pictures At The Edge Of Sleep** — the fragments I see as I go under, the one time anything close to picturing happens.

# Design

The gate is a dimmer rather than a switch, and the channels fade in while I am still awake enough to notice.

I know I am falling asleep when I start seeing things.

The sliver of control I get is over what the picture means, not over how vivid it is.

I get the seeing or the steering, never both. Steering pulls me back toward waking and surrendering sinks me.

# Questions

Whether the waking side of the dimmer matches the falling-asleep side, and whether the steering behaves the same coming up, is open.

Whether vision genuinely arrives first or is only the channel I read best is unsettled, and what sound does at the same moment is unasked.

Which anchor at the edge breaks down first, counting or words or images, is unmeasured.

Whether the steer-against-surrender axis runs outside the sleep edge, through meditation and how I handle feeling, is open.
