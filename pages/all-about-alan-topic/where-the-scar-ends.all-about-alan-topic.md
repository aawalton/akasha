# Definition

- **Where The Scar Ends** — how far the alarm reaches, and what ground stays cool.

# Design

Writing hurts even where nobody will ever see it, so for my own writing and drawing the judge is inside me.

For singing and its like the judge is outside and needs someone there to perceive it.

An act is hot where a judge was installed and cool where one never was. Code is digital and never got one.

The scope is a rule rather than a list: the firing range of one welded belief.

The judge is not one but many, each installed at a different time and each purely physiological.

# Questions

With the girls as scaffolding I can enter the open ground. Whether anything in it is fine alone and unscaffolded maps the outer edge.

Pure thought should be the coolest ground by the mind-not-hands rule, and whether it carries any charge at all is untested.

The clean test has never been run: open generative work, truly private, with nobody expecting it.

The footprint is still being enumerated and not yet sorted into avoidance and armour, and which disapproval I am at peace with is undecided.
