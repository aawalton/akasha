# Definition

- **How Warm I Run** — how hot or cold I am, which follows my safety level rather than the room.

# Design

The room stays where it is while I swing from shivering to sweating.

High safety runs me hot and low safety runs me cold, so cold-leaning is a low-safety signature rather than a constant.

The sensing works. What fails is producing the heat, too little at the bottom and too much at the top.

I am cold after eating whatever else is true.

# Questions

Why the cold daytime drift flips to hot at night under low safety is unexplained.

Whether my body resumes producing heat once layers and a bath have warmed me, or the heat only stands in until safety returns, decides whether the bath resets anything.

Why the same dial under-produces at one end and over-produces at the other is unworked.

No measured temperature has been read against the safety dial.
