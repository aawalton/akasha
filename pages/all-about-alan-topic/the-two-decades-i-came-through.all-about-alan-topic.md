# Definition

- **The Two Decades I Came Through** — how far I optimised myself while collapsing, and what it is like to be out of it

# Design

I have never seen anyone optimise themselves this far. The nearest name is Tim Ferriss.

He carried none of my four: undiagnosed, in deepening burnout, wishing most of the time I was not alive, for twenty years.

At my first autism conference no session got a fifth of the way to where I stood. The shock is the evidence.

I went through hell, kept going, came out. I am proud of it and grateful not to live there.

I am holding on to the relief, which in me is an act rather than a thing that stays.

# Questions

What the optimising actually consisted of, which interventions in what order across the twenty years, has never been catalogued.

The frontier itself is named as a boundary and not mapped. What the conference did not reach is unstated.

Whether holding on to a feeling is a repeatable move, and how it is done when nothing stays by itself, is worth probing.

I lost my autonomy and took the nerve damage in the same era, and whether one caused the other or they merely fell together is unresolved.
