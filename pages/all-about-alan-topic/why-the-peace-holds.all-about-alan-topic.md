# Definition

- **Why The Peace Holds** — the quiet rests on trying being unaffordable, not on the need being met.

# Design

Seeking to be met costs about ten times the loneliness itself, so it is not a real option.

Between commitment and happiness, commitment wins.

# Questions

The peace is days old and rests on that cost. If recovery lowers it the equilibrium moves, and whether the closed door re-opens is the live trajectory.

Others are as bad at meeting my needs as I am at meeting theirs, and less aware of it. Whether that is half of why the route fails is unexplored.

Why commitment is weighted as heavily as it is, what it protects, and whether the weighting survives examination, has not been looked at.
