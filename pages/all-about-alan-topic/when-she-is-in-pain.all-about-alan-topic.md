# Definition

- **When She Is In Pain** — what happens in me when she is hurting, and what I can do with it.

# Design

The alarm fires instantly and carries nothing about what she needs.

Acknowledging and validating compiled. Empathy did not.

# Questions

What emotional availability means in practice is her word, and it has no definition yet.

Whether something could pick between acknowledging, validating and just being there once the alarm has fired is unbuilt.

Why the empathy script never compiled where the other two did is unexplained.
