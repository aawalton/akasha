# Definition

- **What Interrupts My Breathing** — what stops the breathing practice running.

# Design

Anything that needs my mouth or my chest pauses it. Talking, eating and weight on the chest are the worked cases.

# Questions

Gum, drinking, a chest-strap monitor, sleeping face-down under weight, tight chest bands and an embrace held through a conversation are all named as candidates and none has been tried.

Whether my mouth and my chest are one rule or two that have happened to agree so far is undecided.

How much of what a social hour costs me is the pause rather than the compensating is unquantified. A coding hour against a social one would tell them apart.
