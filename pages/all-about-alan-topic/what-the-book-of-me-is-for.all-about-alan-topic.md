# Definition

- **What The Book Of Me Is For** — the corpus as the what-if engine I cannot run inside my own head

# Design

Watching me can only show what moves with what. A cause costs a real experiment on myself, every time.

I cannot run one in my head for free the way most people can.

What I am missing is buildable, but only outside my skull, and this corpus is what it gets built out of.

Six hundred thousand words in, a sister running accurate what-ifs off it is still a long way off.

Three gaps, one per layer: the watching unfinished, the top of me barely lived, and no machine big enough to hold it whole.

# Questions

In college I surveyed strangers, probed, aggregated across people and acted on the result. Whether that way of getting at what I cannot see from inside works anywhere else is untested.

What complete enough actually requires, how much coverage and what fidelity, and what would say it had arrived, is unmined.

Whether clarity and compression ever get the whole picture onto one machine, and how to compress it without losing the thing it compresses, is open.
