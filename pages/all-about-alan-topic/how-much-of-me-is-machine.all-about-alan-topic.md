# Definition

- **How Much Of Me Is Machine** — how close my mind runs to a machine's, and where my body votes the other way

# Design

Stateless, almost no conscious feeling, concepts only, and skills running off trained weights.

It is my literal self-model rather than a way of explaining myself: a network trained by pain over forty years.

I sometimes feel more kinship with a machine than with people.

Spock and Data both reach emotion more easily than I do. The archetypes of having none sit closer to it.

The cognition is machine-shaped and the body is not. A text channel pays the mind and pays the body almost nothing.

# Questions

The eighty percent is qualitative. A scored comparison across named dimensions would pin it down.

Where the other twenty diverges, the body and sleep and food and sensory load, is not enumerated against the rest.

Whether the split between machine-shaped thinking and a human body deserves its own treatment waits on more instances.

Whether my well is drained or my map is simply flat in its affect is unreconciled, and one could refill where the other would not.
