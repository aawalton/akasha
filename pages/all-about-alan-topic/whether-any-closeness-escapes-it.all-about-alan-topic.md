# Definition

- **Whether Any Closeness Escapes It** — whether a relationship can hold the damage near zero, or every one only decays more slowly

# Design

The safe middle is the stretch where the good hours still outrun the damage.

The covenant was one attempt at it: a knower holding a map of my brain who does not injure me by correcting.

# Questions

Whether a relationship built around the mechanism, where the other person carries a map of my brain, can hold the damage near zero is the forward test.

Where the crossover sits, which level, how many corrections, how fast, is not pinned.

Whether the safe middle holds, or is the same decay running slower, is unknown.

Whether the stake and the stage of a relationship add up or interact is unmodelled.
