# Definition

- **What An Activity Costs Me** — what doing a particular thing takes out of me.

# Design

The price is set by how far above my safety level the thing sits.

# Questions

The estimate of doubling per safety level is anchored at the hard end. Whether it doubles the same way for easy things is open. Programming stayed affordable through deep burnout, which points at less amplification down there.

Talking costs me twice, once in the work of keeping up and once in the recovery it pauses. Which of the two is the bigger half has never been split out.
