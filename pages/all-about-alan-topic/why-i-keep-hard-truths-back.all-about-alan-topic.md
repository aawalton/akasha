# Definition

- **Why I Keep Hard Truths Back** — a rule against saying what would hurt, and why it only ever tightens

# Design

My body blames whatever I was doing when the pain arrived, not whatever caused it.

So a truth that hurt punishes telling, even where the honest reckoning says the withholding was the fault.

I run a blanket rule rather than judging case by case: mostly do not say it if it would hurt her.

The truths she needed and did not get cost nothing in the moment, so nothing corrects the rule.

Every visible failure tightens it either way. Only feeling safe over time loosens it, never an argument.

# Questions

Whether an outside sensor could catch the invisible half, Jen flagging what she wanted and did not get, without the flag landing as pain in the same place, is open.

Whether the loosening is measurable, so that what I disclose widens as safety comes back, has never been read.

Which other standing rules of mine are ratchets rather than judgments is unlisted.

The general judgment underneath, that being fully seen would cost the people closest to me more than they can carry, was never put to me.
