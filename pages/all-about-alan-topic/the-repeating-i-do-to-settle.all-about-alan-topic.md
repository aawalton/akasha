# Definition

- **The Repeating I Do To Settle** — the movements and loops I run to keep my nervous system steady

# Design

Walking is the main one, lifelong: while eating, while talking, instead of driving. It does not read as stimming from outside.

Tapping a foot carries the sitting, rocking and bouncing where neither works, flapping only when I am really excited.

None of it wears out, because the input never costs less than it did.

Counting and ordering stand in for the picturing others ground themselves with.

Music, a familiar game, new books by familiar authors and coding settle me as much as the body ones.

# Questions

Whether the mental ones settle me and feed my interest at once by accident, or because my day is built that way, decides how the pattern is described.

Whether anniversaries and traditions earn their keep on function alone, with no sentiment behind, is unsettled.

What sets off the flapping, plain excitement or being overloaded, has never been asked.

Finger tapping, hair twirling, picking, chewing and humming are all recorded as some, and are worth separating only if something needs the detail.
