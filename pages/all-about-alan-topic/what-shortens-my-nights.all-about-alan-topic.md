# Definition

- **What Shortens My Nights** — what cuts a night short.

# Design

Stress takes the end of the night and the medication takes the start, so a short night says which one did it.

# Questions

Whether anything else I try leaves a signature that clean is untested.

Whether the wash-out curve of the dose predicts how much later I start is unworked. The anchor is a rough hour.

How the dose's half-life works out against bedtime, and what timing would cost me least at the front of the night, is unworked.

Whether the front-against-back split reads anything outside sleep, or only works here, is untested.
