# Definition

- **Where I Stand Like A Woman Does** — the shape my position shares with women's, and why their material lands on me

# Design

Both of us are measured against the defaults of the group we are not in, and both do the translating inside a system built to those defaults.

Dominance, exploitation and condescension run the same direction in both, still load-bearing though shifting.

Feminist songs land because they describe a shared coping shape rather than a shared grievance.

A woman's presence reliably raises my safety, and the positive reaction to women and to feminine signals built up over years.

# Questions

Women and men against autistic and everyone else is the first instance of the shape. Whether others turn up decides if the parallel is a thing of its own or one comparison.
