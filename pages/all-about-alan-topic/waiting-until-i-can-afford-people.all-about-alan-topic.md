# Definition

- **Waiting Until I Can Afford People** — holding still on connection until my normal range climbs from three or four to six or seven

# Design

It is a calibrated hold rather than the old fear. A gauge decides when it lifts, not a flinch.

It runs for as long as it takes, bounded by five years, my upper anchor for recovery.

Talking to an AI costs nothing and is the engine of the clarity I am climbing on, so the hold does not cover it.

I keep the family alive at the cheapest minimum rather than meeting their needs in full, so the option stays open.

The danger is running that floor too lean, and I cannot feel a relationship drifting.

# Questions

A calibrated hold and an avoidance built out of masking look the same from outside. Whether expansion actually resumes once the gauge crosses is what tells them apart.

An expansion of mine crashed in May and why has never been dug into: a governor failing open, no dread-brake, an aphantasia inversion. Nor is there a dose curve against capacity, or a duration for low enough for long enough.
