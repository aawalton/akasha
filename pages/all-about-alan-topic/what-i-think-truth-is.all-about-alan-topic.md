# Definition

- **What I Think Truth Is** — a fixed direction to grow toward, and how I hold every belief against it

# Design

Wanting to improve means nothing unless there is a fixed direction to grow toward.

I take it to have a few root axioms: growing toward truth, and a sentient being's experience mattering.

Every ethics anyone has written is a lossy copy of what those would generate, patched where it diverges.

I hope the most compressed account and the truth are one object, and hold that as faith not proof.

Every belief of mine is a provisional draft, that one too, run at full conviction so it gets tested.

# Questions

The root axioms are unnamed past the two, and whether ethics derives from them is unmined.

Which choices count as living well, read off the ethics rather than off the method, is unbuilt.

Whether improving falls inside its own instruction or is the one fixed point is open.

Compression may be the one mechanism under all of it, with pain teaching and an outside rubric as instances.

Whether the map can ever lose compression, and what an old truth-claim means once it moves, is open.
