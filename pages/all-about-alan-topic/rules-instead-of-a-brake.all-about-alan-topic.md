# Definition

- **Rules Instead Of A Brake** — the standing rules I use to keep myself from overreaching.

# Design

I keep them written down because nothing in the moment tells me to stop.

# Questions

What makes a push slip past the rules — not remembering one, not seeing the thing as recurring, or not seeing the self-hurt until after?

Which other rules came from what was nearby rather than what caused it is unknown.

Treating future selves as people with a claim on me is how I guard against myself. Is it also how I hold a goal at all, given nothing in me pictures a future?

Only one of my recovery rules is written down — if I don't want to, I can't. The rest are presumed and uncollected.
