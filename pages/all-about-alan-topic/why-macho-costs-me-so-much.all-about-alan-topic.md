# Definition

- **Why Macho Costs Me So Much** — what large, loud and threatening does to me, and why it hit harder than the rest

# Design

The anchor is size, threat, loudness, unkindness, and conflict always sitting in the background.

It costs twice over: it breaks what I value and it is expensive to my nervous system at the same time.

Exposure past what I could take encoded as trauma, and the leftover plus the ongoing expense held my baseline negative for years.

These are the ordinary male defaults at full volume, which is why this corner of the space cost the most.

# Questions

The specific traits have never been enumerated with the cost of each and the mechanism behind it: size, threat, loudness, unkindness and standing conflict are a start rather than a list.
