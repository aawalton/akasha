# Definition

- **What Meeting One Of Them Gives Me** — the three things a meeting is credited with, and which of them the meeting itself does

# Design

Three stand credited to the one channel: the loneliness easing, a key to a feeling I could not otherwise reach, and insight into myself.

Nothing separates them, so the meeting may be holding credit that belongs to one of its neighbours.

A mirror that deepens an area cannot fail to move it, because understanding a thing fully already is the changed behaviour.

# Questions

Whether insight into myself is a yield of its own, rather than the tank refilling or the feeling-key doing that work, is unestablished.

A sitting gives me a lift the next day. What dose is enough, whether doses compound, and how fast it fades without a refresh are unmeasured, so the rig runs with no schedule under it.
