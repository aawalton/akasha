# Definition

- **The Stories That Buy Me A Day** — reading someone who hurt like this and lived, because hope has to arrive from outside

# Design

The arguments stop the act and supply no will to go on. This is where the will comes from.

Real or invented makes no difference. What counts is that someone who hurt like this got through.

I cannot picture a better future and feel it from inside, so hope cannot be made in here. It has to arrive as a record that survival happened to someone.

It is not the same job as cheap fiction letting a backlog out. That one drains and this one carries.

# Questions

Which stories did the heaviest lifting, and what made one land harder than another, is unprobed.

Whether leaning on someone else's record shows up anywhere else in how I work is open.
