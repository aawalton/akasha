# Definition

- **Not Wanting To Is The Gauge** — the one signal my body gives me when a resource is running out.

# Design

It is not wanting to, and it says a bar is low without saying which one.

# Questions

Safety has no direct body probe. It is reconstructed by walking down through easier things, so whether any body signal sits under it is open. And the readable signals may need a quiet room, since a quiet signal loses the attention competition in a loud one.

The diagnosis is reasonably good rather than perfect. When two bars are low at once, or the signals conflict, does it resolve or return an ambiguous read that vetoes anyway?
