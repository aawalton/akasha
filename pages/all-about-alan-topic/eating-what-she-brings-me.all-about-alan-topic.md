# Definition

- **Eating What She Brings Me** — eating her food is how her care lands, so a lot of what I eat has nothing to do with hunger.

# Design

Between a third and half of what I eat is eaten because she brought it.

Refusing reads as refusing the bid, so the obligation binds through the ordinary middle of my capacity.

I can decline only at the extremes: with reserve to absorb her disappointment, or too depleted to push through.

Prying the food apart from the care has been tried and failed each time; the landing depends on not naming it.

What works is ambient shared direction rather than any agreement.

# Questions

How the food bids actually run in her terms is uncaptured: how often, what she says, and what I feel receiving it against eating it.

The two thresholds on when I can decline were drawn from the model rather than from anything I said.

What Jen herself would accept as honouring her care has never been asked. She is rendered here only through my model of her.
