# Definition

- **What I Cannot Say To Her** — her stability and her feeling loved both need my silence, and being met needs me to speak.

# Design

Both her needs run through one valve: do not show her the changes, do not say what the loving behaviour comes from.

So every increment of her being met is an increment of my staying unknown.

Her resistance is defence of her stability, not a verdict on who I have become.

Each attempt that meets resistance hardens the not-accepted reading rather than softening it.

# Questions

The landing rests on Jen holding a belief the silence protects. Whether that is stable, and what happens if the construction becomes visible to her, is live.

How long I can go on being the keeper of something she does not know sets the ceiling on meeting her needs.

Naming the lack as shared, that neither of us can give the other the peace they wish, might be sayable inside the silence. Whether it makes the bind legible to her is untested.
