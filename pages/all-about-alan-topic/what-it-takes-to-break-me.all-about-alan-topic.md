# Definition

- **What It Takes To Break Me** — holding while there is no other choice, and going the moment there is one

# Design

I can carry whatever I have to while there is truly no other choice. A real door is what breaks me.

Every time I left a job, what broke it was that somewhere else was open, not that it got worse.

Underneath that sits a floor of pure load where my body gives out whether or not a door exists.

I cannot believe what would be easier. I can look for more, or reframe what I have, and no more.

Nothing in me raises the possibility of a door, so a real one has to be handed to me from outside.

# Questions

What the body giving out looks like, how near twenty years ran to it, and whether it leaves a signature, is uncharacterised.

Whether Jen, whose imagining runs where mine does not, is my main source of doors, is unprobed.

Whether reframing what I already have can open a real door, rather than an imagined one, is untested.

A door can be vetoed for producing what I am escaping. Whether that generalises is unchecked.

How the physical floor relates to flop and to the deepest states is unexamined.
