# Definition

- **Stamina Bar** — what my body has left to move with.

# Questions

Nothing below zero has surfaced for stamina either, and the same three readings fit as for mana.

How stamina actually moves is uncaptured: what sleep puts back, what the day takes out, and what fatigue, sleep debt, hunger and thirst do to it.
