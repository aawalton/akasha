# Definition

- **Having ADHD** — the ADHD half of how I am built, what it costs and what it gives

# Design

It is how I am built rather than something wrong with me, and I would not take a pill against it.

The heart of it is executive function, and I get a double dose because autism brings its own.

Its social face is milder than autism's, and it has real treatments where autism has almost none.

Milder socially is not milder. Left unmanaged it is sharply disabling for the one who has it.

# Questions

Which levers I actually use, medication and systems and controls on my surroundings, has never been set down.

What it gives me that I would not trade, and whether those gifts separate cleanly from autism's, is unanswered.

Which part of it has cost me most, whether executive function or attention or time or working memory, is unranked.

Medication roughly doubles my resilience, and which of reserve, recovery speed or cost multiplier it moves is unsplit. Coming off it has no steps yet.
