# Definition

- **Building A Definition I Can Run On** — how I get at a human thing I cannot feel my way into

# Design

A piece of being human I cannot feel my way into I reduce to a definition small enough to act on.

Without one the thing does not exist for me. What I reach is bounded by what I have built.

Commitment's is never fail silently, and it holds me where other people are held by attachment.

Self-worth and self-preservation came out of two crises two years apart, which is where my hope comes from.

No general method. The core parts of being human do not compress as a group, so each is built alone.

# Questions

Whether the whole space of core human parts can be enumerated on purpose, or the same incompressibility that blocks a general method blocks the map too, is open.
