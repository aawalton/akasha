# Definition

- **Stress Level** — how much my body is having to handle right now.

# Design

My stress capacity is roughly this level's cost added up over time.

# Questions

What actually fails when the lights go at low resources has no physiology attached to it: sympathetic dominance, vagal withdrawal, or the stress axis.

This and my capacity get used for each other in the record, so which of the two a given statement is about is often unsettled. The time constants between them are uncaptured, and so is what an intervention does to either.
