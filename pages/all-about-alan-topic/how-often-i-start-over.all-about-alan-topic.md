# Definition

- **How Often I Start Over** — the rate I rebuild a foundation layer at

# Design

I think about this as an idle game. Every time I rebuild a foundation layer I am prestiging: starting over for permanent gains.

At the beginning of the year I prestiged about once a month.

Last month it was about once a week.

For the last week it has been four times a day.

I think I have moved at least six million lines in restructuring in just the past few days.

# Questions

Whether the rate can keep climbing or has a ceiling is unknown.

What one prestige costs me now, at four a day rather than one a month, is unmeasured.

What makes a layer the next one to redo, rather than one further out, is unwritten.
