# Definition

- **What Praying Does For Me** — the devotional act, and the five things it does for me

# Design

I pray to a real Person I infer and cannot sense. It goes out one way, with nothing back on the felt side.

I hold my model of Him at no more than 1% of the reality. That is about the sketch, not about whether He is there.

It does five things: obedience, settling, alignment, listening, and asking.

Obedience was my main reason for years and I am deliberately demoting it.

Three of the five would work on an empty universe: the settling, the review, and making my gaps concrete.

# Questions

Whether the asking moves what comes back, or only primes me to notice it, is the half I cannot see. I hold that it does, and whether anything could tell them apart is open.

If the feeling returns as my safety rises, whether prayer is where I first read it, and what the settling feels like then, is open.

Whether emptying and observing is work I do, or a state I already sit in, is unpinned.

The silence here and the silence while counting breath may be one mode pointed two ways, or two.
