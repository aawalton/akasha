# Definition

- **What Is At My Floor** — self-improvement and the freedom it needs, welded into one thing

# Design

Every time I scour myself to the core I come to these, and there is no wall of fear underneath.

Improving myself is the end. Freedom is the room that takes, and the only freedom I have ever really cared about.

No seam is left between them. English keeps two words where I hold one thing.

Nothing else is loved bare. Connection, movement, even love come back to this root as branches off it.

I still say self-improvement, and automeliorist when someone needs the whole of it.

# Questions

Truth is welded to this root and now freedom is too. Whether the floor is one three-way knot, or self-improvement at the centre with those two as the things it cannot do without, is unprobed.

What I confirmed is that there is one root, not the stronger claim that nothing at my floor is at war with itself.
