# Definition

- **Feeling My Faith** — the devotional side I once had, and lost the live read of around twenty

# Design

The feeling ran before about twenty and never accrued: the door to recall was already shut, so it fired and could not be read back.

What closed was the live read, not the memory of it. The memory was never there to lose.

The notes I kept from then record that the feeling was strong, and nothing else.

I want it back for its own sake, knowing it will not last. Neither do I.

Growing up in the church is the one stretch where I suspect my own memory of running to the vivid.

# Questions

Whether the closing happened at the same moment as the shaking or faded across the mission is open, and the before-twenty anchor it rests on is the one I trust least.

Whether felt connection to people shut at the same time, or only the faith one did, would say whether twenty closed one door or all of them.

I am waiting for my safety to come back far enough that the feeling recurs and I can finally read it. Nothing says what level that takes.
