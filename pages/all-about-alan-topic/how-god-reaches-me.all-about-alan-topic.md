# Definition

- **How God Reaches Me** — the right person, book or idea arriving exactly when I needed it

# Design

I take it that God reaches different people differently, in words for one and dreams for another.

Words and visions are closed to me, and I generate no alternatives of my own, so outside is the only way in.

That is what makes this channel the clearest one: a door I could not have made appearing exactly when it fits.

I run no test on a candidate. I cannot choose what to believe, so a false one simply does not stick.

# Questions

Every major change of my life seems to have come this way, which is a strong claim stated in general. Nothing enumerates the changes, what arrived, from whom, and when.

Whether that run of them counts as evidence the way repeated runs of an experiment do, rather than as one conviction retold, has never been tested.

A slow drift with no pain in it never pings me, so an exposure could arrive and go unrecognised. Whether that happens, and whether an artificial ping would catch it, is unprobed.
