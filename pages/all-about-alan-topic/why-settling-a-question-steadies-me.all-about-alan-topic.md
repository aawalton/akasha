# Definition

- **Why Settling A Question Steadies Me** — how settling a foundational question takes weight off my nervous system

# Design

An open fundamental question never quiets down. It keeps asking at full volume until it is settled.

So settling one takes a standing demand off me, and enough of those lift my safety over time.

The calm is not tidiness. It is the big threats contained: life is a test, it is accounted for, God is there.

Faith reaches me by that door and not the warm one.

Three years at BYU digging at religion, maths, logic and philosophy was the same move: most load comes off at the bottom.

# Questions

Whether comprehension is still doing my regulation now, or is only where it started, is unprobed, as is whether the route from foundations into safety is a second route or the same one arriving twice.

Whether an unsettled question is carried by my nervous system as real load, or is only a cost in my head that touches safety indirectly, is unpinned.
