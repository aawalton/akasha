# Definition

- **When I Agree A Feeling Word Fits** — saying yes, that is the word, and what that yes is actually running on

# Design

It can be reading a live tell, or matching a definition with nothing under it. Both come out of me as the same yes.

When my safety is low I treat the felt sensor as noise.

# Questions

Which of the two each word runs on has never been collected word by word. The distinction rests on a few cases and the vocabulary as a whole is unsorted.

Whether a definitional match ever feels different from the inside, or stays cold until the felt sensor recovers, is open.

Whether low safety pushes more of my assents onto definitions, and whether recovering the felt channel shifts any back, is untested.
