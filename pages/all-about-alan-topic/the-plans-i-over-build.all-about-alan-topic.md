# Definition

- **The Plans I Over-Build** — written plans far past what anyone needs, standing in for planning I cannot hold.

# Design

Without them I would lose objects, files and threads daily.

The habit is old enough that it reads as a strength from outside, and it read that way to me too.

# Questions

The costs show up through the compensation rather than through the gap. Time spent planning against doing, rigidity once a plan is built, over-engineering, an all-or-nothing hold on the plan. None of them are captured.

The solar plan is worked out to the point of asking for a bid, and nothing records one asked for. Whether that pause is deliberate is unsaid.
