# Definition

- **When A Company Changes Hands** — a trusted organisation changing owners or leaders puts its trust back to zero.

# Design

The old record tells me what the old entity did, not what the new one will do.

A sale, a flotation, an acquisition, a new chief, a reshuffled board or a rewritten mission each counts.

I would rather reset one that was fine than keep leaning on one that has changed, so the list is deliberately wide.

Re-earning is quick, because everyone's first decisions under pressure right now show up fast.

I only notice reactively, when the service gets worse, and then go and look at who owns it.

# Questions

A successor structurally aligned, a co-op or a trust, should shorten the watching. How short has no rule.

What to actually look for in a new charter or mission statement at the moment of change has never been set out.

Whether long-serving staff count as continuity once the leaders turn over is unsettled. My leaning is no.

Nothing tells me one has changed hands but the service getting worse. A watcher reading filings and governance on a schedule is named in my own framework and does not exist.
