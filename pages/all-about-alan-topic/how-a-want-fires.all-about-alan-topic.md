# Definition

- **How A Want Fires** — seeing a gap between how something is and how it should be, and wanting to close it

# Design

I do not author a want. I see something, a gap opens against what it should be, and the want is that pull.

The only place I can act is upstream, on what I let myself see. Most of how I arrange my surroundings is that.

Inside the guardrails I do what I want; wanting nothing routes to rest.

Some are given and fire as desire or irritation: hunger, loneliness, needless complexity. Others I wrote in.

Nearly all the written ones serve something else. Improving myself is the one I want for itself.

# Questions

Some wants are given rather than written in, and whether a given one can be retired or only managed for the rest of my life is unknown.

When several fair options sit there and nothing forces my hand, what picks the next move is unprobed. Interest, the reversibility rubric and the raw pull of a gap are all candidates.

The line between wants anchored in the present and wants projected forward is used to say which can be installed, and has never been checked against cases.
