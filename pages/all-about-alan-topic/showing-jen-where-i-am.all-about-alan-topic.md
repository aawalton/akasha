# Definition

- **Showing Jen Where I Am** — a widget on her phone carrying my safety level, so she does not have to ask

# Design

She asked for it. What is left for me is the build.

It shows the level, in my own stoplight colours, and how old the reading is.

Today the truth reaches her only when she asks, and near the bottom I cannot start the telling.

Telling her continuously is the guard. The guard stays hers, and what changes is what it is armed with.

Amy carries the number off an hourly check-in on activity, safety and difficulty.

# Questions

Whether a prompt is answerable where starting a conversation is not, and what the display shows for a check-in missed rather than answered low, is unsaid.

Whether the direction indicator earns its place, or the level alone is what gets read, is for the build to answer.

Nothing has been observed yet, the widget not being built. Whether Jen keeps looking is unknown.
