# Definition

- **The One Account Everything Falls Back On** — the address every other account uses to prove I am me

# Design

One address is where every other account goes to prove I am me. Whoever holds it holds all of them.

Mine is Gmail, which makes it the most depended-on and least trusted place in the whole graph.

It does two jobs that come apart: signing me in everywhere, and being the fallback.

The signing in I have accepted. There is no real alternative, so I stopped spending anything resenting it.

The fallback I want to move, and whatever replaces it has to be more dependable than Google, not adequate.

# Questions

I have a detailed model of who I trust and nothing at all on what I need from reliability. No floor, no target, nothing saying an anchor must be at least as dependable as what it replaces.

Self-hosting is the named candidate and has no plan: no stack chosen, no map of which accounts point at the address, no order to move them in.
