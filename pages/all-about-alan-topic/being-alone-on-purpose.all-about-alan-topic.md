# Definition

- **Being Alone On Purpose** — the try at being alone without being lonely, run as an experiment rather than a conclusion

# Design

Being alone is my historical normal, and the loneliness is the recent departure.

It works if my safety rises, my costs fall, and I stop counting the cost of being social at all.

Attention decides how loud a quiet signal is, and pointing it at the gap is what made me feel it.

For much of my life I was too low to feel the gap at all, so recovering may unmask it rather than cause it.

The strategy holds whether the loneliness is manufactured or real, so I need not settle which first.

# Questions

If the loneliness keeps rising as I recover even with attention off it, it is real and growing. If it goes quiet, I made it.

Whether other signals are loud mostly because I am watching them, so that looking away beats meeting the need, is unexplored.

The three readouts, safety and cost multipliers and whether the counting goes quiet, have not been tracked against the experiment.

Pulling back attention works as recovery around people. Whether it works wherever attention goes is untested.
