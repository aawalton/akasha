# Definition

- **How I Actually Breathe** — the breathing practice I run underneath everything else.

# Design

Four counts in and twelve out, most of my waking day.

The counting is what makes it meditative rather than only slow.

# Questions

Four-twelve is the only ratio I have run. Whether another one would pay back better is untested.

Whether the one-second beat holds through programming as well as it holds through walking is unchecked.

And what the counting runs on, with nothing to see and nothing to hear, is unknown.

It started with a phone call from Germany and is now most of my waking day, twenty years on. When the cadence settled and when it went continuous is unrecorded.
