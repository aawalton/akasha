# Definition

- **How Well I Can Measure** — how sharply I can read one of my own resources.

# Design

The ladder runs from not knowing a resource exists up to a real number.

# Questions

Safety sits at numbered levels with no numeric scale under them. It has been the limiting resource long enough that further resolution might land. Will the anchors go numeric?

Can a reading go backwards? Does a resource lose resolution when I stop attending to it, or do the anchors hold once they have landed?

And does the ladder work on anything I track outside my resources?
