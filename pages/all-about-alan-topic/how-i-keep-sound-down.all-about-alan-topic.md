# Definition

- **How I Keep Sound Down** — the quiet office, the earbuds, and the noise I put over the top.

# Design

Full cancelling is the default, and I pull one earbud out when I need to hear.

The earbuds hurt after a while, so the office carries the hours they cannot.

Pencils and chalk cost me whatever the volume, and cancelling helps least with exactly those.

# Questions

How many hours I can wear them before the pressure costs more than the noise would is unquantified.

The drop from half my daily cost to rare is anchored at the two ends only, never as a curve against my safety level.

And what the masking noise actually saves, beyond helping a lot, has no number.
