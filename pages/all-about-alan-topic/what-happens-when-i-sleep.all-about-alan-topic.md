# Definition

- **What Happens When I Sleep** — the one state where the whole machine runs, senses and feelings and all

# Design

Asleep I get novel-length stories, half a dozen characters, full settings, and an emotional score under them.

That is the proof the break is at recall and nowhere earlier. The parts that build it are all there.

More of my cortisol at bedtime means more nightmares. That is the whole of what I claim.

I have gone through phases of writing them down on waking, as story ideas.

# Questions

If waking recall is broken, how enough of a dream survives to know it held senses and feelings, and to be written down, is open.

Whether sleep assembles the compound feelings, grief and anxiety and hope, that I can rarely assemble awake, is unprobed.

Whether the nightmare content carries recoverable signal about what my feelings do out of sight, or is noise, is unasked.

A measured bedtime reading against nightmare frequency would turn a long-held observation into something falsifiable.
