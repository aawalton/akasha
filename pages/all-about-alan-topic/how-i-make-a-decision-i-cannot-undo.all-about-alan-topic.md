# Definition

- **How I Make A Decision I Cannot Undo** — building a rubric to stand in for the future I cannot pre-play

# Design

A decision I can undo I simply make, and let the world tell me. The world is my forward model.

One I cannot undo gets a rubric: name the factors, score the candidates, iterate until it all fits.

The rubric stands in for the pre-play I cannot run. Touring a house reads a preference I cannot introspect.

Walking through it gives me data, sometimes on a dimension I had not thought of. It is not a verdict.

Where my whole read and the rubric disagree, the more compact account of the two wins.

# Questions

How I know the iteration is finished, whether there is a test for it or it simply stops when no pair flips, is unstated.

How I turn an unnamed whole-picture verdict into an explicit factor is named as a step and not as a method.

Whether anything tells a signal-bearing read from a noisy one in the moment, before the consistency check catches it, is unprobed.
