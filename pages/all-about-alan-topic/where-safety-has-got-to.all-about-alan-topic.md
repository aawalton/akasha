# Definition

- **Where Safety Has Got To** — how far my safety level has actually come, and what is holding the climb up.

# Design

In late August 2026 I read myself at two to three, still on the lower end.

# Questions

The 2026 forecast, a 3.5 to 4.5 baseline and a climb back to 4 to 5, is falsifiable once the June illness clears. The same check answers whether reclimbing runs faster than the original four-month climb. My August reading of two to three sits below that forecast baseline, and nothing yet says whether the forecast is wrong or the illness has not cleared.

Each step in the one-sigma low unlocks a named class: conflict bearable at 4, criticism bearable at 5, conflict recurringly initiate-able at 4. Read as recovery milestones rather than as numbers.

My stated medians and the recorded figures disagree, and nothing says which quantity each reads.
