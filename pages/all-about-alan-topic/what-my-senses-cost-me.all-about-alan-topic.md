# Definition

- **What My Senses Cost Me** — what sound, light and touch take out of me.

# Design

It moves with how safe I feel rather than staying fixed.

# Questions

The auditory case is worked and anchored. The visual side is inferred from it and has not been measured.

I shift how bright I read at with the time of day without deciding to. Whether that tracks the clock itself, or something under it like how much light I can take or how tired my eyes are, is open.

Whether the sensory-processing label names anything this does not already cover is an open framing question.
