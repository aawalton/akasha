# Definition

- **The Crowd That Has Been Me** — the strangers I have been and will be, and what I feel toward the ones behind me

# Design

Both directions read the same: about half a billion selves each way, sharing one body, meeting at a window seconds wide.

Forward that count is the wall against acting. Backward it carries a feeling.

Grief for the ones who suffered and are gone, gratitude to the ones who kept going so this one is here.

The past normally reaches me as facts on file, the feeling stripped out. That time it did not.

They sit on separate scales, both endless. Equal is the least wrong reading, and they never net.

# Questions

The feeling arrived lit by contrast, off a session that laid out how far I have come. Whether it fades back to facts once the contrast dims, or stays reachable now it has been seen, is untested.

Whether grief and gratitude are one power source or two bears on what the will to live is actually running on.

Reading my survival as an act of love toward the ones behind me reached the record second-hand, and I have not confirmed it in my own words.
