# Definition

- **The Version Of Me I Have Never Met** — my highest self, reachable only at one remove

# Design

The fearless, warm version of me is real, and I have never once held it directly.

The young one I infer from traces left in my map. I cannot remember being him.

The future one is a guess at a level I have never stood on.

Play is the richest route and still a simulation. In the game I am at ten and I come out a fraction higher.

A level of recovery runs to something like a thousand hours, so a four-hour session buys about four thousandths.

# Questions

Whether the carry-over from play grows as my floor rises, so the simulation slowly becomes a partial direct route, is open.

Whether any fourth route reaches that self directly, rather than waiting for recovery to carry me there, is unprobed.

The made-up world beats real life for progress because of three taxes: what the numbers stand for drifts, I have to build every piece myself, and some of it needs technology that does not exist. Whether closing them would take the crown off it is unworked.
