# Definition

- **How Understanding Arrives** — in quakes rather than by degrees, and usually late.

# Design

An idea is held in a word, and the naming is the compression completing.

It settles offline over days, often in sleep, and arrives as clarity.

An unnoticed conflict costs me nothing. A noticed one keeps demanding at full volume until it resolves.

When the first restructuring is not the right model yet, several quakes fire close together.

Nothing teaches like pain. The error that corrects me arrives late and one-sided.

# Questions

When the whole verdict overrules the rubric, how I reverse-engineer it into a named factor is unprobed.

How wide the swings between optimism and pessimism run, whether I want them damped, and whether a stable middle is reachable when the only restoring force is late pain, is open.

When several quakes fire together, whether there is a pattern to how many and how fast, and what says the pressure has discharged rather than gone into a wrong model, is unprobed.
