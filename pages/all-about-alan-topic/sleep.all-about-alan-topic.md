# Definition

- **Sleep** — how I sleep, and what it does for me.

# Design

Nine or ten hours, and I almost always wake without an alarm.

It is the nearest thing to a cure-all I have found.

# Questions

Whether nine or ten hours is what I need or what I currently owe is unknown. If it is a debt it should come down as I recover.

Whether how often I surface between cycles tracks anything, how much I have left or how stressed I am, is unprobed. I treat it as harmless.

Whether the nights that go hard ever cross into a disorder, or stay variation driven by my state, is unclassified.
