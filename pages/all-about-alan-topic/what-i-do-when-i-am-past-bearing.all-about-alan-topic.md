# Definition

- **What I Do When I Am Past Bearing** — the five things my body does once the load goes past what it can carry

# Design

Meltdown and shutdown name what it looks like from outside. Underneath is fight, flight, freeze, fawn or flop.

The first four run hot, at minus one or worse, and in the moment I cannot tell them apart.

Flop is the body giving up at minus two, and fires only where I see no way out at all. Half a dozen times in my life.

A flop day swings rather than sitting flat, up when a way out looks possible and back down each time one fails.

Knowing what is happening took the terror out of it.

# Questions

Whether knowing the mechanism helps with the other four the way it helps with flop is unasked.

Only flop has a number. How often each of the others fires is unrecorded.

Coming back out is worked only for flop, where it takes a way forward that holds under load. What helps with the other four, how long it takes and what does not help, is unprobed.

Where pain crosses into the kind that generates longing is unplaced, and whether that moves with my state or only with the pain is unknown.
