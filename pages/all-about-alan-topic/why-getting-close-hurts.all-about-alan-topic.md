# Definition

- **Why Getting Close Hurts** — why the people I have loved longest are the least safe for me to be near

# Design

Two counts run on one person: safety from good hours, damage from corrections meant kindly.

Past a certain closeness the damage outruns the safety, one bad hour needing about ten good ones.

The injury needs my capacity low and their best behaviour dropped, and dropping it is what closeness is.

The first-contact edges wore smooth across thousands of people. The deep ones are one per person.

None of mine has escaped it, and the two who came back only outlasted the last injury.

# Questions

Whether that damage is the main driver of what connection costs me, or a minor one beside the rest, only I can settle.

Closeness is also what makes a judge inescapable. Two models put it at the centre of different harms and nothing joins them.

How far the inescapable-judge line reaches across my other close relationships is undeveloped.

Whether I am bad at connection or only at its emotional channel is a reframe put to me and never confirmed.
