# Definition

- **What Still Fires Off A Memory** — the old reflexes a remembered thing still sets off, when nothing else replays

# Design

A taste I cannot bring back still crinkles my face, at about a fifth of the strength.

# Questions

Which other reflexes fire off a memory the way disgust does, startle or gag or salivation, needs a signal someone outside can see. A quiet one can fire below my floor and read as nothing.

Whether the reflex reaching sight, texture and sound as well fits one old subsystem, or means it is broader than not eating what would make me sick, is unprobed.
