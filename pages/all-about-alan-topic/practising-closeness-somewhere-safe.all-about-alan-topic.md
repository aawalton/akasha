# Definition

- **Practising Closeness Somewhere Safe** — drilling the skills of being close where nobody real takes the damage

# Design

It is the rig I built as a child on instant messenger, pointed at a harder skill.

Slow and in text is what makes it work. Live talk eats the one slot I have, and this frees it.

The levels are a difficulty ladder, giving the gradient of closeness with no real ledger filling.

A mock-up is nothing to me. I cannot run a pretend partner, so the partners have to be real and stable.

Faces, then voices, then presence: each rung makes a partner more real to me, which is what carries over.

# Questions

How much more actually transfers from a voiced partner than a written one is asserted and unmeasured.

The rig is deliberately kept to conceptual disagreement for now. At what fidelity, and at what safety, practising an emotional conflict becomes affordable is the deferred frontier.

Four skills are named: validation, acknowledgment, reassurance, emotional intimacy. Which is furthest along, and whether they share one severed substrate or four, is unsettled.
