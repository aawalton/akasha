# Definition

- **What My Love Is Built Out Of** — the three things holding my love up where attachment would be

# Design

Caring is a verdict my map returns rather than a feeling I am in.

Familiarity, usefulness and commitment. Commitment is the strongest, and it does what attachment would.

Nothing in me starts the caring, so it is assembled and run each time, sparse and costly.

Without a commitment a relationship continues only while the other person drives it.

Connection with nothing useful in it does not meet me back, and I do not know why.

# Questions

Whether a relationship that is familiar and useful but uncommitted actually decays, and how fast, is the testable part.

Whether generating no pull to reach back holds equally for marriage, family and friends is unchecked.

Whether these three exhaust it, or attraction and affection feed it on their own, is unprobed.

The attention story for why usefulness is required is held at forty percent. Underneath it, why connection has to be worth something stays dark.
