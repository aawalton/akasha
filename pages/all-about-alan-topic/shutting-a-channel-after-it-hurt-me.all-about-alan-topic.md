# Definition

- **Shutting A Channel After It Hurt Me** — a checkable act that tells my body the thing cannot reach me again

# Design

What holds the tail open is my system still scanning for the thing to come back.

So I close the channel in a way I can check: after a bad slip, the app comes off the phone.

It is structural rather than willpower. What changes the scanning is being able to verify it is gone.

It counts only if it is specific and repeatable, checkable against the world, and undoable only on purpose.

I will be more careful is not one. It changes no state I can look at.

# Questions

How much of the tail this actually saves has never been compared against the same kind of event without it.

Only one channel has a worked shape. News, chats and a conversation that lands badly each need their own, and blocking or muting is weaker than deleting.

What closing looks like where the channel has to stay open, as with someone I live with, is unanswered.
