# Definition

- **What Pulls Me Into Doing Something** — the two ways a thing gets me moving, one I feel and one I reason out.

# Design

One channel runs on how familiar and how new a thing is, and I only notice it afterwards.

The other runs on importance, and it is the one I can use on purpose.

The instinctive one wins when it fires, so I take urgency out of my surroundings rather than trying to override it.

Familiar alone or new alone is not enough. Both have to fire together.

# Questions

What decides when interest crosses into actually starting is unworked, and whether both channels cross at the same point is open.

The instinctive channel is only visible afterwards. Whether a body cue or a time of day gives it away earlier is unprobed.

Where interest sits in the resource accounting is unsettled, whether it spends mana or stamina or sits outside them.

Whether one dimension fires it everywhere, or which one leads shifts by context, rests on a single case.
