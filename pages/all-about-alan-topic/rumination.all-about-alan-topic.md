# Definition

- **Rumination** — a thought my mind grabs and grinds when I go to sleep stressed.

# Design

Stress starts it, not an idle mind. The worry is the stress finding a face to wear.

It grinds only where the thought is important, unsolved and open. Anything else rides along and fades.

It is conscious focus, so it holds me awake, and the grip releases the moment my attention moves off it.

A low-grade anchor crowds it out. Doing nothing does not.

# Questions

Whether the problem it grabs is the day's most unresolved one, or any open problem of the right size, is unprobed.

Whether part of what my agent harness does is pre-empt this, by keeping important open problems outside my head, is floated and unconfirmed.
