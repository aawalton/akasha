# Definition

- **Tight Clothes** — tight everything, so I know where my body is and stop noticing fabric.

# Design

Tight is the default day and night, and on a low day I swap the outer layers to loose.

It never stops working, though I stop noticing it.

# Questions

What counts as tight enough, and what counts as too tight even by default, has no boundary.

Fabric stretches with wear, and when a garment stops holding the floor is unnamed.

Ten to twenty-five percent of my load is a wide range, and no controlled comparison has narrowed it.
