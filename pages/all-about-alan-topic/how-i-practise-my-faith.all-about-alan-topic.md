# Definition

- **How I Practise My Faith** — prayer, worship, ordinances and attendance, as things I actually do

# Design

Church is a judgment setting with survival stakes, and the judgment there always runs through people rather than through a felt God.

# Questions

Whether any worship actually moves me is uncaptured.

The mission, worthiness and leadership — bishop, calling, temple — and the congregation settings including talks and singing are three areas with almost nothing written.

Ordinances, covenants, the sacrament, tithing and the priesthood are blank: whether any of them lands as practice rather than as a claim about what is true is uncaptured.

What my observance and attendance currently are is unwritten.
