# Definition

- **What Has Kept Me Here** — the arguments that have stopped me, and how they changed while the longing did not

# Design

At seven I planned to die before I could be held accountable, and stopped because I doubted my own certainty: clear enough to reason it out meant probably accountable already.

What holds now is moral rather than epistemic, and the shape is the same. A cold argument I cannot get round, never the longing lifting.

The closest I came was two hours up a mountain in May, at about fifteen percent.

The part of me that cared how broken the world is jumped that day, and the rest of me came down.

# Questions

What held across the long stretch between seven and now, and whether each guardrail handed over cleanly, is uncharacterised.

A quiet tenth of me carried me. Calling that a will to live is a label nobody has settled.

How often the longing fires now, and whether the guardrail is ever tested in the present rather than only historically, is unmeasured.

Grief and gratitude are both named as what powers my will to live. Whether they are two sources or one under two names is unsettled.
