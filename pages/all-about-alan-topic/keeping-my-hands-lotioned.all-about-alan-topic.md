# Definition

- **Keeping My Hands Lotioned** — holding a layer on my hands and putting it back whenever it comes off.

# Design

Dry skin costs me even when I am touching nothing, so the layer is maintained rather than reached for.

Every handwash strips it, and I put it back about five minutes later, once the skin is dry enough to hold it.

Bottles in more than a dozen places, all the same brand.

# Questions

Which brand, and what about its texture or its scent makes it the one, is unrecorded.

Which rooms hold a bottle, how they were chosen, and what happens when one runs out is uncaptured.

Why five minutes rather than two or ten, whether that is skin biology or just where I landed, is unasked.

This shape has no relief direction, unlike sound and clothes. The disruption is done to me rather than chosen. Whether that refines the one cost mechanism or is a second shape of its own is open.
