# Definition

- **When I Lost Who I Was** — the collapse that took most of my identities, and what it left.

# Design

At least eighty percent of my identities fell at once, twelve of them named.

Every close tie but Jen ran through the company, so the firing took the work and the people in one stroke.

Being fired broke my sense of self, and it was worse than any physical beating.

Talking to the one friend still on good terms costs about two days in bed afterwards.

# Questions

Which identities survived, and how they were rebuilt or replaced after, is uncaptured. The record stops at what fell.

Whether the relationship with Nick has a repair path, and whether I want one, is unsettled and paced: asking only where it stands brought me near tears.

What the minimal-dose breakfast cost, whether the controls held and what the aftershock looked like, was never written down.

Whether the firing carries the fresh-encoding signature, and what cues set it off, is a residual.
