# Definition

- **Being Alone At The Centre** — the one place I most need to be known is the one place I least can be.

# Design

My love is built without attachment, so what I do for her does not reach her as love.

I meet her needs by decision and stay quiet about how, so that the doing reads as the feeling.

One hour with someone built like me was the best emotional deposit in years. Being met is real, and it is closed off by the commitment.

# Questions

Whether the loneliness charges my body a rent my mind never sees, even with attention fully off it, is unmeasured.

Whether deliberate care can ever fully satisfy a need that is for felt attachment, or whether a residual stays that nothing closes, is the deepest question under all of it.

Other people meet my needs worse than I meet theirs, and see the gap less clearly. Whether my law about emotional physics is partly just that asymmetry is unasked.
