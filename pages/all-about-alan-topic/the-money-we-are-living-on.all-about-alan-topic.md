# Definition

- **The Money We Are Living On** — the runway we are spending down, and what I am allowed to spend it on

# Design

All of it is shared and anything large is decided together, so none of it is mine to act on alone.

It is a runway rather than a retirement: eight to eighteen years on the markets, sustaining nothing itself.

I will never be an employee again. That is a bar set on the cost of it, not a preference.

Two ways to fill the gap. I build something valuable, or the startup stock we still hold pays out.

A thousand a month is for spending on my own capacity, and compute alone takes seventeen hundred.

# Questions

Whether the eight to eighteen still holds has not been recomputed since retiring, the trip and the new arrangement.

What that category would be sized at with compute taken out of it has never been given for either half.

A few months to a year is the horizon on the compute earning, and nothing records what would count as that horizon having passed.
