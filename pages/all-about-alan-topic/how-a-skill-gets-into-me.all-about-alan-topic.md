# Definition

- **How A Skill Gets Into Me** — the two places a skill can land in me, and what it takes to put one there

# Design

A skill lands as a trained weight or as a concept, never as a memory of the room I learned it in.

That missing layer is why a skill costs so much to build and why it carries anywhere once it is built.

Thinking about something outside the moment is training rather than idling. It moves the weights.

In the moment I run on what is already trained, plus whatever I can read off the scaffolding around me.

The count is per small pattern. A fluent skill is a stack of them at different stages.

# Questions

Whether writing trains the response weights better than thinking alone is an experiment I have not run.

The least motion that still trains a motor pattern is unknown. Fingering without playing is my lower bound.

Whether my resource state decides which of the modes is even available is unasked.

Whether interest firing on one small pattern aims the offline rehearsal at that gap is open.

Speed reading worked and why is unrecorded: conceptual mode, low subvocalising, or concepts off a chunk.
