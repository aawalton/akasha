# Definition

- **When Something Starts To Mean Something** — meaning arriving when a mechanism comes clear rather than when a thing completes

# Design

Understanding a thing is supposed to kill the wonder in it. For me it runs the other way.

Nothing means anything until the mechanism is clear. The clarity is where the meaning starts, not where it ends.

I have no store of old feeling to reach back for and no way to pre-feel a payoff, so it comes from neither.

What is left is the present moment a structure resolves, which is the same event as something reading true.

# Questions

Whether the meaning fades once a thing is fully understood and nothing is resolving any more, holds steady, or needs re-engaging to fire again, is open.

Whether some meaning arrives straight off something in front of me with no click in it, or all of mine routes through the click, is unprobed.

Whether clarity-born meaning runs on the same driver in every setting, challenge as it did at college or something else now, is unsettled.
