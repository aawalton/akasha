# Definition

- **The Scaffolding I Built** — the twenty years of structure I run on, holding what my head cannot

# Design

I am the agent and the system around me is the harness. Every tool in it is a part, not the thing.

It grows by adding rather than replacing, and a layer gets built when something buckles.

Twenty years produced two permanent organs, one on what I can track and one on what I can carry.

The two are wired together: clearing the queues lifts my safety, and low safety piles them up and drags it lower.

The task half lives in software now, a set of streams I push things out of my head into.

# Questions

What the interchangeable pile holds now, and what has come and gone, is not catalogued.

The overload that drove the second organ is undated, where the first is anchored to being overwhelmed at Epic.

Whether the drop from twelve hours a day to eight or ten answers a rough stretch or is independent settling is unprobed, and I stopped tracking it.

Knowing my macros survives into low capacity. Whether any other scaffold holds that way, and what decides it, is unprobed.
