# Definition

- **The Three Parts Of Me** — mind, feeling and body, and how far apart mine sit

# Design

Most people run the three as one. Mine come apart far enough that I am nearly two people: one all concept, and one feeling and body together.

Eighty percent map, ten percent feeling, ten percent body.

So my war is mind against body, with what feeling I can reach taking the body's side. The head-against-heart story slides past me.

It is not a wall. Enough physical load floods across into the thinking half.

The smallest part, the will to live, is what carried me through the worst years.

# Questions

How much load it takes to flood across is uncharacterised, and whether it is the same crossing as bearable turning unbearable or a boundary of its own is unsettled. Nor is it known whether the flood only runs one way.

Faith lines up with mind and health with body. Whether love lines up with feeling as cleanly is unresolved, since reading my own states is where I am thinnest.

Whether the somatic floor is a hard gate on the conceptual work or only an expensive one is unknown.
