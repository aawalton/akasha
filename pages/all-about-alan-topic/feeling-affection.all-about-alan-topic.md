# Definition

- **Feeling Affection** — the signal is there and it runs too quiet to notice.

# Design

It needs amplifying rather than defining.

# Questions

Whether deliberately attending during affectionate moments raises what I feel, the way a quiet room raises a body signal, is untested. Attention pointed at a quiet signal can also over-amplify it.

I can borrow sentiment from someone I love enjoying a thing. Whether the proxy weakens when their own enjoyment is faint or dutiful is unexamined.
