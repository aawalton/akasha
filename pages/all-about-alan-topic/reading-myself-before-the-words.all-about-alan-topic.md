# Definition

- **Reading Myself Before The Words** — the signals I get before anything has resolved into words.

# Design

A hunch points a direction with low accuracy. Dissatisfaction pushes, where dislike just sits there flat.

# Questions

How reliably I can read either of them before it resolves into words, given how little of my feeling reaches me, is open.

When an emotion word comes up I either read a tell in myself or match the word to its definition. Whether generosity and forward-going care ever feel different from cold duty, or are always the second, is unresolved.

Which other pairs of states a loose self-report collapses is uncollected, and whether naming a feeling sharpens with use the way a hunch does is untested.
