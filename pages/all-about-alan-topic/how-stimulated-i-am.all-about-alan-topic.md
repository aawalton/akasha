# Definition

- **How Stimulated I Am** — how wound up or how flat I am, which I keep in a band by hand.

# Design

Nothing levels it for me, so I run the loop by hand: a reading, then a knob.

The knobs are the game, the music and the room light.

Wandering focus says I am out of band, and how the wandering feels says which way.

Cold means I am overstimulated and hot means I am understimulated.

My safety level sets how wide the band is, and it is still narrow.

# Questions

How much of the remaining narrowness widens as I recover, and how much is simply how I am built, is unknown. I read it as still recovering.

The extra wiring in my nervous system may raise what it can carry, or only what it costs to run, and which is unsettled.

Whether walking, temperature, conversation or the breathing are knobs in the same loop, and how they rank, is open.

Nothing instruments the band. No measured stand-in for arousal has been read against being in it or out of it.
