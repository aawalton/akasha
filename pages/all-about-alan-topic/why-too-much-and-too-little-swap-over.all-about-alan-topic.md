# Definition

- **Why Too Much And Too Little Swap Over** — the same load reading as overwhelming or as empty depending on what I have left

# Design

My wiring carries more than most: more connections, less pruned away. That part does not change.

What changes is whether the metering sitting on top of it is working.

Low on resources nothing meters, and the same traffic jams. The jam is the overwhelm.

High on resources it clears so cleanly the day can feel empty at exactly the same load.

The metering runs warm, which is why cold reads as too much and hot reads as too little.

# Questions

I take one neurotype to sit under both diagnoses, with autism and ADHD as surface presentations of it. What would confirm or falsify that, and whether the two ever come apart cleanly, is unprobed.

Whether the extra wiring raises my ceiling too, so there is more attention available when the metering holds, or only changes what it draws at rest, is open.

What actually fails in the metering when I am low is unpinned.
