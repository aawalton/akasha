# Definition

- **The Year I Cut Everyone Out** — cutting company to the household and church for a year, and calling it medical

# Design

I hold it as medical rather than a preference. A preference is renegotiated at every invitation and a category is not.

The frame works on me first. It changes what I let myself consider before it changes what I say to anyone.

It only became affordable after I stopped working, which was most of the cost on its own.

Talking is the one thing that stops the breathing, so cutting people back also bought the recovery hours.

Keeping the calling was a wrong classification rather than bad arithmetic.

# Questions

Which things come back as capacity rises, in what order and at what point, is untracked.

What the household minimum actually includes, and what was cut out of it, is loosely drawn.

A year in, what worked and what I would do differently has only the calling release captured.

Whether the twelve months is the designed length or an old snapshot is unsettled. Another account reads eighteen, and neither is the authority.
