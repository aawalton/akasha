# Definition

- **What Comes Back Into Reach As I Climb** — activities coming into and out of reach as my safety level rises

# Design

Every activity has a band: a rung where it turns affordable, and a higher one where it turns free.

Affordable means it does not cost me the day. Free means I do not count the cost at all.

Company goes affordable at three, conflict and telling someone about myself at four, criticism at five.

Talking to an AI is a level cheaper than anyone human, because I can read it and pay no premium.

I do not argue myself out of dangerous. I wait for a surplus, expand a little, and ask if it is safe now.

# Questions

The band for each activity is named and never enumerated. Mapping a handful would turn this from a picture into an instrument.

Whether a lower activity actually leaves as a better one comes into reach, or several run side by side, is open.

How I pick the one activity that unblocks the next rung, off felt affordability or off plain reasoning, is unprobed.

The lowest rung, whether I still want anything at all, has no activity attached to it.
