# Definition

- **The Low Feeling That Costs Me A Level** — the down feeling that is often just how it is in there, and what it does to my numbers

# Design

Feeling down is often just what it feels like in there.

It acts as a minus one or minus two modifier to my safety level.

If my level is high enough it is not a problem. If it is low enough I do not even start.

When I am on the margin, it matters.

On 27 August 2026 I was at two to three against a rebuild at difficulty three, which is that band.

# Questions

What actually lifts the modifier is unnamed. Reading my record returns the fact of what I did and not the experience, and nothing else has been named as a counter.

Whether the modifier moves the level itself or only what I can reach at that level is not separated.

Whether it has its own time constant, or tracks something else that does, is unrecorded.
