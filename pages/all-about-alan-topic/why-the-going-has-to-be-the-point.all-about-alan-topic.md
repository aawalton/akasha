# Definition

- **Why The Going Has To Be The Point** — why a long piece of work only works if the going itself is worth being in

# Design

A long piece of work is lived by a chain of me, and only one is there when it pays off.

So the going has to be worth being in, or nearly every one of them did the work for nothing.

A goal rewritten moves the end into today: not retire, but more freedom tomorrow than I had today.

Most of twenty years of work was drudgery I did believing my needs came last. The rule is the repair.

I cannot reach those selves, so the mourning goes forward into treating the ones I can reach better.

# Questions

What better beyond choice the present and future ones are owed, and how choice cashes out across the different parts of my life, is open.

Whether grieving a self who is not real to me is a present response to the shape of what they went through, rather than sadness felt again, is unprobed.

Whether the drudgery was spread evenly across the twenty years or concentrated in stretches, and whether the rule maps to one turning point, is open.
