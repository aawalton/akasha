# Definition

- **Building My Wants Into The Room** — authoring what surrounds me, because only what I can see comes to mind

# Design

What surrounds me is my memory and my agenda. What is in it can come to mind and what is not cannot.

Out of sight mostly stops existing. There is no quiet backlog for a dropped intention to return from.

So I take cues out to quiet a want: no visible clocks, and very little in the room.

And I put cues in to fire one: the weight set moved into my office bought forty workouts in two months.

I keep the scattering cues out. Something that fires me in ten directions authors my attention for me.

# Questions

As my surroundings widen again after the years I could not afford them, what gets a standing cue first, and what orders the rebuild, is unsettled.

Not everything that fell out should come back, and the rule for which vanished wants earn a cue again is unprobed.

What it costs to keep a whole agenda standing in cues, and what becomes of that upkeep when my capacity drops, is open.
