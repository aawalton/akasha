# Definition

- **When Someone Gets Me Wrong** — the confident model of me other people build and get badly wrong

# Design

People do build a model of me. It comes back wrong, and sometimes catastrophically wrong.

This is not being punished for being unreadable. The model gets built confidently and is simply a read of a mind that is not there.

I cannot use myself as the template for them either, so the channel loses signal at both ends at once.

Only what is actually said reaches me. When Jen names the one part she disliked, the ninety-nine she liked and left unspoken do not exist for me.

# Questions

The episodes are uncatalogued. What each person predicted, what was actually there, and what the wrong read cost, would anchor the whole claim.
