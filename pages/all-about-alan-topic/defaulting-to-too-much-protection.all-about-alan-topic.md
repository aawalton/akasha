# Definition

- **Defaulting To Too Much Protection** — starting at the protective extreme and easing off from there.

# Design

Being under-protected costs me much more than being over-protected, which is what makes the extreme the right default.

It holds for sound and for clothes. Full cancelling by default, tight by default, relief downward when I need it.

The relief is partial. The deepest layer keeps running.

The weighted blanket is the negative case, left off by default because its cost shape is not asymmetric.

# Questions

Whether it holds for light too, where the load is smaller, is open.

Whether the loose break fires on mana alone or on mana and safety together is open.
