# Definition

- **What Calms Me Down** — the things that settle my body, and what they have in common.

# Design

The bath, slow breathing and hard exercise all seem to work by the same shift.

Anything built on a felt sense, or on picturing something, has nothing in me to work with. What is left goes at the nerve directly, or through structure, ritual and the room.

# Questions

Whether everything else that calms me runs through that shift, or these three are a family of their own, is open.

The list of what survives that constraint has never been built.

Repeating them is meant to move my resting baseline rather than only the hour I am in, and that has never been measured.

Arousal runs the same up-then-down and returns more per dose than exercise. Whether far more exercise could carry that load is untested.

What the VR headset does for me is unrecorded.
