# Definition

- **Playing The Long Game** — starting slower than everyone else and ending higher, and picking games long enough for that

# Design

I do not break even against other people until about five thousand goes at a thing.

Below that they look permanently better. Past it their slope flattens and mine keeps climbing.

It is a different curve rather than a delayed one, so late bloomer undersells it.

Ten years spent learning how to learn is what made twenty years of building the harness possible.

Picking my battles holds, but the test is how long the horizon runs, not what I love.

# Questions

Whether the five-thousand figure was measured against anything or estimated from watching myself is unanchored, and how the crossover looks from outside is undescribed.

Speed reading worked where the memory palace could not, and why is on record as a result with no mechanism.

Which parts of my life the strategy is actually running in has never been listed out.

What ended the learning decade and started the harness one, whether the meta-skill was judged enough or life forced it, is unasked.
