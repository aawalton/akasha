# Definition

- **How Far Back I Expect To Come** — the shape of my recovery, and how little of it I can see ahead

# Design

Sixteen months into an expected three to five years, and that figure comes from other autists rather than from me.

I can see the next half step and no further, and only once I am close to it.

So whether the climb holds its rate, levels off or softens shows up one half step at a time.

What moved the curve was not the naming but the better things to try that the naming made findable.

Programming came back the moment safety crossed into being able to work, and it stuck at once.

# Questions

Whose recoveries the three-to-five-year figure actually rests on has never been named.

Whether seeing only the next half step holds across everything I track or only for safety is open.

Where the envelope breaks is uncharted: which stressors would put me back into deficit, and how far.

Whether the climb is the fall run backwards, and what my measured trajectory shows, is unworked.

Three figures about the fall and the climb were woven from a capture and I have never confirmed them.
