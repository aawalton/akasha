# Definition

- **The Things I Never Weigh** — obligations filed as compulsory, so what they cost never reaches them

# Design

Some obligations never reach the sums. They are filed as must, and the sums run on what is left.

The filing came from family, church and culture. Nobody chose it, so it stopped looking like a filing.

No amount of better budgeting reaches a thing filed that way. The filing has to open first.

I open one by saying it aloud: I treat this as a must because. Where the because is I always have, it moves.

Asking to be released from my church calling is the case. Once it opened, the sums cut it.

# Questions

What I have already refiled, what is still a must and could move, and what is correctly a must, was never inventoried.

What actually makes one open, cost evidence or an outside push or simply naming it enough times, is unprobed.

The gate runs both ways, and which things should become a must again as my capacity rises is unasked.
