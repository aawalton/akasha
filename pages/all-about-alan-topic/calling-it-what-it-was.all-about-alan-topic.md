# Definition

- **Calling It What It Was** — coming to name what happened at home as abuse, and what the naming does

# Design

The recognition came as an arc: cared for, then neglected, then abused.

# Questions

What that recognition felt like is nowhere in my own words.

Why the care sealed the abuse over more strongly than it sealed the neglect is unanswered.

What the standard extent for my generation includes and excludes, normal for the era against abuse by today's naming, without collapsing either into the other, is unsettled.

Whether naming it changes the story of how the perfection and people-aren't-safe verdicts were installed is mine to answer rather than anyone's to reason out.
