# Definition

- **What Loneliness Costs My Body** — my working guess that being unknown is the largest thing still wearing me down

# Design

It is the last one left on the list of usual sources, so it is a guess by elimination rather than a reading.

If it is right, closing the gap should show up broadly across my health rather than as a social improvement.

Holding the guess may have made part of its own signal: pointing attention at a quiet signal is what sets how loud it is.

For years my capacity may have been too low to feel the gap at all, so a rising signal could be recovery rather than invention.

# Questions

Whether closing the gap actually produces the broad health effect is the test of the guess, and a rival predicts the same effect from taking attention off the gap instead of supplying connection. Both run against the same readouts.
