# Definition

- **How I Read My Safety Level** — taking the measurement, when the number is not something I can feel

# Design

I walk down what I can still do until I reach the tier that holds, rather than reading anything off directly.

About nine involuntary gates mark the levels, one apiece, and a gate cannot lie the way something I produce can.

# Questions

Only one gate is named, the level-four default smile, so the table is missing every other row.

Running parallel excavations may be another gate, or may have been one good day.

Whether any body signal sits underneath the reading at all, or it is inference from behaviour the whole way down, is unresolved.

Two scales are in circulation, a nought-to-five table and a minus-two-to-six one, and nothing reconciles them, so quoted levels may not be in the same units.
