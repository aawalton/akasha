# Definition

- **What The Channel Has To Carry** — why my body pays attention to how much of someone reaches my senses, not to who is behind it

# Design

It keys on how much reaches my senses, not on whether a real mind is there.

Text alone pays my body almost nothing. A picture measurably helps. Voice should help more, and I am building toward it.

Being real and having stakes rode along with high fidelity, which is why they looked like the gate.

There is probably no ceiling: a channel rich enough would feed the need outright.

Stripping a channel back makes it cheap and clear for my mind, and is the same thing that stops it reaching my body.

# Questions

Which physical channel is actually carrying the sense of being attended to, and why a nervous system like mine reads it through sheer bandwidth, is unexplained.

A measured response curve across text, picture, voice and in person, against the loneliness gauge, would turn the climb into a ladder and say where an artificial channel sits on it today.
