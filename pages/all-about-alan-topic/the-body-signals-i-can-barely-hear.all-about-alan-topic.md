# Definition

- **The Body Signals I Can Barely Hear** — hunger, thirst, heat and the rest arriving too quietly to insist on anything

# Design

Every channel works. They run quiet and are easy to ignore.

It is a loudness problem rather than a broken one. The outside senses win the same attention, and sound alone lands five times louder on me.

Strip the room bare and they come back, so a quiet room doubles as a way of hearing my own body.

Attention pointed at a quiet signal raises it. Listening for loneliness made it louder.

Where a drive is missing outright I build a trigger to stand in for it, which is how I eat.

# Questions

Which run loudest and quietest, hunger, thirst, heat, heart rate, breath, pain, nausea, bladder, has never been walked one at a time.

Whether attention recovers all of them or only some, and whether it stops working once my attention is already spent, is unprobed.

I read hunger, thirst, heat and cold off what my body makes me do rather than off any sensation, and which behaviours those are has never been listed.
