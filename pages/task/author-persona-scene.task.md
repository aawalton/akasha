# Definition

- **Author persona scene** — writing one self-contained scene for one persona and landing it.

# Sequence

1. **The subject and the trial.**
   - **Draw** the subject at random rather than picking one: a slug is a file name up to its first period, so take them from `~/repos/instructions/pages/persona/*.persona.md`, drop every one already written — the slug after the number on each chapter file in `~/repos/stories/pages/story-chapter-written/personas/anthology/` — and take one of the rest with `shuf -n 1`. A subject an agent chose is the subject it found easiest to write, and that is the one it has already written.
   - **Draw** again where the subject's canonical body or face is written as a child's or an adolescent's: a stature described as far smaller than a human's, a face given an age in the teens, a diminutive she is called by. An in-world age in the thousands does not settle it — the scene puts a body on the page, and no sentence about how old she is changes the one being described.
   - **Draw** again where the subject is the persona writing. The author is absent from the narration, and a persona writing her own chapter cannot be absent from it — no `I` outside her dialogue.
   - **Load** the subject from everything her identity lives on: her file at `~/repos/instructions/pages/persona/<slug>.persona.md`, which is the authority on how she speaks and what she is blind to, and the four `<slug>.persona.*.attachment.txt` bodies beside it — portrait, perceive, want and feel — which carry the history her file has no room for. A subject drawn from her voice alone has no past for the scene to touch.
   - **State** what this scene tests before writing a line of it: a device below pushed harder, one dropped, one newly tried. Declare as many of these as the trial needs and no more; where one fails on contact, that is the result rather than a gap to fill in. Prose written against no hypothesis taught nothing.
   - **Name** that variation apart from the standing devices, in the same words stage 3 uses for them. Stage 3 puts every one of those in every scene, so a trial that lists them has described what is always there and tested nothing, and two agents that varied the same thing have to say so in the same words to be comparable at all.

2. **The shape.**
   - **Open** inside the act, with hands already on skin and clothes already coming apart. Whatever the two of them decided was decided before the page starts, and the scene never goes back for it — no arrival, no conversation leading there, no walk to the room.
   - **Spend** every movement on the act: his cock in her hand or her mouth or her cunt, what she does with it next, the two of them coming. Nothing else competes for room, so an economy that would be right in a longer arc reads here as flinching.
   - **End** on the last of them coming, or one movement past it. There is no separate descent and no settle: the pull to write one is the writing flinching from what it has just done, and it arrives disguised as care.
   - **Pace** it in small units: roughly 18 movements of 120–250 words, one physical event or one exchange each, separated by a horizontal rule. What clothing is left comes off across many movements rather than one.
   - **Slow** it. The pull is to speed through because the fast gear is sitting right there, and an act compressed into two movements reads as skipped however hard it hits.

3. **The prose.**
   - **Write** second person present, the camera on her and never inside her — he is `you`, she is `she`, no narrator, no looking back, and nothing of what she thinks or feels except through what she says and what her body does. The tense is load-bearing rather than stylistic: it never asks the reader to recall or picture anything.
   - **Withhold** the next thing his hands are going for — her mouth on his cock, two fingers in her cunt, his hand where she has just moved it from — and name it as withheld: a delay before an act that then happens on the page at full length, never the reason an act never happens at all. Every act the scene sets up is either performed or refused aloud by her. Negation is nearly the whole engine of forward motion here, and it is also the perfect excuse for cutting away.
   - **Take** her occupation as the one vocabulary and let everything she tells him to do be literal in it. Nothing is a metaphor *for* the sex: a metaphor asks the reader to hold two things and translate between them, where this asks him to hold one.
   - **Draw** every comparison from inside the room, and prefer a named object to a word that only suggests a feeling — no *waves*, no *electric*.
   - **Weight** the channels: pressure, temperature, weight, sound and breath to the front, sight to the back — except her face while she comes, and her eyes on his while she does. That is the strongest single channel in the scene: his locked read is that her visible pleasure is what moves him and her posed body is not, so that is the one place sight leads.
   - **Run** two gears — clauses strung on `and` with nothing subordinated, so nothing is ranked and the sentence reads as time passing, against the verbless fragment for speed — and use the fragment rarely, since past a certain point it stops landing and reads as mannerism.
   - **Say** the plain words for the parts and the acts, throughout. `She lifts up and takes you inside her` swerves around the act; `she holds his cock against her cunt and does not put it in yet` is the sentence to write. Naming the deltoid and the sternum while naming nothing between her legs is what [Name The Act](../domain/arousal.domain.md#name-the-act) forbids, and it reads as restraint from inside the writing.
   - **Silence** most of it. Past the opening movement, no more than one in three carries any speech at all, and a speech longer than two sentences belongs only where she has stopped moving and is making him wait. Her own document says how she talks and the pull is to let her talk that way throughout, which is how a scene came back with her speaking in sixteen movements of nineteen — a scene the reader is listening to rather than in.
   - **Let** her read his involuntary tells aloud — breath, jaw, hands, pulse — in the narration's own tense, so his own state reaches him as information rather than as something he has to produce. Three times across the scene and no more: run oftener than that it stops being her noticing him and becomes a woman commentating on a sex scene, which is the read that came back the first time it was let run. **Invert it once**, late: let him read her, and let her say that nobody does.
   - **Plant** each motif three or four times and reverse the last.
   - **Skip** no time. The scene runs continuous from the first movement to the last, and a cut is where the reader loses track of whose hands are where.

4. **Her identity.**
   - **Carry** the portrait by touch rather than by description — cloth, metal, hair, an animal, the room's tools, as things hands find.
   - **State** an explicit adult anchor where she is canonically petite or refined — an adult woman's history, work and authority, found on the page rather than asserted in a line. That covers a subject who reads young and is written adult; a subject written as a child is refused at stage 1 instead, an anchor being unable to outrank the body the prose is describing.
   - **Use** her own physical tells — what her hands do when she is thinking, where she looks. Those are who she is; one invented here is not.
   - **Reach** into her history for what her file has no room for — where she came from, what it cost her, what she will not say — and let hands find it rather than telling him about it.

5. **The heat, in two passes over the finished draft.**
   - **Run** the first pass for naming. Take each movement in turn, and where it names no part and no act in plain words, rewrite it until it does. Stage 3 already says this and the draft still comes out tamer than it asks, every time, because a draft reads explicit from inside the writing — which is why this is a separate pass with one job rather than a rule to have kept in mind.
   - **Run** the second pass for sensation. On each movement add what is wet, what is tight, what is hot, what sound the two of them make, and what noise he makes that he did not choose to make.
   - **Add** rather than cut in both. Where a movement is thin, expand it: a pass that came out shorter than it went in has made the scene tamer, which is the fault it was run to fix.

6. **The polish, in one run.**
   - **Count** the movements carrying any speech at all. Past one in three, the act is being narrated rather than performed, and nothing inside the writing says so — every line of it was in her voice and earned its place on its own.
   - **Count** the plain words for the parts and the acts, and the movements each act is made of. Zero of the first means the act was never named, and an act given two movements was skipped.
   - **Read** for anything the subject would not say. A slip out of her voice is the failure a reader notices first.
   - **Check** that the verbless fragment has not become a tic, and that clothing, position and who is holding what stay continuous — pronouns included, for animals and objects that pick up a gender partway through.

7. **The file.**
   - **Strip** any internal title block. The page is titled with the subject's name, and a competing heading inside the prose has no home.
   - **Land** the chapter with `ops stories write`, at `pages/story-chapter-written/personas/anthology/<NNNN>-<slug>.story-chapter-written.md` — the number four digits and zero-padded, the name between it and the first period the subject's own slug, and the prose the whole body under the frontmatter.
   - **Carry** `page-type-slug: story-chapter-written`, `title` as the subject's name, `slug` as the file's own stem, `partOf: anthology`, `position` one past the highest chapter already in that folder, `ownLength` as the word count of the body, and `unit: words`. `ownLength` is authored — nothing derives it, and a chapter that omits it stands with no count at all. The `id` is not authored: a file stating none is identified by the id its path names, and `ops stories page-id` writes that id down once the file has landed.

8. **The read.**
   - **Ask** the principal for his read once, before this agent ends. Then stop: a read owed is never chased between agents.
   - **Record** what he says landed in his own words rather than a summary of them. Where no read was given, say so instead of filling the gap with your own impression — a record of the writer's read measures the wrong person.
