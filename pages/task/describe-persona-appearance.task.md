# Definition

- **Describe persona appearance** — writing one persona's appearance paragraph off her anchor image.

# Sequence

1. **What her folder holds.**
   - **List** her folder before reading on. Six of the forty hold the anchor alone, and for those the bullets below about a recipe and about the pictures beside the anchor both stand aside. One `ls` settles every one of them, and a seat that reaches those two bullets before listing has paid for instructions with nothing to apply to. A recipe is sometimes filed under `attempts/MANIFEST.md` instead.
   - **Look** at `~/Personas/<Name>/<name>-anchor.png` before touching anything, and take it as the only evidence of who she is rather than the best of it. A folder beside it holds near misses a seat harmonises rather than discards: one of `nimue`'s compose studies has her hair and her brows and is olive where the anchor is fair, and *warm olive skin* stood in her file until an anchor check caught it.
   - **Read** `<name>-anchor.recipe.txt` where there is one, which most folders do not carry. It carries what no image can — her locked age, the garments, and what was tried and rejected and why. Never put a real person's name in her paragraph — one recipe carried one, and it had pulled her face toward the actress.

2. **The light, before any color of hers is written down.**
   - **`magick` is what this stage runs on.** It and `identify` stand on this workstation and read both a magnification and a pixel value out of an anchor. Python's imaging library is not installed here.
   - **Magnify the whole picture to settle whether anything in it emits light, never to settle what color it is.** A small patch of strongly saturated color reads as emitting at full frame and plainly does not at four times — opaque lashes across an iris, no spill onto lid or cheek. **True emission clips**: a core losing its color toward white with a symmetric halo, and, against an empty background, a warm gradient falling into a region with nothing in it to reflect it.
   - **Where the ambient light explains a cast, the anchor has not settled the value.** Hue on a dark object carries no tell that is independent of the light: a black tank under cyan panels reads navy at 2.5x, so magnifying raises the confidence and not the accuracy. One seat nearly filed that as drift, using the very instrument written to prevent one.
   - **The frame limits what its own light can be, and this needs no assumption about any surface in it.** A green key light scales red down on every surface at once, so anything reading R above G rules one out however green the picture looks — which is also why a cool grey-green iris under warm light is the iris's own, and what let one seat retire *pale silver*. Six facial points on a woman in a forest read R above G, settling her hair at −22 against foliage at −53 as her own pigment.
   - **A surface of known reflectance settles what the light IS, where the bullet above only rules one out.** Cream cloth known to be near-white and measuring flat neutral says the key light is near-neutral, so warm-brown hair beside it is the hair's own — a warm cast under a warm ambient light — the one case nothing else here reaches. The same measurement tells a real flush in her cheek from one the light put there.
   - **A surface lit from behind or within has no known reflectance, whatever it is made of.** The bullet above is what a seat reaches for, and a lampshade meets every word of it — cream cloth, near-white, the only pale thing in the frame. One measured (251,227,147), and taking that reading for white would have pushed a yellow-green iris to exactly the cool grey-green already standing stale in that file.

3. **What the picture shows, described before you reread her paragraph.**
   - **Describe** the anchor before you reread her paragraph. Reading either document first turns the run into a check of what is already written, and an omission her file and her paragraph share survives that check untouched: one persona had been East Asian in every image of her and in no version of either. The voice task has you read her file first, so this order has to be taken deliberately.
   - **Describe in measured terms and not in the manner a surface invites.** The abstract vocabulary for a multi-hued thing is tiny and shared — *iridescent*, *prismatic*, *no two the same* — so two files reach for it independently and land near-verbatim. Described in measured terms, they part: one seat's *prismatic* became *a wedge of yellow-green against a cobalt that goes violet at the pupil*, closing an overlap with the one opal dragon among the forty that nothing had shown her.
   - **A drift finding goes in the hand-back and need not become a sentence in her paragraph.** Five landed appearances carried a line saying that nothing about her glows, and two of those were near-verbatim, because each seat magnified, found no emission, and wrote that in. Nobody assigned it.

4. **Her paragraph.**
   - **Read** her file and her appearance here and not earlier, through `ops read` on `pages/persona/<name>.persona.md` and on `pages/persona-appearance/<name>-appearance.persona-appearance.md`, which hands over the page types bounding them and every document required for them. The write is refused for any of those unread and for her appearance file itself, and dispatched on its own this task names her and nothing else, so nothing else opens them.
   - **Set the paragraph beside the appearance files describing a woman who looks like her, and they are not her voice neighbours.** Resemblance is closed in words rather than in pictures, so the set to read is the landed paragraphs under `pages/persona-appearance/` and not the anchors behind them. The voice task's set is aimed at the ear, so a shared pose is caught there only by luck: one seat dodged `rhia`'s turn-back-over-the-shoulder because she was in that set for unrelated reasons.
   - **Choose** which of her features reach the paragraph and in what order, because that is voice rather than fact. Hair, skin, eyes, clothes and a closing tableau is the house template, and several files follow it — the tableau being where the model's own default reappeared once the summarizing close was banned. Everything her voice is set to binds here as much as in her history: a woman at the abstract end of concreteness who lists seven of her own features has left her own voice.
   - **Write** it in her own voice and in the first person, as she would describe herself: one paragraph, which is all the shape allows. It is not her history, so it samples her in a second mode, and a voice describing differs from a voice addressing someone. End it on something other than a line summarizing what she is.

5. **The hand-back.**
   - **Report** the drift you found between her anchor and what her file already said, and say what you left out of her paragraph. A drift finding belongs in the hand-back rather than in the paragraph, and it reaches whoever dispatched you and nobody else.
