# Definition

- **Review initiative** — settling which of an initiative's intents are met and what moves the rest.

# Sequence

1. **Read the intents against what now stands.**
   - **Settle** each intent by measuring rather than by recalling, and delete every line that now holds. The lines still open are the whole of what the initiative reports, so one settled from memory is the reading nobody else will take.
   - **Leave** every line that still holds exactly as written. A line still open already reports what is missing, and editing that into it turns an intent into a status that starts going stale.
   - **Put** an intent back on its domain where this initiative gives it up unmet. Nothing else holds it, so one dropped here is gone.

2. **Establish what is moving.**
   - **Search** the seat pages for an `initiative-slug:` key naming this initiative. A seat is what carries work against an intent, so an intent no seat states is not moving however alive the initiative reads from its own file.
   - **Check** that a seat page still stands for each seat found. A seat's page goes when no agent is present, so a seat still being worked and one whose agent has left read alike from anywhere else.

3. **Name the blocker for whatever is not moving.**
   - **Separate** blocked from unowned. An intent waiting on another is still progress, and an intent in the wrong initiative is nobody's.
   - **Say** so where the blocker is yours.

4. **The report.**
   - **Offer** what you would do, so the whole of it can be ruled on in one reply.
   - **Send** it to Alan, and wait only where something in it is his to settle. A review whose every intervention follows from intents he already agreed carries no decision, and halting on one spends his turn to be told to carry on.

5. **When no intent is left open.**
   - **Read** the domain against what now stands. An initiative closes on the lines it held, and those are rarely the whole of the gap it was aimed at.
   - **Carry** the closing to Alan before anything is removed, with every intent it held, the reading that resolved it, and whatever that reading left open beside them. He agreed the intents as a proxy for the gap, so he is the one who can see all of them met and the gap still open — and re-aiming an initiative still standing costs less than creating its replacement.
   - **Delete** the initiative last.
