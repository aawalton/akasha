# Definition

- **Challenge words** — asking the author if words could be simpler, plainer, clearer, or shorter without being less clear.

# Sequence

1. **The question, put to the author.**
   - **Ask** if the words could be "simpler, plainer, clearer, or shorter without being less clear".

2. **Only where something caught you.**
   - **Point** to specific places where the words feel not simple, not plain, not clear, or not needed.

3. **Let it go.**
   - **Send** it back with `ops seat send`.
   - **Let** the author decide what to do with the feedback.
