# Definition

- **Ops akasha work-tree** — akasha's work drawn as one tree of initiatives.

# Design

The roots are the initiatives standing under no other, alphabetical by slug.

An initiative is keyed by the slug it declares rather than by the name of its file.

A root project naming no initiative that stands is drawn under the sentinel.

A sentinel nothing falls to is not drawn.

A row a seat states carries that seat's color.
