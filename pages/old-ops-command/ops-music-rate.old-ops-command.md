# Definition

- **Ops music rate** — Alan's rating and capture prose recorded onto one artist or song page.

# Help

Record Alan's read of a song or artist onto its page — the Eppie loop's capture edge. Writes the F→S rating and the interview-capture fields (song: personal-connections / insights; artist: reaction) onto the page's own file, each prose field standing beside it as a large file. Set any subset: a rating without prose, or prose without a rating. At least one field is required.
