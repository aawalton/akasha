Rear foot elevated on a bench or chair, dumbbell held vertically against the chest (goblet). Lower straight down on the front leg until the rear knee approaches the floor, chest tall. Drive through the front heel to stand. Complete all reps on one leg, then switch; count reps as the total across both legs.
