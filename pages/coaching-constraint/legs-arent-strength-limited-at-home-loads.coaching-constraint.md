Hiking base → strong legs; bilateral home-load squats aren't strength-limited (grip/arms + breathing cap goblet work first). Load legs via unilateral work (reverse lunge, split squat, Bulgarian, single-leg RDL, step-ups), higher reps, and tempo/pauses. A weighted vest/ruck is the proposed unlock for true hands-free leg loading.
