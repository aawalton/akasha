Strict wall-slide reaches ~50% to full overhead before losing contact. Don't force full overhead lockout in pressing — it drives a lumbar arch. Lead push with horizontal pressing (DB bench / floor press); do overhead only in pain-free range, ribs-down/braced, or sub an incline press.
