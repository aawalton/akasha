Subliminal asthmatic bronchitis: exerting with particulates in the air can trigger a weeks-long flare. Poor AQI → NO outdoor cardio/exertion, full stop. Check `ops tracking air-quality` before ever recommending outdoor work; on bad-air days pivot to indoor respiratory-safe movement only.
