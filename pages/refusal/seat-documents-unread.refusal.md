# Refusal

You are {attributes}.{assignment} {count} document(s) are required reading for you there that you have not read. One call names every one of them and reads what one answer holds: `ops read --seat`. What clears the refusal is the read record at `{record}`, which `ops read` opens: one entry per path, naming the body it was read at and when. Run that read, open the record to see what landed, then act again.
