# Refusal

`{attribute}` is listed under `attributes-slugs:` by {recorded} and under `writes-slugs:` by {computed}.

A producer writes one half of a node and a deriver the other, so an attribute is recorded or computed and never both — whichever it is, the other list is naming a half nothing writes it to.
