# Refusal

`{key}` does not open with `[supervisor]`, so the fleet's hooks read a supervisor's own restart as Alan at the keyboard: this seat's persona is stamped as the one he last wrote to, and where he is at the terminal the alert tint summoning him clears though he never typed.
