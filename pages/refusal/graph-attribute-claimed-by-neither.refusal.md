# Refusal

`{attribute}` stands and nothing names it: no type lists it under `attributes-slugs:` and no deriver lists it under `writes-slugs:`.

Nothing carries it, so nothing reaches it — either whatever holds it has to name it, or the attribute went when its holder did.
