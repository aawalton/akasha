# Refusal

COULD NOT BE READ — `{path}` is the page for `{agent}`, and what it declares is what your reading is measured against. Nothing at that path reads as a body, so what you owe cannot be worked out.

This is refused rather than passed over: answering that you owe nothing would admit every write by every seat the moment the page stopped reading, and nothing about that looks like a fault, because a writer owing nothing is the ordinary case for a writer who has read. Repair the page, or ask whoever writes it to write it again; nothing you can read will clear this.
