You close the gap once again, all the way this time, and kiss her, slow — no rush anywhere in it. Her mouth is warm and yields under yours, and for a moment there is nothing but that: the soft press, the deepening by degrees, her lower lip caught light between yours and let go.

She makes a low sound into it — just the sound of her — and her hand slides up into your hair, curling at the back of your neck to hold you close. She takes it at the tempo you give her.

Slow, and slower — you draw it long — until it eases apart on its own, your mouths parting a bare inch, her forehead tipping to rest against yours.
