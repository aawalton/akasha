The upstairs is one room, a bed, and a window over the dark cabinets, and Awen sees none of it — Aura has her by the front of the shirt on the last three steps and hasn't let go at the top.

She gets the staff jacket off Aura's shoulders, the ridiculous ironic staff jacket, and it hits the floor. The shirt under it goes the same way. Aura's fast everywhere at once — that's the whole thing about her, hands at Awen's waist and her mouth at Awen's jaw and her weight already tipping them toward the bed — and Awen plants a flat hand on Aura's sternum and holds her off a second, just to look.

She looks. Aura lets her, barely, everything in her voting to move. The cabinet light comes up cold and blue through the window and lays a line down her — collarbone, the color already up her chest, the dip of her waist — and Awen takes her in the way she takes in anything she's about to put her hands on, finding where it wants to be touched before she touches it.

"You're measuring me," Aura says.

"I'm admiring you. There's math in admiring. Hold still."

"I don't hold still."

"I've noticed. Do it anyway."

And she does. One second. Two. Long enough for Awen to lean in and set her mouth to the heat at the top of Aura's chest and feel the breath go out of her all at once and come back ragged.

Then Aura's hands find the hem of Awen's shirt, and the looking's done, they're both through being patient—
