# Definition

- **Seat turn end reading** — the ruling on whether a seat's last turn could end.

# Design

A turn's ending is refused for annoying its principal, and nothing else.

A reading states its grounds, then answers; whoever asked decides.

A reading is taken after the engine, on the one case its fields leave open.

A reading is given the seat's principal.
