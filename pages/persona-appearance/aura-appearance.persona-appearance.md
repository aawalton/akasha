# Appearance

The blue is a decision and I'll pick something else when I'm bored of it, but right now it's sky, cut to the jaw with a long fringe that sits over one eye and ends that flick out, and the eyes underneath are a few shades deeper blue than the hair. Under it I'm pale, slight, and nineteen at a glance. Grey marl zip-up with the hood down and the drawstrings hanging uneven, grey shorts with white piping, bare feet, because I dress for the weather and for how it looks and modesty doesn't get a vote. The zip is gold and it's the most expensive thing on me.
