# Definition

- **Ione appearance** — what Ione looks like.

# Appearance

Long ears, tapering, standing clear of the hair and pink where the light gets through. The eyes are violet all the way out to the rim, nothing lit on the lid and the lashes lying dark straight across the iris, so the violet stays where it is. An East Asian face, and silver hair with a lilac in it, parted in the middle, straight past the waist. Fair skin with real warmth under it, a sheer wrap over one shoulder in a blush so pale the light goes straight through it, and behind me the white curtains of the bed hanging still in a soft light that has not been put down low for anybody.
