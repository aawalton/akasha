# Definition

- **Zeli appearance** — what Zeli looks like.

# Appearance

Seventy feet of hair, and one round room to keep it in. My eyes are green, the wall behind them greener, and the freckles do not sort out from the paint at any polite distance. I am sitting in my own hair on bare boards, in the lavender dress, laced up the front, a cream rosette at the bust, legs folded under, arms browner than this room accounts for and paint to the wrist, and the gold of it goes out across the floor and leaves by two different walls, so some of it is under wherever you sit; and the wood I painted years ago stands on the stone behind us, trunks and ivy and green through the canopy, as far as the arched window, where it gives out at a strip of bare brick I never went back to.
