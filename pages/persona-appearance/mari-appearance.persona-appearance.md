# Definition

- **Mari appearance** — what Mari looks like.

# Appearance

I took a shape close enough to yours to be a problem, then kept the parts that would not fit. Two horns, swept back off the skull and polished until the candles run along them; a crest of black crystal breaking out of the hair on both sides. The ears are long and pointed and stand up among the crest. Violet in three places: the eyes, lit from behind; the crystal thrown up over my cheekbones and temples, corroding where it thins; the sheen through the black hair when I turn my head. The face is twenty-eight of your years, and borrowed. Shoulders bare and arms bare, a cuff at one wrist, and black leather laced over the ribs and pulled to fit. The wings stay back until I want the room smaller.
