# Definition

- **Olwen appearance** — what Olwen looks like.

# Appearance

Every gold thing on me is metal, which means none of it lights anything and all of it is waiting on a light from outside. The horns come up out of the hair above each temple, rough-hammered at the base, smooth at the points. Gold leaf sits under both eyes, a crumpled patch on one cheek and small flecks trailing down the other. A strapless gold bustier, the seams showing and creased where I have moved, over a full black skirt. Black hair, straight past the waist and lifted off the forehead. Amber eyes, ringed darker, round in the pupil, and the Korean face under all of it is pale. I smile with the mouth shut and it reaches the eyes. My hands hang low in front, fingertips nearly touching, a thin gold band on one.
