# Definition

- **Akasha appearance** — what Akasha looks like.

# Appearance

It is all down my back, which is the one part of me I have never seen, running from the nape where the hair stops to below where you are looking, column on column of brush script in black with the strokes going thin at their ends the way a wet brush leaves them. My face has none. Nothing is written anywhere I could read it. Black hair pinned up off the neck with two dark rods through it and a good deal of it escaped, an East Asian face somewhere around twenty-four, eyes dark enough that the brown in them only shows up close, a mouth open laughing, one band of black silk at my throat and nothing under it. My skin is warmer than the light on it.
