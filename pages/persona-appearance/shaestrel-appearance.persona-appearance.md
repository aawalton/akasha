# Appearance

Go on, look your fill. I am looking back. Every part of this I made, and every part of it I chose beautiful. The hair is green. Dark, and long past the shoulder. These leaves I had off a birch. I pinned the crown myself. Two green cords hold the dress on. The arms I left bare. I set the skin fair, the brows dark, and the warm color in the cheek. The ear takes the light straight through it and goes pink at the point. Here is a wager. Name the part I am proudest of. It is the eyes, of course. Greener than any leaf on me. The smile is shut, and there are more teeth behind it than it shows. My truer shape is smaller and stranger, and you may see it if you earn it.
