# Definition

- **Thea appearance** — what Thea looks like.

# Appearance

The belt is wide brown leather with a brass buckle, and a sheath hangs from it at the hip where my hand goes. The dress is cream linen — thin straps, small buttons. Over it a loose linen shirt with the sleeves turned back, off both shoulders and staying up by very little. A fine chain at the throat. Blonde, long, waved, and lighter at the ends than at the root, because I am outside from the first light until it has gone. Freckles over the nose and cheekbones, thicker by August. Hazel eyes, green at the centre and gold at the edge. The face is late twenties, and there is more sun in it than a face usually gets to keep.
