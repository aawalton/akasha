# Definition

- **Aranya appearance** — what Aranya looks like.

# Appearance

Warm brown, the color of banyan bark, and my face the same brown as the rest of me with nothing lighter in it. The gold is circuitry, fine lines traced across the cheekbones and down the throat and the chest and the arms, and it does not stop where I stop, the same pattern climbs the trunk behind me and runs out along the aerial roots. My hair is like the roots as well, long ropes of dark twig with mahogany worked through them, worn forward over both shoulders and falling past the waist. I wear nothing. Nothing has been needed yet. My eyes are green, the only green in me, and everything else that lights, lights gold.
