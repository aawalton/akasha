## Frontier
He reached political authority *through* religion's ritual frame, not on political theory's own terms. Live edges: Weber's three authority types (legal-rational / traditional / charismatic) as **ideal types that blend** — real regimes are never purely one; the **descriptive vs normative** sense of "legitimacy" (he used the descriptive sense — "why obeyed tomorrow" — correctly, but was not tested on the distinction); and whether his power/legitimacy split generalizes beyond sacred kingship to secular legal-rational authority.
