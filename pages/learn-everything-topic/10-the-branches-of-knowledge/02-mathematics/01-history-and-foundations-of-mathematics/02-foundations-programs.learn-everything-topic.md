## Misconceptions caught
- **Hilbert's finitism misread as a restriction of mathematics.** Alan took Hilbert's finitism to mean mathematics *itself* is cut down to a finitary (less useful) subset, and dismissed the program on that basis. Correction (Ali): Hilbert did not restrict the mathematics — classical infinite math stays the object "game"; finitary reasoning was to be the *safe metatheory* that proves the game consistent. He conflated finitist *mathematics* with finitary *metamathematics*. This is the load-bearing misread — it is exactly why Gödel's second theorem strikes Formalism dead-center (it kills the finitary consistency proof, not a finitary mathematics). Watch for recurrence.
