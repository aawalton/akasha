He commands the aesthetic-truth structure generatively — the two-truths independence (literal vs experiential) and the universal-vs-particular distinction — and rederived Aristotle's founding Poetics move cold (a D5 flash). He then extended the theory into a complete chain — all cold, on the structural/conceptual channel, under push:
- **Execution split into means vs end.** Execution as a *means* = how well it carries the aesthetic truth. Execution as an *end* = beauty appreciated for its own sake — including the beauty of clean craft even when the subject is ugly. This is Aristotle's corpse-pleasure observation: we take real pleasure in a skillful depiction of a corpse; the form holds a beauty the subject doesn't.
- **Autonomist position** in the live autonomism-vs-moralism/ethicism debate: beauty and aesthetic truth are independent — "those are separate kinds of beauty and can exist independently." Proved with his signature off-diagonal cells: *beautiful-but-abhorrent* (he can appreciate the aesthetic beauty of works whose meaning he finds abhorrent) and *aligned-but-disappointing* (he can be let down by the beauty of works whose meaning he endorses). Both exist for him → the beauty and truth axes are free.
