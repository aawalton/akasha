## Misconceptions caught
- **Magnetic field from "spinning iron" / magnetized iron** — corrected: a rigidly spinning solid has no relative internal flow, so no sustained currents and no field; the field is a self-exciting **dynamo** (a convecting conductor inducing currents routed into reinforcing loops), with rotation organizing it (Coriolis) rather than causing it. He surfaced the imprecision himself via the wound-generator question.
