## Frontier
The connective tissue that makes Mesoamerica one civilization rather than a list of polities — never probed. The unifying substrate diffused across distinct, non-contemporaneous peoples: the interlocking 260-day ritual and 365-day solar calendars (the 52-year Calendar Round), base-20 bar-and-dot numerals, the rubber ballgame, a shared pantheon (Quetzalcoatl, Tlaloc), and monumental pyramid-building. This is what an assessment of the culture-area-as-a-unit would test.
