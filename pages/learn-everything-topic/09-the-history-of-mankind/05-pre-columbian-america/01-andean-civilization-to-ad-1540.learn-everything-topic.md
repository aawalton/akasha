Same problem, two solutions; the cleanest comparative probe for the depth-then-rotate move off the Mesoamerican thread (`02-meso-american-civilization-to-ad-1540`).

- **Quipu as a non-phonetic external-memory medium ↔ Teotihuacan / Information Theory** (within Part 9, reaching to Part 10) — _archivist-recorded back-edge._ The Teotihuacan leaf (`02-meso-american-civilization-to-ad-1540/04-teotihuacan`, D3) scored on **decoupling the function from the medium** — the job is transfer of information across time and space; phonetic writing is one medium, the Andean **quipu** another (notation as a writing-substitute).
