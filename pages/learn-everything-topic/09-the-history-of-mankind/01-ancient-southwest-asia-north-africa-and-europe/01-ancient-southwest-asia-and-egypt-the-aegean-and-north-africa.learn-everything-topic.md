## Integration
The session's standout: he composed three live frames into one closed loop on fresh ground. **Surplus → specialization → population-scaled technology growth** (his collective-brain / population-technology frame, Henrich): once producers reliably overproduce, a standing class can be freed from getting its own food, and specialization is what that surplus buys. He then closed the loop to **external memory**: transferring surplus across time and space means the *information about* it (how much, where, whose, why) must travel too, and the moment that bookkeeping outruns one mind's reliable hold, it has to be set down outside the mind — writing as administrative external memory, invented for the load that broke the single head. And that last link he tied himself to the morning's **compression / information** thread: the granary is a battery for calories, the tablet a battery for the facts about them — "the same compression-and-storage problem, wearing a clay coat." ## Misconceptions caught
He caught the framing's own loose joints three times, unprompted. (1) He distrusted "simultaneous civilization" before explaining it — vindicated: the pattern resolves into "simultaneous agriculture (global common cause), staggered civilization (regionally-paced downstream consequence)." (2) Offered the flag, he instead pressed "are we certain agriculture came before civilization?" — recalled Göbekli Tepe (hunter-gatherer monumental megaliths, ~9500 BC, pre-farming) and generated the survivorship caveat himself (sedentism makes evidence easier to leave, so the agriculture↔civilization correlation is partly a preservation artifact). The flat claim is false; the real gate for cities/states/writing is a *storable, concentrated, fixed-place surplus* (agriculture just wins every one of those axes at scale), not agriculture per se. (3) He held his **nomadic-storage caveat** against an overstatement that foragers can't store — and was right to: pastoralists store on the hoof, Pacific-NW groups smoked salmon into winter surpluses and got hereditary rank and monumental art without farming.
