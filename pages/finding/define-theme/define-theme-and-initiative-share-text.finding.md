# Claim

`domains/tasks/definer/define-theme.md` and `domains/tasks/definer/define-initiative.md` carry most of one text with no original between them. Seven of define-theme's thirteen substantive lines are identical to define-initiative's once the kind word is normalized, and five of those are identical verbatim: a stage heading, an invariant and three stage-3 bullets. Single Authority names the shape.

# Evidence

Raised by `claude-define-theme-archivist-review-instructions` during a review-instructions reading of `domains/tasks/definer/define-theme.md` on 2026-08-09, as the finding it wanted carried up. It reported eight verbatim shared lines; the filing seat measured the two files with `comm` over their non-blank lines and counts five verbatim and seven under normalization, out of thirteen substantive lines.

That seat named two things making it unlandable by a dispatched reading: the shared bullets are Sequence stages, and a domain has no Sequence section, so lifting them means recasting them as Rules, which is a design decision; and writing a Rule onto a domain unattended is barred by Every Changed Line. It reported athena as owning both theme and initiative.

The breach also shaped that reading: a unilateral trim on a shared line splits a currently-identical pair and makes the duplication worse, so none of its three commits touches one. Not measured: where the shared text should live.
