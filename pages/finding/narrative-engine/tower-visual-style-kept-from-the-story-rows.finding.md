# Claim

The one read story whose `visualStyle` was in real use is Incremental Torture, and its text is kept here because the story rows were deleted without a property to carry it. Every other read story's `visualStyle` was generated rather than chosen, and went with those rows.

# Evidence

`incremental-torture-an-idle-system-tower-climbing` — "Incremental Torture", the tower-climbing idle system story on Royal Road, at `https://www.royalroad.com/fiction/83133/incremental-torture-an-idle-system-tower-climbing`.

Its visual style read:

    grim dungeon-crawl digital art; claustrophobic palette of torch-amber, stone grey,
    and bloody red; flickering firelight against deep shadow; gritty textured rendering;
    oppressive escalating dread

127 of the 146 read story rows carried a `visualStyle`, and no file carries one — no property declares that key on a story. Alan judged that only this one was real and that the rest could go with the rows.

Whatever defines a visual style later can take this text as its first value rather than generating a new one.
