# Claim

Eleven of the forty-one Wandering Inn conditions spell a body part before the condition's own name, so the slot is inside the name and nothing holds it separately.

# Evidence

Measured 2026-08-19, extracting conditions and aspects from the System's own announcement form across the story's 828 chapters.

The System announces a condition as `[Condition – Terrible Hunger Received.]` or `[Condition: Body – Icy Flesh obtained!]`. Forty-one distinct conditions stand across forty-five mentions. Eleven of them carry a part before the name, in four spellings of the separator: `Body – Breath of Jewels`, `Body – Fortune's Scent`, `Body – Greater Resistance: Sickness`, `Body – Oldblood Wings`, `Body – Scales of Opulence`, `Body: Slashing Resistance`, `Body: Solar Storage`, `Body – Icy Flesh`, `Heart: Heroic Lionheart`, `Feet – Nature's Passage (Snow)`, and `Condition (Body): Constitution of the Champion`.

Three parts appear: body, heart and feet. Every other condition names no part at all.

Two further namings sit inside the same field. `Battlefield of the Silent` and `Battlefield of the Silent (Dreams)` are one condition written twice, the parenthesis marking where it holds rather than which condition it is; the sole aspect `Odour of Carrion (Dreams)` carries the same parenthesis. `Plaguesteel` and `Plaguesteel (Minor)` differ by a severity written the same way.

Left as written for now, so a condition's slug carries its part and its qualifier. Nothing computes from either.

Not measured: whether any other story announces conditions, or whether the same parenthesis convention appears on skills and spells.
