# Claim

`dispatch-project` has one stage holding two acts and named for one. Stage 1 is "The authorization"; its second bullet is the dispatch the document is named for. The task schema says a stage names what the seat is doing there, and `define-project.md`'s own invariant already reads this document as two acts. Renaming makes the stage name a restatement of the title; splitting into two stages is an Add every reader pays for at boot.

# Evidence

Raised by the review-instructions reading of 2026-08-07, which landed one commit — the reviewed-at record — and kept every other line. It called this a shape call rather than a defect and left it.

The reading's other keeps, each checked against machinery rather than prose: the two claim-wait statuses and the parent gate against `ops project move-to --help`; `--initiative` as a real flag on `ops seat start`, with "which you alone know" true rather than rhetorical because the initiative is not a key on the project schema and `create`, `show` and `list` name it nowhere; the singleton hand-back corroborated at both ends. I re-ran none of those.

It also recorded why the invariant earns its place: `move-to` gates exactly four custody transfers and `awaiting_worker_claim` is not among them, so nothing in the machinery stops a lead moving an undefined singleton straight to dispatch, and that line is the only thing standing there.
