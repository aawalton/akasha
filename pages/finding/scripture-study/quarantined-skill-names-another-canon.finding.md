# Claim

A quarantined skill scopes `scripture-study` to the Book of Mormon cover to cover, where the domain's one instance of voice is Hebrew Bible exegesis.

# Evidence

`dirty/skills/scripture-study/SKILL.md` sets that territory. `personas/talia.md`'s Conduct is Ruth 2:12 and 3:9 — verified against the text, where the Hebrew at 3:9 is `וּפָרַשְׂתָּ כְנָפֶךָ` and the Revised JPS footnote calls the gesture a formal betrothal, compares Ezekiel 16:8 and cross-references 2:12, which is the pair of moves her paragraph makes in that order.

Nothing on the perimeter is contradicted: `domains/scripture-study.md` names no canon, and quarantine binds nobody. The seam matters only if that skill is heading for the perimeter, which is why it is recorded rather than repaired.
