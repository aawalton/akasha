# Claim

The Definition on `domains/seat-name.md` may lead with purpose and names the lesser of two jobs. It reads "the address composed from what a seat states." `domains/domain-definition.md` says a Definition holds no clause saying what the thing is for, and "address" is what a name is for. It also drops the exclusion — `tools/lib/compose-seat-name.ts` records name distinctness as the whole of what keeps two seats off one row, which is the larger of the two jobs.

# Evidence

Raised by a review-instructions seat on `domains/seat-name.md`, which proposed no wording and said why: every non-circular phrasing it reached either restored a purpose clause or smuggled in the exclusion as a second one. It also flagged that whether "address" counts as a purpose clause at all is a judgment about how that rule is meant to bite.

I verified the Definition reads as quoted and that `domain-definition.md` says a Definition section "holds no clause saying what the thing is for, why it is worth having, or where it sits."

Not measured: whether the exclusion belongs in a Definition or in a Design entry, which is the shape question underneath this.
