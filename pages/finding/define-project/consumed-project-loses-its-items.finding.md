# Claim

Nothing binds a lead to read what a project holds before folding it into another. `domains/tasks/lead/define-project.md` grants the act unguarded — "Combine, split, sequence and close as freely as the work needs" — and the loss is silent by construction, the consuming project's criteria coming out complete against the headline it absorbed. `ops project move-to`'s obligation gate fires at the terminal close but sees only obligations somebody declared.

# Evidence

A standing ruling of 2026-07-27, held in `dirty/skills/agent-harness/rulings/rows.md` and reached by an ingest seat emptying that source. Kept verbatim at `dirty/maybe-keep/skills/agent-harness/rulings/rows.md`; filed here because that copy is queued for its own sweep.

The act is live and named. `define-project.md`'s Invariants: "The shape of the project set is yours to change for as long as it is undispatched. Combine, split, sequence and close as freely as the work needs." The only thing guarded beside it is which two decisions stay Alan's.

THE GATE THAT LOOKS LIKE IT COVERS THIS DOES NOT. `ops project move-to --help` describes an obligation gate firing at the terminal close — `done`, `not_doing` and `duplicate` alike — demanding every obligation be RESOLVED, and says "what this closes is the SILENCE". It can only see obligations somebody DECLARED. A project's own items, never written as obligations, are exactly what a `duplicate` close carries off with nothing reporting it.

Swept all of `domains/` for `combine|fold|absorb|consumed|duplicate|merge|covered by` case-insensitively. The nearest hits are that invariant, `decide-principle-or-rule.md`'s "Stop where the behaviour occurs and standing prose absorbs it downstream" — a different subject — and `define-principle-or-rule.md`'s "name which candidates a merge drew on", which is the same worry about traceability inside one document rather than across rows.

Duplicate check, run as its own call: `rg -uuu` over `~/memory/findings/` for `folding|folded into|consuming row|enumerate`, then `lost|silent` under `findings/project/` and `findings/define-project/`. Nothing carries it.

NOT MEASURED: whether any live project has lost an item this way. The source's specimen — one row holding three items of which two landed, and four live problems surviving a drain because an exclusion list was written with reasons — I did not re-measure; both are readings of 2026-07-27.
