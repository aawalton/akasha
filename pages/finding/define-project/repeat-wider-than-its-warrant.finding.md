# Claim

"Repeat the two stages above" on `domains/tasks/lead/define-project.md` is wider than the warrant beside it, which argues only for the objective: of stage 1's three bullets, one repeats, one does not apply to a child, and one inverts a decision made a bullet earlier.

# Evidence

Measured 2026-08-11, on a `review-instructions` reading of `domains/tasks/lead/define-project.md` dispatched from `review-documents`. The reading traced each of stage 1's bullets against the instruction and left the line standing, the repair being structural.

The warrant argues for repeating the objective, which is stage 2. Against stage 1: the Hold does repeat, though only by inference from the release bullet, which says "every child you create"; Ground does not, a child having no independent ask; and Establish inverts the decision made a bullet earlier.

So the line has two readings that ask for different work — repeat both stages as written, or repeat what the warrant argues for — and neither is settled by anything on the document.

`domains/tasks/archivist/review-instructions.md` sends a line back to the principal where its two readings ask for different things, and calls out that ambiguity is the harder of its two rewrite cases "because it is short and reads clean".

Not measured: whether any run has repeated stage 1 in full, or what a child created by the wider reading would carry that one created by the narrower would not.
