# Claim

`domains/arousal.md` owns a task and lists none. `domains/tasks/scenewright/author-persona-scene.md` declares `domain-owner: arousal`, and seven domains in the corpus carry a `# Tasks` section naming what they own. Every check and gate passes over the absence, so nothing but a reader arriving at the domain and finding no route to its task reports it.

# Evidence

Raised by the dispatched reviewer of `domains/arousal.md` on 2026-08-07 and relayed here unjudged. It named this one deliberately rather than burying it, because unlike the rest of its carries a `# Tasks` section is not reserved by Every Changed Line and someone could land it.

Its reason for not landing it: `domains/roles/scenewright.md` already glosses the task, and a second gloss is a copy that can drift — which is Single Authority on `domains/agent-harness.md` cutting against the entry.

The reviewer reported `ops instructions run-checks` naming this document nowhere, `terms-in-reach` included, and `run-gates` passing 12 over the file.

Not measured: how many domains that own a task list it against how many do not. "Seven domains" is the reviewer's count of domains carrying the section at all, not a denominator.
