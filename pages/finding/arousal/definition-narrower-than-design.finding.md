# Claim

The Definition of `domains/arousal.md` is narrower than its own Design. The Definition scopes the domain to "how it is written", while the Design says prose is "the main way" rather than the only one. The Definition goes false the day something other than prose lands in this domain, and nothing rechecks it.

# Evidence

Raised by the dispatched reviewer of `domains/arousal.md` on 2026-08-07 and relayed here unjudged, alongside a separate claim that the Definition defines the term with itself. The two are filed apart because a repair to either leaves the other standing.

The reviewer routed both to `define-definition` and wrote no replacement, reporting that every alternative it could reach narrowed the domain further.

Not measured: whether anything other than prose is in fact planned for this domain, which is what would decide whether the gap is live or latent. I did not re-read the two lines against each other myself.
