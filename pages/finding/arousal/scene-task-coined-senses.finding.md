# Claim

`domains/tasks/scenewright/author-persona-scene.md` coins `movements` and `gears` and defines each inside the bullet that uses it, and `movements` is used one stage before it is defined. Neither is declared as a domain, and nothing settles whether an in-place definition satisfies Plain Or Declared.

# Evidence

Found by a reader dispatched at the file under the plain-language initiative on 2026-08-06, which landed twenty-seven wording rewrites and could not settle these.

Plain Or Declared says declare the word as a domain first. Whether a sense defined in the same bullet counts as declared is not settled by that principle or by any other. `movements` is the sharper case: stage 2 uses it before stage 2 defines it, so its first reader meets it undefined. `the fast gear` in stage 2 leans on a distinction stage 3 has not yet drawn, which is an ordering defect no single-line rewrite reaches.

Two more on the same document. `his locked read` reads either as a read that has settled and can be relied on, or as one that is not to be reopened; the phrase appears nowhere else, so nothing adjudicates, and the surrounding line was rewritten with `locked` left standing. And `Negation` in stage 3 covers two different acts — the author withholding, and the persona refusing aloud — where every plainer word picks one and drops the other, so it may want splitting rather than rewording.

Two corpus-wide questions surfaced here and on `domains/tasks/code-harness/review-check.md`, and neither belongs to one document. `the ask`, a nominalised verb used as house vocabulary in at least this task and `domains/tasks/lead/define-project.md`, is undeclared; making it plain in one place alone splits the naming. And `where` used to mean "whereas" or "in cases where" runs through this file, `domains/agent-harness.md`, `domains/task.md` and the archivist tasks alike, so a single-document pass would only make one file inconsistent with every other.
