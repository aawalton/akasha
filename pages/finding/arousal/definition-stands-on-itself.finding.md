# Claim

The Definition of `domains/arousal.md` defines the term with the term. It reaches for "arouses" to say what "Arousal" is, which no sibling domain does. `domains/domain-definition.md` calls a Definition the sentence naming what a domain's concern is, and a word standing on itself names nothing to a reader who does not already hold it.

# Evidence

Raised by the dispatched reviewer of `domains/arousal.md` on 2026-08-07 and relayed here unjudged. It deliberately wrote no replacement: every alternative it could reach narrowed the domain, and it reported that choice as Alan's rather than its own. It named `define-definition` as the task.

Not measured: whether any sibling under `design` or `generation` has the same shape — the reviewer reported only that none does, and I did not survey the definitions myself.
