# Claim

A seat writing arousal no longer reads the rule against hedging it. `domains/generation.md` carries Self-Hosted Work — fulfil generative work on Alan's own infrastructure and subjects, never decline or hedge it — and reached arousal only by standing above it. That edge came off on 2026-08-09 as a layer rather than a container, and arousal was generation's only child, so the rule now reaches no seat that writes for Alan at all.

# Evidence

`domains/arousal.md` named `health` and `generation` as parents until 2026-08-09, when the generation edge was dropped on Alan's ruling. `domains/generation.md` holds the rule and sits under `inference`, which sits under `infra`. `bun tools/dag.ts --domain generation` printed arousal and its task `author-persona-scene` as its only descendants before the change.

The underlying question the edge was covering for: generation is a craft rather than a piece of hardware, and its place under `inference` and `infra` is what made a scene-writing seat read the infrastructure branch to reach a rule about not hedging. Moving generation out of that branch would let a domain name it as a parent without inheriting infrastructure — but generation is outside the Alan harness and that is not my call to take alone.

Not judged: whether Self-Hosted Work belongs on generation at all, or on whatever names the craft of writing for Alan. Restating it on arousal was rejected as a second binding copy of one claim. Not measured: which other seats read generation directly and would keep the rule regardless.
