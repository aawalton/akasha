# Claim

A lead's standing ruling on the sleep domain: what Alan would tolerate already running at his bedside at 3 a.m. is a preference, not a fact. The mid-night re-sleep case is one of the two he named at intake and the only one never tried, so nothing about it has been observed — and the design pressure runs the other way, because everything built so far requires him to start it, which is a focal act at the hour that cannot afford one. The question of what may reach him there is his alone to settle.
