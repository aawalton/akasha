# Claim

Alan wants an experiment-driven sleep-audio program built on the existing story-chapter audio system (Ione's committed voice), with intake settled through falling-asleep and falling-back-asleep cases and an arousal/attention-capture-vs-release dial as the core tuning variable — but no Story or Chapter page has been authored for it yet.

# Evidence

Project #15572 (domain: sleep), status someday_maybe, live-on: deploy. Carried no `# Objective`; these notes are the observation.

Origin: intake handoff from Alan's alanNotes sweep (via Áine), verbatim item "Ione sleep audio" — an audio surface at the sleep threshold, Ione's voice carrying Alan over as the lights go down.

Intake interview with Alan settled the intent over three turns (2026-07-16, turns logged 06:15/06:21/06:27Z):
- Not one fixed form: Alan wants an EXPERIMENT program, testing his way to what works. Two cases to test separately: falling asleep at night's start, and falling BACK asleep mid-night. Structure: a Story page in the experiment collections, with a Story Chapter page per experiment (one trial each).
- Core tuning variable: arousal level of the content — enough to hold attention off the day's churn, light enough to still drift off (attention-capture vs release).
- Delivery settled: use the EXISTING story-chapter audio system (author each experiment as a Chapter, render in Ione's voice, Alan presses play from bed) — no new delivery infrastructure needed. Open research (not Alan's to answer): which collection homes the Story; whether story-chapter audio already supports per-story narrator voice selection and how to trigger a render specifically in Ione's voice.
- Alan's starting priors for the arousal dial: must feel SAFE (no threat/suspense — threat is the reliable wake-up); some arousal from attraction is fine; a sense of PROGRESS is good (why LitRPG works for him — visible incremental wins). Design implication for experiment 1: drift-story, small safe accumulating wins, gentle progression, low register, light attraction, no stakes.

Recorded as INTENT SETTLED, next steps named as research then authoring the Story and Chapter 1 — neither had happened before this project was captured off the row's retired `notes` attribute on 2026-08-15.
