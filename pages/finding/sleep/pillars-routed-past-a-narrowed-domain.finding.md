# Claim

The task and breathing pillars are routed to the persona who owns `sleep`, and Alan has since ruled that the concern is sleep alone.

# Evidence

`domains/personas/ione.md` declares `championed-domain: sleep`. `packages/alanwalton/daily-tracking/src/persona-day-points.ts` maps `sleepPoints`, `taskPoints` and `breathingPoints` all to Ione, and `scripts/src/retarget-task-breathing-to-ione.script.ts` is the migration that put the last two there.

Alan renamed the domain from `sleep-hygiene` to `sleep` on 2026-07-27. Asked on 2026-08-06 whether that shortened the name or narrowed the concern, he answered that it narrowed the concern to sleep. `domains/sleep.md` now reads "the rest Alan has to work for", which reaches neither household tasks nor breathing.

The quarantined account at `dirty/skills/sleep/findings.md` records the task half as roughly two fifths of that persona's lifetime pillar total, and one day whose whole standing was carried by it while sleep read zero. So the routing is load-bearing rather than vestigial, and whoever the pillars belong to instead is unnamed.
