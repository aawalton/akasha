# Claim

Which persona owns a domain is written by hand on both sides and nothing computes either from the other. `persona-champion-slug:` on a domain and `championed-domain-slug:` on a persona say the same thing from opposite ends, and neither property definition carries `computed:`. The two agree only while whoever edits one remembers the other.

# Evidence

Noticed during the review-instructions reading of `domains/agent-harness.md` on 2026-08-19, where `persona-champion-slug: athena` and `championed-domain-slug: agent-harness` on `domains/personas/athena.md` do agree today.

Measured: that both property definitions lack `computed:`, and that this one pair agrees. Not measured: whether any pair in the corpus currently disagrees, and whether some gate or audit already compares the two sides.
