# Claim

Nothing Unexplained stands on `design` while the four directives of the same family stand a level below it, on the children of `design-principles`. The two placements are not the same instruction: standing on `design` the principle also reaches `design-tokens` and `design-patterns`, which it would not reach from `design-principles`. Nothing says which reach was meant.

# Evidence

`ops instructions dag --domain design` on 2026-08-22 prints three children of `design`: `design-patterns`, `design-principles` and `design-tokens`. Under `design-principles` it prints `alignment`, `contrast`, `proximity` and `repetition`, and nothing else. Each of those four carries a directive of its own; `pages/domain/design-principles.md` reads `**Design principles** — what every design choice is measured against.`

`pages/domain/design.md` carries `Nothing Unexplained` under `# Principles`, and it is the only principle on the document.

Raised by the review-instructions reading of `pages/domain/design.md`, finished 2026-08-22, at its line 6, which named the placement and left it: "That call is the principal's." `pages/page-type/domain.md` Every Changed Line reserves a line in a domain's Principles to Alan, so the reading could not move it.

`pages/domain/context-push.md` Dilution puts a line on the narrowest document it serves. Which document that is here depends on whether design tokens and design patterns are meant to be measured against Nothing Unexplained, and nothing read says.

Not measured: whether `design-tokens` or `design-patterns` or anything beneath them has ever been written against this principle, and whether a fifth sibling under `design-principles` was ever intended.
