# Claim

A signal in a design dimension lives in the variation across that dimension, never in its level.

Two of the four dimensions hold an instance of this and `design` states it nowhere. `Obvious Or None` on `contrast` holds the saturation instance: where everything differs, nothing does. The proximity claim proposed on 2026-08-06 holds the level instance: raising every gap changes nothing, because only the differences between gaps group. Neither document knows the other states the same truth.

# Evidence

`domains/contrast.md` line 17 carries "Where everything differs, nothing does" as the second aid under `Obvious Or None`.

The proximity claim carrying the other instance was proposed and not landed, so at the time of filing it stands only in a proposal: "More space everywhere changes nothing; only the difference does." That sentence is the one part of the standing `Tighter Inside` that survived a full rewrite pass, tested against two rendered cases — a uniform padding rise leaves both violations exactly where they were.

`domains/design.md` carries one principle, `Nothing Unexplained`. Its third sentence, "Sameness needs a reason too", is about owing a reason for sameness rather than about level carrying no signal, so it is not this claim.

Two instances rather than three is why nothing was lifted. Lifting to `design` would charge the readers of all four dimensions, and a pattern of two does not yet pay for that.

Not measured: whether `alignment` or `repetition` holds a third instance. Both documents were read in full and neither was judged to carry one — `One strong edge beats three weak ones` on `alignment` is about concentrating a signal rather than about level, and that reading was not tested against a case. Whether the two existing instances should come down if a general claim is ever lifted was not considered. Whether the general claim is true of dimensions outside these four was not considered.
