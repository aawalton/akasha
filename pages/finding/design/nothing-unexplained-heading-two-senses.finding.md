# Claim

The heading `## Nothing Unexplained` on `pages/domain/design.md` and the aid beneath it use "explain" in two senses. In the heading it means without a reason. In the aid `Explain nothing to a reader who knows the design.` it means stated in words. One word, two senses, inside one directive, and the heading is the part a reader meets first — so the heading can be read as asking for the thing its own aid forbids.

# Evidence

`pages/domain/design.md` as it stands on 2026-08-22 carries one principle. Its heading is `## Nothing Unexplained`, its act `Give every difference a reader can see a reason they can find.`, its warrant `A difference with no reason reads as one the reader missed, so they watch for it and stop reading.`, and its two aids `Never give one look to two things that differ.` and `Explain nothing to a reader who knows the design.` I read the file whole.

Raised by the review-instructions reading of that document, finished 2026-08-22, at its line 5. That reading kept every one of the nine lines it listed and moved nothing in the body; the only commit against the subject was `c501f14b4`, which restamped `reviewed-date:` alone.

Why the reading did not take it: `pages/page-type/domain.md` Every Changed Line shows Alan each line an agent would change in a domain's Definition, Design, Intent, Principles or Rules, and a heading in Principles is such a line. Which of the two words gives way is a judgment, and nobody was awake to take it.

Not measured: whether any reader has in fact read the heading against the aid, and whether the same two senses of "explain" collide anywhere else under `design`.
