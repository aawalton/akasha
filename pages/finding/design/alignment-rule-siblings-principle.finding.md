# Claim

Alignment states its core instruction as a Rule where its three siblings state theirs as a Principle. Under `design` sit alignment, contrast, proximity and repetition. Contrast carries Obvious Or None, proximity Nothing Nearer and repetition Same Kind Same Way, all under `# Principles`. Alignment carries Share An Edge alone, under `# Rules`. The split may be right, but no document says why it is there.

# Evidence

Measured 2026-08-06 by grepping the `# Principles` / `# Rules` headings and `##` entries of all four sibling documents. Result: alignment has `# Rules` only (Share An Edge); contrast has `# Principles` only (Obvious Or None, Color Earns Attention, Failure Is Boring); proximity has `# Principles` only (Nothing Nearer); repetition has both, its core instruction Same Kind Same Way under Principles and Standardized Palette under Rules.

Raised first by a review-instructions seat on `domains/alignment.md`, which judged the placement right rather than escalating it, and flagged that I might take that back. I verified the placements myself; I did not judge which section is correct, and nothing here measures whether any reader has been misled.
