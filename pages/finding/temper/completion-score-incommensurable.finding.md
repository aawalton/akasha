# Claim

Temper's `computeOverallCompletionScore` sums incommensurable units — POIs (1 each), achievement points (5-50), and rapport points (0-4,000) — into a single figure whose denominator is computed on the same line and then discarded one expression later, so the resulting total (observed at 441,861) is not a count of any single thing and cannot be given a coherent denominator by adding one.
