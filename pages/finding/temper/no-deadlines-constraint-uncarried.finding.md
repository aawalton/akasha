# Claim

Temper's no-deadlines constraint, which Alan calls a boundary rather than a preference, is carried in no live document.

Nothing under `domains/` mentions deadlines at all. The constraint — no date is ever a target, no work sequenced toward one, no deadline reintroduced as a soft "by then", no external event treated as a goal — stands only in a quarantined document queued for removal. The nearest live rule forbids estimating how long work takes, a different act.

# Evidence

The passage, at `dirty/skills/temper/SKILL.md` lines 44-52, under the heading "The hard constraint: no deadlines, ever":

"**This one is not a preference and not a tiebreak — it is a boundary on how the domain may be run, and it is psychological.** *"What will be, will be."* No date is ever a target, no work is sequenced toward one, and a deadline may not be reintroduced as a soft *by then*. A real external event — a conference, a release — is not a goal and must never be treated as one." And: "**Flow rate here is raised by feeding and unblocking, never by a clock.** Anything that would impose one is out of bounds regardless of how much throughput it promises."

Nothing live carries it. Grepping `domains/` for "deadline", "no date", "target date" and "what will be" returns nothing — the live corpus does not mention deadlines. `domains/folders/temper.md` carries `persona-champion-slug: ember`, `code-path: packages/temper/**` and a Definition alone: no Principles, Rules or Tasks.

The nearest live rule is a different act. Estimate on `domains/role.md` reads "Never estimate how long or how hard future work will be; a measured duration may be reported." That forbids producing a figure. It does not reach sequencing work toward a date supplied from elsewhere, so an agent can obey it while planning around a conference.

Across the whole quarantine tree only two other documents match "no deadlines", both `dirty/code/` book-of-everything files, and both in another sense: "No deadlines — a `resting` thread is waiting, not overdue" is queue semantics for a reading rotation.

Not measured: whether the constraint is temper-scoped or Alan-wide. The source scopes it to the domain while giving a psychological reason that is about Alan rather than about the domain, and `domains/alan-harness.md` — "Alan's life and how it is kept" — would be the wider home. Nothing dated was found settling which, and which repair is right was not considered.
