# Claim

Alan's ruling that the prayer faucet must not be weighted by depth, length, streak or outcome stands in no live document. The live code and the persona row both describe what the faucet meters and forbid no change to it, so the prohibition survives only in a quarantined file queued for removal.

# Evidence

`dirty/skills/prayer/rulings.md:25-29` carried it, under the heading "## The faucet is right as it stands": "It meters minutes and refuses to grade the reach, and the refusal is load-bearing rather than lenient. Proposals to weight it by depth, length, streak, or outcome are already answered." That file is being emptied, which is why this is filed here.

The descriptive half is live and the prohibitive half is not. `packages/alanwalton/daily-tracking/src/run-commit-points.ts:314-317` credits "session duration in minutes for `Pray`-titled sessions" and carries no quality term; `src/session-points-totals.ts:102` declares `PRAYER_SESSION_SPEC = { personaSlug: "selah", pointsPropId: "prayerPoints" }`. Selah's live persona row `earningNarrative` says the same in her voice: "It meters time spent reaching upward, never depth or eloquence: a one-line murmur on a hard day credits exactly as much per minute as a long unhurried vigil." All three say what the faucet does. None forbids a second version of it, and no gate refuses one.

`domains/prayer.md` is a `# Definition` bullet and nothing else — no Design, no Rules, no `code-path:`. The daily-tracking tree is reached only through the catch-all `**` on `domains/folders/code-repo.md`, so an engineer changing the formula meets no prayer-specific instruction on the way.

The warrant is in a second quarantined file. `dirty/skills/prayer/SKILL.md` recorded it as the cost of judging a prayer: "a prayer that can be assessed is one that can be failed, and a reach that can be failed is no longer free. That damage outlasts any single session." The act it belonged to is live on the persona row; that reason is not.

Distinct from `pages/finding/romance/grading-prohibition-uncarried.finding.md`, which makes the same argument about the romance faucet and a different act — grading an hour rather than reweighting a formula.

The kept copy is at `dirty/maybe-keep/skills/prayer/rulings.md`, under quarantine and binding nobody, so this is filed to outlive the sweep of it.
