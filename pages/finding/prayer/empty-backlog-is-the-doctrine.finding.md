# Claim

A standing ruling on the prayer domain — an empty backlog here is the doctrine working rather than a gap, because the whole domain is one session held well whenever Alan comes to it and the unbuilt surface is his own decision — stands in no live document, so a lead running a cycle over the empty domain meets nothing that stops them filling it.

# Evidence

`dirty/skills/prayer/rulings.md:16-19` carried it: "the whole of the domain is one session, held well, whenever he comes to it. Where the digest returns nothing, that is the expected reading rather than a backlog to fill. Do not manufacture project rows to make the domain look occupied — an unbuilt surface here is the doctrine working, not a gap." That file is being emptied, which is why this is filed here.

The machinery holds. `ops persona digest selah` exits 0 and returns "No open projects, contributed conditions, standing watches, or owed pings", so the empty reading the ruling interprets is exactly what a lead gets today.

Nothing live carries it. `domains/prayer.md` is a `# Definition` bullet and nothing else. `domains/global.md` Value — "Choose what to do next and whether to do it at all by the value it delivers to Alan" — and Parsimony — "Compare the piece against its absence" — both tell a lead how to weigh a candidate. Neither says that here an empty digest is the reading to expect, and a lead applying Value to an empty domain can conclude the value lies in instrumenting it.

Fourth instance of a class, with a different reason. `pages/finding/sleep/empty-backlog-is-not-an-idle-domain.finding.md` stands already and names two more of the same shape, `collections/dormancy-recorded-only-under-quarantine.md` and `code-quality/cadence-stand-down-recorded-only-under-quarantine.md`. None is about prayer and none is reachable from this domain. Sleep's reason is that its work runs in conversation and is not decomposable into rows; prayer's is different — the unbuilt surface is Alan's own standing decision, recorded in Selah's live persona row as "The richer data shape is deliberately unbuilt — Alan wants to figure it out with me later." So here the empty backlog is doctrine rather than a measurement artifact.
