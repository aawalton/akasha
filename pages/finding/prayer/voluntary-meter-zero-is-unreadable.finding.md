# Claim

The prayer faucet's zero is not a claim about the practice. Logging is optional by Selah's own doctrine and the faucet only sees a session whose title carries the keyword, so a zero day is equally consistent with no prayer, with prayer that went unlogged, and with prayer logged under another title, and nothing distinguishes them. Unlike the other recorded instances of this shape, the blindness here is a consequence of a rule Alan has ruled must stand rather than a defect awaiting repair.
