# Claim

The lead's verdict once asked whether what landed served the domain's vision, and no live
surface asks that at a row. The retired corpus carries the rung and records it as dropped
without a decision, and the same retired file states two other measures for the same verdict.

# Evidence

Measured 2026-08-04, sweeping `dirty/` for prior art on whether a landing is weighed against
a domain vision.

`dirty/skills/agent-harness/role-taxonomy.md:23` states the rung as the lead's whole question:
*"| `lead` | a **goal-verdict** on one parent | *Did what landed serve the vision?* |"*. Line 121
states what it was for: *"a change meeting every criterion and missing the vision is what the
second axis catches"*.

The same file states a different measure eight lines later, at :30 — *"Lead judges one parent
against the criteria intake wrote"* — and `dirty/docs/domain-first-agents.md:76` a third,
*"renders the goal-verdict against the intent it wrote"*. Three measures for one verdict, in
two files of one corpus.

`dirty/questions/instructions-review-divided-contracts.md:12` records how the rung left:
*"Neither was rebuilt and neither appears in the dispatching brief's list of deliberate cuts,
so both went unnoticed rather than decided."*

In the live estate the comparison stands at the initiative rather than the row.
`tasks/lead/review-initiative.md` stage 5 carries it — *"A programme closes on the objectives it
set, which is rarely the whole of the gap it was aimed at"* — and `tasks/lead/verify-handback.md`
measures a row against its criteria and its named instruments only. None of the fourteen checks
`ops instructions run-checks` runs reads a `# Vision`.

Not measured: whether any row since the retired corpus was retired has landed against a vision.

design and is not a precedent for the normal flow"*, so it does not settle the question either way.
