# Claim

Sixteen ranked units stand on `domains/role.md` and none of them is about a bounded set of responsibilities an agent is answerable for, which is what the domain is defined as.

# Evidence

Counted on 2026-08-03: `domains/role.md` carries 16 ranked units — 5 principles and 11 rules. No other domain in the estate carries more than four. `domains/global.md` has four, `domains/agent-harness.md` four, and every other domain one or two. The count is not the finding, because a surface every seat reads may legitimately carry the most.

What the units are about is. None of the sixteen concerns a bounded set of responsibilities an agent is answerable for, which is what `role` is defined as. Retention is about tool results being cleared, Publication about sending content to a service you do not control, Irreversibility about destructive commands, Foreign State about another agent's work in progress. That is agent conduct, and it is here because `e28f55f5` cut eight principles from `domains/agent-harness.md` deliberately, leaving conduct with no surface of its own and only this one read by every seat.

The direction of travel is the second half. `8b4ab584` reads "principles: cut six from role, promoting Turn End to stand on its own", so this file has already been pruned once. It has since grown past where that cut left it, and three of the sixteen were added on 2026-08-03 alone: Proportion in `288c14e8`, Horizontal Change in `9159932b`, and Stopping replacing Turn End in `bef37a65`. A file that regrows after pruning is not carrying an excess; it is serving as the default destination for anything with nowhere else to go.

Not measured: whether any of the sixteen would be read differently standing on a conduct surface of its own, and whether any reader has ever taken one for a claim about roles.
