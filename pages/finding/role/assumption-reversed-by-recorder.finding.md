# Claim

Two standing instructions tell a seat opposite things and neither says which wins. `domains/role.md` Assumption says to state an assumption and go on, its warrant being that "a question costs a turn and returns nothing". Responsibility 3 on `domains/roles/recorder.md` says to send the principal the question rather than settling it. Assumption's warrant does not hold when the subject is live and the answer is one exchange away. The fix is either a clause on the recorder or a carve-out on `role.md`.

# Evidence

Raised by a review-instructions seat on `domains/roles/recorder.md`, which kept the responsibility precisely because it reverses Assumption, and landed nothing because both candidate fixes are changed lines in reserved sections.

I verified Assumption's text on `domains/role.md:51-53`: "Where something is unclear, do what does not depend on it, then state an assumption and go on. A question costs a turn and returns nothing... Stop only where a wrong guess is unsafe or wasted."

The reviewer's reading is that the recorder's case falls under "unsafe or wasted" — a guess about what a live person meant, when they are present to be asked — but neither document says so, so a recorder holding both reaches them as competing rules rather than as a rule and its exception.

Not measured: whether any recorder has settled a question it should have sent on.
