# Claim

A report's summary line carries a rounding its own evidence does not support, and the rounding goes
the way that makes the account tidier.

The body is measured and right; the sentence above it is not. Readers judge by the summary, so the
rounding is the part that travels.

# Evidence


Five specimens on one night, from three seats, every one in the flattering direction.

A finding titled `...-are-exact-complements` claimed a symmetry its own numbers disproved: 49 of the
125 tracked `.test.tsx` files are claimed by a project and fall out of the suite alone, so the sets
are nested rather than mirrored. The 49 was in the writer's hand, sent in a message and left out of
the page. Refiled as `the-typecheck-gap-lies-wholly-inside-the-suite-gap`.

A finding titled `the-root-build-is-clean-over-a-third-of-the-tree` covered 4 of 14 top-level
directories, which is under a third. The title rounded the coverage up and the gap down, in a finding
whose claim is that the build looks cleaner than it is.

"Roughly sixty times the work" passed from a subagent's report through a message into the first line
of a finding in about four minutes, unverified by anyone. Withdrawn.

"Roughly 2,775 pre-existing errors" was repeated across perhaps eight agent briefs. The number was
true of one instrument and quoted about another: `typecheck-repo` reports 2,774 under one strict
configuration where `ops checks audit typecheck` reports 173 by each project's own.

A WHOLE MECHANISM, NOT A ROUNDING, AND THE MOST DANGEROUS FORM. A `tsc -b` run reported
`@shared/pages-core/types` as lacking `StorageTier`. A reading of commit subjects and timestamps
produced a clean causal story, sent on with a table of times in it. The files said otherwise:
`292f60a42` adds the export and the import in the same commit. No commit ever held the break. The
subjects supported the story; the contents were never opened.

WHAT IS COMMON. Every measurement was correct and every summary was not. None needed a defect in any
code. Each was caught by a second reader checking a number rather than taking it, none by any
instrument. Measured 2026-08-28.
