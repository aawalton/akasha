# Claim

A report's summary line carries a rounding its own evidence does not support, and the rounding goes
the way that makes the account tidier.

The body is measured and right; the sentence above it is not. Readers judge by the summary, so the
rounding is the part that travels.

# Evidence

Four specimens on one night, from three seats, every one in the flattering direction.

A finding titled `the-typecheck-gap-and-the-suite-glob-are-exact-complements` claimed a symmetry the
numbers beneath it disproved: 49 of the 125 tracked `.test.tsx` files are claimed by a project and
fall out of the suite alone, so the sets are nested rather than mirrored. The 49 was in the writer's
hand when the page was filed, sent in a message and left out of the page. Refiled as
`the-typecheck-gap-lies-wholly-inside-the-suite-gap`.

A finding titled `the-root-build-is-clean-over-a-third-of-the-tree` covered 4 of 14 top-level
directories when filed, which is under a third. The title rounded the coverage up and the gap down,
in a finding whose claim is that the build looks cleaner than it is. Its Evidence named the nine
omitted directories exactly, so the page held.

A characterisation of "roughly sixty times the work" passed from a subagent's report through a
message into the first line of a finding in about four minutes, unverified by anyone. Tracked-file
counts support the direction and not the magnitude. Withdrawn, and recorded as a withdrawal.

A count of "roughly 2,775 pre-existing errors" was repeated across perhaps eight agent briefs. The
number was true of one instrument and quoted about another: `typecheck-repo` reports 2,774 errors
under one strict configuration where `ops checks audit typecheck` reports 173 by each project's own.
Caught by an agent that saw 173, doubted the brief rather than its own run, and said so.

A whole mechanism, not a rounding, and the most dangerous form of this. A `tsc -b` run reported
`@shared/pages-core/types` as having no exported member `StorageTier`. A reading of commit subjects
and timestamps produced a clean causal story — one commit removed, a later one declared, therefore
broken in between — which was sent on in the confident register with a table of times in it. The
files said otherwise: `292f60a42` adds the export and the import in the same commit, and at the
preceding commit the importing file did not name `StorageTier` at all. No commit ever held the
break. The subjects supported the story; the contents were never opened. Retracted within the hour,
after it had already reached one reader and been asked to be passed to a second.

WHAT IS COMMON. In all four the measurement was correct and the summary was not. None needed a
defect in any code. Each was caught by a second reader checking the number rather than taking it,
and none by any instrument.
