# Claim

The Trust act on `domains/file-kinds/tests.md` reads two ways. "Treat a failure that clears on a re-run as broken: fix or delete it" predicates brokenness of the failure, then hands `it` to a noun nothing can delete. Both readings ask for the same act, so it is a plainness defect rather than a fork — but the plainest rewrite a reviewer reached measured 109 characters against the act's 100.

# Evidence

Raised by the dispatched reviewer of `domains/file-kinds/tests.md` on 2026-08-07 against Simple Language on `domains/agent-harness.md`, and relayed here unjudged. It left the act standing, Every Changed Line on `domains/domain.md` reserving a domain's Rules for Alan.

The 100 is the act ceiling `SM` from `tools/document/tokens.ts` by way of `principle-or-rule.ts`; the reviewer read both. The 109 is one candidate rewrite it wrote and measured, not a proof that no shorter plain form exists — that is the open part of this.

The same reviewer recorded, and correctly declined to file as a defect, that four of the five descriptions on this document sit at 198 to 200 against a 200 ceiling. Headroom on `domains/agent-harness.md` makes that the mechanism working. It bears on this claim only in that a repair here has no slack to borrow.

Not measured: whether a plain form under 100 exists. I did not attempt one.
