# Claim

The Departure kind, as `domains/domain-design.md` glosses it, admits any unguessable true statement about a domain, so the section's limit to three kinds does less limiting than it reads as doing.

# Evidence

Reported by the review of `domains/domain-design.md` on 2026-08-15. The reviewer split the 993 Design entries then live across 440 domains on whether they carry a contrastive marker (rather than, never, not, no, only) and reports 475 carrying one against 518 flat stipulations — "5,000 of them is a green day", "It takes is and on or after", "A section is a block holding a heading and the blocks beneath it". Each is unguessable and so satisfies the gloss, while nothing was departed from. The split is a crude proxy the reviewer named as one, and the counts were not re-measured here. The reviewer also reports that `page-body-shapes/domain.md` declares Design as a single free-text slot bounded on size and count alone, so nothing mechanical enforces the three kinds. It did not attempt to settle the question: git carries c675db3d9, "reverse the Design Surprises rename across the corpus", so this vocabulary has been argued and deliberately restored, and the elasticity may be the settled intent.
