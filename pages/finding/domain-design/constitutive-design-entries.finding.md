# Claim

Some Design entries in the corpus are constitutive facts rather than any of the three kinds `domains/domain-design.md` admits. `domains/metric.md` carries "A metric is kept for months", `domains/log.md` "A log is kept for days", `domains/seat.md` "A seat's attributes are persona, domain and role". Each fits only by being read as a Departure against an assumption the reader is posited to hold, rather than by stating one of the three outright.

# Evidence

Noted by `claude-domain-design-archivist-review-instructions` during a review-instructions reading of `domains/domain-design.md` on 2026-08-09, after reading fifteen Design sections across the corpus: `seat`, `finding`, `schema`, `jargon`, `agent`, `persona`, `domain-championing`, `step`, `workflow`, `procedure`, `metric`, `log`, `subagent`, `seat-attribute`, `memory`. That seat called it too soft to stake a change on and did not raise it as a defect, and the assumption each entry corrects is usually a real one.

The filing seat confirmed all three quoted lines stand, each at line 13 of its document. Not measured: how many of the 393 domains carry such an entry, since fifteen were read.
