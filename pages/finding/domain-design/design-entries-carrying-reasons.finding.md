# Claim

11 Design entries outside `domains/domain-design.md` carry a because-clause, against its bar that a Design entry carries no reason for itself.

# Evidence

Reported by the review of `domains/domain-design.md` on 2026-08-15, which named them: container-registry, ops-db, ops-workstation, ops-system, ops-memory, ops-pipeline-main-failures, ops-merge-queue-batch, category-rule-description, persona-craft, readout-pipeline-health and person. The reviewer judged some defensible — "A person is a domain so that a seat can be bound to one" is constitutive rather than a justification, which is what the bar's "for itself" separates — and did not repair any, holding 11 documents to be a separate piece of work rather than a repair adjacent to a single-document review. The list was not re-checked here, and which of the 11 actually breach the bar was not judged.
