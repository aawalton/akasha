# Claim

The Judge bullet on `verify-handback` reads two ways. "Judge an amended criterion on the amendment" is either "assess the amendment on its merits rather than for departing from the original", which the tail supports, or — taking "judge X on Y" in its ordinary sense — circular, with the work as the intended object. The two readings put a different object under the verb, so repairing the head means deciding what the bullet is for.
