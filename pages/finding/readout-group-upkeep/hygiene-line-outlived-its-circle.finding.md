# Claim

`readout-group-upkeep`'s Design still describes hygiene's figure, though hygiene left the upkeep row and its circle is gone.

# Evidence

Read on 2026-08-18. `domains/readout-groups/readout-group-upkeep.md` carries the Design line "Hygiene's figure is a mean of six colours, not a reading of anything."

The six upkeep readout documents give places 1 to 6 as plants, activity, sleep, surplus, capacity, safety, and `readout-hygiene` holds no place and no group. `hygieneCircle` appears nowhere on `origin/main`. So the line describes a circle that is not among the six the document is about.

This finding is what is left of an earlier one naming two stale lines on this document. The other — the order line — was put to Alan, approved in his own words, and landed on 2026-08-18 as "The six circles hold one order: three things Alan does, then three readings of what those produced." That earlier finding was deleted when its fix landed, and this stands in its place so the remaining line does not go with it.

The hygiene line has not been put to Alan and is not covered by what he approved, `Every Changed Line` binding per line. A replacement was drafted by the seat that delivered the change — "The surplus reading is what Alan slept less what the day cost." — and nobody has approved it.

Not established: whether the line should be replaced or simply dropped. What the six circles hold is the document's subject, and a line about a seventh that no longer draws may be owed nothing at all.
