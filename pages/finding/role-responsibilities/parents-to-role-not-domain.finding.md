# Claim

`domains/role-responsibilities.md` parents to `role` where every sibling section-domain parents toward `domain`. Measured: `domain-definition`, `domain-design` and `domain-intent` all carry `domain-parents: domain`; `principle` and `rule` carry `instruction`, which reaches `domain`; `role-responsibilities` alone carries `role`. That placement is why the Responsibility Change rule and its release sentence must be restated on this document rather than inherited.

# Evidence

Raised by a review-instructions seat on `domains/role-responsibilities.md`, which returned it rather than landing anything: the arrangement is defensible, since a seat writing a responsibility does want `role.md` in front of it, but it is not the placement a reader predicts and it reaches the whole read chain.

I measured the parents myself across all six section-domains and they are as stated.

The reviewer connected this to a second observation it verified: `ops instructions governs --file-path domains/roles/lead.md` shows `domain.md` reaching a role document only as parent of the section-domains, each scoped to its own section, so a seat changing only Responsibilities never reads `domain.md` — which is what makes the restated rule and release sentence necessary rather than a Single Authority breach. I did not re-run that command.

Not measured: whether any reader has looked for the rule under `domain` and failed to find it.
