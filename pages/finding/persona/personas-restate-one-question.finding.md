# Claim

Fifteen persona bodies each restate the One Question rule in their own voice. `grep -rln "exactly one question\|one question per turn\|one question at a time" domains/personas/` returns 15 files. The rule itself stands on `domains/alan-harness-agents.md`, which every one of those personas already reads.

# Evidence

Read off the `review-instructions` reading of `domains/alan-harness-agents.md` finished 2026-08-21, read line by line, bottom to top. That reading ran the grep while checking whether any second document claims the rule, and reports that no document outside the subject states it — the fifteen are persona prose rather than a second document claiming it.

Not measured here: I did not run the grep myself, did not open any of the fifteen bodies to see how closely each tracks the rule, and did not check the other directives on the subject for the same pattern. Whether a persona's own voice restating a rule counts as a second claimant under Single Authority, or is exempt as her voice rather than instruction, is not settled here.
