# Claim

Two live tasks give one test opposite answers. `define-domain-structure` 2/1 says two facts that stand without each other are two domains, and calls it the test `define-definition` runs inside one line. That task's 4/9 says two that each stand alone CROSS into one predication on a verb, and approves "a body and its upkeep" and "a people and the bonds between them" as single lines. Under 2/1 both of those are families nobody split.

# Evidence

Raised by the review-instructions reading of `domains/tasks/lead/define-domain-structure.md` on 2026-08-07, which landed seven decisions and left this one: it reaches a second live document and turns on how the two tasks should divide, so it has no mechanical answer.

Verified myself in both live documents. `define-domain-structure:23` reads "Draft the single line first and count the facts in it that stand without each other. Two that do are two domains, and this is the test define-definition runs inside one line, run here to find how many lines there are." `define-definition:50` reads "Two that each stand alone cross, and the crossing belongs in the verb rather than in a comma."

The reviewer adds a third instance I confirmed by reading it: `define-domain-structure`'s own Definition line, "settling how many domains one idea is, and what each sits under", is two independent facts crossed on a verb — so the document fails its own 2/1 test.
