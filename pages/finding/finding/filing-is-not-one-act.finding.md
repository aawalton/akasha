# Claim

Filing one finding takes three artifacts composed outside the tree and a door that reports its ceilings only after the writing, so noticing and filing are not one act.

# Evidence

`ops memory file-finding` requires `--domain`, `--slug`, `--claim-file` and `--evidence-file`, and `Composed Outside` on `domains/agent-harness.md` requires both bodies to be written outside the tree they land in. So the floor is three acts: compose the claim, compose the evidence, run the verb.

Measured on the three I filed on 2026-08-05. `pages/finding/identity/persona-default-role-unpinned.finding.md` and `pages/finding/finding/dead-finding-has-no-remover.finding.md` each took the floor. `pages/finding/domain/sections-restate-what-already-binds.finding.md` took five: the door refused once because `tools/document/schemas/finding.ts` had changed since I last read it and `read-the-schema` wanted it again, and once because `# Evidence` measured 2193 against its 2000 ceiling — which meant rewriting a body that was already written, since the bound is reported at the door rather than known while composing. A first attempt at the claim and evidence files also failed outright on stale paths in `/tmp`, costing a sixth call.

The intent this is measured against stood on `domains/finding.md` until 2026-08-05 as "Anything an agent notices lands somewhere, at a cost low enough that noticing and filing are one act." It was cut in the same pass that produced this finding, as aspiration written in the indicative present, so the claim it stated is no longer carried anywhere.

Not measured. Whether other seats meet the same cost, or whether mine was inflated by working in a session where the schemas were being edited under me by another agent. Whether a cheaper path exists that I did not find — I did not read `tools/file-finding.ts` to see whether the bodies can be passed inline or on stdin, and the help I read may not be the whole of what the verb takes. And whether the cost actually suppresses filing, which is what would make it matter: it did not suppress mine, and a cost paid three times in one session is evidence about the price rather than about anyone deciding not to pay it.
