# Claim

A seat that establishes a finding's claim is no longer true has no surface telling it to remove the finding, and Foreign State points it the other way, so a dead finding survives until its owner happens to read it.

# Evidence

The decision stood until 2026-08-05 on `domains/finding.md`, in the `# Vision` section, as: "That last is a fact rather than a judgment, so whoever establishes it removes the finding whether or not they own it, and the commit is what tells the owner." It was migrated into that file's new `# Design` section and then pulled back out the same day, and now stands nowhere.

It left each candidate home for a stated reason rather than by oversight. `# Design` admits a departure or an absence — each a statement about the artifact — and this is an act a seat performs, which is why Alan called it role guidance rather than design. `tasks/lead/review-findings.md` is scoped to the owner at every stage: it opens on what you actually own, re-homes what names ground you do not hold, and judges what is yours, so the non-owner case fits none of its stages without changing what that pass is. It is not written as a rule, and a rule lands by Alan's direct approval rather than by any procedure standing today.

What points the other way is `Foreign State` on `domains/role.md`: treat state you did not create as another agent's work until you find out otherwise. A finding filed by another lead is exactly that, so a seat meeting a dead one and reading only what binds it will leave it standing. `domains/finding.md` still enumerates "its claim no longer true of the estate" among the grounds for deletion, so the ground exists with nobody assigned to act on it.

Not measured, and this is the weakness of the claim: I have not counted findings in the store whose claims no longer hold. A sweep on 2026-08-05 counted 335 findings but tested none of them against the estate as it stands. The hazard is reasoned from what binds rather than observed in the store, so the population could be zero. What would settle it is running the evidence of a sample and counting how many reproduce.
