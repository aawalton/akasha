# Claim

A finding recording a defect its own author caught is written as though the defect still stands, so a store of successful catches reads as a store of live failures. The lead who prices a domain by reading its findings counts each catch as an open gap, and a pass sent to write a standing claim against one arrives to find the mechanism it would have added already working and already named.

# Evidence

Measured 2026-08-04, first-hand, during a `define-principle-or-rule` pass over `findings/agent-harness/display-value-miscounted.md`, removed in the commit before this one.

Four findings in the live store describe one defect class — a figure measured over one population carried into a claim about another. Two of the four are written as reports of an unreported gap, by the seat that reported it.

`display-value-miscounted` stated "Nothing caught the substitution". Its own Evidence header reads "Measured 2026-08-01, first-hand, while defining #17441" — and #17441 is the pass that caught it. That row's exploration states the correction outright ("the parent's value claim counts a population that has no terminal"), re-derives the count split by `mode`, and the row is then judged worth doing on reasons that do not turn on the population. It shipped and Alan's ruling passed it. #17439's own notes still carry the figure uncorrected, in two places: the catch reached the decision, never the record.

`measured-narrow-concluded-wide` states "nothing reports the gap" and then reports four instances of it, all firsthand. Two of those instances are themselves findings; neither is in the store today.

The other two are honest about being catches. `corpus-counts-depend-on-the-grep` names the verifying lead's own earlier bad figure. `counted-evidence-unreproducible` records the re-measurements that produced it.

Findings are read a domain at a time, so a lead meets these as a set — and read as a set they price `agent-harness` for a defect the same record shows being absorbed by `role`/Secondhand and `define-project` stage 1.

Not measured: how many of the 136 findings now in the store record a catch rather than a live defect. Not measured: whether the shape holds outside this one defect class.
