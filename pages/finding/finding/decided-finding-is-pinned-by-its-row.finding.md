# Claim

A finding handed to a project cannot then be deleted, so the store keeps precisely the findings that have been decided. `domains/finding.md` says a finding is deleted when a decision is made about it, and handing it to a row is that decision. But the row cites the finding as the carrier of its readings, and the removal gate refuses any deletion that would strand a citation. The design says delete and the mechanism refuses, and neither is wrong.

# Evidence

MET THREE TIMES IN ONE `review-findings` RUN on 2026-08-11, each time as a refusal from `ops memory rm` naming the citing line:

- #18633 names `graph-checks-sleep-through-tsconfig.md`, and the sentence around it reads "Her finding is … and carries the readings." The row was cut from that finding and points back at it for its measurements.
- #18587 names two at once, `narrowed-to-the-extinct-member.md` and `role-parity-is-outside-the-stub-check.md`.
- A fourth case is a tree rather than a row: #18682 points at `rows-owed-by-the-finished-check-audit.md` as the home of seven pieces of work, so that finding is load-bearing for a tree of 87 children.

WHY IT IS NOT JUST A CITATION TO REWRITE. In one case it was — a row named a finding only to characterise its own defect, so the clause was rewritten to carry its own point and the finding went. In the three above the finding holds readings the row does not restate, so rewriting the citation means either copying the evidence onto the row, which `Single Authority` refuses, or dropping it.

WHAT THE STORE THEREFORE READS AS. Every finding nobody has acted on can be deleted freely; every finding that has been handed to work is pinned until that work lands. So a reader taking the store's size as a measure of unjudged observations reads it backwards, and the residue is heaviest exactly where the estate has been most diligent.

NOT ESTABLISHED: how many of the standing findings are pinned this way — I met four while judging one domain's set and did not sweep for the shape. Nor whether the right repair is at the finding end (a decided finding survives until its work lands, stated as design) or at the project end (a row cut from a finding carries the readings it needs, so the citation is provenance rather than a load-bearing reference). Both are coherent and they are not the same estate.
