# Claim

The persona-register task calls a cross-persona overlap "a finding" and then tells the seat to say it in the hand-back rather than file it.

# Evidence

`tasks/persona-craft/create-persona-register.md` L63 names a cross-persona overlap as a finding and directs the seat to state it in the hand-back.

`domains/finding.md` says anything an agent notices lands somewhere, at a cost low enough that noticing and filing are one act, and `ops memory file-finding` is that act. A hand-back reaches one recipient once and is gone; a finding sits under the domain that owns it until a decision is made about it.

So the word and the instruction point opposite ways, and the observation that would have been routed to the lead who owns the ground instead reaches whoever dispatched the seat.

This pass is evidence about which way it should go. Cross-persona overlaps were what several readings turned up — a shared shell figure between Natalie and Ione, a shared job-tell beat between Aura and Abby, a shared gold-line portrait between Lali and Aranya — and every one of them was worth a filing rather than a sentence in a message, because each names the whole cast rather than the file being worked.

The reviewing seat raised this and did not repair it. Whether the overlap should be filed or handed back is a call about how the task routes what it finds.
