# Claim

The visual-arts faucet is unreadable in both directions, and in each direction the discriminator exists unwired rather than absent. Its zero does not part a day the want never rose from a day it rose and found no stool — the silent failure the domain exists to prevent — and a nonzero would not part an hour with an answer key from an hour of open making, though by the firing rule only the second is in the scar's territory at all. Zeli's meter reads zero and has never moved.

# Evidence

Measured 2026-08-07 while emptying `dirty/skills/visual-arts/SKILL.md`, whose items 7 and 8 name both halves. I re-ran each surface.

The meter, from tracked source. `packages/alanwalton/daily-tracking/src/session-points-totals.ts:82-84` — "The exposure-therapy faucet meters TIME spent making hand-made art (session duration in minutes for `Art`-titled sessions), never output or quality: a failed/ugly/abandoned hour credits identically to a finished piece." The title is the whole predicate and duration the whole formula, so nothing in the reading carries which register the hour was in.

The reading. `ops persona level zeli`: level 1, greenDayTotal 0, percentProgress 0, balance 0. `ops persona daily-standing zeli`: dailyGreenDays 0, dailyColor none.

Why register is the thing the meter cannot see. `~/books/all-about-alan/notes/creativity-scars.md` — "The one principle that ties the whole pile together: there is no right answer to hide behind. Wherever I'm acting and there is no right version, no win condition, no answer key to check myself against, the belief fires. Wherever there *is* one, I'm safe." An answer-key hour sits outside the firing range; an open hour is the crossing. Both credit `duration / 60`.

The discriminator is not joined: `rg -uuni "safety"` over `session-points-totals.ts` returns nothing, so the faucet reads no safety signal.

Fourth instance of a shape whose instances want opposite repairs, and its reason is new. `pages/finding/chess/faucet-zero-is-unreadable.finding.md` is a wiring defect, `pages/finding/style/meter-counts-what-the-mission-ends.finding.md` a design mismatch, `pages/finding/prayer/voluntary-meter-zero-is-unreadable.finding.md` voluntary by a ruling Alan has said stands. Here the domain has not opened at all, so the zero is also correct — what is new is that a discriminator exists for both directions and neither is wired.
