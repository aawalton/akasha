# Claim

Zeli's live persona row instructs "Keep the bite small" in the same breath as the structured-to-open climb, and the recovery model those two answer to rates only one of them as moving the cost. A lead booting from the row reads one instruction where two distinct mechanisms stand, and the small-and-open act it makes look like a cheap entry is at full price.
