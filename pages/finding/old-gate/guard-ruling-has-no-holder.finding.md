# Claim

Nothing binds who may loosen a lifecycle guard. `domains/gate.md` and `domains/code-gate.md` are bare definitions and `domains/check.md` carries only Change Reach, so a guard Alan reviewed one at a time and ruled hard can be re-opened by whoever holds the project that touches it. The guards themselves are the only record that a ruling happened, and they do not say who ruled.

# Evidence

A standing ruling of 2026-07-27, held in `dirty/skills/agent-harness/rulings/rows.md` and reached by an ingest seat emptying that source. Its words: "Alan reviewed every hard transition prevention one at a time and ruled each. Nine went in; six survive, two die. Do not re-open a line of this without him." Cut from the source rather than kept, because its referent is that nine-row table and the table is now either live in the verbs or superseded. This is the part with no live carrier.

THE SURFACE DOES MOVE, measured here rather than supposed. The table records `parent -> awaiting_manager_claim` as hard, with the message deriving from `SETTLED_BOUNDARY`. `ops project move-to --help` today: the gate "asks about STARTABLE WORK rather than about settledness, and those differ exactly where it matters: settledness is a rank whose far end is the exits, because an exit is the most settled a row ever gets, so a rank read a parent whose only child was DROPPED as a fully defined tree." So one of the nine was re-opened and re-ruled after Alan's review. The other eight hold: I checked each against `move-to --help` and `create --help`.

WHAT I COULD NOT ESTABLISH, and it is the whole question: whether that change was made with Alan or without him. Nothing on the verb, in `domains/`, or in the ruling records who re-opened it. A change made with him and one made without leave the same help text.

`domains/gate.md`: a definition. `domains/code-gate.md`: a definition. `domains/check.md`: Change Reach, about what a check may measure. `domains/tasks/lead/verify-handback.md` carries to Alan "only what no instrument settles and no principle decides", which routes a question to him and says nothing about a decision he already made.

NOT MEASURED: whether any other guard in the estate carries a personal ruling, and whether any has been loosened since. I looked at this one table.
