# Claim

`domains/tasks/code-harness/review-check.md` carries three phrases whose two readings send a reviewer to measure different things, and no line in the document picks between them.

# Evidence

Found by a reader dispatched at the file under the plain-language initiative on 2026-08-06, which landed eighteen wording rewrites and could not settle these three.

Stage 3, "parting a check that states none from one declaring none while enumerating". Either a check silent about its population, which is the plain breach of Population on `domains/instrument.md`, or a check that reports its population is zero, which is honest. Under the first the contrast is silence against self-contradiction; under the second it is an honest zero against a zero printed while the check is visibly enumerating.

Stage 5, "part the acquisition from the judgement". Acquisition is either what the check spends getting itself ready — checkout, install, boot — or what it spends gathering the data it will judge. The example three clauses later, three rules over three repo walks, points at the second; a step's own start-up is exactly the other thing `step-cost` charges, so the first stays live. The two send a reviewer to time different things.

Stage 4, "the crossing pays for a condition that fails nothing else". Either no other check fails on this condition, which fits the sentence before it, or this condition breaks nothing else in the system, which fits the clause after it.

Also on the same document: `cohort` is used once, appears nowhere else in the corpus, and was rendered as "the files it covers" on the judgement that the two readings collapse for a reviewer's purposes. If it names something narrower — a package, a build target — that rewrite is wrong.
