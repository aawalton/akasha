# Claim

Of the two landed anthology chapters, one holds the stated pacing band and the other loosens: 9 of Aelwyn's 25 movements fall outside 120–250 words against 2 of Athena's 27.

# Evidence

Split on the markdown rule, as `author-persona-scene` 2.3 states the unit: Aelwyn 25 movements, mean 203 words, 9 outside the 120–250 band; Athena 27 movements, mean 189 words, 2 outside. "Roughly 25 movements" is exact in both.

The reviewing seat kept the line, reading this as one chapter loosening rather than a wrong band, and the measurement is recorded here because it bears on the chapters rather than on the instruction. Nobody has judged whether the drift matters.
