# Claim

A warrant on `author-persona-scene` L30 goes false by succeeding. It says a thing has "twice ... produced his strongest results on record", which stops being true the third time it happens — and the document gives no instrument that measures it, so nobody will notice the moment it turns. A count stated as a warrant is a claim with an expiry nothing watches.

# Evidence

Raised by the review-instructions reading of 2026-08-07, which also listed this among the two things it could not verify: no instrument in reach measures "strongest results on record".

So the line carries a figure that cannot be checked and cannot be maintained. The reading left it rather than cutting, the claim being a warrant rather than an act.

The same reading declined to write `maturityRating` into the Create line for a related reason: it is unanimous at `R` across the landed rows, but it describes one scene's content rather than the row's kind, so unanimity is not derivation. That is the discipline this finding is the counter-example to.
