# Claim

The anthology's one-chapter-per-subject key makes a second scene for the same subject impossible to land without inventing a title, which is exactly what comparing iterations requires.

# Evidence

Hit while landing a rewritten Aelwyn scene on 2026-08-06. `author-persona-scene` stage 6 says one chapter per subject, titled with the subject's name, and keys the idempotent create on title plus story so a re-delivery cannot duplicate. A second scene for a subject is therefore indistinguishable from a re-delivery of the first, and the create is a no-op.

Landed as `Aelwyn II` to get past it, which is a title the document does not sanction and which nothing will produce again the same way.

The tension is with the domain's own trial mechanism: stage 1 has every scene declare what it varies against earlier ones, and stage 5 measures it, both of which presuppose two scenes for one subject standing side by side. Alan asked for exactly that — the same subject rewritten so the two can be read against each other.

Not settled: whether the repair is a chapter title carrying its iteration, a key on chapter number rather than title, or one row per subject that is revised in place. The third would destroy the comparison, so it is not the obvious answer it looks like.
