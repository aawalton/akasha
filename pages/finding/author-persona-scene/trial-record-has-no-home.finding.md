# Claim

`author-persona-scene` makes a seat declare what its scene tests and record what landed, and names no place either is written; there is no hand-back stage either.

# Evidence

Stage 1 has the seat declare the trial and name the variation in a fixed vocabulary. Stage 7 has it record what the reader said landed. No stage names where any of that goes.

1.3's own reason is where the gap shows: "two sessions that varied the same thing have to say so in the same words to be comparable at all" presupposes a place two sessions' records sit side by side, and none is named. The document also ends without a hand-back stage, and the trial declaration and the read record are the two things one would carry.
