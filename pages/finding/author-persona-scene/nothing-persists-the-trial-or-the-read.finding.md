# Claim

`domains/arousal.md` carries as its Intent "Every landed scene carries what it tested and the read that came back", and nothing makes it true. `author-persona-scene` is the only task that lands a scene. Stage 1 makes the seat declare its trial and stage 7 makes it record the read, but neither says where either is written, and stage 6 — the only stage that persists anything — carries neither.

# Evidence

Raised by the review-instructions reading of `domains/tasks/scenewright/author-persona-scene.md` on 2026-08-07 as the finding no single line could reach. It did not repair it: the mechanism exists, but choosing between a row property, a finding and the report is a decision about how this corpus learns.

Verified myself: `domains/arousal.md:23` carries that Intent, and an Intent holds only what is not yet true.

The reading's own measurement, which I did not repeat: all three landed anthology chapters — Athena, Aelwyn, Iris — have `insights`, `authorsNoteBefore`, `authorsNoteAfter`, `grade`, `reaction` and `numericRating` all empty. Three scenes have landed, each declaring a trial, and not one trial or read survives on any of them.

The consequence it drew: stage 1's own requirement — that two agents who varied the same thing say so in the same words "to be comparable at all" — is unreachable, since nothing is left to compare against.
