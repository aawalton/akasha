# Claim

`stabilize` and `pleasure` are used as terms of art and declared nowhere. They appear in no file under `domains/` except the one line that uses them, and `Plain Or Declared` says to declare such a word as a domain first.

# Evidence

Measured 2026-08-06. A search of the whole `domains/` tree for `stabilize` or `pleasure` returns exactly one file: `domains/tasks/scenewright/author-persona-scene.md` itself, at line 23.

`domains/global.md` — "**Plain Or Declared** — Write the plain phrase; where you give a word a sense of its own, declare it as a domain first. Nobody looks up a word they read as ordinary, so the wrong sense is carried off silently."

Its Glossary defines the class this falls in: "**Jargon** — a word used in a sense plain English does not carry, which no domain defines."

The document says what each word DOES to the weighting and never what it IS. The reading that raised it lists the live possibilities — a mode named in the ask, a literal value, something else — and could not tell from the text which.

`pleasure` in plain English is a feeling; here it governs a proportion of movements. `stabilize` in plain English is an act on a system; here it constrains a crest. Neither sense is the ordinary one, which is the condition the principle names.

Filed rather than repaired: declaring a domain is well past a slice repair, and the words cannot be replaced by plain phrases without first settling what they refer to, which the document does not say.

Same class as `pages/finding/project/red-is-an-undeclared-coinage.finding.md`, filed the same day from a different family. `Plain Or Declared` landed on 2026-08-06 and nothing has been swept against it; two independent readings have now hit it by accident. The instrument that would sweep — `terms-in-reach` — tracks declared terms only, so it cannot see this class at all.
