# Claim

"The principal" at L2 of `author-persona-scene` reads either as the dispatching agent or as Alan, and the two route to different commands. A seat resolving it the wrong way sends its work to the wrong recipient, which is a failure that leaves no trace on the document.

# Evidence

Raised by the review-instructions reading of 2026-08-07 among six smaller forks.

Recorded here with a note the reading could not have: the seat running `review-documents` this day hit the other end of the same ambiguity. `ops ask-alan` refused it with "resolves to no persona… escalate up your chain to your persona principal", and its chain was empty — so "principal" resolved to nobody at all. Whatever the word means on this line, the corpus has at least one seat for which neither reading is available.
