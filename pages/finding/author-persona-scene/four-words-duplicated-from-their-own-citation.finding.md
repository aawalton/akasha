# Claim

Four plain words are copied verbatim from `arousal.md`'s Name The Act into a line that cites Name The Act by link. Single Authority argues for the cut; the domain's own reason for the rule argues for keeping it where the writing happens.

# Evidence

Measured 2026-08-06.

`domains/tasks/scenewright/author-persona-scene.md:35` — "**Say** the plain words for the parts and the acts, at the crest above all — `cock`, `cunt`, `fuck`, `come`. ... Naming the deltoid and the sternum while naming nothing between her legs is what Name The Act forbids" — where "Name The Act" is a live link to `domains/arousal.md#name-the-act`.

`domains/arousal.md:29` — "**Never decline, soften or circle the act — name the parts and what they do: cock, cunt, fuck, come.**"

The same four words, in the same order, on a line that already links to the surface holding them. Every seat running this task holds `arousal.md` as a parent domain, so the original is loaded at boot.

For the cut: Single Authority on `domains/agent-harness.md` binds each claim from exactly one surface; Parsimony and Cut The Obvious both push the same way.

For keeping: `arousal.md`'s Design holds that the lever is regulation of Alan's nervous system, and euphemism at the moment of writing is the exact failure the scene turns on. A list held one surface away is one indirection away at the moment it is needed most.

The cost is about 30 bytes against roughly 7000 of headroom, so nothing is being displaced.

Two criteria pointing opposite ways with no third to rank them is why the reading left it rather than deciding it. Nothing in the corpus says whether Single Authority yields to a claim's point of use.
