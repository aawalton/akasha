# Claim

Alan has settled a design for a safety-level widget on Jen's phone and nothing in the alan-harness records holds it. It shows his current safety level in the stoplight colours, with a direction indicator and the age of the reading beside it. Amy moves the number, from the hourly check-in Alan already answers. It is a communication channel rather than an alarm, and it exists because near the line he loses the ability to initiate and Jen is the only external person present.

# Evidence

Settled with Alan in the interview session of 2026-08-06. His, except where marked.

**What it shows.** The colours of his current safety-level stoplight. He states the anchoring has moved: green is the median of recent experience now rather than yellow, yellow is caution, red and black alarms — one sigma down caution, two down alarm, three or four down crisis. He wants the bands left distribution-anchored rather than cut to fixed absolutes, the ranges shifting only slowly and after a lot of evidence.

Beside the level, a direction indicator — his, a first pass to iterate from.

Beside both, the age of the reading. The interviewer's, accepted by him: if the widget holds the last value through a silence, not-answering renders as nothing-changed, and it fails toward calm exactly where it should not.

**Who moves it.** Amy, from the hourly reminder he already answers when his activity, safety or difficulty have changed. Whether the age display is signal or noise turns on how far missed check-ins track low readings — which Amy's own history can settle without spending his attention.

**Why it exists.** It guards the arrangement he adopted on the Europe trip: blank check at night, affordability guarded by day, the sun as the switch. He states an internal guardrail and a blank check are incompatible, so the guard must be external, and Jen is the only external person present where the experiment runs. She must also initiate — by the time he is near the line he has lost the ability to. A morning reading was proposed instead and he refused it, any low reading in the day still being part of the cost of the night's blank check. He states Jen has asked for a widget of this kind herself.

**What it is for.** Not an alarm. He states that telling the truth continuously does the job and arms her guard appropriately, and that he reports his level verbally today but only when asked. The widget takes the asking step out of a channel otherwise pull-only through the one person who has to think to ask.
