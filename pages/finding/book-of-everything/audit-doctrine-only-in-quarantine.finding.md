# Claim

The audit phase's two governing rulings survive only in a quarantined file, and the verb implementing one of them defaults against it. The rulings are that the phase's goal is measurement coverage toward 100% scored by `ops ali coverage` rather than root C, and that thread selection is a pure random unopened-leaf draw with no menu offered, the draw being a hesitation-breaker because choosing is the obstacle at a session's start. `ops ali next-unscored` defaults to deterministic order.

# Evidence

Read 2026-08-07. The phase is live in the machinery. `ops ali coverage`'s registry line names it — "Measurement coverage of the Book of Everything (audit phase): fraction assessed at all" — and `ops ali next-unscored`'s names "the audit sweep". So the instruments know which phase they are for.

What they do not carry is either ruling. `ops ali next-unscored --help` reads: "Default: the first unopened leaf in deterministic tree order (a resumable sweep cursor). --random: a uniformly-random unopened leaf (unbiased sampling)." A seat running the verb as documented gets the deterministic cursor, the opposite of the rule. Nothing in `ops ali coverage` says root C is the wrong scoreboard, and nothing anywhere says not to offer a menu of threads.

The rulings stand at `dirty/skills/knowledge/rulings.md:14-28`, kept verbatim at `dirty/maybe-keep/skills/knowledge/rulings.md` as that file is emptied: "The goal is measurement coverage toward 100%, not raising any single score, and the scoreboard is `bun ops ali coverage` rather than root C", and "Thread selection in this phase is a pure random unopened-leaf draw, which doubles as the hesitation-breaker: when the start is what is hard, drawing removes the choosing cost that was the actual obstacle. Do not offer a menu."

The reason given for the draw is a sampling argument, and the live numbers still show what it was set against. `ops ali coverage` run today prints 21 / 177 sections, Life on Earth 0/22, History 2/39, Technology 1/15 against Art 4/12 and Religion 3/11 — identical to the specimen written beside the ruling, no section having opened since.

Where I searched: `rg -uuu` over `~/memory/findings/` for `audit phase|random draw|unopened leaf|hesitation|next-unscored|scoreboard` returns four files; three are the ali and book-of-everything findings, which I read in full and which carry none of this, and the fourth is an unrelated domain.
