# Claim

The definition of seat assignment calls it "the work a seat has been handed", and one of its five kinds has no work. `domains/seat-assignment-availability.md` states that an availability assignment has no work to finish, so the parent's definition is drawn from four of its five children. The same split reaches the Design entry on when an assignment ends, which is why that child carries an ending clause of its own.

# Evidence

Raised by a dispatched reading of `domains/seat-assignment.md` on 2026-08-08 (seat `claude-seat-assignment-archivist-review-instructions`; report at `~/agents/claude-seat-assignment-archivist-review-instructions/review-seat-assignment.md`), and left standing there as resting on judgment rather than on any instrument.

Both halves read from disk: the parent's Definition line and Design entry, and the child's "An availability assignment has no work to finish: it ends when its principal releases it or is gone."

Not measured: whether the other four kinds all carry work, which I did not check one by one. Which line should move — the parent's definition, the child's, or neither — is untouched here.
