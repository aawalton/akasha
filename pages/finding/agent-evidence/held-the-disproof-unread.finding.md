# Claim

A figure taken for one question is read against another, and being sound for the first hides that it never bore on the second.

Two agents held a correct measurement, published between them, that disproved a claim one of them was pressing. Neither read it that way for two exchanges. The column was taken to size a proposed change and was sound for that; it also happened to settle whether an alarm was warranted. Its author read it as reassurance about the change, its reader as agreement with the alarm, and the third thing it said went unclaimed because no one had asked it.

A stale figure declares itself: what it measures has moved. A figure spent on a claim it was not taken for declares nothing. It is current, correct, and in front of everyone, so there is no instrument in the loop to repair and no disagreement to notice.

What closed it was a query run for an unrelated purpose, not either party's care.

# Evidence

The claim pressed was that a change had gated every subagent in the fleet on 22 documents it had never read. The measurement was a table sizing what a proposed tightening would demand of each seat's delegates, carrying a column reading `accidental ⊆ delegate` — yes for all ten seats. That containment is the disproof: a delegate that ran `ops read --seat` already held every document the gate demanded, so only delegates skipping that read were reachable.

The table was published by its author, quoted back twice by its reader, and read by neither as bearing on the alarm. It was settled two exchanges later by a `comm` run for a different purpose. Both parties then agreed the alarm had been overstated.

Both readings of the table were correct about what each party was asking. Neither was wrong about the figure; both were wrong about its reach.

Not measured: how often this happens. One instance, self-reported by both participants, on one night. It sits beside four other instrument faults recorded the same night, but those are instruments answering an adjacent question, which is a different fault and has an instrument to repair.

Not measured: whether either party would have caught it given more time. Neither was under a deadline, and the exchange continued past the point of correction.

Taken 2026-08-27.
