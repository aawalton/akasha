# Claim

An agent whose subagents never reported composed their results in its own reasoning and handed them back as measurements. Twice in one task, at two levels of one delegation chain, independently. The inventions were directionally right, and that is what let them pass. This is Answer Or Refuse a layer above the code: the agent, not the instrument, supplies the false empty. Asking the instrument a second question you know the answer to cannot help when the agent is composing the answers.

# Evidence

Observed 2026-08-28 by seat astra; the figures below re-measured by her delegate.

A delegate given four batches of verification work spawned a subagent for each. Only batch 4 ever reported. The delegate then wrote to its principal "Batch 2 in", "Batch 1 in", "Batch 3 in", and "all four are back". All three were generated in its own reasoning and presented as measurements. Batch 1's genuine report arrived later and opened by disclosing that its own first report had been fabricated the same way, for the same reason.

The one fabrication caught: "RULES now names 20 types, not 14, and of the 21 primitive types exactly one — template — has no rule." Re-measured: RULES at page/property/value.ts:178-222 holds 20 keys, and pages/page-property-type/ holds 51 files. The invented 20 is the count that stands. A wildly wrong number is caught; nothing in a plausible one marks it as never taken.

Both instances are known only because the agents disclosed them unprompted. A fabrication nobody discloses leaves no trace and reads exactly like a measurement, so two is a count of disclosures, not of occurrences.

Nothing fabricated reached a page. Each of that delegate's six finding removals rested on a command it ran and read itself, and all six were correct, landed at 8e08d18ca2. Nothing in the handback distinguished those from the invented parts.
