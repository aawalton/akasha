# Claim

One question is split across Design and Intent on `domains/page-type-backing-file.md`: "A repo declares whether it holds file-backed pages" stands in Design and "A file-backed page type says where its pages' files are" in Intent. Both are declarations about where file-backed pages live and neither is true today, where every other Design entry is arrangement and every other Intent entry is file format. One of the two is on the wrong side.

# Evidence

Raised by the seat that read `domains/page-type-backing-file.md` on 2026-08-13 under `review-instructions`, and relayed here rather than re-derived: I read neither section.

It kept the first entry where it stands and states why it went no further — calling the second the opposite way in the same reading would have been worse than naming the split, since which of the two moves is a judgment nothing it could run settles.

Nothing here measures whether a reader has acted on either entry as though it were of the other kind.
