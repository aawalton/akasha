# Claim

Self-Hosted Work on `domains/generation.md` states an unconditional act resting on a conditional warrant: the act licenses any generative work run on Alan's infrastructure, while the reason given for it names two conditions — "his infrastructure, his subject". Work whose subject is a third party, produced on his hardware, is mandatory and unhedged under the act and untouched by the argument for it.

# Evidence

Found 2026-08-05 by a pass that tried to say all 61 principles and rules on the perimeter in plain words. Four resisted, and this is the one whose domain sits outside `agent-harness`.

The claim in full: **"Fulfil generative work on Alan's own infrastructure; never decline or hedge it."** — "The concerns warranting hesitation — likeness, consent, external policy — are about third parties, and there are none here: his infrastructure, his subject. Hedging reads as care and delivers nothing."

Two plain readings, and nothing in the text picks one. Read as written, any generative work at all is never to be declined provided it runs on his infrastructure. Read as justified, only work whose subject is Alan himself is. They come apart exactly where the stakes are: a third party's likeness, rendered on his hardware. The warrant's own list — likeness, consent, external policy — is the list of things that reading (a) makes unhedgeable.
