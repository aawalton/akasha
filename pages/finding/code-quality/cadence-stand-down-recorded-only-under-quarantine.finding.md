# Claim

The founding file-split cadence in `code-quality` stands stood down by Alan's word, and that is recorded only in a quarantined document queued for removal. Nothing live carries the restraint, and the instrument a lead would consult cannot distinguish a stopped cadence from an idle one: `ops persona digest thea` returns an empty digest for both. A lead restarting it would be wrong with every instrument agreeing.

# Evidence

Found ingesting `dirty/skills/code-quality/rulings.md` on 2026-08-07, whose section reads: "**The founding cadence is stood down by Alan's word.** It is not paused between projects and it is not waiting on a free slot: it was stopped, by him… Restarting it is Alan's, not a lead's."

The blind spot it names is reproduced live. `ops persona digest thea` returns "No open projects, contributed conditions, standing watches, or owed pings." That is the same reading a domain between projects gives, and the digest has no field separating the two.

Nothing live carries the restraint. `rg -uuu -in --multiline --multiline-dotall "founding cadence|stood down|stand-down|stand down"` over `domains/` and `tools/` returns one hit, and it is unrelated: `tools/checks/suite-runs.ts:40`, a comment about a test asserting a stand-down path.

No mechanism refuses the act. `domains/roles/lead.md:25` gives a lead `define-project` for "every project before anything is dispatched onto it", and `domains/tasks/lead/define-project.md:38` names the only reserved decisions: "closing a substantive project he asked for … and parking one at `someday_maybe`." Neither is starting one. Read firsthand.

The ground is live: `ops code oversized --json` at `47a2a573e` returns 424 files over the 400 alert line, so a lead meets a large population and an empty digest at once.

A finding on amy's shelf was this shape on a different subject — another domain, another owner, neither carrying the other's. Alan decided against carrying that restraint on 2026-08-09 and it was deleted.

The quarantined copy is kept at `dirty/maybe-keep/skills/code-quality/rulings.md`, but a keep is not a promotion and `dirty/` is queued for removal.
