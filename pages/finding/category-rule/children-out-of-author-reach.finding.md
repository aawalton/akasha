# Claim

`domains/category-rule.md` names no `glossary:`, so three of its own children never reach the rule author it is handed to: `category-rule-counterpart`, `category-rule-mode` and `category-rule-match-condition` sit below it and the walk runs upward. Somebody writing `counterpart-within-days: 7` has read that a rule may require a counterpart and has never read what one is.

# Evidence

Raised by the seat that read `domains/category-rule.md` on 2026-08-13 under `review-instructions`, and relayed here rather than re-derived: I did not run the glossary walk or open the three children.

That seat reports the `terms-in-reach` check as unable to name this, on the check's own stated ground that reach is something an author states rather than something an instrument recovers, and reports the subject appearing in none of that check's eight advisories.

It left the document alone because an Add resting on judgment goes back to the principal: which terms to name is a call somebody could reasonably disagree with.

Nothing here measures whether a rule has actually been written wrong for want of the three definitions.
