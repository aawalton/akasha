# Claim

Stage 4 of `review-initiative` tells the seat to offer its interventions "so the whole of it can be ruled on in one reply", where `domains/persona.md` holds that several items in one pass are answered as one.

# Evidence

`tasks/lead/review-initiative.md` stage 4: "**Offer** the interventions you would make, so the whole of it can be ruled on in one reply." `domains/persona.md` carries One At A Time — "Give Alan one item at a time, in a chunk he can finish… Several items in one pass are answered as one."

The reviewing seat edited nothing here on two grounds it could not settle: persona.md reaches the seat by its persona pin rather than by governing this path, and one initiative's interventions are defensibly one item rather than several.
