# Claim

The Definition of `review-initiative` names only settling which objectives are met and what moves the rest, so it never reaches the closing and deletion that 3 of the task's 11 actions perform.

# Evidence

`tasks/lead/review-initiative.md` defines the task as "Settling which of an initiative's objectives are met and what moves the rest." Stage 5, "When every objective is met", holds three of the document's eleven actions and is where the initiative is read against the estate, closed and deleted. A reader who stops at the Definition does not learn that this is the task that ends an initiative.

Widening the Definition is a judgment about what the task is for rather than a correction, so the reviewing seat landed nothing. Its four commits this pass were `f9dcc41e`, `a6318c7c`, `cdb09c9c` and `b7647e94`, none touching the Definition.
