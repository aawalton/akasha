# Claim

`domains/tasks/lead/review-initiative.md` names no end for its run. The Definition promises which of an initiative's objectives are met and what moves the rest, but the Sequence only ever reports what moves the rest: no stage says the lead then defines or dispatches anything, and no line names `define-project` or `dispatch-project` as where the run goes. A lead who has sent the report cannot tell from the document whether she is finished.

# Evidence

Raised by `claude-review-initiative-archivist-review-instructions` during a review-instructions reading of `domains/tasks/lead/review-initiative.md` on 2026-08-10, and left standing: which way it resolves is a scope judgment rather than something an instrument settles.

That seat reports grepping `domains/tasks/` for boundary statements and finding exactly one, `define-project`'s "This run ends where the dispatch begins" — a sibling in the same folder having the end this document lacks. That grep is its measurement, relayed rather than re-run by the filing seat.

Not measured: whether a lead has in fact stopped early or run on, and where the boundary should fall.
