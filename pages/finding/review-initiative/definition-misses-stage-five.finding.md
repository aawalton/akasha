# Claim

`review-initiative`'s Definition covers four of its five stages. It reads "settling which of an initiative's objectives are met and what moves the rest" — stages 1 to 4. Stage 5, "When every objective is met", is the only stage that ends anything, and the Definition does not reach it. No reading can land the repair: Every Changed Line on `domains/domain.md` makes a domain's Definition Alan's, and this document declares `domain-slug:`.

# Evidence

Raised by the review-instructions reading of 2026-08-07, which named it and could not land it.

Verified myself in the live document: the Definition bullet reads as quoted, and the Sequence carries five numbered stages — "Read the objectives against what now stands", "Establish what is moving", "Name the blocker for whatever is not moving", "The report", and "When every objective is met".
