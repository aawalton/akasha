# Claim

Stage 4 sends a report to Alan with no condition on it, and stage 5 carries a closing to him when every objective is met. The document does not say whether a pass that meets every objective runs both, so it may reach him twice for one reading.

# Evidence

Measured 2026-08-06 on `domains/tasks/lead/review-initiative.md` at `reviewed-at: 2026-08-06`.

`domains/tasks/lead/review-initiative.md:28-30`, stage 4 "The report" — "**Offer** the interventions you would make, so the whole of it can be ruled on in one reply." and "**Send** it to Alan and act on what he rules before taking the next initiative." Neither line carries a condition.

`domains/tasks/lead/review-initiative.md:32-35`, stage 5 "When every objective is met" — "**Carry** the closing to Alan before anything is removed, with every objective as met and whatever that reading left open beside it."

Stage 5's heading is the only condition anywhere in the sequence, and it selects the case where stage 4's report would say every box is ticked and no intervention is proposed. Whether stage 5 replaces stage 4 in that case, follows it, or is folded into the one reply stage 4 asks for, is not written.

Stage 4's own line "so the whole of it can be ruled on in one reply" reads as an argument against sending twice, but it is an argument about stage 4's contents rather than an instruction about stage 5, so it settles nothing on its own.
