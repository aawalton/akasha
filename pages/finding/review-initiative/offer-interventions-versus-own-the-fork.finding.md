# Claim

Stage 4 instructs the lead to offer Alan the interventions she would make; Own The Fork on `domains/persona.md` bars offering him a menu of your own acts. `persona.md` does not govern this document, so nothing adjudicates which of the two holds.

# Evidence

Measured 2026-08-06 on `domains/tasks/lead/review-initiative.md` at `reviewed-at: 2026-08-06`.

`domains/tasks/lead/review-initiative.md:29` — "**Offer** the interventions you would make, so the whole of it can be ruled on in one reply."

`domains/persona.md`, Own The Fork — "Take the choice you were placed to make, rather than offering Alan a menu of your own acts. A menu spends his turn on judgment he already delegated. He answers outside it, or approves what you would have done anyway. What his preference settles, or what only he can do, stays his."

`ops instructions governs --file-path domains/tasks/lead/review-initiative.md` returns eight whole surfaces — agent-harness, instructions-repo, global, initiative, memory, role, lead, task — and four section-anchored pairs. `domains/persona.md` is not among them. So this is not a breach of a binding claim; it is a tension no governing surface settles.

Which way it goes turns on what an intervention is, and both readings have a carrier:

- An act on the initiative document. `domains/tasks/lead/define-initiative.md:31` — "Alan settles what the document says and you write it down." Under this reading offering them is Own The Fork's own last clause working, since what only he can do stays his.
- The lead's own act, such as dispatching a project against a stalled objective. `domains/roles/lead.md:10` names the lead "answerable for what a domain is and becomes, settled with Alan" — which does not cleanly settle it either, because "settled with Alan" is itself a joint act rather than a delegation.

Not established: whether `domains/persona.md` is meant to reach task documents at all, or only the registers under `domains/personas/`. Nothing on either surface says.

Raised by the `review-instructions` reading of this document on 2026-08-06, which landed three commits and returned this fork rather than settling it.
