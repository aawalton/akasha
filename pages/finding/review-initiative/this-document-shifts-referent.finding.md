# Claim

`review-initiative` calls the initiative "this document", "the line" and "the document" while the seat reading it also holds the task document, so each reference resolves on a second reading and none on the first.

# Evidence

A seat running `tasks/lead/review-initiative.md` holds two documents at once: the task and the initiative it is reviewing. The initiative is named "this document" at line 14, "the line" at line 15 and "the document" at line 36, with nothing distinguishing those from the task the seat is reading them in.

`domains/persona.md` carries Standalone, which reaches this. One vocabulary would touch several lines and which word is right is a judgment no instrument settles, so nothing was landed.
