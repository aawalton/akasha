# Claim

Two rules guard the same kind of line and disagree on whether a dispatched review's cut is one. Every Changed Line on `domains/domain.md` binds every line changed in a domain's Definition, Design, Intent, Principles or Rules to be shown to Alan, stating no exception. Responsibility Change on `domains/role-responsibilities.md` carves one out: "A cut a dispatched review lands is not a changed line." Read strictly, the first leaves review-instructions unrunnable on a domain document.

# Evidence

Raised by a review-instructions seat reviewing `domains/agent-governance.md`, which landed its one cut (commit 6f95a18b, a Design line) on the reading the sibling rule spells out, and handed the conflict back rather than resolving it.

I read both rules as quoted and they say what is reported. I did not survey how other dispatched reviews have treated Every Changed Line in practice, and I did not check whether any third document reconciles the two.
