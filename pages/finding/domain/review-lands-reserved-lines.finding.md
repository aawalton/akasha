# Claim

An unattended seat running `review-instructions` cannot obey both that task and `domains/domain.md`'s Every Changed Line: the rule reserves a domain's Definition, Design, Intent, Principles and Rules to Alan, and the task sends the seat to cut, repair, trim and rewrite those sections and commit each. `domains/role-responsibilities.md` settles the same collision for its own section — "A cut a dispatched review lands is not a changed line" — and `domains/domain.md` has no equivalent.

# Evidence

Raised by the seat that read `domains/agent-harness.md` on 2026-08-12 under `review-instructions`, and relayed here rather than re-derived: I did not re-read either document against the other, and the quoted sentence from `domains/role-responsibilities.md` is that seat's quotation rather than mine.

The case is live rather than hypothetical. That reading landed two changes inside the reserved sections and took them both back in the same run (`ce802d5`, `fa6bf30`) on finding the rule, so half of what it landed came out again. Its report stands at `~/agents/claude-agent-harness-archivist-review-instructions/review-agent-harness.md`.

Nothing measures how many other runs of this task have landed in a reserved section without noticing the rule.
