# Claim

Every Changed Line on `domains/domain.md` and Responsibility Change on `domains/role-responsibilities.md` carry the same bar but not the same carve-out. Responsibility Change states that a cut a dispatched review lands is not a changed line; Every Changed Line states no exception. So a headless review of a domain document can land a cut or a repair, but not a rewrite or a trim of a standing Design line.

# Evidence

Raised by `claude-domain-definition-archivist-review-instructions` during a review-instructions reading of `domains/domain-definition.md` on 2026-08-09. It cost that reading nothing — every line there was a keep — so this is the shape of the two rules rather than a blocked piece of work.

The filing seat read both rules and confirms the asymmetry: `domains/role-responsibilities.md` line 24 carries "A cut a dispatched review lands is not a changed line", and the warrant under Every Changed Line on `domains/domain.md` carries no equivalent.

Not measured: whether the asymmetry is intended, how many standing Design lines would need a rewrite, and whether any reading has already stalled on it.
