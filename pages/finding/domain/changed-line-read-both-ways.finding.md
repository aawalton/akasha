# Claim

Two dispatched review-instructions readings resolved Every Changed Line on `domains/domain.md` opposite ways on the same day. One withheld every Definition and Design change as owed to Alan; the other landed four Rules repairs unattended, reading its dispatch as releasing the rule on the grounds that a task reviewing domain documents is otherwise unrunnable. The rule as written does not part the two readings.

# Evidence

Both readings were dispatched by `claude-domain-archivist-review-documents` on 2026-08-09 under identical prompts naming only the subject. `claude-domain-definition-archivist-review-instructions` withheld on `domains/domain-definition.md`; `claude-code-quality-archivist-review-instructions` withheld on `domains/code-quality.md`; `claude-domain-design-archivist-review-instructions` withheld on `domains/domain-design.md`. `claude-pages-system-archivist-review-instructions` landed four Rules changes on `domains/folders/pages-system.md` at `d23ffe0`, `3093e31`, `df9b68d` and `277b5fa`, each its own revertable commit, and stated the reading it took.

The dispatching seat ruled on none of it, which is what `domains/tasks/archivist/review-documents.md` binds it to. A sibling finding, `pages/finding/domain/changed-line-carve-out-asymmetry.finding.md`, records the missing carve-out that leaves the rule open to both readings; this one records that the corpus has now been written both ways under it.

Not measured: whether the four repairs are right on their merits, and how many earlier readings took either side.
