# Claim

`domains/domain.md`'s Every Changed Line binds a dispatched review with no carve-out, while the parallel rule one domain over carves one out. `domains/role-responsibilities.md` reads "A cut a dispatched review lands is not a changed line"; the domain rule says only that Alan can release it for a piece of work. A dispatched reading of a domain therefore lands cuts in a Definition or a Design entry under a rule that reads as forbidding them, on an inference that the dispatch was the release.

# Evidence

Both rules read from disk on 2026-08-08. The case is live rather than hypothetical: the reading of `domains/seat-assignment.md` landed `3ae9de603` on a Design entry and `c953d44ec` on the Definition line, unattended and without showing Alan, on exactly that inference.

Not measured: whether any other domain carries a third spelling of the same carve-out, which I did not search for. Whether the carve-out should be written onto `domains/domain.md` is a changed rule line and is stated here as an observation only.
