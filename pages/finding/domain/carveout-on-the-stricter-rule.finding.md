# Claim

The review carve-out sits on the stricter of the two rules and is missing from the looser one, which is the wrong direction for a deliberate difference. Responsibility Change guards a role's Responsibilities, which by its own warrant run unasked and are never dispatched and never refused — and it carries "A cut a dispatched review lands is not a changed line." Every Changed Line guards a domain's five content sections, is the looser rule, and carries no such sentence. That reads as drift.

# Evidence

Raised by a review-instructions seat on `domains/domain.md`, which searched the corpus for the rule's shape and found exactly two hits — Every Changed Line here and Responsibility Change on `domains/role-responsibilities.md`. They cover disjoint sections, so nothing is duplicated between them; the observation is about which of the two carries the exception.

This is the third finding this pass touching the same pair. `pages/finding/domain/changed-line-review-exception.finding.md` records that the two rules disagree in text. `pages/finding/domain/review-readings-diverged.finding.md` records that two reviewers acted on them opposite ways. This one records the argument for reading the gap as drift rather than as design, which is what a lead deciding it would actually weigh.

The reviewer was straight that it blocked nothing on its own run: it found no cut to land in those sections regardless. I did not run the corpus search myself. Nothing here measures whether the asymmetry was ever deliberate — no commit history was read on either rule.
