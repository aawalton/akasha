# Claim

A dispatched review that finds a plain repair in a domain's Principles or Rules has no carve-out from "Every Changed Line" in `page-types/domain.md`, which says to show Alan each such line. `domains/role-responsibilities.md` carves one out for Responsibilities and nothing carves one out here. A review reaching such a repair must either hold it for Alan or land it against a rule that says not to.

# Evidence

Raised by the `review-instructions` reading of `domains/alan-email.md` finished 2026-08-21, read line by line, bottom to top. It did not bind on that reading: every change that reading would have made rested on judgment and went back to its principal rather than into the document, so nothing was landed against the rule.

Not measured here: I did not read `page-types/domain.md` or `domains/role-responsibilities.md` for this, and I did not look for a third document carving out the same case. How often a dispatched review reaches a plain repair in a Rules or Principles section is uncounted, so how live this is stands unread.
