# Claim

"Extraction pass" is `domains/ops-awen.md`'s own term and stands nowhere else in the corpus. The Design entry binding what a pass holds and which three commands it owes gives a reader no way to tell which passes are extraction passes. There are four today — `ingest`, `chronology`, `class-skill` and `authors-note` — and nothing in the document or above it names them.

# Evidence

Found during the review-instructions reading of `domains/ops-awen.md` on 2026-08-19, by grepping the corpus for the term and by listing `tools/commands/awen/` for the twelve `{pass}-{plan,status,quarantine}.ts` files.

Measured: that the term appears in this document alone, and that four passes carry the trio today. Not measured: whether naming them in the entry is worth what it costs as passes are added — the reviewer left the entry generic rather than settle that.
