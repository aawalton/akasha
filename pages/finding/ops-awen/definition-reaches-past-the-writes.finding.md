# Claim

Two words in the `ops-awen` definition do not hold. "The commands that write" reaches 46 of the 77: the per-command documents give 46 intervention against 16 report and 15 ruling, and six commands open their own first line with "Read" or "Emit". "By hand" has two readings that ask for different things — not by deployed code, which `domains/narrative-engine.md` intends, or not by the game master's loop, which is false, the GM calling `design` and `lore` mid-turn.

# Evidence

Counted during the review-instructions reading of `domains/ops-awen.md` on 2026-08-19, by reading the kind on each of the 77 per-command documents and the first line of each command's `--help`.

Measured: the 46/16/15 split and the six commands whose own first word is a read. Not measured: which of the two readings of "by hand" Alan meant — `page-types/domain.md` sends a Definition change to him regardless, so the reviewer left the line whole.
