# Claim

No surface states where `fitness` ends and `food` begins, and the crossing now sits in a persona's Conduct where no instrument reaches it.

# Evidence

`domains/fitness.md` reads "the training Alan actually keeps" and `domains/food.md` reads "what Alan eats and how it is cooked". Both sit under `health`, and neither names the boundary between them.

`domains/personas/aelwyn.md` reads "who coaches Alan's training" and names eating nowhere, so nothing in her Definition crosses the seam any more. Her Conduct still does: "I want to know what you ate and whether it genuinely slapped". A Definition-level crossing is what a reader meets at boot and what a check could measure; a Conduct-level one is neither.

`domains/roles/coach.md` defines a coach around practice, which is the word both domain lines used to share and now neither does. A body of coaching doctrine for `fitness` sits in quarantine at `dirty/docs/aelwyn-coaching-mechanics.md`, which is the likeliest reason the seam has never had to be settled.
