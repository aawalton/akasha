# Claim

Three of Alan's marketing rules archive without unsubscribing, though **Stop The Source** on `domains/alan-email.md` binds both acts and names the classes they match.

# Evidence

Read while reviewing `domains/alan-email.md` on 2026-08-14. **Stop The Source** reads "Unsubscribe from marketing mail as well as archiving it", and its description names "Offers, digests, newsletters and launch announcements".

`email/alan/rules/code/meetup-digest.md`, `backerkit-vault-promo.md` and `audible-credit-reminder.md` each state `actions: archive` alone. `marketing-senders.md`, `lob-marketing.md`, `royalroad-marketing.md`, `tsa-precheck-clear-promo.md` and `twitch-is-live.md` state `archive` and `unsubscribe` together.

A digest and a promo are two of the classes the rule names, so the first two look like the rule not carried out. What is not established here is whether those senders advertise a route to unsubscribe on at all: `ops email unsubscribe` goes by the headers a message carries, and a sender offering none cannot be left. Whether a credit reminder is marketing is a further question this does not settle.

`domains/rules-engine.md` **Ask Before Changing** puts any change to these rules behind Alan's approval, so nothing was changed.
