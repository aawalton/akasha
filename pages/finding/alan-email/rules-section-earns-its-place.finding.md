# Claim

The Rules on `domains/alan-email.md` are not a restatement of the code rules under `email/alan/rules/code/`: refunds, paid surveys and advisory panels are named on the document and no code rule claims them, so for those senders the document is all `email/alan/rules/agent/everything-else.md` has to judge from. Nothing on the document says this, and the document carries no Design section — which is where `domains/domain-design.md` puts what a reader would otherwise get wrong.

# Evidence

Filed by the seat dispatching the 2026-08-14 `review-instructions` reading of `domains/alan-email.md`, from that reading's hand-back.

The standing finding `rules-read-as-restatement.md` names this fork and says the next reviewer will meet it again. It did. What the 2026-08-14 reading adds is the evidence: it reports **Paid Opinions** landing in one commit with `expert-network-calls.md`, standard and machinery together, while refunds, paid surveys and advisory panels named in the same entry have no code rule of their own.

I read `domains/alan-email.md` at `b9fb44ca2` and confirmed it carries Definition, Principles and Rules and no Design section.

Not measured: I did not open the code rule corpus, so the claim that no code rule claims refunds, paid surveys or advisory panels is that reading's and not re-derived here. Nothing here says whether a Design entry is the right carrier, or what it should say.
