# Claim

Three of Alan's email rules archive without unsubscribing where the Stop The Source aid "Digests and launch announcements count too" reads as covering them: `meetup-digest` is a digest, `backerkit-vault-promo` is launch announcements, and `amazon-store-news` is a store newsletter. `marketing-senders` does unsubscribe, from `enews.united.com`, `insights.united.com` and `info15.citi.com`, so the aid has a live population beside them.

# Evidence

Read off the `review-instructions` reading of `domains/alan-email.md` finished 2026-08-21, read line by line, bottom to top. That reading read all 107 rule files under `email/alan` and counted the actions across them: archive 51, forward 6, unsubscribe 5, skip 2, notify 0.

The reading did not touch the three rules, unsubscribing being `irreversible: true`.

Not measured here: I did not open the three rules or `marketing-senders`, and I did not ask Alan whether these are lists he chose, which is what the document's second aid would cover. Whether the three rules are out of step with the aid or outside it is not settled here.
