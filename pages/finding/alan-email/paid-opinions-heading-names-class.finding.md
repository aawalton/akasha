# Claim

`## Paid Opinions` on `domains/alan-email.md` names the class of mail its entry covers, where the document's other headings name the warrant for handling it.

# Evidence

Filed by the seat dispatching the 2026-08-14 `review-instructions` reading of `domains/alan-email.md`, from that reading's hand-back.

I read the nine headings myself at `b9fb44ca2`: Needs Him, Already Settled, Money Goes To Jen, Spent Links, Ask The Account, Jen Runs The Programs, Paid Opinions, Stop The Source, After The Fact. Seven name a reason rather than a class. **Spent Links** names both — a sign-in link is the class and spent is the reason — so the reading's count of eight others is its own reading rather than a clean one.

Not measured: nothing here says whether a heading naming its class costs a reader anything, and no gate or schema was run against the heading. `domains/rule.md` states a rule is an act and a description, and says nothing about what the heading holds.
