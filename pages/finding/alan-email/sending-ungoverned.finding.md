# Claim

Mail Alan sends is inside the area `domains/alan-email.md` defines and nothing on the document governs it: every principle and every rule there is about mail arriving. Sending is really in the area — `email/alan/rules/code/jens-invitations.md` says to accept an invitation, and `ops email messages send` is deployed.

# Evidence

Raised by the seat that read `domains/alan-email.md` on 2026-08-13 under `review-instructions`, and relayed here rather than re-derived: I did not read the document's entries or check the deployed verb.

That seat reached this by reading the whole document against the rule corpus rather than by finding a case that went wrong, so nothing here measures whether any sent message has been handled badly for want of an instruction.

It judged the answer to be an absence to name rather than a definition to narrow, and left the document alone because what should be said about sending is a judgment its principal had not delegated.
