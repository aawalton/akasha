# Claim

The Definition of `domains/folders/pages-system.md` and its rules are about different things. The Definition reads "how every entity is stored and rendered, whatever kind it is", while all three rules — Exclusion By Complement, Unbucketed List Read, Rollup And Aggregate Reads — bind reading. Either storing, rendering and reading are one concern stated in three verbs, or the area is more than one domain, which is what `domains/domain-definition.md` says a second concern means.
