# Claim

640 refusals stand against `page-holds-to-its-type` on the corpus as it is, before any change to what the check reads. Two shapes dominate. Every page carrying `repo: akasha` is refused, because the property definition types that key as a select over `code` and `instructions`, the two retired repositories. The rest are undeclared keys on `persona-appearance`, `persona-points-source`, `readout-*` and `graph-*` pages, refused as no property of their type.

# Evidence

The figure 640 is a reading another seat took over 2026-08-27 and 2026-08-28, against a tree that advanced three times during the measurement. It was handed over both as the standing refusal count and as the number of pages refusing; I did not re-derive it and cannot say which of the two it counts.

Re-derived exactly on 2026-08-28: 247 pages carry `repo: akasha` — 241 under `pages/package/` and 6 under `pages/web-app/`. `pages/page-property-definition/package-repo.page-property-definition.md:5-11` declares `defined-on-slug: page-type/package`, `key: repo`, `type: select(slug)`, `values: code, instructions`, `required: true`. `akasha` is neither value, so all 247 are refused. That is the same retired vocabulary `pages/finding/akasha-repo/retired-repository-names-remain-in-check-code.finding.md` inventories in graph node ids, standing here as a value set that refuses live data rather than as residue nothing reads.

Taken on report and not re-derived: roughly 380 refusals of the shape naming a key as no property of its type, on `persona-appearance`, `persona-points-source`, `readout-*` and `graph-*` pages. What I did check on 2026-08-28: 40 pages stand under `pages/persona-appearance/`, and no property definition anywhere declares `defined-on-slug: page-type/persona-appearance`, so any key those pages carry beyond what `page` gives them has nothing declaring it.

Nothing here says the 640 should be cleared rather than the declarations or the check changed. `pages/finding/akasha-repo/checks-written-against-a-small-tree.finding.md` records the same class — a check written against a small tree meeting the full corpus — with `file-length` as the case that showed it; this is a second case.

Not measured: I did not run the check, so I hold no list of the 640, no split between the two shapes beyond what was handed over, and no count of my own for the second shape.
