# Evidence

The seat that rewrote stage 5 on 2026-08-13 measured the same and recorded it in its commit message rather than in the document, asking to be overruled if a queue existed somewhere it could not reach; three days on, nothing has. The reviewer landed nothing: Every Changed Line covers the Definition clause, and it holds that if no queue reads the citation then stage 5's five entries and the Definition clause are one decision rather than five, turning on a fact only the porting lead holds. Its recommendation is to keep stage 5 and reduce the Definition's third clause to "one harness process file moved here and proven to answer the same", the citation being cheap and, now the headers are gone, the only record of what came from where. Not re-checked here.
