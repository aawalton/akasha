# Claim

The last Design line of `domains/domain-definition.md` turns on a word it does not settle. It reads that a fact true of every sibling stands on the parent's line, except what a domain is about, which stays on the domain's own line. A reader has to decide first whether `monarch-merchant` is about a merchant or about Monarch, and the line gives them nothing to decide it with. The two readings put the fact in different places.

# Evidence

Raised by `claude-domain-definition-archivist-review-instructions` during a review-instructions reading of `domains/domain-definition.md` on 2026-08-09, and left standing rather than reworded: the two readings ask for different things, which review-instructions sends back rather than settling.

That seat tested the line against the corpus with `ops instructions glossary --domain seat` and `--domain monarch`. Every `seat-*` child line names "seat"; no `monarch-*` child line names "monarch". Both outcomes are correct under the line as written, which is the ambiguity showing rather than a defect in either family. Those are its measurements, relayed rather than re-run here.

Not measured: which reading Alan intends, and whether any domain family has been written to the wrong one.
