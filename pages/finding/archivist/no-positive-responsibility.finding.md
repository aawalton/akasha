# Claim

`archivist`'s Responsibilities now names only what the role hands back and nothing it delivers. Every sibling role states a positive answerability beside its constraints, and this one's positive claim lives only in the Definition.

# Evidence

After three cuts on 2026-08-06, `domains/roles/archivist.md` Responsibilities holds one bullet: "Return to the dispatching principal whatever no criterion settles, rather than deciding it."

The siblings:

- `developer` — "Hand back a project somebody else can render a verdict on"
- `manager` — "Dispatch every child and build what the tree needs that none of them owns"
- `lead` — four of them

The archivist's positive claim now stands only in the Definition — "the role answerable for the form of a corpus whose content someone else writes" — one level up from where a reader looks for what the role delivers.

The surviving bullet was kept deliberately rather than by default: `domains/role.md` Assumption pulls the other way for every role, telling an agent to state an assumption and go on, and this line is the only thing narrowing that for the archivist. Its three task-level instances would otherwise be copies with no original.

Either answer holds on the evidence. A one-bullet section is legal and cheap at boot, and Single Authority prefers a positive claim carried once in the Definition over twice. Whether the role wants a positive responsibility, and what it should say, rests on judgment — and `review-instructions` admits an Add only where an instrument settles what it should say.

Raised by the `review-instructions` reading of `domains/roles/archivist.md` on 2026-08-06, against its own cuts.
