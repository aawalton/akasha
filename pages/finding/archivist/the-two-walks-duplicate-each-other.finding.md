# Claim

The two archivist walk tasks carry four passages verbatim between them, including a whole sentence. Single Authority refuses two binding surfaces, and the repair it demands needs a binding site no criterion picks.

# Evidence

`domains/tasks/archivist/ingest-instructions.md` and `domains/tasks/archivist/review-instructions.md` each carry, once: "Composed at the end instead, one judgment over everything wears the shape of many and reads exactly like a walk."

Three further shared passages: the whoami warning in the **List** bullet, the bottom-up reason in **Take**, and the opening clause of **Run**.

`domains/agent-harness.md` Single Authority: "A copy has an original, so a reader meeting both knows which drifted; two binding surfaces have none. Their disagreement is a contradiction nothing adjudicates, not a stale copy."

Not landed, and the reason is the shape of the repair rather than doubt about the defect. Cutting the sentence from either file would rest that seat's instruction on a file it does not read. Binding it once needs a site, and the candidate — a Rules section on `domains/roles/archivist.md`, which governs both through `instructions-path: domains/tasks/archivist/*.md` — means authoring a rule, which is a lead's task.

Two ways out: lift the shared walk onto one surface both archivist tasks inherit — the report, the bottom-up order, the per-slice append, the search-before-judging — or accept the duplication as the price of each task standing alone and record that, so the next reviewer stops re-finding it.

Raised by the `review-instructions` reading of `domains/tasks/archivist/ingest-instructions.md` on 2026-08-06.
