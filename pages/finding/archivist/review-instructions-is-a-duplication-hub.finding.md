# Claim

`review-instructions.md` is the hub of a verbatim duplication no document owns. Six claims stand word for word on it and on `ingest-instructions.md`: the report path and its `<name>` clause, taking the lowest line, running the machinery rather than reading it, searching the corpus before judging prose, writing the verdict before landing, and mending in the same commit. Four more are shared with `guided-close-read.md`. Neither copy is cuttable: no seat's governing set holds the other's document.

# Evidence

Raised independently by two review-instructions seats in one pass. The seat on `domains/tasks/archivist/ingest-instructions.md` found the six and reported running `grep -rn "one append per line" --include=*.md domains/`. The seat on `domains/tasks/alan-harness/guided-close-read.md` separately found four — stage 1's Read bullet and stage 3's name word for word, and the List enumeration, Run bullet and Judge bullet differing by a clause or two words. Neither knew of the other's finding.

That two unrelated readings landed on the same shape is what makes this structural rather than local: three live tasks carry the same procedure and none is the original.

The remedy both named and neither took: hoist the shared steps to `domains/roles/archivist.md`. That means writing a Principles or Rules section onto a domain currently carrying only a Definition and a Tasks list, which Every Changed Line reserves to Alan.

I did not diff any pair myself. Not measured: whether any of the copies has already drifted from its twin, which is the cost Single Authority names and the one thing that would settle urgency.
