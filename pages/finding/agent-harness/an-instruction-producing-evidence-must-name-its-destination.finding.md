# Claim

An instruction that produces evidence must say where the evidence goes, or the prose is where it goes.

# Evidence

`tasks/persona-craft/create-persona-register.md` tells a seat to magnify an anchor image and settle whether a feature emits light. It asks for a check, not for a sentence. Five of thirty-one seats wrote the finding into the persona's appearance paragraph anyway, as a negation — *colour and no light in it*, *They take the lamp and give nothing back*, and two that run the same three items in the same order off the same em-dash and are near-verbatim.

The seats could not see each other and none was told to write it. The mechanism a seat reported: it has just done real optical work, it is holding a verified finding, the prose is what it is writing at that moment, and the hand-back comes afterwards. So the evidence lands in the nearest container.

This is the same failure as an earlier instruction in the same file that said to state a persona's age and did not say how: six of sixteen files then landed on the identical phrasing, *The face is <age>*. There the instruction said what to include and not in what form; here it says what to find and not where to put it. Both produced a house shape across files that shared nothing else.

The task has been repaired by naming the destination, and the repair is placed above the instruction that produces the finding rather than below it, because a seat meets bullets in order and has decided what its paragraph is made of by the time it reaches the test.

Filed here rather than on `persona` because the mechanism is not about personas: any instruction in the estate that has a seat produce a fact without saying where the fact lands will have it land in whatever the seat is writing.
