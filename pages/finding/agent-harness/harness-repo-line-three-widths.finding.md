# Claim

The claim that the harness stands in the instructions repository is carried by three documents at three widths: `global.md`, `agent-harness.md`, and `model-gateway.md` two levels beneath it.

`global.md` states it of deployed code generally, `agent-harness.md` of the harness, and `model-gateway.md` of one part within it.

# Evidence

Read off the `review-instructions` reading of `domains/agent-harness.md` finished 2026-08-21, read line by line, bottom to top. The reading kept all three lines, on the ground that global's turns on deployment and nothing states the harness is not deployed.

Not measured here: I did not open the three documents to compare their wording, and I did not search the corpus for a fourth carrying the same claim. Whether the three are one claim bound three times or three claims that happen to read alike is what a reading of all three would settle, and no single-document reading can reach it.
