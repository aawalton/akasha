# Claim

Pinned counts in instruction prose rot silently between readings. Five stood in the 64 lines of `domains/tasks/agent-harness/port-supervisor-file.md` and all five had drifted by 2026-08-13: forty headers measured 37, "four files here do that" measured 161, eight outstanding files measured 22, 3,368 directories measured 3,374 and rising on every run of the checks, five headers measured 6. Each was measurable in under a minute and nothing in the repository measures any of them.

# Evidence

Raised by the seat that read `domains/tasks/agent-harness/port-supervisor-file.md` on 2026-08-13 under `review-instructions`, and relayed here rather than re-derived: every remeasurement is that seat's, and I repeated none of them.

It dropped four of the five figures and stated the magnitude instead, on the ground that the magnitude carried everything the digits did, and repaired the fifth upward — "four files" to "over a hundred" — because understating that hazard is the direction that lands a citation in the wrong file.

It states the question it could not settle: whether the corpus wants an instrument that finds pinned figures in instruction prose.

One document is the whole of the population measured. Nothing here counts how many pinned figures stand elsewhere in the corpus, or how far any of them has drifted.
