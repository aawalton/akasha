# Claim

Cut The Obvious does not say what "without it" removes the instruction from — a bare model, or a seat already holding everything its boot put in front of it. The two readings decide slices differently, and the sentence is restated verbatim on three task surfaces that each drive a cut.

# Evidence

`domains/agent-harness.md` states: "Keep an instruction only where Opus 5 would consistently make avoidable mistakes without it."

Against a bare model, a slice that reads as defensible preference fails the test and is cut. Against a seat holding its boot, the same slice can be a live reversal of what that seat is carrying, and passes. Nothing on the surface picks between them.

The sentence is restated verbatim, each time driving a cut or a recommendation:

- `domains/tasks/archivist/review-instructions.md:21` — "**Cut** unless keeping, repairing, trimming or rewriting clearly passes"
- `domains/tasks/archivist/ingest-instructions.md:23` — "**Name** as residue only what passes … which is the whole of the bar"
- `domains/tasks/alan-harness/guided-close-read.md:25` — "**Recommend** cutting unless keeping, repairing or rewriting clearly passes"

Quarantine records the same objection at `dirty/questions/instructions-review.md:15`, which binds nobody.

Raised by the `review-instructions` reading of `domains/agent-harness.md` on 2026-08-05. That seat applied the bare-model reading, which is what the act says on its face, and stated the assumption rather than resolving it — so the reading's own verdicts rest on the unresolved half.
