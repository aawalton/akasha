# Claim

The middle sentence of Replace Before Removing has two readings, and the nearer one dissolves its warrant: "A replacement lands on a commit and a removal needs one" leaves "one" landing on "a commit", under which both sides need only a commit, the two acts are symmetric, and nothing is left to say why the replacement goes first. The sense the rule depends on is that the removal needs a deploy.

# Evidence

Raised by the seat that read `domains/agent-harness.md` on 2026-08-12 under `review-instructions`, which landed a rewrite of the sentence and took it back (`ce802d5`) on finding the section reserved to Alan by `domains/domain.md`. Its proposed replacement, which nobody has ruled on, was: "A replacement is live on its commit while a removal waits for that deploy, so the fix stands first."

I did not verify the character count that seat reports for the proposal (198 against a ceiling of 200), and nothing measures whether any reader has actually taken the sentence the wrong way.
