# Claim

A delegate cannot land a write whose `words-read` reading only the delegator holds.

# Evidence

Twenty-seven composed files were handed to a sonnet delegate to put through `ops instructions write`, the reading they were owed being boilerplate the delegator had authored. Twenty-five landed. The two naming `Memory` were refused by `words-read`, which reads per agent: the delegator had already read `domains/memory.md`, the delegate had not, and the refusal names a reading the delegate had no reason to take on a word it had not chosen.

The delegator then landed both itself in one call, `words-read` passing on its own record — commit `23209d94`.

Delegating a gated write is the pattern the register work leaned on six times, precisely because the owed reading is what the delegation exists to keep out of the delegator context. Where the write names a coined word, that reading is the one thing the delegate cannot supply cheaply, and the refusal arrives after the batch has already been split and run.
