# Claim

Three unrelated live surfaces each bind "state the expected outcome before producing the
artifact" for their own readers, in their own words, and nothing binds it generally.

# Evidence

Measured 2026-08-04, firsthand, sweeping every live surface for standing claims while
running `define-principle-or-rule`. The three:

`domains/schema.md` **Vision** — "An author holds the specification before writing against
it. The alternative is imitating a neighbouring document and being corrected afterwards,
and the correction arrives too late to have shaped anything."

`tasks/scenewright/author-persona-scene.md` — "**State** what this scene tests before
writing a line of it... Prose produced against no hypothesis taught nothing."

`tasks/projects/build-parent-deploy.md` stage 4 — "**State** what would demonstrate the
assembled tree works and what a failure would look like, **before you run anything**, then
run each one and record its verdict." Its two siblings, `build-child-deploy.md` and
`build-singleton-deploy.md`, carry the same bullet without the ordering clause, so the
ordering holds for a parent row and not for a child or a singleton.

Each is written for its own reader in its own vocabulary — a schema author, a scene, a
row's verification — and none cites another. The claim underneath them is the same one.
`domains/global.md`, `domains/agent-harness.md` and `domains/role.md` carry nothing on
this axis; the sweep covered every `# Principles` and `# Rules` section in the live tree,
19 files in all.

Not measured: whether the three were authored independently or one was copied from
another — the histories were not read. Whether a general claim would be worth its boot
cost against `agent-harness.md` **Dilution** was not decided, and this finding takes no
position on whether one should be written or on which surface would carry it.
