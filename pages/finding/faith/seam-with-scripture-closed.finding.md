# Claim

Alan has closed the question of where prayer ends and scripture study begins — he does not want a boundary drawn between them, so a case falling between belongs to whichever domain is in the room and neither lead should spend anything settling it; the ruling is recorded only in a quarantined file queued for removal, and no live document carries it.

# Evidence

`dirty/skills/prayer/rulings.md:57-62` carried it, under the heading "## The seam with scripture needs no rule, by Alan's ruling": "A prayer that arises from a passage mid-scripture-session belongs to whichever of the two Faith domains is in the room; he does not want a nuanced boundary drawn between them. So this is closed rather than open, and neither lead should spend anything settling it. Where the two overlap, act — do not adjudicate." That file is being emptied, which is why this is filed here.

The machinery holds. `domains/prayer.md` and `domains/scripture-study.md` are both live, both ten lines, both `domain-parents: faith`, owned by selah and talia. Nothing live carries the ruling. `rg -uuu -in "adjudicate|overlap|seam|boundary" domains/` returns fifteen lines and not one is about a domain boundary Alan has closed: seven are check names in a list document, one is Single Authority, one is where an audit draws its boundary, three are persona-craft tasks about overlap, one is a felled seam in a persona's Conduct.
