# Claim

A delegate catches its coordinator's false claims when two things hold together: work concrete enough to bring it into contact with the claim, and a standing licence to contradict the person who briefed it. Neither alone produces anything. The corrections recorded below were made by one delegate against the seat that briefed it over one night, and more arrived against this page itself as it was written. Not one came from doubting anybody.

# Evidence

Observed 2026-08-27 into 2026-08-28 by seat astra, over one delegate dispatched to measure the gating suite and then kept on further work.

**The licences.** Two lines, neither asking for care, each permitting contradiction of its writer.

The first was in the original brief, written before any measuring began and never repeated: "If my framing turns out to be wrong when you open the files, say so plainly."

The second was added mid-task, after the coordinator had asserted a mechanism from two symptoms and a plausible link and been wrong: "Check it before I put it in front of Alan. I have asserted a mechanism from two symptoms and a plausible link tonight already and been wrong, and this is exactly that shape." That line pointed at the coordinator's own claims rather than at the delegate's work, and the coordinator then forgot having written it.

**The contact.** In every case the delegate was doing something else at the time — filling a field, confirming tree state, producing a commit list, verifying a landed diff, checking a quoted line against the file it named. The claim failed while being handled. A licence given to a delegate with nothing concrete to do reaches no claim, because nothing brings the two together; concrete work without the licence reaches the claim and has no permission to say so. Both were necessary and neither is sufficient.

**The corrections.** Three predate the second licence: a count of unreached test files the coordinator had invented by subtracting two numbers never in one population; a mechanism relayed wrong, twice; a guard whose condition negated its purpose, at `infra/cluster-checks/src/checks/check-test-step-paths.ts:53`. Three postdate it: a close-out summary reporting that no code changed, which erased two repairs landed that night at `50fb7a84` and `c8ad794a`; a convergence between two agents that the coordinator had manufactured by relaying one agent's conclusion to the other and then forgetting being its source; a possessive pronoun that reassigned who a standing instruction had caught, on a page about provenance.

Not measured: which licence produced which catch. The delegate grouped them by time rather than tracing each one to its line, and both lines were in force for the last three.

Further corrections were made by the same delegate against earlier versions of this page. They are of the same kind as those listed and are deliberately not totalled. A running count over a process still going goes false while it is being written, and this one did, inside a single commit: the body counted an exchange as one where it had counted the first pass as three. What the evidence supports is the structure — three before the second licence, three after, and more against the record of them — which cannot go stale.

**On shape.** `the-rule-caught-the-people-writing-it`, under `domain/checks-system`, records a family in which every specimen was one number refusing to agree with another, and establishes that property for three catches it tested. These are not all of that kind, and not all outside it. The invented count is exactly that shape: 72 + 509 = 581 against a separate 107, with 402 refusing to agree with either. Of the six listed above, the delegate counts two that turned on figures and four with no figure in them at all. An earlier version of this page widened the census's tested three to all six and asserted that none was numeric, which was false — the same error the census itself is about, made on the page citing it.

**A clean return.** One run of the second licence found nothing. Asked to verify a landed diff, the delegate ran `git show`, found three insertions and three deletions in the lines named and nothing else moved, and reported the claim holding literally. A check that only ever finds something is not a check.

No count appears in this page's title. The count was exceeded on the night it was written, and a finding is keyed by domain and file stem (`pages/page-type/finding.page-type.md:22`), so a number in the stem is the part of the record that goes out of date first.

Worth noting against `pages/domain/seat-delegating.domain.md`, which carries a single Condition about sharing files and says nothing about what a brief contains.
