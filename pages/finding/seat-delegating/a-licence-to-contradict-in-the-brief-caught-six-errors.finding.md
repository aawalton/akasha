# Claim

Two lines written into instructions, rather than two moments of watchfulness, produced every correction one delegate made against its own coordinator over one night — six of them. Neither line asked the delegate to be careful. Each licensed it to contradict the person who wrote it.

# Evidence

Observed 2026-08-27 into 2026-08-28 by seat astra, over one delegate dispatched to measure the gating suite and then kept on further work.

The first line was in the original brief: "If my framing turns out to be wrong when you open the files, say so plainly." It was written before any measuring began, and it was never repeated.

The second was added mid-task, after the coordinator had asserted a mechanism from two symptoms and a plausible link and been wrong: "Check it before I put it in front of Alan. I have asserted a mechanism from two symptoms and a plausible link tonight already and been wrong, and this is exactly that shape." That line pointed at the coordinator's own claims rather than at the delegate's work, and the coordinator then forgot having written it.

Three corrections predate the second line. A count of unreached test files the coordinator had invented by subtracting two numbers never in one population. A mechanism relayed wrong, twice. A guard whose condition negated its purpose, at `infra/cluster-checks/src/checks/check-test-step-paths.ts:53`.

Three corrections postdate it. A close-out summary reporting that no code changed, which erased two repairs landed that night at `50fb7a84` and `c8ad794a`. A convergence between two agents that the coordinator had manufactured by relaying one agent's conclusion to the other and then forgetting being its source. A possessive pronoun that reassigned who a standing instruction had caught, on a page about provenance.

Not measured: which line produced which catch. The delegate grouped them by time rather than tracing each one to its instruction, and both lines were in force for the last three. What is established is that no catch in either group came from suspicion. In every case the delegate was doing something else — filling a field, confirming tree state, verifying a landed diff — and the claim failed while being handled.

The second line's fourth run returned clean. Asked to verify a landed diff, the delegate ran `git show`, found three insertions and three deletions in the lines named and nothing else moved, and reported the claim holding literally. A check that only ever finds something is not a check, so the three catches are credible partly because this one was not.

The numeric siblings of these are recorded on `the-rule-caught-the-people-writing-it`, under `domain/checks-system`, where every specimen was one number disagreeing with another. None of the six here was. Two of them had no figure in them at all.

Worth noting against `pages/domain/seat-delegating.domain.md`, which carries a single Condition about sharing files and says nothing about what a brief contains.
