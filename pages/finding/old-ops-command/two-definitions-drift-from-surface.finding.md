# Claim

Two of eight command definitions sampled across namespaces drift from their own surface: `ops-agent-tail`'s definition says "deliveries" where its summary says "messages", and `ops-agent-inbox`'s summary says "your own mailbox" while its `--name` and `--agent-id` flags let a caller name another agent.

# Evidence

Turned up by the 2026-08-15 `review-instructions` reading of `domains/tasks/ops/review-command.md` while checking that document's own "most hold" claim about the 757 definitions, and filed by the seat dispatching it. That reading said plainly that neither drift was its document's business.

The limit of the sample is one that reading stated against itself: it compared each definition to the verb's SUMMARY and not to its body, which is the exact substitution the document's stage 6.1 says will not show you the failure. So eight of eight consistent supports "most hold" without confirming it, and these two drifts are the ones a summary comparison can catch.

Not measured: I drew no sample and opened neither command. Eight of 757 is what was looked at, and nothing here says the rate holds beyond it.
