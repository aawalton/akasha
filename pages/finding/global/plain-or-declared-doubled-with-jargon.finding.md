# Claim

`Plain Or Declared` on `domains/global.md` and the Intent of `domains/jargon.md` carry the same claim in two shapes.

# Evidence

Reported by the review of `domains/global.md` on 2026-08-15: `jargon.md`'s Intent says a reader meeting a word either takes its plain sense and is right or finds it declared as a domain, and that a word declared as a domain carries that one sense everywhere. The reviewer reports this is not resolvable inside a single-document review — removing the principle would leave no seat reading global bound to the act, global's glossary not naming `jargon` and `domain-system` being a foundational layer most seats never read, while removing jargon.md's Intent is a changed line on another domain. Which of the two should hold the claim was not judged.
