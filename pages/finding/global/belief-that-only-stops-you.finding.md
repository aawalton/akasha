# Claim

A belief whose only effect is to stop an act is never tested, because the act is the only test it has. It therefore holds its confidence for exactly as long as it is obeyed, and the obedience reads as evidence for it. The medicine domain ran on one for months — that the platform permits no on-demand health read — and its whole instrument shape was built around the stop rather than around a measurement.

# Evidence

The instance. A quarantined medicine rulings document, emptied on 2026-08-07 and since removed with the tree that held it — the domain now stands at `pages/domain/medicine.domain.md` — carried the shape in the lead's own words: "the domain's entire instrument shape rests on a belief about what the platform permits, and a belief whose only effect is to stop you is never tested by acting." The belief was that a HealthKit read off the macbook is infeasible, settled by one research pass and then held for every route rather than for the one it was tested on.

That the stop was still holding after the ground moved is checkable, and it had moved. Re-measured 2026-08-27 in `/var/home/walton/repos/akasha`: `native-shell/alanwalton/scripts/ios-seam/06-health-samples-intent/` and `native-shell/alanwalton/scripts/ios-seam/07-health-samples-drain/` hold the seam, the latter carrying `01-anchored-read.sh`, `02-cursorless-backstop.sh`, `03-route-request.sh` and `04-remembered-state.sh`, and `infra/cluster-checks/src/lib/app-intent-brand-word-violations.unit.test.ts` names `SyncActiveEnergyIntent` at lines 15, 23, 116, 169, 212 and 246. A headless on-device health read exists and carries a metric for another domain. Nobody in medicine had tried it, the belief's whole effect being to stop them trying.

Nothing in the corpus names this shape. Re-run 2026-08-27: `rg -uuu -il 'never tested by acting|only effect is to stop|a belief that stops|unfalsifiab|belief whose only' pages/` returns four files — this finding, the notification feed, and two other findings — and the same search over `pages/domain/`, `pages/page-type/`, `pages/role/` and `pages/task/` returns nothing, so no domain, page type, role or task names it. `pages/finding/role/discredited-count-becomes-bare-universal.finding.md` is a quantifier widened to escape a challenge. `pages/finding/role/confirming-test-cannot-discriminate.finding.md` is a test that runs and returns a result both hypotheses predict. Both are about a WEAK test; this is about no test, and the two want different repairs — one wants a discriminating test designed, the other wants somebody to notice none is scheduled.

Against `pages/domain/global.domain.md`. **Grounding** says an agent updates what it believes and what it intends together, so the goal bends the belief. This is the reverse coupling on the same joint: where the belief's only issue is an intention NOT to act, no act follows, no observation follows, and nothing bends the belief back.
