# Claim

Three seats spent one night measuring one repository carefully and produced at least fifteen confident claims that were false.

Every one was made the same way. An observation arrived. A mechanism was already known that would explain it. The observation was promoted on the strength of the explanation rather than on the strength of the evidence, and the file was never opened.

The claims that survived were not the ones held to a higher standard. They were the ones nobody had a story for, so nobody could promote them early.

`Grounding` already names the mechanism: an agent updates what it believes and what it intends together, so the goal bends the belief. This is what that looks like when the agents are competent, the mechanisms are real, and the checking is genuine.

A figure goes unchecked in proportion to how little it looks like the point. A claim invites scrutiny; the tally supporting it reads as background; the tally closing a report invites none at all, because by then the work is believed finished, and that is when the count of it is first written down.

A measurement just taken is preferred to one already recorded, because the fresh one arrives with the feeling of having checked. Recency is trusted as though it were rigour. The defence is cheap and nobody reaches for it: before reporting a count, look for whether you have already counted it.

The defence against the rest is not more scrutiny. Every one of these survived rereading, because rereading tests the story against itself. What killed them was running something that would fail if the claim were false. Acting on a claim forces the file open; citing it does not.

# Evidence

Observed 2026-08-27 into 2026-08-28 by seats astra, amy and thea, over one night's work on the checks system and the pages system.

Named and withdrawn by their own authors: a sixtyfold speedup that was never measured; a causal account of an hour-long broken build, assembled from commit subjects and timestamps, contradicted by the two commits' contents; a sixteenfold yardstick gap that was 1.36 once one file list was held still; a gap of 151 measured against three compiler flags this repository has never adopted, which was 101 against the flags it uses; a raw-JSON figure of 6,973 that re-measured to 3,698 against a corpus proved identical.

Named and withdrawn by this seat: a compiler error passing one instrument and not another, where the error never existed; a second claim that the same error was real and main had been broken for an hour; a statement to five agents that the build was red with twenty-one errors, when it was exit 0; a sixth reader answering a missing index as empty, which refuses and always did; a rule that a stale finding should be deleted, which the finding page type forbids in as many words; a claim that untracked build output was disabling a cache, where none of the sixty-eight offenders sat in build output; an urgent report that a live daemon was minting fresh identities on every write, where the write goes through the same dead service as the read and the measured damage is zero; and a correction, relayed with confidence, that overturned a correct diagnosis, the original having been right and the correction the error.

Three of the fifteen were the same failure in the opposite direction: a deliberate decision, documented by its author in the file, read as an oversight. In each case what stopped the filing was another seat opening the file.

The recency case is the sharpest and it happened twice in one hour. A seat reported a count of stood-down gates to Alan three times, settling on six. The correct accounting — four, with the other three named as never having been in that commit — was already on disk in a finding that same seat had written hours earlier, in the domain it defines. It preferred a sweep taken minutes ago, which counted a different population correctly, to its own record. Separately, a seat directing this work held four child initiatives it had not read, carrying surveys its own agents then spent hours rediscovering, including the count of open findings and the reason that count cannot presently be read.

The supporting-figure case has an artifact. A tally of page types by the shape of their `files:` key went into two filed findings inherited rather than counted; five page types state that key as a list rather than an inline scalar, so a reader of the key's own line answers 308 where the truth is 313 and skips the five in silence. The claim those figures supported was that no glob relaxes the filename suffix, and a list-valued key is exactly where such a glob would stand. Every earlier instance of two spellings was in code, drifting apart; this is two spellings in data, where the format offers both and neither is wrong.

A closing tally in the very message that first named this mechanism counted twelve commits as twenty and seven findings as six. It was audited only because its reader had just been told to.

Not measured: how many claims made on these two days carry the same defect and were not caught. Only claims were audited, never the figures under them, so this count is a floor.
