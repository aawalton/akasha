# Claim

"Opus 5" names the reader in one claim and the yardstick in another, one edge apart on the same ancestor chain, and nothing says a reader should carry the two apart.

# Evidence

`domains/global.md`, Parsimony: "Opus 5 most often goes wrong here." The model is the agent liable to err, and the sentence is a diagnosis of the reader it is handed to.

`domains/agent-harness.md`, Cut The Obvious: "Keep an instruction only where Opus 5 would consistently make avoidable mistakes without it." The model is the counterfactual a slice is measured against.

`agent-harness` declares `domain-parents: global`, so the two stand one edge apart and every agent-harness reader holds both.

Raised by the `review-instructions` reading of `domains/agent-harness.md` on 2026-08-05, which could reach only one of the two surfaces.
