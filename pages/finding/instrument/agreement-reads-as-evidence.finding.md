# Claim

Negative Control's warrant is written for an instrument that returns nothing, so a reader building a check that returns AGREEMENT does not recognise the principle as covering them. A check sharing a source with the thing it checks reports agreement rather than silence, and that agreement reads as the strongest evidence available rather than as the absence of evidence it is.

# Evidence

The principle reads: "A blind instrument and a clean one both return nothing. Show it the case it must catch while you build it — afterwards no result of its own says it was never looking." Both aid sentences turn on `nothing`, and the rules beside it (Population, Horizon) are likewise about a reading that came back empty or short.

Under #18718 a dry run reported every persona's stored running total beside what her recipe recomputes. Five of the six Health personas recomputed to exactly what they already stored, which was read — reasonably — as the strongest evidence in the run that those totals were sound.

They were not. `withDerivedActiveCalories` stamps Alan's `activeCalories` onto every Health-scoped row rather than the cardio owner's; `cardioPoints` is the formula `activeCalories`, and the `points` formula sums it. So the same injection wrote the stored figure and computed the check. Ione's stored total carried 18,291.25 of Alan's own exercise over 65 rows; Elaine's 17,088.34 was 17,087.34 of it, 99.99% of her total.

Nobody skipped a control out of haste. The seat building the dry run had a positive result in hand, so the principle — as written — did not present itself as applying. It was found later by a different seat toggling the injection off and measuring both ways, which is Negative Control performed exactly, on a case its own text does not describe.

The gap is in reach rather than in truth: an instrument that cannot see is one that agrees with whatever it cannot see past, and returning nothing is only the most visible way that happens.
