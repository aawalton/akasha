# Claim

Nothing in the corpus binds the claim that a bias engineered out of one level of a mechanism reappears at the level above it, where nothing is watching for it.

# Evidence

Found in the same source: "A failure chosen for the principle it would settle is a failure chosen for its answer — the same bias `tools/failures.ts` is blind by construction to avoid, arriving one level up where nothing is watching for it." The instrument named is gone from the tree.

The first half of that sentence I read as already bound by Grounding on `domains/global.md` — "An agent updates what it believes and what it intends in one pass, so the goal bends the belief. Research done after the plan is formed reads identically and confirms where it should have tested." The second half is what this finding is about, and I found nothing binding it.

FILED UNCERTAIN. I am not confident this is a claim rather than an observation about one mechanism that no longer exists. The generalisation — that a guard against selection bias relocates the bias upward rather than removing it — was asserted once, by a document whose apparatus is gone, and I have no second instance of it. Recorded here so the observation survives the ingest rather than because it has earned anything.
