# Claim

An instrument's misleading zero has four distinct causes, and `instrument.md` names only two of them. Population empty, horizon short, classifier blind, suppressor unseen — the third and fourth have no carrier, and the third is the one where a population positive control passes and the reading is still wrong.

# Evidence

Separated during a `decide-principle-or-rule` run on 2026-08-07. Athena, declining the rule that carving was drafted for, wrote that the four modes "are a better carving than anything my two documents currently make" and that it "belongs somewhere whatever happens to the principle." This is that somewhere — filed rather than placed, because where it belongs is a judgment nobody has made.

**Population empty** — the instrument looked at nothing. Carried by § Population. Instance: a planted control tree for `check-pglite-auth-stub-parity` whose files were untracked, so `git ls-files` returned nothing and the check reported a clean green over an empty tree.

**Horizon short** — it looked at a window and the reader read it as history. Carried by § Horizon, and converted to a reader's duty once already at `domains/tasks/code-harness/review-check.md:32`.

**Classifier blind** — it looked at everything and filed the numerator in a bucket named clean. **No carrier**, and the dangerous one: a population positive control passes and the reading is still wrong, because the denominator is full and the finding is inside the clean bucket. Instance: `ops seat halt-census` classes a retired seat among its legal endings, so a claim about retiring returns a clean zero over a full denominator with its whole numerator in the clean bucket.

**Suppressor unseen** — population full, zero honest, something upstream turning the act away before it could occur. **No carrier**, and deliberately outside the rule drafted here: reaching it would have made the claim one about causal inference rather than about instruments.

The consequence: a reviewer who has satisfied § Population and § Horizon has cleared two of four with no prompt toward the others, and the third is invisible to exactly the control that clears the first.
