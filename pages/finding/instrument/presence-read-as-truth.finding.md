# Claim

An instrument that measures whether a property is PRESENT is read as measuring whether it is RIGHT, and the reading is most confident exactly where the number is 100%. A fabricated claim carries the most perfect citation of all, because the fabricator picks the citation to fit — so a check counting filled provenance fields scores laundering higher than honest work. `pages/domain/instrument.domain.md` carries nothing on it.
