# Claim

An instrument whose verdict authorises a destructive act — reaping a seat, deleting a row — should admit its reading only in the direction that spares. High activity proves work is happening and may excuse; low activity proves nothing, an idle party and a wedged one being equally quiet, so it may never accuse. A symmetric threshold has to be calibrated, and a mis-calibration kills live work. Nothing under the domain pages binds this: Irreversibility, at `pages/page-type/role.page-type.md`, binds the actor, not the wiring.
