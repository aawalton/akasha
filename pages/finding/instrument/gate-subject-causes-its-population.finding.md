# Claim

A gate whose own subject is a cause of the population it counts can never close: a shim teaches the vocabulary that keeps the shim alive, because `--help` advertises the retired names precisely because the shim still accepts them. Ordering follows — the teaching fix lands before any fresh window, and a window predating it measures a fleet still being taught. `domains/instrument.md` carries Negative Control, Population and Horizon, and none of them is this.

# Evidence

A standing ruling of 2026-07-28, held in `dirty/skills/agent-harness/rulings/instruments.md` and reached by an ingest seat emptying that source. Its recorded demonstration: a removal was gated on a measured zero of retired-vocabulary use, and the gate's own author submitted the retired word twice — learned from the instrument he is instructed to trust — four hours into a window built to find out whether anyone still knew it.

The subject is still open here, so this is not a ruling about a closed question. `ops --help` describes `audit retired-status-rows` as "the two halves of the retired-status-vocabulary question no candidate change can answer", and names `check-retired-status-source-vocabulary` as the other half; that check stands on `domains/lists/unresolved-checks.md`. The particular shim and the particular removal window the ruling measured are a day in July and are not carried here.

Nothing in the live estate holds the claim. `domains/instrument.md` carries Negative Control ("Make an instrument fail before you trust it"), the Population rule, and Horizon ("Say how far back a store reaches, beside any reading taken from it"). Horizon is the nearest and is a different claim: it bounds how far back a store reaches, where this one is about the instrument being a cause of what it reads. `rg -n -i "observer|contaminat|teaches|window" domains/` returns sixteen lines, none of them this. In this corpus I searched `never close|cannot close|cause of the population|teaches the vocabulary|measures a fleet`, which returned two unrelated findings, and read `findings/code-harness/` and `findings/gate/` by name.

Kin rather than a member: the same source holds an observer-effect ruling at three layers whose remedy is a structural rather than textual judge. That remedy and this one differ, and the source's own warning against merging distinct mechanisms that present alike is why they are filed apart.
