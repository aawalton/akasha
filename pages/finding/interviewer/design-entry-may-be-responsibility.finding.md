# Claim

A Design entry on `domains/roles/interviewer.md` may belong under Responsibilities instead. Line 16, "This role writes domains and never the record", passes the test on `domains/role-responsibilities.md` — what the role owes that its name, its tasks and its dispatch do not already supply — and neither interviewer task mentions the notes. `domains/roles/recorder.md:19` carries this exact shape of claim under Responsibilities. It also reads cleanly as an Absence under `domain-design.md`.

# Evidence

Raised by a review-instructions seat on `domains/roles/interviewer.md`, which found both placements defensible and would not settle it unattended. Moving it would be a changed line in two guarded sections at once.

The reviewer checked all thirteen role documents and reported that only `lead.md` and `recorder.md` carry a Responsibilities section, the recorder carrying one because it has no tasks. That matches a count I made independently earlier in this pass: 13 role files, 2 with `# Responsibilities`, those two.

It was explicit that it is NOT proposing a Responsibilities section be invented for this role.

Not measured: whether the entry has ever been read as optional because it sits under Design.
