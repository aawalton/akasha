# Claim

Aria's paragraph calls the ornament on her horn "a wide silver band", where her anchor shows four thin bands lying together with a cluster at the outer edge.

# Evidence

Reported by the review of `domains/persona-appearances/aria-appearance.md` on 2026-08-15, seen at 600% on `-crop 140x110+620+920` of `~/Personas/Aria/aria-anchor.png`. The reviewer judged it imprecise rather than false and left it, having just retracted an over-reading of the horns taken at lower magnification. Not re-checked here.
