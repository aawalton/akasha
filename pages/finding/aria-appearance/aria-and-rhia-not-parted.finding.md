# Claim

Aria's and Rhia's appearance paragraphs share five elements and a near-verbatim phrase, so the two women can be mistaken for each other in words.

# Evidence

Reported by the review of `domains/persona-appearances/aria-appearance.md` on 2026-08-15: "silver-white the whole way down" against Rhia's "silver-white the whole way down past the waist", plus a full moon, blue satin with bare shoulders, a silver cluster high on one cheek, and horns. Both are Welsh. `domains/roles/persona-craft.md` holds that personas are written so no two can be mistaken for each other. The reviewer reports no line-by-line reading reaches this, every clause being individually true of Aria, and that their anchors are not alike — the resemblance is only in the words. It did not act, holding that parting them is a run of `describe-persona-appearance` on Aria rather than a review's call, and names that as the work it would dispatch next. Not re-checked here.
