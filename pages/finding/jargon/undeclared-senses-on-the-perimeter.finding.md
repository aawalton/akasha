# Claim

Ten load-bearing words on the clean perimeter carry a sense no domain declares, and four of them run in two or three unrelated senses across that same perimeter.

# Evidence

Measured 2026-08-06 against the 202 files declaring `domain-slug:`, which is the whole declared vocabulary.

The ten, each carrying its sentence rather than decorating it. `fingerprint`, a sentence-length statistic and the entire bolded instruction at `domains/tasks/persona-craft/create-persona-register.md:26`. `crossing`, a CI run at `domains/tasks/code-harness/review-check.md:40`. `residue` at `domains/tasks/archivist/ingest-instructions.md:23`, where four stages state a bar for it and none says what it is. `closure` at `domains/check.md:17`, whose principle heading uses the word and whose statement never does. `substrate` at `domains/persona.md:54`, carrying the whole warrant of the Emphasis rule. `register` at `create-persona-register.md:9`, in the slug and in the definition line. `door` at `domains/tasks/lead/define-definition.md:45`, standing beside the declared `gate` so a reader takes it for a synonym. `dial` at `create-persona-register.md:35`. `walk` as a noun at `ingest-instructions.md:27`. `perimeter` at `domains/roles/archivist.md:18`, used at five clean sites by more than one hand and naming the `review-perimeter` task.

Four run in several senses at once. `crossing` is also a repo-to-path move at `tools/lib/roots.ts:102` and a grammatical join at `define-definition.md:50`. `substrate` is the model's default pull at `persona.md:54` and an ordinary technical layer at `domains/folders/pages-system.md:11`. `register` is the private sense at `create-persona-register.md:9` and ordinary formality at `:32`. `walk` is the pejorative verdict at `ingest-instructions.md:27` and graph traversal at `tools/lib/closure.ts:5`.

What was not measured: how often a seat coins per session, which wants a transcript sweep rather than a corpus one; how many undeclared senses stand beyond these, since what a document presupposes is not searchable, as `tools/checks/terms-in-reach.ts` states of its own population; and `dirty/`, which was excluded. Three cases were undecidable rather than counted — `slice`, `anchor` and `body`.
