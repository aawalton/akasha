# Claim

A Design line on `domains/jargon.md` carries two readings that ask for different things. Line 15 reads "How widely a word is written says nothing about whether it is jargon." Read as entrenchment inside this corpus — the sense it was written against — it is true and useful. Read as how current the word is in the language, it is false against the Definition above it, which makes a word jargon only where "a plainer word could replace" it. The word `written` carries the ambiguity.

# Evidence

Raised by a review-instructions seat on `domains/jargon.md`, which could not land the repair under Every Changed Line. It reported reading `git show 42af134a`, the commit that landed the line, which records Alan's own words for what it was written against: "I was reading 52 live sites of `pass` as evidence that retiring it had been the error." That fixes the intended sense as entrenchment.

I verified line 15 reads as quoted and that the Definition above it is "a word no domain defines that a plainer word could replace."

The reviewer judged the claim worth keeping and the sentence in need of naming entrenchment explicitly. I did not read commit 42af134a myself. Not measured: whether a reader has ever taken the currency reading and discarded the evidence the test runs on.
