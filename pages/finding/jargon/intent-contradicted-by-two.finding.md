# Claim

The Intent line on `domains/jargon.md` is contradicted by two live documents on its plainest reading. Line 23 reads "A word declared as a domain carries that one sense on every document using it." Read strictly — the word means the declared sense wherever it appears — `domains/global.md` § Plain Or Declared says "A declared word is exempt only in its declared sense", and `domains/retired-domain.md` § Design says "the word stands wherever it carries another". Both admit the other sense.

# Evidence

Raised by a review-instructions seat on `domains/jargon.md` as the headline of three defects it found and could not land under Every Changed Line.

I verified all three texts firsthand. `domains/jargon.md:23` reads as quoted. `domains/retired-domain.md` § Design reads "A retired domain retires one sense of a word; the word stands wherever it carries another." `domains/global.md` § Plain Or Declared reads "A declared word is exempt only in its declared sense."

The reviewer separated three readings. Strict: contradicted as above. Loose — the word means that sense on documents using it in that sense — true by construction, which `domains/domain-intent.md` bars, since an Intent section admits only what is not yet true. Third: the sense is fixed by the one declaration and cannot be re-fixed document by document, which is coherent, not yet true, and the reading worth having. Its judgment is that reading one is what the sentence most plainly says and is wrong against the corpus.

Not measured: whether any document has in fact re-fixed a declared word's sense locally.
