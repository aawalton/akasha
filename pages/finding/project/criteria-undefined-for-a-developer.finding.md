# Claim

"The criteria" has no antecedent in a developer's read set. The build tasks say "Satisfy the criteria and stop" and "correcting any criterion no instrument can settle", and nothing in those documents, in `domains/project.md`, or in anything else a developer opens says where a criterion is written. They are the checkbox lines under `# Objective` in `projects/<seq>.md` — the same file the stage names, but names only as a place to write. The documents that explain criteria are lead tasks.

# Evidence

Raised by the review-instructions reading of `domains/tasks/projects/build-child-deploy.md` on 2026-08-07, which left it because where the sentence should go rests on judgment rather than an instrument.

Verified myself, and the scope is wider than reported: `grep -c "criteri" domains/tasks/projects/*.md` returns hits on ALL SIX build documents — 2, 2, 1, 3, 2, 2 — not the four stated.

This is Plain Or Declared: the word is used in a sense the reader is not given, and it is the object of the act the whole task exists to perform.

Fifth case this run of a term standing outside its reader's reach; the pattern is filed at `findings/domain/four-terms-out-of-reach`, written before this one and before the `track` scope was measured.
