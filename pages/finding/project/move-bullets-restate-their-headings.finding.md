# Claim

Thirty-two `Move the row` bullets across six project task documents each restate their own stage heading, since the heading is the status token.

# Evidence

Counted over `tasks/projects/`: build-parent-deploy 8, build-singleton-deploy 8, build-child-deploy 5, build-parent-instructions 4, build-singleton-instructions 4, build-child-instructions 3.

Judged alone each bullet is necessary. Judged as a set, seven of build-parent-deploy's eight are the eighth with one word changed, and one Invariants entry carries the whole rule.

This is a six-file change and a restructuring rather than a defect, so the reviewing seat recommended it rather than landing it.
