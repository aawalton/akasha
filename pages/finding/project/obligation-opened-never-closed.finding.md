# Claim

Stage 1 of the parent-deploy task sends an obligation onto the tree and no stage between there and the gate that demands it be resolved says to close one.

# Evidence

Stage 1's **Name** bullet sends a gap nobody owns onto the tree's `obligations`. `ops project move-to --help` documents an obligation gate firing at `awaiting_lead_verification` — this document's own stage 8 — demanding every obligation carry a `resolution` object recording `discharged` or `released`.

Against adding a line: the gate refuses in its own words, so the silence costs one refused call, where a line is paid by every reader at every boot. The instrument settles what such a line would say and cannot settle whether it should exist.
