# Claim

"Both read as ordinary rows from inside either one" stands verbatim on two project task documents, two of its terms have no nearby antecedent, and the claim is now partly false at one destination.

# Evidence

The sentence closes stage 1's **Name** bullet on `tasks/projects/build-parent-deploy.md` and stands verbatim on `build-parent-instructions.md`. It is recoverable only by unpicking.

`ops project move-to --help` documents an obligation gate that refuses an unresolved obligation at three boundaries, so a row carrying one does not read as ordinary work downstream.

A rewrite has to assert what the sentence meant, which is why nothing was landed. Rewriting, narrowing it to the row destination, and cutting it are all open.
