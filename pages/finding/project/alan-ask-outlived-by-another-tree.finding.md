# Claim

A project parked on Alan's verification can have its evidence destroyed by work landing elsewhere, and nothing reports it. The row keeps sitting in his queue asking for an observation that has become impossible to make, and the only thing that surfaces it is Alan getting round to the row himself.

# Evidence

#17551 asked Alan to install a build and leave his 4:05 am Shortcuts automation untouched for one night, so that a firing of `SyncActiveEnergyIntent` could be observed totalling the right day. It reached `awaiting_alan_verification` on 2026-08-06 at 21:41 UTC.

#18149 landed on `main` at `359379e00a` and deleted that intent from `apply-ios-seam.sh`, retiring every writer of the `activeCalories` scalar in favour of a read-time derivation over `health_samples`. The two rows belong to different trees and neither names the other.

From that moment the ask on Alan was unanswerable: the automation he was asked to leave alone drives an intent that a build no longer carries. Nothing changed on the row, nothing reached him, and nothing in the estate reported the pair. `ops project census --state awaiting-alan` reports how long a row has stood and the ask it carries, which is a duration rather than a test of whether the ask can still be met.

It stood three days and surfaced on 2026-08-09 only because Alan asked for it to be looked at. Measured that day: `activeCalories` on `daily-tracking` last carried a value on 2026-06-26, and every row since is empty — so the circle the project existed to repair had been black throughout, on both sides of the install.
