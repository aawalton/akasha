# Evidence

Its own view, which I record rather than endorse: the ABSENCE of the citation is the defensible side, on the ground Composed Outside already binds it — which is what the `build-parent-deploy` reading cut a similar citation for, in commit `17b762fa` this same day. What wants a ruling is the split, not either side of it.

The second half is the one with a consequence: a manager reading the commit-track parent is given the sweep's coverage without its limit.

I did not open the four cited lines. This sits inside the larger claim at `findings/project/six-documents-are-a-grid` — a family with no original drifts at exactly this grain.
