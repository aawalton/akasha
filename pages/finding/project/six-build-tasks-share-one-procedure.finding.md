# Claim

Six build tasks carry the same procedure. Six bullets stand on four or more of them and one invariant stands on all six, and `domains/project.md` governs every one — but the six documents run from 7 bullets to 31, so hoisting the shared text would empty the smallest and barely touch the largest.

# Evidence

Measured 2026-08-06 across `domains/tasks/projects/build-*.md`, verifying a fork returned by the reading of `build-child-commit.md`.

Bullets standing verbatim on four or more of the six:

- "**Move** the project: `ops project move-to <seq> --status understand`." — 6
- "**Move** the project: `ops project move-to <seq> --status implementation`." — 6
- "**Move** the project: `ops project move-to <seq> --status checks`." — 5
- "**Satisfy** the criteria and stop. Behaviour past what they demand is speculation, and no criterion on the project asked for it." — 4
- "**Move** the project: `ops project move-to <seq> --status awaiting_lead_verification`." — 4
- "**Ground** the project's objective in the running system rather than the record of it, correcting any criterion no instrument can settle. A skewed belief reads exactly like a sound one." — 4

The invariant containing "may turn out not to want doing" stands once on each of all six.

`ops instructions governs` returns `domains/project.md` for `build-child-commit.md`, `build-parent-deploy.md` and `build-singleton-commit.md`, so a claim written there reaches every reader that today gets a copy.

What a reading of one document cannot see is how unevenly the copy falls. Total bullets: `build-child-commit` 7, `build-singleton-commit` 10, `build-child-deploy` 14, `build-parent-commit` 17, `build-singleton-deploy` 25, `build-parent-deploy` 31. So the shared six are most of the smallest document and a fifth of the largest, and hoisting them would leave `build-child-commit` holding almost nothing of its own.

Why it is filed rather than repaired: the fork is placement. `domains/project.md` reaches all six but charges every reader of the project domain at boot, where today the six task readers pay it. That is a live trade against Dilution rather than a clerical move, and Single Authority does not say which way it goes.

Raised by the `review-instructions` reading of `build-child-commit.md` on 2026-08-06, which landed one commit and declined to cut a shared line from one of six copies.
