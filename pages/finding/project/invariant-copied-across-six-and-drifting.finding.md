# Claim

One invariant stands in all six project task documents with no original, and the two parent variants differ from the other four by two words, so the copies have already drifted.

# Evidence

All six `tasks/projects/*.md` carry it. Four read "The row may turn out not to want doing… Building it anyway is the easier path"; `build-parent-deploy.md` and `build-parent-instructions.md` read "The tree" and "Carrying it anyway". The middle sentence is identical in all six.

All six declare `domain-parents: [project, task]`, so `domains/project.md` governs every one and is the single surface that could hold the claim. Six copies have no original, which is what Single Authority names: their disagreement would be a contradiction nothing adjudicates rather than a stale copy, and two words have already moved.

Landing it is a six-file horizontal change, and the wording variance means consolidating has to pick which reading binds.
