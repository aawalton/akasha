# Claim

My Math is written by a recorder on the All About Alan pattern, and the two Rules that
make that pattern safe do not reach it. Authorship and Voice sit on the All About Alan
domain, which nothing binding My Math names. What holds authorship in My Math is the
seat's role rather than anything the pages carry, so a seat writing without that role
meets no rule and no gate that would say so.

# Evidence

My Math is `pages/book/my-math.book.md`, with its chapters under
`pages/book-chapter/my-math/`.

`pages/domain/all-about-alan.domain.md` carries both Rules: Authorship, "Never write a
claim about Alan he has not made", and Voice, "Write every note in Alan's first person".
It reaches a seat through the domain that seat is bound to, never through the path being
written.

`ops akasha required-reading` on a My Math chapter names the repository page, the page
type chain and the body-section specifications, and nothing else. The All About Alan
domain is not among them. The verdict here is an absence: no page binding My Math
carries a first-person restriction, an authorship rule, or a bar on writing a claim the
writer cannot answer for.

The recorder role binds the seat rather than the pages. A role reaches whoever is
sitting and nothing else, so another seat writing into the same chapters is bound by
nothing those chapters carry.

The seat that filed this wrote into My Math with the native `Write` tool, and the write
hooks admitted every one: they held every document governing the path, and none of those
documents said anything about authorship.

The pattern instruction reached that seat through its principal, which stated that Alan
had directed it. That seat did not hear him say it.

NOT ESTABLISHED: whether the repair is a rule carried on the book page, a rule raised
onto every written book, or a decision that My Math is deliberately looser than All
About Alan.
