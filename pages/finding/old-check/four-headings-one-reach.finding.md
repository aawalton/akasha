# Claim

Four headings on `domains/check.md` end in "Reach" and each means something different by the word: Change Reach, Superseded Reach and Derived Reach under Principles, and Dispatch Reach under Rules. No one heading is wrong.

# Evidence

Read off the `review-instructions` reading of `domains/check.md` finished 2026-08-21, read line by line, bottom to top, 58 entries. The reading reached this as a whole-document call, every heading earning its place on its own, and landed nothing: renaming is authoring rather than repair.

What it bears on is "Ubiquitous Naming" on `domains/global.md`, which asks one name for one concept — here one word carries four.

Not measured here: I did not open the four sections to say how far apart the four senses are, and I did not check whether "Reach" is used in a fifth sense elsewhere in the corpus.
