# Claim

A readout that draws only as many readings as fit its tile contradicts the Design line saying a readout draws however many readings its feed sends.

# Evidence

Met on 2026-08-13 while managing the #18969 ring tree, in the plan for its second child.

`domains/readouts.md` states in Design: "A readout draws however many readings its feed sends, and refuses only a feed that sends none." Project #18971 carries a criterion Alan settled the same day: the persona tile's circle measures what an upkeep circle measures, "and the tile drawing as many as fit in name order and no more".

The two part on one case and it is the case that stands. The persona roster holds about forty. Sized to match an upkeep circle rather than shrunk to fit, the delivering seat measured that all forty fit at every home-screen size but the smallest, where some do not. There the tile draws fewer readings than its feed sent, which the criterion requires and the Design line forbids.

Neither reading is strained. The Design line was written against a tile hardcoding a count or refusing a partial feed, and reads as forbidding both. The criterion is about a fixed circle size beating a grid that reshapes every time a persona is added. What has no answer is which governs a feed whose readings outnumber the room.

This is filed rather than decided: the criterion is explicit, Alan wrote it, and #18971 is right to follow it. What is unresolved is whether the Design line should say what happens past the room, or whether a tile dropping a reading is a defect in this tile rather than a general permission.

What settles it: a ruling on whether a readout may draw fewer readings than its feed sent, and the Design line saying so either way.
