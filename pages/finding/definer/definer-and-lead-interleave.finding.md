# Claim

Definer carries three of lead's five tasks while lead carries review-initiative and definer carries define-initiative, so the two roles interleave rather than divide at a rung.

# Evidence

Reported by the review of `domains/roles/definer.md` on 2026-08-16: the three are define-project, dispatch-project and verify-handback, and lead's own definition is "one initiative's objectives being met, project by project". The reviewer states either definer's definition is narrower than the role or its task list is wider, and did not act — which one moves is what the role is. It also verified that all nine task entries resolve and that each linked task's guidance line is true of it. Not re-checked here.
