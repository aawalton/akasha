# Claim

Four definer and lead task documents carry each other's lines verbatim: `define-initiative.md` and `define-theme.md` share five, `review-initiative.md` and `review-theme.md` share ten — two binding documents on one claim, which Single Authority bars.

# Evidence

Measured 2026-08-11, on a `review-instructions` reading of `domains/tasks/definer/define-initiative.md` dispatched from `review-documents`. The reading found the first pair; both counts below were taken here.

`define-initiative.md` and `define-theme.md` hold five byte-identical lines: a stage head, the Invariants bullet, and all three stage 3 children. Two more — the Settle and Open children — differ only in the words initiative and theme, making six of `define-theme.md`'s eleven prose lines. `domains/tasks/lead/review-initiative.md` and `domains/tasks/definer/review-theme.md` hold ten byte-identical.

`domains/agent-harness.md`, Single Authority: "A copy has an original, so a reader meeting both knows which drifted; two binding documents have none."

It is live rather than theoretical. The reading found a pronoun with a coherent wrong reading standing verbatim on both members of the first pair, and landed the repair on both in one commit; reading either alone would have left the other carrying it.

The reading could not cut: `define-theme.md` does not govern `define-initiative.md`, so cutting from one strands its reader rather than deferring to an original. Its candidate homes were `domains/memory.md` (governs both, already carries Measured Figure and End State, but charges every memory reader), `domains/roles/definer.md` (governs both task files, but reaches definer tasks that write no objectives), or leaving the pair deliberately — the reasoning `theme.ts` gives for copying the objective shape rather than sharing it, in which case nothing currently records that the duplication is intended.

Not measured: whether any of the four has already drifted from its twin, or whether other task pairs share lines the same way. Two seams a 2026-08-07 reading carried back are subsumed by this fork if the objective-writing lines move to `domains/memory.md`.
