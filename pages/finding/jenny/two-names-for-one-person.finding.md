# Claim

One person carries two names across the corpus: prose says "Jen" — `domains/romance.md`, `domains/personas/ruby.md`, two agent email rules and `domains/alan-email.md` — while every identifier says `jenny`, in `domains/persons/jenny.md` and seven `forward-to: jenny` keys. `domains/global.md`'s Ubiquitous Naming asks for one name in code, data, interface and prose alike.

# Evidence

Raised by the seat that read `domains/alan-email.md` on 2026-08-13 under `review-instructions`, and relayed here rather than re-derived: the list of sites and the count of seven keys are that seat's, and I opened none of them.

That seat left it deliberately, on two grounds it states: repairing this one document would deepen the split rather than close it, and most of the sites stand in sections `domains/domain.md` reserves to Alan, so the change is horizontal and not a reviewer's to land one file at a time.

It reports `domains/tasks/alan-harness/capture-time-tracking.md` as already recording that "Jen" is ambiguous as a linking term, so part of this is known. Nothing here measures whether either name has ever been resolved to the wrong person.
