# Claim

Alan's wife is spelled three ways across the instructions corpus: `Jen` in prose, `jenny` in domain slugs, file names and rule identifiers, and `smilingjenny` in the folder domains named for her business. `pages/domain/global.domain.md` **Ubiquitous Naming** binds one name across code, data, interface and prose.

# Evidence

Filed by the seat dispatching the 2026-08-14 `review-instructions` reading of `domains/alan-email.md`, which raised it there and judged it corpus-wide rather than a repair on that document. Sited here rather than on `alan-email` because the referent is the person.

I ran the two searches myself over `~/instructions`. `Jen` as a standalone word stands in six files: `domains/romance.md`, `domains/tasks/alan-harness/capture-time-tracking.md`, `domains/alan-email.md`, `domains/persona-points-sources/ruby-points-source.md`, `email/alan/rules/agent/ki-invitations.md`, `email/alan/rules/agent/jens-invitations.md`. `jenny` stands in fifteen, among them `domains/persons/jenny.md`, `domains/readout-upkeep-stoplights.md`, six code rules under `email/alan/rules/code/`, and `email/alan/rules/agent/everything-else.md`, `jens-invitations.md` and `jen-other.md`. `jens-invitations.md` carries both spellings.

Re-checked 2026-08-27 in akasha. All three spellings stand, at moved paths. `Jen` as a standalone word is on `pages/domain/romance.domain.md`, `pages/domain/alan-email.domain.md` (five times), `pages/task/capture-time-tracking.task.md`, `pages/persona-points-source/ruby-points-source.persona-points-source.md`, `pages/email-rule-agent/alan/ki-invitations.email-rule-agent.md` and `pages/email-rule-agent/alan/jens-invitations.email-rule-agent.md` — the same six documents. `jenny` is in 123 tracked paths, among them `pages/person/jenny.person.md`, which still records her email as `smilingjenny@gmail.com`. `smilingjenny` is a folder at the repository root and a domain at `pages/domain/smilingjenny.domain.md`.

Two of the three have a cause outside naming discipline: `domains/persons/jenny.md` records her email as `smilingjenny@gmail.com`, which is where the identifier spelling comes from, and `smilingjenny`, `smilingjenny-web` and `smilingjenny-ios` are folder domains named for a business rather than for her.

Not measured: I counted files, not mentions, so this does not match the reading's count of eleven against fourteen. Nothing here says which spelling should win, whether the business folders belong in the count at all, or whether any agent has ever addressed the wrong person over it.
