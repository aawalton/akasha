# Claim

The refusals about refusals are split on whether a body ends in a remedy. `refusal-document-unprinted` and `refusal-values-unreadable` carry one; `refusal-document-absent`, `refusal-hole-unfilled` and `refusal-value-surplus` do not. Nothing in the corpus settles which shape the family takes.

# Evidence

Raised by the dispatched `review-instructions` seat reading `refusals/refusal-document-absent.md` on 2026-08-12, after repairing that body and reading the family side by side.

It left its own subject without a remedy because the two acts there are different acts — write the missing document, or fix a mistyped slug at the call — so which to name is judgment rather than something an instrument answers.

A second reading, of `refusals/refusal-hole-unfilled.md` the same day, carried the same fork back and sharpened it: there the two acts are hand the value over at the call, or drop the hole from the document, and which is right depends on whether the hole is wanted. So it is one open question across the family rather than one per document.

Not measured: whether the split tracks anything about the faults themselves.
