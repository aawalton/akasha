# Claim

The unreadable-side refusals are split on whether the body names what to go looking for. `halt-writer-unreadable` names its two states and `halt-reader-without-vocabulary` its three; `prompt-shape-mirror-unreadable`, `project-status-vocabulary-unreadable`, `statusline-side-unreadable` and `resume-notices-unreadable` state the consequence alone. On this class the causes are the act, since a reader has to know which to check.

# Evidence

Surveyed by the dispatched `review-instructions` seat reading `refusals/resume-notices-unreadable.md` on 2026-08-12, and the bodies re-read at the dispatching seat: the two that name states do, the four that do not state only what nothing now compares.

An earlier reading, of `project-status-vocabulary-unreadable.md` the same day, ran all four branches of its own check and found the causes instrument-settled, then declined to add them because an add grows what every reader pays for at boot. Its draft: "the checkout is not there, `{constant}` was renamed or moved, or it is no longer a bracketed list of double-quoted strings." The `resume-notices` reader named its own two states the same way: the pinned constant is stale, or the notice document is not there.

That earlier reading was filed as a claim that one document was the only such body, which this survey falsifies; this finding replaces it.

Not measured: the other seven documents in `refusals/` whose names end in unreadable.
