# Claim

`refusals/statusline-store-dir-disagrees.md` names which side to change and not why the opposite fix is destructive. `tools/lib/seats-dir.ts` records that moving the store's directory was staged across a project — new directory beside the old, buckets copied forward, the old removed only once nothing read it — because a seat that cannot read what it stated loses the one thing outliving its compaction.

# Evidence

Raised by the dispatched `review-instructions` seat reading the document on 2026-08-12, which had just landed the direction into the body on three instruments' authority.

It deliberately left the reason out: how much a blocked agent needs rests on its judgment rather than on anything an instrument settles, and an add is the one act that grows what every reader pays for at boot. Its own view is that the reason is what would make the direction stick.

Not measured: whether an agent has ever taken the inverted fix.
