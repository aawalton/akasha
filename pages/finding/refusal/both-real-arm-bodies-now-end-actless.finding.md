# Claim

Both real-arm bodies of `hooks-delivered` now end without an act, a false clause having been cut from each, and the measurement that would replace it exists unspent: cycling the seat closes one of the two causes, and a divergence surviving the cycle means the seat was not launched by the supervisor.

# Evidence

Measured 2026-08-11, on a `review-instructions` reading of `refusals/hook-missing-from-payload.md` dispatched from `review-documents`. The reading cut the clause, took the measurement, and declined to spend it.

The cut clause was "and cycling the seat does not close it", standing on `refusals/hook-missing-from-payload.md` and, identically, on `refusals/hook-extra-in-payload.md`. It is false: `materializeSpawnSettings` called against the live file for both override values diffs clean against the registry — missing=0, extra=0 — at digests byte-identical to the payloads the fleet carries now. So cycling is the lever rather than the thing that fails.

Nothing replaced it. The printer leaves this arm two causes and cycling closes one, so the positive sentence rests on judgment.

The recommendation the reading made and did not take: give both bodies a closing sentence saying to cycle the seat, and that a divergence surviving the cycle means the seat was not launched by the supervisor. That splits the two causes by an act the reader can take.

Why it needs a decision: it puts knowledge of the spawn path into a check deliberately built to hold none — the same question standing at `pages/finding/refusal/remedy-blocked-by-what-the-check-passes.finding.md`, now with the measurement in hand rather than absent.

A third body has since declined the same remedy on a misreading of the cut. The reading of `refusals/hook-payload-not-json.md` recorded cycling as "exactly what you stripped" at `79c115cd2`. The diff is the opposite: what went was the NEGATIVE claim, "and cycling the seat does not close it". Naming the cycle is what the measurement supports, not what it removed. So the recommendation reaches three bodies, and one seat has already been turned away from it by the shape of the cut.

Not measured: whether a divergence has ever survived a cycle, or what the added sentence would cost every reader of the check's output.
