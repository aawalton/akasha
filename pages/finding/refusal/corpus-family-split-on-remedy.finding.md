# Claim

The seat-name-corpus refusals are split on remedy: `seat-name-corpus-diverged` ends "Copy one over the other", while `seat-name-corpus-empty` and `seat-name-corpus-unreadable` end without one. A remedy on the latter two has to name a recovery source — git history, or the code repository's byte-equal copy — and which is right is judgment rather than something an instrument settles.

# Evidence

Raised by the dispatched `review-instructions` seat reading `refusals/seat-name-corpus-empty.md` on 2026-08-12, after building a scratch root with an emptied corpus and confirming both trees refuse it loudly.

Its own recommendation was to leave it out: the act is "put the cases back", which is close to what Cut The Obvious refuses to spend a line on.

The reading of `seat-name-corpus-unreadable.md` immediately after gave a positive reason for the asymmetry rather than an absence of one: the diverged body ends with an act because which of two copies wins is information the reader cannot derive, whereas for an unreadable or emptied corpus the only available act is restoring the local file. On that reading the family is consistent by design rather than split. That seat said it was confident and named this as the one judgment worth disagreeing with.

Not measured: whether either recovery source is reliably available when this fires.
