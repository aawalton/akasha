# Claim

Thirteen refusal bodies spell a path in prose that is also held as a constant in the check printing them, so the two can drift silently: `refusals-bound` pairs holes against call sites and never paths. A hole would close it, at the cost of a key and a code change on each. It is the corpus's settled convention rather than a fault of any one document.

# Evidence

Raised by the dispatched `review-instructions` seat reading `refusals/schema-vocabulary-unread.md` on 2026-08-12, which confirmed both markers its own body names are on disk at the path it spells, and that the path is also the check's `TYPES` constant.

It declined to move off the convention in one file: that would leave the corpus saying two things, and revisiting it wants one run across all thirteen rather than a reading of one.

Not measured: whether any of the thirteen has already drifted.
