# Claim

`refusals/prompt-prefix-disagrees.md` and `refusals/prompt-prefix-undefined.md` cover the same two arms and disagree about which side moves. The undefined one closes "It must say {spelled}", foreclosing the choice; the disagrees one closes on the consequence and leaves the act to be derived. `prompt-shape-mirror.ts` sets out that sometimes the file is wrong and sometimes the declaration is, and no instrument settles which — so they cannot both be right.

# Evidence

Raised by the dispatched `review-instructions` seat reading `prompt-prefix-disagrees.md` on 2026-08-12, which rendered both bodies through the printer with the values the call site hands over rather than reading the templates.

Its own view: the disagrees document is the correct one and the sibling is at fault. It did not land that, the sibling being a document its run was not given and the call resting on judgment rather than on anything measurable.

Not measured: whether either arm has fired, and which side moved when it did.
