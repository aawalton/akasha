# Claim

The four restart-clause refusals each carry a reason sentence copied near-verbatim from the `notices/resume.md` preamble, one of them word for word. Single Authority argues for cutting them, and each remedy is fully mechanical without its reason. Splitting the family on one document is what a single dispatched reading can do and is the wrong shape for the change.
