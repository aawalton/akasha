# Claim

`domains/ops-instructions.md` is built on `verb`, a word `domains/retired/verb.md` retires in favour of `command`, and 170 other live documents use it too.

# Evidence

Reported by the review of `domains/ops-instructions.md` on 2026-08-15: six uses on that document, the Definition line included, and `bun tools/retired-words.ts --word verb --no-code` reporting 394 sites across 170 live documents. The tool's own help says it reports sites and never violations. The reviewer left the word alone because rewriting one document would put it out of step with `domains/ops-memory.md`, its mirror, read the same day and keeping the word. Whether the retirement is carried through at all, and in what order, was not judged. The counts were not re-measured here.
