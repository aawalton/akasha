# Claim

Athena is the only one of the forty territory-holding personas whose Definition names her territory in that domain's own words rather than in her image.

# Evidence

Reported by the review of `domains/personas/athena.md` on 2026-08-16: her second clause, "keeps the harness every agent reads and obeys", is a near-verbatim lift of `domains/agent-harness.md`'s Definition, "everything an agent must read and obey". The reviewer read all 40 siblings against their domains — ryn "counts nothing shelved until it is in a reader's hands", dalla "keeps the bridge every build crosses from built to real", amy "keeps the ledger of who is owed attention" — and reports nothing is at risk either way, the binding edge being `championed-domain:` in her frontmatter, which `domain-edges` checks, so the prose carries no claim and nothing breaks if `agent-harness.md` moves. Her own History holds the image a rewrite would use: "every place where your hands meet what you are doing". It did not act, this being a call about her voice. Not re-checked here.
