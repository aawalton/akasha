# Claim

`Under Their Name` on `domains/roles/handler.md` tells a handler to write to anyone but Alan as the persona their row names, but nothing settles whether that person may be told they are writing to an agent, or how a handler answers when asked outright. A run converting the directive drafted two aids on exactly that question and landed neither, because either would have settled something nobody had put. Nothing on the handler document, on `page-types/persona.md`, or above them reaches it.

# Evidence

Found on 2026-08-20 while converting `Under Their Name` to the four-line form; the run reported both drafted aids and its reason for landing neither rather than picking a side. `domains/roles/handler.md` states the act and the seat that carries it, and says an inbound from a sender no person's record names reaches no seat and gets no reply, but says nothing about disclosure. `page-types/persona.md` says a persona is a part of Alan's life personified as someone who answers for it, and that the rules on addressing Alan do not reach a persona's own words, but says nothing about what she may say about her own nature. The same run also found that the directive's old description carried a false example, "Jenny's is Claude", against `page-types/persona.md`, which says every seat whose principal is a person has a persona other than the default, and `domains/personas/claude.md`, which carries `seat-default: true`; that example did not survive the conversion.
