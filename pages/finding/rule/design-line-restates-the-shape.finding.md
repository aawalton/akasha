# Claim

The one Design line on `domains/rule.md` restates what `page-body-shapes/domain.md` already enforces, and if it is kept it belongs once on `domains/instruction.md`.

# Evidence

The line is "A rule is an act and a description." The review of `domains/rule.md` on 2026-08-16 reached a verdict of removal and could not land it, `page-types/domain.md`'s Every Changed Line covering this document's whole body and its dispatch releasing none of it. Its evidence: `page-body-shapes/domain.md` declares the Rules block as a heading, a bolded act slot and a description slot, using the same two nouns, and a scratch-tree probe refused a rule with a description and no bolded act and refused one with a bolded act and no description. So the claim is bound in enforced machinery, which makes the prose a second binding document against Single Authority and leaves it preventing nothing `page-holds-shape` does not already refuse; it is also none of the three kinds `domains/domain-design.md` allows, and no document in the corpus links to `domains/rule.md`. On placement: `domains/instruction.md` already carries the neighbouring unenforced claim that an instruction's shape bounds its size, and `domains/principle.md` line 20 says the identical thing with one noun changed, so one line there would replace two. The reviewer notes the sibling-fact rule pointing that way is written for Definitions and that carrying it to Design is its own reading. Nothing was re-run here.
