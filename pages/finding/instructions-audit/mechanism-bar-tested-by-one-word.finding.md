# Claim

`tools/checks/resume-notices.ts` enforces a proxy narrower than the bar it exists for. `notices/resume.md` states the bar as "the clause names no mechanism"; the check tests the restart-recovery clause for the single word `queue`. A clause reading "it is stranded in the socket buffer of the dead process" names a mechanism, hands every restarting seat a guess to reason from, and passes. A clause denying any mechanism, but using the word, is refused.

# Evidence

Probed by the dispatched `review-instructions` seat reading `refusals/restart-clause-names-a-mechanism.md` on 2026-08-12, against four clause bodies: the live clause passes, a queue clause prints, a socket-buffer clause passes, and a clause denying a mechanism prints. `tools/tests/resume-notices.test.ts` pins the middle two rows only, so neither end was covered.

It did not close the gap: that means deciding what else to bar and whether a word list is the right shape at all. What it could do from the document it did — the refusal now names the word it caught, so nobody reading it hunts for a mechanism that is not in their text.

Not measured: whether any live clause has ever named a mechanism in other words.
