# Claim

`prepare-interview` assumes the corpus it prepares against already stands, and against an empty one it pushes the interviewer to import a frame from neighbouring corpora instead of asking from the blank page.

# Evidence

Stage 2 says to read every domain the direction reaches and the findings filed against the subject; stage 3 says to find where the subject is thin or says two things at once. Both take a standing corpus as their material.

On the session that opened `my-strategy`, the folder was created empty in the same run. With nothing to read there, the interviewer read `~/memory/themes/` and `~/memory/initiatives/` instead and put the direction found there to Alan as the opening question. He named it as the wrong approach: "you're reaching into other places for content, rather than just asking me the question from the blank page."
