# Claim

The Definition on `domains/roles/lead.md` is thin over a third of its own Tasks section. It reads "what a domain is and becomes, settled with Alan", which covers the responsibilities and the initiative entries. `define-project`, `dispatch-project` and `verify-handback` are three of the eight task entries, and projects are how a domain becomes what it becomes. The line is not false; it is thin.

# Evidence

Raised by a review-instructions seat on `domains/roles/lead.md`, which left it and deliberately drafted no replacement: Every Changed Line reserves a Definition to Alan with no carve-out for a dispatched review, unlike Responsibility Change. It cited commit fec0404f on `domains/roles/interviewer.md` — another reviewer's revert this same pass — as the precedent for treating that reservation as absolute.

I did not count the task entries myself or read the three project task documents.

Not measured: whether a reader has ever taken the Definition as excluding project work.
