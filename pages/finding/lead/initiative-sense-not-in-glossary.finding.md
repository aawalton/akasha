# Claim

`domains/roles/lead.md` carries no `glossary:` key while using a word with a declared sense. `domains/initiative.md` defines an initiative as "a bounded effort to close one or more gaps between a domain and its intent", which is not the ordinary sense, and the word stands in two task entries a lead reads without that document in its boot. Against adding the key: both uses sit inside links to task files that carry the sense in context.

# Evidence

Raised by a review-instructions seat on `domains/roles/lead.md`, which stated the fork and leaned against adding the key: no instrument settles it, and the Add step of `review-instructions` sends anything resting on judgment back rather than growing what every reader pays at boot.

Worth weighing against `pages/finding/domain/glossary-free-at-boot.finding.md`, filed earlier in this same pass, which records that a glossary entry costs a composed boot nothing — term definitions reach a reader through `ops instructions read` and are absent from a boot entirely. If that is right, the reviewer's cost argument here is the mistake that finding describes, and the two should be read together.

I did not read `domains/initiative.md` or check where the word stands on the lead document.
