# Claim

A lead carrying a ruling to Alan has no instruction about where his answer lands, and the carrier it is given deletes itself.

# Evidence

`verify-handback` says to carry what no instrument settles and no principle decides to Alan through `ops project ask`, which requires the ask to stand in the project document's `# Notes`. His answer then lands on that same document. A memory document is deleted when its purpose is complete, so a ruling that shapes a domain is recorded in the one place certain to be thrown away, and the next reader meets the same gap with no record it was ever settled.

On 2026-08-12 Amy parked #18792 on Alan asking where the upkeep bars should be authored, his earlier ruling having stopped them borrowing the persona rows. He answered with a strategy rather than a project decision — readouts are defined in files that project to data that is read by code — and said he was surprised it had not come back to him for intent. It had not, because nothing told her to look for one. The answer was domain intent for `readouts` and now stands there. Taken as the project answer it asked to be, it would have gone out with the project document, and every later readout would have been cut without it.

This is not an argument against `ops project ask`. The ask belongs on the project: that is the row's own prose and the machinery tests for it. What is missing is the step after the answer — reading it for whether it binds beyond the project that surfaced it, and placing it where it does.
