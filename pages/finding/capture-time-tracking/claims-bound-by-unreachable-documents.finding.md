# Claim

Four claims on `capture-time-tracking` are also bound by three documents no seat working that task reads.

# Evidence

Reported by the review of `domains/tasks/alan-harness/capture-time-tracking.md` on 2026-08-16: `domains/safety-level.md` binds the safety carry-forward; `domains/session-difficulty.md` binds difficulty not carrying and the no-activity refusal, its Definition almost word for word the task's own gloss; `domains/ops-tracking.md` binds that refusal too and the refuse-while-open rule. None of the three is an ancestor of the task, whose parents are alan-harness and task, so removing the restatements would take the fact out of the seat's hands rather than move it. The reviewer states the fork as adding session or ops-tracking to `domain-parents` and then removing the restatements, against leaving the duplication standing — the first buying Single Authority at the cost of every capture seat's boot weight, the second keeping the reading cheap and leaving four claims in two places each with nothing to say which drifted. It did not act, this being a call about what a seat pays at boot. Not re-checked here.
