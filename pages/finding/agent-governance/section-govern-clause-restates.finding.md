# Claim

The Design line "A document may govern one named section of the files it matches, rather than the whole of every file" on `domains/agent-governance.md` closes on a clause carrying no claim. "The files it matches" already carries the plural and "one named section" already carries the contrast, so the trailing clause restates both.

# Evidence

Raised by the dispatched reviewer of `domains/agent-governance.md` on 2026-08-07 and relayed here unjudged. It proposed the line without the trailing clause and left it standing, Every Changed Line on `domains/domain.md` reserving a domain's Design for Alan.

This is the smallest of the four repairs that reading proposed and the only one that changes no claim. Recorded so it is not lost between reviews rather than because it is urgent.

Not measured: whether the clause was written against a misreading someone actually had, which would be the case for keeping it.
