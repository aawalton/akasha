# Claim

One claim stands on two surfaces in two tenses: `domains/instruction.md` carries "A schema limits every instruction" as an end state not yet reached, and `domains/code-quality.md` asserts the same sentence as present fact in a rule's warrant.

# Evidence

`domains/instruction.md`, `# Intent`: "A schema limits every instruction." An Intent section holds only what is not yet true — `domains/domain-intent.md` binds that: "An Intent section holds only what is not yet true."

`domains/code-quality.md`, `# Rules` → `## Code Comments`, warrant: "Every instruction is limited by a schema, which caps its act, its reason and how many stand together."

Both landed 2026-08-06, minutes apart, at one hand. Neither cites the other.

A reader meeting both cannot tell which is claimed. There is a reading on which they do not conflict — the warrant naming a schema as the only thing that limits an instruction, the Intent claiming every instruction has one — but nothing in either wording picks it, and the present indicative on both is what hides the difference.

Not settled: whether the repair is the warrant stating the norm rather than the fact, the Intent going once the sweep closes it, or the two being one claim that should bind from one surface under Single Authority.
