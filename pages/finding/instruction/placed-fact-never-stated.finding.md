# Claim

Nothing binds an author to state the fact a thing's placement is carrying. The estate expresses much by position — a domain's folder records its kind, `domain-parents:` is a read obligation, responsibilities stand in priority order — and each of those is written down, so the practice is right by habit rather than by rule. An author cannot test their own document for the gap, because a placed fact and a stated one read alike until something moves.

# Evidence

A standing ruling of 2026-07-28, held in `dirty/skills/agent-harness/rulings/surfaces.md` and reached by an ingest seat emptying that source. Kept verbatim at `dirty/maybe-keep/skills/agent-harness/rulings/surfaces.md`; filed here because that copy is queued for its own sweep.

Its specimen: two rulings sat under a heading reading "Owed to the manage contract", and their owed-ness was carried only by sitting there. "The move did not break the signal; it revealed the signal was never written down."

Searched all of `domains/` for `by position|positional|convention|unstated|reads as coverage` case-insensitively: six lines. The two nearest are about something else — `domains/domain.md` Path Globs ("It reads as coverage") is about a glob claiming files nobody looked at, and `domains/role.md` Adjacent Repair ("unstated it reads as a gate") is about ownership. No mechanism reaches it: a schema constrains what a section may hold, never what its placement is being asked to mean.

WHAT ARGUES AGAINST IT, recorded because it is real. This corpus does state its positional conventions. `domains/domain.md`'s Design says a domain's folder records its kind and that every document declaring `domain-slug:` is a domain whatever folder it sits in; `domains/role-responsibilities.md` says "Responsibilities stand in priority order". Each is the ruling being obeyed without the ruling standing.

Duplicate check, run as its own call: `rg -uuu -il "by position|positional|convention.*unwritten|placement"` over `~/memory/findings/`, eight files, none of them this.
