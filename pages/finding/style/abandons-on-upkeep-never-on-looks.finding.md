# Claim

Alan abandons on maintenance cost, never on aesthetics. In the style domain this is the only claim ever recorded overturning a choice already made, and it did so at three scales: about twelve long-sleeves bought and never worn against one loose shirt worn constantly; a year of shaved legs he "loved the look, hated the upkeep" of; and a coach's own proposal of navy for the neutral chassis, withdrawn when he objected that it "would import a decision into the one system built to have none."
