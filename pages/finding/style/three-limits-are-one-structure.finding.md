# Claim

Three limits Alan named separately about clothes are one structure. He tends monochrome, likes clothes loose or tight and never in between, and likes them subtle or bold and never in between. A pole is a decision already made; the middle is a decision deferred forever, so a mid garment re-asks its question at every wearing. Read that way it is a design spec rather than a limitation, and anything landing mid on any of the three axes is refused without further argument.
