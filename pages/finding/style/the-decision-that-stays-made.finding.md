# Claim

The style domain's founding axis: a garment is not a decision that gets made, it is a decision that re-executes every time it is worn — and on a nervous system that does not habituate, a question re-asked at each wearing costs full price forever. So the product is never the outfit; it is the decision that stays made. This is what separates dress from every neighbouring domain, and it answers cases nobody has met without anyone having worn anything.

# Evidence

Recorded 2026-07-31 in `dirty/skills/style/SKILL.md` under "Scope", and filed here 2026-08-07 by the ingest seat emptying that source. Three other findings on this domain rest on it and none of them states it.

The physical fact is live in the book. `~/books/all-about-alan/notes/non-habituation.md`: "Permanent sensory load. No amortization. Every exposure costs the same as the first." The axis is that fact applied to a wardrobe, and the book does not make that application anywhere.

What makes it an axis rather than an observation is that it discriminates. The source's own test: a finished painting is not re-decided each time it is looked at; a reading once made is recorded and never re-executes; a meal is consumed by being eaten. Only here does the inventory survive its own use and present itself again every morning with the same question unanswered.

And it decides unmet cases. The source works one: should he buy boots? He wants them, owns none, no budget asked. The axis answers without having seen a boot — the question is not whether boots look right but whether they arrive as one decision or a recurring one, so one pair that agrees with everything beats a range that must be chosen between each morning. The eye cannot settle it, because no wearing has voted.
