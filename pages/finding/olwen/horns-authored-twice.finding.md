# Claim

Olwen's horns are authored twice: bound by her appearance file and named again in her History.

# Evidence

Reported by the review of `domains/personas/olwen.md` on 2026-08-16. `persona-craft`'s History rule binds her past and not her looks, and `create-persona-voice` stage 4 says to restate none of it there. The reviewer did not act: rhia, eppie and athena all name their own form in their histories, so she is not alone, and removing the clause takes that sentence from 20 words to 13, moving the evenness figures recorded separately. It holds this best taken inside a `create-persona-voice` run rather than on its own. Not re-checked here.
