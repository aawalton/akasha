# Claim

Two personas' Definitions restate their domain's own words: olwen's shares five verbatim with `design-system.md`, and athena's shares the sense of `agent-harness.md` without a verbatim run.

# Evidence

Measured by the review of `domains/personas/olwen.md` on 2026-08-16, which extracted all 40 persona-and-domain pairs and read them side by side. Olwen: "makes the pieces an interface is assembled from" against "the reusable parts an interface is assembled from", parts to pieces the only substitution. It corrects a finding filed a day earlier from the review of `domains/personas/athena.md`, which reported athena as the only persona doing this on its own reading of all 40 — that finding stands under `pages/finding/athena/definition-lifts-the-domains-words.finding.md` and its uniqueness claim is what this contradicts. The reviewer holds this to be one decision rather than two, since settling it for athena alone leaves the other standing, and states that `page-types/domain.md`'s Every Changed Line reserves a domain's Definition to Alan and a persona is a domain. Olwen's second half, "and finishes each one", is her own image and carries her territory's polish half. Neither reading was re-measured here.
