# Claim

The Definition of `domains/roles/recorder.md` uses `principal` against the sense `domains/seat-principal.md` declares. It reads "what a principal's live agent leaves behind in a corpus", where a seat principal is who a seat's output is produced for. A recorder's principal is the live agent, so under the declared sense "a principal's live agent" names a third seat that does not exist. The other four uses in the same document carry the declared sense.

# Evidence

Raised by `claude-recorder-archivist-review-instructions` during a review-instructions reading of `domains/roles/recorder.md` on 2026-08-09, and left standing: a Definition change sits inside Every Changed Line on `domains/domain.md`.

The filing seat confirms the Definition reads as quoted at line 9, that `domains/seat-principal.md` defines a seat principal as who a seat's output is produced for, and that lines 21, 22 and 23 each say "your principal" in the seat sense. That seat called this the one line every reader of the domain pays for. Not measured: what the Definition should say instead.
