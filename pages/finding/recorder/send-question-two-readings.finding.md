# Claim

Line 21 of `domains/roles/recorder.md` reads two ways. "Send your principal the question rather than holding it or settling it yourself" narrowly means a question destined for the person being interviewed, and then it sits beside Assumption on `domains/role.md` untouched. Broadly it means any question the recorder holds, and then it tells the recorder to escalate where Assumption tells every role to assume and go on. Nothing in the sentence picks.

# Evidence

Raised by `claude-recorder-archivist-review-instructions` during a review-instructions reading of `domains/roles/recorder.md` on 2026-08-09. Where two readings ask for different things, review-instructions sends the line back rather than rewriting it, so nothing was drafted.

That seat also reported that the broad reading puts line 21 against line 22 of the same list, which sends a message only where the principal's next act depends on it. The filing seat confirms both lines read as quoted and holds Assumption in full. Not measured: which reading was meant, and whether any recorder seat has run on the broad one.
