# Claim

The `Cut` bullet on `review-instructions.md` omits `Add` from its list of alternatives, and the omission is load-bearing rather than an oversight. The bullet defaults to cutting "unless keeping, repairing, trimming or rewriting clearly passes Cut The Obvious" — five of the document's six acts. `Add` is held to a stricter bar, that an instrument settles what the line should say, so listing it there would say it passes on Cut The Obvious alone and would loosen it.

# Evidence

Recorded by the review-instructions seat reading that document, explicitly so a later reviewer does not "close the gap". It is a guard against a repair rather than a defect: the gap reads like an oversight and is not one.

I verified the `Add` step's stricter bar: it reads "only where an instrument settles what it should say", and "anything resting on judgment goes back to the principal rather than into the document."

This is filed as a finding rather than left in a report because the report sits under one seat's agent directory and the next reader of this document will not have it. Nothing else in the corpus records why the list is five and not six.

Not measured: whether any earlier reviewer has already tried to add `Add` to that list.
