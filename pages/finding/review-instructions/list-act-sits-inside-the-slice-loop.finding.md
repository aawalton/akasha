# Claim

The **List** bullet is a once-per-document act sitting inside a stage headed "Each slice, bottom to top", and the two sibling walks place the same act differently from each other and from this one.

# Evidence

`domains/tasks/archivist/review-instructions.md` stage 2 is headed "Each slice, bottom to top." Its first bullet, **List**, tells the seat to list every slice before judging any — an act performed once per document rather than once per slice.

The siblings disagree:

- `domains/tasks/archivist/ingest-instructions.md` puts the equivalent act in stage 1.
- `domains/tasks/alan-harness/guided-close-read.md` gives it a stage of its own.

Three walks, three placements, and nothing states which shape is right. Moving it is a judgment about the shape of a walk rather than a correction any instrument settles.
