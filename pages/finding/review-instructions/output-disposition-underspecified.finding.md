# Claim

The document's account of what becomes of a reading's output is incomplete in three places at once: the Definition says everything is landed, stage 3 names things with no disposition, and stage 4 does not classify what stage 3 produced.

# Evidence

`domains/tasks/archivist/review-instructions.md`:

- Definition — "reading one surface unattended and landing what it should become". Stage 4 carries forks back, so not everything is landed.
- Stage 2 has a **Land** bullet. Stage 3 has none. Its **Name** bullet tells the seat to name what no slice could reach and stops there.
- Stage 4 asks for "what you changed and what you ran to justify each, what you left standing with the fork stated". Neither category names a stage 3 observation, which is not a slice the seat left standing and not something it changed.

The reading of 2026-08-06 treated its own stage 3 output as carry-back, which is an assumption rather than something the document states.

`domains/roles/archivist.md` states the landed-versus-returned split in its own gloss for this task, so the claim is not unheld — whether the Definition should carry it, and whether stage 3 owes a disposition bullet, is the open question.
