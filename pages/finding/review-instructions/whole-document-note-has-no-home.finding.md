# Claim

Stage 3 of `review-instructions.md` tells a seat to name what no line could reach and never says where it goes. The report takes per-line verdicts. The hand-back takes what changed, what was left standing with the fork stated, and where the report is. A whole-document observation is none of the three, so each seat picks its own destination.

# Evidence

Raised by the review-instructions seat reading `domains/tasks/archivist/review-instructions.md` itself, which reported putting its own whole-document observations in both places — "a choice the document did not make for me".

I can corroborate this from the receiving end. Across thirty readings in this pass, whole-document observations reached me in the hand-back every time, but their placement varied: some seats set them under a forks heading, some under a separate "things no line could reach" heading, and some folded them into the per-line section. Nothing was lost, but nothing was consistent either.

Not measured: whether any seat has dropped such an observation entirely for want of a destination — which is the failure the gap would produce and the one I could not see from here.
