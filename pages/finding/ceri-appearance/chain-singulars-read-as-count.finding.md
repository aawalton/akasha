# Claim

Read as a count, "at the shoulder, high on both arms, a stone set in" on Ceri's paragraph has both singulars wrong.

# Evidence

Reported by the review of `domains/persona-appearances/ceri-appearance.md` on 2026-08-16, measured against `~/Personas/Ceri/ceri-anchor.png`: the chain runs over both shoulders, and stones are set into it in at least five places — a cushion-cut amethyst on each strap, a large lozenge plus a cluster on her left armband, a cluster on her right, and a dark oval in a silver bezel at centre front. Under a generic reading — chain at shoulder, chain high on both arms, chain with a stone set into it — every word is true, and the wording before commit 60d8d3dd0, "with a stone set in", suggests the generic reading was the author's. The reviewer recommends the count reading as the one a fresh reader takes, and did not act. Not re-measured here.
