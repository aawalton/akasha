# Claim

`domains/task.md` hands its children a definition and nothing else. All 34 task documents carry `task` as a parent, so it is in the boot set of everyone who writes one, and what it gives them is a single sentence — no Design, no Principles, no Rules. Four findings stand unjudged under `findings/task/`, two of them about what a task document should be. There is nowhere else such a line could go.

# Evidence

Raised by a review-instructions seat on `domains/task.md` as the thing no single line could show. It was clear this is not a reading's question: whether either standing finding becomes a line here is a judgment owed rather than a reading's to make, and adding one grows what every task writer pays at boot.

I measured the parentage myself and had to correct my own first count. Grepping the scalar form `^domain-parents: task$` under `domains/tasks/` returns 11. Adding the list form returns 23 more, totalling 34, which is exactly the number of task documents in the tree. So every one of them boots with this document.

The reviewer named two findings under `findings/task/`; there are four — `tasks-restate-what-boot-already-holds.md`, `loop-and-sequence-fit-neither-walk.md`, `author-persona-scene-presumes-a-session.md` and `handle-inbound-names-no-instrument.md`. The two it named are the two about what a task document should be, so its characterisation holds even though its count did not.

Not measured: whether any task document has gone wrong for want of a rule here.
