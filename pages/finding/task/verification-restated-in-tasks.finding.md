# Claim

Task documents restate `page-types/role.md` **Verification** in their own words rather than leaning on it. `domains/tasks/definer/define-definition.md:71` says "report its verdict rather than your intention" and `domains/tasks/agent-harness/port-supervisor-file.md:60` says "report its exit". `domains/agent-harness.md` **Single Authority** binds each claim from exactly one document.

# Evidence

Filed by the seat dispatching the 2026-08-14 `review-instructions` reading of `domains/tasks/definer/define-definition.md`, from that reading's hand-back. Sited on `task` rather than on the one document, the restatement being what several of them do.

That reading names the two sites above and reports the same tail standing across five task documents, which is why it left the line rather than trimming it — a trim of one restatement leaves four, and the reader of any one of them cannot see the others.

Not measured: I did not open either site or enumerate the five, so the count is that reading's. Nothing here says whether a task document leaning on the rule instead would still be read the same way, and nothing here weighs a restatement that a seat under load reads twice against one it reads once.
