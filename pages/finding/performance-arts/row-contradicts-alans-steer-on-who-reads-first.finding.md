# Claim

Eppie's persona row tells a seat to give her read of a song first; Alan's steer, given directly, is that his read comes first and her layers after. The row's `conduct` step 3 says "My read first, drawing him in, landing on his", while the opening paragraph of the same value says "the read is Alan's own ... never substituting my own" — so the row contradicts itself as well as him. His steer survives only in quarantined documents; the row is the live surface, and the one a fresh seat loads.

# Evidence

Measured 2026-08-07 while emptying `dirty/skills/performance-arts/findings.md`.

The row, read live: `ops page show 019ee19d-d6e5-7ed5-b1ad-27ffe6f34987
--properties conduct`. Step 3 is "Dissect — once it's playing, lead the
post-listen read in register: what the song said and meant, whether it's true,
what made it good and where it fell short, the personal connections it pulls
on. My read first, drawing him in, landing on his — the heart of the loop."

The same value's opening paragraph is "**The read is his; I companion it.** In
the live session the read is Alan's own — his verdict on the music, in his
terms — and I draw it out and record *that*, never substituting my own." Both
sentences are in one property. A seat loading the row gets the instruction and
its contradiction together.

His steer survives only under quarantine. `rg -uuu -n -i "his read first|read
is Alan's own|pre-load"` over `domains/` exits 1 — no live document carries it.
Three quarantined ones do: `dirty/skills/performance-arts/findings.md` quotes
him as "Going forward, invite ALAN's read of the song FIRST ... THEN offer the
context/layers he might be missing"; `dirty/skills/performance-arts/SKILL.md:105`
makes it the first numbered principle, "His reading comes first, and it comes
from a clean floor ... In his words: invite his read"; and
`dirty/skills/performance-arts/rulings.md` rules that "his direct instruction
outranks every other surface", that reconciling it "is a row edit, not a code
change", and that "the wording is his to give".

`domains/personas/eppie.md` is live and settles nothing — its `# Conduct` is a
voice sample carrying no procedure, so the live persona document does not
contradict the row and does not confirm it either.

The asymmetry is the point. Every document recording what Alan asked for is
queued for deletion; the surface contradicting him is a database row that no
sweep touches and no check reads. Nothing compares a persona row's conduct
prose against anything.
