# Claim

`tasks/lead/define-initiative.md` opens every initiative by settling the domain's vision, and where the domain has none it makes writing one the initiative's first objective — while `tasks/lead/review-domain-vision.md` walks every vision out of the estate until no domain carries one. A lead running the first task on a domain the second has already cleared is directed to write back exactly what was removed, and neither task names the other.

# Evidence

Found 2026-08-05 while opening an initiative against `agent-harness`, which carries no `# Vision` section.

`define-initiative.md:14` reads "Settle the vision by define-domain-vision where the domain has one to settle, and where it has none make it this initiative's first objective instead." `review-domain-vision.md:9` defines its own act as "walking one domain's vision line by line with Alan until none of it is left", and `roles/lead.md:23` lists it as owed by "any domain still carrying a `# Vision`, until none does". It landed at 2026-08-05 17:31, after `define-initiative.md` was last reviewed on 2026-08-04.

What survives a vision walk moves to `# Design`, per `review-domain-vision.md:26`, and `domains/finding.md` already carries that section and no vision. So the construct an initiative is told to measure gaps against is being retired in favour of one that records what a domain's shape turns on, which is not the same thing: `domains/domain-vision.md` defines a vision as "the state a domain should be in, and what every gap in it is measured against", and nothing yet says what replaces that role once the last vision goes.

Both readings are live in the lead's own task list, which is the surface a lead boots into.
