# Claim

Two readout documents describe a readout nothing draws and nothing computes: `domains/readouts/readout-hygiene.md` and `domains/readout-sources/readout-source-hygiene.md`. Neither line in them is false. What is gone is the thing they describe.

# Evidence

Hygiene left the upkeep row at `11fa91febb`, throughput taking its place among the six. Project #19294 then dropped `get_hygiene_stoplight` from the database on 2026-08-17, along with the seven other circle-returning functions and the eight helpers beneath them, and deleted the three `readout` declaration rows they read.

After that drop, `hygiene` appears in the code repository in no TypeScript file at all outside a generated icon keyword list. Nothing computes a mean of the six inbox colours, and nothing asks for one. The four database tests that guarded that mean went with the drop.

`readout-hygiene.md` says the figure is a mean of the six inbox colours, and `readout-source-hygiene.md` says the source is what the day's six inbox colours average to. Both remain accurate descriptions of a readout that no longer exists anywhere but in these two documents.

`page-types/domain.md` says a domain stays even when nothing needs it any more, and goes only when it no longer fits the structure — so this is a question about the structure rather than a defect, and it is not one project #19294 was placed to settle.
