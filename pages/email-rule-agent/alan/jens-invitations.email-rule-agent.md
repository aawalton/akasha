# Match

- **from** is
  - `smilingjenny@gmail.com`
- **subject** contains
  - `invitation:`

# Rule

**Accept every invitation Jen sends Alan, then archive it.**

Their calendars should agree, so an invitation from her is a yes by default. It covers what she organizes and never an invitation she was merely sent. One already out of his inbox stays untouched.
