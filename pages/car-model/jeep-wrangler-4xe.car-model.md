It pairs the 2.0L turbo I4 ('Hurricane 4') with two electric motors and a 17.3 kWh battery, sending 375 hp / 470 lb-ft through an eight-speed automatic to a Selec-Trac or Rock-Trac transfer case. Stellantis announced in January 2026 that the 4xe program (Wrangler and Grand Cherokee) is terminated effective with MY2026 — the 2025 model is the final Wrangler 4xe sold new in the US, and remaining 2025 inventory remains on dealer lots through 2026 under a Stellantis $7,500 manufacturer rebate. The replacement is a planned Wrangler REEV using the new Grand Wagoneer 4xe range-extender architecture, no timeline yet.
