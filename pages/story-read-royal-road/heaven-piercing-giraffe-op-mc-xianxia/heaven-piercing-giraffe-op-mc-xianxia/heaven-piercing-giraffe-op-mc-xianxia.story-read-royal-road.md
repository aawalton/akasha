A giraffe spent centuries eating leaves, accidentally pierced the heavens, pissed off several gods, and got bored.

So when the system asked:

[Do you wish to reincarnate as a human?]

It selected [Yes].

Now she's a human girl with the worst possible element — Earth, the one least expected to fly — in a world where altitude means power. Her sect thinks she's just an arrogant young master.

She just wants to reach the acacia leaves.

Quietly. Without the gods who remember her finding out she's back.

🦒 Monster Girl, OP MC, Xianxia Satire.
