What doesn’t kill him, just makes him stronger.

The System changed everything, granting humanity a path to power while fracturing the world into distinct, brutal difficulty tiers.

Jake and his neighbours drew the ultimate short straw. Trapped in a surprise Hell Difficulty Tutorial, their ordinary apartment complex has warped into an endless vertical labyrinth—a tower of shifting realms, lethal rules, and monsters that actively want them dead.

Armed with [Absolute Perception] and a low-tier healing ability, he embraces a grim philosophy: whatever can be broken can be mended stronger.

To survive a hell tier, Jake is going to break his own limits—and his own body—over and over again, until he becomes the strongest apex predator the System has ever seen.
