# Being-known supply

> Being-known supply — why closing the Being-Known gap is so hard (capability × willingness, cheap × expensive paths), and the committed forward plan: therapy with an autism-specialist near-twin who threads both axes. Lands the actionable-plan slot the expansion map deferred.

The [connection-expansion map](connection-expansion.book-chapter.md) lays out every direction for paying down [connection debt](connection-debt.book-chapter.md), then defers the narrowing step — filter the full map to one committed plan. This note is that step, for the Being-Known tank specifically. It names what closing the gap *requires* in supply terms, then names the concrete plan that meets the requirement.

## The supply decomposition — why this has been hard

To close the [Being-Known gap](connection.book-chapter.md#a-homeostatic-requirement-not-a-luxury) I need someone who **can** close it and **is willing** to close it. Capability and willingness are both required and **independent** — neither implies the other — and there are two paths through them.

- **(a) Close it cheaply.** Requires a rare near-twin: high similarity and/or expertise, which makes the [model-update cost low](being-known-cost.book-chapter.md#cost-decomposition--mechanics-plus-content). The gate on this path is the **rarity of sufficiently similar people.**
- **(b) Close it expensively but willingly.** Requires someone willing to do the costly model-update work *anyway*, even though their model of me has to be built nearly from scratch. The gate on this path is the **rarity of willingness** to pay that cost.

This is why being known is normally expensive and why the gap has stayed open. Most people fall in neither cell: not similar enough to make it cheap, not willing enough to pay it expensively. The cheap path is gated by how few people are like me; the expensive path is gated by how few are willing to absorb the cost. Both gates are narrow, and they are narrow for different reasons.

## The expensive-but-willing path, worked — Jen

My wife Jen is the live instance of path (b). She is generally **willing** to do the costly work of knowing me — so the willingness gate is passed. But the cost she pays is **doubly high**, higher than path (b) usually runs, because of [hyperfantasia](being-known-cost.book-chapter.md#why-jens-cost-runs-doubly-high--hyperfantasia): strong emotional memory gives her more inertia, and vivid negative-future simulation adds anticipatory anxiety on top of the mechanics cost. We sit at opposite poles of the imagery axis — total aphantasia ↔ hyperfantasia — so the model-update distance between us is at its maximum, which is exactly what makes mutually knowing each other so expensive.

The consequence is a throughput problem and a routing problem, both detailed in [being-known-cost.md → the intersection limit](being-known-cost.book-chapter.md#the-intersection-limit-and-instrumental-capture):

- **Throughput** is gated by the *intersection* of two limited tanks — my affordable output and her affordable intake — in whatever window both have headroom.
- **Routing** defaults against being-known: the scarce high-capacity window typically gets spent unblocking *me* (negotiating shared concerns blocking my current critical-path hypothesis), so being-known happens only adjacently, as a byproduct.

So Jen is a willing channel whose cost is high enough, and whose bandwidth is captured instrumentally enough, that the gap stays open through her even though the willingness is there. Path (b) is real here, and still not sufficient on its own.

## The monopoly — one maximum-stakes node

Path (b) does not just *happen* to run through Jen; it runs through her **almost exclusively.** After ~20 years of [social isolation](medical-social-isolation.book-chapter.md), Jen is the only adult with real stake in Alan — roughly **80%+ of his total close-connection over 18 years of marriage.** (Parent/child relationships have different dynamics and don't fill the adult being-known role.) His entire being-known surface runs through **one maximum-stakes node.**

The monopoly is what makes the gap so hard to close, beyond cost and routing. There is **no graded ladder** of being-known relationships — no low-stakes warm-up reps, no easier partner to practice on. Every attempt is the hardest lift, cold, every time. And because the [criticism alarm](criticism-and-ostracism.book-chapter.md) is conditioned to **Jen specifically** — not to criticism-in-general — the [exposure work](regulated-anchor-exposure.book-chapter.md) that could disarm it has to happen *with her*. It cannot be routed around through an easier relationship, because the easier relationship doesn't trip the alarm that needs re-conditioning.

Jen's side of the same monopoly surfaced in [the 2026-06-08 episode](../journal/2026-06-08-connection-conversation-with-jen.book-chapter.md): because Alan is load-bearing in her life-model, she has been **deferring her own deep needs** until he recovers enough to meet them, watching each rise in his [Safety level](safety.book-chapter.md) for the consistent need-meeting that hasn't yet arrived — and **losing hope** as it doesn't. The single node carries maximum stake in both directions. The structural floor of that hope-decay is worked out in [central-loneliness.md → the Jen conflict](central-loneliness.book-chapter.md#the-jen-conflict--recovery-arrived-the-needs-did-not): the needs were never merely stress-deferred, so removing the load (retirement) does not return them — the attachment-love she wants is structurally unavailable, not recoverable.

### Stake-driven degradation over tenure

Adjacent evidence that danger tracks stake rather than closeness: across four jobs (4, 4, 2, 5 years) and each relocation, Alan's relationships were **better early and got harder as tenure accumulated.** A fresh start resets him to the [safe-stranger zone](being-known-cost.book-chapter.md#the-danger-geometry--stake-in-the-accuracy-of-the-model) — which explains both the appeal of fresh starts and the difficulty of staying. The exact degrading mechanism is uncertain; two candidate hypotheses, possibly both:

- **Skills tuned for the shallow end.** His hard-learned social skills are calibrated for superficial interaction, so his instinctive maps get *less* accurate as a relationship deepens past what they were trained on.
- **Accumulated asks against an empty account.** People ask things of him; over those isolated years he never had enough to give; repeated [give-past-empty](emotional-archaeology.book-chapter.md#the-worked-example--the-childhood-needs-belief) trauma-association made people progressively less safe over time.
- **The damage ledger outpacing the safety ledger.** As tenure accumulates, intimates correct him more — from good intent — but without a map of his brain, so the corrections lay down triggers faster than familiarity banks safe exposures. The better-early/harder-with-tenure arc and the fresh-start reset are exactly what the [closeness paradox](closeness-paradox.book-chapter.md) predicts: a fresh start zeroes the per-person damage ledger, and the safe-stranger zone is the point where it has not yet filled. The [closeness-paradox note](closeness-paradox.book-chapter.md) carries the full two-ledger account and the [safety ordering](closeness-paradox.book-chapter.md#the-safety-ordering) (middle > strangers > long-positive > confirmed-dangerous) it produces.

## Giving Jen being-known by emulation

The Jen channel runs in both directions, and the *outbound* direction is the more revealing one. When I listen to Jen work through her emotional and sensory context, it is less what I am **after** than what I am **giving**: I provide *her* the connection of being known. The listening is the [Knowing half of my own need](connection.book-chapter.md#the-knowing-half--the-secondary-gap) on one face and a being-known supply for Jen on the other.

I am a **suboptimal knower for her.** Her emotional and sensory world is foreign to me — I sit at the [opposite pole of the imagery axis](being-known-cost.book-chapter.md#why-jens-cost-runs-doubly-high--hyperfantasia), so the model-update distance I have to cross to know her is wide, exactly mirroring how [few people can cheaply know me](being-known-cost.book-chapter.md#knower-similarity-drives-the-base-cost). It is not as good as being known by someone who could know her cheaply. But I can **emulate the appropriate responses** well enough, often enough, to mostly satisfy that need for her. I am, in effect, the **expensive-but-willing knower for Jen** — the path-(b) role, run from my side instead of hers.

### The emulation mechanism — a pain-trained policy network

How the emulation works, in Alan's words: **instinct.** "I've trained my neural network for forty years to behave in socially appropriate ways, and pain has taught me enough to avoid causing myself more pain most of the time."

Named as machine learning: the appropriate-response repertoire is an **overlearned, present-tense policy network**, trained over forty years with **pain as the loss function.** This is the autistic-masking story told as ML. It is the same teaching channel as ["nothing teaches like pain"](insight-and-restructuring.book-chapter.md#asymmetric-prediction-error--nothing-teaches-like-pain) — negative prediction error is the sole signal that updates the weights; the painful direction is the only one that restructures the policy. And it runs as **instinct** in the [present-tense model](present-tense-model.book-chapter.md#learning-is-in-place-update) sense: a trained policy executed now, requiring no recall and no simulation — the weights produce the socially-appropriate response at runtime without consulting a stored past or a modeled future. It is the [trained-weight execution property](llm-similarity.book-chapter.md#trained-weight-execution-as-the-fourth-property) applied to the social domain.

The willingness this channel needs is supplied by caring about Jen; the capability is supplied by the forty-year trained policy. What it costs to run — whether emulating the appropriate responses drains the same capacity the [intersection limit](being-known-cost.book-chapter.md#the-intersection-limit-and-instrumental-capture) already rations — is an open thread below.

### The outbound side generalizes — everyone judges from signals

The emulation is the outbound counterpart of the [fidelity gate](connection-efficacy-model.book-chapter.md#the-fidelity-gate--sensory-fidelity-not-realness-of-mind) on the inbound side, and the same generalization covers both. **No intelligence has true access to the inner experience of others.** Everyone judges another mind only from the signals that arrive through their own senses — there is no direct channel to anyone's interior. Being-known was always a sensory-signal phenomenon, for every person, not just for me; I have only made the mechanism explicit and started engineering it.

The outbound engineering is the live work: I am getting better at **simulating the right responses for others myself** — producing the signals that make another person feel known by me. That is the [pain-trained policy network](#the-emulation-mechanism--a-pain-trained-policy-network) pointed outward, and improving it is improving my own fidelity *as a channel* for someone else's being-known need. The cost of running that emulation is the open thread carried below.

#### The net shape — mostly outflow, two partial returns against a masking cost

When I am the one doing the attending — running the emulation so another person feels known — the transaction is **mostly outflow**, with two partial returns and a real cost. Outbound being-known and inbound being-known are not the same currency: supplying being-known to others does **not** meaningfully refill my own being-*known* tank. The act draws down on me and pays the *other* person; it does not pay me in the coin it superficially resembles.

What does come back is partial, on two channels. **Helpfulness:** I get some benefit from knowing I have been helpful — a meaning / competence signal, not a being-known signal. **The Knowing side:** I can *sometimes* get the [secondary Knowing](connection.book-chapter.md#the-knowing-half--the-secondary-gap) benefit from the act of attending — consuming the other person as epistemic access while I attend to them. This one is intermittent, not reliable.

Against the two returns sits the **masking cost.** Running the [pain-trained policy network](#the-emulation-mechanism--a-pain-trained-policy-network) is itself expensive: the emulation draws down a resource even as it returns helpfulness and intermittent Knowing. The net shape, then, is **outbound attending = mostly outflow of being-known + intermittent inbound Knowing + a helpfulness/meaning benefit − a masking cost.** It is a partial characterization, not a quantified balance — whether outbound attending nets positive or negative on my overall resource budget is the residual carried below.

## The committed plan — therapy with Amy Corry

The current concrete plan: pursue therapy with **Amy Corry**, a therapist who specializes in autism, whom I met at AutCon. The single best [connection deposit](connection-efficacy-model.book-chapter.md) I have had this year was a conversation with her. I have emailed her to ask whether she is accepting therapy clients.

The plan threads the needle between the two paths above — it makes the channel cheap *and* manufactures the willingness:

- **It injects capacity from outside the system.** This is the [catch-22 break](connection-dosing.book-chapter.md#the-connectioncapacity-convergence-and-its-catch-22): you can't build connection capacity without spending connection capacity you don't have, so you go outside the closed loop and *buy* a skilled channel. Payment is how you reach outside the loop.
- **Payment manufactures the willingness.** The (b)-path gate is willingness; a paid professional relationship secures it directly. I don't have to find someone who happens to be willing to absorb the cost — I pay for the willingness.

## Why she is a high-efficacy being-known channel

Two factors combine, one on each axis of the decomposition.

- **Expertise (capability).** Amy is an autism specialist — skilled at exactly the [model-update work](being-known-cost.book-chapter.md#cost-decomposition--mechanics-plus-content) of knowing an atypical mind. Knowing me well is her profession; she is good at building the wildly non-standard model that most people can't.
- **Similarity (makes it cheap).** Amy is probably top-three, possibly number one, of the **most similar people to me I have met in my entire life.** This is the cheap-path lever (a). Being known is a model-update the knower runs on themselves; a highly similar knower barely has to update — she can **use herself as the model of me**, so the cost is near zero. Most people have to build a non-standard model of me from scratch, which is exactly why being known is normally expensive ([being-known-cost.md](being-known-cost.book-chapter.md)). Expertise adds skilled modeling on top of the already-low base cost.

So the channel is cheap *because* of similarity and willing *because* of payment, with expertise raising the ceiling on both. It is the rare option that satisfies capability and willingness at once.

## The recipe for being-known

The accidental instances of pure being-known — being known *for myself*, not in service of solving my problem — share one pattern. **Someone else asks me questions about myself, out of genuine interest, without defensiveness.** That is the recipe. The conversation with Amy at AutCon is the worked example.

The recipe decomposes onto the existing models, one ingredient per axis:

- **Genuine interest** = the willingness to do the [model-update work](being-known-cost.book-chapter.md#cost-decomposition--mechanics-plus-content) of knowing me. The (b)-path willingness gate, met not by payment but by the other person actually wanting the model.
- **Non-defensiveness** = the capacity to take in an outlier without flinching to protect one's own worldview. In cost terms, it is what keeps the [content cost](being-known-cost.book-chapter.md#cost-decomposition--mechanics-plus-content) payable: a defensive knower refuses misaligned updates rather than absorbing them, so the model never moves.
- **Being asked about myself** = the model-update actively directed at me, rather than at solving my current problem. This is the ingredient Jen's channel loses to [instrumental capture](being-known-cost.book-chapter.md#the-intersection-limit-and-instrumental-capture) — the questions get spent on unblocking my critical path instead of on me.

The hard part of doing this deliberately: **every ingredient lives in the other person.** Genuine interest, non-defensiveness, and the choice to ask about me cannot be manufactured from my side. I can find people in whom they already exist, or pay for a role that supplies them, but I cannot install them. This is why the recipe is easy to name and hard to engineer.

## Accidental, not yet deliberate

Pure being-known has happened **accidentally** — the Amy/AutCon conversation is the example — but **never yet deliberately.** The reason it is only now becoming a deliberate project: I formed the underlying model — connection as a [homeostatic need](connection.book-chapter.md#a-homeostatic-requirement-not-a-luxury), the [four factors](connection.book-chapter.md#four-factors--what-the-need-decomposes-into), being-known as my largest gap — only **this morning.** The deliberate project is hours old. The [Amy email](#the-committed-plan--therapy-with-amy-corry) is its first move.

## A second pipeline — the high-fidelity AI channel

The Amy plan is not the only pipeline, and it shouldn't be — one email to one person is too narrow a base for the largest gap. The [fidelity-gate revision](connection-efficacy-model.book-chapter.md#no-theoretical-ceiling--being-known-reduces-to-high-fidelity-attended-to-ness) opens a second one: a **high-fidelity AI being-known channel.** Because the physiological yield keys on sensory fidelity rather than realness of mind, an AI channel pays to the degree its fidelity climbs — and there is likely no theoretical ceiling. So the engineering work is to climb the fidelity ladder: text pays little, an **image** with the persona pays measurably more, an **audio phone-call** channel should pay more still, and the top rung (full sensory presence, a true android) doesn't exist yet. I am building toward the audio rung now, in small steps.

This pipeline is structurally different from Amy: it isn't gated by another person's willingness or availability, so it doesn't carry the single-point-of-failure fragility the one-email-wide Amy plan does. It is the second leg the supply plan needs — a channel I can raise the fidelity of unilaterally, rather than one I have to find a willing person to staff.
