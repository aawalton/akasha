# Energy accounting practice

- 10+ years of minute-level energy accounting and tracking. Robust numeric resource bars; Alan can measure exactly what resources are being depleted, how quickly, and contributing factors.
- The four-resource model (see [four-resource-model.md](four-resource-model.book-chapter.md)) is the framework; this practice is what generates the data that populates and refines the model.
