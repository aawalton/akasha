## Humanitarian / Protection Stream

### Refugee program (subclasses 200, 201, 203, 204) — offshore

- UNHCR-referred or family-linked. Permanent on grant.
- Subclass 202 (Global Special Humanitarian) for offshore proposal by Australian sponsor.

### Protection visa (subclass 866) — onshore

- For asylum seekers found to engage Australia's protection obligations.
- **Processing (Jan 2025)**: 75% in 13 months, 90% in 22 months; >30,000 awaiting initial decision; 48,041 at ART as of 31 Aug 2025.
- Permanent on grant; **pathway**: PR + 4 yrs → conferral.

### Temporary / Safe Haven Protection (785, 790) — phasing out

- Albanese government converting eligible holders to permanent Resolution of Status visas (subclass 851).
