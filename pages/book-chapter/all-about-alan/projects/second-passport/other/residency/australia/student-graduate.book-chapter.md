## Student / Graduate Stream

### Student visa (subclass 500)

- **23 March 2024**: Genuine Student (GS) test replaced the Genuine Temporary Entrant (GTE) test.
- **23 March 2024**: English-language test scores valid only 1 year (was 3); 485 graduate threshold raised to IELTS 6.5 overall (5.5 each band).
- Sector caps and visa fee increases (now AUD 1,600+) introduced through 2024–25.

### Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485)

- **Major restrictions effective March–July 2024**:
  - **1 July 2024**: visa duration tied to qualification (bachelor/master coursework: 2 yrs; master research/PhD: 3 yrs; diploma/trade: 18 mo).
  - **Age cap lowered to 35** (from 50).
  - **Onshore re-application to subclass 500 blocked** from 1 July 2024.
- Not a PR visa; bridge to 482/186/189 pathways.
