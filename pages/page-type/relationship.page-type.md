# Definition

- **Relationship** — one person in Alan's life, and how close they stand.

# Design

A relationship is named by the person's own name, and carries no slug of its own.

Four ratings stand apart: what this person changes, how much Alan wants them, how connected they already are, and what he means to keep putting in.
