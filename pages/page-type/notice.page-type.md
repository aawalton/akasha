# Definition

- **Notice** — message text written ahead of time and asked for by name.

# Design

A notice arrives as a turn of its own.

# Rules

## Nothing To Wait For

**Give a seat something to do now in a notice, never something to wait for.**

A seat cannot wait inside a turn, so waiting ends it idle and something else must start it again.

Naming a risk is not telling a seat to wait.

'Shortly', 'later' and 'hold off' all say wait.
