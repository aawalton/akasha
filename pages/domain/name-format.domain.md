# Definition

- **Name format** — how a name's parts are joined and capitalized.

# Principles

## Same Words Everywhere

**Give a name the same words in every place, in the format that place uses.**

The words carry the meaning; the format belongs to the place.

Change a name's format where it crosses over.

Never change its words to fit a format.
