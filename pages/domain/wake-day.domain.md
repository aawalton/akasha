# Definition

- **Wake day** — which day something falls in, counted from the moment Alan wakes.

# Design

The wake day sits inside the ESO day, and only says where inside it the boundary falls.

The boundaries are worked out when they are read, from the sleep sessions as they stand, and stored nowhere.

The wake day is derived in one place, and a second derivation is a second answer.

A day whose waking cannot be found starts where its ESO day starts.
