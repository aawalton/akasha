# Definition

- **Persona points** — a measure of the attention Alan gave one part of his life.

# Design

Attention counts where it made that part of Alan's life better, whoever did the work.

The stoplight built from a persona's points is what prompts Alan.

A change to a persona's green day points rescores her history as far back as her stored days reach.

A persona's daily points and her running total read the same source, and for some sources the total is the sum of the days.

A persona's running total only ever rises, unless a rebuild rewrites it downward.

A day's color is computed, never stored.

# Intent

A stored day agrees with what its recipe computes, for every persona at once.

A persona's points compute the same wherever the command is run from.

Nothing tells Alan a persona has no figure while something else shows him one.

No figure Alan reads is arrived at by counting files in a directory.

Every persona standing under a value can move that value's light.
