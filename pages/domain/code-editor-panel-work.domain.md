# Definition

- **Code editor panel work** — the panel drawing the work as a tree of initiatives.

# Design

The panel's roots are the initiatives standing under no other, alphabetical by slug.

A row with a seat of its own is drawn in that seat's color.

A row carries the highest color of the rows beneath it, green over blue over yellow over the rest.

A color the ranking does not name sits below every color it does.

# Intent

Every row in the panel is an initiative.
