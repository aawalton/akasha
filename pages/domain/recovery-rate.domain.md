# Definition

- **Recovery rate** — how much stress capacity an hour of an activity gives back.

# Design

A rate is matched by the word Alan writes in a session title.

A session naming several recovery activities credits at the best rate, never at their sum.

# Intent

A session naming several recovery activities credits at the sum of their rates.
