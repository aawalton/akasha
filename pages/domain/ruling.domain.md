# Definition

- **Ruling** — an instrument's answer that decides an act.

# Intent

Every ruling has a stated cost budget it must be given inside.

A run that goes past its budget refuses, rather than giving a late ruling.

Where a ruling is given settles the budget it must meet.

Every instrument giving a ruling has a measured cost against its budget.
