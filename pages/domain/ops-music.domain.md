# Definition

- **Ops music** — the commands that work Alan's Spotify playback and the music pages that keep his listening.

# Design

A multi-track set is handed to Spotify's own queue rather than advanced from here.

Playback and the listening record come from Spotify; the catalogue of artists and songs comes from MusicBrainz.
