# Definition

- **Seat assignment on-call** — a seat being there for its principal, whether or not they are working.

# Design

A seat of either mode can hold an on-call assignment.

Every seat started in interactive mode holds an on-call assignment from Alan.

A seat holding an on-call assignment is started by its principal.

An on-call assignment has no work to finish: it ends only when ended by its principal or Alan.
