# Definition

- **World invariant** — what is true in a world.

# Design

A sketch becomes lore when a story has told it.

# Rules

## Lore Stands

**Change lore only at Alan's asking; a sketch is yours to change.**

A chapter somebody read goes false when its lore changes, and nothing re-reads it to catch that.

New lore that makes a chapter false is a change.

Take a clash between two pieces of lore to Alan.
