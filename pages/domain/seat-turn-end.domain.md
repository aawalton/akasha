# Definition

- **Seat turn end** — a turn ending in a seat.

# Design

A turn ending does not itself stop the seat, and the halt guard may refuse a headless turn end.

The words a headless seat ends a turn with start nothing.

An on-call turn end is read; every other is decided from state, with no model called.

A turn end not on-call is refused only where dispatched work stands with nothing arranged to return to it.

A reading that cannot settle allows the turn end; a state that cannot be read refuses it.
