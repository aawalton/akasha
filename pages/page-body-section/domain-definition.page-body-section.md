# Definition

- **Domain definition** — the sentence naming what a domain's concern is.

# Design

A Definition section holds one bullet and nothing else.

A Definition section holds no clause saying what the thing is for, why it is worth having, or where it sits.

A Definition names one concern. Where a second is needed to cover the area, the area is more than one domain.

A fact true of every sibling stands on the parent's line rather than on each sibling's own line. What a domain is about is the exception: it stays on the domain's own line, even where every sibling is about it too.
