# Definition

- **Domain design** — a domain invariant that holds now.

# Design

A Design section holds no instruction to a seat. It takes only an invariant a reader gets wrong, in these kinds and no others.

- **Departure** — a decision a reader would not guess right; knowing it stops them undoing it.
- **Absence** — something the domain deliberately leaves out; knowing it stops a reader adding it.
- **Constraint** — a limit from outside the domain; knowing it stops a reader asking for the impossible.

A Design entry carries no reason for itself.
