Alan's read (first): a survival-and-celebration song; he caught the dissociation himself this time — 'I saw my life in a stranger's face... and it was mine.' Added: the chorus is the barest possible declaration ('I'm still breathing / I'm alive') because survival needs no ornament, only the fact; the verses carry the cost (thunderstorm, the demons, taught to cry in your pillow). The crucial distinction is Alan's own — the song's 'alive' is the FLOOR (not-dead, still breathing); he is now moving from that survival-alive toward actually FEELING alive again (this year). He is living past where the song ends — the verse Sia's narrator never reaches.
