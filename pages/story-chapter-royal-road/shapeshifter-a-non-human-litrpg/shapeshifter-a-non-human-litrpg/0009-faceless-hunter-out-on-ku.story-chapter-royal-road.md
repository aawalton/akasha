The day is finally here.

Not only is this the first book I've written since Reborn as a Demonic Tree started three years ago, but it was also almost dropped. But after trying to start a dozen other projects, I came back to this one, reworked it, and it turned out way better than I expected. I really enjoy writing it, and I've enjoyed the story's direction so far. Regardless of the book's performance on Amazon, I will bring it to a satisfying conclusion either way. However, if the series does very well, I will happily write it as a longer web serial, like Reborn as a Demonic Tree, with hundreds of chapters, which would be awesome.

So, I ask for your help, as this is my first time self-publishing, meaning I don't have a publisher to push the launch. Not to mention Amazon is SUPER broken right now, with even the biggest of books barely getting any ratings through their new AI detection system that barely works. This means that if I get one bad rating at the start, it could really affect the launch, so any ratings would be highly appreciated. If you can't buy or rate the book, there are also two Reddit threads linked below that could use some love!
