Bros!!! This is big.

Well, it's been a moment. Hope you've all been well. Even if not, the fist is always there for you.

I've had, well, a lot of life since then, mostly good, a little bad. Things have changed since the Road to Mastery days, both personally and professionally, but it's nice to take a step back every once in a while. This is a chance for me to return to that early era, where Road to Mastery was my life, and see it through the lens of my slightly more experienced self. It's like my child came to visit, now older and married with kids of its own. Phew.

Anyway, the webcomic is out. I hope you'll give it a shot. It's beautiful, and you can relive the adventures of Jack and Brock through the all-new art of Bayi Studios.
