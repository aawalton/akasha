Hello all. I felt this was important enough to post as an additional chapter rather than just an author note, going into the holidays here. Forge of Destiny has been more successful than I could have ever believed back when I first started writing it. It has been great, seeing so many people read and comment and enjoy the story thus far. As those of you who keep an eye on the author notes know, I've had self published volumes of the story available as books for a long time now, but the time has come to take the next step here. Forge of Destiny has gotten a publisher, and the books will soon be going through a relaunch! More than that, the first three books will be receiving a full retail release in the next year or so.

But that does mean I will have to go through something I held off on much longer than most here, and that is stubbing. For now the first three books of content will be getting stubbed starting in the first week of the new year, the prologues will remain up, but past that will only be available in e book and audiobook format. Thank you all for reading, and I hope you'll continue to do so, and consider supporting me by doing your next reread on kindle, come January 20th.
