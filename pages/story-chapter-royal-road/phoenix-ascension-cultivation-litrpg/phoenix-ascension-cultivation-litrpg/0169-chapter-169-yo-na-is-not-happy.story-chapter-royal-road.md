The dragon towered over the arena, and I realised it must have been a little overkill, but oh well.

It let out a surprisingly realistic roar and dove down toward Hwei Badai.

He let out a scream— the first sound he’d made the entire tournament, but it quickly got drowned out by the crackling of electricity.

I could already see Meng Mao slapping his own face, and wanting to leave and have nothing to do with me anymore.

I chuckled a little at the thought.

The shadows stopped grabbing onto me, and actually started running away. Who would have figured that shadows were afraid of lightning?

I had no idea; I didn’t get to fight that many shadow cultivators. Most of them died or gave up for some reason.

Anyway, the Lightning Dragon’s main target, Hwei Badai, was…Lying on the ground, completely paralyzed. His muscles twitched every so often, though he wasn’t looking all that well.

It took the elder a good moment or two to realise what had happened, and then he finally announced me as the winner.

There was no point in holding back anymore. Hwei Badai was a good fighter, but I was growing tired of these children’s games. Peng Li would be no different.

Also,o everyone already knew I could use lightning,g so summoning a dragon was the only right solution in such a scenario.

I stepped off the stage and let the staff handle the paralyzed guy. He would be fine, but wouldn’t be able to move for a short while.

With that, I wasn’t sure when the next match would start. It could be that they would wait until the next day or just a few hours.

Either way, I really had nothing better to do than to meditate and think. As a result, I went and sat next to Meng Mao, who’d grown numb to my antics.

It didn’t take long for me to start the meditation, and the first thing I wanted to do was to check up on the sword. It felt like it was starting to become active again.

From what I could tell, there was nothing out of the ordinary, though I couldn’t help being a little concerned.

I could barely control it as it was… No, I couldn’t control it at all. It had a mind of its own, and my current cultivation level was far too low.

But it didn’t look like it was going to do anything for the moment. My Qi was, sadly, merely suppressing it. Refining the sword was a matter for future me.

Why did I have to go and pick up a sentient sword? Could the Little Emperor help me refine it, though? It was living herent-free and eating my resources.

I didn’t dare try that though. I needed to get to a Foundation Establishment level of cultivation; only then would I attempt that, since I needed the assurance.

Now that I’d thought about the Little Emperor, I couldn’t help but wonder where Yo Na was. I thought she was supposed to come… to… the Golden Arch… City…

And I was not in the city…

I could only hope that Shu Wao Tu and Shu Xu Ning were able to keep her happy… I didn’t want to think about what an angry oversized cat in human form could do.

Yo Na was not happy.

It hasn’t been long since they’d reached Golden Arch City, a name that she found to be rather awful, but could see why it was chosen. She did not like it, but she could tolerate it.

The city, she liked even less. It was busy, loud, cramped, hot, smelly, and any and all bad adjectives she could think of. It was… unfit for her.

Currently, she was dressed in the robe that the bird had given her, and they had not had any more issues arriving in the city. Unfortunately, she didn’t know how to find that stupid bird.

Whatever it was that she’d done last time didn’t happen again, and since she could barely read anything, she didn’t know what that even was.

As a result, they were at the mercy of time.

The Shu brothers were just as lost as she was, but Shang Hue and Xing Ya Chen, possibly already used to city life, quickly gathered information while looking out for rooms to rent.

Apparently, something huge happened some time ago. A dragon was in the sky, and the prominent Guo clan had fallen out of favor and had mostly lost all of their assets. That was… not something she cared about, but somehow she could smell the stink of that bird.

She had to have had a role there. Everywhere she went, she caused trouble!

Of course, she couldn’t know for sure, but there was no way she wasn’t involved.

She kept that to herself, though; there was no reason for the others to know.

Xing Ya Chen, with the help of Shang Hue, managed to snag a pretty nice place for the three… four? How many were they again?

One, two, three, four, five…

So there were five of them, and they got two rooms at a nice inn, separating them by gender.

Although Yo Na didn’t really care about gender or any of that stuff, humans were humans, and she was Yo Na.

Although she couldn’t say she hated the comfort of a nice bed, warm food and water, and just how relaxing baths were.

But she was still a Spirit Beast; she had to respect herself!

Even in the comfort of the room, a thought bothered her.

Just what is happening to my baby?

She’d never been away from him for so long, but she knew that she had no choice. Through their connection, though, she could feel that he was changing. Yo Na didn’t know why yet, but she was worried.

Realistically, she knew that there would be no issues, even if the Phoenix was in trouble, but deep inside, she worried.

Why did you have to go away, you bastard of a husband? She cursed him inwardly, though she knew someone like him had important matters to attend to.

One idea did strike her, though, since the Phoenix didn’t seem to have any parents either, although she was pretty young.

Maybe the reason her husband had to leave was also the reason her parents weren’t present?

What could possibly be happening to warrant such measures?

She could only wonder, as despite how much she boasted, she wasn’t anywhere near strong enough to be privy to such matters.

The biggest issue she currently had was finding that damned Phoenix, though. Where in the world could she possibly be, and why hasn’t she contacted her yet?

As it turned out, they were not going to wait until tomorrow for the last fight. That was good, since I didn’t know what to do with myself anymore.

I’d literally looked deep inside myself only to discover that I still didn’t have any soil, and that I was massively bottlenecked!

Now I was getting angry.

A few hours had passed since I’d won against Hwei Badai, and he was nowhere to be seen. He was fine now, probably, and just didn’t want to show himself.

I had to say it was pretty impressive how he managed to keep his misty veil on despite being paralyzed and beaten so badly.

He definitely had some sort of secret, but that was of no importance to me unless he had soil.

Heavens, whoever came up with this cultivation method should get struck by lightning. I could have just used a normal technique… except the only one I remembered was the Ghost Qi Technique, and I didn’t want to become a ghost!

Whatever, I just had to keep going and get that stupid soil. How could none of these shops have any good soil? Damn it.

“Miss Xiaolan?” The elder cleared his throat. I must have gotten lost in thought in the middle of the arena.

“Ah, my apologies.” I bowed.

Nodding, he instructed us to bow to each other once again, to the audience, and all the other stuff.

Then the fight began.

Or so I thought, but Peng Li was not doing anything.

Did he realise it was hopeless? Was he going to surrender? Was he going to give me a show with that strange technique of his?

I doubted he could do anything to me even with that technique.

But he made it to the top two; he should be proud of himself!

I wasn’t going to show him any mercy. I had to walk North for a long time after all…

Hwei Badai didn’t know what was happening outside, nor did he give a rat’s ass. He was tired, and he could barely believe that he’d gotten defeated like that.

He’d never felt so weak, so helpless, so humiliated! No matter what he did, Hwei Badai could do no damage to that woman. She was toying with him.

TOYING!

He had never been so humiliated in his life. How could someone like that be so strong at Qi Condensation? He’d never met anyone stronger than him at the same realm.

Besides anger and humiliation, there was one thing that he also felt. Unwillingness.

He was unwilling to accept that someone like that was stronger than him.

Just as he was about to rush out of the room, a voice called out to him in his head.

“Settle down, boy!” It sounded ancient, time having taken its toll on him. Still, Hwei Badai wasn’t surprised.

The voice inside his head was his master, after all.

“Old Man Yao…”

“Boy, you’re losing your head just because of a woman? You still got third place. Although that is going to make it harder for my plan to be accomplished. This minor realm is all it is, minor. Don’t lose your head over it.”

He made some good points, but Hwei Badai was still unwilling to accept such a result. After all, he’d been undefeated since he was young, thanks in part to the spirit of the old man he’d found in a ring, but also because of his hard work and perseverance.

Back in his clan, he was nothing but a cripple, but he never stopped training, never stopped trying. He’d paid back all the humiliation he’d gotten from his fellow clansmena hundredfoldd, and was now trying to forge his own path.

Since he had nothing and had come from nothing, even this realm his master called a minor would help advance him greatly.

Although the ranking only affected the time at which you could enter the realm, it made all the difference. That was at least based on his research.

“I know. I… I was harsh. Did you figure out anything about her?”

“Hehehe…” Old Man Yao chuckled in Hwei Badai’s head. “She is no simple person. If I were to tell you that she wasn’t even using ten percent of her strength against you, would you believe me?”

“What?! How can that be? She’s only at the Qi Condensation stage; if she toyed with me that easily at the same realm…”

“Only! Ha! He says only! Boy, the difference between the two of you is like the distance between heaven and earth. I don’t know how someone from this backwater continent managed it, but I will tell you one secret I am sure of. She has reached the peak of Qi Condensation! Broke past the ninth stage and achieved the tenth!”

“…What?!”
