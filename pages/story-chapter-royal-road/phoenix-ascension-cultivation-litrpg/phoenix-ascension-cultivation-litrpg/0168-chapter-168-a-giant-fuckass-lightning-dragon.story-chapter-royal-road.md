Peng Li made his way out of the outer sect into the inner sect through a secret passage. It was an exit meant to be used in the worst-case scenario, but he had no choice and had to take it.

He couldn’t allow other people to see him in such a state.

Blood dripped down the corner of his lips, staining his black hanfu. The Qi of the Reaper Sect ran rampant through his body. He hadn’t claimed a life after using that technique, so the backlash was intense.

Fucking Jiang twins and your stupid technique. He cursed inwardly, blaming his opponent instead of his lack of power.

If he were stronger, he could have defeated Jiang Ji without the use of such a technique, but he wasn’t, and he couldn’t see it. He refused to see his weakness.

Footsteps approached from the direction of the exit, and he knew exactly who it was.

The only person who knew about his secret escape plans was his grandfather and the patriarch of the Reaper Sect, Peng Long.

There was not a hint of compassion on his face. He looked at Peng Li with distaste, even.

“You should not have used that technique.” He scolded the boy, his voice harsh.

“There was no other way to win, I-”

“If you can’t use normal techniques to beat such a weakling, then you should have just lost!” He yelled, berating him. “You are a disgrace. To think I used so many resources on you, and for what?”

“I didn’t have enough time to train, I-”

“WRONG!” His scream almost pierced through the tunnel into the outside world, but he kept it contained. “You had plenty of time to train. You’ve trained all your life. So what if your cultivation was held back, if your Qi was tainted? You used the same techniques every day, went on missions, and broke your body over and over. And this is what you show me? I have given you that technique for emergencies. I told you that for you to use that technique, a life must be given in return. It is the core of the Reaper Sect; that is where the REAPER comes from. What did I tell you would happen if you didn’t sacrifice a life to it?”

“That I would die or have my cultivation crippled.”

“And why did you use it?!”

“I didn’t want to lose!” Peng Li screamed, his mouth filled with blood. “I couldn’t lose.”

“Your ego and your pride have caused you to lose sight of the true goal.” Peng Long sighed and waved his hand, dragging someone along from the other end of the tunnel.

It was a middle-aged servant, his eyes filled with panic. His legs shook as he stared at the duo, not understanding what was going on.

“Kill him.” Peng Long ordered, and Peng Li hesitated.

“But-”

“KILL HIM!” He barked out, leaving no room for negotiation. “Or I will kill you with my own two hands.”

His grandfather’s savagery made Peng Li shake. He’d never taken a life before. Even on sect missions, he rarely had to fight someone to the death.

The concept of killing someone… It irked him.

But it was the path he chose to walk down. This was the consequence of his actions.

He channeled Qi through his fist, calling upon the Reaper Shadow’s gluttony, and punched the servant, ending his life as the man’s head exploded like a crushed watermelon.

The pressure circulating inside Peng Li’s body gradually vanished, and a strange sort of… energy coursed through him.

“Grandfather-”

“Do not speak of it. I will explain things later; for now you must return and rest.”

“I-” His eyes ran toward the corpse of the elder. Peng Long waved his hand, and a dark Qi shot toward the corpse, enveloping it completely.

Then, as it faded, so did the corpse, blood,d and everything that might have pointed to a crime having taken place there.

Just like that, that man’s life was snuffed out, his body erased, ed and probably any trace of his existence, too, would go away.

“Go.”

“Yes, grandfather.”

He turned back the other way, the strange energy disappearing into his dantian.

He felt like he no longer had a choice in the matter. He had to win; he had to take first place, and he had to conquer the Four Corner Sword Realm no matter what.

After eating some very enjoyable food, I went to a remote area and sat down. Not even cross-legged, I just sat down. This day was really beginning to annoy me.

The food was nice, but I didn’t get any gifts, and now we had to wait for Peng Li to deal with the costs of whatever it was that he’d used.

Seriously, how inconsiderate of him. There was no way the Reaper Sect took this long of a break for any other reason.

I closed my eyes and waited, my head empty. I even began to yawn.

However, I couldn’t even relax properly, as I sensed a sudden… change.

It was minuscule, but the experience of one and a tenth lifetimes told me that something fishy just happened.

Peng Li made himself known again, reappearing from who knows where, but something was different about him.

His aura had become… sharper. Whatever happened after using that technique must have messed with his head.

But the biggest change that I’d noticed was somewhere around his dantian, as I recognised an unorthodox technique when I saw it.

Squinting my eyes, I returned to my sleeping position. What people did with souls wasn’t my problem.

Although it was a scummy practice.

Regardless, I wasn’t going to be the righteous person to scream ‘SOUL EATER’. I was pretty sure the entirety of the Reaper Sect was using souls for their cultivation, although Peng Li clearly didn’t know what to do with it.

Well, since he was back, then the fights could resume at least. There were only three more until the grand winner would be decided.

Three more fights, and I would take part in two of them. I knew that much for sure, unless something happened, but that was about as likely as lightning striking.

I looked up at the sky just to be sure. I did have a gluttonous sword in my dantian.

Alright, everything seemed fine.

I returned to my nap. It was a nice day out, so might as well enjoy it, right?

I hoped that I wouldn’t be called for the next fight.

And it seemed like the heavens were kind to me, as the next fighters were number eight Fah Xing and number twelve Peng Li.

Honestly, I didn’t even need to look at the match to know the outcome.Even if Peng Li didn’t use his technique, Fah Xing didn’t stand a chance against him. I’d seen him fight, and I knew what he was capable of.

I decided to just take a proper nap, hoping that the match would go on for a while.

I sighed, having to stand up. The announcehad r already called my name a few times, so I had no choice.

Looking at where I was… I could drop right down on the arena. That would be a little cool…

I decided to take the stairs instead; it was much better.

The silence was a little awkward, if I had to be honest, and there were far too many gazes on me.

But I made my way onto the stage and in front of Hwei Badai.

He looked as mysteriously creepy as ever.

After doing all that bowing bullshit, the elder announced the start of the fight.

Hwei Badai attacked immediately, his entire being becoming one with the shadows. He knew very well that he had to do something crazy to beat me.

I was wondering just how crazy it would get. Not that anything would faze me, but still.

He decided to go with the basic attacks at first, like slashing at me with shadow daggers and weaving in and out of the shadows. A good tactic, but not enough.

I dodged every attack at the very last minute, as I already knew what shadow techniques could do.

I couldn’t read Hwei Badai’s face, but he looked rather upset.

All the while, I was pondering whether or not I should end the fight early. I could, and it would save me some trouble. It wasn’t like I could staylow-keyy anymore, so did it really matter?

Although seeing him try so badly to hit me did give me a sense of pity.

Yeah, it was best for me to finish this fight as soon as possible.

I’d caught his hand just as he was about to weave back into the shadows. Even through the veil, it was easy to tell that he hadn’t expected something like that to happen.

Unfortunate for him, but I wasn’t an average cultivator.

I held on tightly to his wrist and channeled some of my lightning Qi into my hands.

It wasn’t obvious, but his eyes widened in surprise.

Then, all of that travelled through his body, paralyzing him. The poor guy couldn’t really take that much of it.

However, what happened next really surprised me. Not only did he have enough strength to resist me for a moment, but his hand, and entire body for that matter, had become a true shadow.

It was only for a moment, but that was enough for him to get rid of my grasp. I couldn’t help but feel impressed.

Nodding, I allowed him to retreat, wanting to see what he would do next.

He was still recovering from the paralysis when shadows wrapped around his body. I couldn’t feel an ounce of Qi escaping; it was all used with one hundred percent efficiency.

I had to give it to him, if nothing else. Even under such circumstances, he was good enough to keep his Qi under control.

But a person like him shouldn’t be participating in such a tournament.

Well, I shouldn’t either, if we were to be honest. I suppose we all had our own agendas.

I didn’t really care about him as long as he didn’t interfere with mine, but something told me that he would indeed interfere.

Someone this ambitious definitely wanted ambitious rewards.

Smiling, I sent him a provoking glare.

He kept himself together, surprisingly enough.

Once the shadows covered his entire body to the point that I could not even see his misty face, Hewi Badai attacked.

His figure became a true blur as he disappeared from my senses. I had no way of knowing which way he attacked from.

I clicked my tongue. There were limits to my cultivation even if I was at a peak. It was unfortunate, but I could only cheat for so long.

However, that didn’t mean I couldn’t use my brain.

I activated Heaven’s Protection at the same moment that Hwei Badai attacked, his slashes bouncing off my barrier.

That was… a little sad. It couldn’t even crack it. I suppose I never had the option to truly test how durable and effective those skills were against other humans. Usually I just overpowered them, but now when I didn’t want to kill and had to hold back, I could at least see their strength.

It was sad to say, but humans truly couldn’t hold a candle to Spirit Beasts. In a world where the system existed, even less so. With that in mind, I couldn’t help but wonder… why then were humans still dominating this world?

Maybe someone from the organisation that the Spirit Beast who tried to snatch my flower told me about would know.

Lesser or Spirit Beasts that didn’t interact much with humans, I could understand, but powerful beings like Mother? It seemed absurd.

I would definitely like to have an answer.

As I was thinking, I noticed that Hwei Badai had almost exhausted himself trying to break through Heaven’s Protection. Good to know that the skill was useful against strong Qi Condensation cultivators.

Nodding to myself, I turned it off and used the power of my body to deflect his attack.

His hand became incorporeal, unfortunately, but his dagger didn’t hit me either.

So it was that kind of skill.

You would either need to choose between your attack landing or getting hit yourself.

In this scenario, any hit or touch from me would result in a loss for him.

Although I could just summon my sword and be done with it as well, it was either or.

He took a few steps back, his breathing heavy. I’d never seen him so exhausted in any fight ever since the tournament started, and now he was seriously struggling.

It was a little funny, I had to admit. These types of people would normally be the main characters, but I supposed I stole the show.

“Alright, that’s enough,” I murmured under my breath and decided that I’d had enough fun. There was no way I could continue fighting him in this state, plus I still had one other fight to get through today.

Pulling out my normal elemental sword, the one that didn’t want to eat me, I used the techniques from the Divine Lightning Construction Technique.

They weren’t anything fancy, but I’d cultivated them to a high degree. I hadn’t fully mastered them; that much was for sure, but this was a good opportunity as any to practice.

Using the first form of the foot technique and the fourth form of the sword technique, I approached Hwei Badai, who was currently doing his best to run away.

Lightning surrounded my blade as I chased after him, my feet sparking with electricity.

I wasn’t sure if I had a crazy expression on my face, but it sure as hell felt like it, considering how much Hwei Badai was trying to get away from me.

He’d probably gotten a taste of lightning and was now really scared of it. Or maybe he was trying to lure me into a trap. It was really hard to say which was which.

However, no matter what he had plotted, if he did indeed have anything plotted. I was going to crush him either way.

Before long, he turned toward me as he ran, hand signs weaving like mad.

So he did have something planned.

I wasn’t paying attention, bu tit seemed like he was creating a formation on the ground. The shadows didn’t move from their spot, and as he finished the last of his hand signs, I found myself in the middle of the formation.

Immediately after, hundreds, thousands of shadowy hands reached toward me from the ground. They grabbed onto my legs, arms, hips, ps and head, trying to disarm me.

Their strength was impressive, but not enough to hold me down, not really.

I chuckled and saw his expression fall despite the shadows covering his face.

I summoned the only thing I naturally could in that moment— A giant fuckass Lightning Dragon.
