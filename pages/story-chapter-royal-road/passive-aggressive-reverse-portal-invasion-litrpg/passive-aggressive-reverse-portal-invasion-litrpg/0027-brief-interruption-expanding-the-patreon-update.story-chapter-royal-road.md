Okay. So, a couple of people have told me that they thought the number of advance chapters you'd be able to get access to on my Patreon were on the low side.

I absolutely agree!

I've honestly been holding back a bit - afraid that this launch on Passive Aggressive would see me run out of chapters and unable to even offer ANYTHING.  But, to my surprise, I plan to be back to writing Monday or Tuesday next week. And as long as my future writer self isn't a slacker, that should hopefully leave me with a decent buffer for the foreseeable future!

So, to sum up:

The $5 tier is expanded from 3 advance chapters to 5.

The $10 tier is expanded from 6 advance chapters to 10.

The $20 tier is expanded from 12 advance chapters to 20

I am well aware that a lot of amazing writers offer a good deal more. I hope to be able to do the same in the future. But, for now, this is what I feel I'll be able to maintain and not implode. :-)

I will go straight from this post and to expanding access on Patreon. Expect it to be all up and accessible within the next eight hours.

Check it out if you care. Ignore it entirely if it's not for you. It's all good, and I love you all! <3
