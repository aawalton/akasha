Outside the verdant empire, another Hell Hound was on the hunt, and Grick was too. The Hell Hound in question was Grick’s best friend, and he sat atop her back as she sniffed out the next nasty human they would kill.

Lily’s ears perked as she suddenly looked up, tail wagging behind her.

“You find nasty humans?” Grick whispered.

“Hunt…” Lily whispered back into his mind through pack bond.

Grick then became Shadow Grick as Lily blended in with the darkness beneath him as well, and she started padding forward more urgently.

Grick wasn’t very good at counting, but he was pretty sure they had killed most of the nasty humans they needed to for the quest. Once they were done with that, they would get to go back to the empire and have the tasty foods there. Lily had an easy time finding tasty things to eat out here in the woods, but Grick was stuck eating the dry meat stuff humans called jerky. If he had time to fashion together a fishing pole, he could have had some tasty fishies too, but they were too busy hunting humans.

It really was a lot of work, but Grick knew doing this would mean he gets to be strong, and he would be more able to help Puppy and Lily whenever they needed him. It was thanks to Puppy and Lily that Grick got to live such an interesting life. He got to eat tasty foods he never knew about, see new places, meet nice humans like Mei, and there were opportunities to obtain many lovely treasures.

Fox, he wasn’t sure if she was nice or not, but when she was in her human form, she had those very nice human things that Grick got to see a couple of times. Puppy always stopped him from looking, which he thought was unfair, but he liked Puppy anyway. Puppy was his best friend, or maybe second best friend. He liked both Lily and Puppy but liked Lily just a little more. Still, he wished Puppy would be back with them soon.

With that thought, he subconsciously scratched Lily’s head, which she always liked, but this time she complained.

“Griiiick. We hunt!”

“Yes! Hunt nasty human,” Grick whispered back as he ceased his patting.

She lowered down slightly as she poked her muzzle through a bush. “They are here.”

Grick’s ears perked as he listened; he could hear the humans muttering to each other.

“My god, what the hell are these things doing around here?” one said.

“We should have left that first night. When the boss was killed. Why the hell did we stick around!?” said the other.

“Well screw it, we’re getting the fuck out of this cursed forest now. Those fools who insist on staying behind— Bah! Idiots all of ’em! If they want to be food for the damn hell beasts, then let them!”

“But where are we supposed to go after this?”

“Back to the Iron March, of course. Shit, just don’t be stupid and use our real names. Find a warband who won’t give a damn long as we fightin’ for em’. Don’t know what the hell we were thinking in the first place. Joining up with those fools to live in a damn forest. Fucking stupid.”

Lily opened her mouth, and a human voice came from her throat. “I-Is someone there!? H-Help, I’m injured!”

The humans froze, then one called back, “Hang on—”

“Hey! Keep it down!” the other said in a loud whisper.

“Help!” Lily cried. “I need!”

“We’re coming just keep it down! You want the whole damn forest on us?”

“I’m sorry— I-it’s my leg. I—”

The humans slowed again. Grick had big ears that were good for hearing, so he perked one up as the other lay flat against his temple and he listened as they spoke lower.

“Fuck, if those monsters are out there, do we really wanna get slowed down dragging someone injured along?”

“But we can’t just leave them…”

“There’s no choice. Think about it, you wanna be next? With all that noise that fool’s making, he’ll serve as a good distraction to keep the damn monsters off of us while we get out of here.”

Lily looked up to Grick and asked through pack bond,  “What do they say?” It is not that Lily didn’t have good hearing too, but she didn’t always understand human words.

“They leaving fake scared human,” Grick whispered.

Lily huffed in frustration, then opened her mouth and tried again. “P-Please, please help! Don’t leave me here like this!”

“We won’t, we won’t, just, keep it down, alright?” one called back in a loud whisper.

“No— Y-You’re leaving me! Pleeease I don’t want to die!”

“We’re not leaving!” the other said reassuringly. “We’re just… ah… we’re going back for help, yeah? We can’t carry you on our own, you know? So uhh…” He cleared his throat. “You just stay there, and stay quiet, yeah? And we’ll be right back.”

“Pleeeease it hurts! It hurts so much!” Lily cried.

“Screw it, let’s just fucking go!” the other said.

The second called back. “We’ll be right back! Ah— I promise!”

And then the humans were leaving.

“Nasty nasty humans.” Grick tisked, shaking his head.

Lily let out an annoyed huff, then said, “Let’s do that special attack!” her tail wagging.

“You mean Grick’s Super Shadow Phantasma?” Grick asked. He wasn’t sure where he heard the word phantasma before, but he thought it was a good word.

“That one!” Lily agreed, tail wagging.

“Okay! Let’s go get nasty humans!” Grick cheered.

“Griiick! You must be quiet!” Lily complained. “We are hunting!”

“Sorry! Grick will be quiet!” Grick whispered back.

Lily split into clones, each with their own fake Shadow Grick atop them, then the clones split off. Two arced wide to either flank while the real Lily and Grick headed right for the humans.

It was really Lily and Grick’s abilities that made Grick’s Super Shadow Phantasma possible. The System called them Umbral Passage and Pounce and Bounce. But put together, Grick thought they deserved an even nicer name!

Lily stayed low as her paws quickly brought her closer and closer to the humans. Lily was always very quiet, and nothing could ever hear her approaching, that is, until she wanted them to.

Her mouth opened. “You said you’d help me…” she whined.

They both jolted and spun around, raising their swords. “Wh-What the hell!?” one gasped, shocked as the supposedly injured human should have been far behind.

Lily and Grick’s eyes both glowed red as she rose from her crouch, causing the humans to take terrified steps backward, but as they did so, Lily’s clones got into position behind them.

“I-It’s the monster!” one gasped.

“Oh gods!” the other cried.

Lily turned her head, tongue panting out from between her white fangs as her words turned from a stressed whine to a growl. “Why did you leave me to diiieeee?”

She suddenly charged at them, and Grick leaped off her back, leading his descent with his sword. As soon as she felt his blade slice into a human, he used Umbral Passage to switch to one of the other Shadow Gricks. That Shadow Grick’s Shadow Lily was already on the move for the next attack, so once the fake Grick became the real Grick, he did the same exact strike with Pounce and Bounce, then used Umbral Passage again. Over and over, Grick and Lily attacked from all sides. Whenever they tried to attack, their weapons would go through a Lily who had become fake.

After several volleys of this, the humans were quickly broken down, screaming and swinging their swords wildly in any direction they could. They were helpless to defend against Grick’s Super Shadow Phantasma.

With his final jump, Grick landed on the human’s back, wrapping his legs under his shoulders and placing his sword under his chin.

“P-Please don’t—” the pathetic human tried to say, but Grick was without mercy as he slid the blade across the nasty, nasty human’s throat.

Meanwhile, the other human gave a final scream as Lily found his neck, and shook until there was a snapping sound and the humans finally went quiet.

As all the extra Shadow Lilies and Shadow Gricks disappeared, Grick heard the System’s voice.

Foe Eliminated! LvL 13 Human

Experience Gained!

Foe Eliminated! LvL 15 Human

Experience Gained!

Level up!

You are now level 12!

+ 5 Strength

+ 7 Dexterity

+ 5 Vigor

+ 3 Attunement

+ 9 Guile

“Grick levels up!” Grick said.

Lily gave him a congratulatory “Yip!” and licked his face. Her breath smelled like blood, which Grick didn’t mind too much since she was his best friend, but he laughed as he pushed her neck back so she’d stop licking the bloody smell onto him.

Lily sat on her haunches and said, “We killed all the humans we needed.”

“We did?” Grick asked.

“We hunt monsters now! I’m hungry.” She nudged him with her muzzle, pushing him towards her back as her tail wagged. “Come on, Grick!”

Grick looked up to the sky. It was pretty deep in the night by now, but he was getting used to the late nights of hunting since meeting Lily and Puppy. Besides, he was looking forward to completing the quest and going back to the empire for tasty food!

***

Killing the rest of the necessary monsters was much simpler than their human hunting had been. Lily killed and ate a moss grazer, then found another and left it to the woods, and then the third and final one of their night caused the System to speak, congratulating them on the completion of their quest.

After that, the pair found a good spot to hunker down for the night and get to sleep, Grick lying against Lily as she curled her body around him.

But Grick noticed Lily was having trouble sleeping. Her breathing never quite found its usual steady sleeping rhythm, and she kept stirring, which in turn kept Grick awake.

“Lily okay?” Grick asked, rolling over.

Lily let out a huff as her eyes drifted open, staring ahead. Then she said, “Miss Sister.”

Grick frowned, then reached over and patted the top of Lily’s head. “Grick miss Puppy too, but Puppy will be back! That’s why Grick and Lily get strong. So when Puppy comes back, we can help Puppy!”

Lily looked at Grick. “I know. But…” She whined softly. “Lily was only without Sister once. It was after…”

Grick knew what she was talking about and hugged her neck, patting her head all the while. He didn’t know the rest of Puppy’s family, but he knew both she and Lily missed them so much. It is why they fought so hard, and why they kept getting stronger.

“Don’t worry, Lily. Grick is here! Grick will get strong too, and Grick will keep helping until Lily and Puppy have family back! Grick knows everything will work out.”

Lily’s tail batted once. “You think so?”

“Yes! Lily strong, and Puppy strong, and Grick will be strong too! Nobody will hurt nice puppies as long as Grick is around!” he said excitedly, thrusting a fist into the air.

Lily leaned towards him and licked his face, making him laugh. “Grick is Lily’s best friend too,” Lily said.

After that, Lily had an easier time getting to sleep. Her breathing soon slowed to a calm state, and both she and Grick were soon snoring.

***

The following morning, they started the day making their way back to the empire. Lily skipped hunting breakfast that morning because she had a very late dinner and she was more interested in getting back and hopefully having some Puffstork available.

She was very good at navigating the forest, occasionally putting her nose to the ground to help find her way, and soon they found a familiar road that would lead right back.

Grick sometimes wished he could carry Lily instead, because though he liked riding Lily and he liked giving his legs a break, he sometimes thought it would be nice to give Lily a break too. But really, she never seemed to mind one way or the other.

Grick will just have to make it up to Lily in other ways! he assured himself. That’s why Grick cannot be lazy. Grick has to keep hunting and fighting and becoming powerful. Grick must transcend and reach Grick’s ultimate form!

Soon, the empire was in sight, but this time, as they approached the gate, something was different.

Two guards who Grick wasn’t sure he had seen before were there. At least he didn’t think so, but humans often looked the same to him, so he couldn’t be sure. That is, except for nice girl humans. The pretty ones were always easy to remember for Grick.

But these were male humans, and they instantly regarded Grick and Lily’s approach differently, snapping to attention before one ran away, shouting, “They’re here! They’re here!”

Grick felt alarmed at first. Were they not happy to see Lily and Grick? But, no, it was the opposite. The other human was smiling and waving them forward. “you’ve returned! Please, come, come!”

Then Grick heard the one running off, now at a distance, “He’s here! The hero has returned!”

Grick blinked as Lily trotted him past the remaining guard, who bowed to him as they trotted by. “Welcome back, Hero Grick!”

“Hero… Grick…?” Grick muttered back.

“Are these humans being… strange?” Lily asked through pack bond.

“Grick not sure.”

Then, as they came out onto the fields, it seemed every single person tending the fields put their tools aside and began clapping and cheering.

“Please, this way, this way, Sir Grick,” the guard who’d announced their arrival said, now ushering them forward. Lily followed, ears folded back as the workers continued to cheer for them.

Grick wasn’t sure what to make of all this. He caught the eyes of a group of three farmer girls, who giggled and waved at him. He waved back and then their faces instantly filled with color as they nudged each other and huddled closer together while whispering and pointing.

Someone else shouted, “Thank you, Hero Grick!”

“Human happy Grick kills nasty humans?” Grick whispered to Lily.

“Humans… are strange…” Lily said uncertainly.

As they made it down the path, more groups of humans gathered near the entrance of the city gates, whistling and holding up signs. The signs with writing Grick couldn’t read, but others had drawings, all of them looked like Lily and Grick! Most had Grick riding atop Lily, raising his sword. One was just a drawing of a hell hound face with purple eyes that Grick thought must be Lily.

A human lady holding a child stepped forward from the crowd and bowed. “Thank you for protecting us, Hero Grick,” she said, as the child put a necklace made of flowers around Grick’s neck and giggled. The mother quickly carried the child back into the crowd.

Then Grick looked ahead to see a group of guards in green standing around a very familiar human. It was Rin.

Her metal hands clapped together as the soldier pointed and nodded approvingly. Lily came to a stop before them as one of the soldiers said, “So, this is the hero keeping the woods safe.”

“Of course,” Rin said, then smiled at Grick. “Welcome home, Grick.”

“Um… It’s not just Grick,” Grick said, patting Lily’s head. “Grick couldn’t kill nasty humans without Lily.” He couldn’t deny a bubble of excitement forming in his stomach at how much these humans seemed to like him. Especially when he thought about how that group of girls had looked at him. He liked that most of all.

“Indeed, a truly powerful duo the two of you are.” A larger soldier nodded.

Another one with a scar over his eye, not dressed in armor but more like Rin’s nicer-looking attire, nodded as well. “Surely, we are very lucky to have the Hero Grick fighting on our side,” the scarred man said.

Rin smiled and nodded behind them. “Come, Grick. There are several important matters on which we will soon require your input.”

Grick’s ears perked. “Grick’s input?”

"Of course,” Rin said, walking beside them as Lily trotted towards a carriage that was waiting for them. “You have such unique experiences dealing with the threats against our lands.” She turned enough to show her teeth in a smile. “And besides that, you’re a very smart goblin. Your advice will be invaluable to us.”

Grick did know some things. He was good at hunting, sneaking, fishing, and finding shinies in caves. He wasn’t good at trading shinies, but Puppy taught him to be better at it! But he wasn’t sure any of that would help Rin. What advice could he possibly give?

The finely dressed man with the scar nodded. “With Grick’s brilliant mind and unconventional approach to warfare, I suspect his counsel will offer perspectives our traditional strategists have overlooked.”

Lily looked up at Grick and asked, “What are humans saying?”

“They think Grick smart,” Grick said to Lily through pack bond.

“Oh.” Lily looked amongst the humans walking around her and asked, “Is Grick smart?”

“Yes, Grick very smart!” Grick answered without hesitation. “Humans finally notice!”

He felt a bubble of pride as the carriage was held open. He hopped off Lily’s back and let her jump in first, then he climbed in after her. Rin and the scarred man got in right behind them.

“We look forward to hearing your mission report,” Rin said, as she settled down. “But I suppose such conversations can wait just a little longer. You ought to take some time to clean up and have a proper meal worthy of a returning hero.”

Lily must have known the word meal because she told Grick, “Ask if they have Puffstork!”

“Do humans have tasty puffstork for Lily?” Grick asked.

Rin interlaced her metal fingers in her lap. “Of course. I’ve personally seen to it that our Puffstork shipments are prioritized towards Lily first, and of course, many tasty fishies for you as well, Grick.”

Lily’s tail wagged at the promise of Puffstork.

The carriage started moving, and Grick pushed himself up to look out the window. The humans lining the road were still cheering and waving. Some of them ran alongside the carriage a short distance before falling behind, while children held up drawings of Grick and Lily battling monsters.

One of the drawings showed Grick standing atop an enormous pile of dead humans, a sword in each hand, while Lily breathed fire beneath him.

Grick did not remember using two swords, or Lily breathing fire, but he thought it was a very good drawing anyway.
