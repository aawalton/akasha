Hey All! I like to do this thing after I finish a book where I ask for feedback. This is very much a first draft, and as a writer, it can sometimes be hard to see what worked and what didn’t, so please let me know.

Here are some questions to get you thinking:

What did you like? What was your favorite moments? What do you want to see more of?

What did you not like? What didn’t work? What seemed out of character or forced?

I appreciate all y’all, and I’m having a ton of fun with this series. Schedule should be continuing as normal Friday.
