Just wanted to make a post to thank everyone for the (in some cases) years of support that I've received for Randidly Ghosthound.

I know it's sometimes a hard story to love. I started mostly with the goal of having fun and finishing the story, weaving a lot of different storylines that I thought were interesting without considering the consequences. Then for two years I tried to untangle all those originally inconsequential decisions. Now, we start accelerating toward the end.

(I say accelerating, but really it's probably 2200-2300 finish...)

Anyway, I polished and rewrote so much of the early stuff. The links are above. Definitely made Randidly a fuller character and gave some care to some... unpopular early characters. Please read and rate on Amazon, it really helps.

Let's keep making imaginary worlds together.
