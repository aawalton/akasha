Hello everyone, I'm excited to announce the launch of a new series from me! Reborn as the Fated Villain is out on Kindle and Audible!

Being the first book I ever wrote and signed for a publishing deal, it's surreal to see the book that started my career finally getting its chance in the spotlight on Amazon.

What is it about?

It's a combination of my favourite Korean novel trends: being reincarnated as a magic professor, who is also a villain and despised by everyone around them. The MC is also a vampire, so if he can survive the storyline (which he knows about), then he will be able to enjoy an immortal life. But he's playing life on hard mode as a hidden setting called The Villain's Fate makes the world seek out his demise.

Synopsis:

Power up and become an unbeatable final boss.

Damien Nightshade, a discarded royal masquerading as a Professor in one of the continent's greatest magic academies, is considered a mid-level boss—one fated to die in every playthrough.

Now, I have transmigrated into his body.

A game-like world has become my reality, and instead of being its hero, I'm cursed to die a pitiful death as a fated villain.

But why should I accept life as a third-rate villain when I can strive to become the unbeatable final boss?
