Hey everybody, Magnus here.

The big moment has finally arrived. My debut novel, Skill Eater, has made it to amazon in digital, book, and audiobook form. I have a huge favor to ask today as I celebrate this major milestone in my life as an aspiring author.

The first few weeks after a new book hits the market are critical. More downloads, page reads, ratings, and reviews will encourage the algorithm to favor the story and convince more prospective readers to give it a try, helping Skill Eater find new eyeballs and reach its full potential. In short, clicking those buttons and sharing your thoughts is voting for the success of the series in a literal way.

It has been my dream for over 10 years to write for a living full time, and this is the moment of truth. If Skill Eater does well, I will be able to write more books, more often, pumping out those pages every day to bring you the absolute best stories I can write.

Even if you don’t plan on reading it right away, downloading the book will make a big difference. So please, if you have kindle unlimited, head over to zon, add it to your library right now, and open to page one. If you don’t, I would deeply appreciate it if you showed your support for the series by purchasing a copy, but it would also mean a lot if you left a review, even if you can’t pick one up right now (if Amazon allows you do so).

No matter what, I wanted to thank everyone who has read Skill Eater, especially the people who have left insightful comments, asked interesting questions, and offered keen-eyed corrections in my never-ending quest to quash every typo that rears its ugly head.

You people are the real heroes of this story, and I am in your debt.

All the best and thanks for everything,

-Magnus
