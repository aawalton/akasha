Last time I was here I said it might be a while before I wrote something similar to Speedrunning. Well, six months later, here we are!

Introducing Savage Awakening: A LitRPG Apocalypse! This one's a blend of LitRPG and Xianxia, set in a post-System-Apocalypse world with a berserker battle maniac MC. If you liked Speedrunning, I have a feeling you'll like this one. The MC, Zane, fights a lot like Dorian. He also thinks a lot like Dorian in some ways (esp his thrill-seeking), though he's a lot more chill and stoic most of the time. And also a little less of a sociopath. He's just a dude who likes beating things up in a world where he can now do that. He's having the time of his life.

But eventually all his solo dungeon clearing will mean he becomes a great power--and he'll get caught up in the broader politics of his world, and the Universe... and things get interesting. Check it out!

Zane was driving home from work when the world changed.

Thousands of dungeons spawned at once, taking up every inch of Earth. Monsters ran wild and danger lurked around every corner.

But why does he feel so at home in his new cutthroat reality?

I had a blast writing this. I hope you'll have just as much fun reading it.
