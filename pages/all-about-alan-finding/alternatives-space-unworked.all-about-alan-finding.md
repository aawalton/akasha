# Claim

Alan's alternatives space names six tiers — trustworthy larger organisations, smaller and local ones, co-ops and mutuals, self-reliance, decentralised or federated options, and government-as-utility — and holds no worked examples beneath them: no named co-ops with the mechanism that insulates each, no self-reliance categories with their costs, no federated services he would use. What makes an alternative viable is also undefined: no cost ceiling, no quality floor, no test of feasibility.

# Evidence

My own reading of Abby's backlog at `~/abby-backlog-2026-08-06/`, migrated here as a finding. This folds two items under the FRAME thread of `backlog/personal-freedom.md`, both on `notes/alternatives.md`: `alternatives-taxonomy` (line 37, was item 8) and `viability` (line 41, was item 10). One says the tiers have no worked examples and the other says the word viable is undefined; both are the same gap between a named space and a usable one, which is why I took them as one observation. The second is recorded as flagged by the note's own threads section.

The candidate co-ops the first item lists are REI, USAA, credit unions and electric co-ops.

What I did not measure: I did not read `notes/alternatives.md`, so the six tiers and the inline lists it already carries are the backlog's account, and "no worked examples" may understate what is there.
