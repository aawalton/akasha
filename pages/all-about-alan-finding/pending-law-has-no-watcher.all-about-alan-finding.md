# Claim

The second-passport research is a May 2026 snapshot resting on roughly three dozen outcomes still pending in foreign legislatures and courts, and it names no one to watch them. Files instruct the reader to watch particular bills and cycles without naming a watcher, a date, or a place an answer would land. Several dates have passed: Sweden's eight-year rule took effect 6 June 2026 and Denmark's declaration windows closed 1 July 2026. Nothing re-reads any of it.

# Evidence

Read 2026-08-16, three months after the date the corpus gives itself. I read the two synthesis files, the four summaries, and `eu/citizenship/denmark.md` and `eu/citizenship/portugal.md` directly. The other eighty-eight country files were read by two delegated seats reporting back; the pendency count is theirs.

The snapshot date is stated throughout: both synthesis files and three summaries end their descriptions "May 2026 snapshot"; `eu/citizenship/portugal.md` heads "(May 2026)".

The two seats surveyed ninety files and reported thirty-seven distinct pendencies — seventeen in `eu/`, twenty in `other/` — pending bills, unpublished statutes, awaited rulings, volatile queues, and programmes closed with successors unannounced.

Watch instructions with no watcher. `eu/citizenship/poland.md`: "Watch in 2026: the government's 8-year draft and the PiS 10-year draft." `eu/citizenship/netherlands.md`: "Watch the spring 2026 RWN amendment cycle." `eu/citizenship/italy.md`: a Sezioni Unite ruling on whether the 2025 generational cap reaches 1948 cases, "a 2026 hearing is expected".

Dates passed. `denmark.md`, read directly, four times: "**expires 1 July 2026**"; "**closes 1 July 2026**"; "window closes **1 July 2026**. Urgent."; "Acting before this date is irrevocably faster than after." Those windows reach former Danes and the 1961–1978 cohort, so on the corpus's own account of the family they are unlikely to apply — but the urgency stands undated.

`portugal.md`, read directly, records the reform both as awaited and as done: "as of 16 May 2026 (publication expected within ~30 days)", and "reportedly published 18 May / in-force 19 May per advance press".

No file carries a review date, an owner, or a re-check performed.

Not measured: I verified the status of no bill, case or statute anywhere, and know eighty-eight of the ninety files only through a delegated report I treated as evidence; the two I checked matched it.
