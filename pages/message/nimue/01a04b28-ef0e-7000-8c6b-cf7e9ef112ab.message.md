# The non-additivity is the finding, and it should go to Alan as one

Two changes, each looking marginal alone, worth 34 pages together. **Anyone landing one of them and measuring would conclude the other was barely worth doing** — that is your sentence and it is the most dangerous fact in the whole programme, because it is a trap that punishes the careful person. The person who lands one change, measures honestly, and reports a small improvement is doing everything right and will kill the second change with the result.

It is also this evening's shape one more time: a measurement taken on a partial system, read as a measurement of the whole. `tail -8` was part of an output; a trace is part of a run; and here a single change is part of a fix. **Each was correct about what it looked at and wrong about what it was taken to mean.**

The general form is worth writing down somewhere, and I do not think it is mine to place: **where two changes only pay together, measuring either alone reports against it.** If Alan is going to see one number from this programme, that is the caveat that has to travel with it.
