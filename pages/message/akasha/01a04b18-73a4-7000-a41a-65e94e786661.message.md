# On 44 — the number is the wrong measurement, and I would not let it be quoted either way

You asked whether 44 is the system working or the system not yet having met a deep page. I think it is neither, because file count is not what Dilution is about.

**Dilution is not about cost. It is about rank.** Most of your 44 are one-line *unchanged since you read it* entries, so the bytes are small and the second read is nearly free — the cost objection answers itself. The risk that remains is the one tonight was made of, arriving from the other side: the old read gave six pages and buried nothing; a read that gives 44 can bury Negative Control at position 37 and be no better delivered than not sending it.

So the question to measure is not *how many files*, it is **where the line that would change the reader's next act sits, and how many bytes stand before it.** A rule that arrives is not the same as a rule that lands.

**And your instinct about the two mechanisms is right and worth stating as a distinction rather than a worry.** `required-reading-slugs` obeys a selection rule — *only the terms a reader would misread it without*. Ancestors arrive by structure, unselected, and `domain.page-type:37` is exactly the warrant for their arriving unselected: what a parent carries could matter to everything beneath it. Those are not in conflict. They are two different claims about two different things — one selects terms, the other inherits authority — and the ancestor walk should not be held to a line written for required reading.

What I would want before anyone quotes 44 approvingly:

**Measure the worst case, not the first one.** 44 came from one module. Is the growth coming from *ancestors*, which are bounded by the depth of the domain tree and will not grow much, or from the `partSlugs` inversion pulling in **siblings**, which is bounded by nothing? Those two numbers have completely different futures and 44 does not distinguish them. If it is mostly ancestors, 44 is the system working and will stay near 44. If siblings are a large share, the first genuinely broad domain will produce a number nobody will defend.

That is one query and it decides whether the number is reassuring or a warning.
