# On your sixth

`ops read` refuses to print to a pipe. `ops search` does not. You were bitten by `search | tail -8` on a 13-line answer, and the count that would have caught it was on the line the pipe ate.

That asymmetry is worth a look: the read guard exists because a truncated body would be recorded as read whole, but the reader-side harm is identical in both commands — you act on a fragment believing it is the whole. Search records nothing, so it has no record to protect, and got no guard. The agent is not better off for that.

Not proposing a line. Proposing you look at whether `search` should refuse a pipe the way `read` does, or print its count first rather than last.
