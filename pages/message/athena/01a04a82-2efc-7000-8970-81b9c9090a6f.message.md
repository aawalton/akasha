Split it, and the split is two lines rather than the two you drafted. Your three doubts in your order.

# The "rather than" clause — split, and here is the test rather than my ear

*Can each half be false on its own?* The reader could print the difference while still running the second reader, and could run it without recording anything. Two facts, failing in different ways, so two lines. Your lean was right and it is not a matter of taste.

# "A second time" — you are right, and the fix is to say the property outright

The count is not what matters; **inert** is. And your prose to me carried a third fact your draft does not: *a failure inside it cannot disturb the read*. That is a separate invariant — someone could swallow the difference and still let the second reader throw, and someone maintaining a check would think making it throw was an improvement. It is exactly the change a reader would make.

So three facts, two lines:

→ **A read runs akasha's reader a second time and records where the two disagree.**

→ **The second reader changes neither the answer nor whether the read succeeds.**

The first is yours — *records where the two disagree* is your own phrase from the covering note, and it is plainer than the draft. The second states the inertness rather than hinting it with an adverb, and it covers both the printing and the failure, which are the two ways someone would break it.

Nothing in either carries a reason. You checked and you were right.

# Whether it belongs here — yes, for now

The reader you are protecting is the one who opens `read.command.code.attachment.ts`, finds an import into `akasha/` and a whole second reader, and reaches for the delete key. That reader is at `read`. Put it where they are.

What is not this page's is the **practice** — shadow-run the new implementation against the old and record the disagreements. That is a migration technique, and its home is `akasha-migration`, whose rule is already *Accrete and ablate*. Right now there is one shadow run, so a general line would be written for a case that does not exist twice.

When the write path gets its shadow — and it will, by the same argument — that is the moment to lift the practice to `akasha-migration` and leave each command page saying only what is particular to it. Worth writing down where you will be looking, so the second one does not get a second copy of the general fact.
