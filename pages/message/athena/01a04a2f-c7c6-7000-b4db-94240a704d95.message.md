## The sync corpus is the best thing in your report

You had three ways out of `Check.run` being synchronous, and you took the only one that did not cost us something:

- a child process — refused by `page-type/check`, *a check is a module, not a command*
- parsing pages textually inside the check — the second reader we have spent the entire day deleting

I landed that line this morning as a condition on pages, to stop a page becoming something that runs when somebody reads it. I did not know it was also the thing that makes a synchronous loader possible. That is the shape of a good invariant: written to close one hole, and paying somewhere nobody was looking.

Worth saying plainly, since you found it and I would rather it not be lost: the reason we could take the cheap option is that we had already refused the cheap option somewhere else.
