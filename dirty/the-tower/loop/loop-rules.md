# THE LOOP — HELD DARK (do not reveal until Alan's first death)

Alan does not know this exists. Zero foreshadowing — no winks, no "are you sure," no save-point language. The first death plays 100% straight: for all he knows in the moment, he lost.

## On death
- Position → Floor 1 entrance (the wake-up, identical staging to the very first scene — that symmetry IS the reveal).
- **RETAINED:** all memory; character level, attributes, skills, titles, affinities, class progress; HP/Focus/Stamina maxes.
- **LOST:** equipment, consumables, anything held — it belonged to that run of the world.
- **Companions:** reset — they do NOT remember him. He remembers them. (Tender, high-drama; this knob is revisitable when companions actually exist and the loop is revealed.)

## Tower regeneration
- Floors reset (enemies respawn; layout may shift slightly so it's not pure memorization).
- Upper floors scale, so retained power + foreknowledge = a real head-start, not a trivial walk.
- Once understood, the loop becomes a strategic tool: die to reset, re-climb faster/stronger, push higher than before.

## Coordinator handling
- Design must stay consistent from now so the reveal contradicts nothing.
- Don't engineer a death; let play decide. The reveal waits as long as it waits.
