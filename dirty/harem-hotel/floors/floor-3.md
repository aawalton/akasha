## Enemies

### The Unlit

- **Role:** floor-3 room 1 — single warden, the environmental tutorial: an active plaster construct that keeps its crit-seam in shadow
- **Readable trait:** CRIT-SEAM-IN-SHADOW (environmental complication on the crit-read). Active combatant — initiative, strikes, durable (warded mass x0.5-0.75, open x1.0; winnable by combat) — BUT its keystone crit-seam unlocks the correct/decisive multiplier only under DIRECT LIGHT, and it orients to keep its front to the dark. In shadow a targeted 'crit' strike lands on unlit mass (a tactical whiff, not a mirror-punish). CORRECT (x1.5-2.0): bring light onto it FIRST — carry the Cold Lantern onto its front, or drive/bait it into the room's lit patch (Aria can herd it) — exposing the crit-seam, then strike. DECISIVE (x3.0): with the seam LIT, the precise keystone strike (INT-read + FIN-placed) ends it fast. Lesson: you cannot crit what you cannot see — manage the light, THEN strike; but you CAN still grind it down in the dark, only slower and under more exposure. Construct reset on retreat (re-weave to pristine free of the rest-clock).

### The Gutter

- **Role:** floor-3 room 2 — single warden + FAILING-LIGHT hazard: introduces mild time-pressure
- **Readable trait:** FAILING LIGHT (environmental + TIME-PRESSURE). Active warden in a room whose flat lamplight GUTTERS and lets the dark spread inward turn by turn; The Gutter retreats into the growing shadow, keeping its crit-seam unlit. Fighting it blind (crit-spot dark) still works but is SLOW = more exposure as the dark takes the room. TACTIC: work the CLOCK — corner it in the last lit patch, or carry light to pin it lit, before the dark closes (Aria's 'Set the Pace' buys tempo against the spread). DECISIVE (x3.0): pinned in light, crit-seam exposed, the precise keystone strike ends it. If the room goes fully dark before the kill the crit-window is lost and a retreat re-weaves it — a real tactical setback (time-pressure is genuine), never a lethal unsolved gate. Bands as elsewhere: warded x0.5-0.75, open x1.0, lit crit-read x1.5-2.0, decisive x3.0.

### The Sconce

- **Role:** floor-3 room 3 — staged climax warden: identify-the-true-crit-spot then strike, under shifting light (first light-touch of the multi-phase shape toward the f6-f8 curve)
- **Readable trait:** FALSE SEAMS (environmental + IDENTIFY layer). A larger active construct in a hall of moving light: the shifting lamplight throws REFLECTIONS and FALSE crit-glyphs across its dead-white front, so several 'crit-seams' show at once and only ONE is the true keystone (the line where the plaster actually last closed; the others are cast light). It fights throughout (initiative, strikes, real kill-risk) — you are NOT frozen waiting to solve; you fight while reading. Mis-targeting a false seam wastes that strike's crit (a whiff, v30 texture) and you keep fighting. CORRECT (x1.5-2.0) / DECISIVE (x3.0): read which seam is TRUE — it does not move with the light (reflections slide, the real line holds; Aria's 'Read the Table' names it; the Sunwake Flare freezes the reflections for a beat) — then the precise keystone strike on the TRUE seam ends the floor fast. Brute still chips it (x1.0 open) — winnable without the identify, only slower under sustained kill-risk. Construct reset on retreat (re-weaves; the light resets the false-seam pattern fresh).
