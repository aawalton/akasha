Mid-to-late afternoon, the lull holding but the light beginning, just barely, to turn — the gold on the floorboards has shifted a degree toward amber, the earliest signal that the afternoon will eventually tip toward evening, though that turn is still a long way off and nothing in the room registers it yet. Structure unchanged: the back-corner pair stay settled and joined, hands closed in the gap, no body signaling any move to rise or leave; coats long dry. The warmer queued track is now into its sustained middle, soft enough to keep deepening the hush; nothing queued behind it would lift the room. The espresso machine holds idle pressure; the line stays empty; nothing is about to run out. Outside the rain has settled from its thickened press into a softer, steadier even wash, the grey unmoving, the shelter stable. The front glass is fogged low and another shade thicker, the film whole and still unnoticed by either of them — the room's quiet record of how long they've been held inside the warmth. The mid-room cup sits fully cold. Nothing pending demands resolution; no incident waits to land. The only thing in motion is the slow turn of the light, and it moves at the pace of the day.
