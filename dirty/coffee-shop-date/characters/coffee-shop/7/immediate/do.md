Nothing breaks; the room only deepens what it already is. The light shifts another faint degree — the gold along the floorboards thinning toward a warmer amber as the grey outside the windows dims by a shade, the long afternoon making its first slight lean toward the gold of late day. The warmth in the back corner holds, the softened wash of rain steady on the glass, the murmur of the half-full room receding another gentle distance, the shelter closing wholly over the joined hands and asking nothing of them at all — the day turning so slowly around them that neither could say it had moved.
