Inert. Only the slow tending: the cold cup at air temperature, the fog thickening low at the glass, the warmer track unwinding through its middle, the gold easing toward amber as the outside light dims by degrees, the softened rain keeping its steady weight. The shop holds the joined moment exactly as it is and pursues nothing — content to let the afternoon stay unhurried and the touch rest where it landed, the only motion the day's own imperceptible turn.
