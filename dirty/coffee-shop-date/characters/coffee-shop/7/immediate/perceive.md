No new motion crosses the floor — the corner stays wordless and still, the two hands resting joined in the warm gap, both bodies sunk and unmoving, faces turned, breath gone long and even. No scrape, no rising, no voice lifted above the murmur. At the counter the line holds empty; the steam wand stays silent. The warmer track has run deeper into itself now, past its opening phrase and into the low sustained middle of it, the melody unwinding soft beneath the room's hum. The rain has eased to its softer even wash on the glass, steady, the sharper drum gone. At the near window the low fog has crept up another faint shade, whole and still unnoticed by either of them. The gold along the floorboards has thinned and warmed a degree toward amber as the light outside dims behind the grey — the first faint lean of afternoon toward what comes after, too slight for anyone in the chairs to mark.
