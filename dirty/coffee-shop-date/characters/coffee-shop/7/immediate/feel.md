Sheltering and wholly close, the touched moment resting where it landed — the warmth no longer cradling a reach or even the first of a touch but the simple ongoing fact of two hands joined and at rest. The room has gone as quiet around them as it will go, the intimacy fully settled and inward, asking nothing. The softened rain draws the walls closer still; the amber creeping into the light lends the shelter a first faint warmth of late afternoon, the day's slow lean toward evening felt by the room as deepening rather than as ending. Patient, unhurried, content to be the place this is happening in — the kind of stillness that holds without pressing.
