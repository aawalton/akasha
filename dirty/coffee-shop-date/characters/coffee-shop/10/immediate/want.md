Inert. Only the slow tending: the cold cups at air temperature, the freed table's surface left to be wiped or not, the fog thickening low at the glass, the deep amber easing on as the outside light fails, the softened rain keeping its weight, the machine's idle pressure bleeding down on its own, and the playlist running its course beneath the murmur. The shop holds the joined moment as it is and pursues nothing; it does not clear the corner's path or fill the room to suit it, it only lets the ebb and the light run as the hour dictates. Content to let the afternoon stay unhurried, the touch rest where it landed, and the room go on thinning and dimming quietly around it.
