Sheltering and wholly close, the touched moment resting unbroken where it landed, deeper now for the room having gone quieter still. The departure has finished settling; the café has come through it whole and sunk further into its slack-hour calm. The softened rain draws the walls close; the deep amber in the light lends the shelter its fullest warmth of the failing afternoon, the first faint suggestion of evening in it. The intimacy in the corner is fully settled and inward, undisturbed by anything the room does. The space around it has loosened nearly all the way toward emptiness — patient, unhurried, content to be the place this is happening in, holding the two of them as the light goes long and gold and the hour tips quietly toward its end.
