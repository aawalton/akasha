Inert. Only the slow tending: the cold cups at air temperature, the freed table left unwiped, the fog crept higher and whole at the glass, the lamplight steady now that the day's gone, the rain keeping its weight, the city beading in the wet, the playlist running its course toward the next turn beneath the murmur, the counter silent. The shop holds the joined moment as it is and pursues nothing — it does not clear the corner's path nor warm the room to suit it nor hold back the evening's deepening; it only lets the ebb and the lamplit hour run as they will. Content to let the touch rest where it landed and the room go on still and inward around it, turning its small rhythms over as the evening sets in.
