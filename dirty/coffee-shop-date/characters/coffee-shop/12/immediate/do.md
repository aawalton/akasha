The current song thins to its last notes and ends — a bare half-second where there is only the soft wash of rain on the glass — and then the cued track rises into the space it leaves, lower and slower than the one before, a quiet evening melody opening softly beneath the lowered murmur. The room turns the song over as it does, on its own rhythm, asking nothing for it. In the back corner the change registers only as the hush settling a shade deeper around them: the warmth holds, the joined hands stay at rest in the gap, the rain keeps its steady wash, the lamplight steady, and the shelter draws closer still as the evening music folds in over the dark outside the glass.
