Mid-afternoon, deep in the lull, unchanged in its bones. The back-corner pair are settled and warm, coats beginning to give up their cold to the room. The slower track is now established as the room's floor — it will run several minutes before the next turn. The espresso machine still holds idle pressure. Outside, the rain has held at its thickened pace; the grey hasn't moved, which is itself the point — the shelter is stable, the afternoon is not yet tipping. The draft by the door has fully dissipated into the ambient warmth. A cup somewhere mid-room is cooling toward the temperature of the air. The line at the counter is still empty. Nothing pending demands resolution; the room's only motion is the slow one. The window-fog is beginning, a thin breath of condensation gathering at the cold edges of the front glass where warm inside meets cold rain — not yet noticed.
