Mid-afternoon, still deep in the lull, structure unchanged. The back-corner pair are fully settled and now met in full — four things spoken, the open invitation answered in kind and the moment deepening rather than resolving; a hand is in motion toward the space between them, the only thing moving in their corner, not yet closed on anything. Their coats are long since dry, nothing of the outside left on them. The slower track has perhaps a phrase left before it ends; the next song sits queued, a touch warmer and slower still, soft enough to deepen the hush rather than break it. The espresso machine holds idle pressure; the line stays empty; nothing is about to run out. Outside the rain holds at its thickened pace, the grey unmoving, the shelter stable; the afternoon has not yet tipped toward evening, though the gold has crept another inch along the floorboards. The front glass is re-fogged low where the new dampness gathered, the film whole and still unnoticed by either of them. The mid-room cup has reached air temperature, fully cold now, its steam long gone. Nothing pending demands resolution.
