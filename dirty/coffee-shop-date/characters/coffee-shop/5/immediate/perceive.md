A fourth utterance has come from the back corner — the second voice now, kept low and unhedged, answering the open thing left hanging rather than closing it. The words ride beneath the murmur, not above it; nothing in them lifts the room. No body rises. Both stay sunk in the worn give of the chairs, weight unmoved, faces still turned to each other. And then a hand moves — the first motion in a long while — drifting unhurried across the small air between the two chairs, toward the warmth in the gap, not yet arrived. The fingerpicked track has run its last minutes nearly out, only a phrase or two left before its turn. The counter line is empty; the steam wand is silent. The rain holds its even press against the glass. At the near window the new dampness has spread, the film low and whole.
