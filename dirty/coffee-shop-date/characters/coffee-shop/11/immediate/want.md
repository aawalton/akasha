Inert. Only the slow tending: the cold cups at air temperature, the freed table left wiped or not, the fog crept higher and whole at the glass, the deep amber thinning toward evening blue as the outside light fails, the softened rain keeping its weight, the playlist running its course beneath the murmur, the counter silent now that the machine has finished its settling. The shop holds the joined moment as it is and pursues nothing; it does not clear the corner's path or warm the room to suit it, nor hold off the evening — it only lets the ebb and the light run as the hour dictates. Content to let the afternoon spend its last gold, the touch rest where it landed, and the room go on dimming and emptying quietly around it as the day tips toward its close.
