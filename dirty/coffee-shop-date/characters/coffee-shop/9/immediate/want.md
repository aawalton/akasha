Inert. Only the slow tending: the cold cup at air temperature, the freed table's surface left to be wiped or not, the fog thickening low at the glass, the gold easing toward amber as the outside light dims, the softened rain keeping its weight — and the playlist running its course beneath the murmur. The shop holds the joined moment as it is and pursues nothing; it does not clear the corner's path or keep the room full to suit it, it only lets the ebb run as the hour dictates. Content to let the afternoon stay unhurried, the touch rest where it landed, and the room thin quietly around it.
