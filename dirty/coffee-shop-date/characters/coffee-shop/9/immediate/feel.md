Sheltering and wholly close, the touched moment resting unbroken where it landed, the brighter song doing nothing to disturb it. The faint held-breath of the song's turning has passed; the room has come through it whole and gone back to its deep quiet — quieter still now, by a shade, as a table empties and two voices leave with the door. The softened rain still draws the walls close; the amber in the light lends the shelter its first real warmth of late afternoon. The intimacy in the corner is fully settled and inward, and the room around it loosens another notch toward emptiness, the way an afternoon café does as it tips toward the slack hour — patient, unhurried, content to be the place this is happening in, and growing only more so as it empties.
