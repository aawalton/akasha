Two bodies have come to rest in the back-corner armchairs, still carrying the cold of outside on their coats. Their movement has stopped; the small sounds of settling — fabric, a held breath, the give of old cushions — fade into the room's murmur. Neither is speaking. At the counter the barista wipes down the steam wand; a cup clinks onto a saucer somewhere mid-room. The rain keeps up its steady press against the front glass.
