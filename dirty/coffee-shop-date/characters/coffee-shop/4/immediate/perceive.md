A third utterance has come from the corner, the first voice again, kept just as low and just as close — it rides above the murmur without lifting it, and where the earlier exchanges settled, this one stays out in the room a beat longer, an open thing left hanging in the warm air. No body rises. Both stay sunk in the worn give of the chairs, weight unmoved, faces turned to each other and not to the glass or the floor. The fingerpicked track runs on, fewer minutes left in it now. The counter line is empty; the steam wand is quiet. The rain holds its even press. At the near window the clean track left by the slid bead has fogged back over, the film unbroken again, and a new dampness is gathering low where the old one cleared.
