Mid-afternoon, still deep in the lull, structure unchanged. The back-corner pair remain fully settled — three things now spoken between them, the latest left open and unanswered, the held moment deepening rather than closing or breaking. Their coats have finished giving the cold over to the room; there is nothing left of the outside on them. The slower track has only a few minutes before its turn — the next song is already queued, a touch brighter in tempo but still soft, not a thing that would break the hush. The espresso machine holds idle pressure; the line stays empty; nothing is about to run out. Outside the rain holds at its thickened pace, the grey unmoving, the shelter stable, the afternoon not yet tipping toward evening though the gold light has crept a few inches further along the floorboards. The front glass has re-fogged where the bead cleared it; the film is whole again and still unnoticed by either of them. The mid-room cup has nearly reached air temperature, its steam long gone. Nothing pending demands resolution.
