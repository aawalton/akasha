Sheltering and close, the warmth now holding an open thing aloft rather than a settled one — the room cradles the unanswered beat without hurrying it, the way a still room holds a held breath. The intimacy has gone one degree deeper and more deliberate; the music lays itself over an invitation left in the air, not over a pause needing filled. The rain still draws the walls inward. The shelter is fully itself, patient, in no need of anything to happen.
