Sheltering and close, the open thing now not only met but touched — the warmth cradling a joined moment rather than a reaching one. The room has gone a half-degree quieter still around the closed hand, the intimacy fully folded inward, settled and unhurried. The rain draws the walls close. The shelter is wholly itself, patient, asking nothing, content to be the place this is happening in — the kind of stillness that does not want to be disturbed and, being a room, does not disturb it.
