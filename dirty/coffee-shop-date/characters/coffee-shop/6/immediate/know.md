The back-corner pair are fully settled and now joined — the hand that was crossing the gap last tick has arrived and closed over the other; the moment has resolved into touch rather than words, and nothing in either body signals a move to rise or leave. The warmer queued track is now playing, a phrase or two into its run, soft enough to deepen the hush rather than break it; nothing is queued behind it that would lift the room. The espresso machine holds idle pressure; the line stays empty; nothing is about to run out. Outside the rain holds at its thickened pace, the grey unmoving, the shelter stable; the afternoon has not yet tipped toward evening, though the gold has crept another faint inch along the floorboards. The front glass is fogged low and a shade thicker than last tick, the film whole and still unnoticed by either of them. The mid-room cup sits fully cold at air temperature, steam long gone. Nothing pending demands resolution; the room has no incident waiting to land.
