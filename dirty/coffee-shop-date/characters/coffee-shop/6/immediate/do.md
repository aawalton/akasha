Nothing breaks. The room simply tends the moment: the rain settles into a softer, steadier register against the glass — the sharper drum of it easing into a low even wash, the grey curtain on the windows beside them drawing a shade closer — and the warmth folds another half-degree deeper around the two of them in the back corner, the murmur of the half-full room receding to a further, gentler distance, the shelter closing wholly over the joined hands and asking nothing of them at all.
