A second voice has answered out of the corner now — the other body's, low and even, the same register as the first, kept close to the chair. The words ride just above the murmur and stay there: 'It really is. Best decision we've made all week.' No question lifts the sound back up; it settles. Both bodies have sunk a degree deeper into their cushions, weight given fully over to the worn give of the armchairs. No one rises, no one crosses the floor. The fingerpicked track runs on, steady under the exchange. At the counter the line is still empty, the steam wand quiet. The rain keeps its even press, and along the bottom edge of the near window the condensation that began last tick has thickened by a breath.
