Mid-afternoon, deep in the lull, unchanged in its structure. The back-corner pair are now fully settled — both spoken, both content, neither reaching to fill the space; the moment has closed into a held one rather than an opening one. Their coats have nearly finished giving up the cold to the room. The slower track has minutes left before its turn. The espresso machine holds idle pressure; the line stays empty. Outside, the rain holds at its thickened pace — the grey unmoving, the shelter stable, the afternoon not yet tipping. The condensation along the front glass has advanced from a thin breath to a visible film at the cold lower edge, softening the city further; still unnoticed by either of them. A single bead has begun to gather weight where the fog is thickest, not yet heavy enough to run. The mid-room cup continues cooling toward air temperature. Nothing pending demands resolution.
