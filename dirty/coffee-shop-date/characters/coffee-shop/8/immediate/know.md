Mid-to-late afternoon, the lull holding, the light still in its slow imperceptible lean toward evening — the floorboards a degree more amber, the turn real but far off and unregistered by anyone in the room. Structure unchanged: the back-corner pair stay settled and joined, hands closed in the gap, no body signaling any move; coats long dry. The pending fact this tick is the playlist: the warmer track that has cradled the hush is now in its final phrases and about to end, and the track queued behind it runs a shade brighter and quicker — when it lands it will not deepen the hush the way this one has, though softly enough that it need not break the moment either. The espresso machine holds idle pressure; the line stays empty; nothing is about to run out. Outside the rain holds its softer steady wash, the shelter stable. The front glass is fogged low and another shade thicker, the film whole and unnoticed — the room's quiet record of how long they've been held inside. The mid-room cup sits fully cold. The only things in motion are the slow turn of the light and the imminent turn of the song.
