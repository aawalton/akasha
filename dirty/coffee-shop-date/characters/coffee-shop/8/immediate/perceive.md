Still no motion crosses the floor — the back corner holds wordless, the two hands joined and at rest in the warm gap, both bodies sunk deep, faces turned, breath gone long and slow. No scrape, no rising, no voice above the murmur. The warmer track has run on past its sustained middle and come now to its closing phrases — the melody thinning, the last figures unwinding toward the quiet space where it will end. Behind it in the queue the next track waits, a shade brighter and a half-beat quicker, cued and ready to lift the moment it arrives. The rain keeps its softer even wash on the glass, steady, the grey unmoving. The fog at the near window has crept up another faint shade, whole and unnoticed. The gold along the floorboards has thinned further toward amber as the outside light dims another degree behind the grey.
