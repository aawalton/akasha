Inert. Only the slow tending: the cold cup at air temperature, the fog thickening low at the glass, the gold easing toward amber as the outside light dims, the softened rain keeping its weight — and the playlist running its course, one track ending and the next coming on as the room's own rhythm dictates. The shop holds the joined moment as it is and pursues nothing; it does not choose the next song to serve the corner, it only plays what was queued. Content to let the afternoon stay unhurried and the touch rest where it landed.
