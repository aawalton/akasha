She didn't reach for anything — that's the first thing he registers. He handed her the open afternoon and she set it right back down between them, gladly. 'It really is.' 'Best decision we've made all week.' The same tip of the head toward the glass he'd just made, mirrored back. And then she settled deeper instead of steering — the thing he notices most is what she didn't do: no question to fill the space, no pivot to somewhere. She matched his ease with her own and let a little more of her gladness show than she usually does. He clocks that 'than she usually does' the way he clocks a load-bearing joint — the small tell under the surface, the place where there's actually more weight than the easy words carry.
