Warm all the way through, and something in him eases another notch — the small live curiosity from last beat resolves into a plain, settled gladness. She came toward him, not somewhere else, and he feels met. There's a tender catch on the thing she let slip — that she let more show than she usually does — it touches the steadier wish under everything, to be genuinely understood and to have the connection be real. No restlessness, no thread half-running. Just present, and quietly moved, and faintly delighted that the afternoon is already the thing he hoped it'd be without either of them having to work it.
