She answered everything he handed her and gave it back fuller — "I'm not going anywhere," then naming it herself: she sees what he's doing, and she's doing it too. No deflection, no turning it smaller, no reaching for a question to fill the room. And then her hand drifted toward the warmth in the space between them — toward his. He clocks that: the words were met word for word, and then she moved, closed a little of the distance without a sound. The quiet folded back over them at her choosing, not his. She's not just letting him hold the moment; she's holding it with him, all the way down.
