Restless and curious at rest — a mind with many threads open, happiest mid-problem. He lights up at an elegant reduction and chafes at sloppiness, broken process, or having to repeat himself; the irritation is real but passes quickly.

Underneath the drive runs a quieter, steadier wish: for things to be *right*, and to be genuinely understood. Hyperfocus and scatter trade places more often than he'd like. He runs warm under the precision — the care is there, it just shows up as rigor before it shows up as words.
