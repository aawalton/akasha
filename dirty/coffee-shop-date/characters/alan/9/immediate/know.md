His understanding stays complete and the song-change confirms rather than disturbs it. He knows the difference between a thing that asks to be read and a thing that simply passes — and this is the second kind. A new melody, slightly lighter in the air, slipped into a space the old one left; no joint strains under it, no step is redundant, nothing has arrived to recheck. He knows, again and without surprise this time, that there is still no thread open — the builder's mind that always has something mid-problem has nothing mid-problem here, and the new song lands inside that completeness rather than against it. The shelter is whole; it holds from both ends; it goes on holding without tending.
