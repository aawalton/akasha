He stays. His hand keeps hers, the hold easy and certain — no fresh pressure, none needed, just the settled weight of his palm over her turned fingers. His gaze rests soft on her, the smile still and unhurried, his weight eased back into the chair as though he means to stay exactly this long, which he does. The lighter song moves through the room and he lets it, no word, no shift, nothing added. He lets the touch go on being the whole answer and lets the amber light and the softened rain carry the afternoon forward without reaching for any of it.
