The song turned over — that's the whole of what comes to him. The melody that had been cradling the hush thinned to its last notes, a bare half-second of only rain on glass, and then the next one rose into the gap, a shade brighter, a half-beat quicker, opening softly under the room's low murmur. Nothing in the foreground moved: her hand still fitted beneath his palm, her smile soft and resting, her breath gone out slow. The amber leaned another faint degree toward late day; the rain held steady and softened. He registers the change for exactly what it is — the room turning the song over on its own quiet rhythm, asking nothing of either of them.
