To stay, exactly, at the length he said he'd stay. The want is unchanged because what it wants is unchanged and unthreatened — keep this by doing nothing to it. Let the new song play out the way the old one did, let his hand stay where hers turned to fit, let the quiet keep doing the whole of the work. The only motion of wanting is the faint warmth of wanting her to feel as met as he does, and that is already happening; honoring it means not disturbing it. Ask nothing of the moment but that it last.
