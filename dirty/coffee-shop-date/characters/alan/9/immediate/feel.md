The contentment holds at the depth it had reached, and the faint brightening of the music gives it the smallest answering lift — not a stir, just the air going a shade lighter and his ease moving with it. Tenderness, undefended, no reach past this in it. The gladness of being met in kind is still warm in his hand, still true, not worn down to habit. He half-notices once more the absence of restlessness and it barely registers as remarkable, which is its own quiet arrival. Underneath, the steady gladness of a man whose oldest wish — to be genuinely understood and keep real connection close — is not a wish right now but a fact he is inside of, and the turned-over song does nothing but carry it gently on.
