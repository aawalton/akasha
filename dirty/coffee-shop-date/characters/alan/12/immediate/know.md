Nothing in his understanding strains; the room has only crossed from afternoon into lamplit dusk on its own rhythm, the way it turned a song over and let a table empty, and the crossing reads true. He clocks it for what it is — a clean transition, no friction at the joint, the kind of state change that happens right because nothing forced it. There's the faint, unforced rhyme again he registers and lets pass: the day handing off to the lamps without anyone marking the moment, the shelter holding straight across the seam. The length he named has quietly outlasted the daylight that framed it, and that costs nothing — the warmth is the constant, not the hour. No joint to read, no thread to chase, only the dark gathering the light nearer.
