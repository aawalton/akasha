To stay, exactly, at the length he said — now plainly past the daylight that opened it and wanting it no less for that. Unchanged because unthreatened: keep this by doing nothing to it. Let the lamps be the room's light now, let the evening bead and swim beyond the glass, let his hand stay where hers turned to fit. The only motion of wanting is the faint, already-answered warmth of wanting her as gathered-in and sheltered as he is against the dark — honored by not disturbing it. Ask nothing of the hour but that it go on, and let the lamplit dusk be one more thing they're simply in.
