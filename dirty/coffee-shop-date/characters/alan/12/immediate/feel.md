Contentment gone deeper still, drawn closer now the way the room has drawn closer against the dark coming on outside. The crossing into dusk answered somewhere under his ease — the day failing and the warmth not, the shelter only the more theirs for the evening pressing at the glass. Tenderness undefended, no reach past this; her hand turned to fit beneath his still the whole of it in his palm. The oldest wish lived inside rather than wished for, and the deepening, darkening hour carrying it gently on — if anything the gathered-nearer gold makes the held thing feel more held, more chosen against the blue going dark beyond the windows.
