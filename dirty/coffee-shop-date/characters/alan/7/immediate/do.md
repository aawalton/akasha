He stays. His hand keeps hers where her fingers turned to fit beneath his palm, the hold easy and certain; he lets the smallest answering pressure return through his fingers — barely a closing, just enough to meet the turn she made — and his gaze stays soft and resting on her, the smile still and unhurried. No word, no shift, nothing added. He simply lets the touch keep being the whole of the answer, and lets the quiet go on.
