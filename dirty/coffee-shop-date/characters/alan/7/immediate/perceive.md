Her fingers turned under his palm and fit there — a return, not just an allowance. He feels the small adjustment of her hand finding its place beneath his, and her breath going out slow, the whole of her easing further into the chair. Her gaze held his a beat longer and then softened, not breaking away but letting go of the holding, settling. No word came to mark it and none was needed. The beat didn't open anything. It completed the same motion from her side.
