Settled past words and now warmer for the return — the floor under him from last beat hasn't moved, but something has eased on top of it, a quiet gladness at being met in kind. The wish to be understood and to have it be real isn't a question anymore; it's simply true in his hand, and her hand turning under his is that truth coming back to him. Tenderness, undefended, with no reach past this in it. The restlessness is still absent and he still half-notices its absence, the way you keep noticing the rain has stopped. No thread open anywhere. If anything the contentment has gone a shade deeper — not louder, just more fully arrived.
