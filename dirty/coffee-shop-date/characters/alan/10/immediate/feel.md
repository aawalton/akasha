The contentment holds at depth, and the room going one quieter gives it the faintest answering settle — not a stir, just the space loosening around them and his ease moving with it, the shelter feeling a shade more theirs for the gap left behind. Tenderness, undefended, no reach past this. The gladness of being met in kind is still warm in his hand, still true. The absence of restlessness barely registers now — its own quiet arrival, twice over. Underneath, the steady fact of a man whose oldest wish, to be genuinely understood and keep real connection close, is not a wish right now but a thing he is inside of, and the fading bell, the thinning room, the leaning light only carry it gently on.
