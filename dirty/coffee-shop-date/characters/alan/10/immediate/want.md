To stay, exactly, at the length he said he'd stay. The want is unchanged because what it wants is unthreatened — keep this by doing nothing to it. Let the room empty on its own, let the quiet widen, let his hand stay where hers turned to fit. The only motion of wanting is the faint warmth of wanting her to feel as met and as sheltered as he does, and that is already true; honoring it means not disturbing it. Ask nothing of the moment but that it last, and let the widening hour be one more thing they're simply in.
