His understanding holds and the leaving only confirms it. He knows this kind of motion — a thing that passes versus a thing that asks to be read — and a couple stepping out into the rain is plainly the first. No joint strains; the room loses one table and the quiet widens to fit it, and that widening, if anything, draws the shelter a degree closer rather than thinner. He notices, almost wryly and without it pulling at him, that the emptying works in their favor — fewer at the edges, more of the slack hour theirs. Still no thread open, still nothing mid-problem; the builder's mind that always has something to chase has, here, only this, and the departure lands inside that completeness rather than disturbing it. The shelter holds from both ends and goes on holding without tending.
