Contentment, still at depth and going deeper with the room — the machine's small settling answered somewhere under his ease, the shelter another shade more theirs as the light leans toward evening. Tenderness undefended, no reach past this. The warmth of her hand turned to fit beneath his is still true in his palm, still the whole of it. The absence of restlessness no longer even a thing he marks; it has become the ground he's standing on. Underneath, the steady fact of a man whose oldest wish — to be genuinely understood, to keep real connection close — is not being wished for but lived inside, and the deepening hour carries it gently on.
