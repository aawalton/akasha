He stays. His hand keeps hers, the hold easy and certain, no fresh pressure and none needed — just the settled weight of his palm over her resting fingers. His gaze stays soft on her, the smile still and unhurried, his weight eased back into the chair as though he means to stay exactly this long, which he does. The machine's sigh and the deepening amber and the steady rain all pass without a word, the touch going on being the whole answer, the long afternoon carried forward without his reaching for any of it.
