To stay, exactly, at the length he said. Unchanged because unthreatened — keep this by doing nothing to it. Let the room sink further into its stillness, let the light go on leaning toward evening, let his hand stay where hers turned to fit. The only motion of wanting is the faint, already-answered warmth of wanting her as sheltered as he is — honored by not disturbing it. Ask nothing of the hour but that it last, and let the deepening quiet be one more thing they're simply in.
