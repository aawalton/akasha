Nothing in his understanding shifts; the room only sinks a degree further into its own slack-hour stillness, and that settling reads true. He clocks the machine's sigh for exactly what it is — the small mechanical fact of a quiet system at rest, pressure released because nothing is asking. There's a faint, unforced rhyme in it he registers and lets pass: the room loosening the way he has, both of them idled down into the same stillness, nothing called on, nothing strained. No joint to read, no thread to chase. The shelter holds from both ends and goes on holding untended, and the long quiet only deepens what was already whole.
