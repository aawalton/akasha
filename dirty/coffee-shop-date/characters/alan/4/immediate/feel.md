Settled and warm, moved a shade deeper than last beat — the tender catch from before has eased into something fuller and more at rest. He feels the relief of having said the true thing plainly and had it received. There's a quiet gladness that he didn't have to be clever, that the care could just show as words for once instead of rigor. Faintly delighted, wholly present, no restlessness anywhere. The steadier wish under everything — to be understood, to have the connection be real — is being met in real time, and he can feel it being met.
