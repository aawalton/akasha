All the way settled now, and steadier for the touch landing. The relief from last beat has gone quiet and certain — not a wave anymore but a floor under him. The standing wish to be understood and to have it be real isn't a question being answered tick by tick; it's just true in his hand right now, and he can feel the weight of it without having to look at it. A near-wordless tenderness, warm and undefended, with no edge of wanting-more in it — he's not reaching past this, he's in it. No thread open anywhere in him. The restlessness that usually hums under everything is simply absent, and he notices its absence the way you notice rain stopping.
