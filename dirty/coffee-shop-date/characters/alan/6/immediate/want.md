To stay. The want is fully out of words and out of motion now — not to do the next thing, not even to close a distance, because the distance is closed. He wants to let the touch be exactly what it is and not improve it, not narrate it, not move it anywhere. To hold her gaze and let the quiet hold both of them. To be here at the length he already said he'd stay, with nothing asked of the moment but that it continue. The whole of it is: keep this, by doing nothing to it.
