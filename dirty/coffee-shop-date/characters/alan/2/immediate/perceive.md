He said his piece — "this is good, made it just in time" — and let it hang open for her. Now the beat belongs to the silence again, but a different silence than before: this one has his offering in it, waiting. Abby's across from him, hood back, settled in close, the rain a grey wash on the glass beside them. He's watching her face for what comes next — whether she takes the open afternoon and runs somewhere with it, or just meets the ease and lets it sit. The room's still soft around them, murmur and hiss and warmth.
