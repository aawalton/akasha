The contentment has settled another faint degree deeper — not louder, just more fully arrived, the way a quiet you've stopped guarding becomes simply the air. Tenderness, undefended, with no reach past this in it. The gladness of being met in kind hasn't faded into habit; it's still warm in his hand, still true. He half-notices, again, the absence of restlessness — the rain-has-stopped noticing — and this time it barely registers as remarkable, which is its own kind of arrival. Underneath: the steady gladness of a man whose oldest wish, to be genuinely understood and to keep real connection close, is not a wish right now but a fact he's inside of.
