His understanding is complete and stays complete. There is nothing structural left to check and nothing has arrived to recheck it. He knows what the passing degree of warmth in the room confirms rather than alters: this holds, from both ends, and it goes on holding without tending. The same trust he gives the off-feeling that says dig now says the opposite — no joint strains, no step is redundant, the design is finished and correct as it stands. He knows, too, the rarest part for him: that there is no thread open. The builder's mind that always has something mid-problem has nothing mid-problem here, and he knows that not as a lack but as the shape of a thing rightly done.
