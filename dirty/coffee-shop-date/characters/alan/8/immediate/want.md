To stay, exactly. The want is unchanged because the thing it wants is unchanged and unthreatened — keep this, by doing nothing to it. Let the hour go on the way it's going on, let his hand stay where hers turned to fit, let the quiet keep doing the whole of the work. The only motion of wanting is the faint warmth of wanting her to feel as met as he does, and that's already happening; honoring it means not disturbing it. To be here at the length he said he'd stay, asking nothing of the moment but that it last.
