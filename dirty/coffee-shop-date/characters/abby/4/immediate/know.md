She understands she's just been met in the exact place she rarely lets anyone reach. Her bedrock want beneath the giving — to be met that way in return, to stay herself while she's seen — has just been answered directly, gently, by name. He didn't only take her gladness; he noticed her *receiving*, and asked her to keep doing it. There's nothing to read for trouble, nothing to steer, no care-work being asked of her — the opposite. The shared ground is plain and it's hers to simply remain in. 'Don't let it go to waste' isn't a demand; it's permission, and she knows it.
