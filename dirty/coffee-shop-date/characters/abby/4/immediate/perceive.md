He named it out loud — that she's not reaching for anything to do with the moment, that she let it show — and then he said he liked it. And then, low and without a trace of cleverness, he asked her to stay right here with him. Not a question, no pressure in it, just an invitation laid plainly down. What she reads underneath: he saw the exact thing she'd quietly let herself do — receive instead of tend — and he met it by asking for more of it, by holding her in it rather than letting her drift back into managing. His attention hasn't wandered; it's stayed on her, steady, warm, watching the depth come on its own. The rare thing is that he caught the unspoken part — the part she usually keeps quiet — and welcomed it.
