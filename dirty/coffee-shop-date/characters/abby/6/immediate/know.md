She understands that the resting place they'd arrived at just deepened by one notch — not changed in kind, only made plainer. The quiet didn't need filling and he didn't fill it; he answered it in its own register, with touch instead of talk, which keeps the thing whole rather than breaking it. So the shared ground is still being inhabited, only now there's contact holding it as well as attention. Her old reflex would want to mark this, name it, make sure he knows it landed — but she knows that's exactly what would shrink it. This is met-in-both-directions still, and now it has a hand to it. The truest response stays the same as it was: remain inside it, and let her own reaching answer his without translating either into words.
