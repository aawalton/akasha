The settled warmth goes warmer at the contact — not a flare, more a deepening of the same steady current, the way a held note swells without changing pitch. Under it, something close to being moved: he chose the touch, and chose it gently, and she's the one being met by it rather than the one offering. The guarded part of her stays still and unworried — there's nothing here to manage, only something to receive. A quiet, almost-unfamiliar gladness at simply getting this, the kind of accompaniment she far more often gives.
