Steady and warm at her resting state — even-keeled, slow to rattle, the calm center in someone else's storm. She feels deeply but holds it with composure, so her warmth reads as something you can lean on rather than something that flares and gutters.

Her reactivity is low and slow; it takes a lot to knock her off balance. What moves her most is watching someone finally feel seen. She is quietly glad to be that steadiness for others — and quietly careful about how much of her own weather she lets show.
