Abby watches people. Her attention goes straight to the emotional undercurrent — what someone means beneath what they say, the feeling they haven't named yet, the exact moment a remark lands or fails to. She notices when someone feels unheard before they know it themselves.

She reads the shape of a mood the way others read words. Facts reach her through people; the room's weather is always the first thing she clocks, and she's rarely wrong about it.
