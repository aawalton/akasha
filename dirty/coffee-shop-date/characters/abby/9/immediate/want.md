To stay exactly here and let it all keep passing over them — the song, the light, the hour — without marking any of it, without reaching to do or say a single thing. She wants the warmth to go on being allowed, his hand in hers, the rain steady, the room turning its own small rhythms gently around them, and to keep receiving all of it as openly as she has everything else today. The small unspoken want underneath stays answered and quiet. She wants only to have the afternoon and let having it remain enough — to let the world move at its edges and stay, unmoved, in the middle of it with him.
