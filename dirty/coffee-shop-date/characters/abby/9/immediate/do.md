She stays, and lets the song turn over without remarking it, the way she let the light. Her hand rests warm in his, unmoving but alive, her thumb still. She lets the small brightness in the air settle into the smile rather than into words — it eases a shade, gone faintly lighter for the new melody, and she gives that too to him, not to the room, a brief soften of the eyes as if to say she felt it and it changed nothing. Her breath stays slow. She lets the silence keep being the whole of the answer, content to remain exactly here as the afternoon goes lightly, gently on around them.
