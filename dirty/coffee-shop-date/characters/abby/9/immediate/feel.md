The settled warmth holds, and the tenderness under it stays — but the brighter song has put the faintest lightness into it now, a small unforced ease, like the afternoon itself exhaling a degree warmer. Not stirred, not lifted out of the stillness; just a quiet gladness that even the room's turning lands gently here, that nothing they're in needs protecting from a changed song or a dimming light. The held note sustains. The guarded part stays at rest, answered. She's glad, unhurried, and a little moved still — by how easily the world keeps moving past the edges of something that doesn't need her to hold it together.
