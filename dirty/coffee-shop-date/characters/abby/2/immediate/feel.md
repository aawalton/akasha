Warm clear through, and touched in the quiet private place — the less-spoken want met without her having to name it. The gladness settles deeper: not the gladness of having delivered something, but of being here with him in something mutual. A small unguarded gladness she lets herself actually feel, since for once she isn't holding her own weather back to make room for his. Tenderness, steady, with a flicker of lightness behind it.
