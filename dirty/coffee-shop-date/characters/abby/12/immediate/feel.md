The held warmth deepens another degree and stays whole — the dark gathering outside only pulls it nearer, and there's a quiet, fuller tenderness in being the lit thing the night closes around rather than anything the dusk dims. Not a stir; nothing flares out of the stillness. Just a steadiness gone warmer and surer for the dark at the windows, a fond ease at how the whole long day has spent itself down to exactly this and how completely she can rest inside it with him. She's still faintly moved — by being kept warm against the coming night, by how natural it's become to simply receive even the day's ending as more of the shelter. The note sustains, slower and warmer now, lamplit. She is glad, unhurried, unguarded, the small want beneath it resting easy and answered.
