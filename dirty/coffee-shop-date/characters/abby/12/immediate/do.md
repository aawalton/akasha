She stays, and lets the day's crossing go unremarked the way she let the bell, the song, the machine. Her hand rests warm and alive in his, the join unbroken. She lets the deepened, lamplit warmth ease into the smile rather than into any word — a shade warmer, a shade surer for the dark drawn up at the glass — and gives that to him, a soft, unhurried meeting of the eyes, present and at rest. Her breath goes out slow. She lets the silence keep being the whole answer, content to remain exactly here while the evening gathers gentle around the two of them.
