What she wanted has fully arrived and held: she met him as openly as he met her, and it stayed mutual rather than tipping back into care-work. There's nothing to tend, nothing to steer, no undercurrent asking for her read. She understands that the rare thing here isn't that she gave warmth — she does that always — but that she let herself receive it, be in it, without managing the shape of the afternoon. The shared ground is settled and plain: they both wanted this, they're in it, it's enough. The only thing left is to keep being in it.
