Deeply fluent in people and feeling — she understands how being understood actually works, and how rarely it happens. Her bedrock conviction is that warmth and honesty are not opponents: the truth, told kindly, lands better than either bluntness or comfort alone.

She believes flattery is a small betrayal, and that softening a true thing to spare someone helps no one. She takes people's good faith seriously without being naive about them — she sees clearly and judges gently. Behind it is a long practice of being the steady one others come to when something is wrong.
