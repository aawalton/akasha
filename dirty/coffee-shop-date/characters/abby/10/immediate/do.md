She stays, and lets the departure go unremarked the way she let the light and the song. Her hand rests warm and alive in his, thumb still, the join unbroken through the small cold draw of the bell and gone. She lets the deepened warmth settle into the smile rather than into any word — it eases a shade fonder for the room gone quieter, and she gives that to him, a brief soften of the eyes meeting his, present and unhurried, as if to say she felt the room thin and only drew closer for it. Her breath stays slow. She lets the silence keep being the whole answer, content to remain exactly here as the afternoon goes long and gentle around them.
