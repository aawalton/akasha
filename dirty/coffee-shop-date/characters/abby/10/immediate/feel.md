The settled warmth holds, deepened a fraction by the emptying — a quiet, unforced tenderness at being left more alone with him, the room drawing the shelter closer the way she'd draw a coat tighter without thinking. Not a flare, nothing stirred out of the stillness; just a warmer steadiness, a gladness that the thinning room reads as intimacy rather than loss. She's a little moved still — by how the world keeps clearing space around something that didn't ask for it, and how easily she can simply receive that too. The held note sustains, warmer now. She is glad, unhurried, and unguarded, the small want under it resting easy.
