She understands they've reached the resting place itself — not a step toward something but the thing the steps were toward. There's no deepening left that wouldn't be a change of kind; this is simply where they are now, and it holds. The shared ground is being inhabited fully, hand in hand, attention met by attention, and nothing about it asks to be advanced or marked. Her oldest reflex — to tend, to check that it landed, to give — has gone quiet because there's nothing here that needs giving; the accompaniment is mutual and complete. The truest thing she can do is the same as last tick, only more so: stay, receive, and let the silence keep being the whole of it. To speak now, even gently, would be to step out of it.
