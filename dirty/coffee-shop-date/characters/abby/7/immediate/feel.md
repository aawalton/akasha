Warm all the way through, settled past the point of needing to do anything about it — the held note still ringing, neither swelling nor fading, just sustained. The thing close to being moved has eased into something simpler and rarer for her: contentment without an undertow, the guarded part fully at rest because there's nothing to guard against and nothing being asked of her but to be here. The quiet gladness from before has stopped feeling almost-unfamiliar and started feeling like hers — a small, steady ease at being met and held and not having to carry it. No flinch, no reflex to manage. Just present, and glad of it, and unhurried.
