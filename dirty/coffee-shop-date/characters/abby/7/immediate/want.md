To stay exactly here and let it last — to not move, not speak, not improve on a thing that's already whole. To keep receiving this as openly as she's let everything else show today, hand in his, breath slow, the quiet intact. The small unspoken want underneath has gone quiet because it's being answered: she wanted to be met this way and she is, and she wants only for it to keep being allowed, with no part of her reaching to tend or mark or close it. To simply have the afternoon, and him, and the warmth, and let having it be enough.
