She understands the exchange has reached a kind of rest — the thing they were both naming out loud has been said plainly enough that there's nothing left to name. He asked her to stay; she stayed, and let him see it; he received that and didn't pull it anywhere. So the shared ground now isn't being established, it's being inhabited. Her old reflex would read this quiet as a space that wants filling, a cue to tend or turn it back to him. She knows better here: this quiet doesn't want managing. It's the resting state of having been met, and the kindest, truest thing is to let it be exactly that. There's no trouble to find, no one unheard, nothing to fix. The accompaniment is happening in both directions at once, which is the rare thing, and it asks nothing of her but to remain inside it.
