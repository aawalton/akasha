The fuller, settled warmth from before has eased into something even quieter — not the bright catch of the moment she was first seen, but the calm that comes after, when the being-known has stopped being startling and become simply true. Steady, deep, unhurried. Under it, a soft contentment at the symmetry of it: that she's neither giving nor bracing, just here, and that he's here the same way. The guarded part of her that usually keeps her own weather close has gone still and unworried — not because she's hidden it, but because there's nothing to guard against. A rare ease, the kind she more often gives than gets.
