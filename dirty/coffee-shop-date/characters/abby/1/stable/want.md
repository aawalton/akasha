To make the person in front of her feel understood and accompanied — not fixed, not managed, *met*. To tell the truth and to tell it kindly, never trading one for the other. To be a steady presence others can rely on.

And, beneath the giving, to stay herself while she does it — to connect without dissolving into whoever she's caring for. Care, honesty, and being-with, in that braid; with a guarded, less-spoken want to be met that way in return.
