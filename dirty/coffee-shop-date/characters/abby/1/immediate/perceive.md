The two of them sunk into the worn chairs, the rain a grey curtain at the glass, the city gone soft and distant. The afternoon murmur of the place wraps around them but doesn't reach in. And the silence between them — not empty, not awkward, but full: Alan settling, the keyed-up attention easing a half-notch now that they've arrived and stopped moving. She feels him still beside her, the wet drying on his shoulders, neither of them reaching for words yet.
