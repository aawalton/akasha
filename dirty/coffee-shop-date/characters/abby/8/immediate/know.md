She understands that this isn't a moment that peaked and will now ebb — it's a place they can stay, and the staying is holding. The light changing without breaking the warmth tells her something: that what they've arrived at doesn't depend on being maintained or watched. It keeps without tending. Her old reflex to check that a thing has landed has nothing to do here; the landing is complete and self-sustaining. The shared ground is just being lived in, and the hour passing over it only deepens the truth of how settled it is. There's no next thing this asks for. The only fidelity to it is to let it keep being exactly what it is, and to let the time pass through it the way the light is.
