To stay exactly here and let the hour keep passing over them unhurried — to not improve on it, not mark the turning of the light, not reach to do or say a single thing. She wants the warmth to go on being allowed, his hand in hers, the rain steady, the room dimming gently around them, and to keep receiving all of it as openly as she has everything else today. The small unspoken want underneath stays answered and quiet. She wants only to have the afternoon and let having it remain enough — to let the late light come without needing to do anything but be in it with him.
