Warm, settled, and now with a faint new color underneath — a soft awareness of the hour turning, not as loss but as the sweetness of a good thing being allowed to last into the dimming light. Contentment without undertow still, the held note still sustained, but a tenderness has come into it: the particular gladness of watching the afternoon go gold-to-amber around something that doesn't need her to hold it together. The guarded part stays at rest. She's not bracing against the time passing; she's letting it be part of the warmth. Glad, unhurried, and quietly moved by how easy it is to simply remain.
