The settled warmth holds, and the machine's far sigh deepens it by a degree without disturbing it — there's a quiet tenderness in being in the one place the room's tiredness doesn't reach, in being the warmth the settling closes around rather than the thing that settles. Not a flare; nothing stirred out of the stillness. Just a fuller steadiness, a fond ease at how completely the afternoon has cleared down to this and how completely she can simply rest inside it. She's still faintly moved — by the room exhaling around something that asked nothing of it, by how natural it's become to receive even this. The held note sustains, warmer and slower now. She is glad, unhurried, unguarded, the small want beneath it resting easy and answered.
