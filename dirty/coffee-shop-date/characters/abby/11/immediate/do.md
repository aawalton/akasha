She stays, and lets the machine's far sigh go unremarked the way she let the bell, the song, the light. Her hand rests warm and alive in his, thumb still, the join unbroken. She lets the deepened warmth ease into the smile rather than into any word — a shade slower, a shade fonder for the room gone stiller around them — and gives that to him, a soft, unhurried meeting of the eyes, present and at rest. Her breath goes out slow. She lets the silence keep being the whole answer, content to remain exactly here while the afternoon settles long and gentle around the two of them.
