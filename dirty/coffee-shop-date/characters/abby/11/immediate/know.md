She understands the room is past its busy register now and into something deeper — not just thinning but settling, the espresso machine letting its own pressure down because nothing has asked it to hold. The café isn't only emptying around them; it's going quiet at its very center, the last working thing in it bleeding off into rest. And she knows what that means for the two of them: the world isn't just clearing its edges anymore, it's exhaling — and the shelter in the back corner is the one warm constant the settling leaves untouched. The pattern holds, gentler than ever: light, then song, then a table, now the room's own machinery stilling — and none of it has crossed into the back corner, none of it has asked the joined hands to move. There's still no next thing required. The guarded want stays answered. The only fidelity remains exactly what it was — to keep letting it be precisely what it is, and now to let the room's own settling be part of the shelter too, not a thing to notice aloud.
