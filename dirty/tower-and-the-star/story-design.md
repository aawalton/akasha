## premise

Six young Chicagoans with rare Harmony Aspects — a bond-based magic system the Tower was built for — discover that what makes them uniquely powerful is also what makes them uniquely necessary, and that the Tower has been waiting for exactly them.

## themes

["Friendship as Power — Harmony Aspects require genuine connection to function; strength scales with bond depth","Found Family — six people who kept building family floor by floor out of shared danger and difference","Self-Acceptance as Power — each character's wound is a mechanical ceiling; Aspects grow as bearers become themselves","Individual vs Collective — their power only exists collectively in a world that rewards individual achievement","Connection as Knowledge — the highest form of understanding is perceiving and strengthening connections"]

## tone

Warm but not saccharine. Not grimdark — darkness does not win. Humorous (Nadia as valve). Serious stakes without constant suffering. POV warmth through Solenne's Trueweave perception.

## visualStyle

warm luminous urban-fantasy illustration; bright magical bond-light and soft city glow; vivid teal, rose-gold, and starlit violet palette; gentle radiant highlights; clean painterly finish, hopeful and full of connection
