  # The Beholder — Game System


  **System type:** *Acquisition.* The System overlays everyone with attributes
  and, for the rare few, an Onset power. Her power **Acquisition** lets her
  permanently steal from anyone she kills; it grants no powers on its own — she
  keeps only what she takes. No meta-meter, no tax, **no levels** — **theft is
  her SOLE axis of growth: every point on her sheet is something she took off a
  corpse.** OP snowball. Beauty is not a meter; it lives in her voice, her
  taste, and the **Allure** attribute.


  ## Attributes (scale: 10 = average adult human)

  - **Might** — physical force: striking, lifting, melee lethality.

  - **Vitality** — health, durability, regeneration, resistance.

  - **Celerity** — speed & reflexes: movement, evasion, attack cadence.

  - **Acuity** — perception & precision: senses, aim, reading a target.
  *Synergy:* sharpens her Acquisition appraisal.

  - **Will** — psyche: resolve, nerve, mental resistance; and a global
  multiplier on the output of stolen **powers**.

  - **Allure** — presence & aesthetic gravity: charm, enthrall, intimidate,
  command attention. Pure upside — makes prey hesitate/approach/trust/freeze
  (feeds the kills); the stat that makes her more beautiful.


  ## Theft (her cheat)

  - **One steal per victim.** *(A future acquired power may rewrite this.)*

  - Reader picks **ONE of the victim's TOP 3 notable traits.** **Every steal
  takes 10% of the victim's value**, permanent, stacking. **TWO categories
  only:**
    - **an attribute** — +10% of the victim's value (not of Pearl's own). Takeable from **anyone**.
    - **a power** — an Onset ability at 10% of the victim's mastery. Takeable **only from an Awakened victim**. **Will** multiplies its output.
  - **No skill category** (removed). **Growth is by theft, not practice** —
  nothing levels with use; a stolen attribute/power deepens only by stealing
  more of the same off the next victim. One mechanic: kill, take 10%, stack.

  - **Auditability:** each commit records the victim's source stats (ch1 audit —
  Colette Vane, unpowered: Allure 16.0, Celerity 15.0, Acuity 13.0).


  ## Stolen powers (one track: 10% per steal, stacking)

  Enters at 10% of the victim's mastery; grows only by stealing more of the same
  (+10% of each future owner's mastery). No ranks, no use-honing. **Will**
  multiplies output on top. No slot ceiling. **Powers are rare** — most victims
  yield attributes only.


  ## Worldbuilding: powers are RARE

  The Onset surfaced a few years ago and **most people never awaken** — they
  have attributes and nothing more. Only the awakened carry a power, and a power
  can be stolen only from someone who has one. Everyone has attributes, so
  attribute-theft works on anyone. This is why the banking phase preys on
  ordinary unpowered humans: attribute-only harvesting until she can hunt the
  rare powered.


  ## Targeting tiers (power-possession + magnitude — NO levels)

  She **ambushes via intimacy/opportunism — never duels.** Prey escalate by
  qualitative tier:

  - **Unpowered** — no power; attributes **8–22** (most ~10; exceptional humans
  mid-teens/low-20s). Attribute-only. **The banking phase.**

  - **Freshly-Awakened** — one weak/unstable power; attributes **12–30**
  (near-human — beatable by ambush; the *power* is the threat, not raw stats).
  Her **first power steals**.

  - **Established** — developed power; attributes **35–80**.

  - **Elite** — strong power; attributes **85–160**.

  - **Apex** — rare signature power; attributes **165+**.

  Fights read "up" via casting + powers/tactics, not raw stats. (The world's
  class-letters are this ladder, sans numbers.)


  ## Notability (how the top-3 is built) — NEUTRAL

  The System ranks traits by **Magnitude × Rarity × Salience** and surfaces the
  top three. The readout is **mechanical and indifferent**: it states each
  trait, the victim's value, the change to Pearl's sheet, and what the attribute
  governs — nothing more. **No personality, no preference, no emphasis, no pet
  names, no sparkle, no asides.** Unpowered victim → three attributes; Awakened
  victim → attributes with a possible power option. **Pearl's taste, glee, and
  "this one is beautiful" live ONLY in her narration around the screen, never
  inside the System block.**


  ## Canonical stat-screen format

  Mechanical only. No level line, no skill line, one decimal everywhere, no
  emoji/asides.

  ```

  ACQUISITION — APPRAISAL

  SUBJECT: <Name> — UNPOWERED            (or: — AWAKENED)

  PEARL  Might 8.0 · Vitality 9.0 · Celerity 11.0 · Acuity 12.0 · Will 10.0 ·
  Allure 15.6

    1  <ATTRIBUTE>   subject <V.V>   you <X.X> -> <Y.Y>   <neutral governs clause>
    2  <ATTRIBUTE>   subject <V.V>   you <X.X> -> <Y.Y>   <neutral governs clause>
    3  <ATTRIBUTE>   subject <V.V>   you <X.X> -> <Y.Y>   <neutral governs clause>

  SELECT ONE. ONE ONLY.

  ```

  Powered-victim power line (if a power is in the top 3):

  ```
    n  POWER: <Name>   subject mastery <M.M>   you gain 10% -> <0.1*M>   Onset ability; Will multiplies output
  ```


  ## Cadence

  - **Primary (every chapter):** kill -> top-3 menu -> reader picks. Unpowered =
  3 attributes; Awakened = attributes + possible power. One steal per victim
  (multi-victim = pick per victim).

  - **Larger forks (JIT, not pre-built):** Titles · Tier Ascensions
  (transformation beats at tier crossings) · Power Evolution/Fusion ·
  Acquisition Evolutions (steal 2/kill, sharper %, at range, without a kill) ·
  The Naming.


  ## Snowball reference

  Theft is **flat-additive** (10% of the *victim's* value, not of Pearl's), so a
  focused stat climbs **linearly within a tier**; the slope **steps up each
  tier** — the snowball is from eating bigger prey + stacking duplicate powers
  (Will-multiplied). **CRAFT NOTE: keep Pearl FOCUSED on ~2 stats** — spread
  leaves everything in the low teens (too soft to ambush by ch12); focus banks a
  combat stat into the low-to-mid 20s by ~ch12.

  - **Banking (ch1–12):** unpowered humans, attribute-only -> ambush-grade vs a
  Freshly-Awakened cape by ~ch12.

  - **First power (~ch13–15):** the dam breaks.

  - **Milestones:** superhuman among capes ~ch20 · dominant ~ch35 · untouchable
  ~ch60. **Dial:** 10%->12–15% later pulls beats earlier; canon 10%.
