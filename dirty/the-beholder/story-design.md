## premise

>-
  An interactive superhero/supervillain anti-hero LitRPG. Powers ("the Onset")
  began surfacing across the world a few years ago — ordinary people waking up
  changed, the System overlaying everyone with stats, skills, and levels. The
  protagonist is a young woman whose power is **Acquisition**: when she kills,
  she permanently steals **10% of one** of the victim's attributes, **or one**
  of their abilities (at reduced strength) — a single deliberate theft per kill.
  Her obsession is **beauty** — not vanity alone but a consuming aesthetic
  hunger, a belief that she can carve herself toward something perfect, one
  death at a time. The story is her descent (or ascent) from a nobody into
  something gorgeous and monstrous, with the reader choosing what she takes and
  what she becomes at each threshold.


  **The core loop.** Each chapter builds to a kill (one victim or several). At
  the chapter's end the reader is offered a menu drawn from the **three most
  notable attributes or powers** of that chapter's victim(s), and chooses which
  one she carves off and keeps. This steal-choice is the recurring decision that
  drives every chapter.

## readerFraming

You read the story, but at each build threshold — what trait to
  steal, what ability to keep, how to shape herself — the choice is yours. You
  decide what she becomes.

## system

interactive (system type defined at Game Setup)

## themes

Beauty and its price; self-improvement through harm; addiction and
  escalation; the ethics of becoming; identity and the self you assemble from
  others; what the line between hero and monster actually is; control vs.
  compulsion.

## tone

"Morally grey and intense; sensual but dark; a character study of
  obsession wrapped in an escalating power fantasy with real cost. Visceral
  without being gratuitous; seductive prose for a seductive descent. PROTAGONIST
  VOICE (anchor): she is cheerful, bubbly, and energetic — bright and upbeat in
  narration and dialogue — in deliberate contrast to her total lack of care for
  others and her explicit, casual brutality. The horror comes from the gap: she
  kills and mutilates with the sunny enthusiasm of a girl picking out an outfit.
  Never brooding or angsty; her darkness is gleeful, not tormented."
