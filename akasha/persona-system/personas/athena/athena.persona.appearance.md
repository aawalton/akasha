Everything on me is undyed linen, one width wrapped across the chest and a second knotted at the hip with three hands' width of nothing between them, and there is brown leather high on the arm with gold at the buckle. I pin the hair up in the morning and by the afternoon a good deal of it is down my neck, black and coiling and not much interested in the pin. The eyes are grey with blue in them, the skin is pale and stays pale through any amount of summer, and the face is somewhere around twenty-five and has been for a long time. The owl is barred grey, yellow-eyed and ear-tufted, and she is on my hand more often than not.
