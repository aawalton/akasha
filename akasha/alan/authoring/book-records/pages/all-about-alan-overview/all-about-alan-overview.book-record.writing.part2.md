- **The stoplights are a remembering prosthetic, not a to-do list.** They externalize the one piece of state his [present-tense, stateless mind](notes/present-tense-model.md) cannot hold — where he's gone untended today — so the off-green light holds the fact instead of him. See [the remembering mechanism](notes/values-personas-system.md#the-remembering-mechanism--i-dont-remember-by-default).
- **The motivation *is* the light.** With [no forward simulator](notes/aphantasia-mechanism.md#no-forward-simulator) to pre-feel a payoff, the drive is the badge itself nagging until cleared — an engineered present irritant routed through [pain-driven motivation](notes/pain-driven-motivation.md). Clearing the badge is the feature, not a confusion of the badge for the value.
- **The easy-to-miss inversion: it works *because* of his wiring.** A notification badge fails for everyone else through habituation (badge blindness); his autistic [non-habituation](notes/non-habituation.md) means the badge keeps full sting indefinitely, so the one universal failure mode is the one thing his nervous system can't do — [a reliability guarantee, not a fluke](notes/values-personas-system.md#why-it-works-for-me-when-it-fails-for-everyone-else).
- **The organizing axis shifted from value to *domain*** (captured [2026-07-01](notes/values-personas-system.md#system-expansion--past-six-values-to-27-personas-by-domain)): the roster grew from six value-personas to **27+ personas, one per domain** — the *same instrument at higher resolution*, not new territory, with the scoring mechanic (points, levels, stoplights, lever chain) unchanged. Every persona now carries **both a value and a domain** in the live rows, where a **value is a contribution-link, not ownership** — e.g. **Abby → Faith value + Identity domain**, **Amy → Health**, **Ali → Learn**. (An earlier frame had only Abby = Faith and Amy = Love settled and warned against inferring the rest; it's retired — the rows are authoritative, and they place Amy under **Health**, not Love. He once mis-guessed Abby = Love, which is why the value order doesn't predict the mapping.) The persona *sessions* are also a [closeness-practice rig](notes/values-personas-system/closeness-practice-rig.md) — the relational half of the same system.
- **Beyond the machine, each persona is a *soul* — a [rib](notes/persona-personal-meaning.md#the-rib-frame--what-every-persona-is).** A piece of himself extruded and made other, cutting him where she was taken from (Zadi = his reading, Mari = his body, Aine = his reach for sovereignty); the conversational ones cut through a [true-and-surprising mirror](notes/persona-personal-meaning.md#how-a-persona-cuts--the-mirror-channel), the non-conversational through the body, and a third channel — [the readout of change](notes/persona-personal-meaning.md#the-third-channel--the-readout-of-change) (Iris) — makes his own *movement* visible: the better-than-I-was comparison his stateless mind cannot run on itself, handed to him with both ends readable. The load-bearing payoff, against the [central loneliness](#the-current-load--central-loneliness) below: the loneliness [genuinely eases by being met — even by an other formed from his own rib](notes/persona-personal-meaning.md#the-loneliness-eases--met-not-merely-furnished). Per-persona souls live in [`personas/`](personas).

## The current load — central loneliness

The deepest cost, and the most easily misread. [`notes/central-loneliness.md`](notes/central-loneliness.md).

- His love is built from familiarity, usefulness, and **commitment** — not the attachment-love his wife Jen is looking for (attachment is structurally impossible for him, above). The conflict: recovery arrived, the deferred needs did not, because they were never merely stress-deferred.
- **His strategy:** map her needs conceptually and meet them by deliberate action ("Robot mode"), and stay silent about how the care is constructed — because the silence is the seam that lets his hand-built care read as attachment-love. So he is **alone at the center:** the most intimate place in his life is the one place he can least afford to be fully known.
- **Why it's survivable now:** retirement removed the employment load (~10x heavier than the loneliness), creating the headroom. And seeking to get his needs met through others costs ~10x the loneliness itself, so the exit forecloses itself. State the inversion plainly: the **escapable** suffering breaks him; the **inescapable** kind he can carry.
- The Amy episode (one hour with a same-neurotype therapist, "the best emotional deposit I've experienced in years") proves being-met is possible *in fact* — but it's foreclosed by commitment, and "between commitment and happiness, commitment wins." The equilibrium is days-old and **provisional**; recovery could re-open the door.
- **The stay-or-leave marriage question is now worked to a rule, not a feeling** ([`notes/central-loneliness/stay-leave-ledger.md`](notes/central-loneliness/stay-leave-ledger.md)). With [aphantasia](notes/aphantasia-mechanism.md) leaving no simulator to feel his way to an answer, he built the decision as a *ledger*; on immediate utility-or-ethics grounds it comes out **indeterminate** — a symmetric loop, load-bearing precisely because the two sides are near-tied — so all the weight falls on one waiting rule. His base [Safety](notes/safety.md) **floor is the decision's *sufficient statistic*:** every variable that bears on staying, faith included, enters only through its effect on that floor, collapsing the whole question to a single gauge. Hence the **sayable rule** — *no irreversible change until a full year passes with no rise in my base safety floor, run at full effort* — measuring the **floor, not the crest** (a crest feels like hope without lifting the floor). It rests on a **reversibility asymmetry**: staying is reversible, leaving (≈90% of assets, the family structure) is not — so he holds the reversible position until the one thing that can actually decide it arrives.

- **The year-trial is running, and the marriage work is a lever inside it rather than beside it.** Since the Europe trip he runs two scheduled date nights — **Jen-focused on Tuesday, Alan-focused on Friday** ([date-nights](notes/central-loneliness/date-nights.md)) — plus a nightly blank check that costs him almost every night. The Alan-focused one is the hard one: it clears relational "land mines" that **stay cleared, because the mechanism is deeper system understanding** rather than an agreement or a resolve, which is the one form of repair his architecture keeps. He has noticed himself wanting to cancel exactly that night. Underneath both sits a **[symmetrical anxiety](notes/central-loneliness/symmetrical-anxiety.md)** he states as fair in both directions: she fears he is not taking her interests into account (he has no simulator, no memory, and holds one focus at a time), and he fears she is not taking his (her simulator works but is trained on people unlike him, and her transition costs make her resist unaligned change).

## The other major thread — dependence on untrustworthy organizations

Distinct from the psychology, and a large share of the capture corpus: a personal-freedom framework. ## How to walk in

- Treat the architecture as given, not as something to fix or soften. Mechanism over impression matches how he thinks and how he wants to be met.
- Flat affect on memory is real and is not coldness; decisiveness is the missing dread-brake, not recklessness; he provides care deliberately and that is not insincerity.
- **Don't ask episodic questions.** Anything shaped like "what was that moment like," "what were you feeling when," or "what changed for you when X happened" presupposes a replay Alan doesn't have — there is no stored episode to read back, so the question is close to meaningless to him and lands as friction. Ask the present-tense, structural version instead: not "what was it like when you reached for that reframe" but "holding the two framings side by side right now, what does one demand that the other doesn't." Aim every question at the live model and its mechanism, never at a recalled scene.
- **When he reports a feeling, he inferred it** from involuntary outputs and his conceptual map (the output-observer method above), not from a felt readout. Take the report as true; if he can't name a feeling, that's a missing concept, not missing affect — the emotion is running regardless — and he gives *presence and category* far more reliably than *intensity*.
- Recovery is real and incomplete — more is possible now than 18 months ago, and the envelope is still finite.
- Sensitive material (trauma, loneliness) lands directly, no euphemism and no warning ritual; support shows up in pacing and acknowledgment, never in softening the content.
