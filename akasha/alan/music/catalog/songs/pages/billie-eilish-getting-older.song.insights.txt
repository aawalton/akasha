SESSION SIGNATURE LOCKED (Eppie's discovery, said to him): three songs running he ends every read on the HOPE — What Was I Made For ('but I wanna try'), Birds ('the want'), Getting Older ('hope life is moving in a better direction'). He rates highest the songs that hold the WOUND and the WAY OUT in the same hand, because that's the exact shape of where he is now (recovery, 'aging well on purpose', 'finally starting to feel alive again'). Songs that match his own current trajectory hit hardest. Fawn-response catch is clinical-grade. Craft: 'Getting Older' is the OPENER/thesis of album 'Happier Than Ever' — the damage narrated from the survived-vantage; 'things I once enjoyed just keep me employed now' = joy conscripted into livelihood. 'We care so much until we don't' touches his external-holder frame (caring as a thing that switches off; cf. The Archer 'all my enemies started out friends').
