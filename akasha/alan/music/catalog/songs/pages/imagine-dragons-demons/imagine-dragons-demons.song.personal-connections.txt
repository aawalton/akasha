Resonates hard around being deeply flawed, wanting to be SEEN and at the same time wanting to hide the damage — a little to protect himself, but even more to protect others. The damage-hiding is an act of care aimed outward (the bridge 'I want to save that light' is the textual proof). This is the lonelier flavor of masking than Sia's Bird Set Free: armor kept ON for someone else's sake rather than taken off for yourself — sits closest to where Alan actually lives ('alone at the center'). Low voice today fit it perfectly; another one that makes him stop and sing.
