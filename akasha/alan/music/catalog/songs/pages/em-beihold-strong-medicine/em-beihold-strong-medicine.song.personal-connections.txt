Reads it close to literal (with a sense there may be another level): 'strong, strong medicine' — the pain and conflict of needing MEDICATION support to manage your psychology, and how even the people closest to you can't always fill the gap left by chemistry. The two lines that caught him most: 'a spider trapped inside her web, a home that's built to break' and 'wish I could shed my skin like a goddamn snake' — the desire for change and renewal, the wish that you could just BE DIFFERENT.
