
Hungary's 2024 immigration overhaul (Act XC of 2023, in force 1 March 2024) collapsed roughly two dozen legacy categories into a tighter set of purpose-specific permits and abolished the catch-all "Other Purposes" residence permit that historically served retirees and the financially self-sufficient. The new framework retains a residency-by-investment lane (Guest Investor), a remote-work lane (White Card), a self-employment lane, and the standard family / study lanes — but most non-investment lanes have tightened reporting, shortened maximum durations, or been excluded from the path to permanent residence.

Standard naturalization clock: **8 years of lawful residence** (3 years on a temporary permit, then 5+ years on a permanent / long-term permit, totalling 8), **B2 Hungarian** (de facto C1 for the exam), **40-topic civics exam** (20 history + 20 public administration), clean criminal record, demonstrated livelihood. Dual citizenship permitted; the US-citizenship retention constraint is satisfied. Shortened 3-year clock available only to spouses / descendants of Hungarian citizens.

## Active programs

### Guest Investor Program (Act XC of 2023, in force 1 July 2024) — INVESTMENT

The reintroduced "golden visa" — explicitly not a citizenship-by-investment scheme. Produces a 10-year residence permit (renewable +10) that feeds the standard naturalization timeline.

- **Threshold**: EUR 250,000 in a Hungarian National Bank-registered Real Estate Investment Fund (REIF) where ≥40% of net assets are Hungarian residential property; 5-year minimum hold. Two currently accredited funds: Gravitas Hungary Real Estate Fund and SPRINT Hungary Real Estate Development and Investment Fund. The direct-property-purchase option scheduled for January 2025 was cancelled December 20, 2024; the EUR 1M university-trust donation route exists but is above the project's EUR 500k ceiling.
- **Validity**: 10 years, renewable once for 10 more (20-year maximum).
- **Physical presence**: None to maintain the permit.
- **Citizenship clock**: Counts in full toward the 8-year clock. Holder must first obtain national permanent residence (after 3 years of qualifying temporary residence with ≥183 days/year physical presence), then accumulate 5+ further years for a total of 8.
- **Family**: Spouse, minor children, dependent parents included.
- **Processing**: ~6 months end-to-end (1 month due diligence, 1 month Guest Investor Visa, up to 3 months to complete investment after entry, ~2 months permit card issuance). Permit decision itself is 21–30 days.

### White Card (digital nomad residence permit) — REMOTE INCOME

Hungary's digital-nomad permit, launched 2022 and tightened under the 2024 reform. **Does not feed the citizenship clock**: time on a White Card does not count toward the 3-year permanent-residence requirement, and the holder cannot transition the same stay to family reunification or PR. To preserve a Hungary clock, the applicant must exit and re-enter on a different permit category.

- **Threshold**: EUR 3,000/month income from non-Hungarian employer or own non-Hungarian company; EUR 10,000 savings; Hungarian address (rented or owned); non-EU/EEA citizenship; over 18; clean record.
- **Validity**: 1 year, renewable once for 1 more year (2-year ceiling). After the ceiling the holder must leave; a fresh application is permitted but the Hungarian clock resets.
- **Physical presence**: No statutory minimum, but the permit assumes Hungary as primary residence.
- **Citizenship clock**: Does not count.
- **Family**: White Card holder cannot sponsor family reunification.
- **Processing**: ~21–30 days.
- **Use case here**: Probe / lifestyle stay only; not a path-feeder.

### Guest Self-Employment Permit (Önálló vállalkozó) — SELF-EMPLOYMENT

Replaces the legacy self-employed track under the 2024 reform. Aimed at freelancers, consultants, and sole-proprietor business operators registered in Hungary.

- **Threshold**: Annual income, proven via the Hungarian tax authority, must exceed 24× the current Hungarian monthly minimum wage (HUF 322,800 in 2026, i.e. ~EUR 20,500/year). Requires Hungarian business registration, contracts or invoices, Hungarian tax filings, accommodation, and either Hungarian social-security enrolment or private health cover.
- **Validity**: 1 year initial + up to 2-year renewal; **3-year hard ceiling**, after which the holder must leave Hungary before a fresh application. Mandatory **quarterly E-Paper reports** to the National Directorate-General for Aliens Policing (OIF) with invoices, bank statements, and pro-rata income proof — a single missed report can trigger revocation.
- **Physical presence**: De facto required; permit assumes Hungary as primary place of business.
- **Citizenship clock**: Counts toward the 3-year temporary-residence prerequisite for national permanent residence (subject to the 270-days-absence / 4-consecutive-months-absence cap), but the 3-year ceiling forces conversion to PR or another permit category before the clock continues.
- **Processing**: 21–30 days.
- **Use case here**: Most plausible non-investment lane for the applicant's portable-income profile, but the quarterly-reporting overhead and 3-year ceiling make it a bridge, not a destination.

### Family Reunification — DEPENDENT ROUTE

For spouses, registered partners, minor children, and dependent parents of a Hungarian citizen or third-country national resident with a qualifying primary permit.

- **Threshold**: Sponsor must hold a qualifying Hungarian permit (Guest Investor, self-employment, and similar long-form permits qualify; White Card, study, seasonal employment, and guest-worker permits **do not**) and have resided in Hungary ≥6 months. Sponsor income ≥ ~EUR 1,500/month + 50% per additional family member, accommodation, and health cover.
- **Validity**: Tied to the sponsor's permit; renewable.
- **Physical presence**: De facto required.
- **Citizenship clock**: Counts in full. Spouse of a Hungarian citizen gets the shortened 3-year clock.
- **Processing**: Decision within 70 days.
- **Use case here**: Only relevant if the applicant first secures a qualifying primary permit (Guest Investor or self-employment) or marries a Hungarian citizen.

### Student residence permit — LONG-TERM STUDY

Issued to third-country nationals enrolled in full-time studies at a Hungarian state-recognized institution.

- **Threshold**: Admission letter; tuition paid; financial self-sufficiency; accommodation; health cover.
- **Validity**: 1–3 years, renewable for the duration of the studies.
- **Physical presence**: Full-time enrolment required.
- **Citizenship clock**: Time on a student permit counts only at **half** toward the national permanent-residence 3-year requirement (full credit applies only to the EU long-term resident card, which has its own 5-year clock). Student-permit holders cannot sponsor family reunification.
- **Processing**: 21–30 days.
- **Use case here**: Not viable as a primary lane for an adult applicant without a study plan.

## Excluded routes

- **Guest Investor — EUR 1M higher-education-trust donation**: above the EUR 500k one-time-capital ceiling.
- **"Other Purposes" / retirement residence permit**: abolished under the 2024 reform; the long-standing route for financially self-sufficient retirees no longer exists. Government discussion of a replacement retiree permit has been signalled but nothing is expected to be enacted before 2027.
- **Employment / Guest Worker / Investment-Project-Employment permits**: employer-sponsored, outside scope.
- **EU Blue Card**: employer-sponsored, outside scope.
- **Citizenship by descent / simplified naturalization**: requires Hungarian ancestry; applicant has none.

## Bottom line

Hungary retains exactly **one viable PR-feeding lane within the project's constraints: the Guest Investor Program (EUR 250k REIF)**. The Guest Self-Employment permit is technically viable for the applicant's portable-income profile but is bridge-only — the 3-year ceiling and quarterly-reporting burden make it a bad primary clock. The White Card and student permit are excluded as path-feeders by design. Family reunification is a derivative lane that requires another primary permit (or a Hungarian-citizen spouse) to anchor it. The retirement / passive-income lane is closed until at least 2027.
