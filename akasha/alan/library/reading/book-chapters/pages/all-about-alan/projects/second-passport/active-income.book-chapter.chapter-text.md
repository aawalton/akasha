## Self-employment / freelancer / sole proprietorship

All Tier 1 (183+ d/yr presence; ≤6mo continuous absence allowed). Family relocates as accompanying dependents on the main applicant's permit.

| Country | Path | Seed / threshold | Citizenship clock | Key requirement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Belgium | Professional Card (Flanders) → 5y CBN | €22,838 floor + business plan; ~€18.6k–€30k seed | ~5.5y | A2 Dutch + integration + 468 work-days |
| Germany | Freiberufler §21(5) → §10 StAG | ~€10k seed, no capital floor; liberal profession (IT / consulting / journalism) | ~6–7.5y | B1 German + civics + self-support; dual since Jun 2024 |
| France | Profession Libérale (Talent) → 5y decree | Regulated profession + ordre authorization; zero capital | ~7y | B2 French + civic MCQ + 5y French earnings |
| France | Création d'Entreprise (Talent) → 5y decree | €30k + Master's or 5y experience; viability attestation via demarches-simplifiees.fr (rule-based) | ~7y | B2 French + civic MCQ + 5y French earnings |
| Poland | Karta Pobytu JDG (business activity) | US-Poland treaty day-one privilege; renewal ~12× avg salary (~€22–25k) or 2+ Polish FT hires | ~8y + processing | B1 Polish |
| Romania | D/AE PFA | ~€9.8k subsistence proof | ~10–11y | 8y residence + B1 Romanian + Constitution/anthem |

## Top picks

**Fastest 5 (clock):**
1. **Uruguay 3y family-residence** — ~3.5–4y. Spanish informal hearing only.
2. **Belgium 5y CBN (Flanders)** — ~5.5y. 468 work-days + A2 + integration proof.
3. **Germany §10 StAG (Freiberufler)** — ~6–7.5y. Liberal profession + B1 + civics. Dual since Jun 2024.
4. **France 5y decree (PL / CE)** — ~7y. B2 + civic MCQ + 5y French earnings.
5. **Poland (JDG)** — ~8y + processing. US-Poland treaty day-one privilege; B1 Polish.

**Cheapest 5 (city f4 cost):**
1. Bucharest, Romania — ~€3,061/mo
2. Athens, Greece — ~€3,873/mo
3. Warsaw, Poland — ~€4,090/mo
4. Lisbon, Portugal — ~€4,425/mo
5. Brussels, Belgium — ~€5,264/mo

**Sweet-spot (clock ≤7y AND city cost ≤€4,500/mo):**
- **No surviving EU active-income path hits both thresholds simultaneously.** Fastest sub-7y EU is Belgium 5y CBN at Brussels €5,264/mo. Cheapest EU cities below €4,500/mo (Bucharest, Athens, Warsaw, Lisbon) all have clocks ≥8y.
- **Uruguay** is the sole sweet-spot survivor — Montevideo ~USD 2,500–3,500/mo at 3.5–4y clock.

**Lowest financial bar:**
- **Romania D/AE PFA** — ~€9.8k subsistence proof. Slowest active clock (10–11y) but cheapest entry.
- **Germany Freiberufler** — ~€10k seed. 6–7.5y clock, Berlin moderate cost.
- **Poland JDG** — US-Poland treaty day-one privilege, no upfront capital. 8y clock.

## Decision matrix considerations

- **Language friction**: Dutch A2 (Belgium Flanders), German B1 (Germany), French B2 (France), Portuguese A2 (Portugal D8), Polish B1 (Poland), Romanian B1 (Romania), Greek B1 (Greece DNV). Italian B1 (Italy DNV). Spanish (Uruguay) is informal — easiest. **No surviving EU active-income path has a no-language option.**
- **Capital outlay**: Romania PFA (~€9.8k), Germany Freiberufler (~€10k), and Poland JDG (no upfront) are the lowest-capital citizenship-feeding active paths. Belgium Flanders is the highest (~€22.8k floor + business plan).
- **Career-friendly DNV paths**: Greece DNV, Portugal D8, and Italy DNV all permit you to keep your existing remote consulting work without restructuring. Romania DNV has a 3-yr lifetime cap that forces a switch to PFA before the citizenship clock matures.

## Excluded — restrictive continuous-residency requirements

- **Finland** (Tier 2) — max 30 days/yr absence to count toward 8y clock. Toiminimi (sole-trader) on-ramp exists but the strict-presence floor is the price. Excluded.
- **Czechia** (Tier 1 → Tier 2 transition) — Živno temp phase is standard 183+ d/yr, but the 5y PR-qualifying window tightens to max 2mo continuous / 10mo total absence. Cheapest EU SE on-ramp by capital (~€6,400 own funds) — reconsider if the PR-phase rule is acceptable.

## Excluded — full-time study tracks (don't fit working-parent profile)

- **France Étudiant → Art. 21-18 reduced decree** (~4y total — fastest EU path on paper) — requires admission to a French Master's program and 2y full-time study attendance.
- **Sweden PhD residence permit** — full-time doctoral commitment; Master's excluded.

## Excluded — applicant unlikely to qualify

- **Iceland** — Business operation permit; Directorate of Immigration rejects most generic freelancer / portable-income applications.
- **Sweden** — Egenföretagare ~107 approvals in 2024 (down 33% from 2022); lottery odds.
- **Slovakia** — živnostník 700-permit/yr national cap from 1 Jul 2025; lottery odds.
- **Norway** — Self-employed sole proprietorship only on-ramp; UDI discretionary business-viability review with no rule-based alternative.

## Excluded — discretionary individual paths (country survives via rule-based alternative)

- **Luxembourg Independent Worker / Self-Employed** — national-interest test discretionary. **LU survives via Investor Track 1/2 (€500k)** at passive-income.md.
- **Belgium Professional Card (Brussels / Wallonia variants)** — case-by-case discretionary. **BE survives via Flanders (€22,838 rule-based floor)** above.
- **Germany Selbständige §21(1)** + **§7 Abs. 1 S. 3** — discretionary / catch-all. **DE survives via Freiberufler §21(5)** above.
- **Italy Lavoro Autonomo** — Decreto Flussi quota-gated. **IT survives via Investor Visa (passive-income.md) and DNV** above.
- **Poland Karta Pobytu Other Circumstances** — voivode-discretionary. **PL survives via JDG (US-Poland treaty)** above.
