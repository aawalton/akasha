
Portugal operates the broadest set of non-employer residency routes in the EU for US citizens — D7 (passive income), D8 (digital nomad), D2 (entrepreneur / independent professional), the Golden Visa (ARI) post-2023 reform, family reunification, and the D4 student visa. All are Type 1 (initial 2-year) → Type 2 (3-year renewal) → Type 5 (permanent at 5 years) titles administered by AIMA (the replacement for SEF since Oct 2023), and all count toward citizenship — though the clock was extended from 5 → 10 years (7 for CPLP / EU nationals) by the Nationality Law reform reapproved 1 April 2026 and signed 3 May 2026.

## Active programs

### D7 — Passive Income / Retirement Visa

- **Category**: Passive-income residency for retirees, pensioners, rentiers, holders of dividend / royalty / IP income.
- **Income threshold (Jan 2026)**: **€920/mo** (€11,040/yr) main applicant; +50% spouse (€460/mo); +30% per dependent child (€276/mo). Indexed to the Portuguese minimum wage.
- **Income sources accepted**: Pensions, dividends, rental income from non-Portuguese real estate, royalties / IP, financial-investment yield. Not active employment income.
- **Residence permit**: Initial 2-year permit, renewable for 3 years, then permanent residency at 5 years.
- **Physical presence**: Minimum **16 months in the first 2 years** (no absence >6 consecutive months or >8 non-consecutive months). Same rule applies in the 3-year renewal period. Tracked automatically via the EU Entry/Exit System.
- **Citizenship feed**: Time counts toward naturalization. Under the post-reform 10-year clock, A2 Portuguese + culture test required at application.
- **Processing**: AIMA prioritizes D3 / D7 / D8 over Golden Visa. Initial title typically **3–12 months** post-biometrics in 2026 (down from 18–24 months at the 2024 backlog peak). Comprovativo de pedido grants Schengen mobility while card prints.

### D8 — Digital Nomad / Remote Work Visa

- **Category**: Remote-worker residency for non-EU nationals working for foreign employers or foreign clients.
- **Income threshold (2026)**: **€3,680/mo** (4× Portuguese minimum wage) main applicant; +50% spouse; +30% per child. Average gross over preceding 3 months; bank balance ≥ €11,040.
- **Eligible income**: Salary from a non-Portuguese employer, freelance / contractor income from non-Portuguese clients, self-employment with foreign customers. Two sub-routes: temporary-stay visa (1 year, up to 5 renewals, no PR feed) vs **residency visa (D8 proper, PR-feeding)**. The residency variant is what feeds the citizenship clock.
- **Residence permit**: Initial 2-year permit, renewable for 3 years, permanent residency at 5 years. Same Type 1 → Type 2 → Type 5 ladder as D7.
- **Physical presence**: Same as D7 — **16 months in first 2 years**, no absence >6 consecutive months.
- **Citizenship feed**: Time counts toward naturalization on the same post-reform 10-year (7-year CPLP) clock. A2 Portuguese + culture test required.
- **Processing**: 3–9 months in 2026; prioritized alongside D3 / D7.

### Golden Visa (ARI / Autorização de Residência para Investimento)

- **Basis**: Law 23/2007 as amended by Mais Habitação / Law 56/2023 (Oct 2023) — real-estate routes eliminated.
- **Remaining qualifying routes ≤€500k (May 2026)**:
  - **€500,000** into a qualifying **non-real-estate investment fund** (Portuguese VC / PE fund with ≥60% Portuguese assets, no real-estate exposure).
  - **€500,000** into Portuguese **research & development** projects via accredited entities (universities, R&D centres).
  - **€250,000** cultural / artistic donation toward heritage preservation (**€200,000** in low-density areas).
  - **Job creation alone**: **10 new full-time jobs** in Portugal (8 in low-density areas), no minimum capital threshold beyond payroll.
  - **€500,000** capital injection into an existing Portuguese company creating ≥5 jobs for 3 years.
- **Residence permit**: 2-year initial + 3-year renewal (now 3+3 under recent regulatory tweaks; functionally the same 5-year-to-PR ladder).
- **Physical presence**: **7 days in year 1; 14 days in each subsequent 2-year period** — averaging ~7 days/yr. Untouched by 2026 reforms; remains the lowest-presence EU residency.
- **Citizenship feed**: Time counts toward naturalization. The **April 2026 Nationality Law extended the residency clock from 5 → 10 years** (7 for CPLP / EU); applicants who paid the submission fee before the gazette date retain the application-date start, new applicants count from **first card issuance**. Golden Visa itself was not touched by the reform — the path remains intact, only the citizenship clock lengthened.
- **Timeline to citizenship**: ~**11–13 years** total under the new clock (10 residence + 1–3 AIMA processing) for non-CPLP applicants; ~8–10 years for CPLP / EU. A2 + culture test still required.
- **Processing**: AIMA deliberately deprioritizes Golden Visa behind D3 / D7 / D8. Initial title **18–24 months** in 2026.

### D2 — Entrepreneur / Independent Professional Visa

- **Category**: Two sub-routes: (a) entrepreneur founding / acquiring a Portuguese company; (b) **independent service provider** (freelancer) with a service contract / promise of contract from a Portuguese-based entity.
- **Capital threshold**: No fixed statutory minimum. Bank balance ≥ €11,040 (one year of Portuguese minimum wage); practitioners recommend €50,000–100,000 demonstrable. Business plan must show economic, technological, or cultural value to Portugal — job creation, tax contribution, exports.
- **Independent professional route**: Lower-bar — proves an existing self-employment contract (or signed promise) with a Portuguese counterparty. Suitable for freelancers willing to take on a Portuguese client.
- **Residence permit**: Initial 4-month entry visa → 2-year residence permit → 3-year renewal → permanent at 5 years.
- **Physical presence**: Same Type 1 / Type 2 rules — 16 months in first 2 years.
- **Citizenship feed**: Time counts toward the post-reform 10-year clock.
- **Processing**: D2 entry visa 2–3 months; AIMA residence permit subsequent.
- **Note**: This is distinct from the StartUP Visa (IAPMEI endorsement-based, excluded from this snapshot).

### D3 — Highly Qualified Activity Visa (limited self-eligibility)

- **Category**: Highly qualified professionals with a Portuguese employment contract OR a **self-employed service-provider contract with a Portuguese company**.
- **Self-eligibility**: Limited — D3 requires either an employer contract or a service-provider contract with a Portuguese counterparty; not available to fully independent international freelancers. For a self-employed US applicant without a Portuguese client, D2 (independent professional) is the analogous route.
- **Threshold**: Salary ≥ 1.5× Portuguese national average gross wage (~€2,100/mo gross in 2026) OR ≥ 3× Indexante dos Apoios Sociais (IAS). Higher education degree + relevant specialized experience.
- **Residence permit**: Standard Type 1 → Type 2 → Type 5 ladder.
- **Physical presence**: 16 months in first 2 years.
- **Citizenship feed**: Counts toward 10-year clock.
- **Processing**: Prioritized by AIMA alongside D7 / D8.

### Family reunification

- **Category**: Spouse / civil partner, minor children, dependent adult children, dependent parents of a residence-permit holder.
- **Sponsor threshold**: Sponsor must hold a residence permit for **2 years** before applying (reduced to 15 months for spouses who lived with the sponsor abroad ≥18 months; waived for minors and for family of Golden Visa / highly qualified workers under Law 61/2025 amendments).
- **Income**: Sponsor income ≥ Portuguese minimum wage (€870/mo in 2026); +50% for second adult, +30% per child. Adequate housing required.
- **Integration**: Reuniting family must enroll in Portuguese language classes + civic-values training after arrival.
- **Residence permit**: Family members get derivative permits whose validity tracks the sponsor's title; permanent residency / citizenship clock counts from issuance of the reunified permit.
- **Processing**: Decision within 9 months statutory, with extensions in complex cases.

### D4 — Long-term Study Visa

- **Category**: Bachelor's / master's / PhD / accredited internship / 1-year+ volunteering. Non-PR-feeding-but-still-counted: time on D4 counts toward the 5-year permanent residency and 10-year citizenship clocks once converted to a permanent ladder.
- **Threshold**: Enrollment confirmation + savings ≥ €11,040 + health insurance.
- **Residence permit**: 1-year permit initially, renewable annually (or 2-year intervals) until course completion. Part-time work (≤20h/week) permitted during semesters; full-time during holidays.
- **Path forward**: D4 holders typically convert to D2 / D7 / D8 / employer-sponsored permit after graduation; permanent residency available at 5 years total legal residence.

## Excluded routes (not covered here)

- **D1 standard work visa** — employer-sponsored, out of scope per filter.
- **StartUP Visa** — requires IAPMEI accreditation (endorsement-based), out of scope per filter.
- **EU Blue Card** — employer-sponsored, out of scope.
- **Golden Visa real-estate tiers** — closed Oct 2023 (Mais Habitação / Law 56/2023).
