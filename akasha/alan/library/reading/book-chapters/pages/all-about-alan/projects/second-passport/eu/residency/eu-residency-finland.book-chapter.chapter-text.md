
## Active programs (applicant-eligible, May 2026)

### Entrepreneur residence permit (self-employed, NO Business Finland endorsement)

- **Category**: self-employment residence permit under Migri's `Entrepreneur` track (distinct from the endorsement-gated Startup Permit).
- **Eligible business forms**: `toiminimi` (sole trader), `avoin yhtio` (general partnership), `kommandiittiyhtio` (limited partnership), plus Oy/Oyj where the applicant works in the company. For `toiminimi` specifically, you must apply for the FIRST permit from abroad — a `toiminimi` Business ID requires an EEA-registered domicile, so you cannot establish one before arrival, but Migri will accept the application without a Business ID if filed pre-arrival.
- **Assessment**: two-phase. (1) The regional **ELY Centre** issues a partial decision on profitability — reviews business plan, financing, premises, professional competence, binding agreements, solvency, liquidity. (2) Migri then issues the residence-permit decision. No Business Finland endorsement involved.
- **Income floor (2026)**: net monthly EUR 1,210 (southern Finland) / EUR 1,090 (central) / EUR 1,030 (northern), updated July 2025 to reflect regional cost variation. Must demonstrate >=1 year of living expenses available. Until the company has turnover, livelihood may be proven from other income (matches the applicant's portable self-employment / passive income profile).
- **Personal-work requirement**: ownership alone is NOT sufficient — the applicant must personally work in the company in Finland.
- **First permit**: A (continuous) residence permit, typically 1-2 years, renewable.
- **PR clock**: feeds Finland's tightened permanent-residence rules (see PR section below). No capital lock requirement — well under the EUR 500k ceiling.

### Researcher / doctoral student residence permit

- **Category**: residence permit for `scientific research` under Migri (the doctoral track — separate from undergrad/Master's student permits).
- **Eligibility**: hold a Master's or Bachelor's degree, and have signed a **hosting agreement** with a Finnish research organization (typically a university doctoral programme).
- **Financial floor (2026)**: if not in an employment relationship, EUR 1,210/month available, i.e. EUR 14,520 for a one-year permit or EUR 29,040 for a two-year permit, demonstrable upfront. Compatible with the applicant's portable-income profile.
- **PR shortcut for degree completers**: completing a Master's, licentiate, or doctoral degree at a Finnish university unlocks a degree-completed PR pathway under the 2026 reform — PR available without the standard residence-years clock, conditional on a Finnish/Swedish proficiency threshold.

### Other private income / pension residence permit

- **Category**: Migri's general `sufficient financial resources` permit basis. There is **no dedicated retirement visa**, but private income (pension, dividends, rental income, assets) is explicitly listed as acceptable proof of livelihood for both first permits and the P-EU long-term permit.
- **Floor**: ~EUR 1,000-1,210/month equivalent demonstrable, >=1 year buffer. Income cannot derive from Finnish social benefits.
- **PR feeder**: yes — this basis counts toward the continuous residence clock (now 6 years under the January 2026 reform; 4-year fast track available with EUR 40k+ annual income OR Finnish degree + 2 years' work OR C1 language + 3 years' work).
- **Caveat**: Migri publishes no fixed retirement income tier; case-by-case discretion is higher than for entrepreneur/researcher tracks. Confirm with Migri pre-application.

### Family reunification (sponsored by Finnish citizen spouse)

- **Category**: residence permit on `family ties` basis, sponsored by a Finnish-citizen family member (spouse/registered partner).
- **Income requirement**: **NONE** when the sponsor is a Finnish citizen — family members of Finnish citizens are explicitly exempt from the income test (unlike permits sponsored by foreign residents).
- **PR clock**: feeds standard PR timeline (6 years under the 2026 reform).
- **Applicability to profile**: only if the applicant has or acquires a Finnish-citizen spouse — not a route the applicant can construct unilaterally, but the cleanest path on paper if it materializes.

## Permanent residence permit — 2026 tightening (applies to ALL above paths)

- **Effective 8 January 2026** (Amendments to the Aliens Act):
  - Continuous residence clock extended from 4 to **6 years**.
  - New requirement: **satisfactory Finnish or Swedish proficiency** (B1-equivalent in practice).
  - New requirement: **2 years of work or trade activity** before applying.
- **Four-year fast track preserved** if one of the following is also met:
  1. Annual income >= **EUR 40,000**.
  2. Finnish-recognized Master's/postgrad degree + 2 years' Finnish work history.
  3. **C1 or higher** Finnish/Swedish proficiency + 3 years' Finnish work history.
- **Degree-completed shortcut**: Master's, licentiate, or doctoral degree from a Finnish university unlocks PR without the residence-years clock (still requires Finnish/Swedish proficiency).

## Excluded programs

- **Startup Permit (Business Finland)** — EXCLUDED per filter: requires Business Finland endorsement letter.
- **Specialist permit (TTOL)** — EXCLUDED per filter: employer-sponsored.
- **Regular employed-person permit** — EXCLUDED per filter: employer-sponsored, salary-test-gated (2025: EUR 1,600/month minimum).
- **EU Blue Card** — EXCLUDED per filter: employer-sponsored, requires concrete job offer.
- **Digital nomad visa** — DOES NOT EXIST. Finland has no dedicated digital-nomad visa as of May 2026; remote work on a Schengen tourist visa is impermissible. The Migri-recommended substitute for remote workers is the entrepreneur permit above (already covered).
- **Investor / golden visa** — DOES NOT EXIST. Finland has never operated a residence-by-investment program; no capital-threshold lifestyle permit exists.

## Bottom line

Finland retains **>=1 viable residency path** for the applicant profile: the **Entrepreneur (self-employed) residence permit** is the headline option, with no Business Finland endorsement and no capital lock — only an ELY Centre profitability assessment plus the EUR 1,030-1,210/month livelihood floor. The doctoral-researcher track, private-income track, and (contingent) family-ties track are viable backups. PR clock is now 6 years (or 4 years with EUR 40k+ income), and PR requires Finnish/Swedish language proficiency under the 2026 reform — which then feeds the standard 5-year citizenship clock documented in `../citizenship/finland.md`. Finland should **NOT be dropped** from the citizenship summary.
