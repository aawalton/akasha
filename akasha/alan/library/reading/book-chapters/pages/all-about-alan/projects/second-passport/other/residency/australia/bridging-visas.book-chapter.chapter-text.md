
## Bridging Visas (Status-Maintenance Only)

- **BVA**: auto-granted when applying for a substantive visa while holding one; no fixed expiry; no travel rights.
- **BVB**: BVA-holder who needs to travel — apply before leaving, typically ≤3 months overseas.
- **BVC**: for applicants who applied after their previous visa expired; limited work; no travel.
- **BVD**: short-term, ~5 days, to lodge a substantive application or depart.
- **BVE**: for unlawful non-citizens taking steps to resolve status (typically while awaiting protection review or departure); reporting conditions.
- None are PR paths; they maintain lawful status while a substantive decision is pending.
