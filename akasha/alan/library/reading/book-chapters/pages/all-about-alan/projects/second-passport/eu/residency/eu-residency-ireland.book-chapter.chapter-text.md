
## Verdict for the target profile (US citizen, no EU ancestry, no EU spouse, ≤€500k capital, portable income)

**Ireland has no viable residency path that feeds the citizenship clock for this profile.** The only programme the applicant can self-qualify for (Stamp 0 — Person of Independent Means) is explicitly **non-reckonable** for both Long-Term Residence (Stamp 5) and naturalisation. Every other route is filtered out (employer-sponsored, endorsement-gated, family-based, closed investor, or capital-locked above the IIP's historical €1M threshold).

**Recommendation:** drop Ireland from the citizenship-by-residency shortlist. Keep Stamp 0 on file only as a long-term lifestyle/tax-residency option that does not lead to a passport.

## Surviving programmes

### Stamp 0 — Person of Independent Means (a.k.a. retirement / financially independent)

- **Threshold (May 2026):** verifiable annual income ≥€50,000 per person (couple = €100,000 combined, can be earned by either partner); plus a **lump sum equivalent to the price of a dwelling in the State** (working figure: ~€100k+ — must cover a major one-off expense such as buying property). Income must come from non-speculative sources (pensions, dividends, rental, established self-employment). Private medical insurance with full Irish cover is mandatory. Financials must be presented in tabular form in EUR and certified by an Irish-based accountancy firm.
- **Validity / renewal:** **annual renewal indefinitely.** Initial grant is 12 months; each renewal is again 12 months conditional on continued eligibility. (No 1→3→5 escalator like Spain's TIE or Portugal's D7 — this is a key downside.)
- **Physical presence:** none formally mandated under Stamp 0 itself, but renewal requires continued residence and continued income proof; absences may be queried.
- **Citizenship clock:** **does NOT count.** Department of Justice policy is explicit — Stamp 0 is non-reckonable for naturalisation and does not progress to Long-Term Residence (Stamp 5). This is the show-stopper.
- **Right to work:** none. Stamp 0 prohibits employment and any economic activity inside the State. Portable remote income paid by foreign clients into a foreign account is the typical compliant pattern; tax residency in Ireland still attaches after 183 days.
- **Processing time:** initial application typically ~4 months (some published figures cite 6-12 weeks for straightforward cases); renewal IRP card issued within ~15 business days of approval.
- **Recent reforms (2024-2026):** none material to the income threshold or reckonability. The 26 Nov 2025 family-income rule update applies to spouse/de-facto sponsorship, not Stamp 0.

### Stamp 2 — Full-time student (long-term study)

- **Threshold:** enrolment in a course on the Interim List of Eligible Programmes (ILEP); proof of funds (€10,000 access at registration plus tuition).
- **Validity:** issued per academic year; total study time on Stamp 2 capped at **7 years** across degree-level programmes.
- **Citizenship clock:** **does NOT count** for adults. Stamp 2 is non-reckonable. Narrow exception for 18-23 "young adults" who may piggy-back on a parent's reckonable residence — not applicable to the target profile.
- **Work rights:** 20 hrs/week term-time, 40 hrs/week holidays.
- **Verdict:** lifestyle/upskilling route only; no passport path. Excluded for the goal.

## Filtered out (with reason)

| Programme | Reason excluded |
| --- | --- |
| Immigrant Investor Programme (IIP) | **Closed 15 Feb 2023.** Was €1M minimum — already above the €500k cap. No replacement announced as of May 2026. Existing holders' Stamp 4 remains reckonable. |
| Start-Up Entrepreneur Programme (STEP) | Requires Enterprise Ireland evaluation-committee endorsement (innovative product, 10 jobs in 3-4 yrs, €1M sales target). Excluded per filter. |
| Critical Skills Employment Permit | Employer-sponsored. Excluded per filter. |
| General Employment Permit | Employer-sponsored. Excluded per filter. |
| Business Permission (self-employed, non-STEP) | Effectively dormant; outside STEP/IIP, Ireland does not offer a general self-employment immigration route for non-EEA nationals. Practical answer is "no such route". |
| Stamp 4 — Spouse/Civil Partner of Irish citizen | Family-based; requires Irish-citizen spouse. Reckonable; 3 yrs residence in last 5 → naturalisation via marriage. Not applicable (no EU/Irish spouse in profile). |
| Stamp 4 — De Facto Partner of Irish national | Requires ≥2 yrs prior cohabitation with an Irish national plus Irish-citizen sponsor income ≥€40k/yr averaged over preceding 3 yrs (rule effective 26 Nov 2025). Reckonable. Not applicable (no Irish partner in profile). |
| EU Treaty Rights (Stamp 4 EUFAM) | Requires an EU-national family member exercising treaty rights in Ireland. Not applicable. |
| Working Holiday Authorisation | US not on the eligible-nationality list; age-capped; non-reckonable. |
| Digital nomad / remote-worker visa | **Does not exist in Irish immigration law.** Third-party "digital nomad" guides describe the Stamp 0 route. No dedicated programme. |

## Citizenship clock reminder (for cross-reference)

Standard naturalisation requires **5 years reckonable residence in the last 9 years**, with **1 continuous year immediately before application** and overall presence in Ireland. Reckonable stamps include Stamp 1, Stamp 4, Stamp 5, and some humanitarian permissions. **Stamp 0 and Stamp 2 are NOT reckonable.** Full citizenship analysis: see [../citizenship/ireland.md](../citizenship/ireland.book-chapter.md).
