
# Regional Installers

Utah-HQ or neighboring-state companies with multi-state coverage that include Provo / Utah County in their service area.

## Auric Solar

- HQ: West Valley City, UT (Salt Lake area)
- Scale: Regional — UT, ID
- Provo coverage: Yes
- Note: One Yelp listing shows "Auric Energy" as CLOSED in West Valley — Auric brand has had reorganization; verify operating status before contracting
- Sources: [BBB profile](https://www.bbb.org/us/ut/woods-cross/profile/solar-energy-products/auric-solar-llc-1296-1000018230)
- Status: Operating but appears smaller / less active than 2018-2022 peak

## LGCY Power

- HQ: Lehi, UT
- Founded: 2014
- Scale: National-regional — direct sales arm operating across western states
- BBB: Accredited since 2019; A+ rating but with [persistent complaints](https://www.bbb.org/us/ut/lehi/profile/solar-energy-equipment-dealers/lgcy-power-1166-90013444/complaints) about deceptive sales tactics, oversized systems, roof leaks from improper installs, and slow service-call response
- Model: Subcontracts installation via partner network (similar pattern to old Vivint Solar)
- Red flag: Recurring complaint themes about misleading "tax credit / production guarantee" promises — pattern-match to historical Vision Solar / Lumio sales practices

## Sunder Energy (now SunPower-owned)

- HQ: South Jordan, UT
- Acquired by [SunPower Inc. (Complete Solaria) Sept 26, 2025](https://investors.sunpower.com/news-releases/news-release-details/sunpower-closes-sunder-energy-acquisition) for $40M cash + 10M shares
- Scale: 893-person 1099 sales force; ~5,500 contracts/year average $40K
- Model: TPO-heavy — 93% of installs are third-party-owned (lease/PPA); not a good fit for cash buyers
- Note: Acquisition made SunPower Inc. the #5 US residential installer; doubles SunPower's state coverage from 22 to 45
- Risk flag: TPO contracts in Utah are economically inferior to ownership given the Customer Generation Program export rate structure; only viable if you cannot use the federal ITC

## Vision Solar LLC (Utah)

- HQ: Provo, UT (5255 N Edgewood Dr) per EnergySage listing
- Founded: 2013
- Status flag: SolarReviews flags the broader [Vision Solar brand as "Out of Business"](https://www.solarreviews.com/installers/vision-solar-1-reviews) following bankruptcy and a state-attorney-general class action over predatory sales / unpermitted work; verify if the Provo entity is the same legal entity or a survivor before contracting
- Avoid unless you confirm distinct, currently-licensed operation
