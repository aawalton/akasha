
## Citizenship clock context

Poland has no CBI program and no investor-residency fast-track. Naturalization runs through the **standard residency clock**: 5 years temporary residence (Karta Pobytu czasowego) + 3 years permanent residence (Karta Pobytu stałego) = **~8 years total**, plus B1 Polish language certificate, stable income, and a voivode-discretionary "Recognition as Polish Citizen" or presidential grant. The Nawrocki administration's draft reform (in consultation May 2026) would extend the residency requirement to 8 years total *before* PR, raise the language bar to B2, and add a 40-question civics test; not yet enacted. Presidential grants collapsed under Nawrocki — only 25 approved Jan–Feb 2026 vs. 2,200+ in all of 2024 — so the voivode-led "recognition" track is now the realistic route.

## Active programs

### Karta Pobytu — Business activity (Działalność gospodarcza), sole proprietorship route

The straightforward self-funded path. US (and Swiss) citizens have a treaty privilege: they may **register a sole proprietorship (JDG) from day one** without first holding another residence status — a privilege most third-country nationals lack (other TCNs need long-term EU residence or a Blue Card before opening JDG).

- **Threshold (commercial substance test, required at first renewal):** one of two routes —
  - Generate income ≥ **12× the average gross monthly Polish salary** in the 12 months preceding application (~PLN 96,000–110,000 / ~€22k–€25k annualized, indexed by GUS quarterly), **or**
  - Employ ≥ 2 Polish citizens (or work-authorized foreigners) full-time on indefinite contracts for ≥ 12 months.
- **First permit:** issued without the income/employment test if the business plan demonstrates the activity will reach the threshold; voivode discretion. Up to **3 years validity**.
- **Renewal:** subsequent 3-year permits require the threshold to be met. No formal "renewal" — each application is a fresh permit, filed before the prior one expires.
- **Physical presence:** no minimum stay tied to the permit itself, but for the **PR step at year 5**, a single absence > 6 months or cumulative absences > 10 months over the 5 years resets the clock.
- **Feeds citizenship clock:** yes — 5y TRP → PR → 3y PR → naturalize (B1 Polish, civics, clean record).
- **Processing time (May 2026):** 3–12 months depending on voivodeship; Kraków/Małopolska fastest (~145d avg), Opole slowest (~578d avg). MOS v2.0 (mandatory since 1 Jun 2025) trims intake but not voivode review.
- **Includes:** spouse + minor children via family-reunification permit (see below).

### Karta Pobytu — Other Circumstances (Inne okoliczności)

Residual catch-all for purposes not covered by named categories. Voivode-discretionary; case-by-case. Used by retirees, financially independent applicants, and other "purpose justifying stay > 3 months" applicants who don't fit a named track. **No statutory passive-income threshold** — applicant must demonstrate stable funds, health insurance, and registered Polish accommodation; voivode decides whether the stated purpose justifies a permit.

- **Threshold:** stable income ≥ social-assistance floor (PLN 1,010/month single, PLN 823/person family, 2025 figures) — trivial vs. actual cost of living. In practice voivodes look for materially more.
- **Validity:** up to 3 years. Renewable.
- **Physical presence / citizenship clock:** same as business-activity route.
- **Risk:** highly discretionary. There is no guaranteed "retirement visa" — refusals are common when the voivode reads the stated purpose as a workaround for the absent retirement/digital-nomad routes.

### Karta Pobytu — Family reunification

Available if the applicant marries a Polish citizen or a foreigner holding Polish TRP/PR. Sponsor must show stable income and housing.

- **Validity:** **3 years**, renewable. After 4 years of marriage to a Polish citizen + ≥ 3 years PR, the applicant may file for **citizenship by recognition** (skipping the longer naturalization track) — this is the fastest documented residency-to-citizenship path for spouses of Poles.
- **Work rights:** automatic — no separate work permit needed; sole proprietorship allowed.
- **Processing time:** ~180+ days, voivodeship-dependent.

### Karta Pobytu — Study (long-term degree program)

Enrollment in a Polish university (full degree, not language course). 15-month first permit, then up to 3-year renewals for the duration of studies.

- **Threshold:** PLN 15,150 in available funds (15-month period, 2025) + tuition + health insurance + accommodation.
- **Feeds citizenship clock:** **half-time** — study years count as 0.5y per year toward the 5-year TRP requirement for PR. Materially weaker clock than the business or family routes for an adult applicant.

## Filtered-out programs

- **Poland Business Harbour (PBH):** restricted to citizens of Belarus, Russia, Ukraine, Moldova, Georgia, Armenia, Azerbaijan. The 2022 expansion added a "strategic projects" track for any nationality — but **only via sponsorship by a PAIH-registered Polish company**, i.e., an employer-sponsored route, not self-applicant. Excluded under the employer-sponsored filter for a US-citizen self-funded applicant.
- **Blue Card / work permit + TRP:** employer-sponsored, excluded.
- **Digital nomad / freelance visa:** Poland has **no dedicated digital nomad or freelance visa** as of May 2026. The "freelance visa" marketed by relocation agencies is just the business-activity Karta Pobytu via sole proprietorship (see above).
- **Retirement / passive-income visa:** Poland has **no dedicated retirement visa**. Retirees route through Karta Pobytu — Other Circumstances (above), with the discretionary risk noted.
- **Investor / golden visa:** Poland has **no residence-by-investment program** and never has.

## Recent reforms (2024–2026)

- **Jun 2025:** MOS v2.0 platform mandatory for all residence permit submissions; paper and in-person filings refused.
- **2026 citizenship draft (Nawrocki + MSWiA):** proposed extension to 8 years residence + B2 Polish + 40-question civics test + tax-residency cross-check via MOS↔tax data. In consultation as of May 2026, not yet enacted.
- **Presidential grant collapse:** 25 approvals Jan–Feb 2026 vs. 2,200+/year prior. Recognition-by-voivode track now the realistic naturalization route.

## Viability

Poland retains **≥1 viable residency path** for the target profile: the **Karta Pobytu — Business activity (sole proprietorship)** route is well-fitted to a US-citizen self-employed applicant with portable income — the day-one JDG privilege is a real advantage, the income threshold (~€22k–€25k) is achievable with modest portable revenue, and the path feeds the standard 8-year citizenship clock. The Other Circumstances track is a discretionary backup. The 2026 citizenship reform, if enacted, would lengthen the total clock but does not block the residency entry.
