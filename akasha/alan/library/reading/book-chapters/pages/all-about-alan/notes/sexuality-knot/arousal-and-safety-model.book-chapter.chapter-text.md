## The multiplicative hypothesis

The [two-channel attraction model](../attraction-two-channels.book-chapter.md) treats attraction as an additive **sum** — softness (safety) + directness (being-met). But my own data keeps showing safety *gating* arousal, not just adding alongside it. So I proposed a different form:

> I think a more accurate modeling might be arousal × safety = attraction.

It started as a hypothesis "that might explain the current data better." On a later pass I affirmed it: *"the multiplicative model is likely correct, and safety is a multiplier at all levels."* Not settled — the reconciliation with the additive sum is still open — but no longer a coin-flip; I lean multiplicative.

**Why multiplicative earns its keep.** In a sum, a zero in one term still leaves the other term's value standing. In a product, **either factor can zero the whole thing on its own.** Tank safety and attraction goes to zero no matter how high arousal runs. That is exactly what I observe — and a sum can't represent it, because a sum would still pay out the arousal half. The product makes the gate fall out of the arithmetic.

**Safety multiplies at every level, not just at zero.** The sharpening is that safety is not a switch that only matters at the floor. It is a multiplier biting at **all levels**: *"safety is a multiplier at all levels."* So the product scales continuously with safety — higher safety scales the whole thing up, lower safety scales it down — rather than sitting at full value until safety hits zero and then collapsing. The [−2 floor](#the-hard-floor--safety-2-is-full-autonomic-disable) is where the safety factor reaches its hard bottom and the product goes fully to zero; above it, every step of safety is already scaling the product, not waiting for the floor.

**Relation to the additive model.** This does **not** contradict the documented [safety-is-a-prerequisite-for-arousal ordering](../attraction-two-channels.book-chapter.md#safety-is-a-prerequisite-for-arousal--the-gating-order) — it **formalizes** it. "Safety gates arousal" and "safety = 0 ⇒ product = 0" are the same fact, the second written as math. Be honest about what's open: the [additive-sum framing](../attraction-two-channels.book-chapter.md#the-two-channel-resolution) and this multiplicative framing are **two readings of the same attraction system, not yet reconciled.** Which one is right — or whether the truth is some hybrid (a sum gated by a safety multiplier) — is a thread, not a closed question.

## The safety term is the safety of the input

The safety factor in arousal × safety is the **safety of the input** — the specific signal, its source, the context it arrives in — **not my background depletion state.** This is the reading that closes the question an earlier correction left open: if safety multiplies at every level, how does a self-generated arousal still do its regulating work near the floor?

The answer falls out the moment "safety" means input-safety. A self-generated input is the **safest possible input** — fully under my control, no unknown source, nothing to screen. So it scores high on the safety factor *regardless* of how depleted my background sits. Depletion was never in that term. The multiplier reads the input, not the floor I am standing on — so a self-initiated arousal holds the product high even when the background is low, which is exactly why the [regulation lever](regulation-not-desire.book-chapter.md#the-mechanic--arousal-and-climax-move-safety) reaches all the way [down to −1](../safety.book-chapter.md#sub-zero-territory). It is not bypassing a gate; it scores high on a multiplier that was always reading the input.

The same lens reframes a *refused* cue. When an incoming feminine cue gets [noped](../femininity-as-safety-signal.book-chapter.md#self-deploying-the-all-clear--the-singing-scaffold), it is the **cue's** safety that scored low — an unvetted external source — **not my background tanking.** Same multiplier, read on the input either way: a self-deployed cue scores high, an unvetted external one can score low, independent of where the background sits.

## The hard floor — safety −2 is full autonomic disable

The product has a floor, and it is sharp. At [safety −2](../safety.book-chapter.md#sub-zero-territory) the whole autonomic system goes dark — and arousal fails *with* it, not as a special case:

> Yeah, at -2 the SNS and PNS both effectively shut down. My heart rate won't rise with exertion (which means I CAN'T exert), and my body stops digesting food. So it makes sense that the arousal mechanism breaks down as well, since it depends on the SNS and PNS to function.

This locates the −2 cutout precisely: it is **not arousal-specific, and not a special gate on sex.** Both autonomic branches go offline at once — *"the SNS and PNS both effectively shut down."* So my heart rate won't rise with exertion (which means I *can't* exert), and digestion stops. Arousal runs on **the very same machinery** — the [controlled SNS-up / PNS-down cycle](regulation-not-desire.book-chapter.md#the-autonomic-mechanism--a-controlled-sns-up-pns-down-cycle) — so it *"breaks down as well, since it depends on the SNS and PNS to function."* Arousal, heart-rate climb, and digestion all drop together because they share that one axis. Sex needs a working autonomic system the same as a workout or a meal does, and at −2 there isn't one. That is the bottom edge of the model: below it there is no arousal term to multiply, because the machinery that would produce one is offline.

## One law, two output buckets

The same arousal can do two different jobs — but **not by running two different pathways with two different gatings.** It runs the **one multiplicative law** above, arousal × safety, the multiplier biting at every level. What differs between personal and impersonal arousal is the **target bucket** the output deposits into, not the law and not the gate.

> The difference between personal and impersonal arousal comes in the target instead. The arousal has the same physiological effect either way, but the personal side can also deposit in the emotional social connection bucket.

**Bucket 1 — physiological regulation.** Fills the **same either way**. *"The arousal has the same physiological effect either way"* — personal or impersonal, the regulation deposit is identical. This is the person-free lever; its canonical treatment — what it does to safety, why it could never be quit — is [regulation-not-desire.md](regulation-not-desire.book-chapter.md). It needs no person and no connection target to make this deposit.

**Bucket 2 — emotional / social connection.** Only the **personal, connection-aimed** arousal *also* deposits here. *"The personal side can also deposit in the emotional social connection bucket."* This second deposit pays down the emotional debt from the decades in [robot mode](../robot-mode.book-chapter.md), aimed at [Connection](../central-loneliness.book-chapter.md). The impersonal use never touches this bucket; the personal use fills both.

> Where the person matters is when I'm targeting not just the physiological impact but the emotional impact, paying down the emotional debt from the decades in [robot mode](../robot-mode.book-chapter.md). Then the person matters incredibly, because the primary target is [Connection](../central-loneliness.book-chapter.md), and the physiological benefits are incidental.

So **the person matters by selecting the second bucket**, not by switching the law or the gate. Same law, same physiological deposit; the personal side just adds a deposit the impersonal side cannot make. The personal/impersonal difference is the **target**, full stop — there is no separate pathway, no exemption from the safety multiplier, no second gate. (An earlier draft of this note read the split as *two terms of the equation* — a regulation lever running on an ungated `arousal` input versus a connection pull on the gated `attraction` output. That framing is **superseded**: safety multiplies at all levels, so neither use rides an ungated term. The split is in the output bucket, not the input term.)

## Room for movement — the distinctions

The model has more play in it than "sex" suggests, because three things people collapse into one are distinct here:

> I think it's important to distinguish "arousal" here from "sexual arousal", there are other forms of arousal that can deliver that side of the attraction multiplier, and attraction itself is related to but distinct from connection, and sexual arousal is distinct from having sex. So there is room for movement here.

- **arousal ≠ sexual arousal** — other forms of arousal can feed the `arousal` factor of the multiplier.
- **attraction ≠ connection** — related but distinct; attraction is the product, connection is the [scarcest good](../central-loneliness.book-chapter.md) it can point toward.
- **sexual arousal ≠ having sex** — the internal state is not the act.

The consequence is design room: the connection I am starving for **does not have to ride the sexual vehicle at all.** If the `arousal` factor can be fed by non-sexual arousal, and attraction is distinct from both connection and the sexual act, then there are routes to the connection target that do not run through sex. "There is room for movement here."

## The end-to-end model, closed

Stated as one chain:

1. **Arousal is an autonomic excursion** — an [SNS-up / PNS-down cycle](regulation-not-desire.book-chapter.md#the-autonomic-mechanism--a-controlled-sns-up-pns-down-cycle), the same shape as exercise, wired stronger. The regulation *is* that cycle.
2. **The multiplier runs on the safety of the input, not the background.** A self-generated input is the safest there is, so it holds the product high no matter how depleted I am — which is why the [lever works down to −1](regulation-not-desire.book-chapter.md#the-mechanic--arousal-and-climax-move-safety). An unvetted external cue scores low and gets noped. The read is on the input either way.
3. **At background −2 the autonomic system itself goes dark.** SNS and PNS both offline, so heart rate won't rise (can't exert) and digestion stops — and arousal fails alongside them because it runs on the same machinery. The −2 cutout is whole-autonomic, not a gate on sex.

No gate-bypass, no ungated term, no sex-specific floor. One law on the input's safety, two output buckets, and a hard autonomic bottom shared with every other autonomic function.
