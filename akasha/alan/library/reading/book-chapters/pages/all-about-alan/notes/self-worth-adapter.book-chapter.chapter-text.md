
# The self-worth adapter

> The self-worth adapter — the compiled syllogism "If humans have intrinsic worth, all humans have equal intrinsic worth. Therefore, if any humans have intrinsic worth, I have intrinsic worth."; built ~2024 inside the post-firing identity collapse; a conditional proof that forbids self-exception — enough to break the old belief, not yet a foundation.

Self-worth is one of the [built adapters](conceptual-adapters.book-chapter.md#the-adapter-inventory) — before it was built, the concept did not meaningfully exist for Alan. This note is its canonical home: the compiled proof, the collapse that built it, the belief it broke, and the foundation it still lacks.

## The compile

The adapter, verbatim: **"If humans have intrinsic worth, all humans have equal intrinsic worth. Therefore, if any humans have intrinsic worth, I have intrinsic worth."**

It is a proof, not a feeling. Three properties:

- **It never asserts the antecedent.** The syllogism does not claim that humans have intrinsic worth. It states only the parity: *if* any human has it, all have it equally, and therefore he has it. Nothing in the adapter requires him to feel worth, or even to believe worth exists — only to accept that worth, should it exist, admits no exceptions.
- **It attacks the self-exception and nothing else.** The old belief (below) was not a theory that humans lack worth; it was a special-case carve-out of one human — himself. The proof is aimed exactly at the carve-out: equal intrinsic worth leaves no room for a single excluded case. It changes nothing else about his model of worth.
- **It runs on parity logic with no felt input.** Like commitment's ["never fail silently"](conceptual-adapters.book-chapter.md#commitments-adapter--never-fail-silently), it requires no carried feeling — which is what makes it buildable for someone who cannot feel his way to the concept.

## The build — ~2024, inside the identity collapse

Built roughly two years before capture (~2024), in Alan's words "as I was finding my way through full identity collapse after being fired" — the collapse in which at least 80% of his identities went down at once ([identity-collapse.md](identity-collapse.book-chapter.md)). The adapter was not built in calm reflection; it was compiled inside the wreckage, as the tool the moment demanded.

## Not a pair with self-preservation

The adapter inventory's habit of pairing self-worth and self-preservation as one recent build is wrong. They came out of different crises, roughly two years apart: self-worth ~2024, from the identity collapse after the firing; [self-preservation](self-preservation-adapter.book-chapter.md) May 2026, from the turn-outward crisis. The pairing was an artifact of when they were captured, not of when they were built.

## The belief it broke

The old belief, documented at [journey-as-reward.md → the biographical root](journey-as-reward.book-chapter.md#the-biographical-root--why-the-wanting-rule-exists) and worked through in [emotional-archaeology.md](emotional-archaeology.book-chapter.md#the-worked-example--the-childhood-needs-belief): *"my needs aren't important and can only be met once the needs and wants of others have been met."* A self whose needs come last by default is a self carved out of the worth everyone else gets. The syllogism breaks that belief at its logical root: if worth is equal, the carve-out cannot stand.

The same carve-out shows up in the old self-preservation rule ("if it would hurt other people, I wasn't allowed to" — [the rule the May 2026 adapter replaced](self-preservation-adapter.book-chapter.md#the-rule-it-replaced)). Self-worth broke the carve-out two years before self-preservation replaced the rule built on top of it.

## A crowbar, not a foundation

The adapter's limit, in Alan's words: *"The conditional was enough to break the previous belief, which is what I most needed at the time. I aspire to put this on a more solid foundation, but haven't yet."*

The proof was built to break, not yet to hold. Because the antecedent is never asserted, the adapter establishes that he cannot be worth less than other humans — but not that he is worth anything in absolute terms. That was sufficient for the job at hand: the previous belief was a parity violation, and a parity argument kills it. A positive foundation for self-worth — one that asserts rather than conditions — is an open aspiration, not started.

## Worth is not the currency of failure — stress capacity is

When Alan ran [Emotional Archaeology on his commitment-discipline](perfection-is-safe.book-chapter.md), the dig tested whether **worth** was the currency spent by failure — the *"a man is only worth as much as his word"* strand. He knocked it down: *"I don't think that's the primary. I think the primary is a stress capacity."* Worth is a **minor fossil** in the commitment beliefs, not the driver. The driver is [stress-capacity defense](perfection-is-safe.book-chapter.md#the-belief-and-its-currency).

This matters for where the self-worth adapter sits. The adapter is real and load-bearing, but it is **not** the engine under his perfectionism or his commitment rule — those run on stress-capacity accounting, with worth a downstream, derived strand. There *are* "some fossils around worth" in the commitment beliefs, but worth is the adjacent sticky note, not the load path. The self-worth adapter broke a [parity violation](#the-belief-it-broke) (his needs carved out of the worth everyone gets); it did not, and was not built to, address the stress-capacity governor that "only perfection is safe" defends.
