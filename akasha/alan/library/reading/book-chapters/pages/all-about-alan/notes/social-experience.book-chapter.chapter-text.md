
# Social experience

- Social fatigue magnitude is NS-health-gated, not a fixed property — the same load produces different cost depending on NS-health state. At low NS health, social fatigue is more extreme than many autists experience; even at the low point, Alan is not at the extreme tail (there are autists with comparable or worse fatigue). The decline is measurable. At age 20, on a mission for The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints in Novosibirsk, Russia, Alan sustained six months of daily Level-6 social load (door-knocking strangers and street outreach in a foreign language) before his body broke and he could not continue. At the low point ~18 months ago, less than 90 minutes of comparable Level-6 load reached the same break threshold — roughly a thousandfold reduction in sustained-load capacity. Concrete recent low-NS-health data point: a 3-hour conversation requires ~2 days of bed rest to recover from. The 10+ years of minute-level [energy-accounting.md](energy-accounting.book-chapter.md) underlies the measurement. Level 6 sits above the highest tier captured in [stress-capacity-formula.md](stress-capacity-formula.book-chapter.md) (Criticism at 5); sustained social outreach with strangers in a foreign language extends the tier scale.
- Eye contact is highly stimulating even above social interaction, which is itself stimulating. Did it anyway for decades; now forces himself less and tries not to pay the extra cost when it's not affordable. For him this is just raw neurological cost.
- Masking is textbook. Able to mask at a very high level for short interactions with new people, but the ability degrades over time. Better at the early phases of social interaction than the later ones, except with other neurodivergents he doesn't need to mask around. Concrete: in college, 100+ first dates and 3 second dates.
- Small talk drain has a deeper mechanism. Deep conversation is conceptual and has real content for him. A lot of small talk is emotional or sensory and that doesn't have weight, since he experiences those only in the moment and gets no benefit from anticipation or recall. He estimates roughly 10x the impact for emotional and sensory experiences as baseline (positive and negative), counterbalanced on the negative side: nervous-system sensitivity makes him prone to traumatic nervous-system encoding. He resonates a lot with Spock and Data.
- Reading expressions and tone: yes, reduced. Two-layer mechanism. Most autists struggle here because implicit social statistical learning (System B in [automaticity-systems.md](automaticity-systems.book-chapter.md), right pSTS) is reduced — typically the skill compresses out of passive observation and runs effortlessly. Alan gets the added layer of aphantasia: he can't even remember or imagine his own emotions as reference points, much less the emotions of others.
- Theory of mind: yes, reduced on raw input — same shape as expression-reading. Reduced System B (implicit social statistical learning) prevents the mind-modeling skill from compressing out of observation. Compensation runs through System C (schema integration, mPFC/ATL) — explicit, deliberate, conceptual theories of other minds. The age-14 yearlong study of social interaction rules is the canonical instance: Alan integrated explicit social rules into schema, and once integrated they fire on retrieval rather than effortful search. The direct cost is executive function (energy and neurotransmitters); the indirect cost is stress capacity, since long experience of negative social outcomes from the missing System B channel encodes nervous-system trauma over time. Conventional framing — "ToM deficit", Baron-Cohen-era "mindblindness" — names the missing implicit channel as if it were the whole story, missing the robust schema-integration compensation and the layered cost structure. Multi-system view in [automaticity-systems.md](automaticity-systems.book-chapter.md); the substrate-level breakdown lives there rather than being restated here.
- Communication style does NOT match the blunt trope. High nervous-system sensitivity forced him to learn how to navigate social interactions early — more charm than tact (charm uses confidence; tact would require hedging) — since he pays an extreme cost for conflict. Reputation as a peacemaker as a kid, even for adults.
- Perceived as more certain about his claims than he actually is. Mechanism is not communication-style-shaped (the charm overlay does not insert hedges); it is a composition of math-major proof-shape thinking, autism-driven directness, aphantasia-driven crispness, and a global "half my beliefs are wrong" stance that is invisible to listeners. Costs in conflict via listener defensiveness. Canonical home: [perceived-certainty.md](perceived-certainty.book-chapter.md).
- Group size: yes, larger groups are overwhelming. Cost scales roughly with the base-2 log of the number of people involved.
- Empathy is entirely backwards in conventional framing. Autistics have reduced *expression* of empathy as understood by allistics; however, they tend to have significantly *increased* empathy. Two reasons: (1) Increased nervous-system sensitivity creates stronger emotional responses. (2) Lack of habituation reduces the distance between self and others, making pain felt by others more likely to be mirrored as personal pain. Even with total emotional aphantasia and alexithymia, Alan has such a strong emotional response to the emotions of others that other people have found it consistently remarkable throughout his life.

## Composition of total social cost

Social cost has at least two distinct components. The System-B/C compensation cost (above) is only one of them.

- **System-B/C compensation cost.** Explicit conceptual modeling of expressions, tone, and other minds — paid every invocation, never amortized. This is the cost most directly visible from the autism side.
- **Recovery-protocol pause cost.** Alan runs [meditative breathing](meditative-breathing.book-chapter.md) 8-12 hours per day, continuously, stacked under most other activities. Talking is the one major activity that pauses the protocol. Every minute of conversation is a minute the underlying recovery practice is not running. Total social cost = compensation cost + breathing-pause cost.

This composition is part of why social load is so much costlier than coding (programming stacks under the breathing protocol; conversation does not), and why [medical social isolation](medical-social-isolation.book-chapter.md) compounds with the breathing practice — cutting social load is also unblocking 8-12 hours per day of recovery practice.

This section is the **cost ledger** for connection. The same interaction also pays down [connection debt](connection-debt.book-chapter.md) (the benefit ledger). Because connection competes with recovery rather than stacking under it, the two ledgers have to be priced together — that integration, and the dose it implies, is in [connection dosing](connection-dosing.book-chapter.md).

Cohabitation adds a third component: **accommodation cost**. Same input, opposite valence between Alan and his wife — light, sound, and other sensory loads stimulate her where they drain him. Resolution shape is per-room negotiation with default-defer, override only when Alan is at low capacity. Concrete instance in [visual-sensitivity-bundle.md → cohabitation accommodation](visual-sensitivity-bundle.book-chapter.md#cohabitation-accommodation). The accommodation is a continuous draw on Alan's stack while it runs — not a one-time cost when the rule is set.
