
# Absolute truth and ethics

> The metaphysical and ethical foundation under Alan's faith and epistemology — willingness-to-improve implies an absolute truth to grow toward; the root axioms (growth-toward-truth, intrinsic value of sentient experience, maybe more); ethics as lossy approximations of a compact generator; the hoped-for identity of maximal compression and absolute truth (held as faith, Gödel-unprovable); the creed; perception+compression as his current praxis. Rendered as Alan's beliefs, not asserted fact.

This note captures the foundation under two things already documented separately: the [faith-and-testing thread](testing-and-the-seed.book-chapter.md) and the [compression epistemology](compression-epistemology.book-chapter.md). It is rendered as Alan's beliefs, not as asserted fact.

Everything in this note opens out from a single root: [self-improvement, the atom](self-improvement.book-chapter.md) — the one irreducible core Alan's whole worldview compresses into. The absolute truth here is the *direction* that root grows toward; the provisional-draft status of every belief and the commitment-as-engine mechanism below are that root applied to belief itself.

The entry point is the [innermost-core team attribute](testing-and-the-seed.book-chapter.md#the-innermost-core-attribute-the-ability-and-willingness-to-improve): the ability and *willingness* to improve. That attribute, followed out, lands on an absolute truth, a small set of root axioms, an account of where every ethical system stands relative to that truth, and — at the limit — the identity of absolute truth with maximal compression.

## Willingness to improve implies absolute truth

In Alan's mind, **willingness-to-improve implies the existence of absolute truth.**

The argument is short. To improve is to grow *in a direction.* A direction presupposes a fixed reference to grow toward — otherwise "improve" has no meaning, just motion. So holding willingness-to-improve as the core attribute commits you to there being something the growth is *toward.* That something is **absolute truth: a fixed direction to grow toward.**

Growth presupposes a direction; a direction presupposes an absolute reference. The core attribute and the existence of absolute truth are the same commitment seen from two ends.

## The root axioms

Alan suspects absolute truth has a small set of **root axioms** — and that, like a compact generator, the rest follows from them. He has named two so far and expects there are more.

- **Axiom 1 — growth toward truth.** Willingness and ability to improve, oriented on the absolute reference above. This is the innermost-core team attribute restated as a root of the value system.
- **Axiom 2 — the intrinsic value of the experiences of sentient beings.** Alan holds that the experiences of sentient beings have *true intrinsic value* — value that is part of absolute truth itself, not assigned by convention. He listed this as an additional root axiom alongside growth-toward-truth.
- **…maybe more.** He flags that there are likely a few more root axioms he hasn't isolated. The set is open; only the first two are named.

## Ethics as lossy approximations of a compact generator

Alan's hunch: **all of ethics may be derivable from these root axioms** (growth toward truth + intrinsic value of sentient experience + maybe a few more), and **all our actual systems of ethics are imperfect approximations** of what that derivation would yield.

State the compression parallel plainly, because it is the same shape as his [compression epistemology](compression-epistemology.book-chapter.md) applied to morality:

- **True ethics is the compact generator** — the small axiom set plus what follows from it, the maximally compressed account of how to act.
- **Every codified ethical system is a lossy approximation** of that generator — and, like a [false model over a narrow data set](compression-epistemology/failure-mode-and-guard.book-chapter.md#the-failure-mode--compactness-is-data-set-relative), it carries epicycles: special cases, exceptions, and patches bolted on to cover where the approximation diverges from the true generator.

So the relationship between any real ethical system and true ethics is exactly the relationship his epistemology draws between a lossy model and the truth: the codified system is the epicycle-laden surface, the axioms are the compact root it approximates. Moral progress, on this view, is compression — finding the more compact account that subsumes the special cases.

## Compression ≡ absolute truth — hope and faith, not theorem

Asked whether the absolute truth one grows toward is the **same object** as maximal compression — the limit his [compression epistemology](compression-epistemology.book-chapter.md) points at — Alan said he **hopes so.** The two threads are meant to meet: the direction the seed grows toward (absolute truth) and the limit the truth-gauge points at (maximal compression) would be one object.

But he holds the identity as **faith and direction, not as a theorem.** His reason is a Gödelian one. Like the consistency of mathematics — by Gödel's second incompleteness theorem, a sufficiently powerful formal system cannot prove its own consistency from within — **if the compression≡truth hypothesis is true, it is likely unprovable within our frame.** A system can't step outside itself to certify its own foundation.

So the identity of the compression-limit with absolute truth is something Alan **hopes is true and steers by,** not something he claims to have established. It is a direction of travel held on faith, with the unprovability itself expected rather than troubling.

## The creed

Alan's highest allegiance is to truth itself — not to truth as a means to something else, but as the thing claimed wherever it is found. His creed, verbatim:

> My religion is truth, and if there is any truth in heaven, earth, or hell, it belongs to me.

This paraphrases LDS phrasing; it is rendered here as Alan's own stated creed. The shape of it: truth is the allegiance, and it is claimable wherever it turns up — no source is excluded, no domain is off-limits. Truth in hell is still truth, and still his.

## Perception and compression — his current praxis

If absolute truth is the direction, the method for moving toward it is his epistemology. In his words: **"Perception and compression is my best current understanding for how to move in that direction."**

His whole epistemology is the vehicle: **broad perception / intake** (the [structural-breadth guard](compression-epistemology/failure-mode-and-guard.book-chapter.md#the-guard--breadth-is-structural-not-a-hobby) that keeps the data set wide) plus **[compression as the truth-detector](compression-epistemology.book-chapter.md#reward-and-truth-are-the-same-signal)** (the compactness gauge that selects the true model). Together they are how he moves toward the absolute-truth direction.

Note the word **current.** He holds the *method itself* as provisional and improvable — consistent with applying the willingness-to-improve attribute reflexively, to his own epistemology. The method is not exempt from the rule it serves. The cleanest recent evidence: he [swapped coherence for compactness](compression-epistemology.book-chapter.md#origin--the-engine-always-ran-seeing-it-is-recent) in his self-model only during the interview work that produced these notes. The praxis is itself a moving target.

## All beliefs are provisional drafts — including that one

Asked directly whether he holds compression itself as provisional or as bedrock, Alan gave the answer that completes the "current" flag above and resolves it for the whole system:

> All of my beliefs are provisional drafts, including this one.

Compression is **not exempt.** The [willingness-to-improve](self-improvement.book-chapter.md) applies with no exemptions — not to the epistemology, not to the creed, not even to the meta-belief that all beliefs are provisional. That last one is the recursive catch: "all beliefs are provisional drafts" is itself a belief, so it falls inside its own scope. It is a belief that includes itself.

Alan flags the self-inclusion with **a wink, not a flinch.** The recursion is not a paradox to escape; it is the rule completing itself. A rule that exempted itself would be the one belief held as final — the one place the [atom of self-improvement](self-improvement.book-chapter.md#the-atom-is-self-proving) stopped insisting on improvement — and that exemption is exactly what he refuses. The provisionality is total precisely because it does not spare itself.

This is the reflexive completion of the **current**-praxis flag above: the method is provisional, the meta-claim about provisionality is provisional, and the [coherence→compactness swap](compression-epistemology.book-chapter.md#origin--the-engine-always-ran-seeing-it-is-recent) is the standing proof that even the load-bearing pieces do move.

## Commitment is the engine of improvement

There is an apparent tension here. If every belief is a provisional draft, how does Alan give beliefs full conviction — the faith, the creed, the [commitments-as-invariants](discrete-self.book-chapter.md#the-selfother-decision-inversion) he holds as bedrock? Held as drafts, shouldn't they be held loosely?

His answer dissolves the tension:

> Because living aligned with my current understanding is the best and fastest route to increasing my understanding.

The mechanism, stated plainly: **you cannot improve a draft you don't run at full conviction.** Commitment is not the opposite of provisionality — it is the *experimental apparatus by which a provisional belief gets tested.* To revise the seed you must actually run the seed. Hold a belief at half-conviction and reality never talks back hard enough to tell you where it's wrong; commit to it fully, live it out, and the world delivers the disconfirming data that drives the next compression.

This is the same [action → feedback → compression → improvement loop](compression-epistemology.book-chapter.md#one-operation-at-four-scales) documented across the epistemology, here applied to belief itself. A belief run at full conviction is a belief under maximal test. So epistemic humility (all beliefs provisional) and existential commitment (faith, "my religion is truth," the [never-fail-silently commitments](discrete-self.book-chapter.md#the-selfother-decision-inversion)) are not in tension — they are the *same* move. The commitment is how the draft earns its revision. You hold it fully *so that* it can improve, which is the [atom](self-improvement.book-chapter.md) again: commitment is self-improvement's method, not its enemy.
