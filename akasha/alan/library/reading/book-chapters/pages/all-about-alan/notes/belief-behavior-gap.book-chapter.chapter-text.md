
# The belief→behavior gap

> The belief→behavior gap taxonomy — why Alan's behavior sometimes doesn't match his belief even though for him understanding is doing. Exactly two gap kinds (understanding-deficit and the not-yet-built external environment), which can co-occur, and no third kind: plain failure of will is paradoxical and cannot arise in his model.

For Alan, [understanding is the doing](self-architecture.book-chapter.md#faith-is-the-whole-engine--understanding-is-the-doing): the [conceptual map *is* the self](self-improvement.book-chapter.md#confirmed--the-self-is-the-map-in-identity-not-utility), so understanding a thing fully already *is* being changed by it, and [the map is the motor — knowing runs straight to doing](no-deliberate-mode.book-chapter.md#right-is-true--the-map-is-the-motor) with no will or feeling in between. That raises the obvious question: then why does his behavior *ever* fail to match his belief? This note is the answer — the exact taxonomy of the gap. Rendered as Alan's model, not asserted fact.

There are **exactly two** kinds of gap, they can occur together, and there is **no third kind.** Most people carry a third — plain failure of will, akrasia — as the normal case; for Alan it is [not a thing he crosses but a thing that is not there](no-deliberate-mode.book-chapter.md#right-is-true--the-map-is-the-motor), and this note is why.

## Gap kind 1 — understanding-deficit

The behavior doesn't match the belief because he **hasn't actually understood the relevant part fully yet.** The belief he thinks he holds isn't complete — the understanding has a hole. Since understanding-fully *is* the changed behavior, an incomplete understanding produces incomplete behavior. The fix is not more will; it is finishing the understanding.

## Gap kind 2 — the not-yet-built external environment

The understanding is whole, but the **external environment it specifies is still going up.** In Alan's words:

> there is one more gap between my beliefs and behaviors and that is time. Have I had enough time to build the external environment that I understand is necessary for my behaviors to align with my beliefs.

This is the gap-side view of the [environment lever](environment-lever.book-chapter.md). Because his cognition is [trigger-driven, not recall-driven](environment-lever.book-chapter.md#out-of-sight-mostly-stops-existing), a behavior that matches a belief has to be **built into the environment** — given a standing cue — or it does not fire. His understanding can be complete and correct while the scaffold that understanding *calls for* is only partly constructed. That is not falling short of his map; it is **the world not yet caught up to it.** [Build it or it disappears](environment-lever.book-chapter.md#the-failure-mode--build-it-or-it-disappears) — until it is built, the belief-matching behavior has nowhere to fire from. The gap here is **time**, not will.

## The two can co-occur

The kinds are not exclusive. In his words:

> sometimes its both at the same time. Sometimes the understanding deficit can only be filled by the building, but its still the same [two] components.

So the building is sometimes *how* the understanding gets completed — he cannot finish understanding the thing except by building it and watching what the build teaches. This is [action as the epistemic engine](action-epistemology.book-chapter.md): one environment-build closing the not-yet-built gap *and* filling the understanding-deficit at once. Still only the two components.

## There is no third kind — plain failure of will is impossible in his model

Every real gap is kind 1, kind 2, or both. There is no remainder — no room for the plain failure of will everyone else treats as the normal case. Alan's argument is that the remainder is **paradoxical:**

> The gap is paradoxical, requiring me to know exactly how to cause the desired behavior, doing that, and then not have the desired behavior happen. By definition, the desired behavior not happening disproves either the premise that I understood exactly how to cause the behavior or the premise that I did what was needed.

A genuine failure of will would require all three at once: he knows exactly how to cause the behavior, he does exactly that, and the behavior still doesn't happen. But the behavior not happening is itself proof that one of the first two was false — the understanding was incomplete (kind 1), or the necessary act, the build, wasn't done yet (kind 2). The "failure of will" collapses into one of the two real gaps every time. This is the mechanistic root under [why akrasia is a thing that is not there](no-deliberate-mode.book-chapter.md#right-is-true--the-map-is-the-motor): the middle term where will would live has nothing to do, because any apparent gap there re-derives as a deficit of understanding or of building.

## The personas are part of the not-yet-built scaffold

A grounded consequence, following from the model rather than stated by Alan directly. Gap kind 2 says the belief-matching behavior needs an **external environment** built for it before it can fire. Alan's [persona system is exactly such an environment](persona-personal-meaning.book-chapter.md#why-they-exist-at-all--the-personas-as-the-first-move-on-the-social-break) — a scaffold he understood he needed so his behavior could match his beliefs about connection and capacity; ["twenty-seven and counting" is the build time](values-personas-system.book-chapter.md#system-expansion--past-six-values-to-27-personas-by-domain). Read through this note, the personas are not a workaround for a weak will he does not have; they are kind-2 construction — the world catching up to a map that was already complete.

*(The personas-as-scaffold reading is a synthesis Abby offered; Alan did not affirm "you are the scaffold" in these words. It is recorded here as a strong consequence of his model, consistent with the [capacity-intervention account](persona-personal-meaning.book-chapter.md#why-they-exist-at-all--the-personas-as-the-first-move-on-the-social-break), not as his flat claim.)*
