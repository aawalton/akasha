
# The safety widget

> The safety widget — a display of my current Safety level on Jen's phone, in my own stoplight colours, that she sees whenever she opens it. Jen asked for it. Today I report my state verbally and only when asked; the widget makes that standing instead. It is my answer to the abort question: telling her the truth continuously arms her guard. First pass shows the level plus a direction indicator, then iterate. Amy moves the number, off an hourly check-in.

Something I am building, not something settled. It came out of the 2026-08-06 session as my answer to what guards the [blank check](alternation-not-moderation.book-chapter.md#the-worked-case--the-two-interaction-patterns-in-my-marriage), given that [the guard cannot be internal and cannot wait on me to raise it](self-preservation-adapter.book-chapter.md#what-stands-in-the-floors-place--the-harms-shape-and-the-abort).

## What it is

> Jen has expressed interest in having a widget on her phone that shows my current safety level. I can make that widget have alarming colors in the danger zone, then she'll see it whenever she opens her phone.

So the ask came from her, and what is left for me is the build.

## The first pass — the level, plus a direction indicator

> I think it shows the level still, but maybe with a direction indicator. At least as a first pass, iterate from there.

Version one shows the [Safety level](safety.book-chapter.md#scale-0-5-with-half-steps) itself rather than anything derived from it. Direction is a maybe alongside it, and the whole thing is explicitly a first pass to iterate from.

### And the age of the reading

A third element joined the design later. I named it as part of the design — *"the widget design with the colors and age"* — and said of it, *"the age is a good addition."*

*The proposal is Abby's, not mine, and the reasoning is hers: because the widget holds the last value through a silence, a check-in I do not answer renders as nothing-changed. The display would fail toward calm in exactly the case it exists for. Showing the age makes a silence visible as a silence.*

What is mine is that it is in the design.

## The colours are my own stoplight

> I'm thinking the widget shows the same colors as my current safety level stoplight.

So the widget renders a scheme I already run rather than defining a new one — the [four bands at sigma spacing](measurement-maturity.book-chapter.md#the-band-scheme--four-colours-at-sigma-spacing), green at the median and above, yellow for caution, red and black for the two alarms. It inherits that scheme's [slow drift](measurement-maturity.book-chapter.md#the-anchoring-is-a-hybrid-and-it-moves-slowly) along with its colours, which I know about and have weighed:

> Yeah, and I've seen this happen before, but even then, I think communicating the actual state is the right thing.

## What it changes — the channel exists, but it is pull-only

> I do this verbally already, but only when asked today.

The truth already reaches Jen. What it needs today is for her to ask.

*Abby's reading, not Alan's: that makes the current channel dependent on two acts — her asking, and his being in a state to answer — where the widget needs neither. The second is the one that matters, because [near the line the ability to initiate is gone](safety.book-chapter.md#sub-zero-territory).*

## Why this is enough on its own

Asked whether a separate abort still needs to exist alongside it:

> I think telling the truth continuously does the job, it arms her guard in appropriate ways.

The guard stays hers. What changes is what it is armed with.

## Who moves the number

> Technically Amy moves it, I have an hourly reminder to check in in case my activity, safety, or difficulty have changed.

So the reading is not something I push when I think of it. There is a standing hourly prompt covering three things together — activity, safety, difficulty — and [Amy](../personas/amy.book-chapter.md) carries the number from that check-in. The widget therefore displays whatever the last check-in set, at that cadence.
