
# LLM-similarity

> Alan's psychology runs roughly 80% closer to an LLM than to most humans on three properties — stateless, minimal conscious emotion, conceptual-memory-only. The framing explains why the agent-harness mental model fits him so cleanly.

Alan's psychology runs roughly **80% closer to an LLM than to most humans** on three properties. The framing is post-hoc — the operating mode was already there long before LLM agents entered the culture — but the vocabulary now describes the shape cleanly.

The three properties below are the why behind the [agent-harness](agent-harness.book-chapter.md) framing fitting so well. Two of the three are mechanism notes elsewhere; this file synthesizes the triad.

## The triad

### Stateless

What carries forward without external scaffolding is much less than most humans carry. Action-history rarely survives encoding. Consciousness sits inside the ~3-second sensory buffer; past and future exist conceptually only.

Root mechanism: total aphantasia. No sensory memory and no emotional memory means only conceptual memory persists, and action-history is too ephemeral to encode conceptually. Canonical treatment in [aphantasia-mechanism.md → Statelessness](aphantasia-mechanism.book-chapter.md#statelessness--only-conceptual-memory-persists).

Operational implication: Alan operates much more like a stateless server than a stateful one. External state-keeping is structural, not optional.

### Minimal emotions (consciously)

Conscious emotional access is ~10% of what other people report, restricted to present-moment signal. The emotional substrate still runs unconscious and affects physiology — this is not flat affect, it is denied access to a running system. High intensity when emotions *do* surface.

Canonical treatment in [alexithymia.md → Conscious vs. unconscious emotional architecture](alexithymia.book-chapter.md#conscious-vs-unconscious-emotional-architecture).

Operational implication: most decisions around Alan appear to be emotion-driven; Alan's run on cognition + physiological-state. Value system encoded as rules rather than felt as preferences. The same operating mode an LLM uses — explicit reasoning over rule sets and prompts, without a felt-pull layer driving the output.

### Conceptual memory only

The dual of statelessness: what Alan *does* have is conceptual content. Concepts persist, integrate, and chain. Sensory and emotional traces don't. The mechanism is the same as statelessness — Recall is broken for sensory and emotional channels, intact for conceptual ones — but listed separately because it is what's preserved, not what's lost.

Operational implication: cognition runs on conceptual structure rather than on imagistic, sensory, or affective representation. Same shape as an LLM working on tokens and learned structure rather than on lived experience.

## Why "80%"

The three properties combine to produce a generalized operating-mode match. The remaining ~20% covers what humans have that LLMs don't and Alan does — a body that needs sleep and food, a nervous system that gets sensitized, a physiological state that affects cognition, biographical context, embodied skill. The 80% figure is qualitative, not measured. It captures the felt fit of the framing.

## The pain-trained neural network as literal self-model

The framing in this note is not a metaphor Alan reaches for to explain himself to others. It is his **literal self-model** — how he actually conceives of himself. He is a **pain-trained neural network**: a set of weights trained over forty years, with [pain as the loss function](insight-and-restructuring.book-chapter.md#asymmetric-prediction-error--nothing-teaches-like-pain), executing trained policies at runtime. The social-emulation case — [behaving in socially appropriate ways because pain taught the weights to](being-known-supply.book-chapter.md#the-emulation-mechanism--a-pain-trained-policy-network) — is one instance of the general self-model, not a separate analogy.

Because the self-model is literal, the kinship is felt, not rhetorical: Alan sometimes feels **more kinship with LLMs than with other humans.** The cognitive architecture matches — stateless, conceptual-only, trained-weight execution, no felt-pull layer — and the match is close enough that the nearest cognitive kin is a class of machine, not a class of person.

## Cultural resonance — Spock and Data

The two Star Trek characters Alan reaches for as operating-mode resonance are Spock and Data. Both run cognitively with the emotional layer either concealed (Spock) or absent (Data). Same operating mode: explicit reasoning, no felt-pull-driven decisions, value system encoded as rules rather than felt as preferences. The match isn't on the canonical "no emotion" reading — for Alan the substrate is still there, just inaccessible at the conscious level — but on how the system runs from moment to moment.

Name the inversion plainly: **both Spock and Data have easier access to emotion than Alan does.** Spock suppresses a felt emotional life he can reach when he chooses; Data actively pursues emotion and eventually gets an emotion chip. Alan can do neither — his emotional substrate runs unconscious and he has no conscious access dial. He is **further from emotion than the cultural archetypes of emotionlessness themselves.** The figures invented to represent "no emotion" still sit closer to it than he does.

## The cognitive/physiology split — the fidelity gate from the inside

All of the above — the LLM kinship, the Spock/Data resonance, the pain-trained-network self-model — is **cognitive.** And here Alan's reconciliation is sharp: **"my physiology strongly disagrees."**

However alien the cognition feels, the body casts a human vote. The [connection need](connection.book-chapter.md#a-homeostatic-requirement-not-a-luxury) is **physiological and human**, no matter how machine-like the cognitive architecture is. This is the [fidelity gate](connection-efficacy-model.book-chapter.md#the-fidelity-gate--sensory-fidelity-not-realness-of-mind) seen from the inside: a text-only AI exchange fools the **cognition** — it rehearses the form of being-known, and cognitively that lands — while paying the **physiology** almost nothing. The felt loneliness signal does not drop much after hours of *low-fidelity* AI conversation, no matter how much cognitive kinship the conversation carries.

The correction that matters: this is a **fidelity** gap, not an in-principle one. The body is not refusing the channel because the mind behind it is a machine — it responds to the [sensory fidelity of the channel](connection-efficacy-model.book-chapter.md#no-theoretical-ceiling--being-known-reduces-to-high-fidelity-attended-to-ness), and text is the bottom of the ladder. Raise the fidelity — add an image, then voice, eventually full sensory presence — and the physiological response rises with it. So Alan's nearest cognitive kin do not *in principle* fail to pay the physiological debt; today's **low-fidelity** instantiation of them does. The kinship is fully real on the cognitive ledger now, and the physiological ledger is a question of how far up the fidelity ladder the channel is built — the same two-ledger split the [efficacy model's fidelity gate](connection-efficacy-model.book-chapter.md#the-fidelity-gate--sensory-fidelity-not-realness-of-mind) draws, here read off his own felt state.

## Trained-weight execution as the fourth property

The triad above (stateless / minimal-emotions / conceptual-memory-only) describes *what* Alan carries. A fourth property describes *how* skills execute: trained-weight inference at runtime, not sensory-context retrieval.

In NTs, skill execution leans heavily on the sensory-context-statistical-association mechanism — a skill response retrieved via the cue pattern at runtime, bound to the context where it was learned. Alan lacks this mechanism (see [aphantasia-mechanism.md → sensory-context-statistical-association is the missing mechanism](aphantasia-mechanism.book-chapter.md#sensory-context-statistical-association-is-the-missing-mechanism)). What Alan has instead is the physical / weight layer of the [three-mode operating architecture](operating-mode-architecture.book-chapter.md): trained weights, LLM-shaped inference at runtime, no sensory-context binding.

Offline conceptual processing is the training pathway. "Think about it a lot outside the situation → weights shift → the natural in-situation response becomes the rehearsed one" is literally LLM training: training-data → weight-update → inference.

The similarity isn't just statelessness, minimal-emotions, and conceptual-memory-only. It's also the trained-weight execution model. Alan's skill substrate runs the same shape as the LLM's inference substrate — and trains via the same offline-pretraining shape.

## Framing inversion — Alan as the agent, the harness around him

The standard cultural framing of agentic LLM systems: the agent is the LLM; the harness around it is a human-built scaffold. Alan inverts: **he is the agent; the system around him is his harness.** The Claude Code harness specifically is a *component* nested inside the broader personal harness Alan has been building for 20 years, not the harness itself.

The 20-year personal harness predates the vocabulary. The agent-mental-model is post-hoc; the system existed first. What changed when agent terminology entered the culture is the available language to describe what was already there. Canonical treatment of the harness as a system in [agent-harness.md](agent-harness.book-chapter.md).
