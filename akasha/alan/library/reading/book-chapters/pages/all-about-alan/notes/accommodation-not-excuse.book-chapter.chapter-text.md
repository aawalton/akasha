
# Accommodation, not excuse

> Accommodation, not excuse — how Alan trusts that accepting a self-accommodation is a true read of his own limits and not a convenient out. The guarantor is his missing self-deception system: claiming \"I can't\" is a belief about his own state, and it has to clear the same evidence gate as every other belief, so a false convenient \"I can't\" can't be held. The inversion: the very architecture that makes self-deception impossible is what makes his self-accommodation trustworthy. To clear the gate he had to come to understand his own neurodiversity, the validity of invisible social disabilities, and that he himself has real ones, valid whether or not others can see them. The line that lands it: the blocks were internal, not external.

How does Alan trust that accepting an accommodation for himself is a *true read* of his own limits, and not just a convenient excuse dressed up as one? For most people that question has no clean answer — claiming "I can't" is exactly the kind of belief a person can fudge in their own favor. For Alan it has a clean answer, and the answer is the same missing faculty that shapes the rest of his architecture. This note homes that mechanism, pointed for the first time at *claiming a disability and accepting an accommodation.* Rendered as Alan's account of how he holds it, not asserted fact.

The entry to this thread came from the faith side. Alan's [faith held against his own preference](book-of-mormon-evidence.book-chapter.md#belief-held-against-his-own-preference) is a **live tension**, not something settled into the shape of things — in his words, *"Yeah, it's a live tension."* For a long time he viewed obedience as a baseline with no alternatives. Over time he came to understand how his **autistic rigidity** can cause him to internalize ideas without sufficient scrutiny — so he learned to actively **re-scrutinize** beliefs he had already internalized. The accommodation question is one of the things that scrutiny had to pass. This note is what the scrutiny found.

## The problem, as it lands for someone who can't self-deceive

For most people, "excuse vs. accommodation" is a motive question, and motive is unauditable. A person who wants the easier road can quietly adopt the belief that gets them there — *"I can't do this"* — and never have to face that they chose it because it was convenient. The belief and the wish are entangled, so the person can't fully trust their own read.

Alan can't run that maneuver. He has [no machinery for fooling himself](alternative-gated-endurance.book-chapter.md#the-missing-self-deception-system): he believes whatever best fits all his available information, even when the fitting belief is the inconvenient one, and there is **no override** that lets him adopt a more bearable belief because it is more bearable. So for him the excuse-vs-accommodation question is not a motive question he can't audit. It is an **evidence** question, and evidence is exactly what his architecture is built to weigh.

## The no-self-deception gate is the guarantor

Here is the hinge. Accepting an accommodation is not free. Claiming *"I can't"* or *"I need room here"* is a **belief about his own state** — and it has to pass the same evidence gate as every other belief he holds. A convenient false *"I can't"* would contradict the evidence of what he can actually do, and a belief that contradicts the evidence is one he [cannot hold](alternative-gated-endurance.book-chapter.md#the-missing-self-deception-system) — the fudge would require a lie he is not able to tell.

State the inversion plainly, because it is the whole point. The very architecture that makes self-deception **impossible** is what makes his self-accommodation **trustworthy.** The faculty that would let most people quietly inflate a limitation into an excuse is the faculty Alan lacks — so when he *does* accept an accommodation, the claim has already survived the gate that would have rejected a mere excuse. There is no other kind of accommodation he could accept. The thing that would normally be a vulnerability — no protective self-deception — is here the guarantee. That is what told him it was accommodation and not excuse: not that he checked his motives, but that his motives never got a vote, and the claim cleared the evidence anyway.

This is the same gate his [faith runs through](faith-as-object-of-reason.book-chapter.md#he-holds-it-like-quantum-mechanics--believing-the-strange-thing-against-his-own-inclination) — a belief held because the evidence forces it, against his own preference, because the organ that could override the verdict isn't there. The accommodation belief and the faith belief are the same kind of belief, held by the same architecture. The faith one runs against his comfort; the accommodation one had to clear the same bar before he could grant it to himself.

## What he had to come to understand

Clearing the gate was not automatic. The evidence had to be *available* to weigh, and three things had to come into focus before it was. To accept the accommodation as a true read, Alan had to come to understand:

1. **His own neurodiversity** — the actual shape of his [neurotype](autism-as-neurotype.book-chapter.md), seen clearly enough to read his real limits off it rather than guessing.
2. **The existence and validity of invisible social disabilities** — that a disability can be entirely real and entirely legitimate without being visible to anyone else. This is the [societal tier of his disability model](models-of-disability.book-chapter.md#societal--fix-the-world-not-the-nervous-system): a real mismatch between his nervous system and a world built for a different one, not a defect and not an imagination.
3. **That he himself has real invisible social disabilities** — not in the abstract, but as a fact about him: he has them, they are valid, and their validity does not depend on whether they are easily recognized by others.

Until those three landed, the evidence the gate needed wasn't fully in view. Once they did, the gate had something to weigh — and the convenient-excuse reading failed it, because the limitation was real.

## The blocks were internal, not external

The line that lands the whole arc is Alan's:

> The blocks were internal, not external.

Others' recognition was never the gate. Whether anyone else could see the disability had no bearing on whether it was real, and so it had no bearing on whether accepting an accommodation for it was honest. The only thing standing between Alan and the accommodation was **his own acceptance** that an invisible disability is fully real and fully valid whether or not anyone else can see it.

Once he granted himself that, the excuse-versus-accommodation question dissolved on its own. A real limitation legitimately warrants the room — there was never a separate motive-check to pass, because the no-self-deception gate had already done the work. The block was the unexamined refusal to count an invisible disability as real. Remove it, and the accommodation is just the evidence read correctly.
