
# Sex as a hygiene factor, not a core support

> Sex as a hygiene factor, not a core support — why partnered sex nets out as a cost. The 10%: sex is like food and Alan gets ~10% of the benefit most people (including Jen) get, because the value lives in the wings (anticipation before, savoring after) and aphantasia plus flat recall amputate both wings, leaving only the thin present slice already conflated with significant costs. So it can become neutral if costs drop, but cannot anchor a relationship. The affordability math: difficulty ≥5 (innately connected to criticism), a ≥1-level male-physiology discount, so funded safety level 4 is barely affordable and level 5 is enjoyable.

Why partnered sex nets out as a **cost** for me rather than a support. This sits beside [sex-with-jen.md](sex-with-jen.book-chapter.md) (the relationship's interior) and under the [sexuality knot](../sexuality-knot.book-chapter.md) index. The mechanism is structural, not attitudinal — it is not that I want it less, it is that the part most people get is amputated for me.

## The 10% — a structural amputation, not low desire

Sex is like food. And I get a fraction of the return on it that others do.

> Sex for me is like food, and I think I get 10% of the benefit from it that most people (including Jen) get. I have no memory, so I can't reflect back on good experiences. I have no imagination, so I can't anticipate forward to future ones. All I have are the benefits in the moment, which are fleeting and conflated with the costs, which are significant.

For most people the value of food and sex lives in the **wings** — the anticipation before and the savoring after. The act itself is the narrow middle; the wings are where most of the payoff accrues. I have **neither wing.** [No imagination](../aphantasia-mechanism.book-chapter.md#no-forward-simulator) means I cannot anticipate forward to a future experience. [Flat recall](../present-tense-model.book-chapter.md#the-map-is-affectively-flat--structure-no-charge) means I cannot reflect back on a past one — there is no stored warmth to re-feel. What is left is only the present slice: "fleeting and conflated with the costs, which are significant."

So **~10% is a structural amputation, not low desire.** The part that makes sex a core support *for Jen* — the looking-forward and the looking-back — is exactly the part my architecture can't reach. It isn't that I value it 90% less. It's that 90% of the value is in two rooms I have no door to.

## Hygiene factor, not core support

That sets the ceiling on what sex can be for me.

> If we could get the costs down (which we have made progress on), then I could imagine sex becoming a neutral aspect for me, but I don't think it can anchor a relationship. For me sex is a hygiene factor, not a core support.

The framing is exact: **a hygiene factor, not a core support.** A hygiene factor is something whose absence causes a problem but whose presence doesn't build anything — it can be made *neutral*, not *positive*. If we get the costs down far enough (and we have made real progress — see the [remediations in sex-with-jen.md](sex-with-jen.book-chapter.md#the-after-sex-drops)), it could land at neutral for me. But neutral is the ceiling. It cannot **anchor a relationship**, because anchoring takes a core support, and the wings that would make it one are amputated.

## The affordability math

Underneath the "cost" reading is a concrete number on the [safety difficulty gate](../safety.book-chapter.md#safety-is-a-threshold-gate-not-a-metered-budget).

> Currently, sex is primarily a stress point. The difficulty level is at least 5 (innately connected to criticism), but the male physiology in the moment does give at least a 1 level discount, so at level 4, it becomes just barely affordable and at level 5 I can enjoy it.

Read against the [funded-level model](../safety.book-chapter.md#safety-is-a-funded-level-not-a-per-alarm-switch), the math is clean:

- **Base difficulty ≥ 5**, because sex is **innately connected to [criticism](../criticism-and-ostracism.book-chapter.md)** — the social-rejection-as-survival-threat alarm that sets the [top of the difficulty scale](../safety.book-chapter.md#scale-0-5-with-half-steps). That is why it reads "primarily a stress point."
- **Male physiology gives a ≥ 1-level discount** in the moment — arousal lowers the effective difficulty by at least one rung.
- So effective difficulty ≈ 4. At **funded safety level 4** the difficulty *matches* my level → 1x cost → **just barely affordable.** At **level 5** the difficulty sits one rung below my level → it goes from affordable to **enjoyable.**

This is the [gate](../safety.book-chapter.md#safety-is-a-threshold-gate-not-a-metered-budget) doing exactly what it does everywhere else: compare the activity's difficulty to my funded level, and refuse anything above the line. Sex is a high-difficulty act that only clears the line at high safety, and only becomes enjoyable a full level above that. Below funded level 4 it is simply gated out. The physiology discount is the one thing that pulls a ≥5 act down to where a strong day can pay for it.

**The discount is arousal-gated — the subsidy *is* the arousal coming online.** The rung-down is not a property of the topic "sex"; it is the [autonomic regulation lever](regulation-not-desire.book-chapter.md#the-autonomic-mechanism--a-controlled-sns-up-pns-down-cycle) firing. When the physiology comes online, arousal buys the cost down in the moment. When it doesn't, there is nothing online to draw on. That is why the discount only shows up mid-act: the subsidy and the arousal are the same thing.

So **the discount is scoped to the act of sex itself — not to anything sex-adjacent.** In my words, male physiology *"seems to be obscenely optimised around sex"* — and that optimization is exactly what hands the discount, which is also exactly why it does not generalize. A sex-*adjacent* hard thing gets **no discount.** The clearest case is the [conversation to set up the cuddling arrangement](sleep-and-the-bedroom.book-chapter.md#the-proposed-fix--cuddling-at-the-transition): a body-quiet, cold/conceptual planning talk, it sits at its full level-5 difficulty because no arousal is online to pull it down. Only the act of sex itself draws the rung-down; *talking about* sex, *arranging* sex, or any other adjacent load pays full price.
