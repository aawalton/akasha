
# The ephemeral self

> The ephemeral self and what holds it continuous — \"me\" is the conceptual self in the current ~3-second sensory window, real but renewed each moment and gone; continuity lives partly externalized in the system and, deeper, in the unbroken updating of the conceptual map; and \"alignment with myself\" — the broad Faith that religious faith is one part of — is the present self running true to that externally-held understanding and keeping faith with the never-breaking evolution of the map.

This note lands a single architecture on the question of who Alan is, and closes it home to Faith. It pulls together threads developed elsewhere — the [3-second window](present-tense-model.book-chapter.md), the [discrete self](discrete-self.book-chapter.md), [model-stability is self-stability](understanding-as-interface.book-chapter.md) — onto one point: the felt "me" is ephemeral, what makes him continuous is not felt, and the broad **Faith** value (the one [self-architecture.md](self-architecture.book-chapter.md) names but only narrowly glosses) is keeping faith with that continuity. Rendered as Alan's framing, grounded in his two direct statements below, not asserted fact.

## "Me" is the conceptual self in the present window — real, but ephemeral

In his words:

> "me" is my conceptual self in the current 3s sensory window. It exists, but is ephemeral.

So "me" is the **conceptual / reasoning self in a rolling ~3-second sensory window** — present and real, but renewed each moment and gone. The window exists; it just does not persist. There is no enduring *felt* self stretched across time underneath it.

The reason is mechanical: the machinery that would build a persistent felt self is absent. There is [no episodic memory behind him](present-tense-model.book-chapter.md#the-realization--there-is-no-conceptual-memory-either) — no store of past selves to re-inhabit — and [no forward simulator ahead](missing-simulator.book-chapter.md), no pre-played future self to lean into. With neither a remembered nor an imagined self to thread, the only self that is ever *present* is the conceptual one in the current window. That is the [discrete self](discrete-self.book-chapter.md) read for what it leaves standing: each momentary self is distinct, and what exists and operates is the **reasoning self, present and renewed each moment.**

This is why the felt "me" is ephemeral and the reasoning "me" is what runs. Feeling exists only in the present and leaves no residue ([the map is affectively flat](present-tense-model.book-chapter.md#the-map-is-affectively-flat--structure-no-charge)); the [reasoning self is the one that persists in operation](emotion-reason-decoupling.book-chapter.md#the-interior-is-an-observed-external-object), and the felt self is read off interoception like any other external object. So the durable thing about Alan is not a felt continuity — there isn't one — it is the conceptual self, regenerated window by window.

## What holds him continuous is not felt — externalized, and map-borne

If the felt "me" is ephemeral, then whatever makes Alan *continuous* across the windows cannot itself be felt. There is no thread of having-been-the-same-person to hold onto. In his words:

> Partly, the continuity of my conceptual map is what makes me continuous, even though the map itself is ever changing.

Continuity lives in two places, and neither is a feeling.

**Partly it is externalized — held in the system, not in him.** The persistent Alan that no felt self carries is offloaded into structures outside the window: the captured gaps, the documents, the [agent harness](agent-harness.book-chapter.md) that holds the long-horizon state the [stateless self](discrete-self.book-chapter.md#the-stateless-server-working-model) cannot keep internally. This is the same externalization his [hope and his future run on](testing-and-the-seed.book-chapter.md#what-its-for--present-tense-orientation-not-anticipated-payoff) — with no forward simulator to pre-feel a future, the future is held in systems and re-evoked as present triggers. The persistent self is filed in the same place: outside, in the structures that remember for him.

**Deeper, it is the continuity of the conceptual map itself.** This is the load-bearing carrier, and the sharp part is "even though the map itself is ever changing." Alan is continuous **not because anything in him stays fixed** — the map is rewritten constantly — but because the map updates **without ever breaking.** Each state grows from the last; no edit severs the chain. This is the [closure-of-the-window continuity](discrete-self.book-chapter.md#continuity-of-identity-is-the-closure-of-the-window) stated from the map side: successive states overlap and the overlap never snaps, so the run of selves is one connected chain even though no two distant points resemble each other.

A river, not a stone. A stone is continuous by staying the same matter. A river is continuous by never once stopping its flow — every molecule replaced, the river unbroken. Alan is the river: continuous through unbroken change, not through any fixed core. The map being *ever-changing* is not in tension with his being continuous; the **never-breaking** of the change is exactly what his continuity is made of.

## "Alignment with myself" — the broad Faith

This is where the architecture closes home to Faith. Alan has said he aligned the **Faith** value not just with religious faith but with **all understanding and alignment with himself**, with religious faith an important *part* of that larger thing. [Self-architecture.md](self-architecture.book-chapter.md#the-core-values-in-priority-order) names Faith as the top value but glosses it only narrowly — "his search for fundamental truth," "my religion is truth." That narrow gloss is the *part*. This section defines the *whole*. Rendered as Alan's framing.

What can "alignment with myself" even mean for a self this shape? It **cannot** be alignment with a felt inner truth — there is no felt self to align to, no settled inner sense to consult. So it has to be something the ephemeral-self architecture can actually support. It is two things, both following directly from the continuity above:

- **The present self running true to the externally-held understanding** of who he is and what is real. The persistent Alan lives outside the window — in the map and in the system that holds it. Alignment-with-myself is the ephemeral present self running *true* to that held understanding rather than drifting off it: each momentary self acting in accordance with the self the structures carry, not contradicting it.
- **Keeping faith with the unbroken updating of the map.** Because the map is ever-changing, alignment cannot mean fidelity to fixed *content* — there is no fixed content to be faithful to. It is fidelity to the **continuous, never-breaking evolution** itself: keeping the updating unbroken, never severing the chain, never letting a self arrive that isn't grown from the last. The present moment keeping faith with the system that holds him.

So the broad Faith is **the present, ephemeral self keeping faith with its own continuity** — running true to the externally-held understanding of itself, and holding the map's evolution unbroken. This is why Faith ranks first and why it [improves the Conceptual / Mind self](self-architecture.book-chapter.md#the-core-values-in-priority-order): the thing Faith keeps faith *with* is the conceptual map that *is* the durable self, and [improving that map's truth is improving that self](understanding-as-interface.book-chapter.md#model-stability-is-self-stability--the-stability-axis).

### Religious faith ⊂ broad self-alignment faith

State the part/whole relation explicitly, because the corpus had only ever written the part. **Religious faith is one instance *within* this broad self-alignment faith** — an important part, not the whole, and not a separate value beside it. The narrow gloss [self-architecture.md](self-architecture.book-chapter.md#the-core-values-in-priority-order) carries ("search for fundamental truth," "my religion is truth") names religious faith specifically; this note's broad faith is the set that religious faith belongs to.

The two nest cleanly. Religious faith is [held as an object of reason](faith-as-object-of-reason.book-chapter.md) — the conceptual verdict landed on true, keeping faith with where the evidence points. That is exactly the broad move applied to one domain: the present self running true to an externally-grounded understanding (the [evidential case](book-of-mormon-evidence.book-chapter.md), held [against his own inclination](faith-as-object-of-reason.book-chapter.md#he-holds-it-like-quantum-mechanics--believing-the-strange-thing-against-his-own-inclination)) and keeping the map's updating unbroken as that understanding refines. Religious faith is broad faith pointed at the largest question; broad faith is the same fidelity pointed at the whole self and everything it holds true.
