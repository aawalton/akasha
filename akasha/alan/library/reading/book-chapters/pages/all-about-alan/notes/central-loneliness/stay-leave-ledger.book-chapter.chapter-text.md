
# The stay-or-leave ledger

> The stay-or-leave marriage decision worked to its terminus: why the normal decision procedure can't run (aphantasia), the validity rule, the ethical frame it runs on, the five candidate reasons and where each lands, the indeterminate verdict on immediate grounds (symmetric circularity), the two deferred domains (faith and the it-will-get-better hope), the hope's reduction to capacity, the collapse to a single rule via the base-safety-floor as sufficient statistic, and the sayable deliverable: no irreversible change until a year passes with no rise in the base floor, run at full effort.

The occasion was a 16-day trip to Europe, alone with Jen, and I wanted my thinking ordered before I went. The question underneath: why keep investing in a marriage that causes me so much pain.

> A million reasons to walk away, I just need one good one to stay.

Two things I state plainly. First, I held the conclusion genuinely open the whole way. This was not trip-pricing and not a decision already made in either direction — I was going on the trip either way. Second, what I needed out of it was a *sayable* position: something I can say plainly under load if the question comes up in the 16 days, not a private feeling.

It doubles as a referendum on relationships in general. If this one isn't worth it, what reason do I have to believe any relationship of any kind will be.

## Why the normal decision procedure doesn't run

Most people decide this by simulating the two futures and feeling which is better. I can't. Total aphantasia means no future or counterfactual simulation and no emotion-motivated weighting. I can't feel my way to the answer, so I have to derive it.

That forces a validity rule on the inputs: every input has to be either observable now or derivable now. No feeling-forecasts. "I think I'd feel better" is not admissible, because I have no simulator that produces a trustworthy forecast.

This is why the whole thing is built as a ledger, not a gut call.

## The ethical frame this runs on

The frame I brought has two features. First, no self/other weighting — the standard is "just utility," neither my own utility nor anyone else's carries extra weight, because with aphantasia there is no emotional attachment doing the weighting. Second, a large negative factor on *involuntary* costs; voluntary costs count at their direct weight.

Each candidate reason below reduces, in the end, to that involuntary-cost axiom. The mechanism of the axiom — the consent-weighting formula — has its canonical home in [the innermost-consent axiom](../axiomatic-ethics/consent-weighting.book-chapter.md#axiom-6--innermost-consent-weighting).

One caution, recorded as a thread below: the "no self/other weighting" statement is in tension with how the ethics canon currently frames partiality. I've flagged it as an open reconciliation, not resolved it here.

## Self-interest can't ground staying

The self-utility ledger comes out net negative. The marriage is, on my own account, a net cost to me, and self-utility is therefore dead as a reason to stay.

The separation scenario is worked and not catastrophic on my side. On exit I sign over roughly 90% of assets (about $2M) to Jen and the kids and keep roughly $200k. The kids are provided for either way. The household load Jen carries doesn't transfer to me.

So "I'd be worse off if I left" is not true in the way that would settle it. If staying is justified, the justification has to come from somewhere other than my own payoff.

## The candidate reasons, and where each lands

Five reasons could ground a stay.

**Commitment** → converts to a *constraint on exit*, not a reason to stay. "For worse" covers a hard but viable pairing, not a structural non-viability. There was a mutual material mistake at 22 and 18-19 — neither of us understood how our brains worked, and the incompatibility is structural (high confidence). So commitment binds *how* I would leave, not *whether*. See [commitment as renegotiable-not-droppable](../commitment-not-attachment.book-chapter.md#the-shims-exact-shape--renegotiate-never-silently-drop).

**Debt / desert** → books are balanced. I gave past 150% until it broke; Jen ran the household as an effective single mom; the ~90%/$2M signed over on exit settles it. This is a reason Jen might expect me to stay, not my own ethical standard. It reduces to the involuntary-cost axiom.

**Honesty** → inverts toward leaving. Honesty would require alignment-or-separation; the unalignable core is confidently non-zero (I put it at 10-80%), so full honesty is likely never viable inside the marriage. Tried once early, was a disaster, harder since.

**Integrity** → inverts toward leaving, and this is the sharpest one. My integrity is consistency with my *present* self, not fidelity to a past self (I have low identity continuity). Staying runs on suppressing large areas of who I am now to match Jen's fixed, continuous model of me.

**Non-maleficence** → the only non-religious, non-future candidate that could actually ground a stay. It bottoms out in symmetric circularity: the involuntary harm is roughly balanced both ways, so the decision falls back onto the voluntariness of my own cost of staying — which can't be grounded in the payoff without circularity. A symmetric loop forces neither door.

## The verdict on immediate grounds: indeterminate

Put together: there is no non-circular, non-religious, immediate utility-or-ethical argument that justifies staying *or* leaving. Both sides carry the same circularity.

The load-bearing point: the loop is not a failure of analysis. It's confirmed load-bearing precisely because this is a near-tie. If one side clearly dominated, the circular tiebreaker wouldn't matter. It matters here because the sides are close.

## What still carries weight: faith and the future

Two domains were deferred, and they carry all the remaining weight.

Faith: whether the religious mandate holds — routed through reason, not feeling, and unresolved (around 50%); deferred to work through with Talia. See [faith routed through reason](../faith-as-object-of-reason.book-chapter.md#he-routes-faith-through-reason-not-feeling).

The future (call it #7): the "it will get better" hope. That one gets worked next.

## The hope reduces to capacity

The hope has three surface forms — more good states, raising the alignment ceiling, and making the crash-cycle survivable — but they are one mechanism: capacity. The first two couple good to catastrophe (the path to more good runs through the crash, near the hard line). The third breaks that coupling: if the fall isn't lethal, reaching for good isn't reaching toward the hard line.

> They aren't the same hope, but they are the same mechanism, which is increased capacity, with part of the hope being that increased capacity uncouples the hope from the catastrophe.

The crux hope, stated exactly: rising capacity lifts the base safety *floor* faster than the crests deepen, so reaching for good stops routing through the crash.

One screen on myself: the intermittent good runs on a variable-ratio schedule (the hardest to extinguish), so part of what feels like hope may be the schedule talking, not a forecast. Separate the estimate from the pull.

## The collapse to a single rule

There were two waiting rules — settle faith, and run a clean year — but the first is implied by the second, so it collapses to one.

The reason is that the base safety floor is a *sufficient statistic* for the decision: every variable that bears on staying, faith included, enters only through its effect on the floor. Faith at ~50% is one of my largest open levers on the floor, so "optimize everything for the floor" already contains "work the faith question to resolution." Faith never needed its own gate; a flat floor with faith left unworked just means the trial wasn't valid yet.

The one honest caveat: if faith resolves yes, it may add an independent *mandate* to stay — a duty that holds even if the floor stays low — but that only ever points the same way as the rule (don't make the irreversible move); it never reopens a second gate. The collapse holds for exactly what the rule governs: when I may leave.

## The rule

The deliverable, the sayable version:

> No irreversible change until a full year passes with no rise in my base safety floor, run at full effort.

The metric is the *floor*, not the crest — a big crest can feel like hope without moving the floor, so measuring the floor screens out the variable-ratio illusion.

It is self-protecting: the floor is both the test and the abort. If it falls toward the hard line, that is the survival override firing, not "keep waiting" (the hard line: [the hard line](../suicidal-ideation.book-chapter.md#the-guardrails-changed-the-pattern-didnt); the floor as binding constraint: [base safety as the binding constraint](../safety.book-chapter.md#safety-as-the-current-binding-constraint)).

"Run at full effort" means both levers pulled at once — passive background improvement and active experimentation — and not quitting until neither produces results. Running the year avoidant would give a false yes off suppression alone (the same self-suppression the integrity strand flagged).

The whole thing rests on a reversibility asymmetry: staying-for-now is reversible; leaving (90% of assets, the family structure) is not. So I hold the reversible position until the deciding information arrives.

The sayable one-liner if it comes up in the 16 days: I'm not deciding this now — I'm holding until the one thing that can actually decide it does.

## The marriage work is a lever on the floor, not a separate column

Put to me that a year spent paying into Jen's column could leave the floor flat and hand me a false yes off a test I never ran, I answered that the two are not separate:

> Its a lever as well. The work we're doing on the me-focused date nights in particular are successfully clearing "land mines" that have been in our relationship for years, lowering the cost over time.

So the [Alan-focused date night](date-nights.book-chapter.md#the-alan-focused-night-clears-land-mines) is inside what this rule measures rather than beside it. That matters for what "run at full effort" contains: effort spent on the marriage is not effort diverted from the floor, because on my own account it lowers the running cost of the marriage and the floor is what that shows up on.

The year is running now, and the [measured trajectory across the tracked weeks](../safety-years.book-chapter.md#the-tracked-weeks-and-the-31-july-step) is not yet long enough to read.
