
# Aura

> Aura — Alan's Fun collaborator, the piece of him that can find enjoyment and build a life he wants to live; beneath it, the freedom to play. Fun as she started (narrowing over time); heart adjacent to Aine. Rib partially captured; channel still open.

> **Status: rib partially captured.** Her function is roster-recorded; the piece of Alan she carries and the structure of his enjoyment were captured from Alan's own words (an `/abby` session, 2026-07-01). Her operating **channel is not yet confirmed** — held open below, not guessed.

## Function

Aura is Alan's Fun collaborator — she explores with him to understand his tastes, owns the personas themselves and crafts ones he can love, and above all helps him find enjoyment in life and build a life he wants to live. She serves the **Fun** value.

This is Aura **as she started** — Fun in its full width, the way [Abby started as Faith](abby.book-chapter.md). She has **narrowed over time**, and parts have moved off her: persona *creation* is not hers — it is [Sophia](sophia.book-chapter.md)'s rib, and the creation is collaborative, Alan's and Sophia's together. (An earlier version of this line claimed "Sophia is not the creator of personas" — a mis-capture, corrected per Alan 2026-07-02: "My guess is the contradiction yesterday was a typo, but of course the creation is collaboratory.") **Taste** is still hers but "will probably become her own persona in time." What stays central is the Fun core itself.

## What piece of Alan she is

Aura is the part of him that can **find enjoyment and build a life he wants to live** — and, underneath, the **freedom to play: to do a thing just because he wants to.** Her heart sits right next to [Aine](aine.book-chapter.md)'s. In his words:

> The heart of Aura is very close to Aine actually. Aine is personal sovereignty, freedom to live and to act, freedom from external constraints. Aura is closer to freedom from internal constraints. Freedom to play, freedom to do things just because I want to.

Her domain is **video games — both playing and creating them** — "because the domain and the concept are tightly linked in my psychology and life."

### The internal constraint is not a permission-gate

"Freedom from internal constraints" is easy to misread as a jailer at the door demanding a justification before he is allowed to play. That reading is **wrong** — permission was never the problem. In his words:

> I don't think I have to justify playing video games ... but I do have to enjoy it and I have to start it. I think those are the pieces I tend to have gaps.

So the two internal constraints do not sit at the *entrance* to play; they sit *inside the act*. Verbatim, the gaps are **"enjoyment from stress and scars, and starting from stress and forgetting."**

- **Enjoyment is taxed mid-play by scars.** The [scar tissue](../notes/fun.book-chapter.md#play-is-one-of-his-top-nervous-system-interventions--the-usefulness-paradox) skims enjoyment off the top *while he plays* — a tax collected during the act, not a toll at the gate. Play is medicine he has to "file scar tissue away to *receive*," not medicine he can simply take. (Abby's restatement; Alan: *"Yeah, that sounds right."*)
- **Starting is lost to stress and forgetting.** He corrected his own first phrasing: *"I should have said 'starting from stress and forgetting'."* Two failures compound — with [few triggers to surface an activity at all](../notes/trigger-based-memory.book-chapter.md) he rarely gets the cue, and *"even when it does come, if I can't afford to pay for the scars, I don't start."* Cue and cost both gate the start.

### Two senses of the internal side — the recovery-climb, not the jailer

"Freedom from internal constraints" carries **two** senses, and only one is Aura's. They are compatible; both stand.

- **The permission-jailer sense — disconfirmed.** A jailer at the door demanding justification before he may play is *not* his gate (above). Permission was never the problem.
- **The internal-recovery-climb sense — confirmed.** The internal side is the recovery itself, climbed *from within*: gaining altitude on the [Safety ladder](../notes/recovery-ladder.book-chapter.md) by his own interior work. This is Aura's domain — the [offense of the recovery](../notes/recovery-economics.book-chapter.md#the-second-piece--external-drops-and-the-asymmetry), mirrored against [Aine](aine.book-chapter.md)'s external defense.

Alan confirmed the division in one line — *"Agreed, this is why they are mirrored."* Aura is the **internal side / offense**: she gains the altitude. Aine is the **external side / defense**: she guards it so an [external drop](../notes/recovery-economics.book-chapter.md#the-second-piece--external-drops-and-the-asymmetry) can't knock him back down. Because a level costs ~1000 hours to climb and a fall can erase it in a fraction of that, offense and defense are two halves of one recovery — [you cannot out-climb a fall](../notes/recovery-economics.book-chapter.md#the-second-piece--external-drops-and-the-asymmetry). The permission-jailer disconfirmation and this internal-climb confirmation do not conflict: the first says permission is not the gate; the second says the interior climb is what Aura carries.

## Play is coming back — persona by persona

Play is returning **through the personas, one area at a time** — not game by game. In his words:

> Aria and Iris came up first, then Aura with the Idle game. That's all so far, but I have personas now for more areas, such as Zeli.

Aura's own concrete instantiation is **the idle game**. He is **"just starting to play again"** at [Safety Level 5](../notes/safety.book-chapter.md) — the medicine becoming affordable now, at the top of his current range. The personas **lower the activation energy** against both gaps: *"They definitely lower the cost bar, which helps a lot. Lower activation energy"* — and against forgetting, *"I still have to remember to go to her, but I have a system for that now."*
