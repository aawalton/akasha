"I'd rather die than be a wage slave!"

Marina labored up the corporate ladder. The plan: make it big and finally live a slow, relaxing life. After years of fixing column widths on spreadsheets for asshole vice presidents, she was finally promoted to department manager. Then it all came crashing down when the quarterly report pointed to a potential layoff. Her layoff.

Fortunately, she died overdosing on sleeping pills before the layoff could happen!

Waking up in the realm of the Underworld, she is now guided by a quest-giving system screen, on a mission to decorate the dark world using colorful sweet rolls and bread. With the help of a flirtatious vampire, Marina now navigates her glamorous cubical-free afterlife as a dough-wielding magician.
