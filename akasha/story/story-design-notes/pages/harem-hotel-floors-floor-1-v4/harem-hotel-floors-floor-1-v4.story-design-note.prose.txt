## Enemies

### The Doorward

- **Role:** floor-1 warden — a plaster construct the chamber grows; ACTIVE COMBATANT (challengeOwner: character), real kill-risk for a level-1 bound pair
- **Readable trait:** COMBAT PROFILE (recast from the retired puzzle-gate — it now FIGHTS, it does not merely 'answer what is brought'). INITIATIVE + STRIKES: heavy plaster blows and a GRAB that pins a fragile fighter — a pin or a flank on level-1 Alan (low VIT) is the genuine kill-risk line; hold position, use Aria's 'Set the Pace' to slip the grab and deny the flank. DURABLE, NOT FORCE-IMMUNE: a tough plaster body takes REDUCED damage on its warded mass (x0.5-0.75) and NEUTRAL real damage on open lines (x1.0) — sustained cooperative combat wears it down; the fight is WINNABLE with no read at all, only slower and riskier. CRIT-SPOT (tactical optimization): the keystone-seam low at the sternum (where the plaster last closed) is where it takes correct/decisive multipliers — a PERCEPTION/INT read (Aria's 'Read the Table' assists at bound L1) yields the seam's LOCATION as INFO; a targeted strike there lands x1.5-2.0 (correct) or x3.0 (decisive keystone), ending it fast and cutting exposure. NO MIRROR-PUNISH: brute is suboptimal, never the losing line (the retired x0.3 mirror is GONE). WOUND-GUARD ADAPTATION (emergent in play, canon t15): once the crit-seam is struck, the wounded Doorward turns its cracked side to the wall and GUARDS the seam — a SINGLE warden-simple reactive behavior: it protects the one thing that hurt it, and does NOT anticipate tactics, gain new attacks, or grow cleverer than a door-guard. Effect: the seam now requires an EARNED OPENING (force it to commit off the door, bait its guard open) rather than a free repeat-shot — a TACTICAL problem resolved by declaration + dice, NEVER a gate. TWO GUARDS: (1) the seam stays REACHABLE via earned openings (an earned opening + a good roll reopens it; never a permanent lock, no fixed exchange-counter — it reopens when the fiction + dice earn it, keeping momentum, not a repeated-turned-miss stall); (2) the BODY path stays viable-but-risky (x0.5-0.75) as the fallback, so the wound-guard makes the seam HARDER, never GATES the fight. This is a light foreshadow of floor 2's fuller migrating-crit-spot mechanic (the Usher that LEARNS mid-fight). Construct reset on retreat: re-weaves to pristine FREE of the rest-clock; information (seam location, its tells, the layout) PERSISTS, HP does not — so a retreat costs the damage dealt, never the learning, and the fight cannot be attrition-ground.
