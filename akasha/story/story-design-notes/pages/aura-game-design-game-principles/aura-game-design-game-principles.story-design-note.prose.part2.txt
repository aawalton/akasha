**Play should develop preferences *honestly*. Manipulation — FOMO, variable-ratio conditioning — rigs *what you want* and corrupts the value half; worst when extractive, context-dependent, not an absolute ban.**
- *the draw is innocent* — randomness itself just paces exploration; play is a **whole-game property**, so judge a mechanic by its contribution to the whole game, not its solo playability.
