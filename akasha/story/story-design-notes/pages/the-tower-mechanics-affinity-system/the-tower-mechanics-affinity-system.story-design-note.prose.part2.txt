## 8. Coordinator-only

- **Loop interaction (HELD DARK — never write into player-facing flavor).** Per `loop/loop-rules.md`: **affinities
  (rank + eu + emerged essence-skills) are RETAINED** through death; **unabsorbed seeds and held items are LOST**
