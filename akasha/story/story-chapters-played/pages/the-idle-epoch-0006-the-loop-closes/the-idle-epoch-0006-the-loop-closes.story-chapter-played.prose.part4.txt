Callum lay back on his cot and closed his eyes. The Enhancement Construct pulsed. The compound loop cycled. The curve bent upward, accelerating, doubling every seventy-two hours, climbing toward a number that would change everything.

Outside, the Detroit Barrier Zone slept. The Iron Vanguard kept its watch. Drake Morrison, somewhere in the Barrier Hall, made plans built on the assumption that power was linear and the strong stayed strong by fighting harder than everyone else.

He did not know that in a warehouse on the edge of the Foundry District, the math had just changed.

The loop was closed. The aura was lit. Two people slept within its radius, their idle processes humming fifteen percent faster because a skinny engineer with dark circles under his eyes had spent four months building a machine that made other machines better.

The curve was bending. And it would not stop.

Pip pulsed once more -- slow, warm, steady -- and Callum let the hum of his constructs carry him to sleep.

The numbers climbed.
