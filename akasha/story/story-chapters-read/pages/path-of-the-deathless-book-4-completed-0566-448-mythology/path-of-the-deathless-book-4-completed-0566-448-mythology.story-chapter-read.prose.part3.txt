“I will see twelve more Poly-Magi regiments dispatched to reinforce Gate Piety as well. If the Queen is to be present, we would want her life to be at risk from assassins—nor would we like her to be a risk to anyone else’s life, would we?”

Like everything with Veronica, everything she said could be understood as a threat—but also a reward. And the Poly-Magi had been invaluable…

“Only if they’re a bit more like Ircanos than your advisor lackey.”

“Oh, of course, Deathless. Like calls to like, and your Gate is proving useful in another way—just like how you are helping us purge the wrongful elements of our society. Some problem children go south, and some go toward the coast.”

“And you always win, huh?” Shiv asked.

“Always.” Veronica took another sip from her now empty glass—for sake of the vibe, as a performance all her own. “Now. If you want to learn how to win, always, then start expanding your understanding of people. Of who they imagine themselves to be, and who they actually are. After mastering that, well, you will see violence as the refuge of the incompetent or desperate.”

“Or just cruel and mad,” Shiv said, brushing Kathereine and the Ascendants with his words.

“Yes. Or the cruel and mad.”

And through it all, the Faceless Queen remained absent—and Shiv was starting to get a feeling that vacancy was more a deliberate choice caused by Veronica and Kathereine’s presence than just a happenstance of the moment. Well, Queen, here come the consequences of trying to use me so much; got a lot more choices than one. Be figuring out what lies you tell yourself soon.
