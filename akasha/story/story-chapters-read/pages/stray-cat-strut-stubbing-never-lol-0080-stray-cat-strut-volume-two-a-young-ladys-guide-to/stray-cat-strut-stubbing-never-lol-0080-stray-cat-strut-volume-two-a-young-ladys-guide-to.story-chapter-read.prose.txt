The duty of a Samurai is to the people. Mostly people just like themselves.

After her long first day as a bomb-flinging, alien-killing Samurai, Catherine Leblanc finds herself tasked with finding a lost girl, one who may be a fellow Samurai, or a link to an entire font of troubles.

Cat is in the unenviable position of having to explode her way up the corporate ladder in order to uncover certain nasty little secrets.
