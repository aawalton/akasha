We hunted ourselves some breakfast and then made our way back to the empire walls by mid-afternoon. I checked out Lily’s new Path along the way and was quite impressed by what I saw.

Eheheh “Deceiver”! I loved Lily’s new Path’s name!

It seemed the main upgrades from this evolution were to her clones, while also getting a similar trait to Grick’s that focused on their bond as rider and mount. Giving them mass and also allowing her and Grick to switch their true selves between clones could really become overpowered! I could imagine both offensive and defensive uses of it. Like imagine switching your true self right before taking a hit? That could really be overpowered!

Shadow Crucifixion must have been what she was using when she held that bandit in place for our interrogation. Though, her strength was quite low, so I supposed she couldn’t rely on it too much against stronger targets.

But then I couldn’t help but give a wolfy smile at Fear Resonance. My precious little sister is just becoming more and more of a nightmare, isn’t she? I couldn’t be any prouder!

She had gained a few levels since last time I checked. I wasn’t even that far ahead of her anymore, though it did make sense since we were fighting things under my level for a while, and the higher your level, the harder it is to advance. At the rate things were going, I couldn’t imagine how hard it would be to gain levels once I was in my 30s or 40s or even higher.

Soon, we were nearing the wall outside of the Verdant Empire capital, where a set of green-garbed guards leaned into each other and whispered, one pointing towards us. Grick was sitting atop Lily’s back as we prowled up, and the guards cleared their throats.

“Ah—you must be Grick and the two Hell Hounds. We were ordered to escort you back to the palace if you arrived.”

“Thanks, nice humans!” Grick said.

I guess it was only natural to address the person who could respond, rather than the two dogs, though I was sure Grick was getting painted more and more as the hero of this story. It was obvious by the way the farmers pointed in our direction and whispered to each other. They were much more focused on Grick than either Lily or myself.

“Grick, ask them about Mei.”

“Did human girl come?” he asked, looking up at one of the guards from Lily’s back. “Grick rescues human girl named Mei from nasty bandits.”

The guards looked at each other, and one said, “I believe I heard something about that. She was escorted to the Palace, as far as I know.”

Well, if she was this important Mei person for real, I guess she was okay. There was something weird about her though, for sure.

We went through one of the side entrances of the wall and were met by another group of guards who walked with us all the way back to the green-tiled roads that led to the palace grounds. From the gate to the palace was about a good hour-long walk, and once we arrived, we were handed off to yet a third group of guards. I decided it was time to take my human form, but I needed something first, and as soon as the handmaids floated in to tend to our needs, I asked Grick to translate for me.

“Puppy needs human clothes,” Grick said, pointing at me.

“Of course,” Aiko said, “right this way, we’ll get you all taken care of.”

“Where Rin?” Grick asked.

“Rin has been informed of your arrival and will send for you when she’s available,” Aiko assured.

I found that they were prepared for us after all, because a privately sectioned-off portion of our room had two fresh sets of clothes waiting for me. One outfit was another of those flowery kimonos, and I let out a huff as soon as I saw it. I figured I’d have to talk to Aiko about getting me something for evening wear that was a little… I don’t know. Different. Sure, it was comfortable, but I was a predator! Hmmm… well, the cute outfit probably makes me seem less like a threat which might be preferable so long as I’m in the palace walls, but I’d think my small body was doing a good enough job of that already!

Luckily, the other set of clothes was a little more practical. A nice blue tunic with some slacks that could be tied off with a sash.

I switched my Path to Veiledfang Stalker, then transformed and quickly got changed, tying off the sash as best I could, but everything fitted rather loosely. Once I walked out from the privacy of the divider, the girls huddled around me.

“Please, allow us to help you with this Lady Hell Hound,” one said as they started adjusting my clothing.

Lady Hell Hound? I guess they didn’t have a proper name to call me by yet, but that felt a little weird.

“It’s fine,” I said, trying to wave them off.

“Now, now, it’ll only take a moment.” They pulled and tugged at my clothes, and I was honestly somewhere between too exhausted or too apathetic at the moment to really complain too much about it. I just let them do their thing, and once they were done, it fit much more snugly.

“Where’s Fox?” I asked as soon as they released me.

“Lady Fox? She’s… um…” Aiko frowned, folded her arms, and tapped her foot. “I’ll go see if she’s available.” She said it with such annoyance in her voice, I wondered what Fox had been up to while we were away?

Aiko slipped away without another word, her sandals whispering softly against the polished floor as she headed over to Fox’s room.

Grick leaned toward me. “Fox in trouble?”

“I doubt it,” I muttered. “Fox has probably just been being… Fox.”

Lily, however, was clearly uninterested in all this, her attention drifting toward another handmaid coming in and setting down a tray of food. Lily scampered over to it and sniffed as she decided on what to try.

“Just leave me some puffstork legs,” I told her.

It wasn’t long before Aiko returned—but not quite how I expected.

She emerged from Fox’s room with a stiff posture and a very deliberate calm, one hand pressed firmly against her face as if she were trying to physically push something out of her mind.

“…She’ll be with you shortly,” Aiko said, voice tight.

There was a brief pause, then the door behind her cracked open again.

A girl stumbled out—young, red-faced, and very clearly flustered. Her hair was a mess, strands sticking out in every direction, and her clothes… well, they were technically on, but only just. The sash at her waist had been tied in a hurry, slightly crooked, and one sleeve hung lower than the other.

She froze the moment she noticed us.

“…Ah—!” Her face somehow turned an even deeper shade of red. She quickly straightened, or at least tried to. “L-Lady Fox is available now!”

Then she bowed far too quickly before scurrying off down the hall like she was fleeing a burning building.

“Grick, why don’t you go ahead and get some food? I just wanted to ask her about some things,” I said. I was also just slightly worried about what I’d find behind that door.

Grick didn’t need to be told twice about the food, though.

“Okay!” he said, hopping down from Lily’s back and immediately making a beeline for the food like a missile.

Aiko slid the door open for me and I entered where I instantly spotted Fox, sprawled across a mound of cushions like she owned the entire palace.

Actually, scratch that—she looked like she owned the world.

She lay on her stomach, half-sunken into the cushions, a pipe resting between her fingers as she took a slow draw then exhaled a thin stream of smoke towards the ceiling.

Two girls knelt around her—one gently brushing out her tail, the other working her hands against Fox’s back in a slow massage.

On the bed just off to the side, a third girl lay completely passed out, sprawled on her back, breathing slow and even, looking like she’d been utterly spent and then abandoned there.

“Hey, kiddo!” Fox said cheerily. “How’d the hunting go?”

“We completed the quest…” I said, already questioning why I bothered coming in here in the first place.

“Hmmm, fifteen, right? That was fast.”

“Fifteen? Lady Fox?” the girl brushing her tail asked.

“…Ah—don’t worry about that. Business talk,” Fox said to her. She rolled a shoulder as she started to get up, signaling the girls to pause. They each stood at attention, hands folded under their sleeves as Fox sat up and stretched, letting out a long yawn that bared her sharp fangs.

“Would Lady Fox like a glass of wine?” one of the girls asked.

Fox didn’t even think about it. “Mm. Yes, please. That’d be nice.” The girl scurried over to a table to retrieve it.

I immediately cut in. “How much have you had to drink today?”

Fox blinked at me. “Nothing yet. We’re just barely getting up.” Then she grinned. “But no time like the present, right?”

“Fox…” I said dryly.

She raised a brow.

“We’re meeting with Rin soon,” I said flatly. “You’re supposed to be my advisor. Shouldn’t you be sober for that?”

Fox studied me for a second, then let out a quiet huff through her nose. “Mmmm… Fine. You got a point.” She waved a hand lazily at the girl who had just returned with the bottle. “Hold off on that for now.”

The girl bowed her head. “Yes, Lady Fox.”

Fox pushed herself up to a stand, stretching as she did, her tail flicking again as if mildly put out. “Mmmm, has breakfast been set out, at least?”

“…Yeah,” I said. “Grick and Lily are already tearing into it.”

Fox perked up slightly at that, the annoyance fading almost instantly. She turned to the two girls. “Alright, you can take a break. Looks like I need to start getting ready for work.” She winked.

“Yes Lady Fox,” they chimed together, bowing their eyes below their hanging sleeves. One of them walked over to the girl who was passed out on the bed and began to nudge her.

Fox lifted a hand. “Actually, just let her sleep.” She flashed a fang. “She’s had a long night.”

The girl in question let out a content hum as her arms reached out and snagged a pillow that she hugged against herself, and she curled deeper under the covers.

I didn’t even want to know what the hell’s been going on since we left.

But whatever, I didn’t worry about it as I made it back to the breakfast table to find Lily had been very polite about not hogging all the puffstork legs.

“Hey! You two have evolved already, congratulations!” Fox said as she plopped down at the table. “Mmmm, got some levels too. That’s actually insanely good for just a few days.”

“Grick powerful!” the goblin said between sliding a fish into his mouth, and he flexed a toned arm as he did so.

Serving girls floated in, setting cups in front of both me and Fox and filling them with a steaming liquid. I sniffed at it, determining it was tea, which was something I wasn’t particularly interested in.

“Seriously, though,” Fox said, resting her cheek on her palm as she sipped her tea. “It’s not normal to gain multiple levels in a week, let alone just a few days.”

“It’s because of Pack Bond. We can get full experience while hunting as a team,” I explained. “So how much are you kept in the loop around here?”

“In the loop?” Fox asked.

“Do you know what’s going on with my brothers?”

“Ah— You’ll have to ask Rin. I’ve just been settling in myself.”

I gave her a glare, feeling tempted to place a Death Mark on her now that the one on that archer back in the woods had finally worn off. I realized I’d have to be a lot more careful with Death Marks in the future and only use them on someone I was sure I’d be killing soon—aaah, not that I actually intended on killing Fox! I just had the urge to plague her with the fear effect.

Fox chuckled. “Hey, hey! You’re staring daggers.” But then her voice lowered with a bit more hostility. “You got a problem, kid?”

“Not a problem, but you’re only here because they want me. And you haven’t exactly been subtle about taking advantage of the situation.”
