The numbers ticked down relentlessly, as if mocking him, but it was the twist of braided lines somewhere deeper still that caught his eye. The Chartermark was proving to be an exceptional thing. More than luminous lines and strange countdowns, it held secrets. Harker put his forearm inches away from his eyes. Layers on layers, the Water and gold twisted together into knotted patterns that branched into hair-thin lines. A secret in plain sight.

Harker dropped his arm, head snapping toward the opening of his claimed den. Something is here.

It wasn’t so obvious as a snapped branch or a trembling leaf, but Harker could feel a change in the air. The wind blew and the leaf shadows danced across the loam…except one moved oddly. It was a leaf, same as all the rest, but it danced against the breeze.

Harker unsheathed his knife, crawling forward by slow inches, but the wind stopped. The leaves stilled, but the strange one vanished.

The last of his Talent faded completely, and the world dulled. Still, Harker crept out of the den with his knife ready to stab anything that dared move too quickly.

No one was there. The boulders and trees were as empty as before, leaving Harker crouching in the sun-drenched woods with a knife held low. His heart hammered despite it all, adrenaline spiking in his veins almost as hard as Water.

Whatever he’d seen, it had moved on.

It was past time Harker did the same.
