Just as fascinating, however, were the five primary tributaries he'd cut. He modeled them after his mother's brief teachings and Stillwater's example. They branched off of his center, each one a loop of their own. One for each limb, and one for his head.

Tentatively, Harker inspected them all, starting with his left arm. Though he'd fouled it originally, his new additions cut a thorned pathway through his shoulder. There wasn't much he could do to shore up the worn edges, not without some sort of recovery treatment, but Harker deepened the shallow flow of it, and in so doing smoothed out its jagged edges.

In his right arm, he cut something far more efficient and elegant. Same with his left and right leg. They spiraled down his limbs, thorns reaching outward into muscle and bone and blood before terminating at the base of his feet, his palm, and this throat.

At the end of each primary, he found a hardened knot of stone. It was faded, as if it weren’t entirely real, but it stopped the flow of water as surely as any dam.

My Locks. If he could get them open, he’d have far more options for manifesting his Talent, not to mention for refinement. If he could get a proper Current, his power could—

Harker clenched his jaw, heart racing in excitement.

Power.

Harker looked to his Chartermark and the golden lines within, eager to see exactly how much progress he’d made.
