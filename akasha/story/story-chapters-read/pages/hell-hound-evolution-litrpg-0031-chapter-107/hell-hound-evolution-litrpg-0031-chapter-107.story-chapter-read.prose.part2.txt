Was that frog the one who had been communicating with me this whole time? Was he, in fact, the one controlling the System?

"You... Um. Emperor? Y-Your Majesty. Have you been helping me this whole time?"

Rin's smirk widened. "I'm not sure what exactly you're thinking, girl, but it seems you've come up with a rather amusing idea. But in any case, it seems the emperor has accepted your petition and is offering you a place of service for the empire. One that may grow if you are to prove yourself."

"But did he just give me that quest?" I asked.

"Ribbit."

Rin looked at the frog and bowed with respect. "Permission to speak of your Path, Your Majesty."

"Ribbit."

"Very well." Rin dipped towards him once more before turning to me again.

"The Emperor, as you may have surmised, is a monster and one with an incredibly unique Path. There are no known records or accounts of others having this skill. But the emperor alone has the power to grant quests and rewards as the System does."

"Does that mean he is part of the System?” I asked.

Rin looked towards the Emperor as if asking permission.

“Ribbit.”

“No. He is not. Though he is limited in how he may grant rewards. For a great reward, an equally great quest must be bestowed and completed. But it is through this power that His Majesty has built much of what you see before you. Fields restored. Roads reclaimed. Outposts strengthened. Men rewarded for valor. Families compensated for sacrifice. The System grants its own rewards where it wills, but the Emperor may shape one's service with his own incentives.”

I looked back at the frog. He stared back with the same blank little froggy face he’d had the whole time. Yet, now staring at him inspired a feeling of awe. Like I truly was standing before someone great and powerful.

I glanced at the quest window again.

My heart was pounding now, but for an entirely different reason.

This was real. This was actually real.

All those people earlier… the farmer, the blacksmith, the soldiers looking for Captain Hoshin’s brother…

They hadn’t just been hearing a croak. They’d been receiving something. Quests of their own.

That was why some of them had looked confused, while others had understood immediately.

My ears twitched.

Okay. This is actually kind of insane. And also the first thing in this room that had made any real sense.

I looked over my shoulder at the Fox. “Did you get one too?”

Fox merely shrugged a shoulder.

Rin said, “Fox, I’ll speak with you later about your intentions going forward. But it seems the emperor has nothing for you for the time being. However, I will insist that you do not assist these three on their quests.”

“What, why not?” I asked, shooting my gaze back to Rin.

“Because the intention is not only to prove your worth but also to grow your strength. You will gain less of that by relying on someone stronger than yourself.”

Right, I guess that does make sense.

I let out a sigh, but I supposed it was fine. It's not like we ever needed Fox to succeed at hunting before anyway. And to be honest, I was kind of excited about hunting humans again—ones who deserved it, that is. The caves of the Cragpeak Vale were not truly that long ago, but a part of me longed to become a shadow once again.

But first there was one thing I had to do.

I looked at the two options floating in front of me, lifted a claw, and pressed [Yes].
