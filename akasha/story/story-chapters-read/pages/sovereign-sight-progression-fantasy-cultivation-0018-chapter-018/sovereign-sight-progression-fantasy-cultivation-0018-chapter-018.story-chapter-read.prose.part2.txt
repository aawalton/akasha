Harker tilted his head. "Meaning?"

"Meaning your Stature demands a greater repayment. What are you?"

Harker bared his teeth. Stillwater was off balance, and he wanted it that way. "So you owe me three life debts then."

Fangs ground against one another. “So it would seem.”

“And my.. Stature, was it? It's too much for you to overcome."

Stillwater glared.

Harker squatted down. The dizziness had already faded. "Why? What is Stature?"

“I don’t have to tell you that.”

“That’s fine. I can figure it out.” Harker’s mind turned over the Eidhrin’s words. “You called it a Bargain before. The old words don’t mention that but…You called it an oath of service before.” Harker frowned. “You were trying to bind me into a Bargain yourself.”

Stillwater sat up, his short legs flopping out before his rotund body. He grinned. “Great Tides, he’s figured it out!”

Harker lifted his cold iron chain and Stillwater flinched. He put it back around his neck. “Service implies a great deal.”

The Eidhrin had nothing to say to that.

“You can become invisible to the senses. What else can you do, Stillwater?”

“More than you, Talent.”

Harker sighed and stood back up. “However it works, you cannot hurt me…and I find myself no longer tired. Come, we’re traveling.”

Once again, there was that faintest of pressure against Harker's chest, as he said the words, but it vanished in moments.

Stillwater grumbled, but stood.

Interesting. Harker smiled as he lifted his pack. Very interesting.
