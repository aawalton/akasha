Monthly surveillance transfers are sent to the Grand Citadel every 30 days, so it is not unusual for there to be no news for long periods; the Grand Citadel is millions of kilometers away.

What is unusual, however, is receiving an emergency system message signifying a Demon from one of the 5 Great Families has died.

This is what rings in the Vermillion Family Head's system interface as an automated series of notifications are sent out the instant Andras is killed.

Simultaneously, four more automated notifications ping in the systems of other Family Heads, lighting up system messages across all of the higher Colored Zones of the Upper Realm.
