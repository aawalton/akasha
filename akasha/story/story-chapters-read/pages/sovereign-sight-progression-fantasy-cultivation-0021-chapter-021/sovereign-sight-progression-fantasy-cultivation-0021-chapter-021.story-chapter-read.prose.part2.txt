"I'll consider it." Harker offered the kid a smile. For some reason, Johan flinched. "Let's talk about it when I return."

"All right." Johan clasped his hand and gave it a firm squeeze. "Be seeing you soon, then."

Anita was waiting for him at the side of the pen when Johan made his way back. She stared off past his shoulder. "Is he coming back?"

"I don't think so.”

“Ugh. Sven, what did you do that for?"

Johan looked at their hot-headed companion. He was still out cold amidst the mud and charred hay, his mouth a bit scorched but otherwise fine. He hadn’t managed to see all of what Harker had done, but what he had witnessed was curious.

He was so fast, like Sven couldn't touch him, not ‘til the end. But his shoulder got hurt in this fight. That means he hasn't refined his skin yet. That's…strange.

Outside in. That was the rule hammered into all of them by their teachers, parents, and anyone who could get a word in edgewise about how to develop their Talents.

Johan sighed. "Depths, but I wish I could have seen the action.”

By the time he had taken to the air above the fight, it was already over.

Sven had refined his skin. He should have been impervious to the strikes from someone without a refined body. That meant Harker had refined his muscles and bones already.

Skipping the skin… Is he mad? Or a genius?

“Do you think we can change his mind?” Anita asked, chewing her lip.

"Doubtful."

"Just like the other one," Anita sighed. "Why'd you say she was going with us too?"

"Because I believe we can convince her to. We're stronger together." Johan stared at the back of the inn as if he could see through it. Beyond, Harker was strolling with the casual confidence of the mighty. "With them, we could handle anything coming our way."
