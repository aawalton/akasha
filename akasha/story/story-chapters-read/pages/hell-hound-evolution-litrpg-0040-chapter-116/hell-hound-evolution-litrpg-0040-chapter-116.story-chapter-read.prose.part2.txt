I ripped open the Mossgrazer’s belly, being a little more selfish this time as I went for the organs. They were Lily’s favorite part, but I liked them a lot too!

As I chewed on the squishy meat, I thought it over.

The other disadvantage of Arcane Nightstalker is that it would require collecting Elemental Essences, which Rin said I could recharge at a statue in the Capitol, but we didn’t have any of the crystals from the caves anymore, and mana in general did not seem to be in great abundance in this forest. I suppose worst-case scenario, we’d have to run back so I could change Paths and recharge—maybe even snag Grick a longer-reaching weapon.

But as I squished the Mossgrazes kidney between my jaws and ripped it free from the creature’s guts, an idea struck me.

When they spotted me earlier, the archer jumped down from his tree to join in chasing after me…

If I had Lily act as bait again, have her go in for the first attack and then flee, I could ambush the archer.

We still had speed and mobility on our side, so if things got too hairy, we could just make a run for it again.

With the plan beginning to take shape, I turned to my pack and used Pack Bond to start communicating my ideas.
