It covers my heart, internal organs, dantian, and most vital meridians. If all other measures in battle are broken through, this is another added last-resort life-saving treasure.

It cannot be sensed by normal qi refiners, but it has a hidden strength bound inside each strand of silk. A stronger cultivator could easily perceive this is an item of abnormal value with a quick spiritual sense scan.

I practice pulling it in and out of my storage space to be perfectly fitted on my torso beneath my robes within half a second, but after a few tries my concentration wavers when I feel a light popping sensation in my souls.

It isn’t painful; if anything, it actually feels nice, like some kind of internal bottleneck just disappeared and allowed the outer wisps of my souls to expand a little. Even my spiritual senses creep out a few meters for some unknown reason.

My first instinct is that it has something to do with this vest, but I still push my attention out of my mental space and look around.

Lan Xin is still unconscious. Her serpent has fallen into a deep sleep, fatigued after finally ridding both of them of blood qi entirely.

Yet, their souls are in shambles, weaker than some initial-stage stewards that work for me back in my farming plot.

A single wrong move, and they’d shatter apart.

My spiritual senses pick up an abnormality behind me, and I realize what the real reason for this sensation is.

The white egg deep in the metal element portion of this array is starting to hatch.
