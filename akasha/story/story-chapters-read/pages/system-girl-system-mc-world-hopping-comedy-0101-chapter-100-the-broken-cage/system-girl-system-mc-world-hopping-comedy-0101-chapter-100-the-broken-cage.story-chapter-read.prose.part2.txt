In truth, the State kept a magical back door to all contracted individuals for situations exactly like this, acting as a fail-safe against rogue nobility. With this item, Parisa could temporarily transfer the magical contracts from Greenwood to herself, granting her the authority to order them to speak the absolute truth without fear of the Mana Poisoning triggering.

She held the seal high, funneling her immense mana into the artifact. It pulsed with a blinding, blue light.
