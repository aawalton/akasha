Lily slid into the chaotic bestial wrestling match a moment later. It seemed Grick had jumped off of her, and he was either looking for his own opening or keeping lookout.

Lily and I snapped and tore, hurting the Drakewolf more and more with each bite as it snarled and thrashed and whined for mercy.

But when I was finally satisfied that we had done enough damage, I lifted my paw.

{Soul Rend!}

My paw turned ghostly, and I raked it through the Drakewolf’s chest!

It fell limp instantly.

Foe Eliminated! Level 18 Domesticated Drakewolf

Essence extracted!

55 health restored

120 mana restored

150 Stamina restored

+10 to all attributes

Lily let out a victorious yip as her tail wagged, and Grick walked over, sheathing his dagger, which he hadn’t gotten a chance to use in this particular fight. He patted Lily’s side and shook his head as he looked down at our kill. “That’s not good puppy. That nasty puppy,” he said of the Drakewolf.

Then, a different kind of shout broke through the forest.

“They’re over here!”

“Let’s run,” I said to my pack, and instantly began leading the way. But a thought crossed my mind. “I injured another one. Let’s kill it and then wait until night to come back.”

My thinking was that if we were going to take out this camp then we needed as many advantages as we could get, and I think Soul Rending as many weakened and isolated foes as possible would be just the way to do it.
