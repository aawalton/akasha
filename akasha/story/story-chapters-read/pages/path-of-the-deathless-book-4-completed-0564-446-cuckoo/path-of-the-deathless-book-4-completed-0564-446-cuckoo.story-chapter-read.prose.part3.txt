She vanished once more.

Shiv’s black mood spread over to Adam and Uva—with her suffering a particular kind of sourness. “Never in my life have I felt more impotent as a counterintelligence operative.”

“Tell me about it.” Shiv glared at the spot the Faceless Queen once occupied. “Still have no felling clue how she managed to follow me Backstage. Shouldn’t be possible. She never appeared there at all. She even knows about—”

Adam groaned and spat an intuitive guess before anyone else could respond: “You’re not the only entity who leaves context. If I have to make a wager—it’s your Garden that’s compromised. It has a budding personality, does it not? It's more than just a skill—and perhaps that’s how she imprinted herself on it. Or… did something to compromise it, anyway.”

The storm clouds over Shiv became a hurricane of frustration. “Of course. Of course it is.”

On the other side of reality’s curtain, the girl who didn't exist shrieked a slur at the Red Rider’s Hand—which had its middle finger pre-raised in salute.
