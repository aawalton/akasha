Celica paused, vividly remembering her last moments just now. She had been milliseconds away from being completely eviscerated by the crystalline arrow the boss had shot out towards her.

Even she couldn't outplay an attack like that with her current items, job class, equipment and race. When she'd heard the system notification, she'd assumed it was a different one entirely. One that sounded like this.

[You have died.]

Instead, she'd lived. But only barely and by the skin of her teeth. She took a deep breath in and looked down at the empress' corpse. Now it was Celica looking down at the boss, the reverse of how the stage started.

But Celica wasn't displaying arrogance like the boss had earlier. Instead, her gaze was embezzled with respect. The boss had earned Celica's respect from that last attack.

[Since you have cleared this trial with 12 minutes remaining, the system will reward you with reputation. Every extra minute is worth 100 reputation in all of Earthonia. Awarding 1250 reputation in all of Earthonia.]

"1250 Realm Reputation just like that!?"  Celica's eyes widened as she spoke. Then she thought about it more rationally and understood that it actually made sense. "I guess the game thinks what I just did was pretty amazing."

Pretty amazing, was an understatement, actually. Beating a boss almost double a player's level was beyond impressive, especially considering that boss was royalty.

Reflexively, Celica opened up her status page.
