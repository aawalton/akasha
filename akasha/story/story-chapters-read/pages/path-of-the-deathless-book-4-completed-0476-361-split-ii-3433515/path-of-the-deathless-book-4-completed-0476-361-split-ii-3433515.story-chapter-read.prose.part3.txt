She and her summons were promptly pinched in place as the Challenger clasped her with his divine field. Uva let out a hoarse yelp of pain as all her spiderlings were crushed into powder. Her body began cracking—somehow, the Challenger was putting enough pressure on her to clench the gaps that made up her being shut and seal away the Outside.

“Stop!” Shiv shouted. “You motherfucker! Stop! Please!”

But the Challenger ignored him now as well. Instead, he blinked and sighed with wonder as Uva’s corruptive influence spread across his divine mana like a plague. “Oh, and the little Paragon is not the only surprise. Quite the change in her as well. To think I cared little for you before, Little Umbral, besides as one I could hurt to further motivate my Insul.”

And the violence was back on for Shiv. He saw Uva shaking and writhing, trying to free herself. The cracks that sounded from her came to the accompaniment of her biting back screams. But the Challenger’s grip slackened, and he let her breathe and cough and wheeze and recover. “Ah. But you three are best addressed together. And only one truly owes me a debt. Only one must make a proper trade with me.”

Once more, the Challenger leaned in close to Adam. “Wake up, Little Arrow. Wake up, and let me greet you.”

And the weight of the Challenger’s words proved great, for Adam’s eyes snapped open in bloodshot alarm, and the first thing that left his lips was a wail of complete madness as his Awareness tore his mind apart.
