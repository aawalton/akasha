Trey sobbed. He hugged the backpack to him. Someone had died there. A dozen! He’d killed them, and it was a mercy.
“I’ll never forgive them. Never. Ever. I’ll come back and burn it to the ground.”
Trey whispered it out loud. His grief was mixed with his disgust, his horror. His fury. From ahead, the Quarass looked at him, but Trey met her eyes. And it was she who looked away first.
“Yes.”
Gazi knelt by Trey. Her eyes were intent. And she nodded as she rested a hand on his shoulder.
“When that day comes, His Majesty will be there.”
No one said anything after that. The Quarass spoke in a clipped voice as she angled the carpet north and east.
“We fly back to Reim. The King of Destruction has tweaked the world by the nose at Jecrass. Lest he cause offense, we should move with haste.”
“Yes. And we may begin my plan.”
Gazi stood. She looked at Trey, the Quarass. And the ruler of Germina eyed her back, narrow-eyed. But the half-Gazer’s eye shone as the sun set and Trey left the Shield Kingdom of horrors behind. Perhaps he travelled with something just as terrible—or two of them. But Trey—watched the Quarass. Then Gazi. She sat next to him, cleaning her armor.
Trey wiped his eyes and leaned against her, too exhausted to care or fear for the future. He slept, as the King of Destruction met with Gnolls. Before they arrived at their destination, A’ctelios Salash had declared war.
