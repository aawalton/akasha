She was two, maybe three, trips away from done when a horn blew—rapidly picked up by the others as the sound travelled across Remera. Ranthia cursed and hurriedly put the barrel down before she sent an image up on the wall and—she wasn’t wearing her armor, shit—replaced it with another image and shifted to it.

“What’s going on?” She asked the soaked legionaries, her eyes fixed on the horizon.

“Sir! One of the patrol groups is under attack, can’t see much from here, but there’s plenty of water being slung around and I saw Skills!” One of the [Warriors] reported.

Ranthia spent a heartbeat weighing her options. Her armor was on its rack—newly oiled to reduce the damage from water if it was needed—back in her tent. She could get it, but it would take time even with her shifting and stats. But the legionaries were struggling for their lives, and any delay might cost them another life.

“Have [Healers] on standby for wounded! Get a rescue team assembled and tell the base commander to increase readiness, just in case this is about to get big!” Ranthia ordered while she sent an image beyond the wall and started to shift.

She never heard the legionaries’ response; her perspective changed to the flooded terrain outside the base almost the same instant as she finished speaking. Ranthia willed her adamantium to start forming her knives—the rain wasn’t a substantial hindrance to that process, thankfully—even as she began her own dance across the water toward the conflict.

“Fuck! Fall back!” Someone shouted moments after a large splash of water transformed into a frozen sculpture that almost resembled a simple flower.

The white frosty ‘petals’ were tinged with red.

Four legionaries were trying to escape, two were wounded—but the hale ones were trying to support the man with the mangled, half-frozen leg. The other cradled her bloodied arm, but she was running under her own power.

All four seemed to pause as they tried to endure the moment of cognitive dissonance at the sight of a woman with a seemingly dry tunic dancing their way across the water that rushed over the terrain, with knives in her hands.

“Keep moving!” Ranthia ordered as she passed the group.

Her focus was on the ogres that were already in pursuit. There only seemed to be five hostiles present, levels ranged from 403 to 521. The archer—wielding a crude bow—turned his aim to her and loosed the arrow.

Ranthia twirled to the side, but the arrow curved to follow her—there were Skills involved, but she couldn’t be sure yet if it was the host or the parasite. The host was a [Warrior – Wind] after all.

Ranthia activated [Void Edge]—along with the rest of her combat Skills—as the arrow reached her. Her first blade severed the tip from the arrow, which she followed up with another slash through the middle of the shaft. The arrow came apart and spun through the air, bouncing harmlessly off her body as she passed through it.

A few heartbeats later, she reached the lead ogre. He swung his wooden club at her, but Ranthia just ducked under it and swept past the brute, delaying only to pass a knife through the back of his neck.

[*ding!* You have slain an ogre [Desperate Farmer] (Earth, level 421), [Weedsbane] (Wood, level 130)//a shimagu [Slacking Soldier] (Ooze, level 437), [Clubber] (Forest, level 419)!]

Two more ogres were near her position, the archer was behind them, and behind him was a [Mage – Storm] that was seemingly trying to escape. But why…?

[Warrior – Lightning] level 498, [Warrior – Water] level 470. The fleeing ogre had one of the legionaries—wounded, but alive—in his arms!

There were three other human bodies strewn haphazardly about the area, each plainly dead, since [Divine-Touched Identify] wasn’t reacting to them. But Ranthia had no time to worry about the deceased—she had to save the man that could still be helped!

The archer was trying to nock another arrow, but Ranthia had two other brutes to get past—one was wielding a stolen legionary sword (which was hilariously small in an ogre’s hands) and the other wielded a simple wooden spear. The spear was the bigger problem at the moment; it made the ogre more perilous to bypass, even before Skills came into effect.

She had gotten a bit spoiled to ignoring Skills outside of twins, but it wasn’t like she had somehow forgotten her years of experience as an Adventurer and a Ranger—she still knew how to counter classers. …She just wasn’t used to them being able to surprise her anymore. She couldn’t see the class archetypes or the elements of the shimagu within their living hosts, which meant that she needed to bait out their capabilities before she could even try to chase down the runner.

Ranthia danced closer to the two ogres, but they seemed wary. Either their parasites—and possibly they, if either was a twin—were aware enough to know that biding time was the important thing, or they were being cautious after she so easily dispatched the first shimagu soldier.

The sword-wielder spoke a few guttural words of the shimagu language. Most were unfamiliar, but Ranthia recognized a word that meant something like priority or important. The spear-wielder gave a slight nod and shouted one of the unfamiliar words.

It wasn’t a good plan, but Ranthia couldn’t wait for them to act. She leaned on her speed and swept in, trying to close in on the sword-wielder before they could react.

[Combat Awareness] flared in her awareness—directed from the opposite side. The water was already deforming there, so Ranthia didn’t even hesitate, she just threw herself backwards to get clear. A heartbeat later, stone spears erupted from the water and passed through where she had been before they tore into the ogre.

The sword-wielder shouted in pain as he stumbled away from the attack. His free arm was all but shredded, but he was still in the fight. A new ogre poked her head out of the ground, coughing up water. [Sustained Chaos] imbued Ranthia’s right-hand knife with power just before she hurled it at the new threat.

The blade buried itself in her throat.

[*ding!* You have slain an ogre [Rockbreaker] (Earth, level 493), [Summer Laborer] (Fire, level 419)//a shimagu [Strategic Sadist] (Ooze, level 537), [Earthmover] (Mountain, level 499), [Maker of Holes] (Earth, level 371)!]

Ugh, she had even been warned about the shimagu doing exactly that and she was still caught by surprise! [Adamant Commander] pulled the knife back to her hand readily, at least. But the spear-wielder had stepped close enough—faster than she had expected an ogre of his level to move—that he was able to jab his weapon at her.

Ranthia used her thinned bracer to deflect the weapon while she pirouetted clear. She stepped closer before she lashed out with both knives and tried to slice through the spear midway down its shaft. Even with [Echoes of Devastation], she didn’t quite cut through, but the weapon cracked ominously when the ogre swung it clear of her.

In the moment that he hesitated, she threw her Skill-infused knife into his chest. She knew ogre anatomy well enough—a ruined heart was just as fatal for them as it was for humans. Killing the host before the parasite was never ideal, but she was in a hurry. With every moment that the fight dragged on, the running ogre got further away with a comrade.

Ranthia leapt to try to get to the parasite, but the mortally wounded ogre was fast enough to get his weapon up and try to skewer her. Absent a better plan, Ranthia acted on instinct—she delivered a kick to the spear to try to redirect it.

Instead—and luckily—the spear splintered apart where she had damaged it. The pointy end of the weapon was harmlessly flung to the side. Ranthia kicked off the remainder to refocus the momentum that the kick had imparted on her, and she swept her knife through the back of the ogre’s throat before she kicked off his back and flung herself at the injured sword-wielding ogre.

[*ding!* You have slain an ogre [Free Spirit] (Wind, level 403), [Runner] (Wind, level 395)//a shimagu [Sinister Scout] (Ooze, level 408), [The Force] (Gravity, level 309)!]

The ogre managed to get his blade up in time, but he failed to notice the image that appeared behind him. The opponent that he was so focused on shattered on impact with his blade, moments before Ranthia drove a blade through the host and confused parasite.

[*ding!* You have slain an ogre [Brewmaster] (Acid, level 481), [Song Keeper] (Earth, level 440)//a shimagu [Whispering Warrior] (Ooze, level 493), [Aspirant of the Blade] (Mantle, level 492)!]

The archer loosed, but this arrow flew far faster than the last. It punched into—then through—Ranthia’s shoulder before she could react. Pain might have been distant for a time, but it was no less familiar. Ranthia acknowledged it and set it aside while she focused on what she needed to do. Her knife hadn’t left her hand, but the arm still didn’t want to move—which was fine, she had enough speed to do what she needed to.

The ogre was close; he couldn’t nock another arrow before she reached him. Her sole usable blade sliced through his bow before he could stop her. The ogre had the sense to throw the remnants of his weapon at her, but she simply sidestepped them as she danced into the ogre’s personal space.

Her second cut tore deep into the ogre’s forearm as he clumsily tried to punch her. Her third was a stab—into his kidney. The fourth swept across the back of his legs, which dropped him.

The fifth erased his neck.

Honestly, being down an arm barely even dragged out the fight much.

[*ding!* You have slain an ogre [Beast Hunter] (Wind, level 521), [Roaster] (Fire, level 266), [Trapper] (Wood, level 192)//a shimagu [Tricky Tracker] (Ooze, level 519), [Proficient Archer] (Gale, level 505), [Stunning Fist] (Lightning, level 200)!]

“Damn this rain.” Ranthia hissed. Finally [Scattered Reflections] found purchase, though it wasn’t as close to the fleeing ogre as she could have managed if not for the rain.

Once she shifted—and regained use of her other arm—Ranthia resumed her dance as she rapidly closed in on what she hoped was her sole remaining adversary. She had been so focused on the heatwave being a prime opportunity for the shimagu to close in undetected that she never even considered that they might have done it during the rainstorm. There was always the chance that this was just a small—possibly even lost—long-range patrol, but…

Somehow, she suspected that life was about to get far more interesting in Remera.

The fleeing ogre had a solid head start, but she was far faster. Though she was still worried about the fact that the host was [Mage]-tagged. It was the rarest tag to see among the shimagu; most [Mages] could destroy themselves to prevent themselves from becoming puppets under shimagu control. An individual’s vitality defense didn’t apply to their own Skills so, [Resistance] Skills aside, it usually took only a moment and a little mana to self-inflict fatal harm. Of course, Ranthia herself was an exception—her [Mage] class wasn’t capable of inflicting lethal damage so readily.

She was still going to treat the ogre as if he was a twin with up to five classes to use against her.

The distance between them narrowed until the ogre skidded to a stop and spun her way. He kicked water at her, which immediately transformed into spikes of ice that accelerated further. There was enough distance between them that it was trivial to avoid the barrage, but it proved that the shimagu had—at least—the Ice and Ooze elements (since all shimagu were Ooze-aspected). The host had Storm and Wind, but she hadn’t seen enough to guess if the host was working with the parasite or not.

She needed to make her opponent hesitate for a moment. Ranthia tossed her left hand’s knife to the side and thrust her palm out at the ogre.

“<Fire!>” Ranthia roared in her best approximation of the shimagu tongue.

Her opponent recoiled and conjured a barrier of ice, broad and thick.

Ranthia hit the barrier, grabbed it, and vaulted over it. She trusted her vitality to handle how damned cold it was, even as she called her knife back to her hand.

The ogre had his face covered with the unconscious legionary he had abducted. He never even saw his end. Ranthia slammed one knife into the side of the ogre’s head and the other into his neck, even as she passed by.

[*ding!* You have slain an ogre [Weather Watcher] (Storm, level 466), [Wind Whisperer] (Wind, level 397) //a shimagu [Future Leader] (Ooze, level 508), [Ice Caller] (Ice, level 500)!]

The legionary that she saved was thankfully uninfected and would live. But Remera was swarming like a kicked insect den as they prepared the best they could. The base commander seemed to be of the same mind that Ranthia was—the contact with the shimagu meant that something far larger was coming.

They had to be ready, no matter how foul the weather remained.
