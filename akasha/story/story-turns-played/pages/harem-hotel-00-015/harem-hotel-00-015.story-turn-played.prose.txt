You take its door for your ally. The thing will not leave the doorway — cannot; guarding it is all it's for — so you make the door the ground you fight on, circling tight to its front, staying loud in its blank face, keeping yourself the nearer danger and Aria the one it has no attention to spare for.

You work it as before, pulling its front around after you, meaning to bare the split flank to her again.

But it does not come around. The thing has kept the one lesson the last pass taught it — it holds the cracked side turned inward, hunched toward the wall, and gives Aria the flat of its warded shoulder instead, guarding the door and the wound along the same dull line.

Aria goes for the seam anyway, fast and low where it opened before — and there is nothing there to open. Her claws rake across warded plaster and skid off the hard mass with no purchase under them; she pulls the strike and drops back out of reach a beat before the heavy arm comes round for her. No crack this time. "It's got wise to that," she says, landing back at your shoulder.

The Doorward draws its bulk in square between you and the door, the cracked side kept to the wall, and holds there — not lunging now, not giving, watching the both of you for whatever you mean to try next.
