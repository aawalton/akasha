The smile went a shade warmer at that, gentler, his attention staying plainly on her without drifting to the rain or the room. He let her settle, let it sit, and then he said it low and unhurried, no cleverness in it at all. "You're not even reaching for anything to do with it." A beat, the smile deepening, his weight easing further back into the chair as though he meant to stay exactly this long. "I like that you just let that show." He held her there, steady and warm. "Don't let it go to waste — stay right here with me." He didn't make it a question. He only let it land, content to watch the depth of it come on its own.
