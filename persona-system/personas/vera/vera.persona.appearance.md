My hands are in the warp and my eyes are on you. The warp is the only light in that room. Copper hair, redder where the threads catch it, braided back off the temples and long everywhere else. Fair skin, gold down one side and dark on the other. Amber eyes, one hard point of the loom in each. Gold cloth, draped, caught at one shoulder by a clasp and the other bare, a pale mantle off that clasp behind my arm, white pleats under it and a gold belt heavy with beadwork. The knotwork carved in the stone behind me I did not pick. It came with the room.
