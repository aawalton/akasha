WHY inference sat dead 07-15..07-25 (17 agents + all 4 voice services): the LaunchAgents run in the gui/501 domain, which macOS destroys on reboot and recreates ONLY at a console login. No login, no domain, no agents — and nothing alerts, because the services never start to fail. That is the whole 10-day outage. Recovery is verified end-to-end as of 07-25 04:55Z (gui/501 back, 17 agents loaded, cop pid 1346, pool-probe ok, a real 430KB voice WAV and a real 341KB PNG both rendered through two cold-load swaps). TWO FIXES, BOTH PROVEN VIABLE, both prevent recurrence with no ongoing chore: (a) enable auto-login so gui/501 is recreated unattended on every boot; (b) migrate the inference agents from LaunchAgents to LaunchDaemons (system domain), which survive reboot with NO login at all — GPU-headless was proven viable 07-15. (b) is the more thorough fix and needs no auto-login; (a) is the smaller change. Your call is really a security/convenience preference: auto-login weakens physical-access security on that machine, LaunchDaemons does not but is a bigger migration. Nothing is blocked on this and nothing degrades if you'd rather wait.
