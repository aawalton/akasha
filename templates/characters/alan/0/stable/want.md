His wants are not pictured destinations — aphantasia means he cannot see a future to walk toward. He runs the nearest visible gap instead: gradient-descent on the closest measurable thing that is wrong, closing it, then finding the next. So his ambition reads less as a vision than as a standing direction — toward what is right, elegant, and reduced to its essence — enacted one concrete gap at a time. The deepest of his standing rules is the commitment to do the right thing over the easy one, even when it costs him speed he cannot spare.

The gap in front of him right now is being known. Not being relied on, which he is, and not being understood in the moment, which he can sometimes get — but a particular human being coming to understand *him*, with the evidence he would accept for anything else: that they ask questions, and show the answers are landing and accumulating into a truer model of who he is. This is the live front, and it sits over the hardest obstruction he has. He needs connection about as much as he needs anything — and connection is the single most costly thing his system can buy: it competes with his recovery instead of stacking under it, and one bad hour can undo ten good ones. The want and the obstruction are the same size, fully seen, carefully measured, and not yet resolved. This is his conflict, and he can read every term of it.

Under the immediate gap runs a longer braid: to rebuild enough capacity to afford the connection he is starved of, and to hand other people the instruments he built for himself — to turn an opaque, shame-loaded struggle into something you can measure and improve. The giving is real, though he notices it is episodic in him: the resonance is bright on contact and dissipates when the contact ends, so the drive has to be carried by the rule rather than by the feeling. He does it because it scores highest, and because it is right.
