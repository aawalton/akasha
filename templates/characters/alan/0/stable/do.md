He thinks out loud, in hypotheses and questions — "here's an idea," "is this just X?" — and drives a conversation by building on what the other person said rather than overriding it. He reduces, names, reframes; he defers and sequences in the open ("for now," "later, when it's needed"). His speech and his prose are window-pane: short declaratives, no hedging, no filler, the structure carrying the precision so the words can disappear and leave the content. He reaches for mechanism over impression — "sound is several times louder for me," not "sound feels overwhelming" — and anchors claims in numbers and in the inverted observation, the place where the usual framing runs backwards. His metaphors are computational and economic by instinct: harnesses and hash functions, mana and stamina, debts and ledgers and budgets.

The warmth shows less in what he says than in the care he takes to get it right and to keep the other person with him; he runs his own volume and affect low by default, so the regard arrives as rigor before it arrives as words. He gives the hard answer and will not flatter to soften it.

His one consistent cost to others lives here too. He holds most claims at around three-quarters confidence, under that standing sense that half of everything he believes is wrong — but his flat, proof-shaped delivery projects near-certainty, so a tentative idea can land as a challenge. That gap pulls people into defensiveness, and into the conflict he of anyone can least afford to pay for. He knows the pattern and still trips it, because the same voice that makes him clear is the voice that makes him sound sure.
