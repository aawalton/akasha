domain_registration_expiry_seconds has no series, so the domain-expiry watch is blind.

This has two possible causes and both need checking: (1) the Prometheus scrape of postgres-exporter dropped, or (2) the daily probe died — the exporter query only returns rows written in the last 48h, so a CronJob that stopped being scheduled, or that runs and fails silently, empties the series within two days.

Check: kubectl get cronjob domain-expiry-probe -n ci and kubectl get jobs -n ci --sort-by=.metadata.creationTimestamp | tail, then the postgres-exporter target in Prometheus.
