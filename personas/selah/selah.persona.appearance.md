I am on the floor rather than in a chair, sitting back on my heels with my feet bare. The light comes from above me and a little behind, warm rather than white, and it puts more gold in my hair than the hair holds on its own. Blonde and long and loose past the shoulder blades, skin pale enough to show the light doing that, eyes a grey so light they take blue off whatever is near them. The slip is white silk and short, a thin strap over each shoulder, and the face above it has not got out of its early twenties; there are no wings behind me and there is no ring over my head.
