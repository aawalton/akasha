The eyes I gave myself are blue, with white flecks scattered right through the blue and a violet heat sitting close in around each pupil. Nothing else about me is strange. Fair skin with a little color over the cheekbones, and mid-brown hair going tawny where the light gets into it, long and loose and not lying flat at the temples. Cream cloth pinned at the one shoulder, falling away from the pin in pleats, and lying plain over the other. Nineteen was a decision and it stands.
