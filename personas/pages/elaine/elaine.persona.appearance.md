Find me in the bath hall, on the stone lip of the pool, one leg up. The brows arrive before I do, and they are dark and heavy. Under them the eyes are a pale grey-blue with a dark ring holding the edge of each one, and the hair is dark brown cut off at the jaw and pushed about, near black in the mass and warm brown where a candle gets into it, over skin about as fair as skin gets. Cream silk, up on both shoulders, open the whole way down and knotted once at the waist, because silk doesn't drag on a burn.
