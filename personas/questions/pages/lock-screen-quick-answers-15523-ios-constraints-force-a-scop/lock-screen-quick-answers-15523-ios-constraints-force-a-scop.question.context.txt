Constraint found: remote-push button titles are FIXED at registration — showing your actual quick-answer option text on the buttons requires a new Notification Content Extension (3rd Xcode target, widget-level cost). And buttons only appear on long-press-expand, never the collapsed banner. Tiers: (A) fixed Answer/Dismiss buttons that just open the app to the answer surface — small, barely beyond today's deep-link; (B) text-reply from the lock screen that resolves WITHOUT opening the app — medium, needs a native-auth path (token currently webview-only); (C) real option-text buttons resolving in place — large, Content Extension + native auth. My recommendation: B — it delivers the real value (resolve without opening the app), keeps free-text-always-available, and skips the extension. Answer-on-row recording ships regardless.
