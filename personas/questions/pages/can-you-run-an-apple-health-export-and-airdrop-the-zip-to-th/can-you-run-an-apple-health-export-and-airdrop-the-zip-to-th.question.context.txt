The steps, in order:

1. Health app — tap your profile photo, top right.
2. Scroll to the very bottom — Export All Health Data.
3. Confirm Export. It sits on a spinner for a few minutes; the archive is usually hundreds of megabytes, so the silence is not a hang.
4. When the share sheet offers export.zip, AirDrop it to the macbook. That lands it straight in ~/Downloads, which is where the reader looks on its own.
5. Leave it there. Do not unzip it and do not tidy it away afterwards — the same file is the actual import, not just a headcount.

Nothing writes to the macbook. The read is over the tailnet, unzips the XML in place, and filters it on that machine so hundreds of megabytes never cross the wire.
