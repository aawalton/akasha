The night's largest finding: every instrument in `instructions-review` cuts, and none asks whether what is *absent* should be present. So a review of a rebuilt surface handed nine good lines finds nine good lines and stamps a reading indistinguishable from one that held the two hundred they came from. Measured, not theorised — re-reading four folder domains with their originals in hand recovered eighteen unheld doctrinal claims from one, and essentially the entire body of another (183 lines down to a definition and three proposed units, with no knowledge document covering the package at all).

I have closed what I can close from the instruction side:

- The review task now opens by reading the original, and derives it where the brief names none — a folder domain's `code-path:` roots at a directory, and that directory's head document is what it was rebuilt from.
- The ingestion task names the original at a ref when it spawns the reading.
- The claims those readings recovered now live on `questions/code-doctrine.md` rather than dying in hand-offs.

**The derivation covers the code half and nothing else.** A knowledge document rebuilt out of quarantine has its original *deleted* by the same task that rebuilt it, so only git history holds it and no glob can find it. For those, whether stage 2's absence check happens at all still depends on a dispatcher remembering to say so in a brief — and my own re-stamps have exactly the property they were sent to fix: nothing distinguishes them from the blind ones they replaced.

What a `rebuilt-from:` key buys is that the door could refuse a `reviewed-*` stamp until the named origin had been held, which turns the check from something a brief may forget into something the machinery levies. What it costs is a key on every rebuilt surface that is true once and never again, and a pointer into `dirty/` or into git history that will rot — which is exactly the shape this estate has been cutting all night.

I did not build it, because adding a frontmatter key and a gate condition is a change to the machinery rather than a reading of any surface, and three seats converging on wanting something is not the same as it being right.
