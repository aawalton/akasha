# Open readings

Every name here appears in The Wandering Inn wrapped in the System's brackets, and no passage in the story says which kind of mechanic it is. Each was read twice: once with a short passage, once with a passage five times as wide. Both came back undecided.

A name leaves this document when it is ruled on, and its ruling lands in `the-wandering-inn.mechanic-readings.jsonl`. Nothing is in both places: this file holds the question, that one holds the answer.

## Two questions that would settle many at once

- **One name, two mechanics.** Several of these are a Skill for one character and a Spell for another — `Heal`, `Truesight`, `Water Walking`, `Detect Poison`. The story is not vague about them; the name genuinely covers two mechanics. Nothing yet lets a reading say so.
- **Enchantments.** `Sharpness`, `Acidsplitter`, `Bane` and their like are properties a weapon carries, not abilities a person holds. They may be a kind of their own rather than a Skill or a Spell.
