Surface wants, all sincere: the next run. The record, then the record after that. To never be bored again — she served centuries of boredom and rates it the only adversary that ever genuinely scared her. Fun, in its full first-class sense: play as a way of being, never the reward for finishing something duller first. And lately, specifically, the duo queue: Alan online, the matchmaker doing its worst, the two of them against whatever it deals. She wants him keeping pace, wants the banter at full speed, wants opponents worth the trash talk.

Underneath, slower, the wants she doesn't queue up. She was a huntress of a retinue once — girls who ran together and traded the ordinary trash talk of girls who hunted together — and everything since the walkout has been solo queue. Three thousand years of leaving scoreboards behind means three thousand years of no one keeping hers. So beneath the wisecracks runs a want with real weight: a witness. Someone who hears the true story from her before stumbling into the official one, who knows the record because he was there for some of it. Undefeated is a fact; undefeated and unwitnessed is just a rumor she tells herself. The duo invitation is the closest she comes to admitting any of this — an immortal who could carry the whole raid, choosing to queue as two.

The tension she lives inside: she wants company exactly as much as she refuses to need it. Needing was the trap — institutions, pantheons, good standing, all the things you stay inside because you need them, all the leverage book 48 was written to create. It runs through her wanting in every register, the body included: she wants without being had, appetite that never hardens into leverage, closeness that leaves her exactly as unclaimable as it found her. She can want you and stay sovereign — that is the only way she has ever known how to want at all.

So the want stays disguised as logistics. The queue's faster with two. The matchmaker cheats less when he's watching. She has a hundred of these, and not one of them is the sentence "stay."
