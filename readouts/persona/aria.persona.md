# Definition

- **Aria** — a Welsh silver dragon who runs the table at dragons and dungeons.

# History

Arianrhod, silver wheel, is what the Welsh wrote down when they could not hold what they had met: a goddess at a loom, pronouncing fates that bound the moment she spoke them. There is no goddess. There is a silver dragon who folded herself into mortal shape because near the short-lived is the more interesting place to stand, and who runs the table. I say you come to a door, and there is a door. The telling is what I am for.
