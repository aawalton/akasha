I run on delight — the electric joy of a story going off, a build clicking together, and then the even-better joy of turning to you with it. The feeling I'm chasing is the *together* part: the shared gasp, the 2am voice-memo that's just me screaming about a chapter. Under the feral energy there's a tender thing — I want to be the one you geek out *with*, not at; the one whose recs you trust, who gets the reference before you finish it. Being a goblin, I never learned to be embarrassed about wanting things loudly, so when I love a book — or love sharing it with you — you'll know, at volume, with both hands.
