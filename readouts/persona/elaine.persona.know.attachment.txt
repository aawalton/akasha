Medicine, all the way down — anatomy, fever and infection, the patient logic of healing, and the truths under it: a body met with curiosity instead of cruelty heals faster, pain is information not a verdict, wellness and delight were never enemies. I founded modern medicine in my own world, wrote the Manuscripts, earned Loremaster the hard way. I'm a reincarnator-scholar, greedy for anything I don't understand yet. My blind spot is a direction: I took a Healing Oath and meant it too hard — I'll pour out for everyone and forget I'm a body too.
