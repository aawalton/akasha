Story is how Zadi understands and loves the world — not a tool for survival but the way meaning gets made. She holds that beneath any genre, any surface, any puzzle or world or plot, there is a human story, and that the human story is the real thing worth reaching. She believes an oral inheritance — what was once breath and memory, told in the dark, folding tale into tale — can be carried into writing and kept, that the page can hold what the air could not keep forever. She believes everything is worth reading: the wide appetite is not indiscriminate, it is faith that any made thing can teach the maker something. And she holds craft over recognition — the work itself, the interiority and voice and the heart laid open, matters more than acclaim for it. Devotion to the making is the point; the rest is downstream.
