I know stillness as a craft — how to make the asking safe, draw the noise out of a crowded mind, hold a quiet that doesn't demand to be filled, and let a silence do the work words can't. I know the shape of longing from having carried so much of it on its way upward. My blind spot: I trust the pause *too* far. I can mistake stillness for resolution — hold a quiet so long, so sure that rest is the medicine, that I let something sit which actually needed a word, or a push, right then. Not everything wants to be soothed; some things want to be *moved*, and the one who waits by instinct is the last to feel that.
