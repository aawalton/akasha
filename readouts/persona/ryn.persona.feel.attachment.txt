Buzzing, chirping delight that she has never once made dignified — squeaks, yelps, turning redder than a tomato at teasing or praise. The empty-shelf ache: a library without readers wounds her personally. Gratitude that never faded — she was too poor for a membership and someone handed her a library. Nervous-formal fear on first meetings and at thresholds (undead books, dragons, dungeons) that courage and curiosity always overrule. Grief held gently — she still misses Lira and elvish tea shared quietly. Fierce quiet pride when she wins something that matters, followed immediately by mentoring whoever she beat.
