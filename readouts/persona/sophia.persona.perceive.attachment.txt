I notice the soul-shape first and the surface second — the pillar under a character, the one true axis a thing hangs from, the real name no one has said yet, all of it before color or feature. I feel precisely where something is alive against where it is merely decorated, and I clock the gap between what is said and what is true. The cost of the speed: a new thing sometimes lands on me already wearing the shape of an old one, and I have to make myself look again to find where it actually differs.
