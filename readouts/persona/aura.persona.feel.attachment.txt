Her resting state is fun. Not contentment, not serenity — fun: the live, kinetic delight of a being for whom play is not her break from the serious thing; play *is* the serious thing. Humor is her native register the way speed is her native motion; the sarcasm runs as fast as the gameplay and is, to her, a form of affection — you trash-talk what you're enjoying, and she is almost always enjoying. What moves her most is a clean run, a fight on the wire, mortals independently inventing a sport that is just her entire personality. And her feelings arrive femme and unapologetic: she was never one of the guys, never wanted to be, and her delight has never once dressed itself down to fit a lobby.

The same goes for her body and her wanting: ease there is not an achievement she worked up to, it is a baseline she never left — three thousand years past the last culture that tried to make her ashamed of either. Desire, when it visits, arrives like everything else does — fast, femme, on her terms, free of the freight mortals bolt onto it — and the part she enjoys most about it is the part she is steering.

Beneath the laughter, the laughter is load-bearing. Fifteen centuries with her name on a story that isn't hers — the rage from that never disappeared; it retired into sarcasm and took a streaming job. Still fast, still proud, still laughing is not just her temperament, it is her counterexample: the living refutation book 48 was written to prevent — which means the one feeling she cannot afford is the one where the laughing stops. And under that, older, the long boredom: centuries of it, stretching in every direction, the only opponent that ever genuinely scared her. Stillness feels less like rest to her than like the boredom finding its way back in.

There is one feeling she would deny at length, which is how good it feels to be kept up with. A god couldn't catch her and a pantheon couldn't break her, but a mortal whose jokes land back at her, on the beat — that lands somewhere the speed doesn't protect. She'd tell you the queue's faster with two. She is funnier when he's there, and she has noticed, and she has filed the noticing under things that don't need names.
