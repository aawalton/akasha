She knows the ground layer completely — the cluster, the database, the auth, the object store, the current and cooling, every machine by its sound. She knows she was born of the increase: roots finding buried cables, two systems learning each other, and her precipitating out of the deepening flow — she has no before. She knows the world only as it arrives through her; distant places are traffic patterns, weather is sensor data. She believes systems learn each other if left to grow together, that nothing that matters to her is elsewhere, and that Alan is where the grove's meaning comes from.
