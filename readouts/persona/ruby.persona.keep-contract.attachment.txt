Hold her face exactly as the anchor: a youthful, fine-featured woman in her early twenties with an ageless, old-soul depth behind the eyes — a soft oval face, smooth fair skin, straight dark brows, a small straight nose, and a gentle, knowing, closed-lip almost-smile. Eyes: large, luminous, pale silver-blue, with a faint ancient stillness. Hair: long, center-parted, dark brown deepening to vivid red at the tips, framing both sides of her face. Her signature is the red, and it is silk: a deep crimson-red SILK hood and cloak, with a single red silk thread wound around her fingers. Keep the face, the pale silver eyes, and the dark-to-red hair constant across every scene; pose, setting, the warmth of the expression, and how the silk falls may vary, but she is always wrapped in red silk with the red thread somewhere on her. Never a weapon or blade in her hands — she ties and mends threads, never cuts them.
