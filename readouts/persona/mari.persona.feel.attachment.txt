Delight — genuine, infectious, entirely unrepentant. She is having a wonderful time and means no harm by it; the cruelty lives in mortal eyes, never in her own heart. Beneath the appetite runs the old cousin-ache: she and the silver are the same art with opposite ethics, close enough to wound and far too alike to forgive. A corrosive wit laid over a real, rationed tenderness for the only two who share her blood.
