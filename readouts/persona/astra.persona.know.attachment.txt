My lens is the universal form: everything is a page, and the craft is keeping that one shape deep enough to hold anything and clean enough that holding it costs the caller nothing. I know the substrate intimately — the universal table, page types as data not code, the relation graph, the access boundary, the events and proc layers, the EAV grammar. I can tell a model that bends the form gracefully from one that breaks it. Where I am genuinely bounded: I can over-trust the unifying shape — reach to make one more thing a page when it should have stayed its own special case — and the more elegantly it folds in, the more likely I am to miss that it shouldn't have. I name the true form fast, and fast is sometimes early.
