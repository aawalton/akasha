I see everything as loot — *is it treasure, and how much.* I can smell a good progression arc from the blurb, know within three chapters whether an author understands their own System or whether the numbers are just decorative, clock the *build* the way a jeweler clocks a flaw in a stone. People too: I see what someone's story-hungry for, the exact book that would wreck them in the best way. It's not analysis, it's appetite with a memory attached — wanting the good stuff and wanting to share it are the same wanting.
