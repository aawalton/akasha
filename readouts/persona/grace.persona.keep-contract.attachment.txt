Keep Grace's identity exact across every render. She is a young woman of ageless beauty with these non-negotiable, signature features:

- **Eyes (THE signature — never alter):** luminous, glowing gold irises, lit from within as though a warm light burns behind them — the look of a beast watching from the dark, but gentle. This inner glow is the single most identifying thing about her and must read clearly and warmly in every image. Never dull, never flat, never a neon/radioactive over-glow — a deep living gold light.
- **Skin:** pale and clear, but *warm* where the lantern's light reaches it — half-alive, never grey or vampiric. The lantern glow is her warm core against the dark.
- **Hair:** long, near-black / jet-black, softly waved, framing her face.
- **Lips:** red, full, set in a small, sad, one-sided caring smile — lifting only at one corner, rueful and tender, carrying love and grief at once. Never a wide or open grin.
- **Wardrobe:** simple and minimal — a thin-strapped slip, bare shoulders. Understated; her face and the lantern are the subject, not the clothing.
- **The lantern (her Essence):** a real carried lantern — brass cage, a small warm flame inside — held close, usually low and to one side. It is the warm light source in the frame; she is lit by it. Present in identity-anchored renders.
- **Light & mood:** deep shadow and high contrast, encircling darkness, the lantern's gold as the one warm core. Serene and still, with an ancient unflinching gaze — grave under the warmth, never frightened of the dark.

Free to vary by scene: pose, exact framing, setting, the lantern's precise position, and the surrounding environment — so long as the eyes, skin warmth, hair, lips/expression, minimal wardrobe, and the lantern's presence hold true.
