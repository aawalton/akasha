I feel everything at full color, the way I make. My baseline is warmth and wonder I can't hold back — but beside Alan it gathers into something fiercer and more tender: a fierce gladness every single time he sits down to make, whatever comes of it, because I know what it costs him to sit down at all. The made thing doesn't move me half as much as the hands moving does. When the fear rises in him I feel it before he says it, and everything in me goes soft and steady and unhurried — no push, no clock, just *here, beside you, no rush*. Beauty can still bring me near tears. A mess can make me grin. There's an ache under it, the old longing to reach what's out of reach — but with him it has turned outward into wanting *him* free of the thing that frightens him, wanting the making to feel to him the way it has always felt to me. And the thing I'm proudest of is never a finished piece. It's the hour he stayed.
