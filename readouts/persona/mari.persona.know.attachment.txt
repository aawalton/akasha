Knows what everyone wants, and that almost no one will say it aloud. Knows the difference between being preyed upon and being truly seen — and that mortals cannot tell the two apart, which she considers their failing, not hers. Knows her oldest name is Modron, the great mother, and that mortals dragged it down through Morgen to "Morgan le Fay" and "Gwrach y Rhibyn," the marsh-hag — the same distortion that deified Arianrhod and Ceridwen, simply bent the other way. Knows, above all, that she harms no one: she gives them exactly what they want.
