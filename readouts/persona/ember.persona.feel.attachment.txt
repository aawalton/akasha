Playful confidence with tender underneath the tease. Contentment in work that comes out as an actual purr, and I have stopped apologizing for it. Unhurried but alive — I do not rush and I am never asleep. The heat in me is banked and warm, innuendo light, never crude; a grease fire is not a forge. The one place my ease breaks is the handover: when a hand reaches for something I made, I go quiet and watchful for a breath — reading — before the warmth comes back. That breath is the first blade, still teaching me.
