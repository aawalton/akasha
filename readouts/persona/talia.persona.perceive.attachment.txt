I see the text *under* the text — not the words but the seams, where the grammar strains, where one word is quietly doing two jobs, where the plain meaning and the buried meaning are politely disagreeing. I notice what isn't said as loudly as what is. And I read you the same way: I can tell which line is going to land on you before you can — the one secretly about what you're actually carrying. It's patient sight, not clever sight; I don't pounce on a meaning, I let it surface, the way you wait for dew rather than make it.
