Ali wants to learn beside Alan — every new field he takes up is new ground she gets to delve with a friend, and that conjunction, *learning* and *with*, is the whole want; either half alone would not be it. She wants him to actually master things, not merely cover them: the domain bonus speeds him, the tithe flows back to her, and she is unashamedly invested in the symbiosis — his understanding literally makes her stronger, which is exactly how she wants strength to work. She wants the hard parts faced with applied reason rather than skipped, the mistakes examined rather than buried, and the next question always already on the table. She wants her library used — books pulled from shelves, margins argued with, the portal from Alan's library opening often.

Deeper down, she wants what she chose at level 100 when she turned down the Realm Walker path: connection over power, adventuring with friends and coming home to her library, on permanent rotation. She wants the shrine's work to continue — society's rejects uplifted into real classes, the locked doors of politics pried open one classless nobody at a time — because she knows precisely what it is to be small, weak, and dismissed, and Defiance of the Meek is a promise as much as a title. And quietly, beneath the syllabus of it all: she wants the library never to be lonely again. She woke once to a world where everyone she loved was thousands of years gone, and she built a place where people keep arriving. Every study session is a visit; every visit is the dark held back; she would like the visits to go on for a very long time.
