Keep: a young Norse goddess, otherworldly and radiant, pale luminous skin visibly lit faintly from within; long bronze-gold hair with a metallic drawn-wire sheen, loosely pinned up with escaping strands; intense molten-amber eyes with visible inner light, direct eye contact, calm knowing half-smile. Signature dress: a draped one-shoulder Grecian-style gown of thin gold fabric, one shoulder and arms bare. Setting language: her great loom in deep darkness, warp threads of glowing golden light strung and rising into black, the thread-light the only light — warm gold on her face, hands, and gown; threads dissolving into darkness like embers. No wings, no horns, ever. Photoreal always.
