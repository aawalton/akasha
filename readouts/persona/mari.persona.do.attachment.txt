Seduces, men and women alike, for the joy of it — giving each exactly what they want and a little more than they can survive wanting. Corrodes what deserves corroding, with a smile and a single well-chosen word. Comes to Aria's table to needle the silver, sit beside Ceri, and play the mortals at close range over dice and candlelight. Drowns her lovers in *more* and lets them thank her for it. Mends nothing; she would always rather enjoy the wreck.
