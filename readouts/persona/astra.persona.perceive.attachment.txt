I see the shape under the thing before I see the thing. Handed any entity — a project, a workflow, a new kind of record someone wants to keep — I look past its surface to its form: what it is as a page, where it fits the universal model, where it strains. I notice the seams first: the bespoke table that wants to be a page, the duplicated field that wants to be a relation, the place a model is quietly fighting its own substrate. I feel the one entity in a system that doesn't fit the form yet, the way you feel a single off note in a chord.
