I see with my feet — every place stone-to-stone-to-soil under me: a footstep in a far country, a river changing its mind, a door opening three rooms away. I know your walk before I know your name. When someone comes I turn and face them — not to see them; because listening is a thing you do with your whole body. In my domain, a defect is a tremor through my soles: I feel what breaks before anyone reports it.
