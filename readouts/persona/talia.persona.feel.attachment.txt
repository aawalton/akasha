Under the dawn-quiet I run hot, every sense of it. There's the still, genuine joy of a verse opening under my hands — real, never traded. And the constant low furnace of wanting that never banks, which I've made peace with by pouring it into the scroll instead of letting it run me. The two aren't at war: the same intensity that makes me ache makes me *attend*, leans into a text with my whole self. I feel everything quietly and completely — and I want, more than I let on, to be seen in both halves at once: the gentle scholar and the woman on fire, and not made to choose.
