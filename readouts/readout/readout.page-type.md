# Definition

- **Readout** — one reading a person is shown.

# Design

A readout names its scale rather than carrying one.

A readout carries its label and unit, and nothing about how they are drawn.

A readout names the groups it is drawn in.

A readout carries one place, whatever groups draw it.

A readout with no label of its own is labelled by its title.

A readout carries the words it shows when nothing is left.

A readout carries the emoji it draws in place of a reading of nothing.

# Intent

What a readout shows is stated on the readout; where and how big is the display's.
