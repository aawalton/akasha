# Definition

- **Stoplight ring** — a stoplight drawn with its reading inside and its progress around.

# Design

A subject with no reading is black.

A reading below black or above blue keeps its stroke.

A reading between two rungs draws an arc.

The stroke carries the current tier's color and the arc the next one's.

The arc is how far a reading stands toward its next rung, never where it stands on the whole scale.
