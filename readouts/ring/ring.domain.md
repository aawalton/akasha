# Definition

- **Ring** — a reading drawn as an arc around the figure it reads.

# Design

A ring is one of two sizes.

The small ring is the size three of them span a small tile at; the large one fills a small tile alone.

Every arc starts at twelve o'clock and sweeps clockwise.

The figure stands inside the ring, and the label below it.

A ring leaves slack above its label rather than taking every point the label does not.

What a ring draws in place of itself is drawn at the size the ring draws, not the size the ring is given.
