1. Stand upright with your feet together, arms hanging at your sides, and your weight over the middle of your feet.
2. Bend your knees a little and jump, spreading your feet out past shoulder width while you sweep both arms out and overhead until your hands nearly meet.
3. Jump again and bring your feet back together as your arms sweep back down to your sides. That is one repetition.
4. Land through the balls of your feet with soft knees, keep your ribs down rather than arching your back, and hold a steady rhythm for the time set.
