1. Stand with your feet a little wider than your hips, left foot forward and right foot back with the heel lifted, knees soft. Bring both fists up to your cheekbones and tuck your elbows against your ribs. This is your guard, and it is where every punch starts and ends.
2. Throw a jab. Drive your left fist straight out from your cheek, turning the knuckles over at full reach, and snap it straight back to your cheek. Your right fist stays at your face the whole time.
3. Throw a cross. Pivot your right heel outward, turn your hip and shoulder into it, and drive your right fist straight past your chin, then snap it back to guard.
4. Keep your feet moving between punches with small bounces or half-steps. Never let both feet go flat and still.
5. Work it in rounds: punch for thirty seconds, move light for thirty, and repeat until the time is up. Switch your lead foot halfway through.
