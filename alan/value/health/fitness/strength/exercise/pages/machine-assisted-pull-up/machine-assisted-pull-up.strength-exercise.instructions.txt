1. Set the pin in the weight stack for the assistance you want; more weight is more help, so start heavier and come down as you get stronger.
2. Step up onto the platform, take the pull-up handles with an overhand grip a little wider than your shoulders, and kneel on the pad one knee at a time until it carries you at full arm's length. This will be your starting position.
3. Pull yourself upward by driving your elbows down to your sides until your chin clears the handles. Keep your chest up and avoid swinging.
4. Lower yourself under control until your arms are straight again, then repeat.
5. When the set is done, step back onto the platform one foot at a time before letting go of the handles.
