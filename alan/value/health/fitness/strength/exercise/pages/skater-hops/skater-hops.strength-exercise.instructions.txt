1. Stand on your right leg with the knee soft, your left foot lifted behind you, and your chest tall.
2. Push off the right leg and bound sideways to your left, landing on the left leg with the knee bending to absorb it and the right leg swinging behind you.
3. Hold that landing for a moment under control, then push off the left leg and bound back to the right the same way.
4. Keep the bounds short at first and let them lengthen as you warm, landing quietly each time, and keep alternating for the time set.
