1. Stand tall with your feet under your hips and your elbows bent at your sides as though you were jogging.
2. Jog on the spot, and on each step flick your heel up behind you until it touches or nearly touches your buttock.
3. Keep your knees pointing down at the floor rather than driving forward. The movement happens at the knee, not at the hip.
