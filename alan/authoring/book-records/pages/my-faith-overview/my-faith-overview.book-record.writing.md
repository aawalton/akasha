# Overview — the shape of My Faith

This is the orientation the `/talia`
apparatus loads before a study session. Its job is narrow: surface the **method,
the structure, and where the build currently stands** so the interpreter and her
three helpers open warm and oriented, not cold. It is a distillation, not the
corpus — every structural claim links to its convention home; every doctrinal
claim lives in the commentary or the map, never invented here.

## What this book is

The **structured shape of Alan's faith**, built verse by verse from a linear
cover-to-cover read. The throughline is one disciplined separation, held in every
node: **what the authoritative source entails on its own merits** vs **what was
inherited** — carried in from the Protestant Christianity most early members
converted out of, or from later devotional habit. The method specimen is the
Hebrew *eretz* reframing of Noah's flood: the text in its own frame first, the
inherited literalist overlay named only after.

## Organization — two axes at once

- **By source authority** (the top-level tiers, descending most→least
  authoritative; build most-first): Book of Mormon → other authoritative
  scripture / revelation / prophetic counsel → logical closure → Alan's
  speculative musings. See [`sources/CLAUDE.md`](sources/CLAUDE.md).
- **Two artifacts within a tier** (orthogonal, never merged):
  **commentary** indexed by verse, and the **abstraction map** indexed by concept.
  See [`sources/book-of-mormon/CLAUDE.md`](sources/book-of-mormon/CLAUDE.md).

## The reading method (shared by both artifacts)

- **Two lenses per verse, minimum:** dictation-era English (Webster 1828, *not*
  KJV) + original cultures/languages (for the Book of Mormon, both Hebrew/Aramaic
  and Egyptian — the record claims both).
- **Intratextual triangulation:** the book is its own context — cross-reference a
  verse against the text's own statements on the same concept, pulled from a
  **verified verbatim source** and cited, never quoted from memory.
- **Macro-structure lens:** watch for unit-level form (colophon, inclusio,
  chiasm) at the unit boundary; distinguish tight chiasmus from ordinary
  parallelism honestly; never lean on "chiasmus proves antiquity."
- **Alan's read is recorded distinct** from the interpreter's, in his own words.

## The tag legend

Every map claim carries its grounding verses and a tag:

- **`[E]`** entailed — the text directly states it, in its own frame. Sub-label
  the warrant when it matters: **`[E-internal]`** (held on the text's own
  authority — the Layer-1 operation) / **`[E-external]`** (also independently
  established outside the text). Neither is lesser.
- **`[I?]`** inherited-candidate — believed in the tradition; check whether the
  text entails it or it was carried in.
- **`[O]`** open — surfaced, not yet resolved.

## Where the build stands

- The durable package, structure, and conventions exist; the Book of Mormon tier
  is the live frontier.
- The **commentary** and **abstraction map** are filled by the running apparatus
  in live sessions — the recorder writes commentary verse by verse, the archivist
  maintains the map. Early work (1 Nephi) lives in Talia's `~/agents/talia/`
  scratch and migrates here as the apparatus carries it forward.
- The apparatus keeps this OVERVIEW faithful as the corpus grows (the archivist's
  job) — it is the standing map, not a historical snapshot.
