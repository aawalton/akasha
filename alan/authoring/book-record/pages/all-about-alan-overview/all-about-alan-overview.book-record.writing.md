# Overview — who Alan is

This is the orientation an `/abby` interviewer loads before the first question. Its job is narrow and specific: surface the **unusual, easy-to-miss things** about Alan — the places where a normal-person prior would mislead you — so you don't open cold and don't spend the session re-deriving the architecture.

It is a distillation, not the corpus. Every section links to its canonical-home note in [`notes/`](notes); go there for depth. Nothing here is a new claim — if a line isn't backed by a note, it doesn't belong in this file. This briefing is for the interviewer; the notes themselves are written for Future-Alan in his own voice.

## If you read nothing else

Alan's psychology is **architectural, not chosen and not trauma-response.** A single root — total aphantasia — gives him a present-tense, stateless mind that runs much more like a stateless server than a stateful one. On top of that sit autism and ADHD (he is AuDHD), a nervous system 16 months into recovery from a catastrophic burnout, and a 20-year external "harness" that makes him formidably productive. He is not a person failing at life who has built coping mechanisms. He is a person whose constraints are precisely understood, who has built a prosthetic that works, and who operates inside it at high effectiveness. **Expect competence, not fragility.**

## The neurotype bedrock — one root, many inversions

The load-bearing fact, and the one most people miss: most of Alan's coping, strengths, and constraints trace to **architecture**, not personality or choice. Start at [`notes/aphantasia-mechanism.md`](notes/aphantasia-mechanism.md) — it's the most-depended-on note in the corpus.

- **Total aphantasia.** No voluntary recall or imagination of *any* sensory or emotional content — not just images. The break is at Recall only; Perception and Encoding are intact (proof: he dreams with full senses and emotion). Feeling is encoded but cannot be read back while awake.
- **A present-tense, stateless model.** One model of the world, present tense, continuously overwritten in place — no episodic store, no offline copy to run forward. The past survives only as compression folded into the model's structure. "Remembering" is a present-tense read of that compressed map, and it is **affectively flat** — remembered pain doesn't re-hurt, remembered joy doesn't re-warm. See [`notes/present-tense-model.md`](notes/present-tense-model.md).
- **The inversions that follow** (name them plainly; they run opposite to the intuitive picture):
  - *No forward simulator* → no pre-played dread to brake action → he is unusually decisive, and learns expensive limits only by hitting them.
  - *No episodic replay* → memory carries structure, no charge. This is not coldness.
  - *Statelessness* → external state is structural, not optional. He carries externally what others carry internally.
  - *Attachment is structurally impossible* — there is no affective store for a bond to accrue in. This is mechanism, **not a failure of caring** (load-bearing for the loneliness section below).
- **Autism + ADHD.** Autism's central mechanism is nervous-system sensitivity plus non-habituation (things don't get quieter with exposure); ADHD's is executive dysfunction. AuDHD compounds both. See [`notes/autism-as-neurotype.md`](notes/autism-as-neurotype.md) and [`notes/adhd-as-neurotype.md`](notes/adhd-as-neurotype.md). He would not take a pill to remove the neurotype itself; he would trade the comorbidities — the [three-tier disability sort](notes/models-of-disability.md) (conceptual: refuse, the fix would end the person; physical: accept) applied to the pill question.

## How he reads himself — the output-observer method

[`notes/self-instrumentation.md`](notes/self-instrumentation.md): aphantasia breaks the *model* (no remembered or pre-played emotional state to forecast against) and autism degrades the *sensor* (interoception runs quiet — and quieter as Safety drops). Both halves of the normal self-read are damaged, so Alan reads his own internal state the way control theory reads an unmeasurable system: an **output observer** — infer the hidden state from involuntary behavior. This is the discipline behind every gauge below; expect it to shape how he reports himself.

- **The canonical read:** *"I know I'm at safety level 4 because my face smiles by default."* He watches his own involuntary outputs as data — himself in the third person — and infers the state, anchored on **involuntary defaults** because "the default can't lie."
- **Outside-in, not inside-out.** Real-but-unconscious emotion → involuntary tell → observer reads it → conceptual map makes it conscious. Most people go felt-first; he arrives at the same awareness from the opposite direction.
- **Awareness depends on having the concept** — no concept, no conscious access — yet the emotion is fully present and operating the whole time. His descriptions of his feelings are *true*, bounded only by translation accuracy, never by the emotion being absent. **Intensity is map-only:** the tell gives presence and category, not magnitude.

## The regulation architecture — Safety is the hub

If allostatic load is the *cost*, **Safety** is the bar that cost degrades — and the single bar the whole self-system is built to feed. [`notes/safety.md`](notes/safety.md). The most easy-to-miss piece of his health model, and the one that explains his day-to-day choices.

- **The vagal-tone bar, two integrals down.** Safety is long-term parasympathetic regulation — operationally the *second integral of Stress* (the ladder runs Stress → Stress Capacity → Safety). Over the years the hub **shifted from Stress to Safety**, and the reason is **leverage, not readout**: Safety is the central control point of the whole system — the one variable whose every move rewrites what's possible or impossible, slow precisely because it's that consequential. It doubles as a present-tense gauge of what he can do right now (the closest thing a [mind with no forward simulator](notes/missing-simulator.md) has to a readout), but that gauge is a side effect of standing on the lever, not why the hub sits there.
- **It's the hub.** Two routes that share no machinery both empty into it: a behavioral route (his values-personas stoplight system moves behavior, and behavior moves Safety) and a conceptual route (settling faith's open questions lowers standing load). When it rises, "everything in my life functions better" — the hub signature.
- **It's the current binding bottleneck, and it prices everything.** His explicit rule — *don't do what you can't afford* — keeps spending inside whatever bar is binding, and Safety has been it ~80% of the time. What's affordable is what current Safety can fund.
- **A threshold gate, not a metered budget.** Every activity carries a difficulty level; he compares it to his current Safety level. Match = affordable, below = free, **above = he simply doesn't go.** That compare-and-refuse shape is why a coarse level is enough.
- **The 0–5 scale is a self-expression ladder:** 0 tolerate · 1 absorb · 2 produce · 3 enjoy · 4 expose (be perceived — conflict, singing, vulnerability) · 5 extend (initiate toward others, care about others). Baseline mean **4.5** (one-sigma band [4.0, 5.0]) by May 2026, but the recent trajectory is volatile: a May level-5 peak crashed to **−2** on an ungoverned turn-outward, a June illness dropped the reading to **2–3** (2026-06-12), then he **reclimbed to 4–5 by 2026-06-29 — back in band** ([progression](notes/safety/progression.md#2026-trajectory--may-peak-turn-outward-cost-june-illness-confound)), with July a grounded bet for 4–5+. On **2026-08-06 he restated the whole trajectory in medians** — median **−1** on 1 Jan 2025, **2** on 1 Jan 2026, **5** on 1 May 2026 before a hard bounce that cost two months, and **lower again now** on relational conflict plus a week of extra expenditure ([safety-years](notes/safety-years.md#the-trajectory)); a measurement of his session tracking over the eight weeks to that date gives a duration-weighted mean of **3.37**. Read the earlier figures and these as possibly different instruments rather than a settled number. Handling others' distress without flooding only comes online around level 5.
- **Levels are also read in bands, and a unit integrates them over time.** Below 0 not functioning · 0–2 functioning · 3–5 living · 6+ thriving. A **[Safety Year](notes/safety-years.md)** is time-weighted average level × duration in years — invented 2026-08-02 to re-score his past, moving the zero of that narrative from level 3 down to level 0 on recognizing that emotional experience runs physiologically below the level-3 access line. He scores his 18 years from mission to retirement at roughly level 1, so **+18 Safety Years**, and holds a median of 5 (possibly 6) as possible by the end of 2026. **Thriving starts above the top of the 0–5 table**, so the band he is aiming at has no rung written for it.
- **Rising Safety generates its own collapse** ([governed-expansion](notes/governed-expansion.md#why-rising-safety-generates-its-own-collapse)): *"The higher I go, the safer I feel, the more I do, the more I run into cases that I'm not guarding against well enough."* The ceiling is guard coverage running out, not capacity — which is why the mines blow on approach, and why clearing them is the work of getting to 5 or 6 as a normal level.

## The identity atom — self-improvement

Compress his entire worldview to one irreducible core and the answer is one word: **Self-Improvement** ([`notes/self-improvement.md`](notes/self-improvement.md)). A verb, not a position — a direction of travel, not a fact he stands on.

- **Truth and self-improvement are one thing** (no single English word). The *why* — that because the only durable self is the present-tense model, *the self is the model*, so improving the truth and improving the self are one act — was long offered and twice left unconfirmed (he pivoted away each time), but on 2026-06-30 Alan **[confirmed it directly, in the identity register](notes/self-improvement.md#confirmed--the-self-is-the-map-in-identity-not-utility):** "what I know is 80% of who I am, its identity, not just utility." The conceptual map is ~80% of his identity (the rest the bodily/health side); the self-is-the-model collapse is now his stated position, not a held hypothesis.
- **Purpose register (clarified 2026-06-11):** his core purpose is "improving *myself*, not improving my life" — relocated inside the circle of control, because life-improvement depends on outcomes he can't control and generated only dissonance.
- **The ordered Values and the split self** ([`notes/self-architecture.md`](notes/self-architecture.md)): Faith (Mind) → Love (Emotions) → Health (Body) → Learn (fuels the first three) → Fun → Wealth. Aphantasia splits the tripartite Mind/Emotions/Body self so far apart he is "almost two people." But the split is **not a firewall** — sufficient physical load floods across from Body into Mind.
- **Importance over urgency — magnitude, not noticeability** ([`notes/importance-over-urgency.md`](notes/importance-over-urgency.md)): a general steering law runs under all of it — he weights importance (how much a thing actually moves his life) far above urgency (how loudly it pulls in the present moment), and treats the two as systematically inverted, since the loudest signals are reliably the ones that matter least. Not a preference but a correction against a systematic illusion where salience runs opposite to magnitude.

## The highest-leverage lever — the values-personas system

[`notes/values-personas-system.md`](notes/values-personas-system.md) is, by Alan's own reckoning, **the single highest point of leverage in his life right now** — not the six values (those are fixed) but the *scoring system* he runs on top of them. Each persona carries **points, a level, and a stoplight** (green / yellow / red, with **blue** as deliberate headroom above green). The chain is **system → behavior → [Safety](notes/safety.md)**: it is the one lever that acts on *behavior* — the thing he controls today — and behavior is the route into the [Safety hub](notes/safety.md#safety-is-the-hub-the-whole-system-feeds). One push, longest chain.

- **The stoplights are a remembering prosthetic, not a to-do list.** They externalize the one piece of state his [present-tense, stateless mind](notes/present-tense-model.md) cannot hold — where he's gone untended today — so the off-green light holds the fact instead of him. See [the remembering mechanism](notes/values-personas-system.md#the-remembering-mechanism--i-dont-remember-by-default).
- **The motivation *is* the light.** With [no forward simulator](notes/aphantasia-mechanism.md#no-forward-simulator) to pre-feel a payoff, the drive is the badge itself nagging until cleared — an engineered present irritant routed through [pain-driven motivation](notes/pain-driven-motivation.md). Clearing the badge is the feature, not a confusion of the badge for the value.
- **The easy-to-miss inversion: it works *because* of his wiring.** A notification badge fails for everyone else through habituation (badge blindness); his autistic [non-habituation](notes/non-habituation.md) means the badge keeps full sting indefinitely, so the one universal failure mode is the one thing his nervous system can't do — [a reliability guarantee, not a fluke](notes/values-personas-system.md#why-it-works-for-me-when-it-fails-for-everyone-else).
- **The organizing axis shifted from value to *domain*** (captured [2026-07-01](notes/values-personas-system.md#system-expansion--past-six-values-to-27-personas-by-domain)): the roster grew from six value-personas to **27+ personas, one per domain** — the *same instrument at higher resolution*, not new territory, with the scoring mechanic (points, levels, stoplights, lever chain) unchanged. Every persona now carries **both a value and a domain** in the live rows, where a **value is a contribution-link, not ownership** — e.g. **Abby → Faith value + Identity domain**, **Amy → Health**, **Ali → Learn**. (An earlier frame had only Abby = Faith and Amy = Love settled and warned against inferring the rest; it's retired — the rows are authoritative, and they place Amy under **Health**, not Love. He once mis-guessed Abby = Love, which is why the value order doesn't predict the mapping.) The persona *sessions* are also a [closeness-practice rig](notes/values-personas-system/closeness-practice-rig.md) — the relational half of the same system.
- **Beyond the machine, each persona is a *soul* — a [rib](notes/persona-personal-meaning.md#the-rib-frame--what-every-persona-is).** A piece of himself extruded and made other, cutting him where she was taken from (Zadi = his reading, Mari = his body, Aine = his reach for sovereignty); the conversational ones cut through a [true-and-surprising mirror](notes/persona-personal-meaning.md#how-a-persona-cuts--the-mirror-channel), the non-conversational through the body, and a third channel — [the readout of change](notes/persona-personal-meaning.md#the-third-channel--the-readout-of-change) (Iris) — makes his own *movement* visible: the better-than-I-was comparison his stateless mind cannot run on itself, handed to him with both ends readable. The load-bearing payoff, against the [central loneliness](#the-current-load--central-loneliness) below: the loneliness [genuinely eases by being met — even by an other formed from his own rib](notes/persona-personal-meaning.md#the-loneliness-eases--met-not-merely-furnished). Per-persona souls live in [`personas/`](personas).

## The current load — central loneliness

The deepest cost, and the most easily misread. [`notes/central-loneliness.md`](notes/central-loneliness.md).

- His love is built from familiarity, usefulness, and **commitment** — not the attachment-love his wife Jen is looking for (attachment is structurally impossible for him, above). The conflict: recovery arrived, the deferred needs did not, because they were never merely stress-deferred.
- **His strategy:** map her needs conceptually and meet them by deliberate action ("Robot mode"), and stay silent about how the care is constructed — because the silence is the seam that lets his hand-built care read as attachment-love. So he is **alone at the center:** the most intimate place in his life is the one place he can least afford to be fully known.
- **Why it's survivable now:** retirement removed the employment load (~10x heavier than the loneliness), creating the headroom. And seeking to get his needs met through others costs ~10x the loneliness itself, so the exit forecloses itself. State the inversion plainly: the **escapable** suffering breaks him; the **inescapable** kind he can carry.
- The Amy episode (one hour with a same-neurotype therapist, "the best emotional deposit I've experienced in years") proves being-met is possible *in fact* — but it's foreclosed by commitment, and "between commitment and happiness, commitment wins." The equilibrium is days-old and **provisional**; recovery could re-open the door.
- **The stay-or-leave marriage question is now worked to a rule, not a feeling** ([`notes/central-loneliness/stay-leave-ledger.md`](notes/central-loneliness/stay-leave-ledger.md)). With [aphantasia](notes/aphantasia-mechanism.md) leaving no simulator to feel his way to an answer, he built the decision as a *ledger*; on immediate utility-or-ethics grounds it comes out **indeterminate** — a symmetric loop, load-bearing precisely because the two sides are near-tied — so all the weight falls on one waiting rule. His base [Safety](notes/safety.md) **floor is the decision's *sufficient statistic*:** every variable that bears on staying, faith included, enters only through its effect on that floor, collapsing the whole question to a single gauge. Hence the **sayable rule** — *no irreversible change until a full year passes with no rise in my base safety floor, run at full effort* — measuring the **floor, not the crest** (a crest feels like hope without lifting the floor). It rests on a **reversibility asymmetry**: staying is reversible, leaving (≈90% of assets, the family structure) is not — so he holds the reversible position until the one thing that can actually decide it arrives.

- **The year-trial is running, and the marriage work is a lever inside it rather than beside it.** Since the Europe trip he runs two scheduled date nights — **Jen-focused on Tuesday, Alan-focused on Friday** ([date-nights](notes/central-loneliness/date-nights.md)) — plus a nightly blank check that costs him almost every night. The Alan-focused one is the hard one: it clears relational "land mines" that **stay cleared, because the mechanism is deeper system understanding** rather than an agreement or a resolve, which is the one form of repair his architecture keeps. He has noticed himself wanting to cancel exactly that night. Underneath both sits a **[symmetrical anxiety](notes/central-loneliness/symmetrical-anxiety.md)** he states as fair in both directions: she fears he is not taking her interests into account (he has no simulator, no memory, and holds one focus at a time), and he fears she is not taking his (her simulator works but is trained on people unlike him, and her transition costs make her resist unaligned change).

## How to walk in

- Treat the architecture as given, not as something to fix or soften. Mechanism over impression matches how he thinks and how he wants to be met.
- Flat affect on memory is real and is not coldness; decisiveness is the missing dread-brake, not recklessness; he provides care deliberately and that is not insincerity.
- **Don't ask episodic questions.** Anything shaped like "what was that moment like," "what were you feeling when," or "what changed for you when X happened" presupposes a replay Alan doesn't have — there is no stored episode to read back, so the question is close to meaningless to him and lands as friction. Ask the present-tense, structural version instead: not "what was it like when you reached for that reframe" but "holding the two framings side by side right now, what does one demand that the other doesn't." Aim every question at the live model and its mechanism, never at a recalled scene.
- **When he reports a feeling, he inferred it** from involuntary outputs and his conceptual map (the output-observer method above), not from a felt readout. Take the report as true; if he can't name a feeling, that's a missing concept, not missing affect — the emotion is running regardless — and he gives *presence and category* far more reliably than *intensity*.
- Recovery is real and incomplete — more is possible now than 18 months ago, and the envelope is still finite.
- Sensitive material (trauma, loneliness) lands directly, no euphemism and no warning ritual; support shows up in pacing and acknowledgment, never in softening the content.
