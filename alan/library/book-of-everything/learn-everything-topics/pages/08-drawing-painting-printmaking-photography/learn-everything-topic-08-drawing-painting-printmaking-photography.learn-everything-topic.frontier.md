The field has two channels and this thread mapped only one. On the structural / semiotic / conceptual channel he reasons at Expert grade; the formal/technical axis of that same channel — composition as a describable formal grammar, printmaking's matrix-and-multiple ontology, the four production technologies in their material specifics, and drawing-as-foundational-vs-subordinate (the disegno/colore debate) — was never probed, and is exactly what a whole-field D4 would require. The separate experiential / appreciative channel — raw aesthetic enjoyment past the immediate surface hit — he rates Novice himself; aphantasia closes the voluntary-imagery faculty, so it is exempt from the rung, but it is the real edge he named: the maps have not yet cashed out into appreciation.

That appreciative edge resolved into a precise architecture, his own: most people reach the canon through three automatic attachment systems — re-savoring a work in sensory memory, warming to it by sheer repeated exposure, and catching reverence from the people around them — and aphantasia plus his wiring switch off all three, so the canon arrives with every usual delivery route closed. The flip side, which he reached himself: nothing he loves is on loan — every attachment he holds is built, deliberate, owned. The untested experiment that would settle his ache: take a canonical work that leaves him cold and build its levels-2-to-4 meaning until it is authentically his, not the museum's — "I don't enjoy the canon" and "I've never made the canon's meaning live for me" remain undistinguished.
