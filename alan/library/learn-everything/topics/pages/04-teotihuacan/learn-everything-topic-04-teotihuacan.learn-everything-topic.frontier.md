The leaf began as a false-compactness test of his Old-World civilization-origins chain (surplus → specialization → administration → writing) on a ~125,000-person New-World state that ran *without* a developed phonetic script — and the chain survived as a more general model. The climb then localized the real variable and a genuinely open question. The sharpened frontier, in his own corrected terms: unboundedness of expression was never the variable — every human *spoken* language is already unbounded (recursion + an open lexicon that coins new words at will), Teotihuacan included. The variable is whether a society pushes that unbounded range into the **durable** channel (writing) or leaves it in the air. And *what drives that externalization* is underdetermined: demand-pull ("the administrative/ritual/political load needed a full script") and enable-pull ("the institutions never formed because the durable unbounded code was never built") fit the same correlation. He plants his feet on a complex, non-linear cause with no clean attribution — see Evidence. The never-opened Teotihuacan-specific record (corporate governance, the Feathered-Serpent-Pyramid overthrow, collapse, chronology) remains the node's own untouched frontier.
