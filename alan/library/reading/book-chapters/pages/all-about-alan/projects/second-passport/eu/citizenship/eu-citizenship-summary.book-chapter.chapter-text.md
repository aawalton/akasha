> EU citizenship paths remaining for a US-citizen applicant (married, three kids; family relocates together as accompanying dependents) with no EU ancestry, no second non-US citizenship, after stripping closed, ancestry, renunciation, marriage-to-citizen, family-reunification-to-existing-citizen/PR, distinguished-service, functionally-inapplicable, non-US-citizen-only, narrow-discretionary-self-employment (lottery-tier approval rates or hard national quotas), and no-residency-on-ramp routes (May 2026). 27 countries surveyed; 13 retain at least one viable citizenship path AND a viable residency on-ramp (no employer sponsorship, no endorsement, no >€500k capital). EU clocks apply to the main applicant; spouse and kids naturalize concurrently on the same residence start, not on a shortened clock. Detail per-country files live alongside (e.g., austria.md).


Paths below require relocation and physical residence; none permit a passive clock. All retain dual US citizenship.

## Belgium

- **5-year declaration (Art. 12bis §1, 2°)** — 5y residence + A2 + integration proof + 468 work-days; ~5.5y; €1,000 fee.
- **10-year declaration (Art. 12bis §1, 5°)** — 10y residence + A2 + social participation, no work-history needed; ~10.5y; €1,000.

## Cyprus

- **Standard naturalization (7-year)** — 7y residence + 12m continuous + B1 Greek; ~10–11y with backlog.
- **Fast-track highly skilled (§111A)** — 4y + B1 OR 5y + A2 Greek; 8-month statutory cap; ~4.7y or ~5.7y total.

## Czechia

- **Standard (§11–§14)** — 5y PR (after 5y temp) + B1 Czech + civics + tax/income; ~10–12y; CZK 2,000 fee.

## Finland

- **Standard (8 yrs)** — 8y residence + B1 Finnish/Swedish/FSL + livelihood + digital-only filing; ~9–10y total.
- **5-year exception (B1 language)** — 5y residence if applicant already holds B1 Finnish/Swedish/FSL; ~6–7y total.

## France

- **Standard by decree** — 5y residence + B2 + civic MCQ (80%) + 5y French earnings; ~7y minimum from arrival.
- **Reduced 2-year decree (Art. 21-18)** — 2y residence if applicant holds a French Master's-level diploma; ~4y total.

## Germany

- **Standard (§10 StAG)** — 5y residence + B1 + civics test + self-support + Nazi/Israel loyalty declaration; ~6–7.5y total; dual allowed since 2024.

## Greece

- **Standard (Art. 5, 7 yrs)** — 7y residence + Panhellenic Test (B1 + civics 70%) + interview + €700; ~10–12y total with backlogs.

## Hungary

- **Standard (8 yrs)** — 8y residency + B2 Hungarian + 40-topic civics exam; ~9–10y total.

## Italy

- **Standard 10-yr (non-EU)** — 10y legal residence + B1 Italian + income; ~12–13y with processing.

## Luxembourg

- **Naturalization by residency (Art. 9)** — 5y residence + ~8mo processing; Sproochentest B1/A2 is the real filter.
- **20-year long residence (Art. 12)** — 20y residence + 24h language coursework (no test pass); for those who can't pass Sproochentest.
- **CAI integration option** — 5y residence + completed Welcome and Integration Contract + Sproochentest; ~4mo processing.

## Poland

- **Naturalization (recognition)** — ~8y total (5y temp + 3y PR); B1 Polish; voivode discretionary; 6–12mo decision.

## Portugal

- **Standard (Art. 6)** — Post-reform 10y (third-country) + A2 + culture test; 12–24mo processing.

## Romania

- **Article 8 standard** — 8y residence + B1 + Constitution/anthem + livelihood; ~10–11y total.

## No applicable paths

- **Austria, Bulgaria, Croatia, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, Malta, Netherlands, Slovenia, Spain** — every remaining path either requires renouncing US citizenship, is marriage- or ancestry-based, is a distinguished-service grant, or is functionally inapplicable to a US-citizen adult born in the US (anti-statelessness jus soli, refugee/stateless tracks, born-in-country declarations, former-citizen restoration, Nordic/EU/Ibero-American/CPLP cohort tracks).
- **Denmark, Ireland, Slovakia, Sweden** — citizenship paths exist, but no residency on-ramp survives the no-employer / no-endorsement / no-narrow-discretionary filter. Denmark: self-employment closed 2015, no retirement / passive income / DNV, only Start-up Denmark (endorsement). Ireland: Stamp 0 and Stamp 2 are non-reckonable for naturalization; every reckonable stamp requires employer sponsorship, Enterprise Ireland endorsement, Irish-citizen family, or the closed €1M IIP. Slovakia: živnostník is the only on-ramp but the new **700-permit/yr national cap from 1 Jul 2025** (down from ~10,256/yr in 2024 — a 93% reduction) plus mandatory Ministry of Economy opinion makes it lottery-tier; no rule-based alternative. Sweden: Egenföretagare self-employment permit had only ~107 approvals in 2024 (down 33% from 2022) — lottery-tier odds for portable-income applicants; the PhD residence permit requires committing to a full-time doctoral program (Master's excluded) which doesn't fit a working-parent profile.

## Notes

- Fastest paths: **France 2-yr decree ~4y if French Master's**, **Cyprus fast-track ~4.7y if B1 Greek**, **Belgium ~5.5y**, **Luxembourg ~5.7y**, **Germany ~5–6y (post-Jun 2024 reform)**, **Finland ~6–7y if B1 Finnish/Swedish/FSL**.
- Residency-only programs (Golden Visas, investor visas, digital-nomad visas) are tracked separately under [../residency/](../residency/summary.book-chapter.md). They never shorten the naturalization clock unless explicitly tied to it (e.g., the French Master's track).
