
## Closed / historical

### Investment / MEIN (formerly IIP) — TERMINATED

- **History**: Individual Investor Programme (IIP) launched 2014 → relaunched as MEIN ("Malta Exceptional Investor Naturalisation" / "Citizenship by Naturalisation for Exceptional Services by Direct Investment") in 2020 under LN 437/2020.
- **TERMINATED**: **CJEU Grand Chamber Case C-181/23 (Commission v Malta), judgment 29 April 2025** held the scheme contrary to Article 4(3) TEU (sincere cooperation) and Article 20 TFEU — Union citizenship cannot be commercialized via a "transactional procedure" lacking any "genuine link." Malta repealed the scheme by **Act XXI of 2025** on **24 July 2025** (operative 26 July 2025).
- **Pre-ruling terms (for historical reference)**: €600k–€750k non-refundable contribution to the National Development Fund, €700k property purchase or €16k/yr rental for 5 years, €10k philanthropic donation, 36-month (or expedited 12-month for higher contribution) residency. ~400 grants/year cap, ~1,500 total since launch.
- **Transitional cases**: as of May 2026, applications filed **before 29 April 2025** remain in legal limbo. Malta has not published comprehensive transition rules. Some pipeline cases that had completed the eligibility phase before the ruling have been processed to grant; the rest sit unresolved nearly a year on, with the European Commission continuing to press Malta on disposition. Rejected applicants have no clear appeal track under the new framework. Legal experts (Henley & Partners, KPMG, ASIL commentary) have called for honoring legitimate expectations of pre-ruling filers.
- **Ongoing EU pressure**: the Commission monitors Malta's compliance and has signaled it will scrutinize the new Citizenship by Merit route for "back-door" CBI characteristics.
