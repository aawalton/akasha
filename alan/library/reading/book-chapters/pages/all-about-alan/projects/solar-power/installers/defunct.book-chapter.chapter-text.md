
# Defunct / Red-Flag Operators

Companies that have ceased operations, filed bankruptcy, had Utah licenses revoked, or are subject to state enforcement / class actions. Listed for awareness — these names will appear in older review aggregator pages and door-to-door sales literature.

## Hard exits / bankruptcies

- **SunPower Corporation** (legacy) — Chapter 11 Aug 2024; assets sold to Complete Solaria (now operating as SunPower Inc.). Existing legacy-SunPower warranties partially honored by new entity.
- **Lumio** (Lehi, UT — fka Smart Wave Solar legacy entity) — Chapter 11 Sept 2024; 363 sale to Zeo Energy; liquidating plan effective Feb 2025; Utah DOPL B100 license revoked Jan 2025; class action ongoing.
- **Vivint Solar** (Lehi, UT) — Acquired by Sunrun 2020 for $3.2B; brand wound down; existing customers transitioned to Sunrun service.
- **Sunnova** — 2025 bankruptcy; SunStrong assumed assets; warranty service questionable.
- **ADT Solar (Sunpro Solar)** — Exited residential solar Jan 2024 after $89M loss; closed remaining branches March 2024; partial creditor-forced re-entry but business is fragile. [Source](https://www.solarpowerworldonline.com/2024/01/adt-solar-to-close-remaining-branches-exit-residential-solar-business/).
- **Freedom Forever** — Chapter 11 filed; warranty risk elevated.
- **Vision Solar** — Multiple state AG class actions over predatory sales; bankruptcy; SolarReviews marks the brand "Out of Business."

## What "defunct" means for warranty claims

In Utah, when an installer goes bankrupt or loses its license, the workmanship warranty (typically 10-25 years) is functionally unenforceable. The manufacturer warranty on panels and inverters remains valid but requires the homeowner to source and pay for a replacement installer's labor. Solar Insure (third-party workmanship insurance) is the only practical hedge against this scenario — confirm whether any installer you contract with bundles or offers it.
