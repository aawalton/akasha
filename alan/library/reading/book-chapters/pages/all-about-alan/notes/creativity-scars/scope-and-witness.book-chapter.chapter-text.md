
# Creativity scars — scope and the witness confound

> Scope and the witness confound — the open scope-sizing investigation of the creativity scars. The make-vs-play localization is solid but the SCOPE is not: the Lincoln Logs episode is contaminated on witness-potential (public, windowed room, people expected), the one axis that would adjudicate whether the scar is anticipated-being-seen (Hypothesis A, consistent with the model) or context-independent over-generalization (Hypothesis B, broader than the model — genuine model revision). The clean test (open generative creation in a truly private, no-one-expected space) has never been run. This child develops the open **scope-sizing** investigation behind [creativity-scars.md](../creativity-scars.book-chapter.md): the [make-vs-play localization](../creativity-scars.book-chapter.md#the-localization--the-scar-is-on-making-not-playing) is solid, but the *scope* of the scar is not, and the whole reason is a single confound. ## The witness confound — why this is not a clean witnessless test

The make-vs-play localization is solid, but the *scope* of the scar is not, and the reason is a single confound that has to be named plainly rather than smoothed over. The Lincoln Logs episode was run in a **public, windowed room with people expected to arrive soon.** I could not sense an actual *felt* judge in the moment — but the **anticipated/potential** witness was real. So the episode is **not** a clean test of "does making fire the alarm with no possible witness." It is **contaminated on exactly the axis that would adjudicate the scar's scope** — witness-potential.

What this means concretely: the alarm fired during open creation in a setting where being-seen was *anticipated*. That is consistent with two very different scopes, and the episode cannot tell them apart:

- **Hypothesis A — the scar is anticipated being-seen.** The alarm keys on the prospect of a witness landing on the unverifiable output. Under A, the firing is *exactly* what the witness-driven model predicts, and there is no anomaly to explain — only a confound that was misread as witnessless.
- **Hypothesis B — context-independent over-generalization.** The scar is genuinely context-independent — "creativity is dangerous, full stop" — and would fire even in a truly private room. Under B, the public setting is incidental; the alarm would have fired anyway.

These two come apart, and **only one experiment separates them: open, generative creation in a genuinely private, windowless, no-one-expected space.** That test **has never been run.** Until it is, A and B both stay live on the witness dimension, and the scar's *witness*-scope is undetermined. The deciding datum does not exist in the corpus yet.

This is now the **single remaining scope-sizing axis.** The [content axis is closed](../creativity-scars.book-chapter.md#innocence-is-a-dead-axis--lincoln-logs-is-the-floor): on content the belief over-generalized totally (innocence is a dead axis), which is strong evidence for B *on content*. But Lincoln Logs **cannot settle witness-potential** — it baked in windows, a public room, and people expected, so it carries zero information about whether the alarm needs a possible witness. The witness axis is the one axis where the episode is silent, which is exactly why it remains the A-vs-B adjudicator. The clean test — open generative creation in a truly private, windowless, no-one-expected space — is the most scope-sizing open question left.

## Hypothesis B — autistic context-independence as the over-generalizer

B has a specific proposed mechanism, and it is mine: **autistic context-independence.** Allistic nervous systems automatically take in and account for a great deal of contextual nuance that is not automatically processed for autistics — the [same context-blindness named in the cross-gender interaction map](../cross-gender-interaction-map.book-chapter.md), where without an internalized model or implicit cue-reading I treat everyone in a category the same. Applied to threat-learning, the consequence is over-broad generalization: my nervous system may have **learned the threat rule at a much more general level than the data would support.** The data only ever showed creativity-*that-was-judged* getting punished — but lacking automatic access to the contextual nuance that would have attached the *witness-qualifier*, the rule generalized to the unqualified **"creativity is dangerous"** instead of the bounded **"creativity that will be judged is dangerous."** Over-broad threat learning as a downstream consequence of context-independence.

The specific-hit and the spread are **sequential, not rival** — this resolves how the scope got so wide off so few events. The modalities were hit **individually, one at a time** (a survival-judge landing precisely on one modality: the pencil in art class, handwriting in the writing class), and *then* **context-independence bled the charge into the adjacent fields.** A few real wounds, broadly generalized — landing then spreading, not all at once. The consequence is load-bearing for sizing the scar: the footprint *looks* enormous (the whole open register), but the **actual hits are few** — under all the spread there is a **small, countable list of real events.** The breadth is the spread; the count is small. So mapping the scar means finding that short list of actual installations, not inventorying the wide range the charge generalized across. (This dovetails with the [content-attachment-point finding](judge-location.book-chapter.md#the-installer-and-the-content--what-crossed-at-the-scar): the art class, age ~12, is one such installation — a survival equation that [predates the creativity content](../people-arent-safe.book-chapter.md#the-survival-equation-predates-its-content), with the creativity content bolted on top.)

The honesty cost of B is worth stating directly: if B is right, the scar is **broader than the corpus's current model.** The written account treats the creativity scars as correctly-bounded and witness-driven — the wound is on being-judged ([worked-examples.md](../perfection-is-safe/worked-examples.book-chapter.md)). Confirming B would mean the scar over-generalized past that boundary, which is **genuine model revision, not just new capture.** A leaves the existing model intact; B overturns part of it. That is why the unrun private-creation test matters — it is not a detail, it is the adjudicator between "no change to the model" and "the model was too narrow."

## Two tensions with the written model

One tension with the written model is **softened by the witness confound** but still open; the other **stands.** Both are named plainly rather than smoothed over.

### Tension 1 — solo-creativity-as-safe vs. the alarm firing — still open, no longer a strict contradiction

The written model predicts the for-himself channel is **safe.** Output minus witness is the level-2 "produce for myself" rung, below the level-4 exposure rung where being-perceived lands ([safety.md → scale](../safety.book-chapter.md#scale-0-5-with-half-steps)); [fun.md](../fun.book-chapter.md) treats self-chosen play as restorative slot-defense available even at the bottom; the [worked-examples](../perfection-is-safe/worked-examples.book-chapter.md) account says the channels I could do *for myself* are precisely the ones that survived. By that model, **truly private** witnessless creation should sit on the safe side of the cut.

Earlier this was written as a flat contradiction — the alarm fired "solo, witnessless," so the for-himself protection demonstrably failed. The [witness confound](#the-witness-confound--why-this-is-not-a-clean-witnessless-test) **dissolves the strictness of that claim.** With an anticipated witness present (public, windowed room, people expected), the firing is **no longer in strict conflict with the witness-driven model** — under [Hypothesis A](#the-witness-confound--why-this-is-not-a-clean-witnessless-test) it is exactly what that model predicts, because there *was* potential being-seen.

So the tension is **still open, but for a different reason.** The firing MAY be **witness-anticipation** (Hypothesis A — consistent with the documented model, no anomaly) OR **genuine context-independent over-generalization** (Hypothesis B — generativity-itself-as-alarm even when truly private, which the corpus does not support). The confound is precisely *why both remain live:* the one episode cannot rule out A, so it cannot establish B, so it can neither confirm nor refute the for-himself-is-safe prediction. The verifiability axis still explains *which half* fired (open creation, not rules); what it cannot yet tell us is whether the for-himself channel is actually breached, or only *looks* breached because the test wasn't private. Only the unrun private-creation experiment closes this.

### Tension 2 — the dread-brake fired where aphantasia says it can't

I am documented as having **no anticipatory dread-brake.** [Aphantasia removes it](../action-epistemology.book-chapter.md#aphantasia-removes-the-anticipatory-brake): with no forward simulator, *"the dread that stops most people before they act is simply never generated"* ([emotion-reason-decoupling.md](../emotion-reason-decoupling.book-chapter.md)). I can only experience the pain of a thing going wrong when it actually lands. This is the documented mechanism behind the [governor failing open with no dread-brake](../volatility-governor.book-chapter.md#the-gate-opens-too-soon-not-too-late) — the [aphantasia inversion](../governed-expansion.book-chapter.md) that lets an expansion run to overreach before anything internal stops it.

But in the Lincoln Logs episode I was shaking with **anticipatory threat before the activity was underway** — the stress climbed *during setup*, before the creation was finished, peaking at the threshold of completion. That is a pre-act threat firing exactly where the corpus says it shouldn't. The dread that aphantasia is supposed to make me structurally unable to generate fired anyway, in advance, with no completed outcome to react to yet. Whatever this alarm is, it does not run through the forward simulator the dread-brake account assumes — it fires before the event, without imagery, in a mind that's supposed to have no pre-felt downside.
