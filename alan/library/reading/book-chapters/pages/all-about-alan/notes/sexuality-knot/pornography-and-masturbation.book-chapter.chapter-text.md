
# Pornography and masturbation

> Pornography and masturbation — the behavioral footprint, the disclosure wound, and the present-state sort. The footprint: first exposure at 14, internet porn as it became technically possible, self-discovered masturbation, a lifelong pattern with three full-year quits that never held, the help-seeking failures (parents at 16, bishops twice, a mission president once), and the single painful disclosure to Jen — never told to anyone again until now. The sort: one behavior, multi-formed; zero-tolerance on both sides (church and Jen) sealing it secret; and three separable layers — attraction/arousal and pleasure (both kept) vs. the behavior itself (the only layer the faith zero-tolerance and the health-goal target). The mechanism lives in the sibling regulation note.

This note is the behavioral footprint and the disclosure history. The *mechanism* — why these behaviors exist and why they could never be quit — lives in the sibling [regulation-not-desire.md](regulation-not-desire.book-chapter.md); read it for the "why." This note records the "what" and the cost of never being able to say it.

## The footprint

I was exposed to pornography for the first time at **14**, on TV — an early-morning show with models in lingerie under a waterfall. Over time I found internet porn, which was pretty new then: computers had just barely gotten good enough internet connections for images. I discovered masturbation on my own, through experimentation.

Since then I've had **persistent pornography and masturbation behaviors for most of my life.** I put in tremendous efforts to break them, and I succeeded for a **full year at least three times** — but never permanently. They always came back. (The reason they came back is the [regulation mechanic](regulation-not-desire.book-chapter.md#why-i-could-never-quit-it): I was trying to remove a brace from under [safety](../safety.book-chapter.md) with nothing to replace its load.)

## The help-seeking that didn't help

I reached for help repeatedly, and the record is a line of dead ends:

- **At 16, in desperation, I asked my parents for help.** They told me I'd have to figure it out on my own. The wrong target and the aloneness in one stroke — the [people-aren't-safe](../people-arent-safe.book-chapter.md) shape, where the people who should have helped instead handed the problem back. This is the earliest entry in the pattern.
- **I worked with bishops twice and a mission president once.** None of it ever really helped.

The throughline is that none of the help addressed what the behavior actually was. It was treated as a sin to be willed away, never as a regulation tool bracing a nervous system — so the interventions aimed at the symptom and the behavior outlasted all of them.

## The disclosure wound

I disclosed this to **Jen once, early in our marriage.** It was incredibly painful. That disclosure coincided with one of the times I managed to quit the behavior for a year — but after the behaviors came back, **I never disclosed them to anyone, ever again.**

Until now.

That single word marks the exact edge of the seal. This is the [second axis of the being-known deficit](../central-loneliness.book-chapter.md#an-unspoken-domain-compounds-the-loneliness--sexuality-and-attraction) made concrete: not only a person I can't be fully known *by*, but a specific subject I could not be known *about* by anyone — because the one time I opened the channel, it hurt enough to weld it shut for decades. The unspokenness here is its own contributor to the [central loneliness](../central-loneliness.book-chapter.md); a region of myself no [being-known channel](../being-known-supply.book-chapter.md) ever reached, because after Jen the channel was never opened again. The whole [sexuality knot opening](../sexuality-knot.book-chapter.md#i-turned-the-key-myself) is that weld finally breaking — from my side, on purpose.

## One behavior, many forms

There is **only one behavior** here. In my words: *"I think there is only one behavior, but is it multi-formed and complex, and my feelings toward it are complex."* That one behavior is the pornography/masturbation behavior. Everything below sorts it — and my relationship to it — into parts, because the parts rank differently and only one of them is actually in question.

## The bind — zero tolerance on both sides

My religion as organized has effectively **zero tolerance** for the behavior itself. (Some of that could be a leak from mainstream Christianity rather than something grounded in the Book of Mormon — which would make it the first rule in my belief-set like that; the faith-side analysis is in [faith-as-object-of-reason.md → the dissonance-regardless structure](../faith-as-object-of-reason.book-chapter.md#grounding-a-rule-doesnt-settle-it--the-dissonance-regardless-structure).) Jen is very mainstream, so she also has zero tolerance.

Zero tolerance on both sides has one structural consequence. It makes the entire aspect of my life *"secret and hidden, which isn't great."* There is no side I can set it down with — not the church, not the marriage. The secrecy is its **own cost**, separate from the behavior: a whole region sealed because both the people and the institution that would have to hold it won't.

This is the same seal the [disclosure wound](#the-disclosure-wound) records from the other side. There the cost is the unspokenness feeding the [central loneliness](../central-loneliness.book-chapter.md); here it is the two zero-tolerance walls that build the seal.

## The torn-ness

I feel torn. *"I absolutely want to be healthy enough to not depend on it."* At the same time there is very real pleasure and satisfaction involved — which I might enjoy and benefit from *more* as my safety level rises, not less. *"Its hard for me to want to remove a source of enjoyment when I've gone completely without enjoyment for so long."*

The torn-ness is not confusion. It is two true things at once: the dependency is a thing I want gone, and the enjoyment is a thing I do not. The sort below separates them.

## The sort — three separable layers

The one behavior decomposes into three layers. The zero-tolerance rule conflates them, but they are genuinely separable:

1. **Attraction / arousal.** Femininity-tied and durable. The attraction to femininity predates the sexual-arousal layer — the *"sexual arousal side didn't kick in until"* puberty, but the attraction was there before it. Kept; not the question. The canonical home for the femininity-tied attraction is [femininity-as-safety-signal.md](../femininity-as-safety-signal.book-chapter.md).
2. **Pleasure / enjoyment.** The satisfaction in the behavior. Durable (the [need/enjoyment split](#the-needenjoyment-split--and-where-the-torn-ness-lives) below puts the estimate at 90%). Kept; not the question.
3. **The behavior of acting on it through pornography and masturbation specifically.** In my words: *"there is one more seam here, which is the difference between the attraction/arousal going away and the behavior of acting on it directly through pornography and masturbation. Those are separable as well."* This is the **only** layer the religion's zero-tolerance is about, and the only thing actually in question.

Both targets aim at layer 3 and only layer 3. The faith zero-tolerance is about the *behavior*, not the attraction or the enjoyment. The health-goal — being *"healthy enough to not depend on it"* — also targets layer 3, as a dependency to retire. Layers 1 and 2 are kept either way.

## The need/enjoyment split — and where the torn-ness lives

The split between the *need* for the behavior and the *enjoyment* of it is, in my words, *"absolutely right."* It maps onto the three layers:

- The **need** is the *dependency* on the behavior — layer 3 used as a regulation crutch (the [regulation mechanism](regulation-not-desire.book-chapter.md)). I want to be healthy enough not to depend on it. Both the health-goal and the faith-check target this.
- The **enjoyment** spans layers 1–2 and is durable. I am *"only torn about the second part"* — the enjoyment's durability — and I land 90% that it persists.

The 90% is a stated estimate under acknowledged uncertainty, not a settled fact: *"Its hard to know for certain, since I have never had my safety level go that high, but I'm 90% certain that the pleasure will still be here, because its tied directly to the attractiveness of femininity, which is so central to my preferences that I would be amazed if it went away."* The enjoyment is anchored to [femininity](../femininity-as-safety-signal.book-chapter.md), so central to my preferences that I expect it to survive — and possibly grow as safety rises, since [attractive femininity spikes both arousal and safety at once](../femininity-as-safety-signal.book-chapter.md#the-welding--for-attractive-femininity-the-two-arrive-fused).

So the sort resolves the torn-ness by locating it. What I want gone is the *dependency* (the need, layer 3). What I do not want gone is the *enjoyment* (layers 1–2), and I am 90% it stays regardless. The faith-warrants check I intend to run targets layer 3 only — and even grounding it ungrounded would not settle the matter, for the [structural reason recorded on the faith side](../faith-as-object-of-reason.book-chapter.md#grounding-a-rule-doesnt-settle-it--the-dissonance-regardless-structure).
