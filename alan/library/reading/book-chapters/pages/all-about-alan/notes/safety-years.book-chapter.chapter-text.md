
# Safety Years

> Safety Years — the unit I invented on the Alan-focused date night of 2026-08-02: time-weighted average safety level multiplied by duration in years, so a full year at an average level 1 is 1 Safety Year. What it was invented to do: rewrite my narrative of the past, moving the zero of that narrative from Safety Level 3 down to Safety Level 0, on recognizing that I have emotional experiences physiologically before I gain access to them at level 3. The band structure it carries — below 0 not functioning, 0-2 functioning, 3-5 living, 6+ thriving. The past account (18 years from mission to retired at roughly level 1, so +18 Safety Years) and the trajectory: median -1 on 1 Jan 2025, median 2 on 1 Jan 2026, median 5 on 1 May 2026 before bouncing hard and losing two months, lower still now, with a median of 5 and possibly 6 held as possible by the end of 2026.

A unit I invented, with Jen, on [the Alan-focused date night](central-loneliness/date-nights.book-chapter.md#the-one-hard-night-so-far) of Sunday 2026-08-02 — the one hard night run so far, and the thing it resolved into.

> That was the combination that invented Safety Years as a concept and rewrote my narrative of the past from a "zero" level of Safety Level 3 to Safety Level 0, by recognizing that I have emotional experiences on the physiological side even before I gain access to them at Level 3

## The unit

> A Safety Year is my average safetly level (time weighted) times the duration in years. So a full year at an average level 1 is 1 safety year.

Time-weighted average level, multiplied by duration in years. So it is an integral over lived time rather than a score — a year lived at 3 is worth three years lived at 1, and a stretch below zero subtracts.

## What it was invented for — rewriting the past

> The helps me understand my past

The rewrite it performed is specific and it is about where the **zero** of the narrative sits. I had been evaluating the past against level 3 as though 3 were the floor of a life worth counting. Asked whether that was about access — whether level 3 being the rung where the felt signal arrives had made it function as my zero — I corrected it:

> No, level 3 functioning as a zero was in how I evaluated the past. If I couldn't enjoy it, doesn't that make it miserable?

So it was an **evaluative** zero, not an instrumental one. Level 3 is [the rung where I can feel happiness](safety.book-chapter.md#scale-0-5-with-half-steps), and the inference I had been running is the one in that question: if I couldn't enjoy it, that made it misery. Everything below 3 scored as negative, which made most of my life negative.

Moving the zero to Safety Level 0 re-scores all of it. Level 0 is *can tolerate existing*, and below zero is where [the sub-zero territory](safety.book-chapter.md#sub-zero-territory) starts. With the zero there, the stretches at 1 and 2 stop being misery and start being positive quantities — small ones, but on the right side of the line.

### What made the move admissible

> by recognizing that I have emotional experiences on the physiological side even before I gain access to them at Level 3

That is the recognition the rewrite rests on: the emotional experience is happening **physiologically** below level 3; what level 3 gives me is **access** to it. So the years below 3 were not empty of emotional life — they were years I could not read. That severs *couldn't enjoy it* from *was miserable*, which is the exact inference the old zero ran on. See [alexithymia.md](alexithymia.book-chapter.md) for the keyhole access this sharpens.

## The bands

> I now understand 0-2 is functioning, 3-5 is living, 6+ is thriving, below zero is not functioning.

| Band | Range |
| --- | --- |
| Not functioning | below 0 |
| Functioning | 0-2 |
| Living | 3-5 |
| Thriving | 6+ |

These are bands over the same numbers as the [capability-anchor scale](safety.book-chapter.md#scale-0-5-with-half-steps) — a coarser reading laid over it, saying what a stretch at a level amounts to rather than what it lets me do. Two things follow from the boundaries. The line between *functioning* and *living* falls at 3, which is where the old evaluative zero sat — so the band structure keeps the thing 3 was tracking and stops treating it as the origin. And *thriving* starts at 6, which is above the top of the [0-5 anchor table](safety.book-chapter.md#scale-0-5-with-half-steps) and at the top of [my live mapped range of −2 to 6](safety.book-chapter.md#alans-live-mapped-range-is-2-to-6-and-10-is-off-map).

## The past account

> my 18 years from mission to retired I consider as a rough average safety level 1, so that's positive 18 Safety Years.

Eighteen years at roughly level 1 — the *functioning* band, the [can-rest rung](safety.book-chapter.md#scale-0-5-with-half-steps) — scored as **+18 Safety Years**. Positive, on the new zero, where the old one would have scored the same stretch deeply negative. That stretch is [the 18-year compound decline](autism-burnout.book-chapter.md) running from [the mission](mission-break.book-chapter.md) to [employment cessation](employment-cessation.book-chapter.md).

## The trajectory

> I hit a median of 2 being normal on Jan 1 2026, so I've been in a higher band sustained for this entire year. 2025 was recovery upwards from a median of -1 Jan 1 2025, which means my median rose three full levels in 2025.

- **1 Jan 2025 — median −1.** Below zero: [sympathetic dominance](safety.book-chapter.md#sub-zero-territory), the *not functioning* band.
- **1 Jan 2026 — median 2.** Three full levels in a year, and the top of the *functioning* band.
- **1 May 2026 — median 5.** The top of the *living* band.

> Yeah, I actually hit median 5 on 1 May 2026 and then bounced **hard** and lost two months. Now I'm lower still because of relational conflict, and for the past week, extra expenditure.

So the shape is a climb to 5 at the start of May, a hard bounce that cost two months, and a current position lower than that again — with two causes named for the current position: **relational conflict**, and **extra expenditure** over the past week. The May peak and the crash have their own account at [safety/progression.md](safety/progression.book-chapter.md#2026-trajectory--may-peak-turn-outward-cost-june-illness-confound); what this note adds is that the peak was a **median of 5**, not a few days at 5.

### The target for the end of 2026

> I think a median of 5 is definitely possible by the end of 2026, possibly a median of 6

Held as possible rather than forecast. And what stands between me and it is named, and it is not capacity:

> Its not that I can't get to 5 or 6 being normal, I just have to resolve the remaining land mines that blow me up when I get close.

The clearing mechanism is [the Alan-focused date night](central-loneliness/date-nights.book-chapter.md#the-alan-focused-night-clears-land-mines), and why the approach itself is what sets them off is [the guard-coverage lag](governed-expansion.book-chapter.md#why-rising-safety-generates-its-own-collapse).

## Level 6 — three times, still rare

> Yes, I've hit 6 at least three times in the past few months, so I'm starting to understand it, but its still rare.

Two of the three I can say:

> I had a time where I went for a walk and sang for several in public. Recently I practiced a song and then performed it for my family. I remember there being another one, but don't remember the specifics.

Both of the ones I can name are **singing**, and both are performed where someone can hear — a walk sung in public, and a song practiced and then performed for my family. That sits above the [level-4 anchor, where singing and being perceived become affordable](safety.book-chapter.md#scale-0-5-with-half-steps), and above the level-5 turn outward. *My reading: what the top of the range looks like, on the two recorded instances, is voice out loud in front of people. Alan named the two events and their shared feature was not put to him.*

Level 6 is above the top of [the capability-anchor table](safety.book-chapter.md#scale-0-5-with-half-steps), so these three readings have no rung to be written against — which is worth stating because the whole 2026 target is aimed at a band the anchor table does not describe.

## The tracked weeks and the 31 July step

*What follows was measured off my session tracking during the interview and read back to me. I have marked it because it is a reading of the record rather than something I said; my own words on it are quoted at the end.*

Eight weeks, 2026-06-12 to 2026-08-06. Thirty-eight days carry tracked blocks and eighteen are blank, the blanks clustering around the Europe trip. Duration-weighted mean safety across the window: **3.37**. Median of the daily means: **3.39**.

A linear fit slopes down at about −0.11 levels per week — one full level in nine or ten weeks. It does not survive dropping the final week: the six weeks through 07-30 are flat, and the whole slope is carried by the last seven days, which average **2.81** against **3.53** for the thirty-one days before them. A single step down at 31 July fits better than a gradual decline, and 31 July is about when the new arrangement started.

On that reading, my current rate of accumulation is about 3.37 Safety Years per year, against roughly 1 per year across [the eighteen years](#the-past-account) — and the band matters more than the rate, because 1 is *functioning* and 3.37 is *living*.

My answer:

> Yeah, that tracks. The last week is the phase shift where both the sleep and dates kicked in. I'll need more weeks to know what the post-trip trend looks like.

So the step is placed, by me, on the arrangement starting — [the bedroom experiment and the two date nights](central-loneliness/europe-trip.book-chapter.md#what-i-came-back-with) both beginning at once — and whether it is the price of starting or the beginning of a slide is not answerable from eight weeks. More weeks is what settles it.
