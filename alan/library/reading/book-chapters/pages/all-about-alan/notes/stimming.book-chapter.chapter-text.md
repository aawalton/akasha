
# Stimming

> Stimming — body-level and conceptual repetitive activity for nervous-system regulation; walking primary, plus foot-tapping, counting-as-grounding, music-with-novelty, ESO, familiar-source content, and coding-as-soother-and-stim.

- Repetitive self-stimulation as nervous-system regulation. [Non-habituation](non-habituation.book-chapter.md) makes stims continuously effective — the cost of input doesn't decay as it would for a neurotypical nervous system, so a stim never wears out.
- The repertoire is wide. Walking is primary and lifelong, foot-tapping is heavy when sitting, and several conceptual / cognitive stims play as large or larger a regulatory role as the body-level ones. Body-level and conceptual stims often run concurrently — the multi-stream day pattern in [interest-modulator.md](interest-modulator.book-chapter.md) is partly an interest-channel mechanism and partly a stim-stack.

## Body-level stims

- **Walking — primary, lifelong.** Walks while eating, walks while talking, walks instead of driving. Hard to spot externally because it doesn't read as stereotypical autistic stimming.
- **Foot-tapping / leg-shaking when sitting — heavy.** The default sitting stim.
- **Knee-bouncing or rocking — fallback.** When walking and foot-tapping aren't available.
- **Finger tapping / drumming — some.**
- **Hair-twirling, skin-picking, nail-biting — some.**
- **Oral chewing — some.**
- **Humming or vocal repetition — some.**
- **Hand flapping — only when really excited.** State-dependent, low frequency; otherwise absent.

## Conceptual / cognitive stims

Repetitive mental activity functioning as nervous-system regulation, distinct from special interests (content-driven engagement) and routines (sequence-driven function). Several of the activities below also serve the interest-channel maintenance role described in [interest-modulator.md](interest-modulator.book-chapter.md); the dual function is treated separately at the cross-references.

- **[Music](music.book-chapter.md) on Spotify Daily Mixes.** Repetitive music listening as a stim, but plain loops fail because of the ADHD novelty pull. Daily Mixes are the engineered shape — familiar genre / style as the regulatory anchor, novel tracks within it as the dopamine input. AuDHD composition: autism's familiarity → safety + ADHD's novelty → dopamine, both on a single channel (see [interest-modulator.md → instinctive channel](interest-modulator.book-chapter.md)).
- **Counting as grounding.** Counting, list ordering, and similar mental loops used explicitly as a grounding exercise. Substitutes for the visualization-based grounding non-aphantasic people use; aphantasia rules that route out, so structural / numerical grounding takes its place. Inverted observation: standard grounding-exercise advice ("visualize a calm place", "five things you can see") collapses on aphantasia. Numerical / structural grounding is the working substitute.
- **ESO and other low-stakes games.** Long-running familiar-mechanic games run as background regulation activity.
- **Familiar-source content.** Not re-reading known content (which doesn't supply enough novelty), but new content from familiar authors / familiar universes. The pseudo-familiarity transfer pattern from [interest-modulator.md](interest-modulator.book-chapter.md) is operative as a stim mechanism, not just an interest-channel mechanism.
- **Coding as soother and stim.** Programming itself functions both as a soothing activity (steady, structured) and as a stim (repetitive structured problem-solving with continuous micro-resolution). Crosses into special-interest territory but the regulatory function is distinct from the engagement function.

## Cross-references

- **[points-as-hunger-substitute.md](points-as-hunger-substitute.book-chapter.md) → food-stimming.** Eating is one of his stims — [food-stimming](points-as-hunger-substitute.book-chapter.md#food-stimming--eating-as-regulation) is the largest single driver of his eating, "the slower the better" so the regulating activity lasts. The same non-habituation that keeps the body-level stims effective is why it works. The eating keystone is its canonical home; it is listed here as the oral/ingestive member of the stim repertoire.
- **[non-habituation.md](non-habituation.book-chapter.md).** Non-habituation is the underlying mechanism that makes stims continuously effective — input cost doesn't decay, so the regulatory effect doesn't either.
- **[interest-modulator.md](interest-modulator.book-chapter.md) → instinctive channel.** Music-with-novelty, ESO, familiar-source content, and coding all double as interest-channel maintenance and as stims; the multi-stream day pattern is partly an interest-channel mechanism and partly a stim-stack.
- **[aphantasia-mechanism.md](aphantasia-mechanism.book-chapter.md).** Aphantasia rules out visualization-based grounding, which forces structural / numerical substitutes (counting, list ordering). The same composition explains why *familiar-source* content works as a stim while pure *re-reading* does not — there is no internal replay to supply the "familiarity" pole, so external new-content-from-familiar-source carries the regulatory weight.
- **[stimulation-regulation.md](stimulation-regulation.book-chapter.md).** The control layer above the stims: ESO, music, and the room light run as knobs in a manual closed-loop arousal thermostat, with focus quality as the sensor that decides when to add or shed a stimulation stream.
- **[sub-systems.md](sub-systems.book-chapter.md).** Several body-level stims have engineered system counterparts: compression clothing for grounding, weighted blankets for sleep grounding, deep meditative breathing as a controlled stim. The body-level stims here are the unstructured baseline; the systems are the engineered versions.
