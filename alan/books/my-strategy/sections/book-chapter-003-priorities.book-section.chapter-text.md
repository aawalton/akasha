> In general, I have a another top-level prioritization strategy of Stability > Throughput > Functionality > Polish, so that's a second one along with Accelerate Improvement > Apply Improvement.

Stated on their own:

**Stability > Throughput > Functionality > Polish**

**Accelerate Improvement > Apply Improvement**

The second is the one that divides my effort into [two channels](001-two-channels.book-chapter.md). The first is the one I reach for when deciding what a given piece of work should be spending its effort on.

## Where they were put

My first instinct was a list domain:

> we clearly need a global-priorities list domain with these four priorities as children, so we have a place to hang some of this

I changed that in the same session:

> Okay, instead of making this a list, lets try just putting the priorities in order in the glossary and add a design line for the departure that the glossary order is the priority order

And then extended the same treatment:

> Okay, I like that, lets do the same transfor for the five foundational layers

So the four priorities and the five foundational layers both now stand in the `global` domain's glossary in meaningful order, with two Design lines saying so: *"The priorities stand in the glossary in priority order"* and *"The foundational layers stand in the glossary in dependency order."*

---

*Recorder's reading, marked.* The change of mind here is small on the page and worth naming, because it is a pattern rather than a one-off. A list domain would have been a new document whose only job was to hold four words in order. Putting them in the glossary instead spends no new document, and pays for the ordering with a single Design line declaring that the order carries meaning. He then immediately applied the same move to the five layers, which suggests he recognised it as a general technique rather than a fix to one case.

The two orderings are not ranked against each other, and he was not asked to rank them. Nothing in the session says what to do when Stability-first and Accelerate-Improvement-first point at different work.
