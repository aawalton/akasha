## How I pull

> I use a pull strategy here. Take the ones closest to completion and get those finished first, then pull forward, rather than pushing things through.

## Where the selecting actually happens

It was put to me that the pull strategy runs entirely inside the first group — that it is a good rule for *finishing* and does no selecting at all, because everything it can choose between was already chosen when someone defined it. I agreed, and said where the choosing does happen:

> Yes, that is correct. The selection happens at the definition step, when I work with a definer agent to turn an idea into a theme (forgot that one in the first group), initiative, project, or direct change (forgot that one too) depending on the size of the item.

So the definition step is the one that decides, and it turns an idea into one of four things depending on its size: a **theme**, an **initiative**, a **project**, or a **direct change**.
