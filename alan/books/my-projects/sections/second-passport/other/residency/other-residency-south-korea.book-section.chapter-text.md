
## Employment-based (E-series)

### E-1 Professor
- **Eligibility**: tenured / contracted professor at a Korean college, junior college, graduate school, or affiliated research institute. Master's in STEM + 3 yrs experience or doctorate (STEM); Ministry of Education recommendation.
- **Term**: up to 5 yrs, extendable. Feeds F-2 / F-5.

### E-2 Foreign Language Instructor
- **Eligibility**: native speaker of the target language with at least a bachelor's degree from a recognized institution in that country (US, UK, Canada, Australia, NZ, Ireland, South Africa for English); criminal-background check; medical/drug screening on entry.
- **Term**: up to 2 yrs, extendable. Limited PR pathway — counts toward F-5 residence but the standard expectation is to convert to F-2 / F-4 / F-5-1.

### E-3 Researcher
- **Eligibility**: master's + 3 yrs experience or doctorate (career requirement waived if Korean master's or top-ranked foreign degree, or SCIE/SSCI/A&HCI thesis author). Position at government-funded R&D institute, defense-industry research institute, or industrial R&D project led by the government.
- **Term**: up to 5 yrs, extendable.

### E-7 Skilled Worker (specially designated activities)
- **Eligibility**: employer-sponsored hire in one of ≈87 designated professional / skilled occupations; degree or equivalent experience tied to the occupation. Salary and headcount-ratio thresholds set per occupation.
- **Recent changes (2024–2026)**: regional-visa pilot (Apr 2025) lets 15 metropolitan governments tailor E-7 eligibility for local labor needs through 2026.

### E-7-4 Skilled Tradesperson (points-based)
- **Eligibility**: foreign worker with ≥4 years on E-9 / E-10 / H-2 in Korea; points score (typically ≥60/100) over salary, tenure, certifications, and Korean language; employer endorsement. 2025 quota: 35,000.
- **Term**: 3 yrs (extendable). De facto skilled-migrant route — primary track for non-degreed workers seeking long-term stay; feeds F-2-7 then F-5.

### E-7-M (K-CORE)
- **Recent changes (2024–2026)**: under 2026 rules, K-CORE E-7-M holders can convert to F-2 after **3 years** if employed in a designated population-decline region (vs. 5 years otherwise).

## Talent / long-residence (F-2 series)

### F-2-7 Points-based long-term residence
- **Eligibility**: ≥80/100 across age, education, Korean language (KIIP/TOPIK), annual income, and bonuses (KIIP Stage 5, top-500-QS or Korean-university degree, community service). **Fast track**: annual salary ≥ KRW 40M permits immediate application regardless of stay length.
- **Benefits**: 3-yr stay, employment unrestricted, may start a business, dependents (F-2-71) may work. **PR after 3 years on F-2-7.**

### F-2-8 Real Estate Investor
- **Eligibility**: investment in government-designated tourism/leisure facilities in Jeju, Incheon Free Economic Zone (Songdo / Yeongjong / Cheongna), Pyeongchang Alpensia, or Yeosu Gyeongdo. **Threshold doubled from KRW 500M to KRW 1B (~USD 780k) in 2023.** Program extended through 30 Apr 2026.
- **PR pathway**: F-5 after 5 years holding the investment.

### F-2 Top-Tier Visa (senior global talent)
- **Recent changes (2024–2026)**: launched **2 Apr 2025**. Advanced degree from top-100 university, ≥8 yrs experience, ≥ KRW 140M annual salary, employment in AI / semiconductors / displays / batteries / biotech / robotics / defense. F-2 status, **F-5 in 3 years**, family-friendly provisions, tax / housing / education support.

### F-2 K-STAR (STEM)
- **Recent changes (2024–2026)**: 2025 track inviting ≈400 STEM professionals/year in AI, biotech, advanced engineering. Same F-2 status and 3-yr PR pathway as Top-Tier.

## Investor / business (D-series)

### D-8 Corporate Investor
- **Eligibility (D-8-1)**: invest ≥ KRW 100M from overseas-remitted funds in a Korean corporation under the Foreign Investment Promotion Act; hold ≥10% of capital; serve as CEO/co-president; independent office (no shared workspaces); active management.
- **Term**: 1–5 yrs, extendable. Feeds F-5-6 (PR after 3 yrs holding the investment + KRW 300M+ scale) or F-5-1 via 5-yr general route.

### D-9 Trade / Business Manager
- **Eligibility**: managing an overseas-headquartered branch in Korea, import/export agency, or trading business. Higher investment expected than D-8 but no foreign-invested-enterprise registration needed; no tax incentives.

### D-10 Job Seeker
- **Eligibility**: graduates, professionals, researchers, or startup-preparers; KRW 900,000/month × 6 mo (KRW 5.4M) self-support. Recent-graduate exemption from points system if <3 yrs since graduation, TOPIK 4+ / KIIP 4+, and no prior E-1–E-7 history.
- **Term**: up to 6 months, extendable.

## Family (F-series)

### F-6 Marriage Migrant
- **Eligibility**: validly married to a Korean national (F-6-1); custodial parent of a Korean minor after marriage termination (F-6-2); surviving / abandoned spouse not at fault for marriage breakdown (F-6-3).
- **Work rights**: unrestricted.
- **PR pathway**: F-5-2 after 2 years on F-6; simplified naturalization track also available after 2 years (3-yr marriage + 2-yr residence, or 1-yr marriage + 1-yr residence with a child).

### F-4 Overseas Korean
- **Eligibility**: foreign national who (a) personally held Korean nationality at any time, or (b) is a direct descendant (child or grandchild) of a former Korean national. Covers Korean-Americans, Joseonjok (ethnic Koreans with PRC citizenship), Goryeo-saram (post-Soviet ethnic Koreans), Zainichi. Male applicants 18–40 who haven't fulfilled Korean military obligation face restrictions.
- **Term**: 3 yrs, indefinitely renewable; broad work rights (excludes simple labor categories).
- **Note**: F-4 grants residence and most economic rights but **not Korean nationality** — separate from citizenship by descent. Feeds F-5-7 PR after 2 yrs (60-yr-olds: shorter route) and nationality recovery for former Koreans at 65+.

## Permanent residence — F-5 (Nationality Act / Immigration Act)

The F-5 PR status has ~27 sub-categories. Selected high-utility entries:

### F-5-1 General PR (5-year residence)
- **Eligibility**: 5+ yrs in Korea on long-term visas; annual income ≥ 2× national GNI (≈ KRW 105M for current cycle; 2026 GNI updates to ₩52.4M, so 2× ≈ ₩104.8M from 1 Apr 2026); KIIP Stage 5 completion or 60+ on the PR exam; clean criminal record (home + Korea); financial-asset alternative if income short.

### F-5-2 Spouse-based PR
- **Eligibility**: F-6 holder for ≥2 yrs; valid marriage or qualifying termination; income/means and language requirements.

### F-5-5 High-Investment Investor PR
- **Eligibility**: personal investment ≥ **USD 500,000** under FIPA + employment of ≥5 Korean citizens (full-time, ≥minimum wage, employment-insurance-listed ≥6 mo). Investor only — not officers or employees.
- **Benefit**: immediate PR (no prior residence requirement on the investment side beyond the standard pre-grant timeline).

### F-5-6 D-8 graduate PR
- **Eligibility**: D-8 holder for ≥3 yrs with sufficient investment scale and income; effectively the standard PR upgrade for corporate investors who don't meet F-5-5.

### F-5-7 Overseas Korean PR
- **Eligibility**: F-4 holder for ≥2 yrs with stable income and family/community ties.

### Public-Business Investor (F-5-15 / formerly counted as F-5-5 variant in some lists)
- **Eligibility**: ≥ KRW 500M in approved public-business / regional-development projects; can route to immediate PR for designated projects.

PR holders may work freely, sponsor immediate family, and (with the standard 5-yr clock) qualify for general naturalization. PR is conditional — extended absence (>2 yrs) or serious criminal conduct triggers revocation review.

## Short-term and study (D-series, H-1)

### D-2 Student
- **Eligibility**: Certificate of Admission from a Korean institution; financial proof.
- **Term**: matches program duration. Builds points toward F-2-7 (Korean-degree bonus) and counts toward F-5-10/11 study-graduate tracks.

### D-4 Trainee
- **Eligibility**: enrolled in non-degree Korean-language program or technical training at a recognized institute; 70–80% attendance. Part-time work allowed after first 6 months.
- **Term**: 6 mo – 2 yrs.

### H-1 Working Holiday (US eligible)
- **Eligibility**: US citizens currently enrolled or within 1 yr of graduation; aged 18–30 (per US–ROK MoU). Roundtrip ticket + travel plan + financial proof (≥USD 6,000); educational documentation.
- **Term**: 18 months, multiple-entry. Prohibited from regulated professions (medicine, law, language instruction, entertainment, piloting).

## Workation / digital nomad

### F-1-D Workation Visa
- **Eligibility**: launched **Jan 2024**, made permanent thereafter. Earn ≥ 2× Korean prior-year GNI per capita from non-Korean employer/clients (≈ KRW 85M / ~USD 61k for 2024 cohort, rising to ~KRW 88M for current cycle); private health insurance covering ≥ KRW 100M medical/repatriation; no local employment.
- **Term**: 1 yr + 1 yr extension (up to 2 yrs). Dependents (spouse + minor children) may accompany.

## Culture / industry

### K-Culture Training Visa (Hallyu)
- **Eligibility**: launched 2H 2024 on a trial basis. Enrollment at a recognized Korean performing-arts academy or entertainment-industry trainer (K-pop, dance, vocal, acting, modeling). No audition or talent-agency offer required at the visa-issuance stage.
- **Term**: up to 2 yrs.

## Regional / area-specific pilot (2025–2026)

- **Eligibility**: launched 2 Apr 2025; 15 metropolitan local governments selected for the pilot through 2026. Each authority tailors D-2 (student) and E-7 (professional) eligibility to local labor and demographic needs; population-decline regions get incentive structures (e.g., E-7-M K-CORE 3-yr F-2 conversion noted above; extended stays; tax-friendly conditions).
