
Luxembourg has no fast-track residency-by-investment with carve-outs from the standard naturalization clock. Every viable path runs the same 5-year Article 9 clock plus Sproochentest A2/B1 + *Vivre ensemble* civic certificate. Time on long-term residence (LTR) status earned at year 5 makes the citizenship wait portable across EU states.

## Investor Residence Permit — Track 1 (existing company, €500K)

- **Threshold**: €500,000 invested in an existing Luxembourg-registered company; investment + payroll must be maintained 5 years. Real-estate purchase/lease excluded.
- **Validity**: initial 3 years; renewable 3 years if conditions met.
- **Physical presence**: declaration of arrival at the commune; >6-month absence triggers permit return. No minimum-stay quota beyond that.
- **Citizenship clock**: counts toward the standard 5-year naturalization clock. No shortcut — full Sproochentest (A2 speaking / B1 listening) and *Vivre ensemble* required.
- **Processing**: Ministry of the Economy/Finance opinion required *before* arrival; ministry typically responds within 3 months. Silence = rejection. Residence permit filed within 3 months of arrival.
- **2024–2026 reforms**: explanatory note refreshed Apr 2024; residence permit demand form updated Jan 2026 (procedural refinements only — thresholds unchanged).

## Investor Residence Permit — Track 2 (new startup, €500K)

- **Threshold**: €500,000 in a newly incorporated Luxembourg company; **5 jobs created within 3 years**, coordinated with ADEM (the national employment agency).
- **Validity / presence / clock / processing**: same as Track 1 (3 + 3 years; standard 5-year clock; ≤3-month ministry decision).
- **Risk vs Track 1**: payroll covenant is a hard 5-job hurdle inside 36 months. Failure to hit headcount risks non-renewal.

## Investor Residence Permit — Track 3 (€3M fund / €20M deposit) — EXCLUDED

Out of scope per applicant capital ceiling (€500K). Track 3 requires €3M in a management/investment structure (with substance maintenance) or €20M deposited 5 years with a Luxembourg financial institution.

## Independent Worker / Self-Employed Permit

- **Threshold**: no statutory capital floor, but the applicant must (a) hold the qualifications required for the planned activity (diploma, regulated-profession credentials, business permit where applicable), (b) demonstrate **sufficient resources** to operate, and (c) submit a business + financing plan showing national economic or social interest. For regulated activities, an "agreement in principle" from the Ministry of the Economy precedes the immigration application.
- **Validity**: maximum 3 years from declaration of arrival (5 years if applicant already holds LTR status in another EU Member State). Renewable 3 years.
- **Renewal cadence**: apply within 2 months before expiry.
- **Physical presence**: declaration of arrival required; >6-month absence triggers permit return.
- **Citizenship clock**: counts toward standard 5-year naturalization clock; LTR available at year 5. Full Sproochentest + *Vivre ensemble* required.
- **Processing**: 3 months for the temporary authorisation to stay; silence = rejection. €80 fee.
- **Fit for the applicant profile**: this is the most plausible route for a US citizen with portable self-employment income — capital-light, no employer dependency, no €500K lock. The business-plan + "national interest" review is the gating risk.

## Private Reasons / Sufficient Resources Permit

- **Threshold**: monthly net income at or above the **social minimum wage for unskilled workers** (€2,703.74/month gross from 1 January 2026 per the PwC social parameters update; earlier indexation cycles around €2,570). Resources can come from (i) a professional activity in another EU/Schengen Member State, (ii) an old-age, invalidity, or survivor's pension from an EU/Schengen social security institution, or (iii) own resources at the unskilled-SSM floor.
- **Validity**: maximum 3 years; renewable.
- **Renewal cadence**: 2 months before expiry.
- **Physical presence**: declaration of arrival; >6-month absence triggers permit return.
- **Citizenship clock**: counts toward standard 5-year clock; LTR available at year 5. Full Sproochentest + *Vivre ensemble* required.
- **Ancillary work**: the holder may carry out a salaried activity on an *ancillary* basis (≤10 hours/week average over a month) without changing permit category.
- **Processing**: 3 months; silence = rejection.
- **Caveat for US-only retirees**: official guidance frames the pension qualifier around *EU/Schengen* social security institutions. A US-paid Social Security or private pension may not qualify under the pension limb; the own-resources limb (€2,700+/month proven) is the cleaner path for US nationals and remains generous given Luxembourg accepts portable passive income (dividends, rental abroad, business distributions).

## Family Reunification

- **Sponsor eligibility**: any third-country national lawfully resident in Luxembourg who (a) intends to apply for LTR or already holds it, and (b) has stable, regular, sufficient resources at or above the average unskilled SSM over 12 months, plus suitable housing (≥12 m² for first occupant, ≥9 m² per additional person).
- **Eligible family**: spouse / registered partner (18+), unmarried children under 18 in custody. Dependent ascendants and adult dependent children may be admitted on discretion after sponsor's 12-month residence.
- **Validity**: family member permit is **1 year** initially, renewable as long as the sponsor's conditions hold.
- **Autonomous permit**: after 5 years of legal residence the family member can hold an independent permit on their own resources.
- **Citizenship clock**: counts toward standard 5-year clock for the dependent in their own right; spouse of a Luxembourger has a separate marital route (out of scope here).
- **Processing**: ≤9 months statutory cap.

## Long-Term Study (Higher Education)

- **Threshold**: full-time enrollment in a Luxembourg higher-education institution (complete cycle leading to a degree; doctoral researchers eligible). Monthly resources at ≥80% of the social inclusion income (REVIS); scholarship, bank statements (6 months), or signed declaration from a Luxembourg-resident guarantor accepted.
- **Validity**: ≥1 year, up to the length of the study cycle.
- **Renewal cadence**: 2 months before expiry, same documentation.
- **Work rights**: ≤15 hours/week averaged over a month during term; unlimited during holidays, doctoral research, or as a Uni.lu assistant.
- **Citizenship clock**: continuous lawful residence as a student **does** count toward the 5-year LTR clock and the 5-year naturalization clock (no statutory exclusion akin to the EU LTR Directive's discretionary half-counting carve-out is applied as a bar in guichet.lu's naturalization preconditions).
- **Post-study transition**: a separate authorisation lets graduates remain to seek work or start a business after their cycle.
- **Processing**: ~60 days.

## Path to Long-Term Resident (EU LTR) Status

After **5 years of continuous lawful stay** under any of the above permits (excluding students-only in some Member States, but Luxembourg counts continuous student residence) the holder can apply for LTR — valid 5 years, renewable. Absences: ≤6 consecutive months and ≤10 months total over the 5 years (12-month extensions allowed for pregnancy, illness, education). Decision within 6 months. €80 fee. LTR provides portable settlement rights across the EU.

## Naturalization Prerequisites (Reference)

- 5 years lawful residence (Article 9 standard track); 20+ years opens a relaxed option-by-residence with a 24-hour language course instead of the full Sproochentest.
- Sproochentest: A2 speaking, B1 listening (≥50% speaking; listening can offset).
- *Vivre ensemble au Grand-Duché de Luxembourg* certificate: 24-hour course or exam.
- No renunciation of US citizenship required — Luxembourg allows dual.

## Filtered-Out Programs

- **Salaried Worker permit** — employer-sponsored; filtered.
- **EU Blue Card** — employer-sponsored highly-qualified track; filtered.
- **Intra-Corporate Transfer / Posted Worker** — endorsement-based; filtered.
- **Investor Track 3 (€3M fund or €20M deposit)** — exceeds €500K capital ceiling.
- **Visitor visa (≤90 days)** — non-PR-feeding; filtered.
- **Au pair / volunteer / seasonal** — non-PR-feeding; explicitly excluded from LTR eligibility.

## Viability Verdict

Luxembourg retains **multiple** viable residency paths for the profile: the Independent Worker permit is the cleanest fit (capital-light, portable self-employment), with Private Reasons as a parallel option once the €2,700+/month own-resources threshold is shown, and Investor Track 1 or 2 as the capital-deploy variants exactly at the €500K ceiling. All four feed the standard 5-year naturalization clock with no shortcuts; none waive Sproochentest or *Vivre ensemble*.
