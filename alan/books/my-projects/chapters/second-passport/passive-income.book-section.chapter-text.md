> Passive-income / capital-driven residency-to-citizenship paths surviving the active-work-optionality filter for a US-citizen family (married + 3 kids relocating together) pursuing EU/non-EU citizenship without renouncing US. Includes Golden Visa routes (Tier 0 warehouse permits + Tier 1 capital lock) and the two surviving passive-income permits (Portugal D7, France Visitor) where active work is also allowed. Excluded passive paths and excluded Golden Visa paths (Cyprus Reg 6(2), Greece GV — prohibit work) listed at end. Companion to active-income.md. May 2026 snapshot.


For active-work / self-employment / digital-nomad paths, see [active-income.md](active-income.book-chapter.md). ## Tier 0 — Warehouse Golden Visa with full work rights (0–7 d/yr to maintain)

Permit can be held in absentia. Useful for optionality, Schengen access, or holding while deciding to move. Citizenship clock still requires real ≥183 d/yr physical presence.

| Country | Path | Capital | Maintenance | Citizenship clock |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hungary | Guest Investor Program | €250k REIF | 0 d/yr | ~9–10y |
| Portugal | Golden Visa (ARI) | €250k cultural / €500k fund | 7 d/yr | ~12–13y (post-3 May 2026) |

Both grant unrestricted self-employment / salaried-employment / sole-proprietorship rights once issued (Act XC of 2023 §30 Hungary; ARI Art. 90-A Portugal).

## Tier 1 — Capital lock with work bound to invested entity (183+ d/yr)

Work rights apply to the invested entity. Layering general freelancing requires a separate work authorization, except France Talent Investor which grants full work rights under the single Passeport Talent card.

| Country | Path | Capital | Citizenship clock | Work rights |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Italy | Investor Visa | €250k innovative-startup / €500k strategic | ~12–13y | Bound to invested entity; Lavoro Autonomo needs separate quota-gated permit |
| Luxembourg | Investor Track 1/2 → Art. 9 | €500k (existing co. w/ 5 employees / new startup w/ 5 jobs) | ~5.7y | Manage/work invested entity only; freelance needs travailleur indépendant permit |
| France | Talent — Investisseur | €300k investment in French company | ~7y | **Full residence + work** (salaried, freelance, business creation) under one Passeport Talent card |
| Belgium | Professional Card (business-investment, Flanders) | ~€350–500k operating envelope | ~5.5y | The Professional Card IS the self-employment permit; binds to declared activity |

## Top picks

**Fastest 3 (clock):**
1. **Uruguay 3y family-residence** — ~3.5–4y. Spanish informal hearing only.
2. **Luxembourg Investor Track** — ~5.7y. €500k capital + Sproochentest B1/A2 (real filter).
3. **France Talent Investor** — ~7y. €300k + B2 French + civic MCQ.

**Cheapest 3 (city f4 cost):**
1. Montevideo, Uruguay — ~USD 2,500–3,500/mo
2. Budapest, Hungary — ~€3,560/mo
3. Lisbon, Portugal — ~€4,425/mo

**Best maintenance flexibility (Tier 0):**
- **Hungary Guest Investor** — 0 d/yr to maintain; €250k REIF; unrestricted work rights.
- **Portugal Golden Visa** — 7 d/yr to maintain; €250k cultural / €500k fund; unrestricted work rights.

**Lowest financial bar:**
- **Portugal D7** — €11k/yr income floor, no capital lock. Slowest passive clock (12–13y) but the lowest financial bar.
- **France Visitor** — ~€22k/yr (no capital lock). 7y clock if you can manage B2 French + 5y French earnings.

## Excluded — passive-income paths that prohibit active self-employment

Applicant wants optionality to layer digital-nomad / self-employment work on top. Only Portugal D7 (full work rights) and France Visitor (foreign-sourced self-employment OK) survive among the 7 passive paths.

- **Cyprus Category F PR** (€9,568/yr passive) — "Employment or business in Cyprus is prohibited."
- **Belgium D-visa private means** (~€29k/yr) — no work permitted on the permit class; BE survives via Professional Card (Flanders) at active-income.md or business-investment route above.
- **Luxembourg Private Reasons** (€32k/yr) — only ≤10h/week ancillary salaried activity allowed; LU survives via Investor Track 1/2 above.
- **Italy Elective Residency** (~€31k/yr) — explicit no-work prohibition; consulates routinely refuse applicants whose income looks like active labor.
- **Greece FIP** (€3,500/mo) — "FIP holders may not work for or in Greece, including local clients"; GR survives via DNV at active-income.md.

## Excluded — Golden Visa paths that prohibit active self-employment

- **Cyprus Reg 6(2) Cat 6.2 PR** (€300k + €50k/yr foreign income) — declaration of intent not to work in Cyprus; only unpaid directorship of invested company permitted. Cyprus exits short-list once both Cat F and Reg 6(2) are out. **Reconsider only if §111A 4.7y fast-track is verified reachable from Reg 6(2) without the Highly Skilled Employee employer-sponsorship track** — current research suggests §111A requires the employee track, not Reg 6(2).
- **Greece Golden Visa** (€250k heritage / €400k Zone B) — L.5038/2023 (consolidating L.4251/2014 art. 20B): permit "does not grant any right of access to any form of employment" in Greece. Self-employment prohibited. Foreign-client remote work on Greek soil treated as employment-equivalent. GR survives at active-income.md via DNV.

## Decision matrix considerations

- **Language friction**: Luxembourgish (Sproochentest, real filter) gates the fastest passive path. French B2 (France Talent / Visitor) and Portuguese A2 (Portugal D7 / GV) are mid-tier. Spanish (Uruguay) is informal — no test, easiest by far.
- **Capital outlay**: Uruguay (USD 1,500/mo income proof) and Portugal D7 (~€11k/yr) are the lowest-capital citizenship-feeding paths. Luxembourg Investor Track (€500k) is the only sub-7y EU path requiring real capital lock.
- **Tier 0 trade-off**: warehouse permits give optionality (no presence) but don't accelerate citizenship. Once you actually move, the underlying clock applies (8y Hungary, 10y Portugal post-reform).
