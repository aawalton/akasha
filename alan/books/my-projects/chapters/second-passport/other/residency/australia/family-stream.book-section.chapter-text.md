
## Family Stream

### Partner visa (subclass 309 offshore / 100 permanent) and (820 onshore / 801 permanent)

- **Two-stage**: provisional (309 or 820) → permanent (100 or 801) ~2 yrs after lodgement (de facto or marriage; longer relationships can skip straight to permanent).
- **Processing (May 2026)**: 309 median ~16 months (90th percentile ~22–26 months); 100 stage another 13–32 months. ~95,000 partner-visa backlog as of mid-2024.
- **Pathway**: 309/820 + 100/801 → 4 yrs lawful residence → conferral. **No marriage shortcut.**

### Parent visas (subclasses 103 non-contributory, 143/864 contributory)

- **Subclass 103 (standard)**: AUD 5,125 fee; **wait time ~30+ years** due to annual cap (capped queue).
- **Subclass 143 (contributory)**: ~AUD 50,000 per applicant; wait time **12–15 years**.
- 151,596 parent-visa applications on hand as of 30 June 2024.
- Functionally a "purchase a queue spot for your descendants" path; not a planning route for the applicant.

### Other family (Child 101/802, Remaining Relative 115/835, Carer 116/836, Aged Dependent Relative 114/838)

- Various caps; long queues; case-by-case.
