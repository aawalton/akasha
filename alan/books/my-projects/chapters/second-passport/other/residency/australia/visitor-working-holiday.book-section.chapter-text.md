
## Visitor / Working Holiday

### Working Holiday visa (subclass 417) and Work and Holiday (subclass 462)

- **US citizens are eligible for 462 only** (417 is for UK/Ireland/most EU/East Asia).
- 462 requires: 18–30, tertiary education, IELTS 4.5+, ~AUD 5,000 funds, AUD 635 fee.
- Up to 12 months; not a PR path. Extensions via regional work for 2nd/3rd-year visas.

### Visitor visa (subclass 600) / eVisitor / ETA

- The de-facto "digital nomad" workaround — Australia has **no formal digital nomad visa as of May 2026**.
- 600 allows up to 12 months; work for an Australian employer is prohibited but remote work for foreign clients is tolerated in practice.
