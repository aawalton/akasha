
# Incentives and Utah Contractors

Status as of May 2026. Incentive law moves fast; verify each row against the linked program page before booking work.

## Federal — Energy Efficient Home Improvement Credit (IRC §25C)

**Status: expired Dec 31, 2025.** The [One Big Beautiful Bill Act (OBBBA, July 2025)](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/1) repealed §25C ahead of its original 2032 sunset. Projects placed in service on or after Jan 1, 2026 **do not** qualify ([IRS — Energy Efficient Home Improvement Credit](https://www.irs.gov/credits-deductions/energy-efficient-home-improvement-credit), [Alliance to Save Energy — what the new IRS guidance means](https://www.ase.org/blog/what-new-irs-guidance-means-energy-efficiency-tax-incentives), [NAHB — expiring energy tax credits](https://www.nahb.org/blog/2025/07/expiring-energy-tax-credits)).

What this means for envelope retrofits in 2026:

- No federal 30%-of-cost credit on insulation, air sealing, doors, windows.
- No federal $150 home-energy-audit credit.
- No federal $2,000 heat-pump credit. (The §25D residential clean energy credit for PV was also affected by OBBBA on a separate schedule — verify in [pricing.md](../pricing.book-chapter.md) before sizing the PV economics.)

There is **no replacement federal credit** for envelope retrofit costs incurred in 2026. Plan accordingly.

## Federal — IRA Home Energy Rebates (HOMES + HEAR)

**Status in Utah: not yet launched, expected by end of 2026.** Utah Office of Energy Development was approved for **~$101M in HOMES + HEAR funding** in late 2024 ([Utah Clean Energy — federal home energy rebates are coming to Utah](https://utahcleanenergy.org/federal-home-energy-rebates-are-coming-to-utah/), [DOE state-funding tracker — Sep 2025](https://nationalhousingtrust.org/sites/default/files/documents/DOE-Rebates-State-Funding-Tracker-9-25.pdf)).

When live:

- **HEAR (Home Electrification and Appliance Rebates)** — point-of-sale for low/moderate-income households on insulation, air sealing, ventilation, heat pumps, heat-pump water heaters, electric panel + wiring upgrades. **Up to $14,000/home at ≤80% AMI**, 50% cost coverage to $7,000 at 80–150% AMI.
- **HOMES (Home Efficiency Rebates)** — performance-based, rebate scales with measured/modeled energy savings. Pairs with the BPI/RESNET audit deliverable.

**Action for Alan:** check [Utah Energy Hub — energy efficiency incentives](https://hub.utahcleanenergy.org/energy-efficiency/energy-efficiency-incentives/) monthly. If the program launches before the retrofit is in the ground, the rebate stack can cover a meaningful fraction of the $8,000–15,000 retrofit package even at >150% AMI (HOMES has no income cap, just smaller rebates above 80% AMI). If launch slips past the project window, proceed without it.

## State — Rocky Mountain Power Wattsmart

**Status: live, updated Feb 27, 2026** ([Rocky Mountain Power Wattsmart Homes program update Feb 2026](https://wattsmarthomes.com/rebates/insulation-ut/), [Wattsmart insulation rebate detail](https://greenhomespecialties.com/rocky-mountain-power-insulation-rebate/)).

Relevant rebates for this project:

| Measure | Wattsmart rebate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Attic insulation | Up to $0.40/sq ft | R-19 or below → R-49+ |
| Wall insulation | Up to $0.60/sq ft | R-11 or below → R-13+ |
| Air sealing | Bundled into insulation rebate | Requires blower-door pre/post |
| Combined insulation + air sealing + windows + doors | **Cap: $1,100 total** | Whole-house cap, not per-measure |
| Home energy assessment | **Free** | Through approved contractors — see below |
| Heat pump (CCHP) | $400–$1,750 depending on tier | Separate from envelope rebate |

Eligibility:

- Existing home, **not new construction**.
- Homes built 2015 or later are excluded.
- Must have electric heating or central cooling on ≥80% of conditioned floor area. **A house being converted to all-electric heat pump qualifies** once the heat pump is in.

Process: submit online application **within 180 days of installation**; rebate issued within 14 business days post-approval ([Rocky Mountain Power — savings and energy choices](https://www.rockymountainpower.net/savings-energy-choices/home.html)).

The $1,100 cap is small relative to the $8,000–15,000 retrofit cost, but it's free money for paperwork. Apply it.

## State — Dominion Energy ThermWise

**Status: gas-only, not applicable.** Dominion Energy ThermWise rebates require a Dominion natural-gas account at the property. The all-electric retrofit removes any reason to maintain a gas service. Skip this program ([Utah Energy Hub — efficiency incentives](https://hub.utahcleanenergy.org/energy-efficiency/energy-efficiency-incentives/)).

## Utah BPI / RESNET contractors — leads

Vetted starting list for Utah County:

- **GreenHome Specialties** — 465 South 1100 West Suite B, Provo, UT 84601. BPI-Certified Energy Analysts on staff. Free Wattsmart-program audit + paid comprehensive audit with blower door. Also does air sealing, insulation, and Wattsmart-rebate paperwork. ([greenhomespecialties.com](https://greenhomespecialties.com/home-energy/)). **First call.**
- **Griffin Energy Audits** — South Salt Lake. 16+ years auditing. Blower door, thermal imaging, full envelope walk-through. Independent of the install side, so good for the audit-and-spec-only role. ([griffinenergyaudits.com](https://www.griffinenergyaudits.com/)).
- **Utah Energy Conservation Coalition / Energy Rated Homes of Utah** — RESNET-affiliated HERS-rater directory; oldest Utah HERS program. Use for additional RESNET-certified rater referrals if GreenHome and Griffin are booked or biased. ([utahenergy.org](https://utahenergy.org/)).
- **BPI Find a Professional** — national directory filterable by ZIP. Use to surface additional Utah County BPI Building Analysts beyond the two above. ([bpi.org/locator](https://www.bpi.org/certified-professionals/find-professional)).
- **RESNET HERS Rater directory** — same purpose, RESNET side. ([resnet.us](https://www.resnet.us/about/standards/)).

## Bid strategy

1. Free Rocky Mountain Power Wattsmart audit through GreenHome Specialties as no-cost reconnaissance.
2. Independent paid BPI audit + Manual J from Griffin Energy Audits (or equivalent RESNET-certified rater) — keep the audit-and-spec function separate from the retrofit-install bid.
3. Retrofit-install bids from at least two firms against the independent audit's scope of work.
4. Wattsmart rebate application filed by the install contractor (they have the paperwork volume to do it efficiently).
5. Monitor Utah HOMES/HEAR launch monthly via the [Utah Energy Hub page](https://hub.utahcleanenergy.org/energy-efficiency/energy-efficiency-incentives/). If launched pre-install, restructure the contractor billing to capture the rebate; if not, proceed.
