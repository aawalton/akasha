
# National Installers

National multi-state operators that confirmably install in Provo / Utah County. These typically use subcontracted crews rather than W-2 employees, which introduces install-quality variability.

## Tesla Energy

- HQ: Austin, TX; Utah office in Draper
- Scale: National
- Provo coverage: Yes
- Panels: Tesla-branded (built by partners; previously Hanwha Q CELLS, currently mixed)
- Inverters: Tesla Solar Inverter
- Storage: Tesla Powerwall 3 — premium battery; AC- and DC-coupled options
- Model: Online-quote, no in-home sales; subcontracted regional installers
- Warranty: 25-year panel performance, 12.5-year inverter, 10-year roof workmanship
- Strengths: Best price-per-watt for cash buyers nationally; integrated battery; clean install aesthetics
- Risks: Sparse local support; service requires Tesla mobile dispatch; long permit/install lead times in 2024-2025 in Utah specifically — Tesla Preferred Installers like Ask Solar Mike are often a better path to a Powerwall install with local support
- Sources: [SolarReviews](https://www.solarreviews.com/installers/tesla-energy-reviews)

## Sunrun

- HQ: San Francisco, CA; Utah office in Lehi (inherited from Vivint Solar 2020 acquisition)
- Scale: Largest US residential solar installer; ~$3.2B Vivint acquisition gave them deep Utah footprint
- Provo coverage: Yes
- Model: Lease / PPA / loan / cash (heavily promotes lease and PPA)
- BBB: Accredited since 2022
- Warranty: 25 years on lease/PPA systems (full service); shorter on owned systems
- Storage: Tesla Powerwall, Brightbox (their branded battery)
- Risks: Lease/PPA dominant; 20-year contracts with escalators; ownership transfer friction when selling home; mixed reviews on installer subcontractor quality
- Sources: [BBB profile](https://www.bbb.org/us/ut/lehi/profile/solar-energy-contractors/sunrun-inc-1166-22302389); [EnergySage analysis of lease/PPA contracts](https://www.energysage.com/solar/solar-leases/sunrun-solar-lease-ppa-solar-contract-agreement/)
- Recommendation: Avoid lease/PPA in Utah — Customer Generation Program economics favor ownership

## SunPower Inc. (formerly Complete Solaria)

- HQ: Richmond, CA
- Status: Re-formed entity that bought legacy SunPower's assets out of bankruptcy in Sept 2024; rebranded back to "SunPower" April 2025; acquired Sunder Energy Sept 2025 making it #5 US residential installer
- Utah presence: Through Blue Raven (Orem subsidiary) and Sunder (South Jordan subsidiary)
- Panels: Maxeon (independent — no longer SunPower owned)
- Risks: Less than 2 years of operating history under current corporate form; warranty enforceability for systems sold by legacy SunPower Corp. is murky — Complete Solaria committed to honor some but not all
- Recommendation: If you want SunPower-style premium panels, source Maxeon panels through a long-tenured local installer (e.g., Creative Energies) rather than buying directly from this entity

## Trinity Solar

- HQ: Wall, NJ
- Listed by multiple aggregators as serving Provo, but coverage in Utah appears limited / sales-funnel-driven; verify direct quote and licensed installer name before contracting
- Strengths: Family-owned, large East Coast operation
- Risks: Sales-led; actual installs in Utah may be performed by subcontracted partner whose identity you should know

## Freedom Forever

- HQ: Temecula, CA
- Status flag: [Filed Chapter 11 in 2026](https://blog.prevostlawfirm.com/freedom-forever-files-chapter-11-bankruptcy/) — verify current operating status; warranty risk elevated
- Provo coverage: Listed as serving Utah; primarily a "platform" installer for dealer networks
- Recommendation: Avoid for new contracts until bankruptcy resolves

## Sunnova

- HQ: Houston, TX
- Status flag: Sunnova filed bankruptcy in 2025; SunStrong assumed control of certain assets
- Provo coverage: Historic — current status uncertain
- Model: Dealer-network lease/PPA; relied on third-party sales/install partners
- Recommendation: Avoid for new contracts

## ADT Solar (formerly Sunpro Solar)

- Status flag: ADT exited residential solar Jan 2024 after $89M EBITDA loss; closed remaining branches March 2024; partial re-entry under creditor pressure but [the underlying business risk remains acute](https://solarlawfirm.us/2025/07/18/adt-solar-sunpro-shutdown-explained-legal-help-for-affected-homeowners/)
- Recommendation: Avoid for new contracts

## Lumio (formerly Smart Wave — the *old* one)

- Status flag: Chapter 11 Sept 2024; 363 sale to Zeo Energy ($4M cash + 6.2M shares); liquidating plan effective Feb 18, 2025; class action ongoing; Utah DOPL revoked their B100 contractor license Jan 2025
- Recommendation: Defunct — orphaned customers should pursue warranty claims via [solar law firms](https://blog.prevostlawfirm.com/zenith-solar-lumio-bankruptcy/); do not consider for new contracts. Note the active Bluffdale Smart Wave Solar is a separate company.
