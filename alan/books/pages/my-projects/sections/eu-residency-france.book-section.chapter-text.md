
## Reform context (loi Darmanin 2024, in force 1 Jan 2026)

Law 2024-42 of 26 Jan 2024 ("loi Darmanin") and its 1 Jan 2026 implementing decrees tightened both the residency and naturalization gates without rewriting the underlying titre structure:

- **A2 French + Examen Civique** now required for any first multi-year residence permit (CSP).
- **B1 French + Examen Civique** required for the 10-year Carte de Résident (CR).
- **B2 French (oral + written)** required for naturalization; assimilation interpreted more strictly.
- **Talent Passport** renamed simply **"Talent"** (June 2025); 12 sub-categories (up from 11). All references below use the legacy name for continuity with prefecture documents.

The titre ladder is unchanged: **VLS-TS (1y)** → **Carte de Séjour Pluriannuelle / CSP (2-4y, sub-category dependent)** → **Carte de Résident / CR (10y)** → **Naturalisation (Article 21-17 Civil Code, 5y residence; 2y if French Master's per Article 21-18)**.

## Active programs

### Talent — Économic Investor ("Talent Passport Investor")

- **Capital threshold**: direct investment of **≥€300,000** in tangible/intangible fixed assets in a French legal entity (new or acquired); investor must hold ≥30% of capital OR invest directly. Commit to **create or protect jobs** within 4 years.
- **Permit**: **CSP "Talent" 4-year**, renewable on continued investment + job evidence; family ("Talent — Famille") gets matching 4-year card with full work rights.
- **Physical presence**: **no day-count**; prefecture checks investment continuity at renewal. Ties (lease, utility, business activity) suffice.
- **Endorsement**: **none required**. Prefecture verifies objective criteria (capital deployed, jobs, holding %), not subjective merit.
- **Citizenship clock**: years count toward standard 5-year Article 21-17 naturalization. No fast-track.
- **Processing**: ~3 months from complete file to permit.
- **Fees**: €300 admin + €50 stamp + €99 VLS.

### Talent — Profession Libérale (regulated profession)

- **Qualifications**: regulated profession (médecin, architecte, avocat, etc.) — must hold the French/EU authorization to practice (diplôme, ordre registration, autorisation d'exercice). Prefect verifies regulatory compliance before issuing.
- **Income floor**: revenue projections meeting **≥SMIC** (€21,876.40/year, 2026); demonstrate viable independent practice.
- **Permit**: **CSP "Talent" up to 4 years**, renewable on continued practice.
- **Physical presence**: tax residency in France expected; no fixed day count.
- **Endorsement**: **none** beyond regulatory body authorization (objective, profession-specific).
- **Citizenship clock**: counts toward 5-year naturalization.
- **Processing**: ~2-3 months.

### Talent — Création d'Entreprise (entrepreneur, viable business plan)

- **Qualifications**: Master's-equivalent diploma OR 5+ years comparable professional experience.
- **Capital + income**: **€30,000 personal investment** in the project + revenue projections at ≥SMIC.
- **Viability attestation**: required via demarches-simplifiees.fr — a sworn-officer review of business plan / market study / financials. Process is **rule-based document review**, not discretionary endorsement (contrast with the "innovative project" sub-category which **requires public-body endorsement and is excluded** per filter). Attestation valid 12 months.
- **Permit**: **CSP "Talent" up to 4 years**, renewable on continued operations.
- **Physical presence**: no day-count; business activity expected.
- **Citizenship clock**: counts toward 5-year naturalization (or 2-year if a French Master's is held).
- **Processing**: 2-4 months including attestation step.

### Visitor (VLS-TS "visiteur") — passive income

- **Income floor**: stable passive income ≥SMIC (~**€1,843/month**, **€22,000+/year** 2026); +50% for spouse, +30% per child. Pensions, rental, dividends, royalties, portfolio yield all qualify.
- **Permit**: **VLS-TS 1 year**, renewable annually as CST "visiteur"; **no right to work in France** (foreign-sourced self-employment income permitted but no French clients/employer). After loi Darmanin tightening (Jan 2026), renewal requires A2 French + Examen Civique to move to a multi-year CSP.
- **Physical presence**: tax-resident expectation (183+ days) for renewal; consulates increasingly require evidence of genuine residence.
- **Citizenship clock**: counts toward the 5-year Article 21-17 clock **only if** the applicant becomes tax-resident and demonstrates **stable resources** — visitor years on foreign income generally satisfy this; passive-income holders qualify.
- **Processing**: ~2-4 weeks at consulate.

### Vie Privée et Familiale — Conjoint de Français

- **Qualification**: marriage to a French citizen (who retained French nationality on the marriage date and after); effective shared life in France.
- **Permit**: **VLS-TS 1 year "VPF"** (work-authorized) → **CSP 2-year** → **CR 10-year after 3 years of marriage + residence**.
- **Citizenship clock**: **Article 21-2 declaration** route — citizenship by marriage after **4 years** married (5 if not continuously resident in France for 3 years), B2 French. Parallel to standard naturalization, and faster than the 5-year clock.
- **Processing**: ~1-3 months.

### Étudiant (long-term study, Master's pathway)

- **Income floor**: ≥**€615/month** (~€7,380/year); accredited French enrollment required.
- **Permit**: **VLS-TS 1y** → **CSP "étudiant"** for remaining program length (up to 4 additional years). Work allowed up to 964h/year (~20h/week).
- **Citizenship clock — key reduction**: Article 21-18 Civil Code reduces the residence requirement from 5 to **2 years** for holders of a **French Master's degree (M2, 2-year program)**. The 2 years counted are the post-graduation years on a successor titre (typically Talent — Qualified Employee, Talent — Création d'Entreprise, or APS job-search permit). Loi Darmanin retained this provision but tightened the B2 French + civics gates.
- **Processing**: ~2-4 weeks; Campus France Études en France pre-clearance for many countries.

### Carte de Résident (CR, 10-year card) — feeder for all CSPs

- **Qualification**: 5 years continuous legal residence (3 if married to French citizen); B1 French; Examen Civique; stable resources ≥SMIC; integration into French society.
- **Permit**: 10-year renewable. Absence >3 consecutive years invalidates the card.
- **Citizenship clock**: CR years count fully toward Article 21-17; many naturalize directly from CSP without going through CR.

## Filtered out (do not apply)

- **Talent — Innovative Project recognized by a public body**: requires endorsement from a recognized incubator/accelerator/public organism (BPI, La French Tech). Excluded per filter.
- **Talent — Distinguished Talent (national/international renown)**: subjective merit assessment by Ministry of Interior. Excluded.
- **Talent — Qualified Employee / EU Blue Card / ICT**: employer-sponsored. Excluded.
- **Saisonnier / Salarié / TT single permit**: employer-sponsored work tracks. Excluded.
