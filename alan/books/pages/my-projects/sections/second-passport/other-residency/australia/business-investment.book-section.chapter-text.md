
## Business / Investment Stream

### Business Innovation and Investment Program (BIIP) — CLOSED 31 July 2024

- All 188 streams (Business Innovation, Investor, **Significant Investor**, Entrepreneur, Premium Investor) closed to new applications 31 July 2024.
- ~12,778 in-flight 188 applications as of Dec 2024; processing continues against a reduced 1,000-visa annual allocation.
- **Refunds** offered from Sept 2024 for withdrawers.
- **Subclass 888 (permanent)**: still available as the PR conversion for existing 188 holders meeting performance benchmarks.
- **What replaced it**: National Innovation Visa (858, above) — direct PR, no rolling business-performance tests, much higher bar.

### Significant Investor Visa (SIV) — CLOSED 31 July 2024

- Formerly AUD 5 million complying investment, 188C stream. Closed with rest of BIIP.
- No direct successor: the **major-investor stream of the NIV** is the closest analogue but requires nomination + exceptional record, not a fixed investment threshold.
