
## Overview

Andorra splits its residency regime into **active** (lucrative — work or self-employment, ≥183 days/yr) and **passive** (non-lucrative — investment/income-based, ≥90 days/yr). The passive track functions as Andorra's de facto Golden Visa. The country sits outside the EU/EEA and Schengen and runs a distinctively low-tax regime: personal income tax (IRPF) is 0% up to €24,000, 5% to €40,000, and **10% above €40,000**; corporate tax 10%; VAT (IGI) 4.5%; **no wealth tax, no inheritance tax**; capital gains 10% with exemptions (full exemption after 10 years' holding on most assets). These fiscal conditions, not the Schengen access (which Andorra does not provide), drive almost all HNW interest.

## Major 2026 Reform — Omnibus 2 Law

On **22 January 2026** the Consell General approved the Omnibus 2 law overhauling passive residency by investment. Headline changes:

- **Investment floor for passive residency without lucrative activity**: raised from €600,000 to **€1,000,000**.
- **AFA bond** (€50,000 + €12,000/dependant): converted from a **refundable deposit** into a **non-refundable payment to the State** (only refunded if the initial authorization is denied).
- **Real-estate investment**: each unit purchased must exceed **€800,000**.
- **Affordable alternative**: investors may reduce total investment to **€400,000** if invested permanently into the **National Housing Fund** (social-housing instrument).
- **Transition**: applicants who filed before 22 Jan 2026 follow prior rules.

A separate 2025 reform (Llei 5/2025) raised the active self-employment ownership floor from 20% to **34%** of the Andorran company.

## Residency Paths

### Residencia Activa — Self-employed (active)

- **Profile**: founder/administrator of an Andorran company, ≥34% ownership.
- **Requirements**: registered address in Andorra (owned or rented); **non-refundable €50,000 AFA payment**; clean record; private health insurance; effective residence **≥183 days/yr**.
- **Exemption**: "professionals liberals" (regulated professions — lawyers, doctors, architects, engineers) are exempt from the €50,000 AFA payment but, post-2025, subject to the same reservation system.
- **Permit cadence**: 6 months → 1 year → 2 years → 5 years.
- **Path to citizenship**: 20 years (10 if applicant attended Andorran school as a minor).

### Residencia Activa — Employee (active)

- **Profile**: foreign worker with an offer from an Andorran employer.
- **Requirements**: job offer; the sponsoring company must demonstrate no suitable local candidate; effective residence ≥183 days/yr; private health insurance.
- **Quota**: subject to annual work-permit quotas.

### Residencia Passiva — Without lucrative activity (Golden Visa)

- **Profile**: passive HNW residency; the canonical "Andorra Golden Visa."
- **Investment (post-Omnibus 2, 2026)**: **€1,000,000** in Andorran assets — sovereign debt, Andorran company equity, AFA-deposited funds, or real estate (each parcel >€800,000); or **€400,000** into the National Housing Fund.
- **AFA payment**: €50,000 principal + €12,000 per dependant — **non-refundable** as of Jan 2026.
- **Income test**: applicant must show income ≥300% of Andorran minimum wage, +100% per dependant.
- **Physical presence**: **≥90 days/yr**.
- **No work right** in Andorra (passive only).
- **Permit cadence**: 1 year initial → 3-year renewals → 10-year renewals.
- **Path to citizenship**: 20 years residence — but renunciation of prior citizenship is required at naturalization (see [`../citizenship/andorra.md`](../citizenship/andorra.book-chapter.md)).

### Residencia Passiva — Professionals with international projection

- **Profile**: business owner whose Andorran company conducts **≥85% of its activity outside Andorra** (e.g. international consultancy, online business).
- **Requirements**: no €1M investment floor; instead must create/acquire an Andorran company meeting the 85% offshore-activity test, generating sufficient income to support applicant + dependants; AFA payment per the standard schedule; physical presence ≥90 days/yr.
- **Tax position**: the company is Andorra-taxed (10% IS); the resident is Andorra-taxed (≤10% IRPF).
- **Use case**: digital service businesses, location-independent professionals, consultants with foreign clientele.

### Residencia Passiva — Scientific, cultural, sporting interest

- **Profile**: internationally recognized scientists, artists, athletes.
- **Requirements**: no minimum investment; documented international recognition; evidence of economic viability; AFA deposit **€47,500 principal + €9,500/dependant**; ≥90 days/yr presence.
- **Examples**: pro cyclists basing in Andorra for altitude training have been the archetypal users.

### Digital Nomad Residency (Llei 42/2022)

- **Profile**: remote worker billing **≥85% of services to clients outside Andorra**.
- **Requirements**: income ≥3× Andorran minimum wage (+1× per dependant); private health and disability insurance; registered local address; the activity must be telework-based; **no €50,000 AFA payment required**; no Andorran company incorporation required.
- **Physical presence**: ≥90 days/yr.
- **Permit cadence**: 2 years initial → 2 years → 3 years → 10-year renewals.
- **Quota**: **only ~50 permits issued per year** — highly competitive.
- **Fees**: €2,500 application + €500 residence card per applicant/dependant.
- **Live since**: mid-2023 (implementing regulations under Llei 42/2022 of December 2022).

### Family reunification

- **Sponsor**: Andorran citizen, or active resident who has held the permit **≥12 months** (passive residents may reunify three months after permit issuance for immediate family).
- **Eligible relatives**: spouse, minor children, dependent ascendants, children aged 18–25 if unmarried university students financially dependent on the principal.
- **Requirements**: documented family tie, financial means, medical examination, private health insurance.

### Student residency

- **Profile**: foreign student enrolled in an Andorran educational institution.
- **Requirements**: enrollment certificate, sufficient financial means, private health insurance.
- **Note**: counts toward the eventual 10-year reduced naturalization track if combined with mandatory Andorran-schooling years as a minor.

### Residencia Permanent

- **Eligibility**: continuous active or passive residence for **20 years** (consistent with the standard naturalization clock).
- **Effect**: indefinite right of residence; precondition for naturalization but does not by itself confer citizenship.

## Path to Citizenship from Residency

All residency tracks feed the same naturalization clock:

- **20 years** standard.
- **10 years** if applicant completed mandatory schooling in Andorra.
- **3 years marriage + 3 years residence** if married to an Andorran citizen.

In every case, **renunciation of US (or other) citizenship is required** — see [`../citizenship/andorra.md`](../citizenship/andorra.book-chapter.md).

## Practical Assessment — US Citizen Wanting Residency Only

Andorra is **very realistic** as a residency-only destination for a US HNW citizen who explicitly does **not** intend to naturalize:

- **Tax**: Andorra IRPF caps at 10%, no wealth tax, no inheritance tax — substantially below US rates. A US citizen still owes US tax on worldwide income (citizenship-based taxation), but can claim the Foreign Earned Income Exclusion (~$130k in 2025) and Foreign Tax Credit against Andorran tax. Net effective rate typically lands around the US federal rate, but the Andorran piece is itself very low and there is no inheritance/wealth/SALT layer.
- **Capital cost (post-Omnibus 2)**: €1M passive-residency investment + €50k non-refundable AFA + housing — total minimum landed cost roughly €1.1–1.5M depending on housing route. The €400k Housing-Fund alternative is materially cheaper if liquidity permits.
- **Cheaper path**: the **Digital Nomad** visa (no €50k AFA, no €1M investment, only 50 issued/yr) is the cleanest entry for a location-independent US professional billing offshore — provided one of the limited slots is secured.
- **Physical presence**: 90 days/yr is light and compatible with US tax residency planning, US 183-day exposure rules elsewhere, and continued US activity.
- **No Schengen access**: residing in Andorra does **not** grant Schengen rights; entry to neighboring Spain/France is via the Schengen visa-waiver regime applicable to US passport holders.
- **Permanence**: as long as the applicant remains content with permanent-resident status (renewing every 1/3/10 years) and never applies for naturalization, no renunciation pressure arises.
