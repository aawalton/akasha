> Non-EU residency paths for a US-citizen applicant (married, three kids; family relocates together as accompanying dependents on the main applicant's permit) — distinct from citizenship. Only paths with an actual citizenship pathway (dual permitted, no renunciation) AND meeting the self-driven, objective-criteria filter are retained — employer-sponsored, endorsement-based, distinguished-talent, discretionary-special-exception, program-level-discretionary (invitation-gated points systems), narrow-discretionary-self-employment (where regulatory review rejects generic portable-income applicants), individually-discretionary-where-no-rule-based-alternative-exists, family-reunification-to-existing-citizen/PR (sponsor must already hold status), renunciation-required, non-PR-feeding lifestyle, and high-capital (>€500k or CHF 400k/yr+) routes are all excluded. Spouse + kids on derived permits under the main applicant is implicit in every track listed. May 2026 snapshot. 15 non-EU countries surveyed; 2 retain at least one viable residency path.


Residency ≠ citizenship. Each path below feeds the country's residence-physical-presence clock toward naturalization, with dual citizenship preserved at the end. For citizenship paths, see [../citizenship/summary.md](../citizenship/summary.book-chapter.md). For EU residency paths, see [../../eu/residency/summary.md](../../eu/residency/summary.book-chapter.md).

## Income-threshold passive residency

- **Uruguay — Residencia Permanente** — direct PR (no temp stage); ~USD 1,500/mo single / USD 2,500/mo couple income proof (pension / dividend / rental / royalty / self-employment); ~183 days/yr practical floor; 6–12mo processing. Parallel **Tax Residency** under Decree 148/007 — 5 alternative tests, 10y income-tax holiday for new tax residents. Citizenship clock runs from filing date. **A foreign married couple both holding Residencia Permanente qualifies as a "constituted family" under Uruguay's 3y family-residence citizenship rule** — no Uruguayan-citizen relative required (see `../citizenship/summary.md`).
- **Mexico — Temporary Resident → Permanent Resident ladder** — 4y Temporary Resident (~USD 4,400/mo income OR ~USD 72k savings, both sustained over 6–12mo of statements; per-dependent ~USD 1,100–1,400/mo additional under Family Unity, so a family of 5 targets ~USD 8,800–10,000/mo principal income) → convert to Permanent Resident by *arraigo* at year 4. Direct PR at consular filing requires ~USD 7,400/mo or ~USD 300k savings and is typically gated to "retired" applicants. 2026 UMA value MXN 117.31/day; July 2025 SRE directive standardized consulates on UMA-based calculation. Self-employment / freelance for non-Mexican clients with foreign-source income is permitted on the default Temporary Resident card without a *permiso para trabajar*. Naturalization presence floor at the citizenship step is **18 of 24 months** (SRE Reglamento) — Tier 1, ~9 mo/yr. Citizenship clock: ~6.0–6.5y to Carta (see `../citizenship/summary.md`). **Slower and lower-rule-of-law than Uruguay** (CPI 2025: 27 vs 75; Uruguay #1 in LATAM, Mexico last among OECD) but retained as the second non-EU survivor of the filter set.

## Notes

- **Excluded by scope (high-capital, not affordable)**: New Zealand Active Investor Plus (NZD 5M Growth / NZD 10M Balanced), Switzerland Lump-sum tax residence (federal CHF 400k/yr minimum + cantonal floors that can exceed CHF 1M).
- **Excluded by scope (employer-sponsored worker tracks)**: UK Skilled Worker, Australia 482 SID → 186 ENS, NZ Skilled Migrant (Accredited Employer offer), NZ Green List Tier 1, Japan HSP (employer-tied points), Switzerland B permit (TCN employment), South Korea E-7-4 / F-2-7 employment ladder, UK Scale-up, Canada CEC / FST (require Canadian work experience or job offer).
- **Excluded by scope (endorsement / distinguished-talent / special-exception)**: UK Global Talent (endorsing body), UK Innovator Founder (endorsing body), Canada Start-Up Visa (designated org endorsement), Australia 858 NIV (exceptional talent / distinguished talent), Australia 190 / 491 (state-government nomination).
- **Excluded by scope (program-level discretionary, invitation-gated points systems)**: **Australia 189 Skilled Independent** (paper-eligible direct PR via Skills Assessment + points test, but DHA controls invitation rounds + occupation ceilings; 189 invitations deprioritized since 2022–23 in favor of state-nominated 190/491 — de facto unavailable for the generic US-citizen profile without state nomination). **Canada Express Entry FSW (no-employer track)** (paper-eligible via ECA + CRS, but IRCC controls draw size + category mix + CRS cutoff; 2024–26 general-draw cutoff CRS 510–547, which a US-citizen Anglophone with US bachelor's + no Canadian study/work + no French scores ~430–470 against — practically narrow).
- **Excluded by scope (no path to citizenship for a US citizen unwilling to renounce)**: Andorra (Passive Residency + Residencia Activa → 20y + renunciation), Monaco (Carte de Séjour → 10y discretionary + renunciation), San Marino (Atypical / Elective → 20y + 2/3 Council vote, even with 2026 dual-citizenship reform), Japan (J-Skip / Business Manager / Digital Nomad → renunciation), South Korea (F-5-5 → general naturalization renunciation), Liechtenstein (no realistic non-EEA path; 30y citizenship + renunciation).
- **Excluded by scope (non-PR-feeding lifestyle visas)**: Iceland Long-term remote-worker visa (6mo non-renewable, explicitly no PR/citizenship credit), Japan Digital Nomad (6mo, no PR clock), South Korea F-1-D Workation / D-10-2 / K-Culture Training (discretionary lifestyle, no PR feed).
- **Countries surviving the filter**: Uruguay, Mexico. Both permit dual citizenship with the US.
- **Countries dropped (no viable residency on-ramp under the active filter)**:
  - **Iceland** — Business Operation Permit is the only formal self-employment route; Directorate of Immigration rejects most generic freelancer / digital-nomad / portable-income applications. Requires registered Icelandic business with demonstrable local value (employees, physical presence, Icelandic-market activity) — not a fit for portable self-employment. Long-term Remote-Worker Visa is 6mo non-renewable and explicitly non-PR-feeding.
  - **Norway** — Self-employed sole proprietorship is the only on-ramp surviving the no-employer / no-endorsement / no-capital filter (skilled-worker qualifications + credible business plan + Norwegian Register of Business Enterprises registration; AS does not qualify). UDI conducts a discretionary business-viability review with no rule-based alternative (Norway has no Golden Visa, no investor program, no employer-free passive track). Under a strict "no individual discretion where no rule-based alternative exists" filter, Norway falls. Reconsider if you're willing to accept UDI discretion as more rule-bound than the Iceland Business Operation Permit case.
  - **Australia, Canada** — both lose all routes once 189 / FSW are stripped as program-level discretionary; every remaining track requires employer sponsorship, state/provincial nomination, or designated-body endorsement.
- For non-EU CBI options outside Europe (Caribbean: St. Kitts, Dominica, Grenada, Antigua), see future sibling folders under `second-passport/`.
