
Slovakia has **no CBI program** and **no golden-visa RBI program**. All applicant-relevant tracks below feed the standard naturalization clock: 8 years of continuous **permanent residence** immediately preceding the application (§ 7(1)(a) Act 40/1993), plus Slovak language exam, civics, and proven livelihood. Permanent residence itself requires 5 years of immediately preceding continuous **temporary residence** — so the realistic floor from arrival to citizenship is ~13 years for a US applicant with no EU ancestry, no Slovak spouse, and no "Slovak Living Abroad" certificate.

The 1 July 2025 reform (Act 404/2011 amendment) tightened the business track substantially — annual quota, mandatory business plan with Ministry of Economy economic-benefit opinion, consulate-only first filing, and a 24-month seasoning rule before switching purpose to business.

## Active programs

### Temporary residence for business — sole trader (živnostník, SZČO)

The classic self-employed track. Apply as a registered Slovak sole proprietor under the Trade Licensing Act.

- **Thresholds (July 2025 – June 2026 cohort):**
  - Business account balance ≥ EUR 5,682.60 (20× subsistence minimum) at filing.
  - Personal account balance ≥ EUR 3,409.56 (12× subsistence minimum) at filing.
  - **Mandatory business plan** filed with the application; Foreign Police forwards to the Ministry of Economy for an "economic-benefit" opinion. Negative opinion = refusal.
  - On renewal: prove **taxed business income ≥ 20× subsistence minimum** for the prior fiscal year.
- **Validity:** First permit issued for up to 3 years (raised from 2 years on 1 July 2025). Renewable for up to 5 years per renewal cycle, capped at 5 years total per temporary-residence purpose under § 22(1) Act 404/2011.
- **Physical presence:** No fixed day-count, but the permit can be revoked if the holder ceases to actually conduct the declared business in Slovakia. Tax residency (183-day rule or center-of-vital-interests test) is the practical floor for renewal evidence.
- **First filing:** Slovak embassy/consulate abroad only (in-Slovakia first filing was eliminated 1 July 2025). Renewals filed at the Foreign Police department within Slovakia.
- **Annual cap:** 700 business-purpose permits total for 2025 across all consulates (vs. ~10,256 issued in 2024). 2026 quota not yet published as of May 2026 but expected to remain restrictive.
- **Processing:** Up to 90 days from a complete, consulate-submitted application.
- **Citizenship clock:** Counts toward the 5-year temporary-residence prerequisite for permanent residence, which then begins the 8-year permanent-residence clock for naturalization. No acceleration.

### Temporary residence for business — s.r.o. director / company owner

Same statutory category as the sole-trader track, but filed by the statutory body (konateľ) of a Slovak s.r.o. (limited-liability company) of which the applicant is the founder/owner.

- **Thresholds:** Same dual-account proof as sole trader (EUR 5,682.60 business + EUR 3,409.56 personal). On renewal, the **company** must show taxed income ≥ 60× subsistence minimum (~EUR 17,000) attributable to the foreigner-director's role, OR the foreigner must draw equivalent salaried compensation.
- **Validity:** 3 years initial, renewable. Same business-plan + Ministry-of-Economy opinion + consulate-only filing rules as the sole-trader track.
- **Purpose-switch lockout:** A foreigner already holding temporary residence on another basis must hold that prior permit for **at least 24 months** before switching purpose to "business" (effective 1 July 2025). This blocks the common "study → business" laundering route.
- **Citizenship clock:** Same as sole-trader — feeds the 5+8 stack with no acceleration.

### Temporary residence for "other specific activity" (§ 26 Act 404/2011)

Catch-all for activities that are neither employment, business, study, family reunion, nor research — e.g., volunteering, lectorship, sport, journalism, religious activity, internships. **Not a retirement / passive-income permit.** Slovakia has **no retiree visa** and no permit category that accepts passive income alone as the purpose of stay.

- **Threshold:** Proof of an invitation/contract from the Slovak host organization documenting the specific activity, plus subsistence-minimum funds for the stay.
- **Validity:** Bounded by the duration of the underlying activity; max 2 years per the statute.
- **Limited utility for this applicant** — there is no "passive income" or "financially independent person" specific-activity sub-category. A US self-employed/passive-income applicant cannot route through § 26 without an actual Slovak host activity.
- **Citizenship clock:** Counts toward the 5-year temporary-residence prerequisite.

### Temporary residence for family reunification

Available only if the applicant already has a qualifying family member (spouse, parent, or in narrow cases child) who **already** holds Slovak temporary or permanent residence. Not a standalone route for a US applicant with no Slovak/EU family ties — listed for completeness.

- **Threshold:** Verified family relationship + sponsor's income ≥ subsistence minimum × months of intended stay + suitable housing.
- **Validity:** Tied to the sponsor's permit, capped at 5 years.
- **Processing:** 90 days.
- **Citizenship clock:** Counts toward the 5-year temporary-residence prerequisite.

### Temporary residence for study

Practical for an applicant willing to enroll in a Slovak university (full-time only) or in a university-organized Slovak-language preparatory course. Useful as a **bridge year** to build local presence and learn Slovak before the 24-month purpose-switch lockout for a later business permit, but the study permit itself feeds the residence clock more slowly than business.

- **Threshold:** Confirmation-of-studies letter from the host institution, subsistence funds, apostilled criminal record, accommodation proof. Tuition at Slovak public universities is free for full-time programs taught in Slovak; English-taught programs charge tuition (typically EUR 2,000-6,000/year at public universities, much higher at private).
- **Age cap:** Secondary < 20; post-secondary / language prep < 23; university (bachelor/master/PhD) has no statutory age cap.
- **Validity:** Up to 6 years per study purpose.
- **Work rights:** Permitted business or work up to 20 hrs/week without a separate work permit.
- **Processing:** 30 days from a complete application.
- **Citizenship clock:** Counts toward the 5-year temporary-residence prerequisite. **However**, time on a study permit can be discounted by half in some prior interpretations — verify current administrative practice at filing.

## Filtered out

- **Slovak "investor visa" / golden visa** — **does not exist.** Confirmed May 2026: Slovakia has never operated a residence-by-investment or citizenship-by-investment program. The "significant foreign investor" certificate from the Ministry of Economy accelerates *processing time* (~30 days) for an otherwise-standard temporary/permanent residence application but does not lower the financial-presence test, the residence clock, or the language/civics naturalization bar. Excluded as a separate program — it is an administrative-fast-track tag, not a category.
- **Digital nomad visa** — **does not exist** as of May 2026 (confirmed: no draft legislation in 2025-2026 parliamentary cycle). Remote-worker applicants must use the sole-trader business track or rely on Schengen 90/180.
- **Retirement / passive-income visa** — **does not exist.** No "financially independent person" category in Act 404/2011. Excluded.
- **EU long-term-resident transfer** — requires already holding EU long-term residence from another Member State; not applicable to a fresh US applicant.
- **Employer-sponsored work permits, single-permit employee track, EU Blue Card** — excluded per filter (employer-sponsored).
- **National-interest / endorsement routes** — Slovakia has no endorsement-based program (no UK-style Global Talent equivalent). N/A.

## Recent reforms 2024-2026

- **1 July 2025 — Act 404/2011 major amendment.** Annual 700-permit cap on business-purpose permits; mandatory business plan with Ministry of Economy economic-benefit opinion; consulate-only first filing; initial permit extended to 3 years; 24-month purpose-switch lockout into business.
- **October 2025** — Slovak government published revised migrant residence-rules guidance via the Migration Information Centre (IOM-operated, MoI-funded); no further statutory changes through May 2026.
- **No retiree or digital-nomad legislation** has been introduced in the 2024-2026 cycle.

## Verdict

Slovakia retains **one viable PR-feeding residency path** for the applicant profile (US citizen, adult, no EU ancestry, no Slovak family, portable self-employment / passive income, capital budget < EUR 500k): the **sole-trader (živnostník) business permit**. The s.r.o. director variant is functionally equivalent at higher administrative overhead. The study permit is a bridge, not a destination. All other categories are filtered out or inapplicable.

Realistic timeline: ~13 years from arrival to passport (5 years TR + 8 years PR + ~6-12 months naturalization processing), with no fee-or-investment acceleration available. The 700-permit annual cap is the binding constraint on the business track — applicants should target consulates with available slots (Tbilisi, Belgrade, Hanoi published the highest 2025 quotas) and file early in the calendar year.
