
## Active programs

### Investment-based residency (Golden Visa)

- **Category:** investment (residency, not direct CBI).
- **Eligibility:** ≥512,000 EUR in qualifying Bulgarian AIFs / ETFs / government bonds; grants permanent residence on day one; maintain investment 5 years; then apply under Art. 12 (standard 5-year naturalization with A1 language + renunciation rules).
- **Timeline:** ~5 years investment-held PR + ~12-month (real 18–36) processing = ~6.5–8 years to citizenship.
- **Dual citizenship:** subject to standard Art. 12 renunciation rule unless EU/EEA/Swiss.
- **Notes:** no minimum physical-presence requirement to maintain PR (unique among EU Golden Visas as of 2026), but the A1 language requirement remains a hard gate at the citizenship stage. Feeds the standard 5-year naturalization path — does not shortcut the citizenship clock.

## Closed / historical

### Direct citizenship by investment (Art. 14a)

- **Category:** investment / CBI.
- **Status:** **abolished 5 April 2022** by amendments adopted 24 March 2022 under EU pressure. Previous program (active 2009–2022) granted citizenship after 1M EUR investment / 1-year fast-track.
- **Notes:** Bulgaria has since revoked the citizenships of at least 17 prior CBI grantees for failure to make or maintain qualifying investments (post-2022 audits). No remaining transitional applications.
