> EU residency paths for a US-citizen applicant (married, three kids; family relocates together as accompanying dependents on the main applicant's permit) — distinct from citizenship. Only paths with an actual citizenship pathway (dual permitted, no renunciation), meeting the self-driven objective-criteria filter (no employer sponsorship, no endorsement / distinguished-talent / discretionary-special-exception, no narrow-discretionary self-employment where approval rates are lottery-tier or capped by hard national quotas, no individually-discretionary path where a rule-based alternative exists in the same country, no family-reunification-to-existing-citizen/PR sponsor, ≤€500k one-time capital lock, PR-feeding) are tracked. Spouse + kids on derived permits under the main applicant is implicit in every track listed. May 2026 snapshot. 17 EU citizenship-applicable countries surveyed; 13 retain at least one viable residency path.


Residency ≠ citizenship. Each path below feeds the country's standard residence-physical-presence clock toward naturalization with dual citizenship preserved. For citizenship paths, see [../citizenship/summary.md](../citizenship/summary.book-chapter.md). For non-EU residency paths, see [../../other/residency/summary.md](../../other/residency/summary.book-chapter.md).

## Capital-driven (Golden Visa / investor residency, ≤€500k)

- **Greece — Golden Visa** — €250k heritage / commercial-conversion tier; €400k Zone B standard-residential; **0-day stay** to maintain permit (Schengen-access tool); physical residence required to feed 7y citizenship clock.
- **Hungary — Guest Investor Program (eff. 1 Jul 2024)** — €250k REIF; 10y+10y permit; **no presence requirement to maintain**; physical residence still required to feed 8y citizenship clock.
- **Italy — Investor Visa** — €250k innovative-startup tier or €500k strategic-investment company; feeds 10y clock.
- **Portugal — Golden Visa (post-Oct 2023 reform)** — €250k cultural-heritage donation (€200k low-density); €500k fund/R&D; 10-jobs creation (no capital floor); 7d/yr presence preserved; feeds 10y clock (per 3 May 2026 Lei da Nacionalidade signing).
- **Cyprus — Reg 6(2) PR (Cat 6.2)** — €300k new-build property or Cyprus equity/funds + €50k/yr foreign income + €30k pledged deposit; **indefinite PR** at issuance; visit once/2yr to maintain; feeds 7y clock.
- **Luxembourg — Investor Track 1 / Track 2** — €500k each (existing company w/ 5+ employees / new startup w/ 5 jobs in 3y); feeds 5y clock.
- **France — Talent (Investor sub-category)** — €300k investment in French company; CSP 4y renewable.
- **Belgium — Professional Card (business-investment route)** — ~€350k–€500k operating-business envelope (no one-time lock, no real-estate route).

## Passive income / retirement

- **Portugal — D7** — €920/mo single (~€11k/yr); lowest bar in the EU; feeds 10y clock.
- **Belgium — D-visa private means** — ~€2,408.79/mo (110% GAMMI; ~€29k/yr); uncodified but reliable; conflicts with the 5y CBN work-history rule so works best with 10y CBN track.
- **Cyprus — Category F PR** — €9,568/yr passive income (€4,613/dependant); ~€15–20k bank deposit; full-rate clock; **realistic 5–7y processing** per Auditor-General Feb 2026.
- **France — Visitor VLS-TS** — ≥SMIC (~€22k/yr 2026); no work in France; renewable annually; feeds 5y clock.
- **Italy — Elective Residency (Residenza Elettiva)** — ~€31k/yr passive (consulates apply 200–300%, plan €35–40k); no work allowed.
- **Luxembourg — Private Reasons / Sufficient Resources** — €2,704/mo (Jan 2026); US retirees rely on own-resources limb (pension limb is EU/Schengen-only).
- **Greece — FIP (Financially Independent Persons)** — €3,500/mo (~€42k/yr); passive only.

## Self-employment / freelancer / sole proprietorship

- **Germany — Freiberufler §21 Abs. 5** — liberal professions (IT, consulting, journalism); **no capital floor**, ~€10k seed; feeds 5y clock; primary fit for portable self-employment income.
- **Czechia — Živnostenský list ("Živno")** — CZK 156,500 (~€6,400) own funds; 1y visa → 2y permits; 1:1 PR clock; the regional workhorse.
- **Finland — Entrepreneur (sole-trader toiminimi)** — ELY Centre regulatory review (no Business Finland endorsement); income floor €1,030–1,210/mo net.
- **France — Profession Libérale (Talent)** — regulated profession + ordre authorization; zero capital.
- **France — Création d'Entreprise (Talent)** — €30k + Master's/5y experience; viability attestation via demarches-simplifiees.fr (rule-based, not endorsement).
- **Belgium — Professional Card + D-visa (Flanders only)** — Flanders rule-based floor €22,838 + business plan; ~€18.6k–€30k seed; 2y probation → 5y renewals. Brussels and Wallonia variants are case-by-case discretionary and excluded — see Excluded section.
- **Poland — Karta Pobytu Business activity (JDG)** — US citizens have day-one treaty privilege; renewal threshold ~12× avg salary (~€22–25k) or 2+ Polish FT hires; 3y permits.
- **Romania — D/AE PFA** — ~€9.8k subsistence proof; sole-trader workhorse.

## Digital nomad (PR-feeding)

- **Portugal — D8** — €3,680/mo (4× min wage) via residency-visa variant (not the 1y temporary-stay variant); confirmed PR-feeding.
- **Italy — Digital Nomad Visa** (launched 4 Apr 2024) — ~€28k/yr; PR-feeding.
- **Greece — DNV** — €3,500/mo from non-Greek clients; permits active remote work; feeds 7y clock at ≥183 days/yr.
- **Romania — Digital Nomad Visa** — ≥3× avg gross (~€3,700–3,950/mo); 6mo renewals; **3-yr lifetime cap then must switch purpose**.
- **Czechia — Digital Nomad Program (IT/STEM only)** — STEM degree or 3y IT experience + 1.5× avg salary (~CZK 69k/mo); 1y visa + 2y permit (3y cap); launched Jul 2024.

## Long-term study (PhD)

- **France — Étudiant** — French Master's collapses citizenship clock to **2y under Article 21-18** (fastest legal path to an EU passport in this dataset for those with portable funds for 2y of French residence).
- **Czechia / Slovakia / Belgium / Luxembourg / Romania** — study time counts at **half-rate** toward PR clocks; useful as bridge before pivoting to self-employment.
- **Sweden — PhD residence permit** — feeds PR (3y under 11 Jun 2026 bill); Master's explicitly excluded.
- **Belgium Flanders — PhD years count as 2y work-experience** for the 5y CBN track.

## Excluded by scope

- **High-capital (>€500k one-time)**: Greece Golden Visa €800k Attica/Thessaloniki/Mykonos/Santorini tier; Greece pure-financial €500k routes; Hungary Guest Investor €1M university-trust donation; Italy Investor €1M philanthropy / €2M strategic bonds; Luxembourg Investor Track 3 (€3M / €20M); Romania D/AS €1M.
- **Employer-sponsored worker tracks**: EU Blue Card (every country), single-permit / posted-worker / ICT, route-specific Skilled Worker permits.
- **Endorsement / distinguished-talent**: Portugal StartUP Visa (IAPMEI accreditation), Denmark Start-up Denmark (expert panel), Finland Startup Permit (Business Finland), France Talent — Innovative Project / Distinguished Talent, Ireland STEP (Enterprise Ireland), Belgium Parliament Art. 19 CBN naturalization.
- **Non-PR-feeding lifestyle**: Cyprus Digital Nomad Visa (1y, 3y lifetime cap, no PR transition); Hungary White Card (explicitly not PR-feeding); Schengen Type C tourist permits; au pair / seasonal.
- **Closed CBI (historical reference)**: Malta IIP/MEIN (closed Apr 2025 post-CJEU), Cyprus IIP (closed Nov 2020), Bulgaria CBI (closed Mar 2022).

## Excluded — individually-discretionary paths (country survives via rule-based alternative)

- **Italy — Lavoro Autonomo** — Decreto Flussi quota-gated with annual click-days; effectively lottery-tier. **IT survives via Investor Visa, Elective Residency, Digital Nomad Visa.**
- **Luxembourg — Independent Worker / Self-Employed** — business plan + national-interest test is fully discretionary. **LU survives via Private Reasons (€2,704/mo) and Investor Track 1/2 (€500k).**
- **Germany — Selbständige Tätigkeit §21 Abs. 1** — "economic interest" review is discretionary. **DE survives via Freiberufler §21 Abs. 5 for liberal professions.**
- **Germany — §7 Abs. 1 S. 3 financially-independent permit** — fully discretionary catch-all, no statutory minimum. **DE survives via Freiberufler.**
- **Poland — Karta Pobytu Other Circumstances (Inne okoliczności)** — voivode-discretionary, no statutory income threshold; refusals common. **PL survives via JDG (US-Poland treaty day-one privilege, rule-based).**
- **Belgium — Professional Card (Brussels / Wallonia variants)** — case-by-case discretionary in both Brussels-Capital and Wallonia. **BE survives via Flanders (€22,838 rule-based floor + business plan).**

## Countries dropped (no viable residency on-ramp)

- **Denmark** — self-employment permit closed 1 Jan 2015; no retirement / passive income / digital nomad visa; only Start-up Denmark (endorsement-gated). Drops out of the citizenship-applicable shortlist.
- **Ireland** — Stamp 0 (independent means €50k/person + €100k lump sum) and Stamp 2 (study) are **explicitly non-reckonable** for naturalization and do not progress to Stamp 5. Every reckonable stamp requires employer sponsorship, Enterprise Ireland endorsement, Irish-citizen family, or the closed €1M IIP. Drops out.
- **Sweden** — Egenföretagare self-employment permit is the only on-ramp under the filter, but Migrationsverket approved only **~107 applications in 2024** (down 33% from 2022) — closer to lottery odds than a planning route. The PhD residence permit remains under the long-term-study cluster but requires committing to a full-time doctoral program (Master's explicitly excluded), which doesn't fit a portable-income working-parent profile. Drops out of the short-list survivor set.
- **Slovakia** — živnostník (sole trader / SZČO) is the only on-ramp under the filter, but a **700-permit/yr national cap from 1 Jul 2025** (down from ~10,256 in 2024 — a 93% reduction) plus mandatory business plan + Ministry of Economy opinion makes this lottery-tier for portable-income applicants. No rule-based alternative survives. Drops out.

## Notes

- **Fastest passport via residency**: **France 2y if French Master's** (Art. 21-18 reduced decree) → Cyprus fast-track ~4.7y if B1 Greek (highly-skilled §111A) → Belgium ~5.5y (Art. 12bis 5y declaration) → Luxembourg ~5.7y → Germany 5–6y (post-Jun 2024 reform).
- **Cheapest passport via residency** (€ deployed): Portugal D7 (~€11k/yr income floor, lowest in the EU) → Cyprus Cat F (€9,568/yr) → France Visitor (~€22k/yr) → Germany Freiberufler (~€10k seed) → Czechia Živno (~€6,400 own funds).
- **Best stay-flexibility** (low physical-presence): Greece Golden Visa (0 days to maintain permit), Hungary Guest Investor (no presence requirement), Portugal Golden Visa (7d/yr). All require physical residence to *feed* the citizenship clock — flexibility is about *permit maintenance*, not citizenship acceleration.
