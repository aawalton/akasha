## What feeds back in

Safety level is not only a cost. It is also an output:

> Also, my safety level goes up when my pipeline is flowing smoothly.

And so are some of the six values:

> There are other things that feed back in. Health, how my relationship with me Wife is doing, how much I'm aware of world events (more is worse).

These are not inputs from outside. They sit in the second of my [two channels](001-two-channels.book-chapter.md) — things I improve — and they feed back into my capacity while they do it:

> They are still in the second category, and basically everything in the second category has similar positive feedback mechanics, these are just some of the most prominent. The key is that to increase my benefit from those categories requires changes, generally code changes, because I need to iterate on the alan-harness to build in the digital prosthetics that compensate for the parts of my brain that don't work normally (total aphantasia, no experiential memory, no automaticity or habit-formation, for example)

> The limits on infra are difficult to move, the hard limits require hardware changes, which is out of budget on the money dimension.

---

*Recorder's reading, marked.* Two of the seven are worth separating from the rest, because they behave differently.

Safety level is spent, and is also produced by the system it is spent on — a smoothly flowing pipeline raises it, and it falls back into stress capacity when it drops. That makes it a loop rather than a budget, and it was the resource actually binding on the day: it had dropped to 2, and he attributed the lower attention and energy to it. He named the same shape for health and his marriage, and said it holds for "basically everything in the second category," so the loop is the general case rather than a peculiarity of safety level.

This is worth stating against a reading these pages could otherwise invite. A list of seven resources looks like a budget, and a budget implies the six values compete with the machine for a fixed pool. His account is not that. Every gain in the second category is delivered by a code change, and those gains then return capacity to spend on the next one — so the two channels are a supply chain rather than a ranking, and the resources circulate rather than drain.

Money is the only one he described as having a floor he cannot move by working harder. Infra's hard limits need hardware, and hardware is out of budget — so infra is the one foundational layer whose constraint is not addressable from inside the system at all.
