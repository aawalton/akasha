
# Identity collapse

> The identity collapse — the firing took down at least 80% of Alan's identities at once (twelve named); the social topology (company co-founded with his brother, staffed by all his remaining friends) that made one event total; and the relational aftermath — the friend lost earlier, the surviving friend whose conversations cost ~2 days of bedrest each, and the live state of the brother relationship.

[autism-diagnostic-arc.md → the breaking](autism-diagnostic-arc.book-chapter.md#the-breaking) records the mechanics of the firing — medical-retirement disclosure, fired one month later, catastrophic failure of daily living. [employment-cessation.md](employment-cessation.book-chapter.md) records the same event as the implementation date of the 80%-cost cut. This note is the third dimension: what the event did to identity, and what the wreckage left behind in his relationships.

## The scale — at least 80%

In Alan's words: *"A bunch of identities collapsed all at once, at least 80% of my total."* The list, in the order he gave it: **Entrepreneur, Founder, Executive, Software Engineer, Professional, Provider, Colleague, Brother, Friend, Successful, Well-off, Safe.**

Twelve identities, one event. The first seven are work-shaped — a firing reaching them is legible. The last three are state-of-life identities that fell when the income and the standing did. Brother and Friend sit in the middle of the list, and a firing should not have been able to touch them. It touched them because of the topology below.

## The social topology — why one event was total

*"I started the company with my brother and all of my remaining friends (other than my spouse) eventually worked there."*

The org chart and his entire relational world were the same document. Every close tie except Jen routed through the company — co-founder brother, friends as colleagues. So there was no version of losing the job that wasn't losing the people: the firing severed the professional identities and the relational ones in the same stroke. That is why the collapse reached 80% instead of stopping at the work cluster, and why Brother and Friend are on the list.

The [self-worth adapter](self-worth-adapter.book-chapter.md) was compiled inside this collapse — built "as I was finding my way through full identity collapse after being fired."

### The firing as random social punishment

The firing is also the adult proof of the [only-perfection-is-safe](perfection-is-safe.book-chapter.md) belief — the keystone's evidence that the danger is current, not a fossil. In Alan's words: *"I was fired from my last job 18 months ago despite good intent and that was the most painful punishment I ever experienced, worse than any physical beating. That broke my sense of self."*

It is exactly the shape the belief is calibrated for: a [random social punishment](perfection-is-safe.book-chapter.md#the-keystone--the-punishment-is-random-and-social) landing despite good intent, with no clean outcome to forecast it. Other people are the one punisher self-checking can't make safe, and here that punisher took the self down. The collapse and the perfectionism share one root — the firing is where the belief "I've been punished despite good intent... only perfection is safe" got its heaviest confirmation.

## The earlier rupture

The friend losses did not start with the firing: *"I'd already lost one of my two closest friends a few years earlier when he was fired by my brother while I was on vacation in Europe, and my friend blamed me for it."*

Same topology, earlier casualty: when relationships live inside an org chart, the org chart's decisions — made by his brother, in his absence — cost him a closest friend and left him holding blame for an act he didn't commit.

## The surviving friend — exposure at ~2 days of bedrest per conversation

*"My other closest friend is still on reasonably good terms, but every time I talk to him is still exposure therapy and I get a trauma response. Last two conversations averaged 2 days of bedrest afterward to recover."*

Numerical anchor: **~2 days of bedrest per conversation.** The mechanism is the [trauma-encoding](trauma-encoding.book-chapter.md) account exactly: with no emotional or sensory recall, there is no self-exposure between encounters, so only an external re-encounter does extinction work. These conversations *are* the extinction pathway — and the price of each rep is two days. On the [connection-debt](connection-debt.book-chapter.md) ledger, a tie that is "on reasonably good terms" still bills as a trauma exposure, not as a paying interaction.

## The brother — live state

His brother is Nick. The current charge, from the question alone: *"I had a stress response plus intense sadness and grief to the point of almost tears (I almost never cry) just from the question."*

Last interaction: Thanksgiving 2025, which Alan and Jen hosted (*"we host most extended family gatherings, since we have the space for it"*). Nick attended with his girlfriend; Alan *"was able to be polite, though I definitely had trouble regulating for some of the night."*

Next interaction: the morning of 2026-06-13 — a family breakfast at Alan's home, Nick in town.

### The preparation plan — dosing applied live

*"We're hosting, which means I can retreat to my office if I need to. I'm still sick, which gives me an easy excuse to not stick around long without hurting feelings. My base plan is to just minimize how much time I spend around people, come when its time to eat, and otherwise stay isolated."*

And the dosing judgment: *"If I was healthier and my safety level was higher / cost multiplier lower, I might consider more active or intentional interaction for additional exposure therapy, but given where I am, I think minimal is the right call."*

This is the resource model applied live: exposure dose set by current [Safety level](safety.book-chapter.md#scale-0-5-with-half-steps) and [cost multiplier](stress-capacity-formula.book-chapter.md), the same reconditioning logic as [connection-dosing](connection-dosing.book-chapter.md) — enough load to rebuild, not so much that it re-injures, and at a low-Safety, sick baseline the affordable dose is minimal. Hosting supplies the controls: a retreat room, a no-fault exit excuse, and timing (appear to eat, otherwise isolated).
