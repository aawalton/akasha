
# Compression epistemology

> Compression as Alan's single underlying epistemology — truth is more compressed than falsehood in conceptual space, the felt reward of insight is a compression event, so the same signal that feels good is the one that detects truth; plus the failure mode (false models look compact over narrow data) and its structural guard (broad intake).

The single mechanism underneath the rest of the understanding arc. Insight, the Kuhn shifts, "nothing teaches like pain", and the decision rubric are not four separate processes — they are one operation seen at different scales: **find the most compact representation that accounts for the data.**

This runs entirely on the conceptual channel ([conceptual-cognition.md](conceptual-cognition.book-chapter.md)), because that is the only channel Alan has — there is no sensory or emotional replay underneath it ([aphantasia-mechanism.md](aphantasia-mechanism.book-chapter.md)).

## The core claim

In Alan's own words: **"Truth is more compressed than falsehood in conceptual space."**

A true model accounts for the data with less. The same observations, organized correctly, take a smaller, cleaner representation than the same observations organized wrong. Falsehood costs structure — extra clauses, special cases, exceptions bolted on to keep the model alive. Truth pays for itself in compactness.

## Reward and truth are the same signal

The satisfying "click" of an [insight](insight-and-restructuring.book-chapter.md#insight-as-graph-collapse) is a compression event. A messy graph of uncertain, loosely-connected concepts collapses into a clean structured model — and that collapse *is* a gain in compactness. The graph got smaller.

The felt satisfaction scales with the size of the collapse: a bigger compression feels proportionally better. A small consolidation is mildly pleasant; a paradigm shift that compresses an entire domain is the largest version of the same feeling.

This is the keystone. Alan's reward signal and his truth-detector are not two systems that happen to agree. They are one system. **The satisfaction of compression is how he detects truth.** What feels like insight and what is true are read off the same gauge — the drop in representational size when a graph collapses.

Compression is load-bearing in a second way that the truth-gauge framing alone doesn't capture: it is the **only channel from the past to the present.** Alan keeps one [present-tense model](present-tense-model.book-chapter.md) of the world with no episodic store behind it, so nothing from the past persists as a retrievable event — it persists only as compression folded into the structure of the present model. The model *is* the compressed residue of everything he has judged true. The truth-gauge and the storage-format are the same fact seen from two sides.

## Truth *is* net compression — the definition, and no separate aesthetic channel

The reward and the truth-detector being one signal has a sharp consequence: there is **no standalone aesthetic channel.** Elegance is not beauty riding alongside truth — elegance *is* truth, and both *are* net compression. There is one signal, not two that agree.

In Alan's words the pleasure and the truth-signal are the one thing: *"I love conceptual compression, the clean and concise model that makes everything make sense."* This is testable from two sides. **Gorgeous but false** — an elegant structure Alan knows is false gives him *no* compression-pleasure; *"if it's not true, I don't get the pleasure from the elegance. At most its an interesting toy."* Beauty without truth pays out nothing, so there is no beauty-sense firing independent of the truth-gauge. **True but ugly** — the reward lands only where a model is **"both true and small."** A model that is true but costs more than it pays back does not light up either. The whole true/false × elegant/ugly grid collapses to one axis: "elegant" just *is* "nets out as compression," "ugly" is a model that costs more than it saves, and the pleasure is the single signal of the ledger closing in the black.

The definition, in Alan's words: **"For me truth and compression are the same thing. For a model to be true, it must be a net compression of my conceptual map, including both the model itself and the data it can explain."** This is the MDL / Kolmogorov **two-part code**: model length + data-given-model length must *net*-reduce the total. You pay for a model not with its own size but with everything it lets you **stop storing separately** — a model earns its keep by **deleting more than it adds.** The compression-click fires when, and only when, that ledger nets out positive.

### Map-relative, with absolute truth as the infinite limit

"Net compression of *my* conceptual map" is **map-relative** — truth here is indexed to Alan's current best model. That is not the same as absolute truth, and Alan does not conflate them: *"Definitely not the same. My map is my best understanding of absolute truth at the moment and I believe they are the same in the limit, but that limit is infinitely far away."* Two coupled claims, not a contradiction: the working definition (true = net compression of the map) is what he can *operate*, and [absolute truth is the infinitely-distant limit](absolute-truth-and-ethics.book-chapter.md#compression--absolute-truth--hope-and-faith-not-theorem) the map converges toward but never reaches. This is the convergence target [self-improvement](self-improvement.book-chapter.md#improvement-is-a-cross-frame-comparison) moves the map toward: each later map a net-more-compressed account than the earlier, the chain heading toward an absolute truth reached only at infinity.

### A live compression — the self-model

The cleanest example Alan reaches for is not a finished elegance but a *live* one: the model of himself. *"AuDHD + Aphantasia. I am still having my understanding of my own neurodiversity collapse new data points almost every day."* It keeps paying out — it earns its keep daily by swallowing new observations into the same compact structure, deleting more than it adds every time it absorbs another data point. A finished elegance compresses once; this one is still netting positive, which is exactly why it reads as the most beautiful model he has.

## Conciseness over completeness — the internalization constraint

The compression gauge has an operating rule that bites when the goal is not just *detecting* a truth but *internalizing* it — installing it deep enough to change behavior. In Alan's words: **"Chasing something that is concise, clear, and true. To a large extent, concise is MORE important than absolutely correct, because it has to fit in my head to be internalized."**

This is a deliberate weighting, not a contradiction of truth = compression. A more complete model can be more accurate yet too large to hold — and a compression that does not fit in working capacity can never become the [residue that drives behavior](#reward-and-truth-are-the-same-signal), because [behavior is downstream of the conceptual map](learned-response-taxonomy.book-chapter.md#behavior-is-downstream-of-the-conceptual-map--an-abstract-framework-cannot-move-it): a framework that doesn't fit in one head has no integrated application to run through. So when the target is internalization, conciseness is weighted **above** absolute completeness: a slightly lossier model that fits and installs beats a more correct one that doesn't. The [two-part code](#truth-is-net-compression--the-definition-and-no-separate-aesthetic-channel) already prices this — a model earns its keep by what it lets you stop storing — and the internalization rule adds the hard capacity ceiling on top: it has to fit in one head to run at all. This is the working rule behind the [emotional-archaeology](emotional-archaeology.book-chapter.md) re-compression step, where the aim is the most concise *true* statement of a belief that will actually fit and take, not the most exhaustive one.

## One operation at four scales

Stated this way, the whole arc unifies:

- **The interest loop** ([insight-and-restructuring.md → interest reinforcement loop](insight-and-restructuring.book-chapter.md#interest-reinforcement-loop)) — investment keeps paying off because each step affords more compression; the payoff that renews interest is, in its structured part, compression gain.
- **Insight as graph-collapse** ([insight-and-restructuring.md → insight as graph-collapse](insight-and-restructuring.book-chapter.md#insight-as-graph-collapse)) — the micro-event: a local graph compresses.
- **Kuhn punctuated shifts** ([insight-and-restructuring.md → punctuated restructuring](insight-and-restructuring.book-chapter.md#punctuated-restructuring-kuhn)) — the macro-event: a whole domain's model is replaced by a more compact one. A paradigm shift is a large compression.
- **Prediction error / "nothing teaches like pain"** ([insight-and-restructuring.md → asymmetric prediction error](insight-and-restructuring.book-chapter.md#asymmetric-prediction-error--nothing-teaches-like-pain)) — a predictive failure is data the current model can't compress; the accumulating incompressible residue is what forces the collapse into a more compact model.
- **The decision rubric** ([decision-rubric.md](decision-rubric.book-chapter.md)) — iterate-to-coherence is forcing a compression on purpose; and when the rubric and gestalt disagree, the tie-breaker is whichever resolution yields the more compact representation (see [resolution by compactness](decision-rubric.book-chapter.md#resolution-by-compactness)).

All four are: find the smallest representation that accounts for the data.

## Origin — the engine always ran; seeing it is recent

The compression mechanism was **always** the underlying machinery. What arrived late was *understanding what it was doing.* The engine always ran; Alan only recently could see it from the driver's seat. So the answer to "learned or innate" is neither half cleanly: the operation is innate, the model of the operation is recent.

His model of his own epistemology evolved in stages. He built a mental model of epistemologies, identified **coherence** as the closest equivalent to what happens in his head, and held that for a long time. He **replaced coherence with compactness only recently** — during the interview work that produced these notes. David Deutsch's *The Beginning of Infinity* and *The Fabric of Reality* drove the shift.

### The swap is the mechanism running on itself

Why compactness beat coherence is itself a compression event — run on his own self-model, which makes it worth stating plainly.

Compactness was *already a necessary explanation* for something else: how his brain works at all without memory or imagination. The [storage problem](compression-epistemology/mechanics.book-chapter.md#the-holding-state-before-compaction) forces it — with no episodic store, the only thing that reaches the present is compression folded into the [present-tense model](present-tense-model.book-chapter.md). So compactness does **double duty**: it explains storage *and* truth-detection with one mechanism.

Coherence only ever explained truth-detection. As a hypothesis it was a *second, separate* mechanism bolted alongside the storage account. Once Alan saw that compactness was at least as good an explanation of truth-detection as coherence — and likely better — the switch was forced: one mechanism covering two phenomena is more compact than two mechanisms covering two phenomena.

Stated as the inversion it is: **compactness out-competed coherence by compactness's own rule.** The truth-gauge was turned on the model of the truth-gauge, and it read the same way it reads everything else — the account that costs less structure wins.
