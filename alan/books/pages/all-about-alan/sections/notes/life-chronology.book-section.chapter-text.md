
# Life chronology

> Life chronology — the self-authored "Chapters of My Life" narrative scaffold. Three life-eras (Earth/Childhood, Fire/Working Adult, Water/Self-Directed Thriving), each with named chapters and year ranges. Most chapters are not yet filled in; this records the frame and the concrete childhood facts the early chapters carry.

Alan keeps a self-authored life narrative titled **"Chapters of My Life"** — written to reflect on the past in order to better understand the narrative of his existence, as he considers goals for the next chapter. This note records the scaffold and the concrete facts the early chapters carry. It is a **chronological** subject, distinct from [personal-context.md](personal-context.book-chapter.md) (which holds snapshot facts about who the framework is written for, not a life narrative) and from the [slow-build strategy's sequential life arcs](slow-build-strategy.book-chapter.md#sequential-life-arcs) (which read the same span as a strategy, not a timeline).

**Most chapters are an empty scaffold** — headers with year ranges and no body yet. Only the childhood chapters carry content so far. The frame below is recorded verbatim where the era labels and chapter titles carry Alan's framing; the facts under each chapter are only what the narrative actually contains.

## The three-era frame

The narrative divides into three twenty-year life-eras, each mapped to a life-stage and an element:

- **Earth — Childhood (Age 0–20).**
- **Fire — Working Adult (Age 20–40).**
- **Water — Self-Directed Thriving (Age 40–60).**

## Earth — Childhood (0–20)

- **Prologue: Pre-History (1986–1989).** Before memory. In his words: *"My earliest memories don't arrive until the age of four, so everything before that is second hand. I was born, I learned, and I grew."* The age-four memory onset is a biographical anchor for the [memory-onset / dossier model](present-tense-model.book-chapter.md#what-remembering-actually-is) — the earliest entry on a map that holds notes about events, never the events themselves. Implies birth around 1986.
- **Chapter 1: Preschool (1990–1991).** Years in southern Provo, "dim and distant" yet formative. He learned **math up through algebra** and had his **first experiences with computers**, "foreshadowing much of the focus of my later life." This is the earliest instance of the [conceptual precocity](conceptual-cognition.book-chapter.md) the later cognition runs on.
- **Chapter 2: Elementary School (1992–1997).** Moved to northern Provo at age six; started at Rock Canyon Elementary. A year too young for first grade, so given standardized readiness tests — *"I scored at fourth grade or above in every category, and would have scored higher except that I only knew my times tables up to four."* Socially: *"I expanded socially, until I wanted to invite 20 friends to my 10th birthday. I met Rusty, Micah, David, and Kerry. I trusted easily, connected easily, and felt well liked."* This is the [childhood-connection inversion](connection.book-chapter.md#childhood-connection-was-easy--an-early-datum) against the current adult arc. Then he discovered social pressure: in fourth grade *"I came home and told my mom that I didn't want to learn any more math until my classmates caught up. In response, she registered me as a homeschool student"* — he kept attending classes during the school year but also studied through summers. *"I learned to love reading and read voraciously"* (the childhood root of the [broad-intake reading practice](broad-learning.book-chapter.md#read-authors-not-books--depth-per-source)).
- **Chapter 3: Middle School (1998–1999).** Header only.
- **Interlude: Road Trip (Fall 1999).** Header only.
- **Chapter 4: High School (2000–2002).** Header only.
- **Chapter 5: College (2003–2005).** Alan started college at **sixteen**, and rates the three years that followed **the best three years of his life** — on autonomy grounds, not achievement. The load was heavy and self-imposed: 16–18 credits a semester (including several 1-credit dance classes that cost far more time than the credit implies), a part-time job, and a ~hour walk each way to school once or twice a day. He kept a planner for assignments and calendar scheduling — the earliest seed of the [agent harness](agent-harness.book-chapter.md#origin-chronology--the-self-instrumentation-instinct-predates-the-gtd-bedrock). He also deliberately sought out the hardest courses via a stranger-sourced sensing-network. The substantive psychological treatment — autonomy as the driver, the difficulty as self-chosen, and the sensing-network method — lives in [self-chosen-difficulty.md](self-chosen-difficulty.book-chapter.md). This is also the era when social connection was [effortful but affordable](self-chosen-difficulty.book-chapter.md#social-connection-was-affordable-then--a-full-account-in-a-cheap-world) — high capacity + high Safety → low cost multiplier, the inverse of the current adult state — held up by the [two pillars](safety/progression.book-chapter.md#the-two-pillars--what-changed-between-then-and-now) (autonomy + intact nervous system) that the mission years then removed.
- **Interlude: Nauvoo (Fall 2005).** Header only.

## Fire — Working Adult (20–40)

All chapters are headers only:

- **Chapter 6: Mission (2006–2007).** LDS mission in Novosibirsk, Russia, age ~19–20. Two load-bearing events sit in this window. (1) The Russia bodily-danger events (knifepoint, subway assault, street chase in the first six weeks) that trained the [criticism-and-ostracism](criticism-and-ostracism.book-chapter.md) alarm — see [personal-context.md → Russia, age ~20](personal-context.book-chapter.md#formative-experiences--russia-age-20). (2) The onset of nervous-system damage: his body flipped to shaking by default, he read the tremor as demonic opposition, and overrode it across the nine-month mission — 3 months at the MTC (where the drop in autonomy was the hard part) then 6 brutal field months (tracting + street contacting, 100 people a day, >10,000 rejections) — until his body broke and he could not continue. This is the peak-capacity calibration anchor for the [18-year compound decline](autism-burnout.book-chapter.md) and the moment both Safety pillars fell (structure replaced self-direction; the substrate broke). Full narration in [mission-break.md](mission-break.book-chapter.md). Distinct from the later [2024 break](autism-diagnostic-arc.book-chapter.md#the-breaking).
- **Chapter 7: Marriage (2008–2009).** Header only.
- **Chapter 8: Epic (2010–2013).** Header only.
- **Chapter 9: Degreed (2014–2017).** Header only.
- **Chapter 10: VidAngel (2018–2019).** Header only.
- **Chapter 11: Latitude (2020–2024).** Header only.
- **Interlude: Isolation (2025).** Header only.
