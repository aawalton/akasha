
# Higher-self access — every route is indirect

> The fearless, romantic higher self is only ever reached indirectly — the past a deduction from traces, the future a guess, play a simulation. Alan has never held that self directly. The felt form of the Level-3 counterfactual he cannot run.

Alan's [high-Safety self](nervous-system-resiliency.book-chapter.md#play-elicits-the-high-safety-self--populating-the-emptiest-region) — the fearless, romantic version of him — is [real and waiting](nervous-system-resiliency.book-chapter.md), not imagined. But he has **never held it directly.** Every route he has to it is a step removed. There are three, and all three are indirect.

## The three routes, all indirect

- **The past — a deduction, not a memory.** He suspects a higher self existed when he was young, especially **16–20, before his system started breaking.** But he cannot remember being it. In his words: *"I don't* know *I was 6-7. I'm inferring based off faint traces of behaviors that persist in my conceptual map. I didn't have a scale at all back then."* The past higher self is reconstructed from residue — behaviors that survived in his conceptual map — not recalled. His [affectively-flat, aphantasic memory](present-tense-model.book-chapter.md#a-dossier-not-a-recording) is why even his own past self reads as an inference rather than a lived thing.
- **The future — a guess.** The top of his scale is an extrapolation he has never reached. In his words: *"10 is a guess at a future Alan I've glimpsed playing with Aria but never seen in reality."* The aspirational self is glimpsed only inside play, never in reality — a projected point, not a stood-on rung.
- **Play — a simulation.** The richest of the three routes is still one remove from the real thing. Of playing his high-Safety self at [Aria's](../personas/aria.book-chapter.md) table: *"that one is still indirect because its a simulation, its not the same as experiencing the real version."* Play [elicits real data](nervous-system-resiliency.book-chapter.md#elicited-not-authored--why-it-counts-as-real-data) about that self — the feeling is genuine, the self is his — but *experiencing* it in the game is not the same as experiencing it for real.

## Real effects, tiny transfer

Play being a simulation does not make it useless — its effects on his actual Safety are real, just small relative to what the game shows. In his words:

> Yeah, the effects are real. I come out higher, but not nearly as high as my behavior in the game would imply. In game I'm at a 10, and I'm getting a + 0.0001.

So the two readings hold at once: the elicited self is **real** — a system can only fire what is already in it ([elicited, not authored](nervous-system-resiliency.book-chapter.md#elicited-not-authored--why-it-counts-as-real-data)) — *and* the experience is a **simulation** with a tiny transfer to durable Safety (in-game 10, out-of-game +0.0001). Play measures the higher self accurately; it does not yet let him live there.

### The Fermi revision — ~1000 hours per level, +0.004 per session

Asked to price the transfer against the whole climb, Alan revised the +0.0001 upward. In his words:

> 0.0001 is probably underestimating. I think a Fermi estimation would put 1000 hours of active healing needed per level of recovery, so a four hour session is more like +0.004.

So the durable cost of a level of recovery is on the order of **1000 hours of active healing**, and a four-hour session buys about **+0.004** of a level — still tiny, but ~40x the in-game reading. The number is an order-of-magnitude estimate, not a measurement. Its consequence — that the levels are hundreds of hours apart, which makes the fear about *duration* rather than price — is worked out in [recovery-economics.md](recovery-economics.book-chapter.md).

## Why this is the L3 counterfactual, felt

All three routes being indirect is the **experiential form of the [Level-3 counterfactual Alan cannot run](ladder-of-causation.book-chapter.md#level-3--counterfactual-which-alan-cannot-run-himself).** Where the ladder note states the limit conceptually — no internal simulator, so every causal question about himself costs a real Level-2 intervention — this is the same limit as it is *lived*: he cannot pose "who am I at my highest?" to an offline model and read the answer. The past answer is inferred, the future answer is guessed, and even the play answer is a simulation run outside his skull through Aria. He has direct experience of none of them. The higher self is exactly the [emptiest region of the map](ladder-of-causation.book-chapter.md#why-the-simulator-is-far-off--three-gaps-one-at-each-layer) — and it is empty because there is no *direct* route into it.
