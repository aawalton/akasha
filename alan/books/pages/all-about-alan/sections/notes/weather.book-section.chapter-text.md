
# Weather

> Weather — the circle-of-control / circle-of-concern throttle on the awareness-of-conflict engine. A conflict reclassified as outside Alan's control stops generating dissatisfaction entirely; the reclassification has to be a genuine map update, and what's left over is dislike, not drive.

Alan's intellectual drive runs on [awareness of an unresolved conflict](insight-and-restructuring.book-chapter.md#the-engine-is-awareness-of-conflict), and because he [doesn't habituate](non-habituation.book-chapter.md), a known conflict keeps demanding resolution at full intensity forever. **Weather** is the one throttle on that engine: a conceptual map that classifies problems by whether he can change them, so that the unchangeable ones stop generating any pull.

## The classification

Alan maintains a conceptual map that sorts problems he's aware of into categories. The load-bearing category is **weather**: a problem that is real, that he may actively dislike, but that he cannot change. He can't change the weather, so he works around it.

The mechanism is circle-of-control / circle-of-concern. Because a weather problem sits outside his circle of control, it also sits outside his circle of concern — and only problems inside the circle of concern generate **dissatisfaction**, the active discomfort that drives him to worry at a conflict until it resolves. A problem classified as weather generates *no* dissatisfaction. The engine simply doesn't fire on it.

## The reclassification is not by fiat

The crucial precision: Alan cannot *decide* something is weather to make the dissatisfaction stop. Willing it doesn't work. The dissatisfaction only switches off if his conceptual map **genuinely updates** to place the conflict outside his control — and a map update is the same offline, evidence-driven [restructuring](insight-and-restructuring.book-chapter.md#solidifying-is-offline-and-often-happens-in-sleep) every other belief change runs through, not an act of will. He can't shortcut it by deciding the outcome.

When the map does update, the relief is total. The dissatisfaction goes away **entirely** — not damped, not managed, gone. This is what makes weather a throttle rather than a coping tactic: it doesn't suppress the drive, it removes the input the drive reads.

## Dislike is not dissatisfaction

What can remain after a conflict becomes weather is **dislike** — and dislike is categorically different from dissatisfaction:

- **Dissatisfaction** is an active drive. It pushes. It's the engine demanding the conflict be fixed, and it runs at full, non-decaying intensity as long as the conflict is inside the circle of concern.
- **Dislike** is a flat valence that sits still. It registers that something is bad without generating any pull to change it. It costs nothing to carry.

Drawing this line is deliberate, the same hard-won kind of [alexithymia](alexithymia.book-chapter.md) discrimination as naming the [hope on waking from a breakthrough](insight-and-restructuring.book-chapter.md#the-emotional-arc--discouragement-to-hope). Two words that a looser self-report would collapse into one feeling are, for Alan, two distinct states with opposite operational consequences: one drives, one doesn't.

## Concrete example — relativity and quantum mechanics

The [GR/QM inconsistency](compression-epistemology/mechanics.book-chapter.md#conflicts-as-incompatible-partial-compressions) is weather for Alan. It is a real conflict — two true-enough models that can't both be true — with exactly the structure of the conflicts that *do* drive him. The difference is that resolving it is outside his control. So his map files it as weather, it generates no dissatisfaction, and he can name the incompatibility precisely without any pull to spend himself resolving it. He may dislike that physics is inconsistent; he is not driven by it.

## Relation to non-discretionary reclassification

Weather looks adjacent to [non-discretionary reclassification](non-discretionary-reclassification.book-chapter.md), and both are upstream-of-the-system framing moves, but they throttle different systems:

- **Non-discretionary reclassification** decides what the *cost-vs-capacity math* is allowed to see. It moves an obligation from "invisible to the budget" into reach of the cost math so it can be cut.
- **Weather** decides what the *awareness-of-conflict engine* generates dissatisfaction over. It moves a conflict from inside the circle of concern to outside it, switching off the drive to resolve it.

Distinct systems, same shape: a classification gate sitting above a downstream mechanism, where flipping the classification changes what the mechanism does without touching the mechanism itself.

## The same map at the purpose root

On 2026-06-11 the classification got applied one level up — to purpose itself. "Improving my life" as a core purpose depended on factors outside the circle of control and generated consistent dissonance; "improving myself" is "entirely within my control to the extent that it is possible at all." Relocating the purpose inside the circle of control removed the dissonance source the same way a weather reclassification removes dissatisfaction: structurally, by removing the input, not by managing it.
