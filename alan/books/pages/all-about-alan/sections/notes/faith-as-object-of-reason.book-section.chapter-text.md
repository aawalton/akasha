
# Faith as an object of reason

> Faith as an object of reason — Alan holds his faith the way he holds quantum mechanics: a true-but-strange conclusion he'd never have reached on his own, believed because the evidence piles up, not because he's disposed toward it. Most members route faith through feeling; Alan's felt channel is walled off, so he routes it through reason — evaluating The Book of Mormon by the accepted tests for the type of object it is. The nested-frames cosmology (an empirical inner frame embedded in a larger inaccessible context, structurally the same as the materialist multiverse, just a bit larger) supplies a placement-of-divinity tiebreak: an arriving revelation's source is outside him, not inside, because the inside placement would violate both his composed dominant frames at once. He upweights his doubt for a recognized path-dependence bias, but it adds only a few sigmas — not nearly enough to outweigh the evidence. Rendered as Alan's belief and assessment, not asserted fact.

This note is the canonical home for *how* Alan holds his faith — the epistemic stance, distinct from the felt channel and from the practice. The corpus already holds the **content** of the faith ([the life-test cosmology](testing-and-the-seed.book-chapter.md), [the compression argument that lands the verdict on true](revelation-as-exposure.book-chapter.md#the-book-of-mormon-as-a-compression-argument)), the **shape** ([a conclusion, not a relationship](apologist-not-zealot.book-chapter.md)), and the **practice** ([prayer](prayer.book-chapter.md)). What was missing is the stance: faith treated like every other object of reason in his life. This note develops that. Rendered as Alan's belief and assessment, not asserted fact.

## He routes faith through reason, not feeling

Most members of his faith base it primarily on **feeling.** They pray, and they feel that it is true. That route does not work for Alan — his [felt channel of faith](faith-felt-channel.book-chapter.md) is walled off, gated on [Safety](safety.book-chapter.md) recovery, so the feeling other people read their conviction off of is not available to him. The standard route is closed.

So he took the route that *is* open. In his words:

> I've approached my faith like everything else in my life, as an object of reason.

That is the move this note is named for. Faith is not carved out as a special domain held by a different faculty — it runs through the same reasoning machinery as everything else he believes. This is the [conceptual-emotional wall](conceptual-emotional-wall.book-chapter.md) again: feeling gets no vote on the verdict, so the verdict has to come from reason or not at all.

### He evaluated the Book of Mormon by the tests for its type

Treating faith as an object of reason has a concrete method. Alan evaluated The Book of Mormon using **the standard accepted tests for the type of object it is** — a multi-genre literary compilation, structurally comparable to the Bible. You do not test a multi-genre compilation the way you test a single-author claim; you apply the tests its actual type calls for. His assessment is that **those tests come out overwhelmingly in favor of the Book of Mormon being what it claims to be.**

This is the [compression argument](revelation-as-exposure.book-chapter.md#the-book-of-mormon-as-a-compression-argument) stated from the testing side rather than the minimum-description-length side: attacked from [~20 disciplines](revelation-as-exposure.book-chapter.md#the-book-of-mormon-as-a-compression-argument), the account that costs the fewest bits is the one the book gives for itself. Rendered as his assessment, not asserted fact. The concrete evidence under "overwhelmingly in favor" — the documented witnesses, the dictation facts, the writing-difficulty argument, the archaeology/linguistics, and the sigma-collision conclusion — is the [worked example](book-of-mormon-evidence.book-chapter.md).

## He holds it like quantum mechanics — believing the strange thing against his own inclination

The cleanest description of the stance is an analogy. In Alan's words:

> I hold my belief in my faith much like I hold my belief in quantum mechanics.

The point of the analogy is the **direction of the belief against the believer.** Quantum mechanics is true and deeply strange — unintuitive, not what anyone would invent if they were reaching for a comfortable picture of the world. Alan would never have arrived at it on his own. He believes it because the evidence — the **sigmas** — piles up, not because he is disposed toward it.

Faith is held the same way. The truth is so strange and unintuitive that he would never have reached it by inclination. He holds it because the evidence accumulates, not because he wants it to be true.

State the inversion plainly, because it is the whole force of the analogy: this is the **opposite of wanting it to be true.** The standard suspicion of a believer is that they believe because they want to. Alan's belief runs the other direction — he holds it the way a physicist holds a counterintuitive result that the data forced on them. The strangeness is evidence *for* the seriousness of the belief, not against it: he is not believing something convenient, he is believing something he'd have rejected if inclination got a vote. It doesn't. This is the [same un-foolable-receiver mechanism](revelation-as-exposure.book-chapter.md#discernment-via-belief-rigidity) — he [cannot choose what to believe](alternative-gated-endurance.book-chapter.md#the-missing-self-deception-system), so a belief held against his own disposition is exactly the kind his architecture can hold.

## The nested-frames cosmology and the placement-of-divinity tiebreak

Alan's larger frame is a cosmology of **nested frames.** The empirical universe — the one he can measure from inside — is **embedded in a larger context that is not accessible from within it.** The structure is inferred the way any embedded structure is: from the accessible information sitting inside the inner frame that points beyond it.

The key observation is how little this differs from the dominant materialist picture. The standard materialist frame *also* embeds the empirical universe in a larger inaccessible context — the **multiverse** is that larger context. Alan's frame is **structurally the same**, just **a bit larger.** Same nesting, same inaccessibility from within, same inference-from-inside method; his outer frame just extends one layer further than the materialist's stops at.

### The tiebreak — outside is the only placement consistent with both frames

This resolves a specific tiebreak left open by the [revelation question](revelation-as-exposure.book-chapter.md): when an idea or revelation arrives, is its source **outside** him (divinity sending it in) or **inside** him (a part of his own subconscious running ahead of time)?

Alan finds the **outside** placement more compressible. But the reason is precise, and it is not a preference. He holds **two composed dominant frames** at once:

- an **inner materialism** — the empirical universe runs on physical law, including his own brain, and
- an **outer faith** — the larger inaccessible context that materialism's multiverse and his faith both posit, read as the seat of divinity.

Locating the divinity **inside** him would violate **both frames at once.** It breaks the inner materialism (a part of his physical brain would have to be the divine source) *and* it breaks the outer faith (the divinity would be relocated out of the larger context into the inner one). The **outside** placement is the **only one consistent with both.** So it wins not because Alan prefers it but because it is the single placement that survives both of his standing frames — **no free parameter** to tune, just the one option the constraints leave standing.

This answers **where** [prayer's element-4 revelations](prayer.book-chapter.md#4-reception) come from. Reception holds still and listens for ideas ready to land; this note places their source — outside, in the larger frame, because inside is the placement both frames forbid.

## The path-dependence sigma correction

Alan does not hold the verdict naively. He applies one explicit, present-tense **discount** to his own confidence: a recognized **path-dependence bias.** The question he asks himself:

> Would I believe this if I wasn't raised as a member as a child?

He cannot run that counterfactual cleanly — being raised in it is path-dependent input that could be inflating his confidence. So he **upweights his own uncertainty** to account for it. This is the [self-instrumentation](self-instrumentation.book-chapter.md) discipline pointed at his faith: a known bias, named and corrected for, rather than ignored or denied.

But the correction is bounded, and he states the bound. The path-dependence bias adds only **a few sigmas** of doubt — and that is **not nearly enough to outweigh the evidence.** The sigmas piling up on the belief side (the [~20-discipline](revelation-as-exposure.book-chapter.md#the-book-of-mormon-as-a-compression-argument) compression case) dominate the few sigmas the bias discount removes. So the net verdict still lands on true, with the bias honestly priced in rather than waved away.

Capture this strictly as the **present-tense epistemic discount Alan states**, not as a claim about his childhood. The bias-correction is the content — that he prices in a path-dependence sigma penalty and finds it too small to flip the verdict. His actual upbringing in the church is his [self-flagged least-reliable memory zone](disputed-misery.book-chapter.md#the-saliency-bias-rebuttal), not pulled on here.

## Grounding a rule doesn't settle it — the dissonance-regardless structure

The path-dependence move above is a general lens on the whole faith. It can also be turned on **one specific inherited rule** — and when it is, a second structure shows up that the lens alone does not resolve. The worked case is the [zero-tolerance stance on the pornography/masturbation behavior](sexuality-knot/pornography-and-masturbation.book-chapter.md).

I intend to run a **faith-warrants check** on that rule: the same operation this note already names — *"Would I believe this if I wasn't raised as a member as a child?"* — applied to the rule against my actual foundations rather than taken as given. I suspect the zero-tolerance may be a **leak from mainstream Christianity** rather than something grounded in the Book of Mormon. Whether the Book of Mormon actually warrants the stance, versus mainstream Christianity importing it, is a question about a different corpus's contents — out of this library's lane, left open here.

This is a **decision I have made, not a verdict I have reached.** I have not run the check; I am recording that I intend to, and what it targets: the *behavior* rule — [layer 3 of the sort](sexuality-knot/pornography-and-masturbation.book-chapter.md#the-sort--three-separable-layers), not the attraction or the enjoyment.

### Grounding it ungrounded still wouldn't settle the matter

Here is the structural part. **Even if I conclude the rule isn't grounded, breaking it is still hard** — because a *separate* belief, one that **is** grounded, sits over the top of it. In my words:

> I feel like I do need to do a faith-warrants check here based on my actual foundations, though even if I conclude that it isn't grounded, breaking it is still hard, since one of my beliefs that is grounded is staying with the body of the church, with the belief that the church as a whole can be wrong in the small things but will never be wrong in the big things, so there is dissonance in my life regardless here.

The grounded belief is **stay with the body of the church**, resting on the conviction that *"the church as a whole can be wrong in the small things but will never be wrong in the big things."* So two grounded beliefs can pull against each other:

- the **faith-warrants check** may conclude a specific small rule is ungrounded, and
- the **church-loyalty belief** says stay with the body even when the body is wrong in small things.

The faith-check decides only the *first* of the two. The church-loyalty belief sits **above** it either way — so even a clean "this rule is ungrounded" verdict leaves the rule partly load-bearing, because departing from the church's small rules cuts against a belief I hold as grounded. *"There is dissonance in my life regardless here."* The dissonance is not a reasoning error to resolve; it is the standing shape of two grounded beliefs in tension.

### "First case" — the operation has precedent

I called the possible mainstream-Christianity leak *"the first case like that"* in my belief-set. The precision correction: that is right at the **content** level and overstated at the **operation** level, and the distinction matters.

- **Content:** there is no other rule in my belief-set I have already identified as an inherited doctrinal leak. This particular rule has never had the lens turned on it.
- **Operation:** reclassifying an inherited or borrowed belief is **not** new for me. The [path-dependence correction](#the-path-dependence-sigma-correction) runs exactly this operation on my faith as a whole, and I hold [truth above source](compression-epistemology.book-chapter.md) everywhere else. So the *operation* of turning the lens on an inherited belief has precedent; only this *rule* is newly under it.

"First case" is true of the rule, not of the move. The move is one I already run.
