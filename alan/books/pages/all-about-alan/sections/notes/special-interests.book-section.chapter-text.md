
# Special interests

- Sustained deep interests for years — with an AuDHD-specific twist. Autism wants the same thing every day; ADHD wants something different every day. These seem incompatible; resolution: same context, novel content.
- Aphantasia layer: no emotional memory, so his relation to things (interests, places, people) is driven primarily by direct nervous-system response. That response is much slower to form, which reinforces continued investment in areas he has done most.
- Concrete examples: favorite books are the longest (#1: The Wandering Inn at 16M words). Almost exclusively plays one video game (The Elder Scrolls Online); 10+ years; 12000+ hours. These work because the context is always the same but the content is always different (new challenges, new chapters).
- The "same context, novel content" resolution is the AuDHD case of the autism × ADHD sub-mechanisms in the instinctive interest channel: autism's familiarity-driven safety + ADHD's novelty-driven dopamine, both firing at once.
