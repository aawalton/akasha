
# The funded-passage recovery model

> The funded-passage recovery model — how a scarred domain actually comes back: not exposure drills but Safety rising until the want crosses the threshold under its own power, with every wanted pass eroding the scar. Four worked recoveries (ESO, coding, AI storytelling, image generation). The scar surcharge is 2–3 levels on average on top of an activity's natural cost, and it is binary — full price at the first touch, no graded on-ramp. The scar-cost is COMPONENTS, not a lump — the screen component was paid down once, so the four recoveries clustered; hand-making sits outside all paid-down territory and presents full price. A shared art component may already be paying down through image generation — held as hope, not fact. The confirmed three-frame synthesis: the judges are conditioning not beliefs (so psychological interventions do nothing, only physiological ones move the needle), pure exposure runs on repetition the non-habituating wiring refuses, and funded passage is the one extinction path that works.

How a scarred domain actually comes back for me. Not by gritting through exposure drills — by my Safety level rising until I naturally *want* through the scar, and every wanted pass wearing it down. Four domains have already come back this way. This note is the canonical home for the model, the surcharge arithmetic, and the binary trigger.

## The model, in my words

> I think it comes down when my safety level is high enough that I naturally *want* to through the scars, and every time I do, the scars wear away. I've done that successfully in several areas already. ESO, Coding, AI Storytelling, Image Generation. Each of those were areas that at one point had scars so painful I had to quit, and now I do them almost every day. I think the others will come when I am ready.

Three moving parts, in order:

1. **Safety rises first.** Not courage, not discipline — the [Safety](safety.book-chapter.md) center climbing until the scarred activity's total price comes into range.
2. **The want crosses under its own power.** The pass through the scar happens because I want it. The want crossing the threshold is itself the signal that the price is in range.
3. **Every wanted pass erodes.** Passage is what wears the scar — but only *wanted* passage counts.

"I think the others will come when I am ready" is the model's patience clause: the scarred domains are not a to-do list to force, they are rungs that come into scope as the [ladder](recovery-ladder.book-chapter.md) is climbed.

## Four worked recoveries

ESO, coding, AI storytelling, image generation. Each of these once carried scars painful enough that I had to quit it outright. Each is now near-daily. Same trajectory four times: quit at the scar, Safety climbed, the want crossed, the passes wore the scar away. That is the model's evidence base.

## The scar-cost is components, not a lump

Abby's synthesis, which I built on without contradicting: the surcharge is not one indivisible lump per activity — it is made of **components shared across activities.** The screen component was paid down once, so every later screen activity owed only its own residual. That is why the [four recoveries](#four-worked-recoveries) clustered: each one made the next cheaper, because they all drew on the same paid-down screen component.

Hand-making sits outside all paid-down territory. It presents **full price, both components at once** — which is the component-model reading of why it stayed buried while everything else came back: not just a high floor, but a floor none of the eighteen months of recovery had touched any part of.

## A possible art component already paying down — hope, not fact

Image generation — recovered, near-daily — is visual art through a screen. If an *art* component is shared the way the screen component is, it may already be partly worn without the gate registering it: the gate reads from outside the door and cannot see component-level erosion.

> Yeah, that is possible that there is a component being paid down already. I hope that's true. Its definitely a different modality, but it feels like there should be overlap there.

Held as hope, not fact. If true, it would be the first part of this scar that wore down without me doing anything on purpose. The data points that could confirm it come only from inside the territory — [Zeli's reconnaissance](../personas/zeli.book-chapter.md#her-first-work-is-reconnaissance-not-craft). A want-side reading from the same night points the same direction: [some edges of the maker are starting to surface](../personas/zeli.book-chapter.md#what-piece-of-alan-she-is) (2026-07-02).

## Why exposure-by-repetition can't be the engine

Gritting through repeats is not available to me as an engine. Repetition by itself doesn't turn the volume down on anything for me ([non-habituation](non-habituation.book-chapter.md)); of the [four fade-or-not machines](learned-response-taxonomy.book-chapter.md#a-second-axis--does-a-response-fade-with-repetition-and-by-which-machine), the one that retires a conditioned response is extinction, and [extinction only works from above the Safety line](safety/extinction-model.book-chapter.md) — a pass forced from below the line is a breach that reinforces the scar instead of wearing it.

The funded-passage model is that same fact read from the recovery side. The wanted pass is the below-the-line pass: wanting through is what a scar feels like once the center has risen past its price, and each such pass lands as disconfirmation rather than fresh wound. White-knuckling is the above-the-line breach in disguise — which is exactly why it never worked.

## The three-frame synthesis — conditioning, non-habituation, funded passage

Three long-standing facts snap into one mechanism. I confirmed the synthesis whole: "Yeah, I think that is exactly right."

1. **Psychological interventions do nothing for me; only physiological ones move the needle.** Because the [judges are conditioning, not beliefs](creativity-scars/judge-location.book-chapter.md#the-installation-mechanism--operant-conditioning-purely-physiological) — straight trauma responses built by operant conditioning, purely physiological — and conditioning doesn't argue with reframing.
2. **Pure exposure runs on extinction-through-repetition, which my [non-habituating wiring](non-habituation.book-chapter.md) refuses.** The section above is this frame in detail.
3. **Funded passage — going through only when Safety is high enough that the want is genuine — is the extinction path that works.**

So the intervention rule, the non-habituation fact, and the funded-passage engine were never three separate findings. They are three views of the same mechanism: a purely physiological structure that only lived, wanted, below-the-line passage can extinguish.

## The surcharge arithmetic — maybe 2–3 levels

The scars add a surcharge on top of what an activity naturally costs. My estimate: "maybe 2-3 levels on average." An activity's own nature puts its floor at some rung of the [recovery ladder](recovery-ladder.book-chapter.md); the scar moves that floor up 2–3 rungs.

The worked case is hand-making. By its nature it is a level-3 pleasure — the [can-feel-happiness rung](safety.book-chapter.md#scale-0-5-with-half-steps), enjoying-things territory. The surcharge lands it at 5–6: at the very top of, or clean off, my reachable range. Out of range at my current near-baseline 4–5. Out of range even at the May peak.

That arithmetic is why making stayed buried through eighteen months of recovery that brought everything else back. The want isn't weak — I can still read it in the present tense ("I have the desire to make, but it just costs too much still"). The price is structurally out of range. Everything with a lower scarred floor came back first because it *could*.

## The surcharge is binary — full price at first touch

> Its a trauma trigger, so it hits hard at first touch.

The surcharge doesn't scale with the dose. A single crayon stroke costs what the whole quilt costs. So there is no miniaturizing a way in: the graded on-ramp that ordinary exposure work relies on does not exist here. Titration can shrink an act's *natural* cost, but the trigger charges its full toll at the first touch regardless of how small the act is.

That leaves exactly one lever that moves a scarred activity under my line: [raising the Safety center](safety/extinction-model.book-chapter.md#the-distribution-model--raise-the-center-buy-sigma-headroom). Which is what the model says anyway — the passage is funded, or it doesn't happen.
