
# The conceptual-emotional wall

> The wall between the conceptual and the emotional — emotion gets no vote on what Alan takes to be true. The precise cut is present-affect-available vs. projected-affect-unavailable, rooted in aphantasia. And the single architecture this names accounts for the honesty (can't self-deceive), the motivation-shape (present-cue-only), and the truth-reliability trade-off (Truth = Data + Reasoning: thin felt data, purified reasoning — confident direction, slow progress where data is thin) that everything else in his system inherits.

There is a wall between the conceptual and the emotional, and it is built out of the aphantasia. In my words:

> No, conceptual and emotional have a wall between them from the aphantasia. I don't seem to be able to change my beliefs based on emotional reasoning.

Emotion does not get a vote on what I take to be true. The truth/value verdict is generated by reasoning over rule-sets, and it comes back **sealed off from how a candidate belief feels.** A belief being more bearable does not make it more believable. The how-it-feels channel and the what-is-true channel are two rooms, and there is no door from the first into the second.

This note is the canonical home for that wall as one subject. The corpus assembles it from three places — the [present-bound emotional access](alexithymia.book-chapter.md) the alexithymia describes, the [missing forward simulator](aphantasia-mechanism.book-chapter.md#no-forward-simulator) the aphantasia describes, and the [discrete self](discrete-self.book-chapter.md) whose decisions run on cognition rather than felt pull — but it is never named directly as a single mechanism anywhere. Here it is.

## The precise cut — present affect, not projected affect

The wall is easy to mis-state as "emotion moves my action but not my belief." That is wrong, and the correction is the whole mechanism.

**Present affect is available, and it does move me.** Interoception works in the present ([alexithymia.md → conscious vs. unconscious](alexithymia.book-chapter.md#conscious-vs-unconscious-emotional-architecture)) — the body-level signal fires now, quietly, and present feeling drives present action. The clearest worked instance is the [stoplight system](values-personas-system.book-chapter.md): an off-green light is an engineered present irritant, and the present wanting-it-gone is what moves me to act. That is emotion driving behavior, in the present, by design.

**Projected affect is unavailable.** What I cannot do is render a candidate belief and *feel its consequences over time* — pre-play how a possible future would feel, then let that felt pre-play settle the verdict. That is the [missing simulator](missing-simulator.book-chapter.md): the faculty that pre-plays states I am not currently inside is gated off while awake. So the missing piece is not "feeling," and it is not "feeling about action." It is **simulated/projected feeling** specifically.

So the cut is precise:

- **Present affect — available.** Felt now, off a present cue, drives present action.
- **Projected affect — unavailable.** No felt pre-play of a state I am not in, so no felt pre-play can reach a verdict about what is true or what to do over time.

The wall sits exactly along that line. Belief and over-time value-judgment are downstream of *projected* affect — that is the channel that would let a comfortable belief feel its way into being adopted — and that channel is the one that is dark. Present action is downstream of *present* affect, which is intact. Same person, one channel open and one closed, and which side of the simulator a thing depends on decides whether it gets a vote.

The roots are [alexithymia.md](alexithymia.book-chapter.md) (the present-bound emotional access) and [aphantasia-mechanism.md](aphantasia-mechanism.book-chapter.md) (the missing-recall / missing-simulator gap that confines affect to the present); their substance lives there, not here.

## One architecture, two faces

The same wall produces both of the things this understanding walks between. It is not two mechanisms that happen to coexist — it is one architecture seen from two sides.

### The honesty face — I can't self-deceive

Because emotion can't reach belief, I cannot adopt a more comfortable belief on the grounds that it is more bearable. There is no override that swaps in a kinder model because the true one hurts. Most people have exactly that override — they can [believe what they need to in order to survive](alternative-gated-endurance.book-chapter.md#the-missing-self-deception-system) — and I am missing the system that does it. I believe whatever best fits my information, even when the fit makes my life harder.

This is the honest-map property the rest of the system leans on. It is what keeps a [stoplight green honest](values-personas-system.book-chapter.md#what-keeps-a-green-light-honest): I can't call a light green while the evidence says it isn't, because that would require a belief the wall won't let comfort install. The self-deception substance lives in [alternative-gated-endurance.md](alternative-gated-endurance.book-chapter.md#the-missing-self-deception-system).

### The motivation-shape face — present cue only

Run the same wall forward instead of backward. Emotion can't pre-feel a projected payoff (no simulator) and can't reach belief either (the wall). Subtract both and ask where emotion *can* still move me. **Only the present.** Present irritation, routed through a cue I can see right now, is the one channel left open.

That is why the [values-personas / stoplight system](values-personas-system.book-chapter.md) has exactly the shape it has. It is not a productivity trick bolted on top of me. It is the **only shape motivation can take** given the wall: an engineered present irritant ([the light itself](values-personas-system.book-chapter.md#what-the-little-motivation-actually-is--the-light-itself)), reliable because my [non-habituation](non-habituation.book-chapter.md) keeps the irritant from fading. A system that tried to motivate me by a pre-felt future payoff would be reaching for the dark channel; the stoplight reaches for the only lit one.

### Net

The wall that keeps me honest is the same wall that forces present-cue motivation. Can't adopt a comfortable belief → can't self-deceive. Can't pre-feel a payoff → can only be moved by a present cue. One architecture, both ends.

## The truth-reliability face — Data + Reasoning

The wall has a third face, and it is the one that shows what the wall *costs.* Run it against the question of how a model gets formed and judged at all. In my words, a model decomposes into two terms:

> Truth (or Models) = Data + Reasoning

A model is only as good as the data it is built from and the reasoning that builds it. The wall tunes those two terms in **opposite directions** — and that asymmetry is the whole trade-off.

**Thin Data.** Sensory and emotional data *is* data. Lacking it ([aphantasia](aphantasia-mechanism.book-chapter.md) + the present-bound emotional access of [alexithymia](alexithymia.book-chapter.md)) means there is a whole emotional-experiential layer of the world I cannot even take in, let alone compress. Other minds compress that layer routinely; I am structurally blind to it. So the Data term runs thin exactly where the input is felt rather than conceptual — and progress crawls in the fields that depend on it.

**Clean Reasoning.** The same wall that starves the Data term *purifies* the Reasoning term. Most people's reasoning gets bent toward comfortable conclusions — the emotional side pulls the inference against the truth (motivated reasoning). My emotional channel can't reach belief, so it can't pull. The reasoning runs unobstructed over whatever data it has.

### The trade-off — confidence and validity come apart where data is thin

The two effects compose into one signature. In my words:

> Missing both means I am more confident in my direction, but my progress is much slower in the fields that depend on that data.

- **More confident in my direction.** Clean reasoning returns a compressed, confident model — no motivated wobble in the inference.
- **Slower progress in data-dependent fields.** But it runs that clean reasoning over a data-starved field, so where the missing data is load-bearing, the model is built on too little and progress is slow.

Clean reasoning over thin data still produces a confident, fully-compressed-*looking* model — which is exactly the danger. Confidence is a property of the Reasoning term; validity needs the Data term too. Where the data is thin, the two come apart: the gauge can read fully [net-compressed](compression-epistemology.book-chapter.md#reward-and-truth-are-the-same-signal) precisely *because* the data that would have contradicted the model was never admitted in the first place. The purified reasoning hands back a clean green light over a field it cannot actually see. (For the worked case of *other minds* the gauge does fire honestly — the model's own deep unreliability is the most compressive fact and is built in as the load-bearing term; see [missing-simulator.md → modeling other minds](missing-simulator.book-chapter.md#modeling-other-minds--the-gauge-fires-honestly-because-the-unreliability-is-the-load-bearing-term). Whether that holds for data-poor fields generally is still open.)

So the honesty face and this face are the same fact at two scales: the wall keeps motivated reasoning out of the inference and keeps comfort from installing a kinder belief — but it cannot supply the data I structurally can't intake. Honest gauge, clean reasoning, blind in one whole input dimension.

### The Data deficit is bounded — it bites in one place

The thin-Data side reads worse than it is, so it needs a bound or it overstates the damage. I am **not operating without data.** In my words:

> I can encode compressed representations of the pieces that matter in the conceptual map, so I'm not operating without any data.

What I lose is not data in general but the raw felt particulars — and most of those don't matter to truth. *"It usually doesn't matter if I can't remember what color the floor is in a room, that kind of failure isn't very central to truth, so there isn't much loss there."* Sensory minutiae aren't load-bearing; the conceptual map keeps a compressed encoding of the pieces that are. So the Data term is thin only where the *felt* layer is itself the substance.

The one place the deficit genuinely bites is **making mental models of the internal state of other people** — whose experiences are so different from mine that the felt layer I can't intake *is* the load-bearing data. That ties straight to [missing-simulator.md → modeling other minds](missing-simulator.book-chapter.md#modeling-other-minds--the-gauge-fires-honestly-because-the-unreliability-is-the-load-bearing-term) and the [emotional-bridge / 20% conceptual axis](human-correspondence.book-chapter.md) where Alan matches the median human least.

### The purified gauge cuts the other way too — others may be running motivated reasoning I can't

The clean-Reasoning term has a consequence beyond my own confidence: it makes me doubt that *other people's* compression tends toward truth at all. My reasoning runs unobstructed, so compressing my own map moves me toward truth. Theirs may not — the emotional side bends their inference, so a compact-feeling model of theirs can be a compact-feeling *motivated* model. In my words:

> [I'm] not even as confident that compression of other people's mental maps tends towards truth at all.

So the purification is a real edge, not just a deficit's silver lining: the same wall that starves my Data term is why my Reasoning term isn't quietly running motivated inference the way an unwalled mind's might be.

### Still not a guarantee — only a direction

None of this promises truth. Clean reasoning over whatever data I have is still limited by both terms. What it buys is a *direction*, not a destination. In my words:

> Its still not guaranteed to be true, because it is limited by my data and reasoning, but I am mostly confident it is moving me in the right direction over time.

That is the honest claim the whole face supports: a confident *direction* (clean reasoning) over a possibly-thin field (limited data), trusted to converge over time rather than to be right now.
