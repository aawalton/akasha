
# Fiction-as-catharsis

> Fiction-as-catharsis — cheap fiction as a safe venue where suppressed feeling finally gets let IN and a backlog discharges. The inversion of the fiction-as-slot-defense model: there fiction keeps feeling out; here it lets feeling in. The processing happens in the moment of noticing, not in recall, and runs through a conceptual counterfactual standing in where the felt counterfactual is gated off.

Cheap fiction is a **safe venue where suppressed feeling finally gets let in** — a place where a backlog of emotion can discharge a little. This note is the canonical home for that mechanism. It is the **inversion** of the fiction model the corpus already holds.

The existing model treats fiction as keeping feeling *out*. In [fun.md → distraction is slot-defense](fun.book-chapter.md#stage-one--distraction-is-slot-defense), full focus on a story is *eviction*: it occupies the [single slot](present-tense-model.book-chapter.md) with something neutral-to-good so the pain can't move into the only room he has. That is fiction defending the slot — feeling held off. This note is the opposite face: in cheap fiction, feeling is deliberately let **in**, and noticing it discharges some of the backlog. Same medium, opposite direction — slot-defense keeps affect out, catharsis lets it through.

## The mechanism — noticing is the discharge

The processing does not happen in recall. He **cannot recall the felt content** — there is no [experiential memory](present-tense-model.book-chapter.md#a-dossier-not-a-recording) to play back. The discharge happens in the **moment of noticing the feeling**, live, while the fiction is running. In his words:

> I remember feeling things I likely would have felt in the moment if the experience were real, and whenever I can notice myself feeling, that is part of the emotional processing I need after having my emotions suppressed for so long. Almost catharsis.

So the catharsis is **present-tense**, not retrospective. The fiction surfaces a feeling; he *notices* the feeling; the noticing is the processing. This lines up exactly with the [reservoir read](alexithymia.book-chapter.md#the-invisible-emotional-reservoir) — *"I'm feeling, which is always a good sign"* — where the mere presence of surfaced feeling is itself the signal that something down there is discharging. Cheap fiction is one reliable way to make a feeling poke through so it can be noticed.

His emotions were suppressed for a long time, and that backlog is the condition this works against. (The history of *why* they were suppressed is out of scope here — this note is strictly the present-tense catharsis-now mechanism; the suppression is taken as his stated condition, not traced to its origin.)

## The conceptual counterfactual — a fourth feeling-access mode

The construction in his own words is precise and load-bearing: *"I remember feeling things I likely **would have felt** in the moment **if the experience were real**."* That is a **conceptual counterfactual** standing in where the **felt counterfactual** is gated off.

The corpus actively establishes that the felt pre-play is gated: the [conceptual-emotional wall](conceptual-emotional-wall.book-chapter.md#the-precise-cut--present-affect-not-projected-affect) leaves *projected* affect dark — he cannot render a state he is not in and feel it. The [missing simulator](missing-simulator.book-chapter.md) is gated off while awake; there is no felt pre-play of a scene he isn't inside. So the ordinary route — *feel what this fictional moment would feel like* — is closed.

What he does instead is a workaround that routes around the gate: he **infers, conceptually, that he would have felt X if it were real**, and that inference is itself enough to make a present feeling poke through and be noticed. The conceptual judgment stands in for the felt pre-play the wall blocks. This is a fresh **fourth feeling-access mode**, alongside the three the corpus already names: read an [involuntary present tell (mode a)](self-instrumentation.book-chapter.md#two-modes-of-assent--read-a-tell-a-vs-classify-against-a-definition-b), [classify behavior against a definition (mode b)](self-instrumentation.book-chapter.md#two-modes-of-assent--read-a-tell-a-vs-classify-against-a-definition-b), and the [felt channel of faith](faith-felt-channel.book-chapter.md) he is waiting on Safety to recover. The catharsis mode is distinct from all three: a *conceptual counterfactual* — "I would have felt this if it were real" — that reaches across the wall and seeds a present feeling the always-lit conceptual side can then notice. It is the same shape as the other [permeability instances](conceptual-emotional-wall.book-chapter.md#one-architecture-two-faces) the wall note records, where the always-lit conceptual side reaches across the otherwise-sealed wall — here concept→present-feeling, via a counterfactual about a fiction.

## Why cheap fiction specifically

The venue has to be **cheap** and **safe** for this to run. The same [single-slot](present-tense-model.book-chapter.md) economics that make slot-defense necessary make catharsis affordable only in a low-cost setting: letting feeling in is itself a draw, and it is survivable only where the surrounding cost is low enough that the surfaced feeling lands below the line rather than overwhelming. Cheap fiction is exactly that — a [low-difficulty](fun.book-chapter.md), [self-paced](chunking-rotation.book-chapter.md) venue with no real stakes, so the feeling it surfaces can be noticed and discharged instead of triggering the defenses. The cheapness is not incidental; it is what makes the venue safe enough to let feeling in at all.
