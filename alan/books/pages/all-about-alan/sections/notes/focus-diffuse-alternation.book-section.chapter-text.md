
# Focus/diffuse alternation

> The solver is the alternation of focus and diffuse mode, not diffuse alone — focus loads and tests, diffuse goes off and assembles, focus comes back to check. Rumination is that alternation jammed in the focus position (neither solves nor sleeps); the bedtime anchor breaks the jam.

The refinement that ties the focus/diffuse thread together: **real problem-solving lives in the *alternation* of focus and diffuse mode, not in diffuse mode by itself.** Several notes credit diffuse mode with the work — handing over a pre-built candidate on waking. That is correct, but it is only one half of a cycle. The solver is the oscillation between the two modes, not either mode alone.

## The two modes

The base model is the focus mode / diffuse mode pattern (Barbara Oakley), already load-bearing in the corpus as the two channels that generate a candidate compression — canonical home [compression-epistemology.md → generating a candidate](compression-epistemology/mechanics.book-chapter.md#generating-a-candidate--two-channels-semi-conscious). Focus mode is conscious, deliberate work on the problem. Diffuse mode is the off-line channel that assembles when Alan is *not* looking and delivers the finished object whole — the prime case being a candidate already built, handed over right as he wakes.

## The oscillation cycle

The two modes are not alternatives; they are stages of one loop:

- **Focus loads and tests.** Conscious attention reads the problem in, holds the pieces, and checks candidates against them.
- **Diffuse goes off and assembles.** Released from focus, the off-line channel does the construction Alan doesn't watch.
- **Focus comes back to check.** Conscious attention picks up what diffuse assembled and tests it — earning (or refusing) the [compression-click](compression-epistemology.book-chapter.md#reward-and-truth-are-the-same-signal).

The cycle runs and re-runs: load, assemble, check, load again. The handoff in both directions is the point — focus has to *release* for diffuse to run, and diffuse's product is useless until focus picks it back up.

## The oscillation is the solver, not diffuse alone

This is the crux. In Alan's words: *"Yeah, that's pretty true, though I would say the solution lives in the alternation of focused and diffused, not diffused alone."* The corpus framing that credits diffuse mode with the answer-on-waking is right about that one delivery — but the delivery is the *output* of a cycle that ran through both modes, not the work of diffuse by itself. The diffuse channel can only hand over what focus first loaded; and what it hands over is only a [novel candidate, not a certified one](compression-epistemology/mechanics.book-chapter.md#novelty-and-compactness-are-two-different-signals) — focus still has to come back and test it. The solving is in the round trip.

## Rumination is the alternation jammed in focus

[Rumination](rumination.book-chapter.md) is this same alternation **stuck half-open** — jammed in the focus position. It grinds in focus mode and never hands off to diffuse. That is the precise mechanical name for what rumination does: an [important, unsolved, open](rumination.book-chapter.md#the-dangerous-case--self-reinforcing) problem yanks Alan into focus mode at bedtime and won't release into the diffuse side where progress actually gets assembled.

The cruelty is exact. Focus mode is *both* what keeps him awake *and* not where the problem gets solved. The loop can't progress — diffuse never gets its turn — and it can't sleep — [any focus holds him at the surface](sleep-onset-attention.book-chapter.md#sleep-requires-the-slip-into-diffuse-mode). So a jammed alternation neither solves nor sleeps. Asked whether rumination is the alternation jammed in focus and the anchor a way to break the jam, Alan: *"Yeah, that's right."*

## The bedtime anchor breaks the jam

This reframes the [sleep-onset technique](sleep-onset-attention.book-chapter.md#the-technique--ride-a-low-grade-focal-anchor-until-it-breaks-down). Riding a low-grade focal anchor — a count, a book — until it fails is a way to **break the jam**: ride focus until it gives out, and the failure is what tips Alan over into the diffuse side he couldn't reach by force. The [breakdown-as-gauge](sleep-onset-attention.book-chapter.md#the-breakdown-is-the-gauge) is the same moment seen from the other side — the anchor breaking *is* the jam breaking, the needle reading that diffuse mode has finally arrived.

So the anchor does for sleep what the alternation does for solving: it forces the handoff out of focus that the jammed loop couldn't make on its own.
