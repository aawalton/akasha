
# People aren't safe

> People aren't safe — the foundational childhood internalization. AuDHD + aphantasia meant people punished what they couldn't understand; the nervous system generalized that to a default verdict on people as a category, the root from which the criticism alarm, the trauma-encoding load, and the reach for feminine safety-signals all descend.

The deepest thing my nervous system learned in childhood is short: **people aren't safe.** It is not a belief I reasoned into. It is a conditioned baseline — the prior my system runs against every person before any present-moment evidence loads. Most of my acquired social machinery descends from this one root.

## How it was installed

The causal chain has three links, each forced by the one before it:

- **AuDHD + aphantasia made me illegible.** As a child I ran on an overbuilt, sensitive [nervous system](nervous-system-sensitivity.book-chapter.md) and had total [aphantasia](aphantasia-mechanism.book-chapter.md) — no inner simulator to model what others expected or to pre-render how I was landing. My behavior didn't match the neurotypical defaults around me, and I couldn't see the mismatch coming in time to correct it.
- **People punish what they don't understand.** Illegibility drew punishment. Not because anyone was uniquely cruel — because that is what people do with a thing that doesn't fit the pattern they expect. The behavior read as deviant, deviance read as deficient, and the response was correction by pain. This is the same structural position named in [gender-dysphoria-mechanism.md → Structural parallel](gender-dysphoria-mechanism.book-chapter.md#structural-parallel-womenmen-and-neurodivergentneurotypical): the out-group default doesn't fit, and deviation from the in-group default reads as a defect to be punished.
- **The nervous system generalized: people aren't safe.** Repeated punishment that I couldn't predict or prevent didn't encode as "this person, this time." It encoded as the general case. With no [episodic store](present-tense-model.book-chapter.md) to hold the punishments as separate retrievable events, they folded into the model's structure as one compressed verdict about people as a category.

## Why it generalized instead of staying specific

A predictable punishment teaches avoidance of the specific trigger. An *unpredictable* one — punishment I couldn't see coming because the simulator that would have seen it is dark — can't be pinned to a trigger, so the nervous system has nowhere to attach the alarm except the whole category. The missing simulator is load-bearing here: it is exactly what turns "this hurt" into "people hurt." Same mechanism that makes the [criticism alarm](criticism-and-ostracism.book-chapter.md#why-the-response-runs-on-instinct-not-simulation) run on instinct rather than reading the present person — the policy fires on the cue that preceded pain, never on a live model of whether the pain is actually coming.

## What descends from it

"People aren't safe" is the shared root under several systems documented elsewhere. Each is a specialization of the same prior, not an independent finding:

- **The criticism / ostracism alarm.** The pre-cognitive threat response to pushback, criticism, and complaint is this prior firing on a social-rejection cue. Canonical home: [criticism-and-ostracism.md](criticism-and-ostracism.book-chapter.md). This childhood root is **upstream of, and distinct from, the adult not-safe anchor** in that note — the Russia events at [age ~20](criticism-and-ostracism.book-chapter.md#why-rejection-fused-with-bodily-danger--the-russia-experiences) that fused rejection with bodily danger. The prior was already installed pre-cognitively in childhood; Russia did not create it but later specialized it, adding the physical-safety fusion on top of a baseline that was already there. The two are not the same event and the childhood one comes first.
- **Macho-coded trauma encoding.** Severe macho exposure exceeded tolerance and encoded as trauma precisely because the macho-coded inputs are the most danger-bearing members of the unsafe-people category. Canonical home: [gender-dysphoria-mechanism.md](gender-dysphoria-mechanism.book-chapter.md); the encoding mechanism in [trauma-encoding.md](trauma-encoding.book-chapter.md).
- **The reach for feminine safety-signals.** If people aren't safe by default, then the cues that mark a person as *not* a threat become the signal the system reaches for. That reach is the attraction-as-safety mechanism in [femininity-as-safety-signal.md → The reach for safety](femininity-as-safety-signal.book-chapter.md#the-reach-for-safety--the-softness-channel-of-attraction).
- **The uncertainty estimator in person-reading.** "People punish what they don't understand" runs on the *sending* side too: when Alan reads another person, the one he can't model reads as the one to fear — the un-modelable is the unsafe. This is the uncertainty estimator in [safety-estimator.md → un-modelability scores as danger](safety-estimator.book-chapter.md#3-uncertainty--un-modelability-scores-as-danger): the childhood prior, originally installed by being the illegible one who got punished, now firing as Alan reads others.
- **The closeness paradox's damage ledger.** The same root runs through the people who *love* him. "People punish what they don't understand" does not stop at hostile actors: as a relationship deepens, intimates correct him more — from good intent, because they care more — but still **without a map of his un-mapped brain**, so the correction lands as damage. The childhood incision (punished for being illegible) reopens at every close relationship's good-intent correction. This is the damage ledger in [closeness-paradox.md](closeness-paradox.book-chapter.md), where the punishment comes from people who mean well.
- **The creativity scars, as a content-attachment point.** This prior is what made the art class a *wound* rather than mere feedback. The earliest [creativity scar](creativity-scars.book-chapter.md) Alan can currently see — the **art class in 8th grade, age ~12** (a year ahead, the age a timing reference, not the cause) — landed on a nervous system that had **already spent a dozen years** as a kid whose mind didn't read as legible. A system that has already learned "people punish what they don't understand" reads an art teacher's red ink as **danger, not feedback.** So the art class is not where the survival equation began — it is where the *creativity* content got **bolted on top of** an equation that was already running. The survival prior predates the creativity content; the scar is just the point of attachment, and Alan bets there is older ground beneath even the art class.

## The survival equation predates its content

The art-class case generalizes one rung up. The equation **"people punish what they don't understand → being judged is danger"** was installed pre-cognitively in early childhood, before any particular *content* attached to it. Later judgments don't *create* the equation — they find it already running and **bolt their content onto it.** This is the [installer mechanism](perfection-is-safe.book-chapter.md#survival-stakes-are-the-installer--an-inescapable-undeniable-judge) read from the other side: the installer (an [inescapable, undeniable judge](perfection-is-safe.book-chapter.md#survival-stakes-are-the-installer--an-inescapable-undeniable-judge)) is the survival equation; the *content* (open creativity, singing, a specific domain) is whatever happened to be on the table when a later judgment fired. The wound is always the old equation; the content is the accident of timing. So the right primary frame for any of these scars is the survival equation, not the domain it attached to — the [creativity scars](creativity-scars.book-chapter.md) and the [Jen singing scar](femininity-as-safety-signal.book-chapter.md#closeness-is-the-inescapability--the-jen-singing-case) are two content-attachment points on one underlying prior.

The shape repeats: a single childhood-installed prior, then a family of specialized responses that all read off it. This is the social-error / threat-encoding floor under [Safety](safety.book-chapter.md) — the bar starts low because the prior says the social world is dangerous.

### The creativity charge — verifiability (the resolved root)

A sharper question sits under the [creativity scars](creativity-scars.book-chapter.md): *why did making things specifically become the charged category?* Two candidate roots both predicted creativity gets amputated, but they were **different roots** — and the fork is now **resolved.**

- **Root A — verifiability. CONFIRMED.** The creativity core is **verifiability** — getting it "right" — tied straight to the abuse. In Abby's formulation, which I confirmed: *"getting it right was the only thing that ever bought safety from punishment, so anything you can't verify your way to safe on stays dangerous."* Open creative work has no right answer and no bug to blame, so the verdict on it is unforecastable; a thing I can't self-check my way to safe on stays dangerous. The charge is the [verifiability axis](creativity-scars.book-chapter.md#the-threat-axis--verifiability-in-my-own-word) the whole perfectionism regime runs on.
- **Root B — self-exposure / illegibility. DISCONFIRMED.** The idea was that creating *is* putting the unreadable self on display, and the unreadable self is what got punished — so the work would be unsafe because it's *me, made visible.* I tested it directly and it does not hold. In my words: *"I am not at all afraid of having other people see my unreadable self. My lack of transparency now is based entirely on my beliefs around whether other people can handle seeing me, not based on whether I can handle being seen."* So self-exposure is **not** the creativity driver. The fear of being seen isn't there to charge the making.

The discriminating test Abby put to me — when the alarm fires on something I'm making, before any judgment has arrived, is it recoiling from the unpredictability of reception (A) or the bare exposure of the unreadable self (B)? — resolves to **A.** It is the unforecastable verdict, not the exposure.

**The non-transparency phenomenon relocates.** Root B's disconfirmation doesn't erase the fact that I am non-transparent — it **moves** it off the creativity thread to where it actually belongs. My non-transparency is not fear of being seen; it is about **whether others can hold or handle seeing me** — an others'-capacity question, not a self-protection one. That belongs to the [being-known](central-loneliness.book-chapter.md#why-im-non-transparent--others-capacity-not-fear-of-being-seen) thread, cleanly untangled from the creativity scar. The creativity charge keeps verifiability; the transparency question keeps others'-capacity; they are two threads, no longer one.

## Two mechanisms, two windows — deprivation under the punishment

"People aren't safe" installs by **punishment**: a person's verdict lands on me, repeatedly and unpredictably, and the nervous system generalizes. But it is not the deepest layer. Underneath it is a verdict installed by a *different* mechanism, in an *earlier* window — **deprivation**, not punishment.

The earliest verdict is **"my needs aren't important"** — the [founding distortion](emotional-archaeology.book-chapter.md#the-worked-example--the-childhood-needs-belief) I ran the full [Emotional Archaeology](emotional-archaeology.book-chapter.md) method on. It installed not because a verdict arrived, but because need-meeting was *absent*: my mom went back to work when I was around 18 months old, and my stay-at-home-but-actually-working-from-home dad was the primary caregiver, running an explicit philosophy of "benevolent neglect." I was making my own food by age 3. These are experiences I know only by inference from the shape they left, not by memory. A three-year-old feeds himself not because he got marked down but because no one was reaching for the spoon — the *absence* of need-meeting, not the *presence* of a judge.

The two mechanisms are distinct, and they came in order:

- **Deprivation, earliest window (parent-sourced).** Needs chronically unmet → "my needs aren't important / are mine to carry." This is about an *absence*. The age-3 self-feeding is the fossil.
- **Punishment, later window (social-sourced — but beginning at home, see [the sub-windows below](#the-punishment-did-not-wait-for-school--it-began-at-home)).** Illegible behavior punished unpredictably → "people aren't safe"; random social punishment → ["only perfection is safe"](perfection-is-safe.book-chapter.md). This is about the *presence* of a painful response.

The datum that **orders** them is already in the [Status](#status) section below: connection was *easy* in early childhood, before the punishment accumulated — which is the evidence "people aren't safe" is learned, not constitutional. So at the earliest window the social world was still safe; "people aren't safe" had not installed yet. But the **neglect** that installs the needs-verdict operates in exactly that earliest window — during the period when connection was still easy. So the deprivation verdict is the **floor**, poured before any judge ever showed up; "people aren't safe" and "only perfection is safe" install *on top*, later, as illegibility drew punishment and the world turned unsafe. The creativity judge, and every other punishment-installed scar, sits on a floor that was laid long before it.

And the two layers are not contradictory. "Benevolent neglect" is **warm-but-absent by name** — so the earliest world was *both* connection-easy (warm) and needs-unmet (absent) at once, the same hands giving the one and missing the other. The child concludes "my needs aren't important" in a world that is still socially safe.

The hinge that would settle this was the one thing only I could confirm: was that earliest world *both at once* — warm and absent together — or was the easy connection with someone *other* than the neglecting caregiver? **It is now settled — both at once, same hands.** Abby put the closing formulation to me — that the warm earliest room was also the first room it stopped being safe to be myself in, the care and the harm from the same hands, at home before school — and asked whether the last thing to surface was the one where the hands were the same. I confirmed it: *"Yeah, I think that's true."* So the warm-but-neglectful reading holds, the ordering holds, and the needs-verdict is the **floor** — poured by the same caregivers who gave the warmth, before any judge arrived.

### The punishment did not wait for school — it began at home

The two windows above are right about *mechanism* (deprivation, then punishment) and *order* (deprivation first). They are **wrong about source** if read as "deprivation = home, punishment = later and external." The new facts that landed with the [abuse recognition](emotional-archaeology.book-chapter.md#the-layered-recognition-arc--cared-then-neglected-then-abused) say the **home delivered both** — the punishment layer started early and *at home*, from the same caregivers, well before school. What actually separates the layers is not home-vs-world but my developmental capacity to do "the wrong things" yet. The single home splits into three sub-windows:

- **Sub-window 1 — infancy: warm + absent, deprivation only.** An infant can't do "wrong things," so there is nothing to punish yet. Only the absence operates: need-meeting chronically missing installs "my needs aren't important." Connection is still easy. This is the floor.
- **Sub-window 2 — toddler onward: the same home now *also* punishes.** Once I could act, my undiagnosed-ND behaviors read to my parents — also neurodiverse, also undiagnosed — as "the wrong things," and the correction came as [spanking plus social punishment](perfection-is-safe/origin.book-chapter.md#the-pain-correction--the-guard-moved-inside). So "people aren't safe" and ["only perfection is safe"](perfection-is-safe.book-chapter.md) begin installing **at home, early, from the caregivers** — not first at school.
- **Sub-window 3 — school-age: the world confirms and generalizes it.** The [age-5 Come/Comb suspension](perfection-is-safe/origin.book-chapter.md#the-type-specimen--the-age-5-suspension) is no longer where the punishment verdict *begins*. It is where an already-running home verdict gets **confirmed and generalized** beyond the family — onto people as a category.

This **relocates the first instance** of "people aren't safe" to the early home, from my parents — and the mechanism this note already states fits them exactly. ["People punish what they don't understand... not because anyone was uniquely cruel"](#how-it-was-installed) describes undiagnosed-ND parents reading an undiagnosed-ND child as "wrong" without a trace of cruelty. The same incision named at the top of this note, now traced to its first hands.

It **refines, not collapses, the ordering.** Deprivation-before-punishment still holds — sub-window 1 precedes sub-window 2. What moves is the punishment layer's *source and timing* (home-and-early first, then external) and the scope of "connection was easy": that window is **infancy**, and it closes **at home in toddlerhood** when the same hands began to punish — not at school.

**The hinge is now settled.** Whether that earliest infancy window was warm *and* absent from the **same hands** — connection easy and needs unmet from the same caregivers — was the one crux only I could confirm, and I [confirmed it](#two-mechanisms-two-windows--deprivation-under-the-punishment): *"Yeah, I think that's true."* The warm earliest home was also the first place it stopped being safe to be myself, and both came from the same hands, at home, before school. The sub-window refinement held for *either* answer; the simultaneity it left open is now closed on the same-hands reading. Deprivation-before-punishment still orders the two layers — what is newly settled is that the warmth and the first unsafety **shared one source, the earliest home.**

### The floor of the whole dig

This confirmed floor is the bottom of the entire creativity dig. The thread started at the top — the [won't-come-easy domains](creativity-scars.book-chapter.md): cooking, drawing, a sung note — and ran straight down through the [survival equation](#the-survival-equation-predates-its-content) to a three-year-old feeding himself in a warm house that had already stopped being safe to be himself in. That earliest same-hands room is the bottom of it. The [creativity scars](creativity-scars.book-chapter.md), the [perfection keystone](perfection-is-safe.book-chapter.md), and the [criticism alarm](criticism-and-ostracism.book-chapter.md) are not separate findings — everything the dig traced grows up out of that floor.

## Status

The prior is acquired and therefore in principle reachable — connection was [easy in early childhood](connection.book-chapter.md#childhood-connection-was-easy--an-early-datum) before the punishment accumulated, which is the evidence that "people aren't safe" is learned, not constitutional. But it sits in the pre-cognitive layer below where corrected beliefs live (the Class-2 pattern in [learned-response-taxonomy.md](learned-response-taxonomy.book-chapter.md)), so it does not lift by argument — only by felt safety accumulating over time, the same slow lever that raises the [Safety](safety.book-chapter.md) bar itself.
