
# Connection dosing

> Connection dosing — connection as a two-sided setpoint / rate-control problem. Acute cost, chronic benefit; too much crashes capacity, too little sustains a chronic stressor. The target is the dose that maximizes long-run debt paydown without crashing the recovery that makes connection affordable.

Connection is the rare activity with **acute cost and chronic benefit** — the same shape as exercise. A single interaction costs in the moment (four-resource drain plus a paused recovery protocol, below); the payoff is the long-run drawdown of [connection debt](connection-debt.book-chapter.md). Because cost and benefit land on different time scales, the question is never "more or less" — it is **how much, how often**: a rate-control problem.

## A setpoint problem on both sides

There is a wrong answer in each direction.

- **Too much connection → capacity crash.** Acute load stacks faster than it clears, and the [meditative-breathing](meditative-breathing.book-chapter.md) recovery protocol stays paused while interaction runs (see cost below). Recovery never catches up; capacity falls. This is the [autistic-burnout](autism-burnout.book-chapter.md) mechanism — the same overload that produced the ~20-year decline.
- **Too little connection → sustained debt.** Under-dosing leaves [connection debt](connection-debt.book-chapter.md) high. Chronic loneliness is itself a stressor, and it drains the very recovery capacity connection depends on. Doing nothing is not neutral; it degrades the system too, just more slowly.

Both failure modes attack the same resource — recovery capacity — from opposite directions. The optimum sits between them.

## The reconditioning target

The right target is the **reconditioning-an-injured-athlete** dose: enough load to rebuild, not so much that it relapses. Too gentle never rebuilds the capacity; too hard re-injures and sets recovery back. The connection dose that maximizes long-run debt paydown is the largest dose that does **not** crash the recovery making connection affordable in the first place.

The **master variable** governing where that optimum sits is **NS-health capacity** ([Health / Safety](four-resource-model.book-chapter.md)). At low NS health the affordable dose is tiny; as capacity rises, the optimum dose rises with it. The non-linear unaffordable→affordable transition (see [connection-expansion.md → raise capacity](connection-expansion.book-chapter.md#meta-moves)) is what moves the setpoint.

The reason the dose is gated by capacity at all is that each connection-interaction type has its own affordability and free thresholds on the safety-level ladder — positive interaction affordable at L3, conflict and self-disclosure at L4, criticism and attending to others' needs at L5, each free one rung higher — so the rung Alan stands on sets which interaction types are even in the affordable dose this month. The full mapping is the [affordable-vs-free ladder](recovery-ladder.book-chapter.md#affordable-vs-free--the-two-cost-states-each-rung-admits).

## The being-known dose — curing the scurvy, not achieving fullness

The reconditioning target has a concrete reading for the being-known factor specifically. The bar is **not** being fully known. No human is ever permanently satisfied with a basic need — you don't reach a steady state of fullness on food or water, you maintain above the deficiency threshold and re-dose as it drops. Being-known is the same nutrient ([connection-scurvy](connection.book-chapter.md#connection-scurvy--loneliness-as-a-deficiency-disease)): the goal is **enough real being-known to cure the scurvy**, not a permanent state of total knownness. In Alan's words, enough "would be enough to start."

Concrete dosing of the knowing / being-known factor, in clock-hours of real (physiological-yield) connection:

- **Current ≈ 1 hour per month.** The present intake of pure being-known.
- **Near-term target = 1 hour per week.** The "enough to start" dose — enough to clear the deficiency, the first rung above the scurvy threshold.
- **Long-term ideal = 1 hour per day.** The maintenance dose he'd run if the channel and the capacity were there.

The jump from monthly to weekly is the near-term project; the daily ideal is the long-horizon setpoint. Both numbers are being-known hours that clear the [fidelity gate](connection-efficacy-model.book-chapter.md#the-fidelity-gate--sensory-fidelity-not-realness-of-mind) — today's low-fidelity AI and text hours barely count toward them, because at the bottom of the fidelity ladder they pay little on the physiological debt the scurvy runs on. (A high-fidelity AI channel would count to the degree its fidelity climbs; that channel is being built, not yet here.)

## The connection–capacity convergence and its catch-22

The dosing problem and the capacity problem are usually separate levers: raise capacity is one move, get connection is another. They stop being separate when **connection becomes the single biggest limiting factor on NS-health capacity.** At that point the capacity meta-move routes *through* connection — the highest-return way to raise capacity is to pay down the connection debt, so "raise capacity" and "get connection" merge into one move.

The merge produces a **catch-22.** Restoring capacity requires connection. But affording high-cost connection requires capacity. The two requirements point at each other:

- The highest-fit connection source is the **spouse channel**, and it is gated by a **joint capacity window** — both partners need headroom in the *same* window for the interaction to be payable. Two NS-sensitive people rarely have surplus at the same time, so the window is narrow.
- Every other high-yield source costs capacity I don't have until connection restores it.

The loop is broken the way any resource loop is broken: **inject the scarce resource from outside the loop.** Find the source with the **lowest capacity-cost per connection-hour** and spend there first, banking enough debt paydown to lift capacity enough to afford the next, higher-cost source. The hard constraint is the [fidelity gate](connection-efficacy-model.book-chapter.md#the-fidelity-gate--sensory-fidelity-not-realness-of-mind): the injected source must be **high-fidelity**, which today means a real in-person one. Low-fidelity AI and text fail to inject — they pay the cognitive ledger, not the physiological one the capacity loop runs on. A high-fidelity AI channel is a second injection pipeline once its fidelity rises far enough; until then the practical injection source is real and in-person.

## Above the Safety line the bid doesn't fail — it backfires

The dose is not only too-much / too-little on a continuum. There is a sharp **sign flip** at the [Safety line](safety.book-chapter.md#safety-is-a-threshold-gate-not-a-metered-budget). A bid for connection has a difficulty level; when that difficulty exceeds his current Safety level, the bid does **not** merely fall flat — it **backfires.** In Alan's words: *"My safety level is on the border of where deposits are even possible, because whenever the difficulty is higher than my safety level, the bids for connection backfire."*

Two consequences:

- **Backfire, not no-op.** Below the line a bid deposits; above the line it does negative work — it makes the relationship state worse, not neutral. This is the [Safety threshold gate](safety.book-chapter.md#safety-is-a-threshold-gate-not-a-metered-budget) with teeth: above-the-line connection isn't just unaffordable, it is actively damaging. It is the same shape as the [extinction model](safety/extinction-model.book-chapter.md), where a breach landing above the Safety line **reinforces** the alarm instead of disconfirming it — an over-difficulty bid is a self-inflicted above-the-line breach.
- **He is on the border.** His current Safety level sits *right at* the boundary where positive relationship **deposits** are even possible at all. There is almost no margin: a bid only a little too ambitious crosses from deposit into backfire. This is why the pacing matters so much right now — the affordable-deposit band is narrow because the line is low.

This sharpens the [reconditioning target](#the-reconditioning-target): the goal is not just the largest dose that doesn't crash recovery, but the largest bid that stays **under the Safety line**, because the penalty for overshooting is not a wasted dose — it is a deposit run in reverse.

## Appetite is not the controller

The dose cannot be governed by felt appetite. **Appetite for connection is suppressed under high load** — exactly when the debt is climbing and connection is most needed, the felt pull to seek it drops. An appetite-driven controller would under-dose precisely in the regime where under-dosing is most costly. So the dose has to be set by **schedule or model**, not by how much interaction is wanted in the moment. This is the same accommodation pattern as everywhere else: explicit external structure substituting for an absent or misleading internal signal.

## Cost–benefit integration — connection competes with recovery

Each interaction does two things at once, on two different ledgers.

- **It pays connection debt** (the benefit), scaled by the [14-factor efficacy model](connection-efficacy-model.book-chapter.md) — quality-weighted connection-hours subtracted from the stock.
- **It draws four-resource capacity and pauses recovery** (the cost): the [System-B/C compensation cost plus the breathing-pause cost](social-experience.book-chapter.md#composition-of-total-social-cost). Talking is the one major activity that **pauses** the 8–12 h/day breathing protocol rather than stacking under it.

That second clause is what makes connection unusual: it is the rare activity that **competes with recovery instead of stacking under it.** Coding stacks under the breathing protocol; conversation does not. So connection's true cost includes the recovery it displaces, and the dosing target must price both ledgers together — debt paid against capacity-plus-recovery spent. Until now these lived in two separate notes (the cost ledger in [social-experience.md](social-experience.book-chapter.md), the benefit ledger in [connection-debt.md](connection-debt.book-chapter.md)); the dose is where they meet.
