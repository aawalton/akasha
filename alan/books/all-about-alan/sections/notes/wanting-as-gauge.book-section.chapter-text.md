
# Wanting as a gauge

> Wanting as a resource gauge, not a preference — Alan treats "I don't want to" as the conscious read-out that a resource bar is too low, a hard veto rather than resistance to push through. The gauge has no resolution, so he runs a differential diagnosis across interoception and contextual reasoning to find which bar is low.

Alan reads "I don't want to do it" as a **resource gauge**, not a preference. This is the mechanism behind [Rule 1 of the volatility governor](volatility-governor.book-chapter.md) (*"If I don't want to do it, I'm not allowed to."*). It is also a worked instance of the [alexithymia workaround](alexithymia.book-chapter.md#operational-consequences): a felt state is parsed as a proxy signal rather than a verdict.

## Not-wanting is a low-resource read-out

Most people treat "I don't want to" as resistance to push through — a preference to override on the way to the goal. Alan treats it as a hard veto, because for him the not-wanting *is* the conscious read-out that one of his [resource bars](four-resource-model.book-chapter.md) is too low. He can't afford the activity.

The worked example: insufficient [executive function](executive-function.book-chapter.md) to afford a task shows up, consciously, as not wanting to do it. The not-wanting is not laziness or aversion to the task itself; it is the gauge for the depleted bar. Obeying the veto means respecting a real resource constraint instead of [overdrawing](four-resource-model.book-chapter.md#cost-mechanics) — engaging an activity the resource can't cover, which produces damage that has to be paid back from elsewhere.

This is the [value-system-as-rules](alexithymia.book-chapter.md#operational-consequences) pattern turned into an input. Where others register "I want / don't want X" as felt pull, Alan reads the same signal as a meter on a resource level — and then acts on the meter, not on the pull.

## The gauge has no resolution

The veto has a hard limit: the not-wanting tells him a bar is low, but **not which bar.** It is one undifferentiated warning light, not four separate gauges. Wanting is a single magnitude; the four-resource model has at least four distinct resources ([Health, Mana, Stamina, Safety](four-resource-model.book-chapter.md)) that could each be the one that's low. The gauge collapses all of them into one reading.

So a second step is forced: a **differential diagnosis** to localize which resource the warning light is reporting. Alan has gotten reasonably good at it. The diagnosis runs on **interoception** plus some **contextual reasoning** — where the direct read-out is missing, he reasons his way back to the cause. This is the alexithymia workaround in action: the gauge fires, but the label has to be reconstructed.

## Per-resource signatures

Each resource has its own signature, and the diagnosis is the work of matching the present state against them. The probes:

- **Stress capacity (cortisol).** High cortisol feels like jitteriness, and sometimes tightness in the chest. Either is a sign of insufficient [stress capacity](health.book-chapter.md). This is one of the few resources with a fairly direct interoceptive signature.
- **Executive function.** [EF](mana.book-chapter.md) depletion affects *all* of the various executive functions at once, so the test is one of **breadth**: are they all diminished, or just the one in front of him? The probe question is "Do I want to do *anything*?" — a global drop points at EF; a local drop (this one task only) points elsewhere. Global-vs-local discrimination is the discriminating test.
- **Physical energy.** [Stamina](stamina.book-chapter.md) is easier to feel directly than the others. It shows up as fatigue — a comparatively legible signal.
- **Safety.** Probed **downward** through the activity hierarchy: he checks whether he still wants to do lower-tier activities. Low [Safety](safety.book-chapter.md) raises the cost multiplier on higher-tier activities first ([activity-budgeting.md → status thresholds](activity-budgeting.book-chapter.md#status-thresholds)), so if he still wants the lower-tier things but not the higher-tier one, the fault localizes to Safety. This is varying the "load" across the [activity tiers](stress-capacity-formula.book-chapter.md#cost-base-tiers-by-interaction-type) to localize where the bar crosses the threshold.

The shape across all four is the same as the [decision rubric's](decision-rubric.book-chapter.md) tour-for-data move and the [interest modulator's](interest-modulator.book-chapter.md) proxy reading: a felt state is treated as information to be decoded, not as a verdict to obey directly. The veto obeys the *gauge*; the diagnosis decodes *which* gauge.
