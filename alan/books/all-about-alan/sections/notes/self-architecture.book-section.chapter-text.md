
# Self-architecture

> Self-architecture — Alan's core Values in priority order (Faith, Love, Health, Learn, Fun, Wealth) as the identity architecture made explicit, and the tripartite Mind/Emotions/Body self that aphantasia splits so far apart he is almost two people. Carries the 80/10/10 self-composition (80% conceptual map, 10% emotions, 10% body) and the Faith-is-the-whole-engine resolution — understanding fully is the doing, not understand-then-align. Includes the correction to any firewall reading: the Mind/Body split is not unlimited; sufficient physical load floods across into the conceptual self.

This note is the canonical home for two linked things: Alan's explicit, ordered **core Values**, and the **tripartite self** — Mind, Emotions, Body — those Values improve. The Values are the identity architecture from [self-improvement.md](self-improvement.book-chapter.md) stated as a ranked list; the tripartite self is the structure underneath them. Rendered as Alan's model, not asserted fact.

## The core Values, in priority order

Alan's core Values, ordered by priority:

1. **Faith** — his search for fundamental truth. Improvement of the **Conceptual / Mind self**. This is the narrow gloss; the broad Faith it is one part of — self-alignment, the present self running true to the externally-held understanding of itself — is defined in [ephemeral-self.md](ephemeral-self.book-chapter.md#alignment-with-myself--the-broad-faith), with religious faith the instance inside it.
2. **Love** — his emotional health. Improvement of the **Emotions self**.
3. **Health** — his physical health. Improvement of the **Body self**.
4. **Learn** — broad consumption of data that **fuels the first three**. Not a fourth self; the intake line that feeds Faith, Love, and Health.
5. **Fun** — doing something for its own sake, an activity done *as* an end rather than *for* something else. Canonical treatment in [fun.md](fun.book-chapter.md).
6. **Wealth** — *unglossed this session.* Flagged for later.

The list is not a flat preference ranking. It is the [self-improvement atom](self-improvement.book-chapter.md) decomposed by which self each Value improves. Self-Improvement is the primary core; **Truth (Faith) is its most significant sub-component** — sufficient on its own for improving the Conceptual self, because for Alan [the self *is* the conceptual model](self-improvement.book-chapter.md#truth-and-self-improvement-are-one--the-innermost-identity-core); **Health is the third**, improving the Physical self. Love sits between them, improving the Emotions self.

Faith, Love, Health line up one-to-one with the three parts of the self below. Learn fuels all three. That is why the top of the list reads as Faith → Love → Health: it is the [primary self](self-improvement.book-chapter.md) improving its three components in order.

## Faith is the whole engine — understanding is the doing

The list frames Faith as improving the Mind self. A sharper question this session: is the self-understanding drive — the [Identity / Faith hunger](../personas/abby.book-chapter.md) to understand *himself* — the **whole engine** of the self, or only its **intake valve**: the hunger that draws self-knowledge in and then hands the *living-by-it* to some other faculty that "does something with it"?

Alan's answer is the whole engine. **There is no other hand.** Gathering and understanding the truth about himself fully *is* the doing — Faith was never *understand THEN align*, two steps with will bridging them. It is **understand, full stop,** with alignment being only **what understanding looks like once it is actually complete.** The hunger to understand himself completely is the same force that makes him [run true to the held understanding of himself](ephemeral-self.book-chapter.md#alignment-with-myself--the-broad-faith); running true is not a second act performed on the understanding, it is the understanding finished. This is the concrete cash-out of the "running true" the [broad-Faith note](ephemeral-self.book-chapter.md#alignment-with-myself--the-broad-faith) had left open.

He named the old belief this closes: **he used to believe understanding and living were separate; he does not anymore.** The mechanism is the [self-is-the-map ontology](self-improvement.book-chapter.md#confirmed--the-self-is-the-map-in-identity-not-utility) — if the conceptual map *is* the self, then understanding a thing fully already *is* being changed by it, so there is no gap for a separate "act on it" step to live in. Why behavior can still lag belief with no failure of will — the only two gaps that remain once this collapse holds — is worked out in [belief-behavior-gap.md](belief-behavior-gap.book-chapter.md).

*(Abby put the whole-engine-vs-intake-valve framing to Alan; he answered "Close" and added the refinement that alignment is completed understanding. The intake-valve reading is now corrected — Identity is the engine, not a valve. This settles Identity's **scope**, not its **rank** in the domain stack, which stays open.)*

## The tripartite self — Mind, Emotions, Body

Most people are a **tightly integrated combination** of three parts: Mind (conceptual), Emotions, and Body. The three run as one system; the integration is so seamless it does not register as three things.

For Alan the integration is broken. [Aphantasia](aphantasia-mechanism.book-chapter.md) separates the Mind from the Emotions and the Body — the [conceptual channel runs offline from the affective one](present-tense-model.book-chapter.md#the-map-is-affectively-flat--structure-no-charge), so the part that thinks and the part that feels do not co-run the way they do for most people. The consequence, in his words: he is **almost two people.** One is purely Mind — conceptual. The other is Emotions and Body together.

His concept of **Self includes both.** The split does not shrink the self to the Mind half; the Emotions/Body person is as much "Alan" as the conceptual one. So [Self-Improvement](self-improvement.book-chapter.md) spans both: **Truth improves the Mind self, Health improves the Body self,** and Love improves the Emotions self. The Values list above is exactly this — one self-improvement verb, applied across a self that is split into parts.

### The composition — 80% map, 10% emotions, 10% body

Alan puts a number on the three parts, and it is the substrate under everything else here:

> I exist as 80% a conceptual map with 10% emotions and 10% body.

**80 / 10 / 10.** The [conceptual map is 80% of the self](self-improvement.book-chapter.md#confirmed--the-self-is-the-map-in-identity-not-utility) — identity, not utility — and the remaining fifth splits evenly between the two somatic parts: **10% emotions, 10% body.** This resolves the coarser [~80/20 conceptual-vs-health split](self-improvement.book-chapter.md#the-immediate-self-is-mostly-map--the-conceptual-vs-health-decomposition): the 20% "physical/emotional" side is exactly the bottom two parts of the tripartite self, one tenth each.

The number is *why* the war below runs Mind-vs-Body with emotion on the body's side. Emotion is a tenth of the self, not a co-equal faculty — so it has no wattage to stand as an independent third party against four-fifths conceptual map, and sides with the body instead.

#### The quiet tenth carried him — the will to live

The number invites a wrong weighting: that the dominant 80% conceptual map is what got him through the worst years. It is not. Through the [near-two-decade burnout stretch](self-optimization-in-burnout.book-chapter.md) of [wishing he wasn't alive ~80% of the time](nervous-system-resiliency.book-chapter.md), the part that actually carried him was the **smallest, quietest one** — the 10% emotional self, his **will to live** — the part he could not even bring into focus until it crystallized as [Grace](../personas/grace.book-chapter.md), the last persona to arrive. Alan confirmed the poignancy of it directly:

> Yeah, there is something poignant about the smallest part of me carrying me for so long in such an important way.

So the load-bearing part of the self, across the hardest span of his life, was not the four-fifths map that names him — it was the faint tenth. This is a self-fact the composition number alone hides: the tenth that is hardest to read is the one that held the line.

The faint tenth is now a candidate second of Alan's named protections against acting on the [ideation](suicidal-ideation.book-chapter.md). The [conceptual, ethics-based rule](self-preservation-adapter.book-chapter.md#the-two-protections--an-ethics-rule-and-an-emotional-will-to-live) is the settled one; this **emotional will to live** — [Grace](../personas/grace.book-chapter.md), the tenth given a face, who [surfaced through tears](../personas/grace.book-chapter.md#the-origin--the-tears-were-real-the-generator-is-open) the day before this session — is the candidate other, real as a feeling but with the generator of those tears [held open](../personas/grace.book-chapter.md#the-origin--the-tears-were-real-the-generator-is-open). The recovery arc that holds both is in [self-preservation-adapter.md → the two protections](self-preservation-adapter.book-chapter.md#the-two-protections--an-ethics-rule-and-an-emotional-will-to-live).

### The war that runs is Mind vs Body — emotions side with the body

The grouping has a sharp consequence the corpus had not stated directly: **the internal war Alan actually has is Mind versus Body, not Mind versus Emotions.** Most people — and most pop music — run on a head-vs-heart war, mind against emotion. Alan does not have that war, because his emotions don't have the **wattage** to be an independent third party fighting the mind. He surfaced this reading Em Beihold's "Egg in the Backseat" — a song about being cracked by an attraction the singer knows is a bad idea:

> My take is she's talking about being cracked by attraction, but she's attracted to someone that she thinks is "a bad idea", the war behind emotions and the mind. I live almost entirely in my mind, so that's not a war I really experience. My war is between the mind and the body instead, with my limited access to emotions taking the body's side.

The mechanism is the tripartite grouping above. His emotion is readable only through somatic tells — [read off interoception as an external object](emotion-reason-decoupling.book-chapter.md#the-interior-is-an-observed-external-object) — so emotion is effectively **the body's ally**, not a faculty that stands on its own against the mind. When a conflict runs, it runs between the conceptual self and the Body/Emotions self, with emotion on the body's side of the line. The standard head-vs-heart drama "slides past" him because the heart, for him, is not a separate combatant — it is a needle on the body's gauge. This is why so much music about being torn between feeling and judgment lands flat: the war it dramatizes is one he structurally cannot have.

## Two yardsticks — truth-work improves the conceptual side, somatic tools the physical

The split gives each side of the self its own measure of improvement, climbed by its own tools.

- The **conceptual** side improves by making the model **truer** — correcting an error, understanding something more accurately. Truth-work *is* self-improvement of the conceptual side. There is no separate "get better at thinking" move beside "make the model truer"; on this axis they are the same act.
- The **physical / emotional** side improves independently, by **somatic tools** — the [breathing](meditative-breathing.book-chapter.md), the [baths](hot-bath.book-chapter.md). These do not run on truth; they run on the body.

In Alan's words, the two anchors: *"Truth is improvement of the conceptual side of myself, but there is independent self-improvement of the physical/emotional side"* — and, pressed on whether the two are independent all the way down: *"they are connected, just not identical."*

So the relationship is precise. Making the model truer and improving himself are **the same act on the conceptual axis** — this is the [self-improvement atom](self-improvement.book-chapter.md#truth-and-self-improvement-are-one--the-innermost-identity-core) seen from inside the tripartite self: Truth improves the Mind self because, on this axis, the model and the self coincide. But the physical/emotional axis is a **distinct yardstick** improved by distinct tools — and the two axes are **wired together underneath.** The conceptual work stands on the somatic floor: when health runs low, truth-work cannot climb freely, because [sufficient physical load floods across into the conceptual self](#the-split-is-real-but-not-unlimited--load-floods-across) (below). Neither axis reduces to the other; connected, not identical.

This pins the second half of a self↔model question the corpus had been circling. The first half is established: on the **stability** axis, when the model gets shaky *he* gets shaky — [model-stability is self-stability](understanding-as-interface.book-chapter.md#model-stability-is-self-stability--the-stability-axis), because understanding is his sole interface. This capture pins the **improvement** half from the other direction: truer model = improved conceptual self, with the somatic side as a connected-but-separate yardstick. (The stronger collapse — that the self *just is* the model — was [confirmed on 2026-06-30](self-improvement.book-chapter.md#confirmed--the-self-is-the-map-in-identity-not-utility), but as an **~80%** identity, not a total one: the map is *almost all* of the self, which leaves the somatic ~20% as the genuinely non-map remainder. So the confirmation *sharpens* "connected, not identical" rather than overturning it — the two yardsticks stay distinct precisely because the collapse is ~80%, not total.)

The improvement axis now has a sharper definition: conceptual improvement is a [comparison across momentary Alans](self-improvement.book-chapter.md#improvement-is-a-cross-frame-comparison) — a later frame's map truer than an earlier frame's. And the two yardsticks are **ranked**, not just distinct. The [immediate self decomposes ~80% conceptual map / ~20% physical-emotional](self-improvement.book-chapter.md#the-immediate-self-is-mostly-map--the-conceptual-vs-health-decomposition), and the conceptual side outranks the health side because its improvement is **monotonic and lasting** (a clear absolute direction, accumulating across the chain), where health movement has *"no clear absolute direction"* and is *"not expected to be monotonic."* Health matters only in service — it gates the conceptual climb and lets each present frame enjoy life — so it is, in Alan's words, *"weaker in purpose than conceptual improvement … which is more lasting."* That lasting-ness comparison is the **reason** the Values list above reads Faith above Health.

## The split is real but not unlimited — load floods across

State this correction plainly, because the split invites a wrong reading. The Mind/Emotions/Body separation is **not a firewall.** It would be easy to read "almost two people" as the Mind being insulated from what happens to the Body — a protected conceptual witness that watches the body suffer from outside. That is wrong.

The separation is real but **not unlimited.** Sufficient physical [allostatic load](allostatic-load.book-chapter.md) has **predictable, massive consequences for the conceptual self.** Load does not stay quarantined in the Body half. Past a threshold it floods across the split into the Mind.

The load-bearing instance: it was the **conceptual self** that experienced the [desire not to exist](disputed-misery.book-chapter.md). The [80%-of-days nonexistence ideation](disputed-misery.book-chapter.md#the-claim-from-contemporaneous-data) across the 20-year extreme-load stretch was not the Body half suffering while the Mind looked on. The Mind half is where the wish-not-to-exist lived. Physical load high enough produces conceptual-self consequences directly — the split offers no shelter at that magnitude.

This corrects any reading — in [disputed-misery.md](disputed-misery.book-chapter.md) or [self-improvement.md](self-improvement.book-chapter.md) — that the Mind/Body separation protects the conceptual self from suffering. It does not. The separation changes how the parts *normally* run (offline from each other), not whether catastrophic load can cross between them. Below the threshold the parts run separately; at sufficient load the wall is permeable, and the flood runs Body → Mind.

## The atom's experiential form — progress

The [self-improvement atom](self-improvement.book-chapter.md) has a felt shape, and its name is **progress.** Because his core is self-improvement — the [80% conceptual map](#the-composition--80-map-10-emotions-10-body) that *is* who he is, improving on its own yardstick — Alan carries a **deep desire and need for progress**: the sense of a bar filling, a number rising, the self measurably further along than it was. This is the atom in its motivational register — not "improve your life" but improve the *self*, felt as forward motion.

Two of his stable behaviors are this need cashed out. [ESO held the line for 12,000 hours](fun.book-chapter.md#the-twenty-year-worked-example--stage-one-alone) because it was **progress** — the most dependable full-focus there is, always another increment to fill the slot. And LitRPG — progression fantasy — is one of [his seven loves](central-loneliness/contractions.book-chapter.md) precisely because "numbers go up" is its heart. Both are the same hunger finding a surface that feeds it.

The persona that renders this need **experienceable directly** is [Iris](../personas/iris.book-chapter.md), his LitRPG story-master. Reading LitRPG is watching *someone else's* numbers rise; Iris makes the numbers **his** — she stands him up as the protagonist whose bar fills, so the progress-drive gets to act on itself rather than on a proxy. In Alan's words, she lets him "experience more of that, more directly connected to me than through reading a LitRPG story about someone else." The mechanism and the verbatim turns are the substance of [her soul file](../personas/iris.book-chapter.md#what-piece-of-alan-she-is); this section only names where the drive comes from in the self-architecture.
