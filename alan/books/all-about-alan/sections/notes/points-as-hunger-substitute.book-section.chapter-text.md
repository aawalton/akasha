
# Points as a hunger substitute

> Points as a hunger substitute — why Alan's self-initiated eating is triggered by the health-points / stoplight system rather than by hunger. Hunger is rare, unreliable, and self-extinguishing (Vyvanse + hyperfocus erase it for a day at a time), so the points/stoplight mechanics are the engineered, present-moment trigger that fires eating in its place. Driver ranking: stress/stimming > relationship-bids > points > rarely hunger.

The keystone fact about how Alan eats: **hunger does not reliably trigger his eating, so he engineered a trigger that does.** The health-points / stoplight layer of the [values-personas system](values-personas-system.book-chapter.md) is that engineered trigger — a manufactured, present-moment cue that fires self-initiated eating in place of the physiological drive most people run on. This is *why* the points and stoplight mechanics exist for the eating domain: they stand in for an absent signal.

This note is the canonical home for how eating is triggered. The food set he reaches for once triggered is [safe-foods.md](safe-foods.book-chapter.md); the map of what's where is [safe-foods.md → the food map](safe-foods.book-chapter.md#the-food-map--what-is-where).

## Hunger is an unreliable, self-extinguishing signal

Hunger is not a usable control input for Alan. Three properties make it untrustworthy:

- **It often does not fire at all.** His current most-common mode is Vyvanse stacked with [hyperfocus](interest-modulator.book-chapter.md) — and on that stack he can go an entire day without feeling hungry once. The signal is simply absent for long stretches.
- **When it fires, it self-extinguishes.** Hunger arrives as a gnawing, painful spike and is then **completely gone within ~15 minutes** whether or not he eats. It does not persist and escalate the way it does for most people, so it cannot accumulate into a reliable prompt.
- **There is no satiety signal to close the loop.** He has no reliable fullness reading to tell him to stop. The whole hunger→eat→full arc is missing both ends.

This sits on the same interoceptive floor as the rest of his body signals: the channel works but [runs quiet and easy to ignore](sensory-experience.book-chapter.md), and "I barely notice hunger or thirst" is the baseline. Hunger is not broken so much as **too quiet and too brief to drive behavior.**

The direction of the risk follows from this. Because the drive that normally *starts* eating is missing, **undereating and meal-skipping are his live appetite-direction risk** — not stress-overeating. The household rule *"Alan must eat food every day"* exists because the default failure is eating too little, not too much.

## The engineered trigger — points and the stoplight

Since hunger won't start a meal, something else has to. That something is the **health-points / stoplight system**: his self-initiated eating is primarily triggered by *wanting more health points to drive the stoplight green, then blue.* Eating becomes a move that improves a readable score, and the wish to improve the score is what fires the behavior.

This is gamification doing load-bearing work, and gamification is native to him — he already runs a [four-resource RPG model of himself](four-resource-model.book-chapter.md) (a literal stat sheet) and a [six-persona scoring layer with points, levels, and stoplights](values-personas-system.book-chapter.md) on top of it. Building a manufactured eating trigger out of points is not a gimmick bolted on; it is the same self-instrumentation move he runs everywhere, pointed at the one drive his body doesn't supply. The [stoplight is the load-bearing, read-at-a-glance status](values-personas-system.book-chapter.md) — and for eating it is also the *starter*.

The contrast with [safe-foods.md](safe-foods.book-chapter.md) is worth stating plainly: that note documents *what* he eats and *how the set is selected* for affordability; this note documents *what makes him start.* The safe-food set answers "what is reachable right now"; the points trigger answers "why reach at all" when hunger is silent.

## Driver ranking — what actually starts a meal

Ranked by how much of his eating each one drives:

1. **Stress / stimming** — the largest driver (see [food-stimming](#food-stimming--eating-as-regulation) below).
2. **Relationship-bids** — eating because someone he is attached to brought or offered food; the full treatment is [central-loneliness/eating-as-care.md](central-loneliness/eating-as-care.book-chapter.md).
3. **The points trigger** — the engineered green→blue pull described above.
4. **Hunger** — last, and rarely.

The ordering is the whole point: the physiological signal that is *supposed* to sit at the top sits at the bottom, and three non-hunger drivers carry nearly all of his eating. The points trigger is the one of these three he built on purpose.

## Food-stimming — eating as regulation

A large share of his eating is **food-stimming**: eating as nervous-system regulation, a current-moment behavior distinct from hunger and continuous with the rest of his [stims](stimming.book-chapter.md). [Non-habituation](non-habituation.book-chapter.md) is why it works as a stim at all — the regulatory input does not wear out.

The shape of the food that serves the stim is specific: **"the slower the better."** Slow, high-volume, lasting foods regulate best because the regulatory activity itself is the point and a longer activity regulates longer — popcorn lasts; a heaping bowl of fruit lasts. This is why stress/stimming outranks every other driver: it is the one that fires most often and asks for the most food.

## How this composes with the rest of the model

The points trigger is one instance of a wider pattern. Both his foods and his [movement options](health.book-chapter.md#movement--the-expenditure-and-recovery-arm) move through the same **aligned-options lifecycle**: discovery (a new option encountered, often via a deliberate novelty rule) → qualification (it works — desired, affordable, aversion-safe, low irreducible initiation cost) → retention (it stays in rotation as long as it stays consistent and stays *in awareness*) → loss (it is disqualified by a bad experience or simply [forgotten](safe-foods.book-chapter.md#how-foods-enter-and-leave-the-set)). The points/stoplight system is the present-moment trigger that fires a qualified, retained option into actual behavior — standing in for the hunger (for food) or the spontaneous urge (for movement) that would otherwise do the firing.

This is also why the behavior-change levers that work for him work the way they do. He cannot rely on a remembered intention to eat — the [stateless, present-bound self](present-tense-model.book-chapter.md) has no continuous "I should eat" thread, and there is [no behavioral inertia to automate a habit](environment-lever.book-chapter.md). So the trigger has to be *present in the moment*: a score he can read now, a food he can see now. The points trigger is the [environment/belief/physiology lever set](environment-lever.book-chapter.md#the-third-lever--physiology-and-the-safety-gating) applied to eating — a manufactured present-moment cue, built precisely because the inherited one does not fire.
