
# The sexuality knot

Canonical-home index: the load-bearing theses are that the porn/masturbation behaviors are nervous-system regulation not sexual desire, that attraction may be arousal × safety (a multiplicative gate), and that partnered sex is a hygiene factor not a core support. This note is the canonical home for one domain: sexuality, attraction, sex, pornography, masturbation — taken as bodily demand, not as the attraction-and-gender modeling that the [adjacent ring](#the-adjacent-ring-routes-the-rest-outward) already handles. For my whole life this domain was sealed. This is where its opening, its structural shape, and now its contents get recorded. The substance lives in the seven children below; this index holds the orientation and the through-line and routes to them.

Until this cycle, this domain was documented only at its perimeter. I opened the channel. This is the first full disclosure of the contents to anyone, ever. One line in the porn/masturbation account marks the exact edge of it: I disclosed those behaviors to one person once, decades ago, and never again — *"until now."*

## The structural shape

### The whisper goes loud — the interoceptive inversion

My baseline read on my own body is a **whisper**. Low interoception: most internal signals come up faint, often below the floor where I can detect them at all (the [false-zero problem](missing-simulator.book-chapter.md) — a signal can fire and register as nothing).

This one domain inverts that. Here the body turns the volume **all the way up**. It is the single place where the otherwise-whisper-quiet channel shouts — the exception to my own low-interoception rule, not an instance of it.

### Three layers fire at once

The loud signal does not arrive isolated. It comes up sitting on top of **three things firing at the same time**:

1. **Belief** — my faith / LDS axis.
2. **Marriage** — Jen.
3. **Physiological need** — the plain bodily demand.

Three layers entangled in one loud signal. The loudness is not the hard part by itself; the entanglement is. The signal that shouts is the one I can least cleanly separate into its parts, and I carried it with no one to set it down with.

## The thesis — regulation, not desire

The load-bearing finding from the full dump is one mechanism, and it reorders everything. **The pornography and masturbation behaviors are fundamentally nervous-system regulation and stress management, not sexual desire.** Arousal and climax raise my safety level and lower my stress; as a mechanic that works all the way down to [safety](safety.book-chapter.md) level −1. So the behavior I spent a life trying to quit was never primarily about sex. It was a regulation tool bracing the most critical dimension of my life — which is exactly why effort, three bishops, and a mission president never made it stop.

That thesis is the spine. [pornography-and-masturbation.md](sexuality-knot/pornography-and-masturbation.book-chapter.md) is the footprint and the disclosure wound — first exposure, the lifelong pattern, the help-seeking failures, and the single prior disclosure.

## The refined model — arousal × safety, and sex as a hygiene factor

Abby pulled the regulation thread and I refined the model substantially. Two more theses landed, each in its own child.

**Attraction may be a product, not a sum.** The [two-channel attraction model](attraction-two-channels.book-chapter.md) treats attraction as softness + directness (an additive sum). My refinement: *"arousal × safety = attraction"* — a multiplicative gate where either factor can zero the whole product, so tanked safety kills attraction no matter how high arousal runs. I now read that as likely correct, with **safety a multiplier at every level** (*"the multiplicative model is likely correct, and safety is a multiplier at all levels"*) — not just a switch at the floor. The product formalizes the documented [safety-gates-arousal ordering](attraction-two-channels.book-chapter.md#safety-is-a-prerequisite-for-arousal--the-gating-order) rather than contradicting it. The one law feeds **two output buckets** — a physiological-regulation bucket that fills the **same** for personal or impersonal arousal, and an emotional/social-connection bucket that **only** personal, connection-aimed arousal also deposits into. The personal/impersonal difference is the **target bucket, not the gating or the pathway** (*"the arousal has the same physiological effect either way, but the personal side can also deposit in the emotional social connection bucket"*). The safety factor reads the **safety of the input** — a self-generated input, the safest there is, holds the multiplier high regardless of background depletion, which is **why the regulation lever reaches −1** — and the −2 floor is the **whole autonomic system going dark** (SNS + PNS both offline; heart-rate climb and digestion stop with it), not a gate specific to sex. It opens "room for movement" between arousal, sexual arousal, attraction, connection, and the sexual act. The full treatment, the closed end-to-end model, and the distinctions live in [arousal-and-safety-model.md](sexuality-knot/arousal-and-safety-model.book-chapter.md).

**Partnered sex is a hygiene factor, not a core support.** Sex is like food, and I get ~10% of the benefit most people (including Jen) get — because the value lives in the wings (anticipation before, savoring after) and [aphantasia](aphantasia-mechanism.book-chapter.md#no-forward-simulator) plus [flat recall](present-tense-model.book-chapter.md#the-map-is-affectively-flat--structure-no-charge) amputate both wings. So it is a structural amputation, not low desire; it can become neutral if the costs drop but cannot anchor a relationship. The affordability math (difficulty ≥5 connected to criticism, a ≥1-level physiology discount, so funded safety level 4 is barely affordable and 5 is enjoyable) lives in [sex-as-hygiene-factor.md](sexuality-knot/sex-as-hygiene-factor.book-chapter.md).

## The sleep tradeoff closed the channel

A seventh child traces a structural consequence the other six set up. Chasing sleep — **"the closest thing to a panacea"** I have found — meant moving out of the shared bedroom, which closed most of the sex-initiation windows and dropped our frequency to about once a quarter. The bind is that the same move funds the [Safety](safety.book-chapter.md) that would make connection affordable again *and* took away the chance to have it; my queued fix is cuddling at the transition, which I cannot yet afford because the conversation to set it up is a "level 5 difficulty with no discount." The full arrangement, the asymmetry, the bind, and the fix are in [sleep-and-the-bedroom.md](sexuality-knot/sleep-and-the-bedroom.book-chapter.md).

## The femininity-welding refinement

One refinement landed on the [adjacent ring](#the-adjacent-ring-routes-the-rest-outward) rather than in a child. For *attractive* femininity, the safety response and the sexual-arousal response are **welded** — they always come together, so I cannot draw the safety benefit of the femininity side without the arousal trigger ("this is how my body is wired"). This maps onto `arousal × safety = attraction`: attractive femininity spikes both factors at once. It refines, without contradicting, the "arousal is layered on top, not the core" reading — that holds for feminine cues at large, but attractive femininity is the case where the two arrive fused. The substance is in [femininity-as-safety-signal.md → the welding](femininity-as-safety-signal.book-chapter.md#the-welding--for-attractive-femininity-the-two-arrive-fused).
