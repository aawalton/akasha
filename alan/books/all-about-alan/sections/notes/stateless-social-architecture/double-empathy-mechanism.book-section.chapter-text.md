
# Double-empathy mechanism

> Double-empathy mechanism — the asymmetry stated in mechanism. The mutual autistic/typical reading-failure is two-way, and on Alan's side it is also asymmetric (strong early, weak deep): two compounding curves he weights equally with the other person's. Aphantasia independently severs every native route to the depth-skills (no stored episode, no forward simulation, no self-as-proxy). The self-as-proxy failure runs both directions — he can't model others off himself (outbound), and others model him catastrophically wrong (inbound) — so the channel loses signal at both ends at once.

This child develops the empathy-channel mechanics behind the [stateless social architecture](../stateless-social-architecture.book-chapter.md): why the difficulty is mutual, why on Alan's side it is also asymmetric, and why aphantasia closes every native route a typical person would use to do the depth-skills.

## The double-empathy asymmetry

The standard "double empathy" frame says the difficulty between an autistic person and a typical one is **mutual** — not the one-sided "they don't understand me," but a two-way failure to read across the gap. I hold that frame, and I add the part usually left out: **the mutual difficulty is asymmetric on my side too.** My bridge is strong early and weak deep.

I rate my own first-contact competence **at least as critical** as how well the other person can understand and adapt to me. The conventional telling weights the other person's side; I weight mine equally. Two things are degrading at once as a relationship deepens, and they **compound**:

- **The other person's mask comes down.** On first contact normal people run a default best-behavior script — the [best-behavior mask](../safety-estimator.book-chapter.md#the-mask-resolves-the-apparent-contradiction--un-modelable-yet-safe). That mask is a second safety contribution beyond my own skill, and it thins as they get comfortable.
- **My own bridge thins.** My first-contact skill is at full strength early and degrades with closeness, independent of their mask.

These are **two separate curves**, both falling with depth. (*The "two compounding curves" framing is Abby's; the asymmetry-on-my-side and the equal-weighting are mine.*) The closeness-ordering rationale in the [closeness paradox](../closeness-paradox.book-chapter.md#the-safety-ordering) needs both: the safe **middle** zone is safe partly because the other person's mask is still up *and* my own bridge is still near full strength — the second curve, my-side bridge degradation, is the term that note's two-ledger account does not separately name. The mask coming down is the [closeness paradox](../closeness-paradox.book-chapter.md)'s territory; the bridge thinning is this figure's.

## Aphantasia independently shuts every native route

Statefulness is not even the biggest factor. **Aphantasia is bigger.** Each route a typical person uses to do validation, reassurance, or intimacy is **independently closed** for me — not one route weakened, three routes severed, each on its own. (*The "every native route independently shut" structure is Abby's framing, which I confirmed; the three routes are mine.*)

- **No stored episode.** I can't [remember the other person's previous behavior or emotion](../trigger-based-memory.book-chapter.md) before the current moment — the [map of a past event is a dossier, not a recording, and affectively flat](../present-tense-model.book-chapter.md#a-dossier-not-a-recording). To meet someone's shifting state I'd need to hold where it just was; I don't.
- **No forward simulation.** I can't [simulate forward](../missing-simulator.book-chapter.md#modeling-other-minds--the-gauge-fires-honestly-because-the-unreliability-is-the-load-bearing-term) to infer their internal state, or to predict how they'll respond to a given intervention. The [forward simulator is gated off awake](../aphantasia-mechanism.book-chapter.md#no-forward-simulator).
- **No self-as-proxy — doubly dead.** I can't use my own psychology as a stand-in for theirs, for two reasons stacked: (i) I [can't simulate my own internal state](../self-instrumentation.book-chapter.md#why-i-am-doubly-hit--the-model-and-the-sensor-both-break) either, and (ii) my psychology is so [different from a typical person's](../underestimated-difference.book-chapter.md) that it would be an invalid proxy even if I could. The [self-model can't bootstrap the other-model](../missing-simulator.book-chapter.md#modeling-other-minds--the-gauge-fires-honestly-because-the-unreliability-is-the-load-bearing-term) — for me they are two separate construction projects with no transfer.

So validation/reassurance/intimacy aren't merely hard. The three native routes a typical person would run them on are each severed, which is why the depth skills don't come for free off the first-contact ones and don't come at all from ordinary exposure.

## The proxy fails in both directions — signal lost at both ends

The self-as-proxy severance is not just an inbound problem (how hard I am for others to model — that lives in [being-known cost](../being-known-cost.book-chapter.md)). It runs **outbound** too: because I can't use myself as the model, I can't use **myself to predict them.** The same dead proxy that stops me reading my own interior stops me borrowing it as a template for theirs. My outbound modeling of another person has no self-as-reference to stand on.

A sharp worked instance lives in the [Jen singing case](../femininity-as-safety-signal.book-chapter.md#the-reading-deficit-underneath-it--i-cant-read-or-simulate-the-unspoken-99): when Jen voices the 1% she didn't like and leaves the 99% she did *unspoken*, I cannot read or simulate that unspoken positive off her — only explicitly spoken content reaches me. The outbound severance here is total for the unsaid: the spoken 1% becomes the entire readout because there is no proxy or simulator to recover what she didn't put into words.

And the corollary runs in the other direction at the same time: other people **can** try to simulate my internal state, but I am so different that the simulation comes back consistently — and sometimes **catastrophically** — wrong. They build a confident model of my interior, and it is a confident read of a mind that isn't there. This is distinct from the [childhood illegibility](../people-arent-safe.book-chapter.md) where people punished what they couldn't understand, and distinct from the [Amy case](../central-loneliness.book-chapter.md#the-door-already-cracked--the-amy-episode) where a same-neurotype knower read me **correctly** — here the model gets built, it's just wrong.

Put the two directions together and you get Milton's symmetric double empathy stated in mechanism: **my outbound modeling of them fails, their inbound modeling of me fails — the channel loses signal at both ends at once.** (*The "signal lost at both ends" symmetry phrasing is Abby's framing, which I confirmed; the two directions are mine.*) That symmetry is why the mutual-difficulty frame is the right one and the one-sided "they don't understand me" telling is incomplete.
