
# Local Utah Installers

Utah-HQ installers with primary footprint along the Wasatch Front. Sorted roughly by longevity and reputation density.

## Creative Energies Solar

- HQ: Salt Lake City (455 W 1700 S); also serves Wyoming and Idaho
- Founded: 2004 — Utah's longest-tenured residential solar installer
- Scale: Local / regional; off-grid, residential, commercial, EV, storage
- Provo coverage: Yes — explicitly lists Provo, Orem, Park City, Ogden, Logan
- Credentials: B Corp certified; NABCEP-certified installers
- Ratings: A+ BBB; strong reviews on [SolarReviews](https://www.solarreviews.com/installers/creative-energies-reviews) and EnergySage
- Storage: Battery backup standard offering
- Differentiator: Off-grid expertise, employee-owned ethic, 20+ years of cumulative track record — by far the most stable local choice

## Intermountain Wind & Solar

- HQ: Woods Cross / Centerville, UT
- Founded: 2008
- Scale: Regional — Utah, Idaho, Wyoming
- Provo coverage: Yes
- Installs: 6,500+ systems since founding
- Ratings: A+ BBB; 4.5+ on [SolarReviews](https://www.solarreviews.com/installers/intermountain-wind-and-solar-reviews); strong EnergySage rating; 493 reviews on Birdeye
- Common complaint: Slow communication during permitting; otherwise installation quality consistently praised
- Differentiator: Wind + solar dual capability, deep Utah experience

## ES Solar

- HQ: Layton, UT (245 E 2150 N)
- Founded: 2009
- Scale: Regional — UT, NV, CA, ID, TX
- Provo coverage: Yes
- Installs: 15,000+ across all states; 4,000+ Google reviews
- Credentials: NABCEP-certified; BBB Accredited since 2022; A+ BBB; 4.8/5 Yelp (86 reviews)
- Warranty: 25-year panel, 25-year inverter, 10-year workmanship; lifetime equipment warranty mentioned in some listings
- Storage: Tesla Powerwall, Enphase; Rocky Mountain Power Wattsmart Battery partner
- Differentiator: Official RMP Wattsmart Battery installer partnership

## ION Solar

- HQ: Provo, UT (1.8 miles from Alan's address)
- Founded: 2013
- Scale: National — 11 states; 800+ employees; 85,000+ installs; top-10 US residential rooftop installer
- Ownership: Private-equity owned
- BBB: Accredited since 2013, but [extensive 2025 complaints](https://www.bbb.org/us/ut/provo/profile/solar-energy-equipment-dealers/ion-solar-llc-1166-22338175/complaints) — multi-month install delays, missed tax-credit deadlines (one customer lost ~$15K ITC), oversized system / net-metering errors
- Warranty: 25-year equipment, 10-year workmanship, 10-year roof leak
- Storage: Yes — Tesla Powerwall, Enphase
- Differentiator: Largest local installer; door-to-door sales model; reviews skew premium-experience-when-it-works but recent volume problems are real

## Blue Raven Solar

- HQ: Orem, UT (1403 N. Research Way) — ~5 miles from Alan
- Founded: 2014
- Scale: National — 12 states; subsidiary of SunPower (Complete Solaria) since [2024 SunPower bankruptcy asset sale](https://www.energysage.com/solar/sunpower-is-bankrupt-what-now/)
- Ownership flag: New parent (SunPower Inc. / Complete Solaria, Nasdaq SPWR) is a re-formed entity built from the old SunPower's carcass; ~6 months of new-parent operation as of mid-2026
- Credentials: NABCEP-certified installers
- Ratings: 4.56/5 on BestCompany (~2,000 reviews); BBB profile at [Orem, UT](https://www.bbb.org/us/ut/orem/profile/solar-installation/blue-raven-solar-llc-1166-90013405)
- Warranty: 25-year manufacturer (panels), 10-year workmanship
- Financing: Cash, loan; "18 months no payments" promo widely advertised
- Common complaint: High service-call fees for warranty repairs; project delays
- Differentiator: Aggressive pricing; very high install volume

## Smart Wave Solar

- HQ: Bluffdale, UT (formerly known as some references confuse with the defunct "Lumio (fka Smart Wave)" — these are different; the original Smart Wave that became Lumio is gone, but this Bluffdale Smart Wave continues operating)
- Founded: 2015
- Scale: Local
- Provo coverage: Yes
- Credentials: NABCEP-certified
- Warranty: 25-year equipment, 25-year workmanship — one of the longest workmanship warranties available
- Differentiator: No-pressure sales; longest workmanship warranty in the Utah market; outage-resilient designs

## Redstone Solar

- Founded: 2012
- Scale: Local
- Provo coverage: Yes (explicit Provo, Orem, Lehi, American Fork, Pleasant Grove)

## Project Solar

- HQ: Lehi, UT
- Founded: 2016
- Scale: National — operates in 46 states
- Model: DIY-friendly + full-service hybrid; lowest transparent pricing in Utah ($1.26–$1.82/W)
- Warranty: Double-backed 25-year equipment and workmanship; includes panel-related roof leaks
- Ratings: 94% positive on ConsumerAffairs (70+ reviews)
- Differentiator: Best-priced full-service option in the state; DIY option for handy owners; remote-design + ship + you-or-subcontractor-install model — works well if you have an electrician
