
## Active programs

### Golden Visa — residence by investment

- **Category**: residence-by-investment under the Immigration Law; **no CBI shortcut to citizenship exists in Latvia**.
- **Standard tiers (May 2026)**: ~€50k subordinated bank deposit; ~€250k real estate (with conditions); ~€50k+ equity injection into a Latvian company employing locals.
- **Path to citizenship**: investor TRP → 5 years → PR → 5 years → naturalize with full language and civics exam = **~10 years**.
- **Closed since 2022 to Russian and Belarusian citizens** — first-time TRPs in this category are not issued to either nationality.
- **2025–2026 status**: program remains open to other non-EU nationals but is under renewed parliamentary scrutiny (April 2026 LSM reporting) following the €10M fraud probe; full suspension proposed but not enacted as of May 2026.
- **Volume**: 2025 saw ~201 approvals (up ~35% from 2024's 149); no published wait-time issues.
