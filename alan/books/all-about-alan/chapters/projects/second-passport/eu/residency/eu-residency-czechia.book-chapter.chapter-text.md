
Czech permanent residence (`trvalý pobyt`) generally requires 5 continuous years of qualifying lawful **long-term residence** (`dlouhodobý pobyt`) plus a Czech A1 exam and clean record. Naturalization then requires 5 more years of PR plus Czech B1 — so the standard PR-feeding clock is ~10 years total. Student time counts at **half rate** (5 study years = 2.5 PR years). Asylum / subsidiary protection time counts as 1 year per 6 months. Long-term visas (sub-1-year) do **not** count toward PR by themselves — only the subsequent long-term residence permit time counts. Czechia has no CBI program and no fast-track for capital, marriage, or distinguished talent that bypasses PR.

## Active programs (matching the filter)

### Long-Term Visa + Residence Permit for Doing Business (Živnostenský list — "Živno")

- **Threshold**: hold a valid Czech trade license (`živnostenské oprávnění`) covering the intended activity; prove **≥ CZK 156,500** (~USD 6,400) liquid funds for self-sufficiency over the first year; Czech address; criminal-record clearance from US (FBI apostilled) and any country of 6+ month residence in the last 3 years; private health insurance for the initial visa, then mandatory enrollment in Czech public health insurance (VZP) on arrival.
- **Validity**: long-term **visa** issued for up to 1 year (non-renewable in form), then convert to a 2-year long-term **residence permit for doing business** at the IPC inside CZ; permit renewable in 2-year increments.
- **Physical presence**: no statutory minimum-days rule on the permit itself, but the residence purpose must be genuine and registered tax/social-security activity is expected; absences > 6 continuous months (or 10 months over 5 years) break the continuity needed for PR.
- **PR clock**: counts **1:1** toward the 5-year PR-eligibility window. After PR, 5 more years feed naturalization → ~10 years to citizenship, Czech B1 exam, dual citizenship explicitly permitted under Act 186/2013.
- **Recent reforms**: no headline change 2024–2026; the route is the stable freelancer mainstay. US citizens have the unique privilege of filing the initial visa at **any** Czech embassy worldwide (not just the home-jurisdiction consulate).
- **Processing**: trade license ~5 working days at the živnostenský úřad once docs are in CZ; visa decision **60–120 days** at the consulate; in-country conversion to long-term residence permit ~60 days statutory.

### Long-Term Visa for "Other Purpose" — Digital Nomad Program (IT / marketing specialists)

- **Threshold**: citizen of one of the program's listed countries (US included; plus AU, BR, CA, IL, JP, KR, MX, NZ, SG, TW, UK); **≥ 3 years professional IT experience OR a STEM-field university degree**; remote employment by a non-Czech employer (or Czech trade license as OSVČ); proof of monthly income **≥ 1.5× Czech average gross salary** ≈ CZK 69,000/mo (~€2,800–3,000) for the visa duration; Czech address; insurance; clean record. Marketing specialists were added under the program; the field list remains narrow.
- **Validity**: long-term **visa** for up to 1 year (filed under "other purpose" for foreign-employer track or "business" for OSVČ track), then a 2-year long-term residence permit, **max 3 years total** on the nomad track before re-qualifying or switching purpose.
- **Physical presence**: no minimum-days rule; absences > 6 months break continuity.
- **PR clock**: time on the long-term **residence permit** (the 2-year extension, not the initial visa) **does** count 1:1 toward the 5-year PR window — but the **3-year cap on the nomad track** means most applicants must transition to the standard "doing business" or family permit before they finish the PR clock. The Ministry has not confirmed whether the visa year counts; treat conservatively as visa-year-excluded, residence-permit-years-included.
- **Recent reforms**: program launched July 2024 (fast-track variant for IT specialists), expanded mid-2025 to add Brazil, Israel, Mexico, Singapore. Administered by MPO (Ministry of Industry and Trade) via CzechInvest; the IT/marketing field constraint is the binding filter.
- **Processing**: **45–90 days** at the consulate. Faster than the generic Živno route.

### Long-Term Residence Permit for Family Reunification

- **Threshold**: sponsor (spouse, parent of a minor, or qualifying dependent relative) holds a valid Czech long-term residence permit and has lived in CZ ≥ 15 months, OR holds Czech permanent residence (no waiting period), OR is a Czech citizen. Aggregate household income must equal the sum of subsistence minimums for all family members plus normative housing cost (modest threshold, roughly CZK 25–35k/mo for a couple). Joint accommodation and health insurance required.
- **Validity**: matches the sponsor's permit (min 1 year); **2 years if reunifying with a PR holder**, renewable in 2-year increments. Free access to the Czech labour market (no separate work permit).
- **Physical presence**: must reside with the sponsor; cohabitation is checked.
- **PR clock**: counts **1:1**. Family members of a PR holder become PR-eligible after 3 years of residence on this permit (or upon turning 18, whichever is earlier) — a shorter path than the standard 5 years.
- **Recent reforms**: no material 2024–2026 change. Application fee CZK 2,500.
- **Processing**: **270 days** statutory from abroad; **60 days** if filed in CZ.

### Long-Term Visa / Residence Permit for Studies

- **Threshold**: admission to an accredited Czech educational institution (university programme, accredited language school, or research stay); proof of sufficient funds (≈ CZK 7,000/month subsistence minimum × stay duration); accommodation; health insurance.
- **Validity**: visa up to 1 year, then long-term residence permit renewable annually for the duration of studies. Free access to the labour market.
- **Physical presence**: enrolled study workload; standard absence rules apply.
- **PR clock**: counts at **half rate** — 5 years studying = 2.5 PR-eligible years. Effectively means 10 years of pure-study residence to reach the PR threshold, so the route is realistically used as a **bridge** (finish degree → switch to Živno or employment-purpose permit, and the half-count study time still counts toward PR).
- **Recent reforms**: no material 2024–2026 change to the half-count rule; the EU Students & Researchers Directive remained transposed unchanged.
- **Processing**: **60 days** statutory.

### Long-Term Residence Permit for the Purpose of Investment ("Czech Golden Visa")

- **Threshold**: invest **≥ CZK 75 million** (~€3 million / ~USD 3.2 million) AND create **≥ 20 jobs** for EU citizens (or their family members) **sustained for at least 2 years**. Up to 60% of the threshold may be satisfied with tangible / intangible assets (tech, know-how) rather than cash. Credible business plan required; the IT/services sector is acceptable.
- **Validity**: permit issued for up to **2 years** (the investment-execution window), renewable.
- **Physical presence**: none codified; investor must be genuinely involved with the investment.
- **PR clock**: counts 1:1 toward the 5-year PR window; **no acceleration** to PR or citizenship — the standard 10-year naturalization clock applies (B1 Czech, civics exam, etc.).
- **Recent reforms**: no threshold change since the 2017 introduction; the program is rarely used.
- **Processing**: ~120 days at IPC after the investment is documented.
- **Capital-fit verdict**: **excluded** under the project's ≤ €500k cap. Documented here only to map the program landscape; not viable for the applicant.

## Filtered out

- **Employee Card** — employer-sponsored worker track. Excluded.
- **EU Blue Card (CZ)** — highly-skilled employer-sponsored track. Excluded.
- **Intra-Corporate Transferee (ICT) Card** — multinational employer-sponsored. Excluded.
- **Long-Term Residence Permit for Scientific Research** — host research-institution agreement required (effectively employer-sponsored). Excluded.
- **Long-Term Residence Permit for Seeking Employment / Starting a Business** — graduate-of-Czech-university gateway; usable only post-degree at a CZ institution, not an entry route for an outside applicant. Excluded as not directly accessible to the profile.
- **EU Long-Term Residence (post-PR EU directive permit)** — derives from PR, not an entry visa. Out of scope.
- **§15 distinguished-contribution naturalization** — discretionary, endorsement-based. Excluded per filter.
- **Retirement / passive-income visa** — **does not exist as a named program**. The pre-2014 "other purpose" retiree practice no longer functions; consulates require a concrete CZ-tied purpose (business, family, studies). Excluded as a viable standalone route.

## Summary verdict

Czechia retains **four viable PR-feeding residency paths** for the applicant profile, clustered as:

- **Self-employment**: Živno (the workhorse) and the Digital Nomad OSVČ track (faster processing if eligible by field).
- **Remote work for foreign employer**: Digital Nomad "other purpose" track (IT / marketing specialists only).
- **Family**: long-term residence for family reunification (if a Czech / Czech-PR family member exists).
- **Bridge**: long-term study, valuable mostly as a half-count bridge before pivoting to Živno.

Every surviving path feeds the same ~10-year naturalization clock (5 PR-eligible years + 5 PR years + Czech B1) — Czechia offers **no acceleration** by investment, marriage, or talent. The Živno route is the strongest realistic anchor for a US self-employed applicant without EU ancestry, with the Digital Nomad track as a faster on-ramp if the IT/STEM gate is met.
