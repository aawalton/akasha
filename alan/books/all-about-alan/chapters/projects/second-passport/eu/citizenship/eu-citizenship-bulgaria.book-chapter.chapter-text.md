
# Bulgarian Citizenship Paths

Bulgaria's regime is heavily jus sanguinis. The Bulgarian-origin path (§15) is historically the workhorse for diaspora across North Macedonia, Moldova, Ukraine, Israel, the Americas — no residency, no language test, expedited statutory timeline. Real-world Citizenship Council backlogs routinely stretch statutory 9–12-month deadlines to 2–4 years. Bulgaria's CBI program (§14a) closed 5 April 2022; the residence-by-investment "Golden Visa" remains but leads to citizenship only via the 5-year naturalization path.

## Citizenship by birth — jus sanguinis (Art. 8)

- **Category:** birth / descent (automatic).
- **Eligibility:** at least one parent is a Bulgarian citizen at time of birth. Place of birth irrelevant.
- **Timeline:** automatic from birth; birth must be registered with a Bulgarian consulate / civil registry to obtain documents (weeks to months).
- **Dual citizenship:** allowed unconditionally — native-born Bulgarians may hold any other nationality.
- **Notes:** the foundational rule; adult children of émigrés born to a still-Bulgarian parent are already citizens and need only registration, not naturalization.

## Citizenship by birth on territory — limited jus soli (Art. 9)

- **Category:** birth.
- **Eligibility:** born on Bulgarian territory AND would otherwise be stateless (does not acquire citizenship by descent). Also covers foundlings whose parents are unknown.
- **Timeline:** by operation of law; municipal application + Ministry of Justice review (~1 month statutory).
- **Dual citizenship:** allowed (native-born).
- **Notes:** anti-statelessness rule only; not a path for children of foreign citizens born in Bulgaria.

## Citizenship by Bulgarian origin (§15 — naturalization without residency)

- **Category:** descent-based naturalization (the diaspora path).
- **Eligibility:** at least one ascendant up to and including the third degree (parent, grandparent, great-grandparent) was/is of Bulgarian origin. Must be 18+, clean criminal record, no threat to national security.
- **Evidentiary standard (post-2021 reform, in force 15 Mar 2021):** State Agency for Bulgarians Abroad (DABCH) certificate **abolished as a prerequisite**. Applicant submits primary documents directly to the Ministry of Justice: official Bulgarian or foreign civil-registry records linking the applicant to the Bulgarian-origin ascendant by name and family relation. Practice has tightened — vague ethnographic statements and self-declarations no longer suffice; documentary chain must reach an ascendant whose Bulgarian origin is recorded on a state-issued document.
- **Exemptions:** no residency requirement, no Bulgarian language exam, no obligation to renounce prior citizenship, no minimum income.
- **Statutory timeline:** 9 months from application (reduced from 12 in the 2021 reform).
- **Real-world backlog (May 2026):** Citizenship Council + presidential decree pipeline runs 18–36 months in practice; complex cases (North Macedonia, Western Outlands) stretch to 3–4 years. Statutory tacit-refusal remedy (judicial appeal once the 9-month deadline lapses) is being used more aggressively in 2024–2026 to force decisions.
- **Dual citizenship:** allowed — origin applicants are explicitly exempt from renunciation.
- **Recent changes:** 2021 amendments removed DABCH bottleneck, cut statutory timeline 12→9 months, allowed combined application + interview at consulates. 2024 parliamentary discussions floated adding an A1 Bulgarian language test for §15 applicants — **not adopted as of May 2026**. Evidentiary standard for "Bulgarian origin" was tightened administratively in 2021 and again in 2024 (state documentation of ascendant's Bulgarian ethnicity / nationality is now treated as load-bearing; oral history and church records alone are commonly rejected).

## Standard naturalization (Art. 12 — 5 years)

- **Category:** naturalization by residency.
- **Eligibility:** 18+; permanent or long-term residence permit held for ≥5 years before application (≥30 months physical presence within that window is the threshold for getting permanent residence in the first place); clean criminal record + no pending criminal proceedings; demonstrable income / occupation sufficient for self-support; Bulgarian language proficiency at A1 level (20-question multiple-choice exam, pass mark 12); renunciation of prior citizenship required at the point of obtaining Bulgarian citizenship (waivable for EU/EEA/Swiss citizens and reciprocity-treaty countries).
- **Timeline to citizenship:** ~5 years residence + 12-month statutory processing (real: 18–36 months) = ~6.5–8 years total.
- **Dual citizenship:** generally not allowed for naturalized non-EU/EEA/Swiss applicants — must renounce prior citizenship. EU/EEA/Swiss applicants and treaty-country nationals are exempt.

## Naturalization by marriage (Art. 12a)

- **Category:** marriage.
- **Eligibility:** married ≥3 years to a Bulgarian citizen, still married at application; ≥3 years of permanent or long-term residence in Bulgaria; clean criminal record; sufficient income; Bulgarian language at A1.
- **Timeline:** ~3 years marriage + residence + 12-month statutory processing (real 18–36 months) = ~4.5–6 years total.
- **Dual citizenship:** allowed — spouses are explicitly exempt from renunciation.

## Naturalization for refugees / stateless persons (Art. 13)

- **Category:** humanitarian.
- **Eligibility (recognized refugees, Geneva Convention status):** 18+, recognized refugee status held ≥3 years, permanent/long-term residence, clean record, income, A1 Bulgarian.
- **Eligibility (subsidiary / humanitarian protection):** ≥5 years held protection status, otherwise same as above.
- **Eligibility (stateless persons):** permanent/long-term residence ≥3 years; other Art. 12 criteria except origin / renunciation.
- **Timeline:** ~3 years status + 12 months processing (real 18–36); stateless ~3 years + processing.
- **Dual citizenship:** stateless are by definition single-nationality; refugees may retain (renunciation is not required from refugees in practice).

## Investment-based residency and CBI

_Investment-based residency (Golden Visa) and the closed Art. 14a direct CBI program — see [../residency/bulgaria.md](../residency/bulgaria.book-chapter.md). The Golden Visa feeds the standard 5-year naturalization path with no acceleration._

## Citizenship for special merit / state interest (Art. 16)

- **Category:** distinguished contribution / discretionary.
- **Eligibility:** proposal by a competent minister citing specific Bulgarian state interest OR documented special contribution in science, technology, culture, sport, public affairs, or the economy. Art. 12 conditions (residency, language, income, renunciation) **do not apply**.
- **Timeline:** ministerial proposal targeted at 3 months (vs. standard 12); presidential decree thereafter.
- **Dual citizenship:** allowed (renunciation not required).
- **Notes:** rarely granted; high-profile cases (Olympic athletes, recruited scientists, large institutional donors). Not a planning path for ordinary applicants.

## Restoration of lost citizenship (Art. 26)

- **Category:** re-acquisition.
- **Eligibility:** former Bulgarian citizen released (formally renounced under statutory process) from citizenship; clean criminal record; not a threat to public order / security; permanent/long-term residence ≥3 years before application. Persons of Bulgarian origin previously released qualify under the same terms.
- **Hard restriction:** persons who **voluntarily** renounced Bulgarian citizenship (rather than being formally released) lose the right to restoration.
- **Restoration without conditions:** if the original deprivation had no lawful ground or the ground has lost significance (covers e.g. communist-era political expulsions), restoration is granted on application without the 3-year residence requirement.
- **Timeline:** ~3 years residence + 12-month statutory (real 18–36).
- **Dual citizenship:** allowed (returnees treated as native-born status restoration).

## Comparison summary

Ranked by speed × likelihood for an applicant with no current Bulgarian tie:

1. **§15 Bulgarian origin** — fastest practical path **if** documentary chain to a Bulgarian ascendant within 3 generations exists. Statutory 9 months, real-world 18–36 months, no residency or language. Tightened evidentiary standard since 2021 is the binding constraint, not the statute.
2. **Art. 16 special merit** — fast (3-month statutory proposal) but discretionary and rarely accessible.
3. **Marriage (Art. 12a)** — ~4.5–6 years end-to-end; A1 language gate; dual citizenship preserved.
4. **Standard naturalization (Art. 12)** — ~6.5–8 years; A1 language; non-EU applicants must renounce prior citizenship.
5. **Refugee / stateless / restoration** — narrow eligibility; not a planning lever for an ordinary applicant.
6. **Birth on territory (Art. 9)** — anti-statelessness only, not a general path.

(Investment-based residency and the closed Art. 14a CBI are described in [../residency/bulgaria.md](../residency/bulgaria.book-chapter.md); neither shortcuts the standard naturalization clock.)
