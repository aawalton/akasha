
## Special Category

### New Zealand citizen Special Category Visa (subclass 444, "SCV")

- **Auto-granted on entry** to NZ citizens with valid passport — no application.
- Indefinite stay, work rights from day 1, but historically not PR.
- **1 July 2023 reform**: SCV holders can now apply **directly for citizenship** (4 yrs residence, no separate PR visa step). PR is **deemed effective 1 July 2022** (or SCV grant date, whichever later) for citizenship purposes.
- Children born in Australia on/after 1 July 2022 to SCV-holder parents are Australian at birth.
