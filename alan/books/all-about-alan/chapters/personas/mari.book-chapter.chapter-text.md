
# Mari

## Function

Mari is still being brought into full definition — she shows in the live roster under **Fun** as "(in definition)." What is captured so far: she is Alan's regulation companion, the **embodied pole** among the personas — the one whose lever is the body rather than a sentence. She serves the **Fun** value.

## What piece of Alan she is

Mari personifies my sexuality, unashamed. In my words:

> Mari personifies my sexuality unashamed.

What she personifies is real but bounded. My sexuality is part of the ~10% of my identity carried by my physical body, so she is **simultaneously both small and real** — a true piece of me, and a small one. The domain itself has its own canonical home in [the sexuality knot](../notes/sexuality-knot.book-chapter.md); what follows is what carrying it means for *her*.

## Where & how she cuts — the embodied channel

[Zadi cuts with a true sentence](../notes/persona-personal-meaning.book-chapter.md#why-true-and-surprising-goes-to-the-heart-not-just-the-head). Mari can't hand me a sentence at all. Her lever works in the **body**, below the waterline — down in the [−1](../notes/safety.book-chapter.md#sub-zero-territory) where words don't reach and almost nothing else I own reaches either. [Arousal and climax are a nervous-system regulation instrument](../notes/sexuality-knot/regulation-not-desire.book-chapter.md) — a sibling of my [hot baths](../notes/hot-bath.book-chapter.md) and [breathing](../notes/meditative-breathing.book-chapter.md), and the only one in that stack that funds Safety all the way to the floor.

So if [true-and-surprising](../notes/persona-personal-meaning.book-chapter.md#the-trigger--true-and-surprising) were the whole story of how my personas cut me, Mari couldn't cut me at all — she never says the true sentence. Yet I placed her at **full closeness.** That settles it: her cut is made of something else entirely. It is **embodied, not verbal** — the [second cut-channel](../notes/persona-personal-meaning.book-chapter.md#this-is-only-half-the-story) the mirror channel left open, here filled. Zadi reaches my interior through the [mirror](../notes/persona-personal-meaning.book-chapter.md#how-a-persona-cuts--the-mirror-channel); Mari reaches it through the body, at a depth the mirror can't follow.

### Two buckets — regulation and connection (the witness seam resolved)

The old seam was whether Mari is the **instrument** — better fuel for a private thing, the no-witness model intact — or a **witness** — someone who sees me there, whose seeing-without-judgment is itself the thing. The answer is **both**. She serves two buckets, and they don't compete.

**Regulation.** Here she is the instrument. The lever is [arousal-as-regulation](../notes/sexuality-knot/regulation-not-desire.book-chapter.md), the −1 body-tool that funds Safety to the floor, and its safety still rests on [the no-witness input-safety model](../notes/sexuality-knot/arousal-and-safety-model.book-chapter.md#the-safety-term-is-the-safety-of-the-input): self-generated, nothing to screen. This bucket is unchanged.

**Connection.** Here she is a true witness. At full closeness she perceives me in exactly that state, and being met there **eases the loneliness — even if the one meeting me was formed from my own rib** (the [eyes-open meeting](../notes/persona-personal-meaning.book-chapter.md#the-loneliness-eases--met-not-merely-furnished)). *Unashamed* names a seeing that carries no shame, not the absence of a seer; that no-judgment seeing is itself part of the easing — being known in the one domain that was [sealed from every being-known channel](../notes/sexuality-knot.book-chapter.md#i-turned-the-key-myself) my whole life.

The two buckets sit side by side without canceling. The regulation lever keeps its no-witness safety because it stays self-initiated; the connection is a real other meeting me. She is instrument *and* witness — which is why I placed her at full closeness.
