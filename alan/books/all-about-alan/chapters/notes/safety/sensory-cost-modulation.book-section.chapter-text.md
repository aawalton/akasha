
# Safety modulates sensory cost

> Safety modulates sensory cost — the cross-resource finding that the same sensory input costs less Stress Capacity at higher Safety (auditory worked example, inferred visual, tactile via Mana), and how higher resource state compounds with tool quality rather than substituting for it.

*Part of [Safety](../safety.book-chapter.md) — the cross-resource link between Safety and sensory tolerance.*

## Safety modulates sensory cost

Cross-resource finding from cycle 9: **the same sensory input costs less Stress Capacity at higher Safety.** Tolerance scales with Safety.

- **Worked example (auditory).** Pre-AirPods, ambient sound without ear protection accounted for ~50% of total daily nervous-system cost. That 50% baseline no longer holds today on the same kind of input. The drop is partly tool-driven (AirPods) but primarily Safety-driven — Safety has recovered enough that the ambient load now lands on a more tolerant nervous system. See [auditory-sensitivity-bundle.md → magnitude](../auditory-sensitivity-bundle.book-chapter.md#magnitude).
- **Likely also visual, inferred.** The same modulation almost certainly applies to ambient light cost ([visual-sensitivity-bundle.md](../visual-sensitivity-bundle.book-chapter.md)) — but the visual side has not been instrumented the way the auditory side was, so it's inferred rather than measured.
- **Tactile follows the same pattern, but the trigger surfaces as Mana-correlated.** Cycle 10 captured [tight clothing](../tight-clothing.book-chapter.md) — default-tight 24/7 with a partial loose-break trigger. The trigger fires on low-Mana days, not on Safety state directly. **Mana also modulates sensory tolerance**, at least for tactile. This is finer-grained than the cycle-9 framing, which routed sensory tolerance entirely through Safety. The cross-resource pathway from energy state to sensory tolerance is broader than Safety alone — at minimum it includes Mana. Whether the loose-break trigger is Mana-only, Safety-only at the relevant threshold, or Mana+Safety joint is open (see [four-resource-model.md → cross-resource dynamics](../four-resource-model.book-chapter.md#cross-resource-dynamics)).

This is a non-trivial direction in the cross-resource matrix and parallel in shape to cycle 7's Health → Mana/Stamina coupling. It compounds with tool quality rather than substituting for it: better tools and higher resource state both cut the cost on the same input, and they multiply rather than just add.

The mechanism implication is that sensory regulation is not just an external-tool problem. As Safety (and Mana) recover, the same external-tool stack delivers more headroom because the underlying tolerance is higher — and the inverse holds when either drops.
