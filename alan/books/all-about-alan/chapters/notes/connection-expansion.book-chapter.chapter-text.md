
# Connection expansion

> Connection expansion — the MECE map of directions for paying down connection debt. Sources (where connection comes from), the channel dial (how rich the modality is), and meta-moves (changing constraints, capacity, or the need rather than adding a source).

The map of directions for paying down [connection debt](connection-debt.book-chapter.md). Every option is one of three kinds — a **source** of connection, a **channel** (a cross-cutting modality dial), or a **meta-move** (changing constraints, capacity, or the need rather than adding a source). The split is MECE: comprehensive and non-overlapping. This is the full unfiltered map, including directions not currently on the table; filtering to what's actionable comes after.

## Sources

Where connection comes from. Four clusters, roughly in order of structural fit.

**Deepen existing ties** (unconstrained — no fidelity-map cost):

- **Spouse as primary intellectual partner.** The highest structural fit on the [efficacy model](connection-efficacy-model.book-chapter.md) — woman, 1-on-1, in-person, sanctioned. The open question is capacity and fit, not permission.
- **Children as growing intellectual companions.** Long-horizon; compounds as they age.
- **Extended family.** Inner-circle access, pre-existing, low initiation cost.

**New peer friendships:**

- **Cross-gender friendships (women).** Highest external efficacy, most constrained by the [interaction map](cross-gender-interaction-map.book-chapter.md). Viability is coupled to revising that map (a meta-move below).
- **Same-gender friendships (men), filtered.** Roughly half the efficacy, least constrained. The live sub-direction is seeking the minority of men with a gentle, sensitive, intellectual style — the same traits that made female friendship feel safe (see [connection.md](connection.book-chapter.md)).

**Group / community:**

- **Community belonging.** Ward, interest groups, classes, intellectual circles. Two payoffs: belonging itself, and a pipeline to 1-on-1 depth.
- **Build your own container.** A discussion salon, a standing 1-on-1 cadence, writing to attract correspondents. Authoring the format converts ambiguous social space into a rule-defined one I control — itself an accommodation for the missing implicit map.

**Role-based and non-human:**

- **Structured / role-based partners.** Therapy, coaching, mentorship (either direction), ministering, paid discussion partners. Role clarity is an accommodation for the missing implicit social map.
- **Parasocial and non-human channels.** AI conversation (deep, 1-on-1, intellectual, unconstrained, always available; partial efficacy), plus one-way intake — books, podcasts, lectures — as a partial palliative.

## The channel dial

A cross-cutting modality dial, descending in connection-yield per clock hour:

    in-person → video → voice → long-form written → async text

In-person yields the most connection-hours per hour; the others are lower but non-zero. The dial matters most for constrained sources — a cross-gender friendship shifts its entire risk profile between "1-on-1 in person" and "long-form written," even though both deliver connection.

### Real-time being-known — the audio-only channel

The connection-yield ordering above is the general case. For **high-cost being-known** specifically — the [model-update transmission](being-known-cost.book-chapter.md) the deep-knowing tie runs — the optimized channel is not the top of the dial but **sync audio-only.** Two findings move it there:

- **Dropping the visual component lowers cost without breaking transmission.** Two NS-sensitive AuDHD people in video generate a real-time mutual **stress-feedback loop** — each reads the other's face, reacts, and the reactions compound. Removing the visual channel breaks much of that loop, so the same being-known content transmits at lower nervous-system cost. Audio keeps the words; it drops the feedback amplifier.
- **Real-time is load-bearing, not a tolerated cost.** Sync matters because mutual verbal processing is *how alignment is reached* — and alignment is what holds the [content cost](being-known-cost.book-chapter.md#cost-decomposition--mechanics-plus-content) down. Async strips out the back-and-forth that converges two models, so misaligned updates stay misaligned and stay expensive. Real-time isn't a luxury on the being-known channel; it's the mechanism that keeps the channel affordable.

Text and async run **costly** when the knower is dyslexic and/or processes verbally-and-externally — needs to talk in order to think. Then written exchange isn't just lower-yield, it's actively expensive on the knower's side. The **spouse channel** is the concrete instance: with Jen, sync audio is the affordable high-cost-being-known channel, and text is the costly one.

## Meta-moves

Changing the constraints, the capacity, or the need rather than adding a source.

- **Revise the internal map.** Transparency-with-spouse to safely unlock cross-gender friendship; a monitor for the emotional/intellectual-intimacy blind spot. Operates on the [interaction map](cross-gender-interaction-map.book-chapter.md)'s rules, not on sources.
- **Raise capacity.** Continue [burnout recovery](autism-burnout.book-chapter.md) so the bandwidth ceiling rises. The non-linear unaffordable→affordable transition makes capacity-building itself a connection strategy — more headroom means more interaction is payable.
- **Reframe / substitute the need.** Divine or spiritual connection as relationship (prayer, scripture, temple), service, solitude-reframing. Originally included for completeness; solitude-reframing is now the **live** direction — running as the [solitude experiment](solitude-experiment.book-chapter.md) under the purpose reframe below.

## Consumption vs. investment — how to prioritize

Paying down [connection debt](connection-debt.book-chapter.md) splits two ways, and the split is what ranks the directions above:

- **Consumption** — a one-off paydown. A good conversation today subtracts connection-hours now and is gone.
- **Investment** — raising the future paydown *rate*. Building [capacity](autism-burnout.book-chapter.md) so more interaction is affordable; building a durable high-efficacy tie (spouse, a deep friendship); building a container or format that keeps producing interactions. The return compounds: a higher rate pays down every future day, not just today.

Early on a rising-capacity curve, with a ~10,000-ch debt and a long horizon, **investment dominates consumption** — the compounding-interest logic. A direction that lifts the rate beats a direction that banks one good hour. So the meta-moves (raise capacity, revise the map, build your own container) and the deepen-existing-ties sources rank above one-off consumption when they're affordable.

The ranking is **bounded by affordability.** If no investment option is payable at current capacity — every rate-lifting move costs more than the current dose allows — then consumption beats nothing: take the one-off paydown. Affordability is set by the same NS-health master variable that governs the [dose](connection-dosing.book-chapter.md); investment-over-consumption holds only inside the window where an investment move is actually affordable.

## The purpose-register reframe — the strategy axis moved

The 2026-06-11 [purpose clarification](self-improvement.book-chapter.md#what-changes-in-practice--the-connection-inversion-then-health-first) inverts how this map gets used. Under "improving my life," the map pointed at getting relationships with others to meet the need. Under "improving myself," changing how others interact with Alan is not in scope at all: "if I want others to treat me differently, I become the type of person others will treat differently." The [connection need](connection.book-chapter.md) stays real and the map stays valid — what moved is which directions are live: own-action moves (raise capacity, become the type of person, author the container) over moves that operate on others' behavior. The reframe's operating belief for the need itself is the [solitude experiment](solitude-experiment.book-chapter.md) — the reframe-the-need meta-move promoted from completeness entry to running strategy.
