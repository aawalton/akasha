
# Stamina

Stamina is the physical energy bar. Operationally: levels of energy resources in the body available for movement and physical effort.

## Current measurement: stoplight

Stamina is at level 3 of [measurement maturity](measurement-maturity.book-chapter.md) — stoplight, distribution-anchored. The stoplight reads through signed valence on movement:

- **Green:** I want to move.
- **Yellow:** I don't want to move and don't want to not move.
- **Red:** I want to not move.

Red isn't just absence of desire to move — it's a positive desire for stillness. Stamina partitions by direction-of-pull around movement, not by magnitude.

## Distribution-anchored

Same property as [Mana](mana.book-chapter.md) — yellow is the median of the current experiential distribution; green and red are ~±1σ outliers. The anchors recalibrate as the distribution shifts.

## Why still at stoplight

Stamina hasn't been the primary bottleneck during the long Health-bottleneck era or the recent Safety-bottleneck year. Stamina lives at the resolution its current role requires. In Alan's words, Mana and Stamina are *"not very numeric still"* — a [numeric gauge costs years of being the bottleneck](measurement-maturity.book-chapter.md#a-numeric-gauge-costs-years-so-a-gate-bar-stays-level-only), which Stamina hasn't paid.
