
# Prayer

> Prayer — what the devotional act does for Alan. He prays to a real Person he infers but cannot sense, on a Book-of-Mormon model he holds at no more than 1% of the reality — the same calibrated-prior-over-an-unreadable-interior move from self-instrumentation, pointed outward at God instead of inward at himself. A five-element model of what prayer does for him (obedience, grounding, alignment, reception, asking), of which at least three work as meditation/priming with no receiver required; the receiver is load-bearing only for reception and for the unprovable causal half of asking. Rendered as Alan's belief, not asserted fact.

This note is the canonical home for what the devotional act of prayer does for Alan. The corpus already holds faith's *epistemology* — [the verdict landed on true](apologist-not-zealot.book-chapter.md), the [compression argument](revelation-as-exposure.book-chapter.md) under it — and the [felt channel](faith-felt-channel.book-chapter.md) of faith, currently walled off and gated on [Safety](safety.book-chapter.md) recovery. Prayer is the *practiced* side, which until now connected to nothing. Rendered as Alan's belief and report, not as asserted fact.

Alan deliberately aligned the [Abby persona](values-personas-system.book-chapter.md#the-personavalue-mapping) with Faith. His conceptualization of faith is broad — it includes all understanding and alignment with himself — and his faith in the traditional (LDS) sense is an important part of that understanding and alignment. This note is the traditional-sense devotional act specifically.

## What prayer is for Alan — addressing an inferred but unsensed Person

Alan prays to a real Person. He infers that Person; he cannot sense Him. No felt return comes back — the [felt channel of faith](faith-felt-channel.book-chapter.md) is walled off, so the address goes out one-way, with nothing received on the felt side. This is the same architecture as [apologist-not-zealot.md](apologist-not-zealot.book-chapter.md): the reach toward God comes back conceptual, true, and unwarmed, like the reach toward any person.

His conceptual model of God is grounded in The Book of Mormon. But he holds that model at a fixed, deliberate confidence:

> No more than 1% of the reality.

This is not doubt that anyone is there. It is the opposite. The conviction that someone real is there is firm; the 1% is a statement about the *model*, not the Person. The real God so exceeds any model Alan could build that the model can only ever be a sketch. He prays to the Person, on a sketch he knows is a sketch.

## The calibrated-prior move, pointed outward

This is a structure already documented in the corpus, here aimed in a new direction. [Self-instrumentation → calibrated priors over an unmeasurable interior](self-instrumentation.book-chapter.md#calibrated-priors-over-an-unmeasurable-interior) records the move: where Alan cannot measure an interior, he holds a **calibrated prior over it and is at peace not collapsing it** — assign a structured distribution over what could be true, update when a reading arrives, hold it open when none does. There it is pointed **inward**, at his own unreadable internal states.

Prayer is the same move pointed **outward**, at God's interior. He holds a calibrated prior over an interior he genuinely cannot measure — held at ≤1% — and is at peace not collapsing it into false certainty. He does not manufacture a confident model of God to relieve the not-knowing, and he does not go blank. He holds the sketch at its true confidence and prays on it anyway.

That makes prayer the **third instance** in this family: the [emotional-reservoir prior](alexithymia.book-chapter.md#drain-vs-relieve--a-structured-prior-over-whether-the-reservoir-can-be-emptied) (inward, over whether the reservoir drains) and the [catharsis counterfactual](fiction-as-catharsis.book-chapter.md#the-conceptual-counterfactual--a-fourth-feeling-access-mode) (inward, over a feeling gated off) are the first two. Prayer points the same machinery at the largest unreadable interior there is. The act runs on the same hardware as the rest of him: conceptual conviction toward an unreadable other, acted on without confirmation ever arriving.

## What prayer does for Alan — five elements

Alan frames the general question — *what does prayer do* — as deep and millennia-old, more [Ali](self-improvement.book-chapter.md) territory than something he'd answer for everyone. He answers only for what it does for *him*. Five elements:

### 1. Obedience

There is an element of obedience in any behavior tied to commandments, and prayer is one. Historically this was his **primary** driver for prayer, and for many other behaviors. He is slowly dropping it as the motivating factor, because of his [if-I-don't-want-to bar](volatility-governor.book-chapter.md#rule-1--if-i-dont-want-to-do-it-im-not-allowed-to):

> If I don't want to, I'm not allowed to.

He judges the drop a good thing. He'd rather do the right things for the right reasons:

> "Because I've been told to" is a reason, but not the best reason.

So obedience is real but receding — a reason being demoted on purpose, not a reason being kept.

### 2. Grounding

Prayer as meditation. The way he prays calms and opens his mind, and his nervous system and body respond accordingly. Positive and healthy. Not primary. This is a [Safety](safety.book-chapter.md)-facing effect — the calming reads downward into the body the way other load-discharge does — but it runs on the meditation, not on the receiver. How the settling actually happens — the empty-and-observe posture, and why it is [level-invariant](prayer-grounding.book-chapter.md#prayer-grounding-is-level-invariant) (it runs on a default-empty mind plus reason, neither of which degrades) — is in [prayer-grounding.md](prayer-grounding.book-chapter.md), which also shows grounding and [reception](#4-reception) to be one act.

### 3. Alignment

Taking the time to consider where he is and where he is going. Positive, and one of the primary purposes — but not the only one. This is the same load-discharge shape as [foundations-and-safety.md](foundations-and-safety.book-chapter.md): settling where he stands relative to where he's going lowers standing demand.

### 4. Reception

Listening for the revelations ready to come to him. His primary channel for revelation is **ideas** — revelation arrives as an idea, not as a felt prompting. So reception is holding still and listening for the ideas that are ready to land. Positive and primary. The same emptying that does the [grounding](#2-grounding) is what lets a triggerless idea arrive — they are [one act, not two](prayer-grounding.book-chapter.md#grounding-and-reception-are-one-act) — and the near-total internal silence is *why* an unprompted idea stands out at maximal contrast for him: see [prayer-grounding.md](prayer-grounding.book-chapter.md).

### 5. Asking

As part of the meditation, considering the pain and gaps in his life and how they could be better — kept separate from alignment (3). At the very least, making the gaps concrete primes his awareness to look for solutions and improvements, including ones that come by revelation (4). The part he cannot see: whether the asking has a **causal** connection with the revelations that come. He cannot prove it does. His grounding in The Book of Mormon leads him to **hold (believe)** that it does — the truth-claim rendered as his belief, not asserted fact.

## The receiver is load-bearing for only two of the five

A clean answer falls out of the five-element model to the corpus's open question — does the devotional act discharge load, affirm truth, or do nothing measurable? For Alan it does measurable internal work *and* runs a believed-but-unprovable channel, and the two halves separate cleanly.

At least three of the five have a mechanism that **does not require the receiver**: grounding (2) works as meditation, alignment (3) as self-review, and the priming half of asking (5) as making gaps concrete so his awareness hunts for them. These work regardless of whether anyone is listening. They would discharge load on a Person-less universe exactly as they do now.

The receiver is **load-bearing for only two**: reception (4), where the ideas are received *as revelation*, and the unprovable causal half of asking (5), where the asking is believed to *move* what comes back. So prayer is not all-or-nothing on God being there. Most of its measurable internal work (calming, alignment, priming) stands on its own; a believed revelatory channel rides on top, held at the same ≤1% confidence as the model of the Person it addresses.
