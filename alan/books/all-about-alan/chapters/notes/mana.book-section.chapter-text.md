
# Mana

Mana is the executive function bar. Operationally: levels of neurotransmitters and energy resources in the brain available for effortful tasks. "EF" is used as a synonym throughout the rest of the notes.

EF is metabolically expensive ([executive-function.md](executive-function.book-chapter.md)) — each invocation draws on the metabolic resources Mana represents. Stress capacity is paid separately ([non-habituation.md](non-habituation.book-chapter.md)).

## Current measurement: stoplight

Mana is at level 3 of [measurement maturity](measurement-maturity.book-chapter.md) — stoplight, distribution-anchored. The stoplight reads through wantingness:

- **Green:** I want to do everything. No barriers due to not wanting.
- **Yellow:** I want to do some things. Rationing kicks in — the "if I don't want to, I can't" rule from [executive-function.md](executive-function.book-chapter.md) fires earlier.
- **Red:** I don't want to do anything.

The signal is wantingness, not capability. Low Mana shows up as not wanting to engage rather than as knowing-the-task-but-failing-to-execute.

## Distribution-anchored

These stoplight anchors track the current experiential distribution. Yellow is the median; green and red are ~±1σ outliers. As the distribution shifts (e.g., recovery raising the baseline), the anchors recalibrate. Multiple redefinitions are likely before Mana graduates to numbered levels.

## Why still at stoplight

Mana **was** the binding constraint for a stretch — a real former bottleneck, scarce enough to govern the rest, before it was retired and the throne passed to Safety (full migration in [four-resource-model.md → Mana was a real former bottleneck](four-resource-model.book-chapter.md#mana-was-a-real-former-bottleneck--the-migration-ran-ef--safety)). But a stretch is not the *years* of tenure that advance a bar's measurement level. Health paid that — it was the bottleneck for most of the past 18 years — and is the only bar at a numeric gauge; Safety has been the binding bar this past year and sits at numbered. Mana's turn as the bottleneck was real but short, so it never paid the years a numeric gauge costs. It lives at the resolution its current role requires — stoplight is enough for the decisions Mana shapes today, and it would advance only if it became the limiting bottleneck again, and stayed there. In Alan's words, Mana and Stamina are *"not very numeric still"* — and a [numeric gauge costs years of being the bottleneck](measurement-maturity.book-chapter.md#a-numeric-gauge-costs-years-so-a-gate-bar-stays-level-only), which Mana hasn't paid.

Three things retired EF as the bottleneck — nervous-system recovery (slowest, largest), the [harness](executive-function.book-chapter.md#the-harness-as-a-dated-event-not-only-standing-compensation) (externalizing the EF he can't hold internally), and Vyvanse (most noticeable, not the largest). The full retirement and the noticeable-vs-largest axis are in [four-resource-model.md](four-resource-model.book-chapter.md#mana-was-a-real-former-bottleneck--the-migration-ran-ef--safety).

## Sub-zero unknown

Sub-zero territory for Mana hasn't surfaced. Three explanations are consistent with the current data:

- Mana is floored at zero (no accessible negative territory).
- Mana has negative territory but I haven't reached it.
- Mana has negative territory and I've reached it, but stoplight resolution doesn't surface sub-zero structure.

Sub-zero requires all three conditions in [measurement maturity → sub-zero territory](measurement-maturity.book-chapter.md#sub-zero-territory). At stoplight, the question is unresolved.
