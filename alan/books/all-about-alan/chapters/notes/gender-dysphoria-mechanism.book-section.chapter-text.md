
# Alan's model of gender

> Alan's model of gender — a five-layer model (genetic, physical, felt, narrative, performative) where four layers land male/masculine and felt gender is the lone non-binary outlier because autism outshouts gender; felt gender is absence-with-replacement (the automatic statistical sampler is missing, the vacancy filled by safety-signal); and what once read as gender dysphoria was a salience measurement error over safety-signals, not a female gender identity.

Alan's model of gender is multi-layered. Five layers, each mostly M/F with a long tail of rarer edge cases. On four of the five he lands male / masculine. One — felt gender — is the lone outlier, and the reason it is the outlier is the interesting part: at the felt level neurotype outshouts gender. This note is the canonical home for the whole model, including the reinterpretation that what he once called gender dysphoria was a **measurement error**, not a female gender identity.

## The five layers

1. **Genetic sex** — simple male.
2. **Physical expression of gender** — simple male.
3. **Emotional or felt gender** — the outlier: **non-binary** (see below).
4. **Psychological or narrative gender** — masculine.
5. **Performative gender** — masculine.

Four of the five land male/masculine. Layer 3, felt gender, is the only one that does not, and it reads non-binary for a specific reason.

## Why layer 3 reads non-binary — autism outshouts gender

Felt gender reads non-binary not because Alan feels half-female. It is that he **feels autistic much more strongly than he feels gendered.** In the one channel where felt identity is supposed to live, neurotype is the loud signal and gender is barely present. The non-binary read is what's left when the dominant felt identity in that channel isn't a gender at all.

## Felt gender is absence with replacement

The sharp mechanism, in Alan's words: *"I think it is absence with replacement."* The model:

- **Most people grow a felt gender by automatic statistical sampling.** A subconscious sampling process accumulates a felt gender identity over time — the same kind of automatic, implicit-learning architecture that underlies language acquisition.
- **Alan is missing that automatic sampler.** He seems to lack that subconscious process entirely — likely part of his [missing experiential memory](present-tense-model.book-chapter.md) — so a felt gender identity simply never auto-forms the normal way. Nothing fills the slot by default. This is the same signature as his other [reduced automaticity systems](automaticity-systems.book-chapter.md#b-implicit-social-statistical-learning--reduced): the implicit statistical learner that builds a sense of gender the way it builds social rules and language is one of the subsystems that, in Alan, doesn't run.
- **The vacancy gets filled by safety-signal, not by a gender sensation.** What does fill it: his feelings are dominated by how his environment affects his [nervous system](nervous-system-sensitivity.book-chapter.md). He has a strong preference for safety-positive elements and strong aversion for safety-negative ones — and **femininity is one of his strongest safety-positive signals.** So the felt-gender slot, which would normally hold a gender sensation, instead reports a safety read. The replacement is a [Safety](safety.book-chapter.md) reading, not a felt gender.

This is the same read-not-felt architecture as [emotion-reason-decoupling.md](emotion-reason-decoupling.book-chapter.md): the felt channel does not deliver a gender sensation he feels from inside — it reports salience and safety, observed off the body like any other interoceptive read. There is no felt gender to report because the machinery that would build one is absent; what the channel returns instead is a safety signal.

## Gender dysphoria was a measurement error

For years Alan interpreted a persistent negative physiological state as gender dysphoria — "I have gender dysphoria as a thing." About a year ago that resolved into something different. The dysphoria was a **measurement error**, produced by a salience skew over safety-signals — not a female gender identity.

The skew, in his words: **the aligned male attributes were quiet and the unaligned ones were loud, while the aligned female ones were loud and the unaligned ones were quiet, creating a systemic perception skew.** Loud/quiet salience, not actual content, drove the read. The male attributes that fit him didn't register; the ones that didn't fit screamed. The female attributes that fit him registered loudly; the ones that didn't fit went silent. Aggregate that biased sampling and the felt channel reports "female-leaning, male-aversive" — which read as dysphoria. State the inversion plainly: **what felt like a female gender identity was a measurement artifact of which safety-signals were loud and which were quiet.**

This reconciles with the layer model. The felt channel (layer 3) was never sampling a gender — it was sampling safety-signals through a loud/quiet filter, and the skewed aggregate masqueraded as a gender reading. Correct for the salience skew and the female-identity signal disappears; what remains is the safety-signal preference and the non-binary felt read above.

## How the historical pull fits

The earlier framing of this note traced the dysphoria to a [nervous-system state attached to a gendered trigger](#nervous-system-account--the-macho-coded-trigger) (failure to perform macho-coded male norms). That account is correct about the *source* of the loud safety-negative signals — macho-coded inputs are nervous-system expensive and value-violating — but the sharper statement is the measurement-error one above: the felt "this is dysphoria / this is female" read was the loud/quiet salience skew over those safety-signals, not a female psychology underneath.

So the pull toward feminine cues was never wanting to be female. It was the nervous system reaching for a safety-raising signal, misread (through the salience skew) as a gender-identity pull. The lever mechanism is in [femininity-as-safety-signal.md](femininity-as-safety-signal.book-chapter.md); it fits the aphantasia-compatible Safety-lever class (environmental-signals lever, see [safety.md → Aphantasia constraint on intervention design](safety/aphantasia-constraint.book-chapter.md#aphantasia-constraint-on-intervention-design)).

## Nervous-system account — the macho-coded trigger

The source of the loud safety-negative signals is a nervous-system mechanism, retained here because it explains *why* the male-norm space carried so much loud aversion:

- **Anchor: macho-coded norms.** The distaste anchor was everything Alan associates with "macho" — large, threatening, loud, unkind, with conflict as a constant background threat.
- **Two compounding cost vectors.** These macho-coded norms violated Alan's values *and* were nervous-system expensive. Both costs compound on the same input.
- **Trauma encoding when exposure exceeds tolerance.** When severe macho exposure exceeded Alan's nervous-system tolerance, it encoded as trauma — same mechanism as [trauma-encoding.md](trauma-encoding.book-chapter.md).
- **Persistent negative NS baseline.** The encoded trauma plus ongoing NS expense produced a general background negative physiological state — the loud safety-negative side of the skew.

## Narrative/performative measurement: 80%+ male

The "is the *narrative/psychological* layer actually female?" question is falsifiable, and it lands male. Alan applied a prominent male/female archetypes system, then pairwise rank-ordered preferences across the attributes on both positive and negative framings of each. Result: at least 80% male. This measures layers 4 and 5 (narrative and performative), and it confirms them masculine — it is not a measurement of the felt layer, which is the non-binary outlier. Sharpened claim: Alan's narrative psychology is mostly male, but **not neurotypical male**.

## Structural parallel: women↔men and neurodivergent↔neurotypical

The reframe sits on a structural parallel. Women::Men runs parallel to Neurodivergent::Neurotypical, especially Autistic::Allistic, on two dimensions:

- **Default-doesn't-fit / deviation-reads-as-deficient.** Both groups are measured against the in-group's defaults; both do translation work inside a system shaped around those defaults.
- **Dominance, exploitation, condescension.** In-group over out-group. Shifting on both sides but still load-bearing.

This explains why the macho-coded anchor was specifically the trigger: macho norms are the neurotypical-male defaults at maximum amplitude — the part of the male-norm space most expensive for an ND nervous system to inhabit.

## Resonance with women

Two distinct strands compose Alan's resonance with women and feminist material:

- **Shared structural position.** Feminist songs resonate because they describe a shared coping shape — non-perpetrators navigating macho-threat environments, developing overlapping accommodations (lower-conflict communication, sensitivity to threat cues, value-sets that don't license aggression).
- **Conditioned positive NS response.** Women's presence reliably raises Alan's Safety. Over time he developed a strong positive NS reaction to both women and feminine signals. Mechanism and self-deployment are in [femininity-as-safety-signal.md](femininity-as-safety-signal.book-chapter.md).
