
# Concrete sub-systems already named

Index of sub-systems probed in detail and promoted to their own files. The original starter list (this file's purpose at inception) has been fully consumed — both ballroom-derived body control (cycle 8) and safe foods (cycle 9, previously framed as "safe-food rotation") have been promoted. The file now serves as an index of those promoted files plus the "threads to pull on later" section.

Promoted to dedicated files:

- **Safe foods** → [safe-foods.md](safe-foods.book-chapter.md). Low-resource-state-affordable feeding system — a defined set of foods Alan can handle when all four resource bars are low, selected against a four-attribute affordability checklist (low sensory cost, low EF cost, high nutritional return per unit, low taste-aversion risk). Selection is situational, not scheduled — the starter framing of "rotation" was misleading. The starter framing of "taste-management residue from childhood" was also wrong: the system in adult use is structured around current resource state, not childhood preference history. "Safe foods" is the standard term in the autism community for this category. Worked examples: cheese bread, applesauce packets, fruit bowl with ice cream.
- **Ballroom-derived body control** → [ballroom-derived-body-control.md](ballroom-derived-body-control.book-chapter.md). Trained motor sub-system that produced genuine implicit body-awareness, substituting for the typically-missing automatic proprioception channel. Two-phase mechanism — explicit-rule scaffolding bootstraps learning, then concentrated practice volume clears the 5000-iteration threshold and the body-awareness encodes at the physical / weight layer. Layer-independent transfer to ordinary motor activity (MTC dishwashing / navigation as the worked example). Worked example of the [three-mode operating architecture](operating-mode-architecture.book-chapter.md).
- **Lotion regimen** → [lotion-regimen.md](lotion-regimen.book-chapter.md). Baseline-maintenance pattern — protective lotion layer present by default, restored ~5 minutes after each handwash; bottles in 12+ locations; brand-specific. Defends against self-generated cost from un-lotioned dry-skin baseline. One of two systems in the tactile baseline management class, alongside [skin-on-skin-prevention.md](skin-on-skin-prevention.book-chapter.md).
- **Skin-on-skin prevention** → [skin-on-skin-prevention.md](skin-on-skin-prevention.book-chapter.md). Baseline-maintenance pattern — barrier (clothing, body pillows) or posture between any two skin surfaces. Defends against input-doubling cost ([nervous-system-sensitivity.md → input doubling](nervous-system-sensitivity.book-chapter.md#input-doubling-on-skin-on-skin-contact)). Loose clothing works as well as tight for this function; distinct mechanism from [tight-clothing.md](tight-clothing.book-chapter.md)'s two-fold grounding + saturation mechanism.
- **Deep meditative breathing** → [meditative-breathing.md](meditative-breathing.book-chapter.md). 4-count in, 12-count out, run continuously 8-12 hours per day; counting is structural; talking blocks the protocol.
- **Sleep** → [sleep.md](sleep.book-chapter.md). 9-10 hours per night; natural wake; bimodal onset gated by resource state; opposite-signature mechanisms by which stress (back of night) and Vyvanse (front of night) each trim duration.
- **Hot bath** → [hot-bath.md](hot-bath.book-chapter.md). 30 min - 3 hours, 106-108°F, 3-5x per week, load-driven; mechanism is autonomic shift (SNS → PNS) and HPA recalibration, not sweat-mediated cortisol excretion; Mana is the duration ceiling. Dual-purpose: also a thermoregulation rescue during cold-leaning stretches (see [thermoregulation.md](thermoregulation.book-chapter.md)).
- **Visual-sensitivity bundle** → [visual-sensitivity-bundle.md](visual-sensitivity-bundle.book-chapter.md). Sunglasses outdoors on sunny days, lights-off indoors, screen brightness modes by time of day. Felt cost is Stress Capacity drain, not pain — inverted from typical light-sensitivity framing. The four-tier preference hierarchy that organizes it is in [sensory-regulation-tools.md](sensory-regulation-tools.book-chapter.md).
- **Auditory-sensitivity bundle (AirPods Pro + spatial design + dark noise)** → [auditory-sensitivity-bundle.md](auditory-sensitivity-bundle.book-chapter.md). Layered protocol: quiet basement office, AirPods Pro with full ANC default, partial-removal manual relief, iOS dark noise as masking, and tolerance scaling with Safety. Pre-AirPods baseline of ~50% of total daily nervous-system cost has dropped meaningfully as Safety has improved.
- **Tight clothing** → [tight-clothing.md](tight-clothing.book-chapter.md). Tight (not explicit compression) across undergarments, shirts, pants, socks; default-tight 24/7 with partial loose-break under low Mana. Two-fold mechanism: proprioceptive grounding plus touch-stimuli cost reduction. ~10-25% nervous-system load reduction. The starter line previously called this "compression clothing" — the actual category is tight everyday clothing across the full kit, not medical-grade compression. Second confirmed modality (after auditory) for the asymmetric-cost adaptation: default-max with manual relief downward.
- **Weighted blanket** → [weighted-blanket.md](weighted-blanket.book-chapter.md). Default-off, strategic deployment under troubled sleep, coin-flip hit rate. Mechanism is movement restriction plus high-heat-capacity heat sink, not grounding. Chest weight blocks the breathing protocol (extends the oral / airway competition rule to thoracic compression). **Falsification cycle for the asymmetric-cost generalization** — the cycle-10 claim that the default-max-plus-manual-relief shape applies across modalities is sharpened: it generalizes only when under-protection costs much more than over-protection. Tools with symmetric or inverted cost shapes correctly stay default-off.
