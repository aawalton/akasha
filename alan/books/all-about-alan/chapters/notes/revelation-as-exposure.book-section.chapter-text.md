
# Revelation as exposure

> Revelation-as-exposure — Alan's faith channel. He is a member of the LDS Church and grew up in it; his most compact model of the universe must include the Book of Mormon being true, because he has attacked it from ~20 disciplines and cannot explain its existence on its own terms otherwise (compression-grounded faith). God communicates differently with different people; for Alan, revelation arrives as exposure — the right person, book, or idea at exactly the right time. A mind that generates no internal alternatives can only be reached from outside, and the words/dreams/visions channels are closed by aphantasia, so exposure is the one channel that can reach him and the most legible one. Discernment runs through belief-rigidity: a false candidate just doesn't stick. Rendered as Alan's beliefs, not asserted fact.

Alan is a member of **The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints,** and he grew up in it. This note captures how revelation actually works *for Alan* — a channel shaped by his neurotype as much as by his faith. It sits in the [faith cluster](testing-and-the-seed.book-chapter.md) and is rendered as Alan's beliefs, not as asserted fact.

The arc that reaches here runs through [alternative-gated endurance](alternative-gated-endurance.book-chapter.md): a mind that generates no internal alternatives can only be reached from outside. Exposure is the faith reading of that fact.

## The Book of Mormon as a compression argument

Alan's faith is not held against his [compression epistemology](compression-epistemology.book-chapter.md) — it is held *by* it. He has **attacked the Book of Mormon from roughly 20 disciplines** and cannot explain its existence other than in the terms it presents for itself. So his **most compact model of the universe must include it being true.**

This is [minimum-description-length](compression-epistemology/mechanics.book-chapter.md#novelty-and-compactness-are-two-different-signals) reasoning applied to the hardest case. Every naturalistic account he has tried bolts on epicycles — special cases to explain away one discipline's worth of evidence, then another — until the not-true model costs more structure than the true one. The compactness flips the way it flips for [any false model forced past a narrow data set](compression-epistemology/failure-mode-and-guard.book-chapter.md#the-failure-mode--compactness-is-data-set-relative), except here the broad-data verdict lands on *true* rather than false. Across ~20 disciplines of intake, the account that costs the fewest bits is the one the book gives for itself.

That makes this faith **the same operation as the rest of the epistemology, not an exception to it.** The [creed](absolute-truth-and-ethics.book-chapter.md#the-creed) — "my religion is truth" — and this belief are consistent: he holds the book true *because* truth is the allegiance and compactness is the gauge, not in spite of them.

## God communicates differently with different people

Alan believes **God exists and communicates with different people in different ways.** The scriptural pattern he names: for **Nephi, revelation came in words;** for **Lehi, in dreams and visions.** The channel is matched to the recipient, not fixed across recipients.

This sets up the personal claim. If the channel varies by person, then the question "how does revelation reach *Alan*" has a specific, neurotype-shaped answer — and it is neither words nor dreams-and-visions in the ordinary sense.

## For Alan, revelation arrives as exposure

For Alan, revelation comes as **exposure:** the right person, book, idea, or thought **arrives at exactly the right time** — when he didn't even know that was what he needed. **Every major change in his life seems to have followed this pattern.**

Why this channel and not the others — the coherence is worth stating in full, because it is where the faith thread fuses with the neurotype:

- A mind that **generates no internal alternatives** ([alternative-gated endurance → doors come only from outside](alternative-gated-endurance.book-chapter.md#doors-come-only-from-outside)) can only be reached from outside. There is no internal channel for a new direction to arise on.
- The **words and dreams/visions channels are closed to him by [aphantasia](aphantasia-mechanism.book-chapter.md).** He has no [waking voice or imagery](aphantasia-mechanism.book-chapter.md#the-break-is-at-recall-not-perception-or-encoding) to receive an inner word in, and no visions while awake. The Nephi and Lehi channels are mechanism-incompatible with his architecture.
- **Exposure is the one channel that *could* reach him** — and the **most legible** one. An externally-surfaced, perfectly-timed alternative stands out as unmistakably **not self-generated**, precisely because his architecture generates no alternatives internally. The very poverty that closes the other channels makes this one unambiguous: when a door he could not have produced appears at exactly the moment it fits, its externality is the signal.

So the faith claim and the [endurance law](alternative-gated-endurance.book-chapter.md) are one structure. The "doors" that break or redirect him — the real external alternatives that arrive at the right time — **are, in Alan's frame, divine communication.** Revelation *is* the externally-surfaced, perfectly-timed alternative. What the endurance note describes mechanically (only real outside doors move him), the faith note reads theologically (those doors are how God reaches a mind built like his).

## Discernment via belief-rigidity

A mind reached only from outside faces an obvious problem: how to tell a revelatory exposure from noise. Alan's answer is that **he does not try to.**

He always follows **"the path of what is more true."** And because he [cannot choose what to believe](alternative-gated-endurance.book-chapter.md#the-missing-self-deception-system), a false candidate revelation **just doesn't stick** — the truth-detector won't hold it, the same way an [inconsistent hash refuses to be ignored](compression-epistemology/mechanics.book-chapter.md#the-residue-refuses-to-be-ignored). He doesn't run a discernment filter on the front end; the belief machinery rejects the false candidate on its own, because adopting it would require a self-deception lever he lacks.

State the unification plainly, because it is the payoff of the whole arc: **the same deficit is the cost and the gift.** The [missing self-deception system](alternative-gated-endurance.book-chapter.md#the-missing-self-deception-system) — the inability to believe what would be comforting — is exactly what makes Alan a **clean, un-foolable receiver** of truth and revelation. The thing that denies him the survival-painkiller (he can't believe his way out of misery, as [Jen can](disputed-misery.book-chapter.md#jens-doubt-is-plausibly-protective-not-an-error)) is the same thing that means a false revelation can't take root in him either. One mechanism, two faces: the cost of involuntary belief and the discernment-gift of involuntary belief are the same fact.
