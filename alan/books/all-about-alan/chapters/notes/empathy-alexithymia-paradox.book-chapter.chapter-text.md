
# Empathy + alexithymia paradox

- Increased empathy alongside total emotional aphantasia and alexithymia is seemingly contradictory but isn't: alexithymia is about recall/imagination, while empathy is about in-moment response.
- The mechanism: aphantasia removes the ability to remember or imagine emotions, but the in-moment nervous-system response to others' emotions remains intact and is amplified by autistic sensitivity and non-habituation.
- Revision for the distress case (2026-06-12): when the other person's emotion is distress, the intact in-moment response is not transmitted empathy but a conditioned threat forecast about Alan's own near future — self-referential, freeze-producing, carrying no content about the other person's interior. The hardware observation stands; the empathy label does not, for that case. Canonical treatment: [distress-contagion.md](distress-contagion.book-chapter.md).
