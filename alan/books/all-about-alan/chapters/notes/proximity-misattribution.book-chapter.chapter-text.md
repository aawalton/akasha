
# Proximity misattribution — the pain ratchet

> Proximity misattribution — the conditioning layer assigns blame by temporal proximity, not causality. Pain that lands during a disclosure moment punishes telling even when the rational ledger blames the withholding. Paired with a conservative rule it produces a one-way pain ratchet that cannot self-correct; the only loosening force is felt safety over time. The same trainer explains distress-contagion's encoding.

Conditioning does not assign blame the way reasoning does. **The conditioning layer trains on temporal proximity, not causality:** whatever behavior was running when the pain arrived is what gets punished, regardless of what actually caused the pain. The rational ledger can identify the true cause and still lose, because the nervous system was never consulting the ledger — it was consulting the clock.

This is a general property of the trainer, not a quirk of one domain. Two of Alan's documented patterns run on it: the honesty ratchet (the worked case below) and the encoding of [distress contagion](distress-contagion.book-chapter.md).

## The worked case — the honesty ratchet

Alan's honesty modulation runs on a blanket rule rather than a per-case detector — "I have learned through pain to mostly not be honest when the honesty would hurt the other person." The component-level sorting (native expression, simulator-gated calibration, the rule as compensation) lives in [love-decomposition.md → honesty](love-decomposition.book-chapter.md#sorting-the-components--the-walk). What lives here is what the rule's training signal does over time.

A blanket conservative rule has false positives: truths Jen needed that the rule withheld. Those failures are real but structurally unobservable — "I'm certain that error class happens, but those failures are counterfactual and invisible to me." A withheld truth produces no pain in any present moment, so nothing pings. This is the [painless-drift blind spot](pain-driven-motivation.book-chapter.md#the-blind-spot--painless-slow-drifts), honesty edition: the error class that generates no signal never enters the system that would correct it.

At least once, the error class did surface — a withholding failure became visible. The rational lesson was "disclose more." The conditioned lesson ran the other way: "it was so painful that it pushed the rule farther in the direction of non-disclosure." The mechanism is the misattribution: the pain arrived *during the disclosure moment*, so the nervous system punished telling, while the rational ledger blamed the withholding. Alan confirmed the proposed mechanism as "the direction of the pain ratchet."

## The one-way ratchet

Put the two pieces together and the loop has only one direction it can move:

- **Over-disclosure failures** — truth told, pain follows. Pain lands during telling; telling gets punished. The rule tightens.
- **Withholding failures** — truth withheld, harm follows. Almost all are counterfactual and invisible: no pain, no training signal, no correction. The rare one that surfaces still delivers its pain in a disclosure-adjacent moment — and punishes telling anyway. The rule tightens.

Every observable failure, whichever direction it actually ran, trains the same correction: toward silence. The loop cannot self-correct, because the training data is one-sided twice over — one error class is mostly invisible, and the visible instances of *both* classes are misattributed to the same behavior.

## The release — safety

The ratchet has exactly one loosening force. Verbatim: **"The release comes slowly with a feeling of safety over time."** Not argument, not evidence, not a counter-example — the rational ledger already disagrees with the rule and loses. What loosens the conditioned grip is sustained felt safety.

This makes [Safety](safety.book-chapter.md) the master variable yet again: it gates emotional support at level 5, vulnerability at level 4, and is the only force that loosens the honesty ratchet. Three separate care-adjacent capacities, one upstream resource.

## Generalization — distress contagion's encoding

The same trainer explains the [distress-contagion](distress-contagion.book-chapter.md) reflex. Distress cues in others temporally preceded distress in Alan, so the cue itself got encoded as the trigger — a forecast trained on succession, carrying no model of the causal path between her distress and his (and no payload about her interior, because the trainer never needed one). Same operation as the honesty case: the conditioning layer binds the response to what was present when the pain arrived, blind to the causal structure the rational ledger would draw.

The two cases also share the extinction problem. Distress contagion resists extinction because its variable-ratio schedule makes safe exposures look like training data; the honesty ratchet resists correction because its corrective error class produces no observable trials at all. Different mechanisms, same result: a conditioned rule the evidence cannot reach.
