
Romanian permanent residence (`drept de ședere pe termen lung`) generally requires **5 years of continuous lawful temporary residence** (absences ≤ 6 consecutive months, ≤ 10 months total over the 5 years), Romanian-language proof, livelihood at least at the gross minimum wage, health insurance, and clean record. Study time on a D/SD permit counts at **half rate** toward the long-term residence clock. After PR, naturalization under Law 21/1991 art. 8 requires a **total of 8 years lawful residence** (any combination of temporary + permanent permits counts 1:1 toward art. 8) plus B1 Romanian, Constitution + national anthem ("Deșteaptă-te, române!") interview, and proof of livelihood — so the realistic floor is ~10–11 years to passport. Romania entered Schengen by air/sea March 2024 and by land January 2025, which lifts internal-border friction but does **not** alter the residency or naturalization clock. Romania has **no CBI**; the proposed €400k "Golden Visa" residency-by-investment law (announced October 2025) is still in the legislative pipeline as of May 2026 and produces residency only.

## Active programs (matching the filter)

### Long-Stay Visa for Economic / Liberal-Profession Activities (D/AE, D/AP — PFA route)

- **Threshold**: D/AE for sole-trader economic activities (`Persoană Fizică Autorizată` — PFA / `Întreprindere Individuală` — II); D/AP for regulated liberal professions (lawyer, architect, doctor, translator, IT consultant under a recognized liberal-profession code). Proof of **subsistence funds at least at the Romanian gross minimum wage × 12 months** (~RON 48,600 / ~€9,800 in early 2026); credible business plan / liberal-profession qualification; Romanian-registered office; clean criminal record. Catch: a PFA can only be registered after the residence permit is issued (chicken-and-egg: enter on the D-visa, then register PFA + apply for the residence permit in-country).
- **Validity**: long-stay **visa** 90 days, **single entry**; convert to a 1-year **temporary residence permit** at IGI inside RO; renewable in 1-year increments.
- **Physical presence**: no statutory minimum-days rule on the permit, but absences > 6 consecutive months (or > 10 months across the 5-year PR window) break the long-term-residence continuity. Genuine PFA activity (tax declarations, social contributions) is checked at renewal.
- **PR / naturalization clock**: counts **1:1** toward both the 5-year PR window and the 8-year art. 8 naturalization clock. Standard B1 Romanian + Constitution / anthem exam at citizenship stage.
- **Recent reforms**: 2026 fiscal package (active January 2026) raised PFA contributions — CAS 25% above 12 minimum wages, CASS 10% above 6 minimum wages — making the budget threshold tighter; no visa-policy change.
- **Processing**: visa decision **30–60 days** at the consulate (statutory 30 days + up to 15-day extension); in-country permit issuance **~30 days** at IGI.

### Long-Stay Visa for Commercial Activities (D/AC — SRL / SA owner-administrator)

- **Threshold**: foreign shareholder / associate with management or administrative role in a Romanian SRL or SA. Within **12 months** of permit issuance, must either (a) inject **≥ €100,000** in capital or technology contribution **and** create ≥ 10 jobs (SRL) or ≥ 15 jobs (SA), or (b) create those jobs as an alternative to the €100k contribution. Ministry of Economy approval required up front.
- **Validity**: visa 90 days → 1-year temporary residence permit, renewable annually.
- **Physical presence**: same as D/AE — no fixed day count, but genuine company activity is checked; PR-continuity rules (6 / 10-month absence caps) apply.
- **PR / naturalization clock**: 1:1 toward 5-year PR and 8-year art. 8.
- **Recent reforms**: no headline 2024–2026 change. The €50,000 lower-tier "company administrator" path under the IGI "other purposes" category (single administrator, no jobs requirement) sits below D/AC and feeds the same clocks.
- **Processing**: Ministry of Economy approval **30–60 days**; visa **30–60 days**; in-country permit ~30 days.
- **Capital-fit verdict**: €100k (D/AC) and €50k (administrator variant) are both **under the €500k cap**; included.

### Long-Stay Visa for Other Purposes — Digital Nomad Permit (DNV)

- **Threshold**: remote employment by a non-Romanian company **or** ownership of a non-Romanian company; proof of monthly income **≥ 3× Romanian gross average salary** — the Ministry-published benchmark currently lands near **€3,700–€3,950 / month** (figure tracks the official average each year); income must be sustained for the **6 months preceding** the visa application; Romanian accommodation; private health insurance; clean record.
- **Validity**: long-stay **visa** 90 days → 6-month digital-nomad **residence permit** at IGI; renewable in 6-month or 1-year increments up to a **maximum of 3 years cumulative** on the nomad track before switching purpose.
- **Physical presence**: no minimum-days requirement to maintain the permit; continuity rules apply at PR stage.
- **PR / naturalization clock**: counts **1:1** toward the 5-year PR window **and** the 8-year art. 8 naturalization clock; but the **3-year cap** on the nomad track means most applicants must transition to PFA / D/AC / family before completing either clock. DNV holders are tax-resident only if present > 183 days; below that, the income remains taxed abroad.
- **Recent reforms**: program created by Law 22/2022, operational since January 2022; January 2025 schengen-land entry removed internal-border friction for EU travel from a Bucharest base. No threshold change in 2024–2026; the €3,700–€3,950 income benchmark drifts annually with the average-salary index.
- **Processing**: consular decision **15–30 days**; in-country permit ~30 days at IGI.

### Long-Stay Visa for Family Reunification (D/VF)

- **Threshold**: sponsor holds (a) a temporary residence permit valid ≥ 1 year, (b) an EU Blue Card / ICT / Mobile-ICT permit, (c) a long-term residence permit, or (d) refugee / subsidiary-protection status. Eligible family members: spouse, unmarried minor children (own or spouse's, including adopted), dependent parents in limited cases. Stable income at the gross minimum wage level + housing + insurance. Romanian citizens sponsor under a separate but parallel D/VF track.
- **Validity**: visa 90 days → 1-year temporary residence permit matching or exceeding the sponsor's permit; renewable annually. Family members of permit holders gain labour-market access without a separate work permit after 12 months.
- **Physical presence**: cohabitation with the sponsor is checked at renewal; standard PR-continuity caps apply.
- **PR / naturalization clock**: 1:1 toward 5-year PR and 8-year art. 8. Spouses of Romanian citizens additionally qualify for **Article 8(2) reduced naturalization** — 5 years from the marriage date with continuous cohabitation in RO (instead of 8 years).
- **Recent reforms**: no material 2024–2026 change. Decision deadline 3 months from filing.
- **Processing**: family-reunification approval **~90 days**; visa **30–60 days**; in-country permit ~30 days.

### Long-Stay Visa for Studies (D/SD)

- **Threshold**: admission to an accredited Romanian university or pre-university institution (state or accredited private); subsistence funds; accommodation; insurance; clean record. Tuition payable (often modest at state universities; medicine and dentistry programs in English are the most common foreign-applicant track).
- **Validity**: visa 90 days → 1-year temporary residence permit for studies, renewable annually for the duration of the programme; up to 20 hours/week of work without a separate work permit.
- **Physical presence**: enrolment workload; standard PR-continuity caps apply.
- **PR / naturalization clock**: study time counts at **half rate** toward the 5-year PR window; treatment toward the art. 8 clock is the **same half rate**. Realistic use is as a **bridge** — finish degree, switch to PFA / D/AC / D/VF, and the half-count study time still contributes.
- **Recent reforms**: no material 2024–2026 change; EU Students & Researchers Directive transposed.
- **Processing**: visa **30 days** statutory; in-country permit ~30 days.

### Proposed: Romania Golden Visa (Residency-by-Investment)

- **Threshold (proposed)**: **€400,000** in one of four asset classes — Romanian government bonds (≥ 5-yr maturity), Romanian real estate, ASF-authorised investment funds, or listed Romanian shares — held for ≥ 5 years. **5-year renewable residence permit; no physical-presence minimum to maintain the permit.**
- **Status**: **legislative pipeline as of May 2026** — bill announced October 2025, not yet enacted. If passed, produces residency only; citizenship still requires the standard 8-year art. 8 clock.
- **Capital-fit verdict**: €400k is **under the €500k cap**; included as a contingent program. Note: the "no physical presence" feature **defeats the naturalization clock** — art. 8 counts only effective lawful residence, so a Golden-Visa holder living abroad accrues zero passport-eligible time. Useful as Schengen mobility / optionality, not as a citizenship path.

## Filtered out

- **D/AS investor visa for amounts ≥ €1 million** — capital-locked above the €500k cap. Excluded. (The smaller €50k–€100k business-administrator and D/AC routes are kept above.)
- **D/AM employment visa** — employer-sponsored worker track. Excluded.
- **EU Blue Card (RO)** — highly-skilled employer-sponsored track. Excluded.
- **Intra-Corporate Transferee (ICT / Mobile-ICT)** — multinational employer-sponsored. Excluded.
- **D/CS detached worker visa, D/AM seasonal worker** — employer-sponsored / time-capped. Excluded.
- **D/SR scientific research** — host-institution agreement required (effectively endorsement-based). Excluded.
- **Retirement / pensioner visa** — Romania has **no named retiree visa**; the closest analog is the D/AS "other purposes" residual category (pension proof + ~3× minimum-wage income), which works in practice but is consular-discretionary, narrow, and not a stable program. Treat as a fallback, not a primary path.
- **Religious / humanitarian / volunteer / medical-treatment / unpaid-training "other purposes"** — non-PR-feeding lifestyle or single-purpose categories. Excluded per filter.
- **EU long-term residence (post-PR directive permit)** — derives from PR, not an entry visa. Out of scope.

## Summary verdict

Romania retains **five viable PR-feeding residency paths** for the applicant profile (US citizen, adult, no EU ancestry, portable self-employment income, ≤ €500k capital):

- **Self-employment**: D/AE PFA (workhorse) and D/AP liberal profession.
- **Owner-administrator**: D/AC (€100k + 10 jobs) or the €50k single-administrator "other purposes" variant.
- **Remote work for foreign employer**: D/VS Digital Nomad track (€3,700–€3,950/month income, capped at 3 years before switching).
- **Family**: D/VF reunification (if a qualifying sponsor exists; spouse-of-citizen also unlocks the art. 8(2) 5-year reduced naturalization).
- **Bridge**: D/SD studies, useful as a half-count on-ramp.

Every surviving path feeds the same **8-year art. 8 naturalization clock** with B1 Romanian + Constitution / anthem interview + livelihood — Romania offers **no naturalization acceleration** for capital, talent, or general distinguished contribution outside the narrow art. 8¹/8² discretionary channels. The strongest realistic anchor for a US self-employed applicant is **PFA (D/AE)** with the **Digital Nomad permit** as a low-friction first 6–36 months before pivoting to PFA. The proposed €400k Golden Visa, even if enacted, does not feed naturalization because it carries no physical-presence requirement.
