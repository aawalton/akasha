
Japan organizes immigration around the Status of Residence (在留資格 zairyū shikaku) system administered by the Immigration Services Agency (ISA). Status determines permitted activities and the ladder to Permanent Residence (永住権 eijūken). The 2025–2026 reform cycle materially tightened the Business Manager and Permanent Residence regimes; the Highly Skilled Professional fast-track and the new Digital Nomad status remain the bright spots.

## Highly skilled work statuses (PR fast-track)

### Highly Skilled Foreign Professional — HSP (i)/(ii)
- **Eligibility**: points-based system scoring education, work experience, annual salary, age, research output, plus bonus points (JLPT N1 +15 / N2 +10; designated/SGU university grad +10; J-Startup employer +10–20). 70+ points → HSP(i); 5 years on HSP(i) plus continued ≥70 points → HSP(ii) (no period-of-stay limit).
- **PR fast-track**: 70+ points held continuously → PR after **3 years**; 80+ points held continuously → PR after **1 year**.
- **Other benefits**: 5-year period of stay from outset, work in multiple activities, spouse can work full-time, expedited processing of dependants, domestic helper visa allowed at higher score tiers.
- **Recent changes**: J-Startup bonus expanded in 2024; the points calculator remains the binding gate.

### J-Skip — Special Highly-Skilled Professional (launched April 2023)
- **Eligibility (no points test)**: master's degree + ≥¥20M annual income, OR ≥10 years of professional experience + ≥¥20M annual income. For researchers, ≥¥10M plus equivalent academic credentials.
- **Benefits**: 5-year period of stay, eligibility for PR after 1 year, broad work permissions equivalent to HSP(ii). No points-arithmetic uncertainty — pass the salary/credential gate and you're in.

### J-Find — Future Creation Individual (launched April 2023)
- **Eligibility**: graduate of a top-100 global university (per QS/THE/Shanghai rankings) within the last 5 years.
- **Status**: Designated Activities (告示 No. 51) — 2-year job-search/business-launch stay, renewable once. Conversion to a regular work or business status when you land a role or start a company.

## Standard work statuses

### Engineer / Specialist in Humanities / International Services (技人国)
- **Eligibility**: contract with a Japanese employer for white-collar work requiring (a) STEM knowledge, (b) liberal-arts/professional knowledge, or (c) cultural sensitivity unique to a foreign national (e.g., language instruction, international marketing). University degree or 10+ years' relevant experience; pay parity with Japanese counterparts (~¥250k+/month entry baseline).
- **Period of stay**: 1, 3, or 5 years; renewable indefinitely. PR pathway: standard 10-year route (see below).

### Intra-Company Transferee (企業内転勤)
- **Eligibility**: employed ≥1 year at a foreign office of the same corporate group, transferred to the Japanese entity for work that falls within Engineer / Specialist in Humanities / International Services scope. No degree requirement and no academic gate — the 1-year tenure substitutes.
- **Period of stay**: 1, 3, or 5 years. Counts toward PR residence the same as the underlying Engineer/Specialist scope.

### Business Manager — heavily tightened October 2025
- **Eligibility (effective 16 October 2025)**:
  - Registered capital ≥ **¥30M** (raised from ¥5M — 6× increase, no longer one of several alternatives)
  - Hire ≥1 full-time employee with unrestricted work status (Japanese / PR / Special PR / Spouse of Japanese or PR)
  - JLPT N2 / CEFR B2 Japanese for the applicant OR a full-time employee
  - 3+ years of management experience OR a master's / doctoral / professional degree
  - Business plan must pass review by a designated specialist
- **Transition**: existing Business Manager holders extending within 3 years of the amendment are assessed on current performance; after the 3-year window, the new ¥30M floor binds renewals.
- **PR pathway**: standard 10-year route. Japan has no traditional Golden Visa; Business Manager is the closest analog and is now substantially harder to obtain than the headline ¥5M figure once suggested.

## Specified Skilled Worker (SSW) — labor-shortage sectors

### SSW (i)
- **Eligibility**: pass a sector-specific skills exam and a Japanese language test (JLPT N4 or JFT-Basic A2) in one of **16 designated sectors** (expanded from 14 in 2024–early 2025). Technical Intern Training graduates may transfer in-sector without exams.
- **Period**: cumulative cap of 5 years. **No family accompaniment.** Mandatory support from a registered support organization.
- **2026 note**: new SSW(i) applications for the restaurant sector were **suspended on 13 April 2026** as the 50,000-worker sector cap approached saturation.

### SSW (ii)
- **Eligibility**: advanced sector-specific exam plus demonstrated supervisory/team-lead experience. Expanded since August 2023 to nearly all SSW sectors except care work.
- **Benefits**: indefinitely renewable, **family accompaniment allowed**, qualifies toward standard PR.

## Long-term, family, and ancestry-based statuses

### Long-Term Resident (定住者 Teijūsha)
- **Eligibility**: discretionary status granted on humanitarian or ancestry grounds. Primary populations: Nikkei 2nd–4th-generation (Japanese-descent foreigners, most Brazilian/Peruvian), divorced/widowed spouses of Japanese nationals with continued connection to Japan, refugees adjacent cases.
- **Benefits**: no work restrictions whatsoever — work in any field without status conversion. Period of stay up to 5 years.

### Spouse or Child of Japanese National
- **Eligibility**: legally recognized spouse or biological/adopted minor child of a Japanese national.
- **Period of stay**: 6 months, 1, 3, or 5 years; no work restriction.
- **PR pathway**: PR eligible after 3 years of marriage + 1 year of continuous residence in Japan.

### Spouse or Child of Permanent Resident
- **Eligibility**: spouse/minor child of a Permanent Resident or Special Permanent Resident.
- **PR pathway**: standard residence requirements relaxed.

### Special Permanent Resident (特別永住者)
- **Eligibility**: residual category for former Japanese nationals and their descendants who lost Japanese nationality under the 1952 San Francisco Peace Treaty implementation — primarily Zainichi Koreans and Taiwanese. Closed category in practice (no new entrants outside this lineage).
- **Benefits**: stronger than ordinary PR — broader deportation protection, simpler re-entry, intergenerational transmission to children born in Japan.

## Permanent Residence (永住権 Eijūken)

### Standard pathway
- **Eligibility**: 10 years continuous residence in Japan, **of which 5+ on a work-type or spouse-type status**. Continuous = no single absence over 6 months and minimal cumulative absences.
- **Compliance**: clean tax, residence-tax, health insurance, and pension payment record. Independent livelihood. Good conduct.
- **2026 tightening (effective 24 February 2026)**: the ISA now requires the applicant to hold the **longest available period of stay** for their visa category (typically 5 years) at the time of application. Holders of 3-year periods of stay as of 31 March 2027 may still apply under the old rules until that date; after, the 5-year-period requirement is absolute.
- **2024 grounds for revocation**: a 2024 law amendment added new grounds for revoking PR (notably tax/pension delinquency and certain criminal offenses), shifting PR from "near-irrevocable" toward "conditional on continued compliance."

### HSP fast-track to PR
- 70+ points sustained 3 years → PR after **3 years** of residence
- 80+ points sustained 1 year → PR after **1 year** of residence
- J-Skip: PR eligible after **1 year**

## Short / specialized statuses

### Digital Nomad (Designated Activities No. 53) — launched March 2024
- **Eligibility**: annual income ≥**¥10M** (~USD $68k) from non-Japanese sources, private health insurance ≥¥10M coverage, employment with a non-Japanese entity or self-employment with non-Japanese clients only. Eligible nationals from countries with which Japan has a tax treaty and a visa-waiver arrangement (US included).
- **Period of stay**: 6 months, **non-renewable**. Cooling-off period before re-application. Spouse/children admitted on the dependant variant without separate income tests.
- **No PR pathway** — time on this status does not count toward PR residence.

### Working Holiday (Designated Activities)
- **Eligibility**: ages 18–30 from one of the 32 partner countries/regions (as of April 2026). **United States is not a participant** in the Working Holiday Programme — US citizens cannot use this route.
- **Period of stay**: typically 1 year. Work permitted as incidental to holidays.

### Cultural Activities (文化活動)
- **Eligibility**: unpaid study of traditional Japanese arts (tea ceremony, ikebana, martial arts, classical music, calligraphy, etc.) or unpaid academic research. No compensation permitted.

### Religious Activities (宗教)
- **Eligibility**: dispatched clergy / missionaries of a recognized religious organization with a Japanese host body. Period of stay up to 5 years.

### Student (留学)
- **Eligibility**: enrollment at a recognized Japanese educational institution. Limited part-time work (28 hrs/week during term) by separate permit. Time on student status counts only partially toward PR — typically only the half-period or post-graduation work period counts in the 10-year tally.

### Refugee status and Complementary Protection
- **Refugee status**: notoriously rare — **190 refugees recognized in 2024 (1.5% acceptance rate)**, primarily Afghans, Burmese, Yemenis, Palestinians, Chinese (political opinion).
- **Complementary Protection (準難民, established 2023)**: separate track for evacuees from armed conflict. In 2024, **1,616 grants — 1,613 of them Ukrainian**. Protections equivalent to refugee status.
- **2025 amendment**: asylum applications capped at 2 attempts; deportation permissible after the second failure unless new evidence is produced.

## Path summary for a US citizen with no Japanese ancestry

- **Fastest to PR**: J-Skip (¥20M+ income, 1 year) > HSP 80+ (1 year) > HSP 70+ (3 years) > spouse-of-Japanese (3 yrs marriage + 1 yr residence) > everyone else (10 years).
- **Fastest to citizenship**: spouse of Japanese national under Article 7 (1 year residence + 3 years marriage) — the only sub-3-year statutory residence route, but still 12–24 months MOJ processing on top and now ~10 years of residence-history evidence required in practice (April 2026 screening change).
- **No realistic Golden Visa**: Business Manager at ¥30M capital + employee + JLPT N2 + management background is no longer a casual route.
