
## Skilled / employer-sponsored pathways

### Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) Resident Visa — 6-point system
From 9 October 2023, SMC switched from the legacy 180-point system to a streamlined 6-point system. Applicants need ≥6 points from one *primary* skill source (NZ occupational registration, qualification, or income — 3 to 6 points), topped up by skilled NZ work experience (1 point per year, up to 3) if needed. A skilled job (or job offer) with an Accredited Employer paid at or above the median wage (ANZSCO 1–3) or 1.5× median (ANZSCO 4–5) is required. No cap on numbers; straightforward complete applications target 6–8 weeks. Direct route to residence.

### Green List — Tier 1 (Straight to Residence)
A government-published list of high-skill roles in critical shortage. A qualifying job offer plus the role-specific registration / qualification / experience criteria gives the **Straight to Residence Visa** — residence in one step, no work-visa interlude. Includes general practitioners, structural engineers, construction project managers, speech-language therapists, and (since March 2025) primary school teachers. Applicants must be ≤55 at lodgement and meet health, character, and minimum-English standards.

### Green List — Tier 2 (Work to Residence)
For trades and skilled roles slightly below Tier 1. Holder takes an AEWV in a Green List Tier-2 role, works 24 months in the listed occupation in NZ, then applies for residence under the **Work to Residence Visa**. Typical occupations: electrician, plumber, automotive technician, welder.

### Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV)
Replaced the Essential Skills Work Visa in mid-2022 and is the main employer-sponsored temporary work visa. Three-step model: employer accreditation, job check, migrant visa. Major March 2025 reforms:
- Median-wage threshold removed; only the minimum wage applies (NZD 23.50/hr from 1 April 2025).
- Migrant work-experience requirement cut from 3 years to 2.
- ANZSCO Level 4–5 AEWV duration extended to 3 years (matching max stay).
- MSD-engagement requirement for lower-skilled roles became declaration-based.
- Application form simplified from 7 July 2025; ~90 new higher-skilled NOL roles to be recognised from 3 November 2025.
- AEWV itself is *not* a direct residence visa; holders transition via SMC, Green List, or a Work-to-Residence pathway.

## Investor / business pathways

### Active Investor Plus visa — April 2025 reform (the standout 2025 change)
The Active Investor Plus visa (introduced Sep 2022 to replace Investor 1 / Investor 2) was substantially loosened from **1 April 2025** into two tracks:
- **Growth category**: minimum NZD 5M, held for 3 years, into higher-risk investments (managed funds and direct investments in NZ businesses).
- **Balanced category**: minimum NZD 10M, held for 5 years, with broadened acceptable assets including bonds and certain property-type investments.
- Minimum investment dropped from the prior NZD 15M.
- English-language requirement removed.
- Reduced physical-presence requirements: minimal stay days across the hold period (Growth: 21 days over 3 yrs; Balanced: 105 days over 5 yrs).
Uptake has been strong: by 5 May 2026, INZ had received 688 applications covering 2,260 applicants (573 Growth / 115 Balanced); 263 approved with NZD 1.56B in committed investment.

### Business Investor Work Visa (opening November 2025)
Closes and replaces the legacy Entrepreneur Work Visa. Two tiers: NZD 1M invested in an existing NZ business (3-year work-to-residence track) or NZD 2M (12-month fast-track to residence). Applicant must have ≥NZD 500k for personal/family support and invest in a business employing ≥5 FTE.

### Entrepreneur Work Visa (closed to new applications)
Closed in 2025 ahead of the Business Investor Work Visa. Historical settings: NZD 100k investment + business plan + 120 points; 12-month start-up phase then 24 months; science/ICT high-innovation cases could waive the NZD 100k floor.

## Family-stream pathways

### Partner of a New Zealander Resident Visa
For partners of NZ citizens / residents in a genuine and stable partnership ≥12 months. Direct route to residence (no temporary stepping stone required if the relationship is established). After 2 years on the resident visa with continued partnership, holder can apply for a Permanent Resident Visa.

### Parent Resident Visa
Capped, sponsor-income-tested resident pathway. Two-tier ballot since 2022; sponsor income thresholds indexed to median wage and lifted in Feb 2025 (sponsor must meet NZD 33.56/hr × multiples depending on family composition). Long queues — multi-year wait.

### Parent Boost Visitor Visa (opened 29 September 2025)
New long-stay visitor visa (not residence) for parents of NZ citizens / residents. Up to 5 years, multi-entry, renewable once for a total of 10 years. Requires sponsor or applicant income (NZD 33,663/yr single / 51,182/yr couple per Apr 2026 thresholds), funds (NZD 170k / 260k), 12 months of accepted health insurance, and a mid-visa departure + medical at years 3–4. No dependent children allowed.

### Family-stream other
**Dependent Child Resident Visa** (under 24, single, no children of own) for children of residents; **Sibling / Adult Child** categories were closed years ago and remain closed.

## Pacific / Realm pathways

### Pacific Access Category (PAC) Resident Visa
Annual ballot for citizens of Tonga (250), Fiji (250), Kiribati (75), and Tuvalu (75) aged 18–45 with an acceptable NZ job offer. 2025 quotas unchanged. Direct residence on selection + job offer.

### Samoan Quota Resident Visa
Parallel scheme for Samoa, quota 1,650/year (drawn 21 May 2025).

### Recognised Seasonal Employer (RSE) Limited Visa
Temporary seasonal worker visa for citizens of Fiji, Kiribati, Nauru, Palau, PNG, Samoa, Solomon Islands, Timor-Leste, Tonga, Tuvalu, Vanuatu. Up to 7 months in any 11-month period (9 months for Kiribati / Tuvalu). No direct residence pathway, but service can support later AEWV/SMC moves.

## Study / youth pathways

### International student visa + Post-Study Work Visa (PSWV)
NZ student visa is study-purpose. After completing an eligible NZ qualification (NZQCF Level 7 bachelor's or higher, or Level 4–6 with ≥30 weeks), graduates can apply within 3 months (6 months for PhDs) for the PSWV — up to 3 years of open work. Late-2026 expansion: PSWV eligibility broadens to graduate-diploma holders who completed a bachelor's elsewhere; new Short-Term Graduate Work Visa added. PSWV time on a residence-class visa does *not* count toward citizenship — only post-residence days do.

### Working Holiday Scheme (US-eligible)
US citizens aged 18–30 are eligible for a 12-month working holiday visa. The 12-month *extension* program available to UK/Canadian passport holders does **not** extend to US citizens — US holders qualify only for a 3-month Working Holidaymaker Extension Visa after ≥3 months horticultural/viticultural work. Not a residence path on its own; serves as a bridge to AEWV / SMC.

## Remote / nomad pathway

### Digital nomad — visitor visa with remote-work allowance (Jan 2025)
There is no dedicated digital-nomad visa. From 27 January 2025, all visitor visas (including NZeTA-arrivals) permit remote work for an overseas employer or overseas clients. Standard 3-month stay (extendable to 9 months in a 12-month window) on the visitor visa; no minimum income requirement; tax exemption typically applies if the visitor stays ≤92 days in any 12-month period. Not a residence pathway and no direct upgrade to residence.

## Closed-but-recent transitional categories

### 2021 Resident Visa
One-off COVID-era residence pathway. Applications open 1 December 2021 – 31 July 2022. ~106,349 applications for 217,586 people; ~103,961 approved (~212,649 residents). Now fully closed; included here for completeness of recent NZ residence history.

## Recent changes (2024–2026)

- **Apr 2025**: Active Investor Plus restructured to Growth (NZD 5M / 3 yr) and Balanced (NZD 10M / 5 yr); English requirement removed; presence requirements slashed.
- **Mar 2025**: AEWV — median-wage requirement removed, work-experience floor cut 3→2 years, Level 4–5 visas extended to 3 years, MSD engagement made declaration-based.
- **Sep 2025**: Parent Boost Visitor Visa opened.
- **Nov 2025**: Business Investor Work Visa opens; Entrepreneur Work Visa closed.
- **Jan 2025**: Visitor visas allow remote work for overseas employer.
- **Mar 2025**: Primary School Teachers promoted from Green List Tier 2 to Tier 1.
