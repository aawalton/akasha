
## Summary

Denmark is one of the most restrictive EU residence regimes for non-EU/EEA/Swiss citizens. For an adult US citizen with no Danish family tie, no Danish employer sponsor, and portable self-employment / passive income, **no general residence permit is available**. Denmark has no retirement / passive-income visa, no digital nomad visa, no golden visa, and no non-endorsement self-employment route. The old self-employment scheme was closed to new applicants on **1 January 2015** and replaced by Start-up Denmark, which is endorsement-gated (independent expert panel) and therefore filtered out per the project criteria. Family reunification and a PhD study permit are technically available but neither matches the applicant profile (no Danish spouse/partner, not pursuing a Danish PhD).

## Filtered out (do not match profile)

### Start-up Denmark — endorsement-based, filtered out

- Non-EU/EEA/Swiss self-employed founders must apply with a business plan **evaluated by an independent expert panel** at the Danish Business Authority. Approval by the panel is a precondition to filing the residence-and-work permit with SIRI. ([nyidanmark — Start-up Denmark](https://www.nyidanmark.dk/en-GB/You-want-to-apply/Work/Start-up-Denmark); [Danish Business Authority — Start-up Denmark](https://danishbusinessauthority.dk/start-denmark))
- Business must be **innovative and scalable** and serve "particular Danish interests"; restaurants, retail shops, consultancy firms, and import/export are explicitly out of scope. ([startupdenmarkprogramme.dk](https://startupdenmarkprogramme.dk/))
- Permit: 2 years initial, extendable in 3-year increments. Not directly relevant — endorsement filter applies.

### General self-employment permit — closed to new applicants since 2015

- Denmark's pre-2015 self-employment residence permit was abolished on **1 January 2015**. Only **extensions** for permits issued before that date are processed; new applicants must go through Start-up Denmark (above). ([nyidanmark — Self-employment extension, original permit issued before 1 January 2015](https://www.nyidanmark.dk/en-GB/Extension/Arbejde/Self-employment%20-%20old%20rules))
- The legacy criteria (active participation, "particular Danish business interests", innovative potential) are now operationalized via the Start-up Denmark expert panel — there is no parallel non-endorsement self-employment track. ([EU Immigration Portal — Self-employed worker in Denmark](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/migration-and-asylum/eu-immigration-portal/self-employed-worker-denmark_en))

### Researcher / Pay Limit / Positive List / Fast-track — employer-sponsored, filtered out

- All Danish skilled-worker schemes (Pay Limit, Positive List for the Highly Educated, Fast-track, Researcher tax scheme) require a concrete Danish employment offer from an authorized employer. Excluded by the employer-sponsorship filter and by the applicant profile (portable self-employment income, no Danish employer).

### Retirement / passive-income visa — does not exist

- **Denmark has no retirement visa and no passive-income / financially-independent-person permit.** Unlike Portugal (D7) or Spain (NLV), there is no route to residency by demonstrating non-employment income. ([Smart Asset — How to Retire in Denmark](https://smartasset.com/retirement/how-to-retire-in-denmark-costs-visas-and-more); [Unbiased — Retiring in Denmark](https://www.unbiased.com/discover/retirement/retire-in-denmark))
- The only retirement-adjacent route is the standard family-reunification / partner permit, which presupposes a Danish-resident spouse or partner.

### Digital nomad visa — does not exist

- **Denmark does not offer a digital nomad visa.** Remote work on a Schengen tourist entry is not authorized; staying >90 days requires a substantive residence permit, of which none fit a remote freelancer with foreign clients. ([Citizen Remote — Denmark Digital Nomad Visa (status)](https://citizenremote.com/visas/denmark-digital-nomad-visa/); [IOM Denmark — Overview: Digital Nomad Policies in the European Context](https://denmark.iom.int/sites/g/files/tmzbdl1306/files/documents/TalentHub/overview.-digital-nomad-policies-in-the-european-context_clean.pdf))

### Golden visa / investor — does not exist

- **Denmark has no golden visa, no residence-by-investment, and no citizenship-by-investment program.** Capital alone confers no immigration status; passive shareholding is explicitly insufficient. ([citizenx — Denmark Golden Visa](https://citizenx.com/insights/denmark-golden-visa/))

## Possible but profile-mismatched

### Family reunification — requires Danish spouse/partner

- Available if married to or in ≥18-month documented cohabitation with a Danish citizen or a long-resident foreigner with an indefinite permit (held >3 years; in practice ≥8 years legal residence). Both spouses must be ≥24 ("24-year rule"); a 4-of-6 integration test (education, work, language) applies; the sponsor must post a DKK 61,709.34 (2026) bank guarantee. ([nyidanmark — Family reunification as a spouse](https://www.nyidanmark.dk/en-GB/You-want-to-apply/Family/Family-reunification/Spouse-or-cohabiting-partner))
- Mismatch: requires a Danish-resident partner the applicant does not have.

### PhD residence permit — requires admission to a Danish PhD programme

- Non-EU/EEA PhD students get a residence-and-work permit covering enrolment (up to 5 years for 3+5 / 4+4 flexible models) plus a **3-year post-graduation job-seeking permit** automatically granted by SIRI. Financial-support proof of 12 × DKK 7,426 = DKK 89,112 (2026 level) for stays of ≥1 year. ([nyidanmark — PhD studies (full degree)](https://www.nyidanmark.dk/en-GB/You-want-to-apply/PhD/Phd-studies); [University of Copenhagen — Residence permit, non-EU/EEA](https://ism.ku.dk/residence-permit/non-eu-eea/))
- Mismatch: requires admission to a Danish doctoral programme; portable self-employment income is not the qualifying activity.

## Verdict for applicant profile

**No surviving non-employer / non-endorsement / non-renunciation residency path.** Recommend dropping Denmark from the citizenship-via-residency working set:

- No self-employment route survives (only Start-up Denmark, endorsement-gated; old route closed Jan 2015).
- No retirement / passive-income / digital nomad visa exists.
- No golden visa exists.
- Family reunification requires a Danish-side sponsor.
- PhD route requires Danish doctoral admission and a 6+ year academic commitment.
- Even on the citizenship side, Denmark requires ~9 years' continuous residence, Danish language at PD3, full-time work, and (since 2015) renunciation of prior citizenship — see [../citizenship/denmark.md](../citizenship/denmark.book-chapter.md). Without a viable residency entry path, the citizenship requirements are unreachable.
