## Patterns observed

- **Massive 2024-2025 consolidation wave.** SunPower Corp, Lumio, Vivint, Sunnova, ADT/Sunpro, Freedom Forever, Vision Solar all exited within 24 months. Five had major Utah operations. The growth-via-debt residential solar model collapsed when ITC step-downs were proposed and door-to-door sales costs caught up to margins.
- **Door-to-door sales is the single strongest predictor of complaints.** Every installer with widespread complaints about misrepresented tax credits, oversized systems, or unaffordable 25-year loans uses a 1099 commissioned sales force (Lumio, Vision Solar, LGCY, Sunder, Freedom Forever, ION's recent volume). Referral-driven installers (Creative Energies, PPS, Redstone) have far fewer complaints.
- **Lease/PPA in Utah is economically poor.** Rocky Mountain Power's Customer Generation Program credits exports far below retail, so TPO savings models don't pencil the way they do in CA/MA. Cash or solar loan with ownership captures the 30% federal ITC and full self-consumption value. Avoid Sunrun lease/PPA, Sunnova, and Sunder TPO offerings.
- **Provo is the gravitational HQ of US residential solar.** ION (Provo), Blue Raven (Orem), Sunder (South Jordan), Vivint (Lehi), Lumio (Lehi), LGCY (Lehi), Mynt (Lehi), Project Solar (Lehi), Redstone (Provo), Creative Energies (SLC). The BYU-returned-missionary sales pipeline is the historical driver.
- **NABCEP certification is necessary but not sufficient.** Lumio installers were NABCEP-certified. Verify Utah DOPL E200/E201 license status, BBB complaint trend over the last 12 months (not the 10-year average), and request three references from installs completed within the last 6 months in your specific city.
