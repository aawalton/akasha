
## Skilled Stream

### Skilled Independent visa (subclass 189) — Points-tested

- **Direct PR**, no sponsor required. Points-tested invitation-only via SkillSelect.
- **Minimum 65 points**; competitive scores in practice 85–95+ (age, English, experience, qualifications, partner skills).
- **Occupation list**: MLTSSL only.
- **Pathway to citizenship**: PR from day 1 → 4 yrs lawful residence → conferral.
- **2025–26 program**: lower allocation than pre-pandemic; healthcare/early-childhood ed/engineering/IT/trades prioritized.

### Skilled Nominated visa (subclass 190) — State-nominated

- **Direct PR**; requires state/territory nomination (+5 points).
- **Minimum 65 points**; lists are MLTSSL or STSOL.
- **Commitment**: live and work in nominating state for ≥2 years (moral, not enforced as condition).
- **Pathway**: PR from day 1 → 4 yrs → conferral.

### Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) visa (subclass 491) — 5-year provisional

- **Provisional PR-track**; state/territory nomination or eligible-family sponsorship (+15 points — highest in the points test).
- Live/work/study in **designated regional area** for 3 years to qualify for PR conversion via subclass 191.
- **Subclass 191** (Permanent Residence — Skilled Regional): grant of PR after 3 yrs on 491 with ≥AUD 53,900 taxable income each of 3 years.
- **Pathway**: 3 yrs regional (191) + 1 yr PR = 4 yrs total → conferral.

### Skills in Demand visa (subclass 482, "SID") — Replaces TSS

- **Live 7 December 2024**: SID replaced the Temporary Skill Shortage (TSS) visa.
- Three streams: **Specialist Skills** (≥AUD 135,000 salary), **Core Skills** (≥Core Skills Income Threshold AUD 73,150 for 2025–26, indexed), **Labour Agreement**.
- **Validity**: uniform 4-year visa across all streams.
- **Work experience**: reduced from 2 yrs to **1 yr** within last 5 yrs.
- **Occupation list**: Core Skills Occupation List (CSOL), consolidating legacy MLTSSL/STSOL/ROL.
- **Direct ENS pathway**: all SID holders gain access to 186 Temporary Residence Transition stream.
- **Existing TSS holders**: transition to SID at next renewal without re-sponsorship.

### Employer Nomination Scheme (subclass 186) — Direct PR

- **Direct PR**, employer-sponsored. Three streams: **Temporary Residence Transition (TRT)** (from 482/SID/494), **Direct Entry**, Labour Agreement.
- **TRT changes (29 Nov 2025)**: employer must remain Approved Sponsor / LA holder for entire claimed employment period.
- **Pathway**: PR from day 1 → 4 yrs → conferral.

### Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional (Provisional) visa (subclass 494)

- **Provisional 5-yr** employer-sponsored regional visa.
- After 3 yrs work in regional Australia → eligible for 191 PR conversion (same income test as 491).
- **Pathway**: 3 yrs 494 → 191 PR → 1 yr PR → 4 yrs total residence → conferral.

### National Innovation Visa (subclass 858, "NIV") — Replaces Global Talent

- **Live 6 December 2024**: NIV replaced the Global Talent Visa (GTI) and absorbed BIIP aims.
- **Direct PR** from grant; no temporary stage; no age cap.
- **Three streams**: high-performing entrepreneurs, major investors, global researchers/talent.
- **Income/recognition benchmark** (carried from GTI): demonstrate earnings/potential ≥AUD 167,500/yr or comparable record of exceptional achievement.
- **Nomination required** from a recognized individual/organization.
- **Processing**: ~2–6 months in practice; no fixed waiting period.
- **Fee**: AUD 4,305 primary applicant.
- **Citizenship link**: holders qualify for the **Distinguished Talent special residence concession** (480-day presence test in 4 yrs instead of 4-yr lawful residence).

### Distinguished Talent visa (legacy)

- Closed to new applications; absorbed into NIV (above). Existing visa-holders retain concession.
