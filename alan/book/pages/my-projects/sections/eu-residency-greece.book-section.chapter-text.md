
## Applicant filter

US citizen, adult, no EU ancestry, will not renounce US citizenship, capital ceiling **€500,000** one-time lock. Self-employed / passive income. Programs are scored against this profile. Naturalization clock = **7 years** of lawful residence with **≥183 days/year physical presence**.

## Surviving programs

### Golden Visa — €250,000 special tier (Law 5100/2024)

- **Basis**: Law 4251/2014 as amended by **Law 5100/2024**, effective 1 Sept 2024. All transitional grandfather windows (Dec 2024 / Mar 2025 deposit deadlines) have expired — new applications in May 2026 face the full post-reform thresholds.
- **Qualifying investment**: **€250,000** for *either* (a) restoration of a listed/heritage building, *or* (b) conversion of a commercial property to residential use. Anywhere in Greece. Single residential unit, minimum **120 m²**. **Short-term rentals (Airbnb / Booking) are prohibited** on Golden Visa properties.
- **Validity**: 5-year residence permit, **renewable indefinitely** so long as the investment is held. Investor + spouse + children up to 21 (extendable to 24 in education) + dependent parents of both spouses included.
- **Physical-presence requirement**: **ZERO**. The Golden Visa is the most physical-presence-flexible major EU residency program — no minimum days for the permit to remain valid through renewal.
- **Feeds citizenship clock?** Technically yes (it is lawful residence), but practically **no** — naturalization separately requires **≥183 days/year physical presence** for 7 consecutive years, which Golden Visa holders typically never accumulate. Treat this as an **EU residency hedge**, not a passport path.
- **Processing**: 2–6 months end-to-end (file → biometrics → permit issued); blue-paper proof of submission grants legal stay during processing.
- **Score for this applicant**: **INCLUDED — strongest candidate.** The €250k special tier is the only sub-€500k EU Golden Visa with zero stay obligation in May 2026. Capital is recoverable on exit (sell the property), not a sunk fee.

### Golden Visa — €400,000 standard-zone tier (Law 5100/2024)

- **Qualifying investment**: **€400,000** in residential real estate anywhere outside Zone A. Zone A (excluded by the applicant's €500k ceiling) = Attica, Thessaloniki, Mykonos, Santorini, and other islands with population >3,100 — those require €800k. Zone B at €400k = the rest of mainland Greece and the less-populous islands. Single unit, ≥120 m², no short-term rentals.
- **Validity, presence, citizenship, processing**: identical to the €250k tier (5-year renewable, zero stay, does not practically feed the 7y clock, 2–6 months).
- **Score**: **INCLUDED if applicant prefers a non-heritage standard residential unit.** More inventory and simpler title than the heritage/commercial-conversion route, at €150k more capital.

### Financially Independent Persons (FIP) visa

- **Basis**: Law 4251/2014, as amended by **Law 5038/2023** which raised the income floor.
- **Income threshold**: **€3,500/month** stable passive income for the primary applicant (pensions, dividends, rental income, royalties, annuity payments). **+20%** for spouse, **+15%** per child. Bank-deposit alternative ≈ **€126,000** to cover the 3-year validity.
- **Validity**: Initial **3-year** residence permit; renewable in 3-year increments while income persists.
- **Physical-presence requirement**: Expected **≥183 days/year** in Greece (this also makes the holder a Greek tax resident; consider the 7% flat-tax non-dom regime separately).
- **Feeds citizenship clock?** **Yes** — FIP residence at ≥183 days/year counts toward the 7-year naturalization residency requirement.
- **Processing**: D-visa at consulate 1–3 months, then residence permit application within 12 months of arrival; permit issuance another 2–4 months.
- **Score**: **INCLUDED — strongest passport-track candidate.** No capital lockup; the applicant's portable self-employment / passive income meets €3,500/month with margin. The work-prohibition is the binding constraint (FIP holders may not work *for or in* Greece, including local clients) — verify the applicant's income sources are fully foreign-sourced or passive.

### Digital Nomad Visa (DNV)

- **Basis**: Law 4825/2021 / 4826/2021, expanded under subsequent immigration code amendments.
- **Income threshold**: **€3,500/month net** from non-Greek employers or clients. **+20%** spouse, **+15%** per child (family of 4 → €5,250/month).
- **Validity**: **1-year national visa** → convertible to **2-year residence permit**, renewable in **2-year** increments. Holder must be in Greece **≥6 months/year** to renew the residence permit.
- **Physical-presence requirement**: Renewal threshold is 6 months/year; naturalization separately demands ≥183 days/year for 7 consecutive years.
- **Feeds citizenship clock?** **Yes** — lawful DNV residence at ≥183 days/year counts toward the 7-year naturalization clock. (Note: DNV → permanent residence is the typical 5-year track; the 7-year naturalization clock runs in parallel.)
- **Processing**: 10 working days at consulate (statutory target) for the D-visa; in practice 4–8 weeks. Residence permit conversion another 2–4 months.
- **Score**: **INCLUDED — viable passport track for a self-employed remote worker.** Distinct from FIP in that DNV explicitly permits *remote work* (active income from foreign clients) whereas FIP requires passive sources.

### Self-Employment / Freelancer residence permit

- **Basis**: Greece Immigration Code (independent economic activity track). EU Immigration Portal classifies this as *self-employed worker*.
- **Threshold**: **No employment in Greece**; **≥€3,500/month** earnings (or sufficient capital evidence); business plan; relevant skills/qualifications; health insurance; clean record. **+€700/month** unemployed spouse, **+€525/month** per dependent.
- **Validity**: 1-year visa → 2-year temporary residence permit, renewable in 2-year increments.
- **Physical-presence requirement**: Expected to be substantively resident (renewal looks at tax filings and activity); naturalization needs ≥183 days/year × 7 years.
- **Feeds citizenship clock?** **Yes.**
- **Processing**: 3–6 months including consulate D-visa and permit issuance.
- **Score**: **INCLUDED but narrow.** Useful when the applicant wants to invoice *Greek* clients (which DNV and FIP both forbid). Heavier documentation burden than DNV for the same income threshold; choose this only if local-client work is essential.

### Family reunification

- **Basis**: Greece Immigration Code family-reunification provisions (Council Directive 2003/86/EC implementation).
- **Threshold**: Sponsor must hold a valid Greek residence permit for **≥2 years**; demonstrate income ≈ **€8,500–€10,000/year** plus increments per dependent; provide adequate housing and health insurance. Marriage must be legally recognized (civil unions accepted).
- **Validity**: Derivative permit aligned to sponsor's permit duration and renewal cycle.
- **Physical-presence requirement**: Same ≥183 days/year for citizenship credit.
- **Feeds citizenship clock?** **Yes** — derivative lawful residence counts.
- **Score**: **INCLUDED as a dependent path only** — not applicable to a single US-citizen applicant without a Greek-resident sponsor. Listed for completeness; reuse if the applicant later marries a Greek resident.

### Long-term study residence permit

- **Basis**: Greece Immigration Code, student / academic researcher provisions.
- **Threshold**: Acceptance to an accredited Greek institution; proof of tuition and ≈ €400/month subsistence; health insurance.
- **Validity**: Renewable annually for the duration of studies.
- **Physical-presence requirement**: Substantive presence required; ≥183 days/year for naturalization credit.
- **Feeds citizenship clock?** **Partial** — Greek practice historically discounts pure-study years for naturalization (counted at 50% in some interpretations); confirm current Ministry of Interior position before relying on this. Treat as a bridge, not a primary track.
- **Score**: **INCLUDED but weak for this profile.** A self-employed adult without an academic motive should prefer DNV or FIP.

## Filtered out

- **Golden Visa €800k Zone A** (Attica / Thessaloniki / Mykonos / Santorini / populous islands) — exceeds the €500k capital ceiling.
- **Golden Visa €500k pure-financial routes** (Greek shares, government bonds, AIF/mutual funds, capital injection into a Greek company) — at or above the €500k ceiling and capital is fully locked; the €250k heritage / commercial-conversion route dominates them on every axis.
- **EU Blue Card / employer-sponsored work permits** — excluded by filter (employer-sponsored).
- **Posted-worker, intra-corporate transfer, seasonal worker** — employer-sponsored.
- **Endorsement / sponsor-dependent visas** — none meaningful in the Greek system; filter applies trivially.
- **Short-stay / Schengen C visas** — not PR-feeding.
- **Asylum / humanitarian / stateless** — not relevant to applicant profile.

## Recent reforms (2024–2026)

- **Law 5100/2024** (in force 1 Sept 2024): replaced the flat €250k/€500k Golden Visa thresholds with the three-zone €250k/€400k/€800k regime; tightened the 120 m² minimum; banned short-term rentals on Golden Visa properties.
- **Law 5038/2023**: raised FIP minimum income from €2,000/month → €3,500/month and codified the 20%/15% family uplifts.
- **Digital Nomad framework**: introduced under Law 4825/2021, refined by subsequent immigration-code consolidation; renewable 2-year cycles confirmed in 2024 practice.
- **Citizenship side** (cross-reference, not a residency change): naturalization continues to require 7 years of lawful residence with ≥183 days/year physical presence plus the Panhellenic Test (B1 Greek + civics).
