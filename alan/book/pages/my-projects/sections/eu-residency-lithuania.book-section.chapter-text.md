
## Active programs

### Investor-residence permits (startup visa, business-investment visa)

- **Status**: Lithuania has **no citizenship-by-investment program**. Investor-residence permits (startup visa, business-investment visa) exist but only feed into the standard 10-year naturalization clock with all attached requirements (language exam, Constitution exam, oath, renunciation of prior citizenship).
- **Path to citizenship**: investor TRP → PR → 10 years total legal residence → naturalize.
