
## Summary

Sweden has one — and effectively only one — non-employer, non-endorsement, non-renunciation residency path for a US citizen with portable self-employment income: the **Self-Employment Residence Permit (egenföretagare)**, reviewed discretionarily by the Swedish Migration Agency (Migrationsverket). It is famously narrow — only **~107 approvals in 2024**, a 33 % drop from 2022 — but it remains an *open* track in a way Denmark's parallel route is not (Denmark closed its general self-employment scheme on 1 Jan 2015). All other Swedish residence categories either require an employer / Migrationsverket sponsor, a Swedish-resident family member, a Swedish doctoral admission, or do not exist (no retirement, no passive-income, no digital nomad, no golden visa). The right-wing coalition's **6 June 2026 citizenship reform** (8-year clock, civics test, income floor) and the parallel **1 June 2026 work-permit tightening** make this the hardest moment in the modern era to enter and naturalize via Sweden — but the door is not shut.

## Surviving programs

### Self-Employment Residence Permit (Egenföretagare)

The principal — and for this profile, essentially only — discretionary non-endorsement track. Migrationsverket reviews the business plan, finances, and applicant background; there is no external endorsement panel (contrast Denmark's Start-up Denmark), but approval is statistically narrow.

- **Capital / income threshold**: SEK 200,000 in own funds for the applicant + SEK 100,000 for an accompanying spouse + SEK 50,000 per accompanying child, covering two years of self-support before profitability. Money must be the applicant's own and accessible. ([Migrationsverket — Apply: self-employed](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/you-want-to-apply/work/employee-or-self-employed/self-employed-people.html))
- **Qualifications**: Own ≥50 % of and have ultimate operational responsibility for a Swedish business; demonstrated prior experience in the field and in running a business; documented relevant Swedish and/or English knowledge; goods/services must be sold and/or produced in Sweden; realistic business plan with customer contracts / letters of intent. ([Migrationsverket — Apply: self-employed](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/you-want-to-apply/work/employee-or-self-employed/self-employed-people.html); [EU Immigration Portal — Self-employed worker in Sweden](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/migration-and-asylum/eu-immigration-portal/self-employed-worker-sweden_en))
- **Validity & renewal cadence**: Initial permit up to **2 years** (capped at passport validity). Extensions in additional 2-year increments require evidence the business is actually trading and supports the applicant. After **2 years of running the business in Sweden** under the permit, the holder may apply for a **permanent residence permit** at the first extension point — substantially earlier than the 4-year working-permit PR clock and well below the older "5 years total" rule of thumb. ([Migrationsverket — Extend: self-employed](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/you-want-to-extend/work/employee-or-self-employed/self-employed-people.html))
- **Physical-presence requirement**: No statutory minimum-days rule on the initial permit, but PR and citizenship clocks require *actual* habitual residence in Sweden; extended absences invalidate the residency tally and risk extension refusal. The business must be operated from Sweden.
- **Citizenship-clock feed**: Time on this permit counts toward the standard naturalization clock — **8 years from 6 June 2026** under the reform (was 5). Self-employment income explicitly counts toward the new 3-income-base-amount/year self-support floor (≈ SEK 250,200 in 2026). ([Migrationsverket — New rules for Swedish citizenship from 6 June 2026](https://www.migrationsverket.se/nyheter/news-archive/2026-05-06-new-rules-for-swedish-citizenship-from-6-june-2026.html))
- **Approval rate**: ~107 approvals in 2024, down 33 % from 2022; published government statistics combined with rejection-reason breakdowns (weak business plan ~35 %, insufficient funds ~25 %) imply a realistic first-attempt success ceiling around 30–40 % for typical applicants and 60–70 % for well-prepared, well-funded ones. ([Stockholm Valley — Non-EU & Want to engage in Sweden's startup ecosystem? Oxford Admission is Likelier](https://www.stockholmvalley.com/non-eu-wait-2-years-startup-sweden/))
- **Processing time**: Median 9–18 months; Migrationsverket's 75th-percentile decision time is ~24 months. Applications filed from outside Sweden via Swedish embassy/consulate.

### Family reunification — spouse / sambo (cohabiting partner)

Available only if the applicant already has a Swedish-resident spouse or registered cohabiting partner; included for completeness because the project explicitly retained it.

- **Maintenance requirement (on the sponsor in Sweden, not the applicant)**: 2026 normalbelopp is SEK 6,243/month after housing for a single adult; ~SEK 13,400/month for a couple after rent; sponsor must also have a home large enough for both. ([Migrationsverket — Maintenance requirement for the person in Sweden](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/word-explanations/maintenance-requirement-for-the-person-in-sweden.html); [Migrationsverket — Live with a partner](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/you-want-to-apply/live-with-someone/live-with-a-partner-child-or-other-relative/live-with-a-partner.html))
- **Validity & renewal cadence**: New relationships normally get a 2-year time-limited permit ("uppskjuten invandringsprövning"); established relationships may receive longer. PR eligible after the relationship has lasted and the holder has held a permit for the requisite period (typically 3 years) and meets self-support.
- **Physical-presence**: Habitual residence with the sponsor in Sweden.
- **Citizenship-clock feed**: Yes — time on a partner permit counts toward the 8-year clock from 6 June 2026.
- **Processing time**: Highly variable; commonly 12–24 months.
- **Profile match**: **No** — applicant has no Swedish-resident partner. Listed per scope rules; not actionable.

### Doctoral studies (PhD) residence permit

The one study track that feeds both PR and the citizenship clock; Master's-level study does **not** count toward PR or citizenship and is excluded.

- **Qualifications**: Admission to a Swedish doctoral programme. Most Swedish PhD posts are *paid employment positions* (~SEK 32k–37k/month), which is the realistic entry mechanism. ([Migrationsverket — Apply: doctoral studies](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/you-want-to-apply/study/doctoral-studies.html))
- **Validity & renewal cadence**: 1–2 years at a time, renewable through the doctoral programme (typically 4–5 years).
- **PR pathway**: Under the **government bill effective 11 June 2026**, PR eligibility for doctoral students drops from 4-of-7 years to **3 years continuous residence** as a doctoral candidate. ([Government.se — Improved conditions for foreign doctoral students and researchers, Feb 2026](https://www.government.se/press-releases/2026/02/government-proposes-improved-conditions-for-foreign-doctoral-students-and-researchers-and-measures-to-counter-abuse-of-residence-permits-for-studies/); [Migrationsverket — Permanent residence permit for doctoral students](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/you-want-to-apply/study/permanent-residence-permits-for-doctoral-students.html))
- **Citizenship-clock feed**: Yes — doctoral and researcher time counts toward the 8-year clock; non-doctoral study time does **not**.
- **Profile match**: **Low** — requires admission to and completion of a Swedish PhD programme (4–5 years of academic work), a substantial pivot from portable self-employment income. Listed because it remains the only non-family, non-employer, non-self-employment route that feeds the citizenship clock.

## Filtered out (do not match profile or project filters)

### Visitor's residence permit (>90 days) — does not feed PR or citizenship

- Granted for short, time-limited visits up to ~6 months in most cases (extendable to 1 year only in narrow circumstances). Explicitly **not for settlement**, does **not** count toward PR, does **not** count toward the citizenship clock, and **cannot** be converted into a long-term permit from inside Sweden. ([Migrationsverket — Visiting Sweden for more than 90 days](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/you-want-to-apply/visiting-sweden/visiting-sweden-for-more-than-90-days.html))
- Closest Sweden gets to a "passive income" admission and the reason **Sweden has no retirement / rentier / digital nomad visa**.

### Retirement / passive-income / digital nomad visa — does not exist

- Sweden has **no** retirement visa, **no** financially-independent-resident permit, and **no** digital nomad visa. Passive income alone confers no immigration status. The only passive-income-shaped admission is the visitor permit above, which is non-PR-feeding and therefore filtered out.

### Start-up Sweden / "Look for work or start a business" — limited and Master's-gated

- The post-study "Look for work or start a business" permit is **9 months** only, requires a **Swedish-recognized Master's degree** (Bachelor's-only applicants are ineligible), and is intended as a bridge into either an employer-sponsored work permit or a self-employment permit. Not a freestanding residency track; counted by the applicant filter as an endorsement-adjacent bridge to the self-employment route already listed above. ([Migrationsverket — Look for work or start a business](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/you-want-to-apply/work/look-for-work/look-for-work-or-start-a-business.html))
- No standalone "Start-up Sweden" pilot exists in 2026; a government inquiry is reporting March 2026 / March 2027 on whether to launch one.

### Employer-sponsored work permit (AT) — filtered out per project criteria

- Filtered out by the project's "no employer sponsorship" filter. New 1 June 2026 reforms also raise the salary threshold (~80 % of median wage) and tighten genuine-employer scrutiny — independently of the project filter, the track is closing further.

### Investment / CBI — does not exist

- Sweden has **no** citizenship-by-investment, **no** golden visa, and **no** residence-by-investment programme. The 2026 citizenship self-support floor (3 income base amounts/year, ≈ SEK 250,200) is an *eligibility* requirement on the standard 8-year naturalization track, not a CBI substitute.

## Verdict for applicant profile

**Sweden retains exactly one viable self-driven residency path: the Self-Employment Permit (egenföretagare).** It is real, open, non-endorsement (discretionary Migrationsverket review only), and counts toward PR after 2 years of operating the business and toward citizenship after 8 years under the post-6-June-2026 regime. The threshold (SEK 200k own funds, demonstrated prior business experience, Sweden-linked goods/services, viable plan) is achievable for a well-funded, well-prepared US-citizen self-employed applicant — but the ~107-approvals-in-2024 ceiling and 9–24-month processing time mean it is a single narrow door, not a comfortable one.

**Sweden is *not* functionally inaccessible like Denmark** — Denmark's general self-employment scheme is closed since 2015 with no non-endorsement replacement, whereas Sweden's remains formally open. **Recommendation: keep Sweden in the citizenship-via-residency working set**, flagged as a high-friction single-track option, behind clearer routes (Portugal D7/D8, Spain NLV, Italy ENM) but ahead of Denmark, Austria, and the other functionally-closed regimes. The 8-year citizenship clock and post-2026 civics + language + income tests make Sweden an undesirable *naturalization* destination, but residency entry remains achievable.
