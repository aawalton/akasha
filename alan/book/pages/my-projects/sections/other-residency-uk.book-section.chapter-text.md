
The terminal residency status before naturalization is **Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR)** / "Settled Status". On current rules, most work routes give ILR after 5 years continuous residence (≤180 days outside the UK in any 12-month period), Life in UK Test, English B1, and route-specific salary tests at the ILR application date. The May 2025 Labour white paper proposes a default 10-year ILR clock across most routes with a contribution-based "earned" reduction — under consultation, not yet enacted as of May 2026. English requirement is also tightening: B1 → B2 across the board phasing in from spring 2026; HPI already moved 8 January 2026.

## Active programs

### Skilled Worker visa (the workhorse — replaced Tier 2 General)

- **Eligibility:** Sponsored job offer from a UK employer holding a Skilled Worker sponsor licence, role at RQF Level 6+ (degree-equivalent — restored from RQF 3 by July 2025 changes), salary at or above route threshold.
- **Salary threshold (from 22 July 2025):** **£41,700** general threshold (up from £38,700 under April 2024 reform), or the SOC-code going rate, whichever is higher. Transitional rates apply to workers whose first CoS was before 4 April 2024: £31,300 / £28,200 / £25,000 depending on option.
- **Duration:** Up to 5 years per grant; extendable.
- **ILR path:** 5 years continuous residence + salary at ILR-date threshold (no discounts at ILR — must meet full general/going rate) + Life in UK + B1 English (B2 from 26 March 2027 for some sub-routes).
- **Processing:** ~3 weeks (decision), 8 weeks if employer also obtaining sponsor licence.
- **Recent changes (2024–2026):** April 2024 threshold £26,200→£38,700; July 2025 → £41,700, RQF 6+ restored, care-worker overseas sponsorship closed (transitional to 22 Jul 2028). Under white-paper proposals, the 5-year ILR clock would extend to 10 years (under consultation).

### Global Talent visa (replaced Tier 1 Exceptional Talent)

- **Eligibility:** Endorsement from a designated endorsing body (Royal Society, British Academy, Royal Academy of Engineering, Tech Nation successor, Arts Council, UKRI) in science, engineering, humanities, medicine, digital tech, arts/culture — either "exceptional talent" (established leader) or "exceptional promise" (emerging leader). Prize-route endorsements (Nobel, Turing, etc.) available without separate application. No salary or sponsor needed.
- **Duration:** Up to 5 years per grant; renewable indefinitely.
- **ILR path:** 3 years for "exceptional talent" endorsees (and some prize-route); 5 years for "exceptional promise". Continuous-residence + B1 English + Life in UK at ILR.
- **Processing:** Endorsement 8 weeks, visa 3 weeks.
- **Recent changes:** 11 November 2025 — broadened eligibility; **annual cap of 8,000 visas/year** introduced. Further 2026 reforms signaled (white paper marks Global Talent as a "favoured" route).

### Innovator Founder visa (replaced Start-up + old Innovator, 13 April 2023)

- **Eligibility:** Innovative, viable, scalable business idea endorsed by a Home Office-approved endorsing body. **No minimum investment threshold** (the previous £50,000 floor was dropped at the April 2023 reform). Can hold a job alongside the business since 11 March 2024 changes.
- **Duration:** 3 years; extendable in 3-year increments.
- **ILR path:** **3 years** continuous residence + ongoing endorsement showing business progress + B1 English + Life in UK at ILR.
- **Processing:** Endorsement varies by body (4–12 weeks); visa 3 weeks.
- **Recent changes:** October 2025 — Student visa holders can switch directly into Innovator Founder upon course completion; from 25 November 2025, students can self-employ while their Innovator Founder application is pending. Strongly aligned with white-paper "talent" priorities.

### High Potential Individual (HPI) visa

- **Eligibility:** Bachelor's, master's, or PhD awarded within the last 5 years by a university on the Global Universities List (top-ranked per QS / THE / ARWU). **No sponsor required, no job offer.**
- **Duration:** 2 years (bachelor's/master's); 3 years (PhD). **Non-extendable** — must switch to another route (typically Skilled Worker or Innovator Founder) for ILR.
- **ILR path:** No direct ILR — switch to a sponsored or settlement route during the 2/3-year window.
- **Processing:** ~3 weeks.
- **Recent changes:** **4 November 2025** — eligibility expanded from top-42 to **top-100 universities**; annual cap of 8,000 introduced (doubling expected entrants from ~2,000 to ~4,000). **8 January 2026** — English requirement raised from CEFR B1 to **B2**.

### Scale-up Worker visa

- **Eligibility:** Job offer from a "scale-up" sponsor (UK firm with ≥20% annualized revenue or staff growth over 3 years), role at RQF 6+, **salary ≥ £39,100** (from 22 July 2025; £36,300 if CoS dated 4 Apr 2024–21 Jul 2025).
- **Duration:** 2 years initial; first 6 months tied to sponsor, then unsponsored. Extendable +3 years.
- **ILR path:** **5 years** continuous residence + UK PAYE earnings at/above threshold for ≥24 of the 36 months before ILR + Life in UK + B1 English (B2 from 26 March 2027).
- **Processing:** ~3 weeks.

### Health and Care Worker visa

- **Eligibility:** Sponsored job in eligible health/social-care occupations under SOC codes (doctors, nurses, paramedics, plus historically SOC 6135/6136 care workers).
- **Salary threshold:** £25,000 (national pay scale floor from July 2025) or NHS-band rate, whichever higher.
- **ILR path:** 5 years continuous residence + Life in UK + B1 English. **Notably exempt from the proposed 10-year ILR extension** in the white paper.
- **Critical change:** **22 July 2025** — overseas sponsorship of new care workers (SOC 6135/6136) **closed**. Transitional rules to 22 July 2028 allow continued sponsorship only for in-country workers already on Skilled Worker / Health & Care leave in those SOCs before 22 Jul 2025, or in-country workers with ≥3 months prior employment with the sponsor. Doctors/nurses routes unaffected.

### Family visa (Spouse / Partner)

- **Eligibility:** Married to / civil partner of / unmarried partner (2+ years cohabitation) of British citizen or ILR holder. Genuine and subsisting relationship, intent to live together permanently.
- **Income / financial requirement:** **£29,000 minimum** for first-time applicants since 11 April 2024 (up from £18,600). Planned hikes to £34,500 and £38,700 **paused** by Labour pending MAC review; £29,000 remains in force as of late 2025 / May 2026. Existing pre-April-2024 applicants stay on £18,600 grandfather rule. Substitutes: savings (typically £88,500), pension income, or self-employment evidence.
- **Duration:** 2.5 years initial; 2.5 years extension.
- **ILR path:** 5 years on spouse visa → ILR. Naturalization possible immediately after (3-year continuous residence variant).
- **Processing:** 12 weeks (out of country), 8 weeks (in country).
- **English requirement:** A1 at entry, A2 at extension, **B1 at ILR** (B2 expected under white paper).

### UK Ancestry visa (Commonwealth — also covered in citizenship/uk.md)

- **Eligibility:** Commonwealth citizen 17+, at least one grandparent born in the UK, Channel Islands, Isle of Man, or Ireland before 31 March 1922. Must be able and intending to work. **US citizens are NOT eligible.**
- **Duration:** 5 years.
- **ILR path:** **5 years** continuous residence (≤180 days outside in any 12-month rolling window) + continuing employment evidence + Life in UK + B1 English at ILR. Then 12 months ILR → naturalization (6 years total).
- **Processing:** 3 weeks.

### BNO (Hong Kong) visa

- **Eligibility:** BN(O) status (born in Hong Kong before 1 July 1997 to a BN(O) parent / registered in time) + dependent family. Adult children who were under 18 on 1 July 1997 newly eligible independently from **9 February 2026**.
- **Duration:** 2.5+2.5 years OR a single 5-year grant.
- **ILR path:** **5 years** continuous residence (≤180 days outside per year) + Life in UK + B1 English → ILR → 12 months → naturalization (6 years total — the "5+1" route).
- **Processing:** ~12 weeks.

### Graduate visa (post-study work)

- **Eligibility:** Completed a UK degree at a licensed sponsor institution (bachelor's, master's, or PhD).
- **Duration:** 2 years (bachelor's/master's), 3 years (PhD) — **for applications by 31 December 2026**. **From 1 January 2027** the bachelor's/master's grant drops to **18 months** (PhD unchanged at 3 years), per 14 October 2025 Statement of Changes implementing the May 2025 white paper.
- **ILR path:** **No direct ILR** — must switch into Skilled Worker, Innovator Founder, or another settlement route. No work-rights restrictions or salary floor during the 2/18-month window.
- **Processing:** ~8 weeks.

### Student visa (Tier 4 successor — "Student route")

- **Eligibility:** Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) from a licensed sponsor, finances, English at course level.
- **Duration:** Length of course + small wrap-around.
- **ILR path:** **No direct ILR** — student time generally doesn't count toward most settlement routes. Must switch into Graduate, Skilled Worker, Innovator Founder, etc.
- **Work hours:** 20 hours/week term-time for degree-level+ courses, 10 hours/week for sub-degree; full-time in vacation. No self-employment, no professional sport, no entertainer roles, no permanent full-time vacancies.

### Youth Mobility Scheme (T5) — **US not eligible**

- **Eligible nationalities (May 2026):** Australia, Canada, New Zealand, South Korea (ages 18–35); Andorra, Iceland, Japan, Monaco, San Marino, Uruguay (ages 18–30); Hong Kong (SAR), Taiwan (ballot first). India operates under a separate Young Professionals Scheme.
- **Duration:** 2 years (3 years for Australia, Canada, New Zealand, South Korea).
- **ILR path:** **No direct ILR** — must switch routes for settlement.
- **US citizens:** Not eligible — no reciprocal agreement with the US.

### Self-sponsorship (informal route via UK company)

- **Eligibility:** Not a separate visa; an arrangement where you incorporate or acquire a UK company, obtain a Skilled Worker sponsor licence for it, then sponsor yourself as an employee. Since Skilled Worker (Dec 2020) replaced Tier 2, there are no restrictions on the visa-holder being the sole shareholder.
- **Requirements:** UK-resident Authorising Officer, genuine UK trading business, salary at Skilled Worker threshold (£41,700 general from 22 Jul 2025), RQF 6+ role, role passes the genuine-vacancy test on compliance review.
- **Sponsor licence fees:** £574 (small) / £1,579 (medium-large) + £525 per CoS + standard Skilled Worker fees / IHS.
- **Processing:** Sponsor licence ~8 weeks; CoS + visa adds ~3–8 weeks.
- **ILR path:** Same as Skilled Worker — 5 years (or 10 under proposed white-paper rules) → ILR.
- **Compliance risk:** Sham-business and "salary recycling" arrangements are a Home Office enforcement priority; licence-revocation rate has risen since 2024.

## Closed / historical

### Tier 1 Investor visa — closed 17 February 2022

- **Status:** **Closed to new applicants on 17 February 2022** at 4pm, with immediate effect. Decommissioned over security/AML concerns (UK government cited illegitimate wealth provenance and corruption risk) coincident with the Russia–Ukraine crisis.
- **Historical requirement:** £2,000,000 minimum investment in active and trading UK companies (loan or share capital); ILR after 2 years (£10m), 3 years (£5m), or 5 years (£2m).
- **Existing holders:** May apply for visa extensions until **17 February 2026**; ILR applications open until **17 February 2028**.
- **Replacement:** **No replacement program** announced as of May 2026. Innovator Founder and Global Talent absorbed some of the policy intent but are not investment-for-residency routes. The UK has signaled at G20 / OECD level that it does not intend to reopen passive-investment migration.
