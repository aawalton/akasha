
Naturalization baseline (set by citizenship law, repeated here for filtering): standard track is 7 cumulative years legal residence in the last 10 plus 12 continuous months immediately before application, Greek B1, and integration test. Fast-track is 4 years (Greek A2) or 3 years (Greek B1), but is restricted to "Highly Skilled Employees" of Cyprus-registered foreign-interest companies — an employer-sponsored category outside this profile's filter. Time on a **student** permit is **excluded entirely** from the residency clock. All other surviving residence permits below count at the **full rate** toward the 7-year standard track.

## Active programs (US-citizen, adult, portable income)

### Permanent Residency by Investment — Regulation 6(2) ("Fast-Track" / Cat 6.2)

- **Threshold:** €300,000 + VAT in (a) new-build residential real estate from a developer (resale excluded), (b) Cypriot-company shares with ≥5 local employees, (c) Cyprus-registered AIF/AIFLNP units, or (d) commercial real estate of any age. Full investment must be paid **before** filing. Plus secured annual income ≥ €50,000 from abroad (+€15k spouse, +€10k per minor child) and €30k three-year pledged bank deposit.
- **Validity / renewal:** Permanent — indefinite, no renewal. Maintained by visiting Cyprus at least once every 2 years.
- **Physical presence:** None required to maintain PR. To eventually naturalize, the standard 7-year + 12-month continuous clock applies — so de-facto residence is needed only if citizenship is the goal.
- **Citizenship clock:** Full-rate (standard 7-year track). PR by Reg 6(2) confers no acceleration over Cat F holders.
- **Processing:** ~2–3 months after a complete file.
- **2024–26 reforms:** Tightened income-proof rules (Mar 2023, still in force); ongoing emphasis on source-of-funds diligence post-CBI closure. No threshold change in 2025/26.

### Category F — Permanent Residency for Persons of Independent Means

- **Threshold:** Statutory minimum secured annual income from abroad **€9,568** for principal applicant + **€4,613** per dependent. In practice the Migration Department expects €20–30k+ for a comfortable file. No statutory real-estate floor, but a property purchase (any value) and a €15–20k unpledged Cyprus bank deposit are de-facto expected. Income must be passive (pensions, dividends, interest, rent, royalties) — **employment or business in Cyprus is prohibited**.
- **Validity / renewal:** Permanent — indefinite once granted. Maintained by not being absent from Cyprus for 2+ continuous years.
- **Physical presence:** Must not abandon Cyprus as primary residence; in practice expect to be physically present the majority of the year to defend the file.
- **Citizenship clock:** Full-rate (standard 7-year track).
- **Processing:** Officially 12–18 months; **realistic 5–7 years** — per the Auditor-General's Feb 2026 report, CRMD is still working through standard Cat F files submitted in **2020**. Plan accordingly.
- **2024–26 reforms:** No threshold change. The accelerated Cat F sub-track (€30k income + €200k property + €30k 3-yr deposit) remains available for applicants who want speed without going to the €300k Reg 6(2) tier.

### Cyprus Startup Visa (Individual or Team) — extended through Dec 2026

- **Threshold:** €20,000 paid-up capital for the Cyprus startup (€10k if a single founder); ≥25% equity held by the third-country founder(s); business must be "innovative" (R&D spend ≥10% of opex in one of last 3 years, or certification by an approved innovation body). Up to 5 founders/key personnel per team.
- **Validity / renewal:** 3-year initial permit (raised from 2 years effective 1 Jan 2025), renewable in 2-year increments while the company remains compliant.
- **Physical presence:** Operate the business from Cyprus; no fixed day-count, but headquarters and tax residence expected.
- **Citizenship clock:** Full-rate (standard 7-year track). Founders are *not* automatically "Highly Skilled Employees" — the fast-track 3–4 year route requires an employment relationship with a Cyprus-registered foreign-interest company, not self-ownership.
- **Processing:** Initial-approval notification within ~5 weeks of a complete file; full permit issuance ~3–4 months.
- **2024–26 reforms:** Scheme renewed and overhauled effective **1 Jan 2025**: equity threshold lowered from 50% → 25%, permit extended from 2 → 3 years, team cap raised to 5, scheme valid through Dec 2026 (further renewal likely).

### Family Reunification (sponsor-dependent — listed for completeness)

- **Threshold:** Sponsor (a different person, already legally resident in Cyprus ≥2 years on a permit ≥1 year validity) must show ≥€24,000 annual income +20% spouse +15% per child. Applicant must be spouse (married ≥1 yr, ≥21), minor child, or dependent ascendant. **Not actionable as a primary pathway for an unattached US citizen** — included only because it is the standard mechanism to bring a spouse/children once the principal is on Reg 6(2), Cat F, or Startup Visa.
- **Validity / renewal:** First permit 1 year, then matched to sponsor's permit.
- **Citizenship clock:** Full-rate, but tied to sponsor's continued status.

## Filtered-out programs

- **Cyprus Digital Nomad Visa** — excluded as **not PR-feeding**. Capped at 1,000 permits (raised from 500 in Mar 2025; Deputy Ministry hinted at full quota removal in 2026 if demand sustained), €3,500/month net income, 1-year permit renewable to a 3-year total maximum, then the holder must convert to a different category to remain. The standard 7-year naturalization clock requires **5 years of permits that can lead to PR**; the DNV expressly does not, so years on the DNV do not advance toward citizenship even if a later category-switch occurs. Useful as a probationary year, not a pathway.
- **Student permit** — excluded; time on a student permit is statutorily **excluded** from the naturalization residency clock and does not feed PR.
- **Self-employed / freelancer permit (informal "Category D")** — excluded; in current Cyprus practice non-EU self-employment in domestic services is gated by Migration Department discretion, sector restrictions, and case-by-case approval. Not a defined, generally-available program for a US remote worker without local clientele.
- **EU Blue Card (Cyprus)** and other employer-sponsored single-permit tracks — excluded per filter (employer-sponsored).
- **Highly Skilled Employee Fast-Track (4-yr / 3-yr naturalization)** — excluded per filter (employer-sponsored: requires employment by a Cyprus-registered foreign-interest company holding the relevant licence; salary ≥ €2,500/month). Not reachable from self-employment alone.
- **Cyprus Investment Programme / CBI** — closed 1 Nov 2020; final §111A loophole repealed 5 Dec 2025; infringement case closed 11 Mar 2026. See `../citizenship/cyprus.md`.
- **Cat 6.1 Permanent Residency (high-income, pre-2016)** — superseded in practice by Reg 6(2); kept on paper but rarely used and significantly slower.

## Verdict

**Cyprus retains at least one viable residency path.** The strongest US-citizen, no-employer-sponsorship options are: (1) **Reg 6(2) at €300k** — fast, permanent on grant, low physical-presence burden, but capital-locked in real estate; (2) **Category F** — cheapest entry by far (≤€30k/yr passive income, no statutory property floor) but multi-year processing reality; (3) **Startup Visa** — €20k working capital, requires an innovative Cyprus-incorporated business and active operation from Cyprus. All three count full-rate toward the standard 7-year naturalization clock. None of them shortens that clock — fast-track citizenship in Cyprus is structurally gated behind employer-sponsored "Highly Skilled Employee" status and is not reachable from any self-funded residence route.
