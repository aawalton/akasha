
## Federal Economic Immigration — Express Entry

### Express Entry (FSW / CEC / FST + PNP-Enhanced linkage)
- **Eligibility**: profile entered into the Express Entry pool, ranked on the Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS, 1,200 points). Three federal streams — Federal Skilled Worker (points-based human capital), Canadian Experience Class (1 yr skilled Canadian work experience), Federal Skilled Trades (qualified trades). Provincial Nominee Program (PNP) Enhanced nominations add 600 CRS points (effectively guaranteeing an ITA).
- **Threshold (May 2026 trends)**: general draws **CRS 510–547**; category-based draws lower — STEM ~491, healthcare ~423. Category eligibility now requires **12 months** of qualifying work experience (up from 6 months).
- **Outcome**: permanent residency. PR is a path to citizenship via the s. 5(1) 1,095-day rule.
- **Physical-presence requirement (PR maintenance)**: 730 days in any rolling 5-year period.
- **Processing time**: ~6 months service standard after ITA for complete applications.
- **Recent changes (2024–2026)**: 2026–2028 Levels Plan lowers overall PR target to **380,000/yr** (from 395,000); Express Entry allocations modestly reduced; category-based draws doubled work-experience requirement to 12 months in 2026; Carney government announced acceleration of **up to 33,000 in-Canada temporary workers to PR** across 2026–2027.

### Provincial Nominee Program (PNP, overview)
- **Eligibility**: each province (except Quebec) and territory runs streams targeting their labour-market needs — most require a provincial job offer or in-demand occupation; some require prior in-province study/work. Once provincially nominated, federal PR processing follows.
- **Outcome**: permanent residency; path to citizenship.
- **Allocation (2026)**: PNP target **expanded ~66% to 91,500 spots** in the 2026–2028 Levels Plan — a sharp reversal of the 2025 cut to 55,000. PNPs now ~38% of all economic immigration.
- **Processing time**: 6–12 months provincial stage + 6 months federal stage.
- **Recent changes (2024–2026)**: 2025 plan halved allocations; 2026 plan restored and grew them.

## Quebec Immigration

### Programme de sélection des travailleurs qualifiés (PSTQ)
- **Eligibility**: Arrima expression of interest ranked on a 1,400-point grid weighting French proficiency, in-demand TEER occupations, and settlement outside Montreal (Saguenay / Trois-Rivières jobs score nearly 2× a downtown Montreal offer). Four streams by job type, each with its own French requirement.
- **Outcome**: Certificat de sélection du Québec (CSQ) → federal PR; path to citizenship.
- **2026 plan**: ~29,000 PSTQ admissions inside Quebec's 45,000-cap total annual immigration (2026–2029 plan); 64% economic share.
- **Recent changes**: **PSTQ is now the only permanent skilled-worker stream**. The Programme de l'expérience québécoise (PEQ) — Quebec Graduates and Temporary Foreign Workers streams — was **abolished 19 Nov 2025**.

### Quebec Immigrant Investor Program (QIIP)
- **Status**: **active** (relaunched January 2024 after a 2019–2024 suspension), under stricter rules.
- **Investment**: **CAD $1,000,000** five-year zero-interest guaranteed investment + **CAD $200,000** non-refundable contribution = CAD $1.2M total. Financing options reduce the upfront cash to a smaller down payment.
- **Other thresholds**: net worth CAD $2M+; French B2; mandatory Quebec residence; settlement integration commitment.
- **Outcome**: CSQ → PR; path to citizenship.
- **Processing time**: 24–36 months.

## Federal Business / Entrepreneur Streams

### Start-up Visa Program (SUV)
- **Eligibility**: qualifying business; **letter of support from a designated organization** (venture-capital fund, angel-investor group, or business incubator); **CLB 5** English or French; settlement funds ≥ CAD $15,263.
- **Status (2026)**: **program paused** — IRCC stopped accepting new commitment certificates from designated organizations after 31 Dec 2025; pending applications still processed. Holders of a valid 2025 commitment certificate have until **30 Jun 2026** to apply. A targeted replacement entrepreneur pilot is expected in 2026, details TBD.
- **Outcome**: permanent residency; path to citizenship.
- **Processing time**: ~12–36 months (now stretching to **40–52 months** for non-priority files; 3–5 years for priority). 2–4 months for the optional temporary work permit.

### Self-Employed Persons Program (SEP)
- **Status**: **indefinitely paused** since April 2024 (extended into 2026); inventory peaked at ~43,200 cases. New entrepreneur pilot replacement expected in 2026, details TBD. Pending applications still processed.

### Federal Immigrant Investor Program (IIP)
- **Status**: closed since 2014; not reopened. No federal passive-investor / golden-visa route exists.

## Other PR Pilots

### Atlantic Immigration Program (AIP)
- **Eligibility**: job offer from a designated employer in NB, NL, NS, or PEI; meets occupation-specific NOC, language, education, and settlement-funds requirements.
- **Outcome**: PR; path to citizenship.
- **Status**: active.

### Rural and Northern Immigration Pilot (RNIP)
- **Status**: **closed to new applications since 31 Aug 2024**. Successor: the **Rural Community Immigration Pilot (RCIP)**, community-driven, with select communities (e.g., North Bay) issuing recommendations in 2026.

### Home-care worker pilots (HCWP)
- **Status**: 2024-launched Home Child Care Provider Pilot + Home Support Worker Pilot (NOC 44100 / 44101), annual cap 5,500, offered direct PR. **New intake paused indefinitely 2026** — demand vastly exceeded spots; IRCC focusing on processing existing applications and has no plans to reopen in 2026.
- **Outcome (for existing applicants)**: PR; path to citizenship.

### Digital nomad route — visitor-status policy
- **Status (May 2026)**: **no standalone digital-nomad visa is operational**. Under the 2023 Tech Talent Strategy, IRCC permits foreign remote workers to enter on **visitor status for up to 1 year** (extended from 6 months effective 5 Jan 2026), provided the employer and all clients remain outside Canada. A dedicated Federal Digital Nomad Visa is still in provincial consultation with no enacted launch as of May 2026.
- **Outcome**: residency-only — does **not** establish PR or count toward citizenship's 1,095-day rule at 1:1 (visitor-time counts 0.5×, capped at 365 days, only if the holder later becomes a PR).

## Family Sponsorship

### Spouse / common-law partner
- **Eligibility**: Canadian citizen or PR sponsors spouse, common-law (12-mo cohabitation), or conjugal partner; sponsor undertakes 3-year financial support.
- **Outcome**: PR; path to citizenship. Processed as IRCC priority.
- **Processing time**: ~10–12 months inside-Canada / outside-Canada streams.

### Parent and Grandparent (PGP)
- **Status**: **closed to new applications and new sponsorship intake from 1 Jan 2026**. IRCC continues to process only the up-to-10,000 complete applications submitted in 2025 (drawn from the 2020 interest-to-sponsor pool). Super Visa (10-year multi-entry, 5-year per entry) is the promoted alternative.
- **Processing time**: 20–36+ months historically.

### Dependent children and other relatives
- **Eligibility**: standard family-class rules; "lonely Canadian" / orphaned-niece-nephew exceptions limited.
- **Outcome**: PR; path to citizenship.

## Work Permits

### Temporary Foreign Worker Program (TFWP) — LMIA-based
- **Eligibility**: positive Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA) from Service Canada; employer-specific work permit.
- **Status (2026)**: **TFWP target cut 27% to 60,000** under the 2026–2028 Plan. **Low-Wage LMIAs blocked** in any Census Metropolitan Area with ≥6% unemployment; **10% cap** on low-wage TFWs per employer; low-wage permits cut from 2 yrs to **1 yr**. From April 2026, recruitment-advertising extended to **8 consecutive weeks** with at least one active recruitment activity until the decision.
- **PR pathway**: not direct — must transition via Express Entry / PNP / PSTQ.

### International Mobility Program (IMP) — LMIA-exempt
- **Eligibility**: covered by international agreements (CUSMA, CETA), reciprocal arrangements, intra-company transfer, significant-benefit (C10/C11), spousal open work permit, post-graduation, etc.
- **Status (2026)**: **IMP target raised 32% to 170,000**. February 2026 IRCC guidance tightened C10 "significant benefit" — now requires evidence of large-scale Canadian job/training creation. Government is steering employers from LMIA → IMP.
- **PR pathway**: not direct; counts as Canadian work experience for CEC.

### Global Talent Stream (GTS) — Global Skills Strategy
- **Eligibility**: Category A (referred by a designated partner for unique specialized talent) or Category B (Global Talent Occupations List — mostly senior tech roles).
- **Processing time**: **~10 business days** — fastest LMIA stream by a large margin.
- **PR pathway**: not direct; Canadian work experience feeds CEC.

## Study Permits as a PR Stepping Stone

### Study permit → PGWP → CEC
- **Eligibility**: acceptance at a Designated Learning Institution (DLI); Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL) required for most undergraduate / college applicants; **PAL exempt** for master's and doctoral students at public DLIs from 1 Jan 2026; proof of funds, language proficiency.
- **2026 cap**: **309,670 study-permit application spaces**; IRCC issuing up to 408,000 permits (155,000 new arrivals + 253,000 extensions) — **~50% cut from 305,900 new arrivals in 2025**.
- **PR pathway**: graduate → Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP, 1–3 yrs) → ≥1 yr skilled work → Canadian Experience Class via Express Entry. Best leveraged by master's / PhD at a public DLI.
- **Physical-presence count**: pre-PR study + work time counts at 0.5× toward the 1,095-day citizenship rule, capped at 365 days.
