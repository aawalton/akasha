
# My Strategy

> The opening page of My Strategy — Alan's book of how he decides what to improve next and why. It records how the book began on 2026-08-11, the name settled by approval rather than authorship, and the correction he made early in the first session: this book is not about what he is building, it is about improving his life. The substance is in the numbered pages after this one.

This is where I work out what to improve next, and why that rather than something else.

I asked for it like this:

> seat the interviewer role, start a record, and lets create a folder in books for capturing high-level strategy context for me, we can work together on the name

The name was to be worked out with me rather than handed to me, and it was.

## What this book is about

Early in the first session I corrected the frame it had been given:

> This isn't about what I'm trying to build. This is about improving my life.

And on the scope of that:

> My entire life. I organize that into my six values (you can look those up)

So the subject is not a product roadmap and not a company. It is my whole life, organised by my six values, plus the work of getting better at improving it at all. Those two channels are the next page.

I also said, when asked which value the strategy was pointed at:

> I don't, its pointed at all of them

And on what kind of thing an answer here even is:

> That's what we're here for. There is no single strategy for this, its complex, nuances, intuitive, and need puzzling out.

## How this book began, and what is mine in it

*Recorder's reading, marked.* This section is the recorder's, not Alan's, and it is here so that a reader meeting these pages later knows what they are holding.

The book was opened on 2026-08-11, in the opening minutes of a live interview session, before anything about its subject had been said. Alan asked for the folder and for a say in its name; that request, quoted above, is the whole of what he had contributed at the moment the book was created.

The name `my-strategy` and the line under it — *what Alan is building toward, and why that rather than something else* — were proposed to him by the interviewer, on the reasoning that he had reached for the word "strategy" himself and that the `my-` books hold his own working-out rather than a description of him. He answered "sounds great". So the name is his by approval and the wording is hers, and the distinction is worth keeping: nothing in this book is his because he let it stand.

That proposed line has since been overtaken. Six turns later he said "This isn't about what I'm trying to build. This is about improving my life," which is wider than the line the domain still carries. The name survives the correction; the subtitle does not. This is filed as a finding rather than fixed here, because what the book is about is his to settle and not the recorder's.

He also corrected the interview process twice in the opening stretch, and both corrections shape what these pages are:

> Okay, you're reaching into other places for content, rather than just asking me the question from the blank page, and I think that's the wrong approach here.

> Okay, the process here isn't quite right still either. I corrected you earlier for pulling context from outside and not starting in the right place, but you've overcorrected now to only asking questions and making me do all the work. This should be a collaborative interview, not just a rubber duck.

He also had the interviewer's habit of putting propositions to him for knock-down removed outright:

> Yeah, lets strip the claim concept out from this process entirely and focus just on conversational questions instead.

**A gap in the record.** Of the fifty turns in this first session, the recorder received the first two. A misdirected forwarding marker dropped the other forty-eight, and they were recovered afterwards from the session transcript on disk rather than written live. So the quotations in this book are verbatim from that transcript, but the recorder was not present as they were said.
