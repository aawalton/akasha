- What recombination does across lineages is unbuilt. He has mutation as the source of novel alleles and recombination as a remix of alleles already filtered, and that is the right division, but the work recombination does is still confined to one lineage's offspring. The step he has not taken is that recombination gathers two beneficial mutations arisen in two different individuals into one genome, where a clonal lineage must wait for the second to arise inside the first — which is where the speed a sexual population has over a clonal one comes from.
