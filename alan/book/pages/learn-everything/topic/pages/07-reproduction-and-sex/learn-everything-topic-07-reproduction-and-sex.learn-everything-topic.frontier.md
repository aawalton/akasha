- The sexual side of the two-mutation comparison was handed to him rather than reached. He built the asexual side whole — the second mutation must arise again inside the first one's lineage, at the product of the two rates — and the other half was supplied to him, that sex brings the two carriers together in one zygote and so pays the sum rather than the product. He has not run the comparison himself, so whether he can produce it is not known.
- How sex escapes the ratchet. He has the irreversibility in an asexual population — the zero-load class returns only by a mutation reversing the damage — but not the other half, that two loaded sexual parents can recombine a zero-load genome back together out of their clean parts, so a sexual population rebuilds the class an asexual one has lost.
- Linkage as the single cause under both classical arguments. The sentence was supplied to him rather than reached: recombination randomises an allele's genetic background, so selection reads the allele's own average effect rather than the fate of the genome it landed in. He has the two arguments and he has the sentence, but he has not derived either from the other, and linkage disequilibrium is a name he has been given rather than a quantity he can use.
- Sex as three separable processes rather than one. Syngamy, independent assortment and crossing over were named to him rather than drawn out by him, and with them the split that matters — every benefit derived today belongs to crossing over, while the twofold cost he insisted on yesterday is charged for syngamy. He has not yet worked what follows from the bill and the goods arriving on separate accounts.
