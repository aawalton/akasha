- The sexual side of the two-mutation comparison was handed to him rather than reached. He built the asexual side whole — the second mutation must arise again inside the first one's lineage, at the product of the two rates — and the other half was supplied to him, that sex brings the two carriers together in one zygote and so pays the sum rather than the product. He has not run the comparison himself, so whether he can produce it is not known.
- How sex escapes the ratchet. He has the irreversibility in an asexual population — the zero-load class returns only by a mutation reversing the damage — but not the other half, that two loaded sexual parents can recombine a zero-load genome back together out of their clean parts, so a sexual population rebuilds the class an asexual one has lost. Nor has he said what the clicks do as they accumulate, which is to drive the whole population's fitness downward with nothing under it.
