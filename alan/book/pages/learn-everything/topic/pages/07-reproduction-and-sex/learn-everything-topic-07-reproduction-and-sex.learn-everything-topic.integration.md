- **Probability — strong, and load-bearing.** He settles biological questions by pricing them. Given the route to a double mutant in an asexual population he supplied the rate unasked, that the two mutation probabilities multiply, and the Fisher–Muller comparison is then product against sum. The quantitative reach is what carries him to the biology rather than an ornament on it.
- **Dynamical systems — strong.** He reads a population as a system whose state moves: proportions shifting under fitness, fixed fitness as the idealisation doing the work, context-dependent fitness as a complex dynamical system rather than a convergence. He parts the idealisation from the world without being asked to.
- **Frequency-dependent selection — strong, and reached twice.** Where fitness stops being fixed he goes to frequency dependence on his own, both for parasites finding the common genotype and for the two-lineage race. It is the instinct he reaches for reliably when a stable optimum is assumed.
- **Agriculture and monoculture — worked.** The Cavendish banana and Panama disease are his own case for the exposure a uniform genotype carries, produced without a request for an example.
- **Filtering and pre-selected material — forming.** He described recombination as a remix of material that had already cleared a filter, every allele good enough to carry a parent to reproduction, which is the pre-filtered-pool reading in his own words rather than a borrowed one. The connection to selection as a filter on distributions is there but he has not drawn it out.
