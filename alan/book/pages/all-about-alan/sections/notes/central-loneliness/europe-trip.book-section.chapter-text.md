
# The Europe trip

> The 16-day Europe trip with Jen, recorded after it ended. The verdict — slightly positive overall, and the first time that has happened since our honeymoon. The shape — neutral right until the end, then conceptual breakthroughs in the last few days that tipped it. And the two things I came back with: an experiment of sleeping in the bedroom again, and two date nights a week, one aimed at how Jen connects and one at how I connect. The results survived the trip; the context did not arrive with them.

Sixteen days in Europe, alone with Jen. The thinking I wanted ordered before I went is [the stay-or-leave ledger](stay-leave-ledger.book-chapter.md); this note is what the trip itself came to.

## The verdict — slightly positive, and the first since the honeymoon

> I ended the trip slightly positive for the trip overall, which was incredible. I've never done that before since our honeymoon.

## The shape — flat, then a late tip

> It was neutral right until the end and then the last few days we had some conceptual breakthroughs that tipped it over to positive for me.

So the trip did not accumulate to positive across sixteen days. Thirteen or so neutral days, then something conceptual near the end that re-scored the whole thing.

## What I came back with

> Two things, and I don't remember the context yet, but I remember the results.

The results:

- **Sleeping in the bedroom again**, as an experiment. What that arrangement was before, and the transition-cuddle fix that had been queued instead, is in [sleep-and-the-bedroom.md](../sexuality-knot/sleep-and-the-bedroom.book-chapter.md).
- **Two date nights per week** — *"one focused on how she connects and one focused on how I connect."* Their canonical names, set later, are **Alan-focused** and **Jen-focused**; what they turned out to be once running is [date-nights.md](date-nights.book-chapter.md).

A third thing surfaced later in the same sitting, named as another insight from the trip rather than as a result to run: that **relationships require sacrifice to exist**, sacrifice being a negative value on a positive-sum row. It is recorded in [alternation-not-moderation.md](../alternation-not-moderation.book-chapter.md#sacrifice-is-what-a-relationship-requires), and it collides with the [self-preservation adapter](../self-preservation-adapter.book-chapter.md), which forbids exactly that.

Worth keeping as a datum about how the trip is stored rather than about the trip: the results came back without the context. The context did come back afterwards, on being asked what made the bedroom the right experiment, and it is written down in [alternation-not-moderation.md](../alternation-not-moderation.book-chapter.md). This is the ordinary shape of what my write-selection keeps — [biography writes low, explanatory payoff writes high](../encoding-mechanism.book-chapter.md#biography-writes-low).
