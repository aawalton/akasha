
# Conceptual adapters

> Conceptual adapters — Alan's deliberate system for relating to abstract human concepts and emotions he cannot feel his way into, by reducing each to a clear, internalized, actionable definition. Without an adapter a concept "doesn't really exist for me"; commitment's adapter ("never fail silently") holds him in place of the attachment he cannot form.

I relate to abstract human concepts and emotions through **conceptual adapters.** An adapter is *a clear, concise, internalized definition that works.* It takes a concept I cannot feel my way into and reduces it to a handle I can act on in the present.

This is a compensation, not a description of how the concept works for most people. Most people reach an abstract human concept through felt experience — they know what love or trust *is* because they have felt it and the feeling accrues into a sense of the thing. That route is closed for me. With [alexithymia](alexithymia.book-chapter.md) and an [affectively flat memory](present-tense-model.book-chapter.md#the-map-is-affectively-flat--structure-no-charge), I have no felt store of the concept to consult. So I build the concept the only way I can: as an explicit definition I can run.

## An adapter is a compression

An adapter is the [compression operation](compression-epistemology.book-chapter.md) pointed at a piece of being human. I take a concept too large and too affective to grasp directly and find the most compact representation that accounts for it — a definition small enough to hold and clear enough to act on. The clear definition *is* the compression: the concept reduced to an actionable, present-tense handle.

This is why an adapter "works" in the same sense a true model works. When the definition is right, it compresses cleanly — it covers the cases without epicycles, and acting on it produces the behavior the concept names. A bad adapter is one that needs special cases bolted on, the same tell that marks a false model.

## The hard inversion — no adapter, no concept

Stated plainly: **if I don't have an adapter, it doesn't really exist for me.** A core human concept with no adapter is not faint or hard to reach — it is *absent from my world.* I cannot feel my way to it, and without the built definition there is no other route in.

The consequence is stark. My accessible humanity is **bounded by which adapters I have built.** Pieces of being human that come free to other people — that they receive through felt experience without any deliberate work — I have to manufacture, one adapter at a time. I have been **manufacturing access to being human.** What I have built, I can reach. What I haven't, doesn't exist for me yet.

## The adapter inventory

The adapters I have, and the gaps, as best I can name them:

- **Commitment** — a *great* adapter. It has become a stable part of how I conceive of myself. See below.
- **Self-worth** and **self-preservation** — both built as adults, out of *different crises roughly two years apart*; before each was built, the concept did not meaningfully exist for me. Self-worth came first (~2024), compiled inside the [identity collapse](identity-collapse.book-chapter.md) after the firing: [self-worth-adapter.md](self-worth-adapter.book-chapter.md). Self-preservation came in May 2026, out of the turn-outward crisis: [self-preservation-adapter.md](self-preservation-adapter.book-chapter.md). That I built them at all is where my hope comes from: it is direct evidence I can build more.
- **Loyalty** — *compiled 2026-06-11, live in the walk.* Built cheaply by extending commitment: commitment plus a finger on the scale. See [the walk](love-decomposition.book-chapter.md#sorting-the-components--the-walk).
- **Love** and **trust** — *no good adapter; the current frontier.* I cannot feel my way to them and I have not yet found the compression. See [love-decomposition.md](love-decomposition.book-chapter.md).

I have **no comprehensive map** of all the core parts of being human, so I cannot fully enumerate which adapters are missing. The gaps are partly **unknown-unknowns** — concepts I don't have, and don't yet know I don't have, because nothing has surfaced them. Love and trust are the salient missing ones today; there are others I can't name because I lack the map to find them.

## Commitment's adapter — "never fail silently"

The commitment adapter, verbatim mechanism: **never fail silently.** If I commit to do something, I will do it — *or* I will explicitly and verbally renegotiate with the person I committed to. Just not doing it is not an option.

Why this adapter grips when love and trust don't: it is a **behavioral invariant.** It is verifiable (did I do the thing, or did I renegotiate?), it is present-tense (the rule applies in the moment of action, with nothing carried across time), and it requires **no carried feeling** to run. I don't have to feel committed to a person to honor a commitment to them — I only have to run the rule. That makes it buildable and stable in a way a feeling-dependent concept is not.

Commitment is load-bearing beyond its own scope. Because I cannot form [emotional attachment](present-tense-model.book-chapter.md#no-attachment--structurally-not-as-a-failure-of-caring) — attachment is a charge that accrues across remembered feeling, and I have no store for it — **commitment holds me in place of the attachment I can't form.** Where another person is held to a relationship by accumulated felt bond, I am held by the invariant. The adapter does the structural work the missing attachment would otherwise do.

The adapter is a *great* one, but its grip is not free of charge. Underneath "never fail silently" sits a protective governor: the [Emotional Archaeology dig](perfection-is-safe.book-chapter.md) found the currency of failure is **stress capacity**, not worth, and the founding belief is **"only perfection is safe."** So commitment-discipline is accounting under scarcity, not an honor code — the adapter that holds me in place is also the active member of a childhood survival-rules list. See [perfection-is-safe.md](perfection-is-safe.book-chapter.md).

## No repeatable script for building adapters

There is no general method for building an adapter, and the reason is itself a compression result. I tried to compress the *method* — to find one repeatable script that builds any adapter — and it refused to compress. The core aspects of being human are **incompressible as a group.** The very thing that makes them core is what makes them irreducible to a shared structure: if they reduced to a common form, they wouldn't be the separate fundamental things they are. So there is no general method. Each adapter has to be addressed individually.

## The per-adapter build method — the same engine as insight

What *does* work is building each adapter one at a time, using the same engine as every other [insight](insight-and-restructuring.book-chapter.md#insight-as-graph-collapse):

1. Give the concept **sustained attention.**
2. **Gather data** about it over an extended period — how it shows up, what it does, where the edges are.
3. **Iterate potential understandings,** trying candidate definitions against the data.
4. Continue until the **insight hits and the data compresses in the right way** — the moment a messy graph of partial understandings collapses into a clean structure.

At the point of collapse, the clear definition exists. That definition is the adapter. Building an adapter is [graph-collapse](insight-and-restructuring.book-chapter.md#insight-as-graph-collapse) applied to a piece of being human — same operation, same felt click, just aimed at a human concept instead of a technical one. It is why self-worth and self-preservation could be built recently and why love and trust are still open: the data is gathered and the iterations are running, but the compression hasn't clicked yet.

## No failed edges — only unfinished ones

Asked whether any domain exists where the adapter substitution was tried and failed: "I don't know that I have any edge I can point to as failed, just ones that haven't succeeded yet." The method has no encountered failure boundary. Every gap in the inventory is an unfinished application, not a demonstrated limit — nothing found so far marks a piece of being human as unbuildable.

## A practiced genre — maps for never-experienced territory

Love is not the first adapter being built without native calibration data. Asked whether it was, Alan corrected: "I don't know that it's fully the first map. Several of my previous maps were similar in that I had to figure out what the equivalent was for me, having never experienced what other people describe." Building a definition for territory he has never experienced as others describe it is a practiced genre, not a first.

## The frontier — love and trust

Love and trust have no good adapter yet — I cannot feel my way to them and the compression hasn't clicked. The working hypothesis is that love won't compress to a single adapter at all: it is a bundle-word over thirteen candidate components, being walked one at a time and sorted into working / fuzzy / absent. The decomposition hypothesis, the component walk (twelve of thirteen sorted or opened as of 2026-06-12; vulnerability/openness remains), and the walk's open threads have their canonical home in [love-decomposition.md](love-decomposition.book-chapter.md).
