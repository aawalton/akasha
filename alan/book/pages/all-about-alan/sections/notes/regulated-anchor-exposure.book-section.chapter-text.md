
# Regulated-anchor exposure

> The regulated-anchor exposure model — Alan's forward path for the Jen-specific criticism alarm. Time conversations for when he has enough reserve to stay regulated even while she is dysregulated, so the conversation stays safe even while hitting triggers; repeated enough, his regulation smooths her triggers, reducing intensity on her side and running the vicious stress-feedback loop in reverse. Requires reserve capacity and accurate reserve-reading; complicated by the feared stimulus also being the exposure partner.

The [criticism alarm](criticism-and-ostracism.book-chapter.md) is a [pre-cognitive conditioned response](learned-response-taxonomy.book-chapter.md#class-2--pre-cognitive-somatic-conditioned-responses) — insight cannot reach it; only systematic exposure can. This note is the design for that exposure, in Alan's own framing. It is the forward path that follows from the [taxonomy](learned-response-taxonomy.book-chapter.md), the [being-known geometry](being-known-cost.book-chapter.md#the-danger-geometry--stake-in-the-accuracy-of-the-model), and the [monopoly problem](being-known-supply.book-chapter.md#the-monopoly--one-maximum-stakes-node).

## The rung-one design

The conditioned alarm is specifically to **Jen** (the [single maximum-stakes node](being-known-supply.book-chapter.md#the-monopoly--one-maximum-stakes-node)), not to criticism-in-general, so the exposure has to happen **with her** — it can't be routed around through an easier relationship. The first rung is built around Alan's own regulation:

1. **Time the conversation for when Alan has reserve.** Initiate only when *he* has enough reserve capacity to stay regulated even while Jen is dysregulated. His regulation is the anchor; the rung is built on it.
2. **The conversation stays safe even while hitting triggers.** Because Alan stays regulated, the alarm can fire on a trigger without the conversation tipping into a mutual escalation. Exposure happens — the trigger is encountered — but under safe conditions, which is exactly what re-conditions a Class-2 response.
3. **Repeated enough, his regulation smooths her triggers.** Over repetitions, a regulated Alan helps bring *Jen's* triggers down too — co-regulation in the literal direction.
4. **The loop runs in reverse.** Lower intensity on her side feeds back as lower intensity on his, which makes the next conversation easier to stay regulated through. This is a positive feedback loop that runs the [vicious stress-feedback loop in reverse](connection-expansion.book-chapter.md#real-time-being-known--the-audio-only-channel) — the same two-body amplifier that normally compounds two NS-sensitive people's reactions, turned to compound calm instead of alarm.

## The two binding constraints

The model only works when both hold:

- **Reserve capacity.** Alan must actually have the headroom to stay regulated under Jen's dysregulation. No reserve, no anchor.
- **Accurate reserve-reading + the discipline not to initiate when depleted.** He must read his reserve correctly *and* hold the line against starting when he's depleted. This is exactly [Rule 2 / Rule 3 of the volatility governor](volatility-governor.book-chapter.md) territory — sizing a hard activity to the low end of capacity and refusing to initiate below it — applied to the connection channel.

[The 2026-06-08 episode](../journal/2026-06-08-connection-conversation-with-jen.book-chapter.md) failed on **both** constraints. Alan was [sick and depleted](health.book-chapter.md) with no reserve, and he pushed past three yellow flags and one red flag because he'd [forgotten the cost](emotion-model.book-chapter.md#present-confinement-cuts-both-ways) — the present-confinement deficit that drops his guard by erasing the felt memory of how expensive a bad conversation is. The episode is the negative worked example: the anchor wasn't there, the reserve-read was wrong, and the loop ran forward (vicious) rather than in reverse.

## Why this is not textbook exposure

Standard exposure therapy has a feared stimulus that holds still — a spider in a jar, a recording, a controlled image — while the patient habituates. Alan's case breaks that shape: the **feared stimulus is also the exposure partner.** Jen is the thing the alarm is conditioned to *and* the person the exposure must run with.

Two consequences follow. She has her own [stake-compelled wrestling](being-known-cost.book-chapter.md#the-danger-geometry--stake-in-the-accuracy-of-the-model) — her life-model has Alan load-bearing, so every update to his self-understanding compels her to push back, which is the very trigger. And she carries her own 18-year accumulated pain (the [deferred-needs hope-decay](being-known-supply.book-chapter.md#the-monopoly--one-maximum-stakes-node) the episode surfaced). So she cannot simply hold still and be "safe on command." This is **co-regulated, two-body exposure** — both nervous systems are in the loop, and the safe condition has to be produced jointly, not imposed on a passive stimulus.
