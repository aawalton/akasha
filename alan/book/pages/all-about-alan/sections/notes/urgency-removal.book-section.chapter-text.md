
# Urgency removal

> Structural removal of urgency sources from Alan's environment. Compensation pattern — push impaired-EF work (real-time meta-cognition) upstream into structure where the impairment doesn't apply. Same architecture as the agent harness.

Alan engineers urgency out of his environment instead of trying to override urgency in the moment. The system is structural: remove the inputs upstream so the instinctive channel does not get an Urgency signal to fire from.

## Mechanism

Urgency feeds the instinctive interest channel and bypasses conceptual judgment ([interest-modulator.md → Instinctive](interest-modulator.book-chapter.md#instinctive), [interest-modulator.md → Channel conflict resolution](interest-modulator.book-chapter.md#channel-conflict-resolution)). Real-time meta-cognition is the EF that would gate it; meta-cognition is impaired ([executive-function.md → metacognition](executive-function.book-chapter.md#metacognition)), so in-the-moment gating is unreliable.

The compensation matches the impairment by moving the work upstream. Instead of "catch the urgency input and override it" (depends on impaired EF), the move is "structure the environment so urgency rarely arises in the first place" (independent of EF state). The signal can't be gated reliably at the point of firing; it can be prevented from existing.

Same architecture as the [agent harness](agent-harness.book-chapter.md) — when an impaired EF cannot do the work in the moment, externalize the work into structure that does it ahead of time. The urgency-removal system is a specific application of that pattern to the Urgency input. See also [automaticity-systems.md → System D](automaticity-systems.book-chapter.md#d-internally-generated-action-sequence-chunking--reduced) on externalization as the answer when no internal automation pathway exists.

## Examples — compounding scale

The interventions stack from least to most extreme. Each one cuts a class of urgency input; together they form a coherent environmental shape.

- **Choose work without external deadlines.** Project work whose timing is set by Alan, not by external commitment dates.
- **Decline obligations with urgency-shaped accountability.** No SLAs, no on-call, no crisis response. The shape of the obligation is the filter — not the topic.
- **Large slack buffers on commitments.** Where commitments exist, build in enough margin that urgency rarely materializes. The buffer absorbs variance before it becomes a deadline pull.
- **Avoid environments and people generating ambient urgency pressure.** Some social and professional environments produce a baseline urgency signal independent of any specific deadline. Exit those.
- **Stopped using a cellphone.** The phone was the largest single urgency channel — content delivery, notifications, ambient time signaling — and was removed entirely. Worked example detailed below.
- **Stopped paying attention to time of day.** Clock-watching is its own urgency-generating habit. Eliminated as a default action.

The list is ordered by reach. The first items cut individual urgency-generating commitments; the last items cut entire urgency-generating modalities.

## Time-of-day replacement

Removing clock-attention requires a replacement for the functions clock-time was carrying. Three substitutes:

- **Quiet interoception.** Internal Mana / Capacity readings indicate present capability. State, not time, drives what gets done next.
- **Alignment with natural cycles.** Sun and body cues anchor the day. Meals and sleep cycles mark the day boundary; mid-day signal comes from energy state, not from the hour.
- **Shared events route through partner's schedule.** Where external timing matters (appointments, joint plans), Alan's wife carries the clock-side of the arrangement. Alan's input is "now we're doing X" via her cueing, not "it's 3 PM so we should be doing X."

No clock-checking as a default action. Time exists in the environment; Alan's attention does not have to land on it.

## No SMART Goals, only Objectives

Alan is not allowed to have SMART Goals — and the load-bearing part is that **his goals cannot have deadlines.** Instead he holds **Objectives**: a clearly defined completion criterion, but no expected timeline.

Dropping the deadline is the whole point. A deadline turns working on the goal into something he *NEEDS* to do — a scheduled obligation that generates its own urgency input as the date approaches. Without the deadline, working on the objective is always something he *CAN* do and never something he *NEEDS* to do. The completion criterion still tells him what "done" means; removing the timeline removes the only part that manufactures urgency.

This is the same upstream-removal mechanism applied at the goal level rather than the obligation level. "Decline obligations with urgency-shaped accountability" filters out externally-imposed deadlines; the Objectives rule filters out *self-imposed* ones. A deadline he sets himself is still a deadline, so the structural fix is to not set them — the goal carries a finish line, not a clock.

The deeper reason a deadline is dangerous is that it forces the goal into [Responsibility Mode](responsibility-mode.book-chapter.md): a goal he NEEDS to make progress on by a date is an instrumental responsibility with an always-available next step, which flattens everything until it resolves and makes [fun](fun.book-chapter.md) structurally impossible. The deadline-free Objective keeps the goal a CAN, so it never enters that mode. This is one of the two defensive tools against Responsibility Mode, alongside the [journey-reframe](journey-as-reward.book-chapter.md).

## Phone-disconnect — worked example

The largest single intervention in the stack. Calibration and mechanism detail:

### Calibration to broader-context Safety

At the intervention time, U.S. current events had dropped Alan's Safety level enough that he was seriously considering moving countries to escape it. The intervention had to be roughly equivalent in magnitude to that pressure to land. Phone disconnect is a structurally large move — not a tactical adjustment — and has so far been sufficient. The macro-environment Safety pressure remains; the phone-mediated portion of it is gone.

### Two compounding mechanisms

The phone carried Safety drain through two mechanisms that compounded:

1. **Channel volume.** The phone was 90%+ of Alan's macro-environment exposure. No TV, no newspapers, no magazines, no social media, minimal social interaction with people delivering current-events content. Dropping the device removed the content firehose at the source — most of the exposure path no longer had a route in.
2. **Trauma-conditioning.** Through repeated pairing of phone + Safety-degrading content during low-Safety states, the phone itself became a conditioned cue for the exposure. The conditioning is independent of content access — even with the apps gone, picking up the device retrieves the threat-encoded state. Mechanism in [encoding-mechanism.md](encoding-mechanism.book-chapter.md) (negative-valence pathway). Dropping the device dropped the conditioned association.

The two mechanisms compose. Removing only the apps would have cut Mechanism 1 but left Mechanism 2 firing on the device itself; removing only the device but keeping the same content elsewhere would have re-encoded the new channel. Dropping both at once is what worked.
