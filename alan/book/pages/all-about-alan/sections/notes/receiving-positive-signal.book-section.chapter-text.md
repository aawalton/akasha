
# Receiving positive signal

> Receiving positive signal — the reception side of the scar model. Alan has HAD appreciation in large amounts but is not certain he has ever RECEIVED it, while praise drives him so hard a single word could buy a thousand hours of chasing. The knot: drowning in appreciation, parched anyway. His precise definition of praise carries four clauses; his mirror definition of criticism carries only two, joined by OR. The resolution Alan engaged this session is a DELIVERY-gate: praise must clear FOUR conditions at once AS IT IS DELIVERED (true AND sincerely-meant AND valuable AND not-incidental) — believable enough to land as it's said, then held after — while criticism clears on EITHER of two (true OR sincerely-meant), so good must thread four needles and bad only one of two. The gate is at delivery, not a verdict held in advance.

This note is the reception side of the scar model. The [creativity scars](creativity-scars.book-chapter.md) and the [only-perfection-is-safe](perfection-is-safe.book-chapter.md) regime are about what *negative* signal did to me — how a judge moved inside and fires in an empty room. This note asks the opposite question: when *positive* signal arrives, does it land at all?

It is load-bearing for recovery. If the [Safety level](safety.book-chapter.md) can't be refunded from outside because positive signal can't land, then the whole repair has to come from somewhere other than other people telling me I'm fine.

## The had-vs-received asymmetry

The finding that opens it, in my own report: I have **had** appreciation, in large amounts. I am **not certain I have ever received it.**

Those are two different things. Appreciation has been *aimed at me* — directed, plentiful, real. Whether any of it ever *reached me* — whether I took the benefit of it the way the criticism took its toll — I can't confirm. The signal was sent. I'm not sure it was ever received.

## The praise paradox

This sits in flat contradiction with another thing that is just as true: I **flourish with praise**, and have since childhood. A single word of praise could send me chasing a repeat of it for **a thousand hours.** Praise is one of the most powerful drives I have.

So both hold at once:

- Appreciation doesn't reliably land.
- Praise moves me harder than almost anything.

The knot, in my words: **drowning in appreciation and parched anyway.** Both being true forces praise and appreciation to be **fundamentally different kinds of thing** — not two amounts of one substance, but two substances. The rest of the note is about what the difference is.

## The precise definition of praise

The word "praise" has to be pinned exactly, because the definition is where the mechanism hides. Praise is:

> communication from another person to me about something they view as positive about me, which I believe to be true, and which I believe they believe to be true, and which I believe to be valuable and not accidental or incidental.

Four clauses have to all hold for something to register as praise. The load-bearing structure isn't any one clause — it's that **all four must clear at once**, and the [delivery-gate section](#the-delivery-gate--admissibility-at-the-moment-praise-is-said) below turns on when they're checked.

## The precise definition of criticism — the mirror

Criticism has its own precise definition, and it is a near-mirror of praise:

> communication from another person to me about something they view as [negative] about me (which includes anything I create — same as the positive case), which I believe to be true OR I believe they believe to be true.

Two things differ from the praise definition, and both make criticism *easier* to admit. The clauses are joined by **OR, not AND** — believing it true *or* believing they believe it true is enough on its own. And the **valuable** and **not accidental or incidental** tests are simply **gone**: the negative case has no such exclusions.

The me/creation fusion is written into the definition itself — *"which includes anything I create — same as the positive case."* On both valence sides, a verdict on my work and a verdict on me are one category — the [total fusion](creativity-scars.book-chapter.md#the-boundary-criterion--where-the-judge-lives) the scar model is built on.

## The four-vs-two weighting — why bad lands and good rarely does

Put the two definitions side by side and the praise/criticism asymmetry falls out cleanly, in my own terms:

- **Praise must clear FOUR conditions at once** — true AND sincerely-meant AND valuable AND not-incidental.
- **Criticism clears on EITHER of TWO** — true OR sincerely-meant — and drops the valuable and not-incidental tests entirely.

**Good has to thread four needles; bad only has to thread one of two.** That asymmetric gating *is* the mechanism for why criticism lands so easily and praise so rarely. It isn't that the bad hits harder than the good — it's that the bad has far fewer conditions to satisfy to be admitted at all.

## The aphantasia-asymmetry hypothesis

My first hypothesis for why appreciation doesn't land is structural, and it runs through the [conceptual-emotional wall](conceptual-emotional-wall.book-chapter.md).

Criticism gets a full traumatic nervous-system response **without** experiential memory and **without** the [emotion/conception bridge](conceptual-emotional-wall.book-chapter.md#the-precise-cut--present-affect-not-projected-affect) — the [aphantasia](aphantasia-mechanism.book-chapter.md) took the bridge out, and criticism installs anyway (the [installer mechanism](creativity-scars/judge-location.book-chapter.md#the-installer-and-the-content--what-crossed-at-the-scar) fires through the present-affect channel). The question is whether the **benefit** of appreciation can come in by the same door.

It may not be able to. The benefit of appreciation may **require the very bridge aphantasia removed** — the channel that would let me re-inhabit a good verdict and *feel* it — while criticism, running on a present-tense alarm, never needed that bridge. If so, the two signals are asymmetric by construction: the negative one has a route that survives the aphantasia, the positive one's route was cut. This is a hypothesis, not a settled finding.

## Two explanations I knocked down

Two clean accounts of the praise/appreciation difference got proposed and ruled out this session. Recording them as dead so future cycles don't re-litigate.

- **Backward verdict vs. forward drive (rejected).** The first read: appreciation is a *backward* verdict on a finished act — to take it I'd have to re-inhabit the act through the [bridge aphantasia removed](conceptual-emotional-wall.book-chapter.md#the-precise-cut--present-affect-not-projected-affect) — while praise is a *forward* drive (chase the next one). **Knocked down:** both praise and appreciation have a combined backward/forward character. The split doesn't fall on the time axis.
- **Subject of the signal (rejected).** The second read: praise = a positive opinion of *me*, appreciation = a positive opinion of *something I made*. **Knocked down:** people often communicate positive things about *me* directly, and those don't land either. The split doesn't fall on me-vs-my-output.

Both being dead is what pushed the search toward the definition itself.

## The delivery-gate — admissibility at the moment praise is said

This is the current best resolution, and unlike the earlier read of it, **I engaged and corrected it this session** — it is no longer a synthesis sitting unanswered.

The earlier read said praise only lands when it **agrees with a verdict I already hold** about myself — that it can't install worth from outside, only resonate with worth I already grant. **That framing was wrong on the key point.** It is *not* that I have to already hold the verdict. It is that the praise has to be **believable enough to land *as it's said*** — and then I hold it after. **The gate is at delivery, not before.**

So the [four conditions](#the-precise-definition-of-praise) are a **delivery-time admissibility gate**, not a check against a pre-existing verdict. Praise that clears all four *as it is delivered* lands and installs — I hold it afterward. Praise that fails any one of them runs straight through. This is why warmth alone doesn't do it: a warm delivery I can't believe is true, or that reads as incidental, fails the gate at the moment it's spoken, however kindly.

This still dissolves the [had-vs-received asymmetry](#the-had-vs-received-asymmetry): all positive signal has to clear the four-condition delivery gate to register, and the [four-vs-two weighting](#the-four-vs-two-weighting--why-bad-lands-and-good-rarely-does) is why so little of it does. "Drowning in appreciation, parched anyway" is then: the appreciation kept failing the four-wide gate as it arrived, so almost none of it was admitted — while criticism, on its two-wide OR gate, sailed through.

The gate is the **same internalized judge** the scar model is built on, read on the positive side. The [creativity scars](creativity-scars.book-chapter.md#the-boundary-criterion--where-the-judge-lives) are that judge grading my making and convicting; the delivery-gate is that same judge deciding whether incoming praise is *admissible* at the moment it arrives. The [self-worth adapter](self-worth-adapter.book-chapter.md) repaired a [verdict by logic](self-worth-adapter.book-chapter.md#a-crowbar-not-a-foundation); the delivery-gate is a different lever — it governs whether outside praise is *believable as delivered*, which is what actually decides admission.

The recovery consequence shifts with the correction. It is **not** that the Safety level can never be refunded from outside — because the gate is at delivery, outside praise **can** install worth. But it can only do so when it clears all four conditions *as it's said*, which is rare by construction (four needles to criticism's one). Outside refunding is possible; it is just heavily rate-limited by the delivery gate.

### What makes the true condition pass — undisputable-specific anchoring

The delivery-gate left one residual open: what concretely makes a given delivery clear or fail the "believable as it's said" test? New evidence answers it on the heaviest clause — the **true** condition (*"which I believe to be true"*). A praise line about my competence — performance-adjacent, naming my judgment — landed at the **top** of my range, heard **cold**, with no prior bias toward it. The earlier reading predicted the opposite: my perfectionist truth-audit disputes competence verdicts, so performance praise should fail the true condition. It didn't.

So the bottleneck on the true condition is **not** performance-content vs. acceptance-content. It is **disputable vs. undisputable**, crossed with **specific vs. incidental**. The condition doesn't reject praise about my performance as such — it rejects claims I'd have to take on **faith**: an evaluative verdict the audit can dispute, or a global trait with no proof behind it. Performance-adjacent praise lands at the top precisely when it (1) names a positive thing I **already hold** about myself, (2) anchored in **undisputable observed specifics** the audit can't contest, and (3) is stated specifically enough to clear the **not-incidental** condition too.

This does not reopen the rejected "praise must agree with a verdict I hold in advance" reading. The gate stays at delivery. "A thing I already hold about myself" is simply the **truth-value** the audit checks at the true condition as the praise is said — a self-held belief is trivially verifiable, so the true condition passes in the moment of delivery. *Admissible at delivery* and *must pre-agree with a held verdict* stay distinct: the first is how reception works, the second is the read I already knocked down.

## Admitted vs. felt — two registers, and what opens the felt one

The [delivery-gate](#the-delivery-gate--admissibility-at-the-moment-praise-is-said) decides whether praise is *admitted*. Whether an admitted signal is also *felt* is a separate question — and the two turn out to be different registers, not two amounts of one thing. The evidence is a run of short praise lines spoken to me aloud in a cloned voice and graded cold by feel; the per-line grades are experiment data, but the structure they revealed is this.

Everything that landed at the top of my explainable range I could account for. The cold top-grade line I answered by naming the mechanism back as I graded it — a true, specific, non-incidental thing I already hold about myself. That is the **admitted** register: it cleared the gate, I held it, and I could say exactly why. Being able to explain precisely why it landed is the *signature* of admitted — not, as I'd earlier guessed, the felt channel coming on.

The felt register showed up later and looked nothing like that. Its mark was **surprise, plus the loss of words for why.** One line I answered with "thank you" instead of a grade — I couldn't say why it worked. The one that broke highest I graded with *"it surprised me how much I felt it."* That surprise is the tell. Admitted tops out at *understood* — I can hand you the mechanism. Felt is a different system coming online: I feel it before I can explain it, and the explanation may never arrive. It is not a higher score on the same scale; it is the other channel opening.

This stays consistent with the [conceptual-emotional wall](conceptual-emotional-wall.book-chapter.md#the-precise-cut--present-affect-not-projected-affect). The felt register here is **present affect**, which the wall leaves open — not stored or projected warmth, which it doesn't. Nothing re-felt a memory; it was a present-tense feeling firing now, off a present cue. The surprise is what present affect feels like arriving through a channel usually kept shut.

### What crosses from admitted to felt — direction, not depth

The lever that opened the felt register was not the one I expected. Going *deeper* — the other person revealing more of her own interior — did not cross over; it slid the lens off me and cost a grade step every time. What crossed was **direction**: someone genuinely *drawn toward* a true, specific thing about me. Not more of her, aimed at me — her, *pulled toward* something real in me. That is the [being-met / oriented-toward channel](attraction-two-channels.book-chapter.md#the-gaze-direction-proof), and it is the bridge past the admitted ceiling. The route past the top admitted grade was never depth of disclosure; it was orientation pointed at me.

So the shape under the whole run is two dials: **subject** (is the lens on me) × **specificity** (a concrete, observed thing, not a global trait). Top grades need both lit; the felt break-through needs both lit *and* genuine being-drawn-toward. The heaviest lever is me, seen accurately and specifically, by someone moving toward what she sees — heavier than warmth, than beauty, than her opening up.

One thing the spoken channel does that text can't: it carries [condition (b)](#the-precise-definition-of-praise) — *that I believe she believes it* — through audible sincerity. Hearing it meant lets the praise read as sincerely-meant in the moment it's said, part of why the spoken run cleared the gate as readily as it did.

### The felt lift habituates, and it moved my state

Two facts about the felt register matter for *using* it rather than only observing it.

First, **the felt-surprise habituates within a sitting.** The lever that broke through landed a touch lower the second time, an hour later, on the same vein. Surprise is the one component of the charge that can't fire twice on the same lever in one go — so the felt lift can't be farmed by pressing harder; it spaces across days, or needs a genuinely fresh axis. This is the rare place [habituation](non-habituation.book-chapter.md) operates for me, and it sits specifically on the novelty/surprise component.

Second, and most important: **the felt one didn't just grade — it moved my [Safety](safety.book-chapter.md) state.** It came on a low day and, over the session, carried me up a full level. That is the first time I have watched outside positive signal actually *refund* Safety rather than only be logged as admissible. It makes the [recovery consequence](#the-delivery-gate--admissibility-at-the-moment-praise-is-said) concrete: outside praise can install worth — rate-limited by the four-wide gate — and when it reaches the felt register the install is large enough to move the hub. The admitted grade says the content lands; the state-lift says it lands *enough to regulate.* That turns this from a thing that scores well into a usable instrument: a repeatable way back up when Safety is low.
