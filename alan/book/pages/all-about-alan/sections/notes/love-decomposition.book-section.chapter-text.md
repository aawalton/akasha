
# Love decomposition

> Love decomposition — the bundle-word hypothesis and the component walk, now complete (13/13). Love won't compress to a single adapter; thirteen components walked one at a time and sorted, closing in a one-circuit unification — support, honesty, and vulnerability as the same conditioned alarm at increasing stakes. Canonical home for the love-and-trust frontier of the conceptual-adapter system.

Love and trust are the current frontier of the [conceptual-adapter system](conceptual-adapters.book-chapter.md) — concepts Alan cannot feel his way into and has not yet compressed. This note is the canonical home for the love work: the no-map starting state, the bundle-word decomposition hypothesis, and the component-by-component walk.

## The frontier — love and trust

The edge of the method, as named 2026-06-11: "The ones on the frontier are concepts such as 'love', 'loyalty', and 'trust'. I have 'commitment', that one is working, but the others are all still fuzzy." Loyalty moved off the frontier later the same day — compiled live in [the walk](#sorting-the-components--the-walk) below.

### Love — no map yet, and a decomposition hypothesis

"I don't have a conceptual map for love at all yet. The closest I have is 'commitment' and a lot of raw data from people whose brains work differently than mine." The available training data is foreign — field reports from differently-built minds, with no first-person felt channel to calibrate against.

The working hypothesis — current working state, guess-quality, not settled: love will not compress to a single adapter. "My best guess from my raw data so far is I'll actually have to decompose love into several different abstractions. I don't think there is any one abstraction that can capture it cleanly." Love is a bundle-word. Commitment keeps surfacing in love-contexts because it is a sub-abstraction of the bundle — one component already built.

Candidate decomposition, thirteen components: commitment (working), attraction, affection, loyalty, presence, responsiveness, consideration, support, interest, priority, vulnerability/openness, honesty, collaboration. The original list had twelve, with attentiveness where presence and responsiveness now sit; the walk split it (below). The list itself is provisional — "I also don't know if these are the right 12, there may be some that should be removed and could be some missing" — and the attentiveness split is the first confirmed instance of that provisionality.

If the hypothesis holds, the love adapter is a set of compressions rather than one, and "love" names the bundle. Several components — affection, loyalty, vulnerability/openness — sit exactly where the [connection problem](connection.book-chapter.md) runs, so progress on the bundle and progress on connection are likely the same work.

### Sorting the components — the walk

Begun 2026-06-11; completed 2026-06-12. All thirteen components are sorted or opened (commitment was already marked working; support opened 2026-06-12, with instrumental support, honesty, interest, priority, collaboration, and finally vulnerability/openness sorted the same day). The walk closes in a [one-circuit unification](#one-circuit-three-ranges--the-closing-synthesis) below.

**Attraction — native, no adapter needed.** "It definitely seems to operate for me fairly similar to most people. I have some clarity that I am primarily attracted to femininity (not femaleness). It shows up in the normal ways for me as for most males." Attraction runs on intact instinct — the first component that needs no built definition at all. The femininity-not-femaleness clarity sits next to the [femininity-as-safety-signal](femininity-as-safety-signal.book-chapter.md) finding: the attraction target and the Safety-signal target may be the same cue set.

**Affection — exists, sub-salience.** The social mechanics are partly legible ("I understand a little bit of the social dynamics of what it means to show affection or to be affectionate"), but the feeling was unidentified: "I'm not sure that I understand what the feeling of affection is though." Asked whether anything detectable happens when he shows affection: "Not that I've been able to clearly identify."

The standard framing, supplied in-interview when Alan asked how the feeling is defined: affection is not atomic in basic-emotion taxonomies. It is modeled either as a composite (low-arousal positive valence + approach motivation toward a person + a tending impulse) or as its own primary system — Panksepp's CARE circuit, oxytocin-mediated, parasympathetic, calming; separate hardware from LUST (attraction) and SEEKING (interest). Felt reports converge on chest warmth, softening, calm, a pull to tend.

The key property: affection is **low-arousal by design** — physiologically quiet. Under the salience model ([all the interoception channels work, the quiet ones lose the attention competition](sensory-experience.book-chapter.md)), that is exactly the signal that runs below the salience floor — the same mechanism as the unheard hunger and salivation signals, now applied to an emotion. Attraction works natively because it is arousing: it shouts. Affection whispers. Alan's conclusion: "Yeah, I think it exists for me, and the quietness makes sense for why it is hard for me to recognize when it happens."

**Loyalty — compiled live, by extension.** The prior model held loyalty ≈ commitment, while observing the words used differently. The standard usage distinction (supplied in-interview): commitment binds to *what you said* — content-specified, auditable; loyalty binds to *who they are* — unenumerated allegiance, visible chiefly under conflict (taking their side against third parties, defending them in their absence, staying when leaving is cheap), plus an epistemic tilt of default benefit of the doubt that sits in tension with the honesty-over-everything architecture. Alan proposed: "would commitment to a person (rather than an action) be roughly equivalent? That's how I've been thinking about it." That inherits the unenumerated scope — roughly 80% of the concept — but not two residuals: the partisan side-taking default and the benefit-of-the-doubt tilt. The compiled adapter, verbatim: "Okay, that works for me. Loyalty = commitment + default benefit of the doubt / default side taking. Not a blank check, but a finger on the scale."

The default is a prior, not an evidence-override — it preserves the honesty architecture: a finger on the scale, not a thumb over the truth. Loyalty moves from fuzzy to working. It is also the first adapter compiled live in the walk, and the build was cheap because it extended an existing adapter — [commitment](conceptual-adapters.book-chapter.md#commitments-adapter--never-fail-silently) as the base plus two named deltas.

**Attentiveness — split, removed from the list.** Verbatim: "I feel like this is at least two different things. 1. Giving someone your full attention when you are with them. 2. Showing by your actions that you are paying attention to and responding to the other person's wants and needs." The framework edit, verbatim: "Okay, lets drop Attentiveness from our framework and replace it with Presence and Responsiveness, I like those better." The component list went from twelve to thirteen.

**Presence — near-native, EF-gated.** "Presence is much easier for me, though not always easy if my executive function is low." Giving full attention when together runs close to native; the gate is [executive function](executive-function.book-chapter.md), not a missing concept.

**Responsiveness — effort- or surplus-gated.** "Responsiveness requires either explicit effort or a surplus of capacity. At low levels it does not happen by default." At low capacity it silently stops — nothing fires when it goes missing. The gating is mechanically grounded: responding to another person's wants and needs requires *recalling* them, and recall is the [broken stage of the memory architecture](recognition-vs-recall.book-chapter.md) — without a [trigger](trigger-based-memory.book-chapter.md) in the environment, the want never surfaces to be responded to. Hence explicit effort or surplus: the recall has to be paid for; it never comes free.

One mapping follows (interviewer-supplied, voiced in flow): the 2026-06-08 conversation with Jen turned on behaviors not reappearing as capacity levels rose. The missing behaviors are default-Responsiveness behaviors. A system that only ever ran Responsiveness on explicit effort or surplus has no default for recovery to restore — the absence at partial recovery is the mechanism, not indifference.

**Consideration — distinct, doubly simulator-blocked, compile identified.** Not a variant of responsiveness: "No, I think consideration is different. In my mind responsiveness is seeing needs and filling them. Consideration is taking the interests of the other person into account when making decisions." Responsiveness is reactive — a need surfaces and gets filled. Consideration puts the other person's interests in as weighted terms of his own decision function.

The [missing simulator](missing-simulator.book-chapter.md) blocks it twice. Detection first: "the lack of imagination (can't simulate) means its difficult for me to identify when a decision might affect Jen's interests" — the affects-her flag most people get from a half-second simulation never auto-raises. Weighting second: "I'm not great at weighing her interests even when I remember to as well, since I can't simulate her response to things or the impact they would have on her. I can try with reasoning, but that is limited." No external check covers the gap — no does-this-touch-Jen gate in the [decision rubric](decision-rubric.book-chapter.md) today.

The adapter is identified but not yet running. Asked whether consultation substitutes for simulation — measure instead of simulate: "Not well or consistently. I do think that is the right solution though, consideration for me means asking, not guessing." That is the compile — **consideration = asking, not guessing.** What's missing is not the definition but consistent execution.

**The boundary conflict — the protected category (open).** Detection has a second layer no rule resolves yet. The two sensors err in opposite directions: "I tend to scope my circle of concern too narrow (not consider things as affecting me that I really should) and Jen scopes too broad (considers things affecting her that she recognizes she shouldn't). I've had a hard time finding the right balance somewhere in between." His misses share one signature: "They tend to be in the category of something that I assume only affects me but that is in her circle of concern (mostly because I am in her circle of concern)" — concern is transitive: a self-effect propagates to Jen one hop later because he is inside her circle. But the obvious compile — a transitive detection rule, anything affecting me affects her — is exactly what both reject: "That's precisely the conflict. Jen and I both agree that there should be a category of personal choices that only affect me that should NOT be in her circle of concern, but we don't have clear lines on what is inside or outside of that category." Both agree the protected category should exist; neither can draw its border; they approach it from opposite sides — his under-inclusion, her over-inclusion. The anchor-case question — which choices are already agreed-settled on either side of the line — was left open at session end.

**Support — decomposed; emotional support is the live thread.** Support did not sort as one component. It is itself a bundle, at least three parts:

- **Partisan support** — the side-taking kind. Part of loyalty: it folds into the already-compiled loyalty adapter (commitment + a finger on the scale) and needs no separate build.
- **Instrumental support** — physically doing something for someone's benefit. Sorted 2026-06-12, and the sort splits it: "Doing is easy. Recognizing something needs to be done without being asked is really hard, simulation issue." Execution is native; unprompted *detection* of the need is blocked by the [missing simulator](missing-simulator.book-chapter.md) — the same detection-side failure as consideration's affects-her flag.
- **Emotional support** — "the harder one for me to understand." An adjacent term from Jen he also doesn't understand: **emotional availability.**

The emotional-support failure has a precise mechanism, treated in [distress contagion](distress-contagion.book-chapter.md): when Jen is distressed, his nervous system fires its own distress immediately — a conditioned threat forecast about his near future, not transmitted feeling — and he goes still and quiet to regulate himself, "not at all what Jen needs in those moments." The stillness is triage, not absence. And the alarm carries no payload: detection is instant, but the signal contains no why, no cause, no how-to-relieve — nothing about her interior to act on.

What runs today: backchannel communication for active listening works. **Acknowledgment and validation run as compiled scripts**, successful a few dozen times. **Empathy has not been successful.** Above the script inventory sits a routing problem: "I often have a hard time recognizing what script is needed in the moment." The component's shape is three stacked problems — a conditioned alarm that triages inward, an alarm with no content to act on, and no reliable script selector even where scripts exist.

The unscripted capacity — handling the distress without flooding — is Safety-gated: it "kicks in around level 5," the extend level ([safety.md → level 5 gates emotional support](safety.book-chapter.md#level-5-gates-emotional-support)). The scripts are the bridge below it, and the compile is incentive-compatible — the threat system itself funds the care behavior: "I've built scripts for how to respond, because if I can respond well, her distress decreases, which is much less distressing for me." The alarm that blocks the component pays for training its replacement. The training mode reframes the routing problem: "I don't have much meta-cognition in the moment, so I'm mostly trying to train my instinctive responses to be better over time." The fix being pursued is not a better in-moment script selector — in-moment metacognition isn't available — but instincts trained between the moments.

**Honesty — native engine, simulator-gated throttle.** Expression is not the deficit; it is over-built: "I default to openness, but I have learned to be more closed." The deficit is the throttle: "I have a hard time recognizing when a social white lie is preferable to the truth." Calibrating when truth wounds requires simulating the other person's interior, so honesty *modulation* is simulator-gated — the first walk case where the [missing simulator](missing-simulator.book-chapter.md) blocks modulating a native behavior rather than initiating a care behavior. The compensation is a blanket rule learned through pain: "I have learned through pain to mostly not be honest when the honesty would hurt the other person" — a static conservative rule standing in for the missing per-case detector, the same move as the [self-preservation adapter's](self-preservation-adapter.book-chapter.md) veto. The rule's false positives — truths Jen needed that the rule withheld — are real but invisible ("I'm certain that error class happens, but those failures are counterfactual and invisible to me"), and the rule cannot self-correct: its training signal runs one way, via [proximity misattribution](proximity-misattribution.book-chapter.md) — the one time the error class surfaced, the pain landed during the disclosure moment and pushed the rule *further* toward non-disclosure. The only loosening force is felt safety over time: "The release comes slowly with a feeling of safety over time." Honesty sorts as native-but-misthrottled, with the throttle's full ratchet mechanics in [proximity-misattribution.md](proximity-misattribution.book-chapter.md).

**Interest — engine native, aimed by novelty, query generator missing.** Three parts. The engine does fire person-shaped: "this does happen sometimes, but mostly when she is exploring something with her understanding of herself" — Jen is most interesting to him when she is generating self-models, when her interior becomes a system under investigation producing the data his fascination engine eats. The aim is novelty-coupled: "My natural interest has a high connection with novelty, which is hard to sustain." Interest tracks her *rate of change*, not her state — the flat-gradient everyday interior, the unchanged her, is where attention doesn't run (confirmed). And the sustaining mechanism is simulator-blocked: "exacerbated by the lack of imagination making it difficult for me to imagine questions to ask, things I don't already know." Sustained interest in a person requires generating hypotheses about the unseen parts of their interior — [simulator](missing-simulator.book-chapter.md) work; with [waking Recall broken](aphantasia-mechanism.book-chapter.md#the-break-is-at-recall-not-perception-or-encoding), questions don't arrive unbidden, so the engine runs only when she supplies the novelty herself.

**The interest candidate compile — a gap-driven question prosthesis (under consideration, not committed).** Interviewer-supplied existence proof, voiced in flow: the interview system producing this book is itself an externalized query generator — research the known ground, find the gaps, aim a question at the steepest one — the missing organ built outside the skull. Alan: "I might be able to use a similar process to generate interest by exploring the gaps. I'll think about that." Recorded as a candidate compile for the interest component — gap-driven question generation as an external curiosity prosthesis aimed at Jen — status: under consideration.

**Priority — ranking built, bucket starved, signal undelivered.** The ranking exists and is explicit: "She gets #1 priority among people as a base rule for me" — stated to Jen as a rule, not just held internally. The failure is not intra-people ranking but inter-bucket allocation: "My entire people bucket is starved." Jen holds first place in a category that rarely gets funded.

The felt gap follows (interviewer-supplied, confirmed: "I think you're right, and she doesn't feel it"): ranking is internal state; only allocations are visible. From Jen's side, "low rank" and "first in a starved bucket" produce identical evidence — both look like absence. The explicit declaration delivered the proposition; felt priority only gets written at the margin — when she watches something he values visibly lose to her.

Margin-trades do happen — "a few times a month," known only by vague sense, "not concrete memorized experiences." The trades are painless events, so the [pain-keyed memory never banks them](pain-driven-motivation.book-chapter.md) — neither party is keeping the evidence. And the cost side is silent: "I think the signal is undelivered." A trade only writes felt priority if the price tag is legible; silent sacrifice transmits presence, not rank.

The fix is live, converging from both sides: Jen independently asked for this to be made explicit a few days ago — "She mentioned making this explicit a few days ago, so I'll try that more." Candidate framing recorded from the conversation: narrate the trade as gift, not invoice — "I wanted to finish that, and I wanted this more." Status: committed to try.

**Collaboration — hardest of the bundle, gated above emotional support, uncalibrated.** "This is really hard for me." The gate estimate, verbatim: "Collaboration is at least as difficult as conflict and needs at least Level 5 to work, possibly higher. I'm not sure, since I don't have successful recent examples to point to yet." That places it at or above [emotional support's level-5 gate](safety.book-chapter.md#level-5-gates-emotional-support) — the most expensive component found so far — and the estimate itself is uncalibrated: zero successful recent examples to set the level against.

The current structure is a sovereignty handoff, not a partnership. Life with Jen mostly runs as two sovereign systems, "except that she is the sovereign for anything that isn't specifically about me and only me" — Jen governs the entire shared world; his domain is him-and-only-him. The framing confirmed in-interview: this is an installed load-shedding design, not a failed merge. Ceding the shared world is cheaper than jointly governing it — no negotiation, no real-time modeling of her interior, no conflict-adjacent exposure. It may also explain why her circle of concern reads too-broad from his side ([the protected-category conflict](#sorting-the-components--the-walk)): territory not taken but given.

The handoff is a wartime constitution — forced, not chosen: "We've definitely talked about it, but it happened because it had to, not because we decided to do it that way. A consequence of work needing 120% from me just to get by." The talk ratified a fait accompli. The marriage's current constitution was written by his depletion — the same work history that ran the [identity collapse](identity-collapse.book-chapter.md).

The trap is self-protecting: wartime constitutions don't auto-expire when the war ends. Renegotiating the treaty *is* collaboration — gated at the very level the design was built to avoid needing. The skill required to revise the design is the skill the design exists to not need.

Desire is itself level-gated, and the clock is running: "I think there is desire there when my level is high enough, and I agree that it's collaboration work, which is why it has barely even started so far, despite being 18 months out from retirement." Appetite for joint governance appears at high safety; at low levels, relief-that-it's-hers. Retirement in roughly 18 months ends the 120% justification on a known date, and the renegotiation has barely started. One open probe sits under the desire claim: under the [discrete-self model](discrete-self.book-chapter.md), his preference about wanting territory back is a function of state — which self's preference is authoritative for planning the retirement-era constitution.

The authority rule he actually runs is stricter than a simple majority of days — a correction he made the next day: "I think I actually wait for an 80% supermajority to mitigate against the risk of backsliding. The first time I hit a majority at a new level, it often doesn't stick." The first majority at a new level is a spike, not a settlement. Constitutional changes — the marriage renegotiation among them — wait for 80% of days at level 5+ before the high-safety self's preference counts as settled enough to legislate from.

**Vulnerability/openness — disclosure native, exposure conflict-priced (level 4).** The final component, opened deliberately on a sick 2–3 day — workable because the component "takes level 4 to do, not to talk about." The sort has two moves. First, disclosure is not the active ingredient: he is radically disclosing — handed over the pain ratchet, the starved bucket, and the wartime constitution at level 2–3 while sick, and is publishing a book of his entire interior. Telling-about is cheap. Second, the cost is live exposure: "It's not the openness, it's the risk of conflict or rejection that makes it hard. That's why it's level 4, conflict level." Vulnerability = openness + standing in the blast radius of the other person's live reaction — and with no [simulator](missing-simulator.book-chapter.md) to pre-render how a disclosure lands, every consequential telling is a blind exposure. The level-4 price ([safety.md](safety.book-chapter.md)) buys the capacity to stand in that radius, not the words.

### The walk doubles as a hardware inventory

Sorting the components sorts the hardware (interviewer-supplied synthesis, confirmed in flow). Attraction needs no adapter — intact instinct. Affection needs amplification, not definition — the signal exists and runs quiet, the same shape as the [attention-budget](attention-budget.book-chapter.md) reading of interoception. Loyalty needed composition — an existing adapter plus named deltas. Presence needs only executive function. Responsiveness needs capacity routing — the concept is fine; the missing piece is a default execution path. Consideration needs an instrument swap — asking in place of the simulation that isn't running — plus an external trigger to fire the swap. Support splits across the inventory: partisan rides the loyalty compile, instrumental is likely native, and emotional support is blocked upstream by a [conditioned alarm](distress-contagion.book-chapter.md) plus a missing script selector. Interest has a native engine but no internal fuel line — it runs only on novelty the other person supplies, because the query generator that would manufacture novelty is simulator-blocked; the fix shape is an external generator, not a definition. Priority needs neither definition nor detection — the ranking is built and declared; what's missing is allocation (a funded people bucket) and signal delivery (a legible price tag on the trades that do happen). Collaboration is the most expensive entry: gated at or above the top of the safety scale, and blocked not by missing hardware but by an installed structure — the sovereignty handoff — whose revision requires the very skill it was built to avoid needing. Vulnerability needs no disclosure capacity — disclosure is native, even over-built; what level 4 buys is the capacity to stand in the live reaction. With the walk complete, no component landed genuinely absent — every one found working hardware somewhere in the stack. The bundle is heterogeneous: some components on native hardware, some sub-salience, some compiled, some capacity-gated — none, in the end, absent. That heterogeneity is itself a candidate answer for why "love" reads as fuzzy — the word mixes working, quiet, and gated channels under one name.

A second cut across the inventory emerged 2026-06-12, proposed in-interview and confirmed by Alan: **the recurring failure is detection, not execution.** Consideration's affects-her flag never auto-raises; responsiveness depends on recall that never fires unprompted; emotional support's alarm fires instantly but carries nothing about her interior to act on — detection of the cue without detection of the content; instrumental support executes natively but cannot see the unasked need; honesty expresses natively but cannot see when truth wounds; interest's engine fires when novelty arrives but cannot generate the questions that would surface it. The executing machinery is mostly intact, and the [missing simulator](missing-simulator.book-chapter.md) is the common root of the detection failures. The confirmation carries a qualifier that sets the work ahead: "I think that's a valid commonality here, though I still need conceptual bridges for each specific case." Shared diagnosis, separate compiles — one disease, thirteen bridges.

A third interviewer-supplied observation, recorded as a frame Alan found useful rather than his own claim: the candidate list partially re-derives Panksepp's primary affect systems independently — LUST→attraction, CARE→affection, SEEKING→interest, PANIC/GRIEF→the [loneliness signal](connection.book-chapter.md).

### One circuit, three ranges — the closing synthesis

The vulnerability sort closed the walk and unlocked its capstone compression, proposed in-interview and confirmed by Alan ("Yeah, that all tracks"). Conflict and rejection are someone's distress aimed at you — the exact stimulus class the [pre-six variable-ratio conditioning](distress-contagion.book-chapter.md) trained on. One conditioned circuit, firing at three ranges:

- **Her pain near him** → the support freeze — the alarm triages inward instead of toward her.
- **Her pain caused by him** → the [honesty ratchet](proximity-misattribution.book-chapter.md) — the pain lands during the disclosure moment and punishes telling.
- **Her pain about him** → the vulnerability gate — live exposure to conflict or rejection, priced at level 4.

Support, honesty, and vulnerability are the same alarm at increasing stakes. The compression: most of love's hard components are downstream of a single pre-six conditioning loop plus the [missing simulator](missing-simulator.book-chapter.md), with [safety level](safety.book-chapter.md) as the dial that quiets the alarm.
