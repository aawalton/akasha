
# Auditory-sensitivity bundle

> Auditory-sensitivity bundle as a system — quiet office, AirPods Pro with full ANC, manual relief by partial removal, dark-noise masking, layered defaults shaped by an asymmetric cost. Magnitude has dropped meaningfully as Safety has improved.

A coherent system across every noisy environment: keep the load reaching the nervous system as low as the situation allows, with a default that errs on the side of over-dampening. Promoted from a starter sub-system in [sub-systems.md](sub-systems.book-chapter.md) once enough captures named the bundle as one mechanism applied across modes. Naming and structure parallel the [visual-sensitivity bundle](visual-sensitivity-bundle.book-chapter.md).

## Protocol summary

Five layers, applied in order from the environment inward:

1. **Spatial.** Office in the basement at the farthest corner of the house — two layers of acoustic isolation (vertical via below-ground; horizontal via max distance from common areas). Designed, not lucky.
2. **Default tool.** AirPods Pro with full noise cancellation, on when leaving the office for noisier environments.
3. **Manual relief.** Pull one AirPod slightly out of the ear for transparency. Low-friction physical lever — beats fumbling with mode toggle.
4. **Masking.** Built-in iOS dark noise when ANC alone leaves residual that's still too costly. Used "sometimes," not by default.
5. **Tolerance scaling.** The same input costs less Stress Capacity at higher [Safety](safety.book-chapter.md). Improves over time as Safety recovers.

## Magnitude

Pre-AirPods, ambient sound without ear protection accounted for ~50% of total daily nervous-system cost — measured early in adoption against pre-AirPods baseline cost on comparable days, using the four-resource [Stress Capacity tracking](four-resource-model.book-chapter.md). The 50% baseline no longer holds today. Cost is still detectable occasionally but rare now.

The drop is not from better tools alone — it's primarily from Safety recovering. **Same input, less cost at higher Safety.** This is a cross-resource finding worth surfacing on its own (see [safety.md → Safety modulates sensory cost](safety/sensory-cost-modulation.book-chapter.md#safety-modulates-sensory-cost)) and parallel in shape to cycle 7's Health → Mana/Stamina coupling: a non-trivial direction in the cross-resource matrix that compounds with tool quality rather than being independent of it.

## Wear pattern: strategic, not continuous

AirPods are not always on. They have their own cost.

- **AirPods own-pain mechanism.** Consistent pressure from the ear-tip seal against the ear canal. Cumulative — gets worse the longer worn. Lying on that side accelerates it (head pressure stacks with seal pressure). Wear sessions are time- and position-bounded.
- **Saved for higher-noise environments.** The quiet office is the home base; AirPods come on when leaving it.

Bounded use is the structural reason the protocol is layered rather than single-tool: AirPods cannot run all day, so the spatial layer (quiet office) and the masking layer (dark noise) carry the parts of the day AirPods can't.

## Default mode and manual relief

**Default mode is full noise cancellation** (max dampening). The four-tier preference hierarchy ([sensory-regulation-tools.md](sensory-regulation-tools.book-chapter.md)) ranks automatic > flexible > on/off > nothing — yet for AirPods Alan defaults to full-on ANC rather than letting the system adapt.

**The asymmetric cost shape modifies which tier is optimal.** Under-dampening costs much more than over-dampening, so default-max is the safer choice. Auditory load is high enough and high-frequency that misjudgment at the low end taxes Stress Capacity directly; misjudgment at the high end loses some ambient awareness but doesn't drain the bar. Default to the protective end; relieve manually when needed.

**Manual relief: pull an AirPod slightly out of one ear.** Physical lever, no UI traversal. Concrete instance of the "flexible" tier applied with physical UI rather than a software toggle. The combination — full-ANC default plus partial-removal relief — is two tiers stacked, with default at "automatic-max" and override at "flexible-physical."

## Triggers

- **Primary: raw decibels** (ambient volume).
- **Specific timbres independent of volume:** pencils, chalk. Even quiet pencil scratching costs. Inferred mechanism: high-frequency dry-friction component is sensory-keyed rather than volume-keyed.
- **ANC asymmetry against trigger type.** AirPods Pro cancel low-frequency continuous noise (HVAC, traffic) much better than sudden high-frequency impulses (pencils on paper, chalk on board) — sudden high-frequency content is physically harder to cancel. The specific-timbre triggers help less than the volume triggers do.

## Masking layer

When ANC alone leaves residual that's still too costly, **iOS dark noise** stacks on top. Used "sometimes" — when needed, not default. Helps significantly when it runs.

The auditory stack is layered: silent ANC by default; ANC + dark noise when residual is too costly. Dark noise is a masker, not a canceller — it raises the noise floor uniformly so the residual high-frequency triggers stop standing out.

## Spatial strategy

The office is intentionally designed for quietness. Basement + farthest corner from common areas — two independent layers of acoustic isolation. Designed in advance; load-bearing on the protocol because it lets AirPods be reserved for elsewhere.

**Music for enjoyment via office speakers, not AirPods.** Office is quiet enough that speakers work. Clean separation: AirPods = cost-reducer used in noisier environments; speakers = content delivery used in the quiet base. The two never collide.
