
# Closure rituals

A closure ritual is an explicit, repeatable action taken after an exposure event that gives the cognitive system falsifiable evidence the exposure source is contained.

## Mechanism

Aftershock-tail decay is passive — the underlying load evaporates with neglect (see [safety.md → Aftershock dynamics after exposure damage](safety/aftershock-dynamics.book-chapter.md#aftershock-dynamics-after-exposure-damage)). What sustains the tail is the system's residual scanning for re-exposure. A closure ritual gives the cognitive system something concrete to verify: *the exposure source is no longer reachable.* Verification reduces the scanning load, which shortens the tail.

The pathway is aphantasia-compatible (see [safety.md → Aphantasia constraint on intervention design](safety/aphantasia-constraint.book-chapter.md#aphantasia-constraint-on-intervention-design)): no felt-sense decoding is required. The closure action is observable, the post-action state is verifiable, and the cognitive system can update its threat model against external reality rather than against an introspective signal.

## Worked example: Instagram-deletion after self-exposure

Alan uses Instagram occasionally as a regulation tool. The feed is generally safe, but politically-distressing content sometimes slips through. After such a slip, the closure ritual is to delete Instagram from the phone — the exposure channel is now physically unreachable from the device. The cognitive system can verify: *the app is gone.* Re-exposure via this channel cannot happen until the app is reinstalled, which takes deliberate action.

The closure is not about willpower or avoidance of urge — it is structural. The verification is what changes the system's scanning load, not the absence of urge.

## Protocol shape

A closure ritual qualifies when:

- The action is **specific and repeatable** — not a vague intention but a concrete operation that can be done the same way every time.
- The post-action state is **observable and verifiable** — the cognitive system can confirm the new state against external reality.
- The action **structurally limits re-exposure** — re-exposure now requires deliberate effort to undo the closure, not just attention drift.

Vague resolutions (*I'll be more careful*) do not qualify; they produce no verifiable state change.
