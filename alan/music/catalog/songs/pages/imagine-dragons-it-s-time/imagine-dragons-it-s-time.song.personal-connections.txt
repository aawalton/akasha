Does NOT resonate — and the non-resonance is diagnostic. The song's two load-bearing themes both fall flat for Alan, almost laughably: (1) staying attached to your hometown/people — he can't form emotional attachments at all, to anything or anyone (no affective store); (2) being the same person as you grow — he doesn't feel like the same person minute to minute, much less year to year (present-tense, continuously-overwritten self). It's an anthem of constancy written for a kind of self he isn't running; nearly science fiction to him. A clean example of why so little mainstream pop, saturated with roots/constancy/the unchanging core, reaches his autobiographical center.
