The first Em Beihold song Alan ever heard — it hit so hard he still can't sing the whole thing without tears. She nailed his experience. Superficial details are wrong (he's never driven in LA), but the HEART is exact: the feeling, and above all the reaching out — hoping against hope that there are other people who feel the same. He lived that for ~20 years. Still there sometimes, but much less than before (the recovery arc — 'starting to feel alive again'). Hearing the song WAS the me-too he'd been reaching for: a song built out of hoping someone else feels this, that found the person who felt exactly this.
