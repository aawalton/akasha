The lowercase IS the thesis — the song refuses to capitalize/announce itself; the typography enacts the acceptance (trying that gets no trophy, just is). He reads the form, not only the words. Reframe offered: his 'serial quitting' isn't the opposite of the song — by his own rule (don't do what you can't afford), stepping back to rest is AFFORDABILITY, not failure; 'this is me resting so I can afford the next try' is the same acceptance from the other side. He's already living the wisdom; he just doesn't credit it because it looks like giving up. What he hasn't reached isn't the trying — it's the SAFETY: the song is written against his 40-year perfection-as-the-only-safe-place belief, so it reads as a postcard from a Safety level he's climbing toward (where trying, not perfection, is safe enough to rest in) but isn't standing on yet. 'Doesn't feel safe yet' names the exact distance left — and he's moving ('starting to feel alive again').
