Loves that the title is all lowercase and reads it as deliberate. Hears painful ACCEPTANCE that 'trying' is still failure-prone and flawed — we can't be perfect, we'll still make mistakes and say things we regret, it's hard, but at least we're still trying. Lands SOME, not fully: Alan considers himself a 'serial quitter' who takes solace in NOT trying for a while, giving up to rest, and isn't sure he's reached the song's level of acceptance. He held a deep 40-year belief that 'not only is perfection safe, it's the best I can do.' So this calm, lowercase song is still ASPIRATION for him — something to someday achieve. 'Acceptance of this is me trying doesn't feel safe yet.'
