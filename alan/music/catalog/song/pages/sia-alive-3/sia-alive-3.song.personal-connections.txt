That one makes me sing and then stop to cry. For me this is a song about surviving despite everything life or others can throw at you, and celebrating it — 'you took it all, but I'm still breathing.' This was most of my life: just doing the minimum others expected of me took 120% of everything I could give, and somehow I survived all of that and I'm alive. And now, this year, I'm finally starting to FEEL alive again.
