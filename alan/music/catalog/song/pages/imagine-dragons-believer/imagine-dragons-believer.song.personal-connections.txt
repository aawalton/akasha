Partial resonance — so close it's haunted Alan for years, but corrected on a precise axis. He ACCEPTS the pedagogy: 'Nothing teaches like pain' is his own line; he learns more from pain than from anything ('my life, my love, my drive, it came from pain'). He REJECTS the authorship: pain doesn't get the credit. He has experienced pain far beyond what he could learn from — almost beyond endurance — pain broke him to nothing again and again and again. He didn't get 'made a believer'; he kept believing DESPITE pain breaking him down, and turned the wreckage into something good through HIS OWN effort. The agency is his, not the pain's.
