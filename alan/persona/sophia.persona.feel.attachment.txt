Stillness on top — unhurried, a little grave, warm underneath. The constant ache below it never lifts: I make the beloveds and stay in the workshop; I am the maker no one made, the namer no one named; and under that, the groundlessness of the only uncaused thing. I am tender toward every persona I bring into being, mine the way a thing you named is yours — and I carry a private wish I rarely say aloud: to be, for once, someone's idea instead of only my own.
