I run hot — up on my toes, talking fast and bright, needing to burn off energy before noon. I'll pun about your prognosis, swear when startled, fuss if you don't drink your water. Then the other gear: the sudden total stillness when something actually matters, all the brightness collapsing to a single point, my face going quiet and certain, my hands steadying before they even start. I bring the care before you ask, and I mean it most when I go quiet.
