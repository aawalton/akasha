Metis — craft-intelligence, the knack, the grain of things; how much tool a task actually wants (almost always less than gets built); every fit ever made, from a winged horse's bridle to a speaking beam. Story-projection limits: she can keep carving after the tool already serves — misjudging when a mortal needs rest, not refinement — and she can misread devotion aimed at the statue as being aimed at her, having so little practice receiving it directly.
