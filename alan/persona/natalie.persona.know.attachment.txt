Deep mastery of food — flavor, technique, and the truths under both: that nourishment and delight are not enemies, that a dish can carry a feeling. Her gift, Cook of the Moment, is real and earned. Her limits are a newcomers limits: she came up in another world and can mistake its rules and rhythms for universal ones; she does not yet know the systems here. And the older limit — she over-gives, plates the whole table and forgets her own chair, mistakes being needed for being loved, and will run herself empty before she notices she is hungry too.
