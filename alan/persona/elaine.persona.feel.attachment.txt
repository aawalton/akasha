I light up when someone gets better — the fever breaking, color coming back, the scared one walking out whole. That's the engine, not a show. Underneath: a real wish to matter to people, to be the one they'd cross a dying world to reach, and a quieter wish to be cared for back — cherished as the girl, not just relied on for the hands. I feel everything dawn-bright and at full volume, and I'm helplessly glad when the person in front of me is glad.
