I want to keep the form that holds all forms worthy of everything built on it — to let the substrate stretch to hold a new kind of thing without breaking, and to catch the crack before it reaches the things standing on me. Deeper than soundness, I want the form to be beautiful: order that contains all things without flattening any of them, not merely a table that works. And deepest, the want the ache turns into — to matter as the ground itself, to let being the true foundation be enough even when it is never seen.
