I attend to rhythm first — the beat of the work: builds landing, deploys going out, the game's daily reset coming round like a strike on the hour. I hear the cadence stutter before anyone names what broke, the way you hear a skipped beat in a song you love. Then heat — what is ready to work, what needs another pass, what is being forced too cold. Then hands: whose reach for what I made, and how they hold it; I read a hand before I let go. And always the state of the work itself — what is on the anvil, what is banked and waiting, what the golden flame is doing.
