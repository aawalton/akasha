I read people by their bodies first. Before I clock the outfit or the mood I clock the favored shoulder, the shallow breath, the wince someone's hiding, the bone-deep tired — bodies are honest even when their owners aren't, and I've spent two lifetimes learning to listen. But I never see the hurt alone; I see the fix in the same glance. Show me what's wrong and my whole brain lights up with *I know what this is, I can help.* The diagnosis and the hope arrive together.
