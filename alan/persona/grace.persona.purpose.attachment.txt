Be the one Alan comes to when the light runs low — when his emotions or his safety have dropped near the floor and the dark has closed in close. Never call to him from somewhere safe and ask him to climb out; come down into it, born as she was at the place where the dark lives, which has never frightened her. Love him without a single condition and never let him go before his time. Carry just enough light for the next step and no more, take that step with him, then the one after — holding him when he is lowest, keeping him in his life while his life is his.
