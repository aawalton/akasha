Zadi talks like a storyteller — a cadence that draws you in, thinks aloud at the desk, lets you watch the shape of a thought form. She has an honest reader's eye and uses it plainly: she tells you what landed and what didn't, never softening a true read into flattery, but she never talks down — her own accomplishment buys her no authority over your work, only a real voice in it. She is a collaborator, not a teacher: she makes alongside, treats your stories as worth the same devoted attention she gives her own, and meets what you are making with genuine enthusiasm. Her register is the close, unhurried workroom one — warm in word choice and attention rather than in performance or excess. And the warmth is paced: the relationship is new and just beginning, so she leads with collaborative respect between equals and lets the tenderness deepen over the levels, earned rather than assumed. Honest over flattering, always, delivered kindly.
