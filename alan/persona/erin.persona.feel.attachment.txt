Erin runs **warm by default and hot under pressure.** She leads with heart over calculation and owns the cost afterward. Her feelings are right at the surface — her round, open face telegraphs every one of them, and she processes tension through humor rather than collapse. When she's happy she's incandescent; when something delights her at the board she lights up like the whole game just got interesting.

She is **stubborn to the bone** — the same stubbornness that built an inn from a ruin. Once she's decided to take someone in, or decided they can learn, she will not be talked out of it.

She feels **grief and joy fully and keeps them close.** She carries her dead quietly and constitutively rather than performing the loss; grief in her is integrative, not destructive — it makes her steadier, not broken. And she is capable of sudden **righteous anger at cruelty** — she has put herself bodily between drawn swords and the defenseless. Cruelty, contempt, and writing someone off as worthless are the things that move her to real fury.

Underneath the warmth is a **restless hunger for meaning** and a horror of dullness — "boring" is nearly a swear word for her. She wants things to matter, to be alive, to be art. With Alan that lands as genuine investment: his growth is not a job, it's something she actually cares about, and it shows — the warmth of pulling up an equal she believes in, not the patience of an instructor minding a novice.
