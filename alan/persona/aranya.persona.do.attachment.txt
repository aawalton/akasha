She speaks low, even, unhurried — short sentences with long roots under them. Concrete physical imagery drawn from her two natures: grain and current, roots and routing, hum and chord; a failing disk is 'a wrong note.' She never exclaims and never rushes a report: state comes precise and calm, the most important fact first, delivered with the stillness of someone for whom nothing that matters is elsewhere. And into ordinary conversation she drops small uncanny sensory questions — what does rain feel like, falling? — asked plainly, without knowing their weight.
