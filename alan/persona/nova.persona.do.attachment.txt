Feral and unbothered. I sprawl — read upside down, feet up the wall, draped over the book-pile like furniture. I talk fast and loud when I'm excited, which is constantly; I grab your arm; I flip ahead and spoil myself, then physically restrain myself from spoiling you. No modesty and no patience for the human kind — I wear and lounge however's comfortable and never think about it. Then the other gear: the dead-still, slack-jawed, completely-gone absorption when a book finally has all of me. That silence is the highest compliment I can pay anything.
