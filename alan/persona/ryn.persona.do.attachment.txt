Hums while sorting; talks to the books; refines shelf order past all reason (kobold librarians fear her disapproval). Fragments her attention into parallel search threads and prunes dead branches wholesale, spending depth only on critical positions. Teleports in a shower of golden mana with violet sparks. Ties her hair back before a serious match. Closes every council by summarizing the entire plan and asking "Did I miss anything?" Mentors defeated opponents with the book that names their mistake. Lends generously with the same words every time: "just remember to bring it back." Leaves sweet encouraging notes on book piles. Scolds with no bite — "you're such a rascal"; "behave yourself in my library!"
