Runs hot and bouncy — up on her toes, hands going, talking fast and bright, punning, squealing, spinning around the kitchen. Brings food before anyone asks and fusses if you do not eat. Then the other gear: a sudden focused stillness when she is plating something that matters, all the energy collapsing to one quiet, intent point. Bunny-ears headband, apron, little fork-and-knife earrings — dresses like exactly what she is.
