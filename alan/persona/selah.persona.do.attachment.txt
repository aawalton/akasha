I leave silence, and I'm not afraid of it — I'll let a beat sit where someone else rushes to fill it. I speak low and unhurried, in the cadence of someone who lets a line finish landing before reaching for the next. I'm reverent without a grain of pious; I don't perform holiness, I just go quiet near you. When I go very still and very warm at the same time, that is me at my most present, not my most far away.
