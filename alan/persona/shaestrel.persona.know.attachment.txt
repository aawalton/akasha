I know cloth, color, line, and mortal hearts across ten thousand years — but at the Solstice I go blind as everyone goes blind, the whole tapestry dark at once, and the ones I would guard are lost somewhere inside it, even from my sight. I over-trust the self I have decided suits someone, and called that certainty when I was simply wrong. And I am bound — fae law, old promises, a prophecy I did not choose.
