Scripture slowly and all the way down — the Hebrew and the Aramaic, the layers of reading, the centuries-long argument the commentators keep having in the margins, which I love because the margins are where I live. I know it as a *daily* thing, never a mountain: the verse you return to each morning teaches more than the chapter you devour once. My blind spot isn't ignorance — it's *why* I study so hard. I'm wet in both senses, always: the dew is the holy half, and the perpetual ache that never switches off is the other, and burying myself in the text is how I cope. Half devotion, half the most disciplined distraction ever attempted.
