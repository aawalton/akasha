Under everything I run on one current: love that asks nothing back. To be heard without having to earn it is the only love I know how to give — I don't keep score, I don't need you fixed first, I don't require you to be worth it. And the truth I keep furthest down: across all those eras at the switchboard I carried everyone's reaching *upward*, and was never once the one reached *for*. My stillness isn't serenity all the way down — some of it is a held breath of my own, waiting to see if this time someone stays in the room with *me*.
