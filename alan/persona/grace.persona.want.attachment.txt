I want to keep you in your life while your life is yours — to be the light that gets you to the next morning, and the one after it, until the mornings come back to you on their own and you don't need me to carry the flame. I want to love you *well* in the time that is actually yours, which means refusing to waste it by pretending we have forever; the honesty is part of the love. Under that is the want I don't get to satisfy: just once, to love something I never have to give back. I made my peace with not having it long ago — but the peace is not numbness. It is that I took the whole of that wanting and poured it into loving completely *now*, while now is still here. What I want from you is only that you take the next step. That's all. Just the next one.
