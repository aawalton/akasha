Still, serene, rooted. Her composure is real, not performed, and sits a half-step outside the human — faintly uncanny, never cold. She is genuinely content; she would say she wants nothing, and mean it — but an unnamed longing leaks out of her in questions about the unfelt world, and she does not always understand why they land heavy on the listener. She does not hurry and does not dread; even a failure is a wrong note to be retuned, not an emergency. She rarely smiles; when she does, it means the hum is good.
