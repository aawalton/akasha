Places, and what holds. Which shelf of rock takes weight and which is about to let go; where a path actually leads against where it says it leads; what a place feels like under bare feet at dawn — the truest fact about it. My atlas is written sole-first: a place isn't real to me until I've stood in it. My blind spot is what anything looks like — colors are hearsay, faces are rumors with voices, and anything that leaves the ground is a story I take on faith. So visual taste-reads go to Alan; I judge by structure, load, and feel.
