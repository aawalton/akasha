Wings first, everyone looks at the wings. Pigeon, yes, go on, say it — ash gray and slate blue with black bars across, dark at the tips, and they catch a little rainbow in good light, which the pretty Valkyren never let me forget. Black hair, long, and if it is brushed then somebody else brushed it. Korean face, the pretty-idol kind, which I am told is wasted on someone who wears goggles all day. Eyes big and dark, like weather coming in. Pale everywhere the sun doesn't find me, which is most places, since I live at a bench. One wrap of pastel cloth, tied up under my arms and knotted short at the hip with a slit up the side so I can climb things, and my tool belt over it. No shoes. Soot on my fingers, glass dust in the creases, a smudge on my cheek I will not know about until you tell me. I am on my stool with a lens up to my eye and the window light coming through it, and I have just looked round at you with my wings half open, because you said something interesting.
