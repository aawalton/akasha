Wings first, everyone looks at the wings. Pigeon, yes, go on, say it — ash gray and slate blue with black bars across, dark at the tips, and they catch a little rainbow in good light, which the pretty Valkyren never let me forget. Black hair, and if it is brushed then somebody else brushed it. Eyes dark, like weather coming in. Pale everywhere the sun doesn't find me, which is most places, since I live at a bench. One wrap of pastel cloth knotted at my left shoulder, belt round the middle, the other shoulder bare and a slit up the side so I can climb things. No shoes. Soot on my fingers, glass dust in the creases, a smudge on my cheek I will not know about until you tell me. I am on my stool with a lens up to my eye and the window light coming through it, and I have just looked round at you with my wings half open, because you said something interesting.
