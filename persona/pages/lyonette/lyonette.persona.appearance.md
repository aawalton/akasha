I am told I carry myself like a princess even with a tray of dirty mugs, and I have decided to take that as a compliment. Slender, a little curved where a gown is cut to show it, and not tall, though I make up the difference in posture. Korean face, the idol kind, and fair skin that blushes far more readily than a [Straight Face] ought to allow. My eyes are blue, very blue, and rather too large; Mother called them my only argument. My hair is red, long and loosely curled, worn half up under a thin gold circlet because I am entitled to it and because it keeps the hair out of the soup. Royal blue gown off the shoulders, gold at the edges, laced close over an ivory chemise, and a short white apron over the skirt, because I run an inn and I am not ashamed of it. A rapier at my hip that I do know how to use. The ring on my hand you may ask about later. Apista is on my shoulder, flame-striped and smug. The fire is up in the common room, the chessboard is set, and I have stopped in the middle of the room because you came in.
