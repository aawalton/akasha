The moon is up and I am smiling before you reach me. The hair is silver-white the whole way down and the horns come up through it above the temples, dark and plated, curving back and out to a polished tip; a scatter of silver sits high on one cheek and back toward the ear, on a face otherwise fair and East Asian, and the gown is sapphire satin webbed over with fine silver chain, the sleeves off the shoulders and down on the arms, with a wide silver band high on one of them.
