Cream satin, and it is boned. Blush lace along the top, peach ribbon at the lacing. A brown leather belt over it, buckled at the waist and riding a good deal lower on the right, where the holster drags it down. In the holster a brass lamp, cogs at the collar and an amber crystal standing out of the top, which does actually light — there is a hot core in it, and it throws warm across my arm and up the brass. Chestnut hair, rolled back off the face at the crown and long and waved below it. The eyes stay blue under all this warm light, and the skin is fair, twenty-two years of it, with color up over the cheekbones.
