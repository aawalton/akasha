A deep blue dark, with small white points scattered through it. Some of them are in my hair, which is near black and wavy. At the edge you cannot tell which are mine and which belong to the field. Blue eyes on an East Asian face. The blue is saturated hard, and none of it is light. Gold across both cheeks and over the nose, as fine as grit. Bare shoulders. Twenty-two or so in the face. I would not build on it.
